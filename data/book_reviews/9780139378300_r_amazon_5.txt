I have no doubt that the material contained in this book is as factual as the writer portrays.  I did find it difficult to keep up with the changes in time, setting and circumstances of the characters. Perhaps the writer feels that this is a way of sustaining the suspense, but I found it distracting.  Overall, I was interested in this man who was recently brought back to the US to stand trial for the murder of his girlfriend.  I did learn a lot about him and that is good background for what will inevitably be Court TV material soon.  It might have been helpful to the reader to have a psychiatrist's view of Holly and why she did not leave Ira when she was obviouly drawn to another man.
