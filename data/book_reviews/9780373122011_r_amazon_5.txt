The premise is a little different. The male characters are still rather cardboard. Ms. Bianchin favors ruthless alpha males. The language is a bit less awkward than usual, though I've only read three of her works. Production potboiler writers seem to hook their stories to odd details. Roberts to her characterizations, Neels to her road trips, cars, furniture and food. Bianchin is always on about bathing and fresh underwear. And 'killer heels', usually black. It's a quick read.
