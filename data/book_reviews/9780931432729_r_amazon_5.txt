The title of this "older" book sounds like one of those get rich quick shams. Or worse, the only person getting rich is the author trying to make money of naive readers. But Mark Fisher's two books, this one and "Millionaire's Secret" genuinely helped me to focus on my priorities. They served as catalysts to grow my then new business into a successful venture and live a happier life because of better life choices.As with all books, timing and the context of our own psychological conditions often determine the benefits we reap from reading. I reordered these two books digitally to read them again since I'm starting a new business and personal adventure.
