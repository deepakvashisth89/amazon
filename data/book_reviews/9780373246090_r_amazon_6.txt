I hate to see this series end. I need to check and see if our author wrote another book featuring the aunt who matched her nephews to the right woman.  Both our heroine and our hero have have hang ups from the past to overcome. Love triumphs as you cheer for both finding their mojo. What I like about re-reading this set of romances is already knowing the end. I can enjoy each novel in its entirety page by pave.
