As a long-term Lonely-Planet fan I instinctively bought this when planning my Trans-Siberian trip.  Although reading other reports it seems the Trailblazer version is a fraction better (it has more photos!) I actually can't see a huge difference myself between the two - I bought both to compare!  Either one would really be sufficient, so if you like Lonely Planet, stick with the comfortable format and style.  For a bit more detail and info then the Trailblazer is probably better.  They are almost the same size and weight so no benefit there for either one.  All this is said before going on the trip itself, so the conclusion may be dicisive at the other end.Read moreRead less
