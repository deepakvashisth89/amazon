A mansion of the wealthy, assorted guests and gruesome murder.  Christianna Brand takes these standard ingredients and gives a good example of how to make a mystery pie. Two handsome men vying for the love of a beautiful young woman, a near neighbor pining for of one them, a guest of the neighbors wanting, what ?; locked doors, snow on the ground, and murder. There may be some weakness in the structure of the story, but this should make little difference for the devotee of classic mysteries.  The author is a professional, she writes well, clearly and with no literary baggage unnecessary to the story line.  Her Inspector Cockrill is not among the elite of Poirot, Wimsey and the like, but does fulfill his function as the intrusive presence of the law.
