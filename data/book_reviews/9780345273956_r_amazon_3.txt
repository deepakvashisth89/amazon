A great review of the life of the "forgotten" Kennedy. Joe is always mentioned in JFK's and Kennedy family  bios, but it is always a secondary character, the one-to-be that never was, a man in the shadows.This book brings Joe to life, and it includes testimonies from many people who actually knew him and who are often neglected.One get another picture of Joe: witty, loyal and hot-tempered. THe family guy and the womanizer. The loyal brother (yes, even to John).His premature death was a loss for his family but, I guess, also for America, as his political sight, even as embryonic, seems to have been far more complex that his brother's.The whole book is a pleasure, and the author deserves full credit for a great work!!Read moreRead less
