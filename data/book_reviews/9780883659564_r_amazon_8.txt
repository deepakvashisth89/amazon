This record of John F. Kennedy's assassination, originally sought by the Kennedy family themselves, is both profoundly moving and yet difficult to read.  These memories are keenly recalled by all who could muster the strength to relive and talk about those dark days in late November 1963.While much has been written and reported since those painful days, now more than 50 years in our past, this telling still packs an emotional punch. I reread it every year, and every time, I wipe tears from my eyes. Perhaps it should become required reading in History classes across this country.It is an telling, oftentimes heart-clenching, personal revelation of grief, shock, and the immediate struggle to understand an horrific experience in an turbulent period of our country's history.Read moreRead less
