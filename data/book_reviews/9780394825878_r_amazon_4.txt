I read this when I was probably age 11/12? Something stuck with me and I searched online with only the faintest idea of the plot. Lo and behold, THIS is IT!!! I ordered it and read it in one evening and it is even better than I remembered! This is a great book to give to any child or even an adult looking to pass the time. Set in pre-war Europe this story shows how a 'privileged', wealthy young girl who trades places with her newly acquainted friend( an orphan) learns to view life from a different perspective and by the end the reader can clearly see who comes out 'richer'.Read moreRead less
