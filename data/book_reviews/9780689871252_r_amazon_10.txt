I remember this book as being the first book that ever made me sad when I came to the end of it. I always remembered the name (from nearly 30 years ago) so decided to get it to persuade my 12 year old daughter to read it. Alas kids are different in this day and she was more interested in Diary of A Wimpy Kid so I thought I'd read it again - well I couldn't put it down - it is a fabulous story of good and evil - sometimes quite scary but eventually a good ending - although I still can't believe she doesn't find her parents in the end. I was totally 'into' this book (I normally read Linwood Barclay or Greg Iles) if you can get your child to read it - go for it - I am still going to try with my daughter - as I know she will love the descriptions and how the story keeps you guessing right until the end - I honestly thought I was there through the story and 12 again!Read moreRead less
