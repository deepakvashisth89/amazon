Hallie seemed like a very strong character.  She totally acted like one would think a wronged woman would react.  She was trying to balance her work life with her emotional side.  Very hard.  While she seemed like a go getter and put work ahead of things her husband decided to get a little on the side . . . from her freakin' best friend and co-worker who knew where she was.  The husband, Jake, came across as a whiny, sniveling, back-boneless male who seemed to only think of his own desires and wants.  The co-worker so called best friend Olivia was the stereotypical do anything to get to the top kind of person.  Frankly surprised that Hallie would have become friends with her knowing this about her.  Perhaps she just felt sorry about Olivia's life and was being charitable.  I couldn't believe Hallie's mom was so quick to forgive Jake but not Olivia as it takes two to have the affair.  Especially knowing that Jake took Olivia to his wife and his special retreat house and bed.  Perhaps it would have been easier to forgive Jake if it was the one time thing but he continued on with the affair all through the book.  Then suddenly we are to believe that Hallie was willing to forgive all because of a conversation with her mother who basically said that Hallie has to take some blame for the affair.  Sure . . . maybe for the initial fling but come on that guy continued on with Olivia knowing that she was "free with her favors" to so many other people. If she thought it would benefit her in some way she was very eager.Hallie's mom even admitted that Jake always has someone else but that doesn't mean that he cares about the someone else.  Hhhmmmm . . . is that supposed to mean that he cares about Hallie then?  If he cared about Hallie why would he continue to hurt her the way that he did?  See I don't buy the resolution of this story.  If you are looking for a strong woman who manages to pull herself up by her bootstraps and carry on after a damaging betrayal then this is for you.  She was amazing that way.  But if you are looking for a romance book with everything all happily ever after no questions left then don't bother.  Because this reader is left wondering when Jake will stray again.  Perhaps the story of Jake and Hallie's relationship should have ended and he could have been left standing outside looking in until he grew up because I just don't see that he grew up at all and a so called HEA with these too was totally unbelievable.
