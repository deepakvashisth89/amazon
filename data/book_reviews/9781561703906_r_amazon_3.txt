I loved this book. Although the approach and style of writing is very simple (i.e., not sophisticated), I really enjoyed the first half that describes Doreen's upbringing and her struggles as a single mother trying to find love and spirituality, as well as the second half where she provides tips on finding your own path in a loving and guiding tone. I underlined a lot of sections of the book that were meaningful to me. This book is absolutely an important read if you are a lightworker--come on, you know if you are. If you are even wondering if you are, or if you feel you should be doing something important with your life, you ARE a lightworker. I think and hope there are many more of us than we can really imagine!If you don't come from a spiritual or mystical perspective, you will need to suspend your disbelief a little to go along with Doreen's stories and points (not a problem for me personally) but I don't think you need to believe every single word is fact to be inspired and guided by her points.My one complaint is that since I'm not Christian myself, I was a little turned off by Doreen's Christian-sounding language and frame of reference. The fact that she is so careful to make clear that Jesus and Christianity are just one way of looking at spirituality definitely helps make the Christian references go down easier. While I read, I would substitute words that are meaningful to me in place of the words that turned me off, i.e., Spirit instead of God or Him.I've now read a bunch of Doreen's books and this is the best. And definitely the place to start.Read moreRead less
