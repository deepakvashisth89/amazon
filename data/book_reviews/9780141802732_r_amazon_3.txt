Except for a number of grammatical/spelling errors (really, the same for most of Follett's books so far, like farther instead of further -- you'd think he could afford a good copy editor), this book was stunning. It was the cause of a couple of "lacka-sleepa" nights because it's difficult to put down. It's fast-paced, adrenaline-surging, and suspensful. The plot is believable, and because it's based on the global amnesia of the main character, Luke, its plot is wonderfully opaque. A must-read for spy thriller-loving readers.Read moreRead less
