There is an alternative to the worldview that achieving more and more is the end-all of education and human satisfaction.  Let the poet, James Kavanaugh, introduce you to a high flying pelican, Harry Langendorf, who shares a wisdom about relationships which leads to greater happiness than compiling ever greater achievements. Without discounting the value of flying high and far, Kavanaugh, through this tender love story, teaches us what matters most during our brief lives. At the end of your life, what would you imagine to be your best final message to all those who knew you?  Harry Langendorf offers us a deeply meaningful possibility.  If you enjoy the shore, you will never look at pelicans the same . . . ever.  This is a book with a lifetime impact on your point of view.Read moreRead less
