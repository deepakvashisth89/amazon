I hadn't read anything by Ruth Rendell for a few years, but read many books by her, and her pseudonym-counterpart Barbara Vine, once upon a time. Motivated by her recent death, I took on this one, and was immediately, richly reminded of her abundant writerly gifts and how well she employed them.  The book--characters-plot-writing quality--is an absolute pleasure and made me feel grateful to be a reader, and a Rendell reader.  The skillful task of conveying the mindsets of such a diverse cast, the mix of unflinching reality and a measure of compassion for the good, the awful, and the unbalanced, we are fortunate indeed that Ms. Rendell/Vine wrote, and did it so well. If you want a well-paced, intricate, fully-realized mystery, avail yourself of this excellent book.Read moreRead less
