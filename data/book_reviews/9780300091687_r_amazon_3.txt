This is a beautiful volume on Hesse.  Elizabeth Sussman, a wonderful curator and Hesse scholar, organized this exhibition and this is the catalogue. The design, images and layout are just wonderful, the only thing that is somewhat off is the concentration on her earlier work.  Her paintings especially are widely featured and those are not nearly as transcendent and absolute as her later work.  Her great accomplishment as a major artist was done when she "left" the canvas.  The book does not present it as such; I find Lippard's book "Eva Hesse" to be the very best available.
