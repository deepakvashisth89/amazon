A Portrait of the Artist as a Young Man by James Joyce presents the story of Stephen Dedalus from his childhood to his early adulthood, culminating in his departure from Ireland. The story focuses on how Stephen transforms to achieve what he perceives as freedom from the nets (language, religion, and nationality) that hold back his soul. The complexity and stylistic level of the narration changes as Stephen matures. This novel is excellent due to its story, language, and the interpretations it invites.Read moreRead less
