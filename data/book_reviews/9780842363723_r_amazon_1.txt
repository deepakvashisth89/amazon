This book was so hard to put down! It was so exciting! Frank Peretti writes in a way that makes the pages come alive. You can picture the scenes as if watching a movie. I would describe it as entertaining but it is instructive as well. I have a much greater appreciation and understanding of the importance of prayer and the workings of God in this earth and in our lives. It has ignited in me a profound desire to pray like never before. There is a spiritual world that we cannot see with our human eyes but if dare we can see it with our spiritual ones. There are angels all around us that need our prayers to carry out the will of the Father. I want to be a part of that! This was a great read. I highly recommend it!Read moreRead less
