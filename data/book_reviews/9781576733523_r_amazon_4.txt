Most interesting book I have ever read. The storyline is incredible with very well developed characters who come from all lifestyles that are quite normal.  They vary in ages from young adult to retirement ages & would easily represent any slice of our population.  In other words, they are just your average people. Yet, for some unknown reasons, each receives an invitation for a week of personal time at what they perceive as a resort all fully prepaid.  People tend to like freebies. So, through their own tribulations,  each one finds a way to reach the destination site. That's when the story really gets interesting. The book is set at a fast pace & full of action so it never gets boring. Well worth reading!Read moreRead less
