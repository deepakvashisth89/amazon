I ordered this book knowing that it was used, and would likely have some mild damage.  Though it was not as pristine as a new book, there was very little wear on the binding, and was exactly as described.  Shipping was very reasonable and arrived before expected.  I would definitely order from this bookseller again.The book itself is a great resource for any of us still clinging to the Old World of Darkness game environment.  It has a number of familiar merits and flaws, as well as basic information on some of the bloodlines.
