This nice story about children preparing for and later performing a play about the biblical story of Esther is delightful and a good way to teach children about the holiday Purim that commemorates Esther and Mordechai saving Jews from evil Haman. The story is well-written and is suspenseful. The drawings are warm and very colorful. The book includes pages after the story; one The Story of Purim, one How Purim is Celebrated, with the final one being a recipe for Apricot-Orange Hamantascen.The children of a Jewish family celebrate Purim each year, among many other things, by having a Purim play. Two cousins who usually participate are sick and a substitute is needed. Father says that a widow is coming this year and she can play evil Haman.
