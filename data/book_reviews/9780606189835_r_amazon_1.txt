This was another great book from Arthur C. Clarke. Only the main character returns in physical form. The story was very interesting. Also interesting that some of the ideas written in this book actually turned out to be real and by the real people who the author refers to. It would seem that Arthur C. Clarke was able to envision things of the future, much like H.G.Wells. Unfortunately, many of the dates have already passed, but who knows if they may still come to pass in the future. It was interesting that the author chose to repeat an entire chapter, just like in the prior book 2010: Odyssey Two.Over all, another great book. I very much enjoyed reading this book and I highly recommend it. I am looking forward to reading the last book in this series 3001: Odyssey Four.Read moreRead less
