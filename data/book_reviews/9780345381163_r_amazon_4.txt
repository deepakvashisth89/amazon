This book is well written for the person with questions about various aspects of Catholic traditions. It covers each area in a concise format with a few pages devoted to the subject (funerals, the rosary, etcetera).It doesn't go into deep detail but provides enough information for explanation to let the reader decide if he/she wishes to explore the subject more fully, and along the way it does a good job of slaying a few myths about the faith.I think this would be a great book for RCIA directors to have on their shelf to hand out to people that are on the fence but that are put-off by various tropes or misunderstandings.I give it 5-stars for readability and accuracy as well as its utility in ecumenicism.Read moreRead less
