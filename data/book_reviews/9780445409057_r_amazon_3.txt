I have been reading just about every Stuart M Kaminsky book I can find on Amazon.  (Fortunately, there are PLENTY of them.)  I was growing up in the later decades of this brilliant but reclusive man's life.  It was much reported on, due to his wealth from the Hughes Tool Company (oil field equipment), his interest in making movies in Hollywood and his famous World War II 'Folly', the 'SPRUCE GOOSE' plywood air transport plane that could NOT yield enough metal to show up on Radar scopes.  After the period in which this novel is set, Hughes used his vast wealth to move the 'Mob' out of Las Vegas, at least for his lifetime.  So...Toby Peters, Movie Studio problem solver, comes to Hughes' attention due to his success rate in getting things done.
