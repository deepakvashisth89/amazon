A simple review of this book is "I love it". If you have any interest at all in word origins this is a great little book for you. Evan Morris breaks it down in a funny, easy-to-understand way. His book made me think about phrases I use occasionally that I had never put any thought into how they came to exist.  I really enjoyed this book and it sent me to the library to find some similar books and I would definitely recommend his website as well.
