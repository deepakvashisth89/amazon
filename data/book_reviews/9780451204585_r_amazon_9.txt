I am a lover of Jack the Ripper fiction novels so as soon as i came across this book i had to get it! It is an absolute brilliant read, with an engaging story that doesn't drag. The tone of the novel is set by the spooky, sinister, opening chapter and carries on throughout the book. It is also nice to see that as well as the main character (John Darnell)being well-written, the supporting cast are also well-rounded characters. At 212 pages it isn't the longest read, and each chapter is only around 5-6 pages long on average(which suits me fine!). If you want an exciting, gruesome at times, gripping read then look no further than this.Read moreRead less
