The Warriors of God is a superb thriller. The hardcover edition is only 255 pages, so it's pretty short. But then, unlike most authors, William Christie does not load his books with repetitive and overlong action scenes, subplots that go nowhere, and loads of technical or background information. From beginning to end, the story flows smoothly and unhindered.The novel depicts an an undeclared war between the Islamic Republic of Iran and the United States. Beginning with an Iranian-sponsored terrorist attack on a US Navy ship and the US's resultant cruise missile strike against Iran. An Iranian terrorist force is then sent to the US to launch attacks against the US Marines Camp Lejune and, finally, the White House. The narrative follows events from both the perspective of the Iranians as they prepare and carry out their missions, and the men and women of various branches of US military and law enforcement. The central character is a former Marine now working at the Pentagon as a civilian. He's sort of like Jack Ryan, except he's not a flawless superhero.The books provides great detail on how state-sponsored terrorists go about planning and carrying out their attacks, as well as into the operations and procedures of elite units like the FBI Hostage Rescue Team and SEAL Team  Six.This is a really great thriller, and it's too bad it never became a big hit. Vince Flynn most likely read it, as his novel Transfer of Power revolves around a similar siege on the White House. Also worth checking out are William Christie's more recent novels, "The Enemy Inside" and "Threat Level." These also deal with terrorism and special operations, but are set within the Global War on Terror, and are far grittier and realistic than anything Flynn or Thor have written.Read moreRead less
