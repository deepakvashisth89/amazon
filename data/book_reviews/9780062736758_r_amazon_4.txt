Porlier's little book is a good manual for the average, non-technical reader.  It clearly and  simply explains the Y2K problem and what might result from it on 1/1/2000.  The author takes the worst case scenario and  tends to be somewhat alarmist, but as he says, it's best to prepare for the  worst.  Porlier offers a complete survival plan in case of a total  breakdown in government, basic utilities and communication. He has good  advice on stockpiling food, clothing, medicines and other essentials. He  warns you not to count on your check and your medicare payments and he  reminds you to copy important documents - something most of us would have  forgotten. He even covers toilet problems, no small issue. Porlier provides  extensive checklists of things we may need if the worst comes to pass.  I  think, however, his faith in public activism is a little naive, but then I  may be overly cynical.
