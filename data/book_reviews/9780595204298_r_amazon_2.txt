Drama-In-Law is a sharp, funny novel about mean-spirited in laws who doesn't mind delivering the low blows. Although humorous, it has a dark side. Miles is the insensitive husband married to Lauren who reinvents herself. The book has valid arguments. Lauren's friend Amber offers words of wisdom and compassion, yet she's not too preachy.
