Honestly, I am not usually a lover of short stories, which is what these are. However, these stories have actual endings, and that makes them much better than the usual fare. Smiley is just a device used to link the stories and to dispense wisdom that would sound very trite coming from any other character.This book is rather amazing when you think of how much le Carré predicted what would eventually happen with Russia.
