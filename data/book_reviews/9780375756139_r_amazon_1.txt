For any lover of the Silver Screen, 'The Art of the Moving Picture', is a treasure.This book shows us the movie business, and motion pictures, in their infancy.The first hand discussion of the subject in it's earliest stages, is fascinating, as are the ideas expressed therein.I felt as if I was witnessing the birth of an art form.The book treats Motion Pictures as a fine art. One that, with nearly 100 years of hindsight, is appropriate and gives one an idea of the strides that were made in the field.To know what Movies are and were in all the years since the publication of this work adds another layer to the read.One can appreciate those first bold steps when one recognizes how far the art has come.A truly great book and worth the read for anyone who loves the art of film.though definitely dated in style and subject, it never the less delivers a wholly wonderful glimpse into our entertainment past.Read moreRead less
