These old Del Rey collections aren't exactly archival quality, but they did do a fantastic job assembling just the right collection of stories for each volume. Thematically linked by a focus on the strangeness beneath the surface, some of them are as hilarious as they are harrowing. You never really know what's hanging out in your family tree until it slaps you in the face, so take a good long look at a picture of your grandma before you crack this one open. This isn't a "best of" collection and only "The Shadow over Innsmouth" and "Dagon" offer a glimpse into Lovecraft's famous mythology. It's a sampler from Lovecraft's early career, dense, atmospheric tales filled with great ideas and twist endings.I know of no another collection of early Lovecraft with this dense a collection of solid tales packed with great ideas. No story fragments or poetry to be found here, and the longest yarn is one of his late careers finest offerings. Sure there's a sprinkling of the Dream Cycle stuff, but just the right amount. Stories like Arthur Jermyn, The Temple, the Hound, The Lurking Fear, Beyond the wall of Sleep and From Beyond are all excellent and rarely collected in the larger anthologies. The Temple is an especially unique tale, a wartime submarine adventure gone horrifically wrong. It's like Das Boot ala Dagon.No it's not meant for an oak paneled library, but this is a great volume for new fans and old ones seeking few classic tales that slipped through the cracks.Read moreRead less
