It shows Asimov's creativity, his humor, and the lessons he learned as a young author ( for instance, don't use long titles. ) None of this is as brilliant as his later work of course but, since he includes a biographical introduction to each story, you basically get a section of his autobiography as well as these tales.We see his early fascination with robots and his study of chemistry ( which makes for a hilarious story which is disguised as a lab report ( and almost got him in trouble with his Ph.D review committee! ))An enjoyable volume which points us to great things to come.Read moreRead less
