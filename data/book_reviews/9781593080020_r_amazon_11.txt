I bought this for my wife, and while she enjoyed both stories, I can only speak to The Call of the Wild personally. It’s undeniably a classic and has stood the test of time for good reason. Jack London’s storytelling is captivating, and his portrayal of survival in the wilderness is powerful.However, London does have his flaws. He’s prone to overuse of superlatives, often describing things with excessive emphasis, and at times the writing can feel overly detailed. His socialist populism can also feel a bit uneasy, at least for me.That said, he has an incredible linguistic energy. Few writers can match the raw power and vitality he brings to his storytelling.
