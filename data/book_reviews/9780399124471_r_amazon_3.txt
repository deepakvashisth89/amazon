Bittersweet by Susan Strasberg is an honest and often heartbreaking story  of her life and career. Ultimately this is a tale of survival.I ordered this book because I was always fascinated by Christopher Jones and I knew she was married to him.This book did not disappoint. Much of Bittersweet does revolve around her relationship with Jones but the book is so much more. She explores her parental relationships as well as her relationship with Marilyn MonroeAnd Richard Burton. There’s so many more fascinating people in this book but her daughter by Jones, Jennifer, plays a pivotal role in Bittersweet.After I finished this engaging book I googled Strasberg. It was saddened to see that Strasberg had died relatively young. Her spirit is very much alive in Bittersweet as she narrates her life with her famous parents as well as her career and her relationships. She does an excellent job in bring the era of the 1950’s and1960’s especially to life.I loved this book.Read moreRead less
