This book is about grief and how people deal with it.  There was a mother who lives with the illusion that both her children are still alive.  A teenaged girl keeps seeing the ghost of her aborted fetus.  And, a father  who has lost his son.  All three converge together in a ghost town called  Revenant, which is hungry for human grief.There, they must face their loss  and choose to let go of their pain or join the ghostly population.  It was  a great story, the emotions and the settings so well written that I was  totally immersed until I finished the book.Read moreRead less
