This is a must read for the layman who doesn't want to read Economics 101. Many politicians and well-intentioned citizens should realize what they're up against when they try to thwart the laws of economics, whether it's the  minimum wage, ticket scalping, drugs or abortions. This book explains in  clear terms why many laws to regulate these issues are doomed from the  start.  In addition, this book is a great companion for a  macroeconomics course to promote class discussions.  However, if you  already fully understand the laws of supply and demand, then this book is  little more than light reading on a plane, and probably not worth the  price.
