Great book.  Jastrow makes a complex issue simple, and he challenges his own thinking as well as everyone else's.Dr. Robert Jastrow was one the world’s leading astronomers and cosmologists, and was the founding director of NASA's Goddard Institute for Space Studies.  The second sentence of his book “God and the Astronomers” states “in my case it should be understood from the start that I am an agnostic in religious matters.”  After explaining the evidence for the creation of the universe via the Big Bang, Jastrow concluded his book with these two final sentences:“For the scientist who has lived by his faith in the power of reason, the story ends like a bad dream.  He has scaled the mountains of ignorance; he is about to conquer the highest peak; as he pulls himself over the final rock, he is greeted by a band of theologians who have been sitting there for centuries.”In between he takes the subject of the Big Bang and explains it in a way that the layman can understand.  He also gives interesting background info.  I have read quite a few books on the subject of the creation of the universe.  This was the easiest to read and most enjoyable.  For deeper scientific discussion, you can read Rodney Holder or Paul Davies or Barrow/Tipler.Jastrow is the best book for a first look at the big bang.Read moreRead less
