This is the story about Alexa and Scott.  Alexa was engaged to Mark (Scott's brother)and basically Mark jilts Alexa for her friend (Elaine).  On the night of Mark and Elaine's wedding Alexa takes sleeping pills and Scott comes to her rescue.  Scott decides to take Alexa to South America to keep her away from Mark.  They have to get married to go to South America because of his job. While there they have to over come their differences. As with all romance books you can imagine the ending.
