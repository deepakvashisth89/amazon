Colette, writing of her life before Cheri, before the erotic, before the scandalous...and yet so deliciously alive, bright, brilliant, tender, loving...one can see how the passionate writer who penned such works could develop into the voluptuous writer of her future, for it makes sense that a writer who can write so dreamy-lovely about the idyllic days of her youth, could develop into a woman able to appreciate the sensual world as Colette does in later works. This is that sensuality expressed more innocently...and you can see, most definitely, the hint of the Colette who would come.
