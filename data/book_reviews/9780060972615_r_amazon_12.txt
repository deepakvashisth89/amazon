This book reads like a collection of Sunday supplement newspaper columns. My main problem with Shenkman is that he perpetuates as many myths as he destroys. I've read many of these myth buster books - they tend to cover the same topics - and many other equally reputable authors come to different conclusions. Shenkman is especially guilty of promoting/accepting without question major assumptions about vast periods and subjects in history. Okay reading for the smallest room in the house.
