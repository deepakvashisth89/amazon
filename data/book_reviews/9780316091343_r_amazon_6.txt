A painful, but important documentation of one of the darkest periods in human history. This was the second copy of the book that I have obtained, this time as a donation for the library where I live.  I obtained my first copy at the Holocaust Museum in Washington years ago, and have occasionally reviewed it cover to cover, just to verify that this horrendous human slaughter actually happened.
