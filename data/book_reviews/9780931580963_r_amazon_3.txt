This book is great for someone who has a dream and is frustrated with trying to realize it.I actually bought this book years ago, then loaned it to someone who didn't return it.  Rather than bug them about it, I just bought another one.  It is such a great motivator.  It provides a pathway to accomplishing your dream, whatever your dream may be.  I have realized mine to a large degree, and I want to be able to refer to the book over and over again, to see if I can speed things up, or rearrange my dream to be more in line with what I really want as time goes on.The book addresses what can keep us from realizing our dreams, how to overcome those obstacles, choosing the dream you really want to accomplish, becoming passionate about your dream, actually doing what needs to be done, how to handle it when you get there.  FInally - it tells you how to choose new goals once you've reached the old ones.Wonderful book, thanks John-Roger and Peter McWilliams.Read moreRead less
