I selected 3 stars for this book, just because it is written by Sellers.  I was beyond excited to get this book, especially after knowing who Sean Sellers is, doing so much research on him, watching his interviews on Death Row, and knowing that he was in contact with Azerate (Sean Sellers spells the name Ezurate though, but we believe it to be the same Klipotic Deity).  Yeah, so I was excited about getting this book, and read the entire thing through nonstop.  To me dismay this book only contains about 6 pages where Sean Sellers actually writes, talks of, and describes his own personal experiences with Satanism.  This one account is in the very beginning of the book.  So to speak, this book starts out totally awesome, and the first 6 pages get you pumped up wanting more.  Then all of a sudden, out of nowhere, the entire tone of the book changes, and Sean Sellers no longer shares his personal, secret experiences with Satanism, and his interactions with other individuals in dealing with the occult, the rituals he performed himself or in a group, or any of the demons or klipotic forces he dealt with.  Suddenly the book turns into what I can only describe as pure fundamentalist Christian rhetoric.  BLEH!  But I kept reading page after page waiting for Sean to return to actually revealing info on what really happened.  Unfortunately after the first 6 pages my hope was let down.  It kind of made me mad.  Sean Sellers shows some of the symbols allegedly used by occultists, and gives what he believes to be the history of the occult and Satanism.  Each chapter is dedicated to bashing one aspect or another of the Occult, Satanism, Witchcraft, or the New Age movement.  One chapter is dedicating to ripping up Dungeons and Dragons!  One chapter is dedicating to convincing the reader to destroy their Occult books, or to burn their Tarot Cards/Decks.  No way Sean!  Sean goes on to say he will only allow someone to be his disciple if they do these things: burn their occult books, tarot cards, ouija boards, and destroy their Occult related charms, necklaces and jewelry.  Once again Sean, sorry but that ain't happening here!  And for him to say, "I will only disciple someone if..." is humorous.  Like, Sean Sellers, you were on Death Row, from alleged being a Satanic Murderer... so who are you to have disciples?  I found that a little pompous.  I do believe Sean Sellers was a very smart individual but something was just not right with this guy.  I don't understand why Azerate (Sean, once more, calls Ezurate) chose Sean to communicate through back in the mid 1980's.  Oh, speaking of which, this is probably the ultimate let down, and that is, Sean makes no mention of Azerate (Ezurate) because he is too busy kneeling before Christianity and being their lackey.  I really, really thought about this, and I do believe that, especially from reading the first 6 pages, which were awesome, that Sean Sellers originally wanted to write a book consisting of his real experiences, but he realized that if he were to have published a book revealing what he really did, what he was really into, and how things work in the Occult and Satanism that he was involved with, then there would literally be no chance for his clemency or to eventually receive mercy and get off death row.  So I think his lawyers and whoever else was coaching him told him to write this sort of book as an attempt to get out of prison.  Whether or not Sean was truly a Christian or was just faking it to get out, or was just doing it subconsciously to try and get out of prison, but was still a Satanist underneath it all is something we will never really know, because he was put to death a few years ago.  It's kind of sad they killed Sean, because now there are so many questions that we will never get answers to because of the lethal injections he received.  Last but not least, I think Sean really messed up with his Satanism and got sloppy.  A real Satanist wouldn't have done the things he did, because, well, towards the end there, he really did some stupid stuff, so I guess he got what he deserved.  Stupidity is the #1 sin in Satanism.  And Sean just slipped up bad, but he was so young tampering with things that only mature adults should mess around with.  When you mess with the Occult you gotta know there may be a price to pay and you gotta be willing to pay that price, even if you don't like it.  Hey, life isn't fair and it's painful, so stay strong.  Your book is horrible Sean, but I give you 3 stars just because I wanted to hear what you had to say, or what they forced you to say.
