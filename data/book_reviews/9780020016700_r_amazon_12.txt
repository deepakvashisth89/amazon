The book arrived quickly and I started reading it straight away. The instructions are for building a log cabin not a log house. It is simple instruction and easy to understand. There was mention of the notching gauge, rather than dividers, the author suggests making it yourself, but there was no instruction or diagram on how to make it or how to use it. I can guess how to but others may not. The book is basic, so don't expect too much information, but it does help you to understand how to build with hand tools not machinery. The author goes on to explain how to make furniture, fences, gates and many other things which was good to read. If you are wanting to build a simple structure the old fashioned way with hand tools it's good, but if you want a more detailed information guide for building a log house, maybe not this book.Overall it was a good read, simple to understand, has diagrams to follow and is a short read.
