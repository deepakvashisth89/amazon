Mary Higgins Clark's mysteries are good but this one outstanding.  From the very beginning it envelops you in unease and a feeling of dread, so much so that I thought of it often and couldn't wait to get back to it. I just needed to know if the characters I cared about would be okay. The story weaves through inexplicable situations that keep you wondering what the heck is really going on.
