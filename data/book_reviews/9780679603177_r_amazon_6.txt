Marvelous is the only word to describe this first of three volumes of Anton Chekhov's short stories published by the Modern Library.  While the following two compilations are each superlative in their own way (thank you  again, Shelby Foote), this Early Short Stories 1883-88, is a thrilling peek  at genuis not only flowering but seemingly mature; a self-assured young  artist at play in his medium, inventing a new(then) approach to emotions as  easily as passing off a serf's bromide or a bishop's benediction.
