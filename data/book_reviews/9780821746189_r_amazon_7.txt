I am fairly new to historical westerns and a bit lukewarm about them but am a fan of Jo Goodman and like the New York setting.  The railroad aspects add rich detail to the story.  Maggie's background painfully sets her apart from being a typical fashionable New York heiress, so she has meekly spent her life buried in books.  The initial events are very disconnected and unlike Maggie herself.  Perhaps that is why she blocked them out.  Rejected from medical school, she determines to travel west to learn from a hermit miner & healer.  Marriage to Connor is a means to an end and she doesn't deal with her feelings or reactions to him.Connor is desperate to retain his Colorado ranch home that his father is about to sell out from under him to the railroad.  Maggie unknowingly steals his ranch money.  Then his only option is to marry Maggie and receive it as a dowry.She only wants an escort west and a divorce and refuses to deal with her reactions to Connor.  He has been drawn to her from the start.  His promise to divorce her and her fear keeps them apart most of the story.  A couple of realistic dangerous situations provides the catalyst for these two to break the barriers.I had just bought the entire series then received this book from eBook Discovery and jumped at the chance to give my honest review.Read moreRead less
