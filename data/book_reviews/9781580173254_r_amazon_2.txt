If I would have paid $50 for this book I'd still be thrilled. The fact that it costs under $20 for all the information and knowledge it contains is unreal. This book seems to cover everything. Before I bought this book and read the reviews that said that same thing I was like; "Yeah, but what does it cover ?".  Everything, really, okay well, if your chickens have some strange disease and you're looking to diagnose it then go elsewhere.  But other than that it covers types of chickens, chick care, housing, feeding, butchering, and lots lots more. I'm not sure how you could write a negative review; "The author didn't show up at my door to help me"...  I've referred back to this book many times for specs on housing, range requirements, ...
