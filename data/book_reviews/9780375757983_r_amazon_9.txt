The Rise of David Levinsky is the story of a man's immigration to America as a poor Yiddish-speaking Jew from Eastern Europe and his journey of survival, assimilation and advancement in American society as he struggles with his sense of identity. It provides a fascinating insight into the American immigrant experience and for that reason is arguably a must-read for anyone with an interest in American history. It is a particularly meaningful story for American Jews whose ancestors immigrated from Eastern Europe in the late 19th/early 20th century.
