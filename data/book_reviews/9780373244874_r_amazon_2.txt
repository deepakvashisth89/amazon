This was an incredibly sweet novel that had me grinning the whole time. The heroine is Dottie who was originally working at The Grand, which wasn't really all that grand of a hotel. Then, she gets sweeped away into a fairy tale and is cast as a crown princess.Prince Randolph, who was originally the Crown Prince was forced to give up his position due to a mistake his father made years ago. Although reluctant, he meets Dottie at the Grand and is instantly smitten with her easy charm and kindness. Branded as a "nobody", he becomes a somebody to Dottie as he rescues her time after time in the small kingdom which has accepted Dottie as the queen of their hearts. Dottie, originally unhappy about the prospect of becoming queen, eventually accepts her fate and is loved by her subjects.This had all the elements of a fairy tale: a happy ending (to be honest, more of a twist), the charming crown prince, and the independent princess. I loved Dottie because she was an amazing character, and so funny as well. Randolph was very reserved but one couldn't help but to fall in love with him.
