" The Quiet Game"This book's main plot is as relevant today as it was 50 years ago.  The plot takes the reader back to the time when, "sealed for national security" was much more widely accepted.  50 years later, national secrets are stolen and made public, "in the publics' interest" with a blank statement of, "The public has a right to know". The story told here,  provides a glimmer of how such a dramatic change of thought could happen and why.  I could use this plot to encourage a great debate!This  book was a fast paced, easy to read, great story.  It also provides the reader a sub-plot which is based on the attitudes and actions of people in America during the great Civil Rights  Movement.  The characters are believable and true to this period of history.  Fact and fiction intertwine, allowing the reader to observe the interactions of people, not only of different races, but also of different society levels.  People of that time were facing many social issues.  As the story unfolds, the reader can assess the the social changes as well as the lack thereof.  If we are not careful, could today's society or a future society allow America to backtrack to that era?  Is that the direction we should want to go?  What determines a person's  character?I gave this book 5 stars because it was wonderfully written, but also because it lends itself to thoughtful introspection and group discussions!
