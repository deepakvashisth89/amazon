Once again the Author has captured the SF Soldier and put it on "paper".  I enjoyed this book.  The storyline was very believable.  I especially liked 055 (Nod to B-2-10) going around testing the security of different bases/complexes.  I know 10th SFG(a) was involved with such exercises.  The one at the Plattsburgh Air Force Base, Plattsburgh, New York was a great example of one of those type "missions" for an "A-Team".  Bob Mayer has done it again.  Letting the reader get to know the characters.  To get into the story.  I hated breaking away from the book.  Once I put it down, I was always wondering what was going to happen next......and wanting to get back to reading it at the next chance I had.  I can honestly recommend this book as a great read.  The fact that Bob Mayer has been there and done that.......really helps the reader to jump right in.........of course, Bob would probably tell us all how he went through the last hard class at Camp Mackall, right Bob?  ;)  Just kidding, everyone knows that I was in that last hard class!  :O  This book is a great read......buy it today!Read moreRead less
