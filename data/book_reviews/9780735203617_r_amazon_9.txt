Have a look at the 'look inside' function on amazon, the contents pages speak for themselves.Many areas of health are covered, the author lists recipes of oils to use as well as some fresh herbs. I like that the oils she chooses are easy to find and inexpensive, some other aromatherapy writers seem to think that we have an unlimited budget for obscure oils!The writing style is friendly and encouraging. I got many good ideas and I will be using this as a guide book from now on if I have any illnesses.The book is purely about uses of oils for various ailments as well as for beauty and relaxation. Although there is a list of oils and their properties it is not very comprehensive: you'd better buy an essential oil dictionary if that is what you are looking for. It doesn't teach body massage but it does tell you about facial massage.This reminds me of Valerie Worwood's Fragrant Pharmacy except the recipes are much simpler. A relaxing read.Read moreRead less
