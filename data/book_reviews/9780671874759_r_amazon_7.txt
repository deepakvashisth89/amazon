Mist  is ok but too long. this is not vintage King, or master of the genre King. It is ho hum 3* King.But... But...As is fairly common there is a preview of a new book at the end of Mist.  Danny Coughlin's Bad Dream is a complete story from his book to be released May 22, 2024. This story is King at his best, telling a story of a psychic dream that Danny has that forces him to try and do the right thing as a result.
