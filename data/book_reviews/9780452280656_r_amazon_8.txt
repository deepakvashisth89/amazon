Well, I know I'm in the minority here.  But a real history of the connection between music and race in America is something that it desperately needed.  One might expect an academic historian to have provided that.  But this book is NOT a good history - it's highly impressionistic and rather anecdotal and full of ideas that aren't exactly explained.  It jumps around from chapter to chapter making much of the impressions that the author has about the 'hidden' messages within songs, but I'm afraid that he states rather than argues or proves his case.
