This guy is one of the best adventure writers I've ever encountered.  Each book takes place in a different setting -- subArctic seas, Australian desert, Canadian Rockies, etc.  Yet Innes paints such vivid word pictures of each setting that readers cannot doubt that he has not only visited there, but lived there long enough to really get the feel of the setting.  He comes as close as any writer I've ever read to giving me a sense of being there.  Yet he does this with out long detailed descriptions.  Instead, the details are woven into the story line so deftly that it doesn't slow the action.  I just wish he'd written ten times as many books.Read moreRead less
