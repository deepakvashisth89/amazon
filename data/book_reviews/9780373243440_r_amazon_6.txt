I liked this story.  Daniel is a hot, hot hero - not only hot, but a great guy - what's not to love?  I loved the descriptions of how he felt flying.  The setting is Wyoming, small town.  Seems believable.  I liked the story line - they met, had an intense connection, and then were separated.  She ends up pregnant, and though she tries to locate him, cannot.  After 3 years, when he shows up ready to pick up where they left off, heroine is unwilling and afraid.  I really got tired of all of Kendra's dithering, and the roadblocks she put up along the way.  That all just left me cold.  The people around Kendra added nicely to the story.All in all, it is a well written book, and the biggest sticking point to me was the heroine.
