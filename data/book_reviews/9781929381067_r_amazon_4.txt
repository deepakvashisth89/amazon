The story of Antares Dawn was enjoyable and the technical details did not seem to get on the way. The main reason I give this three stars is that the characters and relationships are shallow and do nothing to engage the reader. The love story is undeveloped and anticlimactic and the change of heart by one  of the characters at the end is ridiculously fake.
