The little town of Newbury, CT has a murder rate that is climbing with each Ben Abbott novel. Justin Scott does an excellent job with the characters and this second novel in the series is as good as the first. The plot is intricate and does not involve a lot of mayhem. It follows the first novel characters a bit but it is a new story and a new plot. His politics get a tiny bit intrusive at points (he doesn't like Republicans) but, considering this is Connecticut, it is tolerable.
