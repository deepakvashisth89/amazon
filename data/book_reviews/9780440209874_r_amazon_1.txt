This is one of the most fascinating books I have Ever read. I feel it is true because it rings true, despite one review that 'debunks' this book as a fanciful tale. It is written with a terse and blunt honesty. My gut feeling and the credentials of the author as a historical researcher incline me to believe it, that it was not embellished. I gave this book to my wife and she could not put it down and read it through in one marathon sitting. I'm reading it again and it is unbelievably touching.
