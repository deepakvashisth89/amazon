As a potential future Appalachian Trail hiker, I've been reading a number of books by people who have hiked the trail.  Claudia Brenner's story is a riveting and painful read-painful in the sense that the fear, pain, and loss of her experience was clearly and skillfully articulated rather than, say, how many people might react when being forced to read through a college chemistry textbook.  I couldn't put the book down, read it through in one night, and found myself thinking about it and her experiences for days.  I can't recommend "Eight Bullets" enough.Read moreRead less
