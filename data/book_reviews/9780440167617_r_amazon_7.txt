This is a fine novel, though the Kindle version does a poor job of blocking light and clinging to windows. (Amazon's wonky algorithms for these reviews should be an embarrassment to Mr Bezos.) If you're expecting a cozy British mystery or a sentimental look back at the Blitz, go elsewhere. Though virtually all the violence is off-stage, Barnard provides an unblinking study of domestic cruelty in the larger context of racism in England before, during, and after World War II.
