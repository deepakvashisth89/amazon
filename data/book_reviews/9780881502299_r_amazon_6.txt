I suppose I cannot give new younger readers advice on these books by Phoebe Taylor which I  read and enjoyed many decades ago and am now re-reading. Her works are not classics of the mystery genre. They are light, mildly amusing tales built around a central "character" which have no objective other than to entertain without  inducing sober reflections. I enjoy the characters, in this case, the redoubtable engineer and man of a all work for the Porter Motorcar Company and  its founders Family, the New Englander to the core, Asey Mayo. In this book, Asey's good friend, the  Village doctor has found a body....which has disappeared by the time he has been able to call for help...Asey responds to the call and encounters a group of  young College students doing a site visit in town. There are complications galore and a tricky ending all of which add to the fun.All in all, a good one for reading when not in the mood for contemplation or the seamy side of life.Read moreRead less
