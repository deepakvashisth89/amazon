I’m on a mission to read a series of Roger Angell books and this marked number two, following The Summer Game, which covered 10 seasons, 1962-1971. This book, Five Seasons, picks up where Angell left off, ending with the 1976 World Series. Perhaps because I actually was old enough to remember these later years, I found this volume more interesting. It is more than 100 pages longer and covers a more diverse array of topics.
