Linda Greenlaw rocks! Her books are absolutely wonderful. I read about them in a review in Down East Magazine and purchased all three from Amazon. I started with The Hungry Ocean, next was Lobster Chronicles and finished up with Dry Dock. I couldn't put any of them down. I'm so sad that I'm done reading them - and can't wait for the next! We've been to Maine several times and if we are able to get there again I fully intend to go to the Dry Dock in Portland to see if I can meet her.
