My nephew loves all things spooky and scary. He insists on having a Halloween themed cake for his September birthday every year. My point being this is a kid who likes to be scared. I got him this book for Christmas and he needed to read it immediately. He and my sister in law got about half way through before he insisted he was really tired and needed to go to sleep. She asked him if it's because he was scared, he said "yes" but then they read it the next day and the day after and the day after, he's obsessed. Once he got to the ending and figured the book out it wasn't nearly as scary for him. Since he's 5 and it was this scary for him I can't say that I would suggest getting this for a younger child as they may never want to read it.
