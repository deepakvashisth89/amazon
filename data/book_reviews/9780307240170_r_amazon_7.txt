This is a general, but complete little book for someone interested in life in freshwater aquatic systems. It gives examples of  microscopic life, insects, birds, snakes, frogs and toads, turtles, etc. that might be found near a stream, or in a pond.  It's small, so you can stuff it in a back pocket.
