I first read this book in the late 80’s, and though I only remembered one part of it, I thought I’d give it another read. I don’t know what to make of it. It’s almost as if Vidal sat down with Bob Dylan and the two of them just riffed together a satirical romp in the fictitious Duluth. Sometimes humorous, sometimes sexy, and often scathing, the book is a relatively easy read. It does not compare to his works of historical fiction, it’s just different.Read moreRead less
