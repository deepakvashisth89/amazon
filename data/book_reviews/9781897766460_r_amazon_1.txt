This is great, even for a non-vegan like me.  Having found plenty of recipes to try in Buffet and Barbeque book by Linda Majzlik, I decided I would also buy this one.  I'm very glad I did and it, also, has many recipes that sound interesting.  I would recommend it to anyone.  Don't be put off by the 'vegan' label'.  If you think about it, especially if you can remember rationing, most people during and for some time after the war, had to make do with vegetables most of the time, so there were, in effect, the original vegans, and they managed to live pretty well.
