This reinvention of the vampire lore incorporating the Cantonese take on the vampire by Bram Stoker Award Winning author,  Bentley Little, whom Stephen King calls the horror poet laureate, introduces the Chinese vampire myths to Western audiences,  constituting an unprecedented attempt to bring together vampire lore from two different cultures - one from the East and the other from the West,  and,  hence, represents a significant contribution to vampire mythology.The characters are well developed, the story line engrossing and the entire novel is well written.Well worth the read and constitutes one of Bentley Little's finest efforts.Highly recommended.Read moreRead less
