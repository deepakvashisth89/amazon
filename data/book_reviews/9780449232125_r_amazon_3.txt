What we have here is a story about a doctor that brings into play not only medicine and hospitals but also murder, politics, the arts, religion, philosophy, the law, terrible sicknesses including leprosy and botched abortions, family love and loyalty, family jealousies and hate, unrequited love, true love, and of course bodice-ripping.  It's a book about life and death and everything in between.  Every single thing.  I read it years ago and loved it and was so glad to see it back in print.  But I forgot that when I read it and loved it all those years ago, I also loved soap operas.  This is a long book and I am so impressed that Taylor Caldwell wrote soooo many books, and I am also impressed that I was able to get through it this time.  It is a romance book dressed up in its very best clothes.Read moreRead less
