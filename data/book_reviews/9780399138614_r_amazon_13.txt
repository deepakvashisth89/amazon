After trawling through some of the absolute rubbish in the horror section of the Kindle store I happened to stumble upon this book and decided to give it a go.Unlike some of the other reviewers I quite enjoyed it. I found it well written and interesting. I didn't think the pace of the novel was slow at all, and the characters were compelling and believable.Whilst not an absolute shocker it does have some fairly chilling and graphic moments. The description of a town where something is quite wrong, plus the pagan elements left over from years past that anyone who lives in rural Britain will recognise has made me keen to seek out other work by this author.Read moreRead less
