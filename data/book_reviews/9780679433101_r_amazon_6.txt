It's so easy to get pulled into the Milieu. Complex ideas are liberally sprinkled with realistic behaviour and flowing dialogue. I found all of the books in the Galactic Milieu trilogy very hard to put down and as I turned the last page, found myself wanting more. The books made me do something that is usually left in childhood - daydream about being in the story myself, with psychic abilities, travelling the stars and being immortal.
