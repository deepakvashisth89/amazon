I thought "Fourth Angel" was terrific and started this sequel with great anticipation.  I'm glad I read it, but it was not nearly as good as the first book in the series.  I hate it when reviews give away too much of the plot, so I'll just say that not one but TWO people in this book turn out to be unexpected psychos.  I like my mysteries more plausible than that, and the believability of the villain was a big plus in "Fourth Angel."  I also agree with the reviewer who found much of the writing cliched.  So then, why am I glad I read this book?  Because the two main characters, Georgia and Mac, are interesting and believable, and I also enjoyed a number of the secondary characters.
