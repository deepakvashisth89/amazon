Love the characters and their smarts and their foibles. This woman can really write characters...their inner thoughts and Outer conversations are so well articulated...and so well communicated to the reader.Her references to Historic and Past writers, poets, and famous or infamous characters...direct quotes, references to people and events, and etc. Are a tad dated these days and demand knowledge that is vast and all encompassing that I as a reader often feel a bit, wdll, stupid. Though it gives me a great opportunity to 'look things up'  and learn a lot...it does have a tendency to interrupt the  flow of the tale being told. I do not like to feel this way...and...there you have it.Her vocabulary is amazing and her ability to write each character's monologues and dialogs in the vernacular for the person. (And keep it up throughout the narrative and the book!) Is just , almost, uncanny.Since she is such a Canny ...hohoho...writer it is not surprising...I get a bit confused with her complex stories and all is well by the end of it...she does summarise her plots neatly at the end (and some times during the story itself) so keeping up with what is happening is not terribly difficult.I recommend her books to any cozy Mystery fan who loves a great sense of irony, absurdity, and most of all, Humor!Her books are so much fun and also such a challenge to read that one (I) feel so satisfied at the end of each one.Highly recommended. And...be prepared to read them all...they are addicting...silliness and all.Read moreRead less
