I bought this book largely out of curiosity as I wondered what the Author's participation as a physician was in the Final Solution and how he would rationalize his actions.In the end, Nyiszli (a renowned Jewish forensic specialist) can only be considered a willing participant whose services for the Nazi's were an enabler of the latter's grotesque and horrible genocidal experiments.  When I say 'willing' I mean  in the context that he chose life (and as described a not particularly uncomfortable life) over certain death in the event he'd refused to serve the Nazis.Never having faced such a choice I prefer to think I would not have chosen his path but, regardless, this book has value in providing a look into the depths to which Nazi brutality sank and how individuals' morals were co-opted when faced with such choices.Besides being very disturbing, the book is well written and is after all real history which must be documented and preserved.
