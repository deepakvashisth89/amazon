I first discovered Mars when I was fifteen years old. I rarely read much manga, but I prized good story telling. I was interested initially because the main character Kira was an artist. I was a young artist myself, so it was that aspect that intrigued me. Anyone looking for an amazing story of two lovers against the world with their personal demons, with such realism and emotion--needs to look no further.The first thing a story needs is lovable characters--which it has in the two main characters.Rei--a bad boy, with a very jaded-seeming view of the world, isn't as he first appears to be. He's not your typical playboy. Rei has a passion for racing dirt bikes and courting danger. Because of his own past full of tragedy, he has a fascination for death. He expresses this within the first few pages.Kira--the shy, timid, artist who keeps to herself and doesn't seem to want any human contact, is a figure known at her school. She has the reputation of 'hating men.' The outside world doesn't understand much of her. She may be meek and timid, but she's got a lot of heart.In this story these two characters who are from two different worlds, with dark pasts and uneasy lives, are drawn together.This unlikely couple stir up the school with gossip and Kira is subject to bullying because of it.I really like how the 'villian' of that arc, doesn't stay one-dimensional.If you want a breathtaking romance, action and psychological intrigue. I would recommend this story! I feel like if it was a real novel it would be taken seriously. It really is amazing. You don't have to be a girl to like it either. This book is sexless. It may be classified as a shoujo--I don't know if it is. But I think it speaks about the rawness of human emotion and surviving in a world that throws it's hardest blows at you. It's is so beautiful, pretty dark with some themes and concepts it introduces.There are some twisted character. You could argue that the main characters are twisted themselves. They ponder about morality and often skirt the line of what's right and wrong. Life pushes them into a corner and they react like bitten animals.This story is about two young students who want to live in the world, and they struggle so hard to be 'peaceful'.The main characters are not like the typical lovers. They are not concerned with regular teenage struggles. They just want to be free of suffering and pain, and thus appreciate life in a way most people don't.This book has a lot of adult themes. There is sex in it. But I feel like it is not there just to romanticize, the context that it happens it pretty serious and makes it not just a 'love scene', but a scene in which two people take a step to be closer to one another when the world has thrown them out. It's intense, and the relationship is so poignant that I would recommend it to a younger audience.There is a homosexual character in the book. Nothing happens or comes of it,but there are some suggestive scenes and comments that are made. But this character---like all the other characters in the book, is not as he appears at first glance.The story and developments of manga are amazing. I don't think my review can do it justice.There is the realness to it that hits home. It's poetic and very psychological.Just--just read it.I was a bit concerned that I spent a lot of money buying the whole series, just to relive my fifteen year old obsessions, but I have to say--worth every damn penny!It is a collection that you won't regret having on your shelf.Read moreRead less
