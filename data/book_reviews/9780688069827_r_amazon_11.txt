With all the warmth and astuteness of character insight from this remarkable author, we enjoy being transported into a time of turmoil in English history, back in the 1100s.  Brother Cadfael is a monk in a monastery at Shrewsbury. And he is indeed shrewd. He has had a richly-experienced life as soldier and sailor before devoting himself to the service of the faith and the medication of man.Each of the Cadfael stories (20 in all, I think) has the crime story ingredients of a murder (or two) embroiled in the wider turmoils of divided England when King Stephen and his cousin empress Maud were contesting with armies and destruction over who should reign in England.Enjoyably, comfortingly, in all of the Cadfael stories there is a love story too, with its complications intertwined.
