Kudos to Brash Books for bringing this one back into print. Don't know how I missed it first time around, especially since it won all sorts of awards. It stars a True Grit-like duo of a hardboiled PI and a precocious 14-year-old girl on the trail of kidnapped dog in Los Angeles. The fun comes from their tag team-retelling of the story, the dialogue, the motley cast of characters, and the plot--which is perhaps overly twisty, but always engaging.
