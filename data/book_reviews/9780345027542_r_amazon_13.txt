Wonderful, charming, and yet containing sombre notes within its outrageous tall tales depicted with wit & warmth. Though there have been comparisons between these stories with others in 'Dunsany'-an style, I found these unique, because even the most fantastic of the concepts were presented in language so close to actual scientific stuff (as our narrator recounts, Harry Purvis' tales are so strange that they can only be true) that these stories are examples of literal science-fiction, rather than those of fantasy.
