I cannot believe it has taken me as long as it has to read this novel. I first fell in love with the tale when I saw the John Wayne film when I was a child, it quickly became one of my favourites. Then came The Coen Bros adaptation which, is so true to this book in dialogue and essence, and my favourite film still. But there is something wonderful about reading the book when you grew up in the world. I adore this story and the wit of Mattie Ross.
