Writing a review for a hopelessly out of print and obscure old horror paperback.  Futile?  I agree, but I looked this one up on a lark because something about it has held it in my memory for the last 16 years.  I can't  even remember much of the plot except it had kind of a "Separate  Peace" friendship-gone-wrong thing going on between two young boys  that I found captivating.  The characters were really well done and the  love subplot between the protagonist and his lady friend was really sweet.
