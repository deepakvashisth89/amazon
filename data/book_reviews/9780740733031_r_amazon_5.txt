I, too, am a fan of Lynn Johnston's comic strip, but it at no time conjures up the word "hip."  Much of its charm is that it conveys an "unhip" feeling, even while addressing modern situations.  Wholesomeness, humor, warm family ties and loyal friendships, yes.  Hip?  No, I don't think so.  That does not mean that I don't recommend the book.  It just doesn't scream "hip."The Hipster Handbookillustrates in great detail the hippest of the hip.  If you want to read a book on leaving home that would appeal to the young and the hip, try50 Ways to Leave Your Mother.That said, I still would recommend Leaving Home:  Survival of the Hippest for its humorous take on the pitfalls of kids living on their own for the first time.
