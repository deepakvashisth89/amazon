This is the kind of book the will stick with you for days. Ms. Massie has a gift for making you care about the characters that jump out from the pages of this book. You will finish this book with questions that haven't been answered but therein lies the mystery. I don't want to spoil anything but there are scenes in this book that hits you. Not the "jump in you face violence and Gore" that you'd think you would get from a book like this but it is what she doesn't say or describe that makes it all the more disturbing. The main characters are like us.  Flawed. Unsure of their humanity and faith but that's why you care for them even more. The horror is real. I can see something like this happening.
