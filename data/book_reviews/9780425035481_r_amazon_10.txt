Spider Robinson wrote this partly because he had read a novel by one of the Old Masters and thought "I could write better than that!" He's probably right; this is a supremely confident debut, with plenty of (occasionally improbable) twists and turns. A great, rollicking read, with plenty of moral ambiguity and a tremendous enemy in the Muskies, who turn out to be our friends and allies...  Highly recommended.Read moreRead less
