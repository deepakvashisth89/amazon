Armageddon Summer is written by two of the most gifted and influential writers.  That is made obvious in this book.The book starts out by introducing a religion.  In the book, the religion consists of few people, but still stands strong.  The religion holds the same beliefs as Christians, but drastically more so and to deeper extremities.  The members of this religion are completely and totally devoted and this is vital to be a member.  One day, the prediction of the end of the world is made.  Fortunately, it has also been predicted that a certain number of people can and will be saved.  In order to be saved, members and any others who may be lucky enough, need to meet at a certain place before a certain time.  Members instantly turn their back on everyday life and take off to where they believe they are going to be saved.  They have no doubts whatsoever about the world ending.The book is told from the perspective of two people: A teenage Girl and a teenage Boy.  The girl has lived her life being drilled with the rules and beliefs of the religion and she, like other members, is devoted.  She believes and she wants to be saved.  Life has never been the easiest for her, because the religion is her mother's life and that's something she never lets her daughter forget.  The boy, has little to none belief in the religion.  He's simply going along for the ride and also because his father insists.  He sincerely doesn't believe that anything is going to happen.When the two very different people meet, both their perspectives are changed drastically.  They have a better perception and a better outlook.The book was incredibly insightful. So much perspective was packed in there it was almost overwhelming.  I was engrossed, fascinated.  So much so that I couldn't put the book down.  It gave insight to religion, adolescence, relationships and the almost terrifying influence one person can have.  The two others did well together and their two characters contrasted perfectly.  The book was also packed with emotion that even those whom are very staunch are sure to feel.  I have this book numerous times and I will never be able to quite give it justice.  It truly is an incredible read.Read moreRead less
