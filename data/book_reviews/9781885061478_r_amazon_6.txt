I love this book!  I am new to bird watching, and wanted something to help me easily find what I was seeing.  The color coded pages help to find the right bird in seconds!  And you don't have to look through a bunch of birds that are not in your area!  Love that it shows you where in Illinois they frequent, and if they're here in the winter, summer, or just migrating through!  Male and female birds have their own page, and the opposite sex is in a small photo with their page number, so you can find them easily.  There is also info on what they eat, what they're eggs look like, the kinds of nests they live in and much more!
