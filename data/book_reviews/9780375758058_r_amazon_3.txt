Francis Burney was a favorite author of Jane Austen's, and in Evelina we see Burney's sly wit and sharp eye for the ridiculous in her society (and ours!). The novel is about a shy young girl's first visit to London society in the 1780s. In the background is the fact that her mother's first exposure to society went horribly wrong, leaving Evelina orphaned and penniless. As Evelina moves throughout high and low life in the capital, she learns the difference between snobbery and good manners, opportunism and personal honor, and selfishness and love. Will she be acknowledged by her absentee father? Will she escape her embarrassing cousins? Will she elude the seductive Sir Clement or catch the eye of dashing Lord Orville?
