This is the second in a three-part series set in Early America prior to and just after the Revolutionary War.  Book two, The Untamed, takes place in the mid 1760's in Pennsylvania.  This book incorporates two somewhat minor characters from the first book, The Homecoming, and brings them together in a previously unforeseen manner.  The two main characters, Brighid Cassidy and Phillip Crown are from two very different backgrounds and life styles and yet there is seldom that feeling of “class differences” as these two try to create their lives in a new, uncivilized land.  Miss Michaels brings her wonderful sense of humor to not only the settings and situations but especially to her characters.  As usual she builds on her characters filling out their personalities until you feel a part of their lives. There is romance, action, history, and mystery.
