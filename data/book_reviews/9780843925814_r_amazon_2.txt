I read this book first when i was about 12 years old, and right now i was just sitting here thinking about it, and wanted it for my Kindle, needless to say they do not have it in the Kindle store, however i'm going to find a way somehow to get it there. I love this book, it was awesome, about a single woman who was in a bar one night and ended up meeting a guy she took home with her for a one night stand and from there is where all the goodness of this book starts. Anyone who reads it will surely love it. It starts off from the woman's point of view but eventually turns to the boy and the story of his life from there. Its a horror novel that includes demons, and i remember thinking when i bought the book at the Stars and Stripes when i was in England, that i would never read this book, because i was scared, but since i roomed with my sister i figured i can do it and still have someone there if i was scared and i've never regretted it. Give the book 5 stars simply because it can't be put down once you start and on top of it here 20 something years later, i still remember it, still remember even the boy and his mothers names in the book, never forgot it.
