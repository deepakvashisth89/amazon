Peter Straub usually writes books I can't put down: Shadowland, In the Night Room, Lost Boy Lost Girl and A Dark Matter were gripping. But this one? I think he got inspiration from his to-do list of errands.The prologue started out interesting, then little happened in the first 80% of the book except for one shocking revelation about halfway through. You can easily skip most of this book and not miss anything. The real action starts in the last 20% of the book, and even that was mostly predictable. And one of the main characters was absent from all that. Not written out; just not mentioned.I had to struggle to finish this book, and didn't get much reward for it.Read moreRead less
