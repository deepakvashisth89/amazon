I bought this book for a class where I had to do an extra reading assignment. It was not on the original list of books, but was recommended to me by the professor. It is an older book, but the facts are still shocking...for example, I didn't know that oysters could get testicular cancer. Steingraber tells her story well and integrates it spectacularly with environmental facts and stories. This is a must read for anyone who is an environmentalist, or who is curious about the connection between the environment, environmental change, and human illnesses such as cancer.Read moreRead less
