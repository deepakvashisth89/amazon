I haven't read anything like this book. I found it difficult to put down as the characters came to life and developed. You couldn't help reading it to find out what secrets each character withheld from each other.  Full of wonderful romance, exceptional tenderness and understanding as well undeniable love and the element of evil.  I will say I was expecting a little more of a romantic ending to this book than the author provided. The book was a quick easy read and kept my attention. If you like romance and a strong debonair man this is a good choice.Read moreRead less
