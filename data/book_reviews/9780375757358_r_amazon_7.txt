Very timely topic as it tells the story of a terrorist plot set in czarist Russia. Conrad tells this story in an unusual way, with the English narrator using the diary of the main character (a Russian)  as a kind of source material. Hence the title, telling the story of Russian revolutionists through his Western eyes. Amazing that the story was written in the decade before the revolution. Fascinating characters.
