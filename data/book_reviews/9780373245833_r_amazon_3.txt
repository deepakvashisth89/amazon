Skillfully written, endearing characters, funny, sassy dialogue, great supporting cast with a subtle secondary romance, strong family theme, engaging plot that uses the longer format and the eclectic characters to add depth and enhance the storyline.THE STORY revolves around a professor of mathematics, Prof. Lucy Guerin.  She along with a diverse mix of Christmas travelers, are forced to take shelter during an ice storm at the isolated, un-Christmassy house of a socially inept, emotionally stilted, but good hearted wood-worker, Richard Banner.  Thrown together and missing their families at Christmas, each visitor contributes their talents and give more of themselves to make Christmas special for everyone, even grumpy Banner.  After the weather clears, the "guest" continue their Christmas journey.  But Lucy returns to Banner because she recognizes that underneath his prickly exterior, is an amazing man who can give her everything she wants in life.  If she can just persuade him to give himself a chance.OVERALL, a feisty, endearing romance about recognizing your own strengths, looking behind the words and actions to see the intentions of a good heart, loving unconditionally, and accepting unconditional love in return.  A touching and enjoyable read.You may also enjoy the complex characters and storytelling of N. Bruhns, A. Stuart, R. C. Ryan, E. Palfrey, or L. Wilde.
