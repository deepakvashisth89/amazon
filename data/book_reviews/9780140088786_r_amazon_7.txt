I have read considerably on the history of the "ancient" world.  This is an excellent biography of Alexander, and helps to put his life and time in context and how the present has been affected.  The author discusses the influence of his family and times on Alexander, topography, social expectations, strategy and oh, so much more.  And it is kept intresting.  I bought this book after reading a history of the city of Alexandria.  This book helped me understand influences on the world hundreds of years after Alexander's death.
