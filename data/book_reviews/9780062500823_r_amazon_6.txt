This is the first book I read that was related to Jungian psychology. It gives many insights into our inner psychologycal patterns, seen throught the lens of the Greek Godesses. It offer some really good perspectives, in an anecdotal and highly entertaining way. It is particularly useful to women, as it shows that psychological patterns that are usually considered to be masculine need not be at all. Thus, it puts into question many of the assumptions we have about femininity and masculinity. I highly recommend it to anyone.Read moreRead less
