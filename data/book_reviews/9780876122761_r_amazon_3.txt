Wonderful little book.  Full of tidbits that are easily picked up, reflected on, without entirely investing in a book's narrative progression, but more substantive than aphorisms in isolation.Thirteen chapters divided by subject matter (e.g., 'Achieving Your Goals,' 'Inner Peace,' 'Unconditional Love') within which are nestled nuggets of wisdom, which progress within the chapter, but are beautiful and helpful on their own.  The chapters and ideas build on each other, intersect, amplify one another, but each one shines on its own, should you feel the need for a little inspiration during the day.From practical discipline and the power of the Will to devotional love, this little gem packs a lot of punch.Highest recommendations for those on the path.Read moreRead less
