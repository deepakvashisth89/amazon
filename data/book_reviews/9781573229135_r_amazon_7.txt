I agree with many of the reviews in that the title is slightly misleading.  This is half memoir, half tribute to Norris' mentor, Betty Kray.  Nonetheless, this is a wonderful book, very well written, and a fabulous glimpse into an era that some of us were unable to witness.  So don't dismiss it, just be forewarned.
