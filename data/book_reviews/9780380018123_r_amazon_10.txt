As I am Polish, I first read The Star Diaries at the age of 10, when still living in Poland. Since then I read it so many times, I can quote it from memory. I got this copy for my husband, as he does not speak Polish, he is enjoying it immensely. Lem had the most amazing imagination, knowledge of human nature and curiosity about everything, all wrapped up in his very specific sense of humor. I read all of his books, and all of them are incredibly good, but if I had to choose only one for the rest of my life it would be The Star Diaries. It is not a common science fiction, but rather a satire, and although written so many years ago, it has not aged.Read moreRead less
