I came across a physical copy of this book by happenstance and enjoyed it enough to want to have a copy on my phone for those long train rides. Fox is verbose and although I find him difficult to read, he really seems to understand the strategy of ancient warfare, and he brings in thoughtful parallels throughout history that only a man of his learning could bring. I noticed my physical copy had a number of circled GRE level words, averaging one or two a page. Someone was using the text as a vocabulary builder--I have to admit with over a decade of top level education I too found a few new words. This book is not therefore a light read, but I wouldn't call it hard scholarship either.
