I found the plot of this book a bit diffuse compared to other books in the series. Half of the story has Grijpstra and de Grier investigating a hypothetical murder without a corpse. Finally the two are given a case with an actual corpse. But if the victim was in fact murdered, it had to be by some devious invisible method...The cast of characters includes oversexed young women who don’t feel quite real, as well as muggers and drug dealers.  The conversations among the cops about right and wrong, reality and illusion, get quirky and metaphysical.The ending is oddly harsh.Perhaps van de Wetering was trying in The Mind Murders to present us with a crime novel as elusive and cryptic as a koan. Perhaps the result is a success. I’m uncertain. But I remain a fan of the series...Read moreRead less
