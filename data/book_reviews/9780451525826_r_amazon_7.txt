A book written in the 1930s, and is still ridiculously accurate in its parallels to today. It is disgustingly accurate in it's portrayal of the American public and it's gradual acceptance of fascism. So many moments in this book are shockingly almost exactly word for word in their depiction of politicians and the public bickering that comes with the discourse between fear and apathy for what's happening, and the gleeful pride of the regime and it's cruelty.This book made me feel complicated emotions and heightened my anxiety (which previously hadn't been a bunch of an issue for me). I would think that this book should be required reading in high school, but is probably far more likely to end up on a banned book list for the exact same reasons I think everyone should read it.Sinclair Lewis hit the nail on the head.Read moreRead less
