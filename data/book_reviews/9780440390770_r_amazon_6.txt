This book is a definitive work outlining Tesla’s life and times. I have read this book several times across multiple editions. This most recent purchase of a more modern paperback edition is a worthy addition to the library. This book sticks to the facts and avoids conspiratorial conjecture (which often surrounds Tesla’s more controversial inventions) while still informing the reader about the miraculous contributions of perhaps one of the smartest persons to ever live.This book is accessible to younger readers as well and is a fast read. The content has stood the test of time. You don’t have to be an Electrical Engineer or electricity guru to enjoy this book. Go Tesla! Boo Edison!Read moreRead less
