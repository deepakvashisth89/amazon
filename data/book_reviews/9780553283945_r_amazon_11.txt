This is a very good book.  It starts in the 50's and goes through a few decades.  It is very evocative of that time and the main characters Julia and Mattie are best of friends from the start even though in character they are very different.  They start at the age of 15 and both work in completely different areas and realise that they lost something on the way.  I was born in the 50's so can sympathise with them, it is a book of second chances and I didn't want it to end.
