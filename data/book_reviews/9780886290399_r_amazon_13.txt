This book is a one-off. An amazing story by de Mille, of an incredible imagined culture living by the South Pole, with a completely different outlook on life, wealth and possessions to the Victorian Europeans of the author's time. It is a book which challenges, and, in it's way, demonstrates the ultimate futility of the blind quest for material wealth, and many other things that people always seem to seek.
