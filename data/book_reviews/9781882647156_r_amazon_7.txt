I thoroughly enjoyed the first trilogy, if that's what you call it. This was a good sequal. Not TOO out there. Lots of times action novels/journals can get carried away with trying to keep things alive... this didn't go too far in too many places. Maybe just a little but not enough to make me stop reading, which is easier than you'd think. I have the other novels that come after this one, too.
