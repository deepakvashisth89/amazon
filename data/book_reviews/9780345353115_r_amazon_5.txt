This is one of the few books that I have had the desire to read again. I first read this book in the 60's because the title caught my eye and I had to see what the book was about.Little did I know that it would get me hooked on science fiction. After serving in the military and seeing many of Heinlein's predictions for the future came to pass I have an even greater respect for his work.This book is geared more to younger readers. But I believe that adults will also find it enjoyable. Sure, there are some inaccuracies about the future and the solar system but given when it was written it is amazingly on on target in many respects.
