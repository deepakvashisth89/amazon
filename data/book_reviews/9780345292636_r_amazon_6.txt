I found this an intriguing novel which opens the eyes of the readers as to the problems that future space exploration will face.  Especially in the field of medicine.It forces one to consider alternative means to attain  the proper medical treatment that might be used for long term missions in  our near future.A regular ER in zero gravity.  Full of probable medical  emergencies and the daring that the station's medical personal must take  upon themselves to save the lives of their patients.A departure from  aliens science-fiction that still instills the excitement of space  pioneering.Read moreRead less
