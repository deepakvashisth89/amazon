Dead Boys has reached the pinnacle of the WORST book I have ever read, or should I say tried to read.  There is no story line, character development is nil and after a few chapters I had no idea what the author was trying to convey.  It reminded me of college when everyone would get wasted and talk night about important topics.  If one were record their conversations, it would all be shit, just like this book.  The story was like a lover on crack cocaine with no teeth and bad breath.  The level of hate for this book is beyond compare, and I left it in the bookcase of the beach cottage I just returned from.
