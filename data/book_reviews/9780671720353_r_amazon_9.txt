This is a set of short stories by Larry Niven and his friends about, you guessed it, the man-kzin wars. The first story covers the First Contact between Man and Kzin, and exemplifies the general weakness of all the stories where unarmed, pacifist humans rout the bone-headed, over-aggressive and heavily armed Kzin. It's a suspension of disbelief thing. Why didn't the unarmed humans get eaten? Because thy are just so clever. All the time. OK.The other stories try to fill in the background to the Kzin race, often successfully, but at times leave you thinking that Larry Niven is just trying to whitewash his now un-PC past depictions of the originally non-sentient Kzin females. Enter the intelligent (Gasp), feminist (double Gasp) female Kzin. Some things just are not what they used to be. Still it's an OK read to fill a wet afternoon.
