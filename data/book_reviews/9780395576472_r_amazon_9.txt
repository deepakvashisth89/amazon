🍃“Old Tom Bombadil was a merry fellow.Bright blue his jacket is, and his boots are yellow.”🍃👉The first read and review of the year👍 2026 took off on a jolly good note, eh, matey?😍🎉✨️As part of #TolkienMonth Essential Reading, I picked up 'The Adventures of Tom Bombadil' and it reminded me again why Tolkien’s world feels so alive even after all these years of reading and rereading them.🍃In 'The Adventures of Tom Bombadil', we come across the creative genuis of Tolkein who was also apt at poems, verses, and ballads. Each poem gives the reader a glimpse into Middle-earth, from a rather gentler perspective. 🍃🍃 “The Adventures of Tom Bombadil” introduces us to its most carefree soul, while “Bombadil Goes Boating” shows his quiet place in a changing world. “Errantry” is playful and fast, almost musical, and “The Man in the Moon Stayed Up Too Late” feels like a fireside tale passed down for generations. Then poems like “The Sea-Bell” and "Perry-the-Winkle", "Cat", "Oliphaunt", turn dreamlike and comic, in a way only Tolkien can manage.🍃🍃This book may not have epic quests or rings, elves or dwarves, but it’s essential reading if you want to feel Tolkien’s initial world before diving into his bigger works such as LOTR, Silmarillion, or The Hobbit. It’s folklore, melodic, humorous, and light, all that make Middle-earth feel ancient, loved, and endlessly worth returning to.🍃And honestly, it’s another reminder of why my admiration for Tolkien, and my love for his worlds, will never fade 🍃📖Read moreRead less
