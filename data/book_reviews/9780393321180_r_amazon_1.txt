Even I enjoy reading all kinds of poetry my true love is the short Zen like poetry of Haiku and Senryu.  I have collected numerous books on Haiku and Senryu over the last 60 years and I recently came across this wonderful 363 page soft cover volume (The Haiku Anthology: edited by Cor Van Den Heuvel, 3rd edition) on Amazon for a bargain price.This huge collection has more than 800 of the best English language Haiku.  Interestingly, even though Japanese Haiku is thought of being a three line poem with 17 syllables of 5-7-5; however, the Japanese language actually has certain sounds which we in the west think of as 17 syllables.  This is why many times English Haiku is designed using sometimes less and sometimes more than 17 syllables.  It is not so much counting syllables that is important in writing haiku but rather the moment in time one is attempting to show the reader.This book covers the following material: a note on the selection and layout of the poems, forward to the third edition, book list, with Haiku presses and organizations, preface to the second edition, introduction to the first edition, the Haiku Anthology, the Haiku Society of America, definitions, biographical notes and acknowledgments.If you are a lover of Haiku poetry this is a must book to add to your personal collection. I really love this book.Rating: 5 Stars.  Joseph J. Truncale (Author: Martial Art and Warrior Haiku and Senryu)Read moreRead less
