I have been a big fan of Christopher Rowley since my late teens, reading the entire Fenrille Series (loved The Black Ship), so imagine my delight when I stumbled across another of his books on Amazon that I’d missed. Battlemaster is a sequel to Vang: the Military Form, which I highly recommend as well. If you’ve read that book, then you’ll have a good idea how this one ends, but I was happy with how Battlemaster concluded. Good storyline that builds slowing and then takes off, with plenty of nice twists along the way. Interesting characters (especially the Battlemaster) that all intertwine nicely. My only knock against the Vang books is that, even 55 centuries into the future, mankind is still using guns that fire chemically-propelled bullets same as we do today. But a great book nonetheless and I highly recommend it.Read moreRead less
