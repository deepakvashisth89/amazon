I’m a stickler for reading a book series in order so Fellowship of Fear is my first Gideon Oliver mystery.  Gideon Oliver is a forensic anthropology professor who accepts a teaching position on various US military bases in Europe. He had lost his wife in an accident some time prior so the position seemed like an adventure. He’s quickly drawn into foreign espionage as an innocent bystander. The storyline is a bit dated but still plays ok today. The most entertaining fact is that Gideon is portrayed as 1 part anthropology nerd, 1 part action hero and 1 part man about town. I expect the author had fun making the anthropologist seem exciting while often giving an anthropology lesson to anyone he may be talking to at the time. He’s rather good natured when others poke fun at him about this. The book contains very mild sex scenes that are comical. Not sure if this comes from a man writing what he thinks a woman is thinking and would do or if it is back to making G.O. Into a nerdy man about town. Overlooking the improbability factors it was an entertaining story. I found the anthropology information interesting but others may find it boring.  I look forward to book #2.Read moreRead less
