I purchased this book: 'The George Foreman Lean Mean Fat Reducing Grilling Machine Cookbook' secondhand and therefore it was ultra cheap compared with new, if new copies can be found. My copy was in pristine condition.Choosing this book was a natural choice for me because I had just purchased a genuine George Foreman grill, a new one but at a hugely discounted price. But there was another more important reason for buying this book: cookbooks specifically about grilling are not exactly commonplace and books concerning the George Foreman grill are even rarer still. I don't know of another source of these books other than Amazon.I learned one thing very quickly from this book, the cooking times in many cases are typically halved compared with the instructions found on most food wrappers. I figured this had to do with the grill's 'clamshell' design which cooks from both sides thereby reducing cooking times. What makes the George Foreman grill special is its sloping design which allows fat released from cooking to drain away from the food into a collection tray. This is why the product claims to be 'fat reducing'.This is not a thick book and there is an introduction by George Foreman himself. I have not read this book cover-to-cover and I refer to it now only occasionally because the grilling principles, once learned, are never forgotten. I can see myself giving this book to another inexperienced George Foeman grill user. It provides a good starting point for this style of cooking.Read moreRead less
