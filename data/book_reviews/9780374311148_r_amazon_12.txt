In this magical book, which will be enjoyed by dog-lovers of all ages, Carl the Rottweiler is left to mind the baby on Christmas  Eve.  No sooner than Mum and Dad are out of the door, Carl and Baby begin their Christmas adventure.  Through beautifully detailed illustrations - the only text is on the first page - we follow them through the snow as they shop, sing carols, give gifts, meet Santa and finally go safely back to bed.
