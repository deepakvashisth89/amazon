I've decided that I'm sick of reading doom and gloom and vicious horrible murders or stupid sickly sweet stuff - then I discovered the Golden Age, the 1920/30's detectives, always polite and always well dressed and always interesting with good grammar and nothing to give you nightmares.Apart from Dorothy L. Sayers who is the doyenne of the intelligent detective story, Ngaio  Marsh is the next in line. When you finish the modern day detective story it takes a while to get the pictures of the murder out of your head, when you finish a detective story from the Golden age you feel the satisfaction of a job well done (even though it wasn't you that solved it) and you can sleep knowing that all is right with the world.What's not to like.Read moreRead less
