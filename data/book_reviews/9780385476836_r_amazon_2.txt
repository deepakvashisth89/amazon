I have read several of Joseph Girzone's books. I like them because they make sense to me and encourages broader thinking on my part than narrow and sometimes anal theology. His books are the equivalent of thinking outside of the box but not throwing the box away. The format, language and presentation of ideas make it easy to understand and reflect upon.
