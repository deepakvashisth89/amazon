An amazing book, an amazing series. I first stumbled onto Mongo back when I was a young teen. I had decided to start reading "adult" mystery novels instead of Nancy Drew/Hardy Boys and my only rules were I preferred first person and I liked series so I grabbed a random one of Chesbro's novels from the library and instantly hooked. What's ironic about this novel is despite being written in the 90s you could image it being written today- crazed right wing politicians, possible Russian influences on America government, I wonder what George would have thought about today's America had he lived to see it.
