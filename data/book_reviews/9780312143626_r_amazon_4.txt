This is the first book I read from the Broussard/Kit Franklyn series. Andy Broussard is an overweight chief medical examiner in New Orleans, who loves fine food, is a daily customer in Grandma O`s restaurant and somewhat carelessly, though with the greatest professionalism, takes care of the corpses which arrive to the morgue. Dr. Kit Franklyn is a criminal psychologist, an expert in suicide research. Kit receives mysteriously yellow roses for three successive days, with the third she finds a note indicating to meet the unknown person next day at the Grandma O`s. From this romantic gesture she expects to find her fiancé, Teddy, but when she arrives there on time, the person results to be an aging man. who nevertheless cannot greet her, because he collapses and dies. Several people are found dead within the next days with similar sypmtoms, which are due to a new strain of a virus similar to that of Ebola. While Broussard investigates this strange virus and falls ill to it, Kit is kidnapped and a search for her starts all around the swamps of New Orleans. The kidnapping is related to a money affair of the mysterious stranger, the illness to exotic animals introduced illegally by the kidnappers.
