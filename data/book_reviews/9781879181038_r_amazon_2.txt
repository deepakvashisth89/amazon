Well researched and written book that follows the tradition that Mary was actually the wife of Jesus, and continues that through the idea that she was pregnant when Jesus was crucified.  The concept is that the "Grail", supposedly the cup from the Last Supper and believed to have magical qualities, was actually Mary herself.  She was literally carrying the Blood of Christ in her womb.  She is spirited away to Egypt, then across the Mediterranean into Europe, ala The Da Vinci Code.
