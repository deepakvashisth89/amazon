This one was actually not half bad.  Miss Seeton is as goofy and blathery as ever (if I read of her thinking the word 'one' again it will be too soon) but the locale has changed as she is erroneously sent to Switzerland to help with a case which, of course, she is totally unqualified for.At least we don't have to read about the obnoxious villagers she lives around though.  Seriously, the Nutts are terrible people.  I'm hoping that during this series they become victims of a horrible criminal and are dispatched in a grisly manner.The supporting characters in this book are wonderful...and there are a lot of them.  It would appear that MissEss has a full Conga line of people following her on her confused way.
