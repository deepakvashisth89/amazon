Overall, LOST is a very compelling, dark thriller with real, intense characters and a few unique plot twists.  The dialogue is for adults, very hard edge, and utterly lacking in quick quips.  Reminiscent of COPLAND, LOST gets involved in the gritty realities of small town (or in this case small island) politics, filled with long-standing jealousies that the author does a wonderful job of illuminating.  The structure is tight, though menace could be added to two of the villains in order to heighten tension.The premise-- that a beautiful widow's child is kidnapped by thugs on a Mediterranean isle,  and only a scarred police chief, who is already embroiled in a life long battle with the island mafia, can  help her-is so well- conceived and executed that even the seeming cliches seem fresh and new.  There are two protagonists, Stuart and Alice, and each are deeply flawed individuals who must overcome their personal demons to find Alice's son.  The progression of their romantic relationship is subtle and believable though ultimately tragic.  On the whole, a great read.Read moreRead less
