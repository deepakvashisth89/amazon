I was a little put off at first with the notion that this submarine is supposed to be bigger than an aircraft carrier. Seriously ? You're kidding..right? Well, I kept reading and that doubt went to the back burner and I just went with the story. I'm so glad I did.It was a fascinating look into submarine warfare and the men on both sides of the cold and sometimes hot war that happens under the sea out of our sight. I couldn't put it down. You get to know the characters on both sides of the dispute and wind up caring about all of them as people. Even the supposed bad guys.I'm going to read more of Mr. Taylors novels. I found his writing mesmerizing and it made me think of a line from Jaws. " We're going to need a bigger boat !". Nope...not necessary!  :-)Read moreRead less
