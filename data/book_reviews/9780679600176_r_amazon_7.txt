It's a Pulitzer Prize-winning novel, so it's not standard easy reading.  The best suggestion I got was to find notes or a summary of the characters and narrative first so you don't have to slowly figure it out for yourself as you read it.  Each of the four sections is written from a different character's point of view as the narrator.  The first section is the most difficult, and I liked the third section best (I found it to be funny).  I can see that Faulkner did an excellent job of inhabiting the four separate narrators' characters.  This is one of those books everyone should read, and I'm glad I did, it's just not easy.Read moreRead less
