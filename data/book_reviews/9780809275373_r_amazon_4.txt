This one I like because the plot is finally substantial. The Nome king wishes to conquer the Emerald City and get his enchanted belt back from Ozma. The Nome King's general is collecting allies while the Nome King is digging the tunnel to the City. In the meantime, Dorothy brought her uncle and aunt to the Land of Oz because they could no longer live in Kansas. Apparently, their farm is to be confiscated by a bank or something like that. And while the Nome King is doing his job, Dorothy and her family are travelling around the land of Oz, meeting new people who have absolutely no role, but they're interesting.
