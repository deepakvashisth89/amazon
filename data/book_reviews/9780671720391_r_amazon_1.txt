Classifying this story is actually rather difficult.  L. Sprauge de Camp moved the culture of his world forward and was showing how modern cultural products and philosophies will slowly change another culture.  He does this by moving the time-line forward 25 years and then reintruducing two characters he had brought together before.I had not read the other Krishna books, but still found this an interesting and entertaining read.  Fergus Reith and Alicia Dyckman are well developed and written characters and the interplay between them as former spouses who still clearly loved each other is touching and well thought out.Sadly, the other characters don't hold up as well, and many are at best cliqued cutouts rather than well fleshed out foils for the heros.If you can find this book and like humor and a mix of fantasy and sci-fi, this is an excellent read.
