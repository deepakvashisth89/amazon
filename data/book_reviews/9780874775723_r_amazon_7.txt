If you want some sage advice from a well respected teacher, this is the book to read. Marva Collins went the distance to educate children moving out of the public school system and into her own space to teach the classics, such as Shakespeare and Chaucer to children - who, my opinion, were bored out of their minds. The result? Her classrooms came alive and later, the students, now adults, were found to be leading productive lives. 60 Minutes did a special about her and that's how I found out about her unique style of teaching and her vibrant and wholesome no nonsense character. Read page 144 for a great non punitive way to calm down a classroom of 30 robust children and have a student read it aloud for all. Many years ago, I asked a student to read the passage at an Alternative school and you could have heard a pin drop. I recommend this book and feel it should be required reading for every teacher.Read moreRead less
