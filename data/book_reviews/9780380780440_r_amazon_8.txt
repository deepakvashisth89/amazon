Cole McRae is a Captain during the Civil War (for the North). His ship is currently being repaired and has been tasked with transporting a female prisoner who has been tried and convicted of war crimes. Devon Blake is the prisoner who has just landed in America from England, finding herself on trial and accused of murder and transporting documents for someone who is smuggling ammunition for the Rebels.This story is very basic. Cole has lost a huge number of his men to a criminal named Jonas Sharpe. Devon has been convicted of working for Jonas Sharpe. So the initial meeting between the two is less than amicable. Cole can barely keep his anger in check because he believes that Devon is directly involved with the deaths of his crew members.It is understandable that Cole doesn't believe that Devon is innocent. And Devon does not spend her time crying about it. She proves herself to be incredibly resourceful: escaping, stealing, lying etc. It is great fun to read about how she is able to deftly steal, and con people. It is done with a true purposed, not because she is evil nor greedy.The story goes into detail of Devon's childhood and it is heartbreaking and believable. She is a survivor. She does not spend time feeling sorry for herself. She is not given to histrionics. She is a great character.As Devon and Cole spend more and more time together, you can see how Cole begins to doubt that she has a criminal's heart. I am glad that it too awhile for him to believe her. And they didn't sleep together for awhile either. The sex steams were steamy, but not central to the storyline.The side characters are great - Devon's Uncle Monty, the other soldiers (although fleeting in their presence, it adds more heart to the book).The author does a great job growing the characters as individuals as well as their relationship together.The 'bad guy' gets his just desserts at the ending, and the couple gets their happy ending. No cliffhanger.This book is a great read. Interesting story. Strong heroine who wins the heart of the doubting hero.Read moreRead less
