A young priest is assigned to a small, remote Indian village on the British Columbian mainland across from the northern tip of Vancouver Island.  We learn about the way of life that was vanishing when the book was written some 50 years ago.  The priest demonstrates complete selflessness in learning their way of life and in serving them in the most selfless way.
