A view of the Titanic from the perspective of Southampton and its people, mostly represented among the crew.  Lots of contemporary pictures and overwhelming minutiae, often quite tedius.  Not good for the casual reader, although could be quite valuable to someone looking for insight.  The biggest shortcoming, all too frequent with books about this event, is a nearly complete absence of criticism of the behavior of the crew and the ship's owners and an ad nauseum repetition of the same well known stories of glory.Read moreRead less
