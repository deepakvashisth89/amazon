I first read this book in elementary school. It was one of my favorites in the 'dear america' series. I reread it many times in elementary and decided to grab a copy and read it again now as an adult. I thoroughly enjoyed it now as a 29 yr old just as I did at 10. Based on a teen girl in Boston during the Vietnam war whose brother is a Marine in Vietnam. Book discusses struggles between generations, racism, sexism, and the tensions that arose regarding the war. My only complaint is that I didn't want it to end.The partner book, her brother's diary (Patrick Seamus Flaherty), I have also ordered and can't wait to read again as an adult.Read moreRead less
