I just finished reading this book, and found few stories that were striking or memorable.  That is not necessarily a condemnation.  Most of the pieces are so short (3-5 pages) that little opportunity is afforded for development of character, theme or setting.On the positive side, the volume might be a good companion while you wait for the dentist to finish cleaning your child's teeth, or to pass time while your spouse is getting dressed to go out to dinner, or while you are in the bathroom seeking a few moments of diversion from the preoccupations and cares of everyday life.Most of the inhabited world is represented by the authors and settings.  "Girl" by Jamaica Kincaid takes us to Antigua for a lesson in home economics; "At the River" by Patricia Grace is a dreamlike tale about the Maori in New Zealand; an execution in Cypress is the subject of "Gregory" by Panos Ioannides;  and "Looking for a Rain God" by Bessie Head is about a severe seven-year drought in Botswana.  It is a promising story marred by an unnecessarily lugubrious ending.Although I cannot give the book an enthusiastic recommendation, I am not sorry I purchased and read it.Read moreRead less
