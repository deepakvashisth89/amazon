There's a palpable sense of genuine disgust that pervades this book; you can tell the author has worked and/or lived in and around Hollywood - and didn't much like it.Everyone is using and being used and the human suffering is laid bare on every page. There's plenty of vile imagery for those that crave it, but it's far better written than most novels of a similar ilk, and it actually has some interesting characters and a coherent - rather straightforward - narrative that helps the book move along at a decent pace.The 90s setting can occasionally seem almost quaint, but it actually works well and feels real - I don't know exactly when it was written, but it feels authentic.Not for the squeamish, but if you want a good twist on the old 'Tinsel Town Vampires' trope, then you should read this.Read moreRead less
