This is the story of a child who was filled with the power of Dreaming. She had an old shaman who was called Wanderer who taught her and ended up saving her so that she could be the new priestess of the Mound building people. These people came to be know as the Mississippi people and in my state we have an ancient Mound. The temple resided on the top of the Mound and the Sun people lived there. The more common people lived below the Mound. These people lived with their fields of corn and squash spread out around their village and the river that flowed close by them.
