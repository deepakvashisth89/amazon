Jennifer woke up not remembering who she was, why she was where she was and... who the dead girl was lying so near her.  The girl was stabbed brutally to death.  Somehow, she found her way home but things didn't  getter better nor did she regained her memory.  When morning came, she  discovered that the dead girl was exactually her best friend, Crystal,  but  she still could not remember her.  Although Jennifer had lost her memory,  she firmly believed that she was no murderer, expecially one who could kill  her best friend.  But time was running out and the police were beginning to  suspect that she was the murderer while the real murderer was on the lose.
