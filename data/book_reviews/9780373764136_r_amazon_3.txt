They were childhood best friends who grew up under difficult circumstances. They maintained their friendship into adulthood.Jesse decided that she wanted a child. She considered her options. After thinking about it and checking other potential men as the possible father of her child, she decides to talk it over with Ryan her best friend. She wonders if he would considering being the father of her child. Ryan agrees but he wants to be married to give their child a more normal traditional life. Jesse only agrees to marry after she is pregnant for certain.Ryan insists that Jesse moves in with him after a difficult first few months of pregnancy because she is carrying twins. Jesse doesn't think that a real marriage is possible for them because she believes that Ryan is still grieving over his wife that died. The real obstacles to their relationship are that they are unwilling to open themselves up to one another express their true feelings and really communicate.I enjoyed reading this book. The journey from friendship to love is one of my favorite storylines. Anne Marie Winston does a good job with the character development and the storyline.This particular book is one of my favorites from the Silhouette baby bank series.Read moreRead less
