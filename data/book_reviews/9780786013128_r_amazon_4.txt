This is the first book I've read by Bentley Little.  Reading it today is like going back to the early 90's as the technology they use seems very dated, but remember the story takes place during that time period(when the story was written), so keep that in mind.I think the book was "good" not "great".  The vampire doesn't show up until the last 15% of the book (Kindle estimation), and most of the killings take place without too much reader perspective.  Bodies are found drained of blood, so there is very little suspense as the victims are attacked. In most cases, you find out after the fact. You are not privy to the victims point of view until the book is nearing the end.Even trees and animals are drained blood and life...a little strange, and I think unnecessary to the story, as it adds to an already lengthy book.  The animal and trees had absolutely nothing to do with the story, other than to give the main characters more to speculate about.  The animals, I could buy, but the trees....that's a stretch.The book wasn't scary, but it was interesting and the characters are well developed.  There was some disappointment with various characters (Rich and Sue's apparent attraction to each other), that is not fully developed and seemed unnecessary to the storyline.  I felt the ending was rushed and fell flat.SPOILER ALERT, SPOILER ALERT. SPOILER ALERT.Don't read this if you haven't read the book...OK, you were warned....there was no reason to kill every single member of Rich's family, including Rich himself.  The way he died was unnecessary.  Even his brother states that in the book.  After getting invested in these characters, the author could have easily given the readers some good feeling of resolution, and hope for the main characters instead of the downer ending.Not a bad read, but could have had a better ending and the vampire should have had more "book time", instead of waiting until the book was nearly done.It could have been so much better.
