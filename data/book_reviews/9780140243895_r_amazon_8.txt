Rarely do I find a book that touches me the way this one does.  It speaks to all of us who have felt like misfits in a world that cares so much about outward appearance and conformity.Benjamin, a young boy of 12, is a troubled child who escapes from his reality with his toy, a stuffed letter H he calls Elliot.  It seems his toy is his only companion, and the only thing Benjamin allows to share in his imaginative but unbalanced life.  Through letters from Benjamin's camp counselors, parents and from Benjamin to Elliot, we find how a lonely young boy adapts when his world is stolen from him.
