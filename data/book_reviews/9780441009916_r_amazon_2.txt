I enjoy good solid science fiction, and this novel delivers. The world / universe is dense with detail and cultural differences.  I enjoyed how the book pulled me in with the main character, and then dragged in this unlikely pairing with another, completely different secondary character.  The pair together gave the book enough twists that I was swept along into their world, and it made me think on the world views that both had-- how they differed, and how they were similar-- it made the premise more realistic.
