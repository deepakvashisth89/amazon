Absolutely great book.  I would recommend buying the whole series.  I have read book 1 and book 2 both a fabulous.  You can buy them in a book set entitled "The Rogues Club" and save a little money especially on the kindle addition.  This book at an interesting storyline and unique plot which I thoroughly enjoyed. I absolutely loved Gideon's character.  He was amazing, just the sort of rogue that you wish to find for your self.  I think he makes the story.  He made me laugh, cry, groan with lust, and cheer for him to win over Sabrina and her kids.
