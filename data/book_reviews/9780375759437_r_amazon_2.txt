I ventured up to the Tahltan Territory in 1970. In fact, I was based about 12 miles out of Telegraph Creek;  the memories are strong and the reminiscing even stronger.This wasn't the last frontier, it was the late frontier, but the characters truly were the last of a breed that will never come again. It is our loss. This was a place of fierce independence - a stiff necked people who you could count on to be exactly as they believed.I certainly recommend this to any who wish to know how it was...only yesterday.Read moreRead less
