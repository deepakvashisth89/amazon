This debut novel in a series known as "A Simon Kirby-Jones Mystery", was a delightful surprise to me. We have an expatriate, Simon, having just moved to a English village known as Snupperton Mumsley. Now Simon is a Southern Gentleman formerly from Mississippi. He brings the fact that he is an Historical biographical author whom dabbles in writing romances and mysteries under alias, however, he also just happens to be gay and a vampire with magic little pills.Put the above into a typical stylization of a "Merry Margaret Mead," and you have an up to date English cozy. The characters are humorous, full-bodied and each one has a past they would rather keep to themselves.  The Village post-mistress wants to write a play disclosing these very interesting pasts.This book is Not a run of the mill British cozy though and I was laughing out loud throughout. I agreed with the Author about the victim. I found myself supporting causes and just delighted.Personally, I found myself pleased. I was pleased about the light hand given to the character of Simon.
