This was the first bodice ripper that I ever read. I was probably about seven years old or so and raided my granny's stash of books. From what I recall, Emerald is a gently raised southern girl who finds out from her stepbrother that she has negro blood in her. He tries to rape her, but with help she escapes and joins a wagon trail west bound.She falls in love with a scout named Mace (I think) and then gets captured by some Indians where she is made the wife of some Indian guy who she doesn't seem too mind too much. She's rescued and goes back to Mace where she eventually has her happily ever after.
