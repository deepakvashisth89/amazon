I have read a lot of books about dinosaurs to my son...but this is one of the best I have run across.  The information is presented very well. The book lists the dinosaurs according to the time period in which they evolved. It also has information on climate, diet, special characteristics  of each dinosaur, and little scenarios depicting dinosaur life.  My son  especially liked the comparative studies between different species.  The  pictures are fantastic.  It is detailed enough to answer most questions a  child would ask..yet not too complicated. If your child is  too young to  read well...then be prepared to read aloud till you are hoarse!
