I met Robert Bly when I was a young man and he had a profound impact upon me. To me some of his poetry is incredible, and some of it stinks. Bly appears to have taken a turn halfway in his career into the Dark Side. To me this is babel- a waste of genius. Maybe being in the ivory tower surrounded by weirdos ruined him.
