The first of the Sommerville brother's trilogy:Gaby and Ruarke's life  together starts off as anything but pleasant. They don't speak the same  language.  Gaby lies about her name so he won't know about her abusive  family and Ruarke wants to marry her only because she looks like his dead  first love. This isn't your typical romance novel.
