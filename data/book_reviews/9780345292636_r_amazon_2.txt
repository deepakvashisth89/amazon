Someone wiser than I once said that every story element had been used before the gospels were written, and nothing remains but to try them in new combinations: There is nothing new in this story BUT it is the work of a seasoned writer and its merits lie mainly in the effortless reading that results from the writer's craftsmanship. The style never gets in the way of the story but the skills fill in the background and move the plot along without advertising how it has been done.
