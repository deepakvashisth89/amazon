The title is excellent--funny, memorable, and appropriate for this novel. The author smartly opens the story in-scene, with Monica's wedding. We meet Amber, our narrator, and her best friend, and then we see Amber meet Preston. Amber and Preston's relationship is the novel's focus, and the author smartly puts their first encounter in the first chapter. The novel is well structured, with a clear beginning, middle, and end. We consistently understand why certain actions are taken and the conflict heightens as Amber and Preston's relationship develops and is tested. The story climaxes when Amber ends their relationship. Then, the author provides a very smooth action, in which we see the couple reunite at a funeral. Preston learns to listen to Amber's concerns. The novel ends with a fabulous wedding.Read moreRead less
