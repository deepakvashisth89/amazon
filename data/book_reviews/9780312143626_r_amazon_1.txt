I've been reading this series since the first book, which I had a hard time finding. But since it is a series, I needed to read these in order. I'm so glad that I have, since the characters are being developed nicely. Kit is so headstrong, but I'm beginning to see a glint of maturity starting to happen.Being a retired physician & also holding a degree in medical microbiology & parasitologist from LSU Medical School, the topic of this book fascinated me, such so that I read it in less than 2 days. There is so much excitement happening in this book, to say the least.I no longer live in New Orleans, but the love of my former hometown makes these books all the more special to me.
