I discovered Peter Danielson many years ago when he had several books already written in the "Children of the Lion" series.  Then I would read the next book just when it came out and have to wait another year for the next one.  I believe there are 17 books in the series and I have read all of them THREE times over the years.  "Triumph of the Lion" was a superb addition to the collection.  I devoured it in just a few days, savoring every chapter.  As historical fiction goes--this series is my all-time favorite.
