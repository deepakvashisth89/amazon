An excellent study of two very human people who helped revive a nation.  Although they were vilified by many, they were admired by many more.  I grew up in a town where the local newspaper was titled "The *** Republican"  and was eight years old and playing in our backyard when the lady next door called over to my mother that President Roosevelt had died.  I was terribly upset and hid behind the rosebushes and cried ......because I thought that meant that we would lose the war!  Such was the importance of Roosevelt to us ordinary people, Republicans as well as Democrats,  and this book will show you why.
