The title tells the story. This is a blank book book with some illustrations. This is a lovely journal. I have owned mine for over 10 years and still have not been able to bring myself to mar a page with a single pencil mark. The illustrations are so beautiful, the pages in the book have held together very well. I have not had any issues with it. I have a first edition and it is in perfect condition. I often think of writing in it and then decide that I just can't. I write in cheaper notebooks. Perhaps one day, I will bring myself to the point of writing in this one.To those who complain that they were expecting a book or something more.......read before you purchase. Or "Let the buyer beware". It is what is. A beautiful journal filled with lovely illustrations, you do the writing. And please stop complaining.
