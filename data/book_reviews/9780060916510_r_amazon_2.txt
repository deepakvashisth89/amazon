I was especially interested in this book because Ms Hurston was my next door neighbor in Ft Pierce, Fl for three years before she died in 1960.  I have only read Jonah's Gourd Vine at this time.  I love the way Ms Hurston told the story of her parents and her community while growing up in Alabama and Eatonville, Fl.  She was very authentic in her approach and used the language of the people she wrote about which I can relate to.  The language can be difficult to understand if you are not from the culture but the story is truly remarkable because it's about Human realness in it's purest form and is not contrived or pretentious like so many other books are,  for commercial purposes.
