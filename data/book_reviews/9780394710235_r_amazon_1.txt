Peter Dickinson is known for strange stories, and this one is probably one of the strangest I've ever read.  The setting is in the desert somewhere.  We have an Englishman who is in charge of a Sultan's private zoo, and he finds himself in the middle of an intrigue that ends up taking the life of his patron.  He is charged with finding the murderer of a sultan, and he has to head into the marshes to deal with the marsh people to do this.  The book outlines the marsh culture which is unspeakably barbaric.  But the book is still a cracking good mystery that is complex and difficult to figure out.  To say the book is bizarre is an understatement, but it is a good one never-the-less.Read moreRead less
