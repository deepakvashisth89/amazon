I began reading Patricia Wentworth's books with Maud Silver decades ago.  Over the years, I have come across new printings and pick one up on the chance that I may still enjoy her writing.  With my new IPad, I decided to try one of her books in the ebook version.  Ms. Wentworth's writing skills and her plots, like those of Agatha Christie, make her stories timeless.
