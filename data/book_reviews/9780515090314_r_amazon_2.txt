Having seen the movie inspired by this book many times, I thought I had a good idea of what the book contained.  I was wrong.  The movie, excellent as it is, is a mere shadow of the book.TO SIR WITH LOVE is E.R. Braithwaite's account of his struggle to find employment in post-WWII London.  The acceptance he experienced as a black soldier in uniform is gone, replaced by the prewar prejudice against "colored" people.The story is beautifully told, the prose is elegant, the feelings are powerfully expressed, and the writing is elegant.  It's a sad commentary that Mr. Braithwaite's experiences were as relevant in the late sixties when the movie is set as they were in the late forties when they occurred.  It's even sadder that now, about fifty years later, they're equally relevant.  It's only January, but I'm fairly certain this will be the best book I read this year.Read moreRead less
