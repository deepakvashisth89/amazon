The Loo Sanction is the sequel to the Eiger Sanction and Jonathan Hemlock is at it again. It is a very enjoyable romp through London but not as exciting as the Eiger Sanction. Jonathan remains his acerbic self and his penchant for violence has not diminished. This story is more urbane and yet is a lesser adventure.
