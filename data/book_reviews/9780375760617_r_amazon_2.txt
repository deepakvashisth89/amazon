I have read about Bobbie Ann Mason that if you looked up short story in a dictionary that you would see her name. Now I am not totally sure that is the case (more likely Alice Munro) but I do believe that she is a compelling writer and really speaks to the heart of what people feel when they travel around America and see the different types of people and characters in our varied country. Most of the stories seem to take place in small  towns around America. Characters that are interesting but sometime small-minded.I enjoyed most the story "Rolling into Atlanta" where Annie was tapped by the corporate owner of restaurants to embed into a restaurant and spy on the employees.  She finds herself buying a dog and falling in love with one of her new co-workers. I also enjoyed Three Wheeler where an older lady who is focused on her pottery shed has to either embrace or turn away two young boys looking for companionship and possibly mentoring.
