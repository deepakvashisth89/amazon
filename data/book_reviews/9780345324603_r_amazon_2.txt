The original 8 book series by Cole and Bunch is IMO about the greatest SF series I have ever read.  The writing is excellent with few errors and the later printings do have the few technical errors I noted corrected, mostly in the use of Metric dimensions which the authors, or their editors, were clearly unfamiliar with.
