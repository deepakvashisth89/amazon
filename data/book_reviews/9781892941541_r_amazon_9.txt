While the subject matter of this book (the mostly female rulers of Russia between Peter the Great and Catherine the Great) is certainly interesting, I do feel that Mr. Troyat could have squeezed a more interesting book about these mostly mediocre but very colorful ladies. The book contained a number of strange errors that seemed to suggest that Troyat had not really bothered all that much.
