Frannie held her book up and practiced her part in the Purim play as her brother, Ezra, and her sister, baby Rachel, the "Purim noisemaker," played horsey.  Giddyup!  It was March and it was time for Purim.  The night before the children wore their "costumes to synagogue to hear the story of Purim read."  The children were working on their costumes.  Ezra, who was going to be King Ahasueras, was making a silver foil crown and Frannie was putting sparkly beads on her mother's old robe.  Frannie was going to play the important part of Queen Esther in the play, the queen who "saves the Jewish people."  Of course little Rachel was trying to put a hat on the kitty cat.Frannie looked very concerned when she answered the phone.  It looked like their cousins had the flu and wouldn't be able to act in the play.  David said he wanted to be King Ahasueras, so Ezra would play Mordechai.  Just who was going to play the "villain Haman, the wicked man who tried to murder all the Jews?"  Mama and Papa were busy in the kitchen preparing for the Purim party.  Mama was making  "bet-you-can't-eat-just-one" hamantaschen.  No, Papa wasn't going to play Haman, but Mama claimed Mrs. Teplitzky would.  Frannie knew she was a strange old lady.  Just how could she play a man's part?  Was she going to ruin the Purim play?This is an excellent tale about the Purim play its characters.  Of course the most important character is Queen Esther, played by Frannie.  The house is filled with excitement as everyone prepares for Purim.  I liked the way the story of Purim was incorporated into the story as the children rehearse and prepare their costumes.  Of course "crazy and wonderful things" happen during Purim and Frannie finds that this is very true.  The artwork is nicely detailed and captures the special family feel of Purim.  In the back of the book is a section discussing the story of Purim ("The Feast of Lots"), one that talks of how it is celebrated, and last, but not least, is the recipe for Mama's Famous "Bet-You-Can't-Eat-Just-One" Apricot-Orange Hamantaschen.Read moreRead less
