Carder's 2009 book about Methodist beliefs is engaging and useful. Building chapter-by-chapter he educates the reader in the most critical elements of John Wesley's ideals, then very clearly explains how those ideals should be put to use in today's world. For the academic reader, each chapter also presents a bibliography of resources.
