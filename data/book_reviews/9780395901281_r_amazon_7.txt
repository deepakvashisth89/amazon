The success of David Leavitt surprises me.  He really isn't all that good, and "Arkansas" reflects this.  In it we have three short novels that are gay themed.  The first is a somewhat comical affair with the author being a ghostwriter for college kids, the second about contrived interpersonal drama among friends in Italy, and the last about a struggling writer befriending someone dying of AIDS.  No depth to the writing, with the second novella being the worst.  The last story had promise but it ended rather abruptly.Bottom line: best avoided.Read moreRead less
