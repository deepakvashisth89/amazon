I finished "The Everlasting Stream: A True Story of Rabbits, Guns, Friendship, and Family," by Walt Harrington, last night, for the second time.It's a memoir by a former Washington Post reporter who spent years hunting, in Kentucky, at Thanksgiving, with his father-in-law and others.The book would be worth it on that basis alone.But I was also interested in Harrington's growing realization that our society is emersed in self-defeating, shallow values.People spend so much time striving for money, recognition and success that we have no time to live.But heaven has a way of showing us stuff.Harrington's book was published in 2014, so it's not political, but Harrington's portrait of modern America is a reflection of Donald Trump.Trump is a crumbling abcess of a man who cares about nothing but money, power, recognition.We need to learn from this.Harrington and his family left Washington for Illinois and he now writes books.Read moreRead less
