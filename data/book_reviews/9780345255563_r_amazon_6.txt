Yes this book is dated(It was written over three decades ago).  It failed to anticipate the end of the Cold War, the rise of the Internet, smart phones or even home computers.  Cities around the world were choked with people, polluted and impoverished.  And the USA and USSR were fighting a proxy war in orbit that threatened to start World War Three on the ground.  It's vision of the future was dismal.And yet, there was hope.  Hope that two men, living on the moon, one from the USA and the other from the USSR could maybe make a difference and possibly save the world.  That's what sets the Science Fiction of yesterday with the Science Fiction of the 21st Century.  Back then there was a grain of hope that, while things are bad, there was still hope.  There was an optimism despite tremendous obstacles and the strength to overcome those obstacles..  The Science Fiction of today revolves around a future where there is no hope because there's nothing anybody can do either individually or collectively.  Its all dystopian cyberpunk or revisionist history or Lord Of The Rings wannabee fantasy.That's what makes this book a good read:  Hope.Read moreRead less
