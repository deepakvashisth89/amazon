I didn't know what to expect from a novel written by a former professional wrestler. This was a wonderful first novel; dark and brooding at times but at other times quite light and amusing. The novel was definite "crude" with language and situations which tended to stress negative and even digusting issues in  life. But as it is presented as a "autobiography" in the first person it does seem more reasonable.This is not a read for the immature but only  for those who have developed enough strength of character as to be not negatively affected by the lines. It does give wonderful insight to a troubled life with a most troubled history. I would conditionally recommend this book.Read moreRead less
