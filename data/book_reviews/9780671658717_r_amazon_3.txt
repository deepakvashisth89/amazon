I loved and cringed at the movie Secretary (a movie based upon a short story written by Ms. Gaitskill).  I did a little research and found Mary Gaitskill's Bad Behavior: Stories.  I didn't skip ahead to Secretary where I fully expected to read more about the characters portrayed in the movie.  She's a wonderful writer but the stories were oh so depressing.  I finally got to Secretary and it was nothing like the movie.  Since when is the movie better than the book??? Not usually, right?  But in this case, it was.
