Even though some of the info has 3.0 written all over it, this book is still a solid guide for players and DMs looking to build Keeps, Forts, Castles etc.Any players looking for a base of operations on extended campaigns can easily use this book to build anything from simple outposts to complex Castles and have the associated costs and upkeeps.  It includes things like making certain types of walls and buildings cheaper depending on the landscape.  It's easy to obtain wood by a forest and stone in the mountains for example.The one thing it really doesn't go over (that I saw) that would need to be addressed is followers and cohorts ( as well as players themselves) doing much of the work and upkeep.  As a group of players all with leadership at 8-10th level would have a decent sized workforce following them around already, and they would already be responsible for feeding them and sheltering them, the costs to building the stronghold should be affected immensely.
