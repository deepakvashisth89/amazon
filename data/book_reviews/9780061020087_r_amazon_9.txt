I discovered the Tristram books by accident, browsing in a charity shop, and loved the first one so much I couldn't get it out of my mind. I ordered the rest of them and went through them in about 10 days (a really good dense journey, as I read fast and didn't do much else in those 10 days). So if you enjoy sci-fi and good fantasty - get these. They're not the lightest of fantasy but that's to my taste (fluffy bunny fantasy makes me ill), and Tristram is one of the most satisfying light-to-dark heroes I've come across.Read moreRead less
