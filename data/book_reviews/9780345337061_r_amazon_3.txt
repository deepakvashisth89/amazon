This is one of Ruth Plumly Thompson's best Oz books.  It has a real plot, a daring story (had RPT's crystal ball been working, she might have titled it "The Manchurian Candidate in Oz"), vivid narration, interesting secondary encounters with the Witch of the Black Forest and the Gnomes, and appealing characters, most of all the morally-ambiguous title character.
