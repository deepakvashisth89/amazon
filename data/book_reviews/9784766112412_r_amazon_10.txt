How to Draw Manga: Couples is a pretty good volume in this series. It focuses on people interacting in pairs and how to effectively illustrate the activities they engage in. It's definitely a mature volume given some of the subject matter, but very broad in what it covers. There is a fair bit of nudity in the material, as with other volumes in the series, but I would say it suits the subject matter.As with other volumes, this is a how to draw book, but it is not a step by step how to draw book. There is a lot of great reference material, a bit less instruction than some of the other volumes I have come across, and it all follows the same block style method where you learn as you progress through the book. If you can draw two stick people chances are there is something to take away from this book that will help improve your drawing.Once you reach the half way point it is almost all reference drawings with small explanations scattered throughout. The artwork is pretty standard in comparison to the other volumes I have, but there are a few pages that are really weak. I have already mentioned the nudity, but there are  also some pages that deal with same sex couples, both male and female. Not really a big deal, but some may think otherwise. As with a lot of the content in this series, it is stuff you would not find in a western book on how to draw comics.
