Jacen and Jaina and their friends find themselves swept up into a larger plot. The Empire's back and gearing up for a confrontation. They find themselves facing more than rhetoric, rocks and daggers to be precise.Meanwhile, Tenel Ka joins Luke Skywalker on the hunt for the kidnappers.What results is a decent YA book.
