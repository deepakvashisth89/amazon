Unlike most of the Golden Age mystery writers, Elizabeth Daly had the ability to be genuinely creepy at times. Her premise for this plot is an example of that, and I loved the little chill that went up my spine when the characters finally got "somewhere in the house." Ms. Daly's books are hit or miss for me (mostly hit), and this is one of the best ones, IMHO. I don't know why it took me so long to discover Agatha Christie's favorite author - maybe they weren't being published in the USA?
