I find anything written by Linda Grant thought provoking since she sets her stories in modern historical backgrounds which show how political events always have consequences for future generations. I always learn something more about Jewish history from her writing. I like the structure of this novel, written in two alternating points of view so we see how each character learns to understand and appreciate the other as well as revealing their relevant backstories. The characterisation is fresh and original. Alix is a woman of our times but no cliched feminist and Joseph is an American businessman but no insensitive capitalist stereotype; both have layers and depth.
