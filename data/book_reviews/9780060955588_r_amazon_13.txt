This is an easy reading, beautifully written book.  It touched me from the moment I starting reading it.  John O'Donohue has a great understanding of life whether it be people, animals and nature, all held in the all encompassing love of the Divine and this comes across in a most beautiful, gentle way.  This book has become a companion to me.  One does not have to read it from beginning to end - just a look at the richness of the titles to chapters, will call one to read and savour whatever seems appropriate at the time.
