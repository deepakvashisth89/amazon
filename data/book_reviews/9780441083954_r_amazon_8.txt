Having read "The Good Old Boys" and "The Time It Never Rained", I had high expectations for this Elmer Kelton book.  While it was a good story, and kept my interest, it is not in the same class as those previously mentioned.  Somewhat predictable and having few really interesting characters, "Buffalo Wagons" is average fare, not the compelling reading I'd come to expect from Kelton.
