The next thing you notice about this book is the balanced way they present both the US and Vietnamese sides of the battle. This puts you right into the Ia Drag valley. As a combat veteran I have to read this book slowly and with many breaks But it is still well worth the read.The saddest part of the whole book Is that we have not learned these lessons as the current conflict in Iran and potential conflict in Cuba show.
