I am relatively new to Madeline Baker.  Nevertheless I am hooked.  It seems each book I read gets better and better.  I find myself laughing and crying along with the characters.  UNDER A PRAIRIE MOON is a wonderful time travel.  So far I have read three of her time travels and am amazed at how she has managed to make each one different from the others.
