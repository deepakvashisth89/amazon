After reading other Hawks Way books, this one was a disappointment.  Putting aside the whole 'why would you marry a man who doesn't love you' question (there is an answer that, while not entirely satisfying, worked), the way the romance unfolded just felt contrived.  The trip to the fertility specialist was poorly researched.  The rationale why HE wanted a loveless marriage ... okay ... the dude got burned badly (I won't spoil it for you).The 'tragedy' before the end just totally didn't work for me.  He could have loved and accepted her without it (after all ... nearly a year of trying nightly and not succeeding ... the tragedy was just contrived) and adopted the little girl.
