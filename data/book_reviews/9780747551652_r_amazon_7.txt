This is a classic read - original, funny, shocking and somewhat frightening in places. Un-put-down-able, a tale of a student in swinging 60's London whose chosen subject for his university dissertation is devil worship and the occult. He joins a secret society in the heart of London and details his experiences and training in the underworld.
