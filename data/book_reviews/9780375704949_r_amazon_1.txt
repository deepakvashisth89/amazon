I can't really add to those excellent reviews already written but to say I had great difficulty putting this book down.  This 1971 novel of Rendell's is somewhat different than her later works -- perhaps due to more (albeit, dark) humor injected, as we laugh at the bumbling, evil, greedy, and stupid Stanley Manning.  There were a few twists which were foreshadowed early in the story.  I thought the ending appropriate and fairly satisfying.Read moreRead less
