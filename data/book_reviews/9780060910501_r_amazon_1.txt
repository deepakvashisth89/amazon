I never received this  book because of problems with shipping but the store I purchased from did agree to send me another book of the same value in place of this one. I ordered another one on Barnes and Noble and have started to read it. This is a heartbreaking but courageous collection of stories of victims of sexual abuse. I recommend to anyone going into a field in Human Services. It really allows you to experience first hand accounts of the effects and child's feelings when they are being sexually abused by relatives or strangers. However, this book as far as I have read only focuses on woman who were abused by men so for a male who has been abused I wouldn't recommend it as highly to them as I would a female, if they were looking into ways to try to heal from being victims of the horrible acts.Read moreRead less
