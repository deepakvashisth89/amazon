Cooper's book is an interesting and informative study of a once famous hoax, significant for people's willingness to believe in the patently absurd, a willingness Cooper himself sometimes displays.  The incidents described illustrate superstitions from an earlier period which today's public would surely not believe, and yet the twenty-first century is itself characterised by a continuing belief in all sorts of irrationalities.
