Sleep Toward Heaven was the debut novel for acclaimed novelist, Amanda Eyre Ward. I attended a workshop at Kauai Writers’ Conference that she led and I always try to read the work of the workshop leaders before I participate to give me some sense of their writing style. I didn’t get a chance to read the book before the conference, but she was such a generous and effective instructor, that I did want to make sure I read her work. I am glad I did.This is a skillfully choreographed novel of three women – Karen, a serial killer on death row, Ceila, the widow of one of the men Karen murdered, and Frannie, the physician who cares for Karen, who is dying of aids as she awaits execution.It is a sad story, leavened by the honest humor of the three women, all struggling with their place in the world. The stifling atmosphere of summer in small-town Texas almost made forget that it was ten degrees outside here in Chicago. And the interaction of the women on death row was masterful. Heartbreaking, funny, and very very real.Highly Recommended.Read moreRead less
