I became interested in Colleen McCullough when I watched The Thorn Birds. Then I found out another movie I liked was based on one of her books Tim. When I read the summaries of various books of hers this one stood out to me. I just finished reading An Indecent Obsession and it is filled with plenty of drama and self introspection of the characters. It's set at the end of the Japanese war in a mental hospital in Ward X. Ward X is where soldiers who can't hack it are sent. There are only a few men there and one main nurse that takes care of them. A new patient shows up and things begin to change leading to many dramatic situations that are very well written by McCullough. I felt like I was there in every setting and each character is very believable. I recommend this book to anyone who likes a well written book and a good story with good characters.Read moreRead less
