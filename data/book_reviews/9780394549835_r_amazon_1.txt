The author survived and spent his remaining days as shipyard worker. Upon retirement, he sat down and wrote a memoir of his time as a B17 pilot. For a book as brilliant as this to come from the pen of a non professional writer is a great accomplishment. His description of being in a modern war and its attendant horrors is as good as anything I've come across. His love of flying and the beauty of the skies is wonderfully described. It reminds me of Gabriel Chevalier's memoir of WW1:Fear. Truly a remarkable book.Read moreRead less
