This is a very preliminary review due to the fact that I am only about half-way through "The Last Investigation."I simply want to give potential readers encouragement to go ahead and order this fascinating piece of work.I'd like, first of all, for you  to know how I stumbled upon this publication.  A few months ago I completed Bill O'Reilly and Martin Dugard's "Killing Lincoln." I enjoyed reading that book and decided to purchase their "Killing Kennedy" book next.As I was checking out the Amazon reviews of the Kennedy book I noticed that someone suggested Gaeton Fonzi's "The Last Investigation" instead.  I immediately ordered Fonzi's book.I disagree with the reader who suggested that it's over our heads and difficult to read.  This is an amazing read.  I can see why someone can say this is somewhat a difficult read, however.  There are a lot of characters to follow and names to remember and a lot of subplots to recall throughout the book.At this point. my plan is to read  it a 2nd time just as soon as I finish my first reading.Every believer in a true democracy should read and understand this powerful piece of work.  To the late Mr. Fonzi and his family; may he R.I.P. and thank you very much for this contribution to our nation's history and democracy.  May his words live forever.Read moreRead less
