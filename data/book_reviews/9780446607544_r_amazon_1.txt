This is a first rate thriller.  From the time I opened this book, I was unable to put it down.  I purposely purchaed this book to read while on vacation, which I started on the flight.  Within a few hours after arriving at my vacation destination on the west coast, I finished the book.  It is the type of story that is just impossible to put down.  Clearly, this is the product of a great thriller writer with a fantastic imagination and a superior ability to tell a story.  I especially liked the inter-weaving of facts into the fiction of the novel, to the point of where I was beginning to question the real history of the main theme.  His choice of characters, their different personalities and his integration of their individual character into the theme set this story head and shoulders above others of the same genre.
