If you're a Tupac fan, you need this book. I also recommend, Tupac Shakur from the editors of Vibe. For this book though, there are many, MANY, unpublished and rarely seen photos of Tupac at home, with friends and family, on music and movie sets and on and on.  This essentially is a photo book filled with quotes and excerpts from interviews as well as copies of hand written poems and lyrics. It's a very touching book that will have you feeling like you have followed Tupac's growth from his days as a child to his untimely passing.
