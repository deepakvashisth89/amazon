The Church Visible" is the result of the author's revision and enlargement of a previous edition, published in 1996. I must say at the outset that the physical book is not as polished as the original, mainly in the substitution of a lesser quality of paper. I'm sure the author had nothing to do with this. As a result of this substitution, however, the price has been kept the same as it was 17 years ago.That said, I'm sure the revision will provide the buyer for his lifetime authoritative and punctilious description of everything about the Church except its theology.James-Charles Noonan has the credentials as a protocol expert to document the ceremonies, vesture, ritual and Vatican life of the Church of Rome. In his introduction he makes plain that he is concerned only with the Church defined by the changes of Vatican II. He has been accused of documenting a long gone Medieval panoply. This is not the case. He points out that the ink was hardly dry on the new protocol before some quarters, especially in the United States, began interpreting and implementing it according to the New Age spirit then in vogue. He stresses that Pope Benedict has tried to restore the dignity and beauty that was cast aside in the Spirit of the Age. ("Who marries the Spirit of the Age is a widower in the next.")This book is and deserves to be THE standard authority on the Church. Mr. Noonan's research is outstanding.
