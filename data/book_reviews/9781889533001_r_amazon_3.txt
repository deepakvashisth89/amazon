This is a nicely designed presentation and a highly accessible game system for Star Trek TNG scenarios. It uses D6 in the ICON game system, and is very easy to teach to even beginner RPG players. The tables are readable and the approach is flexible enough to support a wide variety of scenarios. The Tactical chapter is quite limited, except for specific character actions and individual combat, so I use a combination of Starfleet Battles and the Star Trek Fleet Captains system for all starship tactical maneuvers. Despite this gap in starship operations, this book overall it is a fine product.Read moreRead less
