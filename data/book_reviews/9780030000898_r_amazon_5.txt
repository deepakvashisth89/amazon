The Manciple family is somewhere on the spectrum between “eccentric” and “mad”. When Inspector Henry Tibbet of Scotland Yard is called in to investigate the death of outsider Raymond Mason on the Manciple property, he is drawn into a series of misunderstandings and miscommunications which both tangle the mystery of Mason’s death and leave the reader gasping with laughter. I defy you to get through this book without laughing out loud.  The plot is as eccentric as the Manciples, but who cares?Read moreRead less
