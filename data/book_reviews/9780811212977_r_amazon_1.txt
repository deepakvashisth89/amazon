Uwe Timm One of the best living writers in Germany. A great read for anyone looking for a story of love, liberation and learning about one's childhood memories. The setting gives the perspective of regular Germans in the final days of WWII, but it is by no means a war novel. I've read it with classes for as long as it has been out, and the story never gets old.  You don't need to be a student of German or German history to enjoy it - you only need to know what Currywurst - or curried sausage is.
