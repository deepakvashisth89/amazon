A Room of One's Own is a genuine glory, flowing like water... sometimes enervating like gentle rain, sustaining like a deep slow stream in summer, other times challenging like oceans wave crashing on the cliffs of patriarchy. Yet never is Woolf angry, she disparages gently, clearly marking her belief in the difference between women and men. Welcoming the prospect of more genders.
