Like another reviewer said, the author apparently put herself in as a "Mary Sue" self enrichment character to interact with some of TV's favorite sci-fi cast.  I shrug my shoulders at it.  I thought the author writing herself in as the "special security detachment" for Star Fleet was, well, hokey at best.The truth is the only exceptionally good part of this book in the original Bantam books series of novels based on the TV show, was the original cover art.  You had the Grim Reaper holding the U.S.S. Enterprise in an hourglass, clutched by its bony hand.  If made you wonder what was going to happen to the Enterprise, Kirk, Spock and the rest of the gang.  But, alas, the book did not deliver on dramatic content, and referenced some Franz Joseph creations to further her story.I shrug my shoulders at it.  It was something to read.  Sky actually got Spock's nuances down, as well as McCoy and the rest, although Kirk seems to be a bit hit or miss in the characterization department.  There are some other better Trek noves out there, but this one isn't too bad, but it isn't really that good either.
