I was a little surprised to find this book is an autobiography instead of the science fiction and horror that mr. sleator usually writes. It is a must read for william sleator fans who will come to understand the author's source for such amazing ideas for his books. Be warned these are just stories of his typical (or not so typical) youth, and not a mind bending science fiction read. We learn of his unusual upbringing and his off the wall family as the author was growing up. The horrible and funny things his family do to others and each other make for an amusing read.Read moreRead less
