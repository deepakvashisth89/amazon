Midkill must be the best kept secret in the publishing world. La Plante seems to have gone through these experiences himself, such is his detailed description and knowledge on mind control. This is a well weaved plot with  a thrilling pace and a most enjoyable style. Each one of the characters has  something valuable to contibute to the story in terms of motives and  philosophical reasons. La Plante is a daring writer, one who is eager to  explore what others are scared of even considering. I like his choice of a  half American-half Japanese protagonist to illustrate the schism between  the Western and Eastern thoughts. There's really an abyss between them  both.
