A fine collection of military-themed science fiction focused on tanks--usually but not always those gigantic, semi-sentient monsters like the Bolos.  There's a wide range of conditions, some of which I'd not seen before,  and overall the book holds together very well.  One in particular--"Field Test"--frankly has one of the more effective final sentences I've seen in such a story.  It'll make any man who understands the quest for honor move to tears.An excellent collection.  There were only a couple I thought were duds.  RecommendedRead moreRead less
