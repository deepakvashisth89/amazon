This book contains the first two stories in a series of four, they are loosely related, using characters from the previous story.  While this ties them together nicely they are wildly different, but each a gripping story.  I could not put these books down until I had read all four stories, and they are on my "re-read regularly" shelf, and they have to be pretty darn good to make that shelf.  If you are looking for books with really sensual characters and situations that are believable, give this writer a read.  I don't care at all for her later books about boxing, but her earlier books are all great.Read moreRead less
