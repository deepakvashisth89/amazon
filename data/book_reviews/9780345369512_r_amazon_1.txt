When two people end up murdered, it falls to Sheriff Charles Matthews to solve the mystery, in the process revealing some of the dark secrets of a rural community.The mystery is not the most difficult for readers to solve, but the book is very enjoyable. The author does and excellent job portraying a rural town in the Texas Panhandle in the 1980s and inhabits it with memorable characters. This is the first in a series of five books.
