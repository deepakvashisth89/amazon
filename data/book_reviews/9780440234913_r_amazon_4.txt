I gave this 4 rather than 5 stars because the lack of proofreading makes reading it extremely annoying. The author has virtually abandoned the apostrophe. Then there are the words "B renda" several times; "hen" instead of "her" and so on. The story is pretty good. There is a lot of plant info, but if you don't care about those details, you can gloss over them. A nice touch is at the end of the book. A break down of succulents and cacti and a list of which were referenced in the book. An annoyance - the author says to see pictures, go to his website. The only pictures I saw were of his books.
