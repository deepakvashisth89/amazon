I wish I had known about the Catwings when I was a kid. I’d have loved them even more than I do now, reading them to my own children. My oldest has loved the original Catwings since the age of three, when we purchased the Kindle version. We’ve been so disappointed that the other three books have not been released for Kindle, so I broke down and purchased the complete set in paperback. Definitely worth it. The stories are simple and sweet, but so well written. As another reviewer said, they are not dumbed down because they’re for children. The harsh realities of life are not avoided, but LeGuin doesn’t harp on them either; the stories are full of hope, good morals, and characters small children can admire. I can’t recommend this series highly enough.As for age range, they would be the perfect first chapter books for advanced readers, probably 3rd or 4th grade, mostly for vocabulary reasons, but they are fun to read aloud to much younger children.
