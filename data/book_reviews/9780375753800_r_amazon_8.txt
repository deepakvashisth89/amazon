Absolutely hysterical satire!  I laughed out loud and then felt ashamed for laughing because the truths contained therein are not exactly funny.  HOWEVER, IT IS A MUST READ -- IF YOU DARE.  If you didn't know this book was written in the 1930s, you'd swear it was written yesterday.  The more things change, the more they stay the same.  It's amazing how Mr. Schuyler captured so much truth in this 150 page novel.  It's time for the world to know more about Mr. George S. Schuyler.  I totally understand, after reading this phenomenal book, why people at the time of publication used their voices to shut him down and out for so many years.  Although he too was part of the Harlem Renaissance, you'd never know it.  If those afraid of his opinions hadn't been successful at censoring him, our country might look and work a little different today for our Black citizens, of which I am one.
