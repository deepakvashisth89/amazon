Began reading this when it was left on the lunch table at work. I am very impressed with Setlowe as an author. The story was well thought out with multiple fully developed plotlines that complemented each other quite well. The characters are nicely developed throughout the story.Even more impressive is the way he places history and culture at the forefront of his story, teaching us about the region, culture, and politics of the Indonesian area. The book focuses around a Muslim fundamentalist cell drawing from the malay pirate/fishing communities, and does so in extremely plausible fashion. The same cultural forces remain there as did when it was first written, making this a highly relavent story.The only bad thing I would say about this book, is that there are three or four "scenes" includung the climactic final battle, that are just a little bit overly theatric or cliche.
