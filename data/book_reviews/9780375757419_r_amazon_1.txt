This is the first Hardy book I’ve read and I have to say it was unbelievably good. It’s insanely readable, one of those books that make you forget you’re even reading it, you get so lost in narrative. One potential ‘drawback’ is that it’s a bit melodramatic, but I think Hardy was inspired by the Greek tragedians so that may be intentional. In any case, the story, the setting(s), and the two main characters are very well done, and you can see why this book is so famous.Read moreRead less
