Peyton Place has nothing on Waggs Neck Harbor when it comes to dark secrets and hidden (and not so hidden) passions. The story involves the town's Annual Literary Arts Society, Women's Lib, money, and society both high and low. Being a novel of the Nineties, the book is awash with fashion, designer names falling like drops in a downpour. I was pretty sure about the identity of the murderer early on, but the writer's skill in plotting and characterization kept me from skipping to the end.
