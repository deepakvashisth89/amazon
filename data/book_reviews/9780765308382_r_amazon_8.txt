Grania is a very tough, single-minded woman who has had to make her own way in the world. She is also a very caring women who fights to protect and advance the welfare of her children, her crew, and her people. She is joined throughout her life by men who fight against her and those who fight for her.  She is one of the most adventurous and exciting women in current fiction, and I hope her story is read by young women looking for heroines, and older women who will resonate to her struggles and triumphs.
