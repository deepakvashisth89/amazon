Miss Seeton is a character who manages to help the police but not like a Miss Marple. She does not solve the crimes with her keen mind. She blunders around in her innocence and creates chaos which leads the police to solving the crimes. This one is more complicated with witches and con artists and new religions. I did not enjoy it as much as the earlier ones and it seemed to end a bit abruptly. But I like her and will keep reading more. Not a recent series- written years ago.
