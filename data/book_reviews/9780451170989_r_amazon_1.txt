His name doesn't matter; he changes it with every kill, and there's been many! This time he is "Beecham", hired to kill an older lady. He fails and the man who hired him has to do the deed.I don't want to tell you anything else about the storyline; it may ruin the suspense. "Bad Desire" by Gary Devon is filled with surprises and twists and turns. It's a mystery, it's political, it's evil, it's over-the-top obsession, and it's revenge of the sweetest kind for one person. Nothing good happens in this story; but, it shows us how easily the truth can be hidden! This would be a great book for a book club or just discussion with a friend.......very complex!4.5 Stars and I will tell you why!  It's SAD!  No HEA!
