This is the penultimate book in the series and a lot has happened before. It's time to revisit some of the earlier adventures in this book. Baum has blended some new characters in this book with some prominent old ones. That makes it both familiar and interesting. While it takes time to absorb the roles and activities of the new characters and I grappled a bit that. But once you do, the story becomes quite smooth.Story is simple and more of merry initially as friends set out to get exotic birthday gifts for the princess Ozma. They go beyond the boundaries of the city and venture into completely new territories. However, that is when the magic starts to happen. They get stuck and they need to come out of the labyrinths.The simple question is how? Baum has tried to develop this into a different story and if you ask me this is a good standalone story as well if one were to not to consider this a part of the series. As usual, this remains a good read not just for kids but for people of many ages. Once you pick up one book of the series, it will be quite difficult to put down books that you are reading and will be difficult to refrain from picking new ones as well.Hats off to Baum for the imagination of this level.Read moreRead less
