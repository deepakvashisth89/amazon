I read and studied Portrait as a young Lit major in college. Since then I have returned to it often for its bits of wisdom and because of how it challenges conventions, not only thematically but in form as well. Much of it can be disconcerting for a reader unfamiliar with this style of narration which follows inside the mind of the protagonist beginning at infancy, but once you adjust to this stream of consciousness narrative, you will see why it is considered one of the most important books of the 20th century. Though it may seem unstructured at first, patience with reading and thinking about it will pay off with deep insights about self-perception, spirituality, and religion.
