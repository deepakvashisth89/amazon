I read this at the beach. It was a light,  whimsical mystery. The characters were engaging, and often humorous. Despite its  often lighter side, the language and plot were well-written, with several twists and turns. Stick around for the ending. The style is that of a 1930's-1940's  comic mystery that nevertheless entertains throughout.I have already recommended it to my friends who are mystery buffs, and wholeheartedly recommend it to those who are similarly inclined.Read moreRead less
