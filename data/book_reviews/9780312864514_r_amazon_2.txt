Cloned dynasties, a genetic caste system on a Hindu pantheon "lost" colony, deep characterizations of astonishingly vivid protagonists - you name it, Rebel Sutra has it all. I am amused that one of the reviewers cited Anselm's "apparent homosexuality" as a "problem" - you were expecting an extraordinary revolutionary to have ordinary drives? :-) Think Trotsky in India, in a struggle involving succession to the throne of the British Empire, on a galactic palette.
