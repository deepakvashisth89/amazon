This is a very informative book. Barbara's writing style is awesome. She doesn't talk down to the reader. She lets the reader decide if working at home is right for them. She is not just writing about the subject but has had a home business all her adult life.  The book is very honest and doesn't paint a home based business for everyone or a direct road to riches. I really appreciate this when there are so many get rich quick schemes out there.If you are interested in starting a home business this book will help you to determine if it is for you and will give you a foundation to start it.Read moreRead less
