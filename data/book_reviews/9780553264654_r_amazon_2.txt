I really enjoyed this "old-school" sci-fi story, originally written in the 1970s. Harlie, the sentient computer in the story, is not only believable, but a great character to boot. The book is mostly in dialogue between the main scientist and the computer, but it's so interesting it doesn't ever devolve into a "talking heads" scenario. Harlie acts more like a human child than a computer with access to all the knowledge on the internet, which makes sense considering he has only been "awake" for a few years. There are plenty of surprising twists to the story which I won't spoil for you, but everything works out most satisfactorily in the end.
