What a great play. A lot of Shakespeare’s early plays fall short, but this one is very entertaining and well worth reading. Richard is a fantastic villain, and delivers some of Shakespeare’s more memorable speeches. Normally, I’d say, if you’re looking to read Shakespeare’s best stuff, stay away from the early plays. This one marks an exception, it’s worth reading for anyone interested in Shakespeare and I very much enjoy it.The Signet editions are very helpful. They have a highly readable font, they’re a compact size, and they have useful (but not intrusive) footnotes. I recommend them in general for anyone looking to read more Shakespeare.Read moreRead less
