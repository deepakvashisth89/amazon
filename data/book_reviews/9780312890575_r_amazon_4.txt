I first read Glen Cook's The Silver Spike when I was in the Navy getting ready to deploy on my first Boomer Sub patrol. I went to the on base book store and pretty much cleaned out their shelves. After that I started hunting down the series. (Harder to do before the internet). I moved to Chatanooga in 93 and went to Chattacon in 94 and met Glen Cook for the first time he was a dealer there and I of course bought all the books he had along with a John Hancock in each one. ;) He was and is a real neat guy. Wish he'd write quicker but I think all readers wish that of their favorite authors. But back to this book. It does give a good ending to the series and wraps everything up.
