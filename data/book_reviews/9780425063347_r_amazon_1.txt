My first step into a scifi novel by Janet Morris, and it's another example of why I love her storytelling. Rich writing and imaginative concepts, compelling characters. Not to leave spoilers, but the concept of faster-than-light travel is intriguing. The adaption of a girl from a primitive world to a technological culture is fascinating.
