"Love" is not exactly the right word of praise for this book, which is a frightening examination of the chemical contents of the Chicago, Illinois, and Mississippi Rivers. The author, a scientist AND a cancer victim who grew up on the Illinois River in Pekin, Illinois. She studies the cancer clusters occurring near the rivers and how their pollution relates to current industrial and commodity agricultural practices, The book is a sobering look at yet another dying environmental system, tilted over the edge by modern humanity.Read moreRead less
