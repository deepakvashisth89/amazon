Asimov, Atwood, Bradbury, Clarke, Emshwiller. All authors. All Science Fiction authors. All in alphabetical order.  All write of a future dystopia with great prescience. Mister Emshwiller has written a very very good book looking at what is happening in the United (?) States of today and writing of what may come of the history we are building.
