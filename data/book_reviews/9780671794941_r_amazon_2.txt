Here is a fun read on the subject that has generated the biggest question mark in history.For the most part this book is along the same lines of Jim Mars book "Crossfire" only a much lighter read and the cartoon format makes it fun for the whole family. I can't think of a better book to introduce that young person in your family to the subject of JFK's assassination.
