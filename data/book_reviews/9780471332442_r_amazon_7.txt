Very little connections to mathematics in this book. Boring stories (especially by that tedious lazy pompous Jorges Luis Borges) abound. Lewis Carroll, Andrew Marvell, Martin Gardner and Philip K Dick are the exceptions.The blurb is a lie, it is not a wildly inventive treasury, it is a stunningly boring book.And why was "The Definition of Love" by Andrew Marvell included? The only possible explanation is that the vaguely mathematical word "definition" is in the title.
