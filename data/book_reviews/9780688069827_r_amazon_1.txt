A beautiful, rich lady, Judith, has given her house to the church since her husband died,but she still lives there. The rent to the church is one white rose from the garden each year. If the rent isn't paid, the contract is broken. She has many suitors, some of whom don't like the idea that half of her wealth may slip away should she marry one of them. An attempt is made to destroy the rose bush as that would make it impossible to fulfill the contract. However, the priest who takes care of the bush is found stabbed to death and the bush damaged by an unknown assailant.  Judith is kidnapped and the whole town searches for her. A suitor has figured out where she is being kept, but instead of alerting the Sheriff, he tries to rescue her himself, thinking this will persuade Judith to marry him. Instead, he is killed and Judith's abductor agrees to take her to a nearby abbey and release her, thus sparing both of them from disgrace.  She convinces him that she will never marry him and he turns out to be a weak, silly fool. As she is walking the last distance to the abbey, she is attacked but a local man who loves her but has never revealed this comes to the rescue. Cadfael figures out who the murderer is in a surprise twist and the book ends happily. A very pleasant and exciting story. I rate the book 9 of 10.Read moreRead less
