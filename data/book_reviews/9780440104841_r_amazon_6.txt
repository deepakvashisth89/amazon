All of Chesbro's "Mongo" books have a quality that make them impossible to pigeonhole.  This one combines all the characteristics that make the Mongo books so engrossing and irresistible.  The matter-of-fact  dialog between Mongo and his brother Garth, about the most outrageous  situations, made me laugh out loud.  There's a bad guy who has a vision of  how to save the world through reverse genetic engineering.
