This is one of the great classics of modern bodybuilding.  i used this book 25 years ago to win the Mr. Cincinnati bodybuilding contest (tied for overall) while attending medical school.  I was and always had been drug free.  That was my first and last contest.  Fast forward to April 2015.  My daughter and I used this book to train her for the Colorado NPC championships.  Her first contest.  She won the novice overall, won her class in the open and finished in the top 3 in the overall open. No drug use.  Mr. Bass knows his stuff.  His approach works with much less time spent in the gym, no starvation and no drugs.  Essentially, it's about science and understanding the rules of physiology, muscle gain and fat loss.  His subsequent books are all well worth reading.  They have with an ever increasing focus on health and longevity.  But Ripped 2 is the real thing for bodybuilders and fitness trainees who want time tested details on how to build muscle while staying lean.
