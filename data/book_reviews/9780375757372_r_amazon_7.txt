I tried to read this book when I was about twelve years old but I couldn't stick with it. I had no appreciation for the dialects and the subtle humor of it. Having just barely learned English by that time. I couldn't understand a book that broke all the rules and was praised for it. I'm grateful now, at age seventy to have read it and realize how many times our culture references it in speech and psychology. Is Bob Dylan referencing it in "Time out of mind" with his lyric about Miss Mary Jane? He who is from Missouri and aware of this classic... consciously or unconsciously? I'm always speaking of cultural references and I probably missed many by not reading this book. And mostly this book is funny it made me laugh. Laughter is timeless.Read moreRead less
