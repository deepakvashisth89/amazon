If forced to choose, I would keep this one book out of the hundreds I own.  It's one of the few I've re-read cover to cover time and time again.  Each reading fosters new resolve to be my truest, strongest, most creative self.  I've given it to lots of friends involved in the arts.  As if that wasn't enough, it contains some wonderful nuggets regarding the business side of the creative life.
