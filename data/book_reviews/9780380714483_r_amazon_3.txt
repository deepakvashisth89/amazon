The stories of Margery Allingham's "Mr. Albert Campion" are mostly collected in in-print books spanning both Ms. Allingham's and "Albert Campion's" lifetimes.  But this very unusual collection represents some (if not all) previously uncollected stories by Allingham-- if the book's editor is to be believed.At the very least, these stories are fun to read, and most feature Campion, his friends and family, but oe of my mot favorite, is a quirky story with none of the "usual Campion" crowd, and it amazed me!  (I won't reveal its title, since coming across it in the middle of the collection, felt like a special moment.  Try and find a copy for yourselves, you die-hard Campion fans, or if you know any book publishers, tell them to try and get reprint rights to this wonderful collection.
