I inherited this book from my great-grandmother, and even though it's looking a little rough around the edges, it remains one of my all time favorites. Whenever I feel a stroke of boredom coming on I just pull down Fold a Banana, open it to a random page, and do what it says. Bored no more. The drawings are adorable and the suggestions hilarious. Play hide and go seek with a glass of water. Take a bath with your clothes on. Count the corners in your house. The list goes on. Overall, it's an amazing little book which I cherish very much.
