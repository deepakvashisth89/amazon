This is a compilation of short stories written by the recipients of the Grand Master Award, bestowed as a lifetime achievement recognition for writers of science fiction and fantasy.  Fifteen authors have received the award and this work compiles short stories from Jack Vance, Frederik Pohl, Lester Del Rey, Damon Knight and A. E. van Vogt.  This is the third and final volume of the three part series.  The collections were selected and edited by Frederik Pohl, one of the Grand Masters.There were some very good short stories in this collection, and I found it comparable in quality to volume two of the collection, both of which were marginally better than volume one.  As technology increases and society progresses, older works of science fiction many times suffer and such is the case with much of these three collections.
