I recommend this book to any reader with a sense of adventure. It easily earns five stars.Writing a biography is difficult at any time but in Tom Horn's circumstances it must have been even harder. Yet, Tom takes the reader through his earlier life with an easy style and delivers a feel for what it was like then. Readers can taste the dust and feel the saddle sores in the weary miles of riding. We can nod our head at the bond Tom has with Al Sieber and recognise why he relied on the experience Al Sieber shared with him.Tom wears the bullet-proof nonchalance of youth among some of the deadliest of indians, yet readers now can only imagine the dry taste of danger and fear. That's history in the making and how it is in real life. No one is left in any doubt that Tom was a brave and adventurous young man (though he might not have thought so at the time). I found this book authentically interesting easy to read and very rewarding.Read moreRead less
