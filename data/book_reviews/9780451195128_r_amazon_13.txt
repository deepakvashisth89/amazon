I seem to be completely out of step with other reviewers, but although there was nothing to dislike about this book, I didn't like it either.  It was lukewarm, I suppose.  The main characters were nice enough, but it seemed that every plot device from every historical romance had been thrown together here, and my overall feeling was that there was not enough definition.  Scenes and scenarios were played out without consistency- the heroine, a very gifted piano player, launches straight into her more complicated pieces at a dinner party [where she is already feeling the eyes of the assembled diners upon her, notably a gentleman who thinks he recognises her] when she has already been told that her missing twin is barely proficient at the piano; she even repeats her performance.  These sort of silly mistakes are not in line with an intelligent woman bent on self-preservation and this is just one instance.  I felt I should like the book more than I did, but unfortunately it didn't work for me.Read moreRead less
