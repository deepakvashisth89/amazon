Ngaio Marsh is to my mind the outstanding mystery writer of her vintage and all her books are elegant and most are excellent. I personally like best those featuring Inspector Alleyn's wife, the painter Agatha Troy, and second best those set in the theatre, of which this is one. A young director in inspecting a derelict theatre which he wants to restore and open again when he falls through a trap in the stage and is nearly drowned in stagnant water before being rescued by a mysterious millionaire.
