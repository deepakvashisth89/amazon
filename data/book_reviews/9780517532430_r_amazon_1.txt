I loved the character development. I started with Shibumi. Then Satori. I just wish that the Hel saga would continue. I purchased both of these on Audible. There are two different authors and two different narrators so there is a curve of binge reading/listening. Both authors bring the characters and stories to life in amazing detail. My only complaint is that there is not more content- but as Satori author Don Winslow describes (after Satori in the author’s notes), bridging new stories to the original Shibumi novel is a challenge in itself- and Don Winslow delivered by staying true to the source material. Don also spoke to the Trevanian family about how to approach a new book in the Shibumi series. Satori was a beautiful collaboration between the Trevanian family and Winslow. I will be playing Go now.
