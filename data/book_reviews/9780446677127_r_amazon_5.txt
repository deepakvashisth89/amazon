I have not read any of Mr. Turow's fiction but did see the movie "Presumed Innocent" a screenplay of his book by the same name.  There are as many plot twists as in a Christie or Doyle book.  But, with Turow we get a great deal more character development.  It was a joy to read to see more elucidation of one of the main characters in "Presumed Innocent".
