This is everything you want in a novel. It doesn’t matter that it’s a science fiction novel. It has great and interesting characters. It has a dual line plot with a lot of surprises and magnificent world building. But a technical standpoint the writing is just superb. But what is most important, in any work of fiction, is the story. Kevin Macleod knows how to tell a story. If that were his only skill, his books would still be better than 90% of the science fiction out there.
