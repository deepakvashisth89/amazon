Jessie Benton is the daughter of Senator Thomas Hart Benton of Missouri. They live in Washington most of the time and then alternate summers between his family house in St. Louis and her mother’s family home. Jessie helps her father basically as his secretary with all of his writings. Her mother is sick so she is not around to do many duties in the house except to help make decisions once in a while. At some point and time the explorer and map maker Charles Fremont enters their lives. Jessie eventually against her father’s wishes elopes with him. Most of the book is his explorations and her going between family houses.I am learning that this kind of book is just not really my style to read. It was an ok book. It was kind of hard to get through. It was descriptive enough it’s just the Characters were a little boring.
