CarrieBy Stephen King4 StarsCarrie is considered a Horror novel but also can be classified as a Supernatural Thriller. This title is Stephen King’s first published novel. This is my first time reading Stephen King so I felt like I should start with his first novel. I have watched several of his movies and felt pretty confident that I’d like his writing as well.I must say his writing style was different than I expected. It did grow on me and I even became appreciative of it as I read because it gave me great insight to the characters inner thoughts at the same time I was reading what was happening outside of their mind. This technique really enhanced the enjoyment and understanding of this novel for me.Carrie is a teenage girl being raised by a religiously fanatical mother. Carrie also has telekinesis. She is also the subject of severe bullying in her high school. All of this leads her to be socially awkward and ostracized by others. I genuinely felt sadness, disgust, and even horror at what this girl was dealing with. Things appear to change, at least socially, when she is invited to her high school prom.What happens at the prom and after will have you at the edge of your seat. You’ll have to read the story to get all the details. The book is written like it’s an inquiry to the events. You will get several perspectives and that also increases the fullness of the story.I don’t think I’d read this again, but I am glad that I read it. I feel like I’ve opened a door into a whole new world in my reading ventures. I can’t wait to read another of King’s novels!Make sure you check your trigger warnings.Read moreRead less
