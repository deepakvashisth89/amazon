This particular book is out of print; several secondary sellers offer it through Amazon and you should also be able to find it on eBay.  There are many copies out there for less than list price; don't pay a out of print premium on this one.All the Doll Hospital books are very good for young girls; younger more advanced readers will enjoy them and even as an adult I found myself reading them and wishing for more.As other reviewers have written, there are two stories to these Doll Hospital books.  On the surface there is the story of sisters Rose and Lila living with their quirky grandmother, adjusting to a new lifestyle.  The grandmother has a doll repair business in her house; she has the ability to talk to the dolls and to retell their stories to Rose and Lila.  Or is she just making it all up?  That's up to the reader to decide.Goldie's Fortune tells the story of a doll belonging to a little girl during the Depression who's family loses everything.  There is a rather supernatural occurance in this story that to some will send shivers up the spine and to others may be too far-fetched.
