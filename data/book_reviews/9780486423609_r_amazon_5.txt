Sky Island ReviewBy: Nathalie Douglas Age: 10Sky Island is a very delightful book. Many strange and wonderful characters await the reader in this sequel to The Sea Fairies. Meet the Boolooroo of the Blues, and Tourmaline the Queen of the Pinkies. With intense and magical moments, Sky Island will delight all ages. Amazing twists and turns lead the reader on a path around the magical place of Sky Island.Notes from Nathalie's Grandad, Age 69:As I grew up, I discovered my father's L. Frank Baum collection and loved both the Oz books and Sky Island.  When Nathalie was re-reading the Oz books, I remembered that Sky Island and The Sea Fairies were actually better than some of the Oz books and we ordered them for our four grandchildren.  So Baum has entertained four generations of readers:  my father, his children, his grandchildren, and now his great-grandchildren.Read moreRead less
