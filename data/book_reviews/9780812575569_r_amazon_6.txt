As I read this book I kept telling my husband that it's written for men who like soap operas. The best thing about this novel is that I never became entirely sympathetic to any of its characters so there wasn't that  "worry" that a favorite would come to a disasterous ending.  At  times, I wished they all would.  And yet, I kept reading...
