I am three-quarters of the way through with this book. This morning, after reading this book for a VERY long time (it's over four hundred pages long), I decided to see what sort of reviews the book got. Yes, I admit I downloaded this book to my Kindle only because it was free and part of a series. I like to read books in a serial format. I did not, however, realize it was considered to be a "christian" book. It did not take me long to figure that one out.I do not hold organized religion in a high regard. But, with that being said, I do see merit with a presentation of decent, compassionate principles. I am not finished with the book and it may go more overboard with the religious aspects of the story in the last pages. But, what I've read so far has not turned me off that much. I believe the book could have kept the obvious references to a specific religious attitude (that of christianity) in the background. A substitution for basic compassionate dealings amongst men would have accomplished the same thing and not run the risk of alienating some readers.Religious attitudes aside, the book is not that bad. It is an interesting enough story. I will admit there are no surprises in the novel. But, the author is skilled enough to create characters that are fairly realistic and interesting. The reader is quite able to see the plot line unfold in obvious ways. Even at that, however, because the community in the book is so well realized and so interesting, the entertainment part of reading the novel is adequate enough to where one does not feel he or she is wasting time continuing with the story.Yes, I know how the story will end. Yes, it is very simple in its progression of the plot. But, if you read enough of the book to get a glimpse of these characters' lives, you will most likely want to continue on to see how it all plays out in the end. I have read a lot worse books than this. And, yes, I admit I am enjoying it even though I find the religious aspects a weakness that detracts from the story itself.Read moreRead less
