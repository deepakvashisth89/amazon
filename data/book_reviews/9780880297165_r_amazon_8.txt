I found Lillian Jackson Braun's books by accident. I was reading a review of 'The Cats That' series by Karen Anne Golden and someone mentioned 'The Cats Who' series. So I found them on  Amazon and bought this three novels in one book. The stories are similar in the sense that they involve cats 'That' or 'Who' do wondrous things, including some crime solving. These authors have completely different writing styles, but equally great stories. I love Lillian Jackson Braun's James Qwilleran, he is a newspaper reporter that worked the crime scene, but now seems to get assignments that he has either no knowledge or no interest, all with hilarious consequences and in the process has acquired two Siamese cats.
