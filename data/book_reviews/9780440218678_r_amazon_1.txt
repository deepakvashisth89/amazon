I got this book because I enjoyed his book "The Hour Before Dark."  This one did not disappoint.  I always look for something new in a horror novel.  Not so easy in a genre where clichés are rampant.  This one has enough of a complicated plot and a mix of characters to keep you guessing.  I found it well written and easy to read, with characters that I cared about.  He made the final mystery a bit off of what would be an ordinary type of monster.  It was a bit far-fetched with the solution requiring suspension of disbelief.  But I find that easy to do with most horror books.  I really enjoyed this one and recommend it heartily.
