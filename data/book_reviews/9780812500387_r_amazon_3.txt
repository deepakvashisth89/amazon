Very well done post-apoc novel, wide-ranging in scope. Geopolitical setup for novel is disturbingly plausible. Military hardware and systems are well thought out and are logical extensions of current systems. No death rays here, except maybe the anti-sat lasers. For some reason the paperback lives on my desk, and if I happen to pick it up to browse, I find that time has gone by and I've read an eighth-inch of the novel. From any random place I pick.
