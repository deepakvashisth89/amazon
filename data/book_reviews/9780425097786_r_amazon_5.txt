I've been engaged in re-reading (or reading for the first time) every one of Georgette Heyer's mysteries in chronological order.  This was one of her last mysteries and for me, it is the best of the lot.  Her dialogue, details, descriptions and the characters are wonderful and the plot, although a bit thin in a sense (since you know who the murderer is midway through the book)  is just a good.This would make a great movie but it would have to be done all in period.  Can't you just see someone like Anthony Quinn or Orson Welles as Penhallow? Margaret Rutherford as Clara, Lauren Bacall as CHarmain?  Alas, it will never happen.Try her mysteries -- you'll be in for  a really good read and an unusual story (any of the 12).
