I absolutely love this book! This brings back such wonderful memories of me as a kid just giggling at the illustrations, the humorous homonyms. I think as adults we forget that the english language has a lot of homonyms that we forget or do not connect but when you put those in an illustrated form the english language can sometimes be very funny.I think for educational purposes this would be a great book for kids to help distinguish the importance of similar homonyms as they learn the rules and ways of the english languish, but for the rest of us just looking for a good laugh with awesome illustrations, this book is for you.
