Jon Billman writes in vivid color.  He has just enough detail to keep my eyes on the page, but not so much that I can't daydream my self into some reflective thought and ponder what it is that the story invokes for me.  I  especially like the sense I get of his voice in the telling; good story  tellers have this gift of the double personal.  Theirs and ours.  Billman  has a knack for writing about women without making us mere backstory; his  character "Ash" is the best!  If this is his first book, I look  forward to the next.Read moreRead less
