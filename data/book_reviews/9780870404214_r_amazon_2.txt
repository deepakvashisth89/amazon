Even though I have taught the martial arts (Judo, Jujitsu, Karate-Do) and inner arts (Tai Chi, Qigong, and Yoga) for more than 50 years, the last few years have been a real revelation for me.  I have been studying various energy healing systems taking a course and earning my Reiki Master certification. I have also studied Hand Reflexology and other touch therapy healing systems.I recently purchased this very interesting 160 page soft cover book (Acupressure Way of health: Jin Shin Do by Iona Marsaa Teeguarden) for a bargain price on Amazon.  In spite of my more than 50 years of studying numerous Japanese art systems I had never heard of Jin Shin Do until recently.  The author of this book (Iona Marsaa Teeguarden) explains this healing system which she has studied since 1972.  This book explains the basic principles of this art, which like a lot of Asian systems is based upon Zen, Taoism and acupressure theory.This very informative book is organized into nine chapters.  Some of the material covered in this volume includes: Chapter one the chapter of conception which explains what is Jin shin Do? How does Jin Shin Do work?  Chapter two the governing chapter deals with the Tao of body work, Yin and Yang and other topics. Chapter three Knowing ourselves focuses on the Yin and Yang of human expectations.  Chapter four: Magic at your fingertips.  Chapter five the strange flows of acupressure. Chapter six: discovering the thirty main acu-points. Chapter seven: the treatment of touch. Chapter eight how to give a Jin Shin Do acupressure treatment. The final chapter covers an ancient, new age approach to the emotions.Jin Shin Do is an interesting and unique healing system and this book does provide details on how the system works; however, in my opinion you will need an instructor of Jin Shin Juyutsu to actually learn how to use this healing method properly.Rating: 5 Stars.
