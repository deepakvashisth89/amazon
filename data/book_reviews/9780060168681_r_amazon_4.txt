Nancy Pearl described this book as "well written" and I suppose you might say it fits that description in a vague way. However, while it does paint some pretty word pictures, the story, such as it is, moves at a glacier pace. If you enjoy reading the written word merely for the joy of seeing how words can be put together, you might enjoy this, however, I prefer my entertainment to actually have some sort of plot that occasionally moves along. This story has about enough plot to make a short story, but that short story has been dragged out over 290 pages. It might do as a sleep aid, but personally, I got more enjoyment out of reading Dr. Seuss classics out loud to my grandson.Read moreRead less
