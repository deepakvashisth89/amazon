I read this book because the premise was so promising.  Men in a boat, caught in still waters with no wind to move them onward, find a large copper cylinder containing a manuscript.  They take turns reading the manuscript aloud and discussing it between each reading.  So far, so good.The story is quite fascinating. A man and his companion go ashore on an iceberg and their ship is forced by rough seas to leave them stranded.  The companion is killed by cannibals and the other man escapes and finds himself in a very strange world where many facets of life are opposite what he has been accustomed to.  Still okay.  Man goes through all sorts of trials but finally wins and gets the girl.Then, the book ends abruptly with the last reader simply saying, "That's enough for today.  I'm tired and can't read any more.
