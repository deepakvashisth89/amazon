I,m first gen Irish. My father was IRA, 1917-1922. It is a subject which has always interested me. In my trips to Ireland few people were interested in discussing this subject in detail. This book was very informative and educational for me. I recommend it to all people of Irish descent and all others who are interested in the subject.
