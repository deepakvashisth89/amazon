Small Gods tells the tale of the great god Om. Seemingly worshiped by many in the powerful and oppressive Omnian Church, but with only one true believer, Om is now reduced to wandering around as an indignant tortoise. Small Gods is also the story of Brutha, Om’s only believer and the main protagonist in the novel. Brutha is a simple novice with a perfect memory who can neither read nor write but ironically is the only hope Om has. While there are many within the Omnian church, they worship the doctrines and institutions rather than Om himself. The scenes between Om and Brutha are light and funny and this book does have a few laugh out loud moments, but it also has a serious undertone. It explores theology and belief, organized religion and faith, power and corruption, brutality and human nature. There is a lot to take in and the book can get a bit confusing at times. But still it is classic Pratchett and a book that makes you think, not only laugh. 4/5Read moreRead less
