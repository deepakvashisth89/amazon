After finishing this 186 page book, you can probably read 10 more books on the Renaissance and not find an artist or work of art that is not discussed in this book. Johnson really covers it all.However, Johnson also provides some art criticism that makes me wonder if he lacks something in art appreciation. On Michelangelo's Sistine Chapel, "...one sometimes feels that...It is not done well; but you are surprised to find it done at all." On the Mona Lisa, "...the face and the hands are woefully inconsistent..." And on Correggio's Io, "...it requires a strong effort of imagination to find it erotic..." There are other examples.Johnson's art criticism should not bias the appreciation of these and other Renaissance masterpieces. One should read other art books to get a better feel for what the art offers.Still, a great little book on a magical period of human history.Read moreRead less
