This book is great.  It gives a great story about a Tibetan village named Jyeko, and the trials that come to pass with the Communist invasion of Tibet in the 1950's.  It also follows the lives of Scottish radio operator Jamie Wilson and a Tibetan outcast named Puton.  It tells of love and trials.  Of anger turning into unification in the face of neccesity.
