“The Accident” tells a story about a journalist named Eliezer, a survivor of the German death camps, and being haunted about his past. Eliezer gets hit by a speeding taxi cab when stepping off a curb. Eliezer’s in the hospital and the memories of the people that have past kept messing with him. So the haunting of the people in his family are making him think why even live is their even a purpose to live. He can’t even live his life without thinking about it. So when Eliezer’s taken to the hospital he really isn’t fighting for his life. The doctors even ask him why he was giving up on life. When Eliezer recovered from being hit by the cab all he could think about were his memories and he was playing them back in his mind.  He kept thinking why he even fell in love with his wife. He kept thinking about the present and the past when fighting for his life in the hospital, and this story allowed the reader to know the suffering Eliezer’s going through. Eliezer and Kathleen had a strong relationship in this story. They had a genuine, caring, and stuck with each other through the good and the bad. This relationship is basically what every couple wishes to have. This story talks about romance and also about companionship.
