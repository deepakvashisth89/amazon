I first purchased this book the year it was published. I gave it as gifts, one to my colleague, co-director of music at a mainstream church. It was Epiphany and the last day of Chanukah. The minister was on holiday and we took this opportunity to use this popular Christmas song as a template for lessons and carols. We lite a candle after each lesson and tied it to lighting a candle on the menorah.The congregation was in awe as the last candle on the menorah went out at the end of the last lesson.I purchased another book in 2015 for our new minister. Each time we lead the service during this time of the year, we are amazed asto how it is received.
