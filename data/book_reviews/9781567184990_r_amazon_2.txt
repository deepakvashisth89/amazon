Having studied and researched spiritual literature at great length for nearly 48 years, I must say that this book, as well as the others written by Dr. Newton, have unexpectedly become the "pot of gold at the end of the rainbow."  They are like the reward for the adventurer who has spent a lifetime searching.  In years gone by, I have read multitudes of books regarding reincarnation and hypnotic regression.  They were fascinating reading and certainly helped form the foundation of my philosophy, however, when you have read several, the point is made.  I had no desire to read more, but I stumbled on Dr. Newton's books in my search for interesting reading for my Kindle.  I  tried them, and found that I had to read them all.  They certainly represent a magnificent breakthrough in the field of psychology, as well as spiritual knowledge.The vast number of patients (or perhaps clients) that participated in the research of the afterlife experience ( in the many thousands, at least), are enough to convince even a skeptic that there is something here that deserves a second look.  There is such an enormous amount of replication of particulars, such as descriptions of activities that souls participate in, and subjects that we study, groupings of souls, management of the learning and evolution of souls, descriptions of how progress occurs, etc.  There doesn't seem to be identical terminology for all the afterlife activity, but that certainly lends credibility to the descriptions given by the participants.Dr. Newton was able to write the details of the accounts of many of his subjects in such a way that you almost feel that you are there, particapating in the  event.  I, frequently, found him asking his subjects just the questions that I would have asked, if I had been the facilitator.  I must admit, that occasionally he failed to ask the question I hoped for, or he asked it and the subject replied with a vague answer, or was blocked from answering by his guide.  But again, these details lend credability to the whole process.  I'm sure that there are many things that we shouldn't know at certain points on our paths, or that we lack complete understanding of.These books represent groundbreaking work in this field of afterlife or between life experience.  He has established an institute to train hypnotherapists in his pioneering techniques that have facilitated this entirely new field of knowledge.  I highly recommend these books to anyone who wonders what we do after we die, or what the purpose of life really is, or even whether we have a Creator.Although many answers can be found within Newton's books, I must warn you that they will raise many more questions in your minds.
