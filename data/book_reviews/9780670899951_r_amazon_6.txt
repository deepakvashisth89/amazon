I had a hard time getting started on this book, because it seemed like every character's life was incredibly sad and difficult. I knew nothing about coal mining towns other than that everyone is very poor and the miners are not likely to live long because they breathe in that coal dust all the time they are working. The characters in Coal Run have much grief to work through because of a terrible mine explosion that buried a whole shift of miners working that morning. This talented writer grabs a readers attention and keeps it to the end to see how the characters come to terms with the challenges of their lives.Read moreRead less
