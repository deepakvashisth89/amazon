I've read a lot of Tess Gerritsen's books and found this one OK. The prologue was definitely exciting enough to reel you in, but then the plot fizzled to a slow start. Finally the story got going, but then there was an abrupt ending, like there was suddenly a limit on the number of pages left. However, the middle was pretty good. Not her best book, but a good read. The story is about a Baltimore family practitioner who moves to a small town in Maine to remove her son from the bad influence of his peers, only to get embroiled in a rash of uncharacteristic teenage violence.Read moreRead less
