This is the gold standard Essential Oil book.  Do some comparing, and checking and you'll agree.  It is an encyclopedia, as it says.  The information is in good depth, and complete, and it includes nearly all the essential oils in current use.  Julia Lawless is a world renowned expert.  I use this book often.
