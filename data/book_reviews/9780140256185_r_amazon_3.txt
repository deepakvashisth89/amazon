I thoroughly enjoyed this narrative. It may never win any awards for great literature, but the author has a fertile imagination and comes up with fascinating characters. The events in the story kept me riveted. It's  funny, too. But the thing I enjoyed the most was finally reading a novel in  which the main characters were truly heroic in their actions.
