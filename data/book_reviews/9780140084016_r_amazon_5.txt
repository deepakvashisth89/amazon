I found this book to be one of the most realistic books I've ever read even from a modern day perspective. Rhoda's character was portrayed as human as can be and I think in our day and age her behaviour would be more acceptable; she seemed to me ahead of her times. Having read previous reviews, I understand most people didn't like her character, but on the contrary I applauded  her every step of the way, for her courage and integrity, despite all her mistakes and misjudgements, . It was difficult to read for all it's reality, sometimes painful.  The author depicted well the pain of life and how Rhoda accepted it and laughed right through it.Read moreRead less
