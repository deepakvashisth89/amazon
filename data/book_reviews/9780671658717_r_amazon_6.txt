This is a book of short stories which were adapted into the movie, The Secretary. It's a great movie, but an even better book. Although, while reading it, I couldn't imagine the characters as anyone other than James Spader and Maggie Gyllenhall.If you like the movie, you'll enjoy the book. If you haven't seen the movie, you might want to look up some trigger warnings for the collection of stories as they address things some people might not want to be exposed to. I would write the trigger warnings, but I don't want my review to be nuked. But do yourself a favor and look up trigger warnings or, be adventurous, and expose yourself to something new.
