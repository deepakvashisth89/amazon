Though this book is long out of print the mother author still gets letters commending it.  It is the story of Jenny's first 20 years of life, 1960-1980, a story of family life.  Mom and dad and five children., Jenny the second child benefitted greatly by being in such a family when Special Ed was beginning in the schools.  A parent can't sort out one kid from the others, so this book is about family.  They traveled throughout the USA, and during Jenny's first 20 years they lived in Nebraska, California, Wisconsin, Maine and Colorado.  Jenny was never expected to read or write but she thrived far beyond expectation, and continues to do so.  This book is probably more important now than when it was published.Read moreRead less
