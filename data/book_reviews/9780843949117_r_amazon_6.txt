Lady Gypsy by Pam Crooks is a book that should be required reading. Crooks brings two totally different cultures head-to-head. The way it starts, I almost put it down but, the more I read the more intrigued I became as Crooks pulled me into this story and I couldn't put it down. Lady Gypsy has everything that makes a good read whether you agree or disagree with what's happening in the story - love, hate, anger, danger, disappointment, compromise and more.Read moreRead less
