I am an alcoholic with 23 years of sobriety. My entire sobriety I have been attending Alcoholics Anonymous meetings to great effect. At seventeen years the weight of my secrets and lies of omission began to weigh upon me very heavily. I was beginning to feel the same guilt, shame, remorse, and emptiness I felt before sobering up. A loving friend gave me this book and nothing has been the same since. That was 8 years ago. This book helped me to discover that my prejudice against all things religious had kept me from finding the solution that AA had been offering me the whole time. Emmet Fox has a way of cutting away the useless "stuff" and allowing me to see life, Spirituality and God from a different perspective.
