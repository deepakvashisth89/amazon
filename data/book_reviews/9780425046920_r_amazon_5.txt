I stumbled onto this book looking for a cheap book to read.  WOW -- this is the most exciting and interesting book I have read in a very long time.  I thought Sanders wrote a better story than Baldacci, Cusler, Clancy, Kellerman, or any of the currently popular authors.  This man constructs an exciting an realistic series of characters with emotions you can feel.  This is not a mystery in the true sense of the word since you know from the outset who the killer is so the story focuses on the pursuit of the killer by police Captain Delany.  You watch the killer as he spins out of control and begins to lose himself in fantasy and self examination or madness.  You watch as he evolves from a corporate shark into serial murderer looking for love as he falls under the influence of a very mysterious woman who seems as mad as he is.  What I particularly liked about this book was the police, their processes, their politics, and their interactions.  The jargon and attitudes seemed very real.  Captain Delaney aka Iron Balls at times seemed poetic as he describes his emotions and feelings about his seriously ill wife.  This book is a page turner.  I gave it 5 stars and would give it more if possible.Read moreRead less
