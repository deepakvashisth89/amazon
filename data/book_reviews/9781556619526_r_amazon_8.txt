I absolutely love the story telling skills of Janette Oke; her words knit you into her family of choice, you become a member, you eat, work, laugh, cry & even experience death with them.I enjoyed so much "growing up" with Virginia.  I stressed with her wanting to be a part of the "in" group when maybe the description ""in" was only in her heart & mind. I didn't care much for her friend Jenny, but maybe we've all had a Jenny in our lives & only maturity & a touch from Christ can teach us how to deal with it.Ms Oke takes you on a wide spectrum of emotions in her "families," but the one constant is an infusion of love of Christ without beating you over the head with it.  She allows her characters to witness as they live their lives of Faith throughout the entire sequence of story telling.The Tender Years is packed full of life, death & even a little mystery.
