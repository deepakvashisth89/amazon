I have had this book for a good while. I ordered more copies to use in an adult Sunday school class. The class and myself find it readable, touching the essence of each verse and catapulting us into discussions of how our lives are lived out in light of each chapter. It has generated the most honest sharing of life experiences I have encountered in any adult class in which I have participated.
