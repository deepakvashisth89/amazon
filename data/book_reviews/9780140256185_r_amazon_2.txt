I had to buy a used copy of this book as it's out of print! Perhaps it was a little too sentimental of a tale, but strong characters: Prophet and Pearl Eddy carry the plot. This was my least favorite of the books I've read by Thomas Eidson, but I'd be willing to read anything this author writes! The setting of this story was Kansas--so far I've liked his novels set in the west better. The author has a wonderful talent of concocting great complications! His stories are all fast-paced.  I read this, like his other two in this series, in two days--and I'm a slow reader!!! If you like Westerns, you'll love this author's work.  I think his westerns measure up quite nicely to Larry McMurtry, Cormac McCarthy, Louis L'Amour,  Willa Cather, and others!Read moreRead less
