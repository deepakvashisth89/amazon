For me this book was a trip down memory lane. I read it as a very young teen, 50 years ago. I still remember the story like it was yesterday. I loved it then and I still do now, but story telling has moved on and left it dated, hence my rating. If you love a good yarn and don't let reality worry you to much then this story is for you.The boys here are too super human in their ability to do all things technical at such young ages and the doc can get all sorts of things done government wise that even the president couldn't do. But hey, I don't care, I wanted to be them, doing wow stuff when young. Kids need something to aspire to, someone to become. Heinlein in this story gives us a Flash Gordon style escape and the hope to be something more when we grow up. I still haven't grown up and this story still inspires. Read this book as you would Jules Verne and you'll appreciate it. Don't expect the sophistication of today's novels, just enjoy the old school sociology and writing style.
