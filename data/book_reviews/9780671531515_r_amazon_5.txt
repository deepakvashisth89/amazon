I am reviewing this book before I re-read it because I want to give my first impression of it. This is very nostalgic for me because I was one of those kids who loved to read and was reading Harold Robbins in middle school. So this is what I remember at 50 yrs old. There was a lot of action, betrayal, sex and violence and it was and i am sure still is fantastic. I am not going to give it 4 or5 stars because you can't call it great literature, but I am waiting to read it on my summer vacation because you can call it great fun. I am hoping the adult me isn't disappointed because my tastes have changed. Oh who am I kidding, everyone loves a good sexy easy read.
