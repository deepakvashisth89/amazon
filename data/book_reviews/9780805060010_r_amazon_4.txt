Having immensly enjoyed reading two of ROB books, I just had to read one more and what a Gem it was. The Deep Green Sea for me IS the best book I have read (so far) this year as from the first page to the very last I was immensly enthralled in this poetic and wonderfullly written love story. At times I turned the pages quickly to learn what had happened in the past or what would occur next whilst on others I turned them over slower and slower to avoid reaching the end of The Perfect Gem. I've been enthralled by Robert Olen Butler's books an order has just been placed for my 4th and which probably will not be the last. I truthfully wonder why I never discovered this beautiful creative writer earlier, particularly as The Deep Green Sea was published in 1997 but I've 'found' him now!Read moreRead less
