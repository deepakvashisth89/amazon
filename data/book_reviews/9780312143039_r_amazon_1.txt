I really enjoyed reading this book !  The story was very well-written, it was an interesting plot, and the characters were very realistically done.The story revolves around an old English village that has gone thogh the ages of time pretty much unchanged, and the people who live there still have a lot of the old-fashioned ways and morals. When the possibility of the village becoming part of a housing project is mentioned, the villagers are completely against it, and also against the newcomers to the area.Murder is covered up , and an innocent woman is sent to prison, until a quick-thinking detective realizes that not all of the information around her conviction makes sense.Read moreRead less
