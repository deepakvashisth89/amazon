Great book. I enjoyed revisiting one of the three original American Girls- the other two being Samantha and Molly. I really felt for Kirsten, feeling like she didn't fit in, and dealing with a tough teacher- though she was tough because she cared and wanted her students to do their best. I was happy for Kirsten when she met Singing Bird, because she was someone else who didn't fit in, so they had something in common in that regard. I also felt sorry for her at the end, when she had to make a decision that might not have been what she wanted, but she felt it was right, nonetheless.Read moreRead less
