I picked up The Land of Women on a lark and I'm glad I did. This is the first book by McBride that I have read and was quite affected by it. McBride is an extremely talented writer -- very nuanced descriptions with almost a rhythm to the writing.The relationship between Fiona and her mother is extremely complicated and mutlilayered, and McBride illustrates it perfectly without cliches or being excessively overt.The story has a sort of haunting quality that McBride totally pulls off.
