Margaret Truman truly had a close connection and deep love for her father. This book shows it. It also had some parts that fell short of my expectations. The book sometimes had parts that focused on (to me) seemed small and trivial. Example- when the Germany portion of WWII ended, there was more discussion about decorating the White house than the fall of Adolf Hitler. That made the book feel soft.
