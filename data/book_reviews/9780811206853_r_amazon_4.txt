Gary Snyder is a poet whose body of work spans his entire life.  In "Myths and Texts" Snyder shows the upward slope of his evolution as a poet.  His voice is distinct and drives the form of his words.  His cadence is practically one with the surrounding nature of the mythologies he constantly creates and re-creates.
