"The Hand I Fan With" is truly one of my favorite books of all time!  I love how the author, Tina McElroy Ansa can make the most unlikely appear and feel very real.  This is a book that I've read before, but was drawn to read again; and felt the same anticipation and excitement with the build up of Herman's appearance.  Throughout the book, McElroy Ansa provides the reader with a variety of information regarding nature, recipes, family, relationships, intimacy and more!
