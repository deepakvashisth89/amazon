From the Back:What under heaven had driven Bethany Martin from his arms? What had made her abandon their dreams, disappear without a trace? For years Luke Ryan had asked those questions - and cursed Beth's betrayal. But he'd never stopped loving her. Not for a heartbeat.Now Bethany was back. And Luke vowed to learn the truth. For by Beth's side stood the son Luke had never known he had. His long-lost love - and newfound child - nearly broke his heart anew. After all these years could he piece together the cruel lies that had torn them apart? Could he make his shattered family whole?Review:  This story had a very small town Americana theme, but was quite enjoyable overall.
