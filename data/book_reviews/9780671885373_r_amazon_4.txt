While this book was the only biography ever written and authorized by Wyatt Earp, there are parts of it that over the years have become open to question, as far as fact. That said it's a very good read, and more factual than most similar books, that amount to not much more than western fantasy and folklore. Be careful when purchasing copies of this book.
