As a Brit trying to understand the events around the establishment of the Free State I found this very helpful. I associated the idea of the "Irish Volunteer" primarily with the events of 1916, but this volume covers the establishment of the volunteer movement and the varied strands in political thought behind it, all the way through to the Free State armed forces and the Civil War.
