Seriously, sixteen years!!!!? I love reunion romance. It's my jam and this is why I had this hanging around even though I rarely read this category line. But sixteen years. That's where it lost a star because otherwise, I loved it.Luke Ryan and Bethany Martin were childhood sweethearts. And I mean childhood sweethearts. Inseparable since they were about six, by the time they were graduating high school, they knew they were meant to be together forever. So what went wrong?Other people. And it's complicated. Suffice to say, Beth went away with her mother for what she thought was a two week vacation and never came back. The dissolution of her parents marriage and learning that Luke had been unfaithful left Beth devastated. She never got to tell him she was pregnant, believing that he'd married her best friend after getting her up the duff. (pregnant FYI)Now her father is dying and Luke, now the local doctor, wants to grant his last wish to see Beth again. It's not personal. But it means approaching Beth's cranky old grandmother who always hated him.The results surprise everyone, with Beth turning up the next day with a lanky teenager in tow, who is the dead spit of Luke. The whole web of lies unravels fast after that and these two mature people have to figure out how to work together so young Ryan (yes she went there) gets to know his dad.This was a book about mostly nice people barring cranky grandma Rose, who have to make the best out of a situation none of them wanted or deserved.What I liked was that despite the sixteen years, neither Luke of Beth found anyone else to take the place of their childhood sweetheart. It's clear Beth was faithful and there is no mention of another woman ever being in Luke's life. Although he did have a condom in his wallet and if it was there for sixteen years, it must have been quality made. But a minor detail, overall it met my standards for fidelity to their one true love.For a twenty year old book, it has worn well.Read moreRead less
