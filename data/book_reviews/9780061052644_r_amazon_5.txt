I’ll be brief. Egan researches immensely before and during writing. It’s clear in every word of this novel. The perspective of journalist is captured with finesse. A perspective I never really considered but I felt it intently here. The story is laden with politics and at an incredibly deep level. A level that can only be reached through focused studyThe implications of the writing are fun to consider. Some of it I agree with and others I feel we have yet to fully explore. I learned a great deal and have many fun new concepts swimming in my head now. Egan’s writing is incredibly good for that, like no other author I’m aware of.
