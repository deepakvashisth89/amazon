I've been rereading Mark Kingwell's books because-I love them-I love his writing. I love the intellectual aspects and equally, I love his great sense of humor and the humanity he shares with us.  I used to go fishing with my step-father as a child. I miss it much more now as an adult then I did as a child, as my step-father was rather harsh and I'll leave that at that. This is a book you can go back to again and again and learn something new. And somehow you end up finding yourself, and finding connection with another's thoughts and experiences.
