A long term drought in the Pacific Northwest has destroyed much of the historic great forests.  But what has caused this situation.  Jean begins a trek to Eastern Oregon and becoming friends with the Native Americans who live in the area. With her incredible talents, Wilhelm draws parallels to first contacts.  And like any really good science fiction writer, takes off from there.Mysteriously destroyed space stations, a looming world ending nuclear conflict between the Soviets and the United States, provides a backdrop to explore how we deal with each other and the alien in all of us.And this was written way back in the 1970s.
