Sometimes I love this book and other times I just don't understand it. Yet, I keep it and still refer to it often. I like to read books by Marianne Williamson and Paul Ferrini and Joan Guttuso. Those books help me a lot in making sense of the material in A Course In Miracles. I have attended study group meetings on ACIM, and that has been helpful as well. I think there is divine material in ACIM, but I think sometimes the wording is just over my head.So, on the matter of should you buy the book? For me, I just didn't know if it was something I liked until I did buy the book. I checked it out from the library and decided that it was something I wanted to spend more time with. So, you could try that. Also, before I read ACIM, I read "The disappearance of the Universe" and that helped too.My advice is just don't completely discount it because it may seem hard at first. Give it a chance and then decide. There are times when I read something that directly applies to my life at the time and it is a real blessing to me. Also, check out the authors I mentioned above, and that could make things clearer for you.People are so quick to condemn and ridicule what they don't understand.  You have to be open to receive. If you're not, then this may not be the right time for you to read ACIM. We shut the door and remain in our smallness and limitations when we point fingers and condemn because WE don't understand.
