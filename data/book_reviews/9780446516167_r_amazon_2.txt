CJ Cherryh's writing is a little dense, but that's because he packs so much into every sentence. "Heavy Time" is a hybrid space opera/hard sci-fi novel set in the "Alliance/Union" universe which also hosts the "Downbelow Station" and "Cyteen" books. It and its sequel, "Hellburner" present gritty, believable protagonists and antagonists who you may like, love, hate or watch suspiciously, but you will want to watch them. Cherryh has an amazing ability to zoom in and out of his stories; letting you smell the grease and sweat of the space station docks even while an epic tale of interstellar empires and simmering wars moves forward like a hammering tide.Read moreRead less
