A dubious use of good paper and ink, this satire has an interesting premise and takes it nowhere, fortunately the non-journey is quick, and the reader can move on to more profitable fare. The hype the St. Martin's  publicist gives the book makes one wonder if she even read it... It starts  as a moderately interesting look at the inspiration Christian film  "industry" which makes it's point in the first thirty pages and  then keep repeating, until there is enough paper to be bound into a book.
