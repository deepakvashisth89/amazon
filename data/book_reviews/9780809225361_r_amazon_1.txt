One of the best investments you can make for yourself. This book can change your life, and yes, I know that sounds all dramatic and pretty much the premise of any self-help book, but here are some things to consider:--It's the only self-help book I've bought again and again so that I could share the wisdom and ideas with my friends (without sacrificing my own precious copy).--While you will find yourself eventually living a gloriously abundant life, the book is designed to meet you at any stage in the process. Start small. Start big. Totally transform your life or just make little tweaks to enjoy life more.--The ideas are transferable to any situation. This isn't one of those books that you will read, thinking that two-thirds of what is said doesn't apply to you. Nope, these are tips with universal application.--It's not overwhelming or scary. You don't have to go in any order. Just pick something and do it.--Plenty of examples and stories to help you "see" how this works in real life. It's also inspiring to hear about other's successes.A couple of warnings:--many of the tips seem so simple and common-sensical that you might be tempted to roll your eyes and toss the book. DON'T DO IT! Even if it seems trivial, give it a shot. Cleaning your bedroom just might be the key to finding love/landing your dream job/resolving old anguish. Don't knock it 'till you try it. And if one idea just doesn't speak to you, try one of Talane's other suggestions.--there is a little bit of redundancy here & there, but it flows adequately with the book. In other words, it doesn't come off like laziness; it feels like these are such important points they bear repeating.--you will probably want to subscribe to Talane's website, buy more more books, and generally implement all of her good advice once you start seeing the benefits.Since reading this book--and re-reading it a few times--I've switched to a higher paying job, completed a graduate degree, bought a new house, made changes in my family life that have greatly increased our happiness, made better financial decisions, and really, truly enjoy each day more as I keep "perfecting the present."
