Barbara Bush is an excellent storyteller. She was an amazing woman who lived an extraordinary life. Her love of her husband, George, her family and her many dear friends shines through all of her pages.She and her husband were great statesmen and served their country well even after the White House. They traveled all over the world and her calendar was a whirlwind of activity.  Nevertheless. George and Barbara were the salt of the earth who loved to entertain and had constant visitors whether they were summering in Kennebunkport or at home in Texas.The entire Bush family are really wonderful people, and I felt I got to know the family better through reading this book. It is extremely well written and very very interesting. I highly recommend it.Read moreRead less
