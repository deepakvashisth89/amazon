This was  the first book that I read by Jayne Ann Krentz, and I loved it.  The developing relationship between Virginia & Ryerson reminded me a bit of a Hepburn/Tracy type of relationship.  It's a feel good story with a bit of suspense, and quite satisfying.  After I read Joy, I've made it a point to read anything that JAK writes in any genre--historical as Amanda Quick,  futuristic & paranormal as Jayne Ann Castle, and contemporary romance/suspense by JAK.  Under any name, her stories always hold my attention & I know it will be a good read.  She's one of my favorite authors.Read moreRead less
