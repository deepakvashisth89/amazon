This book was required material in my reference course.  As other reviewers have said, the book needs a good editor or proofreader.  I had to read some passages several times because of confusion caused by strange grammar mistakes and typos.  Also, I feel that the book is over-priced for what you get.  It reads more like a poorly written thesis than an actual textbook.  Much of the information on electronic resources is outdated.  Two things saved this book from a one-star review.  First, it did include a somewhat helpful section on conducting a reference interview.  Second, the information on evaluating resources was fairly helpful.  I was able to skim through a lot of the book because little of the information provided was new to me.Read moreRead less
