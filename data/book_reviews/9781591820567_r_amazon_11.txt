MARS is one of thoose rare manga's that tries to mimic real life drama instead of going all sci-fi or magical like many manga's.  It is nothing like marmalade boy as it is not as upbeat, though it tries to be comical...however, it is the tragic and dark moments which make MARS good and stand out from other comics.  Although Keira is the main character, it is Rei who is the more interesting one.  His troubled past and the horrible story of his brother Sei are what drive the narrative.  MARS has many tear jerking moments and violence--thoose looking for an upbeat tounge in cheek manga should look at Love Hina or marmalade boy as comic moments in MARS are either rare or not cheesy enough.
