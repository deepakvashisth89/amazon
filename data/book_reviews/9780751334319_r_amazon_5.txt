This book is ideal for the average person wanting to know the basics about yoga.  It gives an overview of the theory behind it, and discusses many styles of yoga in a very non-threatening way.  Some yoga books can be too "out there", but this one is presented in a really down-to-earth style.I've bought this book to give to friends and family who are brand new to yoga.Not recommended for the advanced or seasoned yogi.
