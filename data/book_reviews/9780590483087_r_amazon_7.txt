Maybe it's the book, or maybe it's just that I'm BSC-weary at this point. Not a fave! It was ok, but I got that dizzy feeling, sort of like that in "Starring the BSC". Just way too much going on. I don't even think an adult could grasp onto this in the manner it was intended. Just inundated with characters. Also....do we need, in all this confusion, two characters with the same name?? And not even the first name, but the last name, too!!!! A Ryan DeWitt on EACH coast! Not a good move at all. Strange that around the 200th page, Dawn has to tell us that Maryann's dad's name is Richard. Ummmmm, I think we know that at this point! I will never truly tire of this series and I do think there is a lesson in every book.
