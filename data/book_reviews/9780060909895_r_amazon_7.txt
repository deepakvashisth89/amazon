In 1984, I had the honor to spend a day with Miss Brooks, and to hear her do a reading of many of the poems in this book. I wish that all of you could have heard that reading, her work is meant to be read aloud. That's  what I would advise you to do, buy this book, and when you get it, read the  poems aloud.
