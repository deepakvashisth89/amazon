Woof.  What a read.  Still can't decide if this borders on sci-fi, or para-normal, or what.  Author definitely tried to make it all seem plausible, but I'm not so sure.  It took maybe 150 pages of a 435 page book for me to get hooked.  I just felt so at sea before that, trying to figure out exactly what genre I was reading here.  And to be fair - the end did seem sort of a let-down for me.  The romance with Tanner Burke is presented very sweetly, and Stanley Levy is quite the interesting character.  Tina is a 14-year-old 100 pound teenager, not the little girl that everyone keeps referring to.  I didn't understand that.  She is hardly a child.  Connor Harrigan was the most likeable character in the book.  Like a Robert DeNiro type, hard-boiled, but with a good heart.  I hated to finish it.  Hard to review without giving everything away.  But I do recommend it.Read moreRead less
