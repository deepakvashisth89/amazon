I’ve never wrote a book review in my life until now because I think its my responsibility as a person who’s read the book to educate others to NOT READ THIS BOOK. There was no useful information in it and it just absolutely amazed me about how negative and petty the author was on almost every page of this book.The entire introduction is talking down to you for always being a bridesmaid and never a bride. When talking about dress types she makes fun of women’s sizes, and calls large hips or “thunder thighs” flaws and that you should hide them. She calls skinny women a “bean”. Not one thing positive was said in this entire book. It makes you really feel bad for the author for feeling this horrible about weddings and makes me feel bad that I spent 50 cents on this at a Goodwill.She says things like how bridesmaids secretly hate the maid of honor and other statements (I can’t remember - I’ve already thrown it away). The book brings anxiety and hate and jealousy into wedding planning and I found it to be an extremely negative and hostile book to read.Read moreRead less
