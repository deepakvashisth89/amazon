I had high hopes that this would be an exciting read because of the basic premise of a family being chased by bad guys in the wilderness.  There were some suspenseful sections, but they were hardly worth reading all the extraneous parts to get to.  I think the author tried too hard at creating literature than in writing a good story.  Characters could see in the dark because of the "gloaming" several times.  At least I was forced to use the dictionary and find that gloaming means twilight. The children here were constantly getting their hair "tousled".  Best (or worst) of all, "the leaves clattered like a million green voices". Yuch!  Maybe my tastes are too simple, but give me a good Stephen King or Dean Koontz book and I think I'll pass on the "literary" novels in the future.Read moreRead less
