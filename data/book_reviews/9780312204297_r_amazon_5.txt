As a memoir, this book read like a diary, for better or worse.  Mary Morris writes very well and this is probably the reason the book held my attention.  Someone who wants a strongly plotted book may not be as engaged  as I was, since the "plot" is simple -- how she deals with being  a young single mother, and the relationship with the child's father.
