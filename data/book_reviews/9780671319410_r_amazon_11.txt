This is a really well written and hard bit of military Science Fiction. I thoroughly enjoyed the build up and world building as the plot worked its way to the big battle. There was a multitude of characters that were all relevant to the story, and although I felt the end was a bit abrupt with a few ends not quite tied off, I was still pleased with the result. I also believe it is the intention that this be the first book in a series, so no doubt any loose ends will be resolved later. I liked the leading characters and I was able to relate to them in the story. All in all this was a very good read and well worth looking at. Recommended.Read moreRead less
