As a lover of true adventure stories, I looked forward to the compilation of the great epics of the last 100 years. The brief accounts are, for the most part, lifted from the journals and stories written by the person involved. While it opened the door to many names and perilous tales I had never before read about, the book was somewhat of a disappointment. I found many of the narratives too short and clipped to really give a good picture of what was involved. Several of the stories were about similar incidents (mountain climbing, sailing, etc.) and I think it would have been more effective to have limited the number and expand a bit on the remaining ones. What this book will do, I believe, is to give the reader the incentive to pursue those epics which are of special interest to him or her, and seek to obtain the full account.
