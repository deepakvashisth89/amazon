The Lolly, Sam, and Spider stories are some of my favorite books by James Marshall. The independent reading kevel is about beginning second grade. These would also be appropriate for teaching children how to make a story more interesting ( in this book, Lolly, Sam, and Spider tell scary stories to make Spider's little brother Willie go home - the first story is somewhat dull, the next one better, and the third even better, but Willie tells the scariest story of all).
