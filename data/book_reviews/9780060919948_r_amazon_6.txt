One of the best novels I've ever read. If you are interested in either the story of Moses, the Exodus, the parallels with the African-American slave experience, the quest for inner and outer freedom, building a relationship with the divine within and/or outside of us or all or some of the above than this book is for you. And Hurston writes about all of this in a rich, evocative, beautiful and soulful prose, the Hebrew characters among themselves speaking in the vernacular of working class African-Americans of her day.
