I was afraid after a few dozen pages of this dark novel that it would be relentlessly grim and almost stopped reading. I am glad that I continued though. John's narrative grabs you and pulls you into into the depths, letting you up for air a few times, before providing redemption in the end.John's fierce telling of an often gruesome tale proves to be worth the read. It is a passion play about the lost paths of addiction and obsession. That it treats an unpleasant subject with gritty realism to the point where we are repelled, is well justified by the seriousness of the theme and offset by the eventual triumph of the main character. John Shirley was able to pull me through this strongly emotional novel and offer some deliverance in the conclusion.John's characters are deeply carved and fully fleshed. The often seem too sympathetic for the trials that they must endure, making the book difficult at some times.This is not a book for children or those looking for simple escape. John pulls no punches in his descriptions of the lost souls in this novel. If you are looking for light reading that you will forget in minutes, go elsewhere. I recommend this book for those willing to think and are not afraid of a real challenge. You will not forget this book!Read moreRead less
