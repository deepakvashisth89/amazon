Yes, the prose is detailed and flowery, but it works with the melodrama. I kept envisioning Lierin being tied to railroad tracks by Snidely Whiplash. Characters are too over-the-top to be real, yet somehow they come alive for me, and the whole of it is so entertaining. This is pre-Civil War South, and Ashton (sp?) is wealthy, and therefore a slave owner, but of course he’s a “good” slave owner. It’s cringey (as it should be), but it’s the way things were and to have written it otherwise would have been bs; just be warned.This is a twisty American pre-Civil War romantic suspense with an extra helping of Old Skool crazy sauce.
