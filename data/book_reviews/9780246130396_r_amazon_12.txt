A classic that I had never got around to reading. My advice to anyone in a similar position is - Read it! Very well written with twists and turns all the way. It is a product of its time - written around 1903 or so, therefore the writing and some of the ideas expressed are very old fashioned today. But that is part of its charm, it provides an education as well as a thrill.
