The Age of Innocence is a library-sale book I've had about for a while, on the "no, really, I'm going to stop reading so much fluff and elevate my average" shelf. Yes, well, one day. I happened to pick up Innocence, literally in passing, and within a couple of pages was thinking "Where have you been all my life?"OK, it wasn't so much love at first sight - but it was surprised delight. My expectations? Stuffy, I guess; stiff; something you'd be forced to read in high school. Something a Merchant Ivory film would be made from (though the adaptation was actually directed by Martin Scorsese). And while the second two are true, the first two very much are not. It was beautifully written, with engaging characters, and it was ... I was going to say "funny", but while I think I did laugh out loud a couple of times, part of that was surprise. Its main goal was not to be funny; it was witty. It was kind of wonderful. I have more Whartons, and they have moved up on my to-read list.Read moreRead less
