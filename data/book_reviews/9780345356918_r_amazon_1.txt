I enjoyed this very much. I recently read the first two fantasy series by Eddings, The Bellgariad and The Mallorean, which led me to this series.I enjoyed both of the first two, but having read this book, I can see how Eddings grew as an author. This book has a significantly more complex plot, which to me makes it more enjoyable. There are the same types of characters and character interactions as in the first two, which I enjoy quite a bit,  but the story has more twists and turns to it, which makes for a more interesting read.Read moreRead less
