Information privacy is a dynamic field.  This is a good introductory book to key concepts.  It is also a nice guide to key legal decisions that have influenced current information privacy policy in the United States.  The legal cases are presented in an approachable, narrative form -- not a lot of legal mumbo-jumbo.  The only shortcoming of this book is that it was published in 1994.  A lot has changed since then.  Even so, I recommend it as a starting point for those just stepping into the realm of information privacy.Read moreRead less
