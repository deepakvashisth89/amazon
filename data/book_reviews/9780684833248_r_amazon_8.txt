Mr. Watkins tells a humble and epic story.  A confederate private shares his unique perspective.  Reveals the grim realities of a glorious cause going from bad to worse and back again.  It is truly amazing he survived four years of warfare.  Most often death was easier than survival. General Bragg routinely court martialed his troops with a firing squad for deserters. Further punishments like barreling, whippings and deprevations were routine. It got better under General Johnston and worse again under General Hood.  The soldiers alternatively cursed and praised the war, its' cause and the Generals.  Yet like in all wars, the men fought for each other. So many soldiers met their Maker, whereby Watkins extolls their virtues and praises.  Eloquently written and graphically descriptive.  Sam's survival is a testimonial to God's protection.
