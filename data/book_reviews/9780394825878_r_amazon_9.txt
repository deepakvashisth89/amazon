I read this book as a ten year old when it was first published in 1978 and have recently shared it with my six year old daughter.  We have been reading the book at bed time and, being an able reader, she has avidly continued to read after I'vegone downstairs for the evening.  The whole experience has been an absolute delight!  The story follows Majorie Malcolm-Scott, the orphaned niece of wealthy Fergus, when she secretly exchanges place with Shona McInnes, a girl from a local orphanage as they are both being evacuated from Edinburgh during the second world war... and what happens when the time comes to change back at the end of the war.
