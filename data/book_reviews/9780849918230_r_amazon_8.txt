As a Freshman in college I was but a young child when the 9/11 attacks assaulted United States Soil.  Therefore for a majority of my life our country has been at war.  Because of this, I have only seen the Middle East, its people and the war as terrible and barbaric.  This is why I greatly appreciate how Cash focused on Christ and emphasized on how Our Lord loves people of all ages, races, genders and religion.  This book helped soften my heart towards the war and the people.  This book is a fantastic reminder that we as Christians must love an respect every person we meet, even if that person has different beliefs than we do.  The other aspect that touched me was all the times God appeared and performed many miracles.  As a person young in my walk with Christ, I find myself doubting and questioning my walk and my Lord at times.  Reading about the moments God worked miracles for the men eased my worries and doubts.  I would recommend  this book to anyone who is doubting that the Lord is at work in the world today.  It is easy to see the Lord's hand on every page of the narrative, whether it is keeping the men alive in extremely dangerous situations, or through the way the men's hearts changed and turned toward God.
