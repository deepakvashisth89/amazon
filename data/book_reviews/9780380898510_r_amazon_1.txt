This is the first historical romance novel I ever read.  I read it almost 18 years ago and was forever hooked on the genre.  This is not your typical, feel good, love story.  It has stories within stories, one after the other as the sword passes through many generations of women through hundreds of years.  Some of the stories are heart wrenching, some are heart breaking, some are heart warming.  All of them are WONDERFUL.  I would highly recommend this book to anyone looking for a good story without the typical story line.Read moreRead less
