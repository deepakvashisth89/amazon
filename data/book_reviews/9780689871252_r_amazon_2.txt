This was first read to me and my elementary school class teacher every day after lunch to allow us to rest after eating and playing. I fell in love with each and every character. Ellen for her strength and wisdom for someone so young. Otto for his courage and tenacity at such a young age. I borrowed the book from the local library over and over. When I started babysitting I spent my first money on my own copy from the local bookstore.And, now, 50+ years later, I still read about Ellen and Otto every fall. It has become a ritual for me. I read it to my daughters when they were little and now, I have a beautiful granddaughter and I cannot wait to read the story to her and give her her first copy of this book.Read moreRead less
