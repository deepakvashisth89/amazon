A Table in the Presence by Carey Cash is an autobiography of his time as a Marine Chaplin during the invasion of Iraqi.  It is a story starting from the days leading to the invasion. Beginning in Kuwait following the journey from desert warzone into the battles of bloody neighborhood streets Baghdad.  Cash describes that God protects the righteous and just from their enemies yet God does not look down on people of other religions.  Cash has to help soldiers be able to cope with deaths of friends and comrades such as team leaders.  More importantly he is able to aid the soldiers in his unit in finding their faith during wartime by providing them with the view that they it is their duty to fight and God is all right with it.  This it a great book for anyone that is struggling to find his or her faith especially military members trying to find where they stand on God and religion during wartime.
