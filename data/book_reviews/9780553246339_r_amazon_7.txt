I read Vulcan! when I was 13-14 years old.  I won't say how long ago that was, but at the time there were not countless Star Trek novels like there are now, with various generations and iterations.  There was just Captain Kirk, Mr. Spock, and the beloved cast & crew of the original series, and a new Star Trek adventure beyond the original "five year mission" was a novel thing.  Whether this is among the greatest Star Trek novels ever written, I cannot say, but it remains a sentimental favorite of mine.  Ms. Sky's writing is crisp and unobtrusive and the story is intriguing.  Although it's been a long time since I last picked it up, I've read my copy more than once and may read it yet again, provided it doesn't fall apart in my hands.  Enjoy!
