Cube Libre is a fast-paced, entertaining jaunt through 1890s Cuba, at the outbreak of war between America and Spain. It's also a wonderful return to the western genre for author Elmore Leonard, whose first efforts were exclusively western stories throughout the 1950s and 60s. This is the story of Ben Tyler, an American cowboy sent to Cuba to sell horses to a sugar plantation. As tensions rise between America and Spain, Tyler is caught up in the intrigue and danger of revolutionary Cuba as he seeks justice, and a fair deal.
