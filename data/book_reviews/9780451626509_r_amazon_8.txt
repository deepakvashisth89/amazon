Although much of this is dated by Jung's constant references to the evils of communism and references to the Iron Curtain, it remains a valuable  treatise on the effects of relying too strongly on outside sources for  one's raison de etre. Jung deftly explains the common thread of blind faith  running through religious, political and scientific beliefs. Pastor,  President, Professor: all these people become mouthpieces for dogma. Follow  the cults of personality at your own peril. The only way out is through the  undiscovered self, the unconscious connection to the "God"  archetype. Faith is important.
