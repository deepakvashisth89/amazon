First I'll say this is not my favorite Glen Cook book, but I actually enjoyed it much more than I thought I would. I've read almost all of Glen Cook's books and he is probably my favorite author. The main reason for only four stars is that the ending left me with an unfinished feeling, like a sequel was perhaps planned but never done. Certainly there is plenty of room for another tale of the Swordbearer. Still a good read and recommended for all Glen Cook fans.
