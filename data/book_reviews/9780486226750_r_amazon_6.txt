George Grosz is well known for his paintings that depict the café life in Germany between the two world wars, with depictions of disfigured war veterans and a vision of the rich industrialist classes in Germany,  for prior to WWII. This book shows another aspect of Grosz's work, with a lighter though sarcastic side to his character.
