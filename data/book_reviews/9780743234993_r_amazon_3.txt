We all have studied the battles and the Generals in the Civil War, but how much do we really know about the state and federal governments in the Confederacy? I realized I didn’t know much and read this book with utter fascination. State’s rights conflicted almost immediately with the interests of the federal Confederate government as it was trying to wage a war against the United States. Both were highly dysfunctional and often competed against one another while the war was waging. One has to wonder if Civil War history might have been different if Confederate governments had not been at odds with one another and not so dysfunctional.
