I remember reading these books in grade school.  I loved them.  Twenty-odd years later, I gave them to my niece.  She loves them too.  It's not sexy vampires or bloody zombies; it's just little kids finding a place for themselves when they feel different (and not being afraid of other people because they are different).  BUT, and this is important, NOT PREACHY AT ALL.  No morals heavily underlined three times here, just books you want to read and pretend to be with your friends (so that you actually soak up the moral).
