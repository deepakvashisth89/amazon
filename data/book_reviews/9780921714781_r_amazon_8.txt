Some of the specifics in the compelling evidence Grant Jeffrey presents in this book have already been established by others, but I found the whole book fascinating, and had difficulty putting it down.Jeffrey covers the largess of space and universe as well as those invisible to the human eye. It's quite the span--and always interesting. He takes complexities and explains them in understandable ways for the non-technical reader. He makes it clear from the onset where he stands, but I did not find Him brow-beating those who need evidence to believe.
