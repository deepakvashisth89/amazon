I first received a copy as a gift from my pastor when I was ordained in 2002.  I've since purchased others as gifts and now that I'm a pastor, I keep one in my office, one in my car and one at home.  It has samples for various services as well as appropriate scriptures, call to worship, etc.  It is protestant based (Baptist) so do not expect services on christenings b/c we do not christen babies, we dedicate babies and baptist those who are able to profess their belief in Jesus as Savior.  With that said there are wonderful sample services and wording for baby dedication services, baptism services, house dedication, ground breaking, and just about everything else you can think of!
