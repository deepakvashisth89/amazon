I bought the Drizzt book at the beginning of COVID. RA has created such an incredible world in such great detail it is so entertaining. He may cringe when he reads this but he has done for the world of Dungeons and Dragons what JK Rowling did for the world of Harry Potter. To be sure RA had the D&D as a back drop but he just layers in so much detail the characters, plot, and locations pop. it’s brilliant. It’s grand like the marvel universe.
