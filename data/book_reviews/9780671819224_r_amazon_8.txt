A highly original book with many interesting points concering the difference/similarities in Western and Oriental way of thinking. The characters seem a bit naive at times but this gives space for many original observations and thoughts on trivial things that we usually take for granted. You may laugh loudly when reading the dialogue or have a small cry inside when you follow the newest installment in the saga of De Giers relationship with animals, namely his cat Oliver.
