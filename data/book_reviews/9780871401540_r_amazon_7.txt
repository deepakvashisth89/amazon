I don't normally finish books of poetry, but I finished this one from start to finish.  I happen to think ee cummings is very creative, and that, in itself, is worthy of introspection.  He's just different, and it's interesting to see him go from the bubbly cheerfulness of childhood in the beginning to the sour, jaded, pessimistic and political aspect of the end.  There's a bit of cubism in there as well, which was both challenging and intriguing.  I recommend even for those not normally disposed to poetry.Read moreRead less
