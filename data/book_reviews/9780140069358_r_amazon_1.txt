Magdalen Nabb puts you right into the scene, and for those who love Florence, Italy, it is a delight to read. The writing is good, includes humor, but mostly visual details of the city and really well defined characters. The Marshal Guanaccia of the Caribinieri  is sick with the flu at the beginning and so you think he will be out of the crime solving of the death of an unpleasant Englishman who had an illegal business. However, with a young and inexperienced Lieutenant he has to take over and right up until the end you wonder if there is a murder. But keep up to the end to find out.Read moreRead less
