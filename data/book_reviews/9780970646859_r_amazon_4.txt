Fantastic guide! This walkthrough is as complete as you would want it to be. It's like playing with that one friend who has mastered the game and knows every nook and cranny of it to not only beat it, but dominate it. Furthermore, it makes you excited to get to the next stage in the game. When the going gets tough, you have something to look forward to so a player can push on.That said, it is not quite a perfect guide. There are a few minor imperfections here and there. But the biggest is near the end of the game, in the last few unexplored areas of Kanto, they leave off the trainer rosters due to a lack of space. I know my Pokemon by that point should have no problem taking them down, but it would still have been nice to know who I'm up against and what the reward is. This gripe may be petty considering the rest of the guide is fantastic, but still...Versus Books' guide is perfect in that it was written from someone who actually has played the game.
