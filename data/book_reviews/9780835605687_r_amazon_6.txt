I love esoterica and I have admired Jung's work for many a year, but having just learned of this one, I am absolutely aglow. Knowing Jung's work on The Shadow, it makes everything much more appreciable to learn of Jung's history with the likes of Gnosticism and Alchemy... Magical things as opposed to dead and dry philosophical concepts, oversaturated with silly logic. This book tells a tale that is dynamic, juicy and entirely live-able. Hoeller did a fine job with this one and will be a good companion if you are in to deeper wisdom, existential insights and especially the Black Arts. Well done...
