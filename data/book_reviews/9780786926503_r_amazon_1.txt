This book is a collection of columns by the late John Husar.  Mr. Husar was the outdoors sports writer for the Chicago Tribune back when I lived in Chicago and, despite the fact that I'm not in any way an outdoor sports kind of guy, I always read his column because the writing was that good.  I'm glad that someone put together this collection so those of us who remember his columns so fondly have something to show to those who never had the opportunity to experience Mr. Husar's writing the first time around.Read moreRead less
