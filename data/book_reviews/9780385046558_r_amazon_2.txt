Canadian Peter Mansfield travels to the tiny Yorkshire village of Shapley which has been in the news after speculation about the presence of a vampire. In this excellent novel, Marc Lovell (Mark McShane) offers an effective and chilling story. It brings together a variety of local folk traditions including a gibbet once used for hangings and a children's game called Ghoulie which originated centuries earlier in rural England when leprosy was common and some lepers tried to pass their illness on to others. Mansfield, passing himself off as a bird watcher, trys to learn the secrets of the village by developing acquaintances among the mysterious residents of Shapley and soon finds himself threatened by a sinister figure who enters his bedroom during the night.Read moreRead less
