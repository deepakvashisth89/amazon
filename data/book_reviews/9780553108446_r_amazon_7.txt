"Ferney" is at the top of the list of my most favorite books.  I even bought a second copy from Amazon, because I had given my first copy to my daughter-in-law who loves to read.  I think it hit a chord in my own heart, because I have often felt that I have known other people and even loved them at another time and place far removed from this one.
