I've been enjoying this older series a lot. The writing is excellent, and the characters are fascinating. Robin was a spy during the Napoleonic wars but has come home with what today would be called severe PTSD. It isn't helped that the woman he wanted to marry fell in love with someone else. He falls in with Maxie, an American woman who is also part Mohawk, trying to get to London to investigate her father's death. The two end up walking with many adventures along the way.I loved the section about the drovers' roads and learned a lot. I've hiked along one such road in Yorkshire and saw the ruins of a Drovers Inn, but reading about such an actual drive is more immersive. The details about the geese, for example, were riveting.The romance is lovely though I thought the ending a bit rushed and flat. It's still a 4.5 stars.Read moreRead less
