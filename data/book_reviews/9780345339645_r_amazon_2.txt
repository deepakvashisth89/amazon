In Hard Rain, the forces of evil join together to crush the idealistic members of the Homicide squad. Corrupt officials investigate the Commissaris for corruption. The whole squad of investigators are put on leave, so they can’t investigate anything.But they investigate anyway. They suspect that a certain suicide wasn’t suicide at all. And that the three junkies who died the same day as the “suicide “ didn’t die by accident.For the Commissaris, the investigation goes beyond a contest between good cops and bad cops.  The evil genius behind everything is his own evil cousin — bad since childhood. The cousins are determined to destroy each other.I’m getting a bit weary of this series with its metaphysical repartee and absurdly eccentric characters. But I keep going, hoping for a return to the jewel-like perfection of earlier novels. That said, this series is unique in crime fiction. I love the peculiar approach to policing in Amsterdam. I’m fond of every member of Amsterdam Homicide — and the oddballs they attract as well. Janwillem van de Wetering is a highly original writer.Read moreRead less
