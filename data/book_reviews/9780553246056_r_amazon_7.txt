This is my third of Ms. Daly's books with her unusual titles.  Mr. Gamadge has gotten up to a lively - what, 36-48 hours or so in which an entire heritage is turned over.  As in the other two of this author I've read, it was nose in book and following closely along.  It's always interesting when there is an imposing but magnetic patriarch or matriarch of means with an assortment of relatives and ward type persons in residence.  These play out to their represented types overall, with some developing as it goes and some more or less unmasked, including a dark horse.  I was personally involved and entertained in following this assortment. The detective was gratifyingly alert to potentialities as circumstances changed, including a propitious change of madam's will.  It's one of those where he caught you up at the end on some not-indicated thought processes in order to sort things out.  Those who say the clues are there as you go along don't seem to get specific about them.  But I'm along for the ride, and this was a good one.Read moreRead less
