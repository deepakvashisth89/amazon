Friend and admirer, Ben Bradlee was among the people with whom President Kennedy enjoyed spending his leisure time.  Bradlee, a superb writer and well as editor and publisher, a legend at the Washington Post, writes this as a tribute to his friend and exhibits his admiration for a politician whom he regarded to be imminently qualified, despite his personal foibles and seeming disregard for the results of his careless adventures.  Their friendship was relatively brief; only the span of time between Kennedy's election to the presidency and his untimely death only 1,000 days after inauguration.  Unlike most of Kennedy's friends, Bradlee and wife and children were participants in the President's family life.  The fact that Mrs. Kennedy enjoyed the Bradlee's company was a significant factor in that relationship.  Kennedy "used" Bradlee the way he did most of his friends, to enhance his political standing.
