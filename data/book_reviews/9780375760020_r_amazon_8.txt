Being neither a reader of Canon Doyle's nor a particular fan of mystery novels, I felt it was time to delve into both.  This collection of short stories exhibited to me that the author truly has a commendable ability to tell an easily understood and very fluid tale.  His exposition is linear and does not rely on flashbacks or forward projections and his utilization of Watson through 1st person singular is unique.  While I still remain less than enamored by mystery tales, I can understand why he is a long-standing favorite of the reading public.The 'gliches' that occur in the translation whenever a number or foriegn phrase is used do become tedious but, when the price is $0.00, one cannot complain too loudly.The tales that are included are:1.
