Brust has done an amazing job of pulling the reader into the dark world of John Agyar - perhaps casting a spell over their senses - and we find human emotion at the center of the intrigue, manipulation, and deceit.  As we join minds with this unsavory character, he takes us to the verge of annihilation, where the stench of death and unending torment await our arrival.
