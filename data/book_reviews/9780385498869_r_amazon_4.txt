I was really excited about this book as I loved Peggy Orenstein's Cinderella Ate My Daughter, perhaps because I am a kindergarten teacher and teaching very young girls, I could constantly relate to Orenstein's studies on girls today. However, this book was not what I expected. It is really only relevant for American women. I am not American but have lived in seven countries including America. The experiences of the women in the book are not easy to relate to for me. Also, I felt it was a negative book and seemed to indicate that women are obsessed with men and/or career and little else.
