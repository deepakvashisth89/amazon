The book has relatively good pictures and instruction as far as drawing instruction books go; however it is nothing new. It's nothing that you couldn't get in a basic How To Draw Manga book. Actually it's a little less than what you could get in your typical How To Draw Manga book as there isn't the female instruction as well. If you're a beginner and you only expect to be drawing males then this is for you; however if you want something a bit more than basics this is not for you.
