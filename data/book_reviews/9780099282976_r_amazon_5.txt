Good book. The main characters were mostly a bit strange if truth be told. I veered between feeling sorry for Robert and thinking he deserved it for being so ineffectual and indecisive. If Robert and Jenny had not met they would probably have lived reasonable normal, boring lives. In spite of the characters being unlikeable and I can't think of one other than the doctor, the story was creepy and suspenseful. The ending was left to your imagination.Read moreRead less
