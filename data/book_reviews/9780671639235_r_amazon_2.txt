Have no idea why some copies are going for literally 100s of dollars, but I received the book in great condition and very quickly, so I was happy about that. The book itself is outstanding - having the origin story of the current Special Forces written by the man who started it all is unimaginably and completely invaluable. It's truly astounding to witness the genesis of the modern day SFAS and SFQC being developed and fleshed out in order to meet the requirements of WWII guerrilla warfare. The reader will see how these early tactics, techniques, and procedures, SOPs, selection standards, and general approaches to by-with-and-through warfare have grown and cemented themselves in modern SF operations. Indeed, Col. Bank painstakingly details exactly where the term Special Forces came from, and why that specific term was chosen over several others. This effectively precludes any other unit in any other military from laying claim to the moniker "Special Forces". SF is not only a noun, but an adjective describing a very specific kind of warfare that is only conducted by the Green Berets.
