Lucas Davenport had met her before, this determined woman … this assassin. Clara Rinker is an expert at her chosen profession. Knew enough about herself to not worry about murdering people. Knew how to think ahead, consider potential problems and overcome them. That’s how she was able to get away from Davenport the first time.But when someone kills her beloved and coincidentally she loses their unborn child, everything changes for Clara. She is out for revenge, but Davenport is once again in her way. And unless you are as clever as author John Sandford, you aren’t going to guess how this ends.Read moreRead less
