Having bought this on impulse, I discovered with surprise and delight that Spencer Dunmore is not only an expert on the technical and operational details of Halifax bombers, but is also a master of how to write a good short story. This eminently readable book consists of  a series of beautifully written linked short stories portraying the experiences, terrors and heroism of all the different characters involved in a fictional squadron of Bomber Command, similar to that based in Pocklington in North Yorkshire. The author writes very well indeed. Readers who come to it, like me, after reading Len Deighton's classic Bomber, will not be disappointed - in its own way this book is just as good.Read moreRead less
