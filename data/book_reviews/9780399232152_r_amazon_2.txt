I searched all over the internet and library catalog for children's books that deal with blending families.  This one was excellent for kids ~7 and up (I think). It shows how a child reluctantly deals with her mother's new relationship and impending marriage. She tries to disrupt the process, but ultimately the family comes together.  I was a single mom for the first 6 years of my son's life. I felt he needed a lot of explanation of how our lives were going to change by adding a "daddy."
