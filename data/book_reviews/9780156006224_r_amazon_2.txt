I thought this might be the sequel to The Only Investment Guide You'll Ever Need, but it wasn't: I should have taken the earlier title at its word and not looked for Part 2 here.  Though not the book I was looking for, I am glad I read My Vast Fortune, and anyone would be well advised to do so.  In both books, Mr. Tobias is candid, insightful, sometimes self-deprecatory and ALWAYS HILARIOUSLY FUNNY.  The book was worth its price just for the jokes he put into the Users Guide to his early (and very profitable) money management software.  That chapter reminded me of his footnotes in the Only Investment Guide, like my favorite: "... and you wonder why your mother is worried about you!" (Footnote -- "What?  You think just because she's deceased that she's not still worried?)"There is plenty of good personal finance advice to be found in what is largely Mr. Tobias' investing autobiography -- his first business experience with student travel while himself a student at Harvard; the struggling journalist buying (profitable) real estate in New York and Florida; campaigning against cigarette ads ON RUSSIAN TELEVISION (that's right); and campaigning in favor if no-fault auto insurance in California (who should oppose him there but the mighty Ralph Nader camp?).  It must have been fun to be Andrew Tobias -- it sure is fun to read his books.  He wanted to make the world a better place, and at least through his rare talent for expressing hard-won money lessons, he has.  The millions he surely made along the way are a drop in the bucket to what he has saved readers (like me) from wasting.  Bravo!Read moreRead less
