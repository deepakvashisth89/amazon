This is the penultimate book of the series and I liked how the author kept the action moving quickly with a number of twists that were totally unexpected. The premise of the search for birthday presents was perfect because it gave a legitimate reason for the characters to be scattered across the country as they hunted for the "right"  gift. I enjoyed seeing the characters from previous novels and appreciated insight into how the various inhabitants of the world interacted with each other. This is one of the stronger books in the series and I would recommend it for all fantasy lovers or children who want to be introduced to the genreRead moreRead less
