I bought this when I saw my boyfriend out with another woman. Of course I was a mess and heartbroken since I wanted to marry this man. However, not belonging to a church and not really finding the help I needed from my friends (they tried), I turned inward and to God. I have known about this book and finally decided that I would buy it. For some reason I felt it was the answer in helping me with the heartbreak. I joined a study group and I did the daily lessons. It took months, but I know have shifted my perspective on everything in my life. I pray for my ex boyfriend that he may be happy and have love with someone else. I am thankful for that experience otherwise I would not be able to realize that love comes from within me and that I could share it with a man that really wants to marry me. I also started making more money, my relationships with my family are shaping into what I’ve always desired, my physical body is healing. This didn’t come from just reading this book. I apply the lessons daily and committed to being the person I have always desired so I can be who God creates me to be. Another big thing is that I feel I have a relationship with God. Not a religion, but God (however you refer to him, Buddha, Allah, etc). If you are inclined to get this, get it, and really go all in.
