Redefined 'God' for me in a way that I will be forever grateful for. An intimate revelation of the reality of the relation and negotiable space and time between subject and object, locating one within the other, and the contingency of the genesis of both within the seeds of the other. Vision, sensation - conscious, mindful apprehension of reality, baptizes the moment, ripening it into its fullest degree; likewise, the object's thrust upon the subject stimulates and invigorates the subject into fuller and fuller being. The act of becoming is a sacred seat where the conscious, attentive subject greets the object of its awareness. This is a space though that is beyond distinction. It is pure, without word. Beyond the substance ontological outlook. Beyond phenomenology.  Something like subject embedded in object, object in subject - but beyond these designations and distinctions. It is the truly ineffable, sacred space, that only the sage, saint, or poet glimpses.
