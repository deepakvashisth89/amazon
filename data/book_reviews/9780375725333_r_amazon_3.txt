We all have had friends who our parents said, "there is something wrong with him/her, don't play with them," and yet we do and they are the ones who lead us to our greatest adventures of life.  They are the ones who always seem to get us in trouble, yet they get away scott-free, and set us on our next adventure without ever realizing the importance.Trevians is the nickname of the New Trier High School sports teams, and I, personally, have worked in this environment for 40 years. I guess I know Rich Cohen, or at least some of his friends, since the picture on the cover of the book includes one of his friends who I know (ironically named Jamie, but not who Drew-licous is based on).  I think the stories of drinking and drugs seems to be over-blown, and I would have liked to know more about the teenage existence, but Rich Cohen has such a remarkably easy style, it is allegory and metaphor magnificent. It isn't really about the rich, spoiled kids of the North Shore (Ferris Bueller, Risky Business (also set in Glencoe, Illinois)), it is about friendships, love, a search for meaning, a hope to make ones life extraordinary and those who seem to be a step or two ahead of us in that process.
