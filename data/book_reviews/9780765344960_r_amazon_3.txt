Cosmonaut Keep was a steady read, unexceptional in many ways. The characters were many yet the pages were sparse, which confused as to who was who and where allegiances laid. Now with Book 2, the political science fiction goes onto the back burner a little bit while a more generous space opera takes places. Dark Light is a necessary sequel to Cosmonaut Keep and it also improves the storyline, which some sequels nowadays can't seem to do. It's not an epic sequel compared to the likes of his Scottish counterpart Peter F. Hamilton. Altogether, when compared to Hamilton and, fellow Scot, Iain M. Banks, MacLeod pales in their shining light. MacLeod is digging his own little niche with the Engines of Light series however and the stamina that the book produces is sufficient enough to garner some money and time to delve into the third book, Engine City.Read moreRead less
