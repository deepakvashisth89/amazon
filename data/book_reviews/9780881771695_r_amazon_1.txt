I got this book for a class I'm taking and have read most of it now.  It is clear and understandable, shows a great deal of knowledge and research into the beginnings of the Methodist belief system, and provides immediate references to assist the reader to go a step further or to validate a point.  Not a history book, it still tells the reader how it all got started and how some people have let it become a bit warped or twisted.  It does not have a negative tone, however.  It does reinforce, re-state, and remind the reader what the core of the Methodist faith is, and how to live that core belief system.  I took a membership class 20 years ago, and have attended Sunday School classes every year since then, yet this book encompasses all I have learned simply and concisely in a very understandable format.
