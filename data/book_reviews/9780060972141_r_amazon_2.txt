What an amazing author! I expected to need a reference guide while reading a book on such a heavy topic but Richard Friedman made it such an easy to digest read that I was shocked! It was the most interesting book that I have read as an adult. He lays out the evidence in an honest, logical manner and then proceeds to exclude each reasonable hypothesis until he comes to the most fitting one that cannot be excluded.
