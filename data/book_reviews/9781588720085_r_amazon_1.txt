This was a WONDERFUL book! I purchased it for the same reason that I purchase a lot of things here on Amazon --> The Reviews! Since they help me out so much, I try to pay back by writing my own. Perhaps I may be able to help, at least one person, decide on whether to click the purchase button or not.This book revealed to me, the power of simplicity. When I first got it out of its box and held it in my hand, I skimmed through the pages and quickly noticed that some only contained a single, short paragraph, and lots of dead/blank space. I've had bad experiences before, where bad writers, intent on making a quick buck, and boosting their ego by becoming "published writers," simply copy and past information from the internet, blow up the font size,  leave lots of empty spaces, blank pages, to fatten the book. For a moment, I wondered if this had happened here, as Yehuda Berg is a new author to me.It was NOT the case AT ALL! This book is LOADED with POWERFUL Wisdom! What had caused worry at the beginning, turned into a boon! Into something that I loved about the book! No fluff, no long windedness, no complex explanations, just the meat and potatoes right up front, right away!Not only does it contain a wealth of potent explanations that very effectively clarify Kabbalistic concepts, it also contains practical tools and examples in the back! Real Life examples of when and how you can apply these tools and allow corrective light in, catalyzing your actual, literal transformation.The highlights, for me; what held the most power, was the analogy of the lightbulb when explaining Resistance. This made so, so much sense to me! And it clarified so much! Right away, I could see how this applies to the patterns in my life, and just as quickly, what I can do to correct the impulsive cycle.For example, at work, a co-worker came over to visit me. Chubby, stinky, the type of person that has to talk and talk and does not let anyone get a word in edgewise. He began to talk about Trump. "I don't like him. I don't like the things he does, but I don't like Biden either. At least Trump was smart to cheat the system. I tell people that if they could cheat the system and get away with it, they would do it..." he went.I could feel my blood begin to boil. The abject ignorance taking shape before me was slowly creating a vortex-like, vacuuming energy, a stirring of a toilet bowl, threatening to pull me in further and further. It was starting to become too much. I wanted so badly to say, "so you think that the leader of our country. The one who represents all of us, Americans, should also represent it being okay to abuse the system? He is always barking about others taking a free handout and abusing our government and it's laws..." but fortunately, I had just read this book and I could see, very clearly, what was actually happening below the surface, at a much deeper level. I saw, put in the words used in this book, that I was about to short-circuit. I instantly recognized this as an opportunity to open myself up, as a vessel, for the Light of The Creator. I let go and allowed Tyrik to be just who he is. I was completely okay with the patterns that were unfolding before me, moment to moment. Reflections of something that had been hidden (unconscious) within me. It had nothing, really, to do with Tyrik. In other words, I didn't take the bait. I didn't REACT. and it felt GREAT!Since I was able to do this with Tyrik, I began to do the same with other situations that presented themselves. I began to view all of this as it is depicted in the book. As a Game. The opponent being our impulse to REACT (our Ego).I loved this book so much that I ended up purchasing two more books by this author!I am amazed at the roads that life puts before us! The places that we eventually find ourselves in! If I could rewind my life back to high school, never would I have imagined that one day I would end up being so utterly fascinated by Kabbalah!I highly recommend this book! It is a quick read. One to two sittings, really, but so infused with Potent Wisdom that you will want to savor every word very slowly. You will find yourself highlighting and closing it, with a book marker in between the pages you left off on, tilting your head back, closing your eyes, and just meditating on the Light that has just cascaded into your Being. The world will seem much, much brighter. The air, purer. Your sense of self, lighter. You will find your heart welling up with Love for this temporal, exquisite dream.
