I've read Forrest "Curious Wine" and loved it despite the lack of character development...but An Emergence of Green more than made up for it. I loved the way Forrest let us inside of all three of the main characters. We get to witness first hand how they perceive themselves and each other. The story flowed. I didn't find it outdated at all. It was great watching Carolyn come into her own. If this book was written after Xena than I'm sure there would have been a lot of Xenaverse references. Val tall dark and strong...Carolyn small and blond. I loved Val's character. She was strong but also vulnerable. Loving Carolyn was not what she wanted in her life...after all she married twice and had a son... all proof that she wasn't a lesbian. Paul, the husband was a Real Jerk...but he was perceptive. He immediately saw Val as a threat...even before the two women knew she was. I always find men and their "Penis Envy" mentality laughable.  I won't say much more, but watching the two women discovering how to love each other was amazing. I loved their honesty..."tell me what you're feeling" This book is truly a keeper.
