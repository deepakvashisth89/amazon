This is a *magic* book--whether reading or listening, it makes people yawn, makes them sleepy, makes eyes droopy and little heads rest against mom's shoulder.  I love reading this book out loud to my kids--it is a long book (takes about 15 minutes, if you read it nice and slowly, which is part of the magic), but it really does evoke cozy, warm, cuddly feelings. Great with a cup of tea and some animal crackers before bed!  I love the illustrations, which weave the words into wonderful, soothing images of all things comforting from childhood; it's fun to play "I spy" with the detailed illustrations, counting the different kinds of cookies or the number of puppies.
