Kayla Calloway has a big problem!  It's her breasts.  They seem to get in the way (literally and figuratively) and might prevent Kayla from pursuing her lifelong dream to be a professional ballerina.  Although this novel from Dorian Cirrone is funny and fast-paced, it also explores questions surrounding body image in a thoughtful way.  Teen readers will enjoy the behind-the-scenes look at the world of dance and the sweet romance between Kayla and Gray.-- Reviewed by Michelle DelisleRead moreRead less
