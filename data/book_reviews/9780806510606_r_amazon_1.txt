In compiling a list of must-haves for UFO related literature, I came across a reference to this book. Frank Edwards, not to be confused for Frank Scully, really did his research, and released it in 1966, the year after a big world wide flap. This book was excellent, Edwards was a real reporter type and his quotes from military establishment were solid and thought provoking. While the copy I purchased is in poor condition, this was a great read and is in my top ten list, time has made this tome obscure, and it must be preserved as required reading for any student of the UFO world.Read moreRead less
