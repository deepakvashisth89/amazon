Remember the old story of the kid writing a report on penguins?  "I learned more about penguins than I ever wanted to know."  That about sums up this book.  When I was planning to get my first small backyard flock I bought this book and used it to answer my many questions. It doesn't leave a single question unanswered. It is a great book for both the small time backyard chicken "farmer" and also for someone wanted to raise larger flocks.  If anything it is geared towards those interested in medium size flocks, and gave me more information than I really needed.  It gave me confidence to raise my chickens.
