Gideon St. Goddard made a promise to a dying soldier to marry his Sister in law and keep her safe without ever having met her.Sabrina is the mother of two, soon to be three children fathered by her now dead husband. They've been through alot and to ensure their safety she's agreed to marry a wealthy man she's never met.The two get more than they bargained for when they meet each other including a marriage born of Gideon's honor. Thinking that he can always resort to giving her an allowance and shipping her off to one of his homes he's surprised a beautiful heavily pregnant woman with the inner strength of an ox has wrangled his home into order and captivated him.The two re-discover themselves and find things in each other that they didn't realize were missing.Both lead characters were great. Sabrina's the heroine that as a mother I love to read about, she'll sacrifice anything for her kids even her happiness but is also vulnerable and broken from past hurts.Gideon is the rich English Gent, honorable, stubborn and smart. He's lived his Bachelor existence for a long time now so is more than ready for a wake up call.It's written well with the language used appropriate to the time with some sayings and words making me laugh. Only noticed very minor editing issues which didn't detract from the story.Overall the book was very enjoyable with the right amount of good v. evil thrown in. At times it did seem a tad perfect for Sabrina as I kept thinking realistically would a single man of high status marry a moody woman with children when he could just give them a fund and ship them off but that's not a very good love story is it?Read moreRead less
