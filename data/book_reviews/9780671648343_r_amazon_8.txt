With superior writing and many, many anecdotes and evidence, Dr. Sacks gives us a wonderful view of an almost not remembered epidemic and the decades-long aftermath of the treatment and patients. I love the references to other books and authors. This was so interesting that I spent much longer on it than expected and didn't want to put it down. So much information about Parkinsonism and its related causes and effects. I never knew that encephalitis could be the fore-runner of Parkinson's disease.
