While this is an early work by Nabokov, I would tremble at the thought of rating his writing as anything but "great". He is one of the few writers I know of who was a grand master at writing English and Russian. The introduction explains one word play that had me a bit confused. I suspect there are more that the editor either missed, or expected that the reader would catch. It is a little awkward reading the Russian in pre-1917 orthography, but it lends a Victorian feel to the work.
