I’ve been waiting for this book. The first two Kerrion Empire books — "Dream Dancer" and "Cruiser Dreams" were phenomenal. This one, "Earth Dreams (Kerrion Empire Book 3)” by Janet Morris, is no less remarkable. I read a lot of books, and have to say this entire series is quite an achievement in world building and story telling. The Kerrion’s vast empire is real to me now. I have been there. I will return again. The characters I have met along the way are indelible in my mind. I will miss them, yet always remember them. This must be a movie some day, although I fear the cinema could not do it justice. Five stars.Read moreRead less
