George R Stewart, English Prof at UC Berkeley in the olden days, deserves to be far more widely known. Before his iconic 'Earth Abides", 'Storm' was a detailed exploration of the birth, growth, and death of a December storm on Northern California, and incidentally it's affects on the human ants caught up in its power who are tasked within mitigated its impact. In the pre-satellite days, weather forecasting is done with barometric pressure and wind speed, integrated in human minds and plotted on the daily map of fronts. The impact of small stochastic events is everywhere evident: an owl blunders into a high-tension wire, so that it's taken down by ice load, affecting electricity distribution for a whole region.[NB: this was one of my father's favorite books, and he always urged me to read it. He passed four years ago, and when I saw it on Amazon immediately bought it. Had the feeling he was looking over my shoulder approvingly, all the while. Nice.
