Enjoyed reading  first two of the  Children of Violence  Series.... This book, the third, is a bit repetitive but I will persevere as I have bought the whole set! An interesting story from the perspective of an insider to a revolutionary phase in the history of politics and social mores. Martha's decisions and delays in making decisions that seem to result in things happening to her almost by accident, can be both infuriating and sadly inevitable. She is a product of her social situation but is constantly engaged in an exasperating and energy-sapping struggle to redefine it. I wish her well in the rest of the series that I have yet to read!Read moreRead less
