This novel has a finish that will really make you think about what is important in life.  I loved the people in the story, especially Wilson, the hero pilot who just did his job and didn't expect to live through the war but knew the world wasn't going to stop turning just because he died.  All he cared about was not letting down his friends and doing what was right.
