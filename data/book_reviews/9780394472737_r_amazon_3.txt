John Updike is the best contemporary writer I have read.  As a Language Arts teacher, I appreciate the tremendous level of craft and mastery of the language as I read again this series.  After forty years, it still stands up nicely.  The characters are still relatable and coherent, given the times and the currents running through it.Page after page, I am amazed at the skill that Updike shows for the plot and the characters in it.  Over the years, I loaned my hard-cover copies of the Rabbit series and I never got them back.  Now I have the resources so I can get those books back.
