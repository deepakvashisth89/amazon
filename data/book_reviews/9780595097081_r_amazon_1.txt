Mr. Donaghe is such a talented writer....his characters are so believable and not only send a wonderful message to all gay people that they deserve love, happiness and stable relationships but also helps his non-gay characters to reflect on their own belief systems. He also realistically portrays the obstacles that gay people face even to this day. I especially loved Joel's character....he was not going to apologize to anyone for loving another man! Fell in love with his supportive parents, too.
