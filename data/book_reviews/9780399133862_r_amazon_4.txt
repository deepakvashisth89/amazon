I liked this book a lot and recommend it to any one interested in the Vietnam War. I thought it was moderate and presented a middle course without ignoring both the orthodox and the revisionist thinking about the war.  It seemed to me to be credible and I was glad to see research done on the issue of spitting on returning vets.  I concluded that the spitting was not a common experience to most, but rather it was occasional, especially for vets going through California airport terminals.
