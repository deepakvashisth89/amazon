A gripping tale of a young man newly trained for the interstellar trade, assigned to a free-trade ship (rather than the more prestigious trading company ships) and his adventurous first time out on a mission to an unknown world.  Andre Norton's style is fast-paced and intriguing - a mixture of engineering marvels, mysterious extraterrestrials, sinister bad guys, futuristic society, and the motley crew of the Solar Queen (including the inscrutable Captain Jellicoe and his pet Sinbad the Cat.)  As far as I know, this is the first of the Solar Queen novels - the others are "Plague Ship" (probably the best!) and "Voodoo Planet."  Good stuff, worth a read!Read moreRead less
