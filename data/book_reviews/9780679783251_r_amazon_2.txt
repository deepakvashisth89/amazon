So you've read lots of stories and watched movies and think you'd make a great explorer?  Think again.  This true tale of an early attempt to not only climb Mount McKinley (Denali), but to just get to it, will leave you exhausted.  I fly over this terrain every day in my job as a bush pilot and I can not imagine what these men went through.  After reading this book I don't have to imagine it.  I can easily say that no one today would willing attempt to do so much with so little as these men did.
