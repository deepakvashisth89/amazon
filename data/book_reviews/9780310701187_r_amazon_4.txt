This book arrived on Christmas Eve and the entire family enjoyed the reading of it on Christmas Day.  We gathered (18 adults and  5 children 0 10 years to 20 mos)  on Christmas day and after lunch we circled up to read the book aloud.  It was a wonderful transition from lunch for  excited children running about to sit for a few minutes to think about Jesus before exchanging gifts.  The book traveled about the room for  each person to read a portion of the story.  When it was revealed at the end of the book,  that the song was written as code for persecuted Christians to acknowledge each other, there was a collective ah ha...
