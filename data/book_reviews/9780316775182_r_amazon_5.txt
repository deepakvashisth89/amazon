This is a cute story about a family whose children put on a Purim play during their annual party.  When two of the children get sick, they need to find replacement actors.  The parents invite a woman from up the street to play Haman.  The children protest (Haman is male and the neighbor is, well, old).  Of course it all works out and Frannie, the little girl who tells the story, now has a new friend.Although this is a nice story, it really isn't all that different than so many other children's Jewish holiday books.  Lonely elderly neighbor gets invited, kids are concerned because he/she looks mean or at least not friendly, everyone grows to love the person.  It is fine to read as one of several Purim books, but I doubt it will be anyone's favorite.Read moreRead less
