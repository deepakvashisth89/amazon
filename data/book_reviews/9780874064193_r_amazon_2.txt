We all know that Scary Stories To Tell In The Dark (with the original Stephen Gammell art) is the king of scary story books. This, however, I would say is the underdog champ. The enticing cover alone sold thousands at Scholastic book fairs, I’m certainly one of those who reflexively grabbed one in fifth grade. These short but sweet tales are great, and the childhood fears of monsters and ghosts are balanced with more modern and realistic terrors that are borderline inappropriate, just what we were looking for!
