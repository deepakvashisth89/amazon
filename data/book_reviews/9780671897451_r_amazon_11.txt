For the first half of this book I was thinking it might be a four star book; worthy if a bit muddled. But as it continued, the flowery language and the overwrought drama became more annoying. Once you add in characters it's hard to care about there's no incentive to keep reading. I gave up at 80% through. That's how much I didn't care about what happened.Saintly (and exceptionally dreary) spinster Norah travels to Ireland to marry an unknown man- the equally dreary and unpleasant Sir Aiden Kane. Romance authors tread a fine line with macho heros. I think Aiden is intended to exude sexy manliness but sadly he comes across as a pig. Within 24 hours of Norah's arrival he is humping her leg and telling her she needs a man. Aiden has a teenage daughter who badly needs some mood stabilisers. Maybe it's an inherited condition as her father makes some pretty clueless decisions. Then there is a dastardly half brother, a vile dead wife and blood thirsty rebels. The plot wanders around in circles. It has many good elements but it's put together in such a jumbled way that it's a hot mess.Read moreRead less
