I just recently discovered Deborah Crombie and I'm delighted that I did. I'm already on her 8th book in her Duncan Kincaid/Gemma James Scotland Yard mysteries in two months so that should be some indicator of how much I enjoy them.  I'm a great fan of British authors from PD James to Anne Perry to Jacqueline Winspear. And I appreciate an American author like Laurie R. King who can write authentic British based novels as she does with her Mary Russell/Sherlock Holmes series.I enjoyed the first book in this series and Crombie just keeps getting better. Her two main protagonists drew me in right away and continue to develop into interesting, complex characters. One of Crombie's devices is to create a back story that feeds into the current plot until at the end they converge and she handles this technique deftly.I'm a stickler for accuracy and have found that Crombie really has British terminology and expressions down amazingly well for someone who had only visited the UK briefly when she wrote her first book in this series. There are a few minor errors in subsequent books, oike calling a "jumper" a sweater but I consider that something her editor should have caught. Otherwise, she does a remarkable job of creating an authentic British experience.I'm not one for giving story outlines in reviews since you can get those from the book reviews included by the page site. I'd prefer to say that I find her main characters appealing and engaging and the peripheral characters well drawn and interesting in their own right. Her plots catch your curiosity right away and keep you wanting more until the end. Then you want to read the next one.Read moreRead less
