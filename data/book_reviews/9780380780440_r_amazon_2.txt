Knight's in Shining Armor come in all shapes and forms, some are human and others only exist in the world of fiction. Like a downtrodden Hollianne inDark Knight in Disguise III: Earthbound Angel 1 (Earthbound Angels Book 1), Deven finds her special Knight.Captured is very captivating story, no pun intended. I liked Deven's character a lot. Her background was very troubling in good ways and in bad. She is sort of clever and naive at the same time. You can empathize and love her quickly. Deven reminded me a little of Spirit: Stallion of Cimarron, because she is courageous, curious and quite cunning. Captain Cole Mc Rae on the other hand rubs you the wrong way from the start. He is somewhat prickly in the beginning, incredibly cocky and borderline irking but as you go along he manages to soften up and you see the a sweeter side to him.The setting for this story for me was a bit jarring. Deven's crafty attempts to escape captivity gave me a few Keira Knightley flash backs from the Pirate's of the Caribbean franchise. The story had quite a few shenanigans that kept it interesting. I really appreciated the humor that came as a relief since the beginning bordered on the creepy side. It's really only in fiction that you can have a background as troubling as Deven's and somehow manage to still have your virtue in tact.The chemistry between Deven and Cole was really strong. The writer really took you on an adventure. I couldn't predict anything in this book so I give Victoria Lynne a thumbs up for the mystery and suspense. It's a lot of reading but quite worth the time it took me to finish. I wished that Cole wasn't so rough around the edges in the beginning. It left a sour taste in my mouth that was very difficult to get rid of.When a character robs you the wrong way from the start, its hard to look past their faults and see the good. But bad start for Cole aside, the romance, thrill and adventure was really wonderful. There weren't any moments in the book that had you wondering how they got from one place to the next because it had a very good flow.It's an overall captivating read in every sense of the word.Read moreRead less
