I have bought this updated version of the LP Guide for New England. I know something about New England having spent the first 18 years of my life there. This book not only tells you what there is to see and do in New England (Maine, Vermont, New Hampshire, Massachusetts, Rhode Island, and Connecticut) but also places to stay and eat in these states.
