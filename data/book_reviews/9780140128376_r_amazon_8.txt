Angela Carter is a genius!  These short stories are woven magically with dark dramaticism and enticing sensuality.  The last three stories ("The Werewolf", "The Company of Wolves", and "Wolf-Alice") were my favorites by far.  These stories are more than new spins on old fairy tales; rather, they are psychological horror shows that delve into the sexual id of the human psyche.  There is a lot between the lines concerning feminine coming-to-power, which as a feminist I loved!  All in all, fantastic read and fabulous writing!Read moreRead less
