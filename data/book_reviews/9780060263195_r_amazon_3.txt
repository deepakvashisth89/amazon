Very Vonnegut. For that matter, very Chermayeff, if you're familiar with his style (NBC, PBS logos). It's an interesting take on the story of the birth of Jesus, told from the point of view of baby Jesus. The book speaks of "The Creator of the Universe," born as a human infant, and refers only to "the Creator." The word "Jesus" does not appear.  The baby is presented as a very human baby, without divine omniscience. I wouldn't say that it's especially religious. We're Jewish, and I feel that I could present it to my young kids as a story that has little to do with Jesus -- as they don't know the Christian story to begin with.This book is only supported on kindles or kindle apps that display color photos -- wouldn't even download on my "regular" Kindle.
