Today news of drone strikes are fairly commonplace. In 1944 pilotless radio controlled aircraft was in its infancy. Packing one with high explosives and flying it remotely into a hardened German V-1 or sub pen was unheard of. Yet that is exactly what the US Navy and the US Army Air Force was attempting to do. However the drones were not exactly pilotless as they could neither take off or land. A pilot and skeleton crew had to get the plane- in this case a B24 Liberator bomber off the ground- arm the explosives and then parachute out of the plane. A controller in a separate plane would then “pilot” the now pilotless drone into its designated target.
