I was not certain what this book was about.  I decided it was a character study and a  picture of the time. I read it as something of an anthropogenic treatise, giving the reader an insight to a terrible time in our world's history. It took some patience to keep at it. But the writing was good, very descriptive. I don't know who I would recommend this book to. I suppose if one likes Doris Lessing, and enjoys history, one might find this a worthy read.Read moreRead less
