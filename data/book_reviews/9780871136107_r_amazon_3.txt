"Water from the Well" was like mother's milk to me.  I grew up in southwest Arkansas, and this book took me back to my roots in a deep, rich, almost primal way.  Myra McLarey's writing quenched some long-buried thirst in me.  I've bought and can't wait to read "The Last Will and Testament of Rosetta Sugars Tramble," after keeping watch for many years for another book from McLarey.  Somehow "The Road to Eden's Ridge" escaped my watchful eye.  I'll be reading that one, too.  I'm so pleased to know "Water from the Well" wasn't the only chance I'd have to enjoy this author's unique and wonderful voice.
