My wife is pretty hard to please when it comes to books. She thought Eat, Pray, Love was stupid even as people were raving over it because she could see so many holes in it. As the 6th child out of 7 and a mother of 3, she has a lot of experience observing sibling interaction. She really thought this book was really well-done and often spot-on.
