I read this book over ten years ago and it changed the way I thought of marketing and advertising, but also how I thought about life. Since then I have re-read it many times, and recently purchased the Kindle version to re-read it again. Roy Williams has a light way of explaining complex issues. Over and over it becomes clear that what we are trained to think about effective advertising is simply a waste of money for most people and businesses. Being able to think outside of the box I was able to increase sales 300% shortly after 9/11 when our competitors were going out of business.
