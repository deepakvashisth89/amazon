This book was good, but not as good as Kaffir Boy or Love in Black and White. I found myself bored at one point, where I put the book down and eventually picked it back up (and the story improved.) He is somewhat repetitive in how he explains rights for all races. I completely and wholly agree with Mr. Mathabane; don't misunderstand. But the way in which he wrote it, which I initially enjoyed, eventually just seemed bland. The story of his life, however, is absolutely fascinating and uplifting. Mr. Mathabane is truly an inspiration. I would recommend it - the good definitely outweighs the one point of boredom that I got! Read it!Read moreRead less
