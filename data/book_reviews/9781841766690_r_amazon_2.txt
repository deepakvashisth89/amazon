A typically serviceable product from the vast and ever-growing Osprey library, this volume presents the usual very brief overview of its subject, accompanied by excellent illustrations.  Twenty-four commanders are given illustrated thumbnail summaries (plus a color rendering and description of their uniform in the traditional Osprey "plates" section), while an additional eighteen or so receive a one-paragraph caption accompanying a single photo, and all this in sixty-four total pages.  As with any "best of" compilation, one can quibble with the selections made by the author.  In this case I would argue that it seems misplaced for some estimable but perhaps not first-rank leaders such as Donald Bennett, Max Horton, Guy Simmonds, and John Slessor to be among the twenty-four short biographies, while Leigh Mallory, Wingate, Pound, and, especially, Mountbatten are relegated to the one-paragraph captions.
