As always the author tells an absorbing story. We get to see old characters & new, who are equally interesting. I was soon rooting for them too.But there was too much left unsaid & several storylines were cut short rather than reaching a satisfying conclusion.E.g. What about the fraudulent sales of shares in colonies? Did Ronnie sort it?How were the NewTex women doing after several months of being needed & respected?What about the theft of Ser Kevil's fortune?What happened to Kate, the Lonestar Ranger?Couldn't we at least get to see Serranos & Suizas at Esmay & Barin's wedding?And which Serrano admiral booted Esmay from Fleet anyway? What was their reason?What happened at Sirrialis after the mutineers left?How were the mutineers finally defeated? What persuaded them to give up or were they all blown away?How did Hobart's wife respond to his assassination?Those are just off the top of my head, I expect others will have similar questions.I hope Elizabeth Moon handles endings better in the Vatta series.Read moreRead less
