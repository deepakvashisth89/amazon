This is a kind of coming of age story in which the protagonist must reach her thirties to achieve maturity; it begins when she is a reasonably happy college drop-out suffering from a difficult childhood.  While there are significant minor characters, it is a rather long book for such a story, told in one voice, and at times the reader becomes almost as impatient with her as her friend Brian.  Fortunately, Goodman is a good writer who leavens the novel with wit and humor, and the protagonist is a well-developed character with a lot to offer, as many of the people in her life’s journey recognize.  She seeks love but also spiritual fulfillment and meaning, and while there are many false starts there are also periods of great happiness and joy along the way.  Characterizing her as a hippie over simplifies, but gives some idea of her.Some of her thoughts: “He wasn’t the only one who had other people in his life!
