Dictated to an aspiring writer, Ellen Henshaw's life story, and that of her often down-and-out yet brilliant piano-playing father, is told in a fascinating ribald style. From her impoverished youth to eventual rise as a high-priced escort, the story also tells of how her father supported them by cranking out unappreciated keyboard accompaniment to silent films, and later, at various other gigs.
