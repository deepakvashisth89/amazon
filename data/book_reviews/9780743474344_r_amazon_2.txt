This is a brilliant book that is not a typical bio in any sense. It pieces together Pac's own words ... quotes, letters, handwritten lyrics, etc into one collection that sheds light on who he actually was. This is almost like cracking open his family's scrapbook. True fans should not pass this up. It makes for a quick, yet insightful and emotional look at a complicated man. On top of that, the photos alone are worth the price! So many rare pics and scans.
