I found this novel a strange read. On the surface it's a coming of age novel set in a high school in Phoenix. It's tentacles reach back into the family histories of the two courting youngsters, and as the atmosphere darkens, violence descends. Interestingly, the author refers to the Lincoln Savings scandal of the late 1980s which wiped out thousands of depositors who thought they were investing in government insured securities. But he ties former Senator Barry Goldwater to the scandal when in fact it was Senator John McCain who became one of the so-called Keating Five, senators who got the government to help Charlie Keating continue his multimillion dollar con game.
