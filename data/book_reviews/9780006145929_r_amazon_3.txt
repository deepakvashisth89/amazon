North StarAs I make my way through the collection of Kindle Innes, I find myself being captivated by the way in which Innes creates a “hero” who is so "passive". A hero who can do little to influence the train events.And it works. The main character is really caught up in events of all different natures: there are clashes between his past and his present, between his personal and his professional lives... All the while he seems to be a spectator to what is going on, but never gives in, and seems to stand by his own principles firmly.A story well-told, typical of Innes, and with an interesting array of characters who interact in a convincing and complex manner. The setting (Shetland and the North Sea) makes for a plausible industrial drama, that brings to light particularly greed and courage in human nature.Read moreRead less
