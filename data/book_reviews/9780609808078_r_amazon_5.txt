Ali Smith a woman of many talents ...a musician who cut her teeth with the NYC lower East side rockabilly group Speedball Baby, she is also a great photographer who's work includes potraits of fellow musicians such as Exene Cervenka, Jon Spencer, Steve Almaas, and Peter Murphy as well as work for major ad campaings for Cosmopolitan Magazine and Jordache Jeans.They say a picture says a 1000 words, but Ms. Smith's photos communicate much more; she captures evocative moments in each portrait.Laws of the Bandit Queen shows off strong women in powerful photos and their own words.
