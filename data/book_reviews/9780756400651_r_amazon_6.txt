As a child, I loved MZBs books. I loved the world she created, all the creativity that went into it. The young characters' struggle with the medieval world they lived in, its duties and rules, and their conflict with their longing for freedom, resonated with me. It still does. I still think MZB was a terrific writer. However, as an adult, and with everything that is now known about her, I can not help but read the books differently. Especially the character of Dyan, and the way he developes, leaves a somewhat bad taste behind. Now, I can't help but wonder what drove her to write his story like this, and why her heroic characters, who clearly know what is wrong, somehow continously fail to do the right thing with this knowledge. I can't help but wonder what kind of person she was, to write like this, and live like she did. I also remember, as much as I loved her books when I was young, I always felt like something was missing, although I could not really tell what it was. I think I do know it now. There is a curious indifference woven into the story. Based on everything that the characters are thinking, the people they are, you expect something to happen, to evolve, and it never does.
