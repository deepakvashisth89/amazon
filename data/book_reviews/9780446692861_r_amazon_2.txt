"English as a Second Language" is another installment of the ever popular  chick-lit genre.  The story revolves around a twenty-something young woman and her adventures, and sadly misadventures, in love.  While the story follows the basic formula of other chick-lits, the book is still an interesting read.Alex Brennan leaves her home in New York City to travel to jolly ol' England for graduate school on a bet from her old boyfriend.  Relationship trouble and hilarity ensue.  She meets a group of new and diverse friends, develops an entirely unhealthy crush on her attractive professor and learns to deal with the hectic, but mostly incredibly drunken, life in English grad school."English as a Second Language" differs itself from other books of the same style by being set in a foreign, and therefore interesting, country.  The author, Megan Crane, actually lived in England for several years, so her take on the British life and culture is accurate.  The quirky British phrases that Crane inserts make the story more authentic and create a wonderful picture of life in England.Although the storyline is somewhat predictable, you know true love will ultimately prevail in the end, the book is still very enjoyable.  The characters are appealing and you actually start to like and feel for Alex.  If you are looking for a deep read, this surely isn't it.
