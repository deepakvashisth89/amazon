I once learned that 'good' science fiction idea required a minimum amount of suspension of present beliefs, and the closer a tale is written to the present the smaller that amount should be.  Here we have a book with one 'change' thought though to a logical conclusion and surrounded with an interesting plot.  I found the characters to be a little flat - either all good or all bad, but oddly some of the minor characters seemed to most real, such as Matt, the teenager in one of the Militias and his girlfriend.
