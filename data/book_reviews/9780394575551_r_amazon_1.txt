This was very informative about a woman that I only recognized by name. She had beauty, power, and brains but no  real character. She had power over nearly every man she met and apparently had intimate relations with many of the powerful ones, yet failed to have a successful home life. This biography ends when she is successfully elected to Congress, has a powerful husband who adores her yet can't live happily with her, and a daughter she is closer to than any other person, but doesn't spend much time with.
