A brilliant story, written so well, and moving your sympathies from one character to another.  The widow in particular has "blind" faith in mankind whilst keeping her family together with a fist of steel.  Set in a prairie town in the 19th century Thomas Eidson takes you into the lives of the characters and keeps you there. He does this in all his books though there are not many. I first read his book The Last Ride and was hooked. I now have all his books.Read moreRead less
