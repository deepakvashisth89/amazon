This lively tale has it all--from two unlikely fugitives camping out in the Scottish wilds, to the high seas, to Queen Elizabeth's court to a tavern where spies and intrigue lurk.  And did I mention a really cranky  horse who hates Englishmen?  This romance is certainly not love at first  sight.  The story opens when the spirited heroine, Fia, shoves the Earl of  Rotherwood out a window.  Although it takes a few chapters for love to  develop, the laughs begin on the first page and continue all the way to the  last.  It's the only book I can remember reading lately where I actually  laughed out loud during the epilogue!
