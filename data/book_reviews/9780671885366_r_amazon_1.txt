This is a modern version of "The Sorcerer's Apprentice" and I found it interesting and unique.  A NYC sidewalk magician performs a trick on the wrong person--an old, creepy man.  Thus begins a journey for the young magician to learn from the old magician and become the ultimate magician.  He realizes too late that this is black magic and he is in for the fight of his life.  What I found most interesting is how easily he gives up all that he loves in his life to apprentice to this weirdo.  Tryon did a great job creating a most memorable weirdo.  This intriguing novel was published in 1995, 4 years after Tryon died.  It was unfinished.  Whoever finished it didn't have the descriptive writing skills that Tryon excelled at.
