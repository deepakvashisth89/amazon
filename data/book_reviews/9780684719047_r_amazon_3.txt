Every teacher and every prospective teacher should read this wonderful book.  It is an amazing account of a country boy with a deep thirst for education finding his niche in the world.  Jesse was well-educated in the ways of farming and the ways of knowing nature there in the Kentucky hills, but his father agreed that Jesse needed a real education.  And Jesse used that education to help other young hill country folks see the need of education, also.  It is an inspiring account of the ways Jesse found to inspire and challenge his back-country students and how they rewarded his efforts beyond belief.Read moreRead less
