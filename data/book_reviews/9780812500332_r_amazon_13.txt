The book is in rough shape but after flipping through the book I don't mind because it's far different from the first one. The endearing childlike nature of Norman Bates has been stripped away from his time institutionalized. Rather than being at the mercy of his other personalities, Norman has changed and is completely in control of what he does. This novel reflected the author's distaste towards the more infamous film his movie spawned. A fair warning to anyone who buys this novel that it contains subjects such as rape, necrophilia and animal death.
