I had purchased several books by this author because I enjoy reading about wildland fire fighting and he seems to be the self delared expert on the subject.  After attempting to read this book, and reading part of several others, I sold all of his books.  His writing style is far too annoying and pretentious for me to be able to enjoy the read or follow the story. His writing smacks of the kind you would expect from someone with a PHD who is writing books about a blue collar trade done by salt of the earth people. What I really want in a WFF read is just that. A down to earth, no frills, plain language story.  Plus it is clear that he is trying to assert himself due to his 15 years on the job which he clearly is still miffed that he had to quit. Such a shame because I am sure there was a great story here hidden under the mess.Read moreRead less
