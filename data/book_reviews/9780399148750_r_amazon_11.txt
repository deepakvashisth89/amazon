Charts Rick Cowin's three years undercover in the world of New York garbage disposal in the late 90s. Recounts meetings he had with mobsters, quoting some conversations word for word. So if you want to know how members of the mafia speak this is the book for you. Also gives a little bit information regarding the history of the mafia from the early days, but nothing new to people who know about the subject.
