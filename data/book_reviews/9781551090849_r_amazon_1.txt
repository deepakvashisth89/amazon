To be perfectly clear, in response to any poor rating this book was given, this is not a book of ghost stories. This is a book of folklore on the subject of ghosts in Nova Scotia. The two are very different. In this book, you will encounter hundreds of accounts of ghosts and the supernatural with some recountings  being as little as a sentence and others a few paragraphs. However, as in any good ghost story, the story is more about people encountering the supernatural than about the supernatural itself. If you are looking for a jump scare, this book is not for you. However, if you can appreciate oral traditions, myths, legends, and customs that might otherwise be lost, then you will appreciate this classic.  It is well worth the read.Read moreRead less
