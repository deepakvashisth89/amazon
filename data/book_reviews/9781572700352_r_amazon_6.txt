The first entry in the Nero Wolfe Mysteries. All the main characters are here, and the formula is set for later books, though the characters are refined in later books to good effect. Wolfe is a little long winded here, and Archie isn't as smooth or cool and sophisticated as he is in later books (which is the only reason I give this 4 stars rather than the usual 5 I give to Stout). But the mystery in this one is probably one of the better ones, with a clever who-dun-it and an even more clever how-dun-it. These books laid the foundation for many series to come and are well worth the read. For a book written 80 years ago this (and the others) are remarkably undated outside of the automobiles and the prices of items. Well worth a read, and a must read for any serious mystery enthusiast.
