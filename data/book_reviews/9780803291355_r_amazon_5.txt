I have a vacation home on Donner Summit where much of this story takes place. As a professional mariner I have studied storms with great interest for the past thirty years. I have always been interested in hydrology. So this story hit home with me on many levels and I tore through it with great interest. Snow on Donner Summit is still measured in feet, not inches. The high water bypasses are still there doing their jobs. The central character in the book is the storm, and how us weak humans hope to cope. This book is not for everyone, but to those with an interest in meteorology and how things get done by people most do not think about, it is a great read.Read moreRead less
