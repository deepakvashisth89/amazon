The last thing Wicca needs associated with it are the words fun and trivia.  This book makes Wicca look less like a serious spiritual path and more like D & D without a dungeonmaster.  I'm really appalled at the number of people who do not see where a book like this is detrimental to Wicca, and I'm even more afraid that these people don't even care.  Technically, this book tests objective information on a very subjective matter.
