Billy and Belle is a beautifully written book. It shows one day in the life of an interracial family as they welcome a new member. There is burnt toast, a barking dog, a little girl that doesn't want to hold still, and a little boy that wonders at the mystery of a hamster that sleeps all day and who the new person is that will be joining them soon. Sarah Garland created a wonderful story that mimics real life and holds it out for us all to observe the beauty that exists all around us.
