I read this book in a handful of days, careering through its chapters without heed to the deliciously confusing bewitchment it was working upon me. Webster Wilde evokes intricately interwoven narrative planes, ranging from fabulous mythic mists and visionary vistas, via the emotionality and sexuality of relationship, to down-at-heel realms of divorce and depression within a disorientingly collapsed time structure (did a month pass? A year, perhaps?) and sets the whole dancing in the play of magical numbers, living geometries of story, people and place. I was - I am - caught inextricably in her web. And at once know that I am at the very centre of my own...
