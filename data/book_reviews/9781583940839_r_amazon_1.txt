I just could not put this book down! I'm a serious metaphysical and spiritual student and have been reading hundreds of books in this category for 25+ years. Rarely do I come across books that expand my mind and teach me so much about myself. Most metaphysical-related books tend to be stale, recycling the same old information in new packaging, thus making it hard to find fresh and new information to read and learn from. So whenever I read something that feels "fresh", it makes me happy. This book truly delivered! Just learning all about Ra and his contribution to the history of humanity just expanded my mind. I knew intuitively this information was genuine.I also saw parallels between David Wilcock's spiritual journey which started in his childhood and my life. I saw a lot of similarities between our psychic and spiritual experiences which helped me understand myself better. For example, the only way I could communicate and receive messages from the higher plane was to lay down on my side. I could not do this while sitting up nor laying on my back. I had to be on my side, then I could "connect" and begin to receive those messages in that stage between waking and dreaming. I thought maybe I was wrong to be in that position, but it worked for me. I never knew anybody else that used this particular position. Well it turns out that David does the exact same thing. He too has to be laying on his side in order to receive his channellings. How intriguing!The author writes this book so well and does a good job of keeping me captivated. He has done extensive research and really knows what he talks about. He presents very interesting findings on the relationship between Edgar Cayce, David Wilcock, and Ra. To me, it does not matter whether it's true or not. What it has taught me is that we each all are so much more than just this physical body we reside in. There are always common threads between all of us. This is a story that has so much to teach us about why we all are here, and what our purpose in life is.Reading this book, has introduced me to "Ra, The Law of One" which I plan to read eagerly next. I also barely spent time reading and learning about Edgar Cayce's life previously. I don't know why I never did despite having a love for this topic. It was probably not the right time for me. Now, I am even more convinced to devour more of the spiritual knowledge that Edgar Cayce brought to the world during his life. I am glad to be able to approach it with no lingering doubt as doubts can hinder growth and understanding.
