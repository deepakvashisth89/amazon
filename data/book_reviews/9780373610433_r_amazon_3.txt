mack bolan the executioner returns to Vietnam in this book to rescue an American held in a maximum security prison after the Vietnam war. How he breaks him out is told  in an action packed 4 hours (179 pages.) With the help of local montagards and an American pilot, mack bolan as Colonel Phoenix takes care of business. This is a well written action packed story which was published in 1982. I  couldn't help thinking of Stallone as Rambo or Chuck Norris rescuing POWs in a movie as I read this.
