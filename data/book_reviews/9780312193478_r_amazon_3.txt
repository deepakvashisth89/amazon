I picked up this book thinking that I would be reading about recent sightings of UFOs and the ongoing investigations.  Unfortunately, that is most certainly not the case.  The only recent events talked about are the movie Independence Day and the joint promotion they did with Nevada.Susan Wright has merely collected many well-known people, investigations, theories, and events from the past and mentioned them all here.  There are many other books that give this to you, so this one does not help.  Also, the narrative seems to skip from point to point without a logical order.If you know absolutely nothing about UFOs, their investigations, and conspiracy theories to cover them up, then you might read this to become familiar with the people and places.
