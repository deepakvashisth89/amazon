Mr. Arthur Waley has proven that a good work can stand the test of time.  First published almost 60 years ago this work does a wonderful jobof explianing the differences between various schools of thought not just in China but in the rest of Asia.The differences between the teachings of Chuang Tzu, Mencius and The realists may not seem like much to us westerners but these three groups have all done a lot to shape Asia.The thing I like most about this book is that it is short and broken up into three parts.  Therefore your mind doesn't do a blowout trying to digest all the material and you can study each school indivudally.Overall-Great book, most of the stories are very deep and will hold some meaning for everyone if you are just willing to listen.Read moreRead less
