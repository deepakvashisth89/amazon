A number of reviewers here have objected to the classification of the images in 'Femalia' as art, on the grounds that they fail to measure up to their aesthetic ideals.  But art, as any public gallery worth the name will attest, is far more than mere pretty pictures.True Art challenges one's worldview.  And 'Femalia' certainly does that.  It is a brave and lonely voice challenging the contemporary view of the female body, so overwhelmingly submerged in fantasy and falsification.This is an important work.
