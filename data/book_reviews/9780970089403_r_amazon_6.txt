I purchased this book for the information. It was a very good in-depth study on the Zohar. I knew very little on the subject when I first started reading.  When I finished the last chapter I felt confident that I could give a good synopsis of the meaning of Zohar. I did notice that as the book progressed it seemed to get more difficult to understand. I had to keep going back and rereading parts of the book to comprehend it all. This is unusual for me. As a rule I don't have that kind of trouble keeping the flow of the book together, Maybe it was because I am not Jewish and not just the main subject was foreign to me but the entirety of the book seemed foreign .
