John Kennedy Melling is a remarkable Englishman. He dresses with bowler hat and umbrella, and wears a monocle. The BBC calls on him whenever they need a stereotype traditional Englishman. A reviewer of John's books once wrote: "John Kennedy Melling evidently thinks he's a character in a PG Wodehouse book." John said afterwards that it was the greatest compliment he'd ever had.
