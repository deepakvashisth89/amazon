Like others of Zahn’s books, the author likes to lead the readers by the nose through a series of twists and turns, keeping the reader in the dark as to what his characters are thinking until there is some reveal after the fact. He uses this trick several times throughout this book... our protagonist has some a-ha moment and formulates a desperate plan, but leaves the reader guessing until after it’s over. Possibly this is meant to substitute for actual suspense, but not really.The plot is also kind of lazy. Hard to really delve into without spoilers, but I’m at a loss for how the aliens who don’t have much of a technological society manage to make observations and manipulate things out in space- within their solar system as well as light years distant. You also have to assume psychic powers or some other kind of space magic on behalf of the aliens, but none of it is even given some pseudo-mystical explanation... no Force, no midichlorians, etc. The aliens are capable of playing a long game with years of planning and devious manipulations... but they are also incredibly stupid, and are easily outwitted by our protagonist, and manipulated or goaded into showing their hand on multiple occasions.
