I just finished reading Ghost In My Soup. I read it many many years ago when I was in elementary school. I thought about the book today for some reason, and wondered if they had it for my kindle. To my surprise they did, and I decided to download it. It’s a kids book, and only 100 pages so it is a really fast read.I couldn’t remember anything about the plot of the book. I had to read it in school, and do a book report on it. I remember thinking it was an extremely boring book that I was forced to read for school when I was a kid.I actually enjoyed the book much more as an adult strangely enough than when I was a kid. I guess because I could relate to the events in the book better with 40 years of life experience under my belt.The book is about dealing with big life changes as a child. In the book the big change is the family moves to a new town. Which I can totally relate to how hard this can be. As my parents up rooted me at age 15, and moved across the country.It’s not a bad story, and it’s easy to get through. However it is written with a child reader in mind.
