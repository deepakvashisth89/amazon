The Deerslayer is a classic book that I have read several times.  I like reading about an honest character with real integrity. It disappoints me that he can't put aside Judith's history and judge her by her present intentions.  I guess either the pre revolutionary culture Cooper was writing about or his own nineteenth century culture made it necessary.  Hawkeye's attitude about innate 'gifts' also disturbs me.  But the story is gripping and I love it. I read the Kindle edition this time and detected five typos.
