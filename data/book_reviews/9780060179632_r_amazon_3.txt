GREAT plot twists and everything ties together. Wolf's amnesia after his first wife's death was real, his second bout was contrived by his psychiatrist with the help of Julia. This wasn't fully explained, but this made the book more enjoyable; there should be a little something you get to decipher on your own.  I've read all the Barrington books, but I found this to be superior to any of those, which points to a deficiency in how I budget my reading time. However, I found this book to be well worth the time I invested and I recommend it to anyone losing faith in the work of Mr. Woods.Read moreRead less
