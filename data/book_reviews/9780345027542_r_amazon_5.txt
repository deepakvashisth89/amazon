I bought this book with high expectations due to the author. However, it did not meet my expectations at all. Most stories even though short were still very slow. It was also very clear this book was written in 1940s and not because of the technology. The writing was a bit antiquated (not in a good way). If you are die-hard fan of Clarke, you'll probably like this, but I wouldn't recommend it to anyone who isn't.Read moreRead less
