The Holocaust Chronicle is ideal for anyone interested in seeing the progression, in concise, easy-to-follow terms, of a war within a war - the murder of millions of people deemed enemies of the Nazi state. This book does not dwell upon the horrors of the concentration camps and the twisted minds of those Nazis who planned, executed and relished in the murder and torture of human beings. For that kind of information, it is necessary to find other resources such as the works of Primo Levi, Jean Amery or to watch the newly released DVD on Auschwitz. However, it is clear from this book that the Nazi regime was not only about the systematic murder of the Jewish people, but also the effects of depraved thinking and out of control hate that led to the murder of Russians, Poles, monks, priests, political moderates, humanists, resistance fighters from every nation, communists, etc. as well as to the destruction of lifestyles and economies of all of Europe.
