What a wonderful resource!  Lyrics and chords to 1,200 songs. I have a mountain of music and books and homemade notebooks --and now digital pages, too-- that together don't have as many favorite, familiar tunes. Cuts way down on what I have to haul and makes things much easier to find. This has everything from spirituals to Broadway tunes, all grouped by themes. As you can see from the preview material this isn't exactly a fakebook; some reviewers have said the musical notation, such as it is, is indecipherable. The introductory material explains it quite clearly and I have no trouble following it, although it might be challenging for novice players.  Note to ukulele players: The keys chosen work well on guitar (tuned to key of E so lots of Es and Ebs and whatnot), not always so easy for uke players. I do find myself transposing on the fly quite a bit, so if you don't already have that skill it might be worth developing. Easy enough with this book since they tend to keep the musical arrangements clean and simple. This is a fabulous resource and well worth the under-$20 price.Read moreRead less
