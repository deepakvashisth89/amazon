First of all disclaimer: I am a huge Vonnegut fan. Having said that, if you have not read any of his stuff or have and didn't like it, I would not recommend "Deadeye Dick". This is strictly for people obsessed with reading everything KV ever wrote (i.e. me). It is crazy dark and depressing, more so than his other works.
