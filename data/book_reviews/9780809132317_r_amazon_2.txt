This is a very concise and helpful introduction to Canon Law.  It is not easy to find many introductory books in English.  This is probably, as far as American publications go, the best one out there.It is an easy read and it would also be worth while to keep this book on one's shelf for reference.  Considering the depth of the Code of Canon Law, Father Coriden was always to the point and quite balanced.  Here and there you can perceive some of the ecclesiastical laws or matters that he takes issue with as well as a little bit of extra emphasis on the sections of the Code that deal with a subject that he is quite interested in: the various rights of the faithful. He has another book on this subject and is intent on emphasizing and writing about what he terms a "bill of rights" for the baptized.  He also does things such as go out of his way only to refer to priests as "presbyters".But again, for a handy introduction to Canon Law, this book has to be one of the best there is out there and I cannot imagine anyone who purchases it not being satisfied.Read moreRead less
