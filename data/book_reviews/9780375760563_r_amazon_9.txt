I love the way Robert Farrar Capon writes - he can take an ordinary subject like 'onions' and write 7 pages on how amazing they are and keep your attention all the way. In this book, Capon tells how to cook a leg of lamb for 8 persons 4 times with many digressions on onions, knife sharpening, wine, selecting guests, the creativity of God, and more. The culinary reflection (subtitle) goes on for 190 fascinating pages.  At the end of the book are recipes (80 pages) in a recognizable format for the full 4 meals.
