It is very difficult to find novels that have Cambodia as the setting other than stuff like The Killing Fields.  This wonderfully talented author has written a story that is not only engaging, it is a profound insight into the culture and history of Cambodia.  It is earthy, it is gritty, it is unsparing in its depictions and revelations.  It takes you on a painful, gut-wrenching journey that raises questions about how these things are possible.  It answers many questions that I had, and includes historical facts that I did not previously have.  It is eye-opening and informative.  It is a "tour de force" for Chanrithy Him.Read moreRead less
