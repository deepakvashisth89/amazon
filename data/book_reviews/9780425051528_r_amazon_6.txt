The first part of the book starts out very slowly- a lot of prose in the beginning to set scenes, leading to a lot of narration and not much action.This changes once you get to the part where Del and Tom visit Del's uncle Coleman and they enter the magician's world, Shadowland. For a contemporary fantasy/horror novel you get sucked in around this part and the vivid descriptions of illusions, spell casting, a girl who exists (or does she?), and a plot which, while a bit repetitive at times, is well-crafted.The only thing holding this book back is the opening part which I felt didn't get us to Coleman's Shadowland mansion fast enough. Overall this book comes highly recommended. I look forward to seeing Shadowland on NBC when the show comes out.Read moreRead less
