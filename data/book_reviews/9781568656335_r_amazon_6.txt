Ann Rule has a way of bringing you into the story like you are sitting right there, witnessing the events she is describing. She gets all of the details correct and really brings the whole story together. She starts with the beginning of it all, slowly draws you in as the story progresses, and has you on the end of your seat waiting for the finale. I have loved every book I have read by her, which is at least a dozen. Perfect author for any true crime junkie.Read moreRead less
