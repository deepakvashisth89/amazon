The Matthew Hope series is every bit as fresh and engaging as the 87th Precinct series. I've tried to formalistically analyze McBain's writing, and I could never figure out what made him so much better than everybody else in the crime genre. Then one day, I realized he writes like your favorite uncle telling you a story--with subtle "asides" and wry commentary along the way.
