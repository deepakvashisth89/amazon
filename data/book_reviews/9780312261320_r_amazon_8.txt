A new view of dolls, a new view of the culture of Jehovah's Witnesses, and a new view of bagpipes.  This story had me feeling right from the beginning, and didn't let me stop until the last page.  Cynthia Thayer's writing is clear, emotional and eloquent throughout.  The title was worked into the story, but not so much that you were hit over the head with it.
