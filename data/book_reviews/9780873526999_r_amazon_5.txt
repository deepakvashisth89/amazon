When I picked this book up I was simply looking for ways to cite internet resources.  It turns out the people at MLA have got a lot more to offer.  The book did a great job at showing the hows and whys of writing well.  As  for citations, a critical aspect of most research, the Guide gave a  multitude of different types of sources and how to cite them, not only in  the Works Cited but also in endnotes and footnotes.  Perhaps my only  complaint would be the repetition in seperate chapters on citation.  It was  not initially clear what the purpose of the repetition was.
