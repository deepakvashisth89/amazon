I have long wanted to know the "secret" of reading Chinese characters, and they make up a significant part of the Japanese writing system.  They just seem to have too many lines!  This book looks at how the characters are devised.  They are based on common images (for example, a person leaning against a tree is "restful").  Being able to see the images behind the characters is very helpful.  It gives you something that is easier to identify with.  I'd highly recommend this book.Read moreRead less
