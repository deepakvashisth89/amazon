For a mass market paperback, treated as little more than an add-on, this book is rather exceptional. It's excellently well-written - and presents the story of "Terminator 2" is great detail. The author, Randall Frakes, has also written a novelisation of the first "Terminator" film (jointly written with Bill Wisher) - and that too is excellent sci-fi. Had these books been published without the films being made, they'd probably be considered contemporary classics of the genre - as they're both fantastic. Yet most people view 'novelisations' with disdain - which is unfortunate.What Frakes does is elaborate in detail with regard to those aspects of the story that really matter. He contributes to the material on which it's based, adding content that takes the reader deeper into the fictional universe of the Terminator saga. So, for instance, we read about the way the T-1000 'thinks' about the world. As the novel was based on an early draft of the screenplay, it does differ from the film. The book, for example, offers a couple of chapters exploring the Future War - concerning John Connor's victory against Skynet. What's great is that it's obvious the film-makers behind "Terminator Genisys" have retuned to sources such as this novel in order to capture the detail, feel and intention behind Cameron's classic T2 movie.If you're a fan of the Terminator saga, I fully recommend this book. If you're unfamiliar with this saga, I suggest watching the original movie - starring Schwarzenegger - alongside reading the Frakes & Wisher novel. Then move on to this book.
