If you purchase the Wrong Murder, you must purchase the Right Murder.  Both books are fantastic!  Actually, I have purchased the entire collection of The John J. Malone, Helene and Jake Justus novels by Ms. Craig Rice.  The plots are intriguing and the characters are unique and funny.  The lawyer, Malone, who knows everyone in Chicago, always finds the solution to the crimes, even without sleep, no funds, and too much to drink.  Helene, a wealthy debutant, and Jake, her husband, drink with Malone and help him with his cases, but seem to land in jail for various reasons.  All of Ms. Rice's books are worth every penny you spend on them, beginning with the novel, 8 Faces at 3 - and they just get better with every novel that follows!
