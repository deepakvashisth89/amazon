I loved this book and it kept me guessing right up to the very end. Eliot takes us to rural England in the years around 1800 and we come to know interesting and very different people, including Dinah, a devout and powerful early Methodist preacher. Adam, of course, is the main character and you cannot help but admire and love him. Then there is Hetty, incredibly beautiful,t he woman Adam loves. The story unfolds with twists and turns and surprises, though some are foreshadowed, as is Eliot's way.
