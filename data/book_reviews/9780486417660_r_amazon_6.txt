My son and I adore the Oz books. This book is the first written by someone other than Baum himself.I am aware that it was written in 1920-1921. As I read this to my son I constantly find myself stopping and explaining how the awful descriptions and illustrations are wrong. The people of the Silver Island are drawn like old-time, racist caricatures of Chinese people with exaggerated slanted eyes, long braids, and clumsy mannerisms. It even says that they are related to Chinese in the book. The way the book talks about the people's culture and food and traditions was wrong then and it is wrong now, but back then it was simply accepted. It has given me a good opportunity to tell my son how people used to think that if people were different then it was wrong. He even knows now that all cultures have thought this way in the past sometime or another, and now we do our best to understand rather than insult.If you are prepared to explain this part to your kid, then your kid can overlook this part of the book and enjoy the adventure story itself.
