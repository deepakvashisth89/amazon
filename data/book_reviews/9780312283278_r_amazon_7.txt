I had read many of Tom Perrotta newer books and to my friends I was referring to him as the "Christian Jodi Picoult."  I thought that was all he wrote about--books with Christian themes. Then I started reading some of his earlier books including this one--Joe College-and realized this must be a newer thing with him.  Joe College made it to one of the earlier NY Times Notable Books lists in the early 2000s and is a pretty good but not remarkable read.  The book is about a young man named Danny who is an undergraduate in Yale who by his own admission is as surprised to be there as anyone else.  He comes from a decidedly middle to lower class family and spends his summers and breaks from college driving the Roach Coach which is his father's lunch truck.  He is what you might call an aspiring lover--although he has had moderate success back home with a girl named Cindy and aspires to Polly who is at Yale but already taken by an older professor.  He has a group of friends who are all fairly oddball in nature and one of his biggest accomplishments outside of school is standing up to the Italian goons who threaten his father's business on a daily basis. I enjoyed the book but not as much as some of his most recent books like Little Children which I thought was quite good and The Leftovers.
