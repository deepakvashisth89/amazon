I love a Western. I love the history of making this country and so much of the excitement seemed to be out west. This story brought in hints of the strife of the Chinese workers and bigotry, taking down the bad guys and overcoming hardship to make life the best you can. It was all done very smoothly and without too much detail to pull you down.I love the strong women in the story from our main character who uses her voice and beauty to sing her way into the dreams of the men and tease them with her mystery, to the younger sister who is in love with a man out of her reach, or is he? The oriental woman who raised them and the English Aunt who took over raising them and polished their rough edges Last but not least is the woman who helped to raise our love interest, a strong no nonsense women with a loving heart and a woman I would love to have as a friend. They all have a great impact on Delilah/Lila and the woman she is trying to become. Great characters!!It is a great journey to take. I loved the story, but as with the other reviews, the ending left me wanting. It was a good ending, don't get me wrong...I just felt it needed one more twist to tie it up.It was the perfect book to curl up with if front of the fire and indulge in after the Christmas Holiday and I would recommend it to anyone who loves American History, strong woman and a good romance.
