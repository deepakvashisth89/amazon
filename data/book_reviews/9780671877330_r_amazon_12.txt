This is one of the best books I have read in a long time. But Damon if it is not difficult to describe. Ok, as far as I can see it this is a book about PTSD (post traumatic stress disorder) and it is defiantly a story about redemption. The author is clever with a circular story line that really hooks you to the characters and gives you a stake in there storyline.
