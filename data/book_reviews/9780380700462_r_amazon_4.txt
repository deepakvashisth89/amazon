This is one of Mr Llosa's earlier books, written in a very different style from his later books. While the tone of the narrative is sparkling and comical at times, the book unravels the complex relationships between various countries in South America and puts together a complex mosaic, all through one central figure - the host of a story telling radio show. It is a profound analysis of the human character and history of a continent, written in the unmistakeable style of a true literary titan.Read moreRead less
