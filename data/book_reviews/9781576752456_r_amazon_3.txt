I didn't have to read this book to be sold on the concept. I bought it so that I could have more facts when people asked me questions. What I gained from reading these many essays is more than simple facts. I gained a deeper understanding of how pervasive both the causes and effects are of time poverty in America. I have had no choice but to look at my own life to see what I can do differently. And I am even more certain that what the Take Back Your Time Day organizers have been doing is of immediate and necessary importance.Why should you read this book?
