I couldn't make myself finish this book. I found I didn't like or care about the characters. Settle packs far too many characters early in the book and the use of omniscience means you are head-hopping among all of them, never getting a grip on any of them. Her use of Turkish and obscure terms made it difficult to comprehend. Google couldn't produce definitions for half of them.
