This is not a review of Bleak House as a novel. Charles Dickens was an absolute genius and I love everything I’ve read of his so far. The Oxford World’s Classics paperback edition was fine except that instead of the header containing chapter titles, both headers simply say “Bleak House”. I am definitely one to appreciate seeing the chapter title in the header. Additionally, the print was a bit too small for me. I usually go for Penguin Classics, but I had read that Oxford World’s was superior. I disagree, and I’m definitely just going to go with Penguin from now on.
