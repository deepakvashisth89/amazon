When lance Harmon was 15 he’d already seen more than his share of sorrows. He was orphaned at a young age, lived on the streets till was taken on the orphans train. He was 15 , nobody wanted him till 1 man came to him, offered him a home, food to eat and a promise if he worked hard he’d always have a home. What lance didn’t know was how he could find the girl he would love at 15 , but he knew the moment he saw Sonnie Mancuso he’d never love another.Sonnie Mancuso has tried her whole life to make her father realize she could run the ranch as good as any man. No she wasn’t the son her father always prayed for but she was the youngest of six girls and the only one who loved the ranch as much as he did. After Sonnie’s mother past her father shipped her away to her Aunts to go to the best schools for women, to become a lady . Sonnie swore one day she’d be back to run the ranch no matter what her father wanted.Sonnie came back 10 years later after her father suffered a heart attack . Lance knew she’d be beautiful but Sonnie was stunning. How was he going to hide his feelings plus protect her during a range war that could cost them everything.
