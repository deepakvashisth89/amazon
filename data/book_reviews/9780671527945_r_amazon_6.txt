Boy! Are the characters developed - and developed -and developed.  The author writes well and weaves an interesting story, but you have to wade through three persons' view of events to get on with the story.  Adding to the book's burdensome pages is the fact that this is number 2 of a trilogy.  Not having read #1 I was kind of left out of what were obviously major events that had already happened.  Cornelius, Sam, Scott, Teresa, Alicia, Emily, Vicky, etc;  these characters are just great with justification and making their actions plausible, at least to themselves.  Somewhere in the book Cornelius says something like 'if you cannot deal with an unfortunate event after 16 years there is something out of kilter in your thinking".  AMEN.There is some redemption in that I found the setting and timeframe to my liking, although it is hard for me to either understand or relate to persons of this enormous wealth.
