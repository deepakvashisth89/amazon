I've read SF for a long time and read many variations on humanity vs. AI.  This is one I'm embarrassed to admit I missed when it first came out.  Finally, I just read it, and absolutely, totally enjoyed it.It's a different style of writing than we are used to in the 21st century, or even from the end of the 20th.  It's spare, it's internal, and it's emotional.  It is, however, the kind of story that keeps your mind involved.  In much SF I'm just reading and watching, not really analyzing, not thinking ahead, not wondering what's next, just riding the wave.  In this story, I was caught off-guard, I was trying to think alongside the protagonist, and I was truly surprised at times by what the author presented.  I believed and sympathized with the character's angst about his evolving relationship because I have known those doubts.  Gerrold took some very real, human experiences and pulled them into a beautifully startling story.I'm just disappointed in myself... that I didn't give his writing more of a chance before now.
