As a spiritual seeker, I have read numerous books on reincarnation and karma. "Messages from Michael" and the other three Michael books by comparison provide comprehensive teachings on the soul's evolutionary process through five successive cycles (Infant Soul, Baby Soul, Young Soul, Mature Soul, Old Soul). The innocent Infant Soul experiences a number of primitive human lives before completing this cycle. Then it moves on to the Baby Soul cycle and loses its innocence in the process. At the Young Soul cycle, the incarnate soul immerses itself in materialism. All this is part of the plan. The Mature Soul has learned from its mistakes and gained some understanding. The Old Soul is more and more enlightened. The Michael entity teaches that there are no bad souls, only young souls. The evolutionary system appears complicated at first but once you have become acquainted with the particular terminology employed by the Michael entity and the meaning it conveys, it gets easier.
