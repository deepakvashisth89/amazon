Living in Cornwall in an area where mining was carried out 150 years ago this story was a must for me. A stark reminder of the class differences that were evident then. Life was tough and the author highlights how hard it was for the working class especially when tin prices dropped  mines were forced to close and many Cornish miners went to Australia to seek a new life.
