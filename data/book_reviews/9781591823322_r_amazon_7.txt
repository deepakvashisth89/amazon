This was my first manga series that I've ever read. My friend introduced me to it and I totally fell in love with it. I own the whole series and I re-read it every chance I get. At first I didn't think I'd be into the whole gay relationship, guy X guy not really my thing. Then I read Fake, vol. 1 and I completely fell in love with Dee and Ryo. I read the first one in the bookstore and immeadiately had to buy that and 2 and 3 as well. Seeing Dee and Ryo fall in love was so heart warming, and sweet, especially during the hard times when learning about Ryo's past and overcoming obsticles to that cliche but totally relevant statement that "love conquers all". Other than that the kissing scenes and the *ahem* "special" scene at the very end was totally hot. (I must say, on a lighter note it's about time that Ryo put out. If I were Dee I'd jump him too! He's been chasing him for a while, it's only fair especially after what he put Dee through...) The ending was kind of abrupt and for all you Carol and Bikky fans out there, they hardly made an appearance except for on one page. It ended sweetly. But I, for one, think Sanami should make more.
