This is one of my favorite Gail Carson Levine Books, which is surprisinflg because I usually prefer more of her fantasy work and this one is only fiction. The plot is amazing and throughout the story you find yourself going through the same things that Dave is. The author makes you long to be there, fighting along side him, as he struggles at the HHB and dances with Irma Lee. I would recommend this book for all tweens and teens, but the adults might like it too.Read moreRead less
