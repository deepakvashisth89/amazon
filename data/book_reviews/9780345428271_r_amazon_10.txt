I have read most of Frey's novels now and there has been, regrettably, a deterioration in quality.  Still, it must be hard to come up with new ideas all the time and we have seen other authors battle with the same problem.This book has plenty of pace, but the plot is reasonably shallow despite an attempt to make it seem pretty deep by introducing an unexpected element.  There were also too many unbelievable elements/conveniences in the story-line.Overall, not a bad effort and the reader is able to align with the "hero" pretty early and enjoy his race against time.
