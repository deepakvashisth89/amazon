This book was written over a decade ago. I don't know what took me so long to buy and read this book, being that I am a huge Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson fan. It was nice to see the evolution of a super star! He came from humble beginnings and as I've followed his career can see he's kept his humble nature. For any WWE or Rock fan, this book is a great read! You will enjoy rooting for this talented man and find yourself laughing out loud at certain parts in the book. It's an easy read and I appreciated the honesty of it all. There was no candy coating the nature of the sports entertainment business.
