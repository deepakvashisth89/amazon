"The Shattered Eye" is my least favorite November Man novel.That being the case, it is still a very good novel. I simply had a hard time figuratively swallowing some of the plot details. I.e. my suspension of disbelief was not up to the task.That isn't to say it was unbelievable. For me, when fiction meets fact, as in plots to assassinate real heads of state, rather then fictitious heads of state, I guess I'm not up to the task of buying it hook line and sinker.It was still a very enjoyable read.
