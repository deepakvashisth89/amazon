An old classic.  Nice to find the book again!  One of the best, but like the OLD "International" book the best. You don't need to be doing WW to use this book and find it helpful. Recipes are clear and simple. I skip the use of margarine and use butter, skip non-natural substitutes people used to use. Many recipes work well as  paleo or Mediterranean diet if you use extra veggies and real dairy and skip some of the starchier and low-fat stuff.  Use whole dairy instead.
