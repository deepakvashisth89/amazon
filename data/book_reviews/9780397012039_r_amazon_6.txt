What can be said about MacDonald that hasn't been said? He was a superb writer as this book demonstrates.  Although it is a bit dated in today's world (financial manipulators have come up with a few new twists to cheat the public) it hits right at the heart of greed and criminal financers abetted by crooked builders. MacDonald has done his research and if there is any fault in this book is the immense detail of  financial flim flam.  The characters are well defined although almost more numerous than the average reader can follow.  Nevertheless this is a very fine book by a very fine author.
