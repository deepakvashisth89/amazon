Pressed for time, I usually read only subjects of interest to me and my writing. Erotica has never been one of them. However, having experienced M.J. Rose's superior writing prior to discovering Lip Service, I was tempted to at least give it a go. Not only did I complete the first chapter, I couldn't put it down after. In the past, a few other books with this subject had no storyline along with the sex, and I passed. Rose's book not only has erotica, but wonderful fully-developed characters I had strong feelings towards. Her plot builds slowly leaving the reader in suspense of guessing when the shocker is coming and it does. The icing on the cake is her writing technique. When a book keeps one's attention, the reader is totally immersed and not aware of the writing process. One realizes the difference between 'showing' and 'telling' by the memories one carries after closing the book. Great read I would highly recommend.Read moreRead less
