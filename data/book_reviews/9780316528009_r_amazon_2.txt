I read this book as a child and am currently doing a project of re-reading books I enjoyed from childhood.This book is written for kids in elementary school. It is about Sally, a girl with cerebral palsy and uses crutches. Sally starts a regular school and initially has trouble making friends. She is less anxious once she gets a puppy, and her friends decide to train their dogs together.Sally makes friends and even ends up helping one friend’s older brother overcome his own social issues.Read moreRead less
