This is a great, encyclopedic review of more cartridges than any one shooter is ever likely to see, let alone shoot.  Maybe Mr Barnes missed some cartridges, but I think he hit everything important and/or interesting.  But why not use scale drawings?  It would have taken little more effort to make all the technical drawings life-size, and it would have increased the usefulness of the book greatly.  As it is, comparisons between cartridges require a scale and some math, which is a nuisance.  Granted, a few of these rounds would need more space on the page, but not many of them.  A 50BMG SHOULD have a bigger entry than a 30-30, but instead it's shown reduced.  But this is a technical complaint about a very excellent encycopedia.
