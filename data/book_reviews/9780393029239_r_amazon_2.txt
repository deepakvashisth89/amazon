Thinking Strategically is one of the first books I ever read on strategy.  Since I read it it has changed my perspective and way of thinking and analyzing all sorts of problems from business to personal life.  The book in a sense has become a manual of problem assessment in my life I have used its concepts in situations where I have to predict rivals moves in business, work and personal life.  Also its concepts help you in situations where you have to bargain or assess opportunities and disadvantages in purchases, investments or even in choosing your partner.  Thinking Strategically is a rational approach to decision making that can reduce the stress and anxiety of uncertainty and will give you better control in life.  In the end outcomes in life are statistical and through good strategy you have a more dominant position reducing risk and loses.The only thing missing in the book might be Harvard's strategy expert Michael Porter, who is one of the most important proponents on business strategy with his theory of the 5 forces.  However Porter's approach is eminently business oriented and it doesn't have much applicability in other types of non-business scenarios.Thinking Strategically is more a general overview of the most fundamental concepts and theories in strategy and definitely a must read for those interested in understanding more about decision making.
