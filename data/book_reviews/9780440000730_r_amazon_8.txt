Hillary Knight's delightful black and white sketches are the perfect accompaniment to this frolicsome cat tale.  Rather hard to follow at first and with a vocabulary pitched considerably above the grade-level of the story line, this book is a suitable read aloud for upper elementary to middle school listeners.  The plot is simple, but told from the cat Hamlet's point of view, it becomes both mysterious and convoluted enough to be interesting to listeners.  The elevated vocabulary presents an excellent opportunity to introduce learns to some new and useful words such as "ensconced" and "lanky" and "sonority,"  It is difficult not to admire Hamlet and to enjoy his antics.Read moreRead less
