We love the Great Illustrated Classics books! They work beautifully for reluctant readers because even though they retell the classic story (pretty accurately), they have illustrations to help maintain interest. The illustrations are not overly detailed so the reader remains engaged but are also not too simplistic. While the storyline is pretty close, there are some formatting issues that should have been edited out prior to release.
