I would love to be able to give this book more stars, it had a lot going for it.  The writing was very descriptive and the author certainly has a way with words, however, what happened was that they got so lost in descriptive imagery that the plot ended up getting a bit lost.  What held my interest for the first half of the book was the very same thing that made me start to lose interest.  At least 100 pages could have been cut without changing the plot or our understanding of the characters at all.  Overall, a decent read but not a quick read.Read moreRead less
