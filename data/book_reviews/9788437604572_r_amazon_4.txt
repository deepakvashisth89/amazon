There are two ways of reading this book and I have read it both ways.  It's a postmodern novel, heavy on philosophy so not for everybody but I find it endlessly fascinating.  I would recommend the Kindle edition because when it skips around the Kindle takes you there with a tap on the screen.  Also, the book references many artists, writers, philosophers, places, etc., so it is nice to have the ease of going to Wikipedia.  Plus you have the Spanish dictionary and the language translator.
