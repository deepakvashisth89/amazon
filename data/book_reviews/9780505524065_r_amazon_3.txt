Not going to give you spoilers.  Being a writer myself I know Cassie Edwards would not appreciate what I'm about to write...  In general her story plots are no worse or better than any I've read, but some of the drama in the story gets to a point where it seems silly.  This book is fairly good, is easy to read, and I did enjoy it but felt it could have been written better.  I've found there are less and less Native American romance novels being wrote these days, and the new ones I've read, especially hers, aren't very good.  Don't know why.  I have found some of her stories to be too realistic for my taste, such as this one.  I would have to say overall, if your looking for a halfway decent romance this book is worth giving a shot.Read moreRead less
