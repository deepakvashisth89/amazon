This is a parents' love story - both for their children and for each other.  This is the third copy I purchased (one for myself, one for a friend, one for a lesbian couple).  The two men want to be parents in the purest sense.  They meet adversity due to their being a gay couple, and they also meet some very kind people who help them.  The story isn't as much about being gay as it is about adoption.  It's well written, as if the author is a friend telling me about this experience.  The book gives a peaceful yet convincing voice to gay and lesbian interests; and in the process does not lose its appeal and relevance for people such as myself, a mother who is straight, and for parents/human beings in general.  I have to say - I love this book.Read moreRead less
