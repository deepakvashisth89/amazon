I have read quite a few Osho books this year, but this one is slightly different. In its tone, mostly, and I am presuming that that is because this was published early nineties and all the others I have read, from just the past several years.But also, the last chapter of this book is from a different place. It reads as though wherever and whenever these words were "taken" from, he was perhaps attempting to throw a little more caution to the wind than usual, and really lay it on the line in certain respects.And bearing in mind (no pun intended) that this book covers a lot of ground concerning death and dying, then for me at least that means that I was experiencing something even more profound than I have with the other books, a real sense that a new door was being edged open and some things were suddenly apparent, things that perhaps I was only now ready to be aware of.Things come to us when we are ready? Or "need" them? In this instance for me, yes. And where I found myself with this last chapter of this most surprising latest-for-me-Osho book, was a place I am grateful to be.Read moreRead less
