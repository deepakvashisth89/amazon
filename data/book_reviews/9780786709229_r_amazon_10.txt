I was disappointed with this book.  I have two others by Colin Wilson (Mammoth Book of True Crime 1 & 2) which are really informative and I assumed this would be much of the same.  However, it seems to be more a murder voyeur's read - lots of pictures with only a snatch of info on the subject.  There's no 'substance' to it at all and it gives me the impression of being just a quick, minimally-researched compilation designed to shock and bring in a few pennies.  If it's just gruesome snap-shots you're after then you'll enoy this book; if you want to learn anything from it - there's so many better books out there.Read moreRead less
