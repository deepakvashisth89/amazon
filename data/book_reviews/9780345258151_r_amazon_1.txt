A blast from the past! I've been re-reading a lot of childhood favorites-some might say it's a midlife crisis kind of thing:) Star Trek: The Animated Series was my favorite cartoon when I was six or seven; I couldn't wait for Saturday morning. Some of the imagery from this series has stuck with me through the years even though the stories had been forgotten. I remembered scenes from an episode called "The Jihad" (which is included in this book), but not the plot. In fact, the plot is somewhat nonsensical and the science improbable as many others have noted, though I think the book, and Alan Dean Foster's explanations of things which are not in the cartoon episode make it a little better. From my recent review of the TV series and the books, I think the premise of many episodes was bizarre and brain-washy for little kids (The Magicks of Megas-Tu most obviously-though not in this book).
