When the police investigate the murder of Marian Avery, everyone suspects the husband of the crime. Everyone except Detective Christine Boland.Overriding the objections of her colleagues, fracturing her friendships, Christine stakes everything, with fatal results.In this stunning work, William Pease provides strong dialogue, nice plot, and downright pizzazz. While his forte may be the law, being a former US attorney, he excels in the psychological. Even by the last page, the reader will be fumbling for answers.The Rage of Innocence is simply a superb work.Read moreRead less
