A fun story set in place I'm familiar with and love. Quirky characters and bizarre scenes make for some serious laughs. There's a bit of instruction about the basics of quantum physics...cool stuff. Loved the appreciation the author has for our four-legged best friends. His comment about all real banditos loving dogs was right on! The only down side for me was the constant drug abuse and mayhem scenes involving his pals Jim and Robert. I'm all for recreational fun but the long descriptions of each episode (and there were many) bit of  became redundant after awhile.
