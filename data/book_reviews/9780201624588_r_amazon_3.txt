One thing I really liked about this well written book was that both very conservative and very liberal readers could walk away with a sense of vindication.  It was balanced, fair reporting.  I worried, however, that an awful lot was said that was very personal.  I hope that all participants agreed.  I found it sadly amusing, however, that the protest that started all the ruckus was done by a man, in what is definitely a man's style (confrontational), over a woman's issue.  When, oh when, will they let us make our own decisions?  However, having said that, I found lots to agree with on both the liberal and conservation side of the issues.
