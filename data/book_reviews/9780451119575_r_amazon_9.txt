This is a timeless coming of age take with a twist.  It’s some years since I read a Beryl Bainbridge book and I’d forgotten what an excellent author she was. She has a keen eye for the minutiae of day to day life and elevates that to create interesting and plausible characters who become involved with the extraordinary.  This story involves the friendship, innocent but controlling, of two young girls as they while away the hours on the beach and area around Formby in post war Britain.  There’s a real sting in the tale and it’s quietly observed.  Brilliant writing and I’m now working through a number of Bainbridges novels.
