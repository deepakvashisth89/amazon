This collection of short pieces by William Maxwell features reminiscences, autobiographical short stories and fables mostly written for the New Yorker where he was an editor for many decades in the last century. They are gold, silver and wood, respectively. Focus on his reminiscing. They are 24k.He grew up in central Illinois in the early 20th century. Moved to Chicago for high school. Then attended the University of Illinois. Later, he moved to New York City where he became successful.50 years younger than Maxwell, I know quite a bit about downstate Illinois, Chicago and the University of Illinois.
