I came across this book as a teenage girl, probably around the mid-70's. This ranks as one of my favorite books, along with only a few others. It made an impression and I have thought back to the book again and again, for many different reasons throughout the years.  Being ageless as a woman was such a striking thought. Now that I've just turned 50, feeling nowhere near that age in terms of looks or outlook, I have been trying to hunt down a copy. I think it will be my Christmas present. I don't hang on to many material things, but this book will be with me the rest of my life.
