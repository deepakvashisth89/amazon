This book wasn't Dick Francis's best, but given how many absolutely phenomenal books he wrote, that doesn't mean it isn't an awesome book, especially for people who like planes. The main characters and their relationships are greatly created, Some of the subplots could have been better solved, but none of them are handled poorly. I really liked how the culprit (one of them, anyway) is revealed only about halfway through the book, and with some clever logic, but the motive remains obscure for a while afterwards.
