Personal Disclosure: The late Bill Corliss was a correspondent and friend for many years, starting in the 1970s. A fellow Marylander. I visited him at his Glen Arm home not far from my parents during return Stateside trips from my research and natural history advisory duties in Southwest Asia. Our correspondence and discussions were fascinating. I collected and now still posess copies of his entire several series, sets, reports, handbooks, and (most) newsletters on anomalies. Indeed, Bill used a couple of my own astronomical observations from California in one of his many publications (not this one, of course). I lost touch with him sometime before the turn of the century.This is my *3rd copy* of the Mysterious Universe handbook. My original set of Bill's books we're victims of the Islamic Revolution, in which I lost my entire personal library in the chaos. Bill *FREELY REPLACED* the plundered volumes of all his works at the time. Years later, unforeseen circumstances in Florida 15 years ago led to the loss of my 2nd copy of Mysterious Universe.Bear in mind that some material in this volume has been superceded by new observations with instruments and techniques not available - or even invented or devised - 40 years ago. Nevertheless, the overwhelming bulk of the obscure and perplexing reports Bill painstakingly unearthed and documented make for hours of entertaining and thoughtful study. I can't imagine the time and effort required for all his massive output.Now, I am ever so delighted and pleased to complete my Bill Corliss collection yet again!Read moreRead less
