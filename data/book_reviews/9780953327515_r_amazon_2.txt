Until I read this book, I had spent 10 years of my life building companies and writing code. I had followed a regimen of pure logic to make my decisions. And,m while it was working financially, it was killing me spiritually. Ian's radical actions and wild travel deeply inspired me. 10 years later, in a mirror image of his great pyramid shenanigan, I found myself under a full moon scaling the pyramid of Chichen Itza, buck naked and being chased by dogs and guards with spotlights. Life is fun. Ian's memoir casts light upon a rarely travelled path. And a crude methodology of intuitive decision making.
