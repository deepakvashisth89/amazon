Written in the 50's when girls didn't get involved in science much and any space pioneering was thought to be a man's world only, this book for all of that lack still is a good role model book for pre-order boys, and a fun read for those of us who never grew out of that wonder and excitement. Though I also am now and for a long time an author, books like this were my inspiration and the path to creativity. I met the author Bob Heinlein at a couple of science fiction conventions and found a modest wonderful man as well as storyteller, always willing to say "What If?" And mean it as you began seeing his Path and hoped one day, you two would meet on it and share an adventure. I highly recommend this book and author.Read moreRead less
