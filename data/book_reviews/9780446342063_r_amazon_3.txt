This is a collection of essays written by Andy Rooney for his spot on 60 Minutes, and his wit and sarcasm convey amazingly well.  Reading these essays, I often could hear Andy's voice.  Most of these essays were written in the 1970's, and some of them are quite dated.  However, most of them stand up to the test of time quite well, and still hit their mark.
