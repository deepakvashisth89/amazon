The concept of this book was interesting.  The Archangel Michael relays the coming of the second flood to Mr. White and his not-so-bright companions.  Mr. White sets out to build a 20th century Ark out of an overturned barn.While there are some good points to this book, they are quickly overshadowed by pages and pages and pages of lists.  The author goes on long winded tangents about what items are in a room, and what has been packed into the makeshift Ark.  People in one small town, thinking that Mr. White is some kind of savior, attempt to follow him in makeshift boats.  A 313 word sentence is used to describe the various people, their appearances, professions, and other stuff I could care less about.  By the time I got near the end of this book, I was just ready for it to be over.If there had been more periods in this book, and less commas, I would have liked it enough to give it 3 stars.Read moreRead less
