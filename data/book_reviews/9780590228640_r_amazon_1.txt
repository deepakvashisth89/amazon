This series of the Baby Sitters Club is pretty cool. I have a few books from this series, and I like Mary Anne's the best. In this book, it tells about Dawn's life before her parents got divorced. It tells about her meeting Sunny, her California best friend, winning an award for being prepared when the kitchen set on fire when she was on a baby sitting job, having adventures with her friends, and so much more.
