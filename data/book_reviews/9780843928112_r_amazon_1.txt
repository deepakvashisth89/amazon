I picked this book up at a used bookstore some years ago, and have since bought a copy from Amazon as well so I would have a second copy, just in case. I loved this book that much. It was so well written that normally I would ignore historical romance, but this kept me intrigued the whole time. It felt like two novels in one. A romance and western. While it was intense since the the heroine is his step-sister, don't let that get in the way, if you are squeamish about such things. I may go read it again as I am writing this review. I have no clue on her other works, as this is the only Catherine Hart book I have read.
