I really wanted to like this book. I really, really wanted to. However, the main character was flat, stupid, and just unlikable. The secondary characters were idiots. I do not recommend this book to anyone. The sequel might be better, but I doubt it.The main issue I have with this book is that the main character is a werewolf. She shows this fact to her best friend, and afterward, her best friend STILL DOES NOT BELIEVE HER. Other than that, the novel follows the same few plot points--the character goes night after night, turning into a werewolf, waking up with blood all over thinking 'oh no I killed someone' only to find that she did not, then she returns to her normal life as a dancer. The 'plot twist' of her boss being a werewolf too was not a twist--I saw it coming a mile away--and the author leaves the reader on a cliff hanger.Read moreRead less
