I’m relatively new to Letham, having read only Amnesia Moon and Gun, With Occasional Music. I liked those two books better, but this an interesting read. It’s sci-fi/speculative fiction about humans fleeing earth for the frontier on another planet. It’s also a tale of an adolescent coming into her own, a girl becoming a woman. Worth checking out if you like being taken on a trip to somewhere you’ll never go again when you read. The book is wholly unique, like the other two Letham novels I’ve read.Read moreRead less
