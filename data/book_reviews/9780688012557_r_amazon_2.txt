"Jane's House" is a touching story of a family's experience of loss. We follow the family from the moment of the wife's death thru their grief and struggle. Scattered throughout are humorous situations that ring so true and are so completely out of the depth of the husband/father, as he tries to become both mother and father to his children.Loving, tender, real.I've bought this book many times over to give to frienda and to re-read.
