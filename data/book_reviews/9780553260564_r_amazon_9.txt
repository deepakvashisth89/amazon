This is the second book by these authors that I have tried to read. I had high hopes, as they are known for pretty much defining the splatterpunk movement. Maybe their books are not for me, but I  found this one and The Scream dated (sometimes I like that - I am drawn to 70s and 80s horror novels, but not in this case) - the writing seemed overblown, as if it were trying to impress readers with how 'cool' and 'edgy' and 'deep' it is; the characters are lacking depth (they seemed interchangeable with the characters in The Scream); and the whole vibe was missing the tension I was hoping for.I thought it would be darker and grittier than it is - not gory (it's not, but there are some disturbing passages), but I had hoped to get a feel of the place and time.I wasn't taken with the story (I didn't realize there'd be an angel!) so maybe this all adds up to taste.Read moreRead less
