A funny look at the day to day madness that every single/widowed mother trying to cope with family, work and friends must undergo.As a guy, I am not sure I should even *have* an opinion about some of the things in here but I will safely say this:  "It's funny, it's warm, it's human and these are very likeable, very real people."  No talking dogs, no aliens living in the basement, no cats who hate Mondays, no penguins with existential problems just reality, but oh such a fun reality.You should buy this book and the rest of the series and settle down for some laughs and a lot of plain old fun.  Buy a set for your mom too, even if she wasn't a single mom or widowed, she's been there.If you're a guy, definitely read these.
