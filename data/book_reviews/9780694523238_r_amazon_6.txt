I loved, loved, LOVED this book and can't believe I had never read it before. I recently went to San Francisco (May 2014), where Armistead Maupin is actually a neighbor of my host, and we saw him walking along the sidewalk, so I just had to get his book(s). I fell in love the with ambiance, the characters. and the story, and his respectful, loving characterizations of gays and lesbians, so I'm on the third installment now. This first book introduces the characters and sets the scene. If you like Alexander McCall-Smith books (No. 1 Ladies Detective Agency, etc.), you'll love these, too.
