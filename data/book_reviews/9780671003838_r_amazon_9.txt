I really liked the episode Flashback, and I LOVE the book. In many ways I think the book is even better than the episode due to all the extra stuff we get to read about. Diane Carey gives the reader so much more than "just" a book version of a great episode and she gives the story a whole new depth and writes it just beautifully. Among other things we get to read about how Chakotay felt about Janeway going in to the mindmeld with Tuvok, a mission Torres and Paris went on to gather sirillium in the nebula and some more scenes with the crew from the past and even one with Tuvok and Spock. Carey even manages to give us a little sidestory with Amelia Earheart from "The 37's". We also get to read a few quotes in between the chapters sometimes. I just love it! All of it! This is truly a great book, and it was so much better than I would have ever guessed it would be.
