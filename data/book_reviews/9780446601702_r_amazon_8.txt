Janet Springer's father was a diamond merchant. He carried with him always an odd little stone that he called his "reminder stone". Janet is confined in a very ritzy sanatorium, suffering from a condition that will not respond to any of the recognized treatments. She is holding her father's "reminder stone" when, after a violent episode, she  is restrained on her bed. When she becomes aware a few hours later, she realizes that something in her has dramatically changed. She is well.
