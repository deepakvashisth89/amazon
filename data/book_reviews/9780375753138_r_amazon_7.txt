This novel may seem boring to some and others may not like it cause it seems like the story does not really have a plot. Yet, I enjoyed that Anderson stayed true to the genre of modernism and took the lives of ordinary people and used that as the center of the novel. I think that is a good idea to keep in mind when reading Winesburg, Ohio - to not think about how it is difficult to connect with the characters of the story and how there are more sub-plots than an actual story line, but instead, to enjoy and find interest in the lives of regular people and the boring and the ordinary.
