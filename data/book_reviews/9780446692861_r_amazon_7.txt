Overall, I enjoyed this book, but it is not without issues. I wasn't really able to identify with the protagonist, which is generally problematic when reading a novel. I felt like I was reading about a teenager as opposed to a graduate student in her mid-to-late twenties.  Perhaps if the author had made her main character a little younger, the story would have made a bit more sense. I can't imagine any self-respecting woman of this age with nothing to do or talk about other than mean girl drama, drinking, smoking, acting like a child when interacting with her parents, and stalking her professor. (Why did nobody on campus have window coverings, by the way? Blinds? Curtains?)  I also tired of the incessant talk about smoking. I get it. The woman smoked. She liked to smoke. We didn't need a whole commentary about it on every page.
