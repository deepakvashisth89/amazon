This is the fourth book in S. J. Rowan”s Bill Smith / Lydia Chin series and these just keep getting better. Smith and Chin are plausible, well developed characters, the mystery, about a deep-seated construction scam, is believable and nuanced and the scene, NY in the 90s, raises memories o f the famous crime novels of the 30s and 40s.
