This is one of those revenge plots that goes awry. Roan marries Lily in revenge on her brother whom he holds responsible for his younger sister's death from an overdose of drugs. Lily is shocked to say the least when on the morning after their wedding night, Roan tells her exactly why he married her. Like all revenge plots, this one doesn't work out as planned.
