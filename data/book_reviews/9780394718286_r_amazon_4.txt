With all respect to The Maltese Falcon, Double Indemnity and The Postman Always Rings Twice this is (at least in my opinion) the best and most important work to come out of American Noir. Gritty, terse, violent and bereft of any simply White-Hat good guys, Hammett distills a the genre down to it's core, and yet he does so without sacrificing anything in terms of narrative or character. While certainly not a pretty or particularly happy novel, Hammett never lets the text get away from him, producing a work that is engaging and easy to read. Above and beyond that, Red Harvest tells a truly original story, to be clear the story itself is new, not just the framing or usages of literary device.
