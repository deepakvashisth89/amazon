As a devourer of all books on simplicity and minimalism, I find this to be one of the best books out there.  It covers stuff that lays deep beneath the surface and touches on areas that have been left untouched by others.  It has provided a new lens with which to see some areas of my life that need refinement (such as perfectionism and defining who you are).One of my favorite parts of the books is Kim's metaphor of the garden.  "In prioritizing your things to do, you'll want the majority of your time to be spent on "perennials" or long-term investments, and then fill in with "annuals," or short-term investments."  Weeding out the not-so-important tasks (and determining which is which) is a wonderful way to create rhythm and routine in my day.I hope everyone enjoys this book as much as I have and are able to glean some wisdom along the way.Read moreRead less
