I stumbled over Miss Seeton by accident and it was love at first page.A mix of parody and crime with an elderly arts teacher in the midst of it, a no-nonsense attitude and a mystery talent to draw more than the eye could see.At first I was baffled by the educated little jokes and the pleasure of smart words and then I was amazed how well the author grasped the feeling of the time Miss Seeton is set in and only then did I look it up to realize that this book is 50 years old and the author long dead. It didn't feel old, it felt fresh and funny.I soon learned that although there is this element of parody of something that reminds of Miss Marple a lot... Miss Seeton is actually not for the faint of heart. Over the series things can get pretty violent and even lethal. She is investigating murder, after all.Please don't read this as serious crime. It is not. Its beauty is in the smart way the author lets MissEs  confuse her words to create ambiguity and in the curious expectation how in all the world her umbrella will get her out of THAT situation, and a small laugh about the endless creativity it seems to have to do so. If you read is all seriously the joke is on you. But if you like the english countryside, overdrawn characters, funny coincidences, witty and educated jokes and some crime mixed in, Miss Seeton would be ideal entertainment.Read moreRead less
