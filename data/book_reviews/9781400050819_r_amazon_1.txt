Steve Shirippa has written the BEST book to inform us all about the proper Goomba! I grew up in an Italian family and there are so many things that were similar to my family but I’m sad to say that I don’t believe that we had a Goomba in our midst. I love how he is so comically descriptive at times and so very series at others. You know when you read this book that everything he is saying is the truth and he absolutely knows what he’s talking about. I have mad respect for Mr Shirippa!
