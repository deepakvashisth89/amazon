I love these books!! I read them to my kids, now I read them to my 3 yr old grand daughter. I've just started buying them (again) so only have given her a few so far. These kids books have a variety of topics including: Little Miss Sunshine, Naughty, Bossy, Brainy, Tiny, etc. There are also "Mr." books like: Mr. Tickle, Happy, Selfie, etc. These cute little paperback books tell a "value" type story using the many personalities of the other "little" people of the town.
