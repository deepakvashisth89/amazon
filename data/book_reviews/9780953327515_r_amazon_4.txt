Ian Winn is the techno pagan octopus messiah! In his tale of his travels through Egypt and the Far East, you feel his pain and joy and then you look at your own existence and dream of escaping from the box we call life. It's  a dream tale, with visions, artifacts and a slew of interesting journeymen  who crop up to help our narrator find his way (whether indirectly or  directly). You also receive some interesting tips on how to save money by  traveling immediately after an international crisis.
