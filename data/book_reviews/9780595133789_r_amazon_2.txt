Barbara Field's The Deeper, the Bluer was a joy to read.  I couldn't put this book down until I finished it and it's been a while since I've found a book that kept me so interested throughout. Although I am not a single Mom, I often wonder what it is like to be one and this book brought that answer to light in a very real, open, vivid and honest way.
