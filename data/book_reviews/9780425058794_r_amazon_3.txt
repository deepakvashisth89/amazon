This is an evaluation and review of the entire series. The speculative science and technology approach fantasy but anything sufficiently advanced might do that. The major characters are interesting, especially Gaia herself, and the minor ones are colorful. The events bring surprise but they have their own logic.Varley indulges his penchant for bizarre sex, which I don't mind, and for depopulating the Earth, which is getting old.
