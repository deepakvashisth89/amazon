When I decided to read all of Lloyd Alexander's books in order I had no idea he had written so many books about cats.  I'm not a cat person, but I have enjoyed Mr. Alexander's takes on this noble creature especially his collection of short stories "The Town Cats and Other Tales".These tales can be read in one gulp or be doled out sparingly, but they all provide that special pleasure that only fairy tales and folk tales provide.  Alexander has the rare gift of making his modern stories feel as if they were created centuries ago and he has recently unearthed them.  The stories are whimsical and wise and appeal to grown-ups as well as children.  Laszlo Kubinyi's line drawings provide an excellent complement to Alexander's stories.I hope this book stays in print so that many future generations can enjoy Lloyd Alexander's clever and magical cats.Read moreRead less
