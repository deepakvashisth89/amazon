This was a good read, and I can see why it was popular and controversial when first published.  But...  I had real trouble identifying with Isadora Wing.  I guess I'm too normal and psychologically well-balanced.  Isadora is such a neurotic character I was unable to enjoy the book as much as I had hoped.
