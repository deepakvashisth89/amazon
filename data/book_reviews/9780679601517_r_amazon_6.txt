"The Fire Next Time" is as powerful in structure as it is in substance.  The opening letter to Baldwin's young nephew is intimate and piercing, while the second essay expands into a potent narrative on power, belief, and insecurity--drawing on the parallels between street entrepreneurs, religious leaders, and the dangerous fiction of racial constructs treated as fact.
