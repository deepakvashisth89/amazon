Margery Allingham is one of my favorite authors and one of the best--imho--to come out of the "golden age of mystery" we hear so much about. She was not only an excellent writer of mystery novels, but she had a wonderful ability to create a scene and a mood. While I enjoy all of her work, with the creation of Charlie Luke she gave Campion a wonderful, extremely well imagined partner in crime solving. He brought an energy to the later works that kept them from becoming stale and is featured in a few of these tales. There is a variety to this collection that kept me reading one story after another. While a few of them are less compelling than the others, I enjoyed each one and will be reading these again. If you are a fan of classical mystery, you are probably already familiar with Margery Allingham and will be thrilled to find this collection.
