A fascinating book about all kinds of science and history, with Harry Potter as a fairly loose thread holding it all together. Better suited to children 11/12+ (in my opinion) as younger ones will just find it too difficult. It's not a book for a Harry Potter fanatic who wants to read about Harry Potter, but a book for someone who wants to learn some really REALLY interesting bits of science and history. Much more challenging than most pop science books, and therefore a much better book. Very enjoyable indeed.Read moreRead less
