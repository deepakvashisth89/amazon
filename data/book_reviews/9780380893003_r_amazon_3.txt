I usually enjoy KEW novels. The reviews on this one made me skeptical. However, I really liked it. It doesn't have a lot of steamy scenes, but the plot was interesting. Amnesia stuff seems a bit far fetched, so I wasn't that hopeful for the plot. However, it ended up being okay. The mystery throughout the novel about her identity really had me questioning myself! I figured it would be easy, but not as transparent as I thought. I always feel KEW has other fringe characters involved in story lines that take too long or seem unimportant-those character elements are in this book too. Overall, an enjoyable read, just not a constant page-turner.Read moreRead less
