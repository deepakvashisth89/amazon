Hailed as providing the reader with a calling card of mirth and laughter that will make you welcome wherever you go, this work of 5000 'gags' will certainly mark you out in a crowd as to be avoided at all costs. It claims that with some of the explosive material in this arsenal of amusement, you'll bowl everyone over!From the 'blurb' I was expecting to find the contents funny and entertaining, but having given it a good go, and reading quite a lot of it, before becoming overcome with mega-boredom, I remained unmoved, un-amused, and surprised that such outrageously inaccurate claims were made.
