A.C. Crispin is a masterful writer for the Star Trek series.  She developed the characters in a thoughtful and realistic way that had me transfixed from the beginning to the end of the book.  The ways in which expounded on the relationship of Spock and Sarek and Sarek and Amanda were gut wrenching at times, but filled in the gaps from the t.v. show, in a very satisfying way.
