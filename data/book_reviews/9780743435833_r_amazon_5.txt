This second book of the trio was written from an initially unexpected angle and only involved the characters from the first book to a peripheral extent.  With the background introduction, each book seems more able to stand alone than books in other Jack Chalker series.  This story tells the tale of a visit by a newly introduced set of characters, members of a salvage crew, (cleverly linked to our previous explorers from the first book), to the second moon of the Three Kings, Melchior.  As usual, the plot and characters are well developed and interesting and punctuated with action and unexpected twists.  A thoroughly good read, building on the overall scenario and integrating the characters in a way which promises that the third book will be the best of the bunch!Read moreRead less
