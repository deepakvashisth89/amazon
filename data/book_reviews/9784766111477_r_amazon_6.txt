I bought this book because I needed some help choreographing fight scenes, and I couldn't be more pleased with it. This is a very detailed and descriptive book on fighting, illustrating various poses at different angles. Also, this book is helpful in that it also shows the reader how to illustrate the aftermath of fighting: bruises, cuts, etc. "Illustrating Battles" is for anyone who wants to know how to draw battles, from slapping to all-out punches and kicks. Another thing it shows is the differences between the genders when fighting, one example being how they fall differently.
