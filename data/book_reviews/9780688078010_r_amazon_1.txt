This book talks about some of the Amendments that comprise the Bill of Rights. The case examples are rich with factual detail, rationale for the court decisions and also includes, in some cases,post litigation results.  The book is, at times, cluttered with interesting but nonessential facts.  Other that that, it was a book that I enjoyed reading through one time and then going back to do the school assignments for my paralegal class.  If you care about your Constitutional rights, you should read this book!Read moreRead less
