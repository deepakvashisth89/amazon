I found the book very entertaining and relaxing. The authors observations of the natural world , and human nature are whimsical, scientifically descriptive, as well as thought provoking.  The book balances the hard aspects of rural living in the Missouri Ozarks with its joys and freedoms. As a biologist and bird watcher I appreciated her observations of the minute yet endlessly fascinating workings of nature.I think women would especially enjoy this work about a single divorcee living her chosen path to free living.Read moreRead less
