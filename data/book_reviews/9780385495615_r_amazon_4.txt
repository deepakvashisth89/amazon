Warriors of God is a textbook example of a writer putting more stock in the story than academic peccadilloes.If you are the kind of person that likes your biographies with strict formatting and tons of citations this may not be your vibe. However, if your like me, Reston does something that’s even better, and that’s giving an entertaining and compelling narrative.Yes, Warriors of God reads more like a novel at times, but by doing so it makes volumes of information easily digestible. Reston makes history infinitely more interesting by focusing more on the story and it largely benefits the readers with a casual interest versus the academics that may already be acquainted with the topic.All in all, I think Warriors of God is masterpiece that gives a comprehensive view into a pivotal point in history that surprisingly few people know about.
