Another great by Wayne Johnston.  I first got into this author with the book Colony of Unrequited Dreams, and while a bit slow, you could hear a certain voice in his writing.  Divine Ryans didn't have a chance to be slow.  It's a fairly short read, yet it's near impossible to put down.  If you want an awesome read, with quite a change of pace from your average novel, make sure to pick this one up; the view from a child's perspective is worth it alone.Read moreRead less
