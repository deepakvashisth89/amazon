To anyone at all interested in aircraft of World War II, this is a great resource. Packed with photographs, statistics, excellent cut-away schematics and detailed profile paintings (a few of which are large fold-outs), the book has information on just about every military aircraft that saw service in the war. The paintings are great but I would not call this a coffee table book, there is too much information in it and the priority of the images is to detail.Read moreRead less
