Unbelievably awful book doing what Islam does best - omitting facts. I will not go to great lengths to criticize this book as there is not enough space. but I will just comment on chapter two ( I am currently re-reading this book for reasons of chronology). It describes the oasis of Yathrib as being 'nearly torn apart by wars between the clans, three of which were Jewish'. There is no evidence of any conflict between the three main Jewish tribes or with the pagan tribes of Banu Aws or Banu Khazraj who fought among themselves. To infer such is disingenuous. The chapter then goes on to describe the Christians and the Jews clinging onto their religions as if Mohammed was offering them something wonderful. There is no mention of the fact that two of the three Jewish tribes: the Banu Qaynuqa and the Banu Nadir were robbed and ejected from the settlement on spurious grounds and that the third, the Banu Qurayza were murdered (men and boys) or sold into slavery (women) by the Muslims after the so called battle of the Trench (failed siege would be a better description) at the instruction of the arch angel Gabriel (Jibreel).
