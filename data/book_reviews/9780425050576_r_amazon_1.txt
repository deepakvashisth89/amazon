Keneally’s writing is every bit as good as my favorite historical fiction writers (Michael and Jeff Shara, Ralph Peters). He presents what appears to me to be the best most authentic description of Confederates’ personal experience during the lead up to the battle at Chancellorsville. His story is gripping with never a dull moment as he follows soldiers, civilians, medical personnel and spies. A definite “ must read “ fans of American Civil War history.Read moreRead less
