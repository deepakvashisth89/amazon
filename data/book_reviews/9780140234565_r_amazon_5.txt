When one reads any Ben Abbott mystery, another mystery is unlocked for much of the country. Justin Scott is a resident of Newtown, CT. He loves his town, and shows this as he speaks of the people and places of fictitious Newbury, CT, which is only a thinly disguised version of a town that, tragically, is much in the news now.
