Of the first 'three' books, this (Volume 3) may be the most 'technical'. Yet, this was the most difficult of all 12 volumes for me to collect, and I had spent more than 4x the cost to procure just this copy. But I am satisfied, not just because my set is uniform and complete but because having read the paperback before, I found this volume demonstrating clearly Tolkien's consistency, meticulousness and technical mastery. Not repeating what Wiki already says in review of this volume, my contribution is to state that apart from exploring manuscripts he abandoned as late as the mid 1950s, these are gorgeous verses which could still be prt of the English literary treasures of our time.
