This book popped up as featured monthly offering and what a pleasant surprise it turned out to be. I have loved Japanese films and novels since childhood, especially the ones with magic and mythology. This novel brought back so many fond memories , while being an edge- of- my- seat thrilling story in it's own right. The characters were believable and well-rounded and the "did she" or "didn't she" had me going to the very end. (With just a little extra suspension of disbelief regarding DNA etc., but perhaps I just watch too much  CSI.) I feel confident recommending this book and hope the author treats us to more like it. I think it would make an awesome movie  I majored in film studies in college and had I gone into the business I'd be fighting to direct it myself.  The story is so visual it screams out to be on film. Just one caveat : the editing gets a little spotty after the first quarter or so, which I find somewhat distracting and so UN-necessary.
