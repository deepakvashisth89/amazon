When Adair succeeds, her poems are marvellous - poignent, sometimes funny, with a brilliant talent for last lines that stay with you (see, for example, Fordham Road;, 1917).  Like all collections, not all poems are successful, of course. But I was so glad to have bought it and to be keeping it for re-reading.
