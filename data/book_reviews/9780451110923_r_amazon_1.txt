I enjoy Mike Resnick's writing style, and this story in particular. I wouldn't say it is his best, but rather an interesting tale of obsession in all of it's incarnations. I don't like spoilers, so I find it hard to write an in-depth review, but I will say that the story didn't quite play out like I thought. The main character, Nicobar Lane, has his demons, (which I think he is unaware of at first), and the semi mythical Soul Eater eventually forces him to face them. Or does it introduce new ones?
