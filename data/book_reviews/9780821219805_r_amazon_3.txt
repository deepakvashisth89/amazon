Ansel Adams is best known for his iconic black and white photography, but what he shot in color is just as stunning.  In particular, the work he did in Yellowstone Natural Park is amazing.  I have a litho of one of these photos ("Pool Detail", pg 70), and this book was helpful in finding other work I want to display.  It's printed on clay-covered paper, giving each page a premium feel and showing crisp detail.
