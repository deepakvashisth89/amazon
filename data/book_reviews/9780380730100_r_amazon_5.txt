I have all of the Bannerman books and this is the one that I read over a couple times a year.  It is pure entertainment and funny about the way that Paul Bannerman, simply by showing up in an area can send the bureaucrats scrambling and pulling their hair.  Momma's Boy and his friends are really fun to read about, including Calamity Carla and Uncle Billy (Bannerman's monster.)  John R.
