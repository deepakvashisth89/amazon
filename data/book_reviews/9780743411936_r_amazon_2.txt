A coming of age tale in a setting of religious cults, child neglect and school violence.  A foster family takes in a teenage girl, and she saves them as much as they save her.The relationships develop in the first part of the book, and this is the most satisfying and realistic part of the book.  The ending is a bit rushed and disjointed in comparison.  Overall, though, the characters are flawed but endearing and make this book a worthwhile read.Read moreRead less
