This is a nice read through a cross-section of classical poetry from the period it covers.  Translation is sensitive and lyrical, aiming to capture mood as well as meaning though this is always hard.  As noted in other reviews, it would have been nice if transliteration had been included to make reading the original a little easier.  The foreword and intros to the poems / poets are informative and offer enough context to help interpreting sub-text, along with thoughts and details prompting further research if interested.
