I really don't get the complaint(s) that this story was nothing more than pornography.  If one took the time to read the summary provided they should have seen the references to "erotica" and "phone sex" thus how could one logically think that the book wouldn't contain something  graphically sexual.  Come on people!!!  That being said "Lip Service" was far more than erotica.  Julia's journey was quite captivating and the story isn't nearly as sexually graphic as some reviewers would lead you to believe.  In fact, there are no love scenes and there are only a few reasonably graphic "phone sex" conversation.  No this story focused more on one woman's journey to reclaim her identity, independence, her personality and her soul than on anything sexually graphic.  In the context of the story, I really did not find the phone sex conversations provocative.  Julia's journey to self-discovery is what I found exciting about this story.
