This was an excellent account of the conflicts between the British, American Revolutionists, and the Seneca Valley Indians.  It was factual and told what it was like for those pioneers that moved into this area from Connecticutt. It also highlighted the hard feelings that the Philadelphia aristrocrats held for those American pioneers. Apparently Connecticutt offered land-grants to those that moved into this area and that is what initially attracted them. For those doing geneology, it was especially important to learn of these boundary lines for during this time this area was considered western Connecticutt and not Pennsylvania.
