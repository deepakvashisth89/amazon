This is a delicious book. Michael Frayn is one of the few authors who makes me smile and laugh. The ill-fated plane trip with international reporters on board and on the tarmac is priceless.  The scenes of the drunken newspaper men lurching from one airport to the next in less and less equipped aircraft contain some of the funniest material I have ever read. together with the ill-fated tv appearance of one of the main characters.So happy Frayn's novels are in print!Read moreRead less
