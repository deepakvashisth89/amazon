I have always enjoyed Andre Norton's books,  from when I was a teenager to now at age 71. This was a good, action packed story with much promise. I am going on to read the next two in the series.My reservations have to do with whomever scanned this into digital format, it needs some serious editing. Spelling errors like 'corn' instead of com, dashes where they didn't belong mid sentence, as if a line break at the end of a sentence occurred but not corrected when transposed, commas appearing where none were needed. It detracted from the quality of the story, having to stop and mentally assess the correct placement.
