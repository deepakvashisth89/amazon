This is a collection of three novellas  that first appeared in magazines in the very early 1960's.Fans of the series are undoubtedly aware that Nero does not like to deal with females, particularly not as clients.  These three stories shed light on why.The first, EENY MEANY MURDER MO, from the March 1962 issue of Ellery Queen Mystery Magazine.  A woman had the effrontery to not only arrive at the brownstone unannounced and during Wolfe's afternoon session with the orchids but to be murdered in his office.The second, DEATH OF A DEMON, first appeared as a serial in the June 1961 Saturday Evening Posts.  Another woman has appeared at the brownstone to confess to Wolfe that she wished her husband was dead but when her wish came true Wolfe and Archie needed to prove that she was not the murderer.The final novella, COUNTERFEIT FOR MURDER, was serialized in three issues of the Saturday Evening Post during January 1961.
