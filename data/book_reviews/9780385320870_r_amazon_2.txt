Despite this book being published in 1994, it is still timely when it comes to teaching our young people the responsibilities that go hand in hand with the privilege of being a driver.  I use this novel in 9th grade English because "a book is a safe place to explore serious themes". A Driver's Ed instructor who is burned out, teenagers excited about driving and dating, add in the risk of stealing street signs, and there will be consequences.
