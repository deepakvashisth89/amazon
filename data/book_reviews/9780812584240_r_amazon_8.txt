💤 It would be a simple matter if lives were lived by hindsight, … There is much we would not begin, if we could see how it would end 💤💤 This book moves both fast and slow: the pacing deftly balances a busy bundle of plot-lines while also taking the time to focus, think and reflect 📖 Upon the appointment of her new mission, Elspeth has grown since returning from the mountains with Gahltha: she's colder, more distant, and quick to anger in this book, but she's also learning - learning to temper her words, her tone of voice, to notice how others are feeling and to think about what they might be going through 😤 Being a leader amongst the Mistfit’s of Obernewtyn has put some pressure on Elspeth’s shoulders, especially now, due to the added pressure of the mission the Eldar of the Guanette birds, Atthis, has given her: to find and destroy the weaponmachines that caused the Great White Holocaust ⚙️ As we spend time with the rebels in Sutrium, though it feels like so much happens, between high-octane action (slavers, Herder faction, The Council, the Gypsies, etc.) and the more subtle revelations that Elspeth has 🏞 As I read this series I find that Isobelle Carmody skilfully weaves thought-provoking ideas and social commentary into her stories, something that tends to be lacking from a lot of books YA these days 🧐 This series touches on a great many relevant themes: environmental destruction, proliferation of "weapons of mass destruction", human greed, religious dogma, censorship, the notion of human superiority, not learning from our mistakes, the welfare of animals, just to mention a few 😊Read moreRead less
