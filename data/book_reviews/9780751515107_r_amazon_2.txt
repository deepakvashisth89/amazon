I disliked this book. The plot seemed interesting but it actually was not at all. First of all, I really had trouble in picturing, in my mind, the characters. Holly, for example, is described several times as posh and elegant but then you read that she actually bites her nails, has always messy hair, removes with her hand a sweet she is eating to talk, offers to others unwrapped sweets scattered in her bag .... is this elegant??? The writer seems confused. Then, the story, which could well be over after 30 page, drags endlessly without really saying anything. The book also ends without telling you anything of what happens to the other characters of the book. Much was concentred on this Holly who was so annoying and so hysterical. I didn't put one star only because, as we say in Italy, il peggio non é mai morto (there is always something worse in life) but definitely it's a NO, NO, NO, don't bother.Read moreRead less
