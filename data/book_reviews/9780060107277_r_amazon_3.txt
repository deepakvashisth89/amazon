I'll be concise. The book differs slightly from the film, as is the case for most films based on literature. Paddy did a great job on this. The copy on back describes Altered States as a modern day Dr. Jekyll & Mr. Hyde, it could not be truer. I know the author was not a fan of the film, but I love both. I found the film first (as a child) and decided to read the source material. To sum up, the film, in my opinion, has more pathos, but the book has more logos and ethos.If you are a fan of the film, buy a copy of the book. You won't regret it.
