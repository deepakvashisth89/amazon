More than anything else I've read on the subject, Robin Kirk's "More Terrible Than Death" brings a degree of clarity and focus to the complicated, multi-layered hornet's nest of terrorism, endemic violence, drugs and corruption that are the scourge of the proud nation of Colombia.It's interesting to note that in spite of documenting a wide range of horrific events, Kirk focuses on a glimmer of hope.  The book's title is perversely positive.  It comes from one of her Colombian human rights associates, who says to her (as she questions whether her efforts may be doing more harm than good) "Don't doubt.  To give up is more terrible than death."The story within the story is Kirk's exemplary bravery in compiling the tales that make up this story.  This is no armchair analysis.Read moreRead less
