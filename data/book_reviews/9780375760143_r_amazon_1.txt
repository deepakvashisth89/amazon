Alamyer’s Folly is Joseph Conrad’s first novel and what I would consider to be a little known classic. Yet, its relative lack of fame does not take away its power. The greed bred by colonialism and the racism that pervades in it are powerfully shown in the book. Almayer was driven by love of wealth and love for his daughter. However, the love for his daughter was distorted by the superiority he felt as an European and ultimately ends in tragedy. Throughout the book, Conrad masterfully describes the environment under colonialism as well as the psychological states of mind of the colonists and the natives.
