Best psychology book I've read, and I've read a fair amount. Most have 1 or 2 gems. This book is chock full of nectar, covering politics, spirituality, psychology, ethics -- all with great panache!I believe self-esteem is at the root of most, if not all, psychological problems -- i.e. the lack of self-esteem or false self-esteem. It is not something that can be given by others. It is not something a birth-right to be given to people -- it's a duty and a noble quest to develop it for oneself. This book explains the meaning, the value and the method. I just started reading it again. I know I didn't get all the nuances first time.Branden was a friend of Ayn Rand, which may put some people off. I have mixed feelings about Rand, as does Branden. I tend to agree with his misgivings about her. He seems to have taken the best of her philosophy and added some artistic and compassionate flair, creating a realistic and profound balance.
