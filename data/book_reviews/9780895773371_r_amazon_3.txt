Everyone has watershed moments from their childhoods and Longfellow's poem, Song of Hiawatha is one of mine. In my grade school (a country church school back in the early 1950s) we had this fantastic collection of poetry and short works that were published together as a single volume; as part of our reading work, would take turns standing in front of the class reading out loud from this book. A shortened version of The Song of Hiawatha was one of the selections and will always bring back happy memories of my classmates and our fascination with the art of poetry and Amerindian culture and folklore.Read moreRead less
