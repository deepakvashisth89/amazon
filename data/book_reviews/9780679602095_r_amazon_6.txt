Unless one's sins are covered by the grace of God, there is a place for every sinner in the nine circles of hell. Dante's hell is uncannily eerie and scary because it is a place where an eye is paid for an eye many fold and for all eternity. The harm and horror one causes to another will be repaid in kind in hell and for a much longer time - eternity. Yes, the aggressor becomes the prey and vice versa.There are rankings to sins ranging from the lightest 'virtuous pagans' placed in the first circle to the most heinous and most grievous 'sins against the master' in the 9th circle where one has the dubious honour of having Satan for eternal companion. Interestingly, Satan is entombed in the frozen icy center of the world, where all sins and grievances and pain flows towards. The 3 headed  Satan is a grotesque parody of the Trinity, he has 3 faces and in each mouth he clamps a sinner whom he rips eternaly with his teeth. The centre face clamps someone whom Dante considered as the greatest sinner of all.Dante has turned my phobia for poetry away because I am hopelessly moved by Inferno, the first division of the Commedia. Filled with graphic symbolism, one must rely on the graphic representations of each circle of hell, the tortured souls and the monsters to fully enjoy Dante's supreme masterpiece. It's easy to google for the graphics as one reads along. Written in the 1200s, there are many references to historical figures and events, mostly in Italy and Rome. There the aid of a good translator and annotation is essential for enjoyment. Therefore I commend John Ciardi for an excellent job withThe Inferno. Don't go for the free version of the book because you will get extremely frustrated as you read names that you don't recognise.We have reached the bottom of hell after visiting all 9 circles of infernal hell. It is no doubt we will have appreciated the hideousness of sin if our hearts are not cold. Perhaps we also saw which sector we most belong to. But with the gift of God's grace, we escape this horrible prospect of eternal damnation and begin our journey upwards now to purgatory.
