Another odd character created by Agatha Christie. In this novel of short stories, he doesn’t start off as a detective. In the first six stories, he starts off as a Mr Roarke kind character, the Horst from the show fantasy island. People come to him to help make their lives better, he helps  a wife make a cheating husband jealous,a bored soldier and city clerk find adventure, help a rich women find a reason to be happy again. Then I guess Christie got bored with this wish profiling character,so she sends him off on vacation, where he gets involved in all sorts of mysteries. From murder, to theft, to kidnapping. So for the rest of the book Pyne becomes more like Poirot and Marple, and uses his brain to solve these mysteries. One final note one of titles of stories in this book is Death on the Nile.it’s not the same plot of the later fantasticPoirot book, but it does involve a murder of a rich women on a boat on the Nile.
