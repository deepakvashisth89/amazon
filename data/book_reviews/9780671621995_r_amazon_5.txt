The first novel in Wooley's Guinevere Trilogy has all the ingredients of a good book.  The characters are engaging, the plot moves along briskly, and the perspective is different.  The familiar Arthur story is told in this series from Guinevere's point of view.  From the beginning, we read about her unpretentious Celtic royal family and her obligation to marry Arthur.  As a Southerner with less than stellar Celtic origins, Arthur needed a wife with authentic Celtic credentials.  With her at his side, he was able to unite the various chieftains under his kingship, at least for a time.  All the accouterments of the Arthur legend are intact in the trilogy.
