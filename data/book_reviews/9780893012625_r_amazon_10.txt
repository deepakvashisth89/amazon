A story of one pioneering woman and her daughter who accomplished the extraordinary feat of walking across America. It is both a story of triumph and tragedy.  Helga’s achievements were not fully recognized in her lifetime due mainly to cultural and familial attitudes of the time.  If not for the interest of future generations her story might never have been told. Besides the story of her personal journey it also serves as an important historical record of the barriers women of her generation faced.
