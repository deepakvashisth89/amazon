I downloaded this on Kindle Unlimited fully expecting to read a chapter or two and dump it. But I read the entire book in about 2 days. Good, concise information about the French Revolution, Napoleon's rise to power, his excursion into Egypt, early military victories, reformations, and the disasters of Moscow and Waterloo. I kind of skimmed some of the material about the lineage of certain horses. And some of the information relating to whether or not certain horses were at certain battles was a bit tedious. But overall, a very interesting insight into Napoleon's upbringing, use of cavalry and his incessant need to procure more and more horses.Read moreRead less
