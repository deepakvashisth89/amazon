Do you like to read about aliens? Do you like stinky cheese? Do you like silly books?  Then here is book for you.  This is about Funny Boy, a "good alien" who has been sent to Earth to save us from "unspeakably disgusting aliens." In two other books Funny Boy saved Earth from alien invaders by using his sense of humor and jokes. He alleviated the Airsick Alien from Andromeda, and then banished the Bubble Brained Barbers from the Big Bang.  Now, the President of the United States has drafted Funny Boy to save us from conniving chitchatting Cheeses from the celestial planet of Chattanooga.
