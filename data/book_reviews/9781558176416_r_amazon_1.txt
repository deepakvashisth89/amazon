Thomas Harris`s Hannibal Lechter used to be who I considered the most dangerous villain ever. That's before I read about Mr. Appel`s Carl Nasson who is a millionair, drop dead handsome, with a genious I.Q. a master of weapons and technology and disguises and a powerful physical fighter. Bill Dacey, N.Y. city Chief of Detectives is stymied. The victims of a serial killer seems to have nothing in common. They're different races, age groups, different looks, different occupations. What they do have in common is ingenious writing by Mr. Appeal. I won't give it away. But you`ll never guess, Dacey convinces his friend Kate Berman (criminologist) and her husband Josh (former m.e.) to help him identify the killer. Nasson kidnaps the Berman`s niece Jenny and there`s a race against time to rescue her. Super suspense, great characterization. Don't miss this one!Read moreRead less
