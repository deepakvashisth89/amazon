The various essays found in this book have been a great foundation to my own travels through Christianity and Buddhism. I have often contemplated and been inspired by many passages from the book. Of special significance, at least for myself, is "Wisdom in Emptiness", a dialogue between Merton and D T Suzuki based upon a study of the Desert Fathers. I have returned to this every so often through the years, always finding something new, and quoted passages when participating on discussion forums between people of various Faiths.
