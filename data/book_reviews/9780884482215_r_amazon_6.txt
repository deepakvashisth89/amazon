A Maine Hamlet by Laura Beam is one of those rare books where the author (who lived her entire life in the hamlet about which she wrote) combines a detailed, fact-oriented account of turn-of-the-century life in a hamlet in  downeast Maine with poetic language that resounds in the heart.  The  book covers a variety of topics, including the passing of the seasons to  courtship and marriage, childhood, as well as a discussion of the effects  of modernity on this hamlet. And when you read each chapter, the people,  situations and settings become alive, speaking to a part of each of us that  is connected to or desirous of a simpler, more natural way of life close to  the earth and to the joy of human relationships.  I wholeheartedly  recommend this book to anyone, not only people who are interested in Maine.
