I had forgotten how marvelous the Darkover tales are. They give us another choice at what we could be if given the chance. They let the modern us and the past us live in one world. A world that does not want to grab everything like greedy children that modern us take for granted  but only want the best of things and them only in small doses.I'm the greedy modern I want to absorb these stories in one gulp. But find myself rereading them and each time they are like new.
