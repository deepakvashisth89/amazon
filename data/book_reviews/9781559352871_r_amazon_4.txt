Bill Gates Speaks provides plenty of insight including numerous pertinent quotations.  Those with little knowledge of Gates yet an interest in his views will find value in this book; however, I struggle to rate this book above three stars as it is merely a scattered collection of thoughts as oppose to a well organized account.  With that said, I would also be hard pressed to rate this book negatively as the subject attracts intrigue on its own right and Lowe appears to be at least both informed and well-researched on the topic.If you have a keen interest in Bill Gates, I would read this book; otherwise, I would search for something a little more engrossing.Read moreRead less
