Years ago my fathers friend gave him a copy of Dicks first book about Cayenne which is called " Left For Dead." He brought the book home and placed it on the coffee table and never read it. A few months went by and something about the book kept grabbing my attention, so I turned the TV off, picked it up - and before I knew it, the sun was peeking through the blinds. I had stayed up the entire night and finished the book. Inspired by Dick's story I drove to the health food store and bought some Cayenne pepper powder and brought it home. In a flurry of excitement and curiosity I mixed a few ounces of water with a small amount of the 40,000 heat unit cayenne and threw it back. Within a minute I felt a warm sensation in my chest, could breath deeper, and felt much better. Little did I know but that shot of cayenne pepper started a now 10 year fascination with cayenne pepper and I have consumed multiple pounds of various varieties from the weak storebought cayenne, all the way up to the 90,000 heat unit and the 160,000 heat unit. We now also grow a wide variety of hot peppers from the trinidad scorpion, naga viper, thai dragon, cayenne, habanero, ghost pepper, carolina reaper, bell pepper, etc. Cayenne pepper started my journey into natural health and healing and I owe it all to the book left for dead. Thank you Dick! Years went by before I stumbled across this book " Death by Deception " which is co-authored by dicks daughter. She has done an absolutely amazing job and her father would be proud to say the least. Dick was a fascinating man and Ive always wanted to know more about him, his life, what he was taking for his health, his family, etc. This book was a lovely explanation to all the questions I had about this mans life. She has done a great job with her words, and at times really makes you feel exactly what the family, including dick was feeling during all of his highs and lows with his health. She is a master-wordsmith and she made it so you could feel the hope, the excitement, as well as the worry that this family went through while their father was sick. I really feel for them all! Dick is one of my biggest inspirations when it comes to natural healing. He was brave and strong, yet tender and loving. He has helped so many people and I continue to recommend his books to people.
