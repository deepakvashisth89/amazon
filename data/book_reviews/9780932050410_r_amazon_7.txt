I seriously considered following the same path as John Coey, as an alternative to the no-win travesty being fought in Viet Nam at the time.  Here was a chance to fight communist insurgents where there existed a will to win.  Coey's life, and his death, are an inspiration to me and my family.  He understood as well as the Rhodesians what the struggle was about, but he understood better than most of them the spiritual implications at a personal level and a national level.
