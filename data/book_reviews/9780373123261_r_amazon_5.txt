This little book has it all: great characters, an interesting plot, and the hook to make you sympathize with the heroine.  Brooke is an independent Australian who falls in love with an Italian, Leo.  Her man-hating mother warns that she'll be miserable with such a man.  Just before her fifth anniversary, her mother's words come back in an all-too-real way: she learns her husband was in love with his late brother's wife before ever meeting her!  Now that this old flame is available, Brooke must decide what to do for her children and herself.  Instead of confronting him, she captures his attention in bed. But is it still only sex for him?
