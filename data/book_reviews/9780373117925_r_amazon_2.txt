Though some have hated this story I have to say it was written back in the 90's. That said the Alpha male type from Bolivia and the way men thought of women then, may be even now, was spot on for me. Raphael thought Georgie a loose woman due to the interference of her step-brother. He never confronted her or let her know what he was led to believe was true. Now years later she needs his help and he is going to help her only on his terms. How this story evolves was great.
