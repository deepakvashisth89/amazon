I Love This Book! I read this book when I was in high school and have read it many times since. I have always checked it out from the library, but decided I just had to have a hard copy. It's just a wonderful mystery with lots of suspense, a little romance, and a main character that somehow I could really relate to. The funny thing is that it's the only book I have ever read from Dorothy Gilman. I'm sure her others are just as good, but I always go back to this one.
