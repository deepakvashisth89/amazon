I would like to have enjoyed this book, as I am in tune with many of Shermer's ideas.  But the scanning did terrible things to the text.  In dozens of cases, perhaps the majority, the scanner transcribed tl as d, so that many adverbs ended in dy, and Hitler mostly became Hider (or once, Hitier; I also enjoyed Fiihrer).  Title was transcribed as tide, Aristotle as Aristode. And many more, such as aU-powerful, and Snyder showed up as Syndefs.  Reprehensible lack of care, and it completely disrupted my concentration.  Clearly no live person did any post-scanning inspection.  I cannot comment on the content of the book, since I cannot finish it.Read moreRead less
