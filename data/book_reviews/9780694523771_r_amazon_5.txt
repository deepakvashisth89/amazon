This book tells the true story of the world's only successful submarine rescue that occurred in 1939.  Swede Momsen and his team innovated a way to save the sailors in a time of low government resources (equipment & money), while on the cusp of WWII.  The survivors of the initial sinking in the submarine demonstrated great courage, intelligence and incredible leadership in an effort to survive.  The team that organized and executed the rescue were just as courageous & driven to save lives.  This is my favorite military book as it tells the story about Americans at their best.
