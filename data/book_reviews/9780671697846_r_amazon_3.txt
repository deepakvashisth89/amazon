I've been reading more Timothy Zahn books this year, especially older ones, as I've enjoyed his Star Wars stories but wanted to learn more how he wrote those so well.  This book was written just prior to his first Star Wars books and I'm amazed by how detailed it was for a period of time where detailed space fantasies were scarce. Zahn does an amazing job detailing aliens and humans, alien species and their wants and desires, and leading people to twists that seem to surprise every time.  Highly recommend Zahn books to everyone.Read moreRead less
