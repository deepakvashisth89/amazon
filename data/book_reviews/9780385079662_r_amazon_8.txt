Indicates the underlying unity of seemingly disparate esoteric teachings throughout history and in different parts of the world.  Fascinating study of the multiple meanings, overt and esoteric, packed into certain words and word-roots.  Also investigates the surprising origins of many common symbols related to inner work.  The lives and contributions of Sufi giants are summarized.  Many aspects and stages of the path are clearly explained.  A masterful work that merits rereading.
