The fact that there is a cultural clash at the root of the narrator's story is no excuse for trying to present the neglect of chilren nor the bad tratmentbof women as an accepable norm.  Since I disagree with the narrator's philosophy I find the book has no place in my library.  However somone may need to read this book to learn how unkindness and selfish interest could be rooted out of their own.  The language is not of a standard I would let my children use, nor would I allow my choice of friends to include habitual use of terms not even in general aceptance.  The author felt a need to tell the tale,  I feel the need to not read it ever again.
