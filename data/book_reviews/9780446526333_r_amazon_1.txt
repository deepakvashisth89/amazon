Another great Sci Fi book on Mars recommended by The Mars Society. The Martian Race is  well written by Nebula award winning Gregory Benford.340 pages of fast paced action.Here's a little of whats going on. I wont ruin the ending.NASA is sending a ship and crew to Mars but the ship and crew blow up. The NASA Mars program gets grounded but there is a plan to have a $30 billion prize to the first group that can send a crew to Mars, do NASA's experiments and return safely.Multibillionare Axelrod forms a consortium of investors to raise money. Also they get lots of sponsors and the astronauts will be rich if they survive. That's questionable as the mission is extremely hazardous.We see them scrounging for used parts, making deals and using NASA launch facilities. They finally select 5 people but only 4 can go on this mission. Viktor an ex Russian cosmonaut as the commander/pilot and  Marc the handsome geologist and  married couple Katherine and Raoul the  systems specialist/scientist and super mechanic/back up pilot are selected. Julia,the biologist, Viktor's lover is extremely qualified but initially does not get selected. She talks to Axelrod and Marc gets bumped off the mission and Julia is in if she will marry Viktor and have a publicised wedding Axelrod will throw.Viktor and Julia say OK. Later Katherine gets scared about the mission and wants a baby. So she deliberately gets pregnant by her husband Raoul. So she is out of the mission. Axelrod pays bug bucks to Airbus, another group sending a nuclear powered ship to Mars in the hopes of winning the $30 billion prize, to get Marc back.Axelrod's ship lands on Mars with the crew but the return vehicle is damaged. How will they get home? Will they beat the Airbus group to win the prize?They find underground water. Julia with Marc's help makes one of the greatest discoveries of all time. She finds life way down inside a vent. Its an algae type growth and what looks like very tiny shrimp like creatures. They hurry back to the hab with their samples while Viktor and Raoul are frantically making repairs to leave. But can they? Can they get these samples home? Will the Airbus nuke ship people help them or let them die?Here comes the powerful Airbus ship landing and deliberately blasting the upper Mars crust/regolith away to get water for the ship's nuclear reactor so they can get home rather than the months and months of drilling Axelrod's crew had to do. Smart?  Chen the Airbus ship commander/biologist and crew women Greta climb even deeper into the extremely dangerous vent in the hopes of finding their own life samples where Julia found hers. Horrible monstrous catastrophe!That's about enough. I don't want to ruin the ending.Author Gregory Benford even has Dr Robert Zubrin the president of The Mars Society as a small part character in the novel and names the landing site after Zubrin. Also the Hab and the Mars research station on Devon Island ( read Mars On Earth 5 stars)in the far arctic on Earth is mentioned.Martian Race is a well written fast paced Mars Sci Fi novel using pre existing technology of TODAY to get to Mars ( read The Case For Mars by Robert Zubrin (5 stars) to learn about the Mars Direct approach). It CAN be done and a Mars mission in the near future may actually be similar to this.As a life Mars Society member I found the book Martian Race an enjoyable read and fascinating how pre existing technology and using Dr Zubrin's Mars Direct Approach is used to get to Mars in this great Sci Fi novel. 5 stars EnjoyRead moreRead less
