This is an excellent read that shows Kathleen Woodiwiss’ talent as a writer. The novel begins with a newly wedded couple making their way home to meet the family. The steamer they’re traveling on is attacked by river pirates and both the hero and heroine are lead to believe that their mate was killed.The characters are introduced through flashbacks and the reader shares in the agony of their losses. As the story develops, there is a fire set by an arsonist, a serious carriage accident, and lots of drama. As a result of the accident, our heroine has amnesia and the story develops around her figuring out who she. Her husband and another man insist they’re both married to her and she knows neither of them.In the end everything works out, but it’s a page turner throughout this story.
