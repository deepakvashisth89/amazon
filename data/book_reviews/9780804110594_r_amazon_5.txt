In a brilliant history and analysis of the sexual revolution in this country, Talese deftly exposes the sanctimony of the American political Right, and to a lesser extent, the hypocrisy of some of the pioneers and practitioners of that revolution.  Very thoroughly researched and coherently presented.  Nobody - not even Hugh Hefner himself - gets away unscathed.  In fact, toward the end, the author details his own participation (cloaked as research for the book) and its ramifications to his personal realm.I would highly recommend this to anybody who came of age between 1960 and 1980.
