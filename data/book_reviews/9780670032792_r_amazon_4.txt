This is my second or third journey through Joseph Gangemi's "Inamorata" and each time is more enjoyable.  What's not to like from beginning to end? Start with the title!  I was once reading the book by myself in a diner and the waitress came up, saw the book, and asked: "What is that beautiful word and what does it mean?"  She was right!  The word "Inmorata" is as mystical as the narrative.  Gangemi does a a great job with the characters...his treatment of the Spiritualist/seance movement is both skeptical and sympathetic...Philadelphia is as much as character in this book as any of the humans and he obviously knows the city and its history so well.  Read it in anytime, but definitely read it again in December like I do...you can practically feel the chill of the winter city and if you don;t, the story will give you the chills!  But it's the LOVE STORY...one, two, three, four, five different way, that will really get to you. A 5-star effort...I only wish we had more from Gangemi to read!Read moreRead less
