At first I found the style--excerpts from tape recordings--difficult to follow. Once I got past that--and began to follow the flow--I really enjoyed the book. It seemed that the author was making a case against/questioning the multiplicity of agencies gaining audio access to individuals' lives via sound recordings. And highlighting the fact that there was no connection/sharing of information among the various law enforcement agencies. Had the agencies had access to all the information a major crime would have been prevented. Interesting way to tell the story.Read moreRead less
