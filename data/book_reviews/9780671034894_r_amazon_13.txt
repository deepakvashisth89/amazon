I have read a number of the Dirk Pitt books, enjoying the originals but not so many of the more recent ones. I was therefore looking for something new to try and found a Clive Cussler writing partner in Craig Diego. It was an interesting story until towards the end where it became a little too unbelievable even for the genre. Still enjoyed it though and I am going to read the rest of the Taft books. It is very Dirk Pitt in essence, not as strong as some of the earlier books but an enjoyable change if you are a little worn out of the Pitt series.Read moreRead less
