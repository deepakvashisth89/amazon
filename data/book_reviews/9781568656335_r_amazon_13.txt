I think that I am too old for true crime stories because I find it very depressing to read over and over how jealous and ruthless people can be. Jerry Harris is a very successful business man who juggles several businesses at once, shifting money from one to another that needs a cash influx. He is always within the letter of the law except he has no driver’s licence. Rule says that he has great intuition about people and that is how he knows after three failed marriages that Susan is the love of his life. That leaves hanging how he keeps being friends with a man who is so jealous of him that he ultimately kills him and how another friend gets him to buy land and a business both of which fail to be what was promised. The big question in my mind is why didn’t he have life insurance or a will to protect Susan when she ultimately becomes his widow. After all he was 18 years older than her and his business affairs were very complicated. However ultimately this is a true story of a supposed friend betraying Jerry in the worst possible  way.
