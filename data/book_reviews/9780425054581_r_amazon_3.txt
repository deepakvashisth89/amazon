One of the very best space operas ever written in the science fiction genre.  No science fiction library should be without the entire series as it provides a fan with a reference to the basis for what has come to be known as the golden age of science fiction.  Adventure and romance, with just the right touch of science, that fired the imagination of a generation of science fiction fans.  While many advances in real life science are not touched on in these books enough of the stories basic far flung imagination makes the transition that they can still excite and entertain even the most discriminating science fiction fan of today.  Epic scale good verses evil battles drive the story lines in every book in the series.  Larger than life characters fill the pages.  Of course the reader must deal with the biases that reflect the time period and attitudes common to the period in which the author wrote, but does not detract from the overall experience of reading what is unquestionably one of the masterpieces of science fiction.  Buy the book, and the rest of the series, for the experience of getting to know what formed the foundation of the science fiction genre.  Enjoy this book and the rest in the series for the pure pleasure of doing so.
