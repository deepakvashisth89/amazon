I just finished the book yesterday and I couldnt put it down. Its a great story of Love, honesty and trust. When Dr. Hart arrives in the west he is lonely since his fiancee has stayed in the East. His yearning for  friendship and companionship drives him to the bording house where he and  the owner Jane Sparks Form a close friendship. Where the story goes from  there is on a long adventure of mixed emotions and torn trusts. When the  Children from Children's Aid arrive looking for a home things really get  moving.
