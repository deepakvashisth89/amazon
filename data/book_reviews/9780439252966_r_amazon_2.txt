My 6yr old son has been reading these since Kindergarten when he was heavily reliant upon pictures to help him infer the words and meanings of words. Even now, he still loves these Ricky Ricotta books and has finished most of the series. Unfortunately he can devour one whole book in about an hour which makes them expensive reads at $6 a piece. But I try to encourage his love of reading so I don’t mind so much. He loves the exciting story lines and is so proud that he can finish them so quickly. He also loves reading them to his 3yr old little sister and she loves listening them as well. I highly recommend these books for 5-8yr olds who are struggling to develop a love for reading.
