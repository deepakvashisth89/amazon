I discovered this novel after discovering Violette Leduc. I adore this book for its fleshing out of Leduc's life. It gives a lush and warm sense of her life and her version of madness, as well as the narrator's  investigation into her own Jewish heritage. In some places, Zackheim's  writing mirrored Leduc's.
