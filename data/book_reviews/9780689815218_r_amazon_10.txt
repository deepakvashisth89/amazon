Read this with my 9 year old daughter who loved it. Caddie is a wonderful lovable character. We have read the entire Little House on the Prairie Series more than once and this gave a fresh take on the same time period and made for great discussions comparing lifestyles and finances, gender roles, etc. We loved that Caddie had a friendship with the Indigenous people and learned from them instead of fearing them. She also ran wild with her brothers, a nice change from girls cooking and cleaning and being proper all of the time and got up to some fun and hilarious adventures. My daughter is also a red head so there was an instant connection to this family. Happy there are more books in the series so Caddie's story is not over.Read moreRead less
