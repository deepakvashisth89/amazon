This, like most of Townsend's work, is dark. There were things that Townsend wanted to comment on that she couldn't do with Mole, so she has made this protagonist a homeless woman on the run. However, as usual she is  on form skewering the middle class, the upper-middle class granola  crunching Gucci Socialists, and a society that allows people to fall so  far. There are less laughs in this book, and there is a lot more darkness.  But it is still Townsend, and her fans should give this one a try.Read moreRead less
