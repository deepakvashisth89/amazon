I haven't read any other Devon novels but this one is superb.  The detail is amazingly apt; usually I don't care for that close an accounting of the air, the light, the skin tones, the landscape, the routes of travel.  But Devon makes sense of them, pulling you so far into the story you think you're standing near the characters.  There is no mystery about who the bad guy is but the mystery about what will happen to his targets and then to him is one that makes the reader hold her breath till the end.  Very readable and satisfying.  The prose is superb.  The story is convincingly told and so full of suspense it's hard to put the book down.Read moreRead less
