This is a remarkable biography of two women who had a lifetime relationship which literally made and altered history. The choosen letters captured not only the personalities of the 2 women but the history of an era and was a forerunner for women’s advancement in the world . The writing flows with just enough information to link the letters together. Much of their relationship remains a mystery as we don’t know what they did during time spent together, but clearly their relationship buoyed ER to levels she otherwise would not have attained which changed history.
