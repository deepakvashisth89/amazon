I don't know why anyone would give this book less than 5 stars. It is an authentic, muddy, engrossing mystery set in England during World War II. It is not a "war novel", although of course any book set in that time is a war novel in some way. This instead is a "legal thriller" with an admirable hero who is forced to choose between his code of honor and his career, while fighting a cover-up by his superiors. I'd recommend this book to anyone who likes mysteries with a lawyer as the "detective". If you like this, check out his Defender as well.Read moreRead less
