Over the years I have read a number of Gore Vidal books, but this is the best. The depictions of some of the gay men the protagonist meets are not unlike many gay men you can meet today. Yet I was shocked by the ending. There are other landmark gay books, like "Better Angel" which also portray gay men without judgement, but the only thing this book and "Angel" have in common is the perceived "happy ever after" goal for the two men, however the ending of this book has far more in common with "Brokeback Mountain". Published in the same year as the Kinsey Report on male sexuality, this book supports Kinsey's findings, that homosexuality was more common than anticipated, but also perpetuates the stereotypes of self-loathing, unable to commit men.
