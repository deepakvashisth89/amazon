This novel is the autobiographical story of the author's childhood years growing up during the early 20th century, and is a snapshot portrait of the dynamics of his family.  The author as "Bunny" is profiled in the first third of the book, followed by his older brother Robert, and lastly their father, all portrayed during a time of crisis in the family.  The story is poignant, the writing is lyrical, and the characters are memorable.  This would be an excellent choice for a book club.
