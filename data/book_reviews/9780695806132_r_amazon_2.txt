As a U.S. Navy veteran I found this book very interesting and informative.  I was only a baby during World War ll but  I did serve in the Vietnam era aboard the USS Oklahoma City which was a guided missile cruiser. I picked up this book for free at a library recently and even though it is not a recent edition (1975), I still found the information very intriguing.This book does not contain all the ships involved in the fighting during World War ll, but it does list quite a few of the vessels that played a valuable role in the history of the war.  This book is organized into six sections.  Section one covers the Battleships, which include The Yamato, The Admiral Graf Spee, The Scharnhorst, The Bismarck, H.M.S. Duke of York, U.S.S. Alabama, U.S.S. Washington, H.M.S. War Spite, U.S.S. Texas and The Kongo. The second section deals with the Aircraft Carriers. They are The H.M.S. Ark Royal, The U.S.S. Enterprise, The U.S.S. Hornet, The U.S.S. Lexington, The Akagi and the Zuikaku. Third section deals with the Cruisers. They include the U.S.S. Indianapolis, The Mogami, the U.S.S. Boise, The H.M.S. Ajax, the H.M.S. Belfast and the Prinz Eugen. The fourth section only tells about two Destroyers, The U.S.S. Johnston and The H.M.S. Saumarez.  The fifth section covers three Submarines: The U-47, The U.S.S. Cavalla and the H.M.S. Thunderbolt. The last section only covers two Escorts, which are the H.M.S. Jervis Bay and the U.S.S. England.  I also found the Chronology of the War at Sea section to be very informative.In conclusion, even though this book is not a comprehensive study of all the ships during World War ll; nevertheless, it does contain some very interesting material on some of the fighting vessels.  I found this old book to be a good read.Rating:  4 Stars.  Joseph J. Truncale  (Author:  The Samurai Soul: An old warrior's poetic tribute)Read moreRead less
