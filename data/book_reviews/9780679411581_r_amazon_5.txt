This book details battles in the early days of the Vietnam war and the actions and heroism of those who participated. A key factor is that it was co-written by Lt. Col. Hal Moore who was commander of his 7th Cavalry unit, and a journalist, Joseph Galloway, the only journalist on the ground in the conflicts described.  The story is compelling and sad at the same time, as the authors describe the grueling conditions they faced from their individual perspectives. I first read this book many years ago, and had the unique honor of corresponding with Joseph Galloway who was a wealth of information and a very intelligent and compassionate man. I have given copies of this book as gifts, and I recommend it to anyone who wants a clear and honest perspective from these two brave and heroic men who survived the horrifying conditions of war.Read moreRead less
