Mr Eidson turns in a third well written and thoroughly engaging story  which will transport you in time to the hard life of the frontier. I found  the book both interesting and profoundly moving in parts - and always  entertaining as well as informative. Great literature: well, no. A great  read - and one which does not resort to mega-block-buster fantasy - yes!
