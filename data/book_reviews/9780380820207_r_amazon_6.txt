The Praxis (Dread Empire's Fall, #1)by Walter Jon WilliamsI feel this book is a science fiction tale showing the end of the Roman empire on a universal scale. The Shaa like the Roman empire has brought the universe to peace, or Praxis. They conquered many worlds. Their powerful weapons easily overpower the worlds they find through the wormholes of the universe. The Latin term may be the aspect that brings me to see the stories this way. That Roman peace had been hard fought by Roman military superiority. Like the Roman empire, The Shaa have died out. Historically like the Roman Empire the vacuum of their absence leaves the Universe in a problematic power struggle. The conquered societies, begin to find that Peace is not able to be held in this vacuum. Walter Williams brings two remarkable characters into this struggle. Caroline "Gredel" Sula and Gareth Martinez, both struggle to find their place of the universe of Praxis. The war may be the way they are able to excel in their futures. Both characters have detailed pasts that show the social and political struggles of the Shaa Empire. The reader finds themselves in their struggles, not only the struggle of political power, but the dynamic struggle or epic space battles, even those with whom they barely survive.
