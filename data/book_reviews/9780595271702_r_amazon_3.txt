This book, 'Wicca 333: Advanced Topics in Wiccan Belief' is great. There are, like many things, pros and cons to the book.Pros:*Well organized ( both in writing and the digital version)* Well documented. It's clear that the author did her research on the topic and brings interesting views and opinions to the table. She doesn't let popular belief take away from her research. An example would be her analysis of 'The Wiccan Rede' and its origins.* Good jumping point to find other books and web pages to read. Because the book is so well documented, you can go to her bibliography from a section of the book that you found interesting and find where she got her information, and research it yourself.Cons:* Dense. For people who know their stuff and are strong in their understanding of the English language. For some, this can be a difficult read.* Title could be misleading. I do think this book is great, but for some it might come off as the 'advanced' book people are looking for in the practices. The topics covered in this books delve into the beliefs and disproves or proves the validity of statements and beliefs commonly held. There may be practices in the latter parts of the book ( have not 100% the book yet), but for the majority it is essentially an in-depth study of the religion and what is taught in the various '101' books. This is not an 'Advanced Practices' book, but one of coming to understand viewpoints and beliefs. Know this at least before you buy.Read moreRead less
