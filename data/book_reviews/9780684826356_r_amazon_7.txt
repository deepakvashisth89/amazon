This book is a mandatory follow-up to "Survival in Auschwitz". Even if you have seen the film version ("The Truce" 1997), as I have, you still need to read this book. They are similar and dissimilar, both great parts of this amazing true story. Primo Levi is a master and this trilogy (Survival in Auschwitz, The Reawakening and Moments of Reprieve) are his finest arts.
