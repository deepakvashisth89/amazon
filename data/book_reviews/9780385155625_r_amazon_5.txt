Typical comic Charlotte MacLeod novel. It is part of the Peter Shandy series and the setting and recurring characters are silly, but enjoyable. There is not a lot of character development, but there isn't time for that in a short mystery, and the author manages to introduce people in such a way that a few sentences lets us form a pretty clear picture of the individual. It is sufficient for this length of book. I read it the first time about twenty years ago and enjoyed it enough to order it from Kindle for a re-read.Read moreRead less
