The Aftermath presents the scenario of an apocalyptic comet strike and a cruise ship full of the world's best engineers at precisely the opposite side of the world, off the coast of the world's resource-richest land. The ship also includes highly trained military personnel. Believable? Not.But the way the engineers approach the task of reconstructing a technological society is believable and significant. They evaluate the resources available, systematically outline the steps needed for reconstruction, and vigorously pursue the task. It makes me think.Glen E.
