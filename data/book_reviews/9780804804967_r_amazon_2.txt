I LOVE this book. It helped me tremendously and was perfect for the stage of learning I was at. The book is structured brilliantly. It gives easy-to-remember stories about how the Kanji were made to help you remember them and what the image depicts, and you’ll learn the components that make up complex characters which will also help you remember their meanings. The example words combine the Kanji you’ve learned so far into compound words that will help you learn the pronounciation AND remember their meanings (氷 is ice, 山 is mountain, and 氷山 means iceberg.) I worked through it slowly and made flash cards with all of the kanji and example words.I wouldn’t recommend this book to anyone who is a complete beginner. It glosses over too many fundamental concepts of the writing system to be useful to someone with no experience with it. I would recommend it to someone who is well versed in hiragana and katakana and has some Kanji experience is ready to improve fluency.
