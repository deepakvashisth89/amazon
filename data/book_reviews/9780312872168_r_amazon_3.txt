Definitely a strong entry by Ben Bova in his Grand Tour series. A lot of hard sci-fi action and most of the book actually takes place in the atmosphere and on the surface of Venus. The science is up to date with most of what is known about the planet and planetary science of Venus. The characters are mostly updated from prior characters in the Grand Tour, but one older character from the Asteroid Belt plays a prominent role. The novel can be read standalone, but maintains good continuity with previous Grand Tour novels. Not so much endless political intrigue, this is a basic, satisfying, hard sci-fi novel.Read moreRead less
