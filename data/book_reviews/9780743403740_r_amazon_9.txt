This is so far the best Star Trek book i've read (I started reading the original series in the order amazon provides, so right now i've read about 7 books). It's got all that makes Star Trek great in my opinion. Intergalactic diplomatic chess games to prevent war, different species and how they compare to one another (humans compared to Vulcans, Vulcans compared to Romulans, Klingons compared to Humans) and the overall theme that things will turn out great if enough great people work towards that goal (if only real life was closer to that ideal...). and all of that makes for a hollywood worthy story that really grabs your attention and makes you want to read on and on because it is so thrilling. 5/5, great read!Read moreRead less
