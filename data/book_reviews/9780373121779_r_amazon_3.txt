First, whoever designed the cover of this book needs to be shot. I don't think I've ever seen two weirder looking people on the cover of a romance book.Second, how can she be his mistress if she never has sex with him. Don't get me wrong, at page 185 we find out that Slade and Daisy get married and they end up with 4 children, so I know at some point they had sex but it just never happened during this story, while she was living with him. I could be wrong, but doesn't a woman have to have sex with a man to be considered a mistress. So that title was as deceptive as they come and insulting as well.Daisy is a woman trying to get through life one day at a time. After the demise of her marriage and loss of her baby (Jenny) she seems constantly on the fringe of having a nervous breakdown. After hitting her with his car, Slade offers Daisy a job as his son's (Francesco's) nanny. She would then have an income and a place to live while she recuperates. So she's his son's nanny and she never has sex with him. She is not a mistress.Because of her baggage, Daisy refuses to get involved with anyone, but Slade is determined to have her. Slade's former mother-in-law, realizing Slade's feelings for Daisy and suspicious of her role in Slade's life tries to cause friction by bringing Daisy's ex-husband to a party they were having for Francesco. The ensuing confrontation brings a lot of issues to a head and I'm actually happy for that scene just to have something semi-exciting occur in this book.This is one of those stories which is fine if you find yourself with a couple of hours with nothing to do. Stuck at the airport, maybe. Other than that, it's not worth the time to read it.Read moreRead less
