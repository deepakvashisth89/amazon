Loved this book.  The characters were believable, it was well written.  Duncan Malone was imprisioned for an accident and when he was let out, after serving his sentence, he went straight back to his small town to see his Mum and be with her, even though she was unconscious.  Franki was a total doll, the only one who didn't have a small mind to go with the rest of the town.  This book is all about Duncan's fight for acceptance by the town and to accept that he is worthy of Franki's love.  Definitely one to read and I will keep my copy on my kindle so I can read it over again.Read moreRead less
