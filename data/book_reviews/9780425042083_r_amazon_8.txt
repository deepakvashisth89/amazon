I was hoping for more than one River World story and was disappointed.  However, I could have been okay with that if some of the stories weren't actually rather offensive.  One involved the hero of the story, an old man, raping a number of women in the nursing home where he lived.  I know that it was a different time when the stories were written, but that kind of thing is not okay in any time.
