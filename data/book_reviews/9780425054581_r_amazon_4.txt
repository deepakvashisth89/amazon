This book rather than Triplanetary (the 1st in the series ) is so amazingly deep, captivating, and will not disappoint. It does get a bit wordy at times, but this epic story of the Lens and the origins of the fledgling Galactic Patrol the mysterious Arisians and their gift of the Lens to a human race struggling for survival against an enemy that has the active support of a race of mentally powerful beings similar to the benevolent Arisians sets the stage for dramatic thriller space warfare with weapons and technology unlike any found in other author's novels. Coupled with strategy and political in fighting so complex and fascinating will grab you and not let go even when you have finished reading it. You want more and lots of it.What is even more remarkable is the authors creative genius. I cannot conceive of of a mind capable of writing such a brilliant and immensely entertaining novel.
