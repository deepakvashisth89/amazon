"Werther" was enormously successful when it was first published, but Goethe soon came to regret writing it, and even distanced himself from the whole Romantic movement. It is a thinly disguised account of Goethe's own young love for a girl who likes him very much, but who is perfectly happy with her fiance/husband, the pain it caused Goethe, and how he thought about resolving it.
