Kane and Abel has been one of favourite books written by Jeffrey Archer. I first read it 40 years ago after being encouraged to read it by my late-mum. While I was recently re-reading it, I discovered that there are two books that follow it. Well, upon learning that I could only do one thing - immediately buy The Prodigal Daughter.The Prodigal Daughter is a curious read . I have to be honest and say that I enjoyed it even more than the first book. Maybe because it felt as I already knew the characters so well, it was as if I was meeting an old friend.Whereas the first book followed both Kane & Abel, this book mainly focuses on Abel's daughter, Florentyna Rosnovski's as we follow her journey from childhood into and through her adult life.It is a gripping page-turner of a book. The only complaint I have about it was that the first quarter of the book felt as if was going over old ground, retelling scenes that had already been told in the first book. Granted some of it was from Florentyna's POV but to me it just felt repetitive. However, that is only a minor grip.I found Florentyna to be so engaging as a character. I couldn't help be enthralled by her journey as she made her way up the political ladder. I have to admit I went through a whole series of emotions while reading this book - cheering and rooting for Florentyna as she continued on her course toward the position she had craved since childhood, striving to overcome every obstacle placed in her way. I even shed a tear at the sudden death of a major character later on in Florentyna's life.Definitely would recommend reading this book if you are a fan of Jeffrey's Archer's storytelling or are just fan this type of genre.I look forward to reading the next book in the series - What Shall We Tell The President.Read moreRead less
