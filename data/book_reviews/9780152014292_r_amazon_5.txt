I adore this book. I've had it since Child #1 was born, and now it's being used with Child #2. Both have adored it, and for Child #2, it's the only book he'll sit peacefully for and listen to. I sing the song (it's hard not to!) and for both children, this is one of the first songs they learn. The backstory/subtext told in Marla Frazee's book is outstanding, and the pictures are beautifully rendered.
