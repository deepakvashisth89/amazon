I’ve worked my way through this series. Generally each one is an improvement on its predecessor. They get nearly perfect a few books after this. “Hanging Garden” is more topical than most. It concerns itself — in part — with bringing to light an “underground railroad” for Nazis fleeing retributive (at the hands of Israelis) justice for their tangible and moral crimes. But this is not Rebus’s central investigative concern. And because of that plot element, the plot and the overarching investigation become overly diffuse. A dissatisfying quasi resolution added to my frustration.
