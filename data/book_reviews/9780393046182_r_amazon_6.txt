Wow....this is a very powerful book!  As soon as I finished reading Oyster I turned back to the first page and began reading it again. During the first reading I found myself going to an online dictionary to translate a few words and phrases. In every case it caused my mind to dig deeper to understand the mesmerizing characters.One must read this book carefully or details will be missed, very important details.  Ms Hospital gripped me with her first paragraph of this book. The underlying current of evil, both actual and implied,kept me holding my breath while the religious fanaticism repused me and kept me reading at the same time.  Does this seem complicated?
