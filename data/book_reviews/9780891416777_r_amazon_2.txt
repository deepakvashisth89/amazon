"We were never there" is the motto of T-Berg, (among others), otherwise known as Field Station Berlin. This book is a very accurate chronology of events that intrigued me due to the fact it is written from "the outside".  The inside story, inside operations at "The Hill", looked very different. The discovery of people like "Paul", known as "Randy" in our world, and "Hall", was devastating to personnel who worked tirelessly in a very unthankful job. To learn that someone who shared a desk with you, ate in the same mess hall, slept in the same barracks was INTENTIONALLY selling information to the very enemy we were fighting was not only devastating to us personally, but professionally made us forever suspicious of everyone else in our lives from then on.  "Just because you trust someone, does not mean that person is trustworthy."  This book dovetails into the book series I am writing, "Six Days to Zeus".  Book five, due for publication this year, sub-titled "Berlin: Back to the Beginning", documents the same stories from the inside. From "OUR" side. The impact of these traitors will never really be known. But suffice it to say, this book will make the reader think....twice...  Great read.
