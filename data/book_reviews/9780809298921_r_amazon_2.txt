This book is a wonderful, easy, and enjoyable read!  It chronicles the life of George Mallory in his quest to climb Mount Everest, as well as the search that took place decades later to find his body.  The book does a great job of describing the history of Everest expeditions and the many attempts to conquer the mountain.
