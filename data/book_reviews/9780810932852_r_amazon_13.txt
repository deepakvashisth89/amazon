Agreeing that this book has neither everything, nor everything as it should be for the purist, I found it an enjoyable, informative book about Jane Austen's books and Regency country life.  The pictures are a delight: so many, and in colour.  It's not a 'scholarly' book; but just right for the reader interested in her stories and curious about the setting and manners. A good foundation for reading Jane's books and other books about the period.
