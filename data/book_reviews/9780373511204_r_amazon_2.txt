Delaney is a college professor who gets a makeover to try to land the assistant director position of the English department where she teaches.  Her father is also the Dean of her department and has always treated her with disdain.  Delaney secretly does book reviews and  works part time on a TV show where she critiques books along with her co-host Sean.  Delaney's comments about a popular author's book draws the attention from the author, Nick Angel.  Nick is hot and attractive and places a bet with Delaney that creates competitive sexual tension.  Delaney criticizes Nick's erotica suspense novels because they lack emotional depth.  Delaney and Nick discover their own emotional attraction during the ensuing competition.
