Philip Jose Farmer (1818-2009) was a prolific writer in the science fiction field. Fortunately he was not one to avoid controversial subjects. I particularly enjoy reading his perceptive fictional speculations concerning organized religion and the afterlife. In addition to this title I would recommend to the interested reader The Riverworld series, and his novels "Jesus on Mars" and "Inside, Outside".Father To The Stars consist of five stories:The Night of Light - 1957 - Magazine of Fantasy and Science Fiction - 80 pgsA very strange story concerning gods and visions on the planet appropriately named Dante's Joy. John Carmondy, before he saw the light and his conversion, revealed that he was a psychotic murderer. On Dante's Joy he keeps seeing visions of his murdered wife while in the hire of two eccentric priests.A Few Miles - 1960 - Magazine of Fantasy and Science Fiction - 44 pgs John Carmondy is a lay brother of the order of Saint Jairus studying at the monastery of Our lady of the Fourth of July. The Superior orders him to travel to the planet Wildenwooly and report to the local Bishop. Having no funds John stumbles around ending up have an egg planted on his chest by an alien bird, a horowitz, from the local zoo.Prometheus - 1961 - Magazine of Fantasy and Science Fiction - 64 pgsFollowing up on "A Few Miles" Prometheus begins with the following sentence: "The man with the egg growing on his chest stepped out of the spaceship". John Carmondy in exchange for passage to Wildenwooly agrees to assist a scientific expedition to the planet Feral. John will mix in with the semi-intelligent avian Horowitz population until the egg hatches. Things get interesting when John exchanges his monk robe for a fake feather bustle and headdress.Father - 1955 - Magazine of Fantasy and Science Fiction - 94 pgs.John Carmondy is now a priest of the order of Saint Jairus. He is accompanying Bishop Andre when the space ship they are traveling in is forced to land on the planet Abatos. The planet turns out to be an Eden with a god like being "Father" that can resurrect the dead. The two priest and the ship's captain must decide if  "Father" is a force for good or evil.Attitudes - 1953 - Magazine of Fantasy and Science Fiction - 24 pgsFather Carmondy rescues a sleazy gambler, Roger Tandum, from an alien game of chance."Father To the Stars" collects all five of Farmer's John Carmondy stories. These related stories were published very early in Farmer's writing career and mirror themes and ideas that are fully matured in his 5 volumes Riverworld series.  This book has only been published twice in 1981 and 1988, both in paperback. Providentially editions are available for purchase on Amazon.
