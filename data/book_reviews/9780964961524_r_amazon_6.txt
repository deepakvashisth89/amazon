I am still in high school and was recommended this book by another friend who shares my interest in WWII history. I could hardly set this book down! As I read this book I understood why Mr. van der Geest titled the book the way he did. The hardships, and victories that he experienced throughout this book were incredible! Not only did he have this experience first hand but 40 years later he shares it with others because he wants people to understand what happened and that it was very real.
