I have felt a couple of the Davenport stories had been distasteful so I had laid off of them for awhile but was glad I came back to read this one.  The story had a sordid side but it was kept just enough below the surface to add an edge to the reading but not make it unappealing.  Lucas is always great and he normally manages to make his fair share of friends and enemies and this story is no different.
