A well rounded story that follows the journey of townie Harry out to the country to spend some time with his grandfather.  Useful for transport topic as he travels in many ways to and from the city, useful for making comparisons between the town and the country but best of all it focuses on relationships and families.  Harry is homesick but a lamb helps him come to terms with this.  I have used it with my reception class with great success but would also use it with Y1.  I would recommend this book to teachers, parents, grandparents.Read moreRead less
