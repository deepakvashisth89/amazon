The first book in this wonderful series is a beautiful romance.  This author writes with such power you feel you are in Wales in 1814.  Nicholas Davies, Earl of Aberdare, had a gypsy mother.  When he was 7 and Marta knew she was dying she brought him to his grandfather.  The only heir to the earldom, his grandfather despised him for his tainted blood.  His childhood was far from happy - he was taught by the local Methodist minister until he was sent to school.  There he forged life-lone friendships with 3 other boys - all from society.  When his grandfather arranged a marriage, Nicholas accepted, hoping to win his approval.  But all went wrong quickly.  The outcome was his grandfather died of a heart attack and Nicholas' wife died in a carriage accident the same night.  Nicholas was found with his young grandfather's second wife - and all the wrong conclusions were drawn.  He left the country for years and comes back to Wales with the thought of breaking the entail so the estate can be sold.  Enter Miss Clare Morgan, daughter of the minister who taught Nicholas as a young child.  She demands he save the village and make the mines safe for his people.  He strikes a bargain with her - he will do so only if Clare agrees to live with him for 3 months - letting the village and world think the worst.  Clare seeing no other way to help the people she cares for, accepts his challenge.  From the first day, they both are swept into circumstances first as adversaries then as friends and lovers.  What follows is mystery, friendship, passion and true love to touch the heart.  Ms. Putney has the power of intrigue and historic fact mixed in with an enchanting love story!
