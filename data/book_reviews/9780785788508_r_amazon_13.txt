I have always been a huge fan of Mary Higgins Clark. I thought I had read all of her books. This I understand was her  first suspense book and some how I have missed it. I found it totally gripping and there is no surprise that she carried on to be one of the top suspense writers of our time. I loved it couldn't wait to  finish it then was sorry I had.
