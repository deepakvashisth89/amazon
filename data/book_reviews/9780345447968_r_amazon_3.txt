Cassie is a suburban Long Island housewife who gets a facelift and hopes this will return her husband to the man who once loved her. However, on returning home from a business trip with his mistress, he takes one look at Cassie and collapses from a stroke. The novel goes from one mess to another as Cassie lets events transpire around her and never takes control of the situation. I kept waiting for her to develop some backbone and really take over her husband's business and the legalities that come with his illness. However, she learns things about her husband, her children and the long-time mistress and never really seems to cope with the situation.  In the end all is wrapped up nicely, but it seemed to me that events just fell apart around Cassie and she never took charge.
