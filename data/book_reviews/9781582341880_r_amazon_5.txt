I admire Leavitt but agree with the reviewers who say this is not his best work.  I have read all of his fiction and especially liked the earliest works--Family Dancing and Lost Language of Cranes. This novel, however, despite the easy flow of the writing, did not stir my interest. The characters weren't fully realized--and I could not conjure feelings for any of them.  The plot was quick paced, but to what end?  So much time was covered so quickly that I had trouble staying fully engaged. Also, credulity was often stretched to the breaking point--I never before sensed this as a problen in Leavitt's writing.
