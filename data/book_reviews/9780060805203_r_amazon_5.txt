This is the 16th installment in the Nigel Strangeways mystery series by Nicholas Blake. While I admit that I have not read the entire series, I have read several. I enjoyed reading all that I have, except this one. For me, the tone was all wrong. Not a single character was likable, not even Strangeways, which left me cold. Additionally, the character interactions felt forced and unreal, not at all like the other books in the series. Finally, this one was way too obvious - easy to figure out, and grittier than I like. Nothing in the story struck a chord with me, which is unusual. Like I said earlier, I have enjoyed several other books in the series, just not this one.Read moreRead less
