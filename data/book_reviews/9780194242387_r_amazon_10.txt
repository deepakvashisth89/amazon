I read this years ago and fancied reading it again as I enjoyed several of John Christopher’s books (the aforementioned Death of Grass, A Wrinkle in the Skin and Empty World) as well as the Tripods TV series.The story is compelling and although possibly not scientifically plausible in terms of it’s actual premise, it nevertheless draws in the reader and the idea of civilisation collapsing quickly under the weight of a sudden and extreme cooling in the northern hemisphere is scary.The characters are well-rounded and the relationships within the story help to drive it forward although the sudden way that the relationships disintegrate are a little baffling and the language and attitude from some of the European characters towards the Africans is unpleasant to put it mildly.  That may well have reflected prevailing attitudes of the time so I guess one has to take it within historical context.All in all, a good book from an author who wrote some very good dystopian fiction.Read moreRead less
