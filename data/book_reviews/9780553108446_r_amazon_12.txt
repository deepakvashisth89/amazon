I had no idea what to expect when I started reading this book on the advice of a friend – and was hooked. Was getting along quite happily with Mike and Gally finding the old house – and then did a double take. What had I just read? The intimation that Fearney was remembering something personal 200 years previously!I couldn’t put the book down. It began ringing so many bells with me. I had not only been brought up in the belief of reincarnation, but had a deep bond with my father, of which my mother told me she, that she had told him when I was born, that she had felt completely excluded. Then 30 odd years later, I recognised my third child from the moment she opened her eyes, in a way I had not known my first two children, beloved and wanted as they were.Over the years, however, I’ve come to believe that we inherit ‘race memories’; or inherit brain patterns just as we inherit facial features (which could explain why so many ‘remember’ they were some particular famous person ie Ramases II, King Henry etc., – which could be simply because that person might well have spread his genes around!) Just a thought.
