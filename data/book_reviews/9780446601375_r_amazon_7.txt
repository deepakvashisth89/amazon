McBain starts off with a few murders, throws in a little sex, mixes in more than a few twists and turns, pops it in the oven and when it is finally done, ices it with half a surprise ending. I say half, because part of the conclusion begins to surface fairly early in the story, the other half is a surprise. I gave it three stars only because the story line was very reminiscent of another McBain book I have read in the past. Since I have read almost every one of his books over a long period of time, I can't remember the title, but farm, beautiful wife, etc. formed a very similar scenario. All that being said, if you enjoy a good old fashioned murder mystery, you can't go wrong with this one.Read moreRead less
