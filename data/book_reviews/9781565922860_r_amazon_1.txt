All I needed was a good reference to figure out the differences between PHP and Perl.  This book did exactly that!  I was able to start programming within a day and I have referenced this book more times than I can count during my most recent development efforts.  The binding is nearly worn out!  I recommend this book to intermediate programmers that only need a small boost to get to work.
