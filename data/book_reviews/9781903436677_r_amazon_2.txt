I am a traditional orthodox Protestant minister who presently must support myself while caring for my congregation. One line of enterprise I have undertaken to pay the bills, as have other preachers before me, is to teach and tutor students. Working with a Korean high schooler on Shakespeare, I came across this title during Internet research on HAMLET.  Glad I ordered it! You could not desire a more comprehensive introduction, glossary, and commentary on this, possibly the greatest work of the Bard. Specially instructive in ferreting out nuances, double entendres and cultural references which 21st century goundlings might miss. The two-volume edition with all three recensions of the play gives great clarity to the problems of text involved, and helped me better appreciate the limits of the new Oxford Complete Shakespeare.
