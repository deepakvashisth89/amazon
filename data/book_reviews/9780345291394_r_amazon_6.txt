Excellent example of old-school scifi. Light on characterization and heavy on ideas. Fuel for quite a few reflections on the relationship between faith, knowledge, reason, and humanity. Plot and names tend toward the absurd, but this keeps the tone rather light and humorous and keeps the novel from being bogged down in its pretensions--which makes the reading brisk, almost too brisk at times.A group of robots on a planet at the edge of the known universe use human psychics to search for Heaven in an attempt to create the perfect faith. Add into the mix a variety of incomprehensible aliens and curious humans, and a conflict brewing over the direction of the robot religion (and possibly the fate of the cosmos). If that synopsis doesn't grab you, then there is no reason to read this book. If it does, then there is no reason not to.Read moreRead less
