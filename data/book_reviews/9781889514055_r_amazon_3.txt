This series is magical!!  I remember reading them when I was 11 and I devoured these books.  They helped sparked my imagination in fairy lands.  Even when our cousins were over, I'd be up in my room reading.  They are that good!  My Aunt had given them to me in the hopes that I would enjoy them, and I thoroughly did!  About a year ago, we passed along the boxed set to a family friend's daughter and she read the books and absolutely loved them!  The characters are fun and the illustrations are beautiful.
