It is hard for me to understand how anyone could give this book less than 5 stars but I respect the opinions of others. Of all the business books and management books I have read, this is an essential read if you are trying to grow to a medium or large organization with a desire to manage it well. In fact, great books such as The Hard Thing About Hard Things and The Road Less Stupid both reference Andy Grove and this book specifically because it was so influential in shaping the successful CEOs that wrote those books.If you are running a small business with 1 or 2 employees, you don't need to read this. This book deals with complicated matters pertaining to organizations at scale, however; the content is concise and very practical, even after all of these years.
