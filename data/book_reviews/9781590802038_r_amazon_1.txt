I watch politics closely and in this post 9/11 era we run a risk in removing rights and personal liberties just for the sake of "safety". This book presents a chilling scenario in which a group of Yale graduates form a secret society to try and bring about a "New World Order" in which liberty will be sacrificed for the elimination of war in a new global government. I've read other political thrillers such as Deception Point by Dan Brown, but no other thriller captures the current fears of out nation quite as well as The Plot does.
