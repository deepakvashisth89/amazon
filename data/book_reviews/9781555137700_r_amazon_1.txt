This was an interesting read.  Ginger is a pretty cool girl and really likable.  She isn't particularly religious, but her mother becomes a Christian while dating a Christian man.  Ginger plays little tricks on him because he's dating her mom.  When she finds out that they are getting married, all hell breaks loose.SPOILER:  In the end, Ginger converts to Christianity.  After hitting rock bottom, she is filled with forgiveness towards her mother, her future stepdad, and everyone around her.  Her grandpa says that this is God's love.  This could be viewed as a subtle way of introducing young readers to Christianity without a lot of scripture and law.  However, it could also be viewed as emotional highjacking.  Overall it's an entertaining story and good for young-preteen readers.Read moreRead less
