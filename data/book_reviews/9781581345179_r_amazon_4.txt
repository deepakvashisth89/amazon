Business for the Glory of God is a short but deeply insightful read that explores how everyday business practices, like ownership, profit, competition, and productivity, can honor God when done with integrity. Wayne Grudem does a wonderful job showing that business isn’t separate from faith, but rather an area where we can reflect biblical principles through our work.The writing is clear and approachable, making complex moral topics easy to understand. It’s the kind of book that challenges how you view money, success, and stewardship, reminding readers that faith and business can coexist beautifully.Whether you’re an entrepreneur, employee, or simply someone who wants to bring more purpose into your work, this book offers timeless wisdom worth reflecting on.
