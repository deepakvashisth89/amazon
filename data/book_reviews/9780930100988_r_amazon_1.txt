I confess at the outset that I am not an expert on poetry. I read a few C20 and C21 poets but do not have great breadth of understanding. I like Ferlinghetti, Kunitz, Frost and now Hughes and others. I like the narrative poets. Sutphen is a thorough pleasure and treasure, if seemingly, at first, regional (why do I care? How can anyone be no regional?) When she finishes an image you are swept into it. The real danger for me is that of envy--why did I not put those thoughts together? This poet is very well known although new to me.
