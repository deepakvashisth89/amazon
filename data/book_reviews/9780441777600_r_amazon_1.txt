This book is not good.  It's not Outright Terrible, and I never felt the urge to throw it across the room, but it's not good.  It has "Doc" Smith's name on it, but as far as I've been able to research, Doc had nothing at all to do with it.  And like the other "not actually written by Doc" series (the Family D'Alembert series), the writer has basically no idea of how to write like Doc.
