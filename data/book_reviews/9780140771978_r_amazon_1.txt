This work added so much to the actual reading of the novel.When I first read The Great Gadsby, it was the first time I read anything of Mr. Fitzgerald's work.  I liked the novel, but in truth did not see why it was considered so iconic.  Therefore I read all of Mr. Fitzgerald's completed novels in the order they were published.  Then I reread the Great Gadsby and then studied this fine work of Miss Parkinson.  I now have a much greater appreciation.of the novel.The Great Gadsby is fiction.  However it is fiction based on the a combination of details about the author's own life and the circumstances of the time period, society, and location.
