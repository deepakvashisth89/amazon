World War III between the US and China is winding down and some soldiers and other personnel are finally going home.  Logan is a soldier who was framed for crimes he didn't commit.  Julia is a nurse who takes care of Logan after he is brutalized by his superiors.On their way home, in an airplane, they are pulled into a parallel Earth called Heart.  It is a world controlled by women and men are drugged to suppress their violent tendencies.  People (usually men) who come through the gateway from Earth to Heart are kept on a farm and drugged.  However, the presence of women provides a dilemma for the women leaders on Heart because they consider women to be superior to men.Heart is being threatened by an alien species and so the Earthlings and the women of Heart work out a deal where the Earthlings will figure out a way to protect Heart.  In exchange the government of Heart will reverse the pull of the gateway and send the Earthlings back to Earth.Only, at the end of the book, the government of Heart tells the Earthlings they don't quite know how to send them back as it has never been done before.There is a mention of a sequel at the end of the book where Logan and Julie are going to try to return to Earth to clear Logan's name.Awesome book.  The chemistry between Logan and Julie is HOT, HOT, HOT but because this book was written back before erotica became mainstream it is not graphic.  Authors who write non-erotic really have to know how to build the attraction by means other than lots of s.x.There is another couple who also get their HEA in the book and it is sweet also.This author, Marilyn Campbell, has recently begun writing again and it is my greatest hope that the sequel will be released in the near future.  Ms. Campbell is an excellent author who tells her stories well.She also has the Innerworld series of books, my favorites were Topaz Dreams (Falcon is like a HOT Mr. Spock and Stardust Dreams, which are great futuristic ones also.
