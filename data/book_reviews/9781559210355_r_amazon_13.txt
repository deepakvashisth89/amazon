This is another wonderful book by the incredible Pearl S. Buck on China and in particular the last Empress of China.  After reading this book I went to Wikipedia and read some more about this woman who essentially ruled China for many years between the 1860's until the early 1900's.  Her influence over three of the last Emperors was unparalleled.  Ms. Buck has woven a romantic story into what is an already consummate tale of power, war and change in China in that era.  I know I am late coming to the table, but I am reading as many of Pearl S. Buck's novels about China as I can fit in right now.
