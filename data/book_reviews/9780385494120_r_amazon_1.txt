It took me about 3 hours to read this book after having read Kalakota and Robinson's 'ebusiness', which took me about one week. That's the difference. This isn't really a serious ebusiness book despite the  endorsements given on the flap by the great and good of Interet business.  It reads as if the writing of the book was scheduled for a couple of days,  predictably covers all the usual stories (Schwab, Amazon, Cisco, Dell) and  relies too much on a handful of interviews with executives from these  companies. Very much an introductory 'storybook' with some nice one-liners  but no real meat. Particularly disappointing that we don't get much  perspective from Mr Siebel himself who has written better elsewhere.
