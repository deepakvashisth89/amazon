This is a pretty good techno-military thriller.  The theme is pretty interesting if you are into Cold War politics.  Here, Russia is threatening NATO's northern flank, with an intimidating build-up of troops and tanks.  Its real goal is to create a bastion in the Arctic Ocean for its SLBM submarines, and to keep US submarines out of the Arctic.  The US challenges this by sending a new model submarine, the Imperitor, into the Arctic.  Much of this story is about the technological speculation as regards next-generation US subs.Mostly, it is all great fun and I enjoyed this novel.  I usually enjoy novels that involve rooting for the US Navy against its enemies, and this one was no exception.  RJB.Read moreRead less
