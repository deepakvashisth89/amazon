"Love Boats" by model, nutritionist, and cruise director Jeraldine Saunders is a breezy slice of life aboard classic cruise ships - all the humor, adventure, and romance.  Ms. Saunders was the real-life "Julie McCoy" - the first female cruise director, and a true pioneer.  She gives the reader an insider's view of life aboard cruise ships in the 60's and 70's.  Amid the entertaining anecdotes, Jeraldine has woven her own fascinating story.  This single mom showed true grit, working 7 days a week, almost 24 hours a day, year after year, on cruise ships where everything, it seemed, was the cruise director's job, from patching up injured travelers to keeping passengers calm during crises.
