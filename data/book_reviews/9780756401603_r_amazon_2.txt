This is a very early book by C.J. Cherryh; and she has improved a great deal since this effort.  For some time she was my favorite Sci-Fi author, one of the best in my opinion, and certainly for my  money the best at writing the mind-set of alien intelligence.  This novel shows that incipient talent, but does not really deliver.  Slow and pedestrian.  Her later work, particularly Cyteen and its sister novels, are much better and show a talent for creating mentally-driven tension unequaled by anyone in any genre.
