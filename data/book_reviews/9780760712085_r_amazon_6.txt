There are very few reviews of this book.  If there were more I suspect there would be more in the 2 -3 star range.  This book was written about experiences with Level 4 viruses decades ago and originally published in 1996.  There is a preface, a  forward and a prologue that bring the book  somewhat into the present with some interesting definitions of viruses in the two Appendices.  I found the book interesting and educational not only regarding the science but also the cultural and political aspects of hunting these viruses.  It is also made clear that these scientists are passionate about what they do and would have to be because the work is so dangerous and often deadly.I suggest reading other reviews. My one comment is that I read this in the Kindle version. It was filled with typos and content errors that I painstakingly corrected. There were at least several hundred.
