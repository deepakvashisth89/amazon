If you like swashbucklers, science fiction, stories rich in culture clash, and novels that make you examine morality anew you will love Pirates of the Thunder.  Follow Hawks and his crew of unlikely revolutionaries as they figure out interstellar travel in a 14 km long starship all the while being chased by Master Systems minions who will stop at nothing to silence them once and for all before they find and use the Five Rings of the Masters.Chalker is ingenious at taking the plot places that you would never suspect all the while keeping his characters interesting and more than one dimensional.  I am pretty good at guessing where plots of movies and books are going, but there were at least two times while reading this where I gasped at a plot twist.  This is not literature but is one of the funnest books I have read in a long tmie.  Highly recommended!Read moreRead less
