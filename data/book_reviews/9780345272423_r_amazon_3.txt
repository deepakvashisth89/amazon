The author is deceased, and the text has never been altered since its original publication in 1972, so some of its facts are outdated.  But the bulk of the book remains insightful and useful: nothing published since has invalidated Kocher's discussion of the moral stances and the nature of evil shown in LotR.  He also provides the best analysis ever published of the character of Aragorn, and what are still the only studies of the rare poems "The Lay of Aotrou and Itroun" and "Imram".
