The author was recommended by my wife's cousin who's family owned a cottage at Point of Woods, Connecticut (Hubbard's Point) and I am reminded of our visits at Uncle Jim and Aunt Ann's cottage throughout the first two books. I must say this is not my normal choice of reading but in deference to Mary Jane I gave it a read. Book 1 was a great read, book 2 damned near had me in tears by the end.
