I have so many conflicting emotions about this. It's a classic for a reason; even in translation Pasternak has a beautiful, melodious way with words that is at times dizzying. The story is complex--it's not just the story of Yurii Zhivago, it's the story of Russia itself, the complexity of the Bolshevik revolution, the people, the corruption, the harsh beauty of the land itself. I think the attention to descriptive detail is one of the best attributes of this book.On the same token, I found that I didn't always like our main characters. I found Lara tragically lovely, as one does, but I also found her frustrating. At times I understood them perfectly; at times I had no idea what they were going on about.
