If you're an English major and/or able to read fiction with a critical eye and also enjoy postmodern effects, this book does an amazing job teasing your brain! I could read it many times over and discover subtleties I hadn't noticed before. I read it for an English class and we had a lot of fun discussing this one as it was more widely understood and enjoyed from different angles. I also liked it because it was set in the slavery era and portrayed the time period in a way that put me there in the thick of it but at the same time let me know I was NOT REALLY there.
