Ladies, servant girls, black slave women, middle-class matrons and native American women ....these are the founding mothers, all of whom played  a crucial role but long neglected role in the economic, political, military, and social life of the colonial America.  Wives fought beside their husbands or widows ran the businesses and plantations and some women were known as camp followers who cooked, sewed and did laundry or whatever was needed to lend their hand in the War of the Revolution.  Freedom is not Free....never has been and we document that here through the description of colonial America.  We are forgetting our roots and the hardships that were given to make this country what some  take for granted today.
