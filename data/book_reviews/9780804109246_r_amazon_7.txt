Just not a style I enjoy. I made it through 12% before I realized I just didn't care enough about the plot or the characters to keep going. The descriptions of people and places was detailed and clear. But it was too much so, as it bogged down any action. It felt as if the author kept wandering off in various directions before remembering he had a plot to follow and getting back to what was going on.Abe Lieberman and his partner are introduced in the first chapter, after a long prelude describing the life and actions of someone who may or may not feature in the mystery. By the time Lieberman is introduced, I found the constant descriptions of everything around the characters, and their dialog, made the story feel as if it had slowed down to a crawl.Too much tell, not enough show for me.
