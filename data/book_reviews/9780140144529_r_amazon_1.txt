I read it in one night and cried for two days (No, I am not a relative).  This was my first Kerouac novel.  (I don't count The Hippos one co-wrote with Burroughs, though it was my first)  I think he wanted them read in chronological order, ideally, even though they were not published that way. He reminds me a bit of myself when it comes to his love of animals.  It's a rather short novel.  Like I said, I read it in one night.  Great book.
