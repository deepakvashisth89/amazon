If it's a book by Christina Dodd you just know it's going to be a great read. I have read every one of her books and loved them all. Her Ladies of Lantern Street are just as wonderful and romantic as her other books. Her hero come out of hiding after have been nearly killed, and thought dead by the world, not fully recovered from wounds he had suffered. Maimed as he is he joins with the heroine of the story to find the crazed killer hunting for her. Of course they fall in love and it's a bumpy road for them hunting a killer and stumbling over dead bodies along the way. Each of those bodies fills in a peace of the puzzle and our hero zeros in on the psycho behind what has been happening. Naturally our Heroine gets kidnapped by the madman and our Hero drops in at the last minute to save her.I leave it at this as I don't want to give away the plot.
