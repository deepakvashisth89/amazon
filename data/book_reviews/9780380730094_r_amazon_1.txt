I started with the first book in the series (The Bannerman Solution) and have just finished reading the last (The Bannerman Ghosts).  John R. Maxim has written a VERY enjoyable and entertaining series.  His main characters are fully developed and each book builds on the previous, so much so that you feel you know these people, even though they are retired assassins!  Would highly recommend.  I'm hoping he will continue the series past "TBGhosts".  I hate it when each book ends but especially the last one because I knew there wasn't another one waiting for me.Read moreRead less
