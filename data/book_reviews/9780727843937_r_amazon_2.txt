This is the novel that was the basis of the movie "Cape Fear".  As is often the case, there often significant differences between the movie and the novel.  I very much enjoy reading a novel and watching the movie for purposes of comparison and contrast.  Sometimes one is significantly better than the other.  Differences are very common.  This novel is not lengthy and is what I consider to be a "light read".  It is straightforward and not extremely complicated.  There is some, but not extensive, character development.  The author is able to keep and hold tension.  Frankly I read this novel faster than I wanted to, because I did get to the point that I simply did not wish to put it down.  If one wishes to study the author, one will find that there are some interesting semi autobiographical aspects of this novel.  I originally read this novel because I wanted to compare it to the movie, but I have become  interested in the author, and plan on reading more of his work.
