An historical fiction, but the only one I know of which is from the perspective of a Caribbean persona. It speaks from every part of the history of the islands, from a perspective of the sea, to the beach, the streams, the old plantations, the deep valleys, the hidden mountain springs and the tops of ridges. It is not about dates or epcohs. It emphasizes and reflects the islanders in how they think, how they move.
