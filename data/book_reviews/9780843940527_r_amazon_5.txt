I know what you're thinking, "If you thought Lovescape was so precious, then why didn't you give it 5 stars?".  Well, I'll get to that later.  Out of the 4 short stories in this novel, the one I adored the  most was "heart Craving".  If you've read this story, you can  sympathize with me when I tell you that I had tears in the corner of my  eyes.  Its one of  the most beautiful tales I've ever read.  "lovescape" was also my first introduction to the work of Sandra  Hill and Dara Joy.  Both write an extremely funny, sensuous, moving tale.
