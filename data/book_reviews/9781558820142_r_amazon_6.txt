Although renowned for his superb detective fantasies, Carr's masterpiece of history and detection (arguably the same philosophy) is his reconstruction of The Murder of Sir Edmund Godfrey.  Despite an obvious anti-Whig bias and his adoration of Charles II, his historical method is excellent, drawing a most dramatically colourful picture of Restoration England and weighing up the odds impartially.  The theories are brilliantly argued, and equally brilliantly demolished, and the final solution is quite convincing.  Superb.Read moreRead less
