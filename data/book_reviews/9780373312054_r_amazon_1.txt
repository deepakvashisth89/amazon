Mr. Darcy turned and smiled at her, "A gamester must live by the motto Style is All.  If you carry off the part, then you are the part.  If you make no notice of the state of your clothes, depend on it that no one will dare bring it to your attention."  And so it goes for Miss Helen Denville, a no-nonsense, on-the-shelf miss who is at the beginning of an adventure.Mistaken for someone else, she is approached by Mr. Darcy (yes, Mr. Darcy) to help him catch a mysterious man who sometimes appears as a woman.  He/she is either Italian (per Mr Darcy) or the Fugitive Frenchman (Helen believes).  Finally convinced to appear as his wife, the two embark on a journey to recoup something Mr. Darcy believes is his.This traditional Regency romance has a unique plot twist with some light suspense.  Boasting a memorable hero and heroine, the dialog between the two seems closer to that of an older married couple.  For the most part they get along and their strength is built with each other's help.  It is a clean romance with little sizzle or sexual tension but the word play between the two helps to make up for it.Read moreRead less
