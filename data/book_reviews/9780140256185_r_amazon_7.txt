This was an enjoyable little book I chanched upon at the supermarket.  Yes, it's predictable, (as Kirkus Review on its high horse spouts), quaint and all that.  Most books are of this kind.  So what, that doesn't diminish the fact that it's still good. I enjoyed the little garden tips, the description of the grassy plains, and the part about burnt toast coffe is interesting.  There's a coffee roasting store near where I live and when I drive by I'm struck by the scent of burnt toast.  Really! Go figure.
