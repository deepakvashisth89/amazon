I have to say this book by Ms. Crane has always been one of my all time favorites.  Also one of the first books I loan out to friends and family when they're looking for a quick, hilarious read.  (Quick warning, though... after loaning it out to my sister, she informed me she had to stop reading it on the subway, because she couldn't control her laughter and was beginning to feel slightly freakish having entire subway cars stare at her.)The characters felt like old friends and I couldn't put this book down once I started.  I've read almost every one of Ms. Crane's books, and I consider this one my favorite.
