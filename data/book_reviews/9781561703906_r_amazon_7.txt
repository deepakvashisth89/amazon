I've known I am a Lightworker from the moment I heard the word, just as when I first read Edgar Cayce I knew immediately that I was a Daughter of the Law of One. There are some things you take for granted and simply live, without having read the initial documents. To me, Doreen Virtue's "The Lightworker's Way" is an initial document. Part I of the book describes Doreen's life journey, how she was spiritually led to the challenges she overcame in order to be the talented superstar she is today. Her experiences are described in a way that is easy to follow, understand, identify with, and aspire to. She freely shares the resources which made a difference to her life. In Part II, she describes guidelines for the self-healing that is necessary to being an effective Lightworker. A Lightworker's mission is to be a ripple of healing in the world. Everything about a Lightworker's life needs to align with Highest Good, and that is the Lightworker's challenge--to live what she teaches.
