After reading Madeline Baker's Native American Romances and enjoying them I was hopeful that this would also be equally entertaining. However, the plot is weak, the characters one dimensional and as one of the other reviewer's mentioned you get no real feel for the background the action is taking place in. OK sex scenes are muted but the torture Jarrett went through certainly was not.
