For those who may not be aware, in 2024 Mattel re-released a physical reprint of "Kirsten Learns a Lesson" to accompany the re-release of the same outfit for the doll. This book was heavily revised to "correct" outdated depictions of Singing Bird (including the omission of words like "Indian" and "savage", plus minor things like the spelling of "tipi," vs "tepee"); to "humanize" Miss Winston; and includes changes which will require Mattel to retcon later text and illustrations in the books. The 2024 re-release also includes a preface apologizing for the cultural insensitivity of the 1986 original.As a Potawatomi, I actually can't stand this kind of woke textual revisionism. The reality is that the settlers did think of the native population as savages (and the other way around). The term "Indian" is historically appropriate for the 1850s. And trying to humanize Miss Winston takes away from both Kirsten's character arc as well as Miss Winston's--and the complicated reality, as children soon find out, that teachers aren't perfect. Neither are parents.The whole "lesson" Kirsten had to learn, apart from her schoolwork and learning English, was centered on her friendship with Singing Bird and overcoming the (again, historically-accurate) prejudices of her parents, and and uncle, as well as the other children and adults.Finally, I think it's important for children now to read non-woke "culturally outdated / offensive / whatever" materials from old literature--i.e. Peter Pan, or the Little House books, as just two examples--rather than being spoon-fed modern PC revisionism. Why? Because it shows them how people used to really think and speak and act. If you make a Swedish immigrant girl from 1854 woke--well--that completely defeats the purpose of the historical American Girls (and further besmirches Pleasant Rowland's original vision for the books and dolls--as if Mattel hasn't done too much of that already).Anyway, all I really wanted to say is: the Kindle version (for now, at least), is NOT the woke piece of revisionist nonsense that Mattel put out in 2024.
