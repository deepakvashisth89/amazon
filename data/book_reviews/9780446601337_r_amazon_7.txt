The mystery surprised me, and it had a good suspenseful ending, but it contained too much pornographic material. I don't know why so many modern mysteries have so much X-rated material. I liked the 87th novels by Ed McBain better, because they didn't seem so pornographic, for the most part. I also found it hard to believe that Mathew Hope could relate so much of the novel about the police detectives on a different mystery (a subplot related to main plot) in first person, when Mathew Hope WASN'T in those scenes--Mathew Hope related scenes in first person exactly, as the conversations were recorded exactly by Hope in scenes he DIDN'T witness first-hand, which seemed hard to believe he could relate so exactly. Good mystery with suprising result, and a lot of suspense at the end, the ending did catch me by suprise, but too much X-rated stuff, and it seemed strange that Hope could tell us exactly what happened, and who said what, so exactly in first person perspective, when he DIDN'T witness those scenes. I always like Ed McBain, but the other comments by other customers led me to believe that "Snow White And Rose Red" was better.
