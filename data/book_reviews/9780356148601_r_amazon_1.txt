Mike Foxwell has been growing his own fruit and vegetables, and producing food since he was a child.  An expert on the subject of self-sufficiency, he makes no assumptions about his readers in terms of prior knowledge, making this book a must for anyone in the initial stages of becoming self-sufficient.An informative, and sometimes terrifying introduction gives countless persuasive reasons for producing your own food.  His explanation of the chemicals added to manufactured produce, including the reason for their use and the possible effects of these chemicals on human consumers, plus other cost-cutting/quality reduction exercises used by profit-hungry producers is proof enough that this is one author who really knows what he is talking about.  Written in 1988, the truth of his assertions have become frighteningly apparent during the last 20 years.Each chapter begins with a more specialized introduction.  For example, the chapter, "Brewing Beer" explains the decline in the quality of beers over many years, the increased use of chemicals and their possible side-effects, the reduction in strength, et al.  This is followed up with an explanation of how the products (beer in this example) are made, what equipment is needed to make them yourself, time taken, problems you may encounter and their remedies and finally, mouth-watering recipes.Mike Foxwell acknowledges that to a complete novice, this idea of being self-reliant and producing your own food is a scary prospect, and so the numerous recipes he includes are easy (yet no less tasty).  Ingredients that may be unfamiliar to the reader are explained in terms of their origin and their purpose.  All recipes have been tested - on Mike himself, his mother and his father!A fantastic read and a perfect place to start for anyone who is looking to turn their back on the capitalist food manufacturing industry.Read moreRead less
