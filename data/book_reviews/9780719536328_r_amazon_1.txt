John Betjeman?  Spurned by some as being too low-brow and adored by scores of others. Compassion, wit, wisdom, tenderness and the ability to pen a magical turn of phrase were among his gifts. He had the ear, and understood, the heart beat of the people. He was the voice men and women on the street.  I am proud that he was chosen as the Poet Laureate in the kingdom of the my birth.  Tramping the dunes having taken the ferry from Padstow to Rock (Cornwall) one arrives at John's grave in the grounds of ancient St. Enodoc's church within earshot of winter's roaring waves -- a simple slate slab strikingly garnished with flowers gives eloquent tribute to the man so loved by so many.
