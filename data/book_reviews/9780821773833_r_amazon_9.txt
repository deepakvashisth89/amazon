This was an enjoyable book with a touching romance and a fast paced mystery. After agreeing to marry the sister of his dying friend, Gideon Duke of Stanthorpe returns home to find Sabrina, a beautiful but very pregnant bride. Sabrina has not met Gideon and is expecting to marry an much elder man. She needs to marry a wealthy man for the sake of her child. Suspicious of Sabrina Gideon poses as a commoner to discover Sabrina's true character. Is she simply a gold digger or is there more to her?If you are looking for light very readable romance this is recommended reading. My only gripe is the book description is unhelpful.
