The MMC & FMC come together in an effort to solve a mystery, however; they end up falling for each other and revealing things that they’ve never told another person. These revelations bring them closer together and help them fall deeper in love. I thoroughly enjoyed reading the story of Kane & Kayla. I should mention that if you read the reviews you might find several that mention “TOO MUCH SEX” and “HOT HOT SEX” and “I HAD TO SKIM OVER THE TOO FREQUENT SEX SCENES”.  This was NOT my take away. Sure, there were descriptions of Kane & Kayla having sex, but it was neither repetitive nor overdone. And, it certainly IMO did not take away from any of the storyline!  Besides, if you read a book that’s described as “steamy” - WHAT WERE YOU EXPECTING?!!  So, take those with a grain of salt. Enjoy!  I certainly did!  ☺️Read moreRead less
