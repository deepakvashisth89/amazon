Of what must be thousands of reviews of this book, I would guess that over 90% refer to Hawthorne’s lack of brevity, although perhaps without the sarcasm. However, I encourage the reader to “keep on truckin’” as it is a surprisingly short book and it will all be worth it in the end. You will find that his gift of language is as engaging as the complex mystery/romance he has woven together. In that regard, you will not be much spoiled to pre-read a synopsis. Furthermore, don’t take the advice of some reviewers who suggest skimming. Even though Hawthorne is wordy, it only takes him a few sentences to impart clues you don’t want to miss.Read moreRead less
