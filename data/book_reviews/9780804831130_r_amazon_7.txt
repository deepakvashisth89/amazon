I noted that quite a lot of books about the art & practice of mindfulness are generally written with esoteric connotations. I have found this particular one to be rather pragmatic.Although it has less than hundred pages, I like the straight talk writing style, and plenty of question time for pondering & reflection for the reader.As Bruce Lee, whose fists shook the martial world, once said, "Knowing is not enough, you must apply; willing is not enough, you must do!" The author has suggested many simple exercises to apply the practice of mindfulnesss to our daily lives. In this respect, he has done a great job.I highly recommend this book for those seeking the profound rewards of stress-free lifestyle, improved health, & greater peace of mind.Read moreRead less
