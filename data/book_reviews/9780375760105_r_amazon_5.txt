This is an amazing and moving description of the hardships faced by women in the 1600's, in the form of a lively life review by a survivor of the times. Daniel Defoe is the author of the first British novel actually published, Robinson Caruso, and this is the second (or maybe third). Before Dickens, before Gulliver's Travels. I learned so much about English society of the 17th century (debtors' prisons, sending British citizens as slaves to the colonies, the thriving criminal class of London), and specifically about the issues and joys of various classes of British women. I highly recommend this annotated version to any student of history because it explains some of the obscure terms and different ideas from the author's time.
