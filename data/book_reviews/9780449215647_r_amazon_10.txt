Mary Stuart’s suspense and gothic romances are supremely enjoyable. Her plots build up to gripping finales and the lovely settings and ambiance, whether in Greece, Syria, France or Scotland, are evoked in language that’s expressive, lyrical and finely crafted. The humour and finesse with which she seamlessly weaves together the threads of romance and mystery in her novels keeps them from ever becoming cloying. And, her heroines are intelligent, brave and adventurous.Airs Above the Ground has all these hallmark qualities and also the introduction of a very likeable teenage Watson to the heroine’s feisty Sherlock. But what really enhances the appeal of this book is the fact that much of the action revolves around a circus and the fascinating Lipizzaner stallions of the Spanish Riding school in Austria.
