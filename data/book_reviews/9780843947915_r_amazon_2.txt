If you enjoy strong heroines, compassionate heroes and memorable characters, this book is for you.  Pat Cody has a real talent for putting emotions into words and evoking the same feelings in the reader.  Her depiction of the Regency period brings the setting to life, complete with flimsy gowns, tight pantaloons and peculiar social mores.  No contrived gimmicks or time-worn plot lines here.  The central characters have true, believable conflicts with each other and within themselves.  As rich a drama as any on the stage, this is one story you won't soon forget.
