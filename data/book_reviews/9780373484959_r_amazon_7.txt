Catherine's story is the first in this double story book of the Calhoun women. I enjoyed C.C., as she is nicknamed, because she is such a "tomboy". C.C. and Trent are complete opposites who fall in love  with each other anyway. Their story is an enjoyable one. Second is Amanda  and Sloan. I liked this story best, out of the two. They bicker back and  forth just to get to each other. It is quite amusing and funny! Both  stories, were quick to jump to the "love" part of it, but they  were good romance reads, which I enjoyed. Next comes Lilah and Suzanne's,  the last two sisters, stories. I can't wait to get started, espically to  see how C.C., Trent, Amanda, and Sloan are doing.Read moreRead less
