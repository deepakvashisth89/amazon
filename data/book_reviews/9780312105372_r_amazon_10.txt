A good and original take on the Jack the Ripper story.After witnessing Jack butchering a harlot in London, fifteen-year-old Trevor vows to kill the psycho and after being press-ganged onto a boat by the killer bound for America the lad is lucky to escape with his life.The story follows Trevor’s Wild West road trip to come face to face again with Jack the Ripper and the book is more of a Cowboy novel than a Ripper tale with Whittle (Jack’s alter ego) disappearing for most of the book.Also, Trevor must be the luckiest young teen alive in terms of sexual conquests.
