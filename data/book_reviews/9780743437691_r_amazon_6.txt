This is still one of my favorite books.I loved it as a kid. It still has that great amount of magic and human elements. I loved how it was about magic, but still so grounded in reality that magic comes across as the norm or just an extra element. Even when switching times it still feels real. The characters actions and emotions are real. I always wanted a sequel, but the ending is great,  but leaves me wanting more. This is a definite read that I will always recommend.
