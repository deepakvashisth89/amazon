Not a bad little book.  Short, moves pretty fast with some well written tension.  Is it better than the movie?  No.  It is more or less what you see in the movie with the exception of the dead guy walking in the hospital, which is not in the book, the back story of Stevie Wayne, which is in the book and thats about it.  The character Nick is awesome in the book, well written, strong, a real mans man if you know what I mean.  I find the movie better because I think the actors make it great.  The setting, music score, the ghost pirates, and of course the Fog and it is just a pretty well put togeter film.  The book by its self is a decent ghost story.  It will not scare you I don't believe and there is no real literary significance to the story.  Perhaps it would have been better if I had never seen the movie.  But I really did like the Nick character, just down right balls to the wall man of action that seemed to be so believeable.  So thats my take on the book anyways.Read moreRead less
