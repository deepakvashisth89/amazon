Susan Minot's prose is spellbinding as always, gutting in its minimalist style. However, I think that beautiful style that works so well in novels actually works against her in the short story setting. Because there is so little time in a short story to set up a character's life and the world in which they live, and therefore so little time for the reader to get to know these women, Minot's protagonists come off distant and cold more often than I think she intends them to.
