This is one of Lauren Haney's earlier stories in the adventures of Lt Bak, the ancient Egyptian Medjay officer who has found a talent for solving crimes.  The Lieutenant is stationed at Buhen, one of the Nile River forts on the edge of the buffer state of Wawat (between Egypt and Kush) and a senior administrator has come down from Thebes to inspect Buhen's garrison, starting a rush of rumours that the Fort is going to be abandoned.
