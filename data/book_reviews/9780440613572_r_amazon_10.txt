I paid 99p for this book and personally wouldnt pay a penny more.  There is too much descriptions with a story line that did not hold my attention.  I must have been drawn to this book under a crime or thriller heading but it was neither.  Crimes were done but they were almost an aside.  I couldnt remember who half the people were because they seemed of no significance and then kept appearing and as i was desperate to finish the book i couldnt be bothered going back to read their relevance.  If it was aimed at folk who liked a bit of family life, best friends scenario with everyone doting on the kids with a couple of murders goong on then it may be enjoyable to them.  Not for me and i believe i got duped.Read moreRead less
