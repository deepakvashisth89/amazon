Ralph Nader is an amazing person, a brilliantly spoken and well argued defender of democracy, in the truest sense.  This collection of writings, spanning much of his public career is a testament to his dedication.  As Barbara Eherenreich states in the preface, "he's gotten more radical over the years, not less."  His concern for fairness and freedom has taken him into many areas of confrontation with the corporate and ruling elite.  He obviously had the most distinguished public record of all candidates in the last presidential election.  But even if we the people don't give him the respect he deserves with our votes, he will continue to work for us, because his work is not to gain power, but to give us back our power.  This collection of essays is of vital importance.  Use it to enlighten yourself and perhaps you will find yourself as driven as Citizen Nader to help restore democracy.
