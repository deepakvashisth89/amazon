Mary Jo Putney writes excellent romance novels, and the regency era novels are my favorites and Dancing on the Wind is one of those. I have enjoyed the fallen angels books and I have not read them and order as it did not seem important, but I had high hopes for Lucian’s book. I must admit, however, then I was a little disappointed. I have to say this book was a little weird. I had a hard time following it for at least the first half of the book. I’m going to give you a little spoiler that being there are twins in this book, which causes no end of confusion. There’s also a kink that shows up out of the blue for no reason and italics and you wonder why. There are good aspects to this book that I enjoyed, and things that I found interesting, but I was also disappointed.
