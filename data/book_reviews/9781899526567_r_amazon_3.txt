This could easily become a favorite for people of the pagan persuasion here in the U.S! This deck is rather hard to find within the States. Ebay or the Amazon.co.uk. The cards are a little expensive, but worth it! I cannot tell you how much depth the cards have provided for myself!The cards are a labor of love that one can see in the production quality. First off, this is not traditional assembly line card printing. It seems that each card is hand printed. This, I know would be a pain-staking process and impossible! The illustrations on the major Trumps all bear a date from the 1980s. Many years and much study underlie each card. The unity here is flawless! The influence of the deck is the old IJJ Swiss deck. The High Priestess is Juno, The Hierophant is Jupiter. I have no problem with this at all. The Swiss deck was my 2nd deck many years ago.After serious study and reflection of the Major Arcana, I felt I was ready for a reading. Wow, did it ring true! As for the Minor Arcana, usually for myself, they must be illustrated. Not so in this case. The meanings are easily written out. The key words are a little different. I feel with a little practice, the good reader would have no problem interpreting them.I feel they could be the next standard in classical Tarot imagery, beside The Rider-Waite and Crowley-Thoth deck. The neo-pagan imagery is startling, vividly colored and illustrated. It is the grass roots production that add to the magic.
