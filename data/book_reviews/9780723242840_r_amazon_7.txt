Hilarious, charming and an interesting take on respecting other types of living creatures. Meet Jeremy Fisher, the idle frog who set out on a seemingly simple tale of catching some fish for a dinner with his friends. Little did Jeremy suspect that this little chore would turn out to give him the scare of his life as the fishes he tried to capture wouldn't be easily caught. The fishes escape Jeremy's efforts, taunt Jeremy and even capture him for a brief second before he is lucky enough to be set free.An intelligent story, that teaches children in a fun way that all life is equally as important. Of course, in the end, Jeremy and his friends end up eating a grasshopper coated with an interesting sauce, but Potter's use of the word "nasty" to refer to the dish seems to emphasize the unusual form of ironic punishment that Jeremy must endure. Unique and original, I recommend for children of all ages.Darien Summers, author of The Mischievous Hare, a children's book.The Mischievous HareRead moreRead less
