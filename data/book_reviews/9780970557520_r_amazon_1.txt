Excellent book.  Jerry Brown's style and story-telling ability compare favorably with the "Vanderbilt Fugitives" of the 1920's...especially Robert Penn Warren.  Brown is at his best in describing Jickie Jennings' Louisiana back-country home and particularly the flashbacks to Jickie's ancestors whose hard-scrabble lifestyle manifested in his rough-hewn hard-drinking father, contrasts so sharply with his ultimate career as a mathematician with U.S. Space Command.In between is a very lively saga of a young boy growing into a young man dealing with life in the racially-charged Louisiana outback replete with tragedies and comedies and all manner of characters brought to life by Brown's writing skills.Read this book twice and will read it a third time.
