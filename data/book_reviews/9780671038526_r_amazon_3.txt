Others here described the plot, but I want to describe how this TV show and this novel in particular effected a lifelong change to my view of myself and the world. At 11 yo, I was an immediate fan of TOS in 1966, anxiously awaited each new episode, and was in tears when it went off the air. Even at such an early age, the theme of IDIC and other life in the universe struck such a chord that they became lifelong beliefs; and let me, a very young sheltered girl from a small town, dream past the boundaries of my family, society and world. Years later, I was overjoyed when I discovered the novels, and I read this one when originally published. It was a vision of how a woman could aspire and achieve anything and be equal in every way to a man (short skirts notwithstanding), with a nice helping of IDIC/aliens and my childhood heroes (Kirk, Spock, McCoy).Read moreRead less
