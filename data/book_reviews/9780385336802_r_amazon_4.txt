This is an excellent book. I do not understand why it is out of print. It has classic properties, depicting the evacuation of a Tibetan village across treacherous terrain, eluding a vengeful Chinese army and led by a young Scot radio technician. It does not spare the reader, and there is a gentle love affair at its heart. The author summons beautifully the challenging landscape, the vagaries of the conflicting cultures, and the harsh climate. All written in beautiful economical and paced prose.Read moreRead less
