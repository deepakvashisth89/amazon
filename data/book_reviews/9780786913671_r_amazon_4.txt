Richard Baker demonstrates he is a proficient, capable writer in this action SciFi story.  In some ways he reminds me of Roger Zelazny in that he can write what is actually an implausible story with shallow characters, but written well enough, and sufficiently fast-moving that one doesn't notice, or doesn't care until after the book has ended.  Seriously, I write that as a complement.  Zelazny is still one of my favorite science fiction writers.Baker focuses on action and he is working in a fictional universe that has been designed to support action, if not close inspection by the reader.  His characters did things that caused me to pause, on occasion, and ask myself "now, why did he (or she) do or say that?  Must be because the author needed it to carry through the plot!"  Implicit in this answer is that perhaps the author should have revised the plot.  But there's no telling how many times the author revised the plot, and finally settled on this one, anyway.But ultimately, the hitches aren't enough to prevent me from finishing the book, which is why it earns four stars, instead of three or only two.
