This is one of Sandford's best. It happens to be the 24th book that I have read on my Kindle in this series. But it is not quite halfway through the series in order of creation. Lucas and his new "girlfriend, Dr Weather Karkinnen, have just returned to Minneapolis after meeting in Wisconsin where they got on famously in sluething another murder mystery.  He moved back to a position as a homicide detective with the Minneapolis Police and she back into a surgical residency with the University of Minnesota Hospitals in Minneapolis.  They decided to live together though neither has mentioned marriage. While Lucas takes on the new case, he is surrounded by a number of good-looking women competing with Weather for his attention: there is a pretty young blond who has been assigned to work with him though she is technically a State Trooper, there is a beautiful and sexy new TV reporter who tries hard to get Lucas' attention, and there is a mature, sexy and endangered blond scheduled to become the final victim.Now this would be a typical Lucas Davenport Mystery except for one thing. Meagan, the young female State Trooper is dying of cancer. She is desperately trying to show people that there is, indeed, a serial killer working in the Twin Cities and to help catch him before she dies. She has already gone through several cycles of remission. This becomes a key element in the timing of this mystery.So they proceed through several mysteries with quite a bit of suspense and action. Does Lucas remain true to Weather? Read the book. It's a matter of considerable gravity.Read moreRead less
