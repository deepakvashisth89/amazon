The author takes you into a practical view of politics, displaying a great command of the field. Morris presents us with a theory of political behavior which is not anchored in a sophisticated research of anthropology (indeed, references are rather scarce throughout the book), yet his analysis is a very convincing case about the actual dynamics of politics. After reading this book you will acquire empirical knowledge of what to do if you want to win elections, govern a constituency, or venture yourself into the public service. The reading is particularly enjoyable, as the author brings forward historical experiences of successful political campaigns to support his views.Read moreRead less
