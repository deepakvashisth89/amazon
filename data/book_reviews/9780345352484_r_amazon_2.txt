Interesting book, although perhaps a bit dated per social mores of the time.  If nothing else, this book is interesting as an artifact of just where men stood in their attitudes about sex, women and gays, circa-1980.  A lot has changed since then, but also much has stayed the same and will be familiar to the modern reader.
