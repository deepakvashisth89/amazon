The cover got my attention and the first few pages got me hooked. For anyone that has ever been married and has experienced that "rut" this will hit home (well, at little exaggerted). Valerie is our heroine who is stuck with a husband who no longer pays any attention to her, only their child. After many attempts to bring the sparks back, her eyes are drawn towards Eddie, the plant guy at the office.This just starts a series of problems in Valerie's life that never seem to improve, only get worse. Her relationships with both men seem to twist and turn, one cold and the other, very, very hot. This is fast reading! I kept telling myself one more page but then couldn't wait till the next.Can't wait for the next sequel to arrive in August!Read moreRead less
