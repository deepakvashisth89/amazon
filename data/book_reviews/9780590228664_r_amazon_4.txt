possibly my favorite BSC mystery to date and my fave BSC BOOK to date! I loved how the mystery involves going back in time, as I am a fan of reminiscing about my high school years, etc. The subplot features the charges being obsessed with Ghostbusters. Could have done without that, but it fit the theme of the book. It was written masterfully and shows how the BSC series can stay true to it's roots without scimping on new, fresh storytelling. On a side note, I never quite understood the introduction of Abby. Did we really need this character? Somehow it was filling a political agenda somewhere.
