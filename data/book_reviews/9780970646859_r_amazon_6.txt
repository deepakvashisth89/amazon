When I first got Pokemon Crystal, I said to myself I'm finally going to get a REAL strategy guide for this game. The Versus guide is my first ever gameboy strategy guide, and it left a great impression. It has a very extensive and detailed walkthrough that covers so much, from the pokemon in a trainer's roster and the money you obtain after beating them, to all the secret areas and items. At the beginning is a page of difference between Gold/Silver and Crystal, and the basics of the game. The pokedex is eye-popping; every pokemon in the game (and not in the game) is shown with the moves it learns naturally, HM and TM-able moves, egg-teachable moves and stats on things like defense, sp. defense, attack, etc.Whenever you arrive at a new route/town you will be delighted to find a well written description of the things you can do there, events that take place and of course the gyms. Each gym has a good chunk of the page dedicated to it with info on the gym minions and leader.This guide has a map for every area in the game; all very clear and sometimes with paths drawn through them to guide you. And that's covering only a fraction of the things in this book. And while you may find a few errors in some places (I saw a picture of a Hitmonchan where there should've been a picture of a Teddiursa), that doesn't take away from the overall quality of the guide. I recommend it for anyone playing Crystal, it's my number 1 choice.Read moreRead less
