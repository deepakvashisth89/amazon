I try to savor reading works by Stephen King because I know he is the only writer capable scratching my literary itch. If fiction is a drug, King manufactures it in the purest and most inimitable form. Firestarter is no exception. Andy and Charlie are some of his most realistic characters, and their harrowing journey is as captivating as it is heartbreaking. John Rainbird is an epic villain, who stands out as one of the most evil characters I’ve ever encountered in a King story. I highly recommend this book.Read moreRead less
