I was pleasantly surprised at this book. I was hoping for more suspense, less romance, but this turned out to be a good read. I liked all the characters, especially Miss Hattie. The booked dragged a little mid way. I kept skipping pages to "get to the point." I will probably read the next 2 books in the series. The only reason I didn't give it 5 stars is that the book is not "lending enabled." When I like a book, I like to lend it to friends & get them hooked on the author/series.Read moreRead less
