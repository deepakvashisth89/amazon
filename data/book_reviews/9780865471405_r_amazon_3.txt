Sanford Friedman's Totempole is the novel that made him famous, but not enough, perhaps...Though noticed by such as Hilton Kramer and Gore Vidal, it should have assured his lasting fame. Instead, it's out of print.  Much more than a gay male buldengsroman, Totempole links personal myth-making in the service of survival, to the myths that inform western culture. From invasive, well-meaning cruelty by parents, to camp and college fetishes and on to experiences of a broader sexuality, not firmly categorized, in an Asian context (Korean prisoners' camp). Friedman alludes to the prehistorical move from matriarchy to patriarchy, later whimsically and terrifyingly developed in Rip Van Winkle.
