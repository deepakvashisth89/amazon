I admit a positive prejudice, as I knew Philip Gray personally. However and whether or not I knew Phil, the book is still a VERY realistic look into the trials and tribulations of Phil and his crew as they flew mission after deadly mission for RAF Bomber Command (out of Stradishall, UK) in 1944 and 1945.
