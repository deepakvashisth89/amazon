I've really liked other novels by Allegra Goodman, and so I finally got around to reading this one. Having finished it, my conclusion is that it's not my favorite novel of hers--the protagonist was just too spacey for me to identify with her as much as I normally do with characters in novels--but Goodman's writing talents do shine through as always.
