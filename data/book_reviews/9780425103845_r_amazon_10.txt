Oh my.... Whilst this is a tiny little book its contents will leave its mark on your memory for a long time I am sure.  As other reviewers have already said it is truly shocking....  But most frustratingly it leaves many many questions unanaswered...However what I would wish to add is that this book has absolutley no bearing on the actual 9 1/2 weeks film; also that Fifty Shades of Grey is a blatant rehashing of the same story/theme (makes you wonder how that writer got away with such plagiarism)….What we do have is the story of two clearly masochistic personalities who are brought together for 9 1/2 weeks (probably the early 70's in New York); one the woman is obviously mentally and emotionally damaged the other is well how can I say it, a masochistic-psychopath.  What transpires is not a 'love affair' but a toxic obsessive sexually violent fuelled 'relationship'.....  Some reviews have said the unamed male 'loved'.... I will let you decide.  In my mind, he manipulated and controlled to a psychopathic degree .... I will let you read the ending and the quotes that I imagine were written from actual conversations ... the implication of how far the male was obviously fantasising of going is truly chilling.From my research it appears that the story is based on true events lived by the now deceased Ingeborg Day (there are a few internet sites naming her and giving the story context circa late 1960's/early 1970's New York).  We never know the name of the male, nor his job.... What we do know is that Ingeborg Day has quite a shocking family history (world war II German father) and the tragedy of her own deceased child and divorce apparently before her 9 1/2 weeks encounter.  She is a professional living the dream high powered job (think Sex and the City) but is clearly en-route to having a major life crises when she meets the 9 1/2 weeks male - I think this in itself is key as to why she allowed herself to be completely submerged by his toxic dysfunctional and near-deadly personality.  Shortly after the affair ends she endures a serious psychiatric episode unsurprisingly.....There is absolutely NOTHING sexy or titillating in this tiny true story......  But it is a fascinating insight into the behaviours of such broken people.  Ingeborg Day did go on to fall in love with another man and had a long marriage thankfully.
