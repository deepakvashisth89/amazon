Love Kipling and love Captains Courageous so, on a whim, I bought this leather-bound version for $20 because I saw that it had been priced at $200. It's $50 as I write this on Dec. 1, 2008.Well, it's leather bound and it's legible and it's Kipling, but I guess I don't know enough about leather binding to know if I really got a good deal or what. My philosophy is always by hardback so I'm glad I purchased this book (I got Just So Stories too), but besides from being leather, they're otherwise plain-jane.The only thing I definitely didn't like (why I subtracted a star) is that these are part of a numbered collection, and the number is embossed on the spine so it's obvious that I only have volumes 12 and 20.That being said, I'm going to buy more (maybe someday all of them) if they get back down to $20 apiece.
