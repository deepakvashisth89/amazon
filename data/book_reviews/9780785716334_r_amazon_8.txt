This book is one I read as a kid, but re-read as soon as the Amazon series was out. Some say the game World of Warcraft is only based on the Warcraft book series, but this book shows it is heavily borrowing from the Wheel of Time books. Forsaken are the bad guys, in the hoard, the White Cloaked men are cloned in the Scarlet Crusade. It is seriously an obvious origin story of the better parts of the game series. I was pretty shocked more of this book was skipped over in the Amazon TV show. Mat is healed in one day in the TV show, and it is a massive plot point in this book that it is not done in one day.
