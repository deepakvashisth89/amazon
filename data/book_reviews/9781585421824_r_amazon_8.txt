This was an interesting book in that it shows the journey of one woman through the British Mysteries as she is shown and experiences them.  Lavishly detailed, this journey is shamanic, though I don't think she ever uses the word intentionally.  She seems a little arrogant to neo-pagans, basing it on one experience of a crowd in a bar, but her abstract insights are worth pondering, especially in regards to becoming a woman warrior in the spiritual sense.
