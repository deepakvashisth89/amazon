In several Maigret stories, there is a reference to an incident early in his career when he endured a three-day stakeout in the rain to try and catch a suspected criminal. I bought this book assuming it was about this incident, but was disappointment to find it wasn't. Instead, it found a story only slightly above a B-grade 'cops and robbers' movie script.
