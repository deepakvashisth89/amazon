I loved this book when I read it as a child; now I share it. Maria had glasses and pigtails like me at the time. I sympathized with her plight and wished I could be her friend. The villains, her governess and the despicable vicar were just horrible enough (in a child's view) to be worth defeating. I loved the professor and the lessons he taught; and thank goodness for Cook, Maria's only ally in that huge house. But most important; the Lilliputians, who taught Maria the importance of respect for others, were interesting in their own right.
