These early Hardy Boys books were written in the late 1920's and 1930's. The same titles written later are not the same books. These books are fun to read not so much for their depth, but as an interesting snapshot of escapism of the time. As most American boys were suffering during the Great Depression, The Hardy Boys has access to cars, motorcycles, motor boats and airplanes, traveled a great deal and were seldom short of money. There is also an interesting lack of supervision, slang and expressions that are no longer used. Light reading that should be enjoyed for what it is.Read moreRead less
