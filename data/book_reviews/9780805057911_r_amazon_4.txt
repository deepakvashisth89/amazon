This is classic, heartfelt, obviously semi-autobiographical Ed Abbey.  I found it superior to his earlier novels in terms of how well written it is.  Of course, it's chockful of the usual Abbey contradictions:  the self-described redneck intellectual, the environmental defender who blithely tosses his empty beer cans out of his car onto the roadside, the misogamist who keeps re-marrying, the misogynist who purports to love women, the irresponsible, devil-may-care man's man who somehow manages to complete so very much fine writing of both fiction, non-fiction, and a combination thereof.Read moreRead less
