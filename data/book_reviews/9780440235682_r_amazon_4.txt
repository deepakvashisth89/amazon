All of Kathleen Givens books are truly wonderful.  She brings you there with her on her journey.  This is the first book I read that brought me to Scotland (if only in my mind).  I love her style of writing, and the characters she shows you.  During and after reading these, you can really imagine the strife of the people of Scotland.  It is historically intriguing.  These books led me to read Diane Galbaldon's Outlander Series.  Some day I will get to Scotland because of these books.
