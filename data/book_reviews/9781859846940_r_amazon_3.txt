This memoir provided a new, more complex understanding of the conflicts in Syria, Lebanon and Israel. It offers important historical background details that are as relevant today as they were 60 years ago.  It's a pleasure to read, interesting, and well paced. I've recommended it to many people and they have all had the same reaction as myself; In the US, we are getting a negatively biased picture of that region of the world.
