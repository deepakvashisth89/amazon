This is one of Anne Gracie’s earliest books and is not part of a series. It is well written. I like how Tallie’s daydreams are presented and her youth and naivety revealed through them. She is sweet—not in a sickly maudlin way, rather in an unsophisticated and humble one. She is resilient and fun. It is delightful to see her mature and still retain that sweetness. Magnus also changes for the better and in a realistic way. Their adventures are not typical of most regency romances as they travel throughout parts of Europe and face challenges there.
