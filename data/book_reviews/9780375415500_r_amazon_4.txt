I had long been familiar with Mick Foley's wrestling career.  I bought his other books, mostly animated, fun stories.  I had even met Mick a few times.  Still, I could never have predicted what he produced in this book.This books runs the gamut of emotions.  At the simplest, it is the story of a boy and his father.  However, it is so much more than that.  It is poignant, sweet, dark, funny, sad, gruesome and still much more.There are a handful of twists, which I never saw coming.  Foley weaved this story, which never is predictable nor falsely exciting.  This is the kind of book that left me wanting more, not because the story was empty, but because it was awesome.Read moreRead less
