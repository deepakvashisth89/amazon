Please lobby the publishers to make that happen.I believe it is the most important work in English speaking philosophy in the 20th century, but it seems to be out of print. It deserves to be studied.It is quite hard but not because of arcane language.It is an exploration of deeply interconnected ideas using a method that is rather like exploring a new place.  You approach a particular spot from many different directions. You  find it looks different from different perspectives. Some spots are neatly ordered, like Manhattan, a grid of streets and avenues. Other spots are more like the oldest neighborhoods of and ancient town where paths were first formed by herders driving sheep to pasture.What ideas? Well, meaning, understanding, language, learning concepts, number, mind, your own and others. Simple stuff like that and much more.You could read it a hundred times and still find new insights. It is a profound treasure.Best approach? Read 10 or 20 paragraphs, then chew on it a bit. Then read it again. Then fight with it, then adapt it to a new similar problem and see what works and what has to change. There is no theory here. Just method. But the method exposes truth and illusion.My eyes are getting old. I need a kindle edition so I can scale the font. I am begging.Oh. By the way, I recommend it. I like it. A lot.Read moreRead less
