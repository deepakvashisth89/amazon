Diane Duane Is one of my all time favorite authors. She has written most of my favorite star trek books, and she has a series set in the universe of the young wizards. The young wizards of the series are young teenage humans, but this particular book is number 2 of 3 in the same universe with many of the same characters but the protagonists are feline. The books are well written with drama and humor combined to make them very enjoyable to read. I highly recommend the entire series to anyone who likes clean, relaxed, well constructed fantasy.But when I say that this book is #2 out of 3, you have to know that the 3rd book, as well as a couple of other books in this series, are not available on amazon. You can find the other books on Diane's website.  I was having trouble loading the books, which are very inexpensive, on to my kindles. Diane very patiently made certain that I understood exactly how to accomplish it.  I am now enjoying very much reading the 3rd book in the series. Check out all of Diane's wonderful books and you'll have many many hours of entertainment.Read moreRead less
