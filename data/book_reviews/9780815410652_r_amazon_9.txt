An impressively researched book, and well presented. Einarson makes the wise decision to focus on a number of specific bands and artists he feels  were at the forefront of the developing genre, rather than trying to cover everything. You may not agree with his choices, but this is Einarson's book. It gets rather rushed towards the end, but that's nitpicking. An essential record of a creative and exciting time. I've read it twice, and I'm sure I'll be returning to it. A top book.Read moreRead less
