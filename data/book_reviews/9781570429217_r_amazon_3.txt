I like to read murder mysteries, to get away from all the research I need to read as a therapist. I tend to read the more delicate and psychological authors such as Louise Penny, Elly Griffiths, Ann Cleeves...but I also read American murder/detective/mysteries as well....but the plots and character development and writing level is just not comparable..very different...So, I am reading the Alex Cross books in small doses. I like the books, they read quickly and are engrossing. But I find that there is unnecessary detail in the gruesome cruelty of the murders. It doesn't add anything to the story. Just sensationalism. I like the books Patterson writes with other authors, as they tend to be less gruesome.
