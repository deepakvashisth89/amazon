I have tried getting all five of the books in "The Dark is Rising Sequence" by Susan Cooper. They were issued on tape some time ago, and again on CD, but do not seem to be currently available except as library remainders or used copies from third parties. I am sad to say this one was remaindered from a public library in Texas, even though it is good quality and easy to use. I know our local library gave away all its children's tapes as CDs are the norm, but tapes are still easy to use and if they are for books no longer out on CD, then why not keep them? In any case, this is one of the shorter stories in this sequence which elaborates on English and Welsh myths including the Pendragon/Arthur/Merlin series, and customs of the past that still exist and are intertwined in an ancient and continued conflict of the Dark and the Light, good and evil, and human destiny in the hands of young people helped by several 'old ones' who are very old indeed. I only gave it four stars because it is on tape and not CD, so I listen to it at home while I really enjoy having these stories to listen to in the car.
