Soul Catcher by Colin KerseySnooping around the U-District in Seattle I chanced across a book shop with a one-dollar table. It was there that I discovered Soul Catcher. I like Seattle. I like sailing. I like a dramatic wind-storm. I like a good mystery. I like anything with a Native American connection. And I have some tolerance for the super-natural. It was easily worth the gamble of a buck. Well, I hit the jackpot. It's got all that, plus action, romance and physical descriptions so vivid they are palpable. I liked it so much I tried to purchase additional copies as gifts. Sadly it was out of print. I couldn't even find any used copies. Now, fifteen years later, what with the advent of the Internet and all, I find that it is available in an electronic version. Way to go Amazon. This is an amazing first book.
