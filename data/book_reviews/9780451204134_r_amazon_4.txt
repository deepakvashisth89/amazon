Henderson’s retelling of the first U.S. Expedition to the North Pole and that fate of Captain Charles Hall and his ship Polaris and crew in the years 1871 & 72 is a riveting tale of adventures in Canada’s far north that will interest those who enjoy sea stories and those who enjoy survivalist’s novel.
