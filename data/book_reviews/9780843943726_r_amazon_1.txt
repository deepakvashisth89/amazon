After I started reading this book, I couldn't put it down. This book will take you through many phases of adventure, passion, love, and time-travel. I loved how it started out with a spirit living in the present and transporting both characters back in time (125 years).Kathy Conley, a recent widow, moved to her husband's family home, ready to fix the old Conley home up and start a new life but she could feel and sense someone in the house.Dalton Crowkiller, a half-breed ghost was haunting the Conley home. Dalton was hung, accused of rape, by relative of Kathy's on the home place. He had haunted the place for 125 years until Kathy came to the house, she was the only who could see him after so many years of being there.Their adventure and love story starts when Kathy can actually touch him and talk to him but gets interesting when both are sent back in time before he was hung. Dalton needing a vision to keep a promise he made to his father, visits his father's tribe and eventually learns that his true purpose is to help his mother.Dalton and Kathy fall madly in love hoping their time together will last forever.It's just a great book and I rated this book a 5.
