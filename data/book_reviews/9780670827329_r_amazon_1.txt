A few years ago, I had the amazing experience of being able to travel to the Galapagos Islands for vacation. I read this book shortly before going and it was wonderful to get the author's perspective of the island and to imagine what it must have been like in the past, before it was such a tourist destination. The Angermeyer family still has a huge presence on the islands, and it was great to go back to where it all began!
