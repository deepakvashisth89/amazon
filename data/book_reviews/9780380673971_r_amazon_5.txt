I think the reviews on this are unfair. Stephen Barry was my uncle and I can vouch for the fact that his lack of gossip and spilling of secrets, came out of the deep respect he had for his boss. Charles appreciated his loyalty and therefore rewarded it with a very comfortable lifestyle. He didn't merely 'cash in' on his life with the royal family. He wanted to tell his story and wouldn't you if you had had his job?Our family were and are extremely proud of his achievements, as he did grow up in a poor working class background. He went to a very ordinary secondary modern in Hammersmith and yet went on to serve royalty. I know that my Nan was very proud of all he achieved.
