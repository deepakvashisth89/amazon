The first Shirley Busbee book I read was "Return to Oak Valley" and after meeting and getting to know Shelly Granger, Sloan Ballinger and their families I was thrilled to find "Coming Home".  It continues the story of the Ballinger and Granger families with the return of Sloan Granger's sister, Roxanne, a supermodel, who comes home for Shelly & Sloan's wedding and decides to stay. I can't wait for another book in this line.
