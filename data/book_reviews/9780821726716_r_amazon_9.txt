I should start out by saying that this book is not going to be for the lighthearted among you, Alisha is living in a settlement with her uncle, some of the men in the settlement capture an indian warrior, she is facinated by him, takes him food and treats his wounds from the whipping the men had given him. He escapes with the help of one of his fellow braves, later he returns and kills almost everyone at the settlement, taking some prisoners,including Alisha who he wants to keep for himself. His name is Gray Eagle and he is the son of the chief of the tribe. Without giving too much away Gray Eagle can speak english a fact he hides from Alisha, so when she talks or rants to him he understands  but she does not know this, Gray Eagle does not always treat  Alisha kindly but he is the son of the chief and she is a white slave she cannot be seen to challange him in front of his tribe, he does smack her around and even has her whipped , although some parts of the book are  hard to take on.
