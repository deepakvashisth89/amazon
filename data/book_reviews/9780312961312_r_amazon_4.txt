I didn't know until I read, that this was from late 60s, by Ms. Pilcher.  It had great imagery as always but I didn't like it as well as later books.  But it was still interesting, and I liked the relationships.  There are only two men in Emma's life--her father, a remote artist who leaves her in boarding school and sees her every few years, and Christo, who has been her stepbrother, but hasn't seen her in years and now wants to get to know her.
