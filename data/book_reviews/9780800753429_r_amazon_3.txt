The modern church is under a “Pelagian Captivity” as the great and late RC Sproul wrote in an excellent 2001 piece.  “Semi-Pelagianism” AKA “Arminianism” states that man is cursed under sin and God must sprinkle some grace (prevenient grace, resistible grace, or pre-regenerating grace) on man so that man can then “decide” whether man wants to accept or deny the saving grace of God.  This is total heresy and gives glory to man if man “chooses” God.  You also run into the heresy that man’s will can overcome God’s will if God wills that saving grace be sent to man and man has the ability to deny it.  This is called “cooperating grace” and is plaguing the Church and getting many people sent to hell.
