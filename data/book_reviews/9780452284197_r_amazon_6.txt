Very descriptive and well written author with good stories to tell.  City of Thieves is his best work (read it if you haven't) and I think When the Nines Roll Over is better than this one too, but this is still a great read for people who enjoy character development and an interesting, original story.  Unfortunately he writes more screenplays now and hasn't written a novel in a long time.  I say unfortunately, but he's made some excellent TV as well.
