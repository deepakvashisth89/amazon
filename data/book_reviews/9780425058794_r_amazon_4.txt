Demon brings a ringing close to the Gaia series. It’s the kind of sci-fi ending that makes you want to stand up and cheer. Sit down, Heinlein. Stand aside, Clarke. Mr. John Varley will show you how it’s done.It’s got action, it’s got love, it’s got an albino anaconda the size of bullet train! Cirocco Jones, once a starship captain, arcs toward her triumphant finish somewhere among the rings of Saturn. She aims to kill an alien god … and finds the real God is the friends you make along the way. Witty, refulgent, and mature. A very fine cigar.Read moreRead less
