I took my 1969 hardback copy of Laura's Luck and gave it to my 8 1/2 year old to take to school for her silent reading that she does each day.  She was delighted by the illustrations and the little peeks into the story that I gave her when we talked about each picture.  She can't wait to get to school and start reading it.The story is as great in 2012 as it was when I read it in the 70's or when Mrs. Sachs wrote it.  Laura has nothing but bad luck, but comes to discover that there are plenty of silver linings if you only choose to see them.  I think most girls will see themselves in this book, if not as Laura, then as one of the other girls at the camp.  A wonderful read!Read moreRead less
