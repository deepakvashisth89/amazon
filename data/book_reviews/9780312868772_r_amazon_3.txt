Everyone knows, Asimov, Heinlein, Clarke, and Bradbury, but in this book you get introduced to some other greats and pioneers of the Golden Age.Included in this book are 17 short stories (about 5-30 pages each) and 3 novelettes (each about 40-50 pages in length) fromLester Del RayFrederik PohlDamon KnightA.E. Van VogtJack VanceSome of my favorite stories were:Black Destroyer by A.E. Van Vogt (this story is said to be the concept that the Alien movies were based upon) and it is a cool story with a very interesting and devious main character.Dear Pen Pal by A.E. Van Vogt epistolary-style story with a nice twistThe Tunnel Under the World by Frederik Pohl, a dreamy, weird, interesting view of commercialism run amuck.The Handler by Damon Knight (Knight had helped define what makes a story science fiction and what makes it fantasy)For I am a Jealous People by Lester Del Rey, a novelette with a different take on where God's interests lie.Sail 25 by Jack Vance you get to meet the very unusual Henry Belt who is on his last cadet training mission.I hope you enjoy these stories as much as I did.Read moreRead less
