Poetry is a wonderful technique. You can convey a vast array of emotions in a few discipline lines. But for most poetry conveys a mysterious form.  Poetry is often considered something foreign only facilitated by thinking men.In the book Writing Poems, Robert Wallace attempts to deconstruct the process, by breaking down the approach to poetry. Wallace is able to empower the writer to take on the challenge. This study aid also is a good reference for those who may find themselves stuck on the mystic process of putting down on paper the mystery that lies from within.
