The continuing story of the first North American settlers takes us into an era when this group lived in houses rather than the teepees we all grew up to know about.This is set in about 1100 CE (AD in my days at school ), shortly after the Battle of Hastings. To find there is archeological evidence of people living in grand palaces, houses and effectively forts at this time is a true shock. The story is well written and draws you in to the fray as a chieftain tries to save his local people at the expense of his subject neihbours.
