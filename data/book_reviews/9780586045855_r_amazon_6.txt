This book is a sequel to the Raj Quartet series of novels. The protagonists are two very minor characters from the earlier books, husband and wife, who remained in India after the British raj departed. The time has jumped ahead to the late 1960s. The now-elderly couple lives precariously on a threadbare pension in a run down hotel, in the small hill city where they were stationed during the palmier days of the raj. This novel has a more light-hearted tone than the quartet, yet there is a strong melancholic undertone as the elderly couple deal with their poverty, loneliness, and helplessness in what has become for them an alien country.Read moreRead less
