No matter what you chose on your road to motherhood, this book gives some great insight. I was very disappointed that Ms. Wolf did not learn from her own mistakes, and therefore ended up right back in the C-sec room. Hindsight is supposed to be 20-20 Naomi. However, there is much more to glean from this tale. The important info is in the sections on relationships with partners and PPD. How comforting to know that many women are not alone in their feelings after childbirth.
