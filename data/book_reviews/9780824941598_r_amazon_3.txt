The first edition of this book was published in the dark days of 1999, before the internet was ubiquitous. It tells you the civil war sites by state and provides a very brief overview of the history at each one. It has an attractive glossy format and lots of photos. The main purpose is to provide contact information for the site, which was hard to find before the internet.Unfortunately, it’s easy to find this information on the internet today – try “civil war sites in Missouri” as an example. As a result, it’s hard to know who will find this book useful.In the internet age, these kinds of books really need to think about “value added.” In 1999, contact information for sites, and a list by state for the traveler, sufficed. These days, I think you probably need to organize a book by *campaign* – follow the Union route from Fort Donelson to Atlanta, say. This provides the reader with an itinerary as well as the history, and lets you connect the sites. The internet does a great job with facts, not so good with connections.It’s a shame – it’s a beautiful book, but time has passed it by.Read moreRead less
