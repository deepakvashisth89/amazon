By page 2, Edward De Angelo's novel had me in its grip.  The Lies that Bind is a book that I didn't want to put down.  The story of Sam and Pete ensnares you with threads so light you don't realise you are bound.The human genome, itself, has reached celebrity status and we have to decide what constitutes family, DNA or connection?  Edward De Angelo's compassion and empathy with his characters, celebrates the relationships we all need to thrive.Even though the title is "The Lies That Bind" , the book evokes "The Ties that Bind" and celebrates that thought.
