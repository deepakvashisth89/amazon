Though the book is not well known, the movie is. This book is the basis of the movie 'Doc Hollywood' starring Michael J. Fox. This is a novel of big city yankee doctor meets small town southern society and hilarity ensues. Shulman's humor is dry at times, but very warm. His best characters tend to be the small ones such as the mechanic and the mayor's daughter, but the entire book is just full of charm and humor. Highly recommended.Read moreRead less
