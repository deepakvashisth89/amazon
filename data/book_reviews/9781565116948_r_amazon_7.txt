This book opened a door for me and for that I am very grateful. As other reviewers have stated it isn't a collection for the highbrow connoisseur but I can't believe anyone would regret having it on the shelf. Keillor's introduction is a fun and rewarding read in itself, he has always done a great job of bringing "culture" to the masses and in the introduction he does an admirable job of explaining why poetry can be appreciated by ANYONE.This book offers up a variety of poets like Emily Dickinson, Howard Nemerov, Yeats, Billy Collins and more to the uninitiated reader in a way that makes you want to read more of their work.  Keillor treads the line between urbane sophist and common man with wit and humility.Read moreRead less
