I'm not completely fluent in Spanish so I don't understand every little detail of this story. Some chapters I find more enjoyable than others. It's a bit slow in my opinion and I am about halfway through the book. I'm very interested to see how it ends. The chapters are narrated by about 6 different people (the main characters). Flavia narrates one chapter, then Albert narrates a chapter, then Turing, etc and then back to Flavia. So the story doesn't progress in a linear manner time-wise; it jumps around a bit. There's a bit of history involved in the story regarding a past dictatorship in Bolivia, which I was not very familiar with before reading this book. Partially because of that it's a little hard for me personally to follow the story sometimes.
