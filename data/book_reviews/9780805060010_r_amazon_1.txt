A most lyrical and engaging story which days later is still very much with me and which will surely remain quite unforgetable even amidst the many books I am always reading.  Although I have never been in Vietnam, The Deep Green Sea deftly paints a vivid picture of the twenty-five year post-War return visit of a US soldier and a touching and haunting relationship he enters into with a younger English-speaking Vietnamese woman. With an unusual and subtle suspense, the text alternates between each of the two characters' thoughts and feeling. A very different kind of book, but one, I feel, you, too, will recommend to friends.Read moreRead less
