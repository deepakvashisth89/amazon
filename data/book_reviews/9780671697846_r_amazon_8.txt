Deadman Switch is a fine example of Zahn's work prior to his claims to fame of reenergizing the Star Wars universe with "Heir To the Empire".  The tech level of the science is written clear and understandably while the world building involved doesn't distract from the characters.  Admittedly Zahn's novel benefits mightily from a killer (no pun intended) concept.  It is to Zahn's credit that he doesn't let such a promising premise go to waste or for that matter opt for the easy way out.The story builds carefully on the premise with twists and turns both predictable and surprising.
