The writing itself is good (it's Tolstoy), however whoever edited this did a shameful job. I'm only on chapter 3 and there are a ridiculous number of typos, especially in the German quotations and titles. Many of the German words are misspelled to the point of being unintelligible. I'm going to assume, because of the renown of both the book and the author, this is an issue with the editor and publisher. Clearly nobody who knew German looked over this. Considering the number of typos in the English words as well, neither the editor nor publisher paid very much attention or cared very much at all.
