On page 1 of the author’s (Eugene E. Whitworth) Preface to this book, Nine Faces of Christ: The Quest for the True Initiate, paragraph 2 states: “Research indicates that inside the Great Religions throughout history there has been a learned, secret, initiate group who work for the good of all mankind against false priests, bigotry, and self-complacency.”This book is one of the most sensitive, beautifully-written books with multiple deep layers of meaning about the metaphysical Initiate Christ. The writer says that this book is not about Jesus Christ, the lord of Christianity. But the book really is about a man who was metaphysically Christed, named Joseph-bar-Joseph or Jeshuay-Jeshua who was crucified 57 BC (not 33 AD) or 91 years before the Lord and Savior was crucified. Jeshua and his trials and tribulations represents the metaphysical Christ in each one of us, (the mystical Christed part of our being) who is transfigured from what he learns from his humble beginnings from each person that he encounters, starting with his parents. The book continues on through to his later ascension, something that all true initiates - whether they are Freemasons, Theosophists, or neither of these – must learn and conquer, to become master of his/ her own fate through trials and tribulations. Most people haven’t thought about this and wouldn’t know how to do it if they wanted to do it.Whitworth’s book helps people get on this path through the process of analogy; correspondence; learning: special techniques, colors and their relationships to other things, managing and controlling power and their surges, learning the difference between light and dark but being able to relate to each within the same continuum, observing the 2 genders (his parents) and learning that these 2 genders also reside within himself, and much, much more.What type of life led Jeshua to be crucified? How could he even be on this path to death when he knew that he would die? Regardless of religion, all initiates of the truth are drawn to a path to prepare them for multiple initiations – secret and metaphysical – whose initiations date back to the ancient fountainhead of training of all monotheistic religions, the Egyptian Mystery Schools. Our ancient philosophers, mathematicians, and others (Aristotle, Pythagorus, etc.) also passed through these portals. Those who successfully pass through them gain wisdom, illumination, enlightenment, and are transfigured in some way through severe trials, problem-solving, and by means of certain people (actors), who give a helping hand (when needed).What are these initiations? What do they build? What do they connect/ correspond to?Look at the chapter titles of this book - particularly at 9. and 10., which deal with the “Man of Tin”. What other book that you are aware of talks about a Tin Man? Answer: The Wonderful Wizard of Oz, by L. Frank Baum, a Theosophist. Chapter 11. in Nine Faces deals with the Mysteries of the Orient, so does Baum’s The Wonderful Wizard of Oz (The witches of 2 directions, etc.). Chapter 15, “Soul Flight”, is a topic also demonstrated in The Wizard of Oz with the professor’s balloon (and also the Hijra in Islam in 622 AD). Note that Baum seems to have the same knowledge fount as Whitworth because they discuss the same things. However, Baum’s work is written for young children, whereas Whitworth’s is written for the adult (at a higher, more cognitively astute, complex, intellectual level). Both books, though, have the same golden thread interwoven throughout them.I have reread this book a number of times and am riveted every time that I read it because it is filled with lessons for the would-be initiate, and it leads readers through the thorny parts of how to traverse life, but as with many of us who have had a good beginning with good parents as role models, we learn resilience through observing our parents and how they deal with things. This is what Jeshua was lucky to have had. This is the basis for having some stability in later learning through his trials, being able to fly, being in two places at one time, recognizing the 2 gender poles at work in himself because of seeing them at work in both of his parents, transcending horrific problems, becoming reconfigured to be “born again” – the same path as Jesus Christ and all of the other major religious leader initiates. This book in many ways follows what is taught of Jesus (and his followers) in the Bible but does so by mixing in metaphysics to tell main bible stories.Nine Faces of Christ also connects in many ways to Manly P. Hall’s acclaimed The Secret Teachings of All Ages. A freemason, Hall discussed many of the same topics as did Whitworth and the Theosophists like Baum.I have bought multiple copies of Nine Faces to give to friends to read, so that we can have critical discourse about this book and what its significance is in our own lives.
