Clay Blair has a genius for taking large masses of statistics and data and turning them into a readable and exciting narrative. I had already read Volume II, THE HUNTED, and his SILENT VICTORY, the story of U.S. submarines in WWII, both of which are well worth buying. Blair's detailed analysis and logical presentation  serve to debunk many of the myths that have been generated over the years regarding U-Boat operations.
