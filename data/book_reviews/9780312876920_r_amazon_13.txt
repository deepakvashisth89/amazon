I bought Hominids after stumbling across its description during some research. It seemed like an intriguing idea: parallel dimensions, ours and one where Neanderthals became the prime human species instead of Homo sapiens. It’s obvious a ton of research went into this book and the writer does a good job not overwhelming us with it, for the most part. Refreshing to see something set in Canada too.
