If there was ever a reason to get into reading manga this is it. Nausicaa of the Valley of Wind tells of the legacy of a devoted Joan of Arc esque princess born in a small peaceful valley. An intricate tale is woven  through absolutely masterful drawings to depict Princess Nausicaa's walk of  life to save not only her home but her despotic rulers, their enemies and  the sea of destruction that would destroy them all.Set in the distant  future, man has all but lost the battle against the poisonous jungles which  now cover much of the earth. What remnants remain of human society wage war  for their own interests further culling the dwindling human population.  With more and more people dying young and fewer children living past  infancy, Princess Nausicaa takes a quest of compassion to save the world  from itself.Miyazaki presents a world of hand to hand sword fighting,  huge flying barges, giant insects and a truly admirable princess in a short  skirt.If you die without having experienced these books then you have  never truly lived.Read moreRead less
