This is a sequel to This Present Darkness, but stands alone as an intriguing read. I love a book that has several plots that come together at the end, and Peretti weaves his plots  masterfully. One aspect that he is teaching the reader is the element of spiritual warfare. I thought his interpretation was interesting, but I found myself skipping some of the ply-by-play among the angels and demons. This is the only reason I gave it four stars and not five. It is one of my favorite fiction books, and I bought this as a replacement of a copy I must have loaned. There are only a handful of fiction books that have survived my brutal empty-nest purge, and this is one. Take note, too, that this edition is a smaller print.
