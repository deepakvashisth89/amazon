The concept of this book alone itself is already so amazing. The single pages which highlights a key issue/concern makes sense as it is concise and targeted. I love that the issue is briefly discussed and supported by quotations from artists and ends with an affirmation. I do not think at all that it is "overtly-positive" or zen-like in the sense that it makes it hard to subscribe to. I use it less as an affirmation and more as an encouragement. In any case, it is positive and just the fact that someone has written so close to my heart and thoughts is enough to make me feel less lonely in the studio. I feel that there is a friend just around the corner that I can turn to whenever I am stuck. be it struck by fear, criticism, self loathing or the gazillion things artist attacks themselves with.
