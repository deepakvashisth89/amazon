I totally disagree with Amazon's reviewer on this book, I thought it was a wonderful book full of mystery and intrigue. At the same time it is beautifully written detailing places and times in a way that lets the reader really envision them, as if they were there. I would ask anyone who enjoys family sagas, mystery, intrigue, adventure and a good read to give it a chance, reviewers are not always right. I am not one to suffer through a bad book and I read this one in two sittings and have passed it on to several friends and all liked it as well as I  did.Read moreRead less
