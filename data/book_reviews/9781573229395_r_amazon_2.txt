This is an absorbing and compelling story of the push and pull between a Holocaust survivor and his much younger girlfriend, who reminisces about him and their relationship as she travels to his funeral.  There are multiple timelines in the book as their relationship unfolds and she gets closer and closer to his funeral.  As she discloses the development of their relationship, he reveals his personal history and tries to explain to her why he acts, thinks, and behaves the way he does.Read moreRead less
