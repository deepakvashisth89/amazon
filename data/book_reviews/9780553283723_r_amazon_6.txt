I have admired Kate Elliot since I stumbled upon her Jaran series at a used book store several years ago. I've since read everything I can find of hers and love her intricate cultures and educated thoughtful characters. Even her more light hearted YA targeted Spiritwalker trilogy was fun with the cold vs fire mages and an alternative New World colonization narrative. I especially liked the troll culture. This early work has some of the same themes as the Jaran world.
