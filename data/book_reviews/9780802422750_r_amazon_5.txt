I don't think I have ever read a sadder story and I have read some pretty awful child abuse memoirs. I don't have words to properly describe how this story effected me. It resonated in my soul.The title of my review is my favorite Bible verse, but I had never previously heard of anyone who truly experienced its' meaning, until I read Ms. Van Stone's book. Abandoned by both her parents at an early age, Ms. Van Stone was beaten every single night without fail at the orphanage her and her sister were sent to and later in uncaring foster homes as well as told she was ugly, worthless, and unloved. With no one to turn to she learned to rely only on herself and survive until a chance event introduced her to her heavenly father who would never abandon her even on her darkest days.
