What a fascinating and charming memoir this is. I thoroughly enjoyed it, especially all the great local characters. Pep, Rafael and Juan Juan were instant favourites. This is a lively, warm and humorous read that gives great insights into what real life in Mallorca is like away from all the tourist spots.
