This is one of my favorite books.  The main character, Allan Gordon, believed he was stupid.  He never did well in school and he overheard the school master tell his mother schooling was a waste of time for him.  Allan quit school, and  after a brief stint working for a tailor, he signed up on a whaling vessel where he could make much more money.  The story really gets going when the ship hits an iceberg and everyone perishes except Allan.  Facing incredible problems, the "stupid lad" survives.In addition to the excellent story, there are all kinds of science facts/theories about the arctic that can be explored further.  The last chapter explores the possibility of the story being true.No longer in publication by Scholastic, I was able to find an independent seller at Amazon.  A perfect gift for my nephew to read over summer vacation.  5 Stars.Read moreRead less
