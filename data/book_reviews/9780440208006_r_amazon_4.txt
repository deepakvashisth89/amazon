When I discovered Elaine Coffman, I bought every one of her books. This is my favorite, by far. In fact, it's in my top 5 favorite books of all time, and that top 5 includes Pride & Prejudice, so I do have great taste. Ross is the quintessential Texan who claims his birthright in Scotland. Annabella is a Englishwoman trapped in a betrothal to a Scot she doesn't want. Oh, the sparks that fly, and the romance that blossoms! Read this book.
