This books will make an excellent edition to any bird enthusiast's library. Full of information and excellently illustrated, this volume is the third in an identification series. The first volume being Seabirds, and the second being Shorebirds. If you are looking for a comprehensive guide with beautiful color plates on waterfowl, this is the book for you. But make sure you look for the original hardcover edition. I also have the softcover edition, but the plates look like lousy photocopies, where some of the plates are very failed in color.
