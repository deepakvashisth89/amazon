I was very excited to purchase this book because my wife and I were in the middle of building our own 288 sq. ft. cabin in the woods.  We knew how we were going to build it, but I thought this book might give us additional ideas.It did give us some ideas, but I was expecting a few more details.  I didn't want blueprints, but more sketches and tips would have been nice.  I realize that when a person builds a cabin it is a very personalized thing, but Fears left me always looking for more information.I am glad that I bought this book, and it will remain in my collection for reference for a long time, but I will keep looking for a somewhat more informative cabin book.Read moreRead less
