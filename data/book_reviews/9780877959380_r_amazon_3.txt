Forget soap operas and melodramas; this story has all the glamour, sex, drugs, and crime you can imagine and then some, and it really happened.  I remember when Cheryl Crane was the most famous 14-year old in the country; she had just murdered her mother's sleazy boyfriend.  Since her mother was Lana Turner, that was Big News.Cheryl's autobiography discusses that night and she reflects in detail on her privileged, but unhappy, childhood, her alienation from her too-busy mother, terrifying abuse, and a long road to eventual happiness.
