Book arrived in condition stated and is a very curious examination of the fragments that connect the early tales of the first of the Germanic tribes to invade Britain to the tale of Beowulf and other "lost" tales of the Dark Ages.  These tales are also corroborated in the various early English (Anglo-Saxon) Chronicles left for us to research.  People forget that Tolkien was not only a brilliant fiction writer, he was also the Master of his craft in the study of early Anglo-Saxon literature.
