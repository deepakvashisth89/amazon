Diana Preston gives a very clear account of the technicalities of Scott's ventures. The book is also commendably strong in assessing the personalities involved and their cultural backgrounds.A view is taken of Scott's weaknesses and as well as his strengths. By Amundsen's standards Scott was an amateur in polar exploration who, Diana Preston says, 'nearly succeeded'.Faint praise perhaps? Given Scott's catalogue of errors, failure was on the cards from the word go. But this is a carefully balanced appraisal: It sees all sides of the various issues. Fully recommended.Read moreRead less
