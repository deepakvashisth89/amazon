The characters are all unlikable, caring only for themselves and their needs.  The story takes place in Cape Breton in eastern Canada.  The characters are very poor Highlanders not many generations removed by the Highland Displacement.  The story begins in 1913.  The characters all seem to be angry at life.A young mother and her son begin the story.  The husband has been gone four years, he is a boxer living in the states..The lifestyle in this small town is one of grinding poverty.  Men are miners, go down into the mines every day, then drink on weekends to forget.  Life is not easy on women and children either.  The religion of the land is harsh, bleak,  Everything pleasureful is sinful.  Severe and harsh.Dr. Daniel Ainslie has risen from this background, become a successful doctor and is revered and respected by the working folk.  He married a beautiful woman from a successful family, not of Highland Scots, and wants to adopt Alan MacNeil, the boxer's son.  Archie MacNeil, living in the states, has been beaten, his face  a terrible mess.  Dr Ainslie's wife cannot have children, possibly due to him.  The townspeople look up to Archie MacNeil for being successful and moving away from Cape Breton Island.  Not all do.  Mollie MacNeil's father has nothing to do with her for marrying that man.  Men brag on Archie, ladies tell their sons to keep up with schoolwork, don't go into such a brutal lifestyle.  Dr. Ainslie feels Mollie will always be what she was born to, her son deserves a better choice in life.Sad, bleak lifestyle.  Too sad.
