This is the most concise and focused book on JavaScript. I had tried two other highly recommended books prior to this one, and both left me with many unanswered questions and an image of JavaScript of being an extremely  awkward and unmanageable language. This book made everything crystal-clear.  There are practically no aspects of the language not revealed in the book.  After reading it you will have a structured and in-depth understanding of  JavaScript, including its most subtle topics. Examples are short and very  helpful. Do not hesitate to buy it.
