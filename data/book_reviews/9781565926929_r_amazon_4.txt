As someone with no experience with Lego Mindstorms, but fascinated by the topic, I found this book an excellent introduction to not only the  mindstorm kit itself, but also, at a very basic level, the issues of  robotics.  The book also does an excellent job of surveying the landscape  of programming platforms that can be used to program mindstorms and points  to many online resources for every aspect of building and programming lego  robots.If you are a professional robotics researcher or an experienced  Mindstorms developer this book might be a bit basic for you, but for a new  comer this book provides a very nice introduction.
