Fascinating look at everything to do with The Other Place, from demons, mythology, to how it's represented in art, music and literature, to where it crops up in popular culture, and even how it's been portrayed in advertising and on greetings cards.  My only real negative - apart from the price - is that it's written very much from an American point of view, so a lot of the stuff about advertising and TV shows was a bit lost on me (though it's been used on plenty of TV advertising this side of the pond too).
