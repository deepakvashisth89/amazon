I have read a number of Le Carre's books -- The Spy Who Came In From the Cold, Tinker Taylor, and The Constant Gardener to name a few.  I love his writing, even if I don't always know for sure what is going on.  He makes me think. A Perfect Spy is by far his best.  The main character is the mystery, and of course the solution is the past.  I know that this sounds like a trope employed by just about every mystery writer out there (how many detectives have a long-buried secret they must confront in the light of a present case??!!) But as usual Le Carre bends, improves and ultimately owns the genre.  Like Monet and Van Gogh; Mozart, Handel or Bach; Le Carre is a true artist.Read moreRead less
