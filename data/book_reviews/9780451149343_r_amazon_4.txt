The folks at Angry Robot Books should be ashamed of themselves for the half-assed production of this book. Punctuation where there should be none, "I' replaced by "J", character names misspelled...the list of offenses goes on. I will be very hesitant to purchase any future titles from Angry Robot. Terrible.That being said, for fans of speculative fiction, you can't go wrong with the work of the man who christened the Steampunk genre. Infernal Devices is a clever, funny romp through Victorian England, complete with clockwork automatons, frog-faced villagers, a mad inventor's son pulled into a madcap adventure, and not a little amount of social and political satire. There's really little I can add to KW Jeter's reputation except to say, if you're a Steampunk fan and you haven't read Jeter, get thee to a bookstore!
