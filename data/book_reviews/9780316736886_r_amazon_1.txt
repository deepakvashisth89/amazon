Jardine's debut novel describes the life of a "train wreck": a would-be artist so strung out on drink, drugs and the New York night scene that she is being evicted for non-payment of rent even though she earns a good living managing a chic bar/restaurant.  Supposedly a work of fiction about Lee, a redhead - but the book cover suggests otherwise - it is a picture of the author, who is blonde. Fiction fantasies based on underlying fact? No one could write like this without some experience!I think it took tremendous courage to write this book,  It reads like a hyper or manic person's attempt to jot down details as fast and accurately as possible when a rush of ideas takes over.  Maybe I am reading into this, because that is what happens to me.  This novel is full of symbols, metaphors and similes that describe a scene or a person eloquently in just a few words. E.G. : "Drugs turned the cardboard box of an ordinary day into a honeycomb, dripping and blond" ... "I didn't buy his act.  His act was that he had no act"....:Trying to be normal...is turning me into more of a freak"... Whenever  I met a new medicine cabinet at a party,.... and  "Brendan looked like Jack Niklaus on crack".I don't think this is just a good book, I think it is a great book.Jardine  still manages a bar/restaurant. she is my favorite bartender!
