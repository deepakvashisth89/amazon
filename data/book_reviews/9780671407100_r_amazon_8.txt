This by far, is one of my favorite novels. I have read it about four times since 2003. Growing up in a very Christian household, this book would never had made it past the front door of my parents house. It has offended many and pleased many, yet religious or not, I don't think it's a book you should stay away from. It's fictional and really made me ponder the life of Jesus Christ. I have always been intrigued by the relationship between Jesus and both Mary's. This book really made me wonder about Jesus fondness for Mary the prostitute, but again this book is fiction. Maybe some took it to seriously or saw it as a major offense to their Christianity, however if you can look past that and read the book for what it is, well then you may find yourself in the middle of a novel you cannot put down.Read moreRead less
