I hated to put this book down. Most Historical Romance books are more or less predictable, but Penelope Neri writes the unexpected...you never know what is going to happen next. She is a new read for me, but I shall be buying more of her books. I'm an avid reader and always looking for an author who keeps my interest, and No Sweeeter Paradise certainly did that.
