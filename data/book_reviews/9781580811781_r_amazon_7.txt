Those who have seen the movie Agnes of God be warned - it can not compare to the actual play.  Reading the play is just as fulfilling as seeing it performed.  Three characters - a shrink, a mother superior, and an innocent nun.  Throughout the play, roles are examined, discarded, built upon and interchanged between the three characters.  The author gives the characters real depth.  Readers can see themselves in any of the three roles without any trouble.Read moreRead less
