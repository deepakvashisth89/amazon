We have a large collection of Boynton books and love nearly all of them. I got this one for my nearly 2 year old for Christmas and now it's one of his favorites (and mine too). I have books that he reaches for that I don't enjoy reading as much (The Belly Button book being one of them), but this one I gladly read over and over.The book is a cute opposites book.
