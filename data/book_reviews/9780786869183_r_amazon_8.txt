I love this book, I am an author and was writing a book on Martha's Vineyard in their rather wonderful library. I tend to get tired of writing after two to three hours but find if I read poetry it's as good as a brief nap. However, this book was on the shelves, I took it out and was entranced. Each day I came back to it. And then I bought a copy for myself and each day I read something to remind me of when the United States of America was great.
