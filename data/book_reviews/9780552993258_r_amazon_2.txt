First the title of this book caught my attention; Rebecca Sheldon sounds quite like my niece's name, and then the synopsis intrigued me. So, while this genre is not one I would normally read, I read the sample online. That did it for me, I downloaded the book (which I was lucky to get free) and for the first time ever - I abandoned the book I was reading in favour of this one. What a good decision it was to download this book, because I really, really loved it. The characters were well developed, I loved the relationships between them all and the descriptions of places and lives were well written. It was a book I thought about each day and looked forward to reading each night, which for me is the acid test of a good book.Read moreRead less
