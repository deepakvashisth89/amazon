The content of this book is fantastic. The essays give insight into photography in a way that I had never thought of before and the photographic essays give context and background for those essays. Any fan of art, philosophy, or just pretty pictures should pick up a copy. The only negative thing I have to say is that this book was formatted HORRIBLY. It looks as if it was made in Microsoft Word.
