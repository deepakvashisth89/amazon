I have only read two books in the series (this book and the first one) and Death Shall Overcome is currently my second favorite by a significant margin.The racial and political message feels dated in some places, but still relevant and powerful in others. The way some of the protestors are treated as jokes left me with mixed feelings, both in context of the book itself and real-life. I read that it may have been supposed to be satirical and comical, but it doesn't come across that way to me.There weren't really enough suspects, and (especially compared to the first book) they weren't developed too much due to the backdrop of the protests.There are a couple of truly infuriating racists in the book but they get comeuppances of sorts.As far as the mystery went, I suspected the right person (although I did have other suspects) but had the motive wrong. Most of the significant clues discussed in the final chapter were ones which I remembered well, but hadn't given any significance or attention to.This was an ok book, but not one I expect to re-read all the way through, and it would have improved by devoting another 3 or 4 chapters to the actual mystery.Read moreRead less
