I like that it is written so that one can feel a bit of sympathy for the aliens, who do hideous damage to the planet - and to any life forms unlucky to accidentally be in their path. I did not like that the only person in the trilogy (Tenth Planet, Oblivion, and Final Assault) who is apparently a believer in God is a dangerous loon who believes that the aliens are a ruse used by the government to kill her family. Another ridiculous idea is that the girl genius, Portia, was homeless for the first half of her life because her parents who were teachers had poor medical insurance. That is idiotic. The teachers' unions have provided their members with better benefits than most of us will ever have. But on the whole, the series is very good.
