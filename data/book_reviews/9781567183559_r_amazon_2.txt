D. A Heeley's debut novel is the most irrelevant, predictable, stereotypical stack of contextual errors I've ever had the misfortune of reading. The simpleton audience he panders to is repeatedly encouraged to research outdated misinformation on contemporary occult practices on their own time, effectively perpetuating a clumsy counter-culture that should have died out with the birth of the Internet. I hate absolutely everything about this book. From the inhale-exhale pace, to the weak and mercilessly redundant descriptions, to the infuriatingly loyal adherence to archetypal characters, to the sloppy settings lazily braided together with half-hearted, unoriginal, loose historical "lore" from "mostly-fantasy" lands.
