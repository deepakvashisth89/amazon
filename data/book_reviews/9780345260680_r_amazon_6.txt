Rocket Ship Galileo is the first book in Robert Heinlein's Juvenile series and was published in 1947.Released 22 years before man landed on the moons surface. In an older review when released it was quoted by someone that the book was "too far out".This book gripped me from the very first page. By far one of my favorite of his juveniles and this being the first in the series makes it a great start for a first time reader of Heinlein.The tale takes place just after WWII. We meet three young budding scientists straight out of high school. Ross, Morrie and Art are founders of their own "Galileo Rocket Club", as the boys test their unmanned rockets in the back of their field. These rockets aren't meant for travel...not yet at least. At their age they were scientifically unable to pursue such silliness until further educated in the scientific method and jet propulsion. Until an unlikely accident leads them to Art's Uncle, Doctor Don Cargraves, well known in the field of rocketry. The boys are as captivated as much by his knowledge as Cargraves is in their rocket club. Hence(with parental permission), the three boys and Art's Uncle have joined forces in an attempt to build a rocket and go to the moon.Reading this, I truly felt like a child underneath the covers past bedtime.
