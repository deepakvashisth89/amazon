Demian, a novel written by Hermann HesseThe novel Demian tells the story of an upper class boy named Emil Sinclair that struggles with his inside demons and finally finds enlightenment.The story takes place in a small town in Germany in the early 1900s.The book starts with Emil talking about the relation between him and his family and how he sees the world divided into 2, the good(light) realm, and the evil(dark) realm.This one day he lies to this other kid, called Franz Kromer  while bragging about stealing some apples, then Franz blackmails Emil by threatening him by telling on him, what Franz wanted was 2 marks(old German money).During this period of time Emil's life becomes dominated by fear of his parents or other authority finding out. One day this new kid Max Demian moves into town and attends to the same school Emil did, the first's days Emil is indifferent about him but this one day they encounter in the park and Demian notices Emil's fear toward Franz but does nothing.For a few weeks Emil did not encountered Franz so he decided to look for him, when he founds him Franz ignores him and tries to evade him. The next day he talks to Demian and he asks if there had been a change in Franz's behavior, Emil quickly asks what he did to Franz but he only said "don't worry, but if he messes with you again just tell me".Several years have passed since last encounter with Demian, Emil and enroll confirmation classed and sat together. While the priest talks about biblical stories Demian interprets them in a very unconventional way.Emil is sent away to a boarding school and falls into a phase of depression and self-hatred because no one liked him. He is invited by an older kid into a bar and gets drunk, from that point on his lifestyle goes from non-healthy to scum.One day he sees a beautiful women from afar, he does not meet her but he worships her and names her Beatrice(he did not know her real name), he gives up his drunken life, he bring up his grades and he starts painting his dreams. He later paints an image from a dream of a bird emerging from a shell, and sends the painting to Demian.Later on he paints a woman that looks like, as he remembers, Demian's mother and he is filled with desire to find her.When he finishes boarding school he looks for Demian and he sees him after almost 4 years, Demian shows Emil where he lives and the next day he looks for Demian's mother, Eva, she was exactly as he pictured her.She tells Emil all that Demian told her about him and how she thinks he is a one of the few people with the mark that made them possible to interpret things in a different way, she also asks him to refer to her as "Frau Eva" which all her student called her.Emil develops a special relationship with Frau Eva, and they often discuss his dreams and his desire to discover his inner self.At the end Emil has achieved a sense of inner peace and harmony with himself. Soon, however, World War I starts, and both Emil and Demian fight in the army. When Emil is injured in the war, he wakes up in a hospital bed to discover that Demian, also injured, is lying in the bed next to him. The next morning Emil discovers that Demian has died. Emil realizes that Demian has always been the key to his true inner self.Warning, this is a really complex book and it is not written for closed minded people.Read moreRead less
