Hunter Thompson is an American icon.  His books are classics as is this one.  I read this book many years ago.  I bought it to read during the campaign of 2020.  I did not realize what a prophet Thompson was about campaigns.  Gonzo politics is here today.  I can only wonder what Thompson would be writing today about the 2020 race.  Because his surreal and way out there description of the 1972 campaign may seem rather normal by today's standards.  I shared this book with a friend and will do so with my adult children.
