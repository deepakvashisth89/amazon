The main problem I find in Aimee Leduc books, although I have liked all of them, is that they refer to a passed time and you are suppose to remember the events. Many initials are used that once I knew but some have even changed meaning and you have a hard time finding what that group was.  The other problem is that the motivation of the main character is not clear. Why she does not go to the police? Why she does not trust anybody and suddenly believe something a stranger says to her.
