After Clinton'simpeachment, his popularity rose 5%.* Voters are a lot less concerned about the abstract qualities acandidate may have than they are about what he will do to help themin their own personal lives.* People are more inclined to vote for, not against.* The art of leadership is to maintain sufficient forward motion to controlevents and steer public policy without losing public support.And this one that every politician must remember:* After you lose, nurture your donors.
