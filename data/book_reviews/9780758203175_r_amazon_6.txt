Quite disappointing.  I bought this book solely on the basis of his stellar short story, The Outline of a Torso in the anthology, Summer Share.  It's still an interesting read with a well-written protagonist and love interest.  But it dwells too much on Harry's relationship with Amity, that I find his interactions with Nicolo wanting.  He becomes too drab at times, too straight-laced in others, compared to the zany machinations of would-be gold digger Amity.I was expecting a romance but what I got was more of a story of friendship.  Maybe, that was my failing.  But it is still a satisfying tale and worthy of your time.Read moreRead less
