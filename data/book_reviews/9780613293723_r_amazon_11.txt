I must admit I was pleasantly surprised by this book. The historical approach was intriguing, far fetched at times, but always capturing. Fictional  history with a great touch. The artefacts, treasures and scrolls from the Library of Alexandria in Egypt, the ancient Romans, Alexander the  Great, and so on. What else could you ask for? I may be a little biased on this one. Possibly, because I am from Rome and I love this kind of stuff. Who knows? You might like it too for other reasons, such as action and adventure.
