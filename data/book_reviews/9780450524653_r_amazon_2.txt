I became a true Paul Simon fan around 1980, when I learned to play guitar and the usual S&G tunes, but particularly when my school cantina played the "Still Crazy" album and me ears stuck up when I heard that wonderful intro with the Fender Rhodes, and then there's that voice, and the weary melody, the verses, the punch line, that fabulous bridge with the flute, and I knew this was an extension of my musical horizon. Two years later, I would bring my first girlfriend to the S&G reunion concert in Rotterdam, and the rest is (personal, musical) history.Seven years, two girlfriends and one PS concert later, I was sitting in my narrow Amsterdam room, third floor, backside, and I had just bought Humphries' book. I settled in my cosy brown armchair, and started to read.It was 06.00 o'clock the next morning when I finished it. 'Nuff said. Just buy it, and read it.
