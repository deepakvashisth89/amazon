I didn't enjoy this book as much as other reviewers.  I thought it was a sad, mournful book; the plot and the characters. There was nothing light or humorous about it. I realize a murder mystery is not light or humorous, but there could have been a few moments that weren't so doleful. It seemed that all of the main characters had some major disturbance in their lives; especially the crippled boy and his drunken mother?, grandmother?.  The wife was unhappy with her current life and also with her new lover. The investigator was trying to get over his former partner. They all had such major problems that it was not a pleasure to read the book.  It was well written, but not anything I would re-read.Read moreRead less
