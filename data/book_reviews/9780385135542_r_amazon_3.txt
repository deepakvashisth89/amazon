Best book ever written for anyone who wants to understand the values and experiences of a first people's family and society. Whittier from the perspective of a historical fiction but fully accurate to the spiritual and society values of the people. Extremely well researched. As I understand it Ms. Hill spent over ten years writing this book in the native Lakota language and them translating it into English. Many equivalent words in English simply do not exist so the values of the first people's and apparent in the lack of words like should or can't. Very enlightening.
