First published in 1978, this was a groundbreaking book. It's still a pleasure to read, although there would be more to add with the development of animal studies. Loosely structured, the book examines the changing relationships between wolves and men from many different angles, paying special attention to indigenous Americans' respectful attitude to the species (which contrasts with the settlers' urge to exterminate them). The book is a pleasure to read in that it combines facts, stories and testimony in a sometimes poetic, always readable manner.
