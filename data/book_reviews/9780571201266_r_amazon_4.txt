Pelevin gets deeper into reality--or maybe just questioning reality-- with each novel.  I love that every plot point, even name and character could be completely crucial, or could not matter at all, simply depending on what you get out of the novel.  His delicately detached protagonist (aptly named Voyd) reminds me of Murakami Haruki's pasta-making anti-heroes (who in fact turn out to be heroes after all).
