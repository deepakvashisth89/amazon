Well, if you are, then you won't be disappointed. The Doctor in all his 8 regenerations (up until that point) is here in all his glory. Every single one of them articulated to the point where you can close your eyes and see him there. As you read along, you'll hear his voice, his intonations. The Doctor is most definitely here, and you'll really enjoy seeing him, if you're a fan.However...If you're here for a well written novel, you might want to look elsewhere. Despite Terrance Dicks' marvelous gift for coaxing the Doctor off the page and onto your mental viewscreen, as it were, the book reads more like a screenplay than a novel. The clunky writing does grate and does take away from what would otherwise be a blissful bit of Whovian fun.Not enough for me to dislike the book, but definitely enough to knock one star off... not that you would care, if you're here for the Doctor.The OCR is slightly flawed (I found it somewhat amusing to hear talk of a "corn unit" - com unit), but not terribly so.All in all, I would recommend it :)Read moreRead less
