At loose ends, with her husband, Rory, and her son otherwise occupied, Troy Alleyn embarks on a pleasure cruise down the Thames.  She is accompanied, of  course, by a colorful cast of characters.  Early on in the cruise, Troy notices the charming countryside, a stand of elms, a local pub and exclaims-"The place is swarming with Constables... A perfect clutch of them". She is referring to John Constable, a British landscape painter who's work consisted of scenes such as the ones Troy was viewing from the boat.  The title could also refer to the number of local constables Troy and her husband interact with during the course of the book.Again, Ms. Marsh is revisiting a familiar scenario of a boating adventure.
