My girlfriend and I saw Steve Earle @ Massey Hall in Toronto in Oct. w/ Emmylou Harris, Robert Plant, Daniel Lanois plus others on stage. It was the intimacy and warmth of Earle's anecdotes that made us recall that we need to read his short stories. This book was bought as a Christmas gift. We also saw Earle @ the Phoenix in Toronto for the 30th anniversary tour of Guitar Town. He continues to be political, forthright and outspoken, and it made me realize we need more artists like him today.
