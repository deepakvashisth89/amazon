Breeder by Clegg was a nice little horror novel to pass the time between more memorable horror novels. It wasn't my first Clegg read and does not stand out in my memory as anything fantastic. I thought of it as a sort of Rosemary's Baby, only without the incredible use of psychological horror. Nothing bad here, but I have nothing that positive to say about it except for it being very well-written and, in the horror field, this is something worthy of mention.Read moreRead less
