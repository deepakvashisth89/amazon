I like this series of books - the quantity of recipes makes up for the lack of pictures, and everything is quick and easy.  It's true that you might want to increase the seasoning, but the recipes themselves are solid and easily tinkered with.  I've cooked quite a few dishes from this book and my favourites were:  sweet potato and lentil soup, aubergine enchiladas, spicy pecan balls in tomato sauce, banana and date chimichangas, and the hot chocolate.
