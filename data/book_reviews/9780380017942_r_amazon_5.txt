Maybe I should hate to admit it, but was it really about forty years ago I first read this? If it wasn’t, it was close. I was in my late teens, if I remember correctly, and Michael Moorcock was my number one author. My only author: I didn’t want to read anyone else! And even if Elric of Melnibone was my especial hero, The Dancers at the End of Time was my favourite book.But maybe you shouldn’t go back? I picked up a copy of one of Elric of Melnibone’s finest a few years afterwards, and I didn’t get very far, so I was cautious approaching The Dancers at the End of Time, and started by buying just An Alien Heat. I needn’t have worried. I still really enjoyed it and I’ve read the other two since. I’m thinking that Elric deserves another visit.Read moreRead less
