Thornton Burgess was a remarkable man who led a multi-faceted life and created a very special world (remember the Green Meadows, the Smiling Pool and the Laughing Brook?) full of interesting creatures (remember Reddy Fox, Peter Rabbit and Johnny Chuck?) whose experiences warmly sketched out in this early but timeless book provide a wonderful nature education for its youthful audience  -- and I think for thoughtful adults as well.
