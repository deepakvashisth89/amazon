This story took a little longer to get going then her first two efforts but once it did, it read quickly. Of course there was some predictability but also enough plot twists to keep the pages turning and further character development that is always nice to see in a series. Even if you have been to Paris several times, this really gives you the feeling of daily life, be it glamorous, seedy or historically rich. This is a fun series for those who love Paris and enjoy a quick read.Read moreRead less
