This is one of my favorite books. I first saw it at a friend's house in 1995 and had to drive over 100 miles to find a bookstore which had a copy. I bought two, one for myself and another to gift to a very dear friend. Both of us still reach for the book with incredible frequency.I think it is a magic book because I use it like a drug when I'm feeling stressed out or looking for a creative spark. I can browse through its pages quickly or I can stop and admire a single page for a long time. Goldsworthy is a visionary whose grasp of the transient quality of art and nature is unlike that of any artist I've ever seen. His natural constructions are breathtaking in the duality of their complexity and simplicity, and of the causal parallelism of natural and man-made space. There is no synchronicity here, just the beautiful illusion of it.Thanks to the internet, I have purchased this book many, many times to give as a gift to only the people whom I have a deep connection with. I have gifted many books over the years, but this is the only one I give people that I absolutely know they look at over and over.It's definitely one of my "trapped on a desert island" books. If I was only allowed one, this would probably be it because I would never tire of it. I've paged through it, easily, 1000+ times. I highly recommend it.Read moreRead less
