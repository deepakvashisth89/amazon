I read all the Willow King books a few years ago ; some good parts and a bit of a change then. Oddly enough Willow and Tom feature briefly, and slightly lamely, in this new series. Some brilliant ideas and quite upsetting at times . My main caveats are the erratic and frankly almost cardboard cut out police investigation coupled with a too demonised Antonia and a far too feeble Ben .Trish seems to veer between single handedly changing the world and then being an OTT " poor me ,how can I cope " person. For me ,this really weakens the many strengths of much good writing .Some readers may find the ending exciting and surprising . I found it a little garbled and had a sense that the author was trying to tie up too many loose ends at  once. I will still try another in the series to see how things develop.Read moreRead less
