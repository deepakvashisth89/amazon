Obviously an early work for Kenyon the story was a good read.  When I found out she had issues with the publisher on this one and plans on reissueing it closer to her original story I was intrigued.  I read and liked this one and I plan on getting the rerelease to see the differences.  From how Kenyon has evolved in the decade since this was published I am expecting an even more enjoyable read.  Purchase this if you are a obsessive collector like me or wait for the new issue.
