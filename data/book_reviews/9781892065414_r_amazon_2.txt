This third entry in the story of Garreth Mikaelian is every bit as good as, and maybe a tiny bit better than, the first two.  In search of a cop-killer, who is possibly a vampire like Garreth, he travels around the country, from Iowa to California and most places in between, finding clues and interesting people.  Now that Garreth has at least one powerful ally, you might think he would not have to do all the work himself, but his vampire "grandmother" jets off to Europe, leaving Garreth on his own.
