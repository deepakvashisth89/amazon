Unlike a few other Ruth Rendell books, this one is memorable. Not for the fact that it is a thriller which keeps you turning pages, but for the setting and suspense as to how the book will eventually end.The story is as the cover reads, a pathological mother's influence on her daughter.For people who like a breathtaking landscape, dark characters with a shade of grey and cold blooded killings it is highly recommended. However, if you prefer a book with a faster pace with a highly anticipated climax, then you may find yourself skimming pages.Personally, I liked the book because of its unique story line and complex set of characters.Read moreRead less
