An interesting collection of short stories, some of which were published elsewhere.  Of particular interest is the author's discussion of how the Dortmunder series of novels came into being.  What we have are 9 interesting Dortmunder stories, one story which I would call a scene rather than a complete short story, and one short story about other characters similar to Dortmunder and Associates.  It is only a dozen if you count the author's discussion.  What the collection contains is"Dortmunder and Me, in Short" - an interesting discussion by the author about the development of the Dortmunder character."Ask a Silly Question" - A solicitation to Dortmunder by an individual who wants to recover something given to his wife.  A usual with Dortmunder, nothing works out right, but there are some side benefits."Horse Laugh" - A man wants assistance in stealing a valuable horse.  Nothing works out as expected."Too Many Crooks" - A beautiful plan to tunnel into a bank's vault.  Oops...someone else had ideas about robbing the bank.  Well, there is more than one way to handle things."A Midsummer Daydream" - Dortmunder is off away from the city while things cool off at home, but gets accused of a robbery.  He has to find the real thief, and there is a little twist at the end."The Dortmunder Workout" - This is really a short scene at OJ's rather than a short story (my reason for knocking a star off the rating).  People should not include writing exercises in their published collections."Party Animal" - Dortmunder's escape from a crime scene takes him into the middle of someone's party.  He has to improvise a bit, and part with some of his loot.  All's well that ends well, at least for Dortmunder."Give Till it Hurts"  - finds Dortmunder escaping from another theft, and this time finding himself in the middle of a "friendly" poker game.  He discovers that you have to use care in how you play a game."Jumble Sale" - Dortmunder finds himself in the middle of an attempted sting.  As the end result, he makes a swap."Now What" - Dortmunder finds himself in possession of a valuable brooch.  The problem that he has is disposing of it as everything goes wrong.  It's like it is cursed.  He finally makes a deal, but it does not turn out as expected."Art and Craft"  - A former acquaintance, whom Dortmunder does not really trust, tries to pull Dortmunder into a robbery scheme.  Dortmunder is not buying, and the results are not what anyone expected."Fugue for Felons" - this is not about Dortmunder, but is about some similar characters who have plans for robbing a bank.
