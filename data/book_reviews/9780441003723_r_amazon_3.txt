When I began reading this novel, I confess that I didn't really like the aliens who populate Starplex, especially the Waldahudin.  But to Sawyer's considerable credit, he did an excellent job of developing all of his characters - humans and aliens alike.  This story started out a bit slow, but by the end of the novel, there was enough action and conflict to please any reader who enjoys good action stories, including seat-of-the pants space piloting, a well-written battle scene, and potential and actual disasters galore.
