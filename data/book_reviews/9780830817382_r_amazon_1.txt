I recommend this book to those who: explode with unidentified anger, believe things are their fault, hide their feelings, avoid conflicts, feel inadequate, and have difficulty trusting others, liking themselves, or don't really know what they like; those with low self esteem. Jim's research is VERY helpful to those who sincerely believe they are the only ones with these issues. He encourages those with pain to turn their pain & parents over to God; admitting that we do not have the ability to change our lives & we are powerless to overcome our hurts without God.  I use Jim's book along with the step study of Celebrate Recovery.
