I first purchased this book second-hand from a thrift store in Phoenix, Arizona. I don’t know that I was necessarily looking for the American Bible of Outlaw Poetry. I believe it found me. At some point during my 10+ years that book disappeared-it needed to be with someone else. I purchased my second copy on Amazon and will hold onto it until it’s time to pass on.  This collection harkens back to the days of cafe’s in Paris, New York, San Francisco-I can smell cigarettes burning in ashtrays while people thump their chests and pound the tiny imbalanced tables with fists to emphasize their impassioned philosophies!
