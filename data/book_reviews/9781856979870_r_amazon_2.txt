I have 3 sons, ages 14, 12 and 8.  When they were little, I read the usual baby books at bedtime -- and boy, did I get bored with Good Night Moon.  I found this book of poetry in a bookstore and supplemented the usual bedtime books with poems from this collection.  It was one of the best things I ever did.  This introduced my sons to classics that I knew, like Rime of the Ancient Mariner and Hiwatha and Stopping by Woods on a Snowy Evening.  As well as Shakespearen sonnets.  And classics that I didn't know, like Xanadu and others.  My boys loved it.  I felt great that I was introducing them to timeless classics that are not read in school anymore.I continued to read poetry to my sons as they got older.  It was a good complement to chapter books.  It kept me sane and they love to hear them.I have looked at other collections but they just don't have this terrific selection of 'great' and 'funny' poems.  I always came back to this book.Lastly, this book makes a great gift to new parents.  Someone will give them "Go Dogs Go" --- but no one will think to give them classic poems.Read moreRead less
