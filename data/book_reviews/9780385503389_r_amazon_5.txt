I'm 74, white, retired LtCol. I've read hundreds of books on WWII and thousands of other books. THIS book is immaculately researched making it even more devastatingly impactful. How Abdul Jabbar found the strength to write this shows his commitment to seeking the truth. My God, beyond that is was personal, it was family. I loved his style of tight self-contained paragraphs each one a story onto itself. I can not imagine the reams of research being each one of them. This should be one of these required readings for every teenager in our country. I cried at the end. I hope this makes as much of a difference for all as it did for me.
