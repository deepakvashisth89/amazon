DESCRIPTION:Born of two worlds, Indigo Wolf was as free and proud as the noble Comanche blood flowing through her veins. No man has ever possessed her elusive spirit - until a magnificent stranger came to work in her family's mine. Jake Rand's bold caress ignited flames of a searing desire that bound her to him. Yet, he was a man she dared not trust.MY THOUGHTS:Although this is the third book in the series and I'd definitely recommend reading the two that came before it first, it can also be read as a standalone. Unfortunately there were things about it that reminded me way too much of book two (Comanche Heart) and ultimately what kept me from loving it. Like Amy in that one it took nearly the entire length of the story before Indigo came to terms with her past.And like Swift before him, Jake puts up with a whole lot from his lady love. I still thought their romance was very beautiful though and I enjoyed seeing how the other members of Indigo's family were doing. For such a long tale I'm happy to say I was never bored either and there were some nice twists too.
