What I like about this book is that even though it is focused on urban living, it still has information and ideas that are functional for any living situation. For example, they had a list of substitute herbs and oils (and even advise which herbs not to use due to stench/toxicity), both authors gave a list of their tool kits, and they even had a chapter about auto-magic.
