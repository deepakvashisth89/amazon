This is a remarkable survey, one that is succinct, good-humored, and detailed. It recounts the entire life story of the Lord of Pain, from pre-Christian beginnings through today, with many a telling and relevant glance at other cultures and thought systems. What matters in the end is less belief in the Devil than his enduring appeal; as Stanford says, we may not believe in vampires, but we love movies and books about them.
