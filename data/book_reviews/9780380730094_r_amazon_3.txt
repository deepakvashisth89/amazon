Bannerman is a stick-figure compared to Bond. His miraclous escapades depend upon his "owning" everybody in sight. Just as unbelievable as Bond's dependence upon fantastic "technology." Both rely too heavily on "deus ex machina" solutions to overwhelming threats. rather than on the moral strength of the hero.The engaging Narrative moves along at a pleasing rate and the other characters seem more alive than the hero.
