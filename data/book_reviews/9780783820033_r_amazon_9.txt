An emotive, thoughtfully, and sensitively written book.I found it gripping and it had it's twists and turns.I couldn't relate to all the characters, but overall I found the story line good, intense at times.'Mercy killing' is a very debatable topic and drawing lines between compassion and selfishness always an interesting subject. I was disappointed with the ending however, and would have liked another chapter to tie up unknowns.Overall I enjoyed reading this thought provoking book.Read moreRead less
