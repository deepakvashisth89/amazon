While I am not normally a big fan of fiction, the fact that this novel is based on a political scandal that is still largly buried in the US media  intrigued me (for the truth about "Blood Trail, search Canadian media  web sites or worldnetdaily) As a political/medical thriller, it is above  par. The writting is a bit pedestrian and the characters, with the  exception of the lead character David Farr, lack depth. However the book  takes you on a wild roller coaster ride filled with suprising plot twists  and excitment.David Farr is out to get those who, by knowingly let tainted  blood be shipped for medical use, infected his son with the aids virus.  Along the way he must breakthrough a vast cover up while trying to outwit  the pursuing "dixie mafia" intent on keeping the story buried by  any means nessacary. The fact that this novel is loosely (very loosely)  based on actual events makes it all the more frighting. Still more  frighting are the attacks, threats and harrasment the author recieved after  this book was published, even though it was written with a  pseudonym.Definitly worth reading.Read moreRead less
