Patricia Highsmith - one of my absolute favourite authers! And "People Who Knock on the Door" is a novel which caught my attention in a special way. It reminded me of my own time at a Christian boarding school and my relationship to my own parents in that time. Which wasn't so bad at all! But so I (understandibly) built myself a shockingly real connection to the plot of this novel and felt the conflicts of the family in my own bones. It was awesome!!! I almost shuttered when the boy had to go to his parents, just to receive another blame from his god-fearing father, who doesn't want to allow him anything which (in his opinion) isn't appropriate to their "Christian" customs. The plot makes religion to something You have kind to fear. This is nowadays a scaring subject, which causes enough violence and even wars in the world, so You have the feeling that the mischief in this book even may reach You in Your own walls - or "knock on the door"!Patricia Highsmith truly knew how to create a topic that stays current over a long time. You can always go back to her novels, because they never will be outdated! I highly recommend them to everybody who loves reading or wants to discover it. Her novels - along with Graham Greene's or Jeffrey Archer's - always show me that old school thrillers are still the best when You are looking for something with permanent effect.Read moreRead less
