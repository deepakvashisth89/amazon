Troy - Roderick Alleyn's artist wife - decides on the spur of the moment to go on a cruise in Constable country.  She wants peace and quiet but virtually as soon as she gets on board  the MV Zodiac she realises something is wrong and has grave suspicions about what her fellow passengers are really up to.  On the first morning she discovers that the person whose place she took has actually been murdered.  Troy's husband is away from home and she starts to get so concerned about what is going on that she phones Scotland Yard and passes on her suspicions.Is there perhaps a connection with a criminal known as `The Jampot'?  Alleyn's colleague Inspector Fox starts to wonder.  What could have been a pleasant and relaxing cruise turns into a nightmare when one of the passengers disappears.  I enjoyed reading about Troy in the guise of detective and thought the rest of the passengers were well done.  I liked the sound of the boat as well and though the somewhat claustrophobic atmosphere was convincing.This is an enjoyable mystery and possibly one of Ngaio Marsh's best in my opinion.
