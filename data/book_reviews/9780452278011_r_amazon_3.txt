This book is an excellent read and full of expert know how to avoid HR and get to the decision makers. Job searches are frustrating simply because so much is outside our control and it becomes a passive process.  This book changes that with insights and tips to help you regain control of the process and the outcome!There are some exceptions to his suggestions,however. He indicates that you should give a working interview and indicate how you will improve the company's bottom line...this does not work in the non profit arena and many of the skills neccessary to flourish in that field are not demonstrable in a meeting.Also, he seems to have experience working mostly with men by the overall tone of his work. He indicates that decision makers will hire you if you can prove that you can do the job. While this is certainly part of the case, women make many of their hiring decisions based on whether or not the candidate's personality will be a good fit. In some cases, this seems to be more important to female decision makers than work ability and skill.I highly recommend that you read for yourself and consider implementing many of his suggestions, keeping in mind the above two exceptions to his information.Read moreRead less
