This is one of my all-time favorite books. Not only is it beautifically written and illustrated, but children LOVE this book.I initially purchased this book when my 16 year old daughter was a toddler, and my 21 year old son was a kindergartener. This book has followed us through many moves, and has been a favorite book to all 6 of my children. My (current) 3 year old has most of it memorized, and it is the book he asks for most often to be read to him before bed. If something ever happened to this book, I would not hestitate to re-purchase it immediately!The rhymes are great, and tells about the creation of the earth. The illustrations are pretty and engaging.
