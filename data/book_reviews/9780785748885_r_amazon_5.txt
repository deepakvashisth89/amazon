This book is a collection of short stories written by Agatha Christie in the 1930’s. There are some familiar elements Agatha Christie fans will recognize: Parker Pyne has a secretary named Miss Lemon, there is a character with the last name Leatheran, and  there is a story with the title Death on the Nile.When I read the first few stories, I thought the book should be retitled Parker Pyne Manipulates. Let me explain, Parker Pyne solves marital problems by hiring a handsome man or a beautiful woman to fool his client or their spouse into thinking they are ‘exciting’ or have someone on the side. Further into the book there are more ‘investigation’ related stories some centered around jewel thieves. Which brings to mind the question, what in the heck is plasticine and why does everyone have some on hand?The first story in the book is titled “The Case of the Middle-Aged Wife” where, you guessed it, marital problems. The husband falls for a younger secretary in the office and the wife goes to Parker Pyne for help. I couldn’t help but think of Agatha Christie’s own marriage and wondered if this was the outcome she was hoping for her own marriage.I rated this book a 3.5/5 because I enjoyed the stories in the book that involved murder and real investigations.Read moreRead less
