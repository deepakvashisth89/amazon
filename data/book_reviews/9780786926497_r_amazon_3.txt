The book promises as delivered. Lots of neat equipment in this book. I had read that there weren't enough of magical items in this book. NOT SO! They are tons of them in here and not all of them are weapons per say. Now there is a rule for collosions and vehicle controls, which is very handy. Now not so sure about the mounts and hireling rules, but I see where they fit under 'arms' as in 'hiring arms'. Excellent rule coverage on that angle. Also 'living equiment aka mounts' this has some superb rules for even mounts most would not have even thought of. Gelatiounos cubes anyone? I'd never have thought of that. Though if you have to color illustrations, then avoid this book. There are no such thing in this book. Yet the illustrations that are in this book are very detailed and well done.
