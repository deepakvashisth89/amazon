This is a fascinating volume of fairly recent history (1844-1892), told by a skilled writer with authenticated and detailed accuracy. It also provides valuable insights into the forces of both light and darkness - along with the complex human motivations behind them - that usually swirl in storms of profound punctuation around the birth of a new religion.
