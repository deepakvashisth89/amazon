I've never read Richard Grant before or since, so I have no basis for comparison against his other works. The plot is straightforward enough, but that's not why this was good.Sometimes, staring out the window of a bus or waiting to fall asleep, whimsical images come across your mind that you sense you should write down. At least in my case, I never seem to have a pen or paper around. But Richard Grant must have written down all of those ideas. His writing is feverishly dreamy, a string of images that latch themselves into your memory, paired with wonderfully evocative names (the "Deep Game Room," an academic bar-club, for instance). It's playful - even silly - but deeply satisfying. Some writers try this and it doesn't work. It seems to work for Grant.Read moreRead less
