Kane and Kayla are an unlikely match. He is closed off from personal relationships and hyper focused on his career. Kayla is looking for anyone to respect her as an intelligent person first and body second.  Her desire to make the family business what it once was is her only focus. Come to think of it maybe they aren’t so different. They both come with family baggage but in totally ways.Kane is under cover investigating Kayla and her business. Is this woman built for sin running a prostitution ring?  Two minutes with her and his instincts are screaming no. But his attraction is screaming yes.  Now that he’s gotten himself into a lie, how does he get out?I loved Kayla and her ability to see most people for what they are. Well, maybe not Kane initially but she tunes in to his personality pretty quickly. Her intelligence makes her such a threat to man and his inability to think with his brain.Kane is the grumpy hero.
