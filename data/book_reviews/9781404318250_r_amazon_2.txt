There's no way Baum is going to have attitudes regarding women that completely pass 21st century muster, but give this at least 2 cheers for its blow against the patriarchy. The wildly problematic portrayal of General Jinjur and her army is counteracted by Glenda's army's strength and ruthless efficiency, and by the fact that the male characters are by and large equally as hapless as Jinjur's gang.
