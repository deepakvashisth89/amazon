Fiction, it is an interesting thing. If an author ignores human weakness, human greatness, base desires, conflict, real emotions amplified and assigned to created and not real people then the author may as well write about what happened to anyone for twelve minutes last Tuesday. Probably boring for 90+% of humanity. To read about how fictional characters with amplified egos and emotions act in fictionally amplified conflict in compressed time where everything hinges on a fictional decision is what brings me, as a reader, back to fiction time and time again. It makes me turn pages to read what some fictional character does (that I would never do) in these situations. And this book is a page turner with amplified emotions and egos doing dangerous things with loose morals in compressed time. Cornwell is a master of this kind of writing. This book is an entertaining fast read that is packed with fictional action and fictional character that none of us is likely (we can't travel to this time anyway) to ever do and if we were doing it, we would probably stop. Not these characters in this book though. They keep doing it, they keep going and doing unbelievable things that Cornwell has convinced us as a reader is completely possible for his characters and I admire that storytelling ability in this author. I highly recommend this book. I suggest you should really start at the beginning of the series and read all the way through though. Great entertainment for such a low price.Read moreRead less
