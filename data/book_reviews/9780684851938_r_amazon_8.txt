This book is REALLY NOT about OLD souls. It's a study of Ian Stevenson's work. He wrote one of the first detailed studies of what seems to be proof of reincarnation. One book called something like "Twenty Cases Suggestive of Reincarnation" and "Children Who Remember Previous Lives."  He went to India, Thailand, Burma and other countries where there were children born into families that started saying as soon as they could talk, that these parents were not their real parents.  This fellow rode around with Ian and reports in detail what he saw, heard and learned.Ian Stevenson used the most exacting and demanding methodology that he could manage. I really enjoyed this book!But the souls that were reborn seemed rather YOUNG. Just jumping back into a body not long after they suffered a violent death. That is, I have ideas about what OLD souls are—people that have lived through many many lives and have grown wise. To jump right into some new baby's body soon after your death is not so wise!
