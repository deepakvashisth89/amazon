I've read other David Eddings books and enjoyed them immensely. This series was no different. It got off to a bit of a slow start while the characters are developed and the world is created for you, but I quickly loved both the characters and the world. This is the first book in a trilogy (followed by another trilogy, The Tamuli). You can stop reading at the end of this trilogy, or continue on to the next if you wish. If you've never read David Eddings, give this series or the Belgariad a try. I think you'll love them both!Read moreRead less
