It arrived yesterday and I started it last night! I’m obsessed with the Russian royal family. The Romanoff’s ruled Russia for 300 years. Before they were chosen the country was ruled by a variety of rulers and permitted a bit of chaos each time a ruler died.This book was originally written in Russian by Edvard Radzinsky and was translated by Marian Schwartz .  The original author was given unprecedented access to Nicholas II diaries. Both Nicholas and Alexandra had the habit of taking time to write an entry into their diaries each night. It was a habit instilled in them from an early age. I look forward to reading this book and I have read the Carolee Erickson biography of Alexandra and her volume on the Grand Duchess’s, her daughters. It was a world very unlike anything we can relate to now. The years after they were removed from power again, something we can’t imagine. But the innocence with which they ultimately faced their executioners made their murders even more terrible, gruesome.This is a book I have wanted to read for years and I now have it in my hands to hear Nicholas’ voice as he lived and died.Read moreRead less
