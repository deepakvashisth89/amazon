This review could fit any of the six Kate Mulholland books that Gini Harztmark wrote, but Ill go with this one.  I dont know why she only wrote six books and stopped in 2000, but I wish she were still at it.  I am not a big fan of this type of novel, and she reminds me a lot of Lisa Scottoline, who I gave up after reading several books.  But, Hartzmark infused Kate with a sense of black humor that comes out while she is telling the story in the first person that I cant resist.  I will say read this one and all five of the others.
