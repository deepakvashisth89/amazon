This is probably one of my very favorite books that I’ve read aloud to my little girls in the last 8 years. It’s just a fantastic, pure, beautiful book. It’s a story about family, no matter if that family is not your standard 2 parents and children. It’s a story about love, and friendship, and being kind, and growing, and helping others, and loving people for who they are, quirks and all. It’s innocent, and will make you laugh out loud, and cry actual tears. I can not recommend this book enough. I first borrowed it from the library, but immediately came to Amazon to buy our own illustrated copy that my girls can read themselves over and over, which they have already started doing.
