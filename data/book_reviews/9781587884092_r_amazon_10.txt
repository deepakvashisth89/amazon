It was my first experience with Greg Iles and it was a good one. The story is very exciting and one can hardly put the book down. I have just wondered  whether the negative impressions given about the FBI during the time of Edgar Hoover and the resulting political implications were real. Obviously they were. Illegal FBI activities during his time seem to be a fact as well as the secret files kept by him, although it remains somewhat unclear whether they were intended  for his own protection or for political blackmail. Two Presidents would have liked to fire Hoover but did not dare to so. I highly recommend the book.Read moreRead less
