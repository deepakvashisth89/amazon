This story of Robert Duncannon, a military man married to a mentally ill woman, and Louisa Elliott, the illegitimate daughter of a single woman, is a MUST read. Louisa has three children with Duncannon but is unable to continue in the relaitonship. Enter her cousin Edward, several years older than Louisa, but  in love with her for years. Together they raise the children and eventually marry. Some might call this beautiful novel a tear-jerker, but it so much more. It is about what makes relationships go sour, the importance of self-respect, and all the steps that Louisa had to take to regain a sense of self worth and discover the kind of love that builds up instead of tearing down. Beautifully written. Again, Ann Victoria Roberts is one of England's most powerful and evocative writers. Start by reading LOUISE ELLIOTT, then read LIAM'S STORY.
