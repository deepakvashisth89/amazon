I admit it, I don't usually read stuff by women, and I almost never read southern fiction.  But...my sister gave me this novel because she read it in her English class, and it went over pretty big.  Usually when I read a book, unless it's by Hemingway or something, I skip over pages.  Most books are too long.  But The School of Beauty and Charm is tight, man!  Every word is necessary.
