When you review one of these audiobook novelisations you are really reviewing three things: the televised story, the novelisation, and the audiobook. Well, this story is completely missing from the BBC archives so we can’t watch it, but the soundtrack sounds great, and we have the telesnaps too. This was only the fourth ever story, and the first historical, and so no-one back then really knew what a Dr Who history story should be. This is a unique tale, encompassing a six month or so journey across China, and they never tried anything like it again. Shame.The novel is good, solidly written, if a bit perfunctory and emotionless in places where the author inevitably had to streamline the seven episode plot to fit the available page count.Zienia Merton, who actually appeared in the original story in 1964, is the narrator of the audiobook and does an excellent job of bringing the characters and situations to life. The minimal music and sound effects are used effectively too.Well worth a listen, if you can afford it!Read moreRead less
