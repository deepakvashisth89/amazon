"Shadow Academy" is a book worth reading if only because of the many references to it in the Star Wars adult novels.  As the second book in the "Young Jedi Knights" series, I found it to stand well on its own.  I'm not sure if the other books are like that or not.Unlike adult novels, the story is less complex.  Jacen, Jaina, and Lowie somehow end up captives of the Shadow Academy.  Of course, they must escape, but it's their time spent there that is most interesting.The novel is short, but I did enjoy it.  The characters are all in character, except for Han and Leia, who leave their kidnapped children for Luke and Tenel Ka to rescue.  At the very least, I would expect Han to be there.  He was not on any other life saving mission, after all.I would recommend this book to young readers old enough to read chapter books and any adult reader who wants to know more about the history of the Shadow Academy referenced so much in future books.Read moreRead less
