I read this book over 20 years ago and I remember it being very helpful. Found the old copy and decided to reread, cover to cover. This is an amazingly helpful book for those "Arbies" (RB's - right-brained) folks who find that left-brained organizational skills just don't come naturally. Great ideas and tips throughout, and never condenscending, only positive and encouraging. I highly recommend this to anyone who hears "you're so disorganized." The first chapter deals with goal-setting, finding a reason to start working on your organizational skills, but not everyone needs that - the rest of the book is loaded with detailed, practical tips on breaking out of the clutter in your life.
