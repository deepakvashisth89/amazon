Rocketship Galileo is a fun sci fi adventure.  Remember it was written in 1947, yes, 1947 so considering the date, some 20+ years before the moon landing there are many "predictions" which Heinlein made which showed vision.  The story follows three young adults (18 years old) and one scientist.  They take a already made ballistic rocket and transform it into atomic powered.They journey to the moon for an adventure."SPOILER"  This may be the first book where Nazis on the moon is used.Robert A Heinlein wrote excellent books in the 1940 to the 1960s.
