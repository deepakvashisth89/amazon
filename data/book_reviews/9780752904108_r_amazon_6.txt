This novel is an awesome account of the Zulu massacre of British troops who invaded their country in 1879.  It develops a number of characters well and gives us insight into the thinking of participants from both sides.  The author does particularly well at bringing us into Zulu culture.  The history of the Battle of Isandhlwana is well known.  However, this book brings us into the tragic battle very well and with more character development and insight that a movie might bring to the story.Read moreRead less
