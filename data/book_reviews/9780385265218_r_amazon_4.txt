This book has a few mistakes like many books in that field but it's a great book for students to have during the class. And if you teach a French class it's a very convenient book for reading practices and explaining French grammar rules. Vocabulary building and "les exercices" are also well done. I am a French native so it helps a lot for my students for readings and learning grammar rules but I found out that with this book it's very easy and fun to teach French. Of course as a French instructor you will have to add your "savoir faire". It worked fine so far, using this book some of my students (motivated - no previous studies in French - age range: 25/30) became fluent in less than a year, with a good command of the language within 5 months.When you order that book make sure to order the new revised edition (1990)- ISBN 0385265212 (too bad Amazon does not show the ISBN number when you order a used book).Read moreRead less
