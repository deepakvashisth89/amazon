Okay, I love this cookbook.  Her ideas are simple and delicious and I have a few "go to" recipes from this one.  Try "Crunchy Pickles," "Macaroon Top Apple Cake" (I make this CONSTANTLY) and her corn bread.  She has so many great ideas for using basic things.  Don't worry about running to the store for some high-priced exotic ingredient! I love the photo of Nikki and David at work inside the back flap. It gives me a nice, nostalgic 70's vibe and reminds me of trips to the health food store with my mom and eating Deaf Smith natural peanut butter.  I am so glad to see that this winsome young couple went on to stellar success.  (In the interest of full disclosure, I found this cookbook on a library shelf sale for 10¢ almost 20 years ago.
