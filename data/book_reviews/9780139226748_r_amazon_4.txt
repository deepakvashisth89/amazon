I just bought this book a few days ago and I am now a little over half way through it. I read the Magic of Believing a few times and found it, for me, to be a little harder to read than this one. May be the simple, straight forward way this one is written. I am just getting through the part where Bristol talks about writing down what you want and reviewing it often. As he says, what he is writing about is not new, he is just saying it in a different way. I have read a lot of similar books, The Game of Life, Key To Yourself, 7 Spiritual Laws of Success.
