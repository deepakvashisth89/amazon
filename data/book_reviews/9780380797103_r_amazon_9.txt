A lovely interesting story I really loved the strong willed Samantha Northrup the vicar's daughter, the selfless healer , bright, Intelligent, but put upon, devoting herself to the villagers who took advantage of her generous nature.  Yale Carderock, the prodigal son, returns to his home to show his father that he wasn't worthless, had made a huge success of his life alone and unaided only too late for his father had died a couple of years earlier. It was so moving to see these two people fall in love despite a miserable start!  After being forced to bring Yale home to nurse him through a serious bout of infulenza they were forced by the hypocritical to marry.  Immediately Yales sense of caring becomes evident as he puts himself in an awkward situation because he realises that for Samantha’s good she needs to leave the selfish people who would continue to use and abuse her even more because of their perceived ruination.  Sharing a cottage .He was honorable no one could have possibly forced him to marry Samantha happened between them, he had been too ill. But recognising the fate that awaited Sam and he was determined to do the right thing, save her, marrying her under a false name seemed the best option.  He was determined to make sure he took care of her for she had saved his life.  The story details Yales passage from a disillusioned and proud  young man to a caring and loving person.
