Amusing and droll French stories of Petit Nicolas and his grumpy dad and and conniving mother on vacation.  These stories are excellent for advanced beginner, intermediate and even advanced readers trying to improve their fluency.I'm an intermediate French student and know about 80-90% of the vocab in these stories.  I'll be using them with my French tutor.  The illustrations break up the text.  This is a smallish book.  You can easily read a story in 5-10 minutes.
