As with their other Oz offering, Ozma of Oz, the publisher did a great job adapting this childhood favorite for Kindle. The illustrations look great, and the text formatting is above average compared to other Kindle books I've read. Nice job folks. I hope you do more of the Frank Baum Oz series.The story itself is just what you'd expect from an Oz book -- adventure, wonky characters, like the man made from sticks and a pumpkin -- magic, and a land where even war is pretty much all sweetness and light. This story doesn't involve Dorothy; it's about Ozma's appearance on the scene. Good fun.Read moreRead less
