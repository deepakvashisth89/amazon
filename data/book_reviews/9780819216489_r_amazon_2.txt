Imagine being found by that which you were seeking for a lifetime .. and realizing for the first time that you are home.  It was like that for me when, after a lifetime in the world of Fundamentalism, I found myself crushed under the weight of dogmatic toxicity and fled.  At that time I found .. or perhaps was found by the Episcopal Church.  Lame and wounded as I was, I knew from the beginning that I had come home, and so it has been.  Then a year or so ago, I became introduced to Benedictine spirituality by way of Fr. Thomas Keating and Contemplative Outreach and it was another of those "aha" experiences that seemed to give substance to things I had pondered in my heart for a very long time.  Along that journey, I was introduced to this book by John McQuiston, and now on my second reading, I am finding it to be a wonderful accompaniment to The Rule of St Benedict, itself, along with another book I am reading by Esther deWaal "Living With Contradiction: An Introduction to Benedictine Spirituality".  Mr. McQuiston doesn't pretend that this is another translation of The Rule .. rather it is a paraphrase that is utterly applicable to one's daily life and certainly complimentary to the Rule itself.  It would almost seem unfair to say that I like one part more than another, because (at least to me) it isn't a book that one reads through like a novel and then sets it aside.  Each morning I read a chapter, just as I do the Rule itself.  If there is one chapter I look more forward to reading (and finding infused into my heart and life), it would be chapter 7 "Humility".
