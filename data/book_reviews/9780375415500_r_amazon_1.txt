This book is good. It is well-written. It is compelling. It has meaning. It is a strong piece of modern literature.This book is bad. It is dark. It is bleak. Just when you think things can't get worse, you're kicked in the gut once again. You may not feel like talking to people for a little while if you read this book.Consider your mindset before you pick up this book. I'm bipolar and suffer from severe depression and PTSD due to past physical and sexual abuse. I wish I'd known more about this book beforehand because I wouldn't have read it. It made me feel bad. Really bad. The story hurt me.Did I enjoy reading it? No. Would I read it again? No. Do I recommend it? If you're strong enough to handle it, yes. Is it good? Yes. Was I compelled to finish it? Yes. Did I feel for the characters? Very much so. Am I happy I read it? Not at all. It really tore me up inside. There is a dichotomy at work here. The book is and isn't an easy read. I couldn't put it down, but I also wanted it away from me.I guess art is supposed to evoke an emotional response. This book did.Mick Foley, you seem like a very nice man. And you're a very good writer. I don't mean to disparage your book because it is VERY good. I just feel like I crawled out of a war, though.Write something happy, okay?XOXOR.Read moreRead less
