This is Book 10 in the Rosato & Associates series.  It focuses on Bennie Rosato, the head of the Law firm, who is taking on a class action suit for the first time, and vying for the position of lead plaintiff.  Her client is murdered, and Bennie is determined to solve the case.  All the while, her evil identical twin is trying to destroy her reputation and kill her.There is never a dull moment in this story - in fact, I didn't want to put it down!  So many twists and turns.
