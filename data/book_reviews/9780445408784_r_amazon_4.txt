Buried Caesars is one of my favorite entries in the Toby Peters mystery series, mainly because it blends a perfect mix of mystery, comedy, action, characters and social commentary.Dashiell Hammett plays a big role in the book, willingly becoming Toby’s sidekick in sleuthing.  Kaminsky’s portrayal of the father of hard boiled detectives is compassionate and insightful, as is his depiction of General Douglas MacArthur as equal parts egomaniac, military genius and man of honor.Gunther plays no major role in the story, nor does Jeremy.  Shelley provides comic relief, ex-wife Ann provides pathos, Mrs. Plaut saves the day and brother Phil loses his temper.Every element a Toby Peters book needs is provided.Highly recommended.Read moreRead less
