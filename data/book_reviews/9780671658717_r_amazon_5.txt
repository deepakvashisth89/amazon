My first impression of this work is that it is dark. Ms Gaitskill writes about the sexual underworld in a similar way that Irvine Welsh writes about the underclass of the UK. There is a pervading sense of emptiness and sickliness. She describes people as if they were reptiles, and the contracts between them as self-serving, animalistic, and without future.The author is an adept writer. There are a few snippets of astringent humour, based on irony and hypocrisy.  There is a richly descriptive style, a very feminine eye for the small details that reveal the bigger picture.The meaning behind these short stories will be different for everyone. The banality of modern urban life. The illusion of attraction. The transience of modern promiscuity.  The author deals with the reality of failed human interractions - brief or flawed relationships that do not always come full circle or make sense. Ultimately though, I sense an oppressive heaviness there.. and I can't say that I liked it.Read moreRead less
