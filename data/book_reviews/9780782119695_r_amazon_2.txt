I like the writing style of Lammle, Lammle, and Chellis. The book is easy to read and follow. They make the subject interesting and provide many anologies that make it easy to understand. The main problem is that most of the book is based on NT ver. 3.51. The last chapter of the book is dedicated to NT ver. 4.0. Having just taken the official Microsoft course study on TCP/IP ver. 4.0 I can say that many areas covered by Microsoft are not included in this book. For example Supernetting, planning DNS implementation, and configuring DNS files.It was worth reading but not in lieu of other study materials more current with version 4.0Read moreRead less
