Marilyn Manson is a genius performance artist who allows us to look at the dark side of ourselves and society objectively. He is a true artist who's deepest objective is to wake people up. I was reminded of Jim Morrison who would scream "Wake up!!" at his concerts, and was subsequently arrested for similar actions as Marilyn. The police and society react the same way to Marilyn now as they did in the late 1960's to Jim! It shows how we are still stuck in the dark ages, and it's pretty frightening. It takes artists like Marilyn to bring forth this knowledge to us today and show how we haven't come very far at all. We still are not "free" and America is not the "land of the free" if you go outside the mainstream religion and clothing uniform. I was impressed with the consciousness of Marilyn in particular the experience with groupies (which another particular rockstar bio makes fun of and takes advantage of viciously) and the revelations of their own harrowing pasts. He infiltrates subjects that other rock stars in his position don't see as worthy, which in fact is what is most interesting. It was most poignant when he went back to his home town to his cousin's wedding, and how mean-spirited and judgmental everyone was to him now that he was successful as if he is not even human. How sick and pathetic the small town world is. Best of all, it is written objectively and there is not a hint of whining in any of this book which is an accomplishment in itself for a "rockstar bio". He is able to step back from himself and society and observe, and in this way I am able to step back and observe from the same standpoint, which makes for an eye-opener. He touches on revelations in his dreams and making connections in real life which is noteworthy. He has followed his destiny. There is an element of dry humor through out this book too and often enough I was laughing my head off. What characters!
