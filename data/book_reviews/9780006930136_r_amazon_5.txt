I have read the "Silver Brumby's" series since I was a child and every few years I re-read the whole series as an adult. I am 42 now! :) I love the Silver Brumby's daughter as it shows the bond between Thowra, the silver stallion and his beautiful silver daughter, Kunama who is hunted as much as her father is for her beautiful shiny coat. She is captured; but can Thowra rescue her from the grasp of men? Fantastic book!I highly recommend the whole series to any one who loves horses, young or old. You can start in the series where you want, but I recommend starting with the Silver Brumby, as this is the first book and the story tells of the birth of a tiny silver colt being born on a stormy hostile night and how he and his half-brother Storm grow, evade being captured by man who longs to own the beautiful silver horses, how they fight for the right to have a herd, how he meets Kunama's mother, and how he fights for the right for supremacy in the hostile Snowy mountains.
