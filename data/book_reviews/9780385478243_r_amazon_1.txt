I found this novel fascinating and intense; in fact, at times I had to put it down and absorb the reality of what I just read. This authordoesn't allow the reader to be an observer- but emotionally invested in the scenes he so well crafts. He allows each of the characters to speak through their own point of view bringing it home to the reader up close and personal. It is masterful how the author works each character in their own chapter and verse and tying it into a complete and unifying story. Great work here - and a great read.
