Roberta Gellis knows well how to create a page-turner. This is a satisfying story, with romance and adventure nicely balanced. I appreciate her research and efforts to be historically accurate while making the period (1136-38 A.D.) accessible and vibrant to the modern reader. The story concerns Audris, the heiress of Jernaeve, which is an important fortified keep south of the Scottish border, and Hugh, a foundling and landless squire, far below her in rank. They fall in love, of course, during a time when the armies of the English King Stephen and the Scottish King David are warring and despoiling northern England. Hugh vows to gain honors in war, so he would be an acceptable husband to Audris' guardians. Gellis paints a pageant of battles, daily life in castle keeps and peasant huts, a tournament joust and knights' armor, and medieval medicine, while the mystery behind Hugh's noble birth is solved.
