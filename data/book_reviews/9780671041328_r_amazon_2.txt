Ms. M.J. Rose has surpassed herself, and will be hard pressed to write another psychological thriller as enticing as this.  As the wife of a busy physician and psychiatrist, with a teenage stepson going away to college, this protagonist is a writer in this novel, who contracts to write a book about "phone sex" in regards to how it helps as a form of therapy for men going through a transition in their own lives.  On the cusp of sexual fantasy and venturing into possibly more dangerous territory, the lady of this novel soon finds herself being liberated from a form of bondage, due to being married to a husband who has not touched her in 15 years, and who has smothered and stifled her own sexuality, to the point of nonexistence.  While discovering her own passions and sexual stamina beginning to return, there emerges a dangerous killer who has kidnapped a younger teenage woman, and is holding her for another possible murder.  When the police refuse to believe our heroine, she then ventures out on the investigation of the serial killer on her own, to prevent the murder of this young woman who is at the mercy of a madman.  Being all too human in her own feelings and weaknesses, is what makes this lady more admirable when she begins to take charge of her own life, and also her strong effort to help someone else, another female whose life is truly in danger.
