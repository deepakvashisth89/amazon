I just got done reading this book this morning! And it was amazing! It was written in 1996 and was the first lesbian romance with a happy ending. I often am discouraged about reading or watching LGBT genre because they often have un happy endings. An unhappy ending....is not reality. It... just isn't, so why would I want to fill my mind with that? The author of the story met her wife in her teenage years in the 50's. It was quit a confusing time for her (I can only imagine! I was confused enough in the 90's!). But they ended up sticking together and after 35 years of living together got married in MASS. WHich is true for a lot of LGBT folks, we don't always break up, we do live happy lives! We don't all kill our selves. The author wrote in her book about how LGBT literature, especially before the 90s couldn't have happy endings, else they wouldn't get published. Like we were censored from the idea that we can live happy lives and have successful marriages. A lot of the books from LGBT genre are still like that too!
