Not the best of S&S but I am their biggest fan so I can't put less than 4 stars on this. Pretty much the only reason this doesn't get 5 stars is because their other novels are so much better than this one. Don't get me wrong, I loved this book, but Light at the End and Animals are their best. Funny because it's their first and last. A lot of alchemy within those two books.For me, The Clean Up lacked the punky vibe and prose style that I fell in love with in Light at the End. The characters certainly were, but the vibe was just off. And I think the book was just too long; this story would have served better as a 20k novella.
