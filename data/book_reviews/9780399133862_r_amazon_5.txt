This a collection of letters written by  Vietnam vets during the late 1980s describing how they were received on their actual return some 15 to 20 years earlier.  The letters clearly disclose how poisonous the Vietnam War really was and the deleterious effects it had on the vets and our society in general.  I strongly suspect that letters written on the same subject now, 50 years after the war's end, would contain the same sentiments.
