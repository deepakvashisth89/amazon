'Certain Prey' is from early on in the Lucas Davenport series, before he shares his home with Weather Karkinnen and their adopted daughter Letty.  This novel is solid detective thriller excitement; family not included!The plot is brilliantly executed and fast-paced.  There are plenty interesting details of police life, often delivered with wry humour and always skilfully woven into the narrative.There is a bit of 'pure luck' around the middle of the story, which had me wondering if Sandford was cheating, or taking the easy way out!  I'll give him the benefit of the doubt.  I guess even the best detectives need a little luck to crack their cases.The ending is a classic and is very cinematic.A must-have for Lucas Davenport fans.
