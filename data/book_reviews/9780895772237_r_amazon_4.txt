David Copperfield, Master Davey, Doady. By all names, he led a fascinating life, at times suffering and joyous, but always purposeful and with honor. I am moved by this classic that I'm proud to have tackled in a week,reading through the middle of the night, hoping and willing his success and happiness. What lovely characters who influenced him (except Murdstone and his awful sister). The rest, I will consider my own own friends and influencers. I'm sure I will want to re-read this book again.
