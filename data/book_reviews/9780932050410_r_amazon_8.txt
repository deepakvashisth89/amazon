In this book, you'll find very personal account of a young man that struggles with, as he would describe it, the plan God made for him. During his journey, he is riddled with doubts about geopolitics, because at first he saw the Rhodesian government as the only one, next to South Africa, actually willing to tackle marxist aggression. The constant theme, his true conviction was that all governments in the West being subverted by globalists that want to replace the Christian civilization he loved. He finds himself disillusioned, not only because of personal failures, finding the Smith government being too liberal, and forseeing its eventual downfall in his final months. You can get a climps into Rhodesian society, but as a big admirer of the government, it put some cracks in the my nostalgia for that country that fought so bravely against the whole world, about all the problems, both societial and economical,that Rhodesia suffered in her final years.A must read for enthusiasts, because it will be hard to find anything that close in proximity and time to the Bush War, albeit longwinded in some places.What did put me off is that a fair chunk of pages in the middle are upside down; maybe it's just my copy, but that shouldn't happen in the first place.Read moreRead less
