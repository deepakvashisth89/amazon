The sound of the mountain's title made me believe that the sound ( the main characters feeling of looming death of old age) would be a large section of the book but the show here gets stolen by the relationships that shingo the main character forms and changes.The growing closeness in between his son's wife and himself are what makes this story enjoyable to read about, this story is honest yet sweet. it's not overly dramatic in its events, and manages to still be a book that has you at the edge of your seat as to what happens next in this plethora of changing relationships. The book is great but those who like epic novels and the like will most likely be bored here, but make no doubt, this story is both frightening with shingo's growing strains with his wife and son and his fear of death but also perplexing yet heartwarming growing relationship with his husband's wife. He secretly lusts after her yet he has tremendous growing respect for her. Seeing this develop had me at the edge of my seat.I would definitely Recomend this great story and the book itself comes in a decently compact size which I prefer and best of all with a decently sized font that won't strain the eyes, If your looking for a interesting story with with interesting characters and dynamic relationships then pick this up!Read moreRead less
