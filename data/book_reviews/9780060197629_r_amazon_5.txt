I got this book after reconnecting with my father who I haven’t heard from in 7 years. I’m not finished with it yet, but this book has been very helpful for me and has made me done a lot of thinking and reflection. I like how there are tons of different scenarios and perspectives discussed in the book, some that I relate to completely and others I don’t, but can still appreciate and feel sympathy and empathy for the people involved.
