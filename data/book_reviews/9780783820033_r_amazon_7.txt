Jodi Picoult has done it yet again.  The characters are developed bit-b-bit and soon become quite real to the reader.  You go through so very many emotions with each character as the story progresses that you seem to be part of the story.  The story has several "climaxes" as it progresses, and each turns the story ever-so-slightly in a different direction... but doesn't change the main direction of the story... you are captivated until the end to learn the outcome ... it could go any of several ways.The research into the legal and judicial systems appears to be excellent... in truly Jodi Picoult style.  The story is woven precisely to keep you interested... at the end you are holding your breath... and finally release that breath.  Thank you, Jodi Picoult, for another well-written book!Read moreRead less
