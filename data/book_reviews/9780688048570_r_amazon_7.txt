Extremely well written. The author provides a picture of one aspect of the mid 90's gay culture. It is accurate in a way but written during the time when HIV seemed an almost inevitable result of "gay life". His characters are essentially without hope and focused mainly on sex and the beauty of physicality and not on the beauty of the person. It is a novel worth reading as the insights into where one might go when life seems without meaning;it makes one think to not go where there is no future of love, compassion and acceptance of oneself.
