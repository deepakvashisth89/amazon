I picked this book up after reading about the author in a newspaper. Alright, I thought, so lots of teens write poetry, it apparently helps to get the angst in their system out. This girl wrote these poems when she was a teen. For being so young, the poems are well written. Also, she takes a different approach to writing about the same things most teens write about, like friends betraying you, boys, and school in general. It should be noted though that most of the poems in Flying With Broken Wings are not the usual poems a teen would write. The author deals with mostly serious topics, that can be a bit depressing, and she doesn't focus too much on what many would consider trite teen issues. You get the feeling she was being sincere and honest when these poems were written. Yeah, she does need to work a bit on her form, but for being what, like 14 when she started these poems, its good stuff.
