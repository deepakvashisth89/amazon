I enjoyed this book as I do all of Chris Matthew's work.  I was unaware of JFK and RMN's cordial relationship before the 1960 election. Mr. Matthews provided a very keen insight into two of the most important politician's of the mid 20th Century. While Kennedy had the looks and the glamour, he was sometimes callous and obviously two-faced.  More than anything, Nixon wanted to be liked.  But his persona and paranoia made that difficult for many.  Both were very intelligent men.  Nixon became the better politician. Although who knows what would have happened had Kennedy lived.
