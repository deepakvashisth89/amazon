This is an exciting true story, carefully researched by the author, of a woman's extraordinary trek across America in order to win a prize that was to save the family farm. Alas, she was cheated out of the money. She gained invaluable insights traversing other states where women's rights were more advanced than in her home state of Washington. Instead of getting a hero's welcome upon her return for her extraordinary achievement, she was shunned and shamed by her Norwegian immigrant family and friends.
