For those fascinated with St. Francis of Assisi, this belongs in your library. Jon Sweeney's accompanying notes on the book's margins create context, and the translation is excellent. This, along with Jorgensen's Francis and Julien Green's God' Fool, are among my favorite portraits using contemporary historical methods in search of the personality of this holy man. Augustine Thompson, O. P., in his new biography, maintains the "Sabatier was the first to evaluate and interpret the sources critically. He created the modern study of Saint Francis.
