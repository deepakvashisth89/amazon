When Tycho digs up a weird egg-shaped object his life will take an unexpected turn. The egg is a time machine. As he changes his past, he also changes the present and the future for himself and his family. Each time it seems to get worse.I didn't expect very much of this one. Boy was I surprised! This story hooked me from the get-go and I had to read on and see how it all turned out. A nice title. This was the first Sleator book I read but not the last.Read moreRead less
