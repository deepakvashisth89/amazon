I don't think I have ever left a review here, but I must after reading several others left.  I am here on this page to add this to my wish list, having just finished reading this edition cover to cover.  (My copy is so old its pages fell out as I read and I know I will want to read these tales again.)  First let me say Vinaver's selection and presentation is perfect - footnotes especially.  Malory's 15th century language is essential to the modern reader, as presented by Vinaver.  But the context is not always enough to understand Malory's diction.
