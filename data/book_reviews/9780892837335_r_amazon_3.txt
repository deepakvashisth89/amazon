"Quotable Saints" is a book containing a collections of sayings from many Saints, Blesseds, and Venerables, both famous and obscure.  Organized into 30 topics, they provide the reader with entertaining reading, leavened by quotes which are insightful, edifying, and, in some cases,  humorous.  Some quotes will help you see life differently, others will spice your speech and writing, and some will just make you laugh.
