In early April 1989 Centcom went on alert and focused on a war in the Middle East.Woodward makes it sound like the Saudis got the F-15 in 1989.  Nope, long before that.  After AIPAC blocked US sales of F-15 C and D models to the Saudis in 1984 the Saudis bought a large number of British Tornados -- an offensive airplane.  and so forth.
