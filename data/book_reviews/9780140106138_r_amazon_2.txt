The Saratoga stories follow the life and times of Charlie Bradshaw, a Saratoga policeman of 20 years in the first one, who eventually resigns from the force and becomes a private detective. Charlie is a likeable man, and it is easy to care about what happens to him. Other characters are eccentric, arrogant, bull-headed, flamboyant, shifty, criminal, caring, greedy or stupid, as fits the story, but always interesting. These books develop Charlie's story across the series, as well as each being a stand-alone mystery. I think it is best to start with the first one and move through them in order, although you could pick one up part way through the series without becoming confused. I don't these books set the world on fire, but they are good solid reads that keep you engaged and guessing to the end, and leave you feeling like the world and the people in it might be okay, in spite of the bad things that can happen.This book has Charlie investigating the death of a local shopkeeper, with similar complexities in plot and characters as the other books. Once again Charlie experiences some personal danger and delves deeper into the world of gambling, which is never far from the story in these Saratoga mysteries.
