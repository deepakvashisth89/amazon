This book is a hidden gem when it comes to believable characters who each have their distinct world view in a very well fleshed out world, but I wished the story had been longer (preferably a trilogy) as the last part of the book felt a little rushed to get to the end. I would also have liked to visit more of the places which are mentioned in the book, and that is my other reason for holding a star back (the first one being that the story is wrapped up too fast).The pacing was also good and I enjoyed the conversations between the different characters which illuminated their contrasting world view (especially when it comes to Tessa who is from our world and have to deal with the fantasy surroundings head on).I will definitely read more of J.
