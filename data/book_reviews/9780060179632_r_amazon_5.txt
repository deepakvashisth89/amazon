I ordered this book based on the fact that I have been to Santa Fe a number of times and wanted to see what Stuart Woods would spin on this one.  I was not disappointed.  I have read about 10 of his books and I thought this one was one of my favorites.  It is very fast paced and Stone Barrington reminds me of someone I know.  Loved the Jewish Ed Eagle.  What attorney could spin a web better than him.  All of Woods' books, especially the Stone Barrington series, are very good.
