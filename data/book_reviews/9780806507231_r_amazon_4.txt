This is a good book - one of the better Three Stooges books out there - but it's not a "great" book. For one thing, the editing of this book is deplorable. There are several errors in the captions of the pictures, and two of the captions from pictures of the same short even contradict each other. Secondly, it felt like Moe rushed through some parts of the book. There is hardly anything about the Shemp era - which I found disappointing. I would have liked to read his views on Columbia's small budget that forced them to make remakes as the 50s progressed. But on the positive side, it's a very easy and fun read, with many stories you won't find elsewhere.
