Not as deeply thought through as Adiamante. The philosophy marked by the necessity and bitterness of action is well balanced in Adiamante showing, in stark terms, that we must accept the consequences of the actions we take to support our ideals.  This book, however, gives the reader keen insight to the beginnings of the process the author will use in the latter work to show the reader the cost analysis associated with real-world effication of ideals.  It is interesting to see which devices he discards and shortens from this work to Adiamante to so much greater effect.
