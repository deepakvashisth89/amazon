This is one of the hardest books I have ever read.The Girl Next Door is absolutely gut-wrenching. I felt rage, heartbreak, disgust, and sadness almost the entire time I was reading it. There were multiple moments where I physically cringed because of the horrific abuse these girls endure. What makes it even more devastating is that the people responsible are the very people who should have protected and cared for them.I know I love splatterpunk and extreme horror, but this was on another level emotionally. I kept hoping things would turn around, that there would be some moment of relief, but the story just continued to spiral into something darker and more heartbreaking.What made this book even more devastating for me was learning that it was inspired by the real-life torture and murder of Sylvia Likens. Knowing there was a real victim behind the inspiration makes the story feel even more horrifying and heartbreaking.This is an incredibly well-written novel, but it is not an easy read. It is emotionally exhausting, deeply disturbing, and one that will stay with me for a very long time.Please, please check the trigger warnings before picking this one up.
