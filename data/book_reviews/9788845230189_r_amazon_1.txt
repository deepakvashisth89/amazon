I am a huge fan of Alberto Moravia and I think I have read most of his novels and short stories by now. However, I was unpleasntly surprised by this one.Not only the title is La Noia, the book itself is indeed really boring - all descriptions and thoughts of the protagonist are way too long and repetitive, so that you can easily skip pages without loosing the thread of narration. Sometime it looked like some kind of literary onanism. I could never imaging being bored reading Moravia books, well, this was the first one (hopeully the last).
