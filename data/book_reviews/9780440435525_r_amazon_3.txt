I wanted to give my granddaughter some books she'd enjoy, and frankly, books for kids today seem to be all about very troubled and insecure children dealing with "interior" problems. I wanted to give her some of the fun books I enjoyed at her age, books that were about making friends, having adventures, thinking up great ideas for having fun, solving problems cleverly, and learning about the wide world. Henry Reed, Inc. is such a book. It's one of those "What could we do?" stories that leaves you with a feeling of possibility.
