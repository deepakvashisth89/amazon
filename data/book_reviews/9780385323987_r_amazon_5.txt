This is a low key crime novel and satire on Hollywood. It's a funny story. I'll paraphrase a previous review which captures the story right on. A man who runs a dry cleaning business owes a bunch of money to a loan shark. He misses a plane, but his luggage doesn't and  when the plane goes down the dry cleaner is believed dead. He and his wife keep quiet to collect the insurance money, settle a lawsuit with the airline, and avoid paying what he owes to the loan shark. Once the money comes in the dry cleaner skips out with the cash, leaves his wife, and heads to Vegas to live his fantasy life.Once the loan shark, Chili Palmer, learns that his mark is still alive and has a bunch of cash, he goes after him. Palmer takes a side job to collect some money from a Hollywood producer, goes to LA and decides that he would like to make movies. Meanwhile, various other criminal types are trying to bump him off, and a great deal of hilarity ensues. The prose is wonderful, like an old Humphrey Bogart movie. Short and to the point, understated, with just the right amount of humor. It is a great look at the world of Hollywood through the eyes of a Miami loan shark.Read moreRead less
