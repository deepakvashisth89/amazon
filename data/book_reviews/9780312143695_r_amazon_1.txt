This comic strip was brought to my attention via REM's "Monster", many years ago.  That horrible excuse for a CD was good for at least one thing!The evil twin of Charlie Brown, Migraine Boy  never gets a break, but that doesn't stop him from taking his frustrations  out on all the neighborhood kids who bug him.  These strips have varying  styles of humor - alternately twisted, violent, morbid, one-liner, and  deadpan.  The art looks like it was drawn by someone actually suffering  from a migraine: very frantic scribbling, wavy lines... all the better to  convey the special atmosphere of this strip.
