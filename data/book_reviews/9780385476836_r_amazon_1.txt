I have read all of Joseph Girzone's books, he is an amazing writer.  The particular book is a must have book for those who want to find their purpose in life and the path that leads them to God.  I appreciate the way that Joseph Girzone reaches through the barriers that often are present in books that address religion and he does not focus on being of a certain faith, but he embraces all faith and brings it back to the reader.  In this way he is able to touch all of us and guide us on a path that is often fraught with questions.  I found a lot of answers in this book and I have reread it a number of times and each time receive more wisdom and defined direction.
