I love Jerusalem and have lived in it for over 40 years. Adina Hoffman who also wrote another book on Jerusalem which I read first, even though it was written later, clearly also loves the city (or did as she has left it as far as I know). So this book is about the picturesque Musrara neighbourhood but it is written by a young and ideologically struck woman.  Ideology always gets in the way of clear thinkng.She is patronizingly in favour of everything Arab and very uncomfortable in her own skin as a Jew. She is too intelligent a writer for this to destroy the whole book but she has two chapters that are nausiating to read. The one where she shows actual hatred for ultra orthodox Jews for hiring someone to cut down a tree. Clearly it is not the tree that is the issue but self hatred as a Jew. The other chapter is where she does some research about the original owner of their home who was presumably Arab. That one gives her a chance to vent all her political corectness on the altar of the "Palestinian cause". Actually, the trip to Jordan also shows some patronizing love for anything Arab. They have a meal in the house of their drivere and melt away moved by love..You would not catch her eating with the families of the ultra orthodox she so clearly loathes, even though they too are "quaint" and "other".In short, if you love Jerusalem, you may read this book but at times you might need some medication to counter nausea.I have met the likes of her too often but at least she write well.Read moreRead less
