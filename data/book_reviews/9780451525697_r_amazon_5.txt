Winesburg Ohio is a town on the edge of the change from agrarian to industrial progress. People are facing traditional decision points with modern demands. Limited by a new uncertainty of the town’s future, Anderson’s characters must decide if old post Civil War values can be maintained, or should the citizens move on into the unknown future. How important are traditional concepts of success, education, courting, loneliness, childbearing, marriage, dreams, and just the enjoyment of living when a train can carry people away from small town Winesburg to big industrial cities with unlimited possibilities?
