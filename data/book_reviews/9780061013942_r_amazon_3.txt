David Cole is an exceptionally talented writer or I would have stopped reading this book. That said I could not accept as realistic the main character's transformation from computer geek to gunshooting vigilante (and apparently accurate as hell the first time she ever shot a gun!).There is so much to like about the writing even though it portrays the very dismal side of reservation life. The thought processes just didn't seem true to nature.In fact I didn't look to see who the author was before reading and several chapters in I looked at the cover to see whether the author was male or female because it wasn't the way I thought a woman would talk or think.
