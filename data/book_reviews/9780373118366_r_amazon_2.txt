This author deals a lot with infidelity. This is not the first book which I have read about one or both characters not keeping the vows of matrimony. I am not a prude just that is not a story I like reading. She married his older brother when she was young. There was a seventeen years difference. He was off making musical career and was never faithful. And after finding out she ended up sleeping with his younger brother.  They tried to make the marriage work but it ended in divorce when she finds out that she was pregnant again. She has raised her daughter alone.
