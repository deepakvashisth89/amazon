This book will be a joy for anyone who loves the craft of writing. Although he says it better than my paraphrase, Mr. Gruber observes that it is not realism we look for in fiction but rather the validation of our own personal experiences. Since "On All Sides Nowhere" is a work of non-fiction, Mr. Gruber gives us the inverse of his hypothesis: he transforms the seemingly unrelated details of life in Alder Creek into one of Vermeer's family portraits that we will always revisit with pleasure.Read moreRead less
