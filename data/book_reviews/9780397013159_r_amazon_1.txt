A good idea, very poorly executed.  Hollis Beckwith, newly appointed chief of New York City's newly formed police force, confronted with strange and perverse serial murders, decides to consult with Edgar Allan Poe.  Beckwith has read Poe's mystery stories and is impressed with Poe's forensic psychological techniques.  But Poe just tags along, a proud, petulant, and very sad, neurotic.  Poe contributes next to nothing to the investigation, it is all about Beckwith, and that is why I give it just one star.  Poe here is just a red herring.Read moreRead less
