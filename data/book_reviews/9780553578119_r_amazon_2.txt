Gregory MacGregor has produced a legal thriller that is among the best of its genre. From the very beginning, the book gallops through the ups and downs of a trial lawyer’s practice in a high stakes litigation. The book is as timely as any as it involves a Presidential candidate alleged to have committed fraud and other crimes and a trial that takes place at the same time as the campaign for President. I highly recommend this book for anyone who likes legal thrillers along the lines of John Grisham.Read moreRead less
