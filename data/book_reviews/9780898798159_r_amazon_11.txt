It should go without saying that characters, and characterisation, are the key to the success of any story. I'd heard that 'Character is Destiny' years ago, without really absorbing what it meant. I bought this book due to a recommendation inPlot and Structure: Techniques and Exercises for Crafting a Plot that Grips Readers from Start to Finish (Write Great Fiction), and also partly out of frustration that I was getting nowhere with my ideas.I hadn't heard of Nancy Kress until I bought this book, but I was pleasantly surprised to discover that she was a sci-fi writer (amongst other genres). Much of the sci-fi I've read seems to be weak in the character department, so it was reassuring to know that a successful, Hugo award winning writer such as Nancy was not one to neglect the importance of character to a good story.My biggest problem so far has been generating plots from my ideas. The book I've mentioned above has been very helpful, but Nancy Kress's book has taken me to the point where I feel almost ready to begin my first novel (I am currently preparing a plot outline using this book as a guide). In spite of the title, half of this book covers how character not only relates, but also shapes and governs how a plot develops. There are also many sections on how to use the concept of character to generate any idea or setting into a plot.Overall, an indispensable book for any writer in my humble opinion.Read moreRead less
