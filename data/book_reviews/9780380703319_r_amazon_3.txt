I love Charlotte Macleod. She is witty, clever and makes you care about her quirky characters. The mysteries are always a delight. She was way ahead of her time with strong women and conservation themes. All three of her series are worth a read. The Grub and Stakers series will take you on a wild ride with fun twists and turns.
