A seriously written and extensively researched view of the origin of man's inhumanity to man, largely from a genetic and sexually aggressive standpoint.  There is a lot of downright interesting information on display here, with the author having put in his time in the wilds looking at our closest primate cousins along with some primitive cultures of today.  He held my interest to the end, and his book  is worth a second read in a couple of months after I digest his conclusions.  This is a provocative tour de force that leaves few stones unturned regarding the faults of the male primate from a genetic point of view.  What makes the male primate so violently aggressive?
