He Done Her Wrong is the eighth book in the Toby Peters 1940's Hollywood detective series and the series has hit its stride and is chugging along nicely.  All the supporting characters are in place (Sheldon Minck D.D.S., burly Jeremy Butler, and little person Gunther Wherthman) and recurring elements appear(Jeremy's poems, Mrs. Plaut's memoirs).  The plots are meandering and more than a bit implausible, but what matters are Toby's comic travails and the eccentric folk he meets in encounters alternately menacing or heartwarming.I'm rereading the series after reading each book as they were published and am enjoying once more Kaminsky's spot on recreation of the 40's era with the big stars and the small time players.  Truly a wonderful series.Highly recommended.Read moreRead less
