Thanks to NetGalley and Bolinda Audio for the advanced listener copy of the audiobook.The story mainly follows two estranged brothers from rural South Japan who are reunited and return to their hometown. Historical context also plays an important role in understanding the story and so I’m sure I missed out in fully understanding all of Oe’s deeper themes.It is not an emotionally easy book to read, but even though the translation it was easy to appreciate the craftsmanship here.The audiobook production and narration were both great.
