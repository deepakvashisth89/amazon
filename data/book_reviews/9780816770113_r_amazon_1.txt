OMG! I love this book. This book is packed with loads of easy, simple and fun projects. You can choose them to be as simple or as complex as you like. There is something for everyone, from paper making to slime making. There were even several recipes for slime as well. I purchased this book on a whim because I was invited to participate in a Cub Scout science fair. It came in very timely manner. As soon as I got it, I was able to choose a project.  I chose to make SLIME!  The kids (and some adults) were able to make slime in any color they wanted. The kids (and adults) said my class was the best class! To my surprise, I was awarded a certificate. The ingredients were very simple. And for the most part safe. Just everyday things you use around the house and kitchen. This was great because it allowed me to compare, explain, and hopefully motivate kids to cook by showing them first hand how cooking is really just chemistry. The entire process was very simple. The instructions were simple. EASY peasy. This book should be called the Incredibly SIMPLE Secret Formula Book. It is a Fun book. I plan to use it again next year.
