I got all 3 of these book of poems from this author for my grandbabies for Christmas. These books were so popular when I was a kid that there was a list at our school library of kids who wanted to check them out. When you were the one to have it you were the cool kid and everyone want to read it. When it came to my daughters, I got them for my daughters, but they really meant something to my oldest daughter. So much so that she’s the one who asked for her kiddos to have their own copies because they had ripped the dust covers on her copies. So they got the new books, but they got her torn dust covers. Lol. These poems stand the test of time…obviously given they’ve made an impact on 3 generations of a single family. The poems are funny, sometimes gross, sometimes thought provoking, and sometimes just plain weird. All things that draw kids in. I got all 3 of his poem books for the kiddos.
