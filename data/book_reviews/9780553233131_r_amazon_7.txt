Utah Blaine takes a position as a ranch manager, and then ranch owner, in Arizona, and finds himself involved in a range war, with four different factions trying to take over his land.  This is an earlier novel by L’Amour; there’s less character development and introspection than in some of his later books, but man, there’s no shortage of action!
