The Easy Guide to Sewing Skirts has clear and detailed diagrams and instructions, and step by step  photographs to guide the sewer to create a skirt from concept to hemming. I needed a skirt for a special occasion and did not find any suitable petite style in the stores.  I used this guide to help me return to the art of sewing garments and I successfully completed my skirt project.  Marcy Tilton shares her expertise and love of sewing garments in this empowering guide.  I also used her companion book EASY GUIDE TO SEWING TOPS & T-SHIRTS  when I create tops so I can have a finished outfit.
