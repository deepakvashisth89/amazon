THE LISTENING SKY is a remarkable story that I actually read more than a decade ago. But since this author's passing I've been wanting to re-read all her older novels and when I was able to find this kindle version at a good price I knew I had to give it another go. Needless to say Ms. Garlock continues to amaze me with her wonderful writing. She has a way of depicting life in a bygone era appear as if you were living it instead of just reading about it. Her characterizations and the way she could tell a story is unlike anything I've ever experienced. She is one of my all-time favorite authors and I would recommend her books to one and all. If you haven't read any of her stories then give one a try. I'm never disappointed and I'm sure you won't be either.As for this story it has a lot of characters that play major roles and at times there are actually three romances taking place, although the main one involves T.C. and Jane Love. Jane comes off as prim and icy in the beginning but once she opens herself up to T.C. and her newfound friends she's actually a very kind and lovely person. T.C. along with all of Garlock's heroes was just perfect! Strong, sexy, rugged, brawny men who you can't help but adore. There is also a unique plot with an unknown enemy out for Jane that'll keep you guessing until the very end.And although all of DG's books can standalone, she more often than not uses characters from her other stories and this one is no exception. Colin Tallman, son of John Tallman from "Yesteryear" and grandson of Rain Tallman from "Dream River" has his own love interest in this book. So if you are like me and love those Tallman men, this is definitely a must read. Then there is T.C. who happens to be the nephew of Moose Kilkenny who made an appearance in "Nightrose" as a bare-knuckle boxer. And while you definitely don't need to read those books to enjoy this one, I'd still recommend reading "Yesteryear" first just so you can get to know Colin a bit better. Overall just a fantastic and entertaining story by an unbelievable author who I can't recommend highly enough!Read moreRead less
