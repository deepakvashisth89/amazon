I have never read anything by this author despite her being so prolific a writer.  The synopsis about the book and it being free to read spurred my interest.  However, it was not what I thought it was.  I was expecting the usual cozy mystery, but this was far from it.  The setting, the characters, the ultimate story of the hidden experiences and stories we all have and how they influence our future life rollout was surprising.  All the characters were quirky, the story odd but familiar.  Very intriguing and a book I could not put down.  A highly recommended read.Read moreRead less
