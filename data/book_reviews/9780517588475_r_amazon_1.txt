I am an unabashed Morse/Lewis fan.  I spent a day soaking up the atmosphere of Morse's Oxford in May 2015.  "The Jewel That Was Ours" is the first of Colin Dexter's Morse novels that I have read, and I did so with some trepidation having grown to like the television series so much.  The book did not disappoint and absorbed me into the story.  Of course, I was led into the plot by the work of my own cinematic mind.
