This is the first book about pets that I actually read from start to finish. It had a lot of great case studies, which provided excellent examples of symptoms cats produce (urinating on furniture, refusal to eat,  etc.), and how to overcome this "crazy" behavior. I recommend  this book to anyone who has a cat(s) and wants to better understand them.  If you want suggestions for training your cat, this book is great!  Biggest  lesson learned: Don't punish your cat; there are better, more effective  solutions to change your cat's behavior.Read moreRead less
