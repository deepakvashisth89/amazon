Creed is a photographer, stalking out a celebrity funeral when he sees something disturbing. He manages to capture it on film,  and this begins a chain of events that will put Creed's life, and those of his loved ones, in danger.I did enjoy the novel but there are quite a lot of references, contemporary to the time it was written, which make it feel dated.
