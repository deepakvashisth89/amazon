If you are a person who loves to, or wants to learn to, preserve their own food, then this is the book for you.  The information is very extensive with recipes on various ways to can and freeze in bulk.  As our world is getting crazier by the minute and our food supplies have a daily crises of listeria and other food born illnesses, I believe we are turning more and more to growing and putting up our own food.  This should not be done by a beginner who does it by a trial and error method as this can cause all kinds of disaster and waste.  This Farm Journal's information about all areas of home preservation is in depth on the safest methods, recipes and array of kitchen utensils needed to use.  I have canned for over 45 years and used to just do the basic green beans and tomatoes but have added new items over the years.  Probably the biggest area of preservation that I've done is in the freezer.  This books has so many recipes for completed food items, not just the whole fruit or veggie.  Very nice to pull a completed main course or side from the freezer.  There is a section for meats both canned and frozen, too.
