Having seen the film which this book inspired, I was anxious to read it and I have not been disappointed.  This book is throughly well written and I am really enjoying reading it.  Only other author I have come across who matches it for realism of writing inthis particular genre is Joseph M. Marshall III (The Long Knives are Crying), himself an American Native.  Taking into consideration that the book was written in English then translated into Dakotah and back into English, it really achieves its goal for me of giving a sense of what it would have been like to live and speak as an American Indian.  The time spent researching the subject plus the translation work was time well spent.  Well done indeed to the authoress for a well written, enjoyable book which really brings to life a culture of which we sadly can only glimpse echoes today.Read moreRead less
