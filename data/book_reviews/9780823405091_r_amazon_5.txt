First off, I will tell you that I am reviewing the edition of MARY HAD A LITTLE LAMB that matches the Amazon description here. I enjoy reading the classic rhyme. I especially like there are several pages that are just additional illustrations without words, so that we can point and talk more about the story.Another baby we know who is 22 months old also enjoys this.
