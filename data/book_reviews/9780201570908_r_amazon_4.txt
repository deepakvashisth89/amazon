Even though this book was written over twenty years ago, it is still relevant today. The book provides some basic insights into how to see customers. It asks the reader to take a look at their business through their customers' eyes.The book is easy to read and contains some interesting anecdotes from well-known businesses and business people.If you are looking to improve the customer service at your organization, this book is one you should read.
