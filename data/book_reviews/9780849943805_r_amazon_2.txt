I cannot sing the praises of Ted Dekker enough!  He has, in my opinion, the most unique and unparalleled ability I have ever come across to weave a story that is not only captivating, but also inspiring, and incorporates so much Biblical truth that I never cease to be amazed.  Furthermore, his stories help me to understand the awesome power, incomprehensible forgiveness, and unbelievable love that our Father has for us all.  When I read the first book in this series, Blessed Child, I came away thinking it was perhaps his best work ever (although to be honest, I've thought that after many of his books).  I was again proven wrong; however, because A Man Called Blessed was just as powerful and wonderful as the first. All I can say to Ted Dekker is keep 'em coming!
