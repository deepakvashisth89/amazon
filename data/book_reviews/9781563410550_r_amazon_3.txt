I first read this in late 2005 when Evelyn loaned it to me. We were in Hawaii. Ev is a fantastic person - full of life, love, and smiles. One day, that smile dimmed. I will never forget the tears and pain as Ev told me about her murdered sister, Rebecca. The book is written by Rebecca's surviving girlfriend Claudia. This tragic story is also a healing story, but one that you will shed tears over. Why read sad stories? Because we must never forget.I originally had a date typo.
