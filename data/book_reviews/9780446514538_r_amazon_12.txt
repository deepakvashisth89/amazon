I liked this reimagining of the assassination of JFK. It is well founded on the events of 22 November 1963 and anyone who remembers that day and followed the investigation and the Warren Commission will enjoy identifying the originals of the characters and the spotting where the author elaborates, borrows or twists the events.Reading it as someone who remembers it all clearly, I cannot imagine how it will resonate with a reader to whom this is history but I advise them to read it. What might have been novels are often muddled with too much hindsight and often axes to grind but this one has some strength, it could just do with a few jokes. John Cassidy is more like Jed Bartlett than KFK ever was but - hey, it's a novel not history.Read and enjoy.Read moreRead less
