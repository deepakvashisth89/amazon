The title tells all, and it's a message that needs to be heard.  In this book, Jack Mitchell discusses the relationship focused approach his family run business has successfully implemented.  Contrary to the belief that customers are numbers, Mitchell and his team focus on building relationships as the number one priority.  The benefits are endless including high retention, referrals, and a better quality of life (other than the Sunday phone calls to the house).  This book is becoming increasingly more important as we shift into a relationship focused economy where the consumers now how an abundance of choice and business owners have to do a lot more for loyalty.
