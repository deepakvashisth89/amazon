This is fine and sometimes funny space opera. Even the wave-of-the-hand technology is interesting. The execution is sometimes a bit ragged, with occasional typos, particularly the unnecessary dialogue attributions, but the plotting is good, the characters interesting and the story stays compelling. I did get a little tired of the constant use of the F-word. These university folks have pretty limited vocabularies. All in all, though, it's worth the price and your time. I will try another of Mr. Cook's efforts. And, BTW, if you read to the end you'll discover why so many (not all, but many) of the starships are named for porn stars.Read moreRead less
