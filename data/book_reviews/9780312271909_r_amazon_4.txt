I found this book to be so haunting that I read it a second time. I enjoyed it even more as I picked up some strange feelings about Jeans childhood friend. I feel that the friend set her up by not warning her. She  said that she invited Jean to come to her wedding after thinking about the  games they used to play.Think this could be a very good movie if they  can create the atmosphere the way the book has.
