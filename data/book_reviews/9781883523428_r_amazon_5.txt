Best friends are women who share their hearts with each other... and touch yours by the sharing of their stories.  The Elegant Gathering of White Snows by Kris Radish is just that... an elegant gathering.  I was enchanted with it from the first line till the ending ...I didn't want to finish, but wanted to savor like the wine these friends shared through their journey.  The book made me realize there were friends I wanted to reconnect with... so I sent them copies of this book.  It wan't just the eight women who remained with me, but those people who met them along the way: the deputy, the old woman in the nursing home, the mothers and daughter. Each one who was affected by their story, each life within the story, was equally as memorable.
