I purchased this book when it was first written in the 60's.  I've since purchased two replacement copies because I've read it so often.  Although it shows its age a bit in the 21st century, the plot is excellent, the characters are delightful and familiar, and the writing is snappy and very witty.  All in all, it's such an enjoyable book all around that I "visit" the situation and the characters once every couple of years.  If you want to thoroughly enjoy a well written mystery with well definied characters and a wonderful touch of humor, DO read this lovely book.
