The copyright says the book was published in 1992. Some reviews have said it’s dated. I like to read books about traveling to places I’ll probably never go. I thought there would be more comparisons to his previous trip in the early ‘70s as the description suggests but not much of that. I don’t know why the book didn’t really grab me. Maybe because sometimes I didn’t really like Bryson, especially in one section where he kept using the word “stupid.” I also wondered why his wife didn’t go with him and why there weren’t any photos of these spectacular places he describes. Anyway, Bryson’s accounts are amusing and it’s a good, somewhat enjoyable book.Read moreRead less
