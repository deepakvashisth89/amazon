Song of the Nereids, by Sandy DenglerIt's 1861 in Singapore, and Margaret Rollin Rice has reason to assist a fine looking man carrying a sick child to find a doctor.  He is Captain Travis Bricker of the ship Arachne which sits in port.  The boy is Gideon, who is too sick to continue aboard the Arachne, so Captain Bricker is out a cabin boy.  And Margaret Erica Rice is struck by a sudden inspiration and offers her brother as cabin boy in Gideon's place, explaining that Eric can go along to serve him on his course to New Zealand and to America, so long as he ends in England to continue his schooling.  So Captain Bricker makes arrangements for Gideon's care and hires young Eric in his place when he comes later for an interview.Then at the last minute before sailing, a beautiful woman named Maude Harrington descends on the Arachne, begging passage and claiming to be fleeing from an estranged, depraved and vicious paramour.  So they set sail.  But her ex is actually the Dutchman, a powerful man who wants her back and has put out hefty rewards for her return.  And in a sudden accident, Captain Travis Bricker discovers that he has not one, but two women aboard, for Eric is in reality Margaret Erica, fleeing from what, she will not say.Meantime, realizing that the Dutchman may well kidnap Gideon to use as a lever against him in order to get Miss Harrington back, Captain Bricker sends Fisher, his bosun, back to Singapore to bring Gideon away with him.  But Fisher arrives a moment after the nick of time, for the Dutchman is already in the act of stealing Gideon.And back on the ship, Captain Bricker has informed Erica that she must leave his ship when they put in at the island of Macassar on the way to New Zealand, as he needs a cabin boy and not a cabin girl.  But by this time, Erica has feelings for the captain and doesn't want to go.  And Miss Harrington, who is actually Mrs. Dutchman and the mother of his children, is making moves on the very moral captain. And the captain is finding when Erica has gone that the place where she was is so--empty.  Fisher has succeeded in rescuing Gideon and is now on a ship filled with Chinese pirates, who are expecting a reward which neither he nor his captain can pay, and are also bound for Macassar.It is a rousing adventure/love story, exactly the sort at which Sandy Dengler excels.  Not a dull moment to be found anywhere.  And if all that isn't enough, it's funny!  God bless Sandy Dengler!Read moreRead less
