The Hemp Handbook, by Gisela Schreiber, is a book about the wonderful plant, hemp (cannabis). It tells about how it is great for the body, the environment, clothes, medicine, and pleasure for the dope smoker. Did you know that hemp paper can save the lives of millions of trees a year?; and a pair of hemp jeans will last almost a lifetime instead of a few years. Yes, hemp can be used to help the environment, make better clothes, healthy food, fuel, plastics, buildings, paint, paper, and natural medicines (no man-made stuff!). It is a book well worth reading.
