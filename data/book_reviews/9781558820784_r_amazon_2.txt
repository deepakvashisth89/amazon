I’m reading Craig Rice’s John J. Malone mysteries in order and this one, the fourth in the series, is the best one so far. (I recommend reading the one before this, The Wrong Murder, before you read this one, as the plots overlap quite a bit.) Like the others, it’s fast-paced and a very easy read. It’s also the funniest of the four, with several laugh-out-loud lines. The mystery itself is also very clever, and the payoff is more than satisfactory. It was written in the 1940s, so there may be a few words or situations that offend a modern reader, but that’s par for the course for mysteries of that era. All in all, a fun and entertaining book if you enjoy light mysteries.Read moreRead less
