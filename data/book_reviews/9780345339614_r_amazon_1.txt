I bought the Lords of Dus series, quite out of order, back when I was in high school, back before there were digital books or Amazon. It took me forever to find the first book of the series, The Lure of the Basilisk, and by the time I did, my interests has moved from fantasy to hard science fiction and crime novels... so I never read the books. I'm making up for that mistake now.Lure of the Basilisk was a good novel, but The Seven Altars of Dussara is even better. The protagonist, Garth the Overman, is a surprisingly nuanced character, a monster (to humans) who at times acts with more empathy than the humans around him. I finished the book pretty quick and I will immediately move on to The Sword of Bheleu.I recommend this books for anyone who likes sword and sorcery fantasy or who is a long time player of fantasy RPGs like D&D.Read moreRead less
