This saga is an easy read if you can keep up with the "time traveling."  The characters are interesting, the plot flows well, and the lady finds true love in the end.  Spanning over 100years, Becky Lee Weyrich keeps you on you toes jumping back and forth from the 1960's - 1860's and back again several times.Entertaining, but don' believe I will read her other books.Authors KarenWhite and Elin Hilderbrand also use similar styles of time traveling.  They are a bit more talented.  This was a fun break from the crime thrillers I have been reading this summer, so it certainly accomplished my purpose.
