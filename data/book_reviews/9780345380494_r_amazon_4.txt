Continuing on fromHammer and Anvilthis story continues the war of Makuran (Persia) against Videssos (Rome). Here, somewhat surprisingly, we are back to Abivard as the lead character. As Shahrbaraz's chief general Abivard has conquered all the way to Videssos the city. Now he has to face off against Maniakes for the control of Videssos.This reliance on only one POV character seems damaging to the series. I had hoped they'd stop that after each character got his own book. Contrary to the last novel, Maniakes is the main driver of events here and yet we only get to see them through Abivard's reaction to them. The book is basically a struggle between two highly successful generals to see which can outthink the other. It is also a struggle between Abivard and his king Shahrbaraz who has become distant and tyrannical, as well as the traitor Tzikas who is just waiting to stab him in the back.
