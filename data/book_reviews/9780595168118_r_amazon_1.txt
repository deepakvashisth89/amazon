I didn't think I'd like this book. But I found myself ongoingly surprised and delighted by the twerpy, unsweetened voice of the cricket Ulysses as he interacted with Julie Maxwell. The dialogue is so authentic. This is a tender story of Julie and Ulysses and how their unusual relationship contributed to her experiencing loss, garnering strength, and stepping into her power and creativity. There is delightfully more, but I don't want to ruin it for you. I was so engaged - I had to read it in one sitting.Read moreRead less
