This prequel to Eon is an interesting introduction to the remarkable world of Lamarkia and its inhabitants.  Bear's imagination never seems to diminish.  His new world is an interesting concept .  His treatment of the human reaction to it is somewhat predictable, but also quite reasonable given humanities previous interactions with new worlds.  An unfortunate inclination for violent intercourse and power grubbing seem to follow us wherever we go.  Will Lamarkia be any different?Bear really brings a story to life.
