As with all anthologies this one has winners and losers, but overall the anthology is designed to trigger that sense of adventure in teenagers that will keep them dreaming and reading.  If you are new to the genre this is a good place to start, or else maybe you will pick up a new author, which is the main reason i read anthologies.  After a while you can pinpoint the bombs and appreciate the gems.
