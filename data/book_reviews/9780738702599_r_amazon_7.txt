I am a novelist doing research for a thriller in an urban setting. In other words I am looking for real observations, insights and fact-based information on primitive movements in modern cities.I bought this book expecting a serious anthropological analysis/study of actual and widespread Urban Primitivism. Gangs, homeless encampments and other sub-cultures - body piercing, tattoos, branding, fetishes - underground nightlife, drugs, dark religions practiced in basements and alleys etc.I was very let-down, I will probably throw it in the trash.This book is a juvenile attempt to blend many of the sillier aspects of modern commercialized Wicca with an cardboard cut-out urban setting. It seemed to me entirely fictional, perhaps even a lame attempt at comedy or satire. I guess this book might appeal to young wicca-wannabes but I felt like I was reading a 3rd grader's creative writing assignment.I would be surprized if the make-believe(?) religion portrayed in the book is practiced by more than a handful of  "Weekend Wiccans". The book certainly would have been far more interesting (to me) if it had focused on studying the many real, fascinating urban-primitive sub-cultures that thrive in the darkness beneath the surface of most large modern cities.Unless you are a Wiccan and are looking for a bizzare, and silly way to "update" your beliefs to an urban setting, this book will be of little worth or interest to you.Do not waste your money.
