At the time of this writing I am a newly minted PhD, and a brand new assistant professor in a new school.  The Academic's Handbook should be handed to every grad student as soon as they become ABD.  Rather than providing one long monologue or any one person's viewpoint on issues facing academics, each chapter is essentially an essay written by a different writer about different academic issue.  Topics range from the general atmosphere at different types of institutions (small, liberal arts universities, for instance), free speech, interdisciplinary relationships, harassment, minorities issues, and more.Because this book is written from the varied perspectives of a number of people it is also not written from the perspective of any particular discipline.  This book is essential for graduate students interested in pursuing careers in teaching or research.  Don't expect this to be an exhaustive treatment of all issues that you may encounter in your new job.  This book will cover many of the big issues in one way or another, and it will give you indicators of the type of atmosphere that you may be inheriting after you start your new life.
