This is indeed a large book, 800 pages, plan on placing the book on a desk or table while you read.  There are pictures throughout that are tied to the current reading. I started out with an interest in WW2 materiel and of course that led to the holocaust. The book so far seems to be all you will ever need to learn about this awful period in human history.  After reading some of the book I just sit back and thanked God that the allies won the war.  Its hard to see how another world War could come about if the world leaders learn from this one. Albert Einstein said he didn't know how the third world war would be fought but the fourth would be fought with clubs and stones.  Getting back to the book, the pages are slick with large founts making for easy reading, there is a time line down at the bottom that gives the date of important events as you read, This book has so much information that it will requiring studying, not just reading so take your time and absorb the material.
