I am reading the series as much for the cultural nature as for the plot. Denmark is a socialist country and is less restrictive in sex and drug policy and that can be witnessed in the operations of the Amsterdam Cops. Example:  They don’t seem to be overly concerned with expenses or reimbursement and senior officers require their juniors to pay for coffee, drinks and an occasional meal.A society with legal prostitution understandably is more sexually free so there is no moral barrier to hurdle. It’s a matter of fact occurrence.Much of this book takes place in Japan and there are interesting cultural nuggets throughout.I recommend reading the series in order since the characters develop and some devastating events affect the characters.
