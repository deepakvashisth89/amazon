I was pretty sure I read this book already but I was about to give away some books and Ms. Fyfield is one of my favorite authors. The writing is exquisite. I usually don't like overly descriptive beginning but Undercurrents starts with such reality that you feel the rain pounding as much as poor sensitive Henry. The characters are so well drawn I felt I knew them. The plot is complicated, yet finding out who "done it", is not hard. Where this book shines is how you come to either like or dislike or aren't sure of your feelings about the characters. The plot is intricate so even if you find out who the murderer is, there are still moments of excitement. This to me is one of Ms. Fyfields more interesting books. I loved how the gay men who take Henry in and pull him into solving the mystery. I also love how her characters are psychologically drawn to be neither totally hateful or lovable.
