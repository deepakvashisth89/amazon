Clearly a book that tried too hard to be on shelves in time for the Apollo 11 20th Anniversary. Typos and other factual errors unbound on just about every page. The problem with all of these errors, the minor ones and the glaringly obvious ones, is that it throws the credibility of everything the author writes and everything he quotes into questions of authenticity. This is a book that should never have made it to the bookshelves as it stands. This is why editors should be utilized. Despite the errors, I wanted to like this book, but I would prefer to read the books penned by the astronauts themselves or by a more conscientious author.For a glaringly obvious mistake, check out the last page in the second set of photos. The Apollo 15 and 16 photos do not correspond to the descriptions.
