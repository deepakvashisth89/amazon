First of all, if you're going to get a copy of this novel and can't afford or don't want to bother to find a classic pressing from the 80s, THIS is the version to get. It's not the strongest one in the Scribner Vintage line, but it's very well manufactured, holds up against wear and is just the right size physically. The cover is a hair silly, but at least it has personality and a charmingly vintage/retro vibe and some work was put into it, which is way more than I can say for the majority of laugh-out-loud hideous graphic designs for most Stephen King books on the market today. Beyond all that, Firestarter itself is actually a surprisingly excellent Stephen King novel -- it flew right past my highest expectations for it. It's a really solid, well-imagined piece of fiction with good characters, and memorable villain, and some really inspired bits of psionic tomfoolery in it. Worth your time.Read moreRead less
