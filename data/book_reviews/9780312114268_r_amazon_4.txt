The author's time pretending to be a journalist appears to have been time well spent, as this may be one of the better written personal histories I have read in many years.  Ignoring the politics, this is incredibly well-written and very interesting.  Also, no matter what his views on a person, the author treats his subjects with dignity and tries to present their point of view, which is difficult to do in a book of this type.  Highly recommended for those who enjoy good prose and interesting stories.  P.S. If you like The Americans, you'll love this book.Read moreRead less
