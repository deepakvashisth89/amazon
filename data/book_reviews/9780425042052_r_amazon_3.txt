A book for precocious children or those who like reading to children. It's the story of an eleven-year-old orphaned girl who finds a colony of 6 inch Lilliputians in the garden of her stately home. Her horrible guardians would like to sell the Lilliputians, and Maria and the Professor (who lives in the garden) and Mrs Noakes (the cook) have to work hard to save them. Funny, good moral questions about power, dictatorship (should Maria be their Queen?)and wonderful descriptions of the Lilliputian community - much better than Swift's.Read moreRead less
