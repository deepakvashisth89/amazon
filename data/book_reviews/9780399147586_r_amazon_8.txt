Lucas Davenport is one of my favorite book characters.  He is so egotistical & kind of crude, however it is impossible not to like him.  This book was especially good - the main character, James Qatar is one of the worst that Davenport has had to face.  I really liked the ending.  I can always read a John Sandford book when I am tired of trying new authors & don't want to get into some of these really intense survival books I usually read.  I enjoy a good rainy day & a good Davenport book!Read moreRead less
