This is a very good mystery with interesting characters in an unusual setting. For those familiar with the SCA and similar re-enactment groups the book serves not only as mystery but nostalgia for the great times had at such events. For those not familiar with that world no loss will be suffered because the characters pop and really are what drives the story. The mystery is intriguing and well developed in the tradition of Agatha Christy. I highly recommend this book.Read moreRead less
