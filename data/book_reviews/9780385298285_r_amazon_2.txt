For those familiar with Keirsey and his books on temperaments, Type Talk makes an excellent companion.  Compared to Keirsey's rather clinical explanations, Kroeger and Thuesen's book explains the temperaments in layman's terms and includes situational examples for easier identification of each type.  For those looking for an introduction to the Temperament Sorter, this insightful book is an engaging beginner's guide to all the temperaments and how each can be easily identified in daily living.  This book doesn't include a temperament test, but any person can accurately identify his/her personality type just by reading the clear examples of each trait.
