This is the best biography of Ben Franklin ever written. You really get to know the man, through his own words, in a way that just isn't the case with other biographies (e.g. the Walter Isaacson bio). And the author is just such a great writer that it's a pleasure to read. I found it after watching old Berkshire Hathaway meetings and Charlie Munger recommended it, and as with many things, Munger knows best.I looked for a kindle version but couldn't find one, so for now you just have to get a used paperback whenever you can find one selling for a decent price.
