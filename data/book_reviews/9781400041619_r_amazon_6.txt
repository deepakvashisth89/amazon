I am so sorry I finished the book - I so enjoyed looking forward to reading it more! I cannot describe how true and real it was to see the characters come to life. And along with very detailed, careful and loving description of the multilayered orthodox world - deep insight into human nature. I literally recognized myself in one of the characters, and my husband (at least in part) - in one of the others. Particularly I felt connected to the phenomenon that she describes - the desire to belong, to be like everyone else and always falling short.
