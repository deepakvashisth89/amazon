Leigh is a young rich girl who is in love with her stepbrother Morgan. Problem is Morgan thinks she's a golddigger just like her mom. His mistrustful nature has him treating Leigh quite shabbily throughout this manga novel, which made me not like him. I would've preferred if the guy was more mellow. He is older and smarter than Leigh and thus really doesn't have an excuse to fly off the handle so much. The art was very nice, but Morgan's meaness didn't really work for me as it felt abusive.The artwork was well done though, and the characters were well portayed...
