Never read such a long Rosie Thomas!  Most of it good. I would have preferred not to have had all the chapters on Italy which I found boring..it would have been better to replace all that with one long sentence about why Julia went there ..she wanted to get away..ok but it could have been to anywhere..found the chapters on gardens and monasteries boring.In place of those chapters I would have preferred more on her search for and finding her biological mother. because apart from a short search and one meeting that was it..nothing about what was to happen in the future. The ending was OK..sensible ending for Julia..if it had been me I would have given up a last chance of a secure future with Alexander to disappear into the sunset with Josh again even knowing what he was like...but then I am not as sensible as Julia!But it was that kind of book..sensible, calm reading, interesting enough but not can't-put-down exciting stuff.
