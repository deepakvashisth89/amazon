Translating always is a form of rewriting, especially in the case of poems.Hermann Hesse is less known for his poetry which is rich in images of nature, love, and death.Hermann Hesse not well known for his poetry, although it is rich in images of nature, love, and death.In the original German, all the 92 poems rhyme and have a romantic and archaic feel to them. Sue Frizzell has succeeded in modernizing them not only by resisting the temptation to apply rhyme schemes to the English version, but also by using more contemporary language. It truly feels like reading two different poems, which I mean as a compliment. At the same time, none of the message or meaning gets lost in translation. Frizzell does a good job explaining places, certain expressions or neologisms she uses. My only complaint is that I would have liked to see the year when the poem was written behind the title.
