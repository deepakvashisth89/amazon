I brought this book with me on a long car trip and found it ideal reading,  and very interesting and well-written. The author hitchhikes (mostly)  across the U.S.A. after doing same some 20 years ago in his hippie days. He  has some anxiety but finds his fears overblown. (Truthfully, this kind of  book really, really, really makes one want to chuck everything, the house,  the mate, the kids, the job, and just hit the open road.  I mean this.) He  meets kind and interesting people, too.  I would compare this book to the  writings of Bill Bryson, though without Bryson's humor. I only wish the  book was longer!Read moreRead less
