This is an amazing work.  A scan of the originals which were created with collages of black and white etchings reminiscent of M.C. Escher, it is a loosely woven pictorial narrative. No words supplied, but available online in PDF at mathewsmeditations.com is one person's suggestion of verbal narration. The omnipresent bird motif displayed here is characteristic of the symbolism in the works of Heironymous Bosch, although not as graphic or disturbing as those, so the title, "A Week of Mercy," is definitely ironic.
