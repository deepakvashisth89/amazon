"Still the Best Hope" is the title of a book written by Dennis Prager that is scheduled for release on April 24, 2012. I seldom pre-order a book but I did that for this one.I have read "Happiness is a Serious Problem" many times and it is my favorite "self help" book. I have also read "Why the Jews?" many times and I think that it is a very serious attempt to address a topic that I have been struggling to understand since 1962 (when I wrote term paper for my college English class)."Think a Second Time" is a collection of 47 essays. Since it covers numerous topics it is not as easy to follow as the author's other books. Nevertheless he makes a courageous attempt to deal with difficult issues.The fact that people started writing reviews about "Think a Second Time" in 1998 and others continued writing thoughtful reviews through 2011 says a lot about the durability of Dennis Prager's ideas.Read moreRead less
