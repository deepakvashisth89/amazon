Narrated by Will Wheaton.  If you know him; you'll either love it or not.  If you don't know him, you'll probably enjoy the story - it is a good story.  On the other hand, Mr. Wheaton is not the author; but the author is not the voice actor that Mr. Wheaton is.  (I, personally kind of miss the Author's readings - but I have heard Roger Zelazny read one of his other books when he came to our local library.)  I haven't finished listening to this whole story yet - there are some audio artifacts, drop outs, and distortions - not unlike the ones that the author has read.  (I work with a recording/mastering engineer - I expect higher quality - hence 4 of 5 stars.)  Each chapter is a single, large MP3 file - making it kind of hard to easily index through the story depending on your playback device.
