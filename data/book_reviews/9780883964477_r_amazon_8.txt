I sent this book to my seventeen year old daughter, who sadly no longer lives with me full time, as on reading it I realised it said so much of what I want to say but can't always find the right time or space to.  It focuses on the eternal love between a parent and child and how much we want the best for them as well as dwelling on deeper aspects of the parent-child relationship, through the inclusion of many personal, very accessible poems.  A tear jerker, but not over sentimental.  A great gift for any child, male or female, aged 10 or above who can no longer live with a parent who very much loves and misses them.Read moreRead less
