Westlake was brilliant, and his Dortmunder books are a delight. They deserve better than the horrible formatting and errors I've encountered in every ebook of his put out by this publisher. I can't remove stars because of the content, but I'd give this and all his other books one star for crappy conversion to ebook. I'm currently up to Nobody's perfect (I read this one out of sequence because it's short stories) and it's rife with stupid, terrible errors because they didn't double-check the scan to text, I guess. It's a shame.So many times separate paragraphs were run together in this book of otherwise wonderful short stories., which was confusing and annoying.
