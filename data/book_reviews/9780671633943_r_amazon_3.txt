This book was absolutely thrilling! Although this book is the fourth in a series, I read it easily as a standalone. Brianna and Creigh's love story happened quickly but endured many tests of loyalty and strength; both physically and mentally. There are multiple villains and multiple characters to mourn but the characters are steadfast and strong.
