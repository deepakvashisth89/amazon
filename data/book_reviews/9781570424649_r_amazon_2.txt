Lavar Burton has a very good grasp of what evil lurks in the hearts of men. And he does a masterful job of setting it in front of us. His vision of just how quickly America could become the epitome of haves and have nots is written with style and a smoothness that gets under your skin. I honestly had to put the book down for a few days because one of the twists shocked me so. It was the realization of just how easily we could find ourselves there. Me. You.
