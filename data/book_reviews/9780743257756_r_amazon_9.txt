A Girl Becomes a Comma Like That follows (mainly) Rachel as she navigates her mother's cancer diagnosis. Rachel seems to be looking for anything to hold onto in order to survive the emotional denial she's going through, so most of the book charts her love life and things she discovers along the way.I will say that the prose is beautiful, however the plot was lacking for me. I think this book is definitely literary in that respect, so if you've never read anything literary, be prepared for it to move slowly. That being said, I did enjoy the book.The dialogue felt very natural and authentic, and the prose genuinely made me laugh and make my insides feel like they were shrivelling because it was so raw. I feel like I learned things reading it.However, I didn't like flipping to Angela or Georgia's viewpoints. They were okay, but I much preferred Rachel and Ella. Angela's section felt like it was simply there to lengthen the book, and I felt I gleaned enough about Georgia in Ella's section that she didn't need her own.All in all, I think this is a book I will come back to time and time again because, despite its slowness, the prose was so beautiful.
