I have read this book hundreds of times. As a child, I was floored when I learned in my school library that there was more than one Oz book, and was extremely excited when I found this one. It has an exciting, compelling storyline for an Edwardian era children’s novel.Parts of it have not aged well. The author Baum was a communist, but also an avid supporter of the suffragist movement. His antagonistic female General Jinjur and her army of stereotypical pretty girls, who use knitting needles to subdue enemies are laughable by today’s standards. Not to mention their happiness at returning to domestic servitude to their men by novels end.But it’s an engaging, fun book that helps flesh out the world of Oz.
