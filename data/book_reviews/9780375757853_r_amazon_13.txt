When I learned that this book was written in 1868 and has been considered to be the first detective novel,  I thought that it would be an interesting read.  I almost stopped during the 1st three chapters, as I struggled with the Victorian English, culture and writing style which seemed stilted and at times boring.  However, discovering that it had been made into a movie,  I persevered to discover a good mystery that also offered insights into the Victorian way of life  and mindset.  It's a long read and the story is told through through the eyes of each of the main characters.
