This is a very well made book, clothbound and sturdy, with easy-to-read pages.  It has an excellent introduction by Martin Seymour-Smith, a chronology, and selected bibliography. What it lacks--and needs--is either a plate showing the parts of a late 19th-century sailing ship, or a glossary, or notes to explain nautical terms.
