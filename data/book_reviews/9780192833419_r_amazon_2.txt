Translation is always a dodgy business. Translating verse is even trickier. Translating rhyming verse seems to beg the impossible. And yet Maya Slater has done it—and done so with beautiful results.Slater produces a remarkably faithful rendition in English of Molière's rhyming Alexandrine couplets written in French. These justly famous plays are a joy to read, and readers will be left in awe not only of the great dramatist's skills but of Slater's own stunning accomplishment.Read moreRead less
