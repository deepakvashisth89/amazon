... I'll die a very happy (albeit tired) man.This book is not Shakespeare. Nor is it Tolstoy. Which is lucky really as I didn't want that to begin with. What I wanted was a bunch of fun anecdotes, jokes, innuendo and all round good times from one of the great rock 'n' roll circus masters, Mr David 'Mee' Roth. And boy did our man deliver. I really enjoyed the stories contained herein and revelled in the simple bohemian depravity that was often alluded to but, tastefully, rarely discussed in depth. The book is actually full of anonymous people, by which I mean that Roth has chosen not to publish the names of most of the other players in these scenes. And weirdly enough while some people may not like that aspect of the book it seemed to be a thoroughly decent thing to do. After all many of the ladies he would have had liaisons with are now respected actresses or mothers or whatever... why drag their name through the mud and embarrass them. I can live with the story and don't need the gossip column name dropping and if you are of a like mind then you should quite enjoy this breezy read. Sure a lot of this book jumps around a lot and some reviewers have pointed out that it would have been nice to have a more coherent and linear reading experience but this jumpy style seems to suit him, Roth being best when he's writing as if he was talking to you rather than any overly studied prose being used.I can understand however that some people would have preferred a more fly on the wall style of discussion about the career of Van Halen and Roths solo bands and the various tours. I, too, would have liked to read such a book. Perhaps this should have been two books, the meatier stuff being lightened by the shenanigans. But what the hey, this book delivered what I thought it would and I'm happy with that. There are other rock bios out there if I feel the need for some heavier doses of the dull scrape that can be life on the road.Overall a fun book if you approach it with total cognizance that this is David Lee Roth and.... seriously what were you expecting?Read moreRead less
