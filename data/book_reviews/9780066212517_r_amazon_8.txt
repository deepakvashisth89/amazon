This is the very interesting history of the research of genetics, evolution, and biology using the very versatile tool <i>Drosophilia melanogaster</i>.  However, it is written for a non-scientific audience.  There are numerous puns and jokes about the humble fruit fly and the explanation of genes and alleles is reduced to an analogy of shoes on people's feet.  This may help people who have absolutly no science background understand the subject, but I found the poor analogies to be distracting.On the other hand, the history was absorbing.  How were maps of genes created before fancy sequencing machines?  The answer lies in the close study of thousands of generations of fruit flys and studying their mutations.  Many discoveries of basic genes that are present in all life forms are first found in the fruit fly.  Many more discoveries are yet to be made.Read moreRead less
