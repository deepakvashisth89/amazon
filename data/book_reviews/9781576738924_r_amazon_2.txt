Our Men's Group has ordered this book for our next study.  On the functional side it comes packed with a built in study guide.  On the impact side it appears to be great.  I say appears only because I have not read whole book.  Yes, it is true I am writing this review without reading the complete book; why/how can I do this? Because it is Steve Farrar and I have read several of his works.  The man is plain spoken, to the point in a folksy way, has a natural way of speaking into the lives of men and is spot on biblical in all that he does.
