This edition is unfortunately a rather disappointing reprint of the original book by the late Thetis Blacker, first published by Barefoot Books. While the paper and print quality are acceptable, the illustrations appear very small—often less than half the page—likely because the publisher did not have access to the original artwork and had to rely on digital images from online sources.The text layout also feels somewhat mechanical: lines are overly long, paragraphs too short, and margins quite narrow. Additionally, all the text appears only on the right-hand pages, leaving many left pages mostly blank. Overall, the result lacks the balance and care of the original publication.Read moreRead less
