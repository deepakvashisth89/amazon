I liked Jim Camp‘s straightforward, no nonsense approach to negotiating. As someone who teaches people about influence and persuasion for a living, I found his approach full of practical wisdom. While he does not site research from the social sciences, Camp intuitively learned research based approaches to interacting with humans. I will say I was a little uncomfortable with his dismissal of a win-win approach but the more I read, the more I understood where he was coming from.
