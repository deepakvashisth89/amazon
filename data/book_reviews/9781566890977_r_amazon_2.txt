From the very first page of this book, I felt as if I were in a lovely trance. I responded to it on a deeply personal level, as a writer who loves language, as a woman, as a person who is enchanted with tales as a window into human experience. I felt as if Mary Sharratt had written this book uniquely for me, but I know that many, many others will feel the same way. Others have summarized the plot so I don't need to go into that here.
