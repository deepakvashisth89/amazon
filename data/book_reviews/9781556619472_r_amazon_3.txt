Julia believes her life is over after the death of her husband and the loss of all their possessions. The one thing the creditors didn't take is the Larkspur Inn, an old, closed railroad inn in the village of Gresham. The last place she wants to move her family is there. But without a home and no means of support, Julia knows she has no choice but to make a go of this abandoned property and turn it into a successful boarding house.As time passes and lodgers move in, Julia comes to realize that this has been a great move for her family - her children are happy and thriving, and her boarding house is full. Larkspur Inn is a success! Julia is content, if not not truly happy. But then a new vicar comes to Gresham. Will he give her back the happiness and love that has been missing in her life?This was a really good story! I'm not big on romances, but I do like historical fiction, which is why I chose to read this. Lots of historical details are included about life in this charming small English village. I can't wait to see what happens next! If you like interesting characters and light romance, check out the Gresham series.Read moreRead less
