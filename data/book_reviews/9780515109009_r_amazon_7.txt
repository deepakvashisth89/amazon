This first book of the series is the first I've read by Margaret Frazer.  I love books about this period, I like whodunits, I like books with good dialogue, so this was a sure thing.  But it also has people to care about, with real weaknesses and strengths.  Not sure about Sister Frevisse herself, or the plot, but her friend Sister Claire, her boss Domina Edith and Sister Alys, the bad tempered cook are all people I want to meet again. I need to find out what happens to them next.  Oh dear, I'm hooked again.Read moreRead less
