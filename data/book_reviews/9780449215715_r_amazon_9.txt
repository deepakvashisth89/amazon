I first read this book as a child in the 60s, and have now read it again nearly fifty years later. This is a magnificent book and story, full of intrigue and sudden surprises. You never really know who is who, or what's really going on. The characters are wonderful and compelling. And some are dangerous. The descriptions of the Northumberland countryside are also vivid, so you can almost smell the grass and hay. Mary Stewart really is the Queen of romantic suspense, and The Ivy Tree is a true classic.Read moreRead less
