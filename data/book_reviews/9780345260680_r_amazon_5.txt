I had been reading and enjoying some of Heinlein’s later “Juvenile Series” books and decided to seek out the first one of the series.  Rocket Ship Galileo was published in 1947.  This was shortly after World War II and it is obvious that the specter of Nazis was still fresh in his mind.  While the plot may be a bit ridiculous and may show Heinlein’s immaturity as a writer, is still a fun read and gives a glimpse into the beginning of a great writing career.Read moreRead less
