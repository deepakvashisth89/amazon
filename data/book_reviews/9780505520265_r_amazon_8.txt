She was an angel cursed down by a witch, from there; this read like a historical romance. There were more elements throughout the story questioning her being a real angel or perhaps nuts(?). But for the most part I forgot what she was. Daemon is a true tortured Kinley MacGregor hero/lord. The villian was perfect and added humor.
