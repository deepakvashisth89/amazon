I recall reading Gene Hill's "Tail Feathers" in Sports Afield in the 1950's when I was in my early teens. A Hunter's Fireside Book is a nostalgic journey through a (for me) simpler time. His stories evoke senses one has in a natural outdoor world. Hill triggers memories of the bite of a pre-dawn icy wind on a duck pond, the smells of oil and gun powder after a clean shot in a snow covered corn field, the sounds of a Woodcock's flutter,  the taste and feel of a sip of Brandy with good conversation after a day in the field, and hundreds of other associated experiences. Written with wit and a subtle compassion for nature, this book is an ideal companion for the fireside or bedside.Read moreRead less
