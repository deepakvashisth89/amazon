I was familiar with T H White'sThe Once and Future Kingbut until a friend put me onto Mistress Masham's Repose through a chance remark recently, I had never heard of it.  It would take an unusual child to plough through it, which is a shame, as it's an enchanting story, of a little girl Maria, living in a vast decaying mansion with her cruel governess, who is in cahoots with the vicar to do her out of her rightful inheritance - at all costs. Maria discovers descendents of the Lilliputians living on an island in the grounds, befriends them, and then enlists the help of an absent minded professor to save them, and herself, from betrayal. The professor is very similar to T H White himself, Maria is a brave and resourceful girl, and the entire book is full of wry humour, with a tendency by the author to address the reader as "my dear Amaryllis" and asides such as: "if you are not a biologist nobody cares a fig for you."
