A few weeks ago I noticed a reference in the paper about the young South Vietnamese girl who was severely burned by napalm in 1972.  Almost everyone older has seen this iconic photograph.  I bought this book to read about the young girl's tragic injuries, her fight to live, and her life since.  Hers is certainly an interesting story and shows the horrors of war.
