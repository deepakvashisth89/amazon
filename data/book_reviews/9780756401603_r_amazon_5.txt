If you like the Foreigner series, you will probably like this.  Two of her older works in one volume, this is like double the fun for one price.  Plus, it still contains all the interplay and conflict we are used to seeing between two cultures... alien vs human, and all the misconceptions and clashes Cherryh does so well.
