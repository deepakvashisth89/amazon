In an age wherein traveling has become more the norm rather than the exception, we have never really looked closely as a society upon the "etiquette" of traveling.  Nadine makes good headway into this new study.  This book and it's context is so valuable I would like to see it expanded upon.  I could even see it as a newspaper weekly with readers writing in "Dear Nadine I will be traveling... what should I do? Nadine handles the subjects she deals with not only with respect but also with humor, making it for a very enjoyable and easy reading book.
