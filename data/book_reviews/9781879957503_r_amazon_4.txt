I had no idea that details of such a meeting might exist, yet they do.  The author takes us day-by-day through the happenings and the conversations of this important meeting, all from actual notes from people there.  I don't think history can be more exciting than sitting at the same table as Churchill, Stalin, and Truman.  After we go through the many days of the meeting, there is a bit of follow-up; that is, what Truman's public position becomes afterward.  This is when we learn that the meeting, rather than being to establish mutual peace, was actually like a meeting of Mafia bosses, to establish boundaries within which each was free to pursue separate domination.
