This is well-written and carefully researched; if you liked Stowers' Innocence Lost or To the Last Breath, this will provide a bleak yet ultimately satisfying read.  The comparisons to books like Blood and Money are overstated, though; as the subject matter just isn't quite as fascinating.  Somewhat reminded me of Double Indemnity as re-enacted by profoundly stupid, revoltingly trashy criminals:  an insurance plot/honor killing goes horribly awry due to mistaken identity and general psychopathic idiocy.The repeated descriptions of the crime are highly disturbing, although the perseverance of the main law enforcement character (who was determined to obtain a measure of justice for the victims and their survivors no matter the personal cost) makes the depressing sections worthwhile after all.Lastly, there are way too many pages devoted to psychics and their pointless visions of the murders, which lead to zero items of admissible evidence; but that's a minor quibble, really.  Recommended, though you might have to take breaks from reading it; it deals with truly reprehensible actions and perpetrators.Read moreRead less
