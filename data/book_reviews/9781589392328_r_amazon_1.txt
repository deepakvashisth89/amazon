"Mortals All" is a fun read.  It is fast-paced with loads of action, punctuated with humor and strong (but not explicit) sexual content.  Once again, Bruce Golden explores what it means to be human, as "artificial humans" and people clash trying to achieve a balance.  One cannot help but to envision the plight of the artificials as other less fortunate members of society trying to relate to the dominant culture, and their places within it.
