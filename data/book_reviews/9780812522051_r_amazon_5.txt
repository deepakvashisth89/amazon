Charnel House is a good, quick read, but it could have benefitted from being a little longer. The lack of character development results in less emotional investment, which is unfortunate. With a greater tug of the heartstrings, it could have gone from being a good book to being a great book, in my opinion.Despite this and the problematic language (it was published in the 70s, so I'll call it a product of its time), I look forward to reading more by this author.Read moreRead less
