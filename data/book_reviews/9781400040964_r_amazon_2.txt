In this self-described "grown man's coming-of-age story," Judge Martin Clark meanders through Bedrock Baptist Fundamentalism wherein there's no such creature as a minor sin -  "There's the straight, correct, narrow route, and the rest is just Plain Heathen Mischief."The Second Book of  Clark is literate - the language sings and zings.  Naming a major player after that whiner in the OJ saga is inspired.  Though it doesn't have the magical mysticism of  his first, "The Many Aspects of Mobile Home Living,"  it's still Plain Heathen Good.  He socks it to insurance companies and American Gumball Jurisprudence like Jeannie C. Riley's  Momma socked it to the Harper Valley PTA.  /TundraVision, Amazon reviewer.Read moreRead less
