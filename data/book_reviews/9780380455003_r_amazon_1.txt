Classic sci-fi writing from the 1950s and 1960s, this is a collection of short stories that make you think about humanity and human acts. I won't detail (or spoil) the stories as other reviewers have already done so. Rather I will state that there's something optimistic about reading these from a time that was filled with mixed emotions of optimism and pessimism as we entered the nuclear age and the cold war.
