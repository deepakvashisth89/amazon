My book club picked this book up after reading American Housewife. In the true style of Helen Ellis this book has darker sense of humor and borders on disturbing and creepy. It’s a fast and easy read with super short chapters. You find yourself doing the “just one more chapter… they are short” before you know it the book is read in 2 days.
