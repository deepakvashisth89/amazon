This is easily one of the best books I have read in a long time, espionage-related or not. Once I started it, I could not put it down.I am fairly new to Le Carre, having read only a couple of his early spy novels - Spy/Cold, Looking Glass War - so I cannot compare this book to the more-recent titles with which most people are already familiar. But it stands on its own as a great book.A friend suggested this book to me and I am so glad he did. It is superbly written, with marvelously drawn characters and a moving story. This is, I believe, Le Carre looking deep into his own life and excavating what he can about his own father, who was, I have read, a con man and somewhat distant and mysterious. Yet, Le Carre has framed his story as a gripping tale of espionage, with all the deceit and tradecraft and historical savvy that he is so well known for.There is a BBC TV production based on this novel, with the same title, and it is superb, too. I highly recommend it. But trust me, read the book. You won't be disappointed.Read moreRead less
