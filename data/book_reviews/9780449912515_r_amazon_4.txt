Remarque is a favorite author of mine. I first read "All Quiet on the Western Front as a teenager, and it had a huge impact on me then, and with every re-read, shaping my world view.In this novel, covering the final months of WW II in a fictional concentration camp in Germany. All of the unimaginable horror is there.  But in the midst of this horror, hope blooms, and the stark contrast of character and the lack of it plays out.I think the character who goes by his number, 509, speaks Remarque's own views. Near the end, he speaks truth and wisdom with courage about what he thinks of totalitarian government when urged to join the communist inmates plans for " after liberation".The story ends with survivors going their own way, and I want to imagine that they lived with an ability to experience joy and hope in spite of all that happened.Read moreRead less
