There's certainly some great and intriguing class features and draconic options in here (such as the iconic dracolich) but what's really the enjoyable aspect of this book is the art and flavor that they add. One of the really truly practical aspects of it is that it contains sample dragons of each age category and sample hoards of their treasure for each CR.
