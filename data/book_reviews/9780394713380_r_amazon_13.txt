The story is entertaining without being too challenging. Without giving the game away, the plot revolves around an alleged murder at the beginning of the story and a suspicious death, later on, that the false inspector attempts to solve on board ship before it docks in New York. It is a tongue-in-cheek whodunnit with some witty dialogue, all too human frailties, twists and turns and a surprise ending. It is an ideal book to while away a few hours on a wet Sunday afternoon.Read moreRead less
