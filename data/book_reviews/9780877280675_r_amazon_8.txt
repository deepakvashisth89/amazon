I just received this book in the mail a few days ago and have been reading non stop since.  I needed to write this review to let potential buyers know that I'm not too proud to admit that I don't grasp what this book is about even a little bit.  Not that a book being hard to understand deserves a 2-star rating, but it's completely useless to me.  Right away, the book describes a technique that if done wrong could get an energy dislodged (permanently?!) and that it's a common mistake.  I certainly don't want to mess around with energy I don't understand with far out techniques that are completely foreign.  All over the place they talk about how if you don't know what you're doing, you can injure yourself in ways that I never knew possible (like scorching your insides/boiling blood when moving energy around).  Don't get this book unless you're heavy into this very particular thing.  Not for the curious! Every sentence is bizarre to me.
