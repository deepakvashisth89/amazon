To say I am hooked on Maxim's books and particularly the Bannerman series would be to put it mildly.  I own his books in paperback and am now downloading and rereading on my Kindle.  These are not deep intellectual treatises.  They are well written escapism set in a Mass. town all of us would like to live in due the assured security maintained by Bannerman and his cohorts.  They are all entertaining and well drawn and even though they are more or less on the most wanted list of every government in the world all of us secretly would like to be one of them.  No one crosses one of Bannerman's people and survives to brag about it -- some are simply strongly admonished, others disappear.  If you want an evening spent with entertaining characters who you would secretly like to join, read the Bannerman novels.
