I highly recommend any Nathaniel Branden book. I think at this point I have read all of them (some twice). This one is a bit more philosophical than some of the later ones. It was his first book and sets the foundation for his life's work. Very important for those who want to better understand human psychology. His later works focus more on personal/self improvement with more examples of real people and with "steps" to follow. Not that this one isn't also personally useful.Read moreRead less
