The Little Rascals have been a favorite to me for a long time. I bought the Little Rascals DVD collection from a BEST BUY store a few years ago and I had the most fun time watching the episodes. I didn't know that there was a book about the Little Rascals until I saw a copy at the Shaker Heights Public Library. I wanted to purchase a copy to have right along with the DVD collection.I browsed AMAZON's website and saw that there were sellers who had copies of the OUR GANG: LIFE AND TIMES OF THE LITTLE RASCALS. I purchased a copy and received it in a timely fashion. I was happy with the book, because it gave complete details of every Little Rascals episode that was created. I thought it was only 88 episodes, as indicated in the DVD collection. But then, I realized that this was not true when I purchased the book and discovered that there were 221 episodes in all!I also liked the added extras in the book-the story of the Hal Roach Studios, brief information of the whereabouts of the main Little Rascals characters, and much more good stuff.The OUR GANG is a must for Little Rascals fans.
