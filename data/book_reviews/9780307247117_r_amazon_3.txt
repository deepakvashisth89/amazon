Most of the Fear Street Seniors Series books are kind of disappointing, but not this one. Phoebe Yamura is one of Stein's best Fear Street protagonists. The plot was clever and decently twisty. The dialogue and character development impressed me. The ending was pretty interesting. If I had one complaint about this book it's that it made me care too much about a character who Stein stupidly killed off in a later novel.Read moreRead less
