I'm not sure what prompted me to get the first book of this series, it was used and I don't even remember where I got it.  I finally started reading it and big surprise, I couldn't put it down.  I loved it so much I looked to the sequels.  One of the joys of children's books for me is many of the books lend themselves well to sequels and they don't let you down.  If I had to choose a favorite, I'd be lost.  I love them all.  This is also a good stand alone book, so if you didn't read the first one you won't be lost.I read children's books for two reasons.  One, they are easy reads and really even though I'm 51, I'm still a kid at heart. Two, I finally have time to write, but all those stories in my head have seemed to disappeared and I'm trying to jog my muse back into place.Wonderful book and I hope there will be more.
