James is an author very close to my heart. I realized that Daisy Miller had a scene at Chillon castle while visiting it. Thence my decidion to read this delightful short story. The character of Daisy is both endearing and masterfully depicted. Perhaps as well depicted are the stern, austere world of Calvinist Geneva and the superficial society of Rome where the end of the story takes place.
