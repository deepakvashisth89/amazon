I happen to like silent movies and anything old.  This is really old, over one hundred years old.  I did find the story a bit difficult to follow, it is almost as if the producers expected you to know the story already and that may very well have been the case.  I'm not very familiar with Oz books with Wizard of Oz movie being my primary reference.I really enjoyed the innovations in this movie, from walking up a wall, to having interesting creatures.  Very imaginative and creative were creatures such as the lions and tigers in Emerald City, and the Patchwork Girl herself.  Quite incredible acrobatics by the actor in that costume as well.  I was rather excited when I saw Scarecrow, Tin Man and Cowardly Lion in the Emerald City ... at last, characters I recognized.  I feel this must have taken place in the storyline of the world of Oz sometime after Dorothy had left since the 3 characters from Wizard of Oz were now in charge of the Emerald City.For a peak at history and a rather entertaining silent classic, this one is worth a look.
