Written in 1979 by a talented storyteller, Mr. Irving manages to make two merchants of death not only likeable but culturally forgivable. One Russian, Vasily Borgneff; one American, Eddie Mancuso. Both 007 Q types, invent Untraceable, Unusual,  Killing Devices. These two not only share a macabre talent and a desire to retire, they also share a girl friend. Retire? Easily? Nuh-Uh. they both know too much. These entertaining, even endearing "friend-enemies"  overcome a menagerie of assassin filled adversity and a common squeeze to help each other eliminate their respective dark teams in the quest for said peaceful retirement, to live the very quiet good life. Yeah right.Clifford Irving spares nothing in creating twists and turns as the two unite to aide each other. Suspend your disbelief and enjoy a light hearted tale of death, friendship and betrayal.
