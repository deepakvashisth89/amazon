Honestly, this book (and also the 3rd book, which I just finished) really do not deserve a 1-star rating, however I'm getting totally fed up with trying to pick books based on amazon reviews that are spammed with 5 star ratings by fanboys and friends of author. There is just no way this series has anything near the quality of a 4/5 star rating.The author does have talent, you will enjoy the characters, the plot is interesting, the action (when it occurs) is good and leaves you wanting to find out how things end...However!Books 2 and 3 are SO full of page after page after page of horrible re-hash and re-explaining of Everything, from the underlying science, to the macro history, to every previous character development, that it leaves you completey annoyed that you payed $7.50 for nearly 40% filler crap.The remaining 60%, sadly, is not all good either. This is one of those authors who will constantly pause any action/plot and fill 2-5 pages with boring background/history lessons. So this leaves you with basically skimming to dialog (you've come across these books before I bet). Doing this, I suspect you will enjoy the story...as long as you ignore how you've been sucked into buying 3 (not cheap) books that could easily have been condensed into one.If these books were $2.50 downloads, I'd probably give them a solid 3 star. As is, I'm ticked off that I allowed myself to get sucked in on the 2nd book, and that I allowed myself to think maybe the 3rd book would be different (its not, its even more re-hash).Read moreRead less
