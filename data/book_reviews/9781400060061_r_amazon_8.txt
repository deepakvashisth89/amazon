As a child I lived in a small town called Bangor,PA which is next to Roseto where the story takes place.  The names of other towns, people, stores etc were so familiar it was almost like reliving my childhood.  Trigani describes her characters so very well that they seem real and almost neighborly.  I loved following the central character from a small child to her demise knowing how a town of people cared and did for each other and for her.The quarry in Bangor still exists and was where my grandfather worked many years ago.  My sister still attends the Mt. Carmel Church in Roseto and my sisters and I always went to the "Big Time" which still takes place every summer.  I related well to this book but even as fiction, the book contains so many facts that it will be informative and entertaining to anyone who reads it.
