A murder mystery with lots of dry British humor. Set in the 1980s, it is the second novel narrated by Oxford don Hilary Tamar about adventures with several young London barristers. The style of narration is extremely witty and the dialogue includes lots of comedy of manners. I love the style but some readers may find it too wordy. I was not able to guess the solution which added to the pleasure. I suggest starting with the first book, Thus Was Adonis Murdered, to best enjoy the relationships among the central characters.
