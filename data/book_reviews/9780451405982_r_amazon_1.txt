Nice romp through the English countryside, on foot :>). Maxi and Robert were great, fully-realized characters and a nice addition to the series. I loved seeing couples from the other books again (though it has been a while since I read them, I remembered when they entered the story). I also loved the way the romance developed. I’m so glad Robert found someone to heal him. No stupid misunderstandings, and the mystery resolution was actually kind of sweet in a way.
