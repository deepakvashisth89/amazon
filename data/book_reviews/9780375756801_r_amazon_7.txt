I found this book fascinating. Not only was Darwin a botanist, but was also very well versed in Geology, and Anthropology. This is Darwin's account of his five year voyage around the world in HMS Beagle, (1832-1836) commanded by Capt. Fitzroy. Sometimes the scientific descriptions of plants and geology gets a bit 'heavy, but fortunately, there is much more of Darwin's descriptions and impressions of all the places and peoples he experienced along the way; more like a travel-log. I was amazed at how much was known during those times about geology and how that played into the evolvement of the earth's topography. The only thing that he seemed not to know of was plate tectonics, which came along much later. I learned so much about all the places he visited than I ever knew before. It's a great read for the 'armchair' traveler.  JC YuillRead moreRead less
