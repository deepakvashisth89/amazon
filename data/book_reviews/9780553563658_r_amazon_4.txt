"Shadows of the Past" is a short novel, written by Geary Gravel, that is set within the continuity of 'Batman: The Animated Series' (from the 1990's). It's only 180 pages in length, but is reasonably well-written, with an adult readership intended (although older children, age 14+ could easily follow the plot).The story concerns an attempt by criminal businessmen to knock down parts of old Gotham City - for re-development. Many of the existing residents don't want to leave, and the criminals have resorted to underhand tactics to force the situation. Yet one old lady finds out what's going on - and so she, Leslie Thompson, is kidnapped by the criminals. Little do they realise that Leslie has a friend - as, many years ago, she comforted a young boy - Bruce Wayne - after his parents were murdered. Now, as Batman, he seeks the villains who threaten this old lady ... Additionally, a gangster has returned to Gotham - to help with the 'renovation' project. He's the man who killed the Grayson family ... and Dick Grayson, now Robin, decides to track him down.This is an exciting story, with lots going on.
