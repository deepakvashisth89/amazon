I loved this book. Peregrine and Sara both had to deal with issues from their past. Peregrines revenge was very well weighted, not something superficial as is usual in romance novels, and it was interesting seeing how it unfolded. At first, Sara seemed to be just one more pawn in his revenge, but she became so much more to him - his companion, his friend, his lover, and ultimately his love. Peregrine slowly realized that revenge cannot change the past, and that there are other, more rewarding ways to heal his wounds.I absolutely adored this book. I loved both the hero and the heroine. I especially liked Peregrines directness, his way of asking questions when he wanted to know something, without a care about propriety and all the petty rules of etiquette. That way he made it possible to deal with some of the more difficult issues, instead of just ignoring them as some would like to do.This was a wonderful beginning of the series, and I can't wait to read the next book :)Read moreRead less
