If you want to understand how it felt to be a slave child, read this book.  With the dialogue and circumstances you feel as though you were there.  It is great to read aloud.  My mentee's teacher has used it to inspire her pupils to write their own diary.  Besides that there is much historical information about life during that time. The pictures that accompany the text help you picture the times and places.  Buy it for young children and others too.  I wonder how the author is able to do this. It's great.Read moreRead less
