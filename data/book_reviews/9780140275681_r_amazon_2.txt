William Haines was a big star in the silent move years. Very popular.  Made a lot of Money for MGM. This book gives a wonderful history of the man. His big secret at the time, he was gay. Hollywood at this time in history had a very active gay community. The studio system hid this fact from the general public.This book gives a fantastic picture of the 1920's Hollywood. The movies were starting to define fashion and culture. The movie stars were treated like living gods. Fan magazines fed the public the latest gossip. William Haines was part of this world.  Only in the 1930's did his star started to fade.  MGM lost interest in him and did very little to build his career up again. This book is well written and a must reading to understand Hollywood in the Silent era and the beginning of the sound era.Read moreRead less
