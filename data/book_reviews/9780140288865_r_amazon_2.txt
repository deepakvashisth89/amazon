This is one the all time great adventure stories and it is well told by Shackleton, for the most part. I greatly enjoyed it but did not find his writing as crisp and forward as in his earlier book regarding the Nimrod expedition.  At times I got the impression that he was seeking to head off criticism of his actions by quoting the diaries of others on the trip to make it seem as if each action was universally accepted by all. I think it would have been interesting to hear why he did not recommend four men for the polar medal and what they did to deserve that treatment, particularly Harry McNish who accompanied him on his open boat voyage to South Georgia Island.Of course these are just minor quibbles and it is a great read.
