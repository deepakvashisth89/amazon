Boy....it's hard to approach these books. Almost any Witch can tell you that these books really shouldn't be taken seriously, and that is true. So the best way to describe such fluffy bunny books is "amusement  factor." Face it, Llewellyn sold out long ago and started putting  these Fluffy bunny books out on the market to accomodate the young  wannabes, but I will give it this: they are fun to read. They are certainly  not helpful, but fun to read. Give it a try if you are looking for a book  to read that doesn't take the subject seriously.
