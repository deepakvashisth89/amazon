I have ME/CFS and know that I hyperventilate when I'm in a lot of pain. I didn't realise how many illnesses are affected by the way we breathe. This book is well written, and very informative, including different breathing techniques for eg asthma, stress etc. Since reading this I've found a very useful four minute Buteyko breathing exercise on Youtube. I do think a practitioner would be helpful to get started and am currently searching for one.
