I absolutely loved this! And I do not usually read this type of book either. I read the first book, which was worth the time, but this one really trumped the first.The book was interesting from beginning to ending, with a great storyline, and there was a nice flow, although the character Trent kept remotely transferring from the past to the future.This story reminds me of the film that starred Denzel Washington called Deja vu; only Denzel's character physically visited the past, whereas Trent visited the past via remote viewing and penetrating others' thoughts (or in Trent's case, even his own thoughts!) I wonder if this book was inspired by the film. Hmmm!I loved this book, and would highly recommend for adult readers.I must also mention that although this book had a few grammatical errors throughout, the issue was not as annoying as I have found with other e-books.Read moreRead less
