The town of Colony, West Virginia is, for lack of a better word, cursed.  Children have been disappearing for as long as folks can remember and yet for some reason people stay put and whistle dixie pretending nothing is amiss.  A young boy and his friends encounter the impetus behind the disappearances and they are forever changed.  One will never walk.  One will never truly be sane.  One will die.  Twenty years later fate and/or other mysterious forces bring one man back to town to quite literally face his demons.The story was interesting and engaging at times, but the pacing was off.  Usually in a horror novel, as the end approaches, you are frantically flipping pages to ascertain how the big-bad will be brought down, but as I drew near the 3/4 mark of this book, I just wanted it to be over.Read moreRead less
