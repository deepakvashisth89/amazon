This is a one-off volume edited by Martin Greenberg, and all the stories are about Superman.The editor had previously offered readers a series of volumes on Batman (see my other reviews). This book takes the same approach as those other volumes. It's a standard novel (not a collection of comics). It consists of several short stories, each by a different author. Some are very good, others less so. The book was first published in the early-to-mid 1990's - and it makes no attempt to fit within the continuity of DC Comics.I found it enjoyable to read stories about Superman, rather than always looking at comic books (which are good, but I prefer novels).
