This is a strange little book.  I love the premise - a lonely innkeeper with a small inn on a small island in the middle of nowhere comes up with the idea of trying to fill his place at Christmas time by placing an ad in several papers and promising to let those who come essentially ignore the holiday.  Come to my inn, he says, and you'll be isolate enough to pretend the holiday doesn't exist.  The ad succeeds in attracting an almost full house, although you can imagine the crowd attracted by such an ad - the lonely, the mad and the recently divorced.  This wonderful premise isn't really delivered upon, in my opinion.  The writing is okay at best - it reminded me of a creative writing student at a community college trying to hard to write like a novelist.  The author also sprinkles in (as I'm aware is her tendency) some ghosts, a murder plot and just some strange happenings.  Although I didn't love this book, I did like it, and I definitely kept reading to the end.  Worth a read.Read moreRead less
