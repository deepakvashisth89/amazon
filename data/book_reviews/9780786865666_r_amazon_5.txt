This is one of the most unusual and unique books (The book of answers by Carol Bolt) I have ever read.  The approach in this book begins with a one page explanation of “How to use the book of answers.” There are just five steps (1) Hold the book in your hand, on hour lap, or on a table.” (2)  Take 10 or 15 seconds to concentrate on your question. Questions should be phrased closed-end. (3) Ask one question (either speaking out loud or visualizing the question) at a time as you place one hand on the book’s front cover and stroke the edge of the pages, back to front.  (4) “When you sense the time is right, open the book and there will be an answer.”  (5)” Repeat the process for as many questions as you have.”This book reminds me of the novelty eight ball I remember as a young child where you would ask the ball a question and then turn it over for the answer. Now instead asking an eight ball you merely ask the book and discover the answer.  Each page has a single paragraph for an answer.  The following is a very small sample of answers this book contains: “Remove your own obstacles.” “It may be difficult but you will find value in it.” ‘Don’t ignore the obvious.” “Be patient.” “Take a chance.”  “Do it early.” “Gentle persistence will pay off.”I found this giant size hardcover book an interesting read.  This is the author’s first book.If you like novelty and “tongue in cheek” kind of books you may want to check out this volume.Rating: 4 Stars.  Joseph J.
