The author is to be congratulated for taking on a huge task: describing the events and trends of a century filled with enormous  changes. He maintains an excellent balance throughout, giving just as much emphasis to familiar areas, such as Western Europe, as he does to far-away places such as Africa and Asia. In addition to reporting incidents, he also explains their significance for the century as a whole. One possible improvement would be to shorten the look-back at the 19th-century that takes up the first 20% of the book.
