On the occasion of Mr. Corliss's passing, I feel compelled to note that this sadly under-appreciated, long neglected book is one of the best, most entertaining reads on screenwriters and screenwriting that's ever been in print. Obviously much has changed in the industry since its publication decades ago, but as a companion to Sarris' "American Cinema," it has never been surpassed. Corliss's canny, insightful explorations of great (and not as great) screenwriters are just as valuable now as ever. Maybe it'll finally get reprinted...?Read moreRead less
