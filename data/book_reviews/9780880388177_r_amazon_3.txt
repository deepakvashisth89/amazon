This is probably one of the first books written on how to be a better dungeon/game master.  At the time of first publication it would have been a treasure trove of knowledge but today it feels a bit dated.  Some of the advice feels like common knowledge and other bits, like those on world building, seem to go completely against modern advice (low prep).
