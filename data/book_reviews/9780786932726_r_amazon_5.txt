This book includes:- 3 new base classes: the Favored Soul, Shugenja, and Spirit Shaman- Waaaay too many prestige classes. The section on prestige classes is like 1/3 of the book.- A bunch of feats that are explicitly stated to be exclusive to Paladins, Clerics, Druids, and Prestige classes, and NOT to the base classes mentioned earlier.- Some info on advancing characters beyond level 20- Relics! But this info is only useful to dungeon masters, not players.- New deities, and new domains for pre-existing ones- A bit of reprinted Greyhawk stuff, because... reasons.- New spells for ALL caster classes, including the Arcane ones! (but not psionics)Although mostly intended for divine casters, and particularly Clerics and Favored Souls, this book has enough general material to make it useful even to players of non-divine classes. It does feel "complete" in the sense that it doesn't rely on other supplemental materials (like the Player's Guide to Faerun does...). I'd strongly recommend it for most players.Read moreRead less
