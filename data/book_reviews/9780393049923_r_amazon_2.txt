This Annotated Wizard of Oz (Centennial Edition) is a comprehensive and in depth exploration of the original children's book by L.Frank Baum. I have long been a fan of the 1939 Hollywood movie version of the story, and have read the original story many times before. However, this annotated version opens the "OZ" world to a whole new level.Initially, there are about 100 pages that deal with the history of Baum, the Oz book, the illustrations, the public reception, critical reviews, the stage and movie treatments, and where it stands today. Outstanding.Next, the true "heart" of the book, is the annotated facsimile of the original Oz book. After re-reading the book this time, along with the detailed annotation notes, it was a whole new experience. I have a new understanding of Baum, his surrounding world and his thought process in writing "The Wonderful Wizard of Oz".This annotated version is NOT a book a would I recommend for young child or a first time reader of Oz. However, if you have "traveled" to Oz few times before, and would like something more, this version will take you to places a general reader could never imagine.Read moreRead less
