I loved this series by Catherine Palmer. I wish this book were on the Kindle, but I wanted to finish up the series badly enough to go hunting for a very good used copy in paper. I wasn't disappointed. The story is about two very strong personalities coming together and often there are fireworks and other times explosions. They resist changing the way things have always been so that they can have the life God created them to have - and Mama Hannah is in the middle with her godly wisdom slowly bringing them to the place where they can hear His voice. A very good read you won't want to put down.Read moreRead less
