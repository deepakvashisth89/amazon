This is a very good story about the romance between Richard Banner and mathmatics professor Dr. Lucy Guerin after they meet two days before Christmas and he has to take her in as well as six other travelers trying to get home for Christmas stranded by an ice storm. Lucy is the only one who sticks around-obsiosly- once everyone else is on their way. There are new people introduced into the story after that mainly their families-especially his-but its mostly just them for the most part at that point.
