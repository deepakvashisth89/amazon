Originally written in the 1930s by Tolkien but not published until over half a century later, “Roverandom” is an interesting tale about a dog who finds adventure in the sea and the moon when he’s turned into a toy.While not as memorable as his Middle Earth works, it’s still a nice fable that does offer some of the same fantasy feels.
