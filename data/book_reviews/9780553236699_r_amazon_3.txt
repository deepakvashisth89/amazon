Set during WWII, this mystery finds series amateur detective Henry Gamadge off the scene during the early going, engaged in unspecified war work in Washington.  Awaiting his arrival, his wife Clara is trying to settle into a vacation rental in rural Connecticut.  She and her housekeeper are disturbed, though, by some rather spooky happenings and eventually a murder.  A varied and colorful cast of acquaintances and locals make up the list of suspects that, in the eyes of the authorities, includes Clara herself.  The mysterious goings-on and the murder are a puzzling muddle until Gamadge arrives and sorts everything out in a surprise ending.
