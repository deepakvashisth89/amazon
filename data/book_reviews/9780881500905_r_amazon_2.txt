Banbury Bog (Asey Mayo Cape Cod Mystery, #13) by Phoebe Atwood Taylor.An East Weesit Stayer is flowing in to cover the town in its dense fog. Soon it will be difficult for even Asey Mayo & Dr. cummings to find their way around the dirt trails. Tabitha sparrow has barely been able to make ends meet with her little B&B since her husband passed. She's waiting for the arrival of the Banbury's who have taken room & board at her Inn hopefully for longer than the usual stay.Phineas Banbury is hardly a man to sit idly by if he knows he's needed and can accomplish major changes. The small town of Weesit needs just that...major changes to bring a spark of life back into its neighbors. The first obstacle Phineas Banbury must hurdle is his daughter Jane. Weesit is not the type of town Jane wants to be stuck in for any length of time.So while Phineas Banbury begins to rebuild the town, someone else sets out to sabotage all his hard work.I so enjoyed the Asey Mayo sleuthing with the assistance of Dr. Cumming. Slowly as each clue is uncovered another suspect is eliminated. Highly recommended.Read moreRead less
