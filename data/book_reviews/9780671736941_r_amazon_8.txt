Jeffrey Archer is a great writer.  The Prodigal Daughter is one of his best.  This is a really long book.  There were times when I just could not put it down.  At the beginning there was no way of telling how the middle or end would turn out. I just kept reading to find out what would happen next. It leads into a story that is a reflection of the political times in today's world. You must read this book.Read moreRead less
