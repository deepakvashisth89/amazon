This book is perfect for summer: light, airy, and thoroughly enjoyable.  I read "Somebody Killed His Boyfriend" last summer and loved it.  I was not disappointed in my longing for Stukas' next mystery.  This book is pure fun, though sometimes in a very biting way, and so tongue-in-cheek it's amazing they don't stick together!  You will laugh out loud, sometimes hysterically, at the witty reparte and over-the-top characterizations.  Still, it can get you to thinking and there are many relateable elements in the story despite it's preposterous circumstances.  It's an easy read, only a handful of hours for a speed reader like me.I officially adore David Stukas and can't wait for next summer and the next mystery!Read moreRead less
