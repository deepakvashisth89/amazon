There was a little bit of everything in this book:  romance, action, war, grifters...I liked the story, didn't love it, and there were times when things dragged a bit.  Especially when the story veered off Cole/Devon, or had to do with uncle Monty.  The best parts were near the beginning when Cole and Devon were feeling each other out, trying to see if the other was trustworthy.
