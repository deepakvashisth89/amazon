The Diary of V series is wonderful!  I felt like I could have substituted "V" for "J" and the story was about me.  Her feelings and reactions to her husband, her love of men finding her attractive, her "crushes", and her friendships with the women of the story rang so true for me.  I just really related to the main character of this book.If you want to read the hilarious tale of a woman going through an affair, a cheating husband, raising a young son, and getting fat on top of that then this is the book for you.  You can relate to V and watch her come out victorious and overcome the hurdles in her life.  It will make you feel her pain and cheer for her when she succeeds.  Great read!Read moreRead less
