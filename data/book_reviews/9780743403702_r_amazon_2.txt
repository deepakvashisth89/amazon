Not a bad book. Worth reading, although as some readers have noted, the star character is NOT Leonard McCoy, as the cover suggests. It's also just as much a backstory of the origins of the people inhabiting Romulus and Remus than it is a story of the main character, a Star Fleet officer and undercover spy. As such, and considering the short length of the book, there's not a lot of plot or action here. There are fascinating details expounded and fleshed out regarding the mysterious enemies of the Federation, but not much else.Read moreRead less
