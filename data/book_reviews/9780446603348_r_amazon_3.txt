Fun series.  These books recount the rise and fall and rise again of a young graduate of the Helmsman academy.  Will not get in to the plot of these books, but must say after reading the first I bought the whole series and have enjoyed it and then re read it to enjoy again.  I can only hope that the author adds at least one more volume to the series tying up all the loose ends.
