It's refreshing to finally find a novel that tells you something new about  a city that you already know.  Fascinating is the journey back to the Twin  Cities' past, the view of Summit Avenue from a historical perspective.  Particularly unique is the foundation of traditional European fairy tales  on which the novel is based and the lyrical style which envelopes it.  Unlike many historical novels, the strong points of Summit Avenue include a  fast-paced plot, believeable, larger-than-life characters and a surprising  story line.  This is not your usual sappy historical romance novel, but a  strong story still full of contemporary relevance.
