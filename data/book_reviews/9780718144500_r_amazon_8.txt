I think this is the 4th (or 5th?) Nicci French title I've read and it follows the familiar pattern of a first-person female narrator who is somehow in peril (stalker, kidnapper, etc.). Formulaic, yes, but winningly so. I enjoy them because they work. They are well written and taut and fulfill the mandate of every thriller to be a page-turner.And this one was a little different than previous titles I've read: there are 3 different female narrators who are all voiced clearly and well.
