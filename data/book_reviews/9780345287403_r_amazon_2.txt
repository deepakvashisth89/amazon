Magda MacDougall has little of her Chinese mother in her, and a whole lot of her Scottish father. While she retains her Chinese heritage and relations, her flaming red hair and business acumen are better suited to a western life style. She has three men in her life, one who loves her, but marriage to a Eurasian is not the *done thing* and she settles on marriage to a German citizen with little fondness for Hitler. Magda's new husband comes with a most unwelcome visit from the homeland, and that leads her into a dangerous game of foreign intrigue with one of Britain's intelligence agents.OK, I love WWII and I've been looking for more Asian settings so I had fairly high hopes for this one, but it was just OK. It took a while for things to get really cooking, but then the plot twists towards the very end just got a bit too far fetched in the only-Magda-can-do-it-alone realm.
