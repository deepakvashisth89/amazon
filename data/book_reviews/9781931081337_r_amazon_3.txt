I had the privilege of growing up next to a wood.  I would cross over the ditch and enter my personal paradise.  There is just something about being in a forest with the wind soughing in the pine boughs.  There is peace there, peace, and a sense of wonder and possibility.  I felt something similar when I read this book.Sarah Jane has gone to help her aunt with the chores.  She found a tiny injured man one day, called her aunt to help him.  I loved how Sarah Jane could look at what seemed to be a clump of grass and twigs, and then look again and see a tiny man.  This act of compassion embroiled them in the affairs of faery, which rarely ever turns out well for humans.I highly recommend this book and the author's other books to anyone who still loves faery tales.Read moreRead less
