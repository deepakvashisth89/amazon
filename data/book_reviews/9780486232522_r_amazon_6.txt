The time frame in which this series were created is astonishing and quite possibly this is what makes it such a mesmerising and theatrical journey. It has a frenetic relentlessness to it. The cohesive distinction of works created uninterrupted is clearly present. Ernst mined, arguably, near contemporary references and his unbound imagination for all the elements to this imagery (with some examination (of originals) there might be the detection of his own line to complete an effect but...I can only guess this maybe the case without access to the originals). He created such a sound world with seamless proportional interiors and exteriors populated by freakish and wonderful creatures the 'reader' is absorbed by it - 'belief' is suspended. The images, pretty generously printed in this affordable publication, are visually overwhelming. Some works so intricate they have the detail and impact of a Durer etching.
