This collection by theologian and author Molly Wolf, taps even deeper into the mystery of human suffering and pain than her previous books.  In these beautifully-written essays, Wolf discusses tragedy, post-traumatic disorder, jealousy, and other issues that rip the human spirit.  Wolf doesn't spare herself or her readers - at times the imagery is very, very powerful and quite graphic.
