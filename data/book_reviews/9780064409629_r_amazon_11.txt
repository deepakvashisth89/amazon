I decided to try and read all of the original Oz books in order and have just finished this (book three). It was a really good read. They genuinely have got better from book to book so far. It is really interesting to see how the stories grow from each other and get more in depth. The second book was noticeably darker than the first one and this one feels more like it had a stronger plot than going from one small adventure to another. I've heard that perhaps because of the author's life, the last few books get darker and I am very interested to see how they progress.On a side note, the 'Return to Oz' film takes heavily from this book (and a bit from the second).
