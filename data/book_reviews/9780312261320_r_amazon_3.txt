This isn't a bad book, and is quite good for a first effort from this author.  It certainly isn't your normal romance story, and there is a definite feeling of hope that comes through.  It is a story about two very strong-willed people and how they clashed at first, and then found out that they complimented each other.  It's also a good view of the Jehovah Witness faith, and let's face it, there's not many books about that.  The writing is good, and the story moves along nicely.
