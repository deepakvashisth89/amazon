In my childhood, I remembered reading this book in my mothers bookshelf. This was her favorite book and I loved reading it too. Sadly I brought it with me to church and I let another kid read it too, but he ended up tearing it apart, leaving my mom and I sad and disappointed. She never held it against me though as I was just we were children and she loved us more than the book. Fast forward to last year and the memory of this book came into my mind and I thought this would be a great gift for her. When I presented it to her, she loved it and was overjoyed to see and read her favorite comic book again. I even enjoyed reading it again and laughing at bits that flew over my head as a child.
