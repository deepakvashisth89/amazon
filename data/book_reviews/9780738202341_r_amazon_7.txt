This is an excellent book for those who are interested in both  physics and the meditative arts. Meditation is inwardly focused, and self directed. This book attempts to provide a quantum physics perspective on what is actually taking place within the body during the normal living process. The first 60% to 70% of the book reviews the physics. This material is accessible to non-physicists and is essential background to the understanding of the latter part of the book, which focuses on consciousness and the mind. The challenge for some readers, who may be primarily interested in the meditative arts, is to stay with the physics in the first part of the book. Those who do will be rewarded with further insight , unlikely to  be attained through meditative practices alone. This is the first book that I have discovered that attempted to provide scientific evidence on the physical location of the mind, and how the mind operates. Unfortunately, the current state of knowledge in quantum physics does not provide a complete, definitive theory of the mind and conciousness. This book provides insight into these  subjects, and  will definitely add perspective to the practice of meditative arts; but it is not yet able to tell the full story.
