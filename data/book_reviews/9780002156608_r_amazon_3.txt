Sometimes it is nice to re-read something that meant a lot during a moment in human history. As I re-read this book, I not only remembered those days but the similarities between then and now were eye-opening. Written by a world leader, Mikhail Gorbachev shares not only his vision of what the Soviet system should be but also takes the past of his nation to task.
