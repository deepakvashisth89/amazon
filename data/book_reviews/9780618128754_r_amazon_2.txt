Faderman has contributed so much to the general history of LGBTQ people, particularly lesbians, that it was delightful to read her own coming-of-age story, starting with her life as a child being raised by her immigrant  Mom and aunt, and ending with the ascent of her professional career as an academic and writer.  She gives us a first-person glimpse into lesbian life in the bad old days--the 50s and 60s--and also documents her struggle to become her own person while still maintaining her strong ties to her Jewish family and heritage.
