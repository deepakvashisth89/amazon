I've greatly enjoyed Tarr's other work, and this book features her trademark, gorgeous lyricism of prose. It's slow to open, but rewarding once you get past the set-up. A book heavy on politics, religion, mysticism, and myth; also contains occasional visits from empathic sabertooth cats, which cannot help but please. Very satisfying.Fair warning, though: lots of people die in this book. Be prepared!Read moreRead less
