T.R. Pearson is part of the  resurgence of southern writers that came to national prominence in the 80's. Like Lee Smith, his books use quiet humor and capture the feel and language of the Southeast, particularly of the area shared by North Carolina and southern Virginia. Both Pearson, as far as I know, and Smith are still writing, but these books from the 80's are a great introduction and a pleasure to read. Recommended, of course.Read moreRead less
