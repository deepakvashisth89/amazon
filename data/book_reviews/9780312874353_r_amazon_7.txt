Not bad for Mr. Sherbaniuk's first novel.  I had a few groans at the awkward writing style and some laughs at the many terrible similes e.g. "Her eyes burned like violet laser beams through a plume of cigarette smoke" and "He squinted up into the sky, glowing like an inverted enamel blue bowl."  Many of the characters were also either cliché or not very believable.Alas, the story's theme of Genetically Modified Organisms for warfare is compelling and a frightening scenario is presented.  We are left with the question of not if, but when something like this will happen.  In an unstable world we live in today, I shudder at the possibilities.Hopefully the sequel, The Devil's Casket, will be published.  It was due to come out in 2004, but I've heard nothing else on it.
