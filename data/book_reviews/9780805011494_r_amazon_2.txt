This is a very intricate plot. It starts with Chief Inspector Henry Tibbett agreeing to help his friends on the Caribbean island of Tampica defeat a plan to make Tampica a stopover for drug traffickers. This feels rather contrived.And it only gets more complicated. The good guys get doubled crossed. The bad guys almost win out.  There’s a murder, and Henry almost gets murdered too. His wife Emmy escapes from one danger after another. It’s a mishmash of misadventures. The story is saved from its convoluted plot by its very engaging characters. But as crazy as things got, I read to the end, so that says something."The White Girl” is island slang for cocaine.The earlier Tibbett books are better than this, but I’m determined to finish the series.
