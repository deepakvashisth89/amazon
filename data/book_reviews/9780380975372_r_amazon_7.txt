Too bad this book is out of print and not available in e-form.  It covers three topics:  How to keep people out of radioactive dumps for 10,000 years, how to send a message about us beyond the solar system for others to find, how to save the planet, especially endangered species, but also considering climate change.The author mainly writes science fiction, but is a working scientist.  He is easy to read.  His ideas were radical when first proposed, but now may seem old hat to some.  People have made fun of the WIPP proposals for the sequestering of material near Carlsbad NM.  Others have made fun of his discussion of geo-engineering to solve climate troubles.  But he is worth reading to bring people up to speed on the issues.Read moreRead less
