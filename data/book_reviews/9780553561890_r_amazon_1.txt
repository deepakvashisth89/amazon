If you haven't read the preceding novel, you should. Although this does cover enough of it to sketch out the situation, there is a lot more there than summarized here.The story continues: the Star Wolf is still in purdah, isolated from other ships, as it tries to decontaminate itself and is taken out of the Big Push against the Morthans. However, it has a Morthan Imp on board, and when the XO asks himself what it is trying to accomplish, the answer may just be: to take advantage of the Big Push to locate and destroy the key military base in the area.There is a tour of the ship with a new crew member on a snipe hunt, and there is background on the XO and his (now deceased, thanks to the Morthans) family, and outwitting an Imp turns out to be almost impossible.Very much worth reading.Read moreRead less
