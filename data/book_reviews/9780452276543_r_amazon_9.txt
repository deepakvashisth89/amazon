I bought this book because it said in the product description that it had recipes for quinoa and other grains. I really wanted some quinoa recipes but there are only 3-4 recipes for quinoa and amaranth. A lot of the recipes are very similar were ingredients are concerned so the author has really tried to eke the recipes out to have 366 recipes, most recipes have black beans and salsa in them.The author is American and she loves cornbread there are so many recipes for cornbread and similar corn based items, I just want to say if you haven't tried cornbread trust me you haven't missed a thing. Most of the bean recipes are for burrito and chili type meals.  Also the portion sizes are again American portion sizes and we all know how slim they are in the US.I have got some American cookbooks and there are a few that are good, this is not one of them.Read moreRead less
