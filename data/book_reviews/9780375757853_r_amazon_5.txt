The Moonstone by Wilkie Collins has been referred to as the first modern English detective novel. It is unique in form , being narrated by no less than 7 characters. In this way Collins enabled the reader to see events from varying points of view. The effect of this is not disunity of form as you might suspect but instead a gradual buildup of suspense that grips the reader and provides numerous thrilling plot twists. This is a Victorian era novel ( Wilkie Collins was a close friend and associate of Charles Dickens) and must be read in that light as a work of historical importance and a classic even if it may not be as well known as the works of his famous friend.The plot revolves around a theft at a Yorkshire state and the story moves between Yorkshire and London as a variety of characters come under suspicion and an equal variety of people either attempt or think they have identified the guilty party. There is enough intrigue in this novel to satisfy any fan of mystery writing and enough quality of writing and depth to satisfy any lover of great literature.Wilkie Collins may now be best remembered as the author of THE WOMAN In WHITE but The Moonstone is reputed to be his masterpiece so I chose to start with this and truly enjoyed reading it.Read moreRead less
