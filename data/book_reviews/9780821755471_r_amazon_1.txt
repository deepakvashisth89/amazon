Shadow and Silk, The Secret Sisters, The Diamond Tiger, and The Ruby...All are written with the same mixture of intense attraction, exotic settings, and a unique plot.  Shadow and Silk is so well researched that you can  easily imagine yourself as Dani Warren, who meets the larger than life  ex-mercenary Shane Crowe on a roof top in Tibet. It was amusing (and  slightly frustrating) to learn that Shane has taken a vow of Celibacy...But  don't worry...the vow ends near the end of the novel.
