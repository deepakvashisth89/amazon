I thoroughly enjoyed this story.  A young couple, after purchasing an old house on the cape, begin to notice that the people in their town do not seem to like them.  They have noticed intruders on their property at night and shots rang out on one occasion.  Their uncle, who had an adventure of his own before returning to the house, two invited house guests and one uninvited house guest quickly become embroiled in more mysterious happenings.  Of course, Asey Mayo has been called in to assist as only he can.It is a typical Asey Mayo story and that is a compliment not a criticism.
