This is a very funny book.  That it is brilliant goes without saying; what Gore Vidal isn't (except maybe Myra Breckenridge, which is kind of revenge porn embedded in a greatest hits collection of his essays).  It deals with the apocalypse with greater insight than Kalki, a less good book but still worthwhile.  It is almost impossible to stop laughing--almost every third sentence is a killer punch line.  I would put this among his five best books.  Which is saying a lot.  Not only is it funny, but it gets at the core of the American sub-psyche as well as the best of his essays, which this book does not incorporate except in spirit.  I love his essays, too; just not inside of his fiction.Oh.  Upon rereading, I realized that the snappy non-sequiturs of the dailogue are patterned upon a movie comedy he admired, Airplane!  'm not kidding.  Check it out!Please keep this book in print and read by many--recommend it to friends!Read moreRead less
