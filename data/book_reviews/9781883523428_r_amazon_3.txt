It is interesting how so many people can feel so differently about the same book. I loved this book. My book club loved this book. It is a great story of female friendship and the journeys these particular women went through. I think it shows us the importance of female friendship without having to be anything sexual. I admit there was some sexual lesbian parts, but it was a small part and who cares...nothing X rated. I did not find it to be "male bashing" as another review stated. It just makes the point that women understand women more than any man can understand a woman.As far as the writing, I did not find it "excruciating" or "amateur". My book club and I found the writing to be easily related to and real. We are not a bunch of back woods illiterates...we are educated women. I try not to read a book as an editor or you will be very unhappy with books very often.I highly recommend this book to any woman over the age of 25 who has a warm heart and a sensitive side.
