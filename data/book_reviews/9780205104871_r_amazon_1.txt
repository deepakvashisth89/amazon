Hands down the BEST theatre history textbook EVER WRITTEN. Brockett is a legend in the world of theatre studies. The textbook is very informative- sometimes so much that it can seem overwhelming. But the narrative element is strong in this text and Brockett weaves together the time periods covered very well so that you not only get facts but a story of the history of theatre. The sections on Asian theatre, African theatre and theatre of the New World sort of have the feeling of an afterthought, which I hope future editions will improve upon (unfortunately, this is not unusual for theatre history textbooks and Brockett's text does handle it much better than others!). I would highly recommend for not only undergraduates but graduate students who are reviewing for comprehensive exams (that's why I purchased it!).
