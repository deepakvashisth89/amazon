This is the story of Ubu Roy and Beautiful Maria raised as brother and sister by Pasco though none of the three are biologically related.  The book opens with Pasco committing suicide leaving his ship Runaway in the hands of his children.The life of independent shippers is coming to an end as the big shipping conglomerates are quickly taking over.  But fortune is perhaps smiling on the two siblings as they encounter an alien ship and their lives are forever changed.This is a well-written story which employs an interesting vernacular.
