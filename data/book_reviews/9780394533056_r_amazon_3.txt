Reading this 1946 book by Albert Camus along with the 2013 book "The Meursault Investigation" by Kamel Daoud was enlightening.  The latter novel takes up where "The Stranger" left off and explores the person who was killed on the beach in "The Stranger".  That person had not been given a name nor was his family discussed.  He was only referred to as 'the Arab'.  In "The Mersault Investigation", we hear from the younger brother of the Arab.  Both are works of fiction and extremely well written.
