A beautiful recounting of the love between Mary and Joseph, and the birth of baby Jesus.This book starts with Mary and Joseph before their betrothal, and introduces family on both sides. We see their love and their courtship, along with both the blessings and trials they endured when Mary's pregnancy becomes apparent.
