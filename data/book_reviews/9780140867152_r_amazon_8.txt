I really enjoy the first book, even though some reviews said the first one was rather boring, but I still enjoyed it. Now picking up the second book...well, it really pulled me in the series.When things started to happen, I thought the book was going away what the first book hold, but that wasn't the case. The book opens up with Roland where he was at the very end of the first one: the beach. But the second book delievers its action pack right off with the gunslinger fighting for his life...only losing two fingers and a toe. Then Roland begins his Dark Tower quest again, this time discovering strange doors that will help him pull people out of he world...and one he must kill.This was where I thought the series would loose what the first book held, but once again, King's one heck of a storyteller who knows what he's doing! Each door (three of them) allows Roland to enter and pull the "host" for his ka. Each of these characters are different, but King made them well developed, what's their problems, and how they would help Roland in the future.
