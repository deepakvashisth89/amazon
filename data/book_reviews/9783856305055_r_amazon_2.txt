This is an amazing book and is essential to anyone who is serious about their spiritual journey. Indeed, pick it up, read a few pages, and you will be drawn into and along with the journey of four young adults during World War II growing into their spiritual illumination and union with God as a new paradigm for the God-Human relationship. For me it was also a compelling and emotional journey as I traveled along their tragic and wonderful path that brought me to a richer and more profound understanding of my own purpose and role in this life.
