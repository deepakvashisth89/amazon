This is not your typical book. Much of it is written as an account based on research done for a doctoral thesis, not necessarily in 3rd or 1st person. I am not an aspiring writer, or screen writer, historian, feminist or a person interested in my Swedish heritage. But, if you are interested in any of these topics this book is a must read.I find it sad that this story has been "swept under the rug" and is only celebrated by a few chapters of The Sisters Of Sweden, once a year. A woman and her daughter walking across America and facing the perils they confronted at the turn of the century should be a bigger story or at least a legend, and nearly no one knows about Helga Estby and her daughter Clara. The under-tones of the difficulties of life as an immigrant also resonate in todays society as the nation still struggles with this touchy topic.This book would make a decent made for TV movie, or even silver screen film if done correctly and either of these media outlets would be the ideal way for this story to be told to more Americans.
