I had owned this book before and it's a great book. Ehrenzweig was an underrated art critic and psychologist who expanded the notion of art behavior and artists' modus operandi. So for content, I would give this book 5 stars. However, the edition I received from Amazon was awkward: Somehow all the pages have been shifted in the printing and the even-numbered pages appear as right hand pages and the odd numbered ones as left hand pages. Although the copyright lists University of California Press as the publisher, 1967 and then 1997, there is a note on the very last page saying: Made in USA, Lexington, KY, 21 August 2018.
