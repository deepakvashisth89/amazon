Susie Bright rocks.  I love the diversity in style reflected in this book, and also throughout the series.  The well-written erotic short story is finally earning its respected place in literature.The subject matter Ms. Bright chose for this volume strikes me as quite physical (well, duh). In other words, it doesn't delve terribly deeply into the psychology and complexity of sex, at least not the deep, difficult aspects of it.  Instead, it sticks more with a celebration of the physical, the erotic turn-ons, the encounters, the shock value and exploration, the acts themselves.Not that this is a problem, mind you; Susie Bright is my kind of editor, and it's obvious she loves her work.  It's a wonderful anthology, and provides a lovely companion to magazines like _Nerve_.My only frustration is that the book is rather thin!Read moreRead less
