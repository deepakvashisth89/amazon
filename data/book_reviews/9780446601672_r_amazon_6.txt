Barton isn't a bad writer, but his narrative style is confusing and it's hard to find something to like in any of his characters. He uses alternating 1st person without really letting you know he's jumping. He also uses moves back and forth to and from 3rd person.I found the book an annoying read, though the premise was intriguing.In the hands of a different writer and different characters, it might have been more engaging. As it was, I had to push myself through the book and found myself grateful to have finally reached the end.Read moreRead less
