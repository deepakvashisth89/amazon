An ideal introduction for a non-Catholic spouse. Contrary to wehat you might think from the cartoon format, it is not at all patronising. When I started reading it, I actually found it quite engaging and felt the urge to keep turning the page, inspite of being a relatively well-educated Catholic. It actually gives one quite some good ideas on how to pitch concepts at non-believers. And the cartoon format makes it fun and informal and not too preachy, and in a way feel like it isn't even an imposisiton on one's time, almost recreational. Personally, I think this is best used by a non-Christian, or an atheist spouse, since it starts from scratch, but a Protestant spouse will definitely also benefit, especially in the absence of any alternative medium to my knowledge. You will be surprised at how concepts that we take for granted are very difficult even to introduce to a non-Catholic. An example is that the physical Universe had a beginning (the Big Bang), the scientifically proven fact that prior to this was nothing. Nothing physical, that is, but all things spiritual and Eternal. Many think that the Universe is eternal or that there was physics even before the Big Bang.
