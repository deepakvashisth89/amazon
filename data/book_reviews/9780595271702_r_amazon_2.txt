I've now read 8 of 16 "sections" (12 "topics" and 4 "Wicca in Practice" sections), and the quality of the material is every bit as good as in her "Wicca 334" (which I bought and read first due to its scarcity).The very first topic is on The Wiccan Rede -- an exposition which SHOULD have been up-front in the Wicca 101 I started to take, but it was not.  In any event, the exposition here is exquisitely explained.  I feel that I could safely teach the topic in a 101 from just the material in this 333.  Every other topic is similarly treated (although, occasionally, she becomes didactic).Not being initiated myself, it is hard to recommend when a novice should read it.
