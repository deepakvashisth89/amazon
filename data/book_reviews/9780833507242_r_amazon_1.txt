This is a great book for young African Americans boys to identify with.  There are not many books left that can keep the readers attention and focused on the stories that Julian tells. The life of Julian is more realistic to black families.  Although, the author is white, her books are intriguing and funny and African American readers enjoy the life of Julian.  I have purchased all of Ann Cameron’s books with African American characters and a few audible cd on Audible.
