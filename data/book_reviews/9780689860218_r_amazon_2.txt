I liked the story quite a bit. The author writes well, I found no typos or point-of-view shifts. Nothing to complain about. It was an easy read; the author didn't send me to the dictionary twenty times. I had trouble putting the book down, which means it was a page turner. The excitement was kept high as he showed the point of view of a German youth anxious to get into WWII and an equally young America small-town boy.
