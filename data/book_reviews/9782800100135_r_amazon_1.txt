This is a great book with wonderful artwork by Franquin.  He is known in Europe as one of the greatest comic artists of all time, and unfortunately his art has not been translated into English for most Americans and English to enjoy.  If you are lucky enough to teach yourself French as I have to enjoy such immaculate work then you will love this book.
