Quite by accident I discovered the book "Mountain Home; the Wilderness Poetry of Ancient China" translated by David Hinton.  That got me started on a search of nature and Zen poetry of China and Japan.  So far I have read at least a few dozen books and this is my favorite!  The anthology covers from 4th century to modern and has quite a few authors represented. The sensitive translations are what, I am sure, attracts me to re-read and highlight this book.
