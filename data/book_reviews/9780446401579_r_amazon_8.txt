In the well-paced story, Lights Out, Abrahams chose a wrongfully imprisoned, vengeful murderer nicknamed Nails, as the hero. Bouncing from present facts to flashbacks to the past helped me understand and sympathize with Eddie Nye, aka Nails, and make predictions about the outcome. Abrahams' characterizations of Nails is rich, but his brother Jack lacks any redeeming qualities. Nails extracts revenge before he leaves prison against prisoners that picked on him, giving me a clue that his easygoing likeable qualities might not hold up. When he eventually emerged, looking younger and more fit than his outside colleagues, he found his errant brother. Nails seemed dumb, and maybe for that reason I wanted him to solve the mystery of why he went to prison, and get the good-looking woman. Abrahams surprised me in the end.
