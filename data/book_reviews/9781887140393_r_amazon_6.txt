I have traveled all over the world and I find Joyce Brooks' book very humorous, easy to read and refreshing.  Both the novice and experienced traveler can benefit from reading about her experiences.Experienced travelers will nod and smile in agreement at having been through similar situations.  The novice traveler can be forewarned of possible hitches while traveling and know that it will turn out OK if everyone stays calm.I especially enjoyed how Joyce was able to resolve every situation by knowing when to stand her ground and when to give in, most often with humor.A very amusing and informative book which even the armchair traveler will enjoy.Read moreRead less
