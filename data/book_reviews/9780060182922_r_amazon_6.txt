Thanks to someone on AMAZON for suggesting Marianne Wesson.  I like courtroom drama and legal story books and RENDER UP THE BODY, book one in the Lucinda Hayes series is a good one.  I point to the book itself, it follows the case from August 1989 until the book ends in the summer of 1993. I don't recall many reads that are that authentic.
