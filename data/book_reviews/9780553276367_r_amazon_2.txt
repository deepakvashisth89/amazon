I loved this book and steamed through it. However, I would have plotted it differently so that there is not such heavy tragedy. The character development is wonderful, New Mexico landscapes evocative and as stark as New Mexico is in those areas. I am puzzled by Zollinger's subjecting his male characters to living near a loved one but not having her (both here and in Riders to Cibola). But I can't ask him, unfortunately.
