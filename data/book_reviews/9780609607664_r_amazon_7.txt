I LOVED this book and wish I could read others just like it except with different stories.  :D  It was very descriptive and well written, and I think I learnt a lot about very random things through it.  For example, I have a lot of knowledge about Mennonites compared to (more than) the average person, but I'd never heard about certain things written about in Batavia's Graveyard.  It gave me a newly rounded out history.  The background given is wonderful for learning about the era.  The entire book is full of intriguing details and information, and the description of sailing was… horrifically wonderful.  Then you get to the drama too.  I'd not even care about the part of the book telling that part of the story, because the rest is so good just as a history book!  The gruesome and sad portion is still an intriguing read.Read moreRead less
