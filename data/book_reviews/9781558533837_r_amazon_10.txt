Back in high school, I loved the show and never missed it. My own buddies and I would hang out at Tim Horton's, our own version of Central Perk. Fast forward 20 years, and now we all hang out at my house...lol.I thought it would be cool to make some stuff from the book and remember the good old days. I was expecting a simple kitschy kinda book, but wow oh wow, these recipes rock.Jack bishop worked for cook's illustrated, so he really knows his stuff.The recipes are awesome! They vary from super easy to sophisticated-looking, but all are doable for everyone.The best are the brownies, phoebe's oatmeal cookies, and cappuccino biscotti. Monica's recipes are more sophisticated (she was a chef, after all). I LOVE the onion tartlets, and white bean dip. Plus the comfort food section is fabulous, with Mac and cheese and lasagna.I was really surprised with the quality of the recipes, and the photos of the sitcom that brings back awesome high school memories. I have a lot of cookbooks, but I am really glad I bought this. Even second hand, it was in pristine shape. Really, don't miss out.
