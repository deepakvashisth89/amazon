I've been a fan of The "Expert" range of books since I was a kid (just to show how accessible these books are), the first one being a copy ofThe Home Expert (Expert books)I picked up at a Garden centre near to the Camelot Theme Park in Lancashire when I was on Holiday there back around 1987/88. The family collection has since expanded to owning "The Garden Expert", "The House Plant Expert", "The Flower Expert", "The Tree & Shrub Expert", "The Lawn Expert" + more recentlyVegetable and Herb Expert.I recently bought this "The Home DIY Expert" as part of a shopping spree to expand the collection, and have just had a flick through it. The flick through suggests that this "The Home DIY Expert" is pretty much exactly the same asThe Home Expert (Expert books), down to the photographs & illustrations inside - except there's a few bits cut out from it... the bit at the start with money management advice + the bit in the "inside the house" section with advice on livingroom / kitchen / diningroom / bedrooms / bathroom, etc.So, if you already haveThe Home Expert (Expert books), I wouldn't bother buying it.If you own / rent a house (or are about to), and you don't haveThe Home Expert (Expert books), then I strongly recommend buying "The Home DIY Expert", as it has absolutely vital information in it.
