A well written tome about one of the most significant voyages in the Age of Discovery.  The fact that Darwin was studying to be a minister and was only brought aboard the Beagle as company for Captain Fitzroy is not commonly understood by most.  Darwin’s discoveries in the Galapagos was almost accidental and he withheld publishing his findings until another anthropologist was about to publish similar findings.
