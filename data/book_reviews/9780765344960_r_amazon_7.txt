An ambitious space opera from this competent writer.  Like several of MacLeod's books, this one mixes space opera with a somewhat unusual political sensibility derived from the author's experience with Trotskyism.  Set in an imaginative universe with a variety of organic and inorganic intelligences, humans are relatively minor players in galaxy dominated by more intelligent species.  To a considerable extent, these books are about human efforts to escape being locked into a defined social order.  This is a recurrent theme in MacLeod's books, often presented in the form of rather out there political movements.  These books feature a relatively complicated plot, whose dimensions are apparent only after reading the final book.  The books have to be read in order and these are not stand alone novels.
