This is the first Lisa Cach book that I have read and I was very impressed with her writing style, characters, and plot.  Lisa Cach cetainly knows how to weave a wonderful story and really bring it to life!  The only reason I have taken 1 star away from my rating of this book is because this book just wasn't for me.In specific I didn't like the constant reference to "The Hand of Destiny", it got sickening.  The red kingfisher that "led" Anne to the island and helped her companions find her when she was lost was just too unrealistic for me.  And finally, I didn't like the setting either.  I guess jungles and strange native peoples just aren't my thing for a romance novel.But I cannot say this was a bad book simply because of these things.  This book just wasn't for me, but I can certainly see how this could be a wonderful book for those who would enjoy such a setting!  Definately a book worth picking up if you think you'd enjoy it!Read moreRead less
