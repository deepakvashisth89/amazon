A very compact paperback! But I'm sure to enjoy the translated plays included in it. And some commentary by Moses Hadas, historian and interpreter of all things ancient-Greece. I have read his book on the singular god, Dionysus, so many times it's wearing out. In at least one of the plays, Dionysus is a speaking character ("Frogs" by Aristophanes) who travels with his slave-companion to Hades, a partly-amusing plot.
