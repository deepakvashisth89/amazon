I ordered this version of A Christmas Carol based on the fact that the illustrations are by Arthur Rackham. I have a couple of versions of this esteemed story but one of the versions lacked the authentic feel and written words. Coming into possession of this Hardcover book is a treasure. The size of the book 7"x10" and 131 pages, is a comfortable weight and handle. The paper is a good weight, heavy enough to convey the print size and ink, making the read, enjoyable. This version is a republication of work from 1915, the reader is transported by the language to another time, a harsher time in the lives of so many and a reminder that we still have a way to go in our treatment of others and our lack of humanity.The most important aspect are the words conveyed by the second of the three Spirits before he leaves Scrooge and as his time on earth ends. He presents two children. The boy is Ignorance. The girl is Want. If you know the story, you cannot help but be moved by how this description has evolved over time and applies to our current world. It is telling in its prophecy. I heartily recommend this version of A Christmas Carol.Read moreRead less
