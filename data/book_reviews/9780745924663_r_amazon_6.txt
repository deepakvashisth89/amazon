I picked up the Kindle version of this book when Amazon made it free for a limited time, and I'm so glad they did (it worked out for Amazon too, because I purchased the other two books as soon as I finished this one).  I had kind of fallen out of reading for pleasure and just bought the Kindle.  This was my second book I read on it and I loved it so much that I've been reading like crazy ever since.  Lawhead totally pulls you into the world he creates.  The characters, environment, battles, and problems are all well developed and you end up totally engrossed.
