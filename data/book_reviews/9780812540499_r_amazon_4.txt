I was interested because this battle in New Mexico represented the only real effort by the South in the Civil War to reach out to the western US by military means. Not much is known today about the victory of the Colorado defenders over the Texas attackers, and I was curious. Now, I know more than I did, and I don't need to read any more books about it ,unless one comes along more interesting than this one. The book appears to be reasonably accurate and is readable.
