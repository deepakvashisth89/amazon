John Updike is a 20th Century author, winner of two Pulitzer Prizes for two books in the "Rabbit" series, "Rabbit is Rich" and "Rabbit at Rest."  "Rabbit Run" and "Rabbit Redux" are first two in the series and one should read "Rabbit Run" before reading "Rabbit Redux." They are an intimate portrait of the life and times of Harry Angstrom (nick-named Rabbit), who lives in the the early to mid-20th Century.  Why is Harry's life of interest?  Because Updike's unique ability to put words together that convey the depth and beauty of ordinary lives and ordinary surroundings is captivating.  I felt very involved in Harry's life and the lives of his family and friends and couldn't wait to get back to it after having to put it down for a while.
