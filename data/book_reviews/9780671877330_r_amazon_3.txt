Unrelenting action - no time to even breath for the first 15% of the Kindle book. What a ride!Initially hard to keep track of everyone as the battle starts, but by the end of the hurly-burly, you have a very good idea of who everyone is, what they have done, and why they are redlined.Then the rest of the story is about what to do with broken soldiers. Government uses up people in wars then they return home. What obligation do the civilians have? What can people who have been so deep in a war that the war is now in them forever do behind the lines?Wow, that sounds preachy and political, but Mr. Drake's Redliners is anything but. The rest of the story is action just like the start of the story, because the government, in the person of the Unity leader, found a solution. Colonize a borderline planet and use the soldiers to protect the civilians from the local flora (yep, that is right,forget about the animals, the plants are crazy dangerous). Strike Force Company C41 most recent mission may be their final one.Mr. Drake has made one of the four best planets biospheres I have ever read. He spent a ridiculous amount of time creating a bunch of very, very deadly trees and vines. Watching the space foot soldiers deal with it is an amazing read.Picked up while free on Kindle.Read moreRead less
