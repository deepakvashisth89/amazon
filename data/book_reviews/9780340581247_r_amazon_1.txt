I had the pleasure of the Author's company during a recent dinner party and on returning home I (of course) googled him... and then ordered a copy of The Better Angel. I read the book in one sitting (a leisurely long Saturday afternoon). And, when I had finished the book I missed the characters, especially Dervla, the long suffering, fun at heart, Aunt. I wanted to go down the pub and over a pint or two, continue the 'story telling' and hear more about her plans for the future. In this way, in terms of bringing fictional characters to life, I can give the novel no higher praise.Frank Ronan's writing is of the classic english novelist… every word is carefully chosen and every sentence important to the development of the characters and story… nothing is wasted. He is better known in England, Ireland and Australia and I hope he is soon discovered over here in the US. I will purchase more of his books for myself and as gifts for others… I am now a fan.Read moreRead less
