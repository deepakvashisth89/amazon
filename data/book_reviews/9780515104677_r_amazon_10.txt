'Steel Tiger' pick's up where 'Rolling Thunder' left off and what a sequel it was. The author puts you right into the cockpit and takes off and leaves you to turn the pages without realising the clock has turned past the midnight hour as the non stop action unfolds.This is a no holds barred account of action told from the air and the slopes of the jungle in war torn Asia in the '60's. The action is there, the politics get involved and the human spirit gets pushed beyond belief.The author has expertly taken his writing experience and taken me on 'tour' into this conflict with this book and left me downloading the next sequel 'Phantom Operator' to see what happens next. 'Steel Tiger' didn't disappoint and you definitely felt like it was your turn to get your very own 'check' ride in the cockpit! Excellent read.Read moreRead less
