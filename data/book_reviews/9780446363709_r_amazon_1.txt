I originally read TENDERNESS maybe a little over a decade ago, not long after I discovered this amazing author. I have since devoured every single one of her western romances, before she went on the write Depression and WWII era novels, of which I'm just not as big a fan of. Since then I have watched and waited for her to go back to her 1800s style stories, but to no avail. Now with her passing I'll never get the chance so I decided to buy a few of her oldies but goodies and re-read some of my favorites on kindle...This was the 19th book I'd read by Ms. Garlock and even having read it before, she continues to blow me away. The woman just writes the most unforgettable stories you'll ever read, and I thought Jesse and Wade were absolutely adorable together! Some of my favorite romances are about young love, and while neither of these two were young, their love was so sweet and tender (I know where she got the title from) it felt like they were. The gentleness with which Wade treats Jesse was just too beautiful for words.Wade has watched Jesse for over a year and is fascinated by her, so when her nursing skills take her up into the hills to treat an epidemic, Wade meets Jesse there to help... And that is the beginning of their love story. Wade has been accused of being "The Looker," a man who sneaks into women's homes when they are alone, blindfolds, fondles, and looks at them. Jesse gives no credence to the rumors concerning Wade though. She believes in his innocence whole-heartedly and stands by her man through it all!This is honestly one of the most beautiful stories I've ever read and even the second time around hasn't changed that opinion. Jesse and Wade's love and absolute need for each other is palpable. You not only sense it but you can practically see and feel it too. The entire tale had such a wonderful and intriguing plot, not to mention a delightful cast of characters, that it was truly just a pleasure and joy to read. Simply put it was another unforgettable story by an unbelievable author that will be very much missed!Rating: 5 STARS and highly recommend to all lovers of historical romance!Pages & Kindle Locations: The story contained 377 pages and ended at 100% and location 4721.Read moreRead less
