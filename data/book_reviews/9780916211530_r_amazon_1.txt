A book very useful for the Rifts dice and paper RPG. It expands the game by offering more races that a game master might allow to be played. Also if players have characters from other Palladium books games, rules may allow the character to be played in the Rifts reality. In many cases players from other Palladium realities can gain skills and powers depending on their Race, Skills, Psychic and or Magic abilities. Those changes only last while in the mystically rich environment of Rifts Earth. Assuming the character survives or chooses to return to his or her game world if the opportunity presents itself.
