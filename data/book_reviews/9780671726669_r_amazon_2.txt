And I've probably read close to a hundred by now. Mostly New York and Chicago Mafia books but this book by Bella Stumbo has no equal. It's as if the author was able to find out every juicy detail and then articulate the story in such a way, you can't stop reading the book. I was so impressed, I wanted to write Ms. Stumbo a note but sadly, she passed away several years ago.I paid over $30 for this book but it's the best $30 I have ever spent.If I had one thing to tell Betty, it would be this. The last thing the thief on the cross "stole" was heaven and Christ was happy to let him steal it. Betty can do the same.....she can steal heaven..but she has to repent, ask for forgiveness, and plead the blood. David's crime was just as heinous but he repented and although he was never the same and guilt affected him the rest of his life.....he repented and God said later......"he was the apple of my eye."...Betty.....you can "steal" heaven...you can pull it off if you want.Read moreRead less
