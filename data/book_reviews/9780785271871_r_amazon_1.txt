I loved the way book three of this series was able to stand alone but drew the other two books into the story. Stephanie Grace Whitson weaves an intriguing story from a tiny detail. Have we not all wondered at the odd keepsakes that are found in the belongings of those who have gone on before us?This is a page turner and will keep you interested until the end, when you will wish to immerse yourself into a book four.
