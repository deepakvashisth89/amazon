Merry is back for her third book, and she's still got what it takes to entertain.  This book has everything you'd expect from the other two, great characters, great mystery, and personal problems for Merry that make life just a little more interesting.  But she faces them all with her faith in the Lord, which helps her through everything.  I was a little disappointed because the mystery takes a back seat to the plots involving abuse.  Still, a very entertaining read that I plan to read many times myself.Read moreRead less
