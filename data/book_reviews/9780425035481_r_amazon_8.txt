I read Telempath about 25 years ago.  I remember liking it a lot, and I wanted to read it again, so I bought a Kindle copy.  Overall, I had a few good times on Memory Lane, but I wouldn't have enjoyed this novel otherwise.Good parts: post-apocalyptic survival fiction.  Cool characters with lots of internal motivation.  Taking a page from many Robert Heinlein books, there are no real bad guys -- every character thinks their side is right, and every character does have a point.  Makes the resolution more interesting than simple "good guy has the biggest hammer".Post-apocalyptic New York City was dated, too -- but I liked the twist where people who want to avoid human contact hide in the ruins on NYC.  (For that matter, it's quite possible, today, to live in NYC and avoid a lot of human contact.)Bad parts: it looks like this book started as a novella.  In the expansion from novella to novel, the muskies went from a straight plot element to an over-the-top trippy element.  I like unorthodox resolutions, but I think Telempath took it too far -- most of the plot tensions dissolve in clouds of reefer smoke.Read moreRead less
