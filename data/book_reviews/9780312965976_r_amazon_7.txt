I liked the seaside setting and the characters. Stockenberg created strong characters and then had them behave in ways that seemed out of character. I felt the author let Maddie down when she caved in to her crazy ex-husband, and I felt Dan, her "true love" became petty and sulky when his woman really needed him. Still, it was a good story, with interesting plot twists and secrets from the past that stirred up trouble. It was a great summer read, and I finished it one day. This was my first book by this author, but I plan to try out a few more.Read moreRead less
