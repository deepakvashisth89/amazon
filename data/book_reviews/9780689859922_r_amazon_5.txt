This fifth book of the series returns to Lucy's point of view.  It is better than the first two in the series but may not be better than the third and fourth.It is the holidays (six weeks with no school) and Lucy is all set to strengthen her relationship with Tony.  But shortly after she finds herself as the only one in the group without a boyfriend.A wish is made and Lucy may have found the absolute perfect boy.  Or is he?In the end the four friends prove their friendship and support of one another.Pretty good pacing and better than Lucy's first narration.  Lots of references to Bridget Jones.  The t-shirts were cute but the slogans are decades old (at least in the US).If you liked the other books in the series, you will be sure to enjoy this one.Read moreRead less
