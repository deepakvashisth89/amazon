See storyline above.Though not very original, this latest by Wiltse does in fact tell a good story and creates a greater depth to his protagonist Billy Tree (remember 'Heartland'). Billy seems to stumble around quite a bit in most of the book, missing alot of clues (e.g. the rabid dog) and shaking in his boots every time he sees or hears a gun. This of course gives him the human quality achieved in this novel. Wiltse's descriptions of the area are accurate and almost make you feel as if you're there, describing the heat and humidity, "Like sitting in an oven, wrapped in a wet horse blanket". A good story that is sometimes too wordy, but moves at a steady moderate pace.Read moreRead less
