I found this book to be fascinating, informative, and quite thought-provoking. Matthew Sweet is a true historian, who brings to bear a keen eye to his topic. Sweet's handling of the subject of the Victorians is so deft, rooted not only in primary sources, but also in an understanding of the ways in which ideas are formed throughout various historical eras. I love the way he deciphers evidence and clues, and connects the formation of interpretations of the past to specific eras (thereby uncovering their origins and intent.) One rarely reads history that is so probing and so thought-provoking. A wonderful book! I look forward to reading more of Sweet's excellent work.Read moreRead less
