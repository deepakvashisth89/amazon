Simone Jaffe, now calling himself Red Diamond hardboiled detective, is at an impasse wondering if he should be Jaffe or Diamond. He gets hired by Las Vegas casino owner Ed Evans to help deal with a rogue Jewish mobster named Moe "Iceberg" Greenberg, who wants to be Bugsy Seigel's heir to Las Vegas. Diamond goes out and cruises the Vegas strip trying to unearth his flame Fifi and his arch rival Rocco Rico, who he believes is at the center of it all. He decimates the men who work for Greenberg and unearths his schemes to topple the independent casino owners before turning his attention onto the mob's operations, from Arson, theft, onto a cheating school run by down on their luck carnies and financed by a South African desperate to earn money in his families trade of mining. Along the way Red meets up with Rosie and her trusty dog Bart, along with master assassin Gulo who likes to kill using unique weapons or methods for his prey.
