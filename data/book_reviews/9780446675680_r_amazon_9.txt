The story is told by a second generation Irish boy growing up in a tough New York neighbourhood.  I was attracted to the book as a first generation Irish person.  It is very well written, informative, touching and sometimes amusing.  You can't help but like 'this kid' as he struggles growing up and making sense of things. The Mary of the title is his mother. It's not a sentimental book I am pleased to say unlike many of this genre. I thoroughly enjoyed it.Read moreRead less
