I had never heard of this story until recently when I was looking at reviews for the movie The Parent Trap. This book was the inspiration for the Disney movie. The story has the same basic premise of twins not aware of each other's existence until they meet by accident at camp. There are several similar scenes recognizable from the movie but since this book takes place in Europe rather than the United States there were differences. That being said, the characters are well drawn and the story is sweet and touching. I read it in an afternoon and quite enjoyed it.Read moreRead less
