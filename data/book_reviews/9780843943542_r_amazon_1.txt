I so enjoyed this book, one of my favorites for sure. I ended up staying up all night reading unable to put it down. Sometimes you get to a slow spot where you can put it down and start again later but it was not the case with one, I truly loved this book. The only thing I would change would be to add an epilogue so I could see how things turned out after a few years for the main characters in the book, now I just have to let my imagination take over. I realize there are some authors that don't add the epilogues to their books but for me it just puts the finishing touches on the story instead of just cutting it off. Either way I was not disappointed in the book, just didn't want it to end quite so quick.Read moreRead less
