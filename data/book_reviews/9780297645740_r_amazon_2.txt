This is a fantastic book. Both myself and my wife couldn't put it down. We read it one after the other. Even if you didn't previously have any particular interest in Jade or perhaps in Burma and China, this book narrates the story of Jade through the story of emperors and tradespeople throughout several centuries and we both became deeply absorbed in the fate of an emperor, an adventurer, the journalists who wrote this book and many other characters depicted. It was extremely stimulating to learn how a stone mined in an inaccessible jungle on the other side of the world affected society in East and West as well as geopolitics. If you read this book you will hear about rulers of ancient cities dressed in gold, the fate of those who invested their lives into Jade in hope for a better future and how this holds true today as it did 300 years ago. A vivid narrative. We are hooked and are now going to search the bibliography for additional reading material to learn more!Read moreRead less
