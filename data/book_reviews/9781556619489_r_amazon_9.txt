An engaging story of the lives and loves in a small English village.  It is filled with diverse characters and sideline stories that give the reader the feeling of being a part of this small rural community in the 1800's.  Having read the first book in this series ("The Widow of Larkspur Inn")  and enjoyed it, it was delightful to see the continuation of certain character's stories and the introduction of new ones.  It is a tale of the victory of characters overcoming their weaknesses and establishing new lives. Overall it is a refreshing and entertaining story that celebrates the redemptive possibilities in each character's life as they attempt to overcome their human flaws and live lives based on Biblical principles.Read moreRead less
