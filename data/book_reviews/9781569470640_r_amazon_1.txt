The Commissaris’ sister needs his help. Her husband Peter Opdijk just died in a freak accident at their home in Maine, and Suzanne wants to go back to Amsterdam. The Commissaris flies out to help her wrap up her affairs. Unknown to him, De Grier follows him to Maine to keep an eye on him. The Commissaris a frail old man.As it happens, five other homeowners besides Opdijk have died in suspicious circumstances. The local sheriff is a clever man and ropes the Dutch visitors into helping him investigate. The Dutchmen are awed by the austere winter landscape and woefully underdressed for the frigid temperatures.The tiny town not only has a plethora of sudden deaths and vacant houses, it also has a local gang of young, quixotic,  intellectual troublemakers. And there’s a local hermit who lives alone on an island with vicious watchdogs and an arsenal of weapons.De Grier finds a beautiful young lover in the little town (or she finds him) — and also finds plenty of use for his elite fighting skills. The Commissaris, who despite his frailty is very tricky, plays a key part in the violent denouement.This novel is a feast of vivid and eccentric characters, quirky dialog, unexpected dangers, and casually profound musings.
