This book was a very enjoyable read.  Bolo fans will notice that instead of being several short stories about different Bolo marks, this is actually a novel about two "obsolete" Mark XXIV Bolos who were thought to be past their prime.  While I'm normally very fond of the short story approach, this novel was well written and held my attention through the entire story.  A "must read" for any Bolo fan!Read moreRead less
