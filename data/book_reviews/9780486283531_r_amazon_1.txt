Thornton W. Burgess was an avid nature lover, conservationist, and an excellent children's author. In "The Adventures of Johnny Chuck," part of the original "Green Meadows Series," Mr. Burgess weaves the adventures of this rugged and feisty woodchuck, also called a groundhog. Young children (and adults too!) will go with Johnny on his first big adventure out into the wide world of the meadows and fields in search of a home to call his own. Along the way are sprinkled natural behaviors of the woodchuck and other animal friends. Johnny gets into a fight for territory, discovers a new home, and discovers the most wonderful thing in the world!
