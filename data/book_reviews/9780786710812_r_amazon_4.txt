Within the past three months I've read a dozen collections of Holmes pastiches, and found this to be the best.  It includes several quotable "plums."  Such plums were one of the key features of the original Holmes stories; they'd be a raisinless rice pudding without them.  Here are a few:P. 13:  The best way to tell a lie effectively is to tell the truth badly.P. 18:  The moral, Watson, is that while life contains its hazards, it is the man who does not know how to calculate the risks who is in real danger.P. 67:  An amateur.  Which means he is either a genius or a dilettante.  There is no in-between in such cases.P. 69:  Swamp gas can only explain so much, sir, so much and no more.P. 154-55:  I saw the small twitch of irony catch the corner of his mouth.
