I love this book. A woman and four kids - two white, two black, it's not sure how everybody is related, but that's fine - go walking to the beach with two dogs.Who chase *everything*. Eventually, they chase the geese, and... well...Let's just say that in real life, geese are vicious creatures that DO NOT BACK DOWN, and they bite, too!It's very bouncy, nothing really scary happens (not even the geese), and the artwork is well done.
