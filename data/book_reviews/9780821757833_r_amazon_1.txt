Ginna is a young woman working at a care and looks forward to her Monday visits to Swan's Way.  She has befriended some of the local residents and they are incredibly fond of her as well.  She also befriends Neal who is not the usual age range for the place.  He is dealing with his guilt over an incident that happened during a plane crash he was in.  He was able to save the little girl but not her mother.  If only things could have turned out different.  Ginna can't figure out how she has seen Neal from somewhere.  Until one day she remembers that it is in the images that appear in the greenhouse at a certain time every day.  While checking it out at just the right time they both are transported back to the past and experience a little bit of Virginia and Channing's life.  Ginna especially likes the journey as she doesn't appear to have a heart condition during her visit to the past.  The book then moves from past to present which Becky does very well.
