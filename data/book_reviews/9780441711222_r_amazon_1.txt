Red Limit Freeway by John DeChancie is the 2nd novel of the Starrigger trilogy. As such, it continues the story, but does not complete it; in fact, it ends with a cliffhanger that is picked up in the 3rd novel, Paradox Alley. There is some exposition interspersed in the first couple of chapters to refresh the memory of those who did not read Starrigger immediately before. The characters are well fleshed out and easy to identify with. The plot moves along fairly quickly, with action, introspection, romance, philosophy, and some answers to some fundamental questions about the Skyway posed in the novel Starrigger.  It was written in the 1980s, but the story holds up very well over 25 years later.
