One of the best novels I have read in ages, and at the end I felt totally drained! What a powerful and emotional roller coaster ride. Quite a long novel, but the build up of tension and merging of subplots is well sustained to the end. Excellent characterisation, with a brilliant expose of the forces of lust, greed, hope, desperation, innocence, courage, obsession, and ruthlessness. The story progresses inexorably to what you know must be a dreadful climax, but it still shocks you when it happens. Utterly compelling.Read moreRead less
