I love Southern writing. I don't really know why but the humour gets to me every time.  I have really enjoyed every book I have read by Billy Letts and would recommend her to anyone.  There is a lack of social status which is very apparent in England where most writers seem to have connections or are middle class with connections.  I appreciate this in the standard of writing I have read from Eudora Welta to Mark Childress and now to Billie Letts. Get the book you'll know what I mean if you read it.
