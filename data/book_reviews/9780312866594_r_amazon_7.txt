All in all this is a pretty good beginner's book for someone who is looking to improve their academic performance.  It covers alot of basic things that will help you write a better paper, study and take tests more effectively, and make better use of your time.One of the most annoying things that he does is devote entire chapters to things that may require a paragraph at most to explain (such as turning in your homework on time, getting to class on time, etc.) Alot of the things he says in these chapters are pretty obvious like coming in late for a class might hurt the relationship you have with your professor.There are better books out there if you want to improve your academic performance.  Like Secrets of the A-Maker by Darrin King (you have to order this book directly from the publisher, so you should do a search on google).
