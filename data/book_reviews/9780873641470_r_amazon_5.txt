Wow! At first I/ was somewhat disappointed due to the lack of actual battle accounts. However; somewhere along the line I became so wrapped up in Jim Moore's descriptive story that I forgot all about the field battles and fire fights I find so fascinating. Moore has a great style that is relaxed yet poignant.I was drawn into his friendships with cowboy , his description of the "Yards" and his special love for the people and the country.An expense amount of information without ever becoming boring or overly verbose.A really exceptional account of the real life affairs of a true soldier who somehow managed to live (and fight)  for years in country w/o becoming hateful or cynical. Absolutely amazing life and a story that describes a side of the Vietnam war rarely written about.In addition,the story was told  a way I have never seen portrayed more honestly.
