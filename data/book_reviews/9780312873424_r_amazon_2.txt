Susan Shwartz has created a very intense character driven story within a solid SF setting. When it comes to SF, I like it hard.  :)  The setting in Second Chances establishes a terrific foundation for some great story telling. She creates some wonderful and very memorable characters that stay with you. Heart wrenching is what this book is. Shwartz's writing is very evocative.Second Chances is set in the same universe as Shwartz's other SF book, Heritage Of Flight. It was the strength of that book and her co-authored Star Trek novels, that I sought out Second Chances, and I'm glad I did. I hope to see more SF from Shwartz and I'm keeping my fingers crossed that we'll see more of some of the characters from Second Chances (Captain Marlow, in particular!)Read moreRead less
