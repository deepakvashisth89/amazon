This book as with all of Donna's books is very informative with many pictures and instructions on how to paint beautiful pieces of art, I've always wanted to be able to paint beautiful flowers, but have always had trouble with shading and highlighting. With Donna's one stroke technique she teaches you how to double load your brush with two colors and magically the shading and highlighting are there with just one stroke. These are easy to follow instructions, whether you have experience in painting or not you will be able to paint gorgeous pieces of art with this book.
