After her parents die in England, Karolina's aunt comes to take her home to America. Although she misses her parents terribly, she has resigned herself to the fact that they are never coming back, and she tries to enjoy  the trip home on the Titanic, sad as she is. Gavin, who is twelve like  Karolina, is earning his passage to America by working in the Titanic's  galley. He misses his family back home in Ireland but is eager to join his  older brother in New York. Gavin and Karolina meet by chance one night and  become friends. On the night the ship sinks, they are thrown together,  fighting to survive the terrible disaster.
