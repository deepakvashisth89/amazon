Janet Morgan succeeds in capturing the spirit of Agatha Christie in this biography.Her writing is extremely easy to read, and I found myself reading for hours on end, as you would expect to do with a great work of fiction.Because it is not an autobiography, we get some idea of how the other people in Agatha's life felt at particular times too - Janet Morgan doesn't always concern herself only with Agatha, but includes infromation about Archie and Madge too.  This I found extremely interesting and informative.Morgan was given sole acess to private letters, this gives her biography depth as it is based on material objects and not just hearsay.I loved this biography and would happily sit down and reread it again any day!Read moreRead less
