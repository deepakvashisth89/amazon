This book is beautiful! I not only enjoyed that it dove into Greek mythology, but that it also explained the attributes of Goddesses and how to detect them in women. This breakdown helped me better understand myself and other women in my life. When I look back in my life I can now see which goddess(es) were activated me and how that impacted my life. Similarly, I can see, based on the words and actions of other women in my life, which goddesses are/were activated in them. As a result, it helps me not take words/actions of others so personally.
