My Father flew 35 mission out of England with the 492nd Bomb Group. I have read many books regarding the air war in WW II and this is by far the best. This is not a macho shoot em up hero worshiping  narrative... although it took a special breed to endure what the author endured, it is a unique but what I can only imagine an extremely accurate perspective on the air war experience. I loved the poetic and reflective writing and if one is turned off by the language they really don't get it.
