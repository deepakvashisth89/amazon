Conrad is a genius.  His works are among the great works of world literature and these stories are among Conrad's best.I read the paperback Modern Library edition of Typhoon and Other Stories.  This is a good edition.  It is very nicely produced and printed.  The introduction by Barry Unsworth is useful and this edition also includes an author's note and short bio of Conrad.  The notes by Nels Pearson are a bit odd and some are distracting.  He makes questionable literary comments and defines some obvious terms but leaves many others undefined.
