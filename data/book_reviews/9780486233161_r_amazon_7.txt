The listing doesn't mention that Nabokov's Anya in Wonderland is in antiquated pre-reform Russian using letters and spellings conventions unfamiliar to most Russian language students. The title on the cover of the Dover edition in pre-reform Russian is clue to what's inside.  Before ordering, click on "Read Sample" and scroll to the first chapter to get a sense of the language. It's interesting that this "unabridged republication of the work originally published... in 1923" is available, but I found the orthography challenging. I offered it to a friend who is a native Russian speaker and they politely declined. Another drawback, the typeface is very small.
