This was a good book about a terrible tragedy, but there were a lot of typos, missing words, extra words, etc., that made reading this book difficult, as encountering them took you out of the story. It definitely needed a good editor.I'm glad Tyler and Stacy finally got justice and hope their families have fared as well as possible over the years. I also think it was unfair that McElroy and Applebaum lost their  elections due to the Stallings case and the money provided to their opposition by Pat Stallings, as they were only going by the evidence the labs provided which showed her son was poisoned. While it was terrible what she went through, it was the labs fault, not the prosecutors.
