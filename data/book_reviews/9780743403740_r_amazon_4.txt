As the summary mentions, the book primarily focuses on Sarek, the father of Spock. Sarek must do his duty as Ambassador, cope with his grief of his wife's death, and his son's grief/anger while working to uncover a plot that could bring down the Federation. While the story has a strong connection to Star Trek: The Undiscovered Country, readers can also find references to Star Trek: Search for Spock, TOS Journey to Babel and TNG Unification I and II. Readers spend time with "old friends" - like Kirk, McCoy, and Spock - meet new characters - Peter Kirk and Valdyr - and some minor characters that were seen in previous Star Trek movies/episodes.If you are a Star Trek TOS fan, this is a book for you. It's entertaining and, though sad in parts, the story is fast paced and keeps the reader's interest from beginning to end.Read moreRead less
