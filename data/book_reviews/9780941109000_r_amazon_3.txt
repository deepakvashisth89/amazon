If organized religion is not your cup of tea, than this book just may be exactly what you've been searching for. The Michael handbook is a blueprint for those of us who don't believe that the bible provides all of the answers. If you've ever asked questions like: Why are we here? What is the purpose of being born just to suffer and die? Is reincarnation real or do we really only live once? I'm certain you will find that this book provides detailed explanations to those questions, while explaining why we are they way that we are as individuals.  I've never been a religious person, but I am very spiritual. This book has provided me with a better understanding of the fact that we are indeed spiritual beings living on this physical plane to have a human experience, rather than a group of unconnected human beings trying to seek out a spiritual path through organized religion.  After you read this book, you will definitely see yourself, your family members and the world in a different light.
