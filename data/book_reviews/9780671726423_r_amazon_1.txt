It is definitely my honor to write a review of another excellent novel by Rebecca Forster.  Perhaps I should preface my remarks by explaining that I have read all of Ms Forster's novels, and I love them all.The Reckless Ones is another excellent novel by a master of the written word.  That is not hyperboyle but the honest truth.The review by Amazon.com is accurate and honest.  This book is very enjoyable and a pleasure to read.  The best part of the novel is the ending.  Venturing away from the "standard" romantic novel and crime novel....  this novel ends in a manner that the reader doesn't expect.  The novel ends in a unique manner that is totally woven together to "clean up all loose ends" and give the reader a feeling of WOW!  what a great novel.This novel combines romance with mystery in such a manner that the reader is fully entertained from beginning to end.I highly recommend Ms Forster and The Reckless Ones as a genuine page turner and value for your time and money.Read moreRead less
