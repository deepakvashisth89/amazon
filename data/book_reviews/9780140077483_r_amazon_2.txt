Mosher's work may be an acquired taste but I read it with jolts of recognition, for example of Noel and Bangor's unusual relationship, speech patterns,  and seldom-used expressions. Even to native Vermonters the Northeast Kingdom, called Kingdom County in Mosher's works, is a separate, distinctive place with its own culture and patois. What comes thorough clearly is the strong feelings of inhabitants of such a special place when outside interests are able to buy it out from under them and change it for the worse.
