This book by Lawana Blackwell, as well as the whole series of The Gresham Chronicles, is most enjoyable. The author interweaves multiple story lines which creates a high interest in the reader. In this particular book, I enjoyed the story line about Mercy Sanders and her difficult position in a household of obnoxious brothers and a selfish, unreasonable father. I read very little Christian fiction anymore because many feel the need to lapse into suggestive descriptions and situations (at which point I get frustrated and close the book). As a reader with Biblical standards, I am not interested in such books. Mrs. Blackwell's books present a life view that is realistic in terms of the sinful behavior one encounters in the world without going into explicit detail in order to "excite" the reader. I appreciate that approach. Also, I find her Biblical values, as presented by the characters, to be right on target. She brings out good Scriptural counsel through the various situations encountered by the characters. There is also humor interwoven throughout. I had two difficulties with the series. One is that, based on the dialogue, I really couldn't think of these characters as English (there was barely an "indeed" in the four novels!) but as "Americanized" English people. (Although her development of the village and its inhabitants was very well done.) Second, as is typical in most Christian books and productions, there is almost ALWAYS a happy ending and things nearly always work out the way people hope they will.(One exception in Blackwell's books was Mr. Clay's bouts with depression; they didn't end when he became a believer.)Even as a believer in Christ, I know that life doesn't always work out the way we want and expect because people often follow their own sinful inclinations rather than God's will. But God gives grace and strength to go on with peace and joy even when life doesn't go as planned. I'd like to see more of this in Christian books and productions.
