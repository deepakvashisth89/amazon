I've read all of Laymon's books and am a die hard horror fan but this was a disappointment.  Although the book had it's moments, it never truly had me.  I measure the worth of a story on how long it takes me to finish.  If I fly through it, it's because the story is so good I'm at it's mercy, feverishly turning pages to find out what's going on.  If it takes me awhile then I'm reading to just finish and that was the case with Flesh.
