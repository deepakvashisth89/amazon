Not only did this book give us many names to add to our list of possibilities for our baby-to-be, it was also an interesting read.  As a mom it was a great reminder of all the classic books I have read, and how much I loved (and hated) the characters within them (too little time to read until the kids get a bit older).This book lists names from ancient to contemporary with a little blurb on who that person was in what work.  A wonderful way to choose a name that means something and just doesn't sound pretty.  The only drawback was that not enough types of works were included, but that is a minor issue - everyone enjoys different types of literature, it would have been impossible to include them all to please everyone.To the person who thinks the names would be easily found online is mistaken.  There were names from books I wouldn't have thought of to look up online - names on my list that I might not have come across otherwise.
