This is book two of a series. I had read the first one a few years ago and enjoyed it. This one was also good. It's a little odd, told in various points of view but it switches sometimes abruptly. I could definitely do without the children's points of view, found them irritating and skipped over them. I understand why they were included but I don't think they added anything. Clary, Polly, and Louise were especially interesting and well-fleshed-out, young adult characters. The ending left a lot up in the air, to be expected with a series but I still thought it ended a bit quickly. I guess I will have to buy the next book to find out what happens.
