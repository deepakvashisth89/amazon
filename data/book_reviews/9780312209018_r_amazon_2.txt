Excellent book about a newsroom of a dying newspaper in the closing days of the 1960s. Martinez makes you feel the grime and shoddiness of the newsroom and all the wonderful, pathetic, heroic characters who occupied it. The relationship between the publisher and his minions is directly on point as well as the public persona vs the reality of the protest movement. All good things, however pure, often suffer because of the failings of the humans who operate them.If you want to know what it was like for newspapermen (and women) in the hectic '60s, read this book.
