This is a fun, quick book to read.  This book is the original story that the classic movie(s), The Parent Trap, is based upon.  The original movie (1961) starring Haley Mills is one of my all time favorite films.  I’ve seen the 1998 version several times also.  I was thrilled to find the ebook version by German author Erich Kastner.  This story is about two look-alike girls who meet at a summer camp and realize they are twin sisters who were separated at birth.Lisa and Lottie is a children’s story, so it’s not my normal reading fare.  As such it’s a simple, quick read.  But it’s not brilliant writing.  Just a story, though at the time it was published (1949), I could have seen it would have been unique.  But after I have watched these movies around 10 times combined, it’s not quite so fresh.  Still, I enjoyed this book.In reading more about Erich Kästner, I discovered he was a world-renowned German author known mostly for his children’s stories, such as this book, Lisa and Lottie.  He wrote other genres as well. He was even nominated for the Nobel Prize for literature six times.  He was on the right side of history too.  Even though he lived in Nazi Germany all throughout the war, he was against the Nazis who even banned his books.  Yet, he remained in Germany throughout the war.  So in my eyes, he’s someone to admire for his willingness to stand courageously by his convictions.He did live to see the success of this particular book as it became the basic for a classic film.
