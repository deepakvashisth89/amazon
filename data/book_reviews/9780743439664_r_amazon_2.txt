Blackbird was a tragic childhood journey which left me praying for a better life for Jennifer at the end. Alas, it was not meant to be. This sequel continues her abusive existense in her adult voice with poignant, painful clarity. She brings to light the reality of a child being unloved and yearning for someone to acknowlege her value in a cast of family members incapable of honesty and devotion.
