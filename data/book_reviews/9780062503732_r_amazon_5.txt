Michael Harner brought the elements of practical shamanism to westerners with his books and through his founding of the Foundation for Shamanic Studies. He was one of the first to realize the core practices that permeate almost all indigenous shamanic practices. (The word "shaman" is taken from a Tungus word for such a healer but has been adopted to mean just about all practitioners of that sort of spiritual healing and guidance.)This book was one of many texts I studied when learning shamanism, and although I have others, it remains the most useful and complete book.Harner's passing from this plane in 2018 was a loss to those of us who studied his work, but I have no doubt he is still passing on wisdom to those who seek him out on a different plane.Read moreRead less
