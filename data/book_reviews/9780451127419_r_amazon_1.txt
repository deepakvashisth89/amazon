I had nowhere to turn back in the 70's, when I was only 19 and having my first baby.  Somehow, and I don't remember how - I was smart enough to pick up a hard copy of Dr. Guttmacher's Pregnancy, Birth and Family Planning.  There weren't a lot of resources back then and I needed information.  I read this book from cover to cover several times during my 9 months, and referred back to it frequently, even AFTER my son was born.The book is easy to read, informative, and clearly addresses any concerns or questions that come up during your pregnancy.  My daughter in law just announced that she is expecting, and even though today's moms are more likely to go to the internet for information, there is something comforting about having a reliable book that you can pick up and read without searching through possibly incorrect info on the web.  I bought it for her and my son.  I'm hoping she takes the time to peruse this book and realize the value of the information contained within.
