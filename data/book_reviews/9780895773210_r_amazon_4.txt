It’s always strange to say a book about disasters is great, but this one is!  Reader’s Digest Great Disasters is an excellent resource for those wanting an introduction to famous disasters.  Each event has a 2-8 page entry that nicely summarizes what happened during the disaster and why it occurred.  Numerous illustrations are included to round out the text and bring the events to life.  For large, well known disasters, like the Black Death and the Titanic, a reasonable overview is provided, but additional research will probably be needed to really understand what happened.  For lesser known disasters, like the Sweating Sickness and Super-Typhoon Vera, enough information is included so you’ll feel like you understand the event (though for things like the Sweating Sickness, which I’d never heard of before, it may inspire deeper research any way).  I strongly recommend this book to anyone interested in disasters and disaster science.
