I thoroughly love "Out to Canaan By Jan Karon. Jan Karon is a wonderful author. The Mitford Series of books are all amazing. I love the plots, the characters, locations - just everything about Mitford. One of the most important characters is God. He is very important in Mitford (and in real life too.) I hope if you haven't, that you will give faith in God a chance. Read the Bible too. Mitford books shows why faith in God is important - even though it's a fiction book. God is a strong part of Mitford, but He is not fiction. I love that Mitford is a small town. I grew up in a small town and it reminds me of my hometown. This series of books is a lot like real life - happy, funny, sad and everything in between. My Dad also loved reading the Mitford books. I recommend the Mitford books to everyone. If I recall correctly there was a review saying there were typos in this book - but I'm wondering if the typos they thought they saw were part of the story. The newspaper editor in the story seems to frequently make typos in his writing. I have noticed no real typos. If you are looking for a good and clean read - but also a book that is very interesting, amazing and engaging - then this is the book for you. I challenge you to read this novel and all of the books in the Mitford Series. If you are like me, you will enjoy this book immensely. (I submitted this review with five stars.
