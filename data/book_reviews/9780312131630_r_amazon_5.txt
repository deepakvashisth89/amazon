What i like about this particular book was not only was it very well written and had a great story,  it was also the MOST DIFFERENT take on the vampire mythology that i think i've read...In my opinion only Anne Rice has as good a backstory behind how vampires came about. This book goes the more "scientific medical" route and the effects and execution are splendid.
