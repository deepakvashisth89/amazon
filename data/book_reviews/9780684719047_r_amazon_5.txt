I DO happen to have a first edition autographed copy given to me as a gift which I cherish.  I have loved this book so much, I could read it yearly.  I've had difficulties finding it through the years to give to colleagues and beginning teachers.  This book should be required reading for all new teachers.  Jesse Stuart was light years ahead of his time.  He was a dedicated, committed, and passionate teacher.  When the students weren't "getting" the math lesson, he looked around the room, noticed the Farm Bureau calendar on the wall, took it down and cut out the numbers for the students to have concrete manipulatives.  Decades later, publishing companies are selling concrete manipulative for students to learn math.  One of the other reviews stated "he wasn't a good writer."  He was a teacher, writing his story.
