I loved this story! The action started at the beginning of the story and did not even slow till the very end. I actually figured out who the mastermind was, before they were revealed. Not by much, granted. Very unusual for me. Anyway, the characters are very interesting from the beginning, the plot was all over the place, with scores of red herrings, as in any great mystery. The premise was stopping the utter revolution of the United Kingdom, although for the life of me, I cannot think of what could have been said to cause such a catastrophe.
