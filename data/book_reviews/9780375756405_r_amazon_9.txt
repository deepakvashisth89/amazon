Wow wow wow!This book is an honest, amazing account from one of the most prolific mountaineers that has ever lived. He did some crazy climbs, and is most famous for surviving a night out in the open just below the summit of K2 - wow. Unusually Bonatti gave up his climbing at the age of 35, which is somewhere around the peak for a climber and focused on photography, which is what many know him for.I really like the structure of this book - each chapter is devoted to a different climb which makes it a perfect book for me - allows it to be read in small stages rather than having to keep going. I like the way that each climb increases in difficulty - you feel like you are going on the journey with him! Some of the winter climbs he does do not even bear thinking about - so tough.There are also some very candid reviews of sadly tragic accidents, which whilst they do not make for light reading are fascinating in their own right and makes the book very interesting to read. The skills and audacity of Bonatti are well beyond anything the average amateur mountaineer can hope to achieve - but it is an inspiring read and will make you want to get out there and climb some stuff! I predict this is going to be a very popular book, off the back of a year that has given documentaries like The Dawn Wall and Free Solo.10/10 book - would recommend to anyone.Read moreRead less
