This book was recommended to me by my therapist. As an adult, I like the feeling of knowing I’m not alone, and seeing content that is relatable - even the not so glamorous stuff. This book does that beautifully for children who are going through divorce. Not every circumstance described in this book hits home for my littles, but a lot of it does. Even the more harsh stuff is good for kids to see that they aren’t the only ones who go through it- because those things actually happen. It’s been very cathartic for them, I feel.
