I have loved Kitty Burns Florey's writng for a very long time.  Her strengths are present- clear characters, ample, but not overwhelming detail, and a sincere affection.However, I found the structure of this otherwised fine book disorienting.  Unlike Margaret Atwood's _Cat's Eye_, another novel which plays with structure, this only regresses until the last chapter, a not entirely satisfying wrap-up.The strongest, most interesting character is Nell.  There is no explicit sex, but Nell's lesbainism is dealt with in a forthright manner.I want to like this book more than I do.  I am not sorry I read it, though.Read moreRead less
