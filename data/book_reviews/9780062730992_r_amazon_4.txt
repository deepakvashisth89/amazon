This is the update edition of "The Cartoon Guide to Genetics" by Larry Gonick and Mark Wheelis.  The original edition was published in 1983, and this First Collins edition was published in 2005.  This is one of a series of Cartoon Guides which Larry Gonick has co-authored with scientists in the field of choice.  Mark Wheelis is the senior lecturer in Microbiology at the University of California at Davis.The book opens with a pre-history of the field; discussing diverse subjects such as domestication of plants and animals, the biblical story of Jacob's Flock, spontaneous generation, selective breeding, and so on.  They move on to give an excellent overview and history of the subject.  Unlike the other Cartoon Guide's that I have read, this one lacks any chapters or sections.  Perhaps they felt the subject was narrow enough that it was not necessary, but for readability it might have been better to have broken the book into chapters.This book serves well as an introduction, overview, review, and/or history of the subject.
