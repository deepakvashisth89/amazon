This book is like the curate's egg. There's a lot of good in it but you have to nibble around the blemishes.On the appetizing side, it's excellent space opera. Lots of action. The author creates a believable group of sympathetic characters. They're an interesting lot and he does a nice job of inhabiting their minds. He imagines daunting situations that push these people to the limit. His action sequences, of which there are many, are well described and exciting. He manages to create an aura of dread that keeps his readers on edge. And his scenarios, his vision of a possible future, are pretty cool.On the less appetizing side, the whole thing begins to feel like Napoleon's retreat from Moscow. I understand that he wants to communicate how desperate things are. But I think there might be better ways to achieve that than by repeating the same battle scenario over and over. Also, I felt that the ending was abrupt. Too many characters with whom we had spent a lot of time are left hanging, especially the Major.I like action-heavy space opera that cares about its characters, and for that reason I enjoyed this book.Read moreRead less
