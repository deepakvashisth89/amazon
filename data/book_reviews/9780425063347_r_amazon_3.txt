I am nearly 70 years old. I started reading science fiction at age 9 or so, with Tom Swift(senior and junior). I read this when it was published in paperback and after restarting college after a rude interruption imposed upon me by Dick Nixon. I have read much science fiction since then. Little of what I read stands as clearly in my memory as does this volume and it's sequels.Janet Morris writes with clarity, originality and foresight seldom seen. Dream Dancer reads as if just written today. Rereading it was a great delight. The story is compelling and proposes dangers in developing technology that are even more alarming than they were 45 years ago.Two caveats: the editing of this edition(Kindle) leaves much to be desired, and, bring a dictionary, you will need it.Read moreRead less
