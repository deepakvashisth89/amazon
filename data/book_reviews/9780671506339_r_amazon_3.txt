Wow, that’s an awful lot of dysfunctional for me.  That protagonist lives inside his head way too much.So, I’m not sure what I can say without plot spoiling so take this as an official alert.The writing was good, I liked how the author played around with phrasing and expressions.  It definitely helped with the atmosphere.  Because of this, I’ll try another Straub book.The protagonist was not at all likeable - that’s intentional to keep you guessing.  To be fair, I’m not sure I liked a single person in this book.  Rinn was OK and that’s the best I can do.  She kept chickens and was old school.And what in the world is Miles’s DEAL, anyway?  I don’t get how he functioned well enough in society to actually maintain a marriage and professorial career as long as he did.  Plus, cousins.  2 of them.  I just can’t even.Here’s the SPOILER: The ending.  I kept going along, hoping he was blanking out and not realizing what he was up to.
