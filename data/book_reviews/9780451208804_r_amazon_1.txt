Hannah March has the talent as a writer to place the reader right in the middle of London in 1760.  Her descriptions of the sights and smells of London of the time are fantastic.  I also love the vernacular of the characters.  There is nothing worse than reading a "period" book when the characters sound like they're in the 21st century.If you enjoy period mysteries full of perfectly described details, don't miss this book!Read moreRead less
