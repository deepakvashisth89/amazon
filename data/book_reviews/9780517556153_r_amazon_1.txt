This book is a must read for anyone. Dyer is not a man with an agenda, he does not attempt to sway one over to this psychologist's thinking, or that historians take or other such nonsense that tends to fill the books of non-historians doing works of this scale and sheer Epic scope. Dyer is one of today's great realists - a mature, smart man who holds no sides but the rational one.War : The Lethal Custom is fact. It is a history of human kind's that does not glorify or berate our violent past, merely presenting facts and their reasoning culled from Dyer's own knowledge and referenced from other great historians, the great John Keegan being an especially prevalent source.This is an important, exceptional book. I was extraordinarily happy to see it stocked as a mandatory book in my University's bookshop. I was only saddened that this was a year after I had to purchase my copy on Amazon - even our local bookshops were sold out of this work for long enough to make me purchase it online. These very things alone turn me toward optimism. This is a life changing book.If you are reading reviews, unsure whether or not to purchase it, then read this; Buy It.
