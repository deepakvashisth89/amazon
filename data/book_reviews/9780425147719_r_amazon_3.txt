Author Deborah Crombie gives her main characters more of a back story in this second book of her series featuring detectives Duncan Kincaid and Gemma James. In this novel, Duncan is driven to pursue the facts surrounding the supposed suicide of his neighbor, Jasmine. Jasmine, an older lady who was terminally ill, looked forward to Duncan’s visits, just as she did other people who called to see her. In this case, the suspects were limited to only a few visitors. There was a younger friend and her boyfriend, a home health nurse, a retired military veteran, and Jasmine’s brother.Although Jasmine had explored assisted suicide as an option, she had recently decided against it. Her body was discovered in bed with all her medicines accounted for and with the suspects all having apparent alibis. Duncan has a hunch that will not allow him to let the case be classified as suicide. Duncan and Gemma must piece together clues from Jasmine’s past to discover the killer. The problem was that Jasmine was very private and not inclined to discuss her past. Duncan has to persuade the higher-ups to allow him to investigate. As the case unfolds, Duncan’s determination, his relationship with his partner, Gemma, and her history are revealed.As with the first book in the series, this mystery will have readers speculate about who did it until the end. I enjoy reading mysteries set in the not-too-distant past where the detectives don’t have quick DNA results and cell phone data to rely on.
