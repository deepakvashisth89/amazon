Bob Terrell starts at the beginning of Southern Gospel music and takes you on a wonderful journey through the years.  The groups, the people, the songwriters, the promoters.  It's all here.  Find out how they made it  through those years.  Some made it, some did not.  The fun part of it and  even the tragic part of losing someone in the industry way too soon.
