This is by far the best edition of Hamelt I have ever found. I have kept H. H. Furness New Variorum and Riverside Editions, both are outstanding editions in their rights. But by comparison this Arden version is the single volume that has all the info you would need for advanced studies or just reading for literary appreciation of this masterpiece.
