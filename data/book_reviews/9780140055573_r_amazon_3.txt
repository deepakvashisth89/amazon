As a fan of historical mysteries, I enjoyed this book for its portrayal of the bizarre Victorian fad for multi-day endurance races.  The victims and suspects were well-drawn, but I was disappointed in the lead detective, Sgt. Cribb, who came across as colorless and uninteresting.  The mystery plot itself was okay, with enough misdirection to keep the reader from suspecting the perpetrator till the end.  There are further books in this series, but I’ll let Sgt.
