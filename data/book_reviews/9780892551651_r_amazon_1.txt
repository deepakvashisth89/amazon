For close to 30 years I had a friend of blessed memory who came to America from Cuba in 1960. She, her husband and six sons arrived in Miami with the clothes on their backs, escaping the Castro regime. Olga and her husband were both respected professionals in Cuba. In this country, as Spanish only speakers, they began at the bottom. My interest in reading Oscar Hijuelos book was to gain more insight to the community who left. It definitely produced the understanding I was looking for and more. Insecurities, trials, struggle to fit in and become part of this strange new world the US.
