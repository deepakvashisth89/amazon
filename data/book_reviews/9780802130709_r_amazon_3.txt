I had higher hopes for Behan's plays before I began reading this anthology of his complete works in English. Although all of his works are packed with humor, they are too chaotic and freewheeling to satisfy a careful reading through. With the possible exception of "The Quare Fellow", there is little relation, or running plot that is perceivable in the longer plays here, and one is soon lost in all of the stage business and the coming and going raft of  a multitude of characters one soon ceases caring about, except as epitomizations of running jokes. Sometimes, or often, less is more, and, as with the always too-loquacious Bernard Shaw, the reader, and the audience member, should be let alone to make up his own mind as to the wisdom of what he is seeing, hearing, and reading. Behan's plays probably play better on the stage than read from the page.
