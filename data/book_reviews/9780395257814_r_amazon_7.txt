Depicted in non-linear time frames, Kosinski dramatically succeeds in presenting the tumultuous life of one man, George Leventer.  Associating with the powerful, beautiful, and lonely people around the world,  Kosinski's character captures that elusive state of accepting the paradox  of being powerful but lonely, requiring love but displaying violence, and  growing old but questioning fate.  His narrative is sharp and cinematic in  structure and tone.  Erotica is insightfully tamed and given in precise  portions.  I became entralled in dissecting the characters intentions as  much as their decisions and actions.  A superb study in pychological drama  and state of consciousness.
