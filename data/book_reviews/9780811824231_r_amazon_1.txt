If you are interested in how most Japanese live, in their daily, ordinary lives, then pick this book up.  It does not illustrate the "traditional" Japanses houses with Zen gardens and tatami mats everywhere, nor does it illustrate the "Ikea-ized" futuristic apartments of rich folks in Tokyo.  Instead it shows how the other half (or make that 85%) of Japanese live: in a word, cramped.  This book showcases real, average apartments and houses.  Most of these dwellings are (relatively) tiny and crammed floor to cieling with STUFF.  This book is interesting in that it shows how a person can literally cram their life into one or two tiny rooms and make it livable.
