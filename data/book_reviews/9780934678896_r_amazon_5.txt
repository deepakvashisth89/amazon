Sarah Strohmeyer looks at Barbie and asks some questions.  Most of them are to do with the implausibility of Barbie, but what if she was real, what are some of the issues she'd have to face.  Is Barbie a healthy or an unhealthy image and why don't we have healthy female imagery and Role-Models in our culture.
