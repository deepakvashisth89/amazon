I've just discovered Heart of the City and I look forward to reading it every day now. I was lucky enough to find this charming and funny collection on Amazon and I've been looking everywhere for any others that might be out there. If you are listening, Mr. Talented Mark Tatulli please consider collecting all the other pst strips of Heart of the City into books for us fans. I've missed a lot of this story so I'd love to be able to fill in the gaps. Best of luck on a sweet, clever and very funny comic strip. My favorite part of Heart's character is her tenaciousness and her absolute love of life. I also really like her long suffering but loving hard working Mom. Mrs Angellini, Dean, her best friend Kat, all add a lot to the story. It's also great that the strip is set in Philadelphia and that Heart speaks openly about being Catholic without in any way whatsoever being preachy. She is bubbly and full of life, with a great imagination. She is a cool, complex kid that so many people can relate to. It's impossible not to get caught up in her world.
