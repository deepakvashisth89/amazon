I don't like Rush Limbaugh--at all-- so I was looking forward to reading this book. It wasn't terrible, as he does makes some decent points, but it could have been a lot better. It isn't written very well and a lot of issues he brings up makes don't really relate to the subject (e.g. athlete's salaries). It is more of a attack on right-wing ideology than an attack on Rush per se (he discusses Milton Friedman in detail, not positively).I read this many years ago, so I apologize for not being more detailed in my review.
