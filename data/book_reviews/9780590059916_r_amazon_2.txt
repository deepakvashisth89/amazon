This book explains the life as a teenager. When Claudia has to make a decision on whether or not she wants to go to 8th grade or stay in 7th, she realizes that she is growing up and will have to make more choices like this one later on in life. Claudia has a steady boyfriend, but in the end, she knows that he is not meant for her at all. This book had a wonderful plot that made sense and was a heartwarming tale.Read moreRead less
