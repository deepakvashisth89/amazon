I have been reading Roberta Gellis's books since they were first printed, I have never been disappointed by any of her books.  She wrote wonderful books.  Once you start reading one of her books, you don't want to put that book down!  She had a college degree in Medival history and she put it to good use.  There are a few authors that write historical romance and you know that they have done their historical research, she is one of them!
