I love Danielle Steel, and have been reading her for years.  I really liked the story line here, and the characters.  It was a good read, until about 1/3 of the way in, when I decided that if I had to read the word 'and' one more time I would lose my mind!  I never saw the word 'and' so many times in one book in my life!  I don't know if she just got lazy, or if the conversion is responsible, but really!  If I know that the grammar is this bad, then it must be bad .. because I am no genius.  The story is still good though, and the people are still interesting.
