Tess and Brad Holcombe are a career oriented couple, married over 10 years, with seemingly everything going for them. Except that Tess cannot get pregnant and give Brad the family he desires . Tess was adopted and although her adoption turned out well with loving parents, she deeply desires for her own flesh and blood . Tess eventually turns, in her despair, her choices and desires over to the will of God.Judge Barbara Woodsley deals with juveniles every day in her courtroom and although Sherry Macomber is not a bad teen , she has been in and out of foster homes her whole life and now at age 14, she finds herself pregnant. Judge Woodsley has always taken a special interest in Sherry and once Sherry decides she can't go through with the abortion she had planned, is open to adoption with the caveat that the couple will take Sherry in and allow her to have the baby and spend a couple of months with him before totally turning him over to the adoptive couple. Tess and Brad agree to this arrangement and now must trust God for the outcome.A very satisfying story and a great ending.Read moreRead less
