October seems to me to be the perfect time to read Phil Rickman as the nights draw in and the fire is lit in the evening. Having devoured all the Merrily Watkins books, I am now reading Phil's backlist.  Curfew is the creepiest of all I've read so far! Believable characters, especially Arnold, that I cared about from the get go, a developing sense of horror and twists and turns I didn't see coming.
