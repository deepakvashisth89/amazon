I have read this book several times. I stumbled across an first run edition in an antique store. As a native myself I read it with much skepticism at first. But after relatively few pages I was drawn in completely. It is an outstanding historically accurate work even though it is classified fiction. My home was just a few miles from La Grange Georgia and after reading the book I drove down and found the Clayton family. They have the "colonel Clayton" manuscript. When it was delivered to them it was in pretty rough shape and Mr Henry did have to fill in quite a bit because of the faded out sections. Overall it is a remarkable work and super important. They were going to donate it to the army but the army wasn't interested because it showed a goodly portion of Custer's vain glorious arrogance and totally reckless conduct that cost several hundred good men including most of his siblings their lives. When asking the family what they intended to do with the manuscript, they said that since Will Henry had done such an outstanding job they would offer it to a university that was interested.
