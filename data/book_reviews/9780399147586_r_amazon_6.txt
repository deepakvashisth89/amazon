From the onset we know who the serial killer is in this series installment. Yet, Mr. Sandford can work a sick mind into the story very well. A police procedural, the mystery is set when the reader wonders if the maniac will be brought to justice or slip through technical cracks in the judicial system.The ending has a good surprise to the procedural, one the reader won't see coming.The usual cast of characters is present, and their personalities well presented.Admittedly, I for one, could well do without Weather's character. I find her a complete departure from the others and plainly annoying.The book is a good steady paced read. Go for it.Read moreRead less
