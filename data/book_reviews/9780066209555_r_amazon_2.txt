Interesting glimpse into the glamourous world of the "Kept Women" of the 18th & 19th centuries.  I'm always intrigued with the lives of women during different eras; and how the social mores of their times influenced how they lived.  The author explains a bit of the history of "kept women", and how they chose that path of life - an indiscreet & impulsive one time love affair could have "ruined" a young women and made her "unsuitable" for marriage.  I guess if you've got nothing to loose, and everyone already thinks your a "tramp" - may as well be a well taken cared of one.  I believe the author used as much source material as she possibly could get her hands on, and researched history of the eras, but I guess I was a little disappointed there were not more juicy and scandalous details - but I guess that may have been the point, these were everyday women, living in a role defined by society for them.  Also, I didn't feel like the women portrayed (each is given basically her own chapter), jumped off the page and came alive for me - I never felt like I knew them.
