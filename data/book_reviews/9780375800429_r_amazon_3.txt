My four-year-old grandson discovered this at the library and insisted on checking it out. He loved it so much that I bought him his own copy. We have a lot of alphabet books, but this is far and away his favorite. He is fascinated by the scenes with familiar Sesame Street characters, and he is starting to get a better understanding of the alphabet and what you can do with it. The numbers part is less interesting to him, but he is already quite comfortable with numbers and how to use them.I wish we had discovered this when he was even younger, but we are delighted that he likes it now.
