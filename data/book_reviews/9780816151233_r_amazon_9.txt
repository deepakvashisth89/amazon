I am not a die hard fan of Stephen King but I enjoy his writing very much. The most attractive part about his writing is that he gives a lot of clues about the horror or supernatural elements throughout his stories for the readers to find it out. Thus, you also can be a part of the story itself. You won't find any demon on the curdboard here, neither under the bed nor beside the doors but The supernatural or horror staffs will be with you the whole moment and you can feel and understand its presence in every page of the book.This is the 9th book of SK for me and I can say it is the most underrated one. I heard about many of his works like The Stand, IT, Salem's Lot etc etc but not this and I feel this book deserves something more because the writing is very much engaging and incredible. The plot is very much different and the tension between the characters are constant throughout the book. I recommend this book to any horror/thriller fans and especially those who are new to his writing styles and confused how to start a SK novel. Its writing style is much easies than the other ones and the book is also just 450 pages and you can go through it very easily. I really loved this one very much.Read moreRead less
