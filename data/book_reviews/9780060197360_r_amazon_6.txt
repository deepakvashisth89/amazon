Pascale Le Draoulec a West Coast food editor who has never tasted American pie has taken a job on the East Coast.  She decides drive from California to the new job in Connecticut and use the trip to experience American pies.  Le Draoulec tells of adventures and scenery and people she meets along the way.  And she also describes regional pie favorites.  Several recipes are included.This is a must read for someone craving light reading and a good slice of pie!Read moreRead less
