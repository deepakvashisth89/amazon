For a book written only 11 years ago, the homophobia in it is just astounding. Anti-gay slurs abound from the mouths of the characters, who, of course, are creations of the author. The most evil man in the novel, a Nazi-like police chief, is a closet case with a predilection for pretty young men. The author created that character too. When another character uses racist words, he is taken to task. Nothing of the sort happens to anyone who uses anti-gay slurs and one can only conclude that the author wanted it that way. I cannot recommend this piece of trash to anyone. I  looked at the author's blog, where he very unconvincingly denies being a homophobe, but quit seeing a doctor who he thought might be interested in him without even finding out if he was right.
