A murder mystery where the murderer has confessed.....There's bound to be more than a simple  murder and the book does not disappoint. The cameo by the public hangman was a good touch. For those that don't know, the botched hanging at Exeter refers to the case of Babbacombe Lee 'the man they couldn't hang'. His sentance was commuted after the third attempt.
