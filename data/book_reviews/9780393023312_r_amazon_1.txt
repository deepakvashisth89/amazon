This book was so refreshingly straightforward and unembellished. Description of the traveling life in Ireland and England circa the 1970s and beyond. Nan is just so honest about the good and bad points of being a traveler. One can really grasp the changes between the classic archaic image of the gypsy and the modern-day equivalent. No doubt much has changed since Nan's day also; I'd love a similar book written today. This was a fascinating read.
