Four unique, real-life people receive mysterious invitations to Haven, Nebraska, but they don't know why or from whom. Curious and fearful, each feels compelled to respond to the invitation. With them, we are drawn into a world abounding in mystery, danger, and miracles as the characters make discoveries about themselves and each other and learn why they have been called and by whom. Salvation is presented and each person is challenged to follow God's calling using the gifts and talents God has given them.
