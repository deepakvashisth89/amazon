First person story of an 8ear old boy in Germany from 1933-1943. Focuses mostly on his experience in the Hitler Youth prior to joining the army. You can see how people were coerced or seduced into following Hitler, as the various characters in the book grow up and the war impacts their families. Unfortunately the story is told in unrelated snippets, and stops abruptly during a battle in 1943.
