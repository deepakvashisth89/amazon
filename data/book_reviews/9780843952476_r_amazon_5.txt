I'm sure you remember as a kid going to fireworks displays and just how many times the supposedly "awesome" ones kind of died in midair.  This is what I feel about FIREWORKS, a rambling, incohesive, dull character study.  While the idea of letting us get to know characters well is commendable, why write a book about an alien spaceship that crashes into a lake and not explore it any further?  Little comes of the spaceship, except the usual government coverup.  Most of the characters are stereotyped to the max, and those few you care about bite the dust.  And then, the spaceship just goes away, no explanations, and we're given this coy cutesy ending.  What a waste of trees.Read moreRead less
