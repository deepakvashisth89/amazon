Fair's fair is a good story.  There are five characters called Jackson, Lillypolly, the dog Growler,and two old men.  The story is set in the middle of old Victorian London.  I enjoyed it because it is an exciting story.  Jackson and Lillypolly have no parents.  When Jackson shares his pie with the dog, it leads to a big adventure.
