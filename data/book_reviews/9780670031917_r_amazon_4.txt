Book review by Denise GodwinThe Sixth Lamentation by William Brodrick.This is the first Brother Anselm mystery in which the Augustinian monk brings his past skills as lawyer to a problem facing his Priory. It is a rich narrative,  set during the French Occupation of Paris, bringing multiple points of view and moving from  the Parisian camp Drancy, briefly to Rome and back to England.  At the centre is the desire, amidst widespread deportations to death camps, to save the Jewish children. We see good people who helped and bad who hindered, and some who are ambiguous in-betweeners, just like real life. The truth of the characters' actions comes out only at the end, holding the reader truly absorbed in a way similar to the writing of John le Carré.Read moreRead less
