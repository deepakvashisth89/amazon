As a conspicuous consumer of vampire novels, I am often looking for  something "new under the moon."  Blood Walk filled the bill most  satisfyingly.  With it's well developed characters and neatly twisting  plot,  it's a refreshing departure from some of the worn cliches of the  genre.  I found myself reading hte final chapters slowly, only because I  wasn't ready to say good-bye to Garreth Mikalean just yet, who by the end  of the book became someone I wanted to give a nice warm bowl of blood to.
