The Vietnam War, for those who did not experience it first hand, is sometimes hard to understand. Sometimes it's easier to understand something as complex and emotionally intense through allegorical stories because the setting forces us to leave our natural prejudices behind and gain a better perspective. This collection of short stories and a novella has made me think about aspects of the Vietnam War and warfare in general that I would have been less likely to examine otherwise.
