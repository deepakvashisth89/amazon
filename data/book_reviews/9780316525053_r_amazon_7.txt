This book is a clear and detailed analysis of the reasons for the blow-out of the WWI and of the development of the conflict. THe author is the famous historian and expert of military theories Sir Basil H. Liddell Hart.The first 80 pages are dedicated to the origins of the war that he attributes to the supremacy of the generals above the politicians, for all the most important nations : Germany was scared by the Russian rearming, Austria was scared by the new Serbia (that doubled her population after the Balcanic wars 1912-13); Russia was scared by the Austro-German alliance and so on.All the others 500 pages are dedicated to the development of the war with a great care to examine the strategical mistakes happened during the war: the British insult to the Ottoman Empire with the requisition of the two dreadnoughts under construction, the movement of the centre of gravity of the first German offensive in France, the confues ideas that brought GReat britain and France to the disastrous landins at Gallipoli, the useless series of Allied offensives on the western Front with their huge amount of losses, and so on.This book is a precious one, don't miss it.Read moreRead less
