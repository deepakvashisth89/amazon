A Man Called Blessed is a pretty good sequel to A Child Called Blessed but I think I’m partial to former. I enjoyed the story as I always do with Ted’s books and was moved by the message of reclaiming your faith. It’s a beautiful reminder of the new covenant. I didn’t love the ending though. A bit anticlimactic for me. Plus I am still unclear about what happened to Ismael. Is he dead? Did Rebecca just knock him out? Not my favorite but still a good read.Read moreRead less
