As with Wisdom Hunter and Jordan's Crossing, Betrayal takes you on a  journey through several countries and states to expose the legalistic  church.  Devastated after her husband leaves her, Rachel McCain turns to  the only one that she thinks she can depend on for support--her church.  Instead of finding the strength and unconditional love that she needs, she  finds a long list of "do's" and "don'ts" that she now  has to live by.  Rachel slowly begins to realize that the strict,  legalistic church does not offer the emotional stabilty that she craves,  and she begins to search for anwers elsewhere.  As in Jordan's Crossing,  Rev. Jason Faircloth plays an important part in Rachel's renewal of her  spirit and her faith in God and the church.  Betrayal is an excellent novel  and will keep you reading whether or not you have ever questioned the  "rules" that go along with Christianity.
