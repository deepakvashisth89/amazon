I purchased the unabridged CD.  The narration, especially by Joe Montanga, was excellent - and the Art of War is a classic that is both short and profound.  The real value in the set, however, is the narrative history and interpretation before and after the actual reading of the Sun Tzu classic.  Taking up nearly 3 of the 4 CD's, this interesting if somewhat belabored look at the application of the theory while hearing the stories of numerous historical Chinese realms and personages really add to the base work.
