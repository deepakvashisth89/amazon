This is a review of the Kindle format of this book, not of the book itself, which, like all Posner's books, is well-written and provocative.The Kindle version of this book is, however, an abomination. The main reason I say that is that the Kindle version does not properly integrate the text and the footnotes. The reader can't toggle between them. That may not seem to be a problem for those who don't read footnotes. But the footnotes are a key part of this book. Posner wants you to read them, and believe me, you want to read them, even if you don't think you do. So the inability to toggle between the two is a disaster for the reader.I read a lot of Kindle books with footnotes. The smooth integration of text and footnotes has become routine in most of these books. There's no excuse for a book that is badly formatted in this regard to still be on the market. The publisher should withdraw the Kindle version and fix this problem.In the meantime, you are far better off buying this book in hard copy, assuming you can still find it.
