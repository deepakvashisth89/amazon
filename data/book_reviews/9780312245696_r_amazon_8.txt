I have found recent "Let's Go" books to be less-than-accurate, and large on wit / short on usefulness. Several items in my 2000 version that out-of-date by March 2000: internet cafe closed, business changed hands. Most directions give mass transit directions only, so it's not helpful for auto travel. Even in March, I found some recommended accomodations were booked; could it be worse in summer?In spite of some inaccuracies (perhaps inevitable with businesses targeting budget travelers), if you are young, and new to budget travel, the "Let's Go" series will enlighten you on the spirit of traveling by backpack.  But mainstream guides (Frommer's) will give you more useful information in a better format. Look at alternate publications before committing yourself to this book: it's heavy, black &white, and too loaded with opinions where it's not called for.Read moreRead less
