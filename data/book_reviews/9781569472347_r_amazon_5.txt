It would be hard not to like this author.  Although his sense of humor is quite different from mine, I laughed aloud & often through the first half. But something happened at almost exactly halfway that the author himself figured out and shared about three-quarters through the trip.  He had chosen an undertaking that gave him (mostly) superficial connections with a variety of Japanese people, but which left him lonely, anxious to be done, & mostly spent by the end.  Three places in the book, I tagged insights that provided better context (i.e., Ainu indigenous peoples), or personal musings on what this was about, that I wanted to go back & read again.
