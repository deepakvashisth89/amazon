I grabbed Yasunari Kawabata's The Sound of the Mountain at the same time I picked up his Snow Country. I loved the latter so much, i dove right in to the former, and made sure I had all of his works in English on order immediately. Definitely talking crazed binge, here. The most magnificent new writer of my experience.The Sound of the Mountain is a troubled domestic drama told largely from the viewpoint of the declining patriarch, as he watches helplessly as his children flail and fail in their marriages. The meditative dialogue of such rich complexity as to evoke Ibsen or Chekhov. The personal drama is set against and amidst the narrative of nature, of climate and animal emotion and evolution. I've only ever experienced such synchronicity of human and innate nature in the works of Madison Smartt Bell.The absolute crystallization and distilled perfection of Kawabata's intuitively masterful prose is unsurpassed.A masterpiece.Read moreRead less
