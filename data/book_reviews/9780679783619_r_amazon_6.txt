I am Russian. I have read the book and I can say it's quite well written but it's not the original text of the Albanov's journal. Original text was strongly abridged (about one-third was lost) and changed. So we have no translation, we have a freestyle retelling of Albanov's story. This interpretation made the original text lose a lot of its details, meanings and the very voice of author. I guess that main part of this bad work was done in the first French version. Sometimes I can't understand what was the reason to replace/distort some words of Albanov with false variants even in insignificant details.
