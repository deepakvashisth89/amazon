This small book tells the story of a great hearted artist who postponed his own self-actualization to provide for his wife and their many pups and grand-pups. The selfless love of the protagonist is both touching and a little sad. One feels wistful that in his lifetime his greatest aspiration was delayed, but becomes overjoyed to realize that the faithful dog artist is rewarded in the next life. This is a childrens' story for young, old, and in between, all who dream and juggle life's demands meanwhile.
