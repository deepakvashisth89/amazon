I have been reading books of a mostly political nature lately, due to the times we are living in. I came across many older books I never got around to and ordered a few of them  When I came across this one, it was something I had always wanted to read. but thought it might be a little intimidating. That I would be in over my head and would have too much over hashed information I couldn't wait to get through. I was very, very wrong.I couldn't put it down. It had so much information that I was ignorant of. Watching TV in those days after the assassination, hours and hours of it, I thought there was nothing new I could know. We have all heard the story many times. But not like this. This was an emotional journey for me.I have a new respect for the people involved. It was catastrophic. I made assumptions of these people based on what little information I had, knowing nothing of what they went through. There are so many details of every aspect of what those involved individually went through. This was an event that shaped the lives of all Americans who lived through it and this book is something every one of us should read. We knew so few details of the actual events that it seems like a completely different story, but it isn't.I have read other books on the subject but they didn't cover the story we needed to know. What happened minute to minute. What happened at the hospital, the situation in Dallas before, during and after the fact, the funeral and most of all the many people personally involved and how it affected each and every one of them. I didn't have any idea.The author knew Kennedy personally and it must have been a horrible ordeal for him to interview the many people involved. He would have to go through the event over and over. Then write about it in such a manner that grips your heart. I am so glad I read this book. It impacted me in a way no other book ever has. It changed my whole outlook on so much - so many years later. I will be reading it again just to make sure I got it all.Read moreRead less
