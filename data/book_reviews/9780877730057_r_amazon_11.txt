Evans-Wentz is a legend.  He has his critics, but he brought many important philosophical and religious texts to English readers that might otherwise have remain hidden; although here he merely introduces a new translation of an existing Zen classic, the Diamond Sutra. The second sutra is introduced by the wonderfullly named Christmas Humphreys.  Evans-Wentz’s message to the reader is, however, equally pertinent to the texts of both Sutras, and is simply to clear your mind and practically apply the teachings if you want to make progress towards the goal of acquiriing wisdom.  That said, the true meaning of the texts is only revealed when you understand the Buddha’s message that when you finally achieve the wisdom you seek you have, in fact, achieved nothing at all.
