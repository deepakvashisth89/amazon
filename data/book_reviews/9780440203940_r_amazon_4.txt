Like many I read the mongo mysteries as they came out in the 1970's and 80's. Of course, back then it wasn't always that easy to get new releases as I lived in a small town. I was delighted when the books were re-released and I bought the entire 14 book series immediately for my Kindle and began re-reading them in order. Sadly, I've been disappointed in "The Cold Smell of Sacred Stone". I'm 70% of the way through and nothing has happened except a LOT of dialogue surrounding outdated ideas about mental health and associated stereotypes. It's biggest sin for being in the "Mongo-verse" is that it is b-o-r-i-n-g.
