I liked how the author spared herself nothing being totally honest, and how she didn't miraculestly with the help of therapy, get better over night.  I beleive a lot of  survivers (incest, child abuse, battered spouses etc.)  will see themselves in the wanting so much to be loved by someone who hurts you. And I also like how she didn't demonize her father, the truth was bad enough ,and even told how sometimes he would do nice things for her. The  possessiveness envoled in this type of abuse and how sex and love hoave nothing to do with it.  The fact that abuse always needs three individuals, so to speak, one to commit the act, one to enable, and one to be the victim /surviver. In this case her mother was the enabler turning up the radio at night when her husband was raping and torturing their daughter.  And all for the sake of presenting the perfect family image to the world.  To concluded this story is both highly disturbing and uplifiting.
