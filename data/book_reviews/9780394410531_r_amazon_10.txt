I loved this book.  Enjoyed every moment of reading it.  It is a story of what happens after a young wife, feeling suffocated, suddenly leaves her marriage, abandoning her husband and four year-old son.  How the seeming catastrophe becomes a stable, happy life is only the start of this tale.  The story is clever, funny, suspenseful and beautifully written.  It is even more enjoyable than the movie (Kramer Versus Kramer) based on it.  Right at the end is an interesting section on Avery Corman which tells us how the circumstances of his life helped him gain deep insight into family dynamics.Brilliant!Read moreRead less
