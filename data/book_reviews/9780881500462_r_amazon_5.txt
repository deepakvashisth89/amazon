If you like all the modern mysteries that talk more about a person's job, sex life, or hobby, you won't like this.  Not only is it dated, it is a pure cozy mystery.  It's corny, dated, and not "literature".  It is a lot of fun.  I own all the Asey Mayo books in paperback and plan to gradually add all of them to my kindle.
