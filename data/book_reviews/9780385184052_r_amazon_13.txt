Cornwall, 1812. The Poldark family finds the new year brings new and surprising ventures. For Ross and Demelza there is some surprising and worrying news. Clowance, finds her entanglement with Stephen Carrington brings happiness and heartache. As the armies battle in Spain, the political situation at home worsens. The Poldark and Warleggan families find themselves thrust into new and turbulent times.Oh it is such a delight to be back with the Poldark's and Graham really is on form with this book. So much happens in 'The Miller's Dance', the situation at the end is completely different to the beginning, there is non-stop action and I loved it. Overall, this is a happier instalment than some of the other Poldark books, of course there is still trouble and heartache for the characters to go through and I would not expect anything less.I enjoyed the main focus being back with the Poldark's. Ross and Demelza have a big plot line and it was such a joy to focus with them again. The Poldark children have big roles in this, which again was great to see and it was wonderful to see their individual characters develop and shine through. The Warleggan's feature as well, not as prominently as they do in other books but they develop a lot in this.Overall, it was an utter joy to be back with the Poldark's and this was a delight to read from start to finish.Read moreRead less
