The suthor, who had spent years in communication with a book store in England, finally had the whereithall to travel to England to meet many of the people with whom she had corresponded.  Over the years, she had built strong friendships and sent packages of food hard to obtain in England.  When her book about this unusual relationship was published, the funds enabled her to visit her pen-pal friends, but on a very strict budget.  The book tells of her joy in seeing the historic sights and meeting people who essentially adopted her, making her trip more pleasurable and memorable.  A great story!
