39 Steps by John Buchan is the font for virtually all action thrillers of the 20th century. The concept of introducing the character (hero), listing the problem and starting the clock, all virtually on the first page influenced not only Alfred Hitchcock but many other writers and film makers.While dated with some allusions which are at best quaint, the story fills in with myriad problems, solutions and questionable characters, it is fun to read. The chase across the moors is a classic, the lying of most of the antagonists will have you sitting on the edge of your morris chair.
