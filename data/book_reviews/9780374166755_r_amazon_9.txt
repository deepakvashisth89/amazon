With a lifelong interest in Greene's work, I've read the major biographies. "Green on Capri" looked like a novelty but it was no such thing. I learned more about the man himself in this little book than in several hundred pages of biographies; he was a complex and original man as well as great writer, and Shirley Hazzard captures all of that in her own excellent prose. Often a rather moving story about how times have changed and what we have lost. I thoroughly enjoyed this book.Read moreRead less
