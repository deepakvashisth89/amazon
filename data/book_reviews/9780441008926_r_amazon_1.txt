Starting in about the year 2050, Earth sent a thousand ships to explore nearby star system for places humans could colonize.  The ships contained a crew of engrams, which were electronic facsimiles/copies of a small group of humans.The story centers mainly on Peter Alander, whose engram contained serious flaws making it difficult for him to concentrate.  An amazing discovery is made on the planet of their ship's destination and he is sent back to Earth to tell of the discovery.Upon returning to Earth he finds that Earth society is almost unrecognizable.  And that turns out to be the least of his problems.The book unfortunately has no satisfactory ending.  I will be reading the sequels to find out how it all ends.Read moreRead less
