This book turned out to be something other than what I first expected, as I was anticipating another "time management" book.  Though there are elements of time management here, what this book really offers is a sound approach to managing projects.  Such clusters of multi-step tasks comprise so much of our lives at work and at home.  Whether you are converting your office software over to Windows Vista or aiming to lower your golf handicap by 10 strokes, you are dealing with projects.At times the authors lay it on a bit heavy about the Franklin Quest (now Franklin Covey) approach, but that is a minor quibble.If you want to learn the sound "blocking and tackling" fundamentals of prohect management, invest time reading this practical book!Read moreRead less
