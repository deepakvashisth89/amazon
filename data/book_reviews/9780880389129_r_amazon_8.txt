I honestly can't think of a Dragonlance book I didn't love. Until now. However, I feel partly to blame. I was very excited by the title. I was fully expecting an action packed Dwarven adventure and couldn't wait to hear about life in Thorbardin. But this book isn't about Thorbardin or Dwarves. It's part of the "heroes" series which means it was about one Dwarf. I was turned off right away by the pack of big cats that was chasing them for some reason. Toss in a few uninteresting Dwarves, and a supporting cast of annoying characters with horrible names. On top of everything else, they didn't even come close to Thorbardin until the last chapter of the book. I'm a HUGE Dragonlance fan but I honestly have nothing good to say about this book. I had to force myself to keep reading it. I never thought I'd hear myself saying words like this about a Dragonlance book. It hurts me just to write this.Read moreRead less
