Bought this book on a whim, sPlot seemed decent. It was much better than my original "eh with a shoulder shake" attitude. The setup was good, it captured my interest and thats what a good author should do. I wanted to know how they would get past everything and have their happily ever after. When I finished the book I had a smile on my face, and a few months later I reread it again. I liked the main characters and the secondary ones were nice to "see" when their presence in the story needed them to be there. You know sometimes youread a book and there is THAT one character you roll your eyes at and cant wait til they go away haha...i have no buyers remorse with this book and would buy again if needed.Read moreRead less
