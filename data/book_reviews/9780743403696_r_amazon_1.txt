I love the portrayal of the world of Star Trek in this novel; Diane Duane's imagination and love of sci-fi details makes the Rihannsu and the incredibly diverse crews of the Starfleet ships feel more alive than ever.It was a joy seeing the depiction of the non-humanoid Starfleet members, and both the Federation and Rihannsu crews to feel like a massive ensemble instead of mere background players (the Horta ensign in particular was a delight).For fans of TOS and sci-fi in general, this is one book you won't want to miss.Read moreRead less
