I'm lowering this from a 2* to 1* partially because I finished it and partially because of the Public Service Announcement I'm putting at the end of my review.Original 2* review:Some authors (Stephen King has a reputation for it) tend to write stories where being dumb/ignorant/etc is a survival trait and having a clue/plan gets you 'et. I think this book decided to go with that.Just observations:Even in the pre LED days I carried a small (keychain) flashlight pretty much 100% of the time (it's like a superpower, something drops under a table (poof) Flashlight Man!). In this book over a hundred people that are 100s of feet underground = 0 flashlights.Over a hundred people that are 100s of feet underground = 0 cigarette lighters.Haven't made it all the way through but... I'm betting nobody is carrying an "engineers purse". Thus named because I've seen some of what a female engineer carries in her purse (multimeter, tape measure, screwdrivers, tape...). She'd FIX the stupid ship, not be running around like a chicken with its head cut off.This group has scientists, soldiers, and random people... And the theme seems to be that they're all idiots.Even the alien ship is an idiot. Funny how its damage comes and goes just right for the plot.2* based on the basic plot idea being cool (I wish a Heinlein-esque author had used it)... And the writing style (dialog etc) is fine.Bottom line: Unless you are trapped with nothing to read, don't read this.(new, slight spoiler for the book)PSA:If someone around you hasA. Suffered a major head injury and is going between hallucinating, unconsciousness, and making comments about their feet being mounted backwards...AndB. Is carrying a firearm (particularly as in this story a fully automatic military rifle) it would be AN OBVIOUSLY GOOD IDEA to remove it from their possession (duh).If I have to tell you this, please avoid having children or being responsible for anyone, and wait for your Darwin Award.Minor note: I read an interview with the author... Sounds like a nice person. Said he was doing mostly Horror now because he lacked the (paraphrase) science background for SF and Horror wasn't as picky. Yes. Please. Do. Thank you.Read moreRead less
