Another terrific story from the best writer of spy novels.  This one follows the career of an agent from his joining the Service in the 1960's until his retirement.  Each chapter tells its own incident, and some include George Smiley (always a plus).  The writing just sucks me in each time I read John LeCarre, and The Secret Pilgrim is no exception.  It's as if I were there, listening to, and watching and participating with the main character.  At 384 pages, I feel I got my "money's worth".  LeCarre writes densely, and much of what he writes  I want to read again and savor, so the book "lasted" for me.  If you enjoy totally immersing yourself in a book, read this one.Read moreRead less
