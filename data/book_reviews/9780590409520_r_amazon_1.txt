Occasionally something crosses our paths in life that causes us to pause and take notice of our own species and its capacity for empathy and understanding. What perhaps is most startling is that those moments come from something far outside ourselves. This story never fails to open us to that empathy because it is so simple, so pure and so human....despite the fact that the stimulus for all of these emotions comes from a gorilla and her need to nurture and feel connection.
