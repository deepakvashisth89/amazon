I have never written a review of any book that I've read before but after reading this book I felt I had to write about it.  One wouldn't be reading such a book unless they were having crisis situations involving painful estrangements.  I have had a very rocky situation with my 19 year old daughter as a result of a relentlessly bad divorce pending from the time she was 14; basically my ex tainted her against me and over the past 4 years we have spent no more than 2 months together because it has been so volatile.  This book taught me to calm the voices and put aside the bad stuff; I have been able to reconcile with my daughter on her terms and from reading this book I've learned techniques to do this.  I would rather be in my daughter's life than not.  This book tells of horrific estrangement stories; and yet it also speaks of amazing reconciliations.  It also deals with how to handle situations where reconciliation is just not possible and how to deal with that as well.  This book is a must read for anyone dealing with estrangement from children, parents and friends.
