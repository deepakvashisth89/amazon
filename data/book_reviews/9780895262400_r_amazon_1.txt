This book is a very quick read and presents a very compelling and straightforward case of how to resolve the social security problem and give everybody a greater and more profitable vested interest in their future.  Written pre-Roth IRA, the ideas here go toward removing government mismanagement, interference and poor performance when it comes to the retirement funds available to U.S. citizens.  The author is a life-long democrat proposing a common sense approach more like that of a libertarian, but grounded in the concept of each of us taking responsibility for our financial future and not hoping some government miracle will create something from nothing for our children and grandchildren.
