I was excited to find this book because my kids have been learning a lot about space in their Co-op classes.  But the sister is always calling the younger brother protagonist names and belittling his interests.  It doesnt even stop after he saves her from his robot that turned against them.  After saving her she calls him a "muffin head".  My son and daughter have a really great relationship.  She is always encouraging him and building him up (at least MOST of the time).  The negativity was foreign to them and I didnt read any of her name calling dialogue.  Poor choice on the authors part.  Would have been a lot more fun without all the name calling.
