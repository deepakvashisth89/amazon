I got this book for my 11 year old son, who absolutely adores chickens. (We even have a pet backyard flock of our own who gives us hours of delight and entertainment just with their comical antics.)  So if chickens are your thing, this is the perfect book for you.  It shows mainly amazing photographs of these birds in a variety of settings.  The only complaint I have about it was that it is not thick enough.  (That's why the author wrote a sequel.)  This is one book that will keep you busy admiring the beautiful birds within for a while.
