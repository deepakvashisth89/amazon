I m half way through Kydd (first book in a series) and am very favorably impressed with the writer's style and the historical detail of the book.  I have all of C.S. Forester's books along with Patrick O'Brian's in my library and can see that the art of detailed historical fiction about life at sea in the Napoleonic Wars continues....whether the quality is as good as Forester's or O'Brian's will be seen...but so far, 'Kydd' does not disappoint me.
