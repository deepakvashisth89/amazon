Stef Ann Holm has a lovely style. This story is squarely about the H/h growing as people and as a couple. Josephine and J.D. were really likable. There are no bad guys interfering with the story and the secondary characters all have charm, including Boots. I loved Boots but his frequent, egregious use of "y'all" in the singular tense was jarring and annoying. This man had owned a plantation, was a southern aristocrat, undoubtedly educated - it just didn't seem something such a man would say, regardless of how cranky he was. That was my only real quibble with this story. I can always rely on this author for an enjoyable historical western read.Read moreRead less
