I first read this book about 8 years ago and thought at the time it was pretty funny.  Those old paperbacks have type that's too small for my eyes anymore, though, so I was glad to see it for the Kindle at $1.99.Reading it again, I didn't enjoy it so much.  The story gets lost in the ocean of booze the characters drink morning, noon and night.  Every problem is improved - and sometimes caused - by having another drink.
