John Muir describes the natural world he encountered in detail. This includes plant life, animal behavior, mountains, glaciers, and the weather. He gives the reader a taste of his experience from stunning views of the aurora borealis to the anxiety of crossing glaciers in the dark.He also introduces the readers to people he meets during his journey. I found myself wondering how accurately he portrays some of the Native Americans when he says they welcomed the teachings of missionaries and deferred to them.
