This is one of the funniest books I have ever read!  "The Secret Diary of Adrian Mole" will strike a chord with any adolescent or anyone who has ever been an adolescent.  Even though Adrian is a character from the '80s, his book is still very fresh and rib-crackingly hilarious.  This is two books in one.  You also get the second Adrian Mole book, "The Growing Pains of Adrain Mole".  If you read the "Secret Diary", read the "Growing Pains" to see what happens to Adrain next.
