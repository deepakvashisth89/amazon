I bought up to volume 3 of this series... and what a waste of money.I found the main character irritating (Shuichi) and the other guy (Yuki) personality just screams stereotype.The series is supposed to be funny- but it just comes off as cheesy and trying too hard.Overall regarding the characters and relationships/romances, it comes across weak and lack of depth. The basic story had potential though.Even though I have up to volume 3, I never even finished the 3rd volume. I just found it so disappointing that I left off in the middle of the book and have never bothered to pick it up again.
