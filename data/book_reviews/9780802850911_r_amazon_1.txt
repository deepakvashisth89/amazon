This was the slowest of all the Gloria Whelan books I have read so I left out one star, but then I want to give some Whelan books six stars so maybe this one is at least a four and a half.  It is lovely and full of poetry, but there just isn't the usual dose of drama in it.  A girl goes to live with grandparents and meets an annoying boy and learns to appreciate a slow-paced rural life.  That's about it.  The story feels like it was written as a vehicle for framing the poetry that appears frequently.  But there is a story even if it is not as exciting as some, and it IS beautifully written, including the poetry.Read moreRead less
