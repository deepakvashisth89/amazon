The Jung Cult is an indispensable history of not only Jung and his spiritual movement but also of fin de siecle Europe and the ideological milieu which spawned Psychoanalysis, Theosophy, and National Socialism.  It is interesting to see the parallels between this era, especially the Ascona period, and the 1960's counterculture: spiritual seeking, sexual liberation, free expression through dance and art, drug use, nudism, immersion in natural environments (e.g. hiking), etc.  I would go so far as to say that both periods are part of the same line of cultural development,or degeneracy, depending on your perspective.  The gift of this work, however, lies in the exposure of Jung's thought as fundamentally irrational and narcissistic.
