Robert Barnard is one of the best mystery writers working today. Bird in a Guilded Cage is not just classic Barnard, it's funny and populated with colorful characters and unexpected twists and turns that keep the reader engaged. When the patriarch of a working class London family inherits the title and the vast estate he never expected to come into, someone one wants him dead. His vaguely larcenous family and friends provide plenty of suspects, but it's up to his eldest son and heir, just released from jail, to figure out who dunnit. It's not just the social satire, it's Barnard's sharp plotting and character that keep this moving along to a surprise ending.Read moreRead less
