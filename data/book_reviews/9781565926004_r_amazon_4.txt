A must buy for any serious user who wants to do more than store phone numbers and keep track of appointments! The native software that comes with the palm organizers is only the beginning. David Pogue explains in  practical and enjoyable terms ways to enhance the capabilities of every  aspect of your organizer. The CD ROM that accompanies the book is an  invaluble source of add on software(freeware, shareware and 3rd party). The  book is an excellent resource for troubleshooting and expanding the  hardware capabilities as well. I purchased the 1st edition and jumped at  the opportunity to get the 2nd edition as the updated information was  current and allowed me to take full advantage of new opportunities with my  Palm III(with 8Mb of memory thanks to this book).
