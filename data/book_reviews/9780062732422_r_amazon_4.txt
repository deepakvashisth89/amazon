Marshal Crenshaw got 27 people to offer a variety of over 300 reviews and there are two appendixes of additional titles. Besides the expected titles with Led Zeppelin, the Beach Boys, the Beatles, the Rolling Stones, Abba, Meat Loaf, KISS, Elvis Presley, Cliff Richard, the Dave Clark 5, Herman's Hermits, and others, obscurities such as Amor a Ritmo de Go-Go )1967), Be My Guest (1964), Beloved Invaders (1965), Beware (1946), Beyond the Doors (1983), Beyond the Valley of the Dolls (1970), The Big Beat (1957), Breaking Glass (1980), Bummer (1973), The Cool Ones (1967), Darktown Strutters (1974), Du-Beat-E-O (1984), Flame (1974), The Ghost Goes Gear (1966), Hello Down There (1969), Human Highway (1982), Jubilee (1978), Live It Up (1967), Look Out Sister (1948), Melanie (1987), El Mundo Loco de los Jovenes (1967), The Nasty Rabbit (1964), Psych-Out (1968), Rat Pfink a Boo Boo (1965), Serious Charge (1959), Smashing Time (1987), Soul Hustler (1975), The Tigers (1968) are in this book, too.Besides the two movie appendixes with capsule entries, there are also sections on cameos, rock actors filmographies, and an index.There are  a lot of missing titles, but with so many titles with entries, it is okay.Each entry in the main section has credits, a section on songs and artists, a write up with info, and 3 star ratings, one for music, one for fun and one for attitude.Now, do keep in mind that tastes can differ. In the case of Grease (1978), the reviewer, who preferred the source play, gives it one star in each category.Despite the idiosyncratic ratings and the missing titles, this is a book by fans for fans, as oppose to a book that felt like it was a cash in job.Read moreRead less
