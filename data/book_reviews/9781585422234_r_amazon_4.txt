This summer I saw some of the worst "shunning" behavior in a group of volunteer women I was working with. I had to quit the committee (I was not the one being shunned) and I immediately thought of this book and how it applied. This book was invaluable when I was working my way up through a (male dominated) industry - not because of the male co-workers but because I could not understand the behavior of my female co-workers. These authors nailed the behavior patterns I was seeing - and I used many of the strategies they talk about to get more cooperation and alliance with female co-workers. The female subordinate issue gets stickier the higher you go, and I got to subsidiary President - with lots of female direct reports because I supported equity. I have mentioned/leant/bought this book to and for many of the women whom I promoted, in order to help them along their journey. The latest person I just sent it to - my daughter.
