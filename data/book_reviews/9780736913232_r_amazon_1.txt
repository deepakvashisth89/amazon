I have read the first 3 of The Kensington Chronicles & they have all been wonderful. I have to say that Who Brings Forth The Wind was my favorite so far. This book combines Romance and bringing people to Christ. Lori Wick is a master at combining these two subjects.I loved the two main characters, Stacey & Tanner and the many others that follow along.  She brings characters from her other Kensington Chronicles books. It’s always nice to catch up with old friends. She is a master at building her characters.
