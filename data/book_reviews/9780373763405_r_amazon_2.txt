This is the first in a multi-author series, Texas Cattleman's Club: Lone Star Jewels. And I think that's the problem.Greene has once again created characters that I care about, and the series premise was interesting: colorful history of the Club, the secret help missions of the members, two foreign countries reconciling, and of course, the rare gems.However, these didn't save the book for me. Introducing a large number of characters and situations for future books in the series really cramped the time with the characters in THIS book. Foreshadowing was awkward and heavy handed, and the dialogue was stilted and artificial. Example: our hero thought and said "Texas Cattleman's Club" like three times a page; not TCC or The Club or Cattleman's, or any number of abbreviations that people would have come up with when something is part of their everyday life. It was so obviously a publisher's push for name recognition - This is part of a SERIES! Read the rest of the SERIES! - that it was jarring.I slogged through parts of this book and cannot recommend.Read moreRead less
