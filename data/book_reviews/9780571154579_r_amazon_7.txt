I had to keep reminding myself that it was quite daring to portray male relationships/loves in 1930's literature.  For that attribute, I can cut the author some slack.  However, this novella was rambling and poorly written.  There is not much psychological depth to the characters.  Their dialogue is often trite and does little contribute to a plot.  For a poet, I thought Spender's language was flat and rarely imaginative.
