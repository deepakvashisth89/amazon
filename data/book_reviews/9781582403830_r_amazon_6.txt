This little story is 100% pure satire. Amongst the laughs, however, it poses some really serious questions about the concept of superheroes; why is it that bad things would happen in a world full of heroes? Where was Superman on September 11th? It's an excellent piece of writing with a good chunk of self-indulgent nastiness.
