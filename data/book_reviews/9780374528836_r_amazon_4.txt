I am a John McPhee fan, for many reasons.  His books are readable, and you learn a lot without ever feeling like you are just having facts thrown at you.  He makes his subject, in this case a fish called a shad, interesting!  In learning about this fish, you will also learn about early USA history, among other things.  Who knew shad had such an impact on early Americana!If it is by John McPhee, it will be a good read, and you will learn things you never would have thought of, and enjoy the painless lessons.  He has a style of writing that is flat out enjoyable, whether his subject is oranges or the crazy geology of California!  Read McPhee.  You won't be sorry, and this book in particular has a lot of interesting things in it circa the founding of this Nation and even present day, while telling us the story of shad.  Things are far more intertwined and complex than we think, and John McPhee takes us on an interesting journey with this book, and I recommend this book to anyone.Read moreRead less
