The formal ritual of taking tea in Japan centers on the exquisitely beautiful yet subtly plain ceramic tea bowls and cups. Through pieces of the tea ritual the novel unfolds family histories, relationships, loves and losses. The main characters struggle with their relationships to each other as well as with the relationship to the tea. However much they protest that they will leave behind taking tea, the tea bowls and cups pull them back to the ceremony and to each other.I knew very little of Japanese traditions coming into this book, and on the basis of the discussion of the tea ceramics alone the reader learned a lot. I feel this education in Japanese culture was heightened by being encased in a novel because it allows the complexity intensely personal nature of the pottery to act as a driving force as the story moves forward.I recommend to readers interested in Japanese culture.Read moreRead less
