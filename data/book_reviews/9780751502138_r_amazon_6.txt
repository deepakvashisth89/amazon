This is an early tale of Cadfael in which he encounters his fiance from before he went to the Crusades. She has married twice, been widowed, and on her second marriage, had a son with a landed gentry on the border of Wales and England.  The laws in the two countries differ, setting up the potential for differing decisions, depending on the country. The Baron is murdered with an overdose of a medicine Cadfael uses, his son is suspected, and Cadfael must sort out who did it.
