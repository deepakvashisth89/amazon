I read the book as a Reader's Digest Book Club pick when I was a child, and I was very pleased to get a copy of the original. The book was in excellent condition and included the dust jacket (which, unfortunately, tore rather quickly — but it was not made of high-grade paper, so something that old is bound to tear). I enjoyed comparing the book to the condensed version, seeing what Reader's Digest changed, and which characters were removed. Note here that I rarely read novels, and Gothic romance is definitely not a genre I would get into.
