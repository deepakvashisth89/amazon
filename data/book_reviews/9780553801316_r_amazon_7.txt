Sallie Bissell knows people; she understands the complexity of personality, and has no inhibition about delving into the darker side of the human mind. In A Darker Justice she has painted a prolific scenario of greed and power counterbalanced by her female protagonist-Mary Crow-who portrays the strength and determination of all women with a purpose.
