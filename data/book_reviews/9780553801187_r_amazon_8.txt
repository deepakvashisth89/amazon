In fact, it may well put you to sleep!  I agree with the S. Berner review and would add the narrator had a definite negative impact on the book.  While using a narrator may be a clever idea in some instances, here it detracts.  We had no idea who this narrator was until about a third of the way in.  The use of British idioms was annoying but finally made some sense when we find out the nationality of the narrator.  This is essentially a story about a non-story, that is, there is nothing to it.  It is really unfortunate for Mesce has written another one about Major Harry Voss, an interesting character, but Mesce fails to do anything with him.
