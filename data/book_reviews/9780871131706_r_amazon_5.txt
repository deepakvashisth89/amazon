OK, Chuck Yeager didn't break the sound barrier in the X-15.  It was the X-1.  They made a movie about it called "The Right Stuff."After reading "For All Mankind" a couple of times right after Chaikin's "A Man on the Moon" and Murray's "Apollo", I'm really disappointed by the number of obvious errors.  Some have already been pointed out above (including a couple that I missed in casual readings).  This isn't like a Star Trek convention -- some of these things are blatant and easily corrected.  And we're talking about common things that anyone can look up in an encyclopedia.I actually like the style of the book, and the perspective from which he writes.  I'm just not sure I can trust the facts in it when I know so many of them to be wrong.
