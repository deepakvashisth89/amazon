In a effort to keep from further burdening my crowded bookcases, I bought this as an eBook. The book itself was everything I’d hoped for as to suspense, an incredibly engaging storyline, and a most believable lead character, but the actual eBook was filled with unnecessary and wildly obvious typographical errors - strings of numbers or special characters mixed with letters, in some cases so ridiculous as to make it impossible to discern the meaning of the missing word or phrase. The ‘publisher’ did a huge disservice to the rare talent of John Bingham, may he rest in peace.I will definitely find more books from this wonderful writer, who himself lived an almost suspense-thriller life in the British Secret Service, but who choose to translate his life into police crime thrillers to stay true to his fellows in the Secret Service to protect that fragile line of knowledge between life and death.Read moreRead less
