Love the Planet Pirates series. I'd recommend The Death of Sleep first, then the 2 Ireta adventures (Dinosaur Planet and Dinosaur Planet survivors, also in a 2 book format: The Mysteries of Ireta). Next I'd read Sassinak, and then finish with Generation Warriors. This series is about family and integrity. Doing what is right in complex situations isn't always easy. Nor is finding love and purpose in life!  Anyway, these books are a good series to read, more story-oriented than tech-specific oriented (unlike David Weber's Honor Harrington series).
