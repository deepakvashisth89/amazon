I have been "power reading" all of the Babylon 5 books which, despite being a huge fan of the show, I had never actually read before. This book, while not the best in the series, is still a worthy read and does a very good job of accurately portraying the characters, while also giving them more depth through interesting, but believable, internal monologues.
