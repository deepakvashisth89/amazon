When a repressed young woman inexplicably disappears from her wealthy family’s New York City home, amateur sleuth Henry Gamadge is called on by a lawyer friend to help locate her.  In other books in this series Gamadge’s route to the discoveries in the mystery takes twists and turns to reach a surprising conclusion.  Not so in this novel.  Gamadge’s investigation is quite straightforward and mostly transparent to the reader.  Even though the narrative unfolds in a more predictable way than in other Daly books, this is still a satisfying read because of the excellent characterizations and the display of Gamadge’s typically perceptive insights into the characters’ psychology.Read moreRead less
