We LOVE Maisy in this house. We are slowly collecting them all. The pacing, artwork, characters, gentleness... All add up to a great series!This book is great for toddlers learning to count. Each stop is numbered and a different character boards the bus. Everyone gets off at the last stop, except little black cat who falls asleep!My kid is really into vehicles and numbers so it's a total win. He gets to call out each bus stop with great enthusiasm.
