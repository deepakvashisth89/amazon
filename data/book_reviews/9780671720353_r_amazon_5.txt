The world created is a wonderful sandbox.  Having read a sample from 'The Children's Hour', I bought this, thinking it would be in it.  Unfortunately, it is not.  I was about to fling the book across the room when I came to the third story, 'Cathouse', which made up for the entirety of the rest of the book by far.
