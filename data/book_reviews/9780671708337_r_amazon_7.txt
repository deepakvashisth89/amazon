The choices of material to include in this 20th Anniversary Collection are pretty head-scratching. For instance, Michael O'Donoghue - probably the most influential writer in NL's history - gets only one article, and it's a rather lame one. The desire to have a fairly equal amount of material from all periods also means there are far too many articles from NL's floundering 80s output.
