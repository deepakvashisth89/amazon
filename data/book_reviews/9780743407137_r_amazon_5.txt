Toby Peters has a new client, Bela Lugosi, who doesn't appreciate copycat Vampires muscling into his act.  Once again, Toby has to withstand a good few beatings as he stubbornly works for his client: his 'thing' is resilience and perseverance rather than brilliant deduction.
