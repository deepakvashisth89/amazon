The time is World War Two, the place is Los Angeles. Private investigator Toby Peters wakes up after drinking a mickey to find John Wayne holding his own gun pointed at him and a body nearby. Peters blearily explains he was asked by the owner of the body to the hotel room for a job and was drugged. Wayne admits he was invited as well and so was the hooker who shows up afterwards. Someone takes a shot at the Duke and Peters searches for the assailant. Along the way, assisted by Swiss midget Gunther Werthman, he's hired by Charlie Chaplin to retrieve some money conned from him by a husband-and-wife team, all the while dodging a tough, suspicious cop and his own brutish police captain brother.Author Stuart M. Kaminsky left Toby Peters for bigger and better things with more than a dozen Toby Peters stories in which the banged-up, low-rent private eye takes on cases for movie stars ranging from Groucho Marx and Judy Garland to Bela Lugosi and Errol Flynn. Kaminsky sets his stories in the late 30's through the early 40's.  A former studio guard, Peters has a nodding acquaintence with some of the movie people which helps position him for his celebrity clients. The results are enough to keep the tough private eye busy.I'm a sucker for celebrity sleuth stories and Kaminksy's are great.Read moreRead less
