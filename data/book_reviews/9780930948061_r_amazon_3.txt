If you want a book that is for the beginning harmonica player, you can not do much better than Harmonica Americana. Indeed, even if you have been playing harmonica for a few years or more, this book will challenge you to improve. Well written, with just enough humor to keep it interesting, Harmonica Americana is one of the absolute best books to purchase if you play harmonica.
