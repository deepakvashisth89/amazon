For lovers of spy/espionage thrillers this is a great read.  Childers, despite his slow(70 page) start maintains a high level of informative tension to almost the end.  Who are the villains and who are the heroes and who can be trusted?  The purpose of the the author,as was John Buchan's 'Thirty-Nine Steps' which was made into a fine Hitchcock film, was to warn the pre-World War I British establishment of the dangerous intentions of Imperial Germany and to awaken Britain from its complacent slumber.  The evolution of the central character from snobbish dandy to a sea-toughened spy is skillfully drawn.
