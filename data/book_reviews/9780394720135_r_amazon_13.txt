I'm a fan of the author and have read most of his work in the last decade [some of them repeatedly] but I'll admit to being new to his Continental Op short stories. Would I recommend them? Well, perhaps not for younger readers or those new to his style. These stories were written for publication in the pulp magazines so popular in the 1930s and 40s and let me just say that his own writing abilities were not yet as mature as in his more famous novels and short stories.
