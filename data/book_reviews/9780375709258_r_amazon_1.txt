When this book first came out, I picked it up out of curiosity and to this day, it is still one of my favorite reads. With haunting poetry and beautiful prose, the verses whisper of things left unsaid and desires fulfilled. There is heartbreak, pain, sexual identities being awakened, realized and explored, as well as so much more layered within the pages of this book.
