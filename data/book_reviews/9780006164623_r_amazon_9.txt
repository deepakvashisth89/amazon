Have enjoyed many of this author's books over the years and this one didn't disappoint.  I enjoy stories about extended families covering many years like this one did.  One woman, although knowing only one blood relative of her own, to whom she wasn't very close, gave and received much love to and from many others.  However I feel that she never seemed to enjoy personal happiness for very long.  Well written by this experienced author although it may have been just a little too long but still recommend it none the less.Read moreRead less
