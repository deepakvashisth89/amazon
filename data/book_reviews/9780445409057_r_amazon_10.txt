This is the fourth in the Toby Peters mystery series, which begins withBullet for a Star: A Toby Peters Mystery (Book One): Toby Peters Mystery Series, Book 1 (Toby Peters Mysteries (Paperback)).  In this 1940's series, Toby Peters is back in Hollywood and is carrying out a job for the not yet totally reclusive, but already quite strange, Howard Hughes.  Claiming that the US will be in WWII in a matter of weeks, he tells Peters that he recently had a dinner party, during which he suspects that secret plans in his study was copied by one of his guests or staff.This involves Peters usual romp through a host of suspects, including the actor Basil Rathbone, a drunk major, suspicious German guests, a Japanese houseboy and a mobster, amongst others.  This is a fun mystery, with a typical day for Peters involving him discovering dead bodies, getting shot at, beaten up, seduced and annoying his detective brother.  There is a mysterious word, written in blood, at the scene of a crime and a whole host of suspicious possible suspects, including cameo appearances from Basil Rathbone and Bertold Brecht.  The author uses the time and place well, ending the novel with the Japanese attack on Pearl Harbor and a phone message from Boris Karloff to lead into the next mystery:Never Cross a Vampire: A Toby Peters Mystery (Book Five): Toby Peters Mystery Series, Book 5.
