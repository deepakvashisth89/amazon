It is a seminal work that profoundly explores the concept and movement of Black Power in the 1960s. But at the same time, the authors have compared situations from today and showed how situation and circumstances have changed and evolved. The concept of systemic racism is linked with black political and economic empowerment. Their argument that true liberation requires structural changes in society remains relevant and thought-provoking.
