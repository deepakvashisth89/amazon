I have always wanted to learn more about the topics of perestroika and glasnost, and this book gives a firsthand insight into Gorbachev's intentions in regards to restructuring and reforming the former USSR. What I find most fascinating is that this was written only a few years before the USSR ultimately collapsed, so the perspective is very interesting since it would be unknown at the time it was written that the Soviet Union would end.
