Houseman is a unique part of a very English heritage. Writing about a vanished age and with a formality of structure that we now see less often, he is nevertheless a joy to re-discover. His poetry has an apparant simplicity as well as the precision you would expect from a great classics scholar, as he expresses his feelings about an England and a generation soon to be lost for ever. I down-loaded the Kindle version, and this is a great way to read the poems. The active contents page is a huge bonus as it permits direct access to your poem of choice (as in a 'real' book) without turning through scores of pages.
