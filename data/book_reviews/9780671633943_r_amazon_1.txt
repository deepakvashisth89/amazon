I loved the characters in this book but have two things that I can't help but warn future readers about.First of all the hero while trying to spy on Cromwell, did some horrible things to the Irish people but was forgiven (I think too easily) by Brianna.SECOND warning--the VERY graphic violence was horrible and game me nightmares for a few nights but I caught something that bothered me in the one scene of violence!  They are in the White Tower in London being tortured, Brianna is hanging by her wrists and it mentions the metal tears into her flesh and rips it open while she's struggling to free herself. I mentions graphically the gore and blood of this torture of her injuries but when she's freed herself and freed the hero----suddenly no more mention of her savaged hands! I know this is a realistic mirror of this time in history.ALSO: the hero has so many attacks on her body that he must be part bionic to survive even with Brianna's possets and herbs!BUT the PLOT, THE PEOPLE and the hero and heroine are so strongly written and 'fleshed in" that you "ARE" them!  You feel their losses and gains and you can't put down the book for wondering how, when and if they can come through and win!  The ending for once recently in this type book is really satisfying and you feel JOY at their life after the savage times they fought through.Read moreRead less
