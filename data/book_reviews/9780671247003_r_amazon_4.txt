This is one of the seriousest ( yes I just made that a word), funny books ever written.  Sam Levenson was more than just a humorist, a storyteller or a comic-he was a keen observer of life and what makes it worthwhile and what doesn’t.  This is a fantastic book that will have you laughing out loud, crying and really thinking about everything written here long after you’ve finished it.
