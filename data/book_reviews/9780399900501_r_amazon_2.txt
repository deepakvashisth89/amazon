The author uses a weird juxtaposition of real names and nicknames, of the main actors, and it is very hard to keep track of whom he is talking about at a given time.  It's an unnecessary and confounding annoyance.  2.  I believe a lot more information should have been given about over 60, similar and possibly connected, murders in other parts of California.
