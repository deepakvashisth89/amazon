This 108 page book was printed in 1993 and is filled with recipes that the average pizza lover would never make. How about these for an example: Gingered Pear Pizza, Curried Fruit and Cream Pizza, Curried Vegetable Pizza, Spinach and Goat Cheese Pizza, Onion and Goat Cheese Pizza, Goat Cheese - Olive and Pesto Pizza (I think they like goat cheese!), Eggplant and Smoked Trout Pizza, Coconut-Ricotta Cream Pizza, Orange and Grand Marnier Pizza - to name a few. There is not one recipe for a plain old sausage pizza or a pepperoni pizza. There are no secrets revealed to us on how to make great pizza. They do list a recipe for basic pizza dough, but they don't even bother to give you a basic pizza sauce recipe.
