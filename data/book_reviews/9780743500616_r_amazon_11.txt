Emotional, funny, erotic, suspenseful, chilling - the book was a real page-turner. Not only was it funny with all kinds of witty remarks and lines, but also very emotional. The hero and the heroine loved each other deeply - rightfully so, I would say after reading the novel, since both characters are lovable. The four female friends are bonded together by emotions as well, which makes the story with it's suspensful plot of murder and terror even more chilling and sad. The story is nontheless neither happy nor sad, but a mix of both. It's like life with it's ups and downs - only zeroed into a short space of time.
