Writer, Craig Rice, (a woman by the way)is a great builder of mysteries.  She never has the murder committed by some character who appears suddenly at the end of the book.  Her novels are well-crafted and the murderer is someone who is always part of the story but the ending is always a surprise.  This is one of her best and most suspenseful.  A great read that you may decide to keep going until you are finished.  This book also includes her great team of crime-solvers-John J. Malone, Jake Justus and his wife, Helene.Read moreRead less
