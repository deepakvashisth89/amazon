The original edition of Hurt's For All Mankind was published fully thirty years ago.  Back then, it was rife with errors and, as one can see here from other Amazon reviews, it was justifiably panned for its inaccuracies.Now, coincident with the 50 year Apollo 11 Anniversary,  Hurt has produced a new edition with a new introduction.  He notes, this time, that a fact-checker reviewed the text and I was hopeful this effort would expunge the many errors from this new text.Sad to say, while many of the misspellings, incorrect dates, and what one reviewer aptly termed "techno-babble"  were fixed, this new edition still contains dozens of errors that should have been caught by a knowledgeable editor.The errata list is too long to post here, but I can send it to anyone interested.Bottom line:  The new edition is a disappointment as much as the original was.
