This book is one of my favorites.  I stumbled upon it in a bookstore and was enchanted by the title.  Some of the wisdom in this book I have not seen elsewhere.For example, the advice to "get interesting".  How do you get interesting?  The author suggests your friends should resemble a New York City jury pool.  Shortly after that was hired to work in a restaurant and the Chinese chefs invited me to visit China town and meet their families, the wait staff became friends and realized life got way more interesting with folks of diverse backgrounds in it.The author also suggests that when you're driving down to Florida and you pass Aunt Ada's Alligator farm, you stop!Do something different. Get out of your ruts.Loved the simple, humorous and profound philosophy in this short book which can change your life positively. It's not a long book--just some short messages to inspire you.
