I feel inspired to write this review after reading five other lojong training manuals,  including Jamgon Kongtrul's classic work.  In my opinion, they are all good,  but  Chogyam Trungpa Rinpoche's  is the best for both thoroughness as well as the directness of writing style.  Other lojong manuals either do not cover all of the slogans or take an approach which is overly abstract.  The "Traning of the Mind"  strikes the right balance between being scholarly and relating to the real world at the same time. I practice with lojong slogans in my daily life and have found them to be invaluable in dealing with many real world situations.
