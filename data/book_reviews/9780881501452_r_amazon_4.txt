Death Lights a Candle (Asey Mayo Cape cod Mystery, #2) by Phoebe Atwood Taylor.Several years ago I read all the Asey Mayo books by P.A.T. and loved them. I enjoyed these books by this remarkable author so much that I've chosen to reread them. At the time I read the Cape Cod series they were in hardcover with no illustration on the cover. That tells us this author didn't need a cute picture to draw attention to her books. The words told the story and her stories were in high demand. Perhaps its time to go back to the golden age of mysteries and enjoy these hidden treasures.Asey Mayo aka the Codfish Sherlock has been snowed in during a huge blizzard that's hit the Cape Cod residents. It's March and the snow has cut off the roads, the phones as well as electricity to the house where Asey and a close-knit group of friends and acquaintances are held up.Mr. Stires (the host of this get together) a well known and financially set man has been found dead in his locked bedroom. Enemies at that time were not known, but arsenic abounds. Asey pieces together the clues which are few and far between in unraveling this case. What exactly lies behind the death of Stires? How was his murder accomplished in a locked room alone?If you haven't read or even heard of Asey Mayo and the Cape Cod mysteries don't hesitate in trying one on for size.Read moreRead less
