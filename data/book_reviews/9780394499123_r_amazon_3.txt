"October Light" not only contains plots within plots within plots, it also contains themes within themes within themes.  Since I got a couple of inside jokes, I suppose there were many more that I missed.  I admit I did enjoy most of the novel.  Also, I had a feeling of conspiring with the author, when he was being self deprecating, cutting down the very genre that we were sharing.  However.  "October Light" has long passages of philosophy, with no hint (or at least none that I got) whether or not they were meant to be serious; as much of the science, obviously, was not.  My feeling is:  If it's serious then I could have just read a philosophy book.  If it's not serious, then there's WAY too much for simply pointing out the irony of the philosophical nature of characters one would not expect to be philosophical.
