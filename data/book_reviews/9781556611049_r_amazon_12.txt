Enjoyed a number of Novels by Michael Phillips and Judith Pella, and found this one interesting.  Corrie is the eldest of 5 children.  Their father and his brother-in-law went West to California.  News came that their father was dead, so they decided to travel to California to stay with her brother on his Ranch.  However, many surprises awaited them.  An Interesting and enjoyable Novel.
