As someone who was interested in aircraft of World War 2 as a child I bought a copy of this book recently as after a gap of about 30 years I started to get interested in them again all I can say is this book is by far the best publication I have ever owned on the subject this book cover just about every aircraft that fought or participated in WW2 even the minor planes that had a bit part in the conflict inside this fine publication you get Beautiful and highly detailed Cutaways of most of the major types and Beautiful line drawings of most the major aircraft types too also added to this book some aircraft get a gatefold display that opens to at least 2 feet long so whether you like Fighters Bombers Seaplanes or Transport aircraft this publication is for you and I would highly recommend it to anyone.Sadly this book is now long been out of print so try to get a copy while you can the author Bill Gunston sadly died a couple of years ago so I think it would be a fine tribute to his work for the publisher who owns the printing rights to Salamander Books to reprint it so people can once again enjoy this Wonderful publication.Read moreRead less
