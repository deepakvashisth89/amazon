Completed the most moving trilogy of WWII I've ever read. Story built around the actual experiences of a variety Allied solders in WWII. Edith Pargeter is an accomplished writer better know by her pen name of Ellis Peters (Brother Cadfael series). She worked as a nurse in a receiving station in Britain in WWII and interviewed many British, French, Polish and American solders as they came through the receiving station. She recorded these interviews and then published then in a fictional series of three volumes. The main protagonist is a young man working on a road crew in the Midlands. He is typical of many who joined the army at that time. He has never been out of the county of his birth until he joins the army. The trilogy titles are "The Eighth Champion of Christendom", "Reluctant Odyssey" and "Warfare Accomplished". If you are interested in the conduct of WWII in multiple theaters these are a must read. An example of the type of eyewitness scenes is where the advancing German Blitzkrieg does not slow down for refugees on the roads. They simply run over the refugees.
