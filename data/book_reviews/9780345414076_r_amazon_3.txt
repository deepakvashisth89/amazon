Not bad, but kind of adolescent.  The author must think we won't keep reading if somebody doesn't die or the hero doesn't get beat up every few pages. And what is with his girlfriend?  Almost everything she says and does is moronic or inappropriate.  I have a long list of detectives with annoying girlfriends, but Kathy takes the cake.The plot was pretty good -- well thought out, interesting, moved right along.  And Thomas Black is a likable character.  This was my first Earl Emerson book.  I'll probably read more, but these books will be on my B-list.Read moreRead less
