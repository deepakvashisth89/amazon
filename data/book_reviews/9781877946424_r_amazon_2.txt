This book was recommended by a friend who teaches Native American literature.  But I found it to be very boring.  I do understand its concept of magical realism and the cyclic nature of Native American writers; however, the writing style is weak, and the author is metaphor happy.  The metaphors get in the way of a clear reading dragging the reader down.  The story is not well told, even allowing for the style of Native American writers.
