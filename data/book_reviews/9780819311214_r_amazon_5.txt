ISBN 0819311219 - A nice, quick story about Fiona, who can't remember anything. Coming from a family of elephants (who, legend has it, never forget), this is a glaring problem. When she signs up for a class with her friend, Felicity, it's important to her that she remember, so her family helps her by suggesting things like using a calendar and tying a string around her trunk. That these tricks work, sort of, makes Aren't You Forgetting Something, Fiona? a neat teaching tool for little ones.The text is simple for beginning readers and the illustrations are eye-catching for the read-to-me set.- AnnaLovesBooksRead moreRead less
