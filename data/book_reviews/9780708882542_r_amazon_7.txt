This book continues the story begun in The Anvil of Ice. In many ways, it's a stronger book than the first. The main characters from the first book here embark on a quest into the unknown with a select group of companions. As with the first book, the grounding in prehistory gives the story a richness; our heroes journey across primeval forest and changing landscapes, and encounter giant animals.Once again there are many tropes in the narrative, although some are altered in intriguing ways, and I'm not a fan of the romantic subplots.
