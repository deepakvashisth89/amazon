Having first read the journals of both John Richardson and George Back I was eager to read a biography of George Back. Peter Steele's interpretation of Back and his exhaustive study of this artist and explorer were probably the best I have read on the subject.  The book was carefully crafted and meticulously written. Peter Steele has a wonderful command of the English language and he put all together in this wonderful book. I didn't want to put it down.Read moreRead less
