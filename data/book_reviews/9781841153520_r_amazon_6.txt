Ask anyone who has read about Napoleon  the name of his most famous charger and the name Marengo will be the response. But did he ever have a horse of that name ?The author has researched the subject extensively and presented a good case to show that Napoleon's best-known horse Marengo was re-named after the battlle of Waterloo.. The name does not appear in preserved French details of the Emperor's horses leading to the suspicion that the animal was re-named when it was taken by the victors.Certainly the horse landed in England was one of Napoleon's Arab stallions but the records do not prove conclusively which one it was. There were a couple of likely contenders.The book also throws an interesting light on the Emperor's horsemanship. The distances he traveled and the amount of time spent on horseback indicate that he was a far better rider than his  high-school-trained critics liked to admit. He was not a classical rider but proved himself a capable and enthusiastic  one.Historians might find this book of little consequence but readers interested in horses and riding will enjoy it.Read moreRead less
