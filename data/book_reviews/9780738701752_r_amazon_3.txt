Great book for beginners.  It teaches that the Tarot does not have to be difficult to understand and use.  The book also includes a lot of great spells involving the cards, candles, incense, spices, oils, and incantations.  No, you don't have to destroy your cards.  The author recommends making color copies of the cards if a spell involves its defacement or destruction.  All spells in this book are for good things like finding romance, success in business, having a happy home, protecting yourself from enemies, etc.  I suggest buying this book after you've read some of the more technical Tarot books out there.Read moreRead less
