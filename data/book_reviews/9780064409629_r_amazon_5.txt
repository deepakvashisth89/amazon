When I was young I read 4 or 5 of the Oz books, and this one was far and away my favorite. The princess who changes her heads instead of her clothes remained with me even after I'd forgotten much of the rest of the plot. And the tension and suspense of having to guess which bibelots are actually the bewitched members of the royal family of Ev also stayed with me. So, I bought Ozma of Oz for my 6 year old niece who is perhaps not quite ready to transition from picture books to chapter books. But boy did she love it! She was completely captivated. We read it until she couldn't keep her eyes open any more, and then she would creep into my bedroom early in the morning and ask me to read more before breakfast. And we read short sentences here and there together, so I'm hoping that this book has helped her to see the magical world that opens up when you can read. Also, Dorothy and Ozma are really courageous, honorable, adventurous, and forthright female characters -- good role models, if that's what you are looking for.Read moreRead less
