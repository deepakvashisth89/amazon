I have started and abandoned a string of books that just couldn’t hold my interest. I saw this one for a couple of bucks and went for it, because I’ve read and enjoyed a lot of Cornwell. As expected, I stuck with this one to the end. I’m not a literary critic, but this is my gauge: If I get interested enough to keep reading (and especially if I’m interested enough to read during the day rather than at night before bed), then it was worthwhile. Always with Cornwell there are some passages that I read a few times just enjoying the way it’s written.I have always read Cornwell’s historical fiction and learned a lot from it. This one wasn’t so much historical fiction, but I think I gained a bit of appreciation for what was going on with the Irish. So there was some history in there. The story here had a lot of twists and turns. In all, it was a fun read. But it was Cornwell, so no surprise.Read moreRead less
