My 14 month old daughter loves this book.  Some of the reviews said that there were too many pictures on the pages, but I think this is great.  We play a game where I ask her certain animals that she's familiar with (she especially likes the kitties) and then she'll pick them out of the rest.  Gradually she's been learning more animals, so the fact that there are lots of pictures on one page is a good thing.  She picks up the book and brings it to us when she wants to play.  She'll also sit and look through it on her own, pointing at animals and either naming them or making the sound that they make.  I find this book has really helped her learn new animals.  She also likes to chew on it too, which is a whole different thing...but the book is priced so well that It's alright if she tears it up.  I really like this product for my daughter.
