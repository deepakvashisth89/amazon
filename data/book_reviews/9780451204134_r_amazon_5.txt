What a book! I found this an incredible book and well told history of the crew who sought to reach the North Pole. At times I had a hard time getting my brain wrapped around so much ice and cold and devastating circumstances!! Mr Hall and Mr Tyson were amazing men. Both had integrity, compassion, intelligence, discernment, discipline, wisdom and strength of character. A few others on the ship also had strong characters, especially Joe. Sadly two in particular were evil, self serving men. They sabotaged the entire expedition. The pain and suffering in this book is really beyond our comprehension. I was crushed for Mr Hall. Very, very sad. The hand of God surely did protect those who were on the ice suffering and starving for all those months of horrible storms, and deprivation. It is a miracle they made it. It’s very upsetting that the Board who inquired about the death of Mr Hall did not pursue the truth more diligently and bring forth criminal charges. A very sad outcome in our history. I am glad I read this book. Men were sure made of “stronger stuff” in this time. Thank you Bruce Henderson for this book. Well done.pamarellaPRCSRead moreRead less
