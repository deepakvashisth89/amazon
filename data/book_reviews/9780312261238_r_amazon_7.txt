I don't normally get this sappy, but since I'm a child of a gay man this is close to my heart.  An American Family is an is an absolutely wonderful book about two gay men who fought against the odds to adopt their son Adam - an incredibly sick baby who wouldn't have been wanted by those who protested against the Galluccios' adoption of that child.  The Galluccio's make their case in simple, heartfelt language.  Their love for their children and each other can be felt through their words.  When they describe meeting Adam for the first time in the hospital it's hard not to cry.  These are brave men who won an important battle for all children.
