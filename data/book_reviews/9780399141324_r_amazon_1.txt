I came to this novel after reading, a novel in the author’s other series, “The Joe and Dottie Loudermilk” series.  I like mysteries and was NOT disappointed by this one.  The author is able to provide the back-story of all characters in a non-cumbersome fashion.  This allows us (the readers) to understand and appreciate the interactions between Aaron Gunner and those around him.
