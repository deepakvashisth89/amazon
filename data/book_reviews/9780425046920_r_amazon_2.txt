**Contains spoilers**Captain Edward Delaney has to figure out who’s killing the citizens of his precinct in what appears to be a stream of unrelated murders.This is a well written, gritty, cop mystery full of rich descriptions that don’t drag the story down and insight into both Delaney’s struggles to cope with his hospitalized wife’s failing health and his need to capture a murderer capable of brutal, senseless violence that lacks motive.At the same time, we’re shown Daniel Blank’s fractured shift from corporate golden boy into a delusional killer as his mental illness takes hold.Sanders writes as if he has first hand experience on both sides of the story, and his detailed vision kept me hooked the whole time I read it.If you don’t care for detailed world and character building, or if you are triggered by violence as a result of mental illness, then this story is not for you.
