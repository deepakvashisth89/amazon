I don't write reviews, but this book was worth the effort.  The story of a sweet-hearted woman who finds a different kind of love when she takes in her orphaned, teenage nephew.  While trying to create a happy home for him and provide the best environment for him to become a fine, upstanding person, she finds herself faced with boys of less than desirable histories crossing paths with her nephew.  As she meets these boys, one-by-one, she sees promise and feels compassion for them.  The book describes how she and the boys gradually became dear friends and how she nurtured each boy with her love, her Sundays of reading to them, singing with them and feeding them and the added activities they all did together.  There are bumps in the road, namely one spiteful woman who is bent on breaking up these friendships.The progression of the story and the milestones these boys reach little by little, is truly uplifting and made my heart want to see people as God sees them and seek to be as obsessed as Miss Victoria Gracen.Read moreRead less
