I read this book years ago when the author published under a different name--Mary Alice Kruesi. I read this novel, and also Second Star to the Right.  I was entranced.I mention that these books were published years ago only to let you know that I have thought about them for almost 15 years.  I am a fiction librarian who recommends them as two of my all-time favorite fantasies.  The world-building is just brilliant, and so is the romance.I wrote to the author in 2000 and asked if she was going to write more books like these, but she said she wasn't.  I was very disappointed--and still am. As an adult, you rarely have the experience of being transported by fairytales, yet these two novels succeed wonderfully.I only wish she'd write more like them.Read moreRead less
