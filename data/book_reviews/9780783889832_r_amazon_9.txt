If you’re looking for a detailed, unfiltered and well explained account of what happened in Mogadishu, this is the book for you. It’s what I was looking for, and so far hasn’t disappointed. I’ve not even finished it yet and but couldn’t recommend it enough. There are plenty of videos, interviews etc about this (I’ve watched/listened to most of them) but none go into the detail presented here. What I’ve found surprising is that a lot of the small things that you see in the film that you might think has been dramatised for the Hollywood blockbuster actually isn’t - it’s based on the people and situations that really happened. As an example…McKnight (portrayed by Tom Sizemore in the film) was said to walk around seemingly unfazed and unbothered about the bullets flying all around him (and this is shown in the film)What these men went through is hard to comprehend but this book does it best to take you right there.
