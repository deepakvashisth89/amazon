When I started reading this novel I was reminded of Irving Wallace's 'The Word'. I enjoyed 'The Word' for the research that had been done in framing the story, and I did enjoy 'The Hoax' for the same reason, although it did seem to me that the research was less sustained.There is no doubt from the title of this novel that it does involve a hoax. But why is it being administered, and with such intensity? Slowly the matter is explained if not resolved. Perhaps it is a bit contrived, but real life does have a way of being 'stranger than fiction'.
