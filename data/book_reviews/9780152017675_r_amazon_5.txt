Told from multiple views, this well written novel takes readers into a world of fanaticism, showing what it's like to be on both sides of a cult/cult-like-group.  By working through young people, the authors are able to take a touchy topic like religion and make it available to people, instead of automatically instigating feelings of polarity.  By making Jed and Marina real, giving them beliefs, desires, and the ability to question the decision making the adults in their lives are doing on their behalf, these teens display worthy strenghts and faults.If it's not already, this book should be made required reading in high school and lower-division college literature, composition, or philosophy classes.Read moreRead less
