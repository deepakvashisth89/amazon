A 5 star book has to be VERY special.I cannot believe that I had missed this very special book by Le Carre and not by some nom deplume.The characterisations are so vivid.  Le Carre has a special ability to let you see behind the ordinariness of the men and women operating in the "circus".What starts as a gentle review of his past gets deeper and deeper into the individuals.Sip the words or go all in, the choice is yours.A tour de force of the spy novel.  No-one can be left with the feeling that they have been short changed.One of the best books I have read in years.Read moreRead less
