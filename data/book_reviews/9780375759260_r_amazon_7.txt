This review is of the edition of The Civilization of the Renaissance in Italy published by BiblioBazaar.  This edition should be recalled by the publisher: there are typo's on every page, making it nearly unreadable.  I attempted to continue reading the book (which is of course a classic) but was too annoyed with trying to figure out what certain jumbled characters meant.I think BiblioBazaar must has scanned the text of an old edition of the book whose copyright had expired, but then never bothered to proofread the finished product.  Very bad business.  Save your money and get an edition by a reputable publisher.Read moreRead less
