Though this book isn't new, I just re-read it and loved it even more than the first time I read it over 10 years ago! I consider myself a discerning reader but I don't get all the criticism this book got from other reviewers. It's definitely a coming-of-age book for women of all ages. I could relate to all the women who were walking and I LOVED the stories of the women who were affected by the women walking. When I read it the first time, I thought, "Wow, wouldn't it be cool to know one of the women walking." When I read it this time, I thought, "Damn! If this were real I would head straight to Wisconsin and walk WITH these women!" I loved it. Then. And now. Say what you will but Kris Radish gets women.Read moreRead less
