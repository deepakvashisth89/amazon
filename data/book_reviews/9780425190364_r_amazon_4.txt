In the Castle of the Flynns was so much fun to read.  Danny, a boy whose parents die, is taken in by his entire family...especially his grandparents and Uncle Tom who watch over him.  How they do this is at times hysterical and at other times so sweet and special.  The perspective is Dan's so you see growing up in a neighborhood and with how a family relates to itself from a young boys eyes.  The antics of kids, the craziness of relatives, I think everyone will find someone they grew up around or with in this book.
