This is a fantastic book to help you if you are learning to read Japanese kanji. Like more learning geared complete dictionaries it provides a simple method to look up kanji so you can find what you want fast (or know that it is not in this book as it is not designed to be nearly as comprehensive). The pictures provide a helpful visual mnemonic for remembering the meaning of many kanji that look similar. I've combine this with other tools in my journey to learn Japanese.Even if you are not interested in learning to speak or read Japanese this is a fun book for the coffee table. Japanese and Chinese characters are popular symbols, designs, decorations, and tattoos.
