Such a good book, regarding bringing up the tough questions to ask prior to marriage. My, now husband, and I went through this while we were dating. We were apart for a while and so we would go through the book on our own and write down or highlight things we wanted to talk about. I really opened to doors for us to be honest with ourselves, each other, and our expectations of what we were wanting out of our marriage. When we went to see a Chaplain prior to our marriage, he asked us a lot of similar hard questions, and we had the answers.
