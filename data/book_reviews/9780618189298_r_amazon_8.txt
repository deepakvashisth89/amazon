Having tromped around the woods, towns, and backroads of Northern Idaho while growing up, I was delighted to have those memories pulled out of deep memory by Bill Gruber's delightful book.  "On All Sides Nowhere" chronicles a place and culture seldom deemed worthy of prose.  Gruber transforms what might seem seedy (yards filled with junked cars, a skillful but alcoholic handyman,  houses continually under contruction) into respectful depictions of everyday life in this hard-scrabble part of the Pacific Northwest. Unlike many writers, Gruber doesn't choose to focus on himself or his family; they are simply the vehicle that allows him insights into the characters around them.Read moreRead less
