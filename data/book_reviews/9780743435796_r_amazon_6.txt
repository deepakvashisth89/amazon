This is by far the worst book I have read that was written by Zelazny.  The story goes on and on without really getting anywhere and did little to hold my interest.  The one redeeming aspect of this book and probably the only reason I finished it was the use of Edgar Allen Poe as one of the characters.  Zelazny attempted to portray the true misery of Poe within the telling of this fictional story.
