Thomas and Angela are only teenagers when their parents pass away and leave them to work the farm and raise three younger siblings. The unfailing love and deep faith of their parents are relied upon daily as they seek to bring the children up in a way that would be pleasing to their parents. While the parenting of the children is their priority, they still encounter many of the dreams and emotions typical of every teenager.
