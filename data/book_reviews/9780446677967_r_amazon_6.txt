Once again Mr. Arthur Clarke does an extraordinary job of giving us a glimpse of our distant future...or at least one of the possibilities that our future could hold for us.  This book starts off with a problem that the protagonist must solve and certain obstacles in his way that he must surpass.  However, this book takes the reader so much further into this distant universe and expands immensely onto the initial story that covered the beginning of the book.Part of the fun of reading this book, is when the reader puts the book down, and is able to just digest everything that happened in the story and how it correlates to certain directions the human race is currently taking our culture (and even points where reality is taking us in quite the opposite direction).  The characters at first appear quite different at first introduction, but Arthur Clarke does a wonderful job of conveying that although millions of years have passed, the core of our values and kindness are all still there.  Give this book a read if you're looking for a great sci-fi read, with heart.Read moreRead less
