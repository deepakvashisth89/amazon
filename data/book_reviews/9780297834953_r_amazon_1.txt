Nigel Nicolson is a knowledgeable guide to the homes and places Jane Austen lived and visited.  He gives information and context to these places and offers wonderful pictures.  I particularly appreciated knowing the address and status of the homes Jane visited in London when she visited her brother Henry, who helped with the publication of some of her books.  (I recently stumbled across a wonderful slide show of "Henry's Townhouse" in London, which offers rooms for travelers to book.
