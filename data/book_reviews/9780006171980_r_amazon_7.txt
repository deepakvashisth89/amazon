Blending fictional characters with historical battles Cornwell continues the adventures, both balls-out and clandestine, of Richard Sharpe. The proud hero hero endures in his quest to climb the prickly ranks of Napoleonic-Era British military, more so than in other Sharpe's tales intrigue abounds in this novel set with multiple moving pieces.
