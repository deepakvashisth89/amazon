The Growing Years is one of my favorite books about parenting. It explains the emotional stages a child goes through starting at birth in a manner that is easily understood and thought-provoking. Although some might feel that the language is a bit out-of-date with respect to gender references and roles, the benefits of the knowledge shared by the expert writers greatly outweigh this downside. I've given the book to friends who are new parents, and think that even non-parents (e.g. aunts and uncles) will appreciate the insights the book provides.Read moreRead less
