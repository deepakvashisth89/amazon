Before WW1 a married archeology student and his lover, find a cave system that is full of Neaderthal and Cro-Magnan hand tools.  This find will make him famous.  Every thing he found is packed up in a trunk and sent to his parents in Paris. He goes off to war leaving his pregnant lover and his wife and young daughter.  He never comes back.Every year the lover visits the cave and celebrates her lover with a picnic.  In 1942 she is found murdered in the little valley.  She had been mutilated with a stone axe and flint knives.  St.Cyr and Koehler are returning from a mission in the ‘zone libre’ and are pulled off of a north-bound train to help find the killer.The daughter of the murdered woman lives in a small village in the vicinity of the cave.  She and her husband are schoolteachers with two young children.  Her husband is an abusive man who recently came back from volunteering to fight for the Germans in Russia.  His right hand and leg well damaged in an attack and he has come home disabled and angry.Before coming to celebrate this last time the murdered woman had been receiving postcards from someone who signed them with her dead(?) husband’s name.  Needing money her in-laws had sold the trunk and it was bought by an enemy of her dead husband.  The archeologist finds the cave and in a second chamber he finds cave paintings and an amulet.  The amulet appears to have a swatstika carved on it.Himmler finds out about the amulet and with the help of Goebbels and Hitler is going to make a movie proving that ‘Aryans’ lived in this valley 100,000 years ago.  Is the man who went off to war now seeking revenge on those who stole his findings(?) It all very complicated.The film crew is right out of central casting…the overbearing director, his slutty star actress wife, her lover, the ingénue (who has a cocaine problem) and is the dead man in the crew?
