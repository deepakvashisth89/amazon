As I recall, two doctors got together to write a guide for hypochondriacs.  They tell you about the most horrible illness you could possibly have for any minor, every-day symptom you might come up with.  Runny nose?  It could be a fractured skull, with cerebra-spinal fluid leaking into your nasal passages (yes, really!).  They teach you what to say and how to act if you want to force an emergency room to keep you overnight on suspicion of a heart attack - even if you EKG is normal.
