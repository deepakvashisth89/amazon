I purchased this book as a daily deal, but didn't have much hope for it as it's not my usual genre. But after reading a couple of chapters, I was sucked into the story and couldn't put it down. The depth of the characters surprised me, as did the intensity of the mystery. The settings were well done; I could almost touch and feel things as the author described them. I will definitely be checking out the rest of the series. Thanks for the excellent read!P.S. The Kindle version did have quite a few errors in it.
