I can't say that I love this novel, as it is completely depressing, but it is very well written, and gives a full glimpse into the life of fractured and damaged people. It also resonates on a personal level. The characters are flawed and human, realistic portraits of people as opposed to a sugary charecature. I first read this novel more than 15 years ago, and it feels way more important now as a study of the human heart. This is an emotional journey as a read, one that starts and stops without a definitive conclusion, but even as such, you will feel like you have been through something upon reading it.Read moreRead less
