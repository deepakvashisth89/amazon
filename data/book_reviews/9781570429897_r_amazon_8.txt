I just started reading Brad Meltzer's books a month ago and now I am on my fifth book of his. After I finish each book I tell myself that the next one won't be as good and to my surprise they keep getting better and better! I love the fact that the many of his books are set in Washington D.C.  This was my second Melter book and I loved it! The characters are lively, the plot has a ton of twists and turns that keep you guessing all the way up until the end. It is an easy read and very enjoyable.  If you like legal/political thrillers then I don't think you will be disappointed reading this one!Read moreRead less
