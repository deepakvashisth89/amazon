The author quotes many women who left the Catholic Church.  My discomfort came not from the fact that they left (many had good reasons to be uncomfortable with their experiences in the Catholic Church) but from where some of them ended up.  If somebody is leaving the religiosity of their church of origin for a church that helps them to grow closer to Jesus Christ, as revealed in Scripture, that is a good thing.  But, when somebody leaves a church for any old thing just to get out of there, that is a problem.  If somebody is going to jump out of the frying pan, don't jump into the fire.  An "anything goes" assembly of people resolutely believing anything or nothing is not a church but a social club.  It may be a very nice social club, but don't confuse it with a church.
