Andy Sirkis is a walking lexicon of voices.  If I had not known better, I would have thought they had a whole cast playing the parts, but it's just Andy doing them all. He does at least 20 distinct voices, plus the distinct voices of Gollum in his different moods.  He's a genius actor, and it shows in his readings.The story itself is unabridged, read in full and totally epic!  For those that have read these books in the past, do yourself a favor and listen to these, they really bring the stories to life.  Andy Sirkis brings all the characters to life, you know who is talking just by his voice and accent; he's that good!For those that haven't read the books, these are the original Fantasy novels.  They were a huge influence on the hippy generation (even Leonard Nimoy aka Mr. Spock sang a song about Frodo!), and beyond.  Half of Led Zeppelin's early catalog seems to be based on Tolkien's works.  They reference them a lot early in their career. Tolkien does an amazing job with Worldbuilding as the story unfolds. Every book brings locales and people to life in the readings, they feel alive in their descriptions, as if they could actually exist.Needless to say, if you haven't had the experience of reading or listening to these books and you like Fantasy Novels, these are all must read/listen books.  Without Tolkien there'd be no Drizzt or Raistlin or Milamber or any of the Fantasy adventurers we all know and love.  It all starts with these books, they've lit the imaginations of hundreds of millions of people since originally published, and will continue to be the 'North Star' of Fantasy Literature; all Fantasy writers give Tolkien props for inspiring them to write.Read moreRead less
