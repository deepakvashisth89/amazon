The character named Jim was one of three Europeans on board a rusty steamship that met with a tragic accident.  Jim was the mate, and was in charge when the accident happened.  All three escaped in a lifeboat.  Jim stood trial.  The other two fled.  Jim was found guilty and lost his licence.  Because everyone knew the story, Jim could not get a job on any ship after that.  He had to go to a place where nobody knew about it.  He wound up in a wild place in the Asian jungle, eventually earning the respect of the people living there.  Death, when it came to him, came from a surprising place.This is one of Conrad's best stories.  It's a good story, and one that always seems real to me.
