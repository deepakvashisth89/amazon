I would have liked to be able to say that it was the translators fault, but vdW wrote in both Dutch and English, so he's the one to blame.  It appears that vdW was trying for a 'zen' like style in this book but ended up with a mish-mash of disconnecting ideas.  The book is broken into two parts and the second is a lot more coherent than the first but that's not so hard since part one is a total mess. There is way to much expostulating by Grijpstra and De Grier as well as the Commissaris.  All the talk reminded me of a late night in college after everyone had too much to drink/smoke and sat around discussing Sartre or Scott Fitzgerald.
