I read all of the Cat Who series books, but as Mrs. Jackson Braun aged they became sadly quite weak.  These stories are from the early days in which Qwilleran acquires Koko and the precious Yum Yum.  It is amazing how good the early books are compared to the last few sad entries.  Reading these will give you the impetus to read on to the next great adventure in this terrific series.
