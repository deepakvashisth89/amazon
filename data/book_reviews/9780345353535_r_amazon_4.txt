This is a thoroughly enjoyable space opera novel set in the authors' Liaden Universe. There are very likable characters, an engaging plot, a romance element that added to my pleasure in the book (which is by no means always the case). More than that, the likable characters repeatedly help and support each other. I think it is because of this last fact that this book made me happy as I read it, a trait it shares with such otherwise very different series as "The No. 1 Ladies' Detective Agency" by Alexander McCall Smith, or Gwynedd Rae's children's books about Mary Plain.
