An obviously predictable ending, this plot ranged over less than a week. Genny arrives in Texas from the East to teach school, leaving her unhappy past behind. There she meets a half Comanche and bounty hunter named  Keenan, known as White Eagle. They are destined to meet as she has seen him in one of her dreams of the future.  Together they must defeat an evil spirit.I enjoyed the western genre but everything happens too quickly.  Genny only ever teaches one lesson, and from that one of the children becomes the child of her heart, and she antagonises a town by day two.The fact that the heroine was occasionally called Germy through bad editing was quite offputting.While reading it the setting and action at the beginning reminded me of the cheesy 1950's western films you see on TV.If the story had stretched over a longer period so the characters could interact more it would have been better.Read moreRead less
