This "mystery" reads like a slightly grown-up Nancy Drew:  lucky coincidences, just in the nick-of-time situations and unbelievable characters.  The great English women mystery writers do not have a competitor in Ms. Moyes.  This book oozes the the author's hope that the movies will want to buy an option on it.
