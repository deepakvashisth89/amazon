First, let me say the artwork was brilliant. The story was well-thought out. Superman takes his cape and goes home-retires, gives up on humanity. This gives rise to a more bloodthirsty lot; the rise of the meta-human. An old man,an observer who is struggling with his faith,becomes a witness to man’s end time,traveling with a being called the Spectre. This being selects the old man, who is also a reverend, to help decide the fate of the world. The story is complex and well worth a read. There are many other stories going on as the Spectre and the Rev pop in and out of conflicts during the story. I won’t spoil any more of the story , but it is for me an awesome tale.
