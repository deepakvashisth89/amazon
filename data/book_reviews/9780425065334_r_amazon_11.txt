This 242-page book by Chelsea Quinn Yarbro is channelled material based on transcripts from a mid-causal entity (a group of beings) called Michael.  Published originally in 1979 and with a later intro from 2004, it is very interesting.  It's complex material that talks about the soul, cycles of reincarnation, the purpose of reincarnations, levels of reality other than physical, the soul-psychology (the aspects of a souls makeup [the overleaves] that are chosen for a given incarnation), the purpose of life (to develop agape - unconditional love and acceptance).  Michael talks matter-of-factly, directly, non-judgementally, often answering questions and saying he wants to inform.Can it be true?  Well I don't think it is fiction (apart from concealment of details e.g., names).  It bears consideration alongside the best material from psychics e.g., from Jane Roberts (Seth) and Edgar Cayce, and gives a description of the structure of the levels of reality and a description of the psychological flavours (the overleaves) when on the physical plane.I am uncertain as to how to reconcile the rather clear structured reality and psychological context described with Seth's freer "you create your own reality" ... there are no closed systems ... we each journey through our own psychological landscape.  I'm intrigued that the overleaves, which are said to shape (and allow one to forecast) personal behaviour, bear no similarity to the best (and best predictive) modern personality type systems i.e., Myers-Briggs  ...
