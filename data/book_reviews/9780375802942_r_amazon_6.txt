This book series is a high interest, medium reading level comprehension and great for primary school children.  The book catches a child's attention immediately because of the illustration on the front cover.  The back cover gives a concise, but good description of what is inside.  Our granddaughters have enjoyed the "Junie B." books and request them both at the library and in book stores and on Amazon.I recommend this to any child, parent, family member, friend or teacher who is trying to find a book that will hold a child's attention and be fun while improvimng the child's reading comprehension skills and encouraging the love of reading.
