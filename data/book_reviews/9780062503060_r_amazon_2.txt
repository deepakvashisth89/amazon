Marcia Falk's translation of The Song of Songs reproduces the Hebrew original beautifully and then translates it, using different typefaces to show the voice of the female speaker, the male speaker, and the group voice.  (In Hebrew, gender is part of the language, but the English reader often has no idea--yet the Song of Songs is remarkable for its equality of attitude and action between the female and the male speakers.)  Falk's commentary is insightful and helps the contemporary reader to understand this important Biblical text.'Read moreRead less
