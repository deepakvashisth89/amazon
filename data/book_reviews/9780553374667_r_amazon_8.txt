I came upon this book after having 2 consecutive miscarriages and looking for information about why this was happening to me. I wanted to understand my body and women's bodies better in order to help my own doctor help me. I have needed very little medical care throughout my adult life, and found this book enlightening and the writter to be very open-minded. I am grateful for this reference and though I don't have answers about why my own body isn't holding onto a baby, I feel very much more informed about my health and women's health. This book also helped me to deal with the stress and emotional issues felt by me as a result from 2 miscarriages. Upon reading just the first few chapters, I began to feel much more loving and patient towards my own body and encouraged about my reproductive process.
