This is less of a book and more of a movie on the life near the river Mississippi. As you read this book, it will create such crystal clear images of the lives of people around the river that you will get so immersed in it. And guys, that is Mark Twain for you. An outstanding writer. It is written with a great deal of detail which my guess is springs from the author's experiences, travels and dialogues and throws the reality colour into it.I have read other books by Twain but this one is simply the best.
