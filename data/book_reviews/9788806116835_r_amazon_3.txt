"La chimera" is a story of a young woman in a small village near Milan at the beginning of 1600, accused of being a witch. It is based on a true story and Vassalli described the social environment very well: superstition, ignorance, enviousness. He discussed also the Spanish administration which was in power, its corrupted governance and soldiers, the suffocating presence of clergy, made of many fanatic priests and few illuminated ones. Vassalli says that the past helps to understand the present by comparison; many societies are still in the dark of ignorance so it is easier to understand fanaticism, but also ever corruption and enviousness.
