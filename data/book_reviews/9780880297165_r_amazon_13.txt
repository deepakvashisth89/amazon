This Berkley edition is three books in one. The cover is paperback flexible.The books are large but not heavy, so you won't break your nose when reading in bed, the font size is okay for the not so young like me.There's more stories out there to read. Keeping always in mind that most of them were written by a very old woman I enjoy even more her shrewd picking up topics from previously written books and creating a new story around these people and places, always logic, always fresh and fair to the reader and the cats. Other writers have lost it in younger years.Some readers call for more evolution in the character of Quill, but whoever heard of evolution in famous detectives like Holmes, Poirot and the like, who would want them to change? The environment and technical world change a lot in the whole stories, just like they do.I love the settings in the arts worlds of painting, antiques collecting and decorating, in later books the worlds of the theatre, museums and culinary establishments are added, as well as country folks peculiarities.And the cats who will stay cats, whatever happens, a joy for cat lovers everywhere.Read moreRead less
