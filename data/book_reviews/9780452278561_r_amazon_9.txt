I have just re-read this book after a long absence and found it as engaging, powerful and moving as I did first time round. Its central characters, all gay men, live in a world which mixes love, promiscuity and the fear of AIDS. Partly because of the fear of testing HIV positive, and partly because of the Provincetown culture which is at the heart of the book, youth seems to end by the mid-20s, and illness brings on premature ageing. At its heart, this is an affecting love story, significant for its insights into the complexity of the emotions and interactions of the key characters.
