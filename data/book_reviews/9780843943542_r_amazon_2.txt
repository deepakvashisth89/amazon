This is an American historical romance involving whites, Indians, and half breeds.  I loved the story.  Chase is handsome, honorable, and willing to help others.  Raegan is sweet and innocent.  Chase tries to ignore his attraction to Raegan, but that is hopeless.  I loved that the book involved them together from almost the start to the finish.  The writer does an excellent  job.
