This book was suggested as a good read by the owner of the Elephant Journal. I had watched a lot of his videos and on one of them, he suggested a couple of books to read. This was one of them. I immediately hopped over to Amazon and ordered it. I was not disappointed. This book is a non-religious book, yet it contains universal spiritual truths. I can't think of any religion that would take issue with what is in this book. I love when a book comes into your life and it's exactly what you need at the time. That's how I felt about this book. I'm currently studying Contemplative Meditation within the Christian faith. This book completely compliments the concepts I'm learning. I'm amazed at how universal truths go beyond religion. I consider myself a spiritual person more than religious. The man who wrote this book was one of Chogyam's followers. He talks about becoming a warrior but not in the sense of fighting but in the way of facing our fears and becoming our true, authentic selves. When we find our root which we can do by following the steps in this book, we are calm and confident. We live without reference to the world. We love ourselves and others in a non-selfish way.
