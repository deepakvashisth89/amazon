OK warning, I'm a Drake fan and have been since Hammer's Slammers first came out. Nobody writes military SF from the point of view of the grunt like Drake (maybe Haldeman ... and Pournelle ... and Weber, now I'm arguing with myself).Anyway, this starts out as a fun kick butt, take names, story. Then somewhere along the way it turns into something else. And that something else is pretty good, too.Bottom line here is a story about appreciating the folks who put their butts in the fire and, too often, their necks in the noose. It may even be a bit of a cathartic for a Vietnam Vet.It is particularly poignant given the, seemingly daily, news about how badly the VA is screwing up.The story itself is good, the characters are developed and easy to relate to. The action is non stop and realistic. Yeah, I got a little sick of killing trees but to say more would require a spoiler alert.End of the day, a good, fast read, with some depth.
