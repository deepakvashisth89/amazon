I love Jane Kirkpatrick's books and though I initially got off to a slow start as I stumbled with some of the names and French words, once I got past the first few chapters, I could hardly put this book down. This story quickly became one of my favorites. I loved learning of Marie Dorian.I so appreciate the author's research and her exceptional talent for bringing historical characters and events to life.  I also value the author's post story notes. With each read I find myself wanting to experience the related sites and landmarks and always have a much stronger sense of gratitude and appreciation for the conveniences of today.
