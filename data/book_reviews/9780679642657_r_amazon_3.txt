This novel is linguistically dense, wrapped in metaphor and symbols, and tells the story of Suttree, a man who's abandoned his family, and bums around on his boat, struggles with poverty, meets a colorful cast of characters dealing with the same challenges, and goes about day to day life as chaos ensues. It's a fun, entertaining read that has all the hallmarks of Cormac McCarthy, while being his most wordy, poetic, and lyrical novel. A meditation on loneliness and the American dream.Read moreRead less
