The writing is very poor. Mr. De Felitta is prone to using adjectives contrary to their meaning, and introducing more and more characters before he has given us time to get to know just one. Even worse, there is no emotional weight to the story. The whole thing is a grander-than-life conspiracy acted out by paper doll characters that rings as hollow as The DaVinci Code.
