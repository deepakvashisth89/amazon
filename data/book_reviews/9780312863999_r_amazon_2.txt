Loved this one, Thornburg's final book, and what a way to end. Charley and Brian Poole (without the Tremeloes) are two brothers, both in love with the glamouress looking Eve. Brian is a bit of an ass though, as he roams around the US making a fool of himself by taking out his wrath on a film studio who are making an unflattering film about him and a dead country singer. When Charley comes to the rescue, Brian treats both him and Eve like dirt, but Charley falls for Eve which lessens the blow. Throw in a handful of weird side characters and you have Thornburg at his best, an easy to read yet clever novelist who should be better known.
