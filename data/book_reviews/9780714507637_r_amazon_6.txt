A classic in every sense of the word. Crisp dialogue. Smooth pacing. Unexpected twists. And a stunning climax. I read the play and saw the movie (the brilliant original 1972 version — avoid the trashy 2007 remake at all costs) years ago, and loved them both. Comparable, if not superior, reads would beDeathtrap: A Thriller in Two Acts, andThree Imaginary Boys(though the latter is a novel, it reads almost like a play or a movie). Bottom line: Sleuth is the best cat & mouse thriller of all time.Here is a link to the original 1972 movie:Sleuth.
