I’d not heard of Simon Brett prior to this book. I enjoyed the twists and turns of the murder mystery. Even more so, I found Mrs. Pargeter intriguing. It’s nice to encounter a lead character where little snippets of their background are dropped like breadcrumbs throughout the story. You get to know them, but also feel like the more you know about them, the less you know about them. On to book #2 in the series!Read moreRead less
