I have probably read the majority of books by Anne Tyler, having been a fan of hers for years. 'An Amateur Marriage', 'A Patchwork Planet', much older 'Saint Maybe', all rich in characterization, absorbing, can't put down reads. Going on holiday, I was looking forward to immersing myself in her latest novel 'A Tin Can Tree'.  What a disappointment.  I read on, feeling that surely I would get to like these characters and feel (even vaguely) interested in their lives.  Not so!  It really felt to me that Ms. Tyler was scraping the barrel here and was almost bored herself.  I find it nearly impossible not to finish a book, even when having doubts about it; always feeling optimistic that enjoymrnt, as a reader will eventually come.  I gave up on this one.
