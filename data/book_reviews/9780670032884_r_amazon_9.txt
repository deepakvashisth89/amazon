The writing of this novel was done beautifully by Mary McGarry Morris, but the story itself, was so depressing that I was unable to enjoy it.  The story is built upon the release of a murderer, Gordon Loomis, after serving twenty-five years in prison.  The premise is not built on the murder or even the time Gordon spent in prison, but the difficulties in trying to return to his hometown and trying to resume a normal life.  That is filled with difficulties, from his relationship with his brother, Dennis, and his family, to trying to deal with a woman, Delores, who is obviously infatuated with him, but for whom he has no genuine feelings.  And a neighbourhood kid, whose own life is worse than Gordon's.  Gordon just wants to live in his childhood home, working hard for a living and be left alone as he has no idea how to socialize.  As I read on I felt that if only Gordon left his hometown where the murder occurred and just struck out somewhere where no one knew him or about his past, he may have stood a better chance.
