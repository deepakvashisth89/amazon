Perfect for anyone who loves cats.I was given this book and I’m glad. It’s a great little book full of quotes about cats, being owned by cats and what it’s like to live with cats. The illustrations are great, too. In one, the cat covers up the words and anyone who has ever been owned by a cat can recall how the cat will inevitably climb onto whatever book is opened and be right in the way. I smiled my way through this short book and gladly encourage everyone to read it.It’s not long and the quotes are quick, but that doesn’t take away from the overall story.If you’re looking for a gift for a cat lover, then you can’t go wrong with this book.originally posted at long and short reviewsRead moreRead less
