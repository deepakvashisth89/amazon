This is an extremely difficult book to read; a true story with a tragic end.  The book provides an incredible view from the inside out of a lifetime of pain and suffering, struggles and trials with alcoholism.  For those who can see this evil spirit interwoven throughout the life of the author, seducing him into a darkness absent of God, hopelessly enthralled with this punishing and progressive disease.
