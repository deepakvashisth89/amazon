Let me just say one thing first: FINALLY! It took the writers nine books to resolve a couple of things. Geez. Anyway...I'm so proud of Jess...she's  really come far in the last few books. I'm so impressed by her ability to  do the right thing. She's rapidly becoming my favorite character, as  opposed to Elizabeth, who is rapidly declining in her moral judgments  (ugh...Sal and Elizabeth: Kill me now! Sal and Anna all the way...she  deserves him more anyway). At any rate, I thank God this book didn't have  anymore love triangle crap in it. Instead, it dealt quite realistically  with elections. I could relate to what was going on, and personally, I  really think Bethel should have won. She deserved to after she told off  those losers. But I also think she didn't really deserve it because of her  campaign. It was downright rude. She should really know better. Anyway.  Suffice to say that the ending of the book is absolutely priceless. Even if  you hate everything else in the book, the last scene is worth the price of  admission. So go buy it! Read it!
