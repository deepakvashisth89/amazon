Ruth Rendell's One Across, Two Down features Vera and Stanley Marsh, a middle-aged couple struggling to endure a miserable marriage that's grown worse by the inclusion in their tiny home of an insufferable in-law.Rendell's character development and plot presentation are masterfully woven, leading the reader to speed through the book.
