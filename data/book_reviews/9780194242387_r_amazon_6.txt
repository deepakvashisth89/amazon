Much better than I was expecting. 4 stars really but 5 because I love John Christopher. ( Death of grass -one of the best,wrinkle of the skin -good adventure,Empty world - very good bleak teen fiction. Tripods-only OK ).Loved the premise of colonialism being reversed . John Christopherson clearly was not racist but was still very much a product of his time which people could find upsetting. The language was supposed to be racist/offensive but equally I am sure he would have used different language today. The hero certainly was flawed. He was initially sanguine about being thrown into the slums, accepting the new order and was prepared to fight against the whites. But as soon as his lot improved he was spending money like nothing had changed (gin and tonics at the club eh chum?) with no evidence that he’d learnt from his experience.  He could have used some of those incredibly skilled (white) refugees to build up some businesses and therefore improve both his own and their situation as per the advice he was given as soon as he arrived in Nigeria but he didn’t. I was  annoyed by the ending , but less so when John Christopher in the epilogue said that this was the third ending after the editor had rejected the original as being “too bleak”!Overall we will always go back to our own if we can.I wonder if I could survive?Would I become a cannibal?Could I build rafts to send people away to die?This is why I read books on the apocalypse!Read moreRead less
