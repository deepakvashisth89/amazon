What can one say about Chinua Achebe except for great praise? I have been an admirer of Mr. Achebe's works since elementary school. He is one of the great storytellers of our time whose narratives are rich with wisdom and culture. He makes one appreciate the continent of Africa, its people, and its various cultures.  He used novels and novellas to chronicle ancient African rituals and beliefs, this is the best way to preserve what can be remembered of the peoples of the Motherland.
