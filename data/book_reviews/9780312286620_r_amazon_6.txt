Clocking in at 105 pages, this is a volume that can be read in an hour or two.  But for as short as this is, and it clearly states that it's an "unauthorized biography" right on the cover, it gives a good enough overview of J. K. (Joanne Kathleen) Rowling's life.It keeps going back to her love of reading and writing and how that got her through trying times in her life.  It also focuses on how she was determined to stay level-headed after the money rolled in and fame came calling.And Mr. Shapiro debunked in minimum space the fantasy that Ms. Rowling was dirt poor and on public assistance when she wrote the first Harry Potter book.  Turns out it was part true, but the media blew out of proportion the public assistance and single mother parts.No matter the level of writing (3rd grade or whatever other grade), Ms. Rowling's "story behind the story" is worth reading.  - Donna Di GiacomoRead moreRead less
