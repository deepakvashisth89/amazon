I understand that authors, need to provide a little background when they’re writing a sequel, but the prodigal daughter did not catch up to the timeline at the end of the first book until 46% into the book. Much of that was additional background on the main character, but large sections of it, maybe a third, seem to be copy and paste from the first book. Horribly repetitive.Beyond that, the story was extremely linear with no twists or turns as existed in the first book. Any obstacles were more like bumps in the road rather than a hurdle or a roadblock. While there was a surprise death, it seemed to have no impact on the rest of the book. I finally stopped reading it on Kindle and focused on just listening on Audible so that I could do other things while I was finishing the book. Admittedly, with 41 minutes to go on Audible there was an interesting scene at the White House. That was about it. I won’t be reading book 3 and recommend you save some time and skip this one.Read moreRead less
