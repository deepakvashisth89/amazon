A set of short stories by Larry Niven and friends, about, you guessed it, the Man-Kzin wars. Definitely a part two, the stories follow-on directly from the previous book in the series and suffers from the same weaknesses, in that it tries to flesh out the back story of the Kzin whilst papering over some of the criticisms of the author's older works. The main story features an intelligent female Kzin (something completely contrary to the previous descriptions of Kzin females, although a reason is given) and a pride of pre-historic Kzin who are even more bone-headed than the modern versions (balanced by the discovery of Human Neandertals), the human hero wins out in the end, as he always does. It's a reasonable set of SF short stories, entertaining enough, but which might irritate more than enlighten Larry Niven buffs.Read moreRead less
