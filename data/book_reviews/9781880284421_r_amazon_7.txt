I am really enjoying these books, but there are a few inconsistencies that are driving me batty. The stories are well researched, descriptive, and all-around enjoyable, and I will definitely read the next one ASAP.That being said...so far, the series doesn’t seem to span more than a year in time, but in one book, Conner is 29 and Miah is 19, and in the next, she’s 32 and he’s in his twenties. Her dog Caspar, is sometimes referred to as “he” and sometimes as “she”, and the dispatcher, Rebecca, goes from 70’s, to 82, to 84. There are several instances of “the” becoming “die”, “corner” becoming “comer” and a lot of typos. And the letter “j” appears randomly.
