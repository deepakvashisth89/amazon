This book had a bit of a split personality. On the one hand, it was a detailed work of historical fiction. On the other, it was a sometimes raunchy romance novel.It worked more as historical fiction, which made the occassional descriptive sex scenes seem way out of place.The relationship between Niall and Emmeline was not at all romantic. I don't think I have ever read a romance novel featuring a sexually active couple where not one word of affection passes between them until the last few pages of the book, as was the case here.The interactions betweem the hero and heroine seemed wooden. There is no tension. Their "matings" seem cold and impersonal.Was this an early effort by this writer? If so, I would like to give some of her later work a try.
