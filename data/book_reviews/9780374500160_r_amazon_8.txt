I greatly admired Hamsun's ability to make me taste his chief character's latent self-concept when it is dragged to the surface(and through the mud?) by interest in, competition for, a capricious woman.  His spare writing style gave only enough detail to prompt my brain to fill in the emotions from my own reservoir and squirm with unhappy recognition.
