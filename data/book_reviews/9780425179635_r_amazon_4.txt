I have enjoyed R.Walkers  stories, his characters are engaging and plots well thought out. Unfortunately the formatting gets in the way. Throughout the text there are hyphenated words, most if not all of these words  shouldn't be hyphenated. The problem is pervasive and definitely detracts from the story. Usually I tend to take formatting and minor grammatical errors  in stride and without comment but unfortunately these detract to much from an otherwise good story. The other "Instinct" books didn't have this issue.  I am using a Kindle app on  an android device ( Acer running I.C.S. ) I really don't think that this is the issue nor is it a hidden message incorporating the different word groups or Morse code. This aside it is an excellent series and an author worth reading.Read moreRead less
