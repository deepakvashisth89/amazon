In this last installment of the Inspector Thanet series Dorothy Simpson wraps it up: Thanet and wife become grandparents! The series is partly character driven. We watch children grow up, adults grow into their varies roles. All this is happening while mysteries, or cases, are being solved.I really hated to see the series come to an end. This last story is great, I really enjoyed it! Mrs Simpson is deserving of 5 stars, one star deducted for poor editing.Mrs Simpson, thank you for giving us Inspector Thanet! He was a refreshing departure from the single alcoholic detective who drives a beat up car! You gave us a caring policeman in a stable marriage.
