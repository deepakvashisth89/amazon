A beautiful story!  Sixteen year old Richard Junior's life makes a dramatic turn around after his parents pass away and his Aunt Victoria invites him to come live with her. Unsure how this would turn out, they agreed to do this on a trial basis.  Read this story and see how her love and compassion reaches beyond her cherished nephew to include what was considered to be some of the bad boys in town.  Discover how a little tender, loving care changed those boys and changed people's opinion of them.  I highly recommend this book to any group of readers.Read moreRead less
