Do you like an author to leave you space to imagine your own landscape, or do you like your story spoonfed to you?. If you are the latter, you will be disappointed because this master story teller gives you room and time to colour this nighthorse world in your own tones. Don't expect a simple descriptive story where that landscape is sharply drawn but rather a somewhat sketched one where you will dream your own illustrations which will be unique to you. The people are flawed and real, the nighthorses are attractive and terrifying. Its a complicated world and a well written pair of books, I love it.Read moreRead less
