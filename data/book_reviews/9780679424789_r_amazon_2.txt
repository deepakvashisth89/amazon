A great introduction to Faulkner’s genius. He deserves his reputation. It is sad that recognition came to him so late after working so long on many books, poems and screenplays. One has to admire his persistence, but then perhaps writing for its own sake must have been all that mattered, or nearly all. Faulkner’s short stories are powerful, raising the senses. Original in style and prose. Wonderfully descriptive of varied people, situations and places. He puts us in the mind of a boy effortlessly in two stories and gives us the unmistakable flavor of southern culture in times gone by.
