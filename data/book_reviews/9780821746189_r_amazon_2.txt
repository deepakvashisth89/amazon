At first I was not sure if I wanted to read this book, as it started in a brothel. Maggie had been lost then abused and nearly kidnapped when Mrs. Hill, theProprietor, told a staff person to take her upstairs and call the doctor. While waiting Mrs. Hill gave her medicine that made her very quiet. Awhile later a man showed up looking for a quiet whore and Mrs. Hill sent him to wrong room where Mary Margaret, called Maggie, was waiting for the doctor. She thought Conner was the doc and was very compliant especially after he urged her to relax and drink some whiskey he had with him. There is some mystery, a forgetting on Maggie's part and Conner is missing his bag of $12,000 that he was going to use to buy his ranch back.
