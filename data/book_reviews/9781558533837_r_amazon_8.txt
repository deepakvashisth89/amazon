I wanted this book when it first came out. I remember it well, I was a teenager and was grocery shopping with my mother. Friends was my favorite show, and I loved cooking. Now, as an adult, I finally found the book I have wanted for almost 20 years, and it is all my own. I love the little references to friends, the photos, and all the neat recipes that pertain to the characters and storylines.
