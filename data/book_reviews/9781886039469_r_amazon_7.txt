I didn't buy this book for marriage purposes! I just met someone and he lives 1000 miles away.  It gave us a good way to start learning about each other. The topics and questions cover just about everything from the basic 3 - religion, sex and politics, to the everyday stuff like, "are the pets allowed to sleep on the bed?"  And while the questions are presented in a breezy and fun manner, it still gets you talking and can lead to deeper discussion.
