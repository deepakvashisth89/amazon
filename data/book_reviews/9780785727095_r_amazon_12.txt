Where questions from the second party only appear in the answers of the main character, Dolores Claiborne.All but accused to have murdered Vera Donovan - the old crone, obviously immobile and halfway down the Alzheimer path, she has a confession of her own to make. Listen well, write down everything and register on tape - such a surprise You won't get every day.Dolores tells the whole story of her life - about her work, her children and her husband Joe. Who is creating a lot of trouble for that hard working woman, on top of the mostly  verbal abuse from Vera Donovan. But when something oh so cruel, oh so unspeakable is happening in her family, it is Vera who moves the right cogs in Dolores' brain. And gives her a helping hand - but only from some mile away. Right on the day of the eclipse someone will pay for combining great mischief, the greatest..., only difficult to imagine.There were questions, that time after the eclipse, oh there were! But Dolores stood up to all of them. Until now...This book cannot be called a thriller - but the tension rises with every word Dolores is giving to the listeners. In her original New England fisherwoman's dialect, right from the deepest, darkest places of her heart she lays her life and destiny into the hands of the law. And the law is judging now the death of Vera Donovan.
