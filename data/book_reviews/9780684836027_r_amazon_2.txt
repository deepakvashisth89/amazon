As a medical student, I've spent an awful lot of time in similar situations as the characters of this book, and I must say that the author describes them poorly.  The story is less believable than it could be.  It  lacks the necessary explanation and description for the way the characters  interact.  Another major problem is that the story jumps around violently  and the characters flip sides too quickly.  The problems found in the  begining resolve way too easily in the end.  It's almost as if the author  and editor were rushed to get this novel done.  This idea is supported by  the glaring spelling errors I found while reading.
