This book has a lot of jokes (in spanish) regarding everyday life nowadays, from a modern woman's point of view. The author, Maitena, is quite well-known in Argentina, and her work has also been published in Spain and France.If you read it, not only will you have fun, you'll also improve your spanish. I recommend it to women, but also to men (I think that after reading this book they are highly likely to understand us better:)!!).On the whole, I think that buying this book is a good idea if you want to laugh a lot, and practise your spanish.
