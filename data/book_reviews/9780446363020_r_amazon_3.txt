Great true crime book about Randy Kraft aka The Scorecard Killer. A brutal sadistic sexual sadist who targeted young teenage boys and young men in their 20s. When arrested in 1983 for suspicion of D.U.I. cops were surprised to find the dead body of a young marine in the passenger seat beside where Kraft was sitting in the driver's seat. Also found was the now infamous Scorecard with over 60 entries in code representing Kraft's many victims. These boys and men suffered horribly because Kraft's MO was to abduct, rape and then sexually torture the young men who made the grave mistake of accepting a ride from Kraft or agreeing to accompany him to his apartment. Kraft was tried and convicted on the 16 murders that the police had the most evidence on and has been on Death Row in California since the late 1980s. In an astonishing coincidence Randy Kraft was only one of Three Homosexual Serial Killers operating in Southern California during the late 1970s and early 1980s. One was very similar to Kraft, as he was also a Sadistic Homosexual Serial Killer who liked to Rape, Torture and then Murder his young male victims, but he also recruited male accomplices to assist him in his depravity. William Bonin aka The Freeway Killer, who was housed with Kraft on Death Row in California until his execution. The other homosexual serial killer operating in that time and place was Patrick Kearney aka The Trashbag Killer. Unlike Sexual Sadists Kraft and Bonin, Kearney was a necrophiliac who took no pleasure in inflicting pain or fear in his victim. His M.O. was to painlessly and quickly murder his victims and then take his time with their corpses, dismembering them and dumping the body parts along the Southern California freeways. Kearney made a full confession to 28 murders and cleared his lover arrested with him of any involvement in his crimes.
