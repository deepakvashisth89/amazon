You can run from your past, you can hide, but it eventually, it will catch up to you.This is what happens to the heroine Ellie, who's afraid to love and hides in a small-town, and trying to blend, lied about her past.But the past, in the form of her blackmailing mother, is coming to get her. Ellie tries to run away, once again, but her mother is murdered and guess who's the prime suspect ? Luckily, her ex-boyfriend, Tommy, refuses to believe in her guilt. He will have to use all his skills as a cop to solve this case !It's a good plot, a nice mystery and the two heroes are well done characters, not too sweet, not to harsh. Perfect.
