I generally like Elliot's books, but this one seemed full of characters, none well developed, a lot of busy action, but I wasn't able to get engaged with the protagonist or the world she was in.  I found myself thinking maybe it is a young adult book.  Near the end I started to get into it and I might read the second volumn in the hopes this is just a slow start on a long story.  On the other had, I didn't dash out and buy it.In fairness, this isn't a bad book by any means, and the action is steady.  It just isn't up to Elliot's potential.Read moreRead less
