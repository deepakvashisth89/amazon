What a treat for Sherlock Holmes fans.  Of course, I've read all of the stories and novels.  Of course I already have the complete collection, and then some.  This book is an amazing addition for fans.First, the downside.  This is a facsimile of the ORIGINAL Strand issues, which means that some pages have text that looks a bit faded, or light, as has been complained about by other reviewers.  But I ask you, what did the typical print look like 126 years ago?  Most readers picked up magazines that had "soft print" off the shelf.  So, I guess it's not a downside after all.To the most striking positive, this is Sherlock Holmes as was read in it's originality!  This is as close to an actual copy as you will get.  Next to my original 1907 edition of "Adventures, Memoirs, and Sign of Four" this is my second favorite collection of stories.  (I admit, I would much rather read this copy, then risk turning the pages of my 110 year old book!To read it in it's original magazine, double column format with artwork from Sidney Paget is simply a treat!  It transports you to a simpler time on the back streets of London.As a devoted fan and fellow Sherlock Holmes author, I cannot but praise it highly!You will not regret this book purchase.Read moreRead less
