I bought this second-hand a few weeks ago (excellent service from the seller and book as expected) as I had not been able to find a recent reprint. I have not quite finished reading the book but I am enjoying it very much. The plot is original  and intriguing, the characters are very much  alive, believable and so interesting you'd love to meet them and be "there" with them. The book belongs to a golden age of "escapist" writing, (and there's nothing wrong with that when the standard is this high), in company with those by Mary Stewart and MM Kaye to mention but two other authors.It's a great lesson on writing novels without having the plot crowded with sex scenes (mostly cloned right through the book and even lent to its eventual sister publications), deviated and /or idiotic personalities (when the characters have them)  and the sad, sad lauds to consumerism of so many contemporary, publicity boosted "bestsellers".Must we truly be plagued by  oceans of badly-written, boring, waste of good money epics, trilogies and series these days? You read them, knowing they'll end up in the recycling bin, and you wonder a) if there still are real editors around, and b) if some of  the stuff  given out to the hungry reader nowadays is written by ghost writers with a contract and no supervision. People like Catherine Gaskin  had care and love for their work. Does no-one write like this any more?A book that has already earned its place on my shelves.Read moreRead less
