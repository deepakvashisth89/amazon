Prostitutes are being murdered on the streets of 18th century London and Sir John Fielding is on the case. However, some were dispatched with a single thrust of a thin blade to just the right place, indicating the murderer knows anatomy. Others were cut open and certain of their internal organs were removed, again by someone with anatomical knowledge. Is this the work of a surgeon? Could it be a butcher? Or might the murderer be a soldier? Or is there more than one assassin?This is yet another of Mr. Alexander's books that conveys a feel for the time place and people.
