This is the third and final novel in the revised, expanded Author's Cuts of Janet Morris' classic Kerrion Empire Trilogy. She saved the best for last, and this volume really delivers. The first time I read this novel in its original edition, I was positively wowed. For me it contained all the great elements of Isaac Asimov, Poul Anderson, and Frank Herbert, to name three, not to mention such great science fiction films such as Star Trek and Star Wars. I really related to Shebat, the earth witch, and could easily understand her odyssey. This is grand adventure on an intergalactic scale, with well-drawn characters, some serious human drama, thoughtful ideas, intelligent concepts, and a fine sense of humor -- all the usual hallmarks you'll find in any of the novels written by Janet Morris (and those with her husband, Chris, too.) Morris is a fabulous author who has a real gift for style and prose, too. She always uses just the right tone for every novel and series she writes. Kudo to Janet Morris!Read moreRead less
