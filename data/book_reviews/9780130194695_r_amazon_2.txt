With 6+ years marketing management experience and an MBA, I feel this book was very helpful in understanding the many facets of technology and its evolution through time.  The book is a must read for anyone who needs to build or brush up on a solid but basic understanding of technology.  However, many of the newest trends in technology are only briefly covered.
