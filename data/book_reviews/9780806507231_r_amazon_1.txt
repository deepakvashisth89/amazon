Even if you are just a small fan of the Three Stooges you will enjoy this book.  It is actually the memoirs of Moe Howard and highlights not only some of his most vivid memories of his childhood and acting career, but also the times in which he lived.  It is well written and mostly humorous, though there are a few sad turns.  It doesn't take long to read and is broken up into nice segments (so you can pick it up when you have a break and get through a chapter quickly).  It lends insight into some of the makings of the Stooges shows and movies, which makes you want to watch their works more closely, and also into why Moe had to switch which brother or "Curly" substitute he worked with throughout his career.  And yes, it lends explanation to how some of their signature moves evolved and became their trademarks. It's also full of awesome pics. I was very glad Mr. Howard took the time to write this book...I was only a slight fan of The Three Stooges before I read it, but after reading it I'm not only a bigger fan, I've come to admire him.Read moreRead less
