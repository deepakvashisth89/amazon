Ace Starry has done a wonderful job of bringing his "novel philosophy" right to your front door... all you have to do is open the door and let it in! Following in the footsteps of authors like Richard Bach  (Illusions) and James Redfield (The Celestine Prophecy), Ace weaves a  delightful and engaging yarn that's an easy-read, yet leaves plenty of  substance and new ideas to think about.  His narrative is believable and  companionable, reminiscent, in some way, of a slice of everyone's life.  As  such, it becomes easy to see the ways you can immediately apply the  philosophy in your own life.
