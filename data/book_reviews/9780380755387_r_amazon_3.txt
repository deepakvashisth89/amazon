I enjoyed the books by Charlotte MacLeod, who also wrote as Alisa Craig, many years ago, but had not seen any of her works in print for decades. This situation with many authors from the past is why I finally bought a Kindle Paperwhite, and I have recently purchased all of the Madoc & Janet Rhys books, as well as a few others from the other series that she wrote. My only complaint is that these books for Kindle are almost always as expensive as getting an actual book, but sometimes the individual books will suddenly be only $2 or $3, instead of $8 or more, and then I try to get them before the price goes up again. I always thought that the whole idea for developing e-readers was to give people the chance to buy book downloads for less than a physical copy would be, but that doesn't seem to be the case. Sellers of e-readers might stop to consider that for an e-reader to be cost-effective, the books should be priced accordingly, especially for these older books.
