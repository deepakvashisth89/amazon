I read one of his stories in the New Yorker, liked it, and got this book out of the library.  Most of the characters are in their 50's.  Their lives aren't working.  Something bad happens.  I like the writing very much.  I don't know what literature is.  I know what fun is.  Reading these stories is fun.I'm about the same age as most of the characters.  Boyle writes the way it is, when you know at least half of your life is over and your abilities are declining.
