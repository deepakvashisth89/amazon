Simple locked room mystery. Massively marred by the incorrect technical details. A transformer is supposed to be used to change 106 volts to 20 volts. Impossible, since the supply is supposed to come from batteries (DC) and a galvanometer is being used to record the voltage (only works on DC). A transformer will only work on AC. No exceptions. At 100 volts DC the shock would not be fatal, so the editor or author has changed it to 416 volts. 400 volts DC is used in electropainting and doesn't produce the classic electrocution death. Sorry to be pedantic but I'm an engineer and know about this stuff. Normally, Mr Lovesey's books are impeccably researched, but this was definitely an off day.Read moreRead less
