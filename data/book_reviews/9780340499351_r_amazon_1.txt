I have read this novel every few years over a span of about four decades--I will buy any used copy I come across at garage sales, library sales, etc. so I have a few copies around when I need a "fix." I think the magic this book has for me is the portrayal of an individual in crisis, dragged down by lassitude and ennui, and the path towards restoration and wholeness. The crucible for this development is an intended vacation (or retreat), and how  a stranger in a strange land can reinvent himself through challenge, friendship and even love. The way in which this happens is the arc of the story.
