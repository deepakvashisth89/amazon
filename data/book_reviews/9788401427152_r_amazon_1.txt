A gem of Spanish Literature. To be read in Spanish, if possible, to enjoy the richness of the language. Sampedro managed to weave the feelings and tenderness of a grandfather towards his baby grandson, talking to him by his cot, together with his memories of the war and the discovery of love at an old age, into this unforgettable novel.Gripping from the very beginning, as we follow Rusconi's steps from the south of Italy to Milan, where he discovers that his days are counted.Read moreRead less
