This review is probably a bit partial because I'm a big Ian McEwan fan. It's nigh impossible to judge a book objectively when one absolutely adores the author. Ian McEwan is the Booker Prize winning of author of 'Amsterdam' and the critically acclaimed 'Atonement'.This book is a collection of 8 short stories and it's author's earliest published work. When you take up reading an Ian McEwan book, you must know exactly what you are getting into. Some of the themes and stories in the book are not for people with delicate sensibilities as it deals with subjects of incestuous rape, child-molesters, an emotionally stunted man-child who likes enclosed spaces, etc.The stories are dark, comical, perverse and lurid all at the same time. I can't think of any other author who could take one on an emotional roller-coaster with such sparse prose. Words are not wasted and yet McEwan is able to make one feel every emotion with such powerful force. I don't want to give anything away because some of these stories have what some might describe as little 'twists' in the end. There is a deep and disturbing undertow to each of these stories.
