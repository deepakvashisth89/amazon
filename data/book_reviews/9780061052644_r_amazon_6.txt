I don't know if I'll ever give Greg Egan less than 5 stars. Maybe more philosophy than science and still more science than any other author I've read. It seems that heaven for Egan may be the same as the one I have contemplated myself: understanding.The exploration of sexuality was both brutal and beautiful. I will make my sisters read this.Distress was a slower start since I'd come from Schild's Ladder and didn't quite want to be stuck on Earth, but once I got going I was reading through my multivariable calculus class.
