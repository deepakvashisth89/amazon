It was hard to put this book down, even though it’s not technically a thriller, a mystery, or a deep romance.Perhaps it is all those genres combined in a quiet, soft, and absorbing way, because I couldn’t wait to get back to Lark’s story whenever I had to put this book down (due to frustrating interruptions in my reading time). The author drew me into Lark’s lonely yet intriguing life the way that those other genres with their characters tend to do.Andrew Holleran hypnotizes the reader with the easy flow of his smooth, beautiful, and enchanting words.I didn’t want this book to end.Eagerly I kept wanting to return to Lark’s story, even if it was in the middle of the night.Although I needed time after finishing this book to reflect on Lark’s journey and how his future may have unfolded — you can’t help wondering this because of the way this author has pulled you in and attached Lark to your mind and heart —  after closing the last page of this book, I eagerly wanted to find and read the rest of  Andrew Holleran’s other works. He is that talented of an author.P.S. In this book, there are descriptions and continuations of some of the characters from the book “Surviving the Plague.” (I think I have that book’s title correct; I can’t go back to check without losing my review here.)That book too, by the way, is a phenomenal and deeply resounding book that I highly recommend.Read moreRead less
