When I sent my first copy of Provinces of Night to a friend, I ordered a second to read again.  The late William Gay was a remarkable writer, this work among his best, in my opinion.  His prose is crafted with clarity and focus.  Throughout this novel, he gifted readers with rich, imaginative passages.
