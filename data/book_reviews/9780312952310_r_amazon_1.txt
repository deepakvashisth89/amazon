TRUTH OR FICTION??? I am not sure which it is, but it kept me on the edge of my seat!!!  I ABSOLUTELY BELIEVE something like this COULD HAVE HAPPENED.  That is what I find so intriguing about this book.  I am not sure in today's world it would be possible, due to media, Homeland Security, social media, etc. But, during the ' Cold War ' years, YES !!!  It seems reasonable.The book reads easily and the characters are engaging, well developed, and draw you in quickly.Anyone who like spy stories, remembers the Cold War, or just a good suspenseful book should enjoy this one.
