I'd love to find more books like this one.  It is always more of an escape to me to read about an "ordinary" heroine who then encounters a series of extraordinary circumstances and issues than to read about some bizarre group or faraway land where all the politics and conventions are completely made up. This book doesnt go too far the other direction either (a common mistake) where a story is just the main character's narration in some cynical, tongue-in-cheek tone.Instead, this book does a delightful job of approaching how someone would struggle to gain understanding of an inexplicable situation while on the run from threats that are viewed as bewildering as well.  Plus, we the readers get to see the story from the side of the hunters, the hunted, and even those caught-in-the-crossfire.I wish the author would write a sequel/series from this book.Read moreRead less
