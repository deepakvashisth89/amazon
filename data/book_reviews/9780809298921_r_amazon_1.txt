Firstbrook uses the 1999 expedition in search of answers about the fate of Mallory and Irving who disappeared in a 1924 summit quest, to recount the history of the principals and Western encounters with Chomolungma. It reads as an exciting adventure/whodunnit, and the account of how Conrad Anker figured out where Mallory likely came to rest, and then found him there, is gripping.
