THE LOST is billed as a Novel of Dark Discoveries and is a subtle horror that continues right to the last page.A prep school teacher in England learns that he is descended from Romanian nobility.  This knowledge puts him on a quest to reclaim his heritage.  There is talk of a family castle.  Considering the situation in Romania after the collapse of Russian Communism, the teacher thinks about turning the castle into an orphanage.  While pleading his case he enlists the aid of a young woman who tells him his claim will be processed more quickly if he claims he wants to start a hotel.  But the castle is very remote and the two are cut off for the winter.  Slowly the teacher learns the true heritage of his family.  A dark heritage involving the strigoi -- the undead (but not vampires).The book is laid out as a collection of letters, journal entries and transcripts from taped logs.  This format actually adds additional depth to the story.  It sort of reminds me of the original The Books of Magic when the battle for the world happens off stage.  This book is a lot like that.  But remember, this is subtle horror.  If you are looking for blood, gore and body parts then this is not for you.  But if you like a classic style in a modern setting, then you should definitely take a look at this book.Read moreRead less
