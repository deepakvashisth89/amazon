Unbelievable wonderful!  This is the 4th time I am reading the Eden Saga in the past 30 years and every time it's as if I were reading  it for the first time.I don't  know if this is truly a romance book because  it is not, but instead it's a tale of a young man that spans 10 years of his life. It's the heartbreaking,  emotional  saga of John Murrey Eden. .We follow John to his arrival at Eden Castle with high hopes of living a dream he had as he was growing up. But after a shaky  start he does obtain it .. but with much more then he ever dreamed.But his world turns into horror  and we follow him on trying  to redeem and restore his broken humunity and soul.And he succeeds. .It is a story very well written. .. you are there.. you feel all the hope, happiness,  sadness,  destruction. .I cried no much reading  this story. I have never read a book that contained  the forbidden and immortal  happenstance that occur, yet written and viewed from both sides , (John & Harriet's) is deemed so innocent  and true.It's an unbelievable  read .. so much happens  and of course it is fiction, but the storytelling  keeps you enthralled and breathless.Loved it ..Read moreRead less
