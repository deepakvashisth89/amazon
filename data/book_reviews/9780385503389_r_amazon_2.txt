While unsure of the authors competence in history I was very very pleased with the book and it's compelling history of the Armor Unit, They fought bravely and with distinction and still had to deal with the blatant bigotry of the time. Those hero's deserved better both in support at the time of the war but also after returning from it. They paved the way for future generations of Soldiers in the Army and I am one who is grateful for their actions. I sincerely recommend the book to anyone who has an interest in the conflict and the dismal race relation of the time. We have come so far and still have a ways to go.
