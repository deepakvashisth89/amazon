I bought this book several years ago, maybe when it was brand new. Unfortunately, something else was going on, and the book got shelved, unread. When I heard of Miller Williams' passing recently, I went to the bookshelf and retrieved it. Every single poem in this book is astonishing in so many ways and on so many levels. His mastery of form is beyond compare--and to have that form imposed on such clear, straightforward thoughts! These poems touch me to my very core.
