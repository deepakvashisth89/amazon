I purchased a 'like new' copy of this book from cARToon Book Empire and that's exactly what I got! The price seemed acceptable for a collector's book and turned out to be a great deal when I saw that this is a large volume of classic Mad with full color comics from the 50's. I've always been a huge fan of Tales Calculated to Drive You MAD, and this book contains several color comics from this as well as the black and white 'cultural commentary' comics from later on.
