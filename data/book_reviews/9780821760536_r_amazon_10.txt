I love Anne Stuart and I have high tolerance for weirdness in historical romance but the setting and themes here are odd - the Wicker Man in the 1700s, though it never comes together. The hero is singularly charmless compared to some of her other hard, sardonic men, and the plot meanders somewhat. Read it if you feel you need to read her entire backlist but it has very little of the usual magic despite the themes, and the central romances don’t carry the usual charge.Read moreRead less
