The one true attribute of Isaac Asimov was his ability to explain things, and to explain things you never really though of before.This is a collection of essays that first appeared in American Way Magazine, that inflight magazine on American Airlines.  They all deal with change and how that change affects the future, whether it was an historical change, like the invention of the strip, or a current one, like the falling birthrate in the Western world.
