Dr. Dan Conway, who was 14 in Texas Heart, is now a young man on the run. He is becoming a bit of a legend.Once again he meets up with the Comanche chief, Two Moons and his sister, Morning Light.The Comanche are again meeting up with death and destruction under the Indian Agent who is one nasty piece of work.We follow Dan as he is wounded and saved by Two Moons and Morning Light.  Two Moons now has a young four year old son who is inhabited by an evil spirit.Dan soon has his work cut out for him in gaining the People's trust and gaining Morning Light's trust and love.The Coyote, a fierce warrior [and a hot head] - wants Morning Light for himself as he plots to become chief of the Comanches.When Morning Light makes the choice and gives herself to Pale Wolf [Dr. Dan] Ishatia plans their downfall and destruction.Little does he know that Pale Wolf is a warrior himself.Dan joins the People in their quest for the great buffalo little knowing that this would be the People's downfall and eventual end.Throughout the story, Dan is being tracked by his brother, Thad [here I thought he was about 14 in this story] - Thad comes across as a young man of strength and ingenuity.  He helps save Morning Light and Runs With The Wind.Dan Conway, who has been on the run, is captured ---- I leave the story up to you to read.  You will certainly enjoy finding out how the whole tale works out and we again meet up with Jessie and Cole Matthews and their youngsters.
