I own nearly ever book by him.I finished this- it is part of a trilogy so if you buy it, do buy it in order of the books that go with it.He has written 3 trilogies thus far- this is the older threesome.I got hooked on him by reading *Prisoner of Birth*- an excellent suspense novel of a man wrongly imprisoned and the repercussions of that imprisonment.  I wish they would make a movie out of it!Basically I enjoyed all his books, some more than others, but hey, with any prolific writer, you are bound to enjoy some books more than others or have your own favorites.This man has been one busy writer. To throw some interesting back story in, he himself had been imprisoned for a few years.  His time in jail only served to inspire him to write more!Give Archer a try- but if you get this book- do remember it is part of a trilogy and reading trilogies out of order can really ruin your reading pleasure.Read moreRead less
