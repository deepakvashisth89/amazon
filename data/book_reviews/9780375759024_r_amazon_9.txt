Horace being a poet much-loved by previous generations of my family, I had naturally obstinately refused to really give him a try, preferring, to be honest, more modern British poets; that said, this poetry is undoubtedly of an extremely high quality and would reward a read from any poetry fan. Some of the sentiments expressed here, now virtually epigrams in their own right ('odi profanum vulgus et arceo' 'dulce est desipere in loco') resonated very strongly with me, and the succinct expression thereof definitely earned Horace my appreciation (by which I'm sure he would be intensely gratified!).
