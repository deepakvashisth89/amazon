This is the second time I,v read this book. Its based on Melville,s experiences of being part of a crew of a sailing ship on a trip from New York to Liverpool. The story describes the day to day life of a sailor and what the City and Port of Liverpool was like in the mid 19th century, going into great detail about its shipping and world beating dock system. He also describes various locations around the city which are still there today. This book is a good read for anyone interested in the period and is written in the style of the period also....Much recommended.Read moreRead less
