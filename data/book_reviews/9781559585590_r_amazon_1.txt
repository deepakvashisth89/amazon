I can not tell you how much I love this cookbook...If my house was on fire and I could only save one cookbook...it would be this one.  Everything I have made out of this book is delicious, and I do mean EVERYTHING!  It's huge and I haven't cooked them all, but I am working on it!  The recipes are easy to follow and not time consuming.  The ingredients are simple and you won't have to go hunting for exotic ingredients that you have never heard of and will never use again.  I have even bought this book for friends as gifts.  Some of the recipes are a little higher in fat (cheese... beautiful, yummy cheese!)but worth it!  I have a lot of vegetarian cookbooks, and a lot of them sit on the bookcase and get dusty.  Some only have 3 or 4 recipes in them that I would use...but this cookbook I seem to use everyday.
