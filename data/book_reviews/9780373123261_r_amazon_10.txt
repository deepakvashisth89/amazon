This is a different M&B to their usual offerings. Here the two leads Brooke and Leo have been married for five years, and have two children a son and daughter. When Leo's older brother dies, he Brooke and their children travel from their home in Australia to Italy.Whist there Leo is busy taking care of the family business as his Father is grieving, but is also not in good health. So long days and late nights, leave Brooke feeling neglected. Then one afternoon Brooke overhears a conversation between Leo's parents. It seems that Leo's Mother fears that Leo is still in love with Francesca, his brothers widow. As Brooke continues to eavesdrop she hears more distressing news. They reveal more information that is news to Brooke. Leo had been engaged to his brothers wife, before she married his brother. Leo's Mother doesn't believe he's working long hours at the office she thinks he is in bed with Francesca. Shocked and horrified, Brooke aims to find out. Brooke is familiar with driving in Italy, so borrows her Mother-in-laws car and drives to Francesca's apartment in Milan, and yes Leo's car is parked outside.This was okay, I quite liked it. There were problems with this though. First I did not get at all Brooke's reaction to finding out that her husband had been engaged to his brothers wife, or to finding his car outside her apartment. Did she confront him? No, she decided to make their sex life more interesting. what I did like was how she looked back on their marriage and realised that all the important decisions had been made by Leo, he wouldn't even allow her to choose either of their children's names! Or resume her work career! In other words she had become a doormat. So when her character did a 360% turn, that was very well done. Throughout the story Leo kept wondering what the hell was going on with his wife. I did wonder that after five years of marriage that they seemed to communicate so little, and didn't talk anything through. But I did find this an interesting read.Read moreRead less
