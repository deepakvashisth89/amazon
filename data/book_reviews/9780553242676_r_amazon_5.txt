When amateur sleuth Henry Gamadge is called upon to witness the opening of a room in a New York mansion that has been sealed for years, he senses that there is something rotten in the Clayborn house.  What’s rotten turns out to be a corpse in the sealed room.  Using his keen perception of human nature and formidable deductive powers, Gamadge sifts through the cast of well-drawn and varied suspects and lays the blame on a most unexpected criminal at the dramatic climax of the novel.
