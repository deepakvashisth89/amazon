I happened across this book several years ago and have picked it up at least 3-4 times a year to re-read it since then.  The story is one of a stuffy scholar and her brother who accompany a half-breed and his soldiers to map a route to California.  There are, of course, many trials & tribulations on their journey and several deaths.  The story takes several unexpected twists, flows very well & is easy to read.  I have a huge collection of "smutty indian" books, and this book is in my "top 5".  It is one of the best books I've ever read!Read moreRead less
