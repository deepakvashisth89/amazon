With Tom Siebel's reputation as a visionary of One-to-One marketing and a software innovator, this book was obvious, fundamental, and predictable, with very little that hasn't been said better and more astutely by other  writers i.e. Evans and Wurstler, Shapiro and Varian.  This is a collection  of the same old success stories culled from the same old interviews and the  same old articles. If you're really interested in the underlying changes in  the business world that the Internet brings, read "Information Rules'  by Shapiro and Varian or "Blown to Bits" by Evans and Wurstler.
