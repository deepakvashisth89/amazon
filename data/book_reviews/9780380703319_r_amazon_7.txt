Dittany lives in a quiet little town where everyone learns to shoot a bow and arrow at a young age. She comes across a plot by a politician backed by the rich elites in town to take over the town's mountain in order to build houses. She rallies the whole town to work together to run a new candidate and raise money to keep their land for bow hunters' and their families. in the ensuing chaos, a man is killed.
