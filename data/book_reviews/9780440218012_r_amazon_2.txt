This book started out a whole lot better than it ended.  Though for me I guess most of it was not news and therefore many people will enjoy it more.  It is simple enough reading for anyone to finish.  And just as a warning, some of the biblical stories are novelized on and off throughout the book.  (I don't know why)  Personally I find metal artifacts that 'should not be' and discovering the roof burnt houses of ancient Sodom fascinating, however naturalizing and explaining dried up rivers and city walls a bit boring.  And though the ark of the covenant should be fascinating as well, their lack of substance and evidence did not make it much of an exciting read.  However, thats also because I've already read Grant Jeffrey's book to which they constantly referred.  All in all I recommend this to those interested.
