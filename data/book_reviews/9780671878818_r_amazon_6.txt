This is the debut volume of the famous Daniel Leary & Adele Mundy series by David Drake. Space Opera and Military Science Fiction at its very best. The Kindle edition marks the third time that I have  read this Premier adventure. It just gets better with each reading. I will be going on to the next volume and rereading the entire series again. Thank goodness Drake is still writing the continuing adventures of Leary & Mundy. Great action with very emotional character development. Leary & Mundy have a high degree of Honor and a great deal of Patriotism for the RCN - the Royal Cinnabar Navy.
