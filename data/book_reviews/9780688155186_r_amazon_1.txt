Robin is homeless after her apartment burns. She moves into her friend, Tomaya's apartment at the Chelsea Hotel. Unfortunately, for Robin, Tomaya is rather free with her keys and Robin is awakened in the middle of the night by a bratty adolescent girl who claims that Tomaya said that she could stay there. The girl, Nadia, speaks with a Slavik accent but won't tell Robin where she is from--just that she is waiting to meet her boyfriend so that they can marry and that her family is not pleased because they've already picked out someone for her to marry. The next day while Robin is at work, Nadia disappears. In the meantime, the boy that Robin thinks is the boyfriend shows up and demands to be taken to Nadia. There is a murder of an art dealer named Wozniak and at first Robin is a suspect because when she goes to the apartment door to see what the noise was, the body falls on top of her.There are a lot of humorous moments in the book.
