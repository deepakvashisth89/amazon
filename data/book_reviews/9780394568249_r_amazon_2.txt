Here we have an examination of the lives of assorted old folks who live in an affluent suburb of San Francisco, and who grew up wealthy and privileged, without ever having the slightest grasp of just how well-off they were.  Now that they're getting old and having to face such things as terminal illness and death, they're doing a bad job of it.Translation:  "A bunch of aging hippies who never understood how well-off they really were."As someone who grew up on the other end of the socio-economic scale, I find it impossible to identify with any of the people in this book, and found myself, again and again, wanting to kick them and scream, "Oh, for God's sake!-- Grow the hell UP, already!!!"Maybe that's the idea behind this book.  Or maybe it isn't.  Absent anything indicating a clear purpose, I can't say.  All I know is that it left me wishing all the characters would just go off somewhere and quietly do themselves in.
