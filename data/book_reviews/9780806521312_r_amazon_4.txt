This book focuses on a devotional prayer life, a relationship with the God and Goddess, rather than a synopsis of what to believe, or rituals. It shows you how to create meaningful personal prayer, as well as prewritten prayers for specific occasions. The prayers are written from the perspective of humility, viewing the Lord and Lady as mystical parents rather than personifications of the self, coequals, or aspects of the same oneness of everything. It is very simplistic and beautiful language. The book views fate as dependent on the gods, it sounds very Christian in how one approaches deity, albeit their are two instead of one in three. It sounds very much like an Anglican book of common prayer.
