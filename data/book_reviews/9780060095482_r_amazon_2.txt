I have been reading alot of Military fact and fiction books lately. This series is about a specialized team of SEALS that are trained to secure objectives and weapons  dealing in Bio, Chemical and Nuclear warfare. The author puts together a great story about missiles being shipped to Sudan aboard a cargo ship during the early days of Desert Shield, Not too give away too much , the SEALS are tasked to seize and control the ship and deal with any personnel, intel and weapons of mass destruction aboard the ship.
