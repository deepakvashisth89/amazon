I was required to do a book review for an early childhood education class and this was the only book from the list of options that was in our college library at the time.  When I began reading, it was as if the author had been living with me since the births of my sons.  I subsequently ordered two copies...one for home and one for the school my children attend.  I have suggested/requested that the children that work with my sons read this book.  Many of the strategies suggested I have found through trial and many errors work for managing my sons' behaviors.
