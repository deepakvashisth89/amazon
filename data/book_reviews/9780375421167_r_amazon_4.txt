I picked this up at a Little Library. Based on the cover and a quick look inside, I figured what's not to love. The Roaring 20s! Set in New York with a magazine publication as the focus!It quickly turned tedious. Too many characters. Way too many. Most of them not doing particularly interesting things. Or at least the description of what the were doing wasn't interesting. The author, resolutely and from beginning to end, says things in an elliptical manner, forcing the reader to constantly re-read to be sure of what is being said.However, I decided to stick around because I did want to get to the highly predictable payoff. It was indeed predictable. And not worth it.Each time I put the book down, I was relieved. Each time I picked it up I really didn't want to. I have thus "completion" complex, though, so on I went. If you share this complex, I suggest you don't start. If you don't share this complex, I'll make the same suggestion.Read moreRead less
