Cornwell is a fantastic writer.  I have read and will continue to read his works over and over (i.e. Sharpe'sx5).  He builds amazing characters, sets them solidly into the tales, and draws the reader into a world both believable and appetizing to imagination.I am not a spy novel reader, so this storyline is not for me.  In fact, I find the mass production of tough guy, know it all, unflappable action figure characters to be repugnant to the intellect and makes people believe there are actual super-humans, and perhaps they might be one if only they take all the supplements offered as exactly what they need.  Digression aside, that's what I got from this character, Paul Shanahan.  I would have enjoyed him more if he had been more vulnerable, learned more through discovery rather than having innate knowledge and if more historically accurate context was made relevant to this story.That's just me, though.  If you're into the action figure genre or just need a story to take your mind off of a crowded plane flight, this book will win your heart.  Just don't expect to learn much or feel the ambiance of another time and place as you might with other excellent Cornwell creations.  Read on!Read moreRead less
