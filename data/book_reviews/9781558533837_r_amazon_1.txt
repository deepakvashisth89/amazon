I have a lot of cookbooks (from Food TV celebrities like Ina Garten's to the super refined America's Test Kitchen to NYT's Mark Bitman) and this is the only one I've found a need to write about. Every recipe in here is a winner. I make the pancakes (Aurora's Pancakes, which is a theme from the episode where Chandler dates a woman who has several other lovers) most often, because it's just the best pancake mix. They come out really fluffy and it's very simple to make (just be sure that your baking powder is fresh, I once made the recipe and used rather old baking powder that was in the pantry and while they still tasted amazing, they weren't as fluffy as they normally are.). It's also a good recipe to make with young children, because it's quick to prepare and you're eating pancakes in no time.Another great recipe is the meatloaf. It's amazing how delicious the glaze is with just a few simple ingredients.Some people have mentioned the brownie recipe and they are correct. I love the brownies and make them often. It's also another good recipe to make with children.I first bought it a couple of years ago to go with my friend's Christmas basket. She and I are huge Friends fans and when I saw it, I thought it went well with the theme of her gift (food gift). I bought one for her and one for myself, because I love Friends and the price was right. And here it is and I'm still leafing through the thing. I love the recipes in it so much and they work. I can't say I'm surprised, since Jack Bishop is a co-author and he's very highly respected at Cooks Illustrated. You'd actually think that the recipes are the same as America's Test Kitchen or Cooks Illustrated recipes, but at least in one occasion it is not. I made a batch of America's Test Kitchen brownies from their 10 Year book and it wasn't nearly as rich and chewy as the Friends cookbook recipe.I love, love, love this book.Read moreRead less
