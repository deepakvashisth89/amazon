Miners of a century ago found gold and silver in the San Juan Mountains.  If you're a mystery fan you'll find gold in "Time Trial".This  is an excellently constructed mystery with captivating plot, intriguing  characters and an outcome even the most experienced reader is unlikely to  predict.For those who've visited the San Juans the theme and events will  bring back fond memories of these majestic mountains and the mines and  miners that brought riches to so many. Whether you've roamed these  mountains or not, though, you'll strike it rich when you read this  novel.Derouin's second book, "San Juan Solution" is already  out and equally absorbing.
