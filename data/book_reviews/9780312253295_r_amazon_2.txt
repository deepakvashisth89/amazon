I deliberated between three and four stars. Decided on four because it was an entertaining book. The main thing I didn’t like about the book is how these ladies acted and were treated like they were old and feeble. Two of the ladies were put into the folks residence when they are in their 60s and quite capable of taking care of them selves. Also I found myself skipping paragraphs here and there because there was just too much detail but not a lot going on in some parts of the book.
