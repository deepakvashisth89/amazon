Broad encyclopedic study of Biblical references, but with little depth about social or historical forces that shape these.  For example, I would have liked some discussion of the Victorian Naturalist or the American  Transcendentalist. Addition there is no discussion about Creation  Spirituality of Thomas Berry or Matthew Fox.  Also I think the author has  unwritten assumptions, about what is right, such as vegetarianism.  I would  have also liked some discussion of the common ethical issues in the  cultures and religion and why the societies do not seem to match the ideals  of their respective religions.  There is still a lot of useful information,  and some common threads in all religions, such as the needless suffering in  animals and stewardship.
