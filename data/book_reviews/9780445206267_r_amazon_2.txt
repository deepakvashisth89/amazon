I have all of Claudia J. Edwards' books; I love them. I wouldn't trade them for love or money. Her writing is not the tightest, but her characters glow with emotion and determination. Her women, especially, are incredibly strong and capable characters. While hardly flawless, her female character struggle through with grit, and that only makes it better.Throw in a lot of horses and all i can wonder, repeatedly, is why there isn't any more!
