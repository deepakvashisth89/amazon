I read this book once when I was in middle school because I was a big fan of LeGuin’s Earthsea books. I didn’t remember much about it beyond liking it, but I didn’t read it over and over the way I read her other ones. It’s a very delicate and melancholy story. It has shades of one of the huge issues our society faces now: namely the inner mental workings of the heterosexual white male teen when it comes to girls and their expectations. It’s so brief it’s almost a short story. And the voice of the protagonist is reminiscent of the style of one of my favorite YA authors, Paul Zindel. Read it.
