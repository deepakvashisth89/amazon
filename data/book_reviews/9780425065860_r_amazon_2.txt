I love that this series kept me guessing all the way through to the end. Aside from the rich world building (I want to see more of the universe that was only hinted at!) and the rich, descriptive language, I love the glimpses at the scientific theories that allow interstellar travel. I noticed small details hearkening back to our era and love how she shows diverging cultures and that her characters are multi-dimensional. The "bad guy" isn't a trope here, it is a point of view.
