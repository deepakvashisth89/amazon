The plot begins like a story about the biblical Job.  Silas, a young man, feels connected to his neighbors and his God.  He works hard and looks forward to a normal life.  But then he suffers an injustice at the hands of his Christian neighbors and blames his community and God.  He suffers a self imposed alienation from both.  He resorts to greediness.  Suffers injustice again and hits bottom.  Then the focus appears to turn to how does such a person redeem his faith in neighbors and God? How does a community redeem an outcast like Silas Marner?  Also interesting was how the Church functions in the community to provide for the aged and and the disabled.  Marner is told he would be taken care of despite the fact that he for years made no contributions to the Church to help it provide for his old age and disability.
