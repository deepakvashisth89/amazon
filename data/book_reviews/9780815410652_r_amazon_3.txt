In search of a book that could help me understand the beginnings of country rock and the astounding commercial success of the Eagles, I took a chance on this book -- and I'm glad I did!  There are numerous books about the drama associated with country rock bands, but I already knew all I wanted to know about that. I wanted to understand the MUSIC, thank you -- where it came from, who inspired it, how it evolved, why it succeeded.  The author does a fine job of telling the musical side of the story.
