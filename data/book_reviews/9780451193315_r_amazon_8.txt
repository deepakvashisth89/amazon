It's been awhile since I had last read a story in this series and was excited to pick it up.  It was a quick read and true to form for Ralph Compton.  Of course there were the geographic issues that he seems to struggle with such as his descriptions of San Francisco (there was dialog about how the two characters would cross the bay if a ferry was not available as if swimming were an option).  The story for the most part roles along and, as usual, Compton tries to tie in characters from past stories and historical events.  Sometimes it seems like the dialog repeats itself to fill pages but we eventually get through the story... or did we?  The ending sort of reminded me of Back to the Future, Part 2 where the big letters come on the screen at the end saying "to be continued."  So now I need to jump into the "Train to Durango: Border Empire 2" and hopefully get to the end of all this.If you have made it through the first 4 books of the series then this is a decent fifth book.
