I've read this series somewhat out of order, starting with China Trade but then leaping forwards to Out for Blood, back to Concourse, then Mandarin Plaid, The Shanghai Moon and now this. I have enjoyed them all, but I would say I think the 'Bill Smith' narrated ones are somewhat stronger, and this is an example. I found the plots of Concourse (property speculation) and No Colder Place (construction), rooted in different forms of corruption in New York, most convincing, though The Shanghai Moon had some excellent historical aspects.
