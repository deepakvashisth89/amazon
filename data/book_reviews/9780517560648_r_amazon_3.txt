This book is slowly growing on me. It is not as good as 'The Autobiography of Henry VXXX' which I read before it. That one was really gripping and hard to put down. However, the slow development of Elizabeth's character and those around her is well done. The historical detail is very good and the use of English to convey the way that people spoke then is excellent. The writer doesn't fall into too much modern English but uses it to help us understand.Some of the love and sex scenes are a bit soap opera like, I would rather they were suggested instead of directly described. That way they come across as more real in the end.All in all I am enjoying the novel.Read moreRead less
