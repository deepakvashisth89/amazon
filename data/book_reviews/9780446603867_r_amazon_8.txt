Book number 21, and I'm coming up on the half way point of this series. When I started this read I thought it would be a monumental task, but it's turning out to be relatively easy. Most of that is due to characterizations of the principal detectives of the 87th Precinct. Not all of them are featured in each book and that seems to keep them fresh and more interesting.This book goes back to the format of having two cases being investigated at the same time. There is little cross over and different detectives handle each one. The main case involves a TV personality who drops dead during his live show. An estimated forty million viewers nationwide witness his death, hence the title.  The secondary case gives a bit more insight into Bert Kling's personal life and has a 'to be continued' feel to it.  Another solid read in the series.Read moreRead less
