I was delighted to find this old chestnut available electronically....very hard to find on paper. And cheap. This book is of interest mostly because it's Taylor's first mystery, and introduces Asey Mayo. Lots of period flavor...16 cylinder automobiles that get four miles to the gallon, household servants, Cape Cod with dirt roads. Otherwise, a conventional early thirties mystery: summer house party, several suspects, motives galore, baffled cops, several false denouements.
