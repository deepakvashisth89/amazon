I love Asey Mayo stories, and I have no problem with this one either. But it was rendered barely readable by how bad this Kindle version is. Hard to even know where to begin with the issues... There were repeated lines, mixed up lines, repeated paragraphs, skipped words, punctuation problems, words that were just totally wrong except for maybe they sounded sort of like the right word, places where two words were melded together or where the space between two words was put in the wrong place... The impression I got was that the transcription was done by somebody listening to the book being read (and also possibly drinking heavily) rather than reading it themselves, because often times I found myself having to read some sentences out loud to figure out what was going on.
