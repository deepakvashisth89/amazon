This book documents the journey of a Lithuanian immigrant who arrives in the USA and finds work in a packing house in Chicago. He marries and has sick children. His wife soon falls ill from the very hard work in the packing house. The drudgery of his job leads him to assault his boss. The Lithuanian loses his job, goes to jail, leaves Chicago and becomes a migrant worker. Later he becomes unemployed and returns to Chicago looking for work. He becomes active in politics and, in time, becomes a Socialist. This book is noted for exposing the horrors of the meatpacking industry at the turn of the Twentieth Century.Read moreRead less
