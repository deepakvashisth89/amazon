Stripping is a collection of stories about women who somehow didn't fit into society for whatever reason, be it that they were too intelligent, raped, too neurotic, etc.  I loved it because of the attention to detail  given to each story, and the authentic voice that each character has.  My  favorite story was the 1st in the book about the girl who develops an  obsession with the death of Elvis.  Each of the short stories will affect  you in some way, and make this a book you won't forget.Read moreRead less
