I first read this book many years ago and have since read it several more times. It is one of my favorite books ever. It was thought-provoking and sparked a life-long interest in questioning conventional perceptions of religious history. It was the first Katherine Neville book I read, and I loved it so much I wrote to the author (pre-internet) which I rarely do. I later read her other books, and of those, I loved The Eight almost as much as The Magic Circle--the others not so much. This book will appeal to anyone smitten with symbolism, codes, obscure history, genealogy, and occultism. I ended up doing separate research on Hitler to find out if his connection to salt was true (and it was!). The book was way ahead of (and entirely different) from Dan Brown's Da Vinci Code, but I have to wonder if Brown was first inspired by The Magic Circle.
