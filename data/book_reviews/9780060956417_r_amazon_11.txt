This is an excellent Latin textbook. The only issues I have are that a few of the more minor case uses could be formally introduced later in the book, that Wheelock's daughters seem to have added unnecessary instances of wives/daughters/women in the practice sentences, and that there is no answer key for the "practice & review" or "sententiae antiquae" (ancient sentences). The last issue is likely necessary though, as it allows the teacher to assign sentences knowing students actually had to translate it themselves and there are still additional exercises in the back of the book for each chapter with answers included.The book takes an excellent approach to gradually introducing new material and subjects onto a solid foundation built up by previous chapters.The hardcover is also of extreme value. Most students will tear through their books within a year with the soft cover while mine has been used regularly (and sometimes extensively) for the last three years and only has minor wear.
