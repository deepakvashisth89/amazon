A rare book where the protagonist is not made so much to be a hero.  The books illustrates just how easy it is to end up on the other side of sanity in a world full of reasons to be crazy.The book starts strong with seeds planted in childhood that sometimes cannot be outgrown and the course one weaves down a troubled path, but the story lost me in its destination.  Straddling the line of fear and aggression the main character never seems to find the line between insanity and brilliance, falling always firmly on the lesser side of the two.Worth reading for the skill and intelligence always evident in O'Brien's work, but this is far from his best.Read moreRead less
