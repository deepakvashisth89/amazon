Sophisticated variant of the country house murder. Which of the inmates did it is ultimately the question. The genre is crossed with a tale about the Earldom being surprisingly inherited by a long-forgotten nephew from Hackney, via Clapham. All is sorted out by his unexpectedly thoughtful heir.  It is very nicely done, and ultimately well plotted. But starts rather slowly, and progress is clogged with country house detail (a bit reminiscent of Michael Innes, but not so deft).
