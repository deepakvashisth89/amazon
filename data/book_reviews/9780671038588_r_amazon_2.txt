This book was very good.  I liked Piper's character, and I enjoyed seeing her working things out.  Honestly, the only thing that somewhat detracted from my enjoyment of the book was that the author attempted to write Scanner's dialect (from Tennessee), and some of it was truly terribly written.  It was so jarring that, sometimes, it really pulled me out of the story.  I liked Scanner's character, but I found myself almost hating for him to say anything at all.
