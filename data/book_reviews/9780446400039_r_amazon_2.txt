Normally, when I pick up a "Mongo" book, I have already entered a state of quite willing disbelief. Generally, I've saved one as a special treat for myself, but Mr. Chesbro definitely needs to write faster, as I am  catching up with him!This time, however, the suspension didn't need to  travel quite so far. This time, the book gave me cold chills.Don't get  me wrong. Mr. Chesbro is ALWAYS fascinating, and I love the characters in  the Mongo series... but this particular book I just finished, and it was  close enough to reality [or maybe it IS reality] that it is actually  scarry! Not that I think his perceptions are wrong - it's just that I'm  terribly afraid that they are correct. I've always believed in the power of  words - I just had not carried that thought to such a conclusion, but I  should have.  Thank you, Mr. Chesbro - it was not only entertaining - it  was enlightening!Read moreRead less
