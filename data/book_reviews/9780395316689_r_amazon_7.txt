I purchased this as a companion book for Pillars of the Earth, which my book club was reading. It was a great aid! I was able to visualize the buiding of the medieval cathedral and was able to identify much more of the action taking place in the novel. This is not an adult book; I would classify it as YA but it's a great reference for anyone who is trying to learn more about medieval architecture.
