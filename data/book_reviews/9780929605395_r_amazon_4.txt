As someone who has read the entire famous forty and beyond I was skeptical of another John R. Neill book.Let's face it, though the man had amazing artistic talents he was not the best storyteller.His Wonder City and Scalawagon books were jumbled messes, partly due to the rather talentless editors at Reilly and Lee who decided to add in all sorts of garbage to ruin his characters. (Giving Jenny Jump a lobotomy for example.)His third book Lucky Bucky was a dull and pointless voyage but at least stayed on track for the most part.I am happy to say that Runaway is his best by a gigantic margin.Probably due to the editing skills of Eric Shanower.The plot is much tighter and better paced, there are very few irrelevant events, and those take nothing away from the story. They include a meeting with Jinjur (Who had been out of the spotlight too long!) and a Poetree!I am not sure how much Eric Shanower changed, or if the lack of Reilly and Lee's editors was what had really held Neill. Whatever the case, I am thankful.Much of Neill's humor is still intact, which is a good thing.Oh, and I haven't mentioned the illustrations! Eric Shanower is even better at Neill in this department. And that is no small feat! While Neill will always remain my favorite (unless Shanower tops himself later!) artist for Oz, Mr. Shanower's art is crisp and easy to make out, not blurry, splotchy or random like Neill's.His pictures of Jenny Jump especially made me grin! (I do believe that girl is Oz's version of a much more wholesome Lady Gaga!)Now, on to the reasons it didn't get four stars from me:The story while good compared to all of Neill's and many of Thompson's in my opinion, falls short of being a really great Oz story.The Patchworl Girl often falls out of character and there seem to be a few lose threads (no pun intended Scraps!).While it seems the perfect story for children, it is a bit dull in places for a read aloud, and I wouldn't recommend it for that unless you've burned through the rest of the FF40 and your kids want more.All in all though I thoroughly enjoyed it!Read moreRead less
