Chris Hume, the original author of the first set of spam haikus back in the '80s (and, thankfully, correctly credited on some of his original haikus reproduced on the first page of Cho's archive website... I also know dozens of people still alive who can attest to hearing Hume's live recitations of these same haikus in college in 1988) never saw a penny from this work. In the final years of his life he stopped sharing any of his voluminous creative work publicly at all, angry at others gaining fame and money off his humor and reproducing it all over the internet, often, as more time went by, without crediting him at all.It's too bad. Hume thought up something that became a worldwide fad and entertained thousands upon thousands of people, many of whom directly copied his original work, and someone else took money and credit for it like Hume didn't even exist.
