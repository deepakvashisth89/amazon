I read and loved this book over 20 years or more ago and recently recalled it doing some drier historical research and its even better to my more mature eyes.  The myriad characters are so distinctive and strong, completely unforgettable (The Duchess ....incomparable) and I’m glad I bought it to keep as more than a memory.  As an historian I’m not a fan of bodice ripping cheesy historical romances that are mostly bereft of actual history (which is always actually more interesting). This is quite historically accurate from the timeless seasonal happenings at Tamworth and it’s lands to the viciousness and licentiousness of Court , mingling true spice and vigor into an intriguing period of history where excess and extravagance began to spiral out of control .
