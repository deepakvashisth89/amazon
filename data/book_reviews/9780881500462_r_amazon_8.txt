Once you read the first in the series, get ready.... the rest only get a LOT better.  Perfect for the beach or whatever, you'll begin to fall in love with Asey and his family.  Asey gets far more sophisticated in the later books.  Also, you WILL be fooled as to who the murderer is! Taylor was a master at this.  Some people really prefer her Leonidas Witherall books, so look for those also.  (He looks like Shakespeare, and the comedy element is ramped up a bit).  These books inspired my friend Mary to name her cat "Fido".
