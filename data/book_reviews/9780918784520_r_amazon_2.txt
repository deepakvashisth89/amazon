I bought this book because I was curious about how closely it resembled the movie "Doc Hollywood". I knew the movie was based on this book and I'd read differing opinions on how related they were. It is somewhat different but the main points of it are the same.  If you had read the book before ever seeing the movie, the book would probably seem like a better story to you then. The author has based his story on personal experiences but changed some of the details to provide entertainment. I found the book entertaining and interesting but at the same time felt it was a somewhat different story. That said, keep in mind it isn't supposed to be exactly like the movie.Read moreRead less
