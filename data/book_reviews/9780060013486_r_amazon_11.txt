I had heard good reports about Mr. Leonard's writing; and "Elmore Leonard's 10 Rules of Writing" is accepted as gospel by authors all over the world. Therefore, I had certain expectations when I decided to read "Escape from Five Shadows".I was disappointed.The end is predictable. Some parts are downright boring. And it contains plot improbabilities. The characters are flat and the intended romantic suspense just isn't there. But I did read the entire book.Disillusioned, I did some research on the Internet. "Five Shadows" was Mr. Leonard's third novel (1956). He went on to write award-winning books, many of which were adapted for movies and television series.I intend to keep reading his work.
