Monk's Hood is the third book in the Chronicles of Brother Cadfael series. It can be read as a standalone book, but characters, and what makes them tick, are given in each book as the series goes along and they reappear throughout the books.In this tale, one of Brother Cadfael's oils, made for joint pain to rub externally, is a fast acting poison if consumed. Shortly after the Bonel family moves into the care of the Shrewsbury Monastery, with the promise of their lands to the Church, Master Bonel is killed with that oil. Edwin, Bonel's stepson and previous heir, looks like the most likely murderer, even though he is only fifteen.
