Ben Cheever (and how many has he heaved?) must be a funny, funny man.  I imagine him cocking his head like a bird listening for a worm as he encounters the silliness of this world.  Famous After Death seems to  have been written by a man fascinated by the mores of this country, day and  time, but not too reverently.
