I was looking for a good bestiary and found this. It has beasts from many different cultures. Some of my favorites include the Simurgh, the Phoenix, the Woodwose, and the Tengu. I was also happy to see brownies in here as well. I am currently reading The Secret Lives of Elves and Fairies and this book is a nice companion. They both remind me of the Spiderwick Chronicles because they're like field guides.The pictures are nice too, but for super detailed artwork of beasts I recommend Dracopedia's Bestiary. The Chimera in that book is amazing.I have a history with Role Playing Games, both for the computer and the classic games with dice. So if anyone wants more immersion into these worlds I highly recommend books like this.Read moreRead less
