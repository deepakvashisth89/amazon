I was very pleased when I received this book. The book's pages and binding are in excellent condition, as advertised. The dust jacket was included, too, which was fantastic. The only comment I can make that isn't keen is that it was shipped in a paper thin, well fitted book envelope. No protection whatsoever. The day it arrived the AZ monsoon's hit hard with a heavy rain with large hail. The delivery person left it on the front porch area (which is fine), but the covering was torn a bit and I felt genuinely grateful that I retrieved it before any water damage occurred. The bottom of the dust jacket was crunched a bit during shipment which could easily have been avoided had the book (a rare title at that) had been more securely packaged. I am happy with the book nonetheless; however, it seemed obvious to me that whomever prepared it had no concern for the value of the title or how it faired once it left their facility.Not sure I would buy from this vendor again, but the book was worth all the challenges experienced.Read moreRead less
