This play leaves the reader with more questions than it does answers, which, of course, is part of the point. Thought provoking and well-written, it will appeal to those who are angry, bitter, scornful, justice-seeking, confused, questioning, thankful and joyful. I have to say while I didn’t expect (or want) everything to be neatly tied in a bow at the end, I did not like the author’s choice of reveal, and was hoping it would be the stranger’s opposite who would choose to reveal himself at the end.
