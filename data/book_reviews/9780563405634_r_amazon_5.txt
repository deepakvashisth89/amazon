I was highly recommended this book as I loved the Eighth Doctor film and wanted to start reading some of the novelizations, but boy, do I wish I had skipped this one! The story had so much promise, but was fleshed out very unevenly, with some of the Doctors virtually ignored and others being given overly long and elaborate adventures one would have had to be unexpectedly familiar with the televised episode it was based on to really enjoy. I found the experience of reading this book an incredible let down as I fluctuated between confusion and total and utter tedium.And that isn't even mentioning the unforgivable spelling and grammatical errors in the novel and the terrible formatting, which, given the high price, is pretty pathetic, actually.Read moreRead less
