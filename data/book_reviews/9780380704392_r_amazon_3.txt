Also known as "The Long Habit of Living" in Australia and elsewhere.What can I say but classic Haldeman gold? Despite being written in 1989, Joe Haldeman has still managed to make the story relevant for today, not appearing dated (except for the price of beer!). Any science fiction fan will enjoy this book.
