A brilliantly written novel that does, for an entire supply chain, what The Goal did for production. It explains clearly the need for a holistic approach that embraces the I.T. function. The book has an excellent example of how addressing a constraint in one area (production) unavoidably leads to disaster in the distribution channel.This book gives I.T. professionals and anyone wondering about the value of their computer systems an incredible business education in a fast-paced 4 hour read.
