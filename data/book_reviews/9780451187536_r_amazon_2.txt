After almost 20 yrs of marriage,I've read my share of marriage manuals, but there are only a few I fondly remember. This is one of them, and unlike most readers who were offended by the course and often explicit language, I found the candid revelations to be, not only a physical turn on, but an intellectual, even spiritual one as well. By "turn on," I mean, like someone turned on a light inside me by allowing me to see that even the most unique sex done in the sanctity of marriage is a heady, beautiful and intimate thing that not only brings mutual pleasure, but enhances intimacy, communication, and closeness. This type of book is not for everyone, and if you easily offend, I'd say stick with books that are more medical or technical.
