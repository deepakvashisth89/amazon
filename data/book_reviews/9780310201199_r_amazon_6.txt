A very believable, compelling novel that is the first book in the "Fire of Heaven" Trilogy (I definitely recommend all 3 books!). The author does a wonderful job of integrating science and spirituality in this writing that revolves around testing the DNA of the "God gene" by injecting the DNA into cold blooded murderer, Michael Coleman.  When Michael Coleman is given a new identity and sent to live with a single mother and her young son, the plot quickly thickens keeping you on the edge of your chair for the duration.Read moreRead less
