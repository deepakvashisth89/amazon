Read the first sentences of “At the Villa Rose” and you’ll be hooked: -It was Mr Ricardo’s habit a soon as the second week of August came round to travel to Aix-les-Bains, in Savoy, where for five or six  weeks he lived pleasantly.  He pretended to take the waters in the morning, he went for a ride in his motor-car in the afternoon, he diner at  the Cercle in the evening, and spent an hour or two afterwards in the baccarat-rooms at the Villa des  Fleurs.The even tenor of Mr Ricardo’s living pleasantly is disrupted, however, when the Villa Rose is the scene of a murder, a jewel theft, and the disappearance of the elderly victim’s young companion.Investigations are in the hands of M. Hanaud, “the cleverest of the French detectives”.  Every clue and every theory is examined and tested.  Some might consider that “who done it” is revealed too soon in the novel’s structure, but “how it was done” is so ingenious that it demands a full treatment.Published in 1910, “At the Villa Rose” ranks as a detective fiction classic.Read moreRead less
