I have read a few of Madeline Baker's books and some fell short for me, but this by far was the best story of hers I have read. It may even be the best Native romance book I had read to this date. The story flowed along like a serene river in summertime. The main caricatures in this story had such a great chemistry. Kathy's character was such a breath of freash air compared to all the women you read of in other romance book, with all that wishy-washy stuff. Kathy was down to earth, wasn't of the whinny type, she was straight forward and said it like it was. Dalton's caricature was all man, not overbearing and bossy, not afraid of letting a woman make some of the decisions or asking her for advice. Shoot, I even want my own Dalton Crowkiller. There is good humor in the story and of course I had to keep my Kleenex box handy too. (I'm a big crybaby.)  And if you haven't figured it out by my review, I recommend this book to everyone. 🤠 Enjoy as much as I did.Read moreRead less
