***Note:  Do NOT READ THIS if you've already read "Battlestations" by this same author!  Battleship is the SEQUEL to Dreadnought and will make the entire novel anti-climactic. Read Dreadnought FIRST, then, if you liked it-- you'll probably like Battlestations.I will do this in greater detail when I'm at my computer, it's slower writing on an iPad. The book was pretty good, all things considered, and surprisingly so, for the minute I saw it was written in the first person I had strong reservations. That, and some slow moments with the main character being overly melodramatic, kept it from the higher star ratings. Suffice it to say that an Academy graduate, fresh from the academy, finds herself caught in a deadly game of "who is my enemy"? A loss is unacceptable as it would spell disaster for the Klingons, The Orion's, The Romulans... and yes, the Federation, indeed, the entire galaxy.Now I said I'd write a somewhat better review at my computer and here it is.  I don't particularly care for novels in the first person--perhaps the only exceptions I can think of are some of the classics like David Copperfield, Huckleberry Finn, and Great Expectations. That said, I fine Ms. Carey's effort here to be fairly decent. Yes, I noticed all the "Mary Sue" comments from others, but honestly the book is NOT that bad. I enjoyed much of the development of the story once you fully realized what was going on, and what was at stake.  It was a bit of a stretch to think that any one megalomaniac could infiltrate Star Fleet to the point of literally orchestrating a coup' de-galaxy, so it lost a few points on the credibility scale there; but again, in just being an enjoyable read--I can forgive it many of those peccadilloes.The principal character AND narrator not being of Star Trek canon--makes the most ardent ST fans a bit edgy-- we like 3rd person, and a LOT of focus on our favorite characters.  Now to finish this up.  The ending is a bit schmalzy, and several sections do stretch the imagination and yet, as critical as I can be (and I can be that!) I actually did enjoy the book--I think most of you will as well.  Now I'm NOT so sure about that being the case for Battleships.  I was not ready for ANOTHER 1st person narrative focused on Piper--and the opening chapters read like something from a naval manual--if you're not up on nautical terms like halyard, capstan, mains'l, foc'sle, and many many others--- you might want to steer clear of that one--not a particularly well done sequel.But this book I like. I can tolerate a well writing 1st person book once in a while... but making sequels to them ---uhhh I'll pass n that one.Read moreRead less
