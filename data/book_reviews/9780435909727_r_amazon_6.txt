I love this book!  I read it years ago before I had children and this book helped me to determine what type of mother I would be: balanced.  I reordered the book because I let a friend borrow it and never got it back, but I needed it to teach an IB course.  My seniors compared this novel to Chinua Achebe's Things Fall Apart.  Paired together these novels both lend themselves to a close look into the lives of two characters--one male; one female--who epitomize the extreme version of the traditional Ibo masculine character, Okonkwo, and the equally extreme version of the traditional Ibo mother, Nnu Ego.  Each novel is set during a different time frame during the Colonization of Nigeria and each character refuses to adapt their traditional ways to a changing society.
