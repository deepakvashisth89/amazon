Patricia Cornwell has recently become one of my favorite authors, with her cover to cover suspense and intrigue, and her in-depth knowledge of the world of forensic medicine.  That seemed to be secondary to the endless  colloquial dialogue of Bubba, Smoke, Weed and the charming Divinity.  Southern Cross went nowhere, and stayed there.  The ending did create some  excitement; not how it ended, but that it finally did end.  However, like  some of the addicts about whom she writes, I am looking forward to my next  Cornwell fix.
