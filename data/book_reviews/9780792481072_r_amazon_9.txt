I read Erica Jong's daughter's autobiography and she mentioned this book as a veiled account of her mother's alcoholism. I have cut back on my drinking and find that reading such memoirs helps me have more alcohol-free days. But I couldn't get into this book at all. It's puerile and repetitive and not very well written so has been consigned to the waste paper basket as I wouldn't want to inflict it on a charity shop. I admired Erica Jong when I was a young woman as she was fearless and counter-culture, and I was saddened to read that she now has dementia and lives in a care home, but I suspect her books were more suited to a bygone era, that we are more nuanced today.Read moreRead less
