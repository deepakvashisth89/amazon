I first read this book in 1995 following on a recommendation from a random stranger on the tube.  I have since bought 3 or 4 copies of this book, which have been lent out and it never comes back! It is an observant read and i like to re-visit it occasionally and see which elements Daniel Burrus got right and which tech are still waiting in the wings. Most of the tech we are using right now are in this book...some are tantalising close. This is is quite something given that the research that went into this book is almost 3 decades ago. This book personally over the years has changed meaning to myself as it has evolved from a forecast of possible trends to a historical analysis of technological progress.
