I read most of The Yellowthread Street books years ago. They are humorous, quirky police procedurals set in Hong Kong prior to the communist takeover. The characters are great and the team of inspectors tug on your feelings of bad days at work. I take great delight in reading these again and highly recommend the series for anyone loving offbeat, intriguing locale mysteries/police procedurals.
