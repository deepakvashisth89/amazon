Wolves -- A Portrait Of The Animal World by Leonard Lee Rue III is a wonderful photograph book filled with many vibrant color images of wolves living in various wilderness environments as well as information on wolves' survival actions throughout the four seasons.  There are beautiful photographs of wolves moving through the snow and more rugged images of wolves feeding on kills of whitetail deer and moose; there are even photographs of wolves having sex.
