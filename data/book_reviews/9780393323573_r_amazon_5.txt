In novels, novellas and shorter fiction, Andrea Barrett has created a web of science connected characters, further joined by blood and affinity, stretching around the world and through centuries in time.  For me, her writing is the best serious fiction I have read (neither Marvel nor Proust,) and I continue to hope for more to come.  Servants of the Map contains four novellas and shorter works which can provide an excellent introduction to any reader looking for an intimate view of characters living and sharing the science involved practical lives of their times and places.Read moreRead less
