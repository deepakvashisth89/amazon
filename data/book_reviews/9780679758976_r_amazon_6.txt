This novel had me on the edge of my seat through the entire book. I couldn’t put it down! The story is fascinating and creative - a professor who studies twins is stumped when she discovers a pair with identical DNA who were born to different parents in different states. I don’t want to put out any spoilers, so I’ll just say that the twists and turns in this book were enough to keep me reading throughout the night.* The book contains some violent scenes that may be disturbing to some readers.
