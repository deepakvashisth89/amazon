What a fun read! The book is structured like many of Agatha Christie's mysteries, with a semi-closed community offering a clear pool of suspects. Mrs Pargater is a fabulous Miss Marple type, but with the wisdom of a good marriage behind her and a slightly shady past which offers an intriguing selection of experts to advise and assist her. The cast of other characters comprises well known favourites: the retired military man, his inarticulate sidekick, the snobbish widow of a titled ne'er-do-well who channels the Queen's facial expressions (my favourite descriptions - I really laughed hard), the attentive, though few, staff, and a proprietress who is determined to maintain the gentility of the establishment in the face of each scandalous and embarrassing crime. The proprietress has the additional challenge of newly arrived Mrs Pargeter who won't conform to the long-fixed routine and has a habit of informality and wanting people to feel comfortable, entirely at odds with the proprietress's preferred strained propriety.
