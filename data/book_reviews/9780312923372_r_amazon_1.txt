A wonderful, informative and very readable guide to interesting corners near and far throughout Britain.  Usable and surprising even to a native Briton, this guide skillfully blends a solid dose of the long and complex history of each site with a brief review of the importance of that historical record.  Using this guide in advance will equip any tourist with as much, or more, knowledge of each site as any professional guide.
