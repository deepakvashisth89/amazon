I'm 75 years old and was lucky enough when I was a kid to have met a couple of old men  that claimed to have known Tom Horn after he had moved to Wyoming.  As near as I can recall, their stories were pretty much in line with what Horn wrote about himself with one major exception.  These men claimed that Horn had a drinking problem,.... something he failed to mention in the book.  The "old timers" that I knew  felt that Tom Horn was hung for a crime he did NOT commit.  One of these men told my father that he believed Horn was hung because the wealthy stock men that had hired him were afraid he might get drunk and implicate them in some of his other "less than legal activities".I especially enjoyed the first part of the book where Horn wrote about his life as a scout during the Apache Indian wars.
