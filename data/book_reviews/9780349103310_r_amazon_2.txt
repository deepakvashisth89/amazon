Being of the generation that inspired this book, I have always meant to read it and I finally did.First off, let me say that the book isn't very long and it won't take much of your time to read.  Because of that and the fact that this is a famous and often referenced book, it is worth the time to read.The characters are self-righteous and they pretend to be so deep and caring about the world but they are really just afraid of failure and success.  They are very whiney as I am and as are many of the same generation.  Reading this made me think that "the world isn't perfect and never will be, but there is no sense in whining about everything".As I said, it is worth the time to read.  It may be a bit pessimistic at times, but that is part of its charm and it is written pretty well.Read moreRead less
