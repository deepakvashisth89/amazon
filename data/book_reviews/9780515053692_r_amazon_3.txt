In truth, there are three villainous males in this plot. There will be no shortage of suspects in the murder of Sybil Foster. Sybil is very rich, rather silly, hypochondriacal, and very susceptible to male charm. Her death in a sort of health spa hotel looks like suicide at first — until Inspector Alleyn interests himself in the case.Gardens and gardening figure in the plot. You can almost smell the roses and lilies. And murder is just one example of the chicanery afoot. Happily there is also a young romance to lighten the atmosphere — as well as a middle-aged flirtation. A sensible middle-aged lady who writes plays is a central and very sympathetic character. Young lovers and veteran policemen alike turn to her for advice.I loved the quality of scenes in this book — from the almost universal fussing over flowers, to the rather obsessive appreciation of old houses, to the late nights in the graveyard.
