I reserve 5 stars for classic authors, or folks I think will become such.  I adored this author's The Crimson Petal and the White and was looking for more by him. This is an early book. I discovered along the way that he also wrote the Grantchester books which have been filmed and on Masterpiece Theatre.This novel features magical realism, another of my favorite things.  His later works do not use this, but I appreciated it here.  The character is fed a drink that allows him to live a long time.  During this long time, he works on the meaning of life in his way.  We are taken from the first uses of chocolate in South America to the Hershey factory.  There are some gorgeous sentences in this book.
