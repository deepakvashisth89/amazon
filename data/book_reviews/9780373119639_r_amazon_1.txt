Giulio and Lucy have a rather complicated relationship. Lucy becomes nanny to his niece and nephew. Giulio's stepmother is a terrible person and his sister is so weak and pathetic it is no wonder the children are mixed up. Lucy's ex boyfriend shows up with Angela,  Giulio's supposed girlfriend, and the big mix up begins. Giulio believes that Lucy is still in love with her ex and Lucy believes that Giulio is going to marry Angela. After many, many mix-ups they finally realize the truth and can be together.
