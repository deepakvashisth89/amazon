If you're looking for advice on achieving the perspective of a marketer, or if you're looking to learn some "how-to" about being a successful marketer, do NOT buy this book.... But if you're not a serious student of marketing, or your desire to learn about marketing is secondary to wanting a good dose of self-help (yes, self-help!) then buy it. I am not totally disappointed, but I did purchase this book because it was recommended by an internet marketing guru I am studying. There is good life advice in it, good business sense, and some entertaining stories (selected from the lives of men like Abraham Lincoln), BUT this is not a book about marketing, in my opinion.
