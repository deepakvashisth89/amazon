I have never read an Ed McBain book that I have not thoroughly enjoyed reading, including the Blackboard Jungle, and that is saying a lot about this author. With most other authors, I get tired of their characters after a while. They either don't grow, start to do the same thing over and over again or get irritating with their apparent perfection. You will never get that with McBain, especially with his 87th precinct books. This is a team of characters with distinct personalities and roles and its a delight to read each and every one of his many books.his time most of the action is handled by Cotton Hawes, working in concert with Steve Carella. I read the Kindle version with an introduction by McBain that explains how unnamed editor wanted him to create a more 'macho' detective and he tries a bit with Hawes in this version -- Hawes generally interviews several of the suspects in this mysterious murder and if they are pretty women, ends up bedding them. That in itself if a bit humorous as its described that he is falling 'in love' with each until after the bedding.This is a solid mystery with solid police work in action. A very good read and one that I stuck to until the very end. Another winner!Read moreRead less
