I am a huge LOTR and The Hobbit fan, I wanted a journal, something different, and this is it. It looks old and is just amazing. Every other page has an illustration on it and the pages are a great color. The only thing I have bad to say about this is, there is a poem on the back which is lovely but they attached a sticker about the company or something right on the poem, lucky I was able to get most of the sticker off with no problems but it did take a little bit of the poem off with it, but not bad to where I can't read through the poem.
