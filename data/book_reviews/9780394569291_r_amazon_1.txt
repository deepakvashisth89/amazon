The first book of Karleen Koen that I read was "Through a Glass Darkly."  I had purchased it at a used book store over 20 years ago. In the process of moving, I picked up the book just to have something to read before my books were packed for moving. I couldn't put the book down! I then researched online to find something out about the author and found  "Now Face to Face" was the sequel. I purchased it immediately and it came within 3-4 days.  I stared reading it when taking breaks from the tedious unpacking I have to do from this move we just made. The sequel finds Barbara, the main character, traveling from 18th century England to 18th century America---the Colonies. She is learning about bigotry, hard work, bigotry between the slaves at the time and her young black page boy, and the allure of, once again, men being captivated by her beauty.  She is smart, intriguing, manipulative, and she uses her beauty to get what she wants.  I have not finished "Now Face to Face" so I cannot say if it is as good as "Through a Glass Darkly."  It is well written and cleverly combines the two worlds of Barbara flawlessly. Kudos to Koen.Read moreRead less
