I spent (too) much time looking for a beautiful publishing of the entire unabridged anne of green gables series for myself and my children.  I came across this edition published by Read & Co. Children's and thought it looked perfect.  I ordered 3 as a sampling and am glad I only ordered the few.  While the cover is BEAUTIFUL, the publisher did not carry that quality to the book itself.  Without the dust cover, you have a plain gray book with no title, nothing, except a bar code on the back.  You literally cannot tell what book it is w/o opening it.  Many of the pages are printed crooked.  I was disappointed to say the least.  I've returned them and if it saves anyone else the trouble, I landed on two publishers that print a nice and reasonably priced copy of the original text, these publishers are Tundra Books (they do edit for misspelled words) and Aladdin.
