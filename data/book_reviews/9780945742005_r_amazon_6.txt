At least the best I own :)  A reasonably modern and complete introduction for English speakers.  It is also now far more affordable than many others (under $10).  It includes a short but complete course in Esperanto which explains the structure of the language.  The second and very useful part of the book is a reader in Esperanto.  The best way to master the grammar and structure of a language is by reading well-written prose.  If you buy one Esperanto textbook this is probably the one.
