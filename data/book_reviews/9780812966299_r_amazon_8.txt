I don't read novels real often and I really enjoyed this one. Written in first person from the point of view of a well traveled African guide and adventurer in the 1880s named Alan Quartermain.He meets two English gentlemen on a ship voyage who hire him to guide him the legendary King Solomon's mines. Quartermain agrees to do so , fully expecting to not survive the arduous journey, but ensuring that his son who is attending medical school will be provided for in the event of his death.Along the way to the mines the trio and their hired servants encounter deadly elephants, a blistering desert, unexpected deaths and an isolated tribe of Africans called Kukuanas.It turns out one of the porters helping the expedition is actually the rightful king of this tribe and the 3 white adventurers help him regain control of his tribe while killing the current King in a brutal hand to hand combat scenario.To show his gratefulness for regaining his kingship ,  Umbopa the porter/king sends them to the mines under the guide of an elderly witch woman Gagool who knows where the mines are.The team makes it to the mines full of millions of dollars of diamonds, but are entombed inside the mine by Gagool. They spend a harrowing couple of days finding an alternate path out of the mines , return to Umbopa and head back to South Africa. On the way home they encounter the brother of one of the men who hired Quartermain, he had been missing for several years and a secondary objective of the trip was to try and find him.This book is eminently readable, easily understood and held my attention. In fact it inspired a whole genre of books known as “Lost world literary genre “I heartily recommend this book , I think you will enjoy it.Read moreRead less
