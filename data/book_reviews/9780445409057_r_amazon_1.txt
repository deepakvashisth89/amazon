It's late 1941 in Los Angeles, and Private Investigator Toby Peters is fumbling his way through life. He's moved into a rooming house with an older, deaf landlady, and a friendly German dwarf in the next room. Toby used to be a policeman in Glendale, and then he was a security guard at one of the movie studios, before branching out on his own.Toby has an ex-wife who doesn't like him, an older brother who doesn't like him, and--well, it seems as if a whole lot of people don't like him. How he managed to get hired on a case for Howard Hughes is unfathomable, but he has. That's when a whole lot more people decide they don't like him and try to kill him. It seems as if Toby Peters, intrepid PI, has stumbled into an espionage caper being conducted by foreign agents--maybe they're Germans, but Japan has been rattling its Samurai swords, too, so it might be them.Along the way, Toby solves the case at the same time he proves to be a boob of incredible, and almost unbelievable stupidity. You'll have to read it to see what I mean, but you will.The author, Stuart M. Kaminsky, has an entertaining style of writing. He has approached his story and painted his protagonist in a humorous light, which took a little getting used to. This is no hard-boiled detective story a la Raymond Chandler of Ross Macdonald.
