This is another in Ellis Peter's delightful Cadfael series. An excellent read, and really draws you into the England of the 1130s. At his monestary on the Welsh border, Cadfael pursues his interests in his herb garden, but feels honor bound to find the person who has used one of his preparations to poison a curmudgeon.Great period piece, intelligently written and moves at a nice clip.
