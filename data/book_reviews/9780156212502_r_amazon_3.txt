What can I say, Virginia Woolf is Virginia Woolf no matter what she writes.  Her writing is luminous, her plots are so common as to be mystical, and her characters are so real they sit next to you as you read.  I have only read three stories as I am savoring them for those times when the non-fiction I normally read gets me down.
