This is a wonderfully written book by one of the most prominent female authors of the 18th century. Fanny Burney's debut novel, it was a best-selling book and quickly gained admiration. Written in 1778, it is a coming of age epistolary novel, and it presents an interesting view of what a young girl's entrance into the world at that time might have been like. As a writer, the social history information that it gives alone for the era is valued, but the story is compelling as well. For anyone looking for a peek into something of an 18th century world, I would recommend this.
