For men of steel who love ships of wood, this, the 8th book in a roughly 20 volume fictional saga of Britain's naval roll in the Napoleonic War is must read. While the series is fictional, it is based upon original sources so finely detailed that the line between truth and fiction is hard to spot.I love the series so much that I am reading it for a second time, and I am enjoying it even more that I did on the first read.Read moreRead less
