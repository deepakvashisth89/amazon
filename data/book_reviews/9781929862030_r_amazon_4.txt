I bought the cook book for recipe ideas, and to help learn how to use my George Foreman Grill. I was a bit disappointed at first, but then decided my expectations were a bit high. Really, how many ideas can there be for cooking meat on a grill. I did learn a few things about how to grill properly, and I have tried alot of the rubs and marinades. They are tasty!  However, rubs and marinades are the going trend at the supermarket lately. You can buy them all ready to go. I do cook more often at home now. I have figured out that with the right seasoning and the grill, I can get that chain restaurant chicken and fish.So overall, the book is sorta useless. I still flip through it for inspiration when writing out my grocery list. It has also given me a bit of a foundation to inspire new creations from myself.
