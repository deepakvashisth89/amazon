Poor Leo and his friend Tim get abducted by very dirty, messy aliens who return Leo but not his friend. He does not remember what happened and is told by the police to see a psychoanalyst. Dr Viridian unfortunately turns out to be from another group of aliens and scrambles poor Leo's memories even further.  Leo begins to realize there are two groups of aliens fighting for dominance of the planet and he and Tim are pawns in the game. Leo is unfortunately the only believable character in the book and it is written from his viewpoint which is slightly immature. The eco friendly ending is mildly predictable but the ending twist is excellent. I would recommend this book to younger readers who can tolerate the slightly bloody ending.Read moreRead less
