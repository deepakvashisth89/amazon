OUR MAN IN HAVANA is an enjoyable and at times very funny read.Greene is not someone you'd think of as a funny writer but he manages take a serious topic and turn it into light comedy for much of the story.The novel is deftly plotted and is delightfully light until the "serious" overwhelms the comedy.Fortunately that doesn't happen until you're about 90% through.Then you find the protagonist Wormold acting in a manner that just doesn't fit with the Wormold you've come to love and know.You also get a large dollop of Greenes politics which strike me as rather childish.At that point he was apparently into convergence theory(Soviet Union and USA all converging into big evil).No real difference.That was nonesense at the time and remains so in retrospect.Wormold's secretary delivers a speech presumably voicing Greenes world view which is a series of warmed over E.M. Forster style banalities .Too bad - the novel could do without Greenes pseudo sophisticated sermonizing.But that doesn't even come close to ruining this marvelous novel.I should also add that ridiculous as Greene can be , he is downright profound and insightful at points.His disscusion of the torturable is very interesting.I think he's on to something here but seems not to  understand or is unwilling to acknowledge that when the world is turned upside down the untorturable become the tortured.Read moreRead less
