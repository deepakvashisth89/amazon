Her books have vivid details that transport you to the crime and into the investigation. A lot of the details do have a basis in real life criminal justice practices from police to forensics and beyond. These details allow anyone especially those in the field to easily identify with the characters and the plot. Some of the books are better than others but no matter what they are all interesting. I'm so glad my sister sent Cornwell's first books to me and encouraged me to read them years ago.
