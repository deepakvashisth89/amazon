I have been collecting herb books and using herbs for more than 50 years and I love this little herb book! Beautiful pictures of herbs in pots for specific issues. I'm not sure if you could really keep these herbs in pots like this, some of the plants get huge, but if you are using them a lot it might work. Regardless, this book is beautiful and inspirational! These gorgeous pots would probably sell really well at farmer's markets, farm stands, or greenhouses. There is great info on many herbs, some that you don't often see, good information on propagation and medicine making. Great book for beginners if they  keep in mind that pots like this may not be easy to maintain. But the info in this book is very good, and the pictures are beautiful!Read moreRead less
