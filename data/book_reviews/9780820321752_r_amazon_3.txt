I chose this book because of the great reviews, and I too, am still suffering.  I almost stopped reading this book, however was able to complete.  Really couldn't put it down, waiting for Sue's recovery.  It is beautifully written with exquisite detail of her surroundings.  The writing is just beautifully done, on such a horrific life of a little girl.  I am so happy that Silverman has been able to recover and find a very valuable purpose in life.  Silverman was able to clearly articulate her feelings now, going through this horror, of her past.  Silverman is an extremely talented  writter.  I am sorry that she went through this terrible ordeal.  Both her parents are the epitome of what evil is in this world.  What I don't understand is how her mother could not do something to protect her child.  Still looking for the cure myself.  Suffer from PTSD, and depression, when my oldest daughter turned 11 years of age.Thank you Sue Silverman for giving me, and the world this autobigraphy.  You give me strength and hope.Serena D.
