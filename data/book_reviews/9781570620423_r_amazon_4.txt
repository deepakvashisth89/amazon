First off, this book is not a novel and the "describe the plot" of this book actually does not apply to this kind of book.  In the past I had read quite a few books (The mythic image, the power of myth and Myths to live by) by Joseph Campbell. I recently purchased this Shambhala pocket classic (The way of myth: Talking with Joseph Campbell by Fraser Boa) and found it to be an interesting and informative read.  This small 196 page pocket size volume is the perfect size book to carry.I have always loved books with a question and answer format.  In this volume Joseph Campbell gives answers to the following topics: Gods, Symbols, Goddesses, initiations, animals, underground, conflict and freedom. He explains the essential relationship between ancient, contemporary and modern myths to everyday life. His wit and insight into the subject of Myth is always interesting. This book is based upon a series of inspiring conversations just before his death in 1987.If you are a Joseph Campbell fan you should checkout this pocket classic.Rating: 4 Stars.  Joseph J.
