This book has a long preamble about his TV career before it gets into the subject matter. The author is uncompromising and this led to a split with his aviator friend and expedition companion. I would suspect there was a massive imbalance in motivation between these pilots. Certainly Keith did not want to put his life at undue risk or break international law. The author just wanted to get the RTW flight done. For anyone who has been involved in micro-lighting or hang gliding this is a good read. This essential level of interest will compensate for the poor grammar and somewhat disjointed account.
