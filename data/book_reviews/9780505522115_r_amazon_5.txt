Sasha Seymour was barefoot and broke, saving all her money up to purchase a sheep station with her business partner Ashton York. Still broken hearted over being left by a past beau she carefully avoids a love situation with Ashton, until his kiss sets her body on fire.Ashton York, owner of a pub in Australia, finds Sasha Seymour and offers to help her out, in more ways than one. In love with her from the moment he laid his eyes on her he vows to make her forget all about the man that left her behind and just think about his embrace, his kiss, and his touch. Until, his past catches up with him.While I did find the pairing of Sasha and Ashton to be very sweet and romantic I think that the story was drug out. I believe it is worth it if you have the patience to read through a long book. I enjoyed the romance, the humor, and Ashton's dog Lightening! Although I stick mostly to Native American and Cowboy stories this was a good read, except for the 400+ pages.Read moreRead less
