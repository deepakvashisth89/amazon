This book is about a boy named Huynh Quang Nhuong, who lived in Vietnam, in a small hamlet on the slope of a mountain. The hamlet had a herd of water buffaloes. One day, his father bought a calf, because the herd didn’t  have enough buffaloes. While the calf grew, the boy’s family taught him to fight. When he wasn’t learning, he played with the boy. When he grew strong enough, he fought with the herd’s leader and won. He became a powerful leader and fought against all dangers: hogs, tigers, crocodiles, etc. In the end he was killed by a shot in the chest.I liked the fights because they are so exciting. I also liked when they went exploring. On the other hand, I didn’t like the first chapters because of the way the author writes and also what he says is boring.
