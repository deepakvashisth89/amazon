I fell in love with William Marshalls' YELLOWTHREAD STREET mysteries and Marshall's exciting and somewhat hallucinatory writing years ago. I was delighted to finally see the series in ebook form, so I am rereading all of them. Marshall makes Hong Kong and particularly Hong Bay, the fictional rather seamy neighborhood wherein this police procedural is set, seem filled with interesting characters and mysteries. The mysteries are set before the British l handover of Hong Kong in 1999, so the cast is multicultural as are the mysteries.In GELIGNITE, the team is set on discovering who is mailing bombs, particularly since Detective Inspector Spencer was almost a victim. "Full of real police procedure, suspense and fine irony, but with whole extra dimensions of the surreal and the poignant, William Marshall's Yellowthread Street novels have no real compare.
