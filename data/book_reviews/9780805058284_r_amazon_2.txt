Sheila Kennedy's fight to prevent the Local Catholic Tribunal from annulling her valid marriage is admirable, brave and inspiring for other women like me, whose husbands (of many years) have decided to annul their marriages, opt out, and marry someone else in the Catholic Church.  Emboldened by her account, I am now fighting a similar battle. The lack of understanding of Catholic Faith that Ms. Kennedy sometimes reveals is not a deterrent to providing a wealth of information about this less-travelled road. With her keen, logical insights, Ms. Kennedy cuts through the inconsistencies and misunderstanding of the Boston Tribunal regarding the truth about the validity of her marriage. It is an account of an exceptionally admirable and inspiring fight of a woman, wife and mother, who true to her inner truth finds God's truth, and encouraged by John Paul's words 'be not afraid' finds her vindication in the Roman Catholic Church.Read moreRead less
