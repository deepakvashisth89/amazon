This is the first in a long series of funny mysteries, and it is my favorite of  all theCharlotte MacLeod's series (though I also like the Peter Shandy series and also the ones written under the name Alisa Craig).  I love the heroine, Sarah Kelling, one of the few sane members of an extended, well to do, and "eccentric" family.  As for the violence - most is off-page, but occasionally people are menaced/injured/killed - but not in a gratuitous way..  I especially like the respect she gives to characters, and the insight.  Her characters may do funny things, but she has empathy for them all, even those that are more 'comic relief' (in the later books).  With one exception (Curse of the Giant Hogweeds in the Shandy series),  I have enjoyed all her books and have re-read them.multiple times .  This isn't a puzzle mystery series, it's a relax and enjoy the flow and at the end it'll be explained (particularly in the later books in the series).  All in all, a good, easy read.  If you like Donna Andrew's Meg Lanslow series (Murder with Peacocks, et cetera), you will probably like these as well.  And the good news is that there are a dozen books in this series, so you'll have a lot of pleasure to look forward to.Read moreRead less
