I remember reading some of Andre Norton's work when I was a teenager from hand me down books received from my older brother. I was unable to afford the cost of her other books at the time and none were carried in our local library. Sargasso of Space was one I did NOT get to read so when I found I could download it as a free Kindle book from Amazon I was thrilled. Although the book was written well before the advent of space flight I found the story to still be almost timeless. Ms. Norton's characters were well fleshed out. I could visualize them in my mind easily. The situations in the story could happen in virtually any time and place. Although Ms. Norton's books have been categorized as being juvenile to young adult fiction I believe almost anyone with an interest in classic science fiction will find this to be a very entertaining read.Read moreRead less
