A wonderful story - it's particularly evocative for me since I've spent some time in Salvador in my youth and I'm familiar with many of the general places described here and used in the film of the same name.  To a degree I suspect that it helps to be comfortable with both the Brazilian culture and Latin American literature in general but the story is timeless and so beautifully drawn that I think almost anyone can get to grips with it.
