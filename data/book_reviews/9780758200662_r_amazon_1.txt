Jessy Niles, daughter of the original partner of Benteen Calder, grows up a tomboy, adept at ranch work as any of the men. From age ten, she loves Ty, but he's sent away to college by his mother, who'd lived in luxury in California. There Ty meets and falls in love with Tara, a wealthy society girl and marries her. Their conflicting interests create turmoil, her many long absences send him to Jessy, who had never forgot her first love.
