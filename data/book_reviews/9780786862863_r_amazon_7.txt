I think that this books approach to meditation is refreshing, but I wonder if it isn't too simplistic. The fact that you can become fixated to a meditation techinque doesn't seem to be reason to abbandon them. I understand that the techinque isn't the point, but say"just be present" seems a flippant as saying"Just be a doctor". I was a zen buddhist for years and had no idea what I was doing till I read a book on theravadin buddhism. Some people need a more systematic approach to meditation than is presented here.
