Journey To The Moon is a gem of a book. It combines sage words of Plato parelled with illustrations of Gustav Dore's labor intensive engravings. The two elements work beaurifully together. This book is a vestige of a gone era - a time of creativity and new hope that has vanished as certainly as has the Summer of Love.
