David Drake at one time owned the military sci-fi space.  Tank Lords is typical of his later work.  This volume is a collection of novellas and short stories, all dealing with his famous Hammer's Slammers, a space-faring mercenary troop.  If you think of the Italian contract armies of the Renaissance you have the model that Drake used.  The slammers contract out to rulers or factions, usually on frontier worlds with less than secure, competent, or decent governments, and kill whomever they get pointed at.  They, at the bottom, are a nasty bunch of killers whose only loyalty is to their unit.The battle stuff is very well done, well imagined, and told.  The political stuff is also well thought out and reasonable seeming.  But the character development does not come up to what modern readers expect at this level of fame and success.Worth reading and Drakes many fans (like me) will want to read it.Read moreRead less
