I loved this book! It is essentially the story of a doomed love affair set in the dying days of World War Two in a soon to be evacuated army hospital. It is a book filled with raw emotions and the pain of unrequited love - I am still thinking about the ending well over a month since I finished it. If you want a quick, easy light read, this is not for you. If you want to read a well-written love story with a difference, pick this book up.Read moreRead less
