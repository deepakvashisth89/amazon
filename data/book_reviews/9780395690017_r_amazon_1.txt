Validates a phrase I first heard in college: "Man is a wolf to man" without politics or diatribe. Shows how and where we have diverged form our closest ape relatives the chimps and bonobos and what we have retained. Covers concepts of reactive violence, collaborative proactive violence, male female relations, sex, infanticide, and self-domestication among others. Am seeing today's events with fresh eyes after reading this and both others by Wrangham. He's been a major contributor in the primary research by evolutionary biologists of the last half century, hanging some old myths out to dry, while asking new and important questions based on who and what we really are versus what we like to think we are!
