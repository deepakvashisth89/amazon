I would like to thank Jim Conway for writing this book with such an open-hearted and honest sharing of the effects of divorce and/or emotional divorce.  Every page gave me a valuable insight and I can't say that about too many books.  It helped me to greatly understand the dynamics of my childhood family and the dysfunctions and I felt such relief that it wasn't all MY fault as children think.  I was also impressed and thankful that Jim himself, being someone who helps others, took off his mask and took reponsibility for his own shortcomings, showing others that us counsellors and psychologist have our "stuff" too and it is in having the courage to understand our stuff, we become better equipped to truly help others.
