I decided to read this after I read a review of the movie in the LA Times.  Plus it was free through the Kindle Free classics series.  I enjoyed the book, but found that the writing was sometimes tough to get through.  James certainly does not prescribe to the Strunk and White adage of (and I paraphrase) "eliminate all unecessary words".
