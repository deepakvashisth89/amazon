A 19-year-old boy gets sent to prison in 1970's Britain for a relationship with a 15-year-old boy in this dramatization of the injustice of discriminatory and anti-gay laws in place at the time.The action of the novel revolves around the exposure of the boys' relationship and the resulting criminal and judicial procedures. I especially liked the portrayal of the older boy, for it's exactly how a young gay man at would have felt and acted in the glare of such exposure.This is a frightening tale of ostracism and isolation, a little bit undercut by the emphasis on the paternalistic rectitude of older adults as they work to make sure the older boy acts strictly within the confines of judicial proceedings.
