I very much enjoyed this book. It took the whole devil possession thing in weird directions I wasn't expecting. I was surprised how big the story was, too. Usually when you have one of those devil possession books, its pretty low key, involving just a family and a couple priests. This book featured bishops and popes and was really large in scope, which was pretty amusing. I did not really find it scary, but it was engaging and kept me interested until the finale.Read moreRead less
