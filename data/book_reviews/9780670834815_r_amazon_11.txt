Unholy Orders: Tragedy at Mount Cashel is a powerful and deeply researched account of one of the most troubling chapters in the history of the Catholic Church in Newfoundland. The author presents the events with sensitivity and attention to detail, exploring not only the tragedy itself but also the institutional failures that allowed it to occur.The book raises important questions about accountability, justice, and the protection of vulnerable individuals.While the subject matter is heavy and can be emotionally challenging, it is important this history is given a voice and not forgotten.Read moreRead less
