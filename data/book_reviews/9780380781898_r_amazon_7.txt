A beautiful warm story of love and hidden memories. Sometimes it does take a child to bring all that was lost into focus. When I became pregnant with my son, his father did not want him. Jim walked away and never did come back or even asked about his son. A friend told him that he had a son but that did not change his feelings. I raised my son with the help of my parents and sisters. My son was so loved. As a baby he was so sweet and warm. Couldn't imagined having so much love inside of me as I did for this son. As he grew, my son loved to cuddle and take naps on the floor with his grandpa. My son was so full of life. He had such a big heart, did not like to see others hurting. Loved to tell jokes to make you laugh or smile. As an adult.. my son went through high school, later worked with an ambulance company as an END. Later took some medical classes to become a paramedic. My son also became a father of 2 precious boys, who are so like their Daddy. 2.5 years ago my son died at the age of 37 from a heart attack. My son is in heaven now with his Lord and other loved ones. When my son was 3 years old, I did meet a wonderful loving man. John asked both Matt and I to marry him. To let him be not only my husband, but Matt's daddy too. Matthew grew up with the love of this beautiful, warm loving man. John showed Matt what loving a family was all about. We gave Matt a sister, who became her protector and supporter in just about everything. Matt is missed every day and is still loved just as much.
