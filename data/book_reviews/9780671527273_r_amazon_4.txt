Really enjoyed the final book in this trilogy.  Jessie is a strong slightly spoiled independent young woman who is determined to return to her birth place.  Her parents sell the family ranch to James a young man whom she had a crush on as a 12 yr old.  He isn't selling her the ranch since it is now his dream & he has worked hard.  Two hard headed people who haven't seen each other in more than 6 yrs have a lot of adjusting to do.  Since she can't get her home back she buys a rundown ranch next to James' place & starts a new life.  James is fascinated by this determined young woman & feels responsible for her success.  It is a rocky road to their love.  I had a hard time putting this book down.Read moreRead less
