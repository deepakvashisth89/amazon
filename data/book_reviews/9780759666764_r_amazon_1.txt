The Dairy of a Slave Girl Roby Jo is a fun filled educational story, that will have you laughing , crying with so many emotions. The story starts out with young  Ruby Jo who takes her little book and journals her experiences as a young slave girl on a plantation in a time where it was against the law for Slaves to be educated. Ruby Jo introduces us to the two girls she cared for in her massas house  her nemesis Letitia Belle , Linda Sue who was very kind to Ruby Jo . Being an African American I was very please with Author Karen McWilliams work, in society we would only hear of the dramatic and drastic dreadful things that has happened in History.
