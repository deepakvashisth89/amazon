As a big Steven king fan I've come to love Jack Ketchum. I've read many horrors in all forms. I can say I've never found one that I could not finish and thoroughly enjoy. This book however is very disturbing to say the least. many times I thought I can't read any more, but it's such a well written story that you can't not either.
