I must have checked out this book a hundred times in my elementary school library; this great book always took me places and lit a fire in me when it came to my love of space exploration.  I had the chance to purchase it at the Air & Space Museum in 5th grade but I didn’t do it.  Years later I could not find a copy in any book store; I found a dealer that had a good copy and now it is finally home and has an honored place on my book case.
