Although I disagree with the extreme negative rating and uninformed statements by the previous reviewer, in some regards the reviewer is correct in saying that this book is "eurocentric"; most books are.  However that type of viewpoint is something that will take centuries for the societal perspectives to move away from.  Most history, at least in the United States, is taught from this "current human apex" angle.  However, as with all histories, the truth is still there even if it is masked, distorted, or adjusted; one merely needs to take a look at the larger picture.  Having looked through this textbook, it does indeed provide a general primer for those curious about the origins and developments of humanity.
