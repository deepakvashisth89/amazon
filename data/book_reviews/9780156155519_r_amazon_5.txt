Growing up in Danzig in the 1930s, 1940s and 1950s must have been a highly challenging and fearful experience.  German Nobel Prize writer Gunter Grass comes back to this time of his life again and again in various books.  In “Cat and Mouse” he presents a fascinating coming of age tale for a group of teenage boys whose story would be fairly unexceptional were it not for their time in the Hitler Youth and WWII.
