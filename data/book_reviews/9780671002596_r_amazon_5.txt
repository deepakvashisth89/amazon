Although a fan of all the series, I have never been much into the Star Trek novels as the few I have read have been less than captivating. But Battle Lines simply blew me away. Reading this book is like watching a top-notch Voyager episode: the visualizations, the suspense, the plot development, the true-to-character dialogues--all excellently done. I have no doubt that if this novel had been used as the basis for a filmed episode it would have earned a placed among the fans' favorites.Too bad that Simon and Schuster does not pass on to consumers the substantial cost-savings of publishing these novels digitally, as the Kindle edition of Battle Lines sells for $8.99.
