Personally, I like a bit more trial narrative, but a lot of people don't.  So I feel those who don't like it will appreciate this book, and that aspect more than I did.I like that the author didn't go into a ton of detail on the town or family histories (I usually skip over those if they get too lengthy),I kept waiting for the mother to whip out the wire coat hangers in this book - it reminded THAT much of Mommy Dearest. Such pressure she put on this poor little girl to be perfect.The biggest issue I have with this book is the same issue I have with many true crime books -- it went to print too soon. The case is not yet fully resolved, so those who pay for it are left not knowing the final outcome. I don't like endings like that. I feel like I've wasted my money or been ripped off.
