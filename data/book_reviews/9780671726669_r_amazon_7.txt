One of the best True Crime book I have ever read.  I finished this book during a snowstorm and it was truly captivating. Thank God is was snowing because I couldn't put the book down.  While I would never justify murder and Betty got what she deserved I can see why she snapped.As far as I'm concerned all the adults (Dan, Linda and Betty) acted spoiled and selfish and didn't care who they were hurting.  Dan wanted the young trophy wife, Linda wanted the rich lawyer and Betty wanted the husband, and riches she thought were rightly hers.  However I blame Betty the least only because it was about power.  Dan had it and Betty didn't.  Many of us would walk away and lick our wounds but Betty was driven to madness.  I realize she made outrageous demands but why didn't Dan just ignore it.  Why put Linda's voice on the answering machine?  Why throw out the wedding china?  Why send her fat  ads?  Why not give her a fair divorce settlement.?  Dan and Linda would be alive today if they had practiced kindness instead of cruelty.Read moreRead less
