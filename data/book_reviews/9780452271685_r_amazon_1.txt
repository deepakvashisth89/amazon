Le Ly Hayslip's 'When Heaven and Earth Changed Places' should be required reading for anyone interested in understanding the Viet Nam-American War. The book details Hayslip's life from childhood to adulthood, a time during which she knew nothing but war. Marrying an American, she escapes war-torn Viet Nam in 1970, and, for the first time in her life, knows what it is like to live in a place of peace.The narrative oscillates between her time as a child and young woman in Viet Nam, and her first post-immigration visit to Viet Nam in 1986. Along the way Hayslip's account of her time spent loyal to the Viet Cong, the betrayals by both Viet Cong and Republican (South Vietnamese) fighters, and her encounters -- by turns deadly, betrayed and uplifting -- with Americans will keep you riveted. You will also learn a great deal about Vietnamese and Vietnamese Buddhist culture, life in a Vietnamese village, and the problems fledgling communist Vietnam faced following the devastation of the war.Through it all, Hayslip's story is presented in all its complexity: as complex as the war, history and life itself. There are no cheap emotions. As someone who spent time on -- and knew intimately -- both sides of the conflict, the perspective and presentation of her experience is unique. Hayslip knew the horror of the war, but also the humanity underneath the brutality. And she came to realize the war's insanity for all involved.For me, perhaps, the most unforgettable characters were Hayslip's parents, through whom Haylsip illuminates the various facets of Vietnamese culture: respect for ancestors, family roots in her village and the Earth, and familial duty.Coming full circle, in 1987 Hayslip founded the East Meets West Foundation, a philanthropic organization based in California whose focus is Viet Nam. The Foundation builds schools and health clinics, and funds operations for children affected by the environmental damage of the war (i.e. agent orange, etc). Two decades on, the East Meets West Foundation is still a thriving enterprise. Check out their website and donate whatever you can.
