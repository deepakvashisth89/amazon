This book starts off feeling quite clumsy, as if Dennis Wheatley came up with a great idea and then spends too long patching the holes in the plot. However, it soon gets into full swing and is a really enjoyable adventure story with, as usual, plenty of food for thought. I often find  a lot to think about after reading one of his books, especially as they fly in the face of modern conventions so much. Excellent read!Read moreRead less
