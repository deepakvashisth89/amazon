This book is Gaeton Fonzi's life work and his bequest to the America people."The Last Investigation" is Gaeton Fonzi's  last attempt to set right our history.  This book is a treasure trove of meticulously researched, historic information, which reveals the dark alliance that existed between the CIA, anti-Castro Cubans, the Mafia, their plots to assassinate Castro and the murder of JFK.Robert D.
