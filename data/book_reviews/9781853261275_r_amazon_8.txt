This review contains spoilers.An excellent book. My 9 year-old daughter is a prolific reader and this was the perfect step-up in complexity.Modern books for her age-group are fairly simplistic and read over a weekend but reading this requires more time and thought. Sentiments aren't always spelled out so she had to think about what was being said.Despite the price the quality of the book is fine. Very impressed. I will get more in the series.Now here's the spoiler.Be aware that this is the FULL story and includes the death of both Marian and Robin.Another thing that modern books shy away from and came as a bit of a shock to us both.Read moreRead less
