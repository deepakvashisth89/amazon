This novel is a British romantic comedy. Sir Buckstone Abbott is forced by financial constraints to open his hideous house to paying guests. One, Tubby Vanringham, is in love with Miss Whittaker, Sir Buckstone's secretary, but she is angry with him. Sir Buckstone's daughter, Jane Abbott, is engaged to Adrian Peake, but he is involved with Princess Dworniteck, who is rich and the stepmother of Joe, Tubby's brother. For his part, Joe is in love with Jane. Well, it all works out in the end in hilarious fashion. If you like British humor this novel will suit you.Read moreRead less
