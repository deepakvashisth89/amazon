Excellent book, if you liked Doom Brigade, you will love Draconian Measures. Main issue is female draconians are written a bit stereotypical... but at the same time I feel like Don Perrin has matured as writer and found his legs since Doom Brigade. It of course, isn't without it's share of faults, but it is in many ways, a very fun read. More detail heavy, more of what made Doom Brigade wonderful. If you love draconians and Dragonlance I'd definitely recommend.Read moreRead less
