When I first heard the rumors that the Bush administration was complicit in 9/11 I dismissed it out of hand. My main reason was that I thought it would have been way to risky; imagine what would have happened if they got caught!David Ray Griffin's "The New Pearl Harbor" poses so many, many unanswered questions and unanswerable "coincidents" that the reader comes away pretty convinced. At least this one did.  This issue is too important to just dismiss with "no, it couldn't be." And the facts Griffin presents speak for themselves.The one issue that it doesn't deal with adequately is: "Why is it that the mass media has completely failed to deal with this, and why is it that Congress and the politicians have also failed to deal with it?" To answer this, one must delve deeper into the nature of this period, something Griffin does not set out to do. Despite this, the facts are all there for anybody to see.
