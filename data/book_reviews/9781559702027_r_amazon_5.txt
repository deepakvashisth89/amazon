This is an excellent but very disturbing journal of what happened at Auschwitz, being written as it was happening. The story of the destruction of the Jews is a heinous one, and very sad and depressing. I do not recommend that anyone who has problems with depression read this account. Dr. Nyiszli is recruited from the Jewish crowds brought to the Auschwitz death camps to perform autopsies on various kinds of people, such as twins; I found myself feeling horrified at the descriptions of the deaths of these poor deceived Jewish people. I identified with Dr. Nyiszli, and found that he was, as was I, so very troubled about the way the Nazis lied to and gathered up the Jews and took them away to camps only there to meet their untimely deaths, I felt the same way. He seems so very moved by the sadness and the shock the Jews faced when they discovered the Nazis had lied to them from the beginning, only to be killed hours, minutes and sometimes seconds later. Then as the journal progresses, he becomes resigned to the continuing deaths...almost immune to the screaming and clamoring that occurs in the 'showers', where the gas is administered; but he must at this point take sleeping medications to get much needed sleep. I wonder how much stress this causes him, unable to concentrate much of the time because of the never-ending worry that he will be shot or gassed himself if he does not do his job well. This book is a must-read for anyone seeking to know the truth of the horror that happened at all the death camps; Auschwitz being where Dr. Nyiszli worked under a true madman, Dr. Mengele, a butcher now known for his demented ideas and false theories...let us pray that these horrific crimes are never ever implemented again in the history of civilization.Read moreRead less
