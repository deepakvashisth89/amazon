This is a very well written book, as interesting as any novel.  I particularly enjoyed learning about the conditions that brought my great, great, great grandfather and grandmother to go "west" to New York State from MA and CT.  The amazing changes that transpired after machines took over the physical labor of the era that was still colonial like in 1790 as well as the improved roads and canals.  I believe they were able to move from their original log cabin into a brick house as described in the book.  In addition, the discussion of conditions of dirt and barnyard waste, heavy drinking and the improvement of those conditions by 1840.
