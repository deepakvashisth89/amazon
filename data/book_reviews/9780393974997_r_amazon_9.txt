This book is a gold mine of information and insight into this beguiling poem. It opens with the poem and Eliot's notes, then there are fascinating extracts from The Golden Bough and From Ritual To Romance, two works that made a deep impression on Eliot. What follows is a wealth of insight from numerous important academics and writers, criticism and insight from many angles and many opinions. I have had this book for two months now and I find myself dipping into it on an almost daily basis. I read it from cover to cover when it arrived and it was an absolute joy. I've read several of the essays multiple times just to enjoy the wonderfully lucid and illuminating writing.I am not a student or an academic so my interest is purely recreational, but I do have a deep personal regard for The Waste Land, it really does fascinate me in many ways. I can't recommend this Norton edition highly enough.
