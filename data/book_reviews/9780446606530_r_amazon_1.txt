This was a decent romantic suspense novel. I liked the insight into how radio talk show hosts would approach topics and the types of strategies they would use to keep the interest of listeners. The two leads are adequate but the story was drawn down by both of them working their way through personal issues and relationship challenges at the same time. I feel the book would have been more enjoyable if there was less focus on the protagonists trying to resolve all the interpersonal issues before the end of the book rather than paying more attention to stopping the murderer. It will pass the time if you are looking for a thriller to read.Read moreRead less
