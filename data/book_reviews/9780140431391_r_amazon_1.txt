Historical fiction set in Tuscany for George Eliot? Yes, quite a departure from her pastoral English classics. The first quarter of "Romola" could be considered tedious as Eliot creates the backdrop and sets the table for future events. The payoff, though, for the reader, is a tale of such brilliance as can only be written by few novelists who've ever picked up the pen.
