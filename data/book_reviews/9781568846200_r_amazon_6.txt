I hope, almost everyone has heard of the "For Dummies" books. The series'  direct method and clear (sometimes playful and fun) language make learning  easy. It is also a well-known computer book series in Russia. "The Internet  For Dummies" is the chance to find out in a simple, clear and fun guide  which takes the horror out of meeting the Internet for the first time.As a computer writer, I am always delighted to discover new great book  that makes understanding the computer. Many so-called Internet books are  nothing more than printed collections of Web addresses. "The Internet For  Dummies" is a guide designed just for newbies. Authors do an exceptional  job of explaining a large number of Internet terms. You'll also find here a  lot of tips for optimizing your browser for speed, building your first Web  page, managing e-mail, subscribing to mailing lists, and go shopping  on-line.
