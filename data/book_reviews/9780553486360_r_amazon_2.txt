Lots of things happen at Christmastime, around the Pine Hollow stables. There's Secret Santas, donkey costumes, Scottish relatives, and glamorous news reporters.There is a damper though. The Marine Corps have been collecting gifts for less-fortunate children to receive on Christmas.
