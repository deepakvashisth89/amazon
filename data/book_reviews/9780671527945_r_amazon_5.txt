I just finished MARKING TIME and am eagerly looking forward to starting the next Cazalet Chronicle...This book shows a British family as it goes through the Blitz of WWII...there is an extended with four children and their spouses along with their children...three are sons and one is a daughter who seems to caretake of everyone.  One of the sons is married to a woman with cancer and is terminal..one son is a womanizer..and the other son is missing since Dunkurk and everyone believes to be dead except his young daughter.  There is much interplay among the family member as they all live in the country house together making room for whomever shows up to visit to needs a place to live.  Two of the sons work and live in London during the week...one of the granddaughters has quit cooking school to become an actress;  some of the grandsons are in public school and some in private school and two of the granddaughters are home tutored.  There is a young man who comes to live there who has had horrendous experience and had shock treatments to help him, he is the son of a sister-in-law and is loved and helped along with all the other characters.  At the end of the book Pearl Harbor has been attacked by the Japanese and everyone now knows it will be a long war.  These characters are all wonderful and well developed.
