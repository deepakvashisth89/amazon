This is the third of Rice’s John J. Malone mysteries and, in my opinion, the best one yet. (I’m  currently reading them in order.) Set in Chicago in the 1940s, it follows Malone and his friends as they try to figure out who committed a couple of related murders involving both the city’s lowlifes and its high society folk. With snappy writing and a well-constructed plot, it’s an entertaining and easy read. There are also some funny running gags and nice little character quirks that add to the fun.
