I was so surprised that I'd never heard of this Book plus one more written by Delia and her husband Mark Owens concerning the terrible problem of the poaching of animals in Africa. It's a marvel of description of the land, animals and efforts made to set up camps, airstrips with total dedication to Africa and preservation of the animals.  I cannot abandon this story and therefore read most all the night time.  The writing is excellent and down to earth.
