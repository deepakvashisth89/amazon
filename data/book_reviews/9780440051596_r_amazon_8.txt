While there have been other stories about a ventriloquist and his dummy wherein the dummy seems to have a life of his own, none have quite mined the depths of horror that this story does.  The dummy Fats will haunt you long after you have finished reading the book.  The characters are sympathetic, which is helpful in getting the reader to care deeply for them and feel hurt when they take a downturn.
