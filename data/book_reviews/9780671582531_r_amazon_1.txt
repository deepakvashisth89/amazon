A solid thriller, in my opinion."Solid" embodies 'Mercury Rising' in every way: the writing style, the story, the characters, the end. Which is to say: the book is satisfying and functional, but not exceptionally so (though I did find the premise of the code-breaking savant to be particularly engaging, and the author demonstrates a sober intelligence throughout the text). The book does have its high points, in several unexpected plot twists and some admirable prose; but, it certainly doesn't push the genre into new territory. In a nut, I found myself enjoying the story to its conclusion, though ready to move on to my next read.One additional strength that bears mention: some meaningful observations and commentaries interspersing the dialogue and events, as to inject some true, real-life wisdom into the book -- never a bad thing, I think, in fiction or otherwise. Consequently, there is something to be learned here.My sincere thanks goes out to this book's author and publisher.
