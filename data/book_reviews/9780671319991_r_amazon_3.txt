This is Jack Chalker's last published series, and Kaspar's box (the last book in this series) was his last published work.  I have read all of the author's published stories and recently re-read the Three King's trilogy.My review contains general very mild spoilers.This is a general review of the three kings series.  I am trying to keep this free of material spoilers.This is the second series based on a premise of an unusual planetary system (the first being the Warden Diamond 4-book series 20 years before).  Unlike the warden diamond books, the reader here eventually realizes that very little of this story is going to take place in the three kings system, yet I was filled with curiosity and questions about them which were mostly never satisfied/answered.  The first and third books in particular left the actual three kings system as almost an afterthought, even though the end of kaspar's box ends the trilogy!so a breakdown of how much of each book takes place in the three kings system and/or on respective planets -balchazzar's serpent - in system 40 pages left, on planet 20 pages left.Melchior's fire - in system 100 pages before end, and on melchior an impressive 50 pages before the endKaspar - arrive in system with 60 pages left, land on kaspar with *20* pages left.  some brief in-book interludes on Melchior prior.I enjoyed it, but overall, it just feels like something is missing, and I can only conclude (particularly with Balshazzar's Serpent, since it was published more or less at the same time as some other novels I don't have this complaint about) that this is how the author intended it.The ending of Kaspar's box felt very abrupt, and given how the flow of books from the author had almost stopped in the 2 years prior to it, I wonder if he already was developing health issues that were impacting his productivity - he normally had 1-2 books a year published if not more but kaspar's box had an almost 2 year gap from the prior one (in this case melchior's fire).  A prolonged productivity/health issue could certainly cause this.  If someone is familiar with the issue please add a comment.Read moreRead less
