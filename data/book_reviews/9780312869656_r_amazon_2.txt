When Catherine and Edgar return to Paris to set up their trading business (with Edgar taking a partnership with Solomon), they discover an unwelcome visitor: a corpse wrapped in a Templar's robe. They must try to decipher the identity and purpose of the body, while surrounded by multiple break-ins and thefts. This is a satisfying mystery, with many twists and turns.
