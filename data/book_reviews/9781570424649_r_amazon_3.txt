With all of his many other talents, Mr. Burton is quite the writer as well. I bought this book on vacation with me the only non-digital book I'd planned on reading. I was more than pleasantly surprised. An very well woven story told from the viewpoint of a few different characters who wind up inextricably linked even though they don't realize that they are. The storyline that brings it all together was captivating and made me with that there was a book 2 to this despite it's satisfying conclusion. I'll include no spoilers but I will say that I'm glad that I made room in my suitcase to bring it.Read moreRead less
