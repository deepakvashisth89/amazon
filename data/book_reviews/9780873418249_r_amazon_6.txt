This book contains some very useful and interesting information.  However, it falls far short of being comprehensive.  Although the title is "Miltary Small Arms..." the work contains entries for firearms  never adopted by any military, and omits significant military-issue  firearms (e.g., the Canadian and British Inglis High Power, the US M4 and  M4A1 Carbines, the Colt Model M General Officer's Pistols in .32 &  .380, etc.).The book is still a worthwhile purchase, though one is left  wondering why certain obscure firearms were included while too many  standard issue firearms were omitted.Read moreRead less
