We hear the phrase "it could happen to any of us" so  often but most of us don't believe it.  This book will tell you how easily it can happen.  The stories of  these women are heartwrenching, but yet, very  educational. I could not put this book down and since reading it, I have been looking for similar books to purchase.  We all need to educate ourselves more about people on the street.  We should be outraged that this can happen in a rich country as ours.
