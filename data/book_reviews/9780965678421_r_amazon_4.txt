I borrowed a copy from our local library to see if it was worth purchasing and was impressed with what I found.  It is not at all a cold, hard to read instruction manual...quite the opposite.  The author could well be sitting at your kitchen table, passing along the details over coffee.The information given is easily understood and worded to build confidence, not induce panic.  The real life stories and situations help raise awareness of your own surroundings and prepares you for scenarios that you may not have considered.The chapters can be read out of order without confusion and are easily broken down into smaller "bites" depending on your topic of interest.
