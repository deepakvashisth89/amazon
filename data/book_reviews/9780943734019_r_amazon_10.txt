I found this book very inspiring as Peace Pilgrim's lifestyle was unique.  She describes a period in her life of her itinerant lifestyle with only a tiny handful of belongings, and gave talks on spirituality wherever she went, and she would sleep outside wherever she found a spot under a tree, barn or if she was lucky, inside someone's house with a welcome meal.  She looks like an inspired soul from the time when there were prophets, if you look at images of her.  It is well worth reading if you are keen on unique lifestyles and spirituality combined!Read moreRead less
