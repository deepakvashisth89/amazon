This is a snapshot of the pre-AIDS era. It is a report from the intersection of journalism and anthropology. THY NEIGHBOR'S WIFE is just purient enough to draw the reader in and just factual enough to prevent it from being pornography. Gay Talese has sent back a travelogue from behind the closed doors of suburbia. If you think that not much has changed since HIV came on the scene, read this and marvel at how naive we all were. The sixties and seventies were swinging times in spite of repressive official attitudes. It is ironic that  attitudes have loosened remarkably while the awareness of the possible costs of modern sexuality has increased.
