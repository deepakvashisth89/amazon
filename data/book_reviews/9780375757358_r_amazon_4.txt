I have been reading Under Western Eyes for over 30 years, starting, stopping, forgetting and then starting again. I had looked forward to reading it because the revolutionary conspiratorial world it is set in was one about which I knew a little, however,  found Conrad's antagonism to Russians and his innate conservatism irksome. The central character Razumov is totally self-centred and venal, the female characters are cardboard cut-outs and the other revolutionaries are either hypocritical clowns or naive fools. Conrad is preaching rather than narrating.My advice would be to go straight to The Secret Agent which is a true masterpiece which is still relevant today.Read moreRead less
