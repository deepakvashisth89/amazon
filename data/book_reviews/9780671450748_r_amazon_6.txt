One of his first and one of my favorites. In fact I think it was the first Dick Francis book I read. In this book you see the despicable villains, the clever and ingenious hero, the magnificent horses and the perfect, lovely girl which characterize many Francis books. Most of all you see the deep passion for racing of the horses and riders. You feel like you are in the in the book. This is why Dick Francis is one of the best mystery writers of all time.
