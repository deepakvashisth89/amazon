Although slow in spots this was a good read. The plot was rich in characters and encapsulated the feel of Kalgoorlie, the rush for gold, and later the first world war and the people that those events shaped, both Australian and European. The story is of two families over two generations and is also a moving love story. Giovanni Gianni, fleeing his part in a family tragedy and Caterina Peruzzi, banished to the other side of the world to protect her family's honour. I felt Judy nailed the atmosphere and fervour of the times perfectly while delivering another gritty story at the same time.Read moreRead less
