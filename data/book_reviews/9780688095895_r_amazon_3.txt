This little book is an original story which unfortunately misses the boat on some important areas.  The heroine, Elfwyn, has a nice Anglo-Saxon name, which would be fine if she were not an Icelander.  The overall thrust of the tale is an explanation of the aurora borealis, which actually may not have been visible during the viking Age in Iceland.  It's a great little story, and I whole-heartedly endorse it as such, just not as a good reflection of early Viking iceland and its beliefs.Read moreRead less
