One day a stranger appears in the quiet village of Viscos. He places the villagers on the horns of a moral dilemma: if they kill somebody for no reason at all he will give them enough gold to last them for the rest of their lives. He gives this message to Chantal Prym, the waitress in the local hotel. She also gets another offer: she knows where the stranger has buries one bar of gold, enough to be able to leave Viscos, her long-time dream. The stranger is trying to find the good and bad in men and via this devilish dilemma he tries to find answers to his vital questions.While the days of the week, the period given to the villagers by the stranger, pass by, the discussions start to divide the homogeneous and quiet society of Viscos: ordinary people become heroes, leaders are found to be villains. At the end of the week Viscos will never be the same...This is part 3 of a trilogy in which the Brazilian author Paulo Coelho describes the lives of people who are all of a sudden confronted with vital questions.
