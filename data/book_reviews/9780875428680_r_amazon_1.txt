This book is precisely what the title describes: a practical, encyclopedic reference to western magical systems and correspondences. I would recommend this for the serious student, the giver of advice or the architect of High spells.If you are not already intimately familiar with number systems, read that section as a book for an eye-opening study.For those that construct their workings using systems of correspondence, this book provides an immediate reference for choices of color, direction, number, shapes and several other attributes. It distills a whole bookshelf of neopagan and OTO/Golden Dawn literature as well as numerous volumes on Near Eastern practice into a single volume for quick reference.For those studying the work of others, especially work constructed using unfamiliar systems, the book provides an indexed cross-referenced encyclopedia of relevant attributes and their meanings. Instead of trying to guess at the system and find the relevant books, one can refer to the Companion for immediate insight. In-depth study can be focused and directed by the systems identified in this book and the associated lists of reference and source volumes.For those using more intuitive or impulsive magic, this book can provide a stronger foundation of knowledge for your practice without trying to take the perspective of any one system or group.Another purpose is to compare several systems side by side to see how different groups have interpreted the meaning of various numbers and symbols.
