Faulker creates an amazing southern world that you become engulfed in. The family itself has many secrets and surprising turns throughout it. The stream-of-conscious writing was a little difficult for me to follow, as well as the very subtle euphemisms, but the great characters and original thought made the book worth it. The family is very disoriented and twisted and it opened my mind to many questions that relate to life now like: Is the truth always right? Is honesty the best policy even if it hurts someone? Should someone be passive and unhappy or push for what they want and risk everything? How much do words matter? Are good intentions as valued as actions that portray them? This book opened my mind to countless possibilities and made me consider how everyone has a story to tell, sometimes its just hard to let it out. Faulker does an amazing job showing people the questions that life poses and writing it general. I would recommend this to anyone that wants insight and a good read.Read moreRead less
