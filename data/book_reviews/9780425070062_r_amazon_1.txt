I have read this great novel several times since the late seventies.  Evil corporate types trying to hide a huge chemical company’s illegal dumping in California.  Jake Pickett, 72, has to fight off corporate thugs as they learn that the chemicals in the lethal soil around his longtime home have given him powers to “Slipt” things.
