"The Wooden Bowl" is a simple, practical introduction to Zen-style meditation, written in an elegantly straightforward style. The book is suitable for readers of any or no religious affiliation who want to learn to meditate, but I think it's even better for experienced meditators who tend, as Strand did, toward an excessively intense and willful approach to spiritual practice.
