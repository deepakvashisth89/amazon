I am heading to the Galapagos in November. I enjoyed Mr. Nichols earlier book 'A Voyage for Madmen" and thought this book would provide an interesting perspective on Darwin and the voyages of the Beagle. I was not disappointed. The story goes far beyond the typical Darwin and the finches history. Lots of insight into sea travel, scientific exploration, and politics in the Victorian age. Not necessarily a book for the casual reader but if you are into science, and want a deeper understanding about how Mr. Darwin came to visit the Galapagos, grab a copy!Read moreRead less
