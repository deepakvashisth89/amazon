Dottie(Dorethea) Hebden is a small town girl living outside of London with her fience Mike and a good job as head waitress in a hotel. She is happy, but just then things begen to change. She is not just a little nobody. She is the heir to the throne of Elluria and they need her.The former heir Randolph was just found out that because of his father's bigamist marriage to his mother he is no longer heir so he decides to go to London to see her and convince Dottie to come to Elluria with him. She and Mike do go and that is just the beginning.At first Dottie is nervous and does not do such a good job, but then she gets into the role of monarch and does better. But then the sparks that were there between her and Randolph come out and they find themselves in love.A very good book, with a surprise endingRead moreRead less
