I started the series this summer & I'm all caught up. I had first heard of the work from a GURPS game of the same name which I never bought but read the back page description. It seemed very interesting so I ordered a used copy of the book here on Amazon but shelved the book for almost a year before I got around to reading it!All I can say is that Gerrold writes with an addictive, straight forward prose & I spent the entire summer going through all four novels & eagerly await the remaining volumes. His characters are offbeat & there is plenty of humor to go around in this nightmare world that he conjured up. What's really strange is that I can always see these odd parallels with our own ecology here in the real world when compared with the chtorrans. For example, like looking at the Hip Hop culture as some strange sub-ecology & trying to figure out what its motive is & why it functions the way it does as if it were its own entity. Or looking at our rapidly over-populating world & trying to determine what its next stage will be in terms of evolutionary process that may just be a fluke in its direction or some sort of design already determined for some higher purpose as the chtorrans are hinted at.The books themselves deal with a lot of evolutionary theory & you can tell that it gets quite complex as you move through them which as I can see from reading Gerrold's website is understandably involving & making the remaining part of his opus still residing in the woodshed...being brushed clean with a fine toothed comb I would wager. "Intelligent Design" theorists should probably not read this book unless they want to cross-over to the Darwinian "dark side". Gerrold spends a great deal of time explaining the process of one ecology consuming another & I really looked forward to reading these parts as they had great shock value & the story keeps moving forward very reliably. You can also see some foreshadowing as such where it might not come back for a book or two distance from where you read it, but you can tell that something might happen with it in the future so it's always nice to make mental footnotes.I eagerly await the rest of the follow-up books & will likely read his other books in the meanwhile to get caught up on all things Gerrold!Read moreRead less
