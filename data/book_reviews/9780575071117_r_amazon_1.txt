"Bedlam Burning" is an absurd and phantasmagorical work that is a rich satire on the writing life, psychological gurus, and pretty much everything you can think of. It reminded of "The End of the Road" by John Barth in some ways. I loved how earnestly the narrator took to his work as a writer-in-residence at an insane asylum. I laughed harder the first read (2008) than the second (2021) as the narrator's predicament and the sinister game of wits going on between him and his friend and the psychological community struck as a little more depressing the second time around. But this a rare, really well done work of absurdity. If you enjoy that kind of thing, you will like this book.Read moreRead less
