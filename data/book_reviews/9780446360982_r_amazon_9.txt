I thought I would struggle with the way this book was written. I got past the first two chapters and then I could not put it down. This is the first time I have read books by Beverley Hughesdon and I cannot believe I had not come across her books before now. Song of Songs is a powerful story of growing up in an elite household where the children are desperate for love. They each make their own, and in their own different way. Helena the middle daughter keeps them together with her undying love. Her life is a battle in itself and takes its own twists and turns. The  use of local dialect is sufficient to determine the prejudices with the gentry and is not overdone in this book and I thank the author for this.
