The imagery in this book is excellent.  One does not have to travel to all the museums that contain the artwork in order to appreciate the work of the Renaissance.  There is detailed bone structures and profiles as well as musculature in the appendix of the book.  The book is not as detailed in anatomical references as the work of Raynes, but it is definitely a better one than "Human Anatomy ...  Elements of Form" book that I returned.
