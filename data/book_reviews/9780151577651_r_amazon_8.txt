Started somewhat promising but still did not provide a straightforward account of what little we know of her from the gospels.  For instance, and this is seen in various accounts, the little we know appears to suggest she had "demons" and Jesus exorcised her of them.  A good approach to me would be to discuss what this was understood to mean at the time.  There is no one clear answer here -- it can be psychological, some sort of religious symbolism of evil forces or whatever.  But, it is a basic part of Jesus' ministry and Mary's own story in particular, both in myth and history.  If this was some other issue -- let's say a study of African tribal life -- would such a basic thing be ignored or not examined as merely "religious" and not open to historical study?Bart Ehrman in his book on Peter, Paul and Mary Magdalen did more here (though not this) to get a sense of who she or someone like her might be in a section of about sixty five pages.  Anyway, then quickly it started to go -- in an academic style that was overly dry to me -- into what people thought of her years later.  It did not do enough to set forth the basics and then was not an engaging read though discussions of film portrayals or the like was of mild interest.  Disappointing read.Read moreRead less
