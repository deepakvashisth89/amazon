The is an okay piece of military sci fi. It is a little dated. I haven't read any of the other books in the series. If I had, it might of changed my opinion.The book takes place in the future. Almost instantaneous interstellar travel exists and humans have colonized a number of worlds. There were no alien encounters in this book. Two factions control Earth, and compete with one another for control of the various colonized planets.Initially the protagonist is a colonel for one of the Earth factions and a brilliant military tactician. He arrives on a colonized world in the midst of a war with his own secret agenda.I found the colonel's very imaginative ground force tactics worth reading the book for. Oddly, serious air power doesn't figure into any of the battles because for some reason given that didn't make sense, air power has largely been banned on the colonized planets.The characters aren't fleshed out and towards the end I began liking the protagonist less and less. The author doesn't let us in on what the protagonist is thinking or planning, which makes it more of a surprise but also made it less satisfying in my opinion.Read moreRead less
