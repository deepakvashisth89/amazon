This book was a breath of fresh air compared to I, Jedi and Children of the Jedi. This book almost felt like a proper sequel to the Thrawn trilogy and the Jedi Academy trilogy. From the opening chapter this felt like a proper Star Wars adventure with the characters we know and love interacting in a way we know they would. The plot while somewhat familiar had an interesting twist with a Hutt trying to develop a Death Star like weapon and the remnants of the Empire seem to have a new leader.We also see Luke and his new love interest off on their own adventure and I admit I wasn’t the biggest fan of the relationship. The issues the relationship had were kind of silly and ridiculous and I hope it’s the end but we will see. I also felt there were too many catchphrases reused in the book that we had seen time and again in the movie and I think that’s a bit corny.Overall, this was pretty fun and provided a boost in my read of the Legends/EU series.Read moreRead less
