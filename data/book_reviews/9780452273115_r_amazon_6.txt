This is a very well-written story about growing up gay in the 1960s and '70s, when prejudice against gays was much more prevalent than now. It is as much as story about a homophobic, and emotionally limited, astronaut father as it is about his gay son. I can't say I enjoyed being reminded of the rejection gay youth experienced at that time -- and some still do -- but it was a good story that kept me turning pages right till the end.Read moreRead less
