An evocative, lyrical sojourn that spans decades through the perspective of a materially and metaphysically divided self living and working in New York City. I hesitate to call this fantasy because, while the fantastic appears, so, too, does the uncanny. The unreal nature of this book is untethered from traditional, expected ideas about how the literature of the fantastic should operate, and this free-form exploration lends texture to the prose that I rarely see anymore in contemporary literature.
