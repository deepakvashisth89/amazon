Another laugh-at-loud book of hilarious unintentional escapades of Miss Seeton who is nothing like her first impression of a sedate,  compliant retired art teacher.  This book is a  real page-turner with an interesting cast of characters, a lot of action, and several surprises thrown in. Descriptions of scenery and clothing well done. Easy to read. Great book to read while traveling.. I recommend the book and the others in the series..
