This has to be one of the most enlightening books I've read. Although the setting leading to his views is a communist dominated country (and of this he paints a clear picture), the contents of his essays are timeless and apply to every society and every group of humans, small or large. The prose is crystal clear and easy reading, a rarity, it seems to me, in today's literary products. Deep humility and compassion shine throughout. Highly recommended.Read moreRead less
