While plenty of comic books pick their titles from random moments, this particular collection takes the silly sentence "pass the loot" from a series of strips based on an interesting situation. Two of the Fox children discover an envelope with a bunch of $20s in it that's marked with nothing other than a name. While debating things back and forth, they end up realizing after a bit of searching that the envelope was intended for a charity drive, and they make the right decision to hand it over.Being one of the earlier Foxtrot books, the collection also features a bunch of dated references that are frequently hilarious. Paige Fox debates with her siblings over what to get at a VHS rental store. Various characters use typewriters that look like they were out of the John F. Kennedy administration. In one dramatic moment, a very pre-Columbine Jason Fox writes Valentine's Day cards to his classmates featuring detailed drawings of war scenes, complete with realistic-looking hearts on the corpses (something that he states to his principal is a concession to holiday tradition). It's interesting to think what would happen to a kid who pulled the same trick nowadays.Foxtrot is a great comic, and this book is highly recommend. Page after page features a bunch of hilarious moments. Bits of actual character development as well, something rare in such an episodic format, make this particularly special.Read moreRead less
