Great opening book in the Antares trilogy. A good old fashioned SF story with space battles and aliens mixed in with a liberal dose of science. It reminds me of Arthur C Clarke - a good story well told. The only problem is you'll want the next two books to find out how the story ends - Dawn does have an ending of its own, but also sets up for the sequels very well.I already had a hard copy of Dawn, bought just after it was published, but had not been able to get copies of the sequels until they were published for Kindle.
