This book has to rank as one of the best true crime stories ever written. A case that has so many characters who were involved, and so many twists and turns, is not easy to relate in a way that is easy to follow. The author managed to do so. The story ufolds in chronological order, keeping the reader in suspense all along the way. Character development was masterful, leaving me feeling I knew each person personally. I liked the way the story was written as if it was a screenplay.Aside from the horrible nature of the crime, the parts that disturbed me most were all the character flaws of the "good guys" that inhibited and negatively influenced the investigation.Read moreRead less
