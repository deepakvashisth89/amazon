I am so tired of formula books or book club issues. I want to read at leisure, to feel what I read with no pressure to have to report on it. H D Lawrence  allows me to turn the words he pens into emotions that flow over one, deep ones that newer books avoid for some reason. But then, Lawrence once abhorred by overly moralizing folk made them feel more than they could handle.
