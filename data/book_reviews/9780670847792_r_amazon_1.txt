This is an original portrayal of a man that tries to make a difference in the lives of Mexican refugees.  He encounters all types of refugees, the drug addicts, glue-sniffers, prostitutes, children, but the one thing they  have in common is their desperation.  Moved by his own personal tortures,  he risks everything to help them.  In the process, he ends up in a Mexican  prison, which is ironic as he  is trying to help the very people who  imprison him.  Coming from a wealthy family, he uses his resources to  continue his efforts to assist the refugees, only to provoke the wrath of  the town's people who want to halt the never ending influx of illegals.
