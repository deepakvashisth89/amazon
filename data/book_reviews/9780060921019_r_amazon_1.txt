I wasn't sure whether to give this book 4 or 5 stars. On the one hand, certain parts of this book changed the way I look at the world for ever, while other parts were not as dramatic.The book is a collection of excerpts from various James Hillman writings. Some the of the excerpts, as I said, were amazing. Others I did not really enjoy as much. Overall though, I really enjoyed the book, and so ultimately chose to give it a 5 stars.Having only read 2 James Hillman books so far (this along with The Souls Code), I don't know how this would compare to other books. This book has definitely made me want to read more James Hillman.Read moreRead less
