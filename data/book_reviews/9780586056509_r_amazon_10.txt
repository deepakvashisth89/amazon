There are 5 or 6 books in this series. I first started to read them when they were originally published but, there being no indication, back then, that more were to follow, I lost track of them and never got beyond The Miko. It's well told and van Lustbader  clearly knows well the Japanese culture and history, including a great deal about its many different forms of martial arts. It takes some patience, dodging back and forth, as he did, between past and present, but that only means that by the time the reader finishes the last page, he or she has not only read a good story, they have been thoroughly informed all through it, and educated, too, re Japan in general.Read moreRead less
