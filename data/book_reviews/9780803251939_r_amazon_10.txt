This wonderful, unspoiled woman, proved herself to be a humble, hard-working, courageous, optimistic and worthy go-getter, who, was left on her own after her husband was killed in a rail accident. Faced with an uncertain future, she rolled up her sleeves and made sound decisions for her and her little two year old daughter. She sought a position as a housekeeper, on the brave new frontier, and set out by train to her new position in Wyoming, to keep house and home for a Scots widower.  But who knew where these decisions  would take her ?Thank goodness her employer was kind and caring. Thank goodness she survived the many situations she found herself in! This book is made up of her letters back to her former employer/friend in Colorado. In them, she shares all her new adventures and experiences on the land with the animals and the new people she meets in her new position in her new land, and it is interesting!
