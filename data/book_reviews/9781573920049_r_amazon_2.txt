This book is not what I expected: I felt too much space was devoted to book history familiar to us all. Also, lifelong readers generally say much more about the act and joy of reading. See, for instance, "The Pleasures of Reading," by Joseph Epstein in Salwak's A Passion for Books. That said, Mr. Burns offers good social commentary and scores a direct hit on media, especially TV. He pulls out some great quotes from other writers and offers  enjoyable anecdotes. For me, the chapter entitled "A Place of One's Own" proved very satisfactory. The Joy of Books gets a 4-star rating because it did not meet expectations, but the book is worth taking a chance on.Read moreRead less
