I simply could not warm to the heroine, which spoiled and otherwise impeccable story and superb depiction of a hero.  I realize the author gave Elizabeth a difficult and traumatic teenage loss to cope with, but maybe she kept her in that mode too long for me to believe in her eventual change of heart.  Elizabeth to me was always part snob, part wimp, and her later love, or rather, lusty enthrallment with Michael wasn't authentic to me at all, it simply seemed to happen for the story's sake, for the sake of a happy ever after, than due to a believeable character development.
