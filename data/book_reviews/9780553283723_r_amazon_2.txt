My copies of all the books in this trilogy are dog-eared and falling apart I have read them so many times.  This is one of my comfort series - books I read when I want a guaranteed good read, and some cheering-up.We meet Lily Ransom on her home planet, a mining consortium of tight knit and insular families where she has never quiet fit in.  She practices martial arts, the only thing she enjoys about her life.  When her mentor is kidnapped by unknown off-world assailants, she decides to follow and rescue him, leaving her family and home behind.  A strong female character, and interesting secondary characters made this a favorite, and I tracked down all the other books this author had written at the time.I was so pleased, years after I read this series to run across some of the characters in the Jaran series.  "Hm, how did you get here?"  I thought.  That was when I discovered Alis Rasmussen was writing under a new name, and creating new worlds for me to enjoy.  If you are a first time reader, enjoy.
