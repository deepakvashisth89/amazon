True atrocities that seem beyond belief. Meanings of treason and of survival are explored. Do horrendous crimes of others justify similarretaliation? These philosophical ideas are left up to the reader. Yet there can be no doubt of the humane answers. Devious and conniving Nazisare a horrible part of the plot. Is there ever redemption for crimes of this magnitude anywhere? This is offset somewhat by scenic German andItalian towns along with Israeli locations as well as sarcastic humor plus unusual love.
