I love and dearly miss Chicago, but was not a dog owner when I grew up in the city.  I long to visit for reasons other than business, but cannot justify taking a vacation to a town where my dog might not have fun.  This book gives me something to do and look forward to with my devoted companion Ben.  I've seen all the museums, the shopping and the water, now I want to experience Chicago from Ben's point of view.  I know he'll love the people and the street food!
