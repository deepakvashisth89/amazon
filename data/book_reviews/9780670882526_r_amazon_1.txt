I just came back to order a second copy of this book to send to my Korean mother-in-law as a gift. Our son loves this book and I wanted to send some of his drawings with this so that his far-away grandparents can start to write back. The story chronicles a little boy in America writing to his halmoni (grandmother) in Korea through pictures, and her writing back in Korean with other objects that help him guess what she means. I appreciate the Korean perspective as there aren't a lot of books in English geared towards Korean or Korean-American toddlers or preschool/kindergartners. The illustrations and words are both beautifully created, and I know we and my mother-in-law will treasure this book and the memories we'll create by following a similar example!Read moreRead less
