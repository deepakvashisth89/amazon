Billion Dollar Brain is a bit of a schizophrenic novel. The first half is first rate cold war espionage fiction. It features subterfuge, suspense, exotic locales, and wonderful characters like Colonel Stok and Dawlish.The second half takes an oddly sharp change of direction. It introduces a criminal mastermind character who doesn't fit at all. It's like half-way through the book, Deighton decided he wanted this to be a James Bond novel. Add to that a sub plot that includes eggs that seems to have been tacked on after the rest of the book was written.It just doesn't add up to a novel that compares well to Funeral in Berlin.Read moreRead less
