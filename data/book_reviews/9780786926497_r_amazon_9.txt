Even though the book is not required for a nice gaming experience it's definitly worth it. It lists a ton of new items, including weapons, armor, magic items loads of them!) and interesting new adveturing gear. There are chapters about vehicles, hirelings and mounts and companions, a table with a lot of new poisons and alchemical items, edible items (perfect for taverns or exotic merchants!) and other goods of trade.While I'm not bad at thinking up random items or magical stuff myself the book is a wonderful source of inspiration. If your players love to equip their characters they will love the book. If you don't have time or run out of ideas to stock a complete bazaar in a desert city this book will help for sure.
