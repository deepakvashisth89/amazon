This is one of CS Lewis's scholarly works. It deals with Medieval allegorical poetry of courtly love. That's quite a topic. It is less accessible than some of his other scholarly work such as Preface to Paradise Lost or The Discarded Image. I would recommend reading those first unless you are already familiar with the Middle Ages or the poetry that this book discusses - Romance of the Rose, Troilus and Crisseyde, and Faerie Queene among others. I really enjoyed it though, and learned a lot.The edition was a paperback Canto Classic published 2013 with a white rose on the cover. It was a sturdy paperback edition with good binding. The paper was a durable thick waxy feeling paper (pardon me for not having better terminology.)
