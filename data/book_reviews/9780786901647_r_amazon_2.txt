This is arguably one of the greatest epic stories spanning lifetimes... Salvatore has a way of pulling you into the characters emotions and mindsets almost as if you're looking into their world from within them... He weaves a spellbinding tale.. with a mesmerizing effect... Especially the fierce encounters between Drizzt and Entreri... you can hear their blades doing their eternal dance, see the sheer effortless concentration and focus on both blade masters... Salvatore effortlessly draws you into their world.. my only complaint about this series is that it does not include the Cleric Quintet...
