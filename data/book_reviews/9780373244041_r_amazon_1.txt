ALMOST A BRIDE is a standalone read with a happy ending.  I downloaded this story in 2012 and I am just now reading it as I have discovered this author in 2022.  I can tell this is one of her earlier works but I found the story very entertaining and I definitely recommend it.  When I read a story if the author does not give me a good description I form one in my head for whatever reasons.  Matty was like that.  I don't think I did her justice from my first impressions.  She was a force not to be trifled with.  She is a likeable character don't get me wrong.  Loved Dave Currick.  The story is set in Wyoming.Dave is a rancher, quite wealthy and successful.  He is a sweet, patient man.  He runs the ranch inherited from his dad.  He is an only child and his parents are retired and traveling the world.  Matty is his first and only love.  They have been together since childhood.  Being older of the two Matty sort of followed along behind Dave letting him lead the way.  Their break up as she heads to college was devastating to her.  She didn't understand it then and doesn't understand it today.  She has always loved him and it broke her heart when he cut her loose.She took advantage of her time.  She graduated college and she has worked various jobs until her uncle passed and left her beloved ranch to her.  However he left it in very bad shape.  Now she is back home facing losing the ranch if she doesn't come up with a large sum of money within the next two years.  Dave is there to help but she doesn't want his help.  But she does need him for a very important reason and he is more than willing to go along with her crazy scheme.This is a fun and entertaining read.  You have to love Dave.  He is a part-time lawyer and part-time rancher.  He is down home and easy going.  After not seeing Matty for over 6 years he is willing to do whatever she wants.  He loves her not as a boy, but as a man.  He would be hard to resist!  It is a good love story as these two stumble along the way as they find their happy ever after.  Good story and I liked and enjoyed it.I was not paid for this review.  I am not a writer nor do I know one.  I am not kin to this author.  I am a reader and this is my opinion, nothing more, nothing less.  4/20/2022Read moreRead less
