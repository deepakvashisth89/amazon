I remember Strange Matter and Strange Forces.  One of my absolute favorite young adult book series!  I loved how the books would depict strange and fascinating monsters.  The storylines were unique and ran the spectrum of the science-fiction and fantasy-from out of control A.I.s to sorcerers attempting to return to this world, and even undead gunslingers!I remember the first time I ever found a copy of the book series...I was at what was is now the MCCS on the Military Base in Mira Mesa, San Diego, California!  What first caught my eye was that the series was using CGI pictures on the cover.  The first book I read I think was "A Place to Hide". As I first read the books, I fell in love.  I would like to think that the series sparked my interest in cryptozoology, the study of unknown animals.  I read all the books, both Strange Matter and Strange Forces.  I was a member of the book club, had two shirts with what could be considered the mascot of both series, Rilo Buru, one of the main characters of the Strange Forces series, on front.  I still have all my books (I ended up losing my copies of books 2 and four of Strange Forces).  I have met the authors of the series, and got two books signed by them (my first copy of"Strange Forces 2", which I lost and "Doorway to Doom".Even now, I miss those books, and it was made worse that one more book was suppose to come out, "Rilo Buru's Summer Vacation", and it never did.
