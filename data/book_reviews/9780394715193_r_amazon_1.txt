As a longtime reader (and a writer), I cannot name a "favorite" book but this is one of the bunch. Fascinating book-length essay or series of essays, filled with stories, on subjects including gift culture vs. transaction culture, what gifts are all about, and Ezra Pound's concept of vegetable money. I've had many copies of The Gift. The tradition is that you read it and then give it someone. My first was given to me by a stranger on an airplane. Eventually I passed it along, bought another, kept that copy a while for random re-reading, then gave it to a friend I thought would appreciate it, bought another ... and here we are today.
