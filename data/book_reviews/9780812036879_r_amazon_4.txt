This book has a lot of good information that can be used for standard chickens as well as bantams. It follows the Barron's tradition of covering the subject quite well while still being concise. Everything a chickenkeeper needs to know is in here.The photos are beautiful and make identifying the differant breeds easy. I just wish there were more of them. There are also some very nice illustrations that you don't have to look at sideways to figure out what they are.One drawback to this book is that it is written for a European audience, so not all the breed names will be found in American catalogs. But the breed descriptions and photos make it easier to figure out which is which. All in all, this is a good basic book that is well worth the money.Read moreRead less
