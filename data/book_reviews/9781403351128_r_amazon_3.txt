I admit, this review is a bit biased.  I have known Ridley online for several months now and know the man behind the writing a bit more than the average reader would.  But, other than that little fact, there is no difference between me or you when it comes to "Examination".  The poetry in this book is some of the better work I have had the pleasure of reading.  Being related to the late Earnest Hemingway, writing is in my blood; and it thrills me to no end to know that, even though a different writing style, Ridley, to me, is in the same league.
