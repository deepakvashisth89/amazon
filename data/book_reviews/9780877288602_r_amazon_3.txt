I liked this book. I read it years ago and recently re-read it. As someone who has had some success with OBEs, I can attest to the book’s merits. However, both its strengths and weaknesses are contained in the details. On the plus side, all the descriptive details give it authenticity; she knows what she’s talking about. Ms. Eby has the eye of a scientist and is scrupulous in portraying the various states of consciousness. I applaud her insightful explanation of the different states as continuums. It’s well illustrated in her accounts. Conversely, she also presents so much needless detail (who cares what color the house was, or if she headed northeast rather than southwest this time) that I hungered for more information on her interactions. All in all, it does make a fine and necessary contribution to the body of work on OBEs, especially for the scientifically minded reader.Read moreRead less
