A friend from Canada gave me 2 Goddard's for Xmas along with a clipping of an article by Stephen King listing Goddard's novels as his top "discovery" of 2008.  I don't read Stephen King because the weird and horrible do not appeal to me.  But that's not Goddard's style so I dove in.  I've read 11 Goddard's since.  The best so far is his first "Past Caring", but Beyond Recall is easily in second place.  The others are all good stuff, but tend to run together in my mind.  These 2 are truly outstanding.
