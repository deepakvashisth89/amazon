Penguin classics is always a go-to edition when it comes to... well, classics. Their editions always have lengthy notes and introductions that, more often than not, provide a lot of historical context to the book in question. In addition to that, the artwork of the cover of the book is often pleasing and in accordance to the book in question. In this particular case, the book was very inexpensive for what it is and it got to me in no time.
