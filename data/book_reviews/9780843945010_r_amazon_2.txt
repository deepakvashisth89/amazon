This dark noir tale is all about imagery and feeling and perspective.  Don't focus too much on the plot.  In my reading, the plot was secondary to the atmosphere Pronzini creates as a man at the end of his rope, failed marriage, failed career, sets out to experience mardi gras and encounters voodoo, murder, sex, and above all, abject terror.
