UPDATE 3: I finished this huge read. I must say that I enjoyed the book overall but still feel that a lot of information could have been placed in an appendix. If you’re looking for a big book on Castro to supplement your degree in Latin American studies, go for this.However, if (like me) you’re just looking to learn more about this most interesting historical figure, you may want to look elsewhere. Or, at the very least, prepare yourself for a barrage of information you will never remember (long lists of names, primarily).UPDATE 2: I'm 75% of the way into this book now. There are parts of the book which are absolutely thrilling and parts which read like a boring textbook and make me want to gouge my eyes out — long lists of names and positions within the Castro regime, for example. These are the parts of the book I feel I must slog through in order to get at the fascinating information I'm really interested in.
