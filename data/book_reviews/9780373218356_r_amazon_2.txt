Merline Lovelace and Candace Irvin are quite obviously the gems in this book. Lovelace's story is solid as always, but Irvin's Unconditional Surrender truly shines. As the longest story in this anthology, it still got cheated. The plot was fresh and intriguing, complex with backstory and rich with danger and excitement.
