First published in 1934, this mystery is timeless.  The plot, the setting, the characters are so well described that I could imagine myself there.A house party in a country home, just for the weekend, has brought an assortment of people into each other's company.  For entertainment, A Murder Game was devised.  One guest would secretly be assigned to be the murderer, and that guest would choose another person with the words "You are the corpse."It's easy to see how things could go wrong, and they do.
