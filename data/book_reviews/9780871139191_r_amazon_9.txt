PJ is a silver-toungued, alcohol-fueled old-school conservative, from a day when they weren't completely insane (his own words).  Somene who can hold his own debating ideas without being an ideologue.  I disagree with many of his viewpoints, but enjoy reading his point of view.  His has lived as a correspondent through so many dangerous zones, he has earned his stripes and the respect of his fellow correspondents.He is definitely not a pundit, but he crafts stories of people and provides perspective of their lives through his interactions  on-site.  His bitter and bleak surroundings have steeped him full of disarming humour even moreso than his distilled beverages.Light, and a fast read, with strong insights, this book covers many war zones he has persnally witnessed - from Kosovo to Egypt, Israel and Iraq, spanning decades of experiences.Yes his irreverence has a purpose - to shake us from our entrenched point of view, inject some humour, to help us question our preconceived notions... One of the most un-politically correct authors I have read, I enjoyed this book greatly.Read moreRead less
