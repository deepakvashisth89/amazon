Good value with lots of content for the money, 31 short stories plus one novel; however, that contributes to my issue with the book itself, bulky at 5"x8" by 2" inches thick and it's heavy for a paperback. Pages are matte cream paper and font is dark enough and a good size for these old eyes, but I find the page margins are too slim. Not a relaxing read when one has to continually pry the book wide open enough to read text near the binding, probably not as much of an issue for those that read in a chair, but I'm a bedtime/horizontal reader. Original copyrights are from 1916 to 1940 and the stories are grouped by The Drones Club, Mr Mulliner, Stanley Featherstone Ukridge, Lord Emsworth, The Golf Stories, Jeeves, and the novel Quick Service.
