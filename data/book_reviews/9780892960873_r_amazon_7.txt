Reading this book, helped me to see Jesus in his humanity, more than any other book I’ve ever read. He was as much human as he was God, but sometimes that gets lost as we focus, mostly on his being the Son of God. He had the same emotions as we do. He was joyful, sorrowful, angry, loving, compassionate, forgiving, frustrated, annoyed.
