I had this book in paperback when I was sixteen. Over the years it had started to come apart and was being thrown away. I have been searching for this book for many years and as soon as I saw it I ordered it. It is a lovely book, abook of horror, a book of mistakes all leading up to murder in Dallas. Oh how I wish it had not happened. But you can't live your life on dreams. Read this biographical, historican, magical, and true account of lives, both lost and ruined by one lone man, allegedly LHO. I am really heartbroken that he could not tell us his story of assassination of one of the greatest Presidents that America has ever had. I also think Robert would have been a better President, but someone shot him as well. Too many coincidences, too many bad calls, too many people keeping their mouth shut. Manchester did a fabulous writing this book to the American people.
