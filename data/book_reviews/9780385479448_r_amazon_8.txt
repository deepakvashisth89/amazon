This is the third John Nance book I’ve read in the last few months. I’d never heard of him before and picked up one for free. After reading it, I was hooked and found these last two and have devoured them. This one has a lot of relevance to our world today. The topic is a deadly virus on a plane with 250 souls aboard that, if let loose, could ravage the world. Sound kinda like Covid?
