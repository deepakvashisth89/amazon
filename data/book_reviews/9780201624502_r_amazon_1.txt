The author articulates the intensely personal elements of both her side of the "coming out" as parent, along with the son's side, with such vision.  I'd never thought about "coming out" as a family experience but the author certainly opened my eyes, and mind, on that.  It's a wonderful memoir, full of insights to be pondered and cherished.
