This book is as honest as an autobiographer can be about the darkest chapter in his life. I don't think the vast majority in this country actually realize how Jim Bakker was railroaded by those within our government and those within the Christian Community. Most people are completely blinded by what and why they think Bakker was sent to prison and it's a disgrace how easily we were to condemn him! Thankfully, a law professor and his students volunteered - without pay - to investigate what really went on; and because of their determination to find the truth and prove a man's innocence BEFORE finding him guilty, Jim Bakker was exonerated of the false charges he was imprisoned for. What this gentle and kind man suffered and lost is disgraceful and unimaginable; and yet, he came out a better man and a stronger Christian - learning his beliefs before were wrong and being strong enough to say so!
