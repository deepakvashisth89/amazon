I decided to read this novel after finishing Sidney Poitier’s autobiography. I had not seen the movie nor did I know much about the book. As a teacher I was engrossed in the story. Having been born in the decade the story takes place also made it interesting.I was astonished at characterisations of women teachers, as well as corporal punishment being accepted. I tried to wrap my mind around a time where teachers and kids cursed, fought physically…. It also reminded me some things have not changed. Kids being passed along through the school system even though they cannot read or write. Teachers burnt out.Anyway, loved the book and now I need to find the movie.Read moreRead less
