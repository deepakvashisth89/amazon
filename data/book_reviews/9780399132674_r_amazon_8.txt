Before I got this book I read all the reviews.  Short version, I loved it and am again sorry that there will be no more books by Heinlein.Hard to beat Stranger in a Strange Land (especially the unabridged version)and yet this is by far at least one of his best!Yes lots of sex, not detailed, not gratuitous, yes, it did pull together so many stories (being his last book)and all the story lines seemed to fit so perfectly.Like one reviewer said it would be good to re-read or read Time Enough for Love, The Number of the Beast and The Cat Who Who Walks Through Walls before reading this (not necessary but helpful.)  It was great to read them again and pull it all together to Sail Beyond the Sunset!Read moreRead less
