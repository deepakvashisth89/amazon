I thought I would enjoy this book. Just another thriller. Politics. High government intrigue. An assassin. The usual. But this book wasn't that. It had hard rhetorical questions. Ones it will not answer. It cannot answer them anymore than you, the reader, can. The questions are deep and disturbing. The author has unusual insight. I described this book to a family member as a rapid page-turning thriller with dark philosophical overtones. If I taught political science at a university, it would be required reading. It calls into question what you think you know. It is a painful book if the questions burrow in. It will haunt me for a long time. But...if you appreciate deep thinking and you aren't offended by things being questioned, l cannot recommend this book enough.Read moreRead less
