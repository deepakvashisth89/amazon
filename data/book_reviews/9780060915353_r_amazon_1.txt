This book takes you from Generals down to the Privates in the fields, sweeping across the skies to the fighting in the island swamps.  I never have read a history book, that came across like a fiction book before.  I have always been fascinated as to what my dad experienced in WWII.  This goes from the beginning of the war, to the end.  Every country and name is given, but in a very lively, and engrossing way. Sometimes, I couldn't put it down, I just had to read, even though I know how it ended up.  European pride, American heroes, British determination, German, Italian and Japanese ambition, and the horror of the Holocaust are all fleshed out and faced with excitement, and sad reality.  Reading this, I felt the  adventure, the honor,  the pain, and the horror of the war, that filled the lives of our greatest generation.  Do I recommend this book?  Yes.Read moreRead less
