This is the second book in the Carramer Crown series.  Prince Michel de Marigny is known as the playboy Prince.  However, Michel's reputation is not exactly accurate, but adopting a child that is believed to be his love child, does not help.  Michel is bitter to the idea of love after seeing what happened to his brother.  Michel believes that a marriage of convience would be the best thing.  Luckily, at the age of thirteen Michel was betrothed to Eleanor, and she is legally obligated to marry him.  When Michel sends for his future bride, unknown to him, her twin sister comes instead.  Caroline has always had a thing for Michel, but more important she wants to protect her sister, who is in love with another man.  Caroline hates to lie and soon things get out of hand.
