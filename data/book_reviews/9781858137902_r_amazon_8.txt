I have several hardback books by Paper Mill Press that look like this “leather bound” edition by Paper Mill Press, so I ordered this to match. This is not a hardback book. It’s like if someone took a paperback book and wrapped it in thin leather. While it does have the styling of the hardback books, it’s a little smaller and doesn’t have the gilded edges. The paper in this version is also thinner, letting some of the text shadow from the back of the page be visible. The real hardbacks have nice, thick paper that isn’t see thru. I can still read it, but I was hoping for a nicer book like the hardbacks.As for the stories, while I knew they were not normal fairy tales, reading them feels like when my 8 year old nephew tells me a story that doesn’t quite make sense, plots randomly change or get resolved with no explanation, and then it ends very abruptly.
