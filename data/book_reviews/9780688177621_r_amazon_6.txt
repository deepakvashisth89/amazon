Another of a.m homes' novels about the dark undercurrents of suburban living in the USA.  After a fire disrupts the rhythms of an affluent American suburb, lives begin to smear into one another. Secrets are revealed,messes are made as members of different families are pushed together in the wake of the fire.  After a while, it seems the lives rising from the aftermath of the fire will be shinier, phoenix like.  Then a shadow falls and the strange interactions prove deadly.  I thoroughly enjoyed the dispassionate writing and was prepared to believe some of the contrivanced interactions between characters but the ending disappointed.  It was almost an American cliche, the shooting of a child by another child. Then it ended when really, the story had just begun. It felt as if the author had simply run out of steam.Read moreRead less
