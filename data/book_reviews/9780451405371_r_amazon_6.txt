I got this book years ago knowing I would have to read it one day.  Last week, that day came. The year before the murders, I was Tyler's babysitter and I babysat Les Howletts kids many times - along with a few other families in the trailer park.  I knew bits and pieces of this story, but I had no idea the number of mess ups, ridiculous mistakes and lack of concern these families had to go through!  The book is as infuriating as it was sad.  As it was a personal story for me, I probably had a greater interest than most.  Seeing the names of people you knew in print is a strange and bizarre read.  The story has a lot of detail, but you need the detail to understand the whole picture!  I felt, enlightened to finally know the details, but it was written in a "just the facts ma'am" way that I didn't cry like I thought I would.  Until the verdict was read....then I did cry.
