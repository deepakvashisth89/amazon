I don't normally enjoy crime syndicate stories, but I'm glad I bought and read this one. Miss Seeton is her usual strong yet almost clueless self. The familiar supporting characters are here again, and a few charming new ones join the scene. The plot is a galloping blend of humor, tension, and occasional violence and murder -- I was never sure what could happen next!
