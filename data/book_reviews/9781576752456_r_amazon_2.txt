Time is what we all need to spend with family, friends, and of course, oruselves. Take Back Your Time is a blueprint for what we need to do to regain our time. Having time is essential for our health and well-being. With high rates of obesity, diabetes, cancer, etc., it's amazing how our country robs us from having enough time to do projects, take vacations, or just do nothing.
