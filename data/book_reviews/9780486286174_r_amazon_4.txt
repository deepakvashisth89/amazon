This is one of the books in the "Old Mother West Wind" series. It's all about the adventures of various woodland animals, and each book is focused on a specific woodland friend.While these books will not tickle the fancy of an older reader, the young-at-heart love them. Rereading this  book brought back many memories of reading this series when I was much  younger. I recommend this book to any nostalgic adults or those with young  children.Read moreRead less
