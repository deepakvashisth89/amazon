I have not heard of this author or book before. I often read African fiction but surprised that this book and author have never popped up in the recommendations on my Kindle. I first heard of the author when the shortlist for this year's Booker Prize was released. The plot  explores the overlap of the effects of colonialism and the tenets of a traditional patriarchal society, and how rules and cultural practices are conveniently adapted to propogate the status of the men.
