I did not realize until last night that these books were a series.  Needless to say I read them out of order, but I am happy to realize that it didn't matter too much.I have enjoyed reading this author and will finish all six books in the series.  One of the things I like about this author is that she does not have sex in her books, but does a great job relating the emotions and how they feel, which I like.  There are a few tensed moments and thoughts but nothing to explicit which is great.  The only reason I gave it 4 starts in stead of 5, is I wish she would have spent a little bit of time giving us a little bit more detail on what actually happens to them instead of giving you the thought of what will be. (Sorry if that came out funny, but I'm trying not to spoil the end) The three books I have read so far are fun and enjoyable and an easy read.
