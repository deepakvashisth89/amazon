This is a great book for someone looking to discover what all the hullabaloo is about fractals.  It provides a wonderful insight into the mind of one of the great mathematical geniuses of our time, Benoit Mandelbrot.  I think some people will find his writing style a bit too stodgy, almost arithmetical, but I find it interesting.  I especially appreciate how marks off when he's going to be tangential with special brackets.  Mandelbrot doesn't delve into too many rigorous mathematical proofs of the various topics he discusses.  He broaches each subject in such a way that should be accessible to people from a wide array of sciences and disciplines.  I don't recommend this book if you're trying to figure out how to create simple fractal programs.  But I enthusiastically recommend it if you want to learn more about fractals, discover a new way to think about and understand nature, or are simply looking for a good bit of erudition.Read moreRead less
