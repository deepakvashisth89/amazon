Inness is known for his landscapes which were amazing and remarkable. Unfortunately this book does not do justice to George Inness. The text is fine and clearly the author is knowledgeable and passionate about the artist but my gripe is with the photo production. The color plates are rather small in a small size book (they are not even full page reproductions for the most part!). The colors are on the dark side and not very clear or focused.
