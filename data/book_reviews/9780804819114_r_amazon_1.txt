Like many others learning Japanese, I struggled with the system of writing and rewriting.  This is of course necessary, but it did not give me any glue with which to commit the characters to my long term memory.Having bought both the books from the Tokyo Uni group, I was able to enjoy the process of learning.  The mnemonic system is just what you need.  It allows you to create links to the components of the complex Kanji.  That is, to identify what makes up the meaning of the characters.  With a mental picture cemented, it is quite simple to make the associations when you are presented with new kanji.I found this invaluable and a pleasant reading experience.
