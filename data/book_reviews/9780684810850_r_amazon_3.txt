It is always difficult to do justice to a book and author when you start in the middle of an established series.  (On the other hand, shouldn't each book in a series be able to stand on its own?) This intallment obviously draws on characters and situations more thoroughly developed in earlier stories.  So I really didn't feel I knew enough about the characters, just from this book alone, to empathise with their predicament and understand their motivations.  Also, the shamanic/Pan theme was quite over-emphasised and distracting and not too well juxtaposed with the other techno theme.  However, the Willa Jansson character has intrigued me sufficiently to want to explore at least one more of the books in this series.Read moreRead less
