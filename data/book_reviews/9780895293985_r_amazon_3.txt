I started using this book when my son was 7 months old.  He is now 22 months old and almost completely potty trained, without any fights, hassle or stress.  The main thing about this book that is different from the others is that it sees potty training as something that takes 12-18 months, not something to be done in a weekend or two.  Its premise is that you teach children all the necessary skills to using the potty so that when their body is ready, the skills are already there.  This book does NOT claim that your child will (or should be) potty trained at 12 months of age.
