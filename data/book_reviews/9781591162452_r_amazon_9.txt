I got the first 5 books of this manga on a whim; The description seemed interesting and it was by my favourite manga-ka, Kaori Yuki, so I figured "Why not?" I could have not been more pleasantly surprised. The art is beautiful, per usual, and the story is rich and compelling so far. But there are a few things some people may have a problem with as far as morals go.One such thing is that the main couple in this particular story, Setsuna and Sara, are blood-related siblings.Now, I was never one to judge for anything so I don't have a problem with incest but that is just something to warn about since other people do.Another thing is that these books have a fair amount of gore, so if you don't like that, I wouldn't recommend this series to you.Other then that, I seriously suggest you give these books a go.
