The final book in this series introduces the reader to the fourth sibling, Dr. Fiona Thornton who is more comfortable with wild animals than with other human beings.  She's independent and doesn't believe she needs anyone. She rarely even sees her own siblings who live in other parts of Africa. Furthermore, she doesn't want anyone interfering with her research work with African elephants, especially not Rogan McCullough who flies his airplane into her remote campsite and arrogantly proposes projects which would exploit her elephants.  She sends him away but it's impossible to keep the man from returning, time after time. Their relationship is a stormy one reminiscent of Kate and Petruchio in Taming of the Shrew.  Gradually Rogan learns a thing or two about elephants and Fiona begins to wonder if she truly wants to spend her life alone...or if there might be room in her heart for a man who is learning to love elephants.
