The Macra Terror (originally broadcast in 1967, starring Patrick Troughton battling against some, er, giant crabs) is one of those Dr Who stories that the BBC wiped the tapes of, threw them in a skip and buried them in landfill. So no-one has seen it for a very long time. But in the 80s, the Beeb were kind enough to get the story's original writer, Ian Stuart Black, to pen this little novel version of it.
