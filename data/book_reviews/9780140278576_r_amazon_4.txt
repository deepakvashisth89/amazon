I read a lot. And by a lot, I mean 2 books a week for years. Many genres, usually biography. I preface my review this way to assure any readers that I’m not a chick lit woman. So now my review. I loved this book.  The author explores what it’s like when women reach mid-life and everything is not as expected. Then he shows what women can do with that life when presented with opportunities. My favorite character is Mrs. Smith-Porter a self-created English woman who survived and then thrived during and after WWII with a lot of hard work and a little larceny. It’s not Shakespeare, nor do I want it to be. It IS very witty and inspiring. I’ve read it 3 times. You need to read it at least once.Read moreRead less
