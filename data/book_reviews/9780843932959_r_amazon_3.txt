This book collects together two horror novels. The first is called "Moon of the wolf" while the second is called "Progeny of the Adder".The first book "Moon of the wolf" is perhaps the more famous of the two due to the fact that it was adapted into a made for television film in 1972. The story is set in an isolated village in Mississippi in the 1930's. Much of the story centres around small town cop Aaron Whitaker. After the mutilated remains of a young girl are found it is up to Whitaker to find and bring the killer to justice. But it is not long before there are more killings and Whitaker is forced to face up to the fact that the killer may be more than human.The second novel "Progeny of the Adder" is set in Washington D.C in the 1960's. The story centres around a homicide detective called Harry Picard and his efforts to bring a killer to justice. A killer with enough strength to fight off a dozen police officers, that is immune to bullets and who leaves his victims drained of blood.Although both novels are advertised as horrors there is not a large amount of graphic violence in either of them. In fact they both read more like investigaion procedure novels. With most of the killings taking place off screen, so to speak, and the protagonists investigating the crime scenes after the killings have taken place. Not that there is anything wrong with that. I usually prefer to read the more gory horror novels and yet I still enjoyed both of these.
