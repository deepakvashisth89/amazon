I can add little enough to the excellent reviews I've seen here, both pro and con.This novel is definitely not for everyone.  It's long and it's complicated.  I discovered Kingsbury when I read his 'Shipwright' short story.  Wondering if he'd written anything else, I looked him up on the internet.  Bingo!  I quickly located a book with his short story The Cauldron, and it was good too.  Psychohistorical Crisis is the best SF novel I've read in years.  Period.BTW, I found the ending to be perfectly clear, and quite upbeat.  I don't keep stories which aren't pretty much "happy ending".  The world is ugly enough I don't need to go stocking my bookshelves with depressing fiction.  So when I bought a copy of the novel he wrote for Niven's Kzin universe, I was terribly disappointed.  It's got the same exquisite detail, and is depressing as hell!  That one wasn't a keeper - for me.This one is!Read moreRead less
