Cooper mines a wellspring of human emotions with such honesty, freshness and realism that PROMISES, PROMISES never descends into stock characterization or cliche. (She even spins a new twist on the oft-trite but de rigueur "baby" plot.)This book has plenty of substance -- Cooper poses some big, real-life questions her characters answer only after considerable soul-searching -- but the story is anything but solemn. Her heroine's opening gambit -- trying to seduce a tenant she's hardly said two words to -- is a doozy of a scene, funny and fun, and the disastrous result is something anyone who's ever put themselves on the line and failed can identify with.And sure, there's frivolity, but that doesn't make this a cotton-candy read. Gretchen and Marco are well-rounded characters trying to get through life the best they can, and darn it all if you don't fall in love with them almost immediately. I wasn't too keen on Gretchen at first -- she seemed a bit too drab and doormat-ish -- but I loved how she summoned the courage to reach for that brass ring.Read moreRead less
