Maggie is the second to youngest Dennehy sister and is determined to become a doctor. A contrived meeting in a bordello leads to a sexual encounter with Connor Holiday (I really didn't like this part as it seemed, as I said above, contrived). However, the book picks up then as the two deal with the fallout.I loved how Maggie got around learning her medical knowledge; Dancer is such a great character. She and Connor are a great couple though I wasn't fond of the love triangle with Beryl. I'm glad it resolved the way it did; that was deft handling by the author.It's another good story in this interesting Western series.Read moreRead less
