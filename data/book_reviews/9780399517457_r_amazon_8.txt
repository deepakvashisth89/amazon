Lynne Finney, herself a survivor of sexual abuse, has written a gentle, encouraging guide that is filled with helpful examples, and concrete, accessible and applicable suggestions for healing. It is also filled with  sound knowledge, articulate writing, and the powerful example of her own  experience. This book is highly memorable, accessible, and readable for all  readers, and offers information that is valuable for everyone who has  experienced sexual abuse, anyone who knows someone who has experienced such  trauma, or anyone who may encounter such a person in the future.Read moreRead less
