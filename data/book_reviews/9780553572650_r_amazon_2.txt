I didn't know who Spinrad was or what he had written until I took a random stab on Kindle, just risking a few bucks in the hope of entertainment.  I got a brilliant send-up of some very important aspects of contemporary society.The set up has a batch of eco-terrorists take over an LA TV station with guns and explosives.  They want to use the event to bring publicity to their cause.  Spinrad very slowly and carefully brings out the character of the four captured station personal and gives enough depth to the terrorists to help us understand them.He also savages the entire Hollywood/TV industry as a gang of terrifyingly self-centered but hugely talented and in some cases, massively intelligent jerks.  He does it plausibly, teasing out the motives, goals, and skills of the people behind the scenes of fantasy town.  He does the same with the American government at the highest levels (this was written in the Clinton administration but could apply across the board).  He draws the terrorists as a gaggle of broken idealists and revolutionary has-beens.  Not a lick of moral dignity among the lot of them, for all their pretensions.We discover that nearly all of us have dubious motives when the stakes get high enough.And we get a thriller so twisty that even in the last few pages we don't really know for sure how the story will end.  I don't get that very often with the stuff I read.We have a long slow journey to get to the good stuff.  Just wait it out.  The wait is worth the payoff.
