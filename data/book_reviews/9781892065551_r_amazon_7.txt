MYTH-ion Improbable (2001) is the eleventh humorous Fantasy novel in the Myth series, although it is really 3.5 in internal chronological order.  It comes afterMyth-Directions.  In the previous volume, Skeeve and his friends play in the Big Game to win the trophy.In this novel, Skeeve is a Klahd from Klah.  He is a magician and the partner of Aahz.  Now he is the Royal Court Magician of Possiltum.Aahz is a Pervect from Perv.  He had been a master magician until he lost his power from a joke.  Now he is Skeeve's partner.Tananda is a Trollop from the Trollia dimension.  She has green skin and hair and a very voluptuous body.  Tanda is Chumley's little sister and an assassin.In this story, Skeeve has a treasure map bought from a panhandler as a small gift.  He forgot about it during the Big Game and has only just rediscovered it his belt pouch.  He decides that it might be what he needs to get Aahz to take him dimension hopping again.  But he doesn't believe that Aahz or Tananda would ever consider it to be a genuine map to great treasure.At first, Aahz dismisses the map as an obvious fake.  But Skeeve tells Aahz that he paid good coin for the thing.  Since money was involved, Aahz pays more attention to the map and asks how Skeeve came by it.After Skeeve tells him about the sale, Aahz asks him in which dimension did the sale take place.  Skeeve tells him the third dimension before the Jahk world.  Aahz goes off to fetch Tanda to learn more about the location.Tanda says that the sale took place in the Mini dimension and Aahz begins to believe that the map might be real.  It seems that the Minikin cannot lie and neither can anyone who has stayed there for more than three days.  Aahz and Tanda decide to follow up on the map and let Skeeve go along.The first step is to consult an expert at the Deva Bazaar.  Aahz tells Skeeve to keep his mouth shut while he and Tanda are talking.  Skeeve immediately opens his mouth to ask why and Tanda seals his lips with magic.The person they consult is a shifter.  As Aahz and Tanda are talking, the shifter's body -- and chair -- slowly change shape.  Skeeve is enthralled by the changes.Aahz and Tanda cut a deal with the shifter for five percent of the value of the treasure for transit to Vortex #1, the entrance to the dimensional cluster.  The shifter agrees and they are suddenly in a dust storm within the target dimension.Tanda leads the way through the dust to a log cabin.  There they check the dimensional map and find that it has changed.  Vortex #1 is now at the center, with paths leading to other dimensions.  Yet neither Aahz or Tanda know how to get these locations.So the party goes back to the Bazaar to get directions to the next dimension.  Naturally the Shifter charges them another five percent before they are transported to that dimension.  They go back to the Bazaar four times and the shifter's cut rises to 25 percent of the final take.This tale eventually takes Skeeve and his friends to the world with the treasure.  They acquire another person along the way.  All four find Kowtow to be strange, with cows, cowboys and horses.  Skeeve doesn't know what a cow is, much less a cowboy.  But he does know about horses.The author suffered from writer's block for years prior to this novel.  He overcame that problem, but the last novel in a six volume contract was a complicated work.  So this novel was written as a practice run forSomething M.Y.T.H. Inc..  Read and enjoy!Recommended for Asprin fans and for anyone else who enjoys tales of magical quests, dimension hopping and attractive women.
