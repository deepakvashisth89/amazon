For me, you can forget Tolkien. My favorite 20th-century fantasy writers are Angela Carter and Michael Moorcock. As with Carter’s better-known novels, ‘Machines’ is full of invention and weirdness. However, I found the last third of the book rather flat, and for once I felt that Carter’s creativity deserted her – also, the ending seemed rushed. If you are already an Angela Carter fan, then you’ll probably enjoy this book. However, if you haven’t yet dipped into her writing, I’d suggest you start with ‘Nights at the Circus’ or ‘Wise Children’. You can always circle back to this later.Read moreRead less
