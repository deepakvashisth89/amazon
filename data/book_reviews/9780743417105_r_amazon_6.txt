Book 2 of the six book Justice League of America series. The tile suggest that the book is focused on Batman (or at least Batman is highlighted as the hero to save the day) but in reality it is somewhat underwhelming. In the end, Batman's actions were the decisive factor in the Justice League winning the fight, but the overall story arc did not highlight Batman's strength - his ability to maximize human potential into superhuman levels of action. As a Justice League story, it is entertaining with Superman, Wonder Woman, Flash, Green Lantern and the Martian Manhunter making appearances. As a matter of fact, one can argue that the title should really be Batman and the Martian Manhunter: The Stone King. Felt like the Manhunter had equal screen time but not equal billing.
