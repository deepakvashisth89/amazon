As a child in elementary school, I was plagued with what would later be called Attention Deficit Disorder (A.D.D.) I could not read anything longer then a magazine article. I tried and tried to read books, but by the time I turned to page 2, my mind had skipped to thinking about yesterdays Gilligan Island episode, save one...This book captured my imagination and I couldn't put it down.
