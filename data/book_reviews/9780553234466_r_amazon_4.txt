Eeny Meeny Murder Moe tells of a very humiliating occurrence for Wolfe. Because he had gotten a spot of sauce on his tie at lunch he removed it and left it on his desk when he went up for his session with the orchids at four o'clock. Archie and Fritz both declined to be his valet so there the tie remained to be used to murder a young woman who had come to consult Wolfe. Archie knew it would be a hard sell to convince Wolfe to take the case and left her in the office while going up to the plant rooms to discuss it with him. It was a very interesting case that took Wolfe's great skill at deduction to expose the killer. It was especially gratifying for him since the wound  to his self esteem was so great.It was also satisfying for him to find the killer in Death of a Demon. His client was arrested almost immediately after her husband was murdered. He was truly an evil man with numerous people who had motives to kill him. The task was to find who, among all of them, actually committed the murder and framed Wolfe's client. Of course Wolfe was able to accomplish that.The client in Counterfeit For Murder was quite a character. She was the complete opposite of the type of female to captivate Archie, but he was on her side from the beginning. After she found a large amount of money hidden in her house she wanted to hire Wolfe to "make the cops eat dirt," and offered to split the reward for finding the money with Archie and Wolfe. It was immediately obvious to Archie that the money was counterfeit , but before a plan could be formed one of the boarders in the client's house was murdered. Wolfe agreed to find the murderer, but her number one goal was still to make the cops eat dirt. Wolfe was able to accomplish both goals for her.The short stories of Rex Stout are good for showing the personality traits of Wolfe and Archie. The third one has  great examples of Archie's sense of humor. The first one shows Wolfe's determination. The second one illustrates their sense of justice.
