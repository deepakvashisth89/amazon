All the Sundays Yet to Come is a wonderful book about sports and life. Although it's primarily focused on the journey of a figure skater, this book crosses over into any kind of experience a reader could bring to it. Any athlete from any sport could relate to Bertine's ups, downs, failures, and successes. People who aren't even athletes could also read this book and find several pieces of Bertine's life to relate to and learn from. I enjoyed this book as much as anything I've read recently, and I'm grateful to Kathryn for putting it out there.Read moreRead less
