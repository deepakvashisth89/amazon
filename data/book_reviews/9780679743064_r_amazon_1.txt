Just finished reading this for the second time (first read it when it was released). I've read all four of his novels (this was the 3rd) and liked them a great deal. What stands out most is the amount of meaning, history and complexity Mooney packed into a single exchange of dialogue, side glance or casual action. The precision and texture of his writing is extraordinary. That said, I was left with some unanswered questions about the main characters and wondered why so many incidental characters were introduced by name. And although I often "cast the movie" when reading a novel, I'd be hard-pressed to tell you who I'd see playing Edith, Andrew, James, Santiago, Mercedes, Marissa and the other characters if it were made into a film. That's kind of odd (for me) to be left with so little a visual impression. The settings, on the other hand, are vivid. Mooney wrote in a way that was extremely cinematic -- I'm astounded none of his books have made it onto the screen. (I met him once and he told me his first had been optioned but nothing came of it.) The writing is first class. Mooney passed away in 2022; his 5th novel was 3/4 written and I hope someone will finish it if he left notes and such a thing is possible.Read moreRead less
