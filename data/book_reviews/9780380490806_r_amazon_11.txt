I started this book being frustrated with the main character and annoyed at her seeming helplessness and wretched life. I really didn't want to read a whole book about a downtrodden penniless wife and heiress. I needn't have worried! What a riveting tale!  I was hooked by the mystery from start to finish. Great characters, great backstories and nicely rolling plot. I can't wait to get into the next instalment of what I feel will be a gripping series.Read moreRead less
