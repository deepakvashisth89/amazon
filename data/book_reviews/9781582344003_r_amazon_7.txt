There was something about the book that left me cold - maybe I just don't like reading about dysfunction in relationships all that much. There were many characters interconnected in the story but only one I cared much about - David. Nathalie - his sister by adoption - well, she had little of my sympathy. And the collapse of Marnie, David's wife, made no sense to me because of her ineptitude at managing her youngest child, rendering her character not believable. However, the book as a whole kept my interest and I found reflections of people I know throughout. Trollope skillfully painted psychologically nuanced characters.
