Waterfowl (Madge) is a great reference. It includes plates illustrating each species and detailed accounts arranged taxonomically. Yes, the plate numbers don't always align with the illustrations and I've compared the hardcover illustrations to my paperback copy and they are a little faded, but it's still a handy (albeit now quite outdated) work. Most accounts include illustrations of breeding/non-breeding males and females as well as juveniles. The text describes their appearance, flight, nesting, habits, distribution, and abundance (though the last probably isn't terribly useful now).
