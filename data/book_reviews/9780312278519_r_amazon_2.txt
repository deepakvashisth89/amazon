As someone who has also struggled with her hair growing up, I related greatly to this author. This book was intensely fascinating and I learned a lot about the significance of hair in Western, particularly North American, culture. I never knew what an impact hair has had on the world in it's many forms, or what a focal point it truly is in society and the author delivered this news in a funny yet sophisticated and scholarly manner.
