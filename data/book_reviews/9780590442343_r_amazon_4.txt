When the baby-sitters club has a new charge who is deaf Jessi takes the job. After all, she loves languages. But then she finds out that the boy can cause some trouble. All over the town kids meet him and want to learn his language. So, of course, the job of teaching them sign language goes to the boy's older sister, Haley.
