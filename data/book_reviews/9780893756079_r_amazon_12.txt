I absolutely loved this book. If you like sea adventures, coming of  age books, this is for you. It's a lovely story and a very satisfying read, although a word of warning if you are buying this as a present! I bought it for someone and they just couldn't get on with it because of the heavy use of dialect in the writing. In the beginning I too found this a little hard but I soon got used to it although I can see why some readers don't like it, it can ruin the flow of a story etc and often writers just suggest the dialect, but for me, it only made it more realistic.
