Hans Kung is regarded by the powers in the Vatican to be untrustworthy as a teacher of Catholics within the Roman Church. But he remains a priest in good standing. You would expect that given the harsh  treatment handed out to him by the authorities, Kung would be resentful and write books that are filled with venom.  Not a bit of it. This book is an excellent, well-balanced study of the history of the church from its earliest times. Kung is a teacher who respects his students, a writer who respects his readers.Read moreRead less
