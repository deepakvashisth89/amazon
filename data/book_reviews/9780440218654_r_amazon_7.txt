This is an interesting book.  I've read quite a few of her crime novels and this, while it involves a crime, a few actually, is very, very different.  I enjoyed it thoroughly and would recommend it, but it's fairly long, so, if you're looking for a quick read, look elsewhere.  As always with this author, the characters are three dimensional and believable.  If I have a criticism, it would be that I would like to have learned more about Eve's fate.  In a way, she remains something of a cypher.  Perhaps this was the intent, but I found it a little frustrating. That said, Ruth Rendell is a wonderful writer and this book is very much worth the time.Read moreRead less
