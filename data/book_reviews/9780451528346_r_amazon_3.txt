The Journals of Lewis and Clark is difficult to rate since the document is an unedited transcript of the actual journals kept in the field as they made their trek of exploration across the interior of this country (now U.S.) to the Pacific Ocean and back in the early 1800s. They relate how they disciplined their crew for various infractions, the country as they saw it, the wildlife they encountered, how they interacted with the native Americans of different tribes, and the food and sometimes lack of it, which they consumed.
