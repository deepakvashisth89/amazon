I'm not a huge fan of re-reading books, because there are too many others waiting in line for a turn. There are a small handful that are an exception.  I've read this 3 times now, and it probably won't be the last. If you enjoy a good old fashioned Southern yarn, look no further. The writing style is somewhat different, as some sentences tend to go on and on , with no end in sight.The story is told by a young boy from the small town, mainly through his dad's interpretation of events. It's hilariously well written.  Even the tragedies have an undertone of humor.If you want to take a nice trip to a small town without leaving your chair, read this book. It's filled with characters you'll never forget and will want to revisit from time to time.
