I bought the book to read about Getty the astute businessman. Not about his sexual behavior and how manywomen he bedded. I don't care, and frankly do not need to know whether he made love to seventeen, seven, and if he did it in a kitchen.Apart from the semi-porn film like aspects of this book, it sheds great insight into the overall life of Getty particularly the unsavory as well as savory aspects.As someone who bought this book for the business insight I found it to be much broader than that but the nuggets of gold there it did contain were very much what I bought it for. This was my only major critique about this book is that it almost over glamorizes Getty as a sort of a playboy womanizer heartthrob. The amorous and salacious aspects are abundant. I do find it slightly deceiving that a book about a businessman would shed just as much light about him as a Hugh Hefner type figure when in reality you wanted to see the more cut throat nitty gritty aspects of how he build what he did.Having said that, a book about a great man whose impact on the world can still be felt. He was a huge American hand in the history of Saudi Arabia apart from the Bechtel family and he was extremely influential worldwide.
