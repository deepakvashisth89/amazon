I completely agree with the 2nd review above: if this book were crammed with 500 entire alphabets, they would all have to be so small you couldn't distinguish one from the other. I think we can all apply a little common sense here. The word "alphabet" doesn't always mean exactly 26 capital letters, 26 small letters and numbers 0 to 9. Sometimes you're given just a few letters, maybe A, B and C with attractive designs built in. I agree that it would be nice to have the stitch count of every single letter and alphabet but maybe this would have raised the cost of book and it certainly would look more messy inside. Better to count your own--the ones you're most interested in: you're going to have to chart them out anyway, right?You can judge a book by its cover--what's shown here is a pretty good sampling of what's inside.
