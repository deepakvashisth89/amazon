This is an interesting novel of medium length published approximately in 1911.  The writing style is consistent with other literature of the times.  There is no obscenity.  There are times I had to pay close attention.I have been meaning to read this novel for years.  Unlike other fine French Novels, I never hear people talk about this novel.  When I got around to reading it, I had mixed expectations.  The story in general seemed far more well known than the novel.  I was quite surprised how much I ended up enjoying this novel.There were times the novel was confusing to me.  There were times it seemed slightly slow.  However a lot of the confusion was addressed by the authors the story proceeded.  I wish to avoid any spoiling of the reading experience.  I think I can say that this is as much a mystery story as a ghost story.  I enjoyed the mixture.  Some of the turns of the story reminded me a little bit of some of Sherlock Holmes stories.I cannot say that this is the best novel that I ever read.  However it is really quite good.  I enjoyed it and there were times I did not wish to put it down.  It was different than what I expected.  I am really glad that I finally read it.
