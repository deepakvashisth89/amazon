Captains Courageous is an incredible classic that teaches kids about hard work, responsibility, and personal growth. The story of Harvey, a spoiled boy who finds himself working on a fishing boat, is both exciting and inspiring.The writing is rich and immersive, and while some language may be old-fashioned, it’s a great way to introduce young readers to classic literature. The adventure at sea, the memorable characters, and the life lessons make this a must-read for kids who love adventure stories!Highly recommend for young readers and families looking for a meaningful, character-building book!Read moreRead less
