Reprising my book 1 review: I happen to personally know one of the authors of the Exordium epic space-opera series, so I'll go right ahead and admit a little bias. Nevertheless, I still have the entire series in trade paperback and greatly enjoyed the story. When I got to the end of the series, I wished there was more, but it still came to a satisfying close.I was thrilled though to see the series be available in both Kindle and audiobook formats. Now I no longer need worry about my paperback collection falling apart and I have many hours of entertainment for listening (I do a fair amount of traveling, and few things pass the time more pleasantly than a good story).In terms of particulars, as far as what I especially liked about the Exordium series, it's that Trowbridge and Smith took care to depict space combat realistically. I mean, really real, in terms of both lightspeed and vast distances playing a big part in tactics. (Most other SF has things happen effectively instantly, which is good for faster pacing, but nearly always risks breaking my suspension of disbelief.)The mix of serious narrative and humor also adds to the enjoyment of losing myself in the Exordium universe, and in my opinion makes the characters much more relatable than the typical standard 'hero' types.I didn't notice any particular problems with the Kindle re-release of this and the other titles in the Exordium series (I have them all, both Kindle and audiobook). The voice performance in my opinion is okay, but not great; the emotional affect seemed to be a little on the flat side. Still it's listenable and after enough time, I forget about that part.My general recommendation though, is that the standalone audiobooks are pricey enough to warrant either using Audible credits for them or buy the Kindle copies, whereupon you can get the audiobooks at a substantial discount. Or wait for a sale.
