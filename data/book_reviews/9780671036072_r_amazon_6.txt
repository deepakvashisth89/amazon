The Tenebrea series are all very good. I just re-read the whole series and found them even better than I remember. The characters are very plausible; and the heroines and heroes each have their own flaws and very "human" personalities. There are no magical technologies or intellectual superpowers here, and no "deux ex machina" plot tricks to jar the reader. The writing is rich and complex, but doesn't dive into philosophical swamps or wander off into dead-ends. The authors know their military history and science too, so it is all perfectly believable, and sometimes even a a bit educational as well.  I just wish they would have written more!Read moreRead less
