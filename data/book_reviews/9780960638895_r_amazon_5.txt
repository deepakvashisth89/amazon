A Course in Miracles is life changing for those who use it. It's not for everyone, but of course that's true of any spiritual path. I can't say enough about it here to do it justice. There's an introduction to it, a small book in pamphlet form, that explains a little about it if someone isn't sure. You can also read the workbook online for free, in bits and pieces. I did that at first but wanted my own copy. I bought this edition used, not realizing it wasn't as complete as the Third Edition. Now I have the newer one on my Kindle and I'm ordering a hardcover copy as well. I will likely never want to be without this book in my life again.My five-stars-minus-one rating is only for this older edition. Although this was an official/approved edition, the reader who might buy should know there were a couple of materials added to the newer Third Edition, (and in the Kindle version section and line numbers so you always know where you are in the book), so it's best to have the newest edition.
