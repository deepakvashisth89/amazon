One of the truly great novels, fit to stand by Wuthering Heights and The Outsider, both of which it resembles to some extent. It is a harsh, uncompromising story built on realities the civilized world both creates and ignores. Superb plotting, magnificent characterization, unbearably authentic settings, themes of real significance and a style that matches subject to perfection.
