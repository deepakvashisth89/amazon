I read this book for a class and it is one of the few required readings I actually enjoyed. The detail Keller goes into about the abuse of the Korean "comfort women" is shocking. Call me corny, but I got goosebumps reading this book and the images that were created in my head haunted me for days.The book alternates between Beccah's(the daughter) and Akiko's (the mother/comfort woman) point of views. The character developement is incredible.This book is definitely not a happy story, but is one that is extremely emotional to the point where you almost feel the character's pain.
