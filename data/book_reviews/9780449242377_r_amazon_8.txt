I have been a Helen MacInnes fan for many years.  The paperbacks I bought during the '70s are falling apart.  I am delighted to see that Amazon has made them available electronically.  Ms. MacInnes was noted for writing thrillers set during the war years.  They seem to me to be well researched and VERY well written.  Not a lot of violence.  No filthy language.
