Maclean is well known for his action adventures. Starting with "HMS Ulyses" and through the likes of "Guns of Navarone" and "Ice Station Zebra." "Seawitch" continues the tradition in grand style as a terrorist seeks control of an oil drilling platform in the Gulf of Mexico. Filled with Maclean's usual twists and turns, the reader is kept guessing up until the final, explosive ending.
