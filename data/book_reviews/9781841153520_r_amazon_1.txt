As its title implys Marengo The Myth of Napoleon's Horse is a facinating and very detailed account of one of the most famous horses that ever lived. The author Jill Hamilton is no horse woman she nevertheless depicts the saga of Napoleon and several of his (mostly) Arabian war horses admirably. Her fast moving narative is exciting and not without many twists and turns. This is one of those books that grows on its reader. However, the author's account of the origen of the famous Arab steed is at varance with the statements made by the great equine historian Anthony Dent, who maintained that Marengo was a 3/4 Arabian foaled in Ireland.
