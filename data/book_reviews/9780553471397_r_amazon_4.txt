A well composed story, told well. My own practice was centered around federal tort cases, so I hesitate to comment much on the merits of this tale except to say it is an enjoyable read. I was never in any cases in criminal courts at the state level, but did see action in theft of trade secrets in Federal Court with Federal juries, so I was intrigued about the FBI's witness protection program. Not unlike the character in the book, I'm not sure I'd be comfortable under it either. Every day I'd be looking over my shoulder every time there came another knock on the door. The Mafia has a long memory, I'm sure. In the1980's they rubbed out a squealer at the stroke of midnight less than a block from my house. Very bloody and very scary, indeed!!
