The paintings in the book are good, and the author goes through several steps that he used to achieve his results. The main problem with this book is that it is weak on technique. What I mean is how to mix paints, how to layer colors, how long to wait for the paint to dry before adding another layer, how to decide exactly how to layer the colors, even how to thin the paint and the results you can expect from a thinned paint. He shows his palette, and tells the colors he's used on each step, but it's useless to the person wanting to learn about oils paints in particular. He instead goes into detail about how to draw a nose, eyes, etc., with illustrations. but this is very different depending on the medium chosen. I have worked in acrylics and know how they work, but I'm trying to branch out into watercolors and oils, and this book has almost no information about actual oil paints.
