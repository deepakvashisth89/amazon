This is a wonderfully written look at sexual surrogacy and how it can benefit various people in therapy.I found it absorbing and certainly one of Irving Wallace's best books. Whether or not you agree with the use of sexual surrogates, I do believe you'll learn a great deal about it from this book and become fans of the surrogates and their clients.And, as always, his characters are beautifully created.
