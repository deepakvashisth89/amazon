I have a copy of The Complete Joy Of Home Brewing, which people always call "the bible" for learning to brew. But that book is huge, and you have to wade through verbose text to get to the nuts and bolts of what to do on the actual day of brewing. This book, in one chapter (really, 4 pages), spells out those steps, and you can refer to them again and again, regardless of the recipe.
