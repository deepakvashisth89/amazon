Meet Teresa Bloomingdale. A mild manner newspaper columnist who is married to her attorney sweetheart. They live in Nebraska, love each other, and love their kids.All 10 of them.You see, Mr. and Mrs. Bloomingdale had 10 kids in 12 years. No twins. No triplets. No quadruplets. 10 separate kids in a time span of 12 years. Talk about labor pain!Teresa Bloomingdale's "Up A Family Tree" takes a humurous look at the life of her big family, taking us from the beginning with the birth of her first four boys, to finally being blessed with the birth of a daughter, all the way to the youngest turning 12 (he was born in 1969 and the book was written in 1981).This book is very, very funny and will have you in stitches from the very beginning. No couple just starting a family should be without this book (though, understandably, they may want to be without that many kids).Read moreRead less
