This is vintage Gordon Korman: Everyone is nuts except the straight-man protagonist Sean, non-stop crazy action, and those poems...I first read this book nearly 20 years ago and just bought it used after scouring the library systems of 2 boroughs. I still laugh hysterically at certain parts (husband: "I don't get it, isn't that a kids' book?") and I still know many of the poems by heart (especially "Fruit Fly", for some reason). I dream of the day that this gets made into a movie which launches it back into print and popularity for a new generation, although I have to wonder what kids would make of the anti-technology message.Read moreRead less
