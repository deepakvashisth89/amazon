This is the 5th of Dell Shannon’s series about LAPD homicide detective Luis Mendoza. This book was written in 1963. One part of it deals with suspects who are homosexuals and unfortunately, all the misconceptions of that time are present.Luis Mendoza and Alison Weir are now married and building a house. At the very end of the book, we hear that Alison is quite likely pregnant, and there is a possibility of twins! Art and Angel Hackett acquire two additions to their family. The first is a Persian cat, Silver Boy, who had been left homeless when his owner was murdered. The second is their son Mark Christopher!
