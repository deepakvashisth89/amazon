I have thoroughly enjoyed reading this book. Doreen Virtue shares her early childhood memories, and the challenges and experiences this phase of life brings. She has drawn on her reflections and grown constantly from that.  Far from having it all, she shares with us the difficulties of early married life, and how she has struggled financially and emotionally. Despite the superficial glamour and abundance, she has often had little emotional stability in her private life, and on occasion, her faith has been her only constant. Her ability to live in the real world, and yet access higher spiritual wisdom is a gift to us all. Her teachings have undoubtedly changed our world, with so many of us now having a waking awareness of angelic realms at our service, if we should need them. Doreen is a very real woman, and I have considerable respect for her.Read moreRead less
