I first came across this book (and Book 2 of the series) in a used book store in Northern Virginia, way back in the mid-1990s.  The copies were well-worn, clearly having been read numerous times.  After reading the two books, I learned why. This series is a great page-turner.  I read the two books two times in short order and have read them repeatedly since.  My only disappointment was that I couldn't find Book 3, and Book 2 clearly ended with plans for a follow-on.  Eventually, I found Book 3 and re-read the full series. Have done so several times since.The author's style reminds me of many of the classic sci-fi authors of the past; I can see influences of past masters.  The story is not as ground-breaking or unique as those masters, but this is a solid sci-fi outing.Read moreRead less
