Can you take the time to enjoy the English countryside of 1820? That is the world of Adam Bede. And like the early novels of Thomas Hardy, I adore sinking back in to that world through literature.George Eliot is flat out brilliant. But not everyone's cup-of-tea.  Most appreciated by those of us brought up on Dickens as a child and who then moved on to Eliot and Hardy as adults.I love Eliot's philosophizing. I don't mind a pause in the action while the author reflects on whatever moral issue is at hand.  Her deep love and appreciation of the simple things in life always comes through.Virginia Woolf said of George Eliot's "Middlemarch" - "It's the only novel written for adults."So challenge yourself to enjoy a more dense (as in more rich) reading experience and enjoy this, one of her earlier ones, "Adam Bede".Read moreRead less
