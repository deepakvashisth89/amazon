I discovered the Dover reprint of this Ambrose Bierce classic when I was in college and reveled in the biting wit. It was lost in many moves since, so when I saw a quote from this edition, I bought it. It has the originally published Devil's Dictionary updated from columns in other news papers for which he wrote in later years. It also has a second half that contains notations and references to Bierce's life and work in connection with most words.
