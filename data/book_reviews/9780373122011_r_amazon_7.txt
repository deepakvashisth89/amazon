A sweet tale of illness, theft, legal contracts, death, mind-blowing passion, and finally...true love. Mikayla would do anything to spare her ill father from spending his last days in jail. He had embezzled money from Raphael so he could care for his terminally ill wife. Before he could repay it he is also stricken with a terminal illness. Mikayla offers herself to Raphael in return for immunity for her father. Raphael is intrigued by her delicate beauty and accepts.
