This is an awesome extension of the Arthur C. Clarke legend. His books have inspired many readers to open their minds to different ideas. I met him years ago at a World Science Fiction Convention while I was helping at a table and he walked by. He was so dignified and easy to chat with. He had a way of mixing real science (remember he first described the geosynchronous satellite orbit) and pure imagination.
