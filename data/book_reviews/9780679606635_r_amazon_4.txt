Someone once asked Chekhov why he didn't write novels. He said that he did, but none of them was over 12,000 words long. These are some of those 'novels'. Each one of them has an intellectual and emotional heft that will blow away most full-size novels. They are like magic tricks, lots of very serious clowns climbing out of an impossibly small car.
