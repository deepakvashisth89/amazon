I have read this book twice and at age 69 it just reaffirms the reality that Santa really does exist.  My first reading was in 2009 and I had such a good feeling when I finished the book.  In 2011, with a new husband, we read a chapter a night starting on December 1st.  We both really loved the story, and the history was fantastic and put everything into perspective thru the ages.  I sent a copy to my four grandchildren and they absolutely fell in love with Santa all over again.  They always believed, because if they didn't they were told there would be no presents under the tree.  This is our family tradition.
