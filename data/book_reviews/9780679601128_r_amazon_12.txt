Shelby Foote is for me the greatest historian of the US Civil War and one of the greatest historians since Thucydides, not excluding McCaulay and Gibbon. So I am biased, and after you have read this book, you may share my views of his esteemed position.I believe the book is a straight excerpt of his recounting the Battle of Gettysburg from his three volume series. It kindled in me a desire to re-read the entire Foote opus, it so reminded me of his narrative greatness.
