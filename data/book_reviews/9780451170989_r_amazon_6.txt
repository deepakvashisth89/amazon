According to the About the Author Page in the book, Gary Devon passed away in 2007.  After reading Bad Desire, I can tell you that his death was a loss to the thriller genre!This book has everything.  Murder.  Illicit desire.  Deadly Obsession.  It's the kind of book where the suspense and action just keep ratcheting up until you simply can't look away.  Slater is such a weasel.  It's fascinating to read how his mind works and to see what happens as his careful plans go spiraling out of control.This is a not to be missed thriller.
