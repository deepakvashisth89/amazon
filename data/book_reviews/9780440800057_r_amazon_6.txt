I bought a bunch of Ramona books for my niece for her birthday to fill in the gaps in her collection. I ended up reading Ramona Forever in like one sitting and they are as fantastic and remarkable as I remember when I first read them 30 years ago. Few ever captured the pains of being very young and misunderstood as Beverly Cleary did, and her insights hold up beautifully upon re-read. I am a 38 year old dude.
