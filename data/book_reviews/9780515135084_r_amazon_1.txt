I thoroughly enjoy this author's writing and after reading the Jimmy Vega series, I was anxious to also read this series. This book is not only a page turner with well developed characters, but a wonderful education about firefighting and the lives of my personal heroes in these roles. The story and dialogue will make you laugh and cry, and there are a lot of surprises in Flashover. I just started reading the third book in this series and am once again hooked.
