Between this and The Starless World I'm not really sure how to rate Eklund as an author, much less a Trek author.  Needless to say intelligent stars in the Dyson Sphere "Starless World" and the demon like critters on this planet ... didn't quite have me wincing, but it had me scratching my head as to what criteria Bantam had for letting authors write for Trek.To me Trek was a really cool navy in space going after bad guys kind of TV show, but the authors of the various Bantam era Trek novels really grabbed things from left field and put them up for public scrutiny.  Sometimes the story would have nearly nothing to do with the mission of the U.S.S. Enterprise, and for some odd reason focus on one of the primary characters.  But I digress.Truth be told I barely remember this book, it was that "memorable".  And one of the things I discovered about Trek novels from the 70s is that they aren't all that well written.  The Galactic Whyirpool is pretty decent by Gerorld.  Some of the "fan fic" in The New Voyages' books are interesting.  Trek to Madworld is king of tongue in cheek fun, and Sky's Vulcan! is a Mary Sue gone off the deep-end.  Still, they were all readable and memorable to some degree.Devil World isn't very memorable, and I spent nearly a decade hunting down a copy to read.
