As is typical of the Gamage series, this narrative is peopled by an interesting and varied cast of characters.  Where it falls down is in its overly intricate whodunit plot.  The background to the crimes hinges on the murderer’s noncredible manipulation of the behavior of other characters—something the reader doesn’t learn about until after the criminal is revealed—and the murders themselves rely too heavily on fortuitousness occurrences and precise timing to be believable.
