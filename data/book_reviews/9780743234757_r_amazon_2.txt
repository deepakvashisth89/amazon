This an overview of the Bush administration's foreign policy decisions from January 1989 to the start of Operation Desert Storm. Boodward provides one of the most precise and complete pictures of Washington during the period and includes many key information on each major player. Under an assertive president, the hawkish Dick Cheney competed with the more circumspect James Baker and Colin Powell on whether to use military force in Panama and Iraq. As the title tells, this book focuses on "the commanders'. While the description of the generals, Cheney, and Powell is insightful, the author does not talk much about the civilian officials.
