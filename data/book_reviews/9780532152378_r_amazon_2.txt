I've been an Allingham reader for years -- or rather a re-reader. This collection of stories is a mixed bag -- the classic with the very good. Two stories deal with a man plotting to kill his burdensome wife, but with just enough twists to make them both interesting. My favorites involve the wonderful character Charlie Luke, the policeman whose expressive hands instantly conjure up scenes and personalities. Here, I think, Allingham proves herself a superior writer, because this is not easy to depict. The attitudes are a bit old-fashioned, but it's fun to think yourself back.Read moreRead less
