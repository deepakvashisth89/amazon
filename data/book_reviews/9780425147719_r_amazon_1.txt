I am so happy to meet Duncan Kincaid and Gemma. This is the second book I’ve read, a difficult and painful book about very human and complicated characters, nine more than the two cops. Duncan is a bit difficult to accept as he is very much in touch with his feminine side, and Gemma is a shade too cliched in her desire for independence, but they are real and charming.
