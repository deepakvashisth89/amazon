I purchased this book for a friend who was new to the internet, but I  couldn't resist cracking it open.  The book is layed out in a very clear,  concise format, if you're interested in a function of Yahoo!, there is  probably a chapter devoted to it.  I was amazed at the versatility of the  site...I used the free email, messenger and companion, but had no idea that  there was so much more to do on Yahoo!.  I love Chess, now I can play  online.  I love getting up-to-date sports scores, now I can get them  through Yahoo!.  I love to buy through my computer, now I can comparison  shop from a single web site, all through Yahoo! Shopping.
