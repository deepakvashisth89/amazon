When I first got this comic, I was very excited and couldn`t wait to read it. However, I noticed the comic only had 48 pages. I bought the cheapest option on Amazon which was 28 dollars and I thought the comic would be over 150 pages at that price but I counted the page numbers in the comics and there wasn`t even 1/3 of that length.The comic is not bad. The artwork done in it would make you say, "Wow! Whoever drew this really has talent." and even though it is in French, you can get a clear idea what is going on in the story. The characters are also well drawn and most of them feel quite likable. It`s too bad we have to pay a lot of money to get this.The only people I can recommend this comic series to is the people who love to collect foreign comics and have a LOT of money to burn.Read moreRead less
