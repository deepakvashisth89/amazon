When I first started reading this, I was put off by the prose—not because it’s very British, but because the point of view jumps all over the place along with other issues. But I will admit that the story itself drew me in. I wanted to know more. To know who the killer was and to know more about Mrs. Pargeter. She was definitely my favorite character. But as I finished the story, I was unsatisfied by the ending. I understand the reasoning for it, but it left me unhappy. I would have preferred a more traditional ending for the killer.
