I read this book so many times as a child the cover fell off. The main character is a girl who loves museums and is dealing with a new school. She volunteers at a museum that is based on the De Young museum in Golden Gate Park, SF. She meets a ghost there, and helps her to unravel a mystery. Beautiful writing, one of my all time favorites. If you like books such as the Narnia series or the Green Knowe books, you will definitely enjoy this book.Read moreRead less
