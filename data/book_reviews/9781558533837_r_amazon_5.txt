Being honest, im disappointed in this. I bought this for my fiance and me because we are die hard Friends fans. The book itself, is perfect. I love all the pictures and quotes from the show; and the cover is beautiful. My biggest complaint with this is that none of the recipes seem to be from the show. I expected to see a recipe for: "Nestle Toulouse" chocolate chip cookies, or Rachels trifle, Joey's Best meatball sandwich, and definitely Ross' after Thanksgiving Sandwich with the moist maker. The recipes that are included have a short paragraph before (kind of) explaining the recipe and where it comes from. Most of them are a stretch and make no real connection.
