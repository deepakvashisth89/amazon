This was advertised as a first edition. The hardcopy I received was a fifth edition and reprinted in China. He book comprises a 30 page biography followed by almost 100 pages of his photographs. However, the printing is pretty awful and much of the glory of Ansels wonderful photographs are rendered flat and lifeless, often with the highlights blow out to pure white. I have many books on Ansel Adams and this is the poorest print-wise by some way I'm afraid.
