Margaret Atwood has once again delivered a simply wonderful collection of her works. Being an avid reader of her work I delight in each new book she puts out. Her writing is so real one can almost feel, touch, taste, or be wherever she is writing about. There is a maturity and higher level of writing expertise that she possesses. I would almost dare to say she is a class of her own. An excellent book. I look forward to more in the future!Dorothy KruskyAuthor The Chrismas Cat - A Child's Christmas Story 2005Life's Ride - A Journal of Poems 2007Read moreRead less
