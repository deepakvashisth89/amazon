"Come Love A Stranger" by Kathleen E. Woodiwiss was a great read.  I love all her books, but the earlier ones are her best.  She tells a love story with a lot of detail, weaving in a mystery (or puzzle really) with it.  It keeps you on edge, wanting to keep reading until it's finished.  I love her characters, I think because the author is very descriptive.  It's like you get to know them over the course of the story.
