Arthur C. Clarke is one heavenly writer. Simplicity laden with beauty, not a wasted word. He carries finesse with his own idiosyncratic expression. Saying much with little and at other times saying much with much. It’s incredible how much wisdom he held within him at age 36 when this was published.His philosophies are on full display through the alien species, the Overlords and the Human race. There is some paranormal activity throughout the story which he regrets, but it’s handled with express attention to filling the mind with wonder rather than throwing away logic and reason.It’s well written, awe inspiring, fun, and really just quite good to experience. You’ll learn plenty and at this time where the world is changing a lot, you may notice quite a few parallels. The Utopia he crafts on the story…we may have certain details embedded within to contend with. If we ever get there. It’s up to us afterall.
