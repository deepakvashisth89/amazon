Pol Martin's Easy Cooking For Today has tasty recipes that don't take forever to prepare. He uses simple ingredients in an interesting way and while he can be heavy on the cream, butter and sauces, there is no denying the recipes are good. The book is color-divided into sections like meat, veg, soups, and is a go-to book when you just can't think of what to have. Beginning cooks will love the big color pictures and the step-by-step instructions. Old time cooks will appreciate the new twists on favorite meals.Read moreRead less
