LAST ACT IN PALMYRA is one of the best mystery novels in the Marcus Didius Falco series. The following series starring Falco's daughter is easily as good as the best of the original series.  To say the the book is centered in Rome is a vile understatement.  It is LIVED IN ROME.  You are given Rome as it was when the action took place.  Or England, or Spain, or Alexandria or, in this case Palmyra.  You are even given a map.  The fun starts with her list of characters and continues until the very  end.  LAST ACT IN PALMYRA and ALEXANDRIA are my two favorites but I own them all. And I have read them often.  Her main characters become friends, and the next of the series is always too far away. These are not just good reads, they are classics. If you like your action lively and your wit both sharp and humane, Lindsey Davis is the writer for you.
