Kathleen Givens writes an obviously well researched historical novel. This book is a sequel to her earlier work Kilgannon. She has a nack for conveying the turmoil of 18th century Scotland and the agonizing choices people had to make.  Her characters and their dialogue are believable and her descriptions of the landscape convey its beauty.  The love story is beautiful but fraught with sadness.  This may have been realistic considering the times, but was disturbing.  My other dissapointment in this book was that the ending was rather inconclusive.
