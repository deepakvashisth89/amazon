Tracy is an interesting character. She's both likable and unlikable. I cringed reading about her attempts to make friends. But I loved watching her grow and learn. Tracey's struggle with reading is relatable, and would be good for kids who also struggle. Giff wrote characters with depth and realism. The plot moves nicely and easily.
