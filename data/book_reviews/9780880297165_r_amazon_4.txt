These are the first three books in the "Cat Who..." series. She wrote these three in the 1960s... and then waited 18 years to continue the series.In "The Cat Who Could Read Backwards", we are introduced to Jim Qwilleran (yes, the spelling is right), veteran reporter trying to make a comeback... and we meet Koko, an extraordinary cat; an intelligent Siamese.In "The Cat Who Ate Danish Modern", we meet Yum Yum, another Siamese, and the team then goes on with "The Cat Who Turned On and Off".Jim Qwilleran is glad to be working again after getting his life back together; he's used to doing crime reporting, or at least investigative reporting, but he now finds himself being assigned to various oddball sections of the paper. Nevertheless, he gets mixed up in mysteries.These are FUN books to read, but because of the characters more than the mysteries.  I don't find the mysteries all that engaging, but the characters are fun to read.  As one goes through the series, things change in Jim Qwilleran's life, so, more than most series, they should be read in order (unlike, say the Nero Wolfe mysteries, in which Archie Goodwin and Nero Wolfe never age).  The "Cat Who" series are light-hearted and are engaging reading.  I'm about to start on a (mostly) second read-through, this time on my Kindle.  If I find anything of relevance about the Kindle version, I will update this.Bottom line: Lots of fun to read, but don't expect a deep Agatha Christie-like mystery.Read moreRead less
