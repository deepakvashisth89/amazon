I hadn't read horror comics in years and kind of just forgot about them until i randomly remembered reading them on the bus as a kid. Checked Amazon to see if anyone even published horror comics anymore and wound up deciding to buy this one, along with a few others. As far as the quality of the book, art and the stories therein, I enjoyed every minute of reading this and i think most other fans of the genre would too. That being said, $20 is a bit high considering i can get 200-500 pages of comics for the same price. It's somewhat understandable, since I'm sure Stephen King is collecting a licensing/royalty on the publisher's sales, but you can get more bang for your buck if you're not concerned with owning Creepshow just because it's a well-known classic. Makes me wonder how high of a percentage Mr. King is demanding, though, considering that books with 5 times as much content can be sold for the same price and still profit.
