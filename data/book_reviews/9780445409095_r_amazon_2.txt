Stuart M. Kaminsky is a professor of the history of the cinema.  He writes a wonderful series in the Toby Peters Mysteries of which this is a fine example.  Kaminsky has the class to keep the star in each of his books as he or she presented himself/herself to the public.  No gossip or scandals here, just a fun frolic through recent history with some of the most unusual and wonderful characters I've found: Toby, Shelley, Gunther, and Toby's by-the-rules cop brother.If you're looking for some deep dark intricate mystery that will keep you on the edge of your seat, you'll need to look elsewhere.  But, if you're looking for a lot of fun with characters who make you laugh on a regular basis and interact with the top stars of the 30's, 40's, and 50's, this is what you're looking for.
