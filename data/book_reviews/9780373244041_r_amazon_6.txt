A true love story that tests the patience of the characters, as they work their way to where they are supposed to be, with each other.  Dave and Matty have both known since they were not much older than toddlers, that they would love each other forever.  But, when they were off to separate colleges, and Dave's parents and Matty's Grams, had a chat with Dave, he made a decision that would affect them both, one that would hold consequences that would keep them apart for over six years.  After the death of her Great Uncle Henry, Matty returns to the homestead left to her, only to find it in a severe financial mess.  When her application for a grant that would mean keeping the ranch, is denied due to a slight technicality, the ever impulsive Matty approaches Dave with a proposition, one he couldn't turn down.  Will these two ever be able to put the past in the past?  Or will their future be lonely, each in their own corner?A wonderfully written story that is easy to follow and will have you falling in love all over again.
