This book looks closely at the evidence collected by scientists from many fields of study and includes a good collection of fossil photos.  It is written in layman's language and is suitable for ages 7 and older.  It takes a skeptical stance regarding Darwin's theory of evolution but refreshingly gives documented evidence as to why.  These kinds of works are becoming more sought after as so many well-respected scientists are beginning to abandon some of the outdated or disproven elements of Darwinian evolution, and kids these days ask a lot more questions and want better answers than we ever thought to ask in school.  True science requires us to look at every possibility, or at the least, consider and be informed about the differing theories out there.  It creates a good foundation from which to counter another's view.  Appropriate for the fact-hungry and information-rich generation who love debate and making up their own minds.
