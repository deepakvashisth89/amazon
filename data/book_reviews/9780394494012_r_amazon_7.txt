I like sci-fi stories typically but decided to try this one since it was by one of my favorite sci-fi authors.  The story is based on true events and there is a lot of historical information interspliced in between the narrative sections.  I actually learned a lot about Victorian England in the course of listening to this.  The story was great, and long enough for a few car rides (about 8.5 hours).  There aren't any voice actors or sound effects or anything like that.  However, the person reading does so with enough inflection that you can easily differentiate the characters that are talking.  The only strange thing is how they split it up amongst the 7 CDs: they don't end a chapter or anything, or even a paragraph.  The CD just stops mid-paragraph and says to put in the next disc, then picks right up.  One of the discs (I think it was 5) ends mid-conversation which I though was strange.
