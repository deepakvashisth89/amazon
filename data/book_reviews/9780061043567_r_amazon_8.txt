I love Lord Peter Wimsey and Montague Egg. Their stories are really good. However, I am not so fond of the other miscellaneous short stories in this book especially the more macabre stories. Having said that though I would still have to say that they are all well written and well worth the read. I know most Sayers fans will love them.
