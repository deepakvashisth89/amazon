There's a very good story here, underneath the countdown and the silliness and totally unbelievable shlock. It's far too padded with intricacies that have no real depth and that takes away from what could have been a great espionage story, suspenseful mystery. Instead we get a lot of little details that don't add anything and lend a sense of overwrought melodrama. That said, it's fun to read Finder in what seems to me should be his element. Sadly, in this case, I think he just missed the mark. That said, it's not bad, just not the book I had hoped it would be.Read moreRead less
