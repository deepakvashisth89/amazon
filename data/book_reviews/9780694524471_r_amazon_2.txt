I absolutely loved this book. It’s a simple story, but there is so much tucked inside it about trusting yourself, following the path that feels right, paying attention to the signs around you, and having the courage to keep going even when you don’t know exactly where you’ll end up.What stayed with me most was the idea that the journey itself changes us. Sometimes what we’re searching for isn’t exactly what we thought it would be, and sometimes we have to wander a little to figure out what really matters.I think this is one of those books that will mean something different depending on where you are in your life when you read it.
