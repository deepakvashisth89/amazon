I found my family name in the list of travelling people of Ireland, and thought I would find out more about them. Nan's life was so vastly different from my own, but I found her such a sympathetic character that I couldn't help but see things from her perspective.  Sharon Gmelch and her husband immersed themselves in the Travellers' lives, and did an admirable job of showing us the brutality and joy of the lives of people like Nan.
