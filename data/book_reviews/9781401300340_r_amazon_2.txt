Every businessperson should read this book.  It should be required reading in every business school.  While other retailers are reducing service to keep overhead down, Jack Mitchell marches to a different drummer.  By giving exceptional service to his customers and by treating his associates wonderfully--and indeed doing sodoes increase costs--his company, Mitchells receives repeat business from satisfied customers as well as referrals. The benefits the company receives more than make up for the added costs because Mitchells has less marketing costs--the company doesn't have to advertise as much as other companies.  Every business can do what Mitchells does.  It's not all that complicated.  Too bad most businesspeople don't get it. Operating a customer-driven business like Mitchells is also a more enjoyable way to work.  Instead of hassling customers you hug them--and they hug you back!  Customers and employees are happier--and when people are happier, it creats an excellent work environment.
