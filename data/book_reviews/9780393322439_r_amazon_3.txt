This has been one of my most favorite Highsmith works.I would urge all Highsmith addicts to read this one. People Who Knock on Doors  is a fascinating depiction of small town American life and covers topics like abortion, the moral majority andThe Religious Right’s need to control others’ personal narratives. This book’s subject matter is a reflection of what we’re living through now with the emergence of Trumpism and the Far Right.
