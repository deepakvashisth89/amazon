The literary world is awash with books on Tibetan Buddhism and as a newbie, I wanted somethng reliable.  Prof. Thurman is as reliable as you can get, having been a monk for many years, and a very close old friend of HH Dalai Lama.  Additionally he teaches Tibetan studies in uni in the USA.  He is regarded, I believe as the world's greatest authority on Tibetan Buddhism.
