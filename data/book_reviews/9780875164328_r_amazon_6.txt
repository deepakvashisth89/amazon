This book presents an imaginative, magical and authoritative study of the effect of color and music on different levels of one's being. Corinne Heline bases her research on historical esoteric teachings as well as on  her own schooled insights. She devoted herself to meditation and the study  of the ancient mystery under the Rosicrucian mystic Max Heindel, however  she claimed her chief inspiration from 'the Immotals' who overshadowed her.  Six topics-1.Astrological Correlations 2.The mystery of White and Black  3.New Age Developents 4.Color and Music of the Four Sacred Seasons  5.Nature's Inner-Plane Ceremonials 6.Some Occult Effects of Music-are rich  in their significance for seership in the New Age.
