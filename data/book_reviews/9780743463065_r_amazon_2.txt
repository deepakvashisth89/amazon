Even though this book was written in the 1940's it is still a very good book.  Some things are dated of course, like forensic science but in a way that is what makes these books so good, the police detectives have to solve the crimes using their minds. Through interviews, hunches and looking for motives the 87th solve the crimes they are faced with.  In this book a blackmailer is shot while walking down a street.  Carella and Hawes start tracking down the people who were being blackmailed in order to find the killer.  Another very good book in this series.Read moreRead less
