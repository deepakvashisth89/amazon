True Grit will forever be placed highly in the annals of literary history as among the greatest of Western novels ever written across all time lines. This novel also has biblical symbols in it like the black mark found on murderer Tom Chaney's head symbolizes the mark on Cain's forehead that is found in Genesis in the Bible. The pit heroine Mattie Ross falls into resembles that of Hades (no, I won't say that other 'H' word) before she is rescued by her companions and is brought back above ground to safety. The pit scene resembles Jesus' descent to and resurrection from the dead. Like Jesus from his resurrection, Mattie Ross is seeing the light of life once again after coming out of the pit in the novel. As for the rest of the details from the book, you'll just have to read True Grit. It's worth it. As for the two movie adaptations of True Grit, I have yet to see either of them but I love to make comparisons with the novel by watching, as well as enjoying, those two films on my own free will.
