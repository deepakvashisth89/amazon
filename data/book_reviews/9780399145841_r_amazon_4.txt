I've read most of the books in Standiford's "Deal" series and have enjoyed them all. This novel, however, is not part of that series and introduces us to some new characters, plus the story is not set in Florida. This time the locale changes from the concrete jungle of New York City (in particular, the underground corridors of the subway system) to the beautiful and dangerous splendors of the Wyoming wilderness.One review used Jon Krakauer's "Into Thin Air" as a comparison, and I can see that a bit. This novel combines that sort of daredevil outdoor adventure with the standard elements of a well written mystery. I found this to be a very gripping and fascinating story. Some scary stuff, some funny stuff, plenty of interesting characters, and Standiford does an outstanding job of describing the beauty and danger of the Wyoming mountain terrain.The novel's protagonist, NYC transit cop Richard Corrigan, is a likeable and memorable character. Less believable --- and less likeable --- are a few of the politicians and associated political vermin who show up in the tale. Parts of the story fall into cliché story at times (particularly one aspect of the story's climax), but overall I thought this was a really good novel. A nice departure from Standiford's typical fare, and a long way from Deal's Florida stomping grounds. I wouldn't mind reading another book featuring the Corrigan character.Read moreRead less
