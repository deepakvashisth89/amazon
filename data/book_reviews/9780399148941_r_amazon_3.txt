I second those reviews which claim this is one of Sandford's best in the "Prey" series.  Lucas Davenport goes to St. Louis to help the FBI track down Clara Rinker, his wily foe, who brings new definition to the term "cold-blooded."  Sandford cleverly portrays Davenport's grudging respect for his nemesis but does not turn her into a sympathetic character.  Had he done so, I think readers would have resented it and given this book a thumbs down.  Sandford is an almost flawless popular writer of thriller fiction.  His characters are real, their dialog is authentic, his plots are creative and fast-moving.  This is one of his best 'Prey' books.Read moreRead less
