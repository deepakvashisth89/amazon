This series of books introduces young children (kindergartens and on) to classic books. These books are condensed, and the stories simplified. I tried to introduce my son to some of these great books, and he consistently balked at the lack of pictures in other similarly condensed books. This series, however, includes enough pictures to keep his interest and give him the basics and desire to read truly wonderful writing. The Great Classics for Children series is a perfect way to transition your child from picture books to books from which they must create the pictures in their minds (as I tell my son). I love this series. We have a bunch from them and my son loves every one. And asks for them. Now that he's learning to read, I hope he will start reading them himself.
