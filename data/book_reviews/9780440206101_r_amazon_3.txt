Brenda Joyce was one of my favorite authors back in the 90s. After she started writing more than romance I tried to follow but fell disinterested. This is one of the books that I missed back in the 90s and it took me back to that time and I did  enjoy the book overall. The only criticism I have is I felt that Jane and the Earl had an overkill of misunderstandings between them. I understand that those kind of misunderstandings are to create tension and interest in the story, but really how can one expect to be barraged with tenderness if you are lashing out, and rejecting the other person...Really?... yet both the earl and Jane apparently felt this way after speaking harshly to one another.  It was a theme through most of the book. Finally in the last 20 or 30 pages they get it together and give each other a chance. Their disagreements did keep you on your seat and want to know if their next encounter would bring peace or heart ache but I guess in my opinion there was too much heart ache and not enough peace.Read moreRead less
