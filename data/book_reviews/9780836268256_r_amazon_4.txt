I had this book many years ago, before I was married. When I married, I brought my stuff with, of course. My wife knows I like comic strips. We then learned we were completely unable to have children, so I took all of the Zits & Baby Blues books I had acquired to a local book reseller so someone else could enjoy them. Sometime later, we started hosting foreign exchange students. All female, and all teenagers. I felt the Zits books calling me back, so I started getting the latest ones.
