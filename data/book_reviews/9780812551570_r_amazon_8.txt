I'm always amazed at the depth of characters in de Lint's books. I have been a fan for twenty years and just can't get enough of his writing. Trader is an interesting study of the question 'who are we?'  Are we how we appear to be or are we our thoughts and memories?  Fascinating study of how our thoughts and actions dictate our lives.
