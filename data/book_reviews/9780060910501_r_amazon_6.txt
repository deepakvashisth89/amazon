Unfortunately, I really don't find Ellen Bass a good writer when it comes to abuse. This may be personal opinion, but neither does many of my friends who are therapists! I think her book "The Courage To Heal" is a starting point for everyone who has ever been subjected to sexual abuse, but one which fades into the background as one learns more and more about the subject.  Bass just doesn't seem to handle her subject matter well. I like Susan Forward much, much better as well as several other authors, particularly on the subject of sexual abuse. Trauma and Recovery holds much more insight to the issue as well as The Right to Innocence.
