The Kama Sutra was never meant to be pornographic in nature. I believe that was a result of Western translation. To my understanding, the Kama Sutra is sort of a guide on how to conduct oneself in relationships among others (i.e., a lover, marriage, etc.) This book in particular is a great translation of the original Kama Sutra by Vatsyayana.
