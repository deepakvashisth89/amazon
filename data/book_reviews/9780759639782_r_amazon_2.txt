I found this book extremely entertaining.  Once I got into it, I couldn't put it down.  It reminded me a lot of one of my favorite books, Steve Perry's 'Aliens vs. Predator: Prey' in that it doesn't take long for the action to start and once it starts, it goes on for the rest of the book.  You get just enough personal information about the main characters to tell them apart and to care about them.  The author doesn't waste time recounting the characters' childhood traumas and personal motivations and such; the focus is on the story, and it's a pretty good one.  He also doesn't go on and on with technical detail.  He identifies a weapon, what its function is, and then it gets put into action, usually blowing something, or someone, to pieces.
