This book was the last of the Jordan Poteet series, which I embarked upon after reading Jeff Abbott's 2 Sam Capra novels and loving them.  What a delicious tale of screwed up family!  My only complaint was the ending, which left poor Jordy in a dismal place in his life.  I know these were written several years ago, but I would still love to see Mr. Abbott write at least one more story, even a kindle book, that wraps up Jordan's life on a happier note.Read moreRead less
