Ken Follett is an outstanding craftsman in the art of story telling. The story winds and weaves around the launch of the Explorer 1 rocket that would allow the US to compete with Russia’s newly successful space program.  The tale has former WW2 operatives and CIA agents, an amnesiac rocket scientist, and beautiful brilliant women connected in a serious situation dominated by the preparation to successfully launch America’s first orbiting satellite.Thanks Mr. Follet for a great tale with a fine plot, lots of action, and interesting characters.Read moreRead less
