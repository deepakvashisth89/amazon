I have given this book away twice because of my own recommendation of it. It is a very important book not just for the story it tells on Imperial Jade but because of it introduces us to the Empress Dowager as we have not experienced her before. One of the most important parts in this story is the visit of the English women (amongst others) to the Empress Dowager. Here we experience the very human side of the Empress Dowager and the hilarious juxtaposition of the English women in accessing this extraordinary woman and how it was that the Empress Dowager saw them. I believe that through books like this and others that we are beginning to see China and the depths of her ordinary and extraordinary people as a wealth shared by no other civilization. This may be a book on imperial jade but it is far, far more and far deeper than that which means this book is a classic.Read moreRead less
