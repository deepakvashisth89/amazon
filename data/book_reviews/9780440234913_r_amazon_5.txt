I am shocked that a book written so long ago has so few reviews. I am so happy that I took a chance on it when I saw it in BookBub because it was a great find. I am delighted that there are more books in the Joe Portugal series so I can renew friendships with him, Gina, Harold, and detectives Casillas and Burns. I am devastated, however, that the second book in the series costs 4 times as much as books 3 and 4. It must be a dandy.I learned more about succulents than I thought I ever would want to know, and now I feel like I want to go out and collect one of every species. The info on euphorbia is brilliantly used to provide new (to me) ways to murder. I only wish that the book's wonderful glossary, or at least Walpow's blog, had color photos of the author's greenhouse residents. Maybe such additions are what made book 2, Death of an Orchid Lover, such a pricey item. I hate to read a series out of order, but I may have to pass on book 2 and go on to book 3.
