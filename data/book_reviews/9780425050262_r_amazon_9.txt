This is book 1 of a five book series, the Riverworld series. First published in 1971, it is the most well known work of one of the great Sci-Fi writers. Luckily, the publishers have seen fit to keep these and many other classics in print to the benefit of all lovers of great classic Sci-Fi.The basic premise here is that everyone who has ever lived and died, from modern humans to pre-homo-sapiens humans who lived millions of years ago, have been mysteriously resurrected on an alien world, the surface of which comprises a single wide, deep river that flows from a lake on the north pole, all the way to the south pole and then back up again to empty back into the same lake from which it originated. It the center of the lake is a tall, dark tower, the supposed home of the unknown entities conducting this great experiment for purposes of their own.All are resurrected naked and bald, each with a ‘grail’, through which they are able to obtain food from magical dispensers set every kilometer along the river. It is up to them to survive and “find salvation”, but not all is utopia as some believe. Awakened with humankind is every prejudice, bias and fear of millions of years of evolution.This first book tells the story of the awakening, and follows Sir Richard Burton (no, not the actor, the famous explorer who discovered Lake Tanganyika) as he builds a bamboo catamaran and sets of up the river in search of the source, battling numerous enemies including the infamous Herman Goering, who is determined to repeat upon this new world the horrors he unleashed in his old life.TYSBG is a Sci-Fi adventure of the old school, but more than stacks up against the writings of today.
