I approached this book as someone learning French, with a native English background. I have read a lot of philosophy and social theory in the past, so I found the French here to be fairly easy to read. Most of the vocabulary is the kind of technical vocabulary that looks very similar in French and English.If you're looking for a feminist manifesto, this is probably the wrong book to approach. This book is something more like Rousseau's Discourse on Inequality, styled on the question of women. The book is extremely thoughtful and I look forward to reading the second volume. The early parts of the book that touch on biology and anthropology are especially exciting, and still feel like fresh insights. The only part of the book that I would advise skipping is the last section, which presents literary analysis of various fiction authors and their presentation of women.
