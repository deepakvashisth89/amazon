The Disaster had destroyed so much of the world. Volcanoes and earthquakes brought civilization crashing down into ruins and permanently changed the planet. The survivors blamed this on the machines. Now people live in  separate medieval communities, worship Spirits and despise technology.  Anyone caught with a machine of any kind is killed.In Winchester, young  Luke Perry is named Prince in Waiting by the Seers. Luke tells a tale of  treachury, murder, and intrigue. The Seers have chosen Luke for a special  mission...This is a good story about friendship, loyalty, suspicion,  pride and belief.
