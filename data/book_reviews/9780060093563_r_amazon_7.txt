This book leaves the feminist in me cold, hence the three stars. When I was ten years old, I started reading Sayers, Christie, Conan Doyle and hard-boiled American crime fiction such as Stout and Gardner. Stewart wasn't on my radar at all. So upon first reading at my advanced age I find I have no patience with the kind of woman portrayed here. I think the heroine is naive and downright stupid far too often for my taste. For fantasy, which this is, I prefer a smarter, more controlled protagonist whatever the sex. I realize that the book was written in the 50's and that tells it all. The prose is chock full of adjectives, highly descriptive of the scenery, the food ,etc. I found myself skimming some of those portions of the book. I do give her credit for exciting plot twists . I also deplore the portrait of the dominant male , Richard. The fantasy that women really want such a man is a fiction that has led to a great many problems in this world. It certainly leaves me cold. In real life, I have never tolerated any kind of threats,of violence. I am sorry to see the myth perpetuated here.Read moreRead less
