This is one of Greene's best-known novels, and justifiably so because it is a good, humorous read. It's about an Englishman living in Havan with his daughter, who is somewhat unwillingly enlisted as a spy. He doesn't know how to enlist other spies as agents, so he begins filing fictional reports which are believed by his superiors and enemy spies as well, putting his life on the line. The ending is one of the best I have ever read, incredibly ironic. Highly recommended!Read moreRead less
