The whole little book is good, as attested to by other reviewers - but this is my favourite part: Joe discovers his new friend cannot read or write. Joe asks the man's kids to"scratch their names in the soil with a stick. They shake their heads shyly. The little one, Miguelina, takes the stick and draws a circle on the ground, then looks up smiling, as if her name is zero."If that doesn't touch your heart, you need to check your batteries because you might not be a human being. The whole of this book is written in this style of simple yet symbolic beauty.The authors say their coffees are named after the saint, the Madonna of the "Altagracia." When I was learning Latin, we were told the world Altus means something like the absolute height of a mountain - the height pls the depth of the valley. How appropriate for this little work.
