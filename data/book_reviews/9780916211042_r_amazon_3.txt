This is a great introduction to the world of the RPG (D20 Role playing games). Palladium RPG games are based on the D20 system, you will need a set of dice to make use of the game rules. Like in most RPG games, the charecter creation will introduce you to charecter types, (O.C.C. and R.C.C. class related abilities and starting equipment). Includes classic illustration of historical equipment from mid-evil fantasy settingLike many palladium game systems the RIFTS conversion manual, can take these charecters into a different setting. The conversion rules from S.D.C. to M.D.C. are simple, and explain the concept of setting basic rules for the environment or world, (100 S.D.C.=1 M.D.C.). There are many usefull basic class types here, that also make use of magic, the most usefull are divining spells or environmental controll spells rather than M.D.C. spellcasting.
