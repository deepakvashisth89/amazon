Marcie MacLean is a temp in the company Temporarily Yours and she has been assigned to this company where 5 temps have already been fired. When she arrives to the company, she meets Jake Campbell, the boss of the company. And the man who had fired her 5 years ago under false pretenses. Together they work together, because Jake deparately needs help and Marcie desparately needs the money. And as they work together, Jake starts to appreciate Marcie's efficiency, character and something more. Then Jake wonders if he had made a mistake 5 years ago in firing her...TAMING THE BOSS was a really good read because they naturally hate each other but then they have to change their perspective of each other because they find out more about each other. Personally, I love these kind of books and TAMING THE BOSS was no exception. It was a really short read for me because I kept wanting to know what happened and how it ended and I promise to you fellow readers, you would really not be disappointed!^_^  ~  IzzyRead moreRead less
