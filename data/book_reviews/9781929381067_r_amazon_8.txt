I enjoyed this story quite a bit.  The author avoids the all too common political sci-fi tropes of "the politicians/civilians don't understand" etc.  The characters in Antares Dawn are neither too good nor too evil, and are quite believable.  The tech and science feels right without going into unnecessary detail.  There is a great sense of mystery and exploration.  Just a fun light read.My only negative would be that it was too short.
