Foster has a knack for creating nonhuman characters that stick with us.  Here, most of the nonhumans are displaced whales of various sorts.  It is entertaining to see how he imagines the different personalities exhibited by different whale species.  For him, the big Sperm Whales are the smartest (followed closely by the Orcas.)  The plankton grazers are more dull and more placid.The people were interesting too.  One of the characters is a large Polynesian man, large as in fat.  But he has enough charisma to attract a sophisticated lady scientist.  Foster does not shy from touching on the differences in cultures as applied to the concept of fidelity, which I think is brave of him.This might not be Foster's best work but it is diverting and clever.Read moreRead less
