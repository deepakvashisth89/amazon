As an author, I never leave reviews for living authors but since Capote is no longer around... I found this "book" (really an amalgam of anecdotes or a pastiche of short stories) completely unreadable. The jet-setting behavior and character portraits would be compelling, but for Capote's obsession with deviance and his propensity to dip into florid descriptions every few lines. I am, by no means, a prude, but this isn't Anais Nin or Henry Miller. Capote has beautiful word selection and his vocabulary is marshaled like poison darts, all hitting their targets, but it is lost entirely in what appears to be a bathhouse-level addiction to describing the male form.
