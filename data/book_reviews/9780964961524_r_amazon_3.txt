This book has been read and appreciated by many as it has passed around our family. I was so taken by it that I bought my own copy to share with others. My father, father-in-law, uncles, aunts and many others served at great sacrifice in WWII. Fortunately for us and generations to come Jack vander Geest was willing to share his unique story and Carol Ordermann did a wonderful job writing and putting it all together. Unfortunately on one side we are losing that great generation who sacrificed so much and on the other side we have newer generations that don't have a clue as to the sacrifices made on our and their behalf. Thanks be to God that we have this book. It is a story of sorrow and sadness but ultimate triumph. Thank you Jack for sharing it! You continue to bless our lives beyond the grave.Read moreRead less
