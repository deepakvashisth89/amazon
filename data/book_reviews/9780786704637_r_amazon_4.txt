No other way to describe By Reason of Insanity except to call it the masterpiece of horror that it is. The story is epic in scope of simple, unrelenting evil. What makes this novel so special is how well it is written. It's fiction that reads so well you think it's non-fiction. I can't recommend this novel enough, but be forewarned By Reason of Insanity is one of most gruesome novels ever written.
