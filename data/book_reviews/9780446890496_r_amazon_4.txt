Robert K. and Suzanne Massie were blessed with the birth of Bobbie, who soon demonstrated the symptoms of classic hemophilia.  Their journey not only led them through the archaic practices of mid-20th hospitals, but to wonderful medical people and discoveries.  It also gave them enormous empathy for the subjects of their award-winning Russian history, "Nicholas and Alexandra".
