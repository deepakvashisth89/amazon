I read this one as a child. A lot of people mention the parallels between this and Lord of the Flies, but I think they have slightly different themes. Lord of the Flies was about the thin veneer of civilization, especially thin among children. The Butterfly Revolution is about the naivete of youthful politics. The Butterfly Revolution is reminiscent of idealistic communist revolutions that turned into dictatorships. LOTF is about children descending into savagery.
