"Homicide Trinity (A Nero Wolfe Mystery Book 36)" is a 1962 compilation of three of Rex Stout's "Nero Wolfe" short stories:- "Eeny Meeny Murder Mo:"  This is a 1962 short story that's the best of the three.  As a matter of fact, the start of this story is one of the best I've ever read.  It's an entirely different genre, but it's right in there with the start of Jim Butcher's 11th Dresden novel ("Turn Coat (The Dresden Files, Book 11)").  I'm rating it at a Near Excellent 4-1/2 stars out of 5.- "Death of a Demon:"  This is a 1961 short story that's the weakest of the three. The story is well done.  But, Nero Wolfe has no evidence to back up his claim.  I'm rating it at an OK 3 stars out of 5.- "Counterfeit for Murder:"  This is a 1961 short story that's a bit better than the previous one in the book.  Again, it's a well done story and this time Wolfe has some evidence.  I'm rating it at a Pretty Good 3-1/2 stars out of 5.I'm not normally one for short stories, but I'm happy with these.  As usual, Rex Stout's writing is excellent and interesting.  The average of my individual ratings for the stories is 3-2/3 stars.  But, they're good enough that I'm able to round that up to a Very Good 4 stars out of 5 without qualm.Read moreRead less
