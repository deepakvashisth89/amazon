This book is my second-favorite Catherine Aird book (Henrietta Who? is my favorite), and I've waited patiently for it to become available on Kindle. Finally I got tired of waiting and ordered a print copy, which I had to read with a magnifier, but even that didn't dim the pleasure. As the title says, the solving of the puzzle become a compelling pursuit for the invalid protagonist, and the reader is hooked right along with him. Since the author is Catherine Aird, the writing is polished, crisp, and clear.
