Having one of the most known artistic anatomy teachers of recent history explaining how to decipher anatomy for you in more than a 100 masters paintings and sketches is invaluable. This is a book for artists who are serious about drawing the figure and understanding human anatomy.If you are familiar with his Drawing Lessons from the Great Masters book, this book follows the same style. Each chapter focuses on a specific area of the human anatomy, like the rib cage, knee and lower leg, etc. In addition to a final ninth chapter that includes anatomical reference plates for the entire body.As in his other book mentioned above, for each drawing, you get two pages facing each other, the first one is a map of a sort of the drawing, where he highlights key areas by assigning different letters and explains those areas. For example, one area he would explain that it is showing the shape of the false ribs that make up the thoracic arch. So not only that you learn to identify anatomical parts, but you also get great information on how to model those areas in certain light/shade and poses. More importantly, you also gain great knowledge on how the masters made decisions and followed their own style (for example, some would always exaggerate certain muscles, etc.).This is a great reference but not for beginners. I recommend using this book once getting familiar with the human anatomy and use this as a reference to gain more knowledge.
