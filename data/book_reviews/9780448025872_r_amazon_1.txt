I first received a copy of this book in 1969 and loved it.  The illustrations are old school; appear to be pen and ink with air brushed highlights. The illustrations are very accurate and the variety of marques of automobiles is actually somewhat mind boggling.  I used this book to learn how to draw automobiles when I was a kid by tracing the illustrations until I got to where I could draw them freehand.Recently I decided to relive my childhood and buy another copy as the original was long gone.  While the artwork is wonderful the construction of the book itself is less than stellar. My first copy fell apart after a couple of years and this copy is already showings signs of going the same route. The bindings are incredibly fragile.  Be aware that if you buy this book you have to treat it gingerly to preserve the bindings. Otherwise it is a wonderful book especially if you have a young man or woman who enjoys automobile historyRead moreRead less
