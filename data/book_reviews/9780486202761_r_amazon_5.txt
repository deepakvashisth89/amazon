This was one of the most important books in my awakening as a seeing, thinking person. It opened my eyes not only to the underlying concepts of art history, which the book is purportedly about, but also to the central tensions in all avenues of culture that produce change. Brilliant. I didn't agree with the above reviewer that it was a hard read, though -- I can't get through any dense academic texts, and I tore through this like it was a thriller.
