This book is not the typical how to.  It is a rare archive of some of the wildest, super-70's, california wood furniture movement.  And while it shows how these things were made, it is a bit more "scholarly".  Truly amazing and any wood worker/designer/sculptor  interested in a wild - funky tangent from  the legacy of Esherick or Nakashima or  Maloof should have this in their reference.
