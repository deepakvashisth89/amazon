Two children and their dog set out to find THE perfect present for their mother. As we all know, finding the perfect present for a friend or  relative can  be quite an ordeal.  The children visit many stores and find  several lovely items that aren't quite what they want. Still, they search  onward and we find out that the children have, in fact, found the perfect  present for their mama.This book's illustrations are fabulous.  They  are cheerful and soft looking.  The children have such simple, yet sweet  expressions.  And, one of my favorite parts of this book is the spoof of  George Seraut's "Sunday Afternoon on the Island of La Grande  Jatte."What a charming and delightful read!
