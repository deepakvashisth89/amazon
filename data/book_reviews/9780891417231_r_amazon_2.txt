Good book about some of the least known aspects of WWII that seem to get glossed over by the much larger events of the war.  Easy reading.  If you are a hard core WWII historian, don't know if you'd like it or not, but hey, for the couple of bucks that it costs, you can always toss it, donate it, or put on your bookshelf to collect dust.
