I'm a little over half way through this book and it is one of the best I've read.  The writing style is reminiscent of toni Morrison.  Thon captures the reality of life in a way that very few authors can achieve.  Reading this book is like digging into the depths of the soul and having your most buried and raw feelings reflected to you through the story.  The emotions of life are described in a way that is astounding to me how perfectly she is able to capture the true nature of humanity.
