Even though it's over 600 pages, it's a brisk read. It's a good look at the chronology of the band, covering all the big moments, but at that length, I thought I'd come away with a little more than a "then they did this, and then this happened, and then this..." style of biography, which becomes more prevalent as the book goes along.
