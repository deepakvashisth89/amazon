This is an interesting collection of travel writing by women for women who enjoy traveling or simply dream about traveling.  More than 50 essays by women travelers from Lady Mary Wortley Montagu (1689-1762, from Embassy to Constantinople)to Leila Philip (1962- ,from The Road Through Miyama). The essays spread across time and across the world -- Eleanor Clark in the Sahara, Mary McCarthy in Florence, Isak Dinesen in Africa, Edith Wharton in Morocco...magic, strange, sad, romantic, frightening, rewarding, reflective.
