This book really helped my husband and I with our Lamaze breathing practice. We did all the exercises and practiced the breathing and I felt confident going through labor. This book does have a few old school pointers (like bringing a bottle of champagne and asking the nurses to put it on ice for after haha) but the straightforward information about what is happening throughout was really helpful for us. It gave my husband an important role in the birth process and also gave him confidence to know what to say during to motivate me. I ended up not needing an epidural and I think a lot of it was because of the practice and info we got from this book.Only giving 4 stars because of the few alcohol recommendations that were scattered in there.
