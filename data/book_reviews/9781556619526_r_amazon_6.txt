I have read other books by Janette Oke which I found quite enjoyable and worth my time, AND they were on an adult reading level. This was not one of them. If I had known it was geared from beginning to end toward young teenagers I wouldn't have bothered at all. I won't be interested in the next 3 books in the series that are necessary to complete the story. Only in rare circumstances will I continue to buy more books to finish the tale, mainly because I resent having to do so. That said, if the book had been described as one for younger readers, I wouldn't be writing this somewhat negative review since I wouldn't have read the book to start with. My 3 star rating is only for a potential teenage reader. For me, it would rate less.  I found the character of Virginia somewhat weak and too easily influenced by an undesirable friend,  continually making the wrong choices due to peer pressure. Perhaps other young teenagers would benefit from reading of her mistakes and their consequences, but it became repetitious and boring for me as an adult. One thread of the story was finally resolved in this book, but others are left hanging. I shouldn't have stuck with it, only to become ultimately frustrated because of having done so.Read moreRead less
