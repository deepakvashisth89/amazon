This Patricia Wentworth book is not a typical cozy mystery. It is more of a thriller. It's still a very good puzzle and if you pick up on the clues you can build a good explanation before it's revealed. The characters are belivable,  and some are very likable getting the reader to root for them.  I guess some will say they are old fashioned which doesnt bode well for us that we consider being honorable as being old fashioned. If you want gore and violence this isn't for you. If you want an intricate adventure, humorous at times, with a satisfying ending, this is a good choice. It takes a bit to get into it, but once I did,  I couldn't put it down.Read moreRead less
