After reading this book I am left with the question of what this story had to do with the Titanic.  The reality is this is more of a coming of age story about a wealthy young boy than it is a book about the Titanic.  The story could take place in Iowa for all the setting had to do with the disaster of 1912.Furthermore, the main character, Sumner Jordan, is quite unbelievable as a 12-year-old boy.  In sections of the book Sumner's thoughts and emotions seem more like that of a 20 something; other times he seems more like a teenager, and once in a while a 12 year old emerges.The only character of any interest in the book is Pierce Andrews who unfortunately occupies a relatively brief part of the book.  Overall the book is long to get started (do we really need all the family background?), slow to develop, and predictably ended.Read moreRead less
