This book was very romantic, and I found the love story very believable. I did believe absolutely that Malvina and Red Wing fell in love at first sight, I thought Red Wing was a bit judgmental and quick to jump to conclusions regarding Malvina's character. I know the circumstances at first would understandably lead Red Wing to believe Malvina a criminal, he still was to quick to believe the worse without any real evidence. He misjudged her several times throughout the book, the last time he even forced her to leave, although he almost immediately regretted it. Malvina didn't help matters when she stubbornly refused to tell him the truth about her quest for revenge, but I felt Red Wing ought to have believed in her more if he loved her so much.
