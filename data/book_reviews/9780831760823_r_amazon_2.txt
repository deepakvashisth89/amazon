I thought this was a pretty good book on the subject. It discusses many aspects of warship design, with the trade-offs that have to be made from among offensive weaponry, defensive protection, radar and sonar sensors, speed and  propulsion machinery, and range. There is a very good discussion on the development of steam and gas turbines and Diesel engines since 1945. The development of anti-submarine, anti-aircraft, and anti-ship missiles is thoroughly discussed. There is also much discussion on the refinement of hull design for sea keeping.I thought an especially fascinating feature was the discussion on Soviet navy developments on these same subjects. The discussions has to be a bit speculative, since the book was published in 1979 and not a whole lot was known for certain about the subjects.The only criticism I have is that the book is definitely dated in its treatment of particular ships and ship classes.
