Must surely have been written for magazines. I enjoyed the first story most, a page turner.I had to keep reading to see if the girl would be alright, lol. Sometimes in Margery Allingham stories people aren't. The second story was odd, I thought. "Zoff" is in many ways the main character but I found myself utterly incapable of finding any way to like her. In spite of the fact that everyone in the story describes how amazing she is, she is not. And too, you have to wade through endless 'feelings'. How this person feels, how the next person feels, etc. Big feelings, important feelings, deep feelings. It's just a mystery story. Spare me the drama of the feelings!
