I agree with another reviewer that a good editor would have reined in the characters and the wobbling plot. At times the storyline was realistic and then it would ask your permission to believe farfetched occurrences, such as a full fledge photo shoot with personnel out the wazoo was put together overnight. Yes, we're reading fiction but not fairytales.  The unit of people, including Tracy, seemed to function more like a business and not a family that has a member that's been kidnapped.  KIDNAPPED mind you. . . all I can say is poor Marisa. This book is good for any student, writer, author, to read as an exercise on how to edit and/or restructure.
