When will I get it through my head to believe critiques and reviews that ring true?!? What I mean is that some people write reviews just to get laughs or to flaunt their writing skills.  But you can tell the difference between those, and fellow bookworms who are just trying to save you time, money, and emotional stress. When you read reviews that tell you that Alton Gansky has written some fantastic books that capture and keep your attention, but this book is not one of them; believe them. When they tell you that he is a skillful wordsmith who can create a vivid scene in your mind, but that the scene plays out way beyond a person’s tolerance for turmoil and the stupidity of one of the characters; believe them! It was not an expensive book, but I stubbornly did not want to waste the money by not finishing the book. Now, I think the amount of time I spent sitting in frustration waiting for resolution was not worth it. And when it finally came after wading through the minutiae for 97% of the book (NOT hyperbole!), it was too conveniently wrapped up. I felt like a chump, knowing my time and emotions meant nothing to this author! Ok, that’s a bit dramatic, but the truth is, in my opinion, a good novel will draw you into its circle of friends, take you on the adventure with them, and make you almost feel bereft when you have to say goodbye to the characters at the end. This book, unfortunately, causes one to breathe a sigh of relief and pray that there isn’t a sequel because I really AM a chump and I tend to think, “Well, maybe Gansky was just having an off season when he penned the book and the next one is sure to be better…”If any of my rambling makes sense, then please believe me and pick any one of the other very good books this author has written and skip Dark Moon.Read moreRead less
