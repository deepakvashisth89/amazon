This is an absolutely a delightful book to read and is part of the series that takes place in England right after WWI has ended. The writing is rich, colorful, and masterly with a plot that keeps you engrossed and keeps you guessing until the very end. Ian Rutledge is a Scotland Yard Inspector just returned from the war in France and dealing with his own personal demons besides treading lightly among a war weary population that has seen its social, class and political structure severely damaged but still in place.It is the England that was and will never be again. With small villages where everyone knows everyone, where there is a constable in every town and village, where English social mores and attitudes are still prewar and where cars are hand cranked to start and tea is served to every guest. In this novel the personal impact of the war on individuals and society is well handled and part of the plot. Charles Todd ranks in the very top tier of writer for this genre.
