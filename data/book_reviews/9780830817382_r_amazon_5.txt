This book was full of useful information about how to treat your children during your divorce to have as little impact as possible on their adult life. I think it should be a must read for all couples with children that are contemplating divorce as a final resolution. I actually used this information in my divorce. Too bad my wife is too ignorant to do the same. I first was recommended this book by a substance abuse counselor who had also been through a tough divorce . I wrote a college paper on this topic using this book and web sites dedicated to the subject. You too will find this information very useful if you are serious about protecting your children during your divorce. Your words, attitude and actions today could have effects on your children that they carry onto their adult life. I would recommend this read if you want to learn something about yourself and make things as easy as possible on you and your children during this horrible time. Every divorce is different.
