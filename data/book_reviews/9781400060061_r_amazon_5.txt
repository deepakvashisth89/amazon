I read "Queen of the Big Time" for two reasons. One because it was a selection from my book club. Two because I really wanted to learn something about my Italian heritage.  I think that Trigiani hit it right on.  I could not relate this to my own life style but it was interesting learning about another; especially the farm life.At first I felt it was a story...just a story about life.  And life happens.  So many of us have dreams...dreams that we just can not reach. But now I look back and admire an ambitious young girl that made some impressive accomplishments in her time.  But later in life looked back and finds that she missed out on so much.  I also enjoyed the few twists and turns and surprises that she adds to the story.I rated this book because I learned a lot about Italian immigrants and I like the message in the story. It is refreshing to read about the love of family.  I also learned a lot about how my own parents must have felt leaving their homeland and starting a new life in a strange land.Read moreRead less
