This book is an easy but good read on Country Rock. It covers the rise of Country Rock from 1963-1973.  Everything from The Byrds, Buffalo Springfield, Linda Ronstadt, and The Eagles are checked and discussed in this book. It outlines the connections between the singers, band members, and the bands to provide some context to how Country Rock got its start and helped shape Americana music. The only complaint is it would be nice to have more photos in the book and it might have been more natural to follow the music genre into the later 1970's when Linda Ronstadt, The Eagles, and bands like Poco and the Pure Prairie League were selling tons of records.Read moreRead less
