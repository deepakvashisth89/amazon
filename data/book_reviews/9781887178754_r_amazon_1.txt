I found this novel to be told in a cold and distant fashion, and I felt the main character to be a very sad, sorry woman.  She never came warm and real for me, and  there were only two times that I found the book other than chilling. That was on page 2 where I laughed at the part where the pregnancy lasted longer than the marriage -- and later, the heat of her husband calling her to task before he left her.  The seduction of her son was a given from her first admiration of the beauty of the boy -- so I was not shocked when it came, but was delighted with his note responding to her attempt.  Anyway, I put the book in the Salvation Army bin after I read it, and am so I'm sorry that I can't be more precise in my recollection.
