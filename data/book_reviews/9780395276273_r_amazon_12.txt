The Thrush Green books are delightful descriptions of all the local characters who live around a green called Thrush Green in the Cotswolds in England. "Miss Read" writes with sensitivity, humour and an understanding of the every day lives and dilemmas of people on different levels of society and with different personalities.  The books are so interesting I read them over and over, like visiting old friends in a place where I once lived.They are based on her own experience living in a town during WWII near the RAF base where her husband was stationed.Read moreRead less
