Most human beings do not realize that we are not just physical beings but spiritual beings as well. Nor do many realize that this thing we call Life is actually God's University. God is the professor, the Angels and Archangels are not just God's messengers but foremost God's graduate assistants and we, All human beings are God's students. We are in this part of Heaven to better understand the part of Heaven so many Christians pray to one day go to, to learn what we can not learn and experience, to enhance our spiritual being. Dr. Doreen Virtue in one of the clearest and simplest ways teaches us some very basic and much needed knowledge and understanding of those Beings, that our Creator God created a little lower than God and a little higher than humankind.
