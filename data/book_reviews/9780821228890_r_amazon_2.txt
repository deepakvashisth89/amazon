June 6, 1944 was my 10th Birthday.  I awoke to the (radio) news of the invasion.  I had three uncles, as I later came to know, who were in either the first, second or third waves.  Like millions of other Americans I followed the war in Europe in the weekly and semi-weekly (black and white) newsreels of that era  in the movie theaters.  As time passed we all came to know more and more through friends, relatives and histories.VOICES OF VALOR IS AN INCREDIBLE summation of that seminal period. Especially so for me since the June 6, 1944 invasion was my  10th birthday. I awoke to the news on the radio, later learning that  three uncles were in the forst three waves on D-Say.
