(OK, so I will have to admit that I thought that was a little juvinille for a plot, but at the same time I suppose the world could produce more strange real life plots and therefore not completely unbelievable)....  What really caught me in the book was the main charactor being a Witch who is on the hunt to figure some strange things out..I really really enjoyed this woman!!!!
