This book is filled with beauty and wisdom. Franck is an authentic guide on the spiritual path and shows us how drawing can lead us to the Truth. I find this to be an unique perspective. In his beautifully illustrated book, he boldly confronts much of the art world as driven by egotism. This humble man has brought meaning and renewed enthusiasm to my art.
