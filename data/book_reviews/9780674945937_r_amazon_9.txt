Vivian Paley has a wonderful way of writing about young children and it is a delight to follow her thinking. I've read a couple of her books and I think this is the best of them. I don't agree with everything she has to say but I'd recommend anyone interested in developing their thinking about children as learners to read and enjoy. One thing, I'd recommend you buy a paper copy, the text in my kindle copy does not scale consistently. This means that if you, like me, tend to increase the font size of the text to make reading easier, you''ll find that some part of the text refuse to scale up.
