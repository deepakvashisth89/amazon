I was pleasantly surprised by this new Star Trek coffee table book which features a wide variety of art.  The book is a celebration of the Federation after the Dominion War.  It takes us in painting and in  Friedmans excellent text to 18 stops from Starbase 11 to the Q-Continuum.  There are short biographical sketches of each of the artists at the end of  the book. I recommend this book to all fans of Star Trek.  It left me  wanting more.Read moreRead less
