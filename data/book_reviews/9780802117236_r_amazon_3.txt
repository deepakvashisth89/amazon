Anderson is a great writer and I enjoyed his biography of Che.  This book is a series of articles Anderson wrote for the New Yorker magazine.  It is obvious that Anderson placed himself at some risk in getting these stories and I enjoyed his viewpoint on post Taliban Afghanistan.  The only good insightful material was about the assasination of Massoud and how it was done.  Little of this has filtered out to the West.  Much of the rest of the book was a rehash of what other journalists have found out about Afghanistan.This is a short enjoyable read by a great writer.
