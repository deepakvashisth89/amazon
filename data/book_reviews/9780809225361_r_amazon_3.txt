Lots of good ideas and tips for making changes in your life -- good for people who can make decisions when presented with lots of information. More difficult for people who "don't know where to start". Miedaner does state a person should start by clearing out all their clutter to free up time and energy for the real work. But the behaviors that created the clutter and time wasters can keep a person from getting past step one. I would recommend using the book more like a menu - pick the easiest quickest thing that might make a difference in your life and start there.
