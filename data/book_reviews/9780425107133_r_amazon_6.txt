Gentle artist & gardener, Miss Seeton has been noticed. She's been very useful - without realizing it - to the local police. Now, not one, but two, nefarious groups are operating near her village  - a new age religion & an actual witches coven. The police want her to help - but this gets dangerous, pretty fast.
