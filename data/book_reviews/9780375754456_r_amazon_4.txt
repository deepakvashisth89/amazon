This book is one of the finest history books I've ever read. If you're interested in U.S. history, race relations, civil rights, etc., this book MUST be part of your library. Yes, the subject matter -- and some of the details of lynchings -- are disturbing to say the least...but it's an important part of our not too distant past. The author has done an impeccable job of researching and compiling this reference book on lynching. Difficult to digest but difficult to put down as well. Of all the books I've read on U.S. history (and I have a degree in history), this might be the most memorable. You don't need to be a history buff to read this, however.Read moreRead less
