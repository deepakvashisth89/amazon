The principles in this book are true and timeless, and the exercises are incredibly useful! This book is great for people who want to become more creative in their work -- whether you're an artist, an entrepreneur, or even if you just want your life to feel meaningful.The only reason I give it 4 stars instead of 5 is that as much as I love history and was curious about da Vinci's life, I think that part of the book longer than it needed to be.Also, the author seemed to have an anti-faith agenda... or maybe that's just the way I read it. At one point it felt like he was saying "the renaissance and progress in general can't happen with religion in the way."So as a person of faith, and yes, partially biased in that aspect, I felt like the agenda pushing was unnecessary.
