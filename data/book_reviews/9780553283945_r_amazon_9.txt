Rosie Thomas has always been one of my favorite writers and this novel again is a hard to put down book.Luckily it is quite long and so particularly satisfying to put it down knowing there are still chapters to get lost in. However,I have a big  criticism: I don't know if it's the publisher or Kindle that should give whoever edited this book a loud talking to. Many, many spelling errors of the sort not picked up by spellcheck (nut for not, build for bridge etc.), the odd strange grammar and incomprehensible sentence. All these things irritate me  a lot as it throws me hard back to reality and takes away completely  the spell of the story.
