I read this series of books years ago and I really liked the author's distopian future universe.  The books are good page-turners and have lots of fun science fiction and fantasy elements.  The first book is the best in my opinion due to the introduction of all the concepts.  Parts of the series can get a little repetitive, but are still entertaining.  I recommend especially the first book and I think if you like it you'll like the others.Read moreRead less
