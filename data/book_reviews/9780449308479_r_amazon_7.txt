Once again Achebe takes us into the world of the developing Nigeria. His customary fine style and grace bear us swiftly and easily into the world of the grandson of the great Okonkwo we met in " Things Fall Apart". Now we see Obi Okonkwo, pride of his village, newly returned from his studies in England and taking up his place in the upper levels of the public service. But the dangers of this society in transition will not stay away from his door. How he confronts these issues in his personal life and in his working life.bring the climax to his story,which may lack the great drama of his grandfather's final days.Nevertheless it is highly revealing of the complex web of expectations at work in the emerging Nigeria.
