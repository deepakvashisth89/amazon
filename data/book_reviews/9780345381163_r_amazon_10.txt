This going to be a very short review as the only real way to access this book is to read it. Now if you have ever wondered why Catholics pray using a Rosary, or make the sign of the cross, or wonder why nuns and priests dress the way they do, or what Holy water is all about, or what the inside of a Catholic church looks like, or why Catholics pray to the Saints or a host of other 'why's'? then this is the book to read. It can be a bit wordy and uses words some non catholics wouldn't recognise but by and large this really does throw some light on the Catholic faith and some of its mysteries. If you have any interest in the Catholic faith or religion in general, or even if you just want to broaden your knowledge then this book is really worth a read.
