This book is dense with information; outstanding if you are searching for the "why" behind Dr. Sear's diet, but tedious if you are looking for a quick way to apply the Zone methodology and move forward.I would have been happier to have an explanation of the diet provided in the beginning, a few chapters dealing with how to apply the diet, and save the supporting science for the later chapters.  I purchased the book, so I don't need to be as thoroughly convinced (through scientific and anecdotal studies) of the efficacy of the diet.While I appreciate the distinguished career that Dr. Sear's has led, I was bored at the multiple vignettes he gave describing how he led himself to believe that food, and not pharmaceuticals were the key to health.Great book, but about 150 pages too long for me.Read moreRead less
