"Why are you reading about this?" was the question many asked me after I told them I picked this book up. Being a woman of color, I was concerned that I wouldn't be able to get through this book without my emotions getting the better of me, but I was pleasantly surprised by how deep I had fallen into this book, and how I was able to go to work the next morning.This book reads like much of the sensational stories of today, unattached, and yet sympathetic to the cause. It gives you the facts, gives some analysis on the climate of the times, but overall reads better and more clear than any history book I've ever had from elementary through college.Read moreRead less
