This is one of Pike's darker and more supernatural thrillers, and is an exciting quick read. Out of all of the Christopher Pike books I have ever read (which is most of em!) this one is still my absolute favourite. The story is compelling, the characters are realistic, and the pace of the story is fast enough to keep you from putting the book down without feeling rushed in the slightest.Despite being written in the 80's, the dialogue is surprisingly not dated and the story has aged very well. I especially like that this story was told from the point of view by not one, but two characters, siblings Cindy and Alex, as they deal with the gruesome events that unfold around them. One of his absolute best works, in my opinion. It's not supposed to be taken seriously and negative reviewers need a reminder that the books are technically written for children, and us adults reading them are satisfying our nostalgic guilty pleasures.
