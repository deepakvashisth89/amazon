I've read and enjoyed most of Mr. Pearson's books. This one is the exception. I made it to page 35 before giving up. If you are willing to wade through the awkward and ponderous dialog, there are some very funny bits sprinkled amongst the verbiage and constant repetition of each of the characters full name. Too bad this couldn't have been edited down to a third of its 382 pages, because Mr. Pearson is a master storyteller. Two stars instead of one out of respect for his talents.Read moreRead less
