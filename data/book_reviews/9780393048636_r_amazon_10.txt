This book is amazingly well-written and very gripping to read. What people endured under the Khmer Rouge is unbelievable but, sadly, true. This book chronicles the childhood of a girl growing up under oppression, forced labor camps and starvation, watching her loved ones die all around her. The miracle is she survived to write the book. A gripping read for people who care to know the truth of what goes on in the world. I feel if the narrator could live it, I can bear witness and honor her journey by reading it.Read moreRead less
