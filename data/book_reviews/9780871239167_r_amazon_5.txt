I am an avid reader and this is the only book, besides my Bible, that I have read more than once.  I usually read it every two or three years.  I grew up spending every-other weekend in the Ozarks and a lot of time in Branson, so I have always been smitten with the setting of this book.  The setting, though, is only the beginning of the beauty to be found within the pages of this book.  Harold Bell Wright did an amazing job of telling a beautiful love story, teaching important life lessons and providing a good laugh along the way.  I have tried for years to get my (now teenage) children to read this and they haven't yet, but we just returned from Branson and I took the kids (three teen boys and two teen girls) to see the drama and they all loved it.
