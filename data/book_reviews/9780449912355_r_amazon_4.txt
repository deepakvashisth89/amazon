Having worked in the field of child protection for fifteen years I was wary about reading this book as other accounts of this nature tend to be full of sickly sweet tales of damaged children who miraculously become healed from years of abuse and neglect through the administrations of one caring worker, or usually some self promoting foster carer. I was pleased that Marc Parent provided factual observations outlining the difficulties which workers face on a daily basis in trying to recognise and respond to the most adverse conditions and trying to protect the most vulnerable members of our society.  It was well written and considered and I've recommend it to students who are considering working in this fieldRead moreRead less
