I am a total novice at Yoga, but have been active all my life in various sports.  My problem is that I am not flexible at all and have had back problems and issues with my hamstrings.  Yoga has really helped me with this.  I have strengthened my back musles and gained flexibility.  Where this product comes in is that it can be an ever changing workout.  I have never taken a yoga class but have several instructional dvd's that I do occasionally.  For some reason I like these cards a lot better than my dvd's and I do them much more often than the dvd's.  The little booklet that comes with the deck has a few suggestions for workouts and I use the back one the most.  I love being able to change it up and try new things and do things in different order.  You can't do this with a dvd and they seem to get monotonous after awhile in my opinion.  These cards are durable and I place them right next to my mat while doing the yoga.  I enjoy them immensely and feel that without them I would just have my yoga mat sitting in the closet.  Your workout can be as long or as short as your time allows and that is a great part about it.  The only thing I might like to have changed is that one side of the cards is so dark that it would be a little bit easier to read if the background wasn't so dark.
