Phoebe Atwood Taylor's THE CAPE COD MYSTERY was published in 1933, but many of the descriptions and attitudes are as fresh as if the stories were written today.Miss Prudence Whitsby brings her niece, Betsy and two friends to a beach cottage to escape the heat of a Boston summer. She subleases a teeny shelter to the well known author, Dale Sanborn. Later the same night Miss Prud discovers the body of Sanborn wrapped in a blanket. A blow to the back of the head is the cause of death.The first thing the local constabulary do is haul Bill Porter off to the pillory as all evidence points to him. Enter Asey Mayo, who works for the Porter family intent on finding a killer and proving Bill's innocence.An excellent beginning to a fun series.
