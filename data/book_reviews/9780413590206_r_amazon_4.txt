Jeannete Winterson is a genius when it comes to crafting a story. Her extremely wicked satire at religion and its mass market culture are evident in this book. Having read all of her current works, Boating For Beginners  is a treat. Its a mesching together of the worst of our modern world and  the typical story of Genesis. I cannot understand why more people do not  know about this book, and why Winterson does not mention it. For it is  indeed a treat to read.
