Chinese Handcuffs is overall a good book. I was assigned the book in my English class and was honestly not looking forward to reading the book but after getting into the middle of the book it caught my attention and I had to keep reading forth to know what happens. Some of the things in the book are unexpected and really build the suspense. The ending of the book was really disappointing to me it didn't really finish the way you would like a book to end. If you are sensitive on certain topics like suicide and molestation then you should not read the book but I would recommend the book maybe to put as a book you read when there is nothing to do that day.Read moreRead less
