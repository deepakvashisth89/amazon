This author writes beautiful descriptions -- over and over.  There are many and while each one is fine, they just go on and on.  So much that I found the plot, the drama, the tension weakened.  Finally, there is no real conclusion to my way of reading.  It is much like a long, very long, "slice of life" novel.  "This is the way it was" is the theme.  I grew tired of plowing ahead, was glad to be finished, but disappointed in the resolutions of the conflicts, such as they were.
