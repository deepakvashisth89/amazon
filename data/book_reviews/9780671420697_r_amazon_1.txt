I first read this book about 30 years ago (or so), immediately went out and bought the ingredients for her 'magic milkshake' and started to make it and drink it on a regular basis.  It's true what she said about starting out slowly with the Brewer's Yeast.  You do have to start out slowly with an 8th of a teaspoon and gradually build up to two or three tablespoons.  If you don't you will experience painful bloating and gas.  That's the nature of yeast.  I wanted instant 'feeling better' results but as with anything it does take time to build up.  I got away from doing the work it takes to make sure you have the ingredients and actually make and drink the shake and have felt so run-down that I decided to try it again.
