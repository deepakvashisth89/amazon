Betrayal is around every corner in the city of Florence. George Eliot highlights this treachery through her fictional characters and surrounds them with the real conspirators in the 15th century Italy. This is a romantic historical fiction that effectively describes the time and place. I was pulled into the drama and felt real sorrow for the characters.
