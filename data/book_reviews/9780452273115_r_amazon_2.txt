A beautifully written though somewhat uncentered look at the breakdown of an American family - an astronaut, his wife and their gay son - through the turbulence and the disillusionment of the 1970's and 80's. Russell's insights into his characters verge on the poetic, but though it leaves the reader with a feeling something like redemption, in the end it feels a bit unearned. The center does not hold.Still, all of Russell's books are worth reading, for the vividness of his prose, the emotional transparency of his characters, and the affection of the author for their - and our - brokenness and thwarted attempts at love.Read moreRead less
