What's with the much-too-many lists, each a paragraph or two long, of the details of the weapons, explosives, night vision binos, etc. etc. that the guys in the book use?  Was Christie paid by the page???  Of course, they are easy to skip, but for me they took me out of whatever suspension of belief that the banal plot may have given me and brought me back to "I'm reading an excessively silly silly book" which isn't what I'm after when I read silly books.As for the plot, ideological, true-believer, brave, racist and murdering Iranians attempt mission impossible and meet blundering American military and intelligence resistance.  The only smart and non-bureaucratic man among the latter is their only real opponent.
