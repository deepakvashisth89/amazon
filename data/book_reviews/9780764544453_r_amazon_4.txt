This is a good book and I enjoyed it.  However, the author is incorrect in some of her comments.  I have owned greyhounds, whippets, and Italian Greyhounds throughout my life and I'm old.  The author is correct in most of her comments about greyhounds but very wrong in what she wrote about Italian Greyhounds.  She stated they were barky dogs which is totally untrue.  In fact, most owners of Italian Greyhounds choose the breed because they rarely, if ever bark.  However, if they are kept with another dog who does bark a lot they can pick up the habit.  None of my I.G.'s ever barked except for one who thought he was a guard dog and barked every time a person rang the doorbell. But he was not a typical I.G.  Another thing the author said was that Italian Greyhounds are known for their shaking.  AS with the greyhounds I've owned the shaking only occurred when the I.G.'s and greyhounds were scared i.e. waiting to see the vet.  Greyhounds are also known to have running noses.  Not all of them but quite a few and that trait is worse when they are scared as well.  Aside from those mistakes I thought it was a good book.
