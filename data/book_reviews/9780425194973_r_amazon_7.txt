In print for almost sixty years as of 2019, this is a very nice read. The hero, as Dick Francis' heroes often do, finds true love and wins a few races. He doesn't win the race the book closes with, but he gains a moral victory of no small size in it.Along the way he busts up a vicious criminal enterprise, and this, of course, is the meat of the book.In this, Dick Francis' first novel, he established the basic parameters from which he did not vary for the entire length of his writing career, producing roughly forty novels. That all of them are still in print (some, like this one, only metaphorically so) tells its own tale.So if you can stand to read a novel written BCP - before cell phones - go ahead and buy this one.
