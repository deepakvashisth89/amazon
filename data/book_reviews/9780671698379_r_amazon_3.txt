This is the first David Drake books that I ever read, and it was absolutely devastating, turning me into a devoted fan.  Drake gives an absolutely heartbreaking look at war by showing a futuristic conflict through the eyes of a young journalist who has been dragooned into the elite mercenary unit Hammer's Slammers, letting the audience learn as he does.  The book is filled with intense action and compelling characters, and creates one of the most moving works of fiction I have read in some time.
