There is something to be said for short stories and anthologies.Sometimes a spooky idea is just that, spooky. It doesn't need to be stretched out, ala King style.Or a smart SCIFI theme that is original and quick to the point doesn't always need to be a miniseries.But the Wild Cards takes this another step. Set against the events from our own history- a very snappy plot, spread across the years and told each decade by a different author.Some chapters were a little hollow, most were top notch.Overall, I really enjoyed this great SCIFI / Fantasy work.Read moreRead less
