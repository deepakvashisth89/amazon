In this second book we find Tarod 'prisoner' in a castle with the time stopped, he himself provoked this stasis. His soul is trapped in a jewel and he waits...Until Cyllan, the drover girl he had met in the first book is caught by a warp and ends in the shores of the castle. Tarod and Cyllan get to know each other, but mainly they discover Tarod, his true nature and what a startling nature that is!  Cyllan becomes very loyal to Tarod, and eventually the stasis is broken and they are again loose in the world...This book is more fast paced than the previous and the relations between the characters are very interesting.
