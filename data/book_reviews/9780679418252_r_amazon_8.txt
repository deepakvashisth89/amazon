Posner goes into great detail in an attempt to show that Oswald was the lone assassin and he provides plenty of facts that show the fallacy of the conspiracy theories - and facts can be stubborn things.  If you are looking for details about what happened, as I was, then this will be a good read for you. He includes information about the Tippit murder, what happened at the theater where Oswald was arrested, details of the goings on at the Dallas jail, Oswald's and Ruby's movements, etc.  I read this immediately after reading The Kennedy Detail.  I had previously read Plausible Denial and Rush to Judgment, both by Mark Lane, and Crossfire by Jim Marrs. Before reading this book I leaned toward the belief that somebody shot Kennedy from the 6th floor window, I just wasn't convinced it was Oswald.  I am now leaning more toward the fact it was Oswald.
