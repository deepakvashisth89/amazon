I am an Allbeury fan and this does not disappoint. An enthralling tale that has certain familiar story lines  from earlier works, young man introduced to communism by benign elders being one , but T.A's innate story telling skill and the breadth of the narrative had me glued to my Kindle after a couple of chapters.
