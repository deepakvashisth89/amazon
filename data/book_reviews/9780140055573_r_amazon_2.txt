The setting for this mystery is the world of pedestrian races in Victorian England, a thing I had no idea even existed before reading the book, which appears to be quite carefully researched. It reads easily and smoothly and the characters are well drawn. As a glimpse into a past world it holds the reader's interest well even though the mystery itself is not as fascinating as one would hope.
