This is a tragic story of adolescents and their seemingly lonely world. It gives an insight into a world of casual sex, drugs, and suicide. I remember my teenage years and can agree with the loneliness, but this seems to take all of the negative elements and throw them together.  As an older person, I can understand that teenagers are struggling with self identity and self discovery, and if you can make it through those dangerous years you are a true survivor.This book makes for thought provoking reading and if you have teenagers, it will reset your mindset on teenagers and may even help you understand the fight they are going through.Read moreRead less
