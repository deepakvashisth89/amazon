I read this and several others of the Myth series books as a teen and thoroughly enjoyed them. I'd read Tolkien, Lewis, Brooks and similar fantasy authors preceding this series and was used to more of the serious, dramatic take on fantasy. When I started reading these Myth books, my eyes were opened to a humorous, light-hearted approach to fantasy and I was completely hooked. Don't get me wrong, I like the serious fantasy books as well but it was a nice change of pace to have comedy and lighter adventure in a genre I already loved.It's been years since I first read these but I just started re-reading this first of the Myth books and still find it a delightful adventure!Read moreRead less
