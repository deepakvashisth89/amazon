Mr. Crow shares valuable insights into using a positive approach to life in order to reach one's goals.  As an amateur musician who has listened to his music, I found his personal stories especially interesting.  But sometimes the "positive thinking" stuff becomes unrealistic (particularly some of his expressed views on human nature), and some of it was cliche.
