A large part of Angela Matelli's charm has come from her interactions with her extended (and supportive) family.  In this entry in the series, her family is minimized to a couple of phone calls and a few minutes in the first chapter.  The plot is unusual--voodoo on a New England college campus and the supporting characters are fairly well written.  But without the help and interference of the Matelli family, Angela seems to lose her edge and could be any number of interchangeable female private investigators.  Despite a better than average writing style and story line, Habeus Campus disappoints.  I probably will wait for the next to come out in paper back and am glad I bought this used.Read moreRead less
