I picked up this book after hearing about it on the radio.  I just finished it, and I have to say that it could have been a really great book.  The plot line was very interesting, and the portrayal of the media, and how a person gets tried and convicted by them before stepping foot in a  court of law was spot on.However, I was very disappointed in the  author's characters.  Not a one of them was very original, and I would say  most of them, especially the kids in the class (except for Elizabeth) and  Nasser and his friends were so stereotypical that I found myself not really  caring what happened.If you can forgive (or at least deal with) the  characters, this is a thrilling, fast, fun read.Read moreRead less
