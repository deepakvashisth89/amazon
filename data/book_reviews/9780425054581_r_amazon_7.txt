An awesome read for any fans of vintage SF. First Lensman is optimistic and camp and has all the gee-whiz flash you'd expect of it, though it suffers from the foibles of its time (the one woman who plays anything resembling a significant role in the story ends up needing to be rescued about two-thirds in, then vanishes from the narrative until the last chapter). The Kindle edition's transcription is garbage -- I'm used to this kind of quality from the free public domain texts available for download. Frequent transformed words ("Me" for "The", Samms inexplicably being called "Sammy" for a couple chapters), randomly inserted page numbers, and a very long chapter which actually contains three chapters which were left undivided by the transcribers. I'd recommend buying a hard copy instead of paying $8 for this.Read moreRead less
