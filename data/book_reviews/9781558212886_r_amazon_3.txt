I ordered this book for Great Grandpa because he is an avid fly fisherman who has fly fished most of the streams across this great county, he even appeared in several issues of Field and Stream back in the 50's.  I thought this book might be a nice trip down memory lane for an now elderly fly fisherman.  It is a nice book comprised of a compilation of some of the best articles from the magazine over the years.  Interesting reading, but it lacks the photos that made the magazine it so interesting back in the day.  There is a small section of about four or so pages with some photos of a select group of cover photos from the vintage issues, but that is about it.  I just thought it would have more photos, more of a trip down memory lane than it was.  Okay for the price, but I cannot honestly say that I would have purchased the book had I seen more of the inside before purchase.Read moreRead less
