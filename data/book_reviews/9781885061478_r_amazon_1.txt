I bought this book for my grand daughter who is 6 years old. She is a voracious reader, but this book would be good for a child who doesnt read since it is filled with many pics of birds that are Illinois specific. My daughters family live is a birdhouse. The reason I say this is because their house is made to have all their living space on the second level. Since their house is surrounded by large mature trees, when you look out the windows it feels like you are in a tree house. There are so many amazing, different species of birds to see. I bought the book about a month ago and when I go over to the house, my grand daughter, names all the differnt birds without the book now. It is a great way to learn about birds native to your area.
