Another excellent grouping of characters juxtaposed with intricate plotting. I have come to compare Lucas Davenport with Harry Bosch from Michael Connelly's detective thrillers. I believe that Harry is a deeper more fully formed character. I love that he's a Vietnam vet, that he grew up in such horrendous circumstances. These things make him who he is, how he thinks, what he believes. Lucas, on the other hand, is not as clear to me. I like that he's a womanizer, that he's considered good looking, that he has a tendency toward violence. However, it's never made clear why he is that way (because he was born that way? because he played hockey?). In a way, that's part of the mystery. Also, Connelly does have Bosch age through his books whereas Sandford never ages Davenport. I just find the comparison of these characters interesting. Sudden Prey is good.
