Published in 1998, this book is one of the best for web performance testing,  covering the technical basics for everything you need to know in order to really understand performance tuning.  It includes such required information as definitions  of various performance metrics, and what those  should be in the real world, and moves along through networks, hardware, and operating systems.  It goes to great pains to cover a variety of  systems, including Windows, Linux, Macintosh,  and a variety of web servers.Michael CzeiszpergerWeb Performance, Inc.
