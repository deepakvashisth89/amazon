Number 8 of the Bantam published Star trek books. Not the best. The crew of the Star Ship Enterprise are exploring the Galactic Core when the receive a distress call from a long missing space ship. Upon answering the call the Enterprise finds itself being pulled towards the surface of a huge artificial construct due to a powerful gravitational pull.The crew has found a Dyson Sphere; an incredibly large artificial world built around an internal sun.Kirk and some of his crew must find  their way inside and find a way to break free of the Sphere before the Enterprise is pulled into the surface and is destroyed. Inside they find a simple people that worships the sun and are willing to do all that their God requires, but their God is leading them to destruction in the form of a black hole.  The biggest question is where did the Dyson Sphere come from.  An interesting book but not the best.Read moreRead less
