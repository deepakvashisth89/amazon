Chances are if you visit D.C., you will visit the Holocaust Museum, but won't dedicate more than one day for that purpose. If you are like me, a single visit is overwhelming.  There is so much to contemplate as you walk through the history of the Holocaust.  I saw this book in the gift shop and made the mistake of not buying it then.  This volume is not the same as the museum, but has much of the same material.  I have been able to read a bit, put it down, process the inhumanity of the time, and re-visit more later.  The topics are well organized with several illustrations in each section.  This edition is well bound with a dust jacket and is a volume that I will want to preserve.
