These cards have a smooth feel, are beautifully designed in themselves and arrive packed into a sturdy box for keeping them together. They are in sections: breathing; stretches/warm-ups; postures; one OM card and meditations. Instructions are on the back with an optimistic thought to repeat as you perform the action or position and a list of the benefits of the particular exercise. Drawings on the front show the poses and the whole deck is appealing, gently coloured in neutrals and dark green, and a suitable size. I have taken several books on yoga to the charity shop because they were too advanced or snapped shut when I was in the middle of a pose but these cards avoid both problems. The approach is not too difficult or too abstract and the meditations are spiritual but soothing for an ordinary working person. An excellent buy for travel or daily use and a possible present for anyone interested in learning yoga or reminding themselves of what they have done in a class. Highly recommended.Read moreRead less
