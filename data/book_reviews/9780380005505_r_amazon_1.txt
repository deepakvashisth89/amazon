I started reading this book not expecting much, but then the story just grabbed me. The plot was well constructed and simple, and the characters were true to life. Jill, the main character, is more of an antihero, and  the way she makes life deliberately hard for herself and others is  sometimes painful to read. Frances Kerns does an excellent job in  developing the characters around Jill. All these people are real and human,  from the alcoholic and abusive Riley who gains my sympathy, to the sweet  hearted Joan, who struggles with jealousy. The ending is tragic on the  basic level, yet it was expected from the outset.
