I was surprised at how thorough this book is on the life of Wilson after reading the other reviews of it being just a 'primer'.  It really goes into how Wilson the man/president was made and what his prejudices, virtues and hotpoints were very well.The beginning of the book is rather boring but once Wilson becomes president it definitely gets more interesting & how Wilson handled the aftermath of WWI very inciteful & apropos for us going into war against Afghanistan now with the modern day League of Nations, the UN, trying to arbitrate their own world order.I think Louis Auchinchloss, cousin of the late Jacqueline Kennedy Onassis did a good job on the research & unless you are a Wilsonophile this should be more than enough.Read moreRead less
