Having really enjoyed Camilleri's Montalbano books, I was hopeful about this, especially after seeing such good reviews. I was greatly disappointed.  The mystery itself was all right, although the red herrings were rather expected, but it was the feel of the story which was the greatest disappointment.  Rather than having an Italian, much less Florentine, flavor permeate the story, I felt as if the author visited for local color and presented details without substance.  Marshall Guarnaccia was barely in the story much of the time, and I got no feel for his personality or quirks.
