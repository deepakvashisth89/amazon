This is a great collection of short fiction!  The talented writers chosen for this edition shine as most entries are smartly written.  Highlights of the book as mentioned in the review above mine are indeed "Midnight  Trash" and "Family Vacation," as well as the great look back  at childhood by Tom Coyne in his "Behind Sharp Branches.  Coyne's  story of pain, pride, guilt, and childhood innocence (perhaps lost) is  startlingly vivid.  In "...Branches," Coyne deftly shows any  child's eagerness to emulate their parents while at the same time defy  them.  Sharply written, including (if not especially) the humorous take on  a Catholic school education.  Kudos.Read moreRead less
