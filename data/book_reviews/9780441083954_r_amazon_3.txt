What a wonderful story! Elmer Kelton includes the details that make this story authentic. It is obvious that he did the research to write convincingly about the life of the Buffalo Hunters, as well as the Comanches who stalked them. The narrative moves swiftly, and crackles with action and suspense, leading to a satisfying conclusion.
