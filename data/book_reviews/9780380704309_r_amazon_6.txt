The BBC includes Adrian Mole on it's top 200 list of favorite books in Britain.  I can see why.  Adrian's diaries are witty, fun, light-reading.  In this second book in the series, Adrian has his 16th birthday.  Things are still up in the air with his parents marriage, and there is a big change at home.  Adrian is still looking out for old age pensioner Bert.  Some old friends fall away, but old enemies turn into friends.
