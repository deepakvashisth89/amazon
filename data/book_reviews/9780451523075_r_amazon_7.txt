This story is set in Egdon Heath in the mid-1800s.  It opens with a reddleman (reddle is a chalk infused with iron ore, making it and those who handle it red, and used to mark sheep) giving a ride home to Thomasina, who had gone to marry Mr. Wildeve, but been unable to do so, because their marriage license was for a town different from that to which they went.  It is the story of Mr. Wildeve's attraction to Eustacia, who is in turn trying to attract the attention of Thomasina's cousin Clem, recently returned from Paris for a visit; the story of the unrequited love the reddleman, Diggory Venn, has for Thomasina, and of the lives of those who live in this small town.  It's well written and fast paced, moreso than many books of that era.
