This book from 1968 is recommended by Williamson Murray and Allan Millett in their [2000] A War to be Won, and by Gerhard Weinberg in his [1994] A World at Arms. It is not a chronological history of the war. Each chapter examines a topic, such as "the economic dimension", "the psychological dimension", "the scientific dimension", "the resistance movements", and so forth.
