Since the first appearance of Harold Gleason's Method of Organ Playing in  1937 it has been the most comprehensive and complete tool on the market for  training first-class organists. The Gleason method is unique among books of  its kind in that it has been in a continual state of evolution throughout  its eight editions. The vast practical and pedagogical knowledge of Harold  Gleason and his wife, concert organist Catharine Crozier Gleason is  presented in the most up-to-date fashion in the current eighth edition.  Every page of the book reflects both first-rate and recent scholarship as  well as tried and true materials for beginning players. The lists of organ  literature, the bibliography and the historical information that  supplements the pedagogical portion of the book makes it a worthwhile  investment for organists at all performing levels. As a beginning player I  was reared at the organ with the fifth and sixth editions of the method and  have returned to it often thoughout my career as a performer and teacher.
