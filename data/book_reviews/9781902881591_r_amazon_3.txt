Water Street is a collection of short stories that revolve around small town life.  The inhabitants of Water Street each have a tale and they share their experiences with us throughout this book.  The characters are so lifelike and their experiences so real, you feel at home and as though you know each of them individually.  Dealing with topics such as love and loss, the stories are full of emotions.  They had me laughing one minute, crying the next. This book is a testament to small communities and the people that live within them.  Crystal Wilkinson has done a superb job with this novel.
