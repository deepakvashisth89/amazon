Funny, sharp and fast moving, but ultimately a profoundly serious novel. It was relevant when it was written (1935) for its detailed awareness of precisely what was going on in Germany just two years into the Third Reich & its forensic grasp of how fascist terror works and how this might work out in America. It also has great and unfortunate relevance for our own times.
