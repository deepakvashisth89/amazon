This was my introduction to SJ Rozan's Bill Smith/Lydia Chin books, and I'm looking forward to tackling another. As an adopted New Yorker, I love it when a book or movie gets the city right, and this one not only captures its spirit but gets the details down pat as well. The characterizations of Smith, Chin and all the other humans who share the book with the city are equally strong, and the plot takes some interesting steps away from the standard whodunit.
