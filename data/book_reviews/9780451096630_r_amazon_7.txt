Dmitri Ivanovich, nine year old son of Tsar Ivan the Terrible, manifests himself in the present day through a college student undergoing age regression hypnosis.  Because of the historical mystery concerning the  circumstances surrounding the death of this boy, the author is able to  develop an unexpected but plausible plot which is interesting and  educational.  Dmitri is written in a light, easy style, spiced with tidbits  which have an air of authenticity.  I would have appreciated some notes or  sources concerning such elements as the fashion of dying the whites of the  eyes black for cosmetic reasons.  Did this really happen?  How?
