Good selection of stories - that is not my problem with the product. I would consider using this in one of my future creative writing courses, only the work presented here is exclusive rather than inclusive. Not a SINGLE gay story by a gay male writer. No Andrew Holleran, Edmund White, nor Felice Picanos. These days, my thought on the best way to teach a class is to have this on hand, scan from it the stories you want to teach, supplement with other stories (gay and straight) and have a packet available at a copy store. Plus assign Burroway and a small press collection by a new writer (that year's Flannery O'Connor Award winner, perhaps).
