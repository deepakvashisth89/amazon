Lilly is the epitome of a damaged woman whose actions are cruel and who is not even aware of the sorrow she inflicts on her daughters. I read this in a fit of absorption- I just had to know what would happen to Anna and her sisters.  Lilly is utterly fascinating and maddening at once.  The author is genius at writing about a time and place, with fantastic details.  i do think she writes very self-consciously, in  hyper poetic prose.
