I love the Oz stories and hate to suggest that this may not be a real Oz story but it is a hard fact to ignore in light of the non-Oz adventure that transpires. If not for the ending that ties the Nome King, Dorthy and the Wizard of Oz to the rest of the story I would be inclined to think this is just a well written adventure story by L. Frank Baum. It definitely has Baum's style written all over it but the vast majority of the story happens outside of Oz. It is a wonderful adventure story that has great characters such as King Rinkitink, Bilbil and Inga and a fantastic plot that makes it a feel good read.
