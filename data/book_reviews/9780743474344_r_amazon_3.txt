After borrowing this book from a friend, I have purchased and gifted this book at least 3x - and will continue to do so.  It is insightful, raw and brutally honest.  I was never a big fan of Tupac UNTIL I read this book.  It's hard to describe his tone, but he is able to connect to the reader, by being both very strong, but also showing weakness.  I personally was captivated by his openness and authenticity, through the good and the bad.  All the ugly parts of ourselves and our lives that we may want to cover up and hide, Tupac reveals.  There is something included here from every part of his life, living in the inner city, his education, the movie industry, family and prison life, no part goes untouched.  With handwritten letters and family photos it is a fascinating journey through his eyes, one that I will never forget and only wish to continue to share with others.
