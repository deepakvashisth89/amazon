Anne Tyler is a recent discovery for me, and so far she can do no wrong. I've read her most recent books, and I'm now catching up with the early ones. (This is her first.) She deals in slightly strange people in odd family relationships, but these are always convincing as you read, however implausible they may suddenly sound if you try telling someone else the story. I find them to be 'page-turners' - I get involved with the characters, and want to know what happens to them.  Do try at least one, and see if it works for you.Read moreRead less
