This book covers CORBA both at a high level as well as in the details.  Not only does it give a good view of CORBA but represents the thinking of the industry overall as to the proper architecture for component-based applications in the Internet age. It will not teach you how to program using CORBA but will prepare a designer or programmer for CORBA-based systems.
