“The Heaven Makers” is a very interesting work of science fiction authored by Frank Herbert.  It is of medium length and is quite readable.  It involves interstellar aliens covertly interacting with humans.  It is mostly clearly written and makes for a very good, albeit somewhat complex audiobook.  I thoroughly enjoyed it.The actual writing within this novel is very good.  The story is clear and artistically composed.  What appealed to me personally is the philosophical aspects of this book.  This is what I mean above by complex.  I was listening to the novel on audiobook.  It was narrated by Scott Brick.  He did an excellent job and this was a performance as much as a narration.The complexity of the story lies within the description of inner thoughts and conversations of the characters.  I sometimes listen to light fiction while commuting or attending to simple chores.  This is not that kind of novel.  I often stopped and read on kindle, and then reread while listening to various episodes of this story.  In that context, although clearly written, this was not a light reading experience.  It is possible that if one is only looking for such a light reading experience one may not enjoy this story as much as I did.In summary I am very glad to have had the opportunity to read this work.  Over the years I have read some science fiction, but have not been captivated by it.  I have just completed this novel.  As I sit here I am trying to think of a science fiction work I enjoyed as much as this one.  I liked some of Kurt Vonnegut’s novels very much.  Specifically in terms of the interactions of interstellar aliens and humans, and certain philosophical aspects thereof, this definitely makes my personal “A List”.
