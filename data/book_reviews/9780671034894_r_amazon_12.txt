Don't be put off by the title, this is a full on thriller.  I enjoyed reading about John Taft's exploits with his buddy Martinez and I loved the idea that Albert Einstein had left a hidden clue to his final equation.  Some have compared this to a Dirk Pitt story, (and I have read a few) but this seemed fresh to me and I am sure I will be reading more from Craig Dirgo.Read moreRead less
