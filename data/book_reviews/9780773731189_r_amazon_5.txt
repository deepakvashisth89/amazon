"Patton's Gap" gives a good overview of the Allies drive through France after D-Day (June-August 1944). It also takes us on a brief visit into the world of an Allied recon pilot during that period. This visit is too brief in my opinion. While I agreed with the author's conclusion as to who was responsible for the gap not being closed sooner and the rational behind that conclusion, I would have enjoyed reading about more of his own experiences during this time.Read moreRead less
