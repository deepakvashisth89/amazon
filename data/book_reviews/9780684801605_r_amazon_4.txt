There are so many modern American authors with nothing special to write about.  They delve into the esoteric and have no real life experience or understanding of their heretage to inspire their writings.  Not so with Lee.  This novel is a wonderful tale of two families unite by tradgedy and form a common bond of love.  The main plot is interwoven with the imaginative tales of LaRue Smalls that reflect the meaning of their lives while bringing comfort and ties that bind.  This African-American writer leaves the anger beneath the surface and shows two ways of dealing with the horrors and poverty of Jim Crow: alcoholism and violence vs love and creativity.  She exchanges beauty for ashes in a universal manner.  The writing is clear and the characters real.  It is a novel that you do not want to miss.Read moreRead less
