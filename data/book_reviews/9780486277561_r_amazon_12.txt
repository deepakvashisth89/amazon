This is the tenth book in the series and I am hooked. This is the most merry story I guess of all the other books in the series. It starts with all assembling to participate in Ozma, the princess of Oz's, banquet. I really loved the chapters The twin islands and the escape. Baum must have some awesome sense of imagination to write and put forward something so different and unique as this.then comes Rinktink, a kindhearted king but he is not the prince charm that princesses await for. He is still quite charming and the king, queen and the prince love him.
