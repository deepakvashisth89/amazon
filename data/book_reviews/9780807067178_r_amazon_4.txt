Written so long ago, but Judy Grahn shared what we knew (and didn't know) about LGBTI history at that time. I still remember facts from when I read it to my lover in 1986. Filled with poems and thoughts about fairies, drag queens, and dykes... this book delves deeply into what I needed to know to soothe my lesbian heart in a time of difficulty and strife.
