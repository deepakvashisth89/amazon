Just started reading it and it's off to a great start! This is a pretty fun and insightful story, and there are plenty of wonderful pictures of Moe and his family. What I personally love about it is that you get to see Moe and his brother's relationship before they become The Three Stooges. There are some very charming and funny moments with his mother. That was a lot of fun to read.It's easy to picture Moe's voice when you read the book, like having an old friend tell you a story.
