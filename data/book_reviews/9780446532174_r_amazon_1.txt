Dr. Morgan Reese is dead. Her body is found on the golf course soon after she is appointed the head of a new mental health center. A young patient who committed suicide was her daughter, one of twins.Lucy O'Malley is from a large Boston/Irish family but love pushes her into the stratosphere of main line Philadelphian familes when she meets Archer Haverill. Archer's mother was Dr. Reese, who had insisted on meeting with her other daughter Avery before she was killed.Lucy is pushed to solve the murder and delve into family secrets that were meant to be kept secret.Some details of this police procedural are off kilter, but it is still a good read and well done.Nash Black, author of SINS OF THE FATHERS and QUALIFYING LAPS.Read moreRead less
