No one writes a better heroine than Elliott.  Most SF with a leading Female focuses entirely on her foibles and romances and perils.  Bashed about, imperiled, betrayed, dismayed, demoralized.  Until finally she staggers over the finish line.  I suppose it sells.  But I can't stand it.  Elliott's MC faces all these things through all seven episodes with amazing courage, skill, insight, imagination and leadership.  Portrayed dramatically, not melodramatically.
