I really enjoyed this book.  The author spent hundreds of hours over several years interviewing everyone involved in the development and evolution of id Software, including Carmack and Romero.  The result is a gripping account of every crucial moment in the company's history that is so detailed it feels like you are there in the room as events unfold around you in real-time, sharing in the celebration of their successes, holding your breath during the tense moments, etc.  I feel like this is a sincere attempt at telling the true story of what really happened behind the scenes, not a hagiography of anyone.  As a result it feels like you get to know the real Carmack and Romero, you get to feel the electricity at the peak of their relationship, and you get to see how they each thought they were doing the best thing for id as their relationship frayed.Read moreRead less
