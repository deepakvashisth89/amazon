I purchased The Middle Age of Mrs Eliot for a dear friend in the UK to read on his Birthday week in Dorset. He was amazed how quickly it came.....2 days in time to travel with him. Angus Wilson is a very special novelist and his novel about a woman starting over and rebuilding her life just when she felt secure and settled. This new life that is thrust upon her gives her the opportunity to open her heart to her brother and his dying lover. The Middle Age of Mrs Eliot is a lovely understated, quietly sensitive statement of human endurance.Read moreRead less
