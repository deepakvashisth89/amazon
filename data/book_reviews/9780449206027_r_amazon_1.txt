Reading this book is like being caught in a nightmare. The tale is intensely gripping - an emotional roller coaster. Moore is a storyteller without peer. His language is clear, and draws no attention to itself. He  simply puts you right into the head of his protagonist, and carries you  along with her, as she battles with her conscience, her fear, death, and,  ultimately, heaven itself.
