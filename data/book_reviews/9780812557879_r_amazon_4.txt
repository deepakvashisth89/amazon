I like Walter Jon Williams.  I was put off from reading this for some time by the 3.5 star rating -- but he's as good as ever here.  Driving plot line, developed characters, fantastic evocation of an imagined world, 'science' that is basically magic, but...The ending could be better in my opinion, but otherwise -- I'm perfectly happy....and this really brings me to my point.  It's not Amazon's fault, but the reader rating system really isn't worth much.  This got 3.5 stars -- and is in my view far better than the average bear.  Conversely, I have burnt my fingers on various pieces of utter garbage that received average ratings of 4.5 stars.  The system is broke.  It doesn't work.  It provides virtually no useful information at all...Perhaps reviews could merely be self-assigned as 'positive' or 'critical.'  The numerical system, as noted, isn't of much value.  Alternatively, perhaps each review's rating could be accompanied by the average rating given by that reviewer.  After all, if I give something five stars and I give everything five stars, the rating doesn't mean much.
