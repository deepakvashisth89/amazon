This book is one of the funniest I've read in a long time.  A professor develops a ray that changes people to statues.  He falls in love with the daughter of a Leprecaun who knows how to change statues to life.  They go to the museum and select a number of old gods such as Neptune, Venus, Mercury, and even Medusa's head to turn to life - and ALL love a good time!  They rustle a cow in downtown New York City and take it to the top floor of one of the city's largest hotels to hide it.  And Baccus, the God of Wine, is determined to make a better drink than the mortals can - even though he never saw distilled liquor before.This book does contain some sex, heavy drinking, and other situations but it is certainly funny!Read moreRead less
