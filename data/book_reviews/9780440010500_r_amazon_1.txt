Loved this book as a ninth grader, and love it now in my thirties! Lauren, a ninth grader, finds herself drawn to an eighth grade boy. There is nothing more taboo than being a cradle robber at Lauren's school. The teasing they endure at school capped off by the fighting at home between her parents defines her 9th grade year. But she learns somewhere along the way that being happy means being true to yourself, and maybe dating an 8th grade boy who's as amazing as Zach is the best thing to make her happy.Read moreRead less
