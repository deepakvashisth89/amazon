While I have to credit the author for an original story, I wound up struggling through the first 3/4ths of the book.  As a matter of fact, I couldn't figure out WHY it was so difficult for me...until I realized.....its the way Lucy, the main character is written....no subtext at all!One thing that makes a good writer in my opinion is the depth of its characters, and one way depth is presented is by subtext.  People in real life do not spill out every thought that pops into their heads.  And everyone censors what they say, to different people in different situations.  Lucy, however, lets her mouth vomit every sarcastic hateful comment no matter who she's speaking to, and frankly after the first 100 pages of reading it, I GOT BORED!How much more interesting it is when you can read what a character thinks and feels on the inside, then see what they choose to reveal on the outside.  Thomas Harris made this an artform.  Too bad this great story idea only skimmed the surface like a bad soap opera.Read moreRead less
