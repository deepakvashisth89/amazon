If I could go back in time to almost 30 years I would congratulate myself for picking up this book, reading it and enjoying it to the point in it leading me to be an avid fan of John Let Carré to the point where I have now read the vast majority of his output.Reading this again, I would say this is good Le Carré rather than great. Its effectively his spy romance novel but done in a typical Le Carré way. The devil, as always, is in the detail. It has great characters that are detailed, well drawn and complex. It has a detailed story and as always with his works, the talking is the main method here. No action and no great reveals, just perfectly judged detail and a narrative that is all smoke and mirrors.I'm pleased I read this again, but it's not his very best.Read moreRead less
