The murder of a local cowboy artist re-opens an even older mystery surrounding a jeweled golden crucifix lost long ago. Sheriff Charles Matthews, a recent arrival to the Texas Panhandle from Dallas, works with his loyal deputies to solve both mysteries.I think one of the best things in this novel was the use of setting. Descriptions of the Texas Panhandle’s weather pull the reader into the story. The characters are similarly well-drawn. Charles is fantastic as the very likeable but less than perfect, out of his element city-slicker in the middle of cattle country. His deputies are great in their supporting roles. This was definitely well-written and fun to read.Read moreRead less
