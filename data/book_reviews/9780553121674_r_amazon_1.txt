This story captured my heart. Possibly because I have loved boys for many years, as a teacher and social worker, it moved me to tears.Tom is a darling dear boy! You can tell from GLH's skilful writing the tremendous depth of love his father and mother bear him. My favourite scene in the entire story is that between Tom and his father near the end, right till the end of that chapter - oh, it was beautiful!Other GLH stories are extremely touching too (e.g. 'Ladybird' is my favourite, along with this now), but 'The Obsession of Victoria Gracen' will hold a special place in my heart. GLH conveyed the inexpressible value of the human soul, of all human souls, for whom Christ died, and how worthwhile it is to do our utmost to strive to bring them to Him.It was also refreshing to read a GLH story that wasn't a romance for once. She also portrayed the villain humorously to create a delightful experience for the reader.A treasure.Read moreRead less
