If you enjoyed Mayle's 'A Year in Provence', you should like this too.  Whereas 'A Year in Provence' was a laid out as a diary of his first year living in the region, this book, published about 2 years later, is a collection of essays, all written with the same wit and keen eye, and sharing his love of the region.  Definitely worth reading.  If you have not yet read 'A Year in Provence', I would recommend reading it first.
