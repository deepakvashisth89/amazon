'Desperados: The Roots of Country Rock' is a detailed study of the roots and development of the country rock genre.John Einarson covers the years from 1963, when Buck Owens and the Buckaroos started to lend a different sound to their Texas honky-tonk numbers, right up to 1973, when the Eagles recorded and released 'Desperado'.It's a very interesting story that was waiting to be told. With a copy of this book and 'Hotel California' by Barney Hoskyns, you're well set up to discover or reaquaint yourself with some excellent bands and artists and quite a few fine albums.Read moreRead less
