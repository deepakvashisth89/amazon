Decided to go way back in time and read the earlier books of a few of my favorite authors.  These are books that I either missed or skipped over when they were originally released.  I've been having a difficult time finding current/new authors, whose writing I love enough to "follow", and reading the new releases from my "go to" favorite authors from the past several decades has been disappointing - at best.  Seems they've either burned out (understandable after decades of trying to keep the characters and storylines fresh) or they're lending their names to works written by others - with only a few lines, or maybe a partial chapter, actually written by their own hand.  In any event - as an avid reader with no where to turn - I've gone back - and thoroughly enjoyed this book!  I remember reading the 2nd in this series in the 90s, so I was familiar with the main character and the geographic setting.  Not sure how I missed this book but - glad to have found it, in light of the current scarcity of interesting reading.  Great storyline, interesting characters and wonderful twists and turns, right to the end.  Highly recommend going back to the "old classics" for those who miss the style of writing where - one can escape into the story - and away from the reality of day to day life, 24/7 news cycles, and....gratuitous profanity and/or overly graphic sex scenes.  For this reader, not a boring page in the book.
