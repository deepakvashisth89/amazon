Information and lots of words. If you are already familiar with the children of alcoholics problems then you wont learn that much from this. If your world is in turmoil and you had an alcoholic parent you will find this interesting and possibly informative. However I am not sure you manage to tease and answers from this book. Although again, it is cheap to buy so bearing in mind what I have said why not try it and see.
