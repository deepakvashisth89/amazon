I prefer Professor Shandy working with clues and the outrageously linked cast of characters in Balaclava County.This vacationing "daydream" or nightmare is rather like a mystery novel crossed with fantasy and given a bad LSD trip.Professor Shandy comes to Wales to try to help solve the issue of an agricultural disaster. A plant, the giant Hogweed, is running rampant and growing all over. Peter, Tim Ames, and Daniel Stott come to help and while out in the field get hijacked into a fantasy version of the "troglodyte" Medieval world, without food, baths, or anything relating to common sense.This is the "it's just a dream" novel that every mystery author seems to feel a requirement to do. It's cute but not much else.It's worth the read for amusement, once.EnjoyRead moreRead less
