Overall, I really enjoyed this book, and, as advertised, it's framework is very similar to Downton Abbey. I happen to love WWI romances because it was a time of such upheaval and great change, and this is seen in many of Phillip Rock's characters who answer the call to duty, some of whom must let go of their once frivolous lives and work toward something larger than themselves. There is a smattering of men and women, all of them interesting characters and very real, though this book definitely focuses more on the POV's of the men. It also gets into great detail of the war, entire chapters devoted to the fighting and not a woman in sight. Now, for me, I do want to see some of the war because the brutality of it is what effects the men and women who see it up close, but I still prefer to see more drama unfolding between the male and female characters than I want to see on the field. And there are some romances in which there is too little seen, and I'd been wishing for more.
