What to say? I've loved this story ever since first reading it nearly ten years ago. I had pulled it off the shelf at my university's library at random -well, not completely. I was walking down an aisle and just had this funny feeling to pull out this book.  Call it crazy, but it felt like the book that I've never seen before wanted me to read it.  At first I was confused by the writing but read on. I'm glad I did and was soon rapt by every page. At the time, it had been a while after things didn't work out with a girl I really liked. I needed a way to understand her more, plus my heart was still sore. This book helped, plus this girl looked exactly like Liza which is what floored me when I first saw the front cover. I read it in secret. This was for me, you see.  I soon bought my own copy.  As time passed, I healed and my love for this book has changed as well.  I often wondered what a sequel would be like.  Then, recently, I found the audio read by Rebecca Lowman who really feels the characters and acts out each one wonderfully.  Her voice is captivating and so closely matches how Liza and Annie sounded in my mind.Whether to heal, to inspire or all in between, this is a great and timeless story that I still enjoy and recommend to all.Read moreRead less
