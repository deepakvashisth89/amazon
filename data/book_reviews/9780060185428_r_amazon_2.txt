On a fluke I picked up this audiobook at the library when they were out of Stephanie Plum novels and anything else that looked interesting. Normally, I don't go for "black" novels, because they have a tendency to embody the "authentic" black experience that smacks back to a misogyny or masculate black women - people that I can't live up to and have no desire to imitate. This book is hilarious, moving, and just feels honest. Highly recommended. I can't wait to start reading other books in the series.Read moreRead less
