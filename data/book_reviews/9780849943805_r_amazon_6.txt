An outstanding book and well written.  I don't think anyone has ever presented all the issues this book brings to light in one book.  It deals with Muslims, Jews and Christians and some of the beliefs and myths held in common by each.  Don't be confused, this is not to be a religious book, it is a story, a thriller, that will have you on the edge of your seat but it also deals with things that religious scholars have wrestled with for centuries.  It is all centered around the Ark of the Covenant and the expectations each has regarding its power and meaning.
