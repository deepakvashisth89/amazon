This book is loaded with a wealth (no pun intended) of information about personal development and even financial advice. The author explains how to navigate the path to personal and financial freedom in practical terms that anyone can understand. His 70/30 rule was something I'd never heard of and will definitely be putting into practice in my personal wealth plan.The author offered so many great anecdotes and examples that I could identify with, which really helped put his financial advice into perspective. A portion of the book is devoted to tips on achieving personal power, and some of them really hit home, causing me to reevaluate my goals and plans for success.One of my favorite quotes from this book was:"Don't just live another year. Instead, gather up the years and invest them in the next one."That statement is so simple yet very true.
