This was my first venture into Clive Cussler books and I found his style of writing to be easy to read and flows well. I must admit some of the factual scenarios seem a little far-fetched but I suppose that's the literary license that a fiction writer has but it also makes the book entertaining.Dirk Pitt comes across kind of a like McGyver of the TV series but that also adds to intrigue of the plot line. I plan to read more in the Cussler series to see how they compare. Over all, a fun casual book.Read moreRead less
