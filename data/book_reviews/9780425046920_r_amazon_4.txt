I Love police procedurals and thrillers. The premise of this book and description of the 'procedures' were good BUT it was VERY repetitive and SOOOOO detailed I became a little bored in places. Sanders created very intricate characters. Like none I've heard of before (which I liked). This book takes place many years ago before some of our more 'modern' tools for police work but I had always heard of these books and how popular they were when they came out so I thought I'd give it a try. The fact they are older doesn't bother me as 'I' remember those days w/o those 'modern' tools (like cell phones :-), e.g.). I like Edward X. Delaney's character but I am not so sure I want to read the next 'Sins' book as it may be more of the same and I might find that a little torturous to get through.Read moreRead less
