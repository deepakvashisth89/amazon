Phoebe Atwood Taylor's Asey Mayo series is now a classic. The second book has Prudence Whisby and her friend, Rowena Fible shanghaied into playing chaperone for a young girl at a house party of men. The problem arises when the host is first late and then discovered dead death from arsenic poisoning.Among the party is an old flame of Pru's who has been asking her to marry him for 27 years.
