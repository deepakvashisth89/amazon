If you like Ruth Rendell as I and many others do this is a very good early work, thoughtful, unpredictable and feeling very real. Wexford isn't particularly at the centre but this leaves the reader at the centre instead. It is hard to say how much it twists without giving spoilers, so suffice to say that at the end we find that nothing is as it seems, yet we are left believing that that is how reality probably is. The title gives nothing away. I couldn't make sense of it till well after I had finished the book, so as clues go it protects its secret very well. It is perhaps a little too slow moving to get a five star rating for me, but it is a satisfying read.Read moreRead less
