Lilliputians unite!  T.H. White's children plot is delightful and engaging with characters you love to hate and enjoy seeing foiled.  Its setting is a run down British estate that happens to also harbor a colony of very little people.  The main character, Maria, learns to see the Lilliputians as real individuals rather than her personal playthings.  The book climaxes in a cliff hanger with all the fun that can be culled from odious villians and bumbling rescuers in an ancient locale.  This book is absorbing for both adults and children.Read moreRead less
