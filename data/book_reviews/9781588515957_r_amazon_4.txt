Though not typically a reader of mystery novels, I am finding Mehl's "Graven Images" to be a pleasant exploration into the genre.Character background is well developed, lending color and flavor to each of the prominent characters with a depth of research that is subtle during the read, but causes you, on reflection, to realize that the author has really done her homework into a variety of fields for this book.While I do find instances of passive language that I would very much love to see as charged narrative with more powerful word selection, it is little more than a background buzz that I notice for having studied writing.  The lives of the characters and the developing story well outdistance any technical issues that I have found.I have not completed "Graven Images" as yet, but the story is driving me to constantly wonder whether in the end it will prove to be a hard realism check, a mystical venture, a deep study of self, or all combined.I will certainly get future works by Ms. Mehl.Read moreRead less
