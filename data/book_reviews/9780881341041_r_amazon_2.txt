I received the original edition of this classic book. I had a copy of the third edition back then and I hated it because it was bouncing around 4 different models and was really confusing. For nostalgia I bought this original edition and man, it was little better. I can't imagine how a new computer user back in 1981 could not have found the contents confusing. For example, the verbose writings about starting up surely would have given any new Apple II or II Plus user a headache.
