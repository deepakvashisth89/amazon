A very long book, but  is  typical Clancy style very in-depth ( as are his other non-fiction works. I've read them all).  Into the Storm, though could be used as a textbook for Army military schools.  The evolution of the Army from the bad old days following Viet Nam to the 21st century is really well laid out. The Army of today is like nothing anyone who served before Desert Storm would know, and how we got there is all in this book.There are some personal trials and tribulations, this book is not without drama.One thing I didn't like so much the naming names of so many commanders.  It's a bit much to keep up with all the generals, but as some points in the book I thought it excessive.Read moreRead less
