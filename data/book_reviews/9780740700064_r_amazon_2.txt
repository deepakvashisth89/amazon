Growing up I devoured the Cathy books that were available.  This past Christmas I decided to introduce her to my daughters.  The sarcastic, feminist, self-loathing, truth telling Cathy is still a hit after all these years.  I was sad to see that a lot of the collections were not readily available for purchase.  I sincerely hope that there will be re-prints available in the very near future, so that a new generation will come to love sarcastic Cathy as she navigates life.Highly recommend for girls or women that may not be into conventional comic strips but want to be entertained where the lead is an insecure female trying to get through the day.
