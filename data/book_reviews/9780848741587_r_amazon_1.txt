Sometimes it seems difficult to find a Christmas book with unusual themes. Novel or creative; yes, unusual; no. This book is an exception. I agree with the earlier reviewer that this is a wonderful book but for a different reason. I find the use of simple color fascinating and there are several spohisticated "old world," examples in this book including snowflakes, Kris Kringle, mittens, and Victorian boy and girl angels. The center spread is of a very colorful set of "The 12 Days of Christmas" and this is followed by a stand alone picture of the words and music in a similar border motif. Just gorgeous! Santa's galore, and a hollyberry clock face are a few more things to tempt the stitcher. In addition there are several gift pieces with differnt motifs and stockings, of course.
