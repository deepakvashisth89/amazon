Wizardborn – Book 3 of the RunelordsI enjoyed this volume more than I did Book 2. There is plenty of action, and the technical descriptions of the reavers add context, and there is some humour to sustain the interest over about 500 pages of the read.I still think there is too little mystery and too much "massive action", without the real intrigue. I can't say I am particularly concerned with the fate of the characters... yet.But, let us read on. There is still something compelling, and I certainly felt it was worth my time more than Book 2.
