Marc Shapiro, gathering from newspaper, magazines, book, and internet  sources, does a wonderful job of telling the reader about the life of, at  one point unknown, British Author J.K. Rowling, mastermind behind the  fantastic Harry Potter books. By reading this book, we see what first  inspired Rowling to write about the life of Harry, and we see what kept her  going throughout numerous setbacks. We also see the influences she had  along the way, whether from friends, or from books she had read and authors  she liked, C.S. Lewis for one (he is also a magnificent author) and we see  much else as well. It has been alleged in other reviews that all Marc  Shapiro did was gather things from other resources and did no work on his  own, but that is untrue, Mr. Shapiro worked very hard putting this book  together, and it is now a valued asset in my collection of all things  Potter!Read moreRead less
