I have a class friend who had a history of childhood sexual abuse. His story is one I can never really understand but I can try. I read this book with that aim in mind and this book did not disappoint.I feel I can better relate to his thoughts as he shares with me. I can see some of his tendencies and have an idea how they came about. I have had a couple of semesters of psychology classes so analyzing is something I do even though it's got its own time and place.The personal experiences and analysis of them have been more enlightening than some other books I've read on the topic and is a nice addition to those insights.I know this isn't specific, but I just wanted to share my thoughts more than the content. The book is more focused on experiences and breaking the short stories down as to what's being expressed in them. It's not a lot of actual psychology talk. It's not a "self-help" book per se and it doesn't read like a text book either. I think it's a happy medium.It's been so helpful that I bought a set of highlighters and colored tabs and plan to re-read it.If this is the first or only book you read on the topic, you won't be disappointed.I hope something I've said is helpful.
