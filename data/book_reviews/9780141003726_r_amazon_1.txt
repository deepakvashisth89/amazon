Although I watch a lot of PBS broadcasts, I was unfamiliar with Rumpole of the Bailey.  I bought this e-edition book on the advice of someone who had read a different Rumpole volume.  This is a collection of short stories told from the perspective of an aging, rotund barrister with great wit.  None of the mysteries, if you will, is more than ankle deep, but still this makes for enjoyable reading.  After coming off reading the lengthy "Fountainhead", this collection of amusing short stories was most refreshing, I have followed it up by purchasing two more Rumpole books in the series.Read moreRead less
