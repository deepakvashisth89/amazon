As an English teacher, I can't live without this book.  For many English speakers, it is difficult to explain some of the more esoteric points of grammar.  In my experience, I've found that most grammar books are too basic, are filled with lots of unnecessary grammar exercises, or are difficult to use.  This book, however, is easy to use and is created for the English teaching professional rather than for the English student.  It's a breeze to check up on difficult points.  All of the grammar points are listed in alphabetical order in an encyclopedic manner.  In my teaching career, I've been asked many difficult questions that were difficult for me to answer.
