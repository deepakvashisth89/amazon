Great book! This truly is the German equal to Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy. Like Hitchhiker's Guide, the story line is rather rambling and is broken into parts (the 13.5 lives of the protagonist, Captain Bluebear) so you can read a bit, put the book away for a while, and pick it up again without missing too much.
