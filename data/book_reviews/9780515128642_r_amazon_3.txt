As always, Patricia Potter does not disappoint.  Her writing is well-crafted, her plot is well-planned, and her characterization is rich and complex.  As an accomplished and well-established author, she doesn’t skimp on any of the characteristics of a good book just to push out another title.  Too many writers do, and it shows.
