Bradbury was a great imaginative storyteller. This is a very short collection from his science fiction output. Dinosaurs fascinated him and these are all creative inventions for how humans might interact with these long extinct creatures. Adults should recognize stories that passed into popular culture and kids might well be fascinated with notions that became common knowledge. Ray, alas, was not a great American poet, but the illustrations for the poetry works really well. A good introduction to a great author for young teens who are old enough to grasp the seriousness incapsulated in these seemingly just thrilling tales.
