I recommend the memoirs for those who enjoy the gamesmanship and people behind the great events of the 20th century.  WSC's six-volume history is a great reference source; this memoir condenses the scope of events of those six volumes in a lengthy but accessible format.  Churchill moves back and forth from trumpeting his insights and triumphs to expressing his frustrations at his inability to secure a better outcome for all parties.
