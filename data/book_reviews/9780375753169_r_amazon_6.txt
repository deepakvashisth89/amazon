As a teacher of a course on the Cold War, I am sometimes appalled by the near total ignorance of young American students regarding this half century and of the nature of the Soviet regime.  This memoir by the wife of prominent Soviet (Jewish) poet Osip Mandelstam gives a clear insight into the daily lives of the thousands of people who were simply set aside by Stalin and his successors for their beliefs.
