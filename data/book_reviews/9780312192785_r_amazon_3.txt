Detective Rebus is one of my favorite characters. He has flaws but takes responsibility for his actions and does not look for sympathy. He is capable of emotional or irresponsible decisions but at the end of the day, he is capable of putting himself in the shoes of those he must oppose. Rankin is a thinking mans author - you have to be involved and you have to be willing to think about the ethics and moral's involved at all times - would you have done it? This is a richly, and thoughtful book that will challenge you emotionally and intellectually.
