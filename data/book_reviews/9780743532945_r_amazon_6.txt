Tami Hoag's "Ashes to Ashes" was a very good book that gathered  interest and intensity the further one read.  I sort of figured part of the mystery out before the end, actually well before the end, but this was of no distraction.  The romance part of the book seemed a bit too contrived and did not really maintain any sort of interest to me.The mystery part and the plot was very well worked out, at least, in my opinion.  Moreover, characterization was very well developed.  I also am very partial to police and detective novels in the Minneapolis/St. Paul, Minnesota area.  It was a bit graphic and violent and this is a consideration for some who do not find this type of reading material interesting.  Towards the end, the book was very hard for me to put down - I found it to be a very good read."Ashes to Ashes" and "Dust to Dust" would be a very good starting place for Tami Hoag in my humble opinion.
