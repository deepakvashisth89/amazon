There are certain books that stand out in ones memory decades after childhood and this is one of them.  I can't even count how many times I read and re-read this as a child. Never got tired of it, and was delighted to find that used copies can still be found--will be getting this for my little nieces.  What was it that was so appealing about this book?  I can say that the first-rate illustrations were one of the factors--they were funny, expressive, and very detailed.  I always found something new on the page that I hadn't noticed with a previous reading.  The story was also engrossing.  I loved mysteries starting from a young age and this was a great introduction to the genre.  The two main characters were also standouts, especially Gwen.  And who could forget Jill's mom, who played such an important role in the story?  There was also a important teaching point to be learned at the end, which is the case for all great children's books.
