This book about heart failure is the sequel to 'Left for Dead', by Dick Quinn, which I also recommend.  The two books are totally different.  If you have one, you do still need to read the other.'Left for Dead' talks about how the author, Dick Quinn, had 2 heart attacks and a couple of bypasses, and was then 'left for dead' by his doctors who said they couldn't do anything more for him.  'Left for Dead' is the story of how he cured himself using cayenne pepper.  It's fascinating, and a must-read for anything with clogged arteries.Dick started writing his second book, 'Death by Deception', but developed heart failure and could not complete the book.  It was completed by his daughter and a friend.  But don't think that just because he died of heart failure that this book has no value.  On the contrary, it's imperative that you read it, to see where he went wrong.  (For example, he was on a medication that he tried to wean himself off without supervision.  The book explains how that if you're on a medication, even a harmful one, if you just stop taking it or try to wean yourself off of it yourself and you do it wrong, your body can be shocked.)  It talks a lot about lifestyle and proper nutrition, and goes far beyond just taking the cayenne that did cure Dick in the first place.Section 1 of the book tells Dick's story, and it's a fascinating narrative.Section 2 explains heart failure - what it is, the causes, the progression, and the standard way that the medical establishment treats it.Section 3 lists herbs that can treat heart failure - what they are, what they do, and how to take them.Section 4, written by a friend of Dick, talks about the world we live in, why so many people have heart failure, and what we can do to help prevent it.In my opinion, these 2 books should be read by anyone with heart disease of any kind, and they should also be read by anyone who wants to avoid having heart disease.
