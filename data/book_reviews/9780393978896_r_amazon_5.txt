For those of you going into this book expecting a beautiful romance tale, I am sorry.  I had read this book when I was younger but recently read it again in a college English class.  The majority of the class was in agreement that this is a WEIRD book with many more revenge elements than love.  I'm among the few who do, indeed, think of it as a love story.I absolutely love the book, but when recommending it to friends I make a point to emphasize that it's a dark read with a lot of mean people doing mean things.
