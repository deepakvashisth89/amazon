Stephen R. Turnbull had penned dozens of books on Japan's feudal warrior caste and the military and social history of that time.  To my knowledge, The Samurai:  a Military History, was his first commercial publication.  It is a scholarly work that grants the English speaking world an accurate picture of Japan's history for the eight centuries beginning from approximately the year 1000 A.D.  Turnbull admirably balances a disciplined academic meticulousness with his readership's need to be entertained.  He accomplishes this by occasionally inserting an interesting anecdote.Asian history was one of my two majors in college over three decades ago.  While my vocation is in an entirely different field, a forgotten detail of Japanese military history does come up in conversations.  This book is invariably the first resource upon which I rely.  Its exhaustive index is greatly appreciated.After three decades the cover is beginning to separate from the sewn signatures.  This kind of deterioration is to be expected given the wear and tear to which the book has been subjected in numerous changes of residence in four different states.This is unquestionably the samurai history treatise to own if you can afford to have only one.Read moreRead less
