I would like to say that this book was delivered several days early, it is in perfect condition, and The price I payed for it( $10) was well worth the price.As with all thing Palladium and Kevin, This book is a mix bag of munchkin and power gamers delight. I did have this book before when it had first come out in the early 1990s, and the things that I liked most back then was the information on Atlantis and the tattooed men. Having yet another Old One on earth, and these were to have been lock away back in the Palladium Fantasy game, Are of such power that one can take on a normal pantheon of gods and defeat them by themselves. Infect, The one that is in this book has imprisoned a deity from the Indian mythos in an Rune weapon, and his minions are hunting down the rest of the gods in this pantheon to do the same.There is no game balance in this book, and one of the adventure hooks in this book is to rescue the trapped Indian god, but you do not find out what happened to him until you find the Rune weapon with him trapped inside it, with no way to get him out. If you are looking for a low MDC world like Robotech, this book is not for you. But if you are looking for extradimensional slavers, that are run by a being that is more powerful than a pantheon of gods by himself, then this book is for you.I still like the Atlanteans and their tattoo men, as they are yes a bit powerful, but manageable in that there are few of them, and they are one of the races that tries to help humans in this world setting. One main theme in this book is all the slave races that are on Atlantis, have the same sort of tragic back story in that they are evil due to being slaves or have been duped into being slaves by this Old One. The standard Palladium backstory of post apocalypse mad max type world, with another apocalypse just around the corner is also here.As with all thing Palladium and Kevin, this is an okay book, but suffers from its age and typos. The art is head and shoulders above the newer books, as it doesn't look like a group of 4 year-olds are its major contributors. Kevin may want to swallow some humble pie and eat a little crow and get Long and Brex back as his main artists.Note: Why have the SPLYN not taken over the world as it would be no problem for them, oh ya according to one of the blurbs in the book a normal human is supposed to take them down.
