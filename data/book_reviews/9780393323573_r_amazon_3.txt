Nothing like a good guide to make the best of a trip, and Barrett is among the best I can imagine. Her prose is memorable and her characters are rich and real. These trips are, like all good journeys, are more than physical travels; they are sojourns through time, intellectual worlds, and topographies of the heart. The catalog of interconnectedness is staggering and the sense of discovery is magnificent.
