I enjoyed the book but then I'm a Little Bighorn nut...Basically it deals with the preliminary results of the archaeological survey of 1984. It's amply illustrated and is even equipped with a rather significant (30 "x42") map showing on one side the fallen markers and on the other it shows where the artifacts were found on the portion of the battlefield that was examined.
