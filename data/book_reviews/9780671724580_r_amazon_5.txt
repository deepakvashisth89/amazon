While the Eagle has landed has a lot of poignant moments, the Eagle has flown is sharp, crisp and un-put-down-able. It is an ideal book to curl up with on a winter evening. The story picks off by saying that Kurt Steiner is alive and has to be rescued form England and he does get rescued. This much is obvious from the cover of the book. But what is not obvious is the beautiful play of characters, emotions and events. The reader is held in the grip of Jack Higgins to such an extent that you sometimes end up supporting the Germans, sometimes the British and in the end realize that you were being played along by a master story teller.
