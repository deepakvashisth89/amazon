Northern Lights is one of the best books I've ever read.  Philip Pullman is an amazing writer and has the power to control you through his books.  Lyra Belacque is a young girl who lives in Jordan Colledge, Oxford.  Her daemon's name is Pantalaimon and together they discover many things.  Her friend, Roger, is kidnapped and Lyra goes North determined to find him.  Her journey itself is marvelously described and very exciting.  The North has armoured bears, with-queens with their clans and a charming, beautiful young lady with more than that to her personality.
