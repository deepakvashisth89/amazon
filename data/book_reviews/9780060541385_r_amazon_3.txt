This book is told from the viewpoint of each of three sisters. While some might be unable to juggle three narrators at once, Maureen Johnson makes each of them unique and fully-realized. I found I rooted for whichever sister I was reading at the time, as each was so convincing and heartfelt in her own way. This is an excellent story of a family dealing with tragedy.
