Buffy, in all her occasionally brainy, perpetually kick-.... attitude, faces her most deceptive opponent with the ever-present help of Giles, her Watcher, Xander, Cordelia, Willow, Oz and Angel, a vampire with a soul. Immortal takes place after Buffy returns to Sunnydale and is forced to face  the fact that she and Angel will never be a couple. They can't even say  "I love you" without Angel losing his soul again, although the  authors do slip at one point and Angel retains his soul. While Buffy deals  with her troubled love life, her mother keeps secrets about her own health  which emphasize the shortness of life to the present Slayer. Veronique, a  new threat in town, displays the ability to jump from body to body while  trying to raise the Triumvirate, an evil which will bring about the  destruction of life on Earth. She has been promised the honor of drinking  the blood of the last man, and no perky blonde Slayer is going to stop her.  Fans of Buffy will adore her latest adventure, and the people in charge of  the Old English Dictionary will find a host of new terms to include in the  next edition.
