Hunting Humans has given me hours of reading pleasure. Being one who is interested in true crime I especially enjoyed reading about the serial killers from Canada. Growing up in Vancouver B.C., I can recall the  paranoia and fear in the teen aged population (which I was a part of) when  Clifford Robert Olson wreaked his terror across the lower mainland. There  were some omissions, such as David William Shearing, who was both a mass  murderer as well as a serial killer in the early 1980's.
