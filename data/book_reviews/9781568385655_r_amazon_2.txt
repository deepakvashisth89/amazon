I chose this book sixteen years ago for a report in my Native American Philosophy class. When I received a review of my work from my professor at the end of the term, he mentioned how many of the other students wrote about my report on The Healing Blanket in their final review of the class. The book is a sensitive account of the lives of three women and their loved ones which is relatable to anyone, regardless of ethnicity, who has gone through difficult times while trying to grow into the life that is your own.Read moreRead less
