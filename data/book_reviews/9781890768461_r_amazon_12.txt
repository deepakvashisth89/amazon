My favourite in the series, probably because I'm a rider and I like the outdoors. It provided a different setting outside the city and I enjoyed it. I know some readers object to too many food/meals descriptions but I don't mind (as long as I have something to nibble on while reading!) and I much prefer that to detailed information on the traffic as it often occurs in most of the books in the series.I recommend the series to people who enjoy a nice read with a mystery to unravel.Read moreRead less
