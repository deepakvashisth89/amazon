This is a wonderful book that exceeded my expectations.  It is a collections of stories (four are different stories about one family; the others are not related to that family.) mostly about white people and their sometimes painful and just plain incompetently managed relationships.  The characterizations are subtle and imaginative.  There is one long story in the end that is about a relatively poor young man and his appalling but finally deeply respectful relations with some black people.  this story blew me away by the subtlety of the relationships and the surprising outcomes.This author is greatly underappreciated.Read moreRead less
