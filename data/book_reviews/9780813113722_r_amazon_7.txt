At best I can describe it as a mix between Grapes of Wrath and To Kill a Mockingbird. A well written tapestry of life in West Virginia at the beginning of the 20th Century, River of Earth embodies the daily struggles of those most affected by the changing nature of life in WV. The characters all show depth and fullness in their existence and you are quickly pulled into their life. If I was forced to make a negative critique it would be that without an antagonist the story lacks in a story arc but rather is more like reading a diary.
