Though at times he lets himself get a bit lofty with his language and introspection, Pelton doesn't linger there long. He drags the reader right back into his world of adventuring with short, tightly written chapters—like snapshots—illustrating exotic locales with vividly drawn descriptions of warlords, villagers, freedom fighters, locals, and foreigners he's met along the way. Really one of the most engaging autobiographies I've read. Pelton's resounding message proliferates throughout this entire piece: Get out there and embrace a little danger.
