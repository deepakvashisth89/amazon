I first read this outstanding book when I was about eight or nine years old, and it made a huge impression on me. It was such a completely new reading experience for me, and it has lost none of its freshness with repeated readings. It is a 'traditional' tale of good versus evil, but it is darker and more exciting than any so-called 'children's' books I've ever read. Absoutely wonderful... it will capture you from the first pages, when Ellen receives a beautiful silver crown for her birthday and decides to go out for a morning walk.
