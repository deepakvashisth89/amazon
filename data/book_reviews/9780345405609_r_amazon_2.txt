I thought this was a very good alternative history. It is intriguing how Mr. Turtledove lays the groudwork for the alliance in How Few Remain (if you are going to read this series, read that paperback first). This book is similar to the earlier World War tetrology, in terms of story flow and  number of characters that it follows, it can get confusing at times, but is  a great way to fully develop alot of the story. One overridding frustration  will be how long it will take for the others to follow (I remember waiting  for the WorldWar series). Now with him doing this AND the 2nd WW series I  worry that it will be longer waits, and/or rushed lower quality. You may  want to wait a few years to pick this series up %^). I do (at the risk of  sounding like a prude) find his little forays into the intimate lives of  the characters pretty pointless. This seems to be a strong thread through  most of the Turtledove I've read, and as far as I'm concerned his most  glaring flaw, maybe he writes romance novels under a secret pen name... ;-)  Be a little careful about these reviews, as many contain spoilers. In fact  stop reading this one here to avoid possible spoilers. I don't totally  understand the criticisms as to how he handled the mormon issues, I  disagree with a previous post that it places the mormons in a bad light,  rather it places the the government in the book in a very bad light.
