I needed to learn a lot about Woody Guthrie to be able to write a piece on him.  This book fit the bill.  It's long, almost 500 pages but a good read.  What a life this man had!  The author does an excellent job of weaving together the facts of Woody's life with a sense of the forces in society.  So we learn about how radio spurred country music, the role of communists in the labor movement, and about several important figures - Alan Lomax, Pete Seeger.  Anyone working in contemporary music should read this book just to get a sense of roots.Read moreRead less
