This is an old book on war from the 1980s when the cold war was looming and the threat of nuclear war seemed real.  The book is a fascinating discussion of various macro and micro aspects of war such as how limited professional wars in the pre-19th century era developed into all-annihilating total wars of the 20th century; how armies train their recruits and officer corps;, how nuclear war theory has evolved, etc.  Insightful macro trends are very well illustrated with specific examples and with many eye opening facts and figures.
