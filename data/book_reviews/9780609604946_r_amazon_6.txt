Siebert's prose style is stunning. Those who think this book is a "failed" children's book (whatever!) miss the point entirely (as did the PW reviewer last month.) Not only does Siebert's style and  composition philosophize on the contemporary dilemma of the sprawling  urbanism of our utterly lost and hopeless humanity, but his eye is sharp  enough to focus on the permanence of this.
