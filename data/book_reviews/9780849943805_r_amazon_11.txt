Having read Blessed Child, I had high expectations of book 2 in the series.A Man Called Blessed is a muscular thriller set in the powder keg of the Middle East. With gripping suspenseful, action packed scenes, politics, spirituality, and romance it's a terrifically entertaining novel. It's fast paced and full of friction,  great drama, and supernatural wonder.Caleb may be the central character but for me Rebecca stole the show. Her life is a shining testimony to one of my all time favourite themes: redemption.A Man Called Blessed is quite simply, great.Read moreRead less
