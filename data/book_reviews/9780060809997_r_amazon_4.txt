I am very glad to have found Peter Lovesey. This is the second book in his Victorian series starring Sergeant Cribb and the long suffering Constable Thackery.  Mr. Lovesey recreates the Victorian atmosphere very accurately.  And this is a new form of English cozy crime.  Mr. Lovesey seems to want to delve into the Victorian sports era.  This particular book is about "knuckle fighting" without the mittens as they call it.  There was a huge following of this sport in England at this time despite the fact that even watching was illegal.  We get right into the whole area and learn all about the clothes, the gear and the lingo.  On top of that it's a cracking good thriller.  I certainly intend to read more in this series.
