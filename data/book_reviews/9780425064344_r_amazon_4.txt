I absolutely love Dune (my dad got me into it recently), and I really enjoyed reading it. It is a bit confusing a times, but luckily there are appendixes at the end of the book as well as terminology of the imperium all in alphabetical order (which helps A LOT when you get confused on some of the terminology of Dune), cartographic notes, a map, and an afterword. Aside from the fact that it was a bit confusing for me at times though, it's an amazing book and I had so much fun reading it as well as watching all of the Dune movies out there.
