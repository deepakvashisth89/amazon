I had this book on my shelf for a long time and then finally by chance reached for it the other day. Howard Owen is the type of writer that possesses a deep understanding of what it means to live in the South. He draws from his memories and he knows intimately the people of the areas that he writes about. This is the story of the two Sweatt brothers; one is Tom Ed and he is running for the office of senator in NC and his hapless brother Jack AKA Lucky.  The novel is extremely well written and I believe that it says a lot about it's author. The novel follows along as Tom Ed tries his best to weedle and talk his way into the voter's hearts. Lucky is brought in to chauffeur his brother around on his campaign stops. A subtle vein of humor runs throughout the book and it hits home when politics or religion is in the author's sights.
