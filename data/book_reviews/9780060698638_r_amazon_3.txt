I chose this rating because the Harper's is the most useful and complete that I have ever found. One reviewer called it encyclopedic in nature. I would agree. The editors attempt to not take any popular slant but to have a balanced approach to the work is commendable and gives strength to the scholarship. One reviewer gave this Dictionary a one star rating because it didn't take the word of God as just the word of God. If you are looking for a resource that will simply enhance your own view this one will at least challenge you.I purchased my first Harper Bible Dictionary while in seminary, in fact it was my third book for my collection (1967).
