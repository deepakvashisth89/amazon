When Pierre, a waiter and long time associate at Rusterman's, is killed in the South guest room of Wolfe's house it is considered to be a family affair. Wolfe is distracted by thinking there is some type of electronic surveillance involved because the events take place about the time of the Watergate affair. There is no client so Wolfe considers the involvement of Saul, Fred and Orrie to make this a family affair. Many hours of investigation and two additional murders are ultimately connected to Pierre's death. Archie and Wolfe are both arrested and spend a weekend in jail. Their detective licenses are revoked. When Archie finally connects all the dots he feels Wolfe has deliberately shut him out.  There are dramatic turns that bring about a startling conclusion. I highly recommend this final book in the Nero Wolfe saga. I have re-read these books many times and never grow tired of them.
