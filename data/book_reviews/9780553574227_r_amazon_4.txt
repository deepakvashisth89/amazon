I really liked this book.  I was concerned before I read it that I might not like it.  Some of the other reviews had me worried.  However, I was pleasantly surprised.  I have not read any of her other books so I cant compare it to those but I would definetly recommend this read.  It is unusual to have a hero that does not have the perfect warriors body but I liked having that different twist.
