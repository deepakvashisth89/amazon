I will comment on the appearance of the book, the superficial aspects, as there are enough reviews about the content.(If you ask me, yes, the content is great, that's why I bought it - it's a top-line introduction to B'ism.)I want to talk about the below points, and I hope the publisher Inner Traditions International (Vermomt) actually cares to read what the result of their output is.---------------------------------------Book Size:I don't understand the logic of why this book has been printed in a large-size format; it's clunky, clumsy, and unnecessary. It could have easily been printed like a normal book, in fact like how some of the older editions were - easier to handle, store, and read.Paper Quality:The paper stock used is quite thin and therefore delicate. Of course, you see the print from the other page, in addition, you must also be careful while underlining the text, as it could easily perforate the pages.Print Quality:The paper is yellowish in color - which in my opinion, is lovely. But the font color is light greyish, and not towards black. Because the paper is so thin, if they printed any darker, your brain will scramble trying to read (too much see-through from the other side). But the current option is also not optimum; the font needs to be a little darker. But that can only happen when the paper quality improves.Pagination & Layout:The text layout is a bit all over the place. On some pages, the text is quite close to the bottom of the page edge, and on some pages it is an inch above the border. This is quite random, irritating, and inexplicable. This is a case study in lazy disinterested design. Shameful.Cover Design:This book has gone through some lovely cover designs and has reached where it is today - a tacky, PowerPoint-assembled image and text. Disappointing.Read moreRead less
