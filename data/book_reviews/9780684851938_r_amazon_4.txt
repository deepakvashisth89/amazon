I am having a little difficulty staying interested while reading this book. The parts that focus on the actual subjects/cases are extremely interesting, however most of the reading consist of all the things that happened in between the cases. There is allot of material expressing the atmosphere, transportation, rooming, conditions, ect... that really could have been left out. I suspect that he wanted to give the reader an idea of what all he had personally experienced, which takes the focus off the subjects. I am still making my way through there are 18 chapters and I have read 13. Normally I would be finished this in a day but as I said it's challenging keeping interest. All in all if you want a tiny bit or a glimpse as to what is an example of past life and reincarnation than sure read the book. I personally feel there is much better information out there, for example Dolores Cannon book on Between Death and Life is 1 example or any number of books that talk about NDE(near death experiences), past lives, and after life.
