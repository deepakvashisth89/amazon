I bought this book because I had never heard of the author and it is published by Apollo.  I read their publication of My Son, My Son written by one of my favorite authors Howard Spring and thought these publishers must be people of taste.  Yes, it is well written and it is a desperately depressing story where absolutely everything goes wrong in this sad little community.  I really do like to have happiness in my life and to feel that I am enriched by books I read.  This one did not do that for me.  I think it best to put it out of my mind.Read moreRead less
