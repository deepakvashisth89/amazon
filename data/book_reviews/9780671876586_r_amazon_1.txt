In the introduction to this book the author states that he polished the original stories and removed several literary and physics potholes...I find that to be frightening. The originals must have been mind meltingly flawed.Pros: Text sentence by sentence is coherent. Most paragraphs hold together ok, though many are stilted and/or bombastic.Cons: Too many literary, in-universe logic, and coherency ones to list. My pet peeve is that folks who write military fiction should either have some military experience or at least be able to fake it well.A grasp of basic physics might be good for space based fiction as well. Also some ideas about radio, biology (even made up biology), etc.There's just too many "it works in this spot because the plot line needs it" and "it won't work in that spot because the it'd hurt the plot line". That's bad in magical fantasy, but we're talking hardware.If the intro hadn't said these had been polished I'd cut a bit more slack for being a beginning author.But far too often, in some parts every 1.5 pages, I'd throw the book down and go "augh!".Bottom line: I got this at a booksale's last day $5 a grocery bag... I still had room in my bag when I left, I should have left a little more.Read moreRead less
