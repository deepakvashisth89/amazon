Rurik was a warrior sent to spy out the strength of his Prince's rival and possible usurper. Zora was the bastard half sister to princess Hermione. Jealousy drove Hermione to plot Zora's abduction in order to remove her from their father's court. Rurik saves Zora from a brutal raping and returns with his men to the Grand Prince.
