Michael Canada and S. J. Gaither showcase their talent once again through the eyes of Poppy; a two thousand year old witch with plenty of supernatural friends.  This book had me reading from start to finish.  I could not put it down and it is a shining example of literary genius.  It's getting hard to run across good old dark humor but fear not there's plenty to go around in this book.  The many colorful characters of their world are reunited once again as Poppy tries to figure out who has put a contract out on her family.  A very excellent read.Read moreRead less
