This is a graphic novel and artist's book by Max Ernst. It comprises images created by cutting up and re-organizing illustrations from Victorian encyclopedias and novels and it is a surrealistic masterpiece, full of strange images of naked women, creatures with bird heads, dragon wings in rooms that may have mirrors that reflect something else or simply flooded with water. It is divided in chapters that correspond to the days of the week and to the "seven capital elements".This book is the collection of full page reproductions of the work.Read moreRead less
