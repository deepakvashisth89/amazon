Virginia Elizabeth Middlebrook's first marriage had been a short taste of  hell, making her vow to never marry again.  A.C. Ryerson had married too  young and swore off love.  When A.C. and Virginia first met, they knew thay  could become friends.  Soon, they overcame Virginia's fears and become  lovers.  Marriage, however, is another story entirely.  Then A.C. wins a  stunning emerald and diamond bracelet in a poker game, unleashing a series  of suspicious accidents.Another delightful tale by JAK.  A.C. and  Virginia are not as driven as some of Krentz's usual heroes and heroines.  They are comfortable with each other from the first, only shying from  committment.
