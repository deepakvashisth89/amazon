I didn't think this book was a five, but mostly because it is somewhat choppy and doesn't flow real well; but I gave it a four because it is memorable. I liked the weaving of life with birds and the symbolism of the refuge to Terry Tempest's soul. I thought some of her writing was forced and didn't flow real well, but some of her insights are really incredible and enjoyable to read. The story is heart-wrenching at times and gives an inside view into what people and families go through when a loved one has cancer. For the most part I enjoyed this book.Read moreRead less
