This book is a fine example of what a person will do for the love of his family and freedom.  Although Orestes was part of the privileged class, an officer in the Air Force of communist Cuba, he became disenchanted when he was exposed to the realities of the outside world that had been denied to him by the communist regime.  Orestes bravely met the challenge issued by Raul Castro - "If he had the guts to steal one of my Migs, let's see if he has the guts to come and get his family."  That is exactly what Orestes did with God as his co-pilot.  Orestes rescued his family and flew them to freedom.  This is a must read by people that value freedom.Read moreRead less
