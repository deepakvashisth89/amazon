Lots of tasty treats here.  One of the ingredients used quite a bit is graham wafer crackers which are soooo good, but hard to find in England unless you find them in an international section in a store, but digestive or tea biscuits could be used instead, probably close enough anyways.Another ingredient that is used a lot is coconut, (and nuts in general), so if you aren't a fan of coconut there will be quite a few recipes that you will be passing over, but Jean Pare who is Canadian and Canadians (that's me!) in general tend to like coconut.  So, I'm in heaven with all these recipes. I've tried a few now and they all turn out well.  Some - a bit sweet - but it's easy to cut the sugar down a bit.So many of these recipes I had grown up with, but had forgotten all about, so getting this book was a nice reminder and a trip down memory lane.
