Sweet Valley Jr. High is a great book by Francine Pascal.  It is about Jessica and her twin sister Elizabeth Wakefield.  Jessica is in trouble for writing an inappropriate poster for their school's homecoming game.  Instead of detention,she is assigned to be the head of the dance committee.  She loves the idea of being on the dance committee.  This is a chance for  her to become popular.  She gets all of her friends to be on her committee.  They don't get along very well with each other.  See how Elizabeth helps  Jessica out with her problems.  I recommend this book for mostly girls.Read moreRead less
