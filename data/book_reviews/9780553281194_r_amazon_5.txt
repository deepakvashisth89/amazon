This is an older book that purports to be a novelization of a Vietnam War special operation that went bad. It is generally thought that the author (writing under a pseudonym) is a former special operator and much of the combat accounts "rings true." However, some of the beginning of the book seems dubious: such as the narrator taking reloading supplies out on the mission and another character having a "sniper-ized" M3 submachine gun.The accounts of combat seem pretty realistic and the narrator is not a superman, he gets beaten and nearly killed. The ending is rather odd; there is no big resolution and the narrative pretty much ends with "I got out of the hospital and got a medical discharge...the end." My personal opinion is that the last half of the book is based on a real mission but the first half, not so much. Still definitely worth a read.Read moreRead less
