Just reread the book for the first time since I first read it 10 or so years ago. I've decided it's one of my all time favorite books, but I won't go into my reasons why because I would have to include some spoilers. I will say that if you're familiar with this author--of whom I'm a big fan in general--it's a bit lighter than some of her other works, nor is it part of a series, nor is it a conventional "whodunnit." If you like stories about quirky yet thoroughly believable characters (not always the case) who follow their quixotic quests to the bitter end, you should definitely give this one a try. And especially if you're any kind of an Anglophile. However, be warned: it's not all sweetness and light; there are some very dark passages, and if you're at all bothered by the occasional four-letter word, you might not like it as much as me.Read moreRead less
