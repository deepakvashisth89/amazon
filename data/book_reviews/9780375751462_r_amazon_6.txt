Not what I had expected. I wanted a hardcover version to supplement my worn and much annotated paperback. On the plus side the font, font size and print quality are very good. On the negative side, the paper, cover and overall quality are poor. Printed in India to offer a low price point. I guess I should have known better. There is a very amateurish anonymous biography or Darwin that is poorly written and riddled with errors and important omissions.
