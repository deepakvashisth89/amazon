Roger Angell is the best baseball writer of the last half-century, much better (for my time and money) than the other Roger - Kahn - most people think of. I have always especially enjoyed his five season collections, and it took me a long time to get to this one, mostly because this set of five seasons occurred prior to my own fandom (hey, I was only 6 years old at the end of the last season covered in this book). In any event, this is Angell at his best - and as I say, his best is as good as it gets.
