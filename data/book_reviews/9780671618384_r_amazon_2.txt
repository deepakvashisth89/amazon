I read this book last year after borrowing it from my local library and it was an extremely quick and entertaining read! If you love to hear about success stories, principles, and dealing with adversity; or just love to eat those mall-bound cookies, then this book is for you! I just wish she'd write another biography/memoir as I hear she's very good on the motivational speaking circuit and know that she has since divorced her husband, her kids are all grown up, and Mrs. Fields now has pre-packaged cookies in supermarkets.
