It's transparent that E.V. Thompson is a believer in transparency, 'cause this is one transparent book. Still an okay book; not boring, not fascinating. Just know that it's obvious in its wherefores and therefores-- it's easy to tell how a storyline will end from the time it begins-- and relies heavily on convenient events to advance the narrative.For a book with a pretty high body count and characters who are prostitutes, it's surprisingly along the lines of something Grandma would enjoy reading. 1870s, miners, do-gooding, no sex scenes, churchy leading lady, celibate leading man.
