In my view either reading le Carre's novel the "The Little Drummer Girl" or watching the TV series are both delightful, though-provoking and somewhat challenging experiences - and you do not need to do both. BUT, to do both is such an indulgence as it shows you the inside thoughts of a wide variety of characters, and the exceptional skill of the writers.
