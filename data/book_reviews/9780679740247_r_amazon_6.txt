I bought this book to read before reading William's book, When Women Were Birds for a book group.  Refuge explores the relationship between 3 generations of Morman women: William's grandmother, mother and herself, and the death of her mother from cancer.  However what I found far more interesting was the other exploration  she writes about, the Wild Life Refuge she works in.  I didn't know about the extent of this place and its amazing wildlife.  The book covers a time period in the 80s when Great Salt Lake rose higher than it had for a number of years and the effect this had on the surrounding communities as well as the Refuge itself.
