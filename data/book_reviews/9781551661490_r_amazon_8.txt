The story is nice, typical, early Diana Palmer writing with the main characters hostile towards one another as a cover for their true feelings of attraction which eventually ends in love. I wish the author had added more to the ending or at least provided an epilogue so the readers are not left wondering what happened as far as when did they marry. Did they move to New York  or stayed in Atlanta? So many questions, but no answers.
