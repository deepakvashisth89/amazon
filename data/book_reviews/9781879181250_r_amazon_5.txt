Have read this a couple of times and recommend it...21st century, extraordinary, mind blowing account that I, for one, believe completely.  Well written with a sense of humor and the illustrations are works of art.  Nobody would make up or could make up such an account.  I say that because her story stretches the bounds of what one considers possible.  Glad something similar hasn't happened to me, as far as I know.  But I very much appreciate being taken into her world of bizarre, extraterrestrial surprises vicariously through this book.
