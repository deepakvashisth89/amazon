I enjoyed this story. It's not a continuation of the story of the Thistledown which was what I was expecting in the third book. Instead it concentrates on the adventures of Olmy ( a younger Olmy than in the previous two books) as an undercover investigator on a planet colonised by dissidents from the Thistledown. It's completely different to the previous two books in setting and content and I found it quite absorbing.Read moreRead less
