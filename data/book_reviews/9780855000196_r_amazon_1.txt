A balance of knowledge and understanding of both the man and his ideas.  Bennett's own travels and search into the known and unknown lend help in understanding the unknowable Gurdieff.  I have greater expectations on my 2nd read of this book for there are hidden gems that hopefully will be more understandable the second time around.  Only the very best are worthy a second read.  Most books are a scan or a reference tool for me but Bennett in this and others of his are a slow meditative, think, absorb type manuscript.
