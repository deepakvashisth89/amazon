This book really details what the Indian badlands are like. Based on the true story of Phoolan Devi, the woman who became their 'most wanted' it is a fascinating insight into the harsh society of caste and hierarchy that abounds in the villages and the areas of Uttar Pradesh, Rajastan etc. She had to fight injustice from the age of eleven and took out her own form of rough justice on higher caste males and officialdom who had all let her down or stolen from her family. I picked this out of print book up purely because I became friends with the late author's husband and I'm really glad that I did, it is a wonderful read.Read moreRead less
