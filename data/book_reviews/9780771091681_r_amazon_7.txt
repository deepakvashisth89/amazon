Employing the narrative device of a found manuscript (successfully used by Edgar Rice Burroughs and William Hope Hodgson, later reinvented as the found footage film) James De Mille recounts the tale of a mariner lost in the Antarctic wilds who chances upon an ancient remnant of humanity with echoes of Greece, Israel and others. Of course, the purpose is to explore our own civilization (especially the culture of acquisition and ownership) not theirs, but the author does an excellent job of presenting a vivid picture of a world that might have been but, unfortunately, never was.Read moreRead less
