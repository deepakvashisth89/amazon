I wanted something light and upbeat to read (as opposed to my typical murder mysteries) and remembered a book I enjoyed a few years ago called "Eat Cake" by Jeanne Ray.  "Step Ball Change" turned out to be better than I expected, clever and unique story, great character descriptions -- I feel like I knew each one of them personally -- and hysterical bits of one-liners thrown in when you least expect it.  I couldn't seem to put the book down and was disappointed when it ended.  I will now be ordering the rest of Ms. Ray's novels and can't wait for the first one to get here.
