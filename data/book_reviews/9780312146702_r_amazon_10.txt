The original premise of the Mary Russell series annoyed me so much I sat down to read fully intending to denigrate. I have not been able, and am approaching the 6th novel, trying to ration myself.  The strictest of Holmes' fans may object but the rest of us (particularly the females) just enjoy the atmosphere, engaging prose, and worthy protagonists with integrity, who generally stay within character. Having visited Jerusalem a while back, I found the narrative spot-on and the history fascinating. I love the 'Wimsical' cameos and the humorous Sophie's World convolutions.
