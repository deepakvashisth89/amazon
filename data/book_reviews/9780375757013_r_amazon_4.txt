Can you really say much about Great Expectations that hasn't already been said countless times?  I'm so happy I finally got around to reading it.  I love Charles Dickens.  You know that great feeling when you find an author that just gets you as a reader.  Everything they write just sings to your readerly heart.  Charles Dickens is that author for me.  I have loved everything that I've read by him and am thankful I don't have to wait weeks or months for the next installment.  In short, I loved it and highly recommend it to everyone.
