Poor Fiona, everyone in her family remembered things except her. If she was asked to bring her mother something, she would bring back the wrong thing. Fiona felt very bad about herself.All of the family tried to give Fiona little ideas to help her remember things and she did them all.  Does Fiona finally remember things like the rest of her family?This is an adorable story and the illustratins are outstanding.
