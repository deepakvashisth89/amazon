This book is fascinating and contained quite a bit of amazing information about the early days of Illinois.  It helped me with my family research, as they are mentioned in the book and verifies their dates of arrival in Illinois and where they settled.  The accounts of daily life, the hardships and early settling helped me to appreciate how blessed we are today with all that we have.  For those that would like to learn more about the early days of Illinois and detailed descriptions of life back then, I would highly recommend this book.
