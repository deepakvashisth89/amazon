Death on Cape CodFlocks of tourists were arriving in Billingsgate for Old Home Week, unaware that odd things had been happening:  fires in Town Hall, stolen keys, and shots in the dark.  Asey Mayo is called in to save the day, hardly expecting to confront a corpse down in sinister Hell Hollow.  And this is only the beginning."It is great sport to have Asey, his cousin Syl, and his cousin's wife Jennie once again rushing down sandy lanes and through pine woods, struggling through cranberry bogs and bayberry thickets, outwitting fog and the State Police at the same time..."
