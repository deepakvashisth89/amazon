I like John Sandford's prey series books because they are fast moving, exciting and not too far from reality.  I also like the continuity of his books, so if reading them in order, it's like reading one long book.  Summarizing one of his Prey books is summarizing all.  I have been a dedicated reader since childhood and have always dreaded coming to the final page knowing that the story has ended.  Even a happy conclusion was a sad thing because as absorbed as I had become in the story I would feel I had been deserted and left alone by the books characters whose stories were continuing even if unwritten.  With Sandford's Prey series I know I have only been left behind for only as long as it takes to purchase the next book in the series.
