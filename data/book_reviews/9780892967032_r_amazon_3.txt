Lansdale tells us in the intro that this was intended as a sort of cockeyed homage to James M Cain...and since every Cain novel I've read has broadly speaking the same basic plot, it's pretty clear early on where things are headed. The plot takes something of a detour, though, so for a while it feels like it's in the process of becoming another kind of story altogether, before getting back on track. This might have annoyed me in less sure hands, though Lansdale uses it to lend the fundamentally nihilistic situation some unexpected emotional weight.
