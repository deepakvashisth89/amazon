I really wanted to like this book.I love California and it sounded interesting from the blurb, like a spiritual quest, but I have to admit that within a few chapters I was honestly bored silly. So much so I practically had to make myself finish the book.In reading her words I have found nothing that inspires any real sympathy or liking for this author. On the contrary, while the prose is well-written it truly lacks in the way of content.Mary Morris talks about being desperately "poor" but thinks nothing of asking her folks to contribute towards the cost of a live-in nanny. She makes a couple of  trips to Vegas, and other places over the course of the book, dropping a "couple hundred" without thought, uses her credit card pretty liberally and yet she claims that getting $50 a week in childcare would make a HUGE difference in her lifestyle.Poor pitiful me, the tale says, but you know I just don't see the sacrificing going on here. If she's not doing Vegas, she's off to the new age massage therapist, et all. If you ask me it's NO WONDER she's always broke at the end of the month, sheesh...As for the rest of the situation. Morris thinks nothing of being her lover's "other woman" for several years, but then faults him for simply being the very non-committal person she got involved with in the first place? Not that the man isn't a two-timing deadbeat, but what does she expect that he will be faithful to HER, the perfect parent to HER child, when it's obvious he couldn't be faithful to his first wife or a particularly good parent to his first set of kids?It's nice that she finally found a better relationship situation and a decent guy to love her and her child but in all honesty while reading her book I had a really hard time believing either she or her lover were mature enough to be have been raising a baby at that particular junction in their lives.In this book the author comes off as very self-involved, often shallow, and frankly a bit self-serving towards the end.Harsh words?Maybe, but that's honestly what I got while reading this one.Every now and again I have to admit that I read a book and I have to wonder exactly why it's considered fit to be published.This is definitely one of those books.The editor who wrote the check for this narcissistic drivel?Should be reduced to flipping burgers about now, IMHO...Read moreRead less
