This was one of the more interesting history books I have read, if only because the style was so unique. The author is an English professor, so instead of restating facts and interviews, or doing investigative writing, Hellman analyzes Kennedy's life from a literary stand point. His main focus is the construction of Kennedy's image, including his pre-presidential years, his years in office, and the Camelot myth he left behind after death. It's an interesting take on history, one that illuminates the public relations aspect of public office.Read moreRead less
