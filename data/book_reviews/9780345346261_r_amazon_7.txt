I re-read this novel after about 36 years because I thought it might provide some perspective on the alleged fraud in the 2020 presidential election. It did, and I also got more out of the novel on its own terms than I did from the first reading. I have only one criticism. On page 267 Vidal refers to Hendricks as being from Illinois. As a Hoosier, I can say categorically that Hendricks was governor of Indiana. This error is the only reason I don’t give the novel five stars.Read moreRead less
