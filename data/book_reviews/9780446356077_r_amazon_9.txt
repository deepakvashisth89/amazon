I first started this series with The Listening Sky and so when I heard that Garrick Rowe had a book of his own I was excited to read it.After coming on here and reading some reviews I wasn't all that excited. It made Katy seem like a shrew and Garrick some love sick fool. Oh, how wrong they were!Here's my review!Katy and her sister and niece are in an abandoned town waiting for word from her sisters no good husband. They are running out of supplies and live in constant fear that somebody is going to come along and attack them... It does, but is in the shape of a large feline!The man to save Katy from the cougar is none other than Rowe himself, a man that they have seen around for the past week but has never actually bothered to introduce himself! Then Rowe walks away... Charming, huh?So, Rowe then see some men killing another and worries for the safety of the women and rushes over to their house and orders them to hide and he'll take care of everything.He kills two men but get's shot in the process and Katy and her sister save his life.Now, during this we find out that Rowe finds Katy very familiar and the word 'Nightrose' keeps coming to mind when he thinks of her. He shouldn't want her. She's blond and blue eyed and thanks to his racist half brother Rowe tends to go towards brunettes, but there's something about her. He's known her before. Loved her before and he intends to love her in this life, too.Sweet, right? Wrong!All the reviews spoke of Katy being this shrew because she knocked Rowe back time and time again, but I could completely relate to Katy and why she wouldn't want to be around this man. She doesn't know him, trust him or even like him and he's telling her that despite her wishes, needs and intentions she's staying in town with him and they're going to be together... A little stalkerish, right?I did enjoy there story, but some reviewers have got it right. It went from 'Stay away from me you randy billy goat' to 'I love you so much' in only a few sentences and was brushed off with Katy being afraid of her only feelings... No, the dude was just too intense.While I didn't like the mention of rape, whipping and other things I did understand that it was from a different period where things like public whipping was kind of the norm. It just wasn't for me, I was on Katy's side there! I just didn't like the fact that Rowe completely disregarded Katy's opinion (yet again) and did what he wanted. And Katy? She just accepted it after a few words from him! Obviously he has more power and more responsibility, but if she felt so strongly about something, then she should have stuck it out, not swooned in his arms. *eyeroll*I really do like this series, but this isn't the best out of them and I was disappointed by their story since I was told how funny it was (there only one funny moment in their relationship). The sister is beyond idiotic, never trusting her own sisters instincts and fears and basically arranging it for Katy to go off with two single men by herself... This from a time when women were branded loose by having their hair down? When women shouldn't swear or show any kind of emotion. It just didn't add up and I actually truly didn't like her sister because of it. The only person I liked was Katy... for about half of the book!The best out of this series is The Listening Sky. I recommend you to read that one.Read moreRead less
