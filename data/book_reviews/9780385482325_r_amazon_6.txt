I find Jan Wong to be an excellent storyteller. Wherever she is, whatever is happening to or in front of her - you are right there experiencing it alongside her.Other reviewers take issue with with her evolving views on Maoism. That did not concern me. I suspect there are more than a few of us who at a certain age, didn't hold beliefs that turned out to be wrong or naive. With further information her views changed and she doesn't hide any of this to make herself look better. It turns out that, like the rest of us, she made assumptions and came to realise she was wrong about something.If you want to know what it was like for a foreigner in China during it's Cultural Revolution - read this book. If you want to read a first hand witness account of the Tiananmen Square Massacre in 1989 - read this book.
