This review is for the Library of America Hawthorne "Collected Novels" book.  I reviewed the Hawethorne tales as "Predictable" due to his last work being published in 1850.  The books from the Library of America are of very good high quality.  Page-paper is very thin but not bible-thin, so these books might not work well for young readers who may tear the delicate paper.
