A good Michener tale but not as engrossing of other books of his which I have read and loved like Chesapeake, Centennial, Covenent, Hawaii, Space or TexasIn the epilogue it is explained that this was originally a few chapters about the gold rush in the book Alaska (an awesome read) but was removed and become a novel in its own right because it was felt that Alaska was too long.It’s interesting but never exciting, there are very few characters in the book and for me just not as riveting as many of his other books.If you like Michener, it’s an OK read but not an epic in my opinion.Read moreRead less
