I picked up this book a while ago, maybe around the Columbus Quincentennial (sp?) in 1992. It could be that I saw it at the Smithsonian American History museum, and went for it, to learn more about Native Americans, but lost touch of that desire. Indeed there is a lot of information that is put forth. But there are not many footnotes to pursue what is stated. One has to rely on the bibliography for further reading. The pictures are quite worthwhile, those of Native clothing especially.I was sad to realize that the author was more interested in subjective comments than in objectively telling the story. I personally don't care about what he thinks about Christian missionaries. There is too much editorial on missionaries, as compared with a detailed description of broken treaties and the Trail of Tears. Some of his descriptions of Native life are a bit mean-spirited and degrading.On the one hand, you would guess the book is for the uninformed, but there is much laid down by the author that presumes the reader has some previous understanding of certain historical events.I will try some other work to satisfy my curiosity on Native American life.Read moreRead less
