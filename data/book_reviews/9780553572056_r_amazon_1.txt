I've heard Sitka is a quaint little drinking town with a bit of a fishing problem. Where else could such a hero as Cecil Younger be spawned? We Americans love John Wayne, Matt Dillon, even Jack Burton type heroes. Men constant in credo, fearlessly and successfully righting wrongs, punishing crooks and bullies as we all would like to. P.I. Younger is deeply flawed; more Jack Taylor than Jack Reacher. He's like trucker in Little Fear's song, ...kicked by the wind, robbed by the sleet, had my head stoved in but I'm still on my feet and I'm still----willin'. Cecil Younger is willing to hope for love, to find someone who will tell him the truth and pay him to sleuth and walk face first into a muddy boot. He is funny, self-deprecating, sometimes bumbling, sometimes competent but always willing. Straley is a terrific storyteller, rough-country poet, amateur naturalist and philosopher.
