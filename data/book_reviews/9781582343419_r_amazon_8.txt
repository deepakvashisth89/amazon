I really like the basis of the story but the writing is too drawn out.  A lot of it wasn't even necessary.  I found myself skimming and skipping pages and don't feel like I missed anything at all. She'll take pages to describe something, than she'll describe the same thing from the point of view of a different person later on in the book.  She has a couple of parts where she lists example of things, and it goes on and on like Bubba Gump listing the many ways shrimp can be cooked.  As others have said, the typos are plentiful.  I have it on Kindle and whoever they used to transfer it does not have english as their first language.  Seeing the number "1" in place of the letter "I" was common. Character names spelled wrong.  A couple of times they had a word so mispelled that it took me a second to recognize what it was supposed to be.  Actually reading it out like a kindergartener because it was new to me.But, as I said, I'm torn.  I think it's a good story with some great characters.
