The author, having known Castro and having been granted a number of interviews, tries hard and succeeds at being objective. Castro who had met great resistance from the old communist party of Cuba eventually accepted aid and advice from Soviet Russia. One wonders what might have happened if the United States had aided him instead. This is yet another story of how the US's refusal to accept nationalism as a major force in the world drove many countries into the arms of the Russians.
