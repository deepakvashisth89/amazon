This book is a really fascinating social history of London in the mid 1800's.  The story of the train robbery, while interesting, takes a back seat to the social and cultural changes that are taking place.  Dickens portrays how the industrial revolution affects the poor; Crichton portrays the rising middle class.  This is very different from the other books by Michael Crichton that I have read, but no less captivating.Read moreRead less
