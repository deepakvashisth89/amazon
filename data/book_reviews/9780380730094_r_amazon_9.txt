I've read all of John R Maxim's Bannerman books - Very difficult to get hold of in the UK, hence having to obtain a ratty, out of print version from the US.  Nevertheless, enjoyed this and all of this series.  When asked to provide a synopsis to friends I've recommended the books to, the best I've come up with is that a bunch of loveable assassins take over a Connecticut town which used to be a CIA training facility and spend their time defending it in a variety of bizarre, effective and brutal ways. Characterisation is good, albeit that all his characters appear to share similar mannerisms like closing one eye when being thoughful (I don't know anyone who hasn't had some form of brain trauma who does that!) All of John Maxims books also share the line "You're a good man, (insert name)"  This being said, all of these books are page-turners and should have been reproduced as films years ago.Read moreRead less
