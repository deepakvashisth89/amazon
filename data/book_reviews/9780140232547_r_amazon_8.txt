This book should not be lost in the crowd. Rarely do I cherish a novel so much that years after reading it and rereading it I post a review.  An unusual relationship develops between the two women who rock the ill babies in the neonatal unit.  As they rock the babies, they reflect on their own stories and losses. Rich storytelling.
