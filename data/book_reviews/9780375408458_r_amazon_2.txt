The novel, Autopsy, is full of political references and isn't as well written as earlier books. There are extensive descriptions of possessions that are indicative of an elite class. The book ends abruptly with a simplistic reveal of the killer. Very little of the suspense that has been a hallmark  of earlier novels. It would have been nice to see a little bit of the old Kay Scarpetta that would have put Maggie in her place one way or another. Overall, while it was somewhat entertaining, it seemed disjointed and did not flow well. I hope that book 26 is better.Read moreRead less
