As a converted vegetarian I found this book very useful to gather facts to defend myself when people disagreed with me. I found the answers to common questions very useful as well as funny. The book as a whole was lighthearted but shocking at the same time. If a meat eater were to read it, I would think that it would persuade them to turn vegetarian. So be warned!
