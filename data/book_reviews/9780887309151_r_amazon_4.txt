Direct from Dell gives an inside look at how Michael Dell started Dell Computer from the very beginning, and how the changing business environment over the years influenced how Dell evolved as a company. Dell talks about the good and bad times at Dell, and how he and his company learned from their mistakes. This book only talks about Michael Dell's childhood very briefly, but it's enough to give you insight on how Michael Dell thought at an early age. The audio version of this book is read by Michael Dell. The audio version isn't a word for word verbatim copy of the printed book, but it's never the less an excellent complement.
