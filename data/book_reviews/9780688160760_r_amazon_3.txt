I found this book in a classroom where I was substitute teaching on a read-a-thon day. The plot is predictable and tired, the characters are fake and the writing style leaves much to be desired. It does address an important subject for tweens: identity and self-image, and if this book gets a young person to read then there is nothing wrong with that. But there are so many books out there that are of higher quality that I would hate to see this one chosen for a student.Read moreRead less
