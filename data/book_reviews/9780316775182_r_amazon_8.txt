I'm going to show my ignorance here and say that before reading this book, I had no idea what Purim is. Reading Purim Play was an educational experience for ME, and I enjoyed it. I especially appreciated the recipe in the back. There is mention of apricot-orange pastries throughout the story, and now I can't wait to try one! :)In case you're like me, Purim is a Jewish holiday celebrating Queen Esther. I'm a Christian, so I'm very familiar with that story. I just didn't realize Queen Esther had her own holiday. If you'd like to learn some of the traditions of others, definitely give this one a try.
