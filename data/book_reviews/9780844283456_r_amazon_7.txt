I decided to buy this book for my Japanese study group, so people would have something to work on while we wait for everyone to show up. Naturally, I have members at different levels, so I figured this would work well. When I read the product description I was a little confused by this part, "They begin with Japanese-to-Spanish..." I decided to purchase the book anyway, under the assumption it is a typo -- and it is. The first 24 puzzles are JP -> EN and the rest are EN -> JP. Like someone mentioned, there is no blacking out in the puzzles (contrary to the picture on the front cover), which makes it hard to know where words end. If you strain hard enough, you can see VERY slight shading on SOME of the puzzles, on the rest -- all white. I can't understand why something like this would be overlooked when making a crossword puzzle. It seems to me that having the blacked out parts plays a role in helping you figure out the word (but that could just be me, since I'm not really a crossword person to begin with). Also, after flipping through some of the puzzles, the answers appeared to be related to the puzzle's title/catergory, but in EVERY puzzle they threw in a handful of completely unrelated words. All in all, it serves the purpose of building vocabulary, and practicing reading and writing hiragana, but that's about it. So, if you completely ignore the puzzle's title, and focus purely on defining the word in front of you, you should be able to finish it without saying to yourself "What does that have to do with (blank)".
