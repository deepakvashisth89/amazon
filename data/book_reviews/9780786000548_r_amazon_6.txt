Nicole has been having weird cravings along with her body being unusually strong, not understanding why she is giving little freedom she decides to follow her father and discuss it with him.  What she sees shocks her, she feels betrayed that her parent didn’t tell her that her father is a vampire and decides to run away to Paris.  In Paris she is robbed and finds her friend has been killed and Marchand La Valois takes her in. As they grow closer, danger is never far away.  Lots of suspense and action packed in this fast paced read.  The chemistry between Nicole and Marchand is excellent.  Looking forward to more in the series.Read moreRead less
