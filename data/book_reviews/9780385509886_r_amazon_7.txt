I borrowed this book from a friend and then ordered it to have a copy of my own in my library for referral.  Anthony Destefano answers the questions that so many of us ask and he backs the answers up with Bible verses.  It is not only an easy read, but is also profound at the same time. The heaven described in the Bible, and the way Anthony puts his book together, helped me to grow in my Faith, and desire the things of heaven (which is permanent) more than here on earth (which is temporal).
