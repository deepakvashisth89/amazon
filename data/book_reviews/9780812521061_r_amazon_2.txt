Richard Laymon's 1984 novel, Night Show got the special treatment from Dark Regions Press in 2018.  It's issued in both trade hardcover (sans dust jacket, with the artwork printed on the boards) and a signed limited edition (limited to 300 copies and signed by Edward Lee, Steve Gerlach, artist Malcolm McClinton, editors James R. Beach and Kelly Laymon).  It also includes a really cool introduction from author Edward Lee plus a very touching and informative Afterward from Steve Gerlach.  Aside from that, there is an insert from Laymon's A Writer's Tale pertaining to Night Show that I found really interesting like it was originally titled Chill Master.  Whew, glad he changed it to the current title!  Lastly, there's xeroxed hand-written pages from Laymon himself with edits and cross-outs included.  Although they're a little hard to read, it's still cool that they are included.As far as the actual novel, I really enjoyed it a lot.  I thought Tony was sort of a weak villain as far as Laymon bad guys go.  I wish Linda had more of a story as her revenge is a lot more messed up than anything Tony does.  And truthfully, there's not too much actual violence in this one (aside from Linda's tirades) but there is quite a bit of steamy sex.  The book isn't all that long and wraps up all nice and neat.  I wish the finale was a bit more wild and brutal but hey, I thought it was ok any way.  I enjoy Richard Laymon no matter what and this one was no exception and the extras really are the icing on top.
