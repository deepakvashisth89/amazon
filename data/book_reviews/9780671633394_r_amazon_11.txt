The book told what happened from her early education to her death in a biography format. It is based on a wide range of interviews and doesn't care if it harms her image or not. It certainly makes her out to be a very different person than how she has presented herself.Parts can be harrowing and help understand why and how she progressed on her journey in life. It is explicit and in no way should be read by children. The book is almost unforgiving, treating her own image as essentially fraud.As a historical work it is poorly referenced and it can be very difficult to work out the source being used at a certain time. The book goes back and forth and although written as a narrative, usually fails at that.The book needs a good editor. You gain a lot of unique information about her life but it's hard and in the end you aren't sure what's true and what's not.
