I am a late comer to Nancy Price's novels, and I am enjoying reading her books.  Night Woman is interesting because it might be possible for a "ghost" writer to be responsible for someone else's success.  I am puzzled how Mary could do all she did for so many people and still find time to be a prolific, best-selling author.  I think her self effacement didn't ring quite true - I doubt that anyone is that totally altruistic.
