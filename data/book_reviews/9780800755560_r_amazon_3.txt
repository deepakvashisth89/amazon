INTENSE. I'm sorry for the reader who wasn't able to appreciate what a critical element in healing, God really is, but instead was put off by scripture references. Emotional abuse is so insidious. It's often denied because it's so often inflicted on us by people who we love and trust. But it inevitably erodes our self-worth, our belief that we deserve to be happy, etc. Pretty soon, we don't give ourselves the chance to be happy the way God intended.This book is like a light that shines on those sensitive areas where we're wounded, thinking we can just carry on if we "let it go" or deny the pain. I got this book and just took a couple of days aside to just absorb what I was reading, without any interruptions.The examples of others' experiences also helped to relate and claim the healing that is so possible when we're able to face and call abuse just what it is rather than going on thinking it's "normal." It was an amazing book that I'll probably read again.
