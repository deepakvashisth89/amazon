Rating:  2.5 stars.  _G rated sexual contentStand alone read.  3rd in The Brubaker Brides seriesEASY READ.  Intriguing storyline, the old-time writing style takes a little getting used to, sassy sometimes funny dialogue, and interesting, but sterotyped secondary cast.  HOWEVER, the writing is wordy, clichéd, and oozes southern charm which becomes irritating, the storyline is overly contrived and contains mildly offensive sexist statements, the heroine is petulant, shallow, and self-focused at the beginning, but matures as the story progresses, her supposed naivety is the basis for the comedic events when in reality she just appears stupid, and the story just drags in places which make the book painful to get through.THE STORY follows extremely sheltered, future heiress, Charlotte Beauchamp, animal trainer, Tex Brubaker, and a pot bellied pig named Toto.  Graduating from boarding school, orphaned Charlotte returns to care for her wealthy, aging Nanna Dorothy.  At her death, ten-years later, Nanna's Last Will stipulated that Charlotte will inherit everything only after the natural death of her beloved, ill mannered, out-of-control pig, now left in Charlotte's care.As the story unfolds, Charlotte moves onto the Brubaker estate unemployed and without a clue.  She immediately runs afoul of Tex over the irresponsible act of leaving her pig in a hot car.  Once brought into the house, Toto runs amuck and Tex reluctantly offers daily lessons to to teach Charlotte how to train Toto.  In the mean time Charlotte falls for one bogus money making scheme after another in an effort to find a suitable job.  Spending time together, they argue and bicker, but Charlotte gradually develops feelings for both Tex and his animals.OVERALL the storyline is beyond contrived, the heroine shallow and silly, and the book drags and is painful to get through.  Skip this book, it is a waste of reading time.  THE BRUBAKER BRIDES series starts with Miss Prim's Untamable Cowboy (1997) and includes eleven books.Try the complex characters and storytelling of N. Bruhns, A. Stuart, and E. Palfrey. For sweet, funny, heart touching romances with great descriptive imagery, try Susan Fox or Cara Colter.Read moreRead less
