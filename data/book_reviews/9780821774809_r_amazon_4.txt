Great story - very different from other love stories.  Fern Michaels is a creative story teller.  This book was about a music star who thinks he’s retarded.  He and his brother had to grow up with very little parental help.  His brother did the best he could but never realized the core issue with his musically-talented younger sibling.  So music and good management made them rich, until a bizarre plan accident gave one an opportunity to reinvent himself.
