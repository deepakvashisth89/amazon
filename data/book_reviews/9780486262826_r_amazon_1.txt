This is a perfect purse sized book of mazes that are great for on the go!  However, I do think they border on the more difficult side of easy.  I got this for my toddler who loves mazes, but I am going to hang onto it for a while until he advances to this level of mazes.  I would highly recommend this for ages 5 and up.  I think he will be able to do it in a few months, but I would say 5 to be on the safe side.  The pictures/graphics are simple, yet cute and appealing to kids.  I would purchase this again in the future, along with other Dover Publications.
