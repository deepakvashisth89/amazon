I have downloaded 37 books by Ed McBain and have read 34 of them.  Lady Killer is a crime fiction about the law enforcement officers at the 87TH precinct in some fictitious city.  The books are all quick reads that serve as good filler material in between longer novels.  All the books are good, nothing exceptional, but the reader develops a familiarity with the police personnel.  Written at a time when crime novels were an attraction, as were westerns in their day.
