I read this for the first time as a teenager, and re-read it recently. It stands up to re-reading. There are two books in this story arc, and you kind of have to take it on faith that it will all come together at the end, but it does. I think if it were published today, they might make it one solid book - each one isn't very long.If you're hoping for an in-depth story of the magic of faerie, you'll probably be disappointed. While there is magic in here, this is really a story about coming of age in a slightly gritty, youth-dominated city with the typical drug and gang problems. Reading it as an adult, the struggle and strife between young people trying to live together, make businesses work, and occasionally get married and move out to the suburbs felt pretty realistic despite the magical setting.Read moreRead less
