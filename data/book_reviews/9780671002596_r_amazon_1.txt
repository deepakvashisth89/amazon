Having read over a dozen ST Voyager novels, you begin to experience common plot lines. Battle Lines, however, did not follow the same pattern. Its story was creative and unique. While many of the other books eventually blend together, I think this one will continue to stand out.One thing I did find odd was that Seven of Nine was never incorporated into the story. She is occasionally referenced, but never included in decisions nor represented as a member of the senior staff. It’s just inconsistent with her actual role on Voyager. In any event, it’s a fun and entertaining read.Read moreRead less
