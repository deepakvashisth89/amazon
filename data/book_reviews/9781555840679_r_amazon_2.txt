I just finished reading the book and I am left with a mixture of sadness and admiration for how Grace handled her role as princess of Monaco and being Rainier's wife.Easy read,every day language. Mrs. Balaban gives very detailed descriptions about the trip on the boat, the pre-wedding, wedding etc..... as if  the reader is there among the bridesmaids. Very well done. I also like the fact there is no gossip about Grace relationships and behavior with her "fiances", prior to ther marriage to Rainier,a sign of respect and true friendship . Thank you Mrs. Balaban for being such a good and loyal friend. I sensed it all along while reading the book. It is the best book I read about the life of G. Kelly: not from newspapers, magazines and from 2-3rd hand references. I also enjoyed the inclusion of the life of Grace's friends over the years, makes it so much more "real".Enjoyed Judith's perpectives and "opinions" (as other revewers mentioned) I could follow her maturing over the years.Makes it more personnal.I recommend this book,absolutely,if you are interested in broader view of Grace as a woman who committed to play the best..."role" she could perform for her husband,children and  her subjects...Bravo Judith for having written this book  with so many vivid descriptions and thank you for having been a good friend to Grace.Read moreRead less
