This book is a collection of three of the four Enderby books written by the late Anthony Burgess.  Enderby is a British poet who is decidedly odd, outrageous and suffers from excessive flatulence (..among other maladies).  In these book we first have Enderby as a reclusive odd man living in rubbish strewn flat on the English coast.  He then escapes to Morocco to only to find more trouble.  And in the third book we have Professor Enderby in New York City stirring plenty of mischief with his rather controversial writings.  It is all rather silly, often quite funny, yet regardless it is hard to resist the brilliance of Anthony Burgess.  His mastery of the English language is exceptional.  Although I don't find these Enderby books to be among his best works I always find myself enjoying the clever ways Burgess twists words around.  Simply brilliant.Bottom line: although decidedly an acquired taste, these Enderby stories provide an interesting outlet to the author's mastery.  Recommended.Read moreRead less
