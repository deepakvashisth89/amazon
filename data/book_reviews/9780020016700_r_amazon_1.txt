This is a great book. Usually how-to books are painful to read but it's more of a nonfiction book than a bulleted step-by-step. I've not read it in its entirety but it doesn't seem anything is left to guess. Leaving anything to the imagination is probably not the best when building a permanent home yourself. We are planning on creating a homestead and building a home the way we want vs the last two homes I bought being someone else's design. Can't move out of this town fast enough and this is a great book to get started. Not a big fan of some of the fencing and furniture (wooden chandeliers and lamps) but there are almost countless instructions on how to build fruit stands, bird and bat homes to protect your home and more. Plenty of authentic pictures of people and their cabins from the late 19th and early 20th century. Big bonus for me.Read moreRead less
