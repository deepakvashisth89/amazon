With this book, Chandler finds his maturity as a writer, I felt.  The previous stories were certainly good — GREAT, even!  But I don’t recall any of the others revealing this much truth about the human condition.  This was character-driven fiction.  Sure, there’s a plot, and it’s a good yarn.  You can read it for that alone.  But, OMG, the insight into human nature!  Whew!  That’s what this book is really about.  You’ll feel like you not only KNOW these characters, but also know what it’s like to be inside their skins.  A delicious read.Read moreRead less
