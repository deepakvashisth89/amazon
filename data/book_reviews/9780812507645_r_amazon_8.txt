One of a very long series, so presumably very popular. Loved the first one, liked the second one, grew bored by the third, as they all seem a variation on the same theme. If you like that theme, you'll love this series. If you want some variety, you need  to look elsewhere. I might have liked this one better if the narrator's completely obnoxious, unbelievably precocious (seven?) year old son had been left where he belonged, in a cage back in England.
