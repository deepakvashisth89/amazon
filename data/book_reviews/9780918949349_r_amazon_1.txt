This book is proof good things come in small packages.  Each short story (and some are only a page long) is chock full of vivid visual imagery, decisively descriptive language, and multi-layered meaning.  Stout Mama is  in her pre "When I Am Old, I Will Wear Purple" stage, but you  know she'll be sporting loud lavenders later on.  She's the kind of woman  we'd like to emulate or evolve into, and Ms. James has the kind of  adjectival accuracy we all envy.  Cherish this woman and this book.Read moreRead less
