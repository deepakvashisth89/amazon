As far as techno-thrillers go, this book definitely deserves some praise:1) The author attempted to weave a complicated net with many characters all coming together at some point.2) The book's premise is interesting and somewhat credible (for a science fiction book that is).Now the criticism:1) Spelling and grammatic errors everywhere. I lost count of how many times he wrote "therefor" instead of "therefore," for example.2) The ending looks like it was put together without much consideration and is unbelievably artificial. It looks as if Mr. Sundman originially intended to write a Greek tragedy, but one week before the deadline changed his mind to write a Hollywood happy-ending - hoping for a sequel and a movie perhaps? :-)Read moreRead less
