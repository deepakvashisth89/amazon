One of my all-time favorite American novels. Ms. Troy nails the disconnect and rootlessness of life in the US West, through the coming of age of Mike. I found him a highly sympathetic character, despite his inarticulateness, and couldn't stop reading to find out how he worked through his hellish personal initiations.
