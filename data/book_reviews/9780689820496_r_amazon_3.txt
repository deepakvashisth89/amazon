Just when I thought I had bought all the commerative items I wanted for living through the Flood of 1997, I found this book.  The book is seen through the eyes of a child, who saw and remembered things that adults  overlook.  From the way the sandbags were made and stacked to the air raid  sirens to the comparison of our snow banks and all our treasures along the  berms waiting to picked up by the crablike machine.  Even if you didn't  experience the Flood of 1997 in Grand Forks, but may have experienced some  other disaster, you will be able to relate that it all comes down to is the  family and friends you have, not the possessions that have been lost.
