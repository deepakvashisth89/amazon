I love this book. This is the funniest book ever written about marriage, and cooking, and in-laws, and gambling, and whores, and cooking school, and nosey neighbors, and the afterlife. If anyone saw the movies made from the book, the book is much better than the movies. Although it was translated from Portugese, and takes place in Brazil, I could relate to the problems and the solutions of our heroine, Dona Flor.Without a doubt this is Amado's best book.
