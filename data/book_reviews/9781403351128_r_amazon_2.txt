Ridley writes of the world we all live in, and shows it to us all thru his eyes as we turn the pages of this book and examine his sole with him. We  walk with him thru his joy and sadness and our own , reveling in beauty thru his eyes remembering what our own have seen. in his words I find a companionship, not only with Him, but with all of us as people, He has put such simple beauties and deep sorrows into his words, and those words let us all know, that no matter how we express it, when we examine our selves and what we are alone, there is much we all share, and always will.I feel there is something in this book that can help each of us walk thru our own feelings and better unerstand those of others. I thank Ridley for sharing his contemplations and emotions with us all.His words are both subtle and powerfull, taking us fully into his mind and heart, making ours dance and swell and fall. They bring pictures to the minds eye and cause us to see things... maybe a bit differently. this is clearly a satisfying read, and a must have for any lover of poetry.Read moreRead less
