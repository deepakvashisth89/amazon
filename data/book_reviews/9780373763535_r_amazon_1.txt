I loved this book, Cindy Gerard is a fantastic writer. She has written so many books. This story really touched me. It was a story I myself related to. Matt and Helena's story is so beautiful. I laughed,cried and felt so much passion between these two.Lone Star Knight is a really great story about a women that is trying to survive from a bad accident and Matt Walker helps her do just that. In the process they develop feelings for one another. If you have not read this book get. You have to buy it used but it's worth every penny..As I said Cindy Gerard is a wonderful author, all her books are so different then any other I have read. I have yet to read a book I did not like from her. Look into her other books...Bodyguard Series, Black Op books they are so great. They are better read in order. So start with the Bodyguard series. Her books are so hard to put down. They are filled with romance,suspense,laughter. There is not one of these books that I have not laughed at certain times. One these books just draw you in. I myself want more at the end of each and every book.Read moreRead less
