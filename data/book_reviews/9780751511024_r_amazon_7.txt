Brother Cadfael, though he is now a (more or less) proper monk living at the Abbey of St. Peter and St. Paul, has only come to the abbey after a long career doing other things, most notably fighting in the Crusades. He is now atoning for what he feels are the sins he committed during that time. Of course, he doesn’t actually have superpowers; his years of experience in other occupations have given him skills in many areas that a monk confined to an abbey or monastery for all his life would not necessarily have.This is a complicated story. It happens during the war between Queen Maude and King Stephen for control of England during the 12th century. Nearer the beginning of the war than the end. When Stephen invades the town of Shrewsbury near the Welch border. The monastery Brother Cadfael belongs to is right outside the town.Several dead bodies require Brother Cadfael’s attention. Of course, there are the 94 defenders of Shrewsbury Castle that King Stephen has had executed after he successfully took over the castle at the urging of his advisers. But when Brother Cadfael counts the bodies as he is having them prepared for burial after the abbot (his boss) has volunteered the abbey to see to this chore, he finds 95 bodies. Stephen barely flinched at having the castle defenders executed, but he is displeased that somebody is using his lesson regarding what happens to his enemies to cover their own crimes and wants the perpetrator of this outrage found. He does not, of course, assign this task to Brother Cadfael. Nevertheless, it falls to Cadfael to figure it out.There are other issues to be considered. Two young ladies appear at the monastery at about the time of the battle as well. One has come to declare her loyalty to King Stephen and pledge the two castles and small force of fighting men she has just inherited from her late father to him and is housed at one of the abbey’s guest houses for the time being. The other came in disguised as a boy, supposedly to be housed with the novices as a way to avoid being conscripted into one of the armies. She is given to Brother Cadfael to help him in the garden. He picks up on her being a girl almost at once and helps her to hide from her former betrothed who seems intent on finding her to hand over to the King as a hostage since her father was second in command of the castle before its downfall and has now escaped the town, presumably to join Queen Maude in France. A couple more dead bodies and several plotters of various types turn up and have to be sorted before Brother Cadfael can make sense of the whole thing.
