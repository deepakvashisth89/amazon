This was a nice marriage in trouble romance. After ten years of marriage and one child, Francesca and Oliver are starting to drift. Once they were successful, Oliver set Frances and their son up in a mansion in the country but he is coming home less and less and the boy is now off at boarding school, leaving Francesca home alone.On the night of their tenth wedding anniversary, Oliver doesn't come home, leaving his secretary to call Francesca and let her know he is heading off to deal with an accident at one of the factories. It's a small thing, but it's a last straw. Especially as the secretary's attitude is possessive of Oliver and dismissive of his wife.Francesca doesn't run away. She goes right to the source. As one of the original founding partners of the business, she has rights and she uses that leverage with the third partner to get a job in the firm.This is a great story of a husband getting a wake up call and a wife willing to make waves. The secretary is the classic other woman but the problem between Oliver and Francesca is not about cheating although the risk was there if they continued on the path they were going down.Nice story and satisfying ending.Read moreRead less
