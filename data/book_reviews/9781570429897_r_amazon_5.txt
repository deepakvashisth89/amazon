A quick little read and very entertaining.I enjoyed the various descriptions of the house at 1600 Pennsylvania Avenue, NW and the Old Executive Office Building.I grew up in Baltimore and first visited the White House, Capitol, Treasury, Smithsonian Institution, and every other building in Washington of any importance to a six grade elementary school class.It was in 1957 and my teacher believed in first hand knowledge of historical places. In addition to DC, we visited Philadelphia and the Chesapeake Bay shoreline where we scavenged for shark teeth. While in the White House we toured all of the rooms on the first floor and the class was surprised when a bald-headed stately gentlemen greeted us with a smile and a handshake. Yes, it was General Eisenhower. Then, there was no fence around the property and we 12 year olds had free rein to go where we wanted…on the first floor. Now, it is much more difficult to visit.
