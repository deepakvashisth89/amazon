This is a very engaging, even inspiring, story of a woman who overcame adversity in many forms.  It is set in a time of cultural upheaval.  Traditional ways faced overwhelming change introduced by American, Christian, and Mormon inroads into Hopi life; and is seen through one woman's story, well told.  Afterwords by herself and her son give the book additional interest.
