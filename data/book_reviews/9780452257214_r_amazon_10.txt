Kenneth Anger's second trip to the film colony, as he calls it, is just as full of unsubstantiated rumours and downright untruths as his first book. But, it does make for fun reading. Hollywood Babylon II has as many pictures and as many juicy tales as its older sibling. In fact, some of the stories are repeated in this book, albeit in a shortened form.My only real quibble about this book (other than all the "fake news") is that so much of it was dedicated to suicides. Page after page of deaths got to be a little bit depressing. I much perfected the stories about dalliances, drinking parties, etc. that were the focus of the first book. That being said, this was still a fun read if only from a historical prospective. So much of what Anger wrote has since been disproved (Google any star's name and you'll find the true story out there along with a bunch of people repeating Anger's tales), but he sure knows how to tell a good story.Read moreRead less
