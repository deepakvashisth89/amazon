Great selection of riveting short stories based on Jack London's time in Alaska during the Klondike Gold Rush. Very well written, as you would expect, and the stories really made you feel like you understood the time and place.  That said, it was pretty relentlessly depressing. I don't want to give any spoilers on this 100 year old collection, but nearly every one of the stories ends in the death of the main character, their wife, their travel companion, their dog, or all of the above. Only a couple remotely happy endings are found, but... I guess that was probably true of the Klondike years generally? I did like that I could get through multiple short stories during a day at the beach and pick it up again weeks later and not have to remember what was happening.
