Having read “Cheaper by the Dozen” when I was young, I decided to re-read it on my Kindle.  It is a fun book about the real-life story of two intelligent parents and their dozen kids.  It is easy-to-read, amusing, and can make you wish life hadn’t changed so much since the time when the Gilbreths raised their family.  A good read!
