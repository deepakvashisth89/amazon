As indicated by the title, this book is narrowly focused on the attempts by the SS to build an economic empire that was to be separate from that of Germany or the Nazi Party. This was to ensure their continued survival after the war, when presumably their services would be hard to justify. Consequently, the book represents a compendium of supporting evidence for Herr Speer's much more important work, 'Inside the Third Reich', in which the SS attempts at economic independence were previously exposed. Unlike 'Inside', this book is disorganized, anecdotal and much more appropriate for a researcher looking for evidence on this subject than for the general public.Read moreRead less
