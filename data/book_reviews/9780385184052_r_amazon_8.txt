Book 9 of the 12 book Poldark series. The Miller's Dance is a continuation of the escapades of the grown up children of the main characters of books 1 - 7. Although Ross and Demelza, Dwight and Caroline, George and all of the other ancillary characters are still prominent, the story line shifts more to the younger generation and more out of Cornwall. There is also more focus on the history of the time in all of England and Europe rather than just in Cornwall. The series keeps getting better with each book! The story lines and characters are complex and realistic, and the second generation is just as lovable (or detestable) as the first generation of characters we all love!Read moreRead less
