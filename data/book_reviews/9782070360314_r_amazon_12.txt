Misers exist in every society, and gold has an uncanny attraction. There is also a daughter who embodies all the virtues. And there is a plot - and romance - no way bereft of twists and turns. We also have a compelling picture of Post-Revolutionary France, and many examples of Balzac's dense evocations of place.
