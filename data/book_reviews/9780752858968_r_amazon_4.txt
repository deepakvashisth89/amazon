Ian Rankin's Inspector Rebus books are always good. Crime fighting John Rebus works his way through another case and solves it in a rather un-expected way. The cast of characters are always good and well developed in a Rankin book and even though many of the characters are from previous Inspector Rebus books, the book is a good one read out of order or on its own.  Inspector Rebus seems like an old friend every time I read one, consistency in character and a well developed plot line with, as always, many twists and turns.Read moreRead less
