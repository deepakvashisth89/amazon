It's quite a good book in that it contains a range of horticultural information from types of plant,  types of growth,  the basic requirements of plants.The selection of plants each have a common and Latin name, a picture, a fairly detailed description, and growing tips.There are approximately 76 plants (1 per page 26-104).There is a glossary of terms with basic but surprisingly useful terms for the amateur.  There is a zonal map of the USA which kind of undermined my confidence a bit as someone living in the UK although I suppose you could just get a UK zonal map. Basically,  some of the common names might not be domestic common names that are familiar to Brits. Not a big deal but worth mentioning.Would I buy again?  Maybe,  but with postage at £2.80 this book comes in at £2.81...  To me that is overpriced. It's worth about 50p from a charity shop.  You decide if £2.81 is a lot or a little.Read moreRead less
