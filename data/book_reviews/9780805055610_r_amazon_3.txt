I'll admit it: I'm a Disney fanatic. My mom was one of Disney's artists. I've lived near Disneyland and (twice) near Disney World. I love all things Disney.So, as my husband & I consider moving to Celebration, I decided to read more about its history and transformation over the years.I already knew about the long-ago, plastic-flamingos-on-the-lawn controversy. I've also witnessed the transformation - not always for the better - of downtown Celebration. (I miss the early days, when it really felt like a step into the past... or at least an ideal past.)But, before deciding whether Celebration is the right place for us, I wanted to know more, and see it through others' eyes.Though dated, since the book was published in 1999, this has been a fascinating book to read from cover to cover.The authors are frank about their own biases.
