I loved Choose Your Own Adventure books when I was a kid, and this was my favorite of the series. I've never gone back to study and analyze them -- there's surely a site out there somewhere that maps out the branchings and rates the books in terms of structure, fairness, etc. I have no idea how Hyperspace would hold up under that kind of grownup magnifying glass. What I do know is: Hyperspace brought in some trippy, meta elements I had never seen before, geared down to where I could understand them. It has characters who know they're in a book, that type of thing. It was mind-expanding in a miniature, kid-friendly way. It enlarged my understanding of what books could do.
