Neil Young's biography arrived from overseas in perfect condition.  I am very much a fan of the LA counter culture of the late 1960s/early 1970s, so therefore am very much looking forward to reading this book. In my younger days, I was a big reader, but a job etc. has got in the way of all this - or so I had thought. Amazon have such a diverse range of books to choose from,and I have read some good ones of late. Now I can read all about one of my musical heroes and his early career. Neil Young is, along with fellow Canadian, Joni Mitchell, the definitive singer songwriter. Youngsters will probably be shocked to learn that back then, singers actually  wrote their own songs! This guy did, and to this day, only chooses to work with other artists on his own terms. He has worked with Crazy Horse on and off since the 1960s, and he also has spells working with my favourite band, Pearl Jam.
