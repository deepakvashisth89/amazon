I quite enjoyed reading this book.  I do enjoy history and reading novels of this period gives you a great insight of the way of life.  I have purchased a number of these books now.  The maroon cover is lovely, the red satin ribbon page marker is just nice.  Again I will have to say that the company printing these books in India need to take more care in their products.  The print changes, its crooked, it goes from light to dark.  A suggestion would be when you receive these books is to look through them before the return cut off date.
