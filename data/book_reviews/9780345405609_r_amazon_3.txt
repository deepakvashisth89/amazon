The author does a fine job of showing how the war of 1914-18 might easily have spread to the Western Hemisphere. All that was needed was an entangling alliance or two with European powers. And once begun it would have progressed much the same way everywhere, because military thinking was totally out of sync with military reality. I like the way the author uses the experiences of ordinary people to advance his story, for the most part. Makes it easier to understand the full impact of total war. I also appreciate how well the author captures the certainty of the pre-war Socialists in their cause, and how cruelly they were disappointed when patriotism and war fever negated any concept of class solidarity.Read moreRead less
