The Hawk's Gray Feather is the opening of an unusual and masterful retelling of the Arthurian legends by Ms. Kennealy-Morrison.  Her graceful prose style has always reminded me of having my own Celtic bard retelling an ancient Celtic legend.  Her use of connections are amazing and completely orginal.
