I rarely review a purchase.  I even more rarely return a book.  The typeface used in this book is terrible, faded, and almost unreadable.  It appears to have been typeset with a typewriter.  Although probably not important to the story, none of the original illustrations are in this edition (it just says "[Illustration]" where there should be an actual illustration). Curling up with this oversized book (I didn't expect 8 1/2 by 11 size pages) would have all the charm of reading an ancient hand-typed grad-school monograph on the crystalline structure of dipenzenemonophosphate.
