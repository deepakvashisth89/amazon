Confession to start - I was named after Evan Hunter. (My mother being a huge fan of his and having no idea that it was a pen name). I also spent many years as a HS Social Studies teacher in the Bronx, and may have spent a long hot summer at the vocational HS that "The Blackboard Jungle" is based on. Many vocational students have about as much interest in history or government as they would Hunter's English class.
