Very good post-apocalyptic novel about a future where the level of solar radiation declines so it becomes perpetual winter in the northern hemisphere and most of the population flees to the warmer climes of Africa. Africa then becomes politically dominant leading to an ironic post-colonialist situation where white waiters kowtow to black masters in Lagos. Written in 1962 the novel contains some conceptions of racial loyalty that read uncomfortably today and the final section jars slightly because of this.
