I’ve now read this book through three times, once in a group study. It’s depth remains tantalizingly out of my reach.Notes for those who wish to study it:The names are each a tiny bio. Each term, like Haji, Mirzá, Shaykh, and Sayyed, mean something about the person. Find that out first, and keep it close to hand.Study this book with other people. The more resources you have, the wider and deeper the understanding you will have.I remember vignettes, not the entire sweep of this book. It is a deep and accurate report of real events, but it also does not teach you all the context in which those events happened. In that way, it is more like an anthology of first- or second-hand stories about events which takes for granted some familiarity with their socio-economic and cultural context.It’s a wonderful and inspiring journey.Read moreRead less
