This is one of my favorite books of all time! It is a masterpiece! A work of art! A must read and a beautiful addition to your collection. I cannot begin to fathom how to put into words how I feel about this author and this particular book. I cried, I laughed, I was left in suspense. I went through all the possible emotions you can go through with this book and for me a book that can play with your emotions in such a way that you are on a roller coaster ride with your feelings, in such a way that you feel a sense of empathy towards the characters is a book with which the author has been able to successfully create a connection between the reader and the characters in the story! I loved this book and I do not think it is possible for anyone to not like it.
