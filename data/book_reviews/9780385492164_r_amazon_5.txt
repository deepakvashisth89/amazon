Bender's work resembles short-short experimental and fantasy fiction--and mostly, it works, as long as you don't have other expectations; it is provocative, and sometimes bordering on erotic. Bender is original, mysterious and dark. In every story, she weaves multiple levels of consciousness and humor. There is a mystic psychology to Bender's prose; they are simple, yet complex. She depicts longing in a fresh, real voice that appeals to today's readers: Get it fast, get it easy, get it on multiple levels, make it painless, make it simple, and make it cross levels of consciousness (as if in a dream). Bender accomplishes all these things. She allows her imagination to run wild, using simple language that often tells multi-dimensional stories.Read moreRead less
