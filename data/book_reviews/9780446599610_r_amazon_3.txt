This is an interesting read. I get the feeling though that to pad the book a bit for lack of excess information he goes into the histories of the people lost, which in no way has anything to do with the mysteries at hand or in solving them. The book's copyright is 1973 so it may even be a bit of a trend writing thing rather than any serious attempt to solve the riddle as this riddle reached something of a high in '73. However there were some interesting theories set fourth in the final chapter. What's curious though is he added some details about a particular disappearance that I had not read before, and as it happens this bit of information actually wholly confirms the conclusion I had reached in answer to what happened to all these people and I will be using this in a third edition of my work.
