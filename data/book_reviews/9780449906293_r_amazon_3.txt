This is an excellent, yet extremely sad and tragic book.  It is extremely helpful to know that sexual abuse of boys is so common.  Sexual abuse is such a taboo subject, and victims so often get blamed rather than perpetrators in our society that this book is a MUST-READ for any male and/or female survivors of sexual abuse - particularly child sexual abuse, and even for parents who are interested to acquire more information about the subject to assist children in recovery.
