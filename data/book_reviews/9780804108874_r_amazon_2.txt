First of all, I love Anne Tyler's books! This is I believe her second effort (after If Morning Ever Comes), and is very good for a young writer. She was in her 20s when she wrote this. One can already see Tyler's uncanny (no pun intended) ability to read characters' minds, their intentions, and motivations. She does this very well even this early on, and really is good at creating believable male characters (especially)!  Overall the book is a good read, far from her best. If you are just starting with Anne Tyler (or have read only her first 2 or 3 books--all of which she herself thinks of as uncharacteristic early attempts), I would recommend her latter works: Breathing Lessons (Pulitzer Prize winner), The Accidental Tourist, The Amateur Marriage, Dinner at the Homesick Restaurant (her personal favorite), and what I think is her best book of the last 10 years: Noah's Compass. Really, just about anything of hers from her 4th book on. Read these first, and then, as I did, go back and read the early stuff.Read moreRead less
