I went through this book as a part of a group Bible study. It was a great way to get together and study what God's word says about prayer.One of the most meaningful chapters to me was “When God is Silent.” When we are going through such a season, we tend to feel alone or as if we have done something awful to turn God away. The book also gives several reasons why God may be “silent” and helped me to evaluate my life. It was comforting and reassuring to know that I’m not alone, and that even though I don’t “feel” Him, God is still there listening to me.I recommend this book to anyone who is looking for a simple introduction to prayer and has a heart to pray for others.
