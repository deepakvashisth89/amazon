I read the novel with a group and decided to buy the Kindle/Audible combination in order to be able to read it as efficiently as possible.  There are sections that really need to be heard more than once, or read as text, in order to fully grasp her meaning.  Eliot is a very intelligent and thoughtful author and her observations on life and character are well worth the effort to parse the sometimes complex sentences in which she expresses them.  I would like to say that the Juliet Stevenson reading is excellent.  She is able to express each character with a distinctive voice, and to read the narration in a way that makes the sometimes complicated language as clear as possible.
