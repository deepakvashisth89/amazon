I have reread this trilogy every 6 years or so over the past 40 years. It continues to be the best science fiction I have ever read. I always feel, as I finish the last book, like I’ve lived the adventures alongside Gabby and Cirocco Jones. From the first landing to the final lines of the third book, Varley expertly builds a world of dangers and wonders.
