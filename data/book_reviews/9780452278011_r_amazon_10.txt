This book has helped me not only to change my job but also to get a different attitude in my (new!) job. What you have to do is so simple and so obvious that you wonder why you haven't thought about and done it before. This book not only gives you a good idea of how the application processes work from the employers' point of view, but also shows you very clearly what you need to do in order to find and also get the right job for you. Believe it or not, shortly after finishing reading this book, I went for another job interview and actually got the job! This is one of the best books I ever read, because it does not give you any of these advices you heard many times before, but because it opens your eyes and makes you act accordingly.
