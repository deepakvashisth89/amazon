The Lurking Fear & Shadow Over Innsmouth are splendidly refreshing. Shame I waited until my 20s to find them.In The Lurking Fear, Lovecraft presents a wonderful look into the world of paranormal investigation. Where the facts are tainted by your expectations. And where the end result is either completely benign, or mind altering.In Shadow Over Innsmouth, he provides an equally quirky town. Nothing unexpected. Small town. Odd happenings. A daring escape. And the military taking eventual action, while denying any involvement. This is where Lovecraft takes it an extra mile. Connecting you to the narrator throughout his entire small town ordeal, his personal revelations and unknown connection to the town become outright personal to the reader.Bravo, well done.Read moreRead less
