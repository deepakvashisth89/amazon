Volume three of the series introduces jealousy, the green-eyed monster, to both Ryo and Dee. The Commissioner tries to get his hands on Ryo, Diana flirts with Dee shamelessly, and Jay as usual is left out in the cold yearning for Dee's affections.While a good and captivating read, I don't think it was quite as good as volume 2. After reading the cover notes on volume two, I think I was expecting more from the physical side of Dee and Ryo's relationship then I actually got.Still, I am looking forward to reading through the rest of it.
