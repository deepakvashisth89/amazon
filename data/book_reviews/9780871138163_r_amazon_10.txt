If you like James Joyce, maybe you will like this. Both authors describe for the sake of describing. But if you want action and consequence, you might be frustrated by the meandering. I felt too impatient to be fair in my reading of it. There were sections which I really enjoyed — Berts falling in love, for instance, and the description of the nun — but I lost patience with the lingering descriptions and the unappealing characters. And I was not convinced that the author was an expert in how separated twins would feel. And the very last sentence backfired. Instead of providing a decent conclusion, it seemed to treat the serious issues of the book as a joke.
