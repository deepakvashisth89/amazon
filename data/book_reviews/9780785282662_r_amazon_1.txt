Barbara Scott and Carrie Younce really wrote a wonderful Christian thriller.I won't spoil the book for anyone.  So don't worry.  The book was written almost twice.  Lines separate the two stories that twist into each other to create a bi-dimensional tale.  One story is in "reality", the world that we all know very well that we can see, taste, touch, etc.  The other story is behind the scenes, if you will.  It's a story of what is happening in the spiritual realm all around the characters from the first story.  They remain almost completely unaware of this realm all around them, yet some are sensitive to its activity on some level.The plot is great.  I experienced the emotions in this book right along with the well-written characters.  I got scared for them, was rooting for them, and celebrated with them when there was cause.  This book should be read by all Christians, especially the ones that don't really feel sensitivity to the Holy Spirit.Non-Christians can even read this book.
