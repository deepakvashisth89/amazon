The Poldark series of books were ALL were wonderful each and every one, and I read through the entire set pretty quick! I got the best price on Amazon because it was hard to find a major bookseller to let me order the entire set, nope they do not carry all of them in their warehouses.  I got them before Christmas which was a delight for me as I was real sick and at home for the holidays.  These books are real period piece and very detailed with history, love, war, death, medical practices of the time and other great things to discover.  The characters are richly developed and just jump off of the page, you will immerse yourself into the world of Poldark. The scenery, towns, markets, churches, how food was prepared, farming, mining, the clothing worn, description of the homes and castles, how women and men were treated during that time was really interesting and description of the war was good historically and you do learn a lot . It is fun when their children start to grow up you also follow their stories too. I loved the Poldark on Masterpiece too but the books tell so much more detail as always. Enjoy, you will love each book and immerse yourself in the world of Poldark!!Read moreRead less
