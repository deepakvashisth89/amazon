As I finish this book I have regained hope; hope in humanity, hope in the path I have to walk. There's lessons on humility, on the value of every person, and the wisdom of nature and the universe, with which we are one. It is a book about the power of love, courage, and the most mundane things that work as a bridge between the rational and the irrational, sanity and madness, the visible and the invisible. Lastly, it is about the balance between the "magical" and our everyday lives not being mutually exclusive but rather symbiotic. Once again, Coelho manages to speak to my soul and remind me it is beautiful to be alive and that I know a lot more than what I give myself credit for: all I have to do is trust the dark night.Read moreRead less
