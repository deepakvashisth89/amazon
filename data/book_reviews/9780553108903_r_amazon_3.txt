Ian introduces each character with intimate detail, then sews a tapestry of these characters into a man-made disaster-story that is international in scope.  All politicians and coastal dwellers should be on guard that this is not a preview of what may actually be in our near future. You are driven to turn pages until you learn the fate of each and every victim in peril.  It is regretful that the editing didn’t find the eleven times that “bum” was used instead of “burn”, it would appear that proof-reading has been turned over to the computer and the human element has been retired.
