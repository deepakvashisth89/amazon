Book ReviewThe First CounselAuthor: Brad MeltzerI became a fan of Brad Meltzer when I read his book 'The Book of fate' a few years back. And since then I have been trying to read every one of them he had written. The First Counsel was his third book according to his bibliography.Meltzer has always been in love with the White House and the Presidency and everything that surrounds it and it is well reflected in every of his book. The First Counsel is the story of a White House lawyer Micheal Garrick and the mystery that starts developing around his life when he goes out on a date with the President's daughter and both of them witness something they weren't supposed to see. To protect her, he ends up admitting to something that he wasn't supposed to and eventually does something that he shouldn't. This leads to a tight gripping mystery thriller with a very fast paced action around every page. Then ending was a little too easy but the mystery quotient makes up for it. Also first time in a Brad Meltzer's book, you actually feel bad, really bad emotionally about the ending. You feel out for the characters.On a whole this brilliant mystery book is a real page turner and one gets hooked to it right from the first page itself.A three and a half stars for The First Counsel.Read moreRead less
