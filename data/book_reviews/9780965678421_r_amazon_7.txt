The Concealed Handgun Manual: How to Choose, Carry, and Shoot a Gun in Self Defense is a must-read if you are thinking about carrying a handgun.  This book is full of information and case studies that cut through the fog of "Should I carry a handgun or not?" The author does a skillful job of presenting the facts without trying to convince you to decide one way or the other.  In addition, this book contains many of the topics covered in the CCL training program. The layout of the information makes it an easy read front to back or you can skip around reading the chapters in any order to satisfy your specific curiosity of the subject.
