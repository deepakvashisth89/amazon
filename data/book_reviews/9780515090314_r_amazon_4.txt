Edward Ricardo Braithwaite is most definitely one of the most interesting people I have had the pleasure of learning about, through this book about a small portion of his life. I was inspired to read/listen after watching the movie starring Sidney Poitier, which is equally amazing. As usual with these kinds of things, however, the book is much more detailed and includes a few more plot lines which could not be put into the movie, for whatever reason.I'd HIGHLY recommend this to anyone who wants to learn about the state of race relations in England after World War II, and how one man became one of the best teachers an inner city London school ever had, despite many things being against him.Read moreRead less
