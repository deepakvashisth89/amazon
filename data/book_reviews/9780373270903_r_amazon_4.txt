I really liked the story.  I love it when the hero is as wonderful and devoted as Mitch was to the heroine.  However, there was an aspect of this book I hated.  The hero was often described as  oh so very handsome, unfortunately (for me) the heroine was barely described.  Though the reader was often reminded about her scar.  It seems to me there is a trend among romance writers, to make the hero beyond gorgeous, but not so much the heroine.  And if per chance the heroine is indeed beautiful then she must be punished by having a commitment phobic jerk for a love interest and/or having her scarred during the progression of the story.  Of course there are many exceptions, such as most of Lisa Marie Rice's books.
