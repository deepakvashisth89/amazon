I have a fancy idea to visit Australia some day.  And if I do, I hope to capture at least some of the lyrical feelings and tensions and wonders that Keneally throws around with such ease.  The word that comes to mind is "evolution".  All the characters (and I use this word with full meaning) are in a constant state of development, as is the continent itself.  No static figures here.  Dispair, mental instability, and even survival are treated with a tentative gentleness that I have not encountered before.My hat is off to Mr. Keneally.
