Iliana has gone away to school only to feel responsible for the problem facing her family back home in New York. She quits school to go back to her family and she realizes that some of the problems can not be so easily  overcomed. Her father is one of the few male characters in the recent past  to be a strong family man and not the typically portrayed latin macho that  has no interest in their own family.
