At least this book takes us away from the tedious Eastender's soap opera that clogs every book located in Rome (that gives it one star). It's still over-written, Falco's mind chatter still has the air of a pimply 15-year-old boy and even then is inauthentic, but hey, it takes place somewhere else, the research is good, she moves the story along. But the writing style makes me feel like I'm in an over-heated pink chintz parlour with too many nick-nacks and doilies. After reading 8 of these books (I went forward to see if they improved), I've come to the conclusion that Davis is a little old lady Cozy's writer trying to do the tough guy thing. It just doesn't work. Strip away the dreck and there would be a series of really good, lean, snappy stories done in a prim but humourous, engaging style, similar to Margery Allingham or Ellis Peters. But, no - she's stuck on the stereotype gumshoe, and not a very good one.Read moreRead less
