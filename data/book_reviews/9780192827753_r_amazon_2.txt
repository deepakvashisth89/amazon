I got this book for a class on 18th century women writers, and I have to say I thoroughly enjoy it. It contains poems by one hundred (!!!) different women poets of that time period, most of whom even I as a literature student had never heard of. The poems cover a wide spectrum of tones and topics, some witty, some sweet, some very political, some funny -- If you are interested in poetry, you should take a look at this book.Read moreRead less
