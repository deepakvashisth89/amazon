I've read 'The Stranger Beside Me' by Ann Rule and thought that was the best Bundy book out there. I was wrong. This book is. Rule's book was too emotional and now I see...not so much an insider's account as it appeared. She overplayed a friendship that I don't think was ever really anything more than a coworker relationship that she played up. This author seems to have known Bundy better...but admits they were never really friends. The honesty was refreshing and he was in contact with Bundy and his wife way more than Ann Rule was. Something stands out. The one time Rule spoke to Bundy's wife all Carole Boone said to her was, "Oh, I've heard of you." before she walked away (Rule wrote this in her book). This author had a friendly relationship with Boone, sitting beside her at trial and even hugging her on her (twisted as heck) wedding day. That tells me Rule was already being a little shady before the convictions and wasn't trusted. Great book.Read moreRead less
