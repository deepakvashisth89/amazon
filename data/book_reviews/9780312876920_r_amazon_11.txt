I bought this book with a couple of others, and decided to read this one first, as I thought I'd enjoy it least! I couldn't have been more wrong. I was hooked from page 1, and (luckily) I was 'snowbound' last week, and managed to spend lots of time just reading! I even sat reading it by candlelight during a power cut one evening.The story is excellent, and it really is worth sticking with it, and trying to get your head round all the 'technical talk' (of which there is quite a bit). I've read a few other 'parallel universe' books, and am glad I have, because I think they have explained the 'science bits' in a simpler way. That's not to say Hominids was too technical to follow - it just seems that the author had really 'done his homework' and wanted to share his knowledge in some detail!I love the 'other' world in this book, it seems preferable to ours in a number of ways!I have ordered the other two books in the series, and eagerly await their arrival (held up due to the snow!).Read moreRead less
