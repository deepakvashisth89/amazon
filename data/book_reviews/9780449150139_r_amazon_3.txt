Two characters created by Joyce Christmas, Lady Margaret Priam and Betty Trenka, meet in this book and join forces to solve a murder.  Two young boys find a woman dead on the beach and they retrieve a computer disk near her body.  Meanwhile Betty Trenka has been asked by her wheelchair-bound friend, Ted Kelso, to meet with Gerald Toth, a computer businessman, to see if he would be a good person with whom to do business.  Toth has offered Kelso a lot of money to test a software program, but Kelso thinks that it is stolen goods.  Betty travels to New York, where she meets Lady Margaret and they attempt to discover Gerald Toth's true character and also, who has murdered the woman on the beach.  The identity of the murderer is hinted at in the book and there are no big surprises when Betty is finally able to point the finger at the perpetrator.
