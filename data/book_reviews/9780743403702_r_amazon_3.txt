It isn't canon, but the story is great. The central plot revolves around Dr. McCoy sneaking behind enemy lines to Romulus to find out what happened to LCDR Terise Haleakala-LoBrutto, who has been a deep-cover operative as a Romulan servant for eight years, but Starfleet Intelligence lost contact with her. Has she gone native? Is she even still alive? It's up to one Leonard McCoy to find out. The secondary storyline reveals the origin of the Romulans as a people beginning with the Sundering when Surak and "reality-truth" as a guiding ethic swept Vulcan by storm, millennia ago. That story by itself would make a fascinating novella, but the weaving together of the stories is well done. It's definitely worth getting.Read moreRead less
