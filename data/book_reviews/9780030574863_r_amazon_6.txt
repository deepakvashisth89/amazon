Fadeout is the first Dave Brandstetter novel by Joseph Hansen. Brandstetter is an insurance investigator in Pima, California to investigate the possible death of Fox Olson, a local radio celebrity who was just breaking out with a chance to take his act national. His car went off a bridge during a raging storm, but although the car was found, Fox's body wasn't. Brandstetter sets about finding the clues as to what really happened that night.The book is loaded with a cast of memorable characters, most of which have a possible motive for offing Fox. Brandstetter himself is very well written in the classic detective tradition. Fans of Dashiell Hammett will find themselves in familiar territory. Mr. Hansen's writing is clean and crisp and the pages turn at a rapid pace as the case unfolds.
