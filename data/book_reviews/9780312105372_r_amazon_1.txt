If it were up to me, I would probably read all day, every day.  But since that's not possible, I do look forward to my designated "reading time" every evening.  Now and then, however, I happen upon a book that makes me want to find bits of time along the way to sneak quick bits from 3-30 pages, and "Savage" was just such a one!  Even though the writing style was classic Laymon, I considered it a departure from his usual work in that it was set during Jack the Ripper's time.  I thoroughly enjoyed this!  Just enough graphic gorey descriptions without being overdone, interwoven throughout a heck of a good story!  It is told in the first-person by a teenage boy who, simply by trying to do the right thing, finds himself time and again in difficult situations.  Occasionally I got a little tired of the overuse of certain phrases ("It felt might good!"), but that was minor compared to my overall enjoyment of the story.  I will leave the plot description to other reviewers, but do want to say that in addition to a really good story, it had an entirely satisfactory ending!
