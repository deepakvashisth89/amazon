I read the book when it first came out.  I saw the movie - noted the differences and still enjoyed.  I think that reading it again, I value the worth of the story even more. I think we all have met a Leland Guant or two in the course of our lives. The one who twists things around to his or her own advantage. The one who makes you feel like your are being done a favor, right before the rug gets pulled out from under you.  Of course in the world of Stephen King, Leland Guuant is a bit more dangerous, a bit more mystical, and a bit more lethal. An entire town is turned upside-down...what's left of it. It is a wonderful read down many twisted roads. Enjoy the adventure! I did! I also saw the lessons embedded in the prose.Read moreRead less
