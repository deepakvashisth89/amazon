This adult romance is mainly set in the Bahamas. Kelly Sanders studied to be a veterinarian, against the wishes of her parents who preferred medical school. Her boyfriend has a surprise for her one evening in Charlotte, when he slips a ring into her glass to propose in front of their friends. But the pager is beeping, and Kelly goes to a difficult foaling which saves her having to give an answer. Neil declares that she doesn't need a career if she marries him.Kelly's well-off grandmother Edie whisks the girl off for a Caribbean holiday. Their hotel on an island near Nassau is simple, but in paradise. Kelly meets Mike Kramer, who runs a dive shop, and his dog. Mike explains that if Kelly wants to come out and see the underwater environment she'll have to start training in a small cove, learn dive theory and demonstrate her swimming ability, then take written tests. Mike's had a holiday romance too many, but he's a genuine guy and senses that Kelly is different. Maybe they could take a friendship further. He doesn't mention that he now owns a string of dive shops. Kelly's mother however has plans to force the girl into marrying Neil and is not averse to emotional blackmail...I loved the descriptions of the beautiful islands. Breadfruit plants mix with banana and coconut trees; hibiscus and orchids are almost as colourful as the squawking parrots, and the air is rich with floral scents. The ocean water is clear because there are no rivers to carry silt to the sea; the divers are in their element.
