Ruth Rendell is a very prolific writer, and I always look forward to reading her novels. This one, The Crocodile Bird, was especially fascinating. It is basically about a young girl growing up in odd circumstances, and what happens to her as she gets older, away from her mother. But it's a real page turner, and I couldn't put it down. It's very thought-provoking and Rendell makes her characters seem so "real". You know there must be people out there like those in this book, even though you don't know any.
