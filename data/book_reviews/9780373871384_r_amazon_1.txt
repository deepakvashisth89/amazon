What a way to start a life together!  God works in mysterious ways!!!Scott was very bitter at this point in his life.  After what had happened to his brother, he didn't think he would ever, ever see God as real again.  No matter what Allie said to him or how much she prayed for him, Scott chose to remain in his bitterness.  But he still had a soft part for his old girlfriend, and he allowed her church two weeks at the old church camp before tearing it down.A series of disasters may be God's way of getting Scott to realize what he has before he loses it.  A couple of cute kids also helped Allie to show him the light.  Between flash flooding, narrow escape from drownings, hiking in the rain, mud slides, and a terrible fall; the foursome became dependent on each other for survival.  Little Cathy was death without her hearing aids; and Randy was a delinquent at the age of ten.  They were both challenges but loved each other very much.  These two children were so adorable, and you can not help falling in love with them!!!And though the miracles may not have been like the miracles of the Bible, I believe they were acts of God.  There were so many, and the author kept me turning pages until I got to sleepy to continue, then I HAD to finish this book today!!!
