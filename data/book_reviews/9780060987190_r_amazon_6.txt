After Mr. Bork passed away I decided to read one of his books as a way of finding out something about the man. This book is a slam dunk indictment of the degeneration of the USA since the sixties. It is very personal for me as a baby boomer(1949) to read Bork's analysis of all that has gone wrong since then and the antecedents in earlier decades. He defines the root problem as the ascendancy of Radical Individualism, if it feels good - do it, and Radical Egalitarianism,  the rejection of merit based reward e.g. quotas.As you read Bork you will come to find he is very astute, a sterling, perceptive mind and a plain spoken, honest critic. If you believe in values this book will upset you. It's distressing that such a fine man was railroaded off the Supreme Court by vaporous, acrid wolves such as the profligate Ted Kennedy. The impact on our society that a fine man cannot serve while a pompous hypocrite is lionized in memoriam is all too visible today.Bork transformed himself from a non-committal pro-choice individual to a pro life proponent. He said it happened when he began to think about abortion rationally. That's a measure of a man.If our present moral decline seems confusing, try Borking yourself. Read this book.Read moreRead less
