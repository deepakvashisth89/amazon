Sheriff Dan Rhodes is one of the best entertainers in the published world.  Even though he puts up with greedy politicians, Babbitt like small town characters and a couple of likeable old misfits that work in in the county jail whose mission in life is to torment him, he is still able to keep his sanity and a good sense of humor.  Anyone who grew up in a small town recognizes every one in the book.  But don't underestimate Sheriff Dan.  He always gets his man, despite his lack of technology, getting beaten up, and the pressure of his upcoming marriage.  His knowledge of what makes people tick is always superior to fancy equipment.  This particular book deals with common small town problems and the evil that is present despite the peaceful setting.  Even though the books in this series are easy to read, humorous, entertaining and just plain fun, there is still enough drama to keep you on the edge of your chair.  I  heartily recommend this good book which is part of a good series.Read moreRead less
