This was a very interesting book because the plot becomes more complex as the story builds by adding additional characters and subplots.  They are on a train met a liquor drummer, tackle a bully working for a rich man and his wife who both are addicts. The rich man asks Jeston Nash to hunt a bear, a very smart cunning gigantic grizzly bear. In addition to fighting to survive a snow blizzard and a ravenous bear, they find themselves hunted by a posse also led by a ruthless leader who wants their heads to take back east for a reward.  The masterful way the story is weaved together makes this one of my favorite recent reads. The persona of Jeston Nash is that of a hapless cowboy who blunders into so many situations that you have to laugh that so much could happen to one person.
