A harrowing but inspiring true story about the unbreakable spirit of a  Tibetan woman  who was suppressed for many years by the Chinese. A voice we all need to hear and which needs to be remembered, as I am sure she is not the only one . In spite of the cruelty this woman lived through, at no point does it become unbearable to read.
