The intertwining of fiction and history was at first interesting, but it wandered to the fantastic when Diego met the Marquis de Sade, Freud, and Hershey (of PA fame) toward the end of the book.  It had good moments when it gave clarity to the Indian culture in Mexico and the Spanish conquest of Mexico.  I realize Diego lived a long life, but he also had a long list of vices-alcohol, gambling, womanizing.  And amazingly he beat the odds and went beyond each one.
