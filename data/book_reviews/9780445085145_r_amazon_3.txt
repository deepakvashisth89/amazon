There are four books in this series, The Conquering Family, The Magnificant Century, The Three Edwards, and The Last Plantagents.  This series covers English history from William the Conquerer to Richard the third.  Costain writes history like a novel which I personally love.  I know that some people object to his approach but I personally feel that the characters of the past come alive and the history is accurate.The Last Plantegents covers the reign of Richard the second through Richard the third and ends with an examination of the case against Richard the third.
