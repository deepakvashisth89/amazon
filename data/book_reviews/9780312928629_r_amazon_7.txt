Mayer’s knowledge of military protocol as well as all the tools of the trade supplements the details of this interesting premise. A conspiracy between opposing Colombian parties commences a Special Forces operation against drug lords. When the conspiracy turns against the American forces, Special Forces warrant officer Riley turns assassin to save the day. The detailed information about the process of Special Forces teams creates believable scenarios. The action is non-stop, sure to keep the reader with the book until the end. Do not look for a love story here.I thoroughly enjoyed the fast pace and the detailed look at military life.  I recommend it for readers of military suspense.
