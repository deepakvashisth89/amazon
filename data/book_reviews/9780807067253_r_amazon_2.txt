I read this book when my daughter was a toddler and it made me me feel so much less alone. Mothers' groups are fine, but moms don't always want to admit to all their worst feelings. This book covers a wide range of feelings. The author isn't afraid to open her heart to us and in journal-like form she really explores the light side and the dark side of motherhood.
