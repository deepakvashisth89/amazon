This novel is one of many in the series featuring Paris police officer Maigret. He is somewhat like Agatha Christie's Hercule Poirot character in that he solves crimes by use of his intelligence rather than by brawn.  in this case he is asked to go to a small town unofficially and look into a case in which a young man was killed and possibly murdered. Arriving there he finds a long-time rival detective, Inspective Carve, there as and on the same case.  Eventually, of course, Maigret solves the mystery.This is the first novel in this series the ai have read and I enjoyed it. The characters are interesting and maigret is a notable protagonist. However I was somewhat disappointed by the ending. Still I recommend this book and series to anyone who enjoys intelligently written murder mysteries.Read moreRead less
