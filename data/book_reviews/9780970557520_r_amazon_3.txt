Plowin' New GroundA first time novelist has encompassed  all the facets of emotion and intrigue into one book.You can see the beauty of the forest and hear the songs of the birds as he walks you through the Louisiana Bayou country.  You experience the heart rendering conflicts and opportunities for learning in familial relationships, grandparents through siblings, AND the changes and challenges that came with integration and acceptance of anyone not Caucasian and Protestant. You rejoice in the enthusiasm and endurance of first love and wrestle with the supreme commitment a later-in- life love demands.Anyone who lived this period of time will appreciate Mr. Brown's writing.  Anyone who didn't can live it with us through his superb story telling.  This book is a "must read."  We have a marvelous new Southern writer in our midst.Five stars for Jerry Wayne Brown.Read moreRead less
