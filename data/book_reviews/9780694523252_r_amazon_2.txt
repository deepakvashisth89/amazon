It's been a great summer being entertained by John Sandford's Prey series.  Winter Prey is the fifth in the series, and I had to wait until I had already gotten to the 18th in the series before I could get this book (library couldn't locate a copy)  In Winter Prey, Davenport finds Winter, (spoiler alert, destined to become his wife some 10 books later) and gets a temporary badge back.This is another well plotted killer who Sandford keeps concealed until the very end.  The plotting is very good, the characters are very well drawn, and the story is fast moving.  For anyone looking for an engaging series you can't go wrong with any of the Prey books by John Sandford.Read moreRead less
