I attended Great Western University that Eugene Whitworth founded in San Francisco. He was my mentor, teacher & friend.  Right after he published Nine Faces of Christ I asked him where he got the incentive & guidance for this story.  His answer was, "I don't know, I sat down to write a mystery and 25 days later this book was finished".  I was raised a Christian but I always thought there was something missing.  This book filled in a lot of those gaps.  The rest were filled in by Mr. Whitworth and his teachings.  That was over 30 years ago and I still, to this day, remember parts of this book in my daily life.  By the way, I'm not from Berkeley or San Francisco. I don't do readings or go to metaphysical fairs.  I'm a retired union electrician that moved to Texas and loves football.
