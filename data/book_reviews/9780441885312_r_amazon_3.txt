This book remains one of my most favorite books. I have no idea how many times I've read it. But I remember after the first time I read it at 16 I passed the book to my mother who felt the same way. My aunt happened to be visiting when my mom finished. And she stole our copy, and went looking for another to give because she wanted to keep it.
