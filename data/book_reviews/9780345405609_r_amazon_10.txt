A well written book. Interesting alternative history novel. Showing how the third American civil war turns in a world war. The confederate states are allied with Great Britain and France, whilst the United states are allied with the Austro Hungarian empire and Germany. World war one conditions on the Canadian/USA boarder,and into Canada. Also same sort of conditions on the US and CSA boarder,and on both sides of said boarders. This novel looks at different historical characters and how they may have fared in this alternate history. It also takes a look at the effects on the lives of the ordinary people caught up in the war. As with other of Mr Turtledove's novels,due to the complexity of the story, this is not a book you could leave, and come back after a long break,and take where you left off ,without a quick flick through to remind yourself of what's happened so far. That said, I found it  a very good read.Read moreRead less
