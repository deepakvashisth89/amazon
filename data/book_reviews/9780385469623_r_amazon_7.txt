I highly recommend this book. As in his other books, Edvard Radzinsky clearly relays information and offers keen insight into the events and personalities influencing Tsar Nicholas--from his early life right up to the time he and his family were murdered. I found it fascinating reading.Initially, I borrowed a copy of this book from a friend, but after getting through only the first few chapters, I knew I needed my own copy for my home collection and for further reference.Included are: an appendix of select sample documents; a bibliography; a collection of photos; and, an index.Read moreRead less
