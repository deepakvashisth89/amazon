"Ashling" is the book where all the real adventure starts in the "Obernewtyn Chronicles".  Elspeth has been hardened by the loss of so many around her, and the awkward formality between her and Rushton is ever there.  The Land around Elspeth is a cold and dangerous place, especially for misfits, and she finds even the rebels may not be on their side.  Don't be threatened by the size of this book; it's addictive.
