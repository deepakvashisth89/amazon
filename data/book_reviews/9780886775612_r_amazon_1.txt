Marion Zimmer Bradley is one of my all time favorite authors. Her books are quite engrossing. Darkover is colonized by a lost earth space ship and then rediscovered by the terrain empire a couple of thousand years later. During that time the inhabitants of the new world have developed their own culture and society, additionally they have also done some cross breeding with a native species which has greatly enhanced the psychic powers of the ruling class of the society. Darkover has developed a technology powered by these psychic abilities, completely in contrast to the mechanical technology of the empire. Darkover which appears to be a feudal society on the surface, has in reality a complex, rich history and a people who have become what they are through adaptation to their environment. Each book is a discreet story that stands on its own, and may be placed in various stages of Darkoven development.
