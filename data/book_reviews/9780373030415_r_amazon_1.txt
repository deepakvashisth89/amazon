Nothing like a MOC marriage in trouble to start the day. Meredith fell in love at first sight and Ryan fell in love with the company shares she was babbling about while trying to get his attention.The hasty marriage was doomed when Meredith finds out on her wedding day that her husband married her for her shares. From then on it was a back and forwards dance as she runs and he pursues. He was aided by the ill health of her elderly relatives and so was she as it gave her a perfect reason to avoid her new husband.This is a sweet fairly lightweight read with a lovely ending and lots of grovelling.Read moreRead less
