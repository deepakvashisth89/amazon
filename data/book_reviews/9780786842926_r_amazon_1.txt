Inspector Gadget is old school, but this is still a good read for kids.  Less than 100 pages, this book is one good read to keep children interested and motivate them to read other adventure books.With his thousands of gadgets, Inspector Gadget works to keep the city clean of crime, but trying to keep all of his parts in check can often be a challenge and lead to some comedy and often time's suspense through out this short novel.
