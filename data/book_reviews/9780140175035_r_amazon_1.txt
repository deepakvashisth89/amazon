This is a remarkable elegant and fascinating story of a young upper-middle-class African American as he graduates from Columbia and starts to make his way in New York's literary world. In beautiful and irresistible prose Pickney takes us inside the mind of a young man as he discovers his identity as a writer, and explores the bohemian city that still existed downtown in the eighties. A great read, by one of the great prose stylists, whose work may be more familiar to readers from his work in The New York Review of Books.Read moreRead less
