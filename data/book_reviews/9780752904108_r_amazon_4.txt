......and it never needed to happen.  Boasting and flaunting their power the English have no worries about the rabble called Zulus.  It happens in the latter part of the 1870's where the English are using their new rifles while the Zulu still uses animal hide shields and carries a long piece of metal on a stick.What could go wrong?  ( famous last words)Our author gives us deep background on both sides  of the men who are about to meet in a head on battle that never needed to happen.  About 70% of the story introduces us to all the major and minor players and  enlightens the reader to the incredibly poor decision making and political  absurdity while in the heat of battle.If you've seen the old ( but one of the best )movies  called "Zulu" about the battle of Rorkes Drift starring Michael Cain....this story is different.The descriptions of battle and heroics on both sides are told without any "candy coating and is a bit gruesome.
