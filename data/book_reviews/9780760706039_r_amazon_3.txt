I am writing a very quick review for this book because it really helped me a lot. As an adult, someone gave this book to me. I read it from cover to cover and went back several times in the text to review... it was so helpful! Listen, I was never very good at math because the explanations were always too lengthy and confusing. Therefore, I left school math behind barely by the skin of my teeth. This book is for conquering every day math... and it WILL help you do that.
