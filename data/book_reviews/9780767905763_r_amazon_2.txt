At first I think it would be easy to find Pelton unbearably self-indulgent in the early going of the book, and more than likely a bit of that tone is needed to write a book like this. But as it continues, you see a sensitive side and his experiences become more enjoyable. In the middle of the book there are a series of photos which go so far to humanizing this faceless tough guy I at first did not like one bit.
