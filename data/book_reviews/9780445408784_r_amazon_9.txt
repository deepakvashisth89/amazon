I am a devotee of Kaminsky's Toby Peters; the storytelling is fast-paced and the dialogue is often 'laugh out loud' funny.  I give a big thumbs up to the preceding 13 books.  So it was disappointing to read this 14th in the series because it moves at a sluggish pace, the humour is limited and, at the end, it was a relief to finish.I also must make mention of the high incidence of typographical errors which have the appalling effect of stopping me 'dead in my tracks' as I try to figure out what correct word should have appeared on the screen - I don't know if this is a problem restricted to the Kindle version.Having said all of that, I have promptly purchased the next in the series - Poor Butterfly - which reminds me ..... these books are terribly overpriced by general comparison with Kindle books.
