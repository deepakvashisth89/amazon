I read The Eight and loved it. So tried Magic Circle. I'm at 92% and don't plan to finish it. The author jumps around from Roman times to modern in a technique that should work, but doesn't.  We jump from Roman legions in Britain, Druids, Boudicea, and Hitler, whose connection with her family lineage calls him "Lucky."  Really?I needed to create a family tree diagram to follow all of Ariel's complicated family ties which also span continents and decades.  Eventually I gave up because I couldn't keep them straight. The hero Ariel is supposed to be a smart nuclear expert nerd with a photographic memory but stupidly falls for a handsome Nazi with who turns out to be a cousin from the convoluted family tree. I really hoped this book would come together at some point but it was just too complicated to follow.If you like this author - read The Eight which is well done and a good romp. This one I'd leave.Read moreRead less
