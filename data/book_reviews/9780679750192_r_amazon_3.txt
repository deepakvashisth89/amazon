Let me start off by saying that I am not a fan of Ian McEwan.  I just can't get into the consistent theme of relational turmoil.  That being said, his debut collection of short stories First Love, Last Rites brilliant intertwines his (admittedly) beautiful, bold voice with some of the darkest stories of magical realism.
