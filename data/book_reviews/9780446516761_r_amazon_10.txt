Larry Bond has written several action packed techno thrillers and the current iteration takes on the topic of a US-Iran showdown. Given that it is taking place prior to 9/11 some elements may seem prophetic, too.The book starts with a warming of relations between the two states, based on a personal friendship between the two protagonists - the commander in chief of the Iranian Army (General Taleh) and a US SOCOM colonel (Peter Thorn) - followed by a raft of terror attacks in the US, all building up to a grand show down in the end.The author is clearly conversant with the subject matter of the military and has done a credible job (even if not so good as inRed Phoenix) of bringing some aspects of it across in the book. Not all is perfect, though, with occassional mistakes (such as wrong small arms calibres) creeping in.Still, the action sequences for the first 80% of the book are quite good and it moves at a very good pace throughout. The main character is relatively likeable if somewhat one dimensional and like inRed Phoenixthe author feels the need to introduce a romantic element into the story.So overall a reasonable action thriller, even if the ending is obvious from about page 20 in the story - which in my book costs it at least the fifth star. Nevertheless, even if not as good as some of his other work, fans will probably be well served in reading the book, and it does introduce some of the characters (Peter Thorn and Helen Gray) whom the author uses in follow-on books, such asDay of Wrath, so reading it is probably useful to the understanding of his later work.Read moreRead less
