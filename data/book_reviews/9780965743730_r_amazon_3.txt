I adore this book and I adore Erik.  I bought a hardcover copy because my paperback copy was falling apart and showed obvious signs of being well loved.  Since I knew this book would continue to be reread many times buying a hardcover copy was a wise investment for me.  This story has a phantom of the opera and beauty and the best nature to it.  It is about a young girl falling in love with a disfigured hermit and their story.  She helps him learn some life lessons as well as learning a major lesson herself.
