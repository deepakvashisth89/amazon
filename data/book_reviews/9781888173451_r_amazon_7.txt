The Inn at the Edge of the World is based on the delightful idea of avoiding Christmas or at least what it has become in the modern world.  An ill-assorted lot of guests ends up on a tiny Scottish island with secrets of its own, including the omnipresent smell of fish.  As usual, Ellis mixes the real and supernatural deftly and believably.  The writing is first rate, the characters real and the ending totally unexpected.Read moreRead less
