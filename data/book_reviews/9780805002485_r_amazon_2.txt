I am an avid Sue Grafton reader.  I haven't read all the books she's written yet, but I am working on it!  I thoroughly enjoyed this one!  One of the things I like about her books is that they are quick-reads - they aren't long and there aren't so many characters that you can't keep them straight.  This book was particularly interesting because it kept me guessing "whodunit?" right up to the last page.  There is always a little heart-racing excitement somewhere in her books and this one didn't disappoint.
