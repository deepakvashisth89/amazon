This book sets out to prove its title is wrong and that Copernicus' book which Arthur Koestler called 'The book nobody read' was in fact thoroughly studied by many people.  Owen Gingerich very interestingly chronicles the years of travel and research which he put into viewing every surviving copy of the two 16'th century editions of "On the Revolutions of the Heavenly Spheres".  In doing so he also provides a fresh perspective on the way the book was received when it was originally printed, as well as a vivid view into the world of book collecting and book stealing.
