As someone with a strong interest in the topic of work-life balance, specifically as it relates to taking your vacation days and time off from work, I knew this handbook must be on my reading list.  While the book was published back in 2003, the relevance of the articles has not changed in the years since.  The Take Back Your Time movement is important to me, but unfortunately progress in the United States remains slow and is not helped by external factors such as the economy and politics.  Success will require a major shift in the thinking of not just American employers and the government, but of working people from every occupation and across the nation.  Therefore reading this compilation of articles is a required starting point to gain a strong understanding of the theme of work-life balance to understand how it relates to the quality of life (aka the pursuit of happiness), prosperity of society, and sustainability of the environment.  These articles all stress the positive outcomes of having more free time and how it is a win-win situation for all parties involved (though you may be skeptical at first).One disadvantage of reading a book on a Kindle device (in my case on my Android mobile phone), is that you don't instantly grasp the thickness of a book or the progress you are making.  Each of the essays was a quick read, so I was glad I could complete reading one while taking the train or sitting at a coffee shop.  However I did not realize the book has 10 parts (who thought there could be so many areas to cover), each of which contains several articles related to a core topic such as The Cost to Civil Society, Health Hazards, and Workplace Solutions.  Every time I reached another "Part" of the book, I was pleasantly surprised to discover there was one more major topic related to work-life balance and taking back your time that I had yet to learn about.  While some articles resonated more with me than others, overall the quality of the writing is very high and each message comes across convincingly.With so many well written and insightful articles by leading experts in their fields, you can quickly learn a lot about the advantages of working fewer hours and having more career flexibility and maybe even become inspired to do something about the problem of "time poverty" as they call it.  Since the articles are each self contained and written by a different author, you can easily pick and choose to read those that will make a impact in your life and career.  It is also worth noting that the appendix contains not just a list of resources, but step by step advice on how to involve the local community and lead the movement to bring about positive change.
