Kendra had had a difficult life.  Her father died in a plane crash when she was very young.  Mom had tried many different men to replace him but none did.  She had spent summers on the family ranch in Wyoming, with cousins.  She was on her way to become a television journalist.  She went to a South American island to do a story on a man that was helping poor island children with food and Medical help.  A hurricane came and she was advised to go home, but she stayed on the Island, and found herself in danger.  A man who didn't speak English stayed with her thru out the storm.  He told his name was Pablo Ayudar(unsure of his said name).  When she returned to base came she realized had become pregnant.  She quit her job and went home to Wyoming and had her baby, Matthew.  Two years later he shows up at her door.  And a very good story ensues.  I enjoyed reading it.  The characters and plot seem real.  I recommend this book to romance genre novels.Read moreRead less
