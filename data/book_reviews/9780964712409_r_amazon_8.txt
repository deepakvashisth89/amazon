It's funny because I knew Manya and Meyer, I met them while attending services at the Ponca City synagogue, but I still found myself asking "are they going to live?" throughout the book. The writing of this book was extremely well done. I didn't feel like I was reading a biographical novel. I learned so much from it as well. I think this book should be used in the upper level Judaism classes at synagogues all over the world. I feel so enlightened and inspired. It makes me wonder how I would have dealt with the same situations those two went through. This is more than a love story between a man and woman, but between a child and parent. Many children these days might ask where mom and dad has been but very few ever really listen. Mazel tov on this wonderful tome.Barbara PappanRead moreRead less
