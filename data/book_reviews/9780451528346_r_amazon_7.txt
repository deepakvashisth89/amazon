I purchased the hardcover of the book as a gift for someone who loves Lewis and Clark. It’s a great book, bigger than expected (thicker) so there’s no lack of information. However, this is a print in Times New Roman font of the words from their journal. Don’t get me wrong. That’s super cool, but I know Lewis and Clark drew doodles and little pictures in their journals if the things they came across and saw to document new things while on their journal. We were really fascinated to see those a long side the text they were writing. This book doesn’t have any of that. Just text. As long as you’re aware of that then it’s a good reproduction of sharing the information that was in their journals.There’s even a note in the beginning saying that other authors corrections and a few altered/unnecessary things were omitted as well as leaving the original misspelling of words in the text in order to keep it authentic.
