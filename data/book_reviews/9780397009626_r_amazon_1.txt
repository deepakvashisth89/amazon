A household full of odd secrets, a past death, and a current murder - but whodunit?  As suspicion falls on one and then another of the family members and friends, it is hard to pick out the murderer, since almost everyone had an opportunity and a reason to kill Arthur Jameson.  And during the investigation of this murder, comes the idea that the death of the deceased's second wife the year before, might not have been an accident after all.  The usual suspects are present - jealousy, money (and the lack of it), romantic triangles, and the announcement that the victim was about to marry again....It is a daunting task to attempt to collect all of the Lockridge mysteries, but I'm working on it.  They are all set in an earlier time (this one written in 1973), and bring back a New England aura of well-bred horror at the idea of a - gasp - murder taking place in such a nice town, among such nice people.
