Growing up in the Vietnam Era I have sought to understand why it happened, how it happened and what happened. I've read other great books on people's experiences. I have traveled to Vietnam to see in person the cities and towns I heard a out on the nightly news. I climbed down into the VC tunnels outside if Siagon to put myself into the minds of the VC.With this book I was really able to place my mind into that of an American soldier and "get it". Mr Caputo was able to open a door to what soldiers experienced, not only in body but also in mind and heart. I went through the range of emotions, frustrations and disillusion with him. I have felt them most of my life but he was able to articulate them I. A way that I could finally reconcile them and accept that they are a part of the American experience.I will definitely recommend this to my son and grandchildren.
