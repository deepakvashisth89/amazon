I have enjoyed all the Oxford Don mysteries, and this one was as well written and interesting as all the others. Spoiler alert: it was slightly different, though, having a Cold War element, rather than just a murder mystery, emerge in the latter chapters of the novel. I have to admit, I did not fully understand all the details at the resolution. It has been awhile since reading this Helen MacInnes style of writing.
