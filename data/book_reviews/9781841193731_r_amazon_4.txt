The 23 short stories in this collection ranges from late Republican Rome to mid 16th century England.  Many of the stories feature characters from their own series and that left me lost a good deal of the time.  Some of the stories are very tight in terms of problem-investigation-solution while others there appears to be more luck than logic, external forces acting in place of main detective's skills, and that can be a problem for the mystery genre period regardless of the setting.The volume is organized chronological in case you really only like Medieval stories or Renaissance or Roman ones; sadly there are no stories from period before Rome or outside of Rome during it's five century imperial stretch.  There have been wonderful mysteries set in ancient Israel, classical Greece, even the earliest periods of Chinese history so it was disappointing to see none of those setting covered.Read moreRead less
