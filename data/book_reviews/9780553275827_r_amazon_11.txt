Platoon Leader is not only another story about the Vietnam War, but also a very good insight into the sensible matter of leading troops in platoon size. James McDonough describes very good the balance between authority and care for your troops. Especially in combat zones where your platoon has casualities leadership is always a very sensible affair. McDonough tells a lot about his thoughts when leading his soldiers and how every aspect of his own words and actions had influence onto his men. One aspect he pronounced is the evaluation of the different characters in his platoon and how he decided where the best place for everyone is. Besides this also the described patrols and perimeter defenses of his outpost and their tactical background are very lively written. There is really suspense when reading his book.I could recommend reading this book not only for military leaders.Read moreRead less
