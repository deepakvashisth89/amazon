Though I love Spalding Gray's movies, I only bought this at a garage sale to re-sell on Amazon, but it goes for pennies so I kept it.So glad I did! Had a free weekend, picked it up, and could not put it down until I finished 24 hours later!Of course, given Gray's sad and untimely death, there's lots of foreboding about his own mortality, but the feeling you get after reading is of hope and transcendence.  This book actually made me THINK more than I have in quite a while.  Not to mention, his various adventures make for a rollicking good time, with copious amounts of sex & drugs along with meditation and enlightenment.  Very touching memories of his family, friends, and lovers.It's worth more than a gosh-darned penny. BUY IT!Read moreRead less
