It is a while, thank God, that I have needed this book - and the others in the series - as my husband has been in recovery for 11 years but it was the best of the very many books I read during that awful period of my life. I have purchased copies over those years for people who are in need and I am about to purchase another one for someone who is in a terrible state trying to help a family member.
