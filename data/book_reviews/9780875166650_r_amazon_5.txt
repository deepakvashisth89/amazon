I was loaned this book to read while I was going through 5 days of drug withdrawal. The chapters are filled with suspense as J seeks enlightenment with different masters. I don't know how I could have endured the withdrawal if I didn't have this book to read. I finished the book just as the withdrawal ended. And just as J was united with God, I hit the pink cloud and was given new life instructions.
