I love L Frank Baum books, I love the Wizard of Oz books in particular. I love the Books of Wonder series, reprinting the original books, by a great author and wonderfully illustrated by artists...not like new books, with ugly drawings and messy pictures. John R Neill's art work is sublime. A bit like Art Nouveau, and so sensual.
