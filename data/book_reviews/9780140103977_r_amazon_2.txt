I have loved Fay Weldon's work for many years.  She has a distinctive style - witty, trenchant, Vonnegut-like.  You know how sometimes when reading you can skip some of the exposition just to get to the good parts?  You can't do that with Fay. Every line is rewarding.  This book has something to say about rural culture, hypocrisy, class, and, like all of her books, the things that women put up with to make their way in the world. If you like your humor dry, this one's for you.Read moreRead less
