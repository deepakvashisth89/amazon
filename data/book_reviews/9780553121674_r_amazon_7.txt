I think this is my first Grace Livingston Hill book.  I will definitely be reading more after this.  This story keeps you hanging on waiting to see what comes next.  I normally read thrillers with lots of action.  Although this is totally different, the story and the way it's written is terrific.  Ms. Hill keeps you hanging on through the whole story with tension between all the characters.  It's a wonderful story with many lessons to be learned.  This one will be worth reading a second time.  It's easily one of the best books I've read.Read moreRead less
