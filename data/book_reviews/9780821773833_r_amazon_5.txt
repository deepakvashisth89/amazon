I found that I have so much enjoyed Annette Blairs books.  I don't normally like sequels but after reading just one I had to get all of them.  You don't get the same thing over and over.  I found myself laughing out loud, love the way she knits the children into the stories and with this first of the books I have read I thoroughly enjoyed all of them.  I recommend this author and this series, you will not be bored stiff.  I enjoy these so much that I have read them all over again when I found myself bored stiff with other authors.  There is a lot of sex, but I must say that it does not bore you to tears or make you want to vomit.  At first the silly antics of Gideon only makes what happens funnier and with the twins, love those little guys and even little sweet pea a family grows.  Sabrina is priceless, as with most women she has gone through much in her life and suffer much, women often do and they come out strong for it.  Reminded me of how my own grandfather's mail order bride traveled through several states to reach him and was brave enough to bring her children with her and married a man she didn't know and had only a couple of written letters from him and an old worn out picture.  He married twice more to mail order brides.  Women were very brave to do such a thing.  Women have left home and country to travel to marry men that they did not know, many of them abussed for their effort.  Some made it and lived a long happy life.
