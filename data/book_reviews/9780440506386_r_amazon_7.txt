This is not one of those "do X to solve your child's Y problem."  It basically just lets you know what is going on with your 2 and 2.5 year old.  The researchers observed 1000's of two year olds over several years and this book is the culmination of their research - in an easy to read format, as well.  They do offer general advice - take it with a grain of salt because some of it is REALLY outdated.  But the insights on what is going on, what to expect, what's "normal", and the list of toys and books appropriate for two year olds is invaluable.
