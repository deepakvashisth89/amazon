Peace Kills is a tour of relatively modern post battle fronts literally and actual fronts in the war for hearts and minds. Peace kills is a revised collection of articles from american literary magazines. It follows on in good form from PJ greats likeHolidays in Hell (Picador thirty). I read the Kindle version of Peace Kills but the print is the same.Compiled in 2004 before the eventual seemingly never ending duration of the second gulf war or the afghanistan intervention were known PJ travels the world sticking his nose in other peoples business and fairly often their liquor cabinets. You might not agree with his libertarian eyes view of the world; but through his travels in the middle east he meets a number of interesting characters mainly in the search of his next drink.Its eminently quotable and in places harrowing but mainly its just hilarious; which makes the fact there is only a single quote on the popular highlights on the Kindle edition a surprise. Its an absolute doozy of a quote though"And no matter how horrific the terrorist attack, its conducted by losers. Winners dont need to hijack airplanes. Winners have an airforce."Peace Kills is a great read and a good introduction to PJ O'Rourke's work.Read moreRead less
