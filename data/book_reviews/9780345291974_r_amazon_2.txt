"Rendezvous with Rama" is classic Clarke/SciFi fiction, ranking with Clarke's "2001: A Space Odyssey." "Rama II" is a trashy soap opera masquerading as science fiction. Don't torture yourself with any "Rama" sequels; instead, get Clarke's "Collected Stories" (about 50% of the collection is worthy of reading) or re-read Stanislaw Lem's "The Invincible" and/or "Solaris."Read moreRead less
