The only reason why this book is 3 stars instead of 2 is because it kept my attention.Aimee was extremely annoying, not so much that it stopped me from reading even though I wanted to. She was annoying because her reasoning just didn't seem logical to me. Her hatred felt contrived and forced. She was ridiculous because her family was starving yet instead of marrying a man that proved he cared her and did not want to hurt her she was carrying unreasonable hatred against him because of where he was born. Even Savannah her companion was reasonable when it came Nick. Like really? The only other option was to become a prostitute and instead of being happy to come out of a desperate situation she curses the man and acts like the decision was really difficult.I might have been able to understand her hatred if as a reader I felt she was just a tortured heroine and she had a reason for feeling the way she did, but I didn't see it.  Especially because from the moment Nick came into her life, her son wasn't starving and she was protected.I also didn't understand why they fell in love with each other. Nick was altogether too sweet and nice and Aimee was the one always carrying around the anger.  I think Aimee did everything possible for Nick to hate her, but he didn't so you could tell he really loved her. Regardless of this, I don't understand their love story. This is one area where I believe many authors make the mistake. They don't allow the characters to develop a relationship. They don't allow them to spend time together and learn about their past and talk about common interest or just life in general. Nick and Aimee conversations consisted of the back and forth of Aimee telling Nick he hated her and Nick telling Aimee he cared and wanted her. Aimee also refused to admit to Nick, even though he suspected and possibly knew, that Brand was his son. So that was what their conversation consisted of.Brand spoke years older than his age at times and it really took me out of the story. It's almost as if the author couldn't quite decide how to make a 5 year old speak like a 5 year old.Read moreRead less
