I don't want to spoil anything here, but the storyline building up to Unity was nothing short of fantastic.  The DS9 relaunch has me completely hooked.  Unity lives up to the storyline, but as with any book the resolution is key to satisfaction.  I found the resolution of a 5 novel storyline to be much too quick and even a little weak.  There are a few other things in this book that happen that I felt could have been left out due to the success of the previous relaunch novels.  There is simply no reason to rewind to the past in this series in order to advance the storyline.All that said I still highly recommend this book.
