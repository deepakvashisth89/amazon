This is said to be the first spy novel ever written. Published in 1903, it is still considered a classic. I found it a fascinating page-turner and a brilliantly-told story. However, if the reader is not somewhat conversant with the basics of sailing and sailors' vocabulary, it probably won't have the impact it would otherwise. The author had intimate, first-hand knowledge of sailing in the area depicted, which requires excellent seamanship because of the extreme weather and tides there. His writing is so good, you can practically smell the salt air. In fact, the author's own life is as extraordinary as this book.Read moreRead less
