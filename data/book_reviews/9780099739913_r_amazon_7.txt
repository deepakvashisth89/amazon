Who would expect a book about a cranky librarian and a boy with an endocrine problem to be so funny and emotionally enthralling.  McCracken can sure do stuff with words: "She had a behind as big as an open dictionary." "She had teeth like cards held sloppily in a player's hand."  But reading the book is like living the life of the librarian, so that you are made to see what she sees and feel what she feels.
