Chief Inspector Douglas Quantrill has a new DS assigned to him who makes him rather uneasy because he is a high flyer and better educated than Quantrill himself.  The discovery of girl's body face down in a shallow river on a May morning at first seems to be a tragic accident but DS Tait thinks it's suspicious.This is a well written police procedural crime novel set in Suffolk.  The characters are well drawn and I particularly liked the spiky relationship between Quantrill and Tait which I thought was very well done.  There are plenty of clues and red herrings to keep the reader entertained and village life is brought vividly to life.I had not heard of this author before but I found this first book in the series well worth reading.
