The Impossible Woman is Emma Darcy's 8th romance novel. When budding landscape gardener Judy Campbell delivered some turf to a new and very impressive mansion, she didn't realise the gorgeous hunk in shorts in the backyard was rich architect Malcolm Stewart. By the time they have laid two truckloads of turf and had a swim in the pool together, Judy finds herself succumbing to Mal's attractions. But as things develop, she discovers who he is and that marriage is the furthest thing from his mind.
