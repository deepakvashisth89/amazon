Three and a half stars (rounded up)While the illustrations may not be great (very simple and old-fashioned for modern society), the contents is solid.At the surface, this is a book about counting and learning to read basic words (goes from 12 down to 1). On each two-page spread, there is that quantity of an endangered animal doing something Eco-friendly like recycling or watching for polluters. While there are various other details on the page that could open a discussion with your child, the pictures are not quite catchy enough to necessarily start the conversation.The back of the book has some more environmentally-minded suggestions on what your family can do to better the environment. They are also great suggestions in a pleasing format.If the illustrations were more eye-catching and appealing, this book would have definitely earned 5 stars from me; as it stands, it isn't very engaging.Read moreRead less
