The Diamond Sutra is a companion sutra, or sermon of the Buddha, to the more famous Heart Sutra.  The Heart Sutra is said to be a summation of the Diamond Sutra.  Suffice to say that the Diamond Sutra is pretty difficult to understand, let alone appreciate.  Thich Nhat Hanh takes on this difficult sutra with patience and thorough explanations.  The repetitous language of the sutra will put off most new Buddhists, but in this book, Thich Nhat Hanh breaks down explanations section by section, so you have time to digest what's being taught.I think Thich Nhat Hanh makes a great effort in teaching such a challenging text, and having worked my way through this book a couple times, I have found the Diamond Sutra to be one of my most favorite sutras in Buddhism.
