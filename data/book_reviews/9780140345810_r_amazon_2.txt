William Sleator is my favorite author, and this is one of his absolute best. Like his other novels, Green Futures takes an intriguing science concept and uses that as a springboard for a very enjoyable, very thought-provoking adventure. Like his all-time best novel (House of Stairs), this one does a fantastic job exploring the damaged interplay between flawed and sometimes horrible people.
