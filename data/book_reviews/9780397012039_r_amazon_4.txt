This book didn’t come through for me.  It could be its age, it was written in 1977.  It could be the format, lots of switching between players.  I picked it up with summer expectations looking for something light and engrossing and in theory it fits, with short chapters, a soap opera-like atmosphere, and a crashing finish, literally.  Unfortunately the ultimate roadblock was the massive number of characters.  My Kindle X-ray listed 100 by the letter M and I couldn’t find a solid attachment to any of them.
