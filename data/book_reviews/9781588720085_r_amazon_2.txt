Determined to understand the Kabbalah and frustrated at having read so many books that spend so much time debating the history of Kabbalah without explaining much else that makes any sense, I stopped in a library when I was in Newport Rhode Island and selected about 10 books on the subject.  I sat in a chair and thumbed my way through them.  Same ol', same ol'... Until I picked up this book.  So fascinating was it to me that I went back over the next three days to finish the book.  Then I ordered it on Amazon as well and reread it.  What the author does with this book is explain the why...and the attitude we must have as we process our way through life.  He does not delve into the history or explain the Sephiroth, nor does he speak of emanations or of pathworking.  But the information he imparts is so is essential to your understanding of the Kabbalah and to spirituality in general that it is a must-read for those trying to experience their own divine nature.Read moreRead less
