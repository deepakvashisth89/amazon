Inspector Cockrill (Cockie) is on an Italian tour in this book, and he’s not liking it.  He’s just too British. He should really holiday in Britain.  And when a young woman in his group is murdered, he is appalled by the sloppy ways and irrational attitude of the local representative of law and order.  And the Grand Duke of the island they’re stuck on has absolute power.  He just wants someone to hang for the crime  — doesn’t much matter who.  Cockie is desperate to prevent a terrible miscarriage of justice.There are wonderful vivid characters in this book.  There’s  a  charmingly unexpected love story, as well as an unlovely love.  Nobody is quite who they seem. The twists just keep coming, and are totally believable. I’ve ordered all the Inspector Cockrill mysteries in print.Read moreRead less
