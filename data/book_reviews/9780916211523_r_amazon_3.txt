First I'd like to start by saying that I purchased a used copy with pretty low expectations on the condition based on both the price and the seller's description. I was pleasently surprised to find that someone had laminated the cover and the bood was in excellent condition. It had some very minor shelf wear but was as close to new as you could want from a used book. I highly encourage others to consider buying used as I have yet to be burned.That said, the book itself has a lot of great info for running vampires as villains in a Rifts setting as well as the possibility of using them for player characters. There are a few maps and the typical regional info that one comes to expect from Palladium's world books. The equipment is definately vampire hunter centric but if that surprises you then this probably isn't/wasn't the book for you. All in all a pretty solid book though I hear this version of the book will soon be obsolete.Read moreRead less
