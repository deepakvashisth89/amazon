This is a very well written book that sheds a great deal of light on the "real" JFK NOT the fake Camelot version.  While some have accused the book of being too negative, I thought it was very fair.  It pointed out the many good qualities that JFK had & the unfortunate flaws, like all of us, that he possessed.  It discussed his bravery during WW2 & other positive qualities.  Much of JFK's problems clearly stemmed from his horrible father & cold mother.  It's interesting to speculate what kind of person he could have been if he had had a normal childhood.
