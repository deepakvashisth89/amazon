I had this book as a child. I bought it for my niece who is dual heritage but identifies as white. I wanted her to be able to identify with other mixed race families and children. The family dynamics of this book mirrors her own which is great.The art work is lovely and the story line is nice. I'd recommend it as an addition to any book collection and especially those that have inclusion and diversity as core principles.The topic of a mix race family isn't directly addressed but there is scope to discuss it if you chose to.My niece enjoys the book.Read moreRead less
