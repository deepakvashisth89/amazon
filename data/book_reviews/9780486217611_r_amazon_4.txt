Insightful, and hopefulSchopenhauer is by no means easy. The vocabulary is difficult, because he is attempting to communicate something that transcends words. I can give you an attempt in few words what this (life long) work is saying. One, my ability to reason is limited by my nature. Two, I comprehend the cosmos through representations, and I am the subject. Three, the drive in me is irrational and insatiable driving me into a miserable state. All of this is a horrible cycle. I am Ouroboros attempting to eat his tail. This unconscious drive is "the Will". Therefore, the cosmos is "will" and representation. Schopenhauer, offers me a possible solution to the nightmare cycle and "Will" which is the cause of all human suffering. I can find a moment of relief in art and become lost in it. Music can aid me. I can become less of my insatiable "will" and deny it. I can glimpse into Platonic ideas and see the Cosmos and not the individual. I can accept that my idea of myself as an individual is an illusion. I can remove my worldly desires. I can deny the "Will".Read moreRead less
