Insightful and interesting.  A super-genius who invented calculus to prove that the Earth moved around the Sun.  Astronomers already believed that to be true but couldn't prove it.  He was shielded from the Catholic Church because he lived in England who had broken from the Catholic Church.  Also invented the reflecting telescope and was constantly investigating all sorts of things like light.  He's called a sorcerer because he even investigated sorcery and probably got mercury poisoning because it was said to central to alchemy (his careful scientific research dispelled the popular belief that alchemy was possible using the methods and equipment available at the time).  He was a cold unfriendly man who couldn't relate to other people.
