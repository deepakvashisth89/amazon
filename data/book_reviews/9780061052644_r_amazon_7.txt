The premise of this well-written book is excellent. The physics that drives the story can be, at times, difficult. But this shouldn’t cause potential readers to shy away. I could only give this book four stars, however, because of the unnecessary, out-of-place, and clumsy “physical” episode that takes place near the end. Why Mr. Egan felt compelled to wander down this path seems a mystery as it does nothing for the book’s plot. Otherwise, an excellent read (if a bit cerebral, which is a good thing).Read moreRead less
