Three and a half stars really.  RENDER UP THE BODY, the first book in the series blew me away, but A SUGGESTION OF DEATH by Marianne Wesson missed the mark.  Our hero Cinda Hayes, a lawyer you want on your side tries to help a young lady deal with memories from her past. The story creeps along and of course is filled with legalize that I found exciting in the first book but the detail in this one slows an already slow pace to a crawl.  The book is ok, but that's it for me, just ok. One hundred pages shorter would have helped.Read moreRead less
