As a fellow Dominicana and also a graduate of Cornell University I could relate in more ways than one with Ms. Perez and many of the struggles that she related in this novel.  The manner in which she expressed the life  situations and approaches taken to ameliorate her familial situations was  priceless.  It is a book worth reading not once but over and over again.  Although there were some loose ends that needed to be tied up, I still felt  like it was a book that made a point and conveyed a very powereful message.
