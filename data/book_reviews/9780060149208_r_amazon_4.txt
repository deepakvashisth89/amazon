The first book I've read of Colleen McCullough. I couldn't put it down! Written so long ago, about a time even longer ago, yet the issues are still so relevant today. I laughed, I cried, I felt the pain and anguish of Sister Langtry. I hoped for a happy ending, but instead cried some more. Great book by a new favorite author.
