A Family Affair by Rex Stout is the last Nero Wolfe mystery novel.  The final book of the Nero Wolfe series.  And it really hits home when a bomb is used to kill a person.  Right in Wolfe’s house.  Making it very much a personal matter.  One to keep in the family.  Wolfe is mad and tries to solve the mystery without a client or hope of pay.  And the end is surprising.  A big surprise which I will not spoil.  First published in 1975 mine was printed in 1976.  Oh, and Watergate is involved in a way.Warning - Don't read this book until you have a few other books of the series under your belt.
