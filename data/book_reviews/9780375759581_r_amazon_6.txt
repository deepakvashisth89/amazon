I came to this book after listening to James Atlas read "Remembering Roth" on Audible. Philip Roth convinced Bellow to work with Atlas but lived to regret the recommendation when Atlas decided to spend as much time in the book discussing Bellow's sex life as his literary life, or so Roth and Bellow decided, which is why Bellow recruited Zachary Leader to write what is now the standard biography of the Great Man. Both are worth reading. The difference is that Leader doesn't fancy himself a novelist and accepts that a biographer fulfills a subordinate role, whereas Atlas seems to resent playing second fiddle, whether to Bellow or Roth. If you want salacious detail, and gossip from the women who Bellow spurned or who spurned him, this is the book for you. Also read Leader. And, of course, revisit Bellow.Read moreRead less
