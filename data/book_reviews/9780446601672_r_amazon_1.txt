I enjoyed this book. I especially liked the character of Astrid Kinkaid. Driven, athletic, obsessive, angry. She was a cool heroine, and kind of the glue that held the whole motley band together.This story was very interesting. Just when you think you understand what is going on, the author changes the rules on you. I can't get into the details without spoiling the plot, but suffice it to say, this book is like a tiger. You grab it by the tail, and hold on for the ride.This book is similar to 2001: A Space Odyssey in that it starts out with somewhat of an action / adventure narrative. A space chase. But it soon transforms into something completely different, and it never stops changing. And like that story, the ending is somewhat open to interpretation.It also reminds me of Alice in Wonderland / Through the Looking Glass. A reality that slowly gets twisted until it gets to the point that you don't know what reality is anymore. It's a lot more ambitious than those stories though, because it focuses on the meaning of existence.I liked the idea of a sci fi author suddenly winding up as God. The right man for the job, so to speak. That was crazy, but fun.The original title of this story was going to be "The Space-Time Juggernaut". "The Transmigration of Souls" is an awesome title too. Both are good titles, and both fit the story quite well.Recommended :)Read moreRead less
