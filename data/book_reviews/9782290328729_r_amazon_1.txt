I bought this book on the recommendation of my French doctor. He told me that it was imperitive that I read the French original to appreciate the nuances of the author's story and characterizations. In fact, I bought both versions and I confess that the English is a great translation in my opinion. I flip-flop between texts. If you are a French reader I recommend the original version.
