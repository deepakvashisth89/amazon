This book is part of the lovable collection of books about Carl and the baby that he takes care of.  I know other reviews are critical of a rottweiler babysitting a child while the parents go out.  It should be obvious to adults, however, that this book and the rest of the Carl books are not really supposed to be a parenting guide.  It's just a fun book about the adventures and love between a baby and her dog.In this book, Carl and the baby go on a Christmas adventure together.  It showcases the baby and Carl having fun together doing holiday activities.  They also participate in some good will activities to illustrate the true meaning of the holidays.  The illustrations in the book are very pretty and as usual, there is very little text which allows little ones to use their own imagination to create the story in the book.  One thing I like to do is to have the children that I am reading it with to "read" it to me, so I can hear their stories and ideas.  This particular Carl book is a great holiday read!Read moreRead less
