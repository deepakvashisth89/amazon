A vivid evocation of life in early twentieth-century Cape Bretón, with a credible range of characters facing themselves and those around them, and trying to make their way in life. Reading this work absorbs you skilfully in the daily challenges of the main characters; the backbreaking work in the mines, the ever-controlling presence of Celtic tradition and culture.
