Not bad. Well-written and packed with lots of lore, but the actual "moving parts" here - the new RCCs, OCCs etc - are either grossly overpowered or are kinda dull. All of this would be fine but the book constantly refers you to read other books for important information, such as the stats for a Worm of Taut, a creature the Necromancer can summon. Why they would include a new OCC such as the Necromancer then tell you to buy another book to get the full picture of which spells he can cast is baffling and I'm genuinely kinda mad about it. On the other hand, the Palladium approach to the Four Horsemen is super cool and has me thinking up ways to include them in my campaign.Unless you're dead-set on running a campaign on the African continent you can probably give this book a pass.
