This is the third book in Federation War Books and I like it. I used to be a military sci-fi snob, Baen Books and that kind of thing. So to put it plainly, I enjoy Rick Shelley's books. They have a good pace, sometimes are predictable but this is not a suspense novel, it's mil sci-fi and that's what I love to read. I'm not going to go into the book, just in case it is purchased by someone but in my opinion unless you are a complete mil sci fi snob you will enjoy the series. I have Amazon Prime and some of these I could get for free but, I still buy them. That's how much I enjoy Rick's writing plus I may want to read them later on and what if I'm not a prime member?
