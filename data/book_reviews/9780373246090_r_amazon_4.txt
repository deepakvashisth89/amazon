This is the third in a series of four.  You don't have to have read the previous two to enjoy this one.  This is the third orphaned boy taken care of by their "spinster" aunt.  The aunt hand picks a wife for this now adult orphan.  Without being obvious, Aunt Destiny  makes it impossible for these two not to fall in love!  The hand of the master (mistress) at work.  The book is a delight and typical of Ms. Woods.  Read it!  You'll love it!Read moreRead less
