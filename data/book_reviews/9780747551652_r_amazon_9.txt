Great plot; superb, vividly drawn, fascinating, varied characterisations; clear, effective prose; marvellous, sick, immature, self-centred bad behaviour;  the blackest of black humour;  genuinely innovative story structure (the diary format referred to by the other reviewer is by turns the instrument and the subject of many of the plot developments - very clever).I love when you get that feeling that "someone has been here ahead of me" when you're reading a book - little things that are put in early on and their significance only revealed much later so that you get that "ohmigod" deja-vu feeling - this has loads of those.The prose is full of marvellous little pearls you wish you'd thought of yourself:  "there is a methedrine to our madness" (on his rationale for something they did on an acid trip) and lots lots more.  Buy it.  I'm getting Arabian Nightmare next.Just one thing Robert, you go DOWN the hill from the station to get to the Maltings! :-)Read moreRead less
