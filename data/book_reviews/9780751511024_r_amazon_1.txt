This is one of the first novels in this wonderful series and sets the stage for the times, the recurring characters and the personalities of these 12th Century people in the town of Shrewsbury in England near the border with Wales .  If you have seen any of the TV shows on the PBS mystery series - starting about 15 years ago, you will enjoy the printed page more.  Ellis Peters language is incisive, intelligent and never mundane. This is a book to enjoy even if you know "who done it" ahead of time. I never knew about the civil war in England in the mid 1100's - before  Henry II (The Lion in Winter) -. before Robin Hood - before the Magna Carta. But through the lens of historical fiction such as the Brother Cadfael novels you can enjoy all those other tales with a richer delight.  A slim book - but a rich story..Read moreRead less
