The residents of Tellingford, a rural English village, learn that murder is a crime which can happen next door, or across the street, or even closer to home.  Ronald Trimm is a model citizen, a successful antiques dealer.  But at week's end when the shop is closed, he indulges in erotic fantasy.  His dreams suddenly come to life when a rich, lonely widow seduces him.  Ronald expects an affair to develop, but is rebuffed.  His next sexual humiliation leads to murder, and the hunt is up!  This story is quoted as "very convincing" by The Financial Times, and "delicious characterization" by The Yorks Post.
