This book was fine - it was fine. All is fine. The plot was fairly generic and a little silly in parts as there was unnecessary drama and angst on our heroine's part near the end, but it was fine. Nothing really grated and nothing really stood out. It was fine (see the theme here).Fairly typical romance which left me unconvinced that the couple had any spark. This is, however, a very clean read with no sex scenes so it could work for younger readers, especially those who have not inured themselves to the trope.On a tangential note though, I was surprised to see that this was published in 1999 - it felt so dated. It's probably the trope of a sectary / assistant falling in love with her boss. The deliberate pursuit of the bosses by the group of assistant struck me as very backward. The trope still exists as does the workplace romance trope but there is less deliberate intent to pursue the hot executive to the alter. I felt I was reading a match making session during which all I could think of was the potential for harassment lawsuits. I felt bad for the men as they didn't know what was going to hit them.
