I love Charlotte MacLeo's booksd (as well as the books she wrote as Alysa Craig).  This one volume is the sole exception.  I have read it twice and truly disliked it.  In my reading group, 1 person loved it, the rest didn't.  There aren't too many character developments that would be important in the  next in the series.  I would suggest waiting until the book goes on sale or using Kindle unlimited if you wish to buy it.  The book just has a different tone than the other Shandy books, or the Kelling books.  It basically goes into a major fantasy sequence, which just didn't click.  She wrote wonderful comic mysteries, but lets say she was no Terry Pratchett.
