Over the past couple of years, I have felt like a bruised reed and a smoldering wick - feeling weak and wavering in my Faith, but still trying to cling onto our Lord and learning to Trust Him.Max Lucado has brought bible stories into the here and now and allowed me to step into the shoes of Jesus and other people in the bible.  Many stories made me cry, as I realized how much Jesus truly understands what we go through as human beings in sometimes difficult relationships.I met a lady at a retreat who was going through a similar season to me (desert, wilderness) and she recommended this book to me - am so glad she did! I would recommend it to others who are feeling weak in faith and hope - it's not a "feel good/self help" book, but one that will remind you of your humanity in a fallen world and how Jesus understands and acknowledges this.
