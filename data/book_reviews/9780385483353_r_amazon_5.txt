I didn't realize how long the book was until I started reading it on my Kindle and I was worried that I would become bored and abandon it before finishing.  There were some parts that I skimmed, especially towards the end, but on the whole I enjoyed it.  I had already read "My Life in France" and "As Always, Julia The Letters of Julia Child and Avis Devoto", so I knew quite a bit about her life.  I admire the author for his attention to detail and that he was able to make the reader "know" Julia Child, even if you already thought you knew everything about her.  I will admit, though, that my attention waned once Paul was out of the picture.
