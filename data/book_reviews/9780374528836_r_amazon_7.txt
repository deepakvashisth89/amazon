A male friend turned me on to John McPhee about a year ago. Since then, I have read six of his books. Not only is he a prolific writer, but he can make a subject like shad fishing (I do not indulge in the sport) totally fascinating. He has a grasp of history and he seems out experts in every aspect of the field he is writing about. As a female, I never would have picked up these books on my own because they seemed male-oriented. Shame on me for being sexist! McPhee's books are just good writing. I highly recommend!Read moreRead less
