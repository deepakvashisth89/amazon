This book was extremely informative and eye opening for me.  There were many things that had not occurred to me that this book talked about.  It uses children (now adults) that were transracially adopted and interviews them for open and honest feedback.  This book is primarily about black children adopted into white families.  I don't believe any other culture was interviewed.  It does a fantastic job at showing the differences in how each child adapted in their families.  I believe anyone that is thinking about adopting out of their own race should read this book.  It really shows the reader what these children aren't getting that they need.  It also shows what they did get (love, family, security) however, that is not the point of the book.  The only thing about this book that I think it missed is interviewing older children that were adopted into a transracial home after infancy.  I would have loved for it to include a one or two of those interviews.  I would even suggest this book if someone isn't thinking of transracially adopting.  The more information obtained before the adoption the better.  I highly recommend this book.Read moreRead less
