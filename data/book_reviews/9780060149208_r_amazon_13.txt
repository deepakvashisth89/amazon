A splendid book which I could not put down until it was finished. Sister Honour lived for her work, such care, dedication and compassion on a ward for soldiers with psychiatric problems.  She was loved by them all  in return.  One almost felt one was with her as each patient was described, and the difficulties and heat with which she coped.  A new patient was not well received by the others but he calmly did his best and succeeded in being accepted.  Sister Honour was romantically drawn to him, but at the end of the war when the ward was disbanded, the ending was sad.  This is a book which I have not forgotten.  Highly recommended for those who enjoy a rehabilitation hospital type story.Read moreRead less
