I actually listened to the audiobook of this and was captivated throughout, so I bought the hardcopy for my wife. This is more like a thorough history of civilization up to new testament times, even detailing discoveries relating to the evolution of homo sapiens as a species, with additional emphasis on the texts that came from these civilizations and how the archaeological record relates to said texts. Rarely did I come across a chapter that didn't hold my interest.
