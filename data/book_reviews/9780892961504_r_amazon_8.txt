This is the 6th of Dell Shannon’s series about LAPD homicide detective Luis Mendoza. There are only two events in the lives of the detectives: El Señor, Luis’ cat, has developed a taste for rye, and at the very end of the book Alison and Luis become the parents of twins, one of each!The primary case in this book is about the murder of Bella Ingram. While it looks straightforward, it turns out to be quite complex. Due to two separate attempts to frame the husband as the murderer, the motive appears to be the deceased woman’s fortune.
