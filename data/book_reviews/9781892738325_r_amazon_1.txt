Written in 1987, this book was like time travel. Once you get past the smoking everywhere (even on airplanes), it is an intriguing novel. It starts as an innocent conversation on a plane, and evolves into a nightmare. This book is not for the faint of heart, there are some rape scenes that are difficult to read. The end left me gasping, but not for the reasons you might suspect in a suspense novel. Read for yourself and see how you like the ending.
