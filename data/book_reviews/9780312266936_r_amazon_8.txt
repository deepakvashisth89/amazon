Although I'm not an expert on Buddhism, I got a real spiritual lift from this book. The author explores Buddhist legend and thinking in a truthful, modern, loving voice. Anyone who is interested in Tibet, Buddhism, or adventure will enjoy "What the Lotus Said."Swanson proves that it is possible to journey to the center of self awareness without denying Western background and values.
