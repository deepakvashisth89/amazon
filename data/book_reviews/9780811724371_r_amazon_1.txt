I have had a copy of Denali Journal for years, and have often gone back to it - for the joy of reading and the the wealth of knowledge it holds about Denali National Park.As a tour guide here, I know some about many aspects of the park, but this book holds decades of fascinating tidbits about the land and wildlife. Any day of the year I can pick up the book and read about what Tom felt and saw in the park on that same day - whether it was a journal entry in the 70's. 80's or 90's.
