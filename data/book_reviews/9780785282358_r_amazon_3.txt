This handy small book serves as a good, biblical quick reference to understanding the bible.  It gives brief yet insightful & practical summary-like spiritual lessons for each chapter of the bible w/ scripture reference.  And here & there, maybe every 5 or so chapters, there is a box of a relevant specific spiritual truth w/ useful bible references.
