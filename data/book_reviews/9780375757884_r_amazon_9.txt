A few months ago I started reading American literature, Hawthorne being on the top of my list. After reading through Scarlett Letter and Blithedale Romance this book was such a wonderful sequel and finale. Filled with short stories written by Hawthorne, first published in 1837 and another edition in 1842 that are just an amazing collection of short stories!Filled with a plethora of themes and characters it takes you through a journey back in time to America in the 19th century. If you want to read American literature, or just Hawthorne, this is a must have!
