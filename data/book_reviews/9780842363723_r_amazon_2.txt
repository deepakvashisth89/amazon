Every Christian should read this book! It is full of action, adventure, suspense, and truths from Godvs Word. God does amazing things through seemingly  insignificant people, places, and circumstances to further his Kingdom here on earth. Always has, and always will. Believe me, i should know.I read this book in the early 1990's, during my college years in California. It was during this time that I was learning  to pray, and engage in real Spiritual Warfare. I had no choice but to embark on this path, as it was a necessity. Well, it turned out to be a knock-down, drag-out battle that lasted six years, and wore me out. This book encouraged me all along the way.I  know God was faithful, as the person I prayed so long and hard for, chose to live and "open themself up to life" when at death's door years later. I pray for their salvation, even now, but they made the choice to live, and that was a great first step.Know this truth. When you pray the kind of warfare prayer I did, Satan will do all he can to entice you to sin, and then rub it in your face, every chance he gets. I learned that lesson the hard way., no one warned me.He will turn even your best friends against you. You may be homeless for a time. But, Jesus will take you through it. The rewards are worth it, and people may get saved, as a result.This book gave me hope, and something positive to focus on during the hard times. I learned  many valuable truths, found in God's word. Most importantly, that God is faithful, and uses everything in our lives for our good (those who trust in Jesus, alone).This book is a prize in illustrating how God uses people, and events to accomplish his purposes in ways that no one could ever imagine. Events around you, or even in your life may perplex you,  frighten you,  or overwhelm you; just stand on God's word, persevere in prayer, and Jesus will see you through.You never know whose life you will influence, or what events you have, or will play a part in. Keep the faith, because its not over, till its over.Thank you Mr. Peretti. Thank you Jesus!Read moreRead less
