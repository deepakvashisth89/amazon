"Empty is the argument of any philosopher who doesn't relieve any human suffering." Epicurus (341-271BC)Philosophy is different than psychology. Psychology looks at controlled scientific experiments to find out how the mind reacts to society. Philosophy is concerned with the conceptional questions like what reality? A psychologist would examine mental illness by asking the client questions. A philosopher would ask, "What is mental Illness?" Two different worlds.
