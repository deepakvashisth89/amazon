Brooding, suspenseful and atmospheric novel and the best of Victoria Holt, a highly prolific author who wrote over 200 novels during her lifetime. She also wrote under the name Jean Plaidy.  As usual, she paints her characters and the scenery, including the homes they live in, vividly, much as an artist paints pictures.  The heroine become more likeable as the story continues, and it is easy to see she is in danger.  The culprit is someone whom the family sees all the time; he weaves himself into their lives and is very crafty, evil and underhanded.  This is a wonderful Gothic romance novel with plenty of atmosphere which will keep you on the edge of your seat.  I personally could not put it down.
