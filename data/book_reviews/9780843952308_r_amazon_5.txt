This is my 3rd Christie book, originally pub'd in 1992 (his first?) and, as he tells us in an intro, updated in 2011. (Don't worry, it is still very timely.) All three emphasize almost constant combat action, this one, The Warriors of God, most of all. Clearly, Mr. Christie works diligently at keeping an extended high-action narrative, and he does it very successfully. He is adept at including the stories' "housekeeping" (exposition, character intro,  setting) right in the action. There IS one negative in The Warriors of God--too many typos and at least one continuity error. When he revised the plot, he should have corrected the text. BUT, the story is so strong that I forgave each typo--until the next one. Highly recommended.Read moreRead less
