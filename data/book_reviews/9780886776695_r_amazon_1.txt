I picked up this book, number two in a series, because it looked like a good bit of space opera.  Unfortunately, the novel did not live up to its promise.  The overall story arc is interesting:  free trader cast into position of leadership in rebellion of free people against a controlling government.  Unfortunately, the characterization of the heroine was so random as to be unbelievable.  The individual situations (e.g., space battles) were not well executed, and there was a tendency to use "science-fiction-ese" mumbo-jumbo instead of creating a reasonably well-thought-out universe.  The soft porn had its redemptive qualities at times (barely), but the created universe of the story appeared to be random in the extreme.  (Need a planet?  Let's throw one in.  Need a planet destroyed?  Let's call it a "nuclear" weapon, eh?)After reading the author's wikipedia.com biography, I suspect that she's a much better editor than an author.  She assuredly would have benefited from editorial suggestions in this book.  I do not intend to read any others in the series.Read moreRead less
