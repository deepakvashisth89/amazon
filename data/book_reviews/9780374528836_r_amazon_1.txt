This book has no right to be as good as it is. It's just about a fish, and one I'd never heard or at that. I got it after hearing the author mentioned in a podcast I listen to. Somehow it held my interest through the whole thing and provided a ton of knowledge that I didn't know I wanted, about a fish I didn't know existed, and kept me chuckling and smirking the whole time. The author isn't outright funny, but his wit and little quips that have been generously sprinkled throughout really make it a pleasure to read.You probably need at least a little bit of fishing experience to truly appreciate it, but overall I highly recommend this book. I bought two copies, kept one myself, and send the other to my father as a gift since it brought back a ton of nostalgic memories of fishing with him as a kid.This is true writing talent and a great read.
