Traditional, character driven mystery featuring the empathetic Inspector Thanet and sidekick Lineham. The murder of Virginia Mintar gives Thanet pause for thought as he uncovers a plethora of suspects and motives and learns of the outrageous behaviour of the victim. Credible characterisation, a steady plot and never as predictable as you may, at first, expect. Recommended for anyone who enjoys a cleverly and well written mystery.
