Thích Nhất Hạnh was both a gifted teacher and a gifted writer. It takes great skill to present sometimes difficult concepts so simply and so concisely. "Old Path White Clouds" is his magnum opus. Having written many books, mostly non-fiction and some fiction, this is his greatest work because he uses the life of the Buddha to present the teachings of the Buddha, mostly from a Mahayana perspective, in many enjoyable, short, easy to read and understandable chapters from his lifelong studying of the subject. Whether you know little about the teachings and the life of the Buddha or a long-time practitioner of Buddhism this is an essential book to read. "Old Path White Clouds" is both a pleasant journey and an adventure. That is why this is my "desert island book", the one book I'd have if I could only have one book.Unlike many similar books who's main goal is to convert people to the faith expounded upon within its pages, Thầy's (Vietnamese for "teacher") goal is not to convert anyone to Buddhism but to show how the teachings of the Buddha can be applied to everyone of any faith. The Buddha's lessons on compassion are copacetic with the teachings of Jesus' teachings on the same subject.Reading a chapter a day, everyday until complete, will change your life because it will change how you see and how you react to what your see.
