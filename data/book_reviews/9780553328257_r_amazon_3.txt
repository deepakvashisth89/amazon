I’ve NEVER read anything that long. Took me a few weeks. And … I thoroughly enjoyed EVERY story.Arthur Conan Doyle is an amazing author! Lots of insights into the late 1800’s - and … just as compelling in 2026. And Sherlock’s analytical intelligence is still wonderful and completely relevant!The stories are amazing. Sherlock and Watson are just as alive, and fascinating, today as well over a century ago.This is the unedited real version, so don’t be surprised when you read a few passages that are horribly discriminating.
