This is another non-review by someone with absolutely no compentence to review a volume like this.  I offer a “reaction comment” as honestly as I can muster.This is the most valuable book published in the West in the last 1,000 years or more.  It captures a millenia of Sufi wisdom in a package a westerner can digest.  It is filled with “scatter” technique, fragments of ideas that pop up later in the text that give some impact or impetus to previous pieces.  They haunt your mind and come back at the oddest times and contexts.I am amazed at the skill of the publishers.  I read this book in 1966 in first edition;  I certify that the current volume I read is NOT that same edition.  It is totally different 50 years later by some skill of miraculous typography.  The voice is quite unusual.  He is not preaching or recruiting or converting.Shah is laying out crisply organized facts about Sufism for educational purposes to those interested in the topic;  I am amazed at some of the negative reviews.  They must have gotten a different edition from mine.I cannot pick out a clever or meaningful passage to quote other than to note the last three chapters are rocket propelled launches to new worlds of mind.I have read the Quoran but sadly only one surah really resonated with me.  The beauty of the Light ayats or the Night Journey were beautiful, entertaining but I don't know what they mean other than they  pique my imagination about things I know nothing about.The surah that resonates with my soul is the surat al munifiqun.  Surah of the hypocrites.  It has my name all over it.  That is not a good thing, accurate yes, comforting no.  One should expect this type of reaction, if this holy book reflects the souls of its readers, then that chapter is a necessity.Here and there in the Sufis there are passages that speak directly to the soul.  It is as if the natural internal monologue ceases and the words speak directly to another part of the mind.  The nafs al amr puts up a fierce defense against wisdom;  some manages to sneak through on occasion,I thank Sayyid Idries Shah for this volume.  It is an enormous gift to mankind.SalamGeorgeRead moreRead less
