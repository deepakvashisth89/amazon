This collection of essays from food writers of Gourmet magazine brings the reader to different places and times, from the summer of 1939 when M.F.K. Fisher stumbled upon a cozy Swiss inn, to 1970 when Richard Cassin writes about a dinner intivation in Taos, New Mexico.My favorite essays were "The Garlic War" by E. Annie Proulx (author of The Shipping News), where the author recounts her Uncle Herbert's eventual acceptance of garlic, and "An Indian Reminiscence" where Madhur Jaffrey fondly writes about her experiences with food as a child growing up in North India.To keep the historical accuracy of the essays, recipes that are included in the book have been kept as they were first printed.  This provides us with a peek into what people ate and how they liked their food through the years.  This book will definitely be a great addition to a food-lovers' library.Read moreRead less
