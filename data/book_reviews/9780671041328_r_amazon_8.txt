This is a strangely twisted plot. A sexually repressed young woman, married to a controlling, inattentive husband, takes a position as telephone sex operator to gather research for a book. Right!! Nothing is what it seems in this little ditty and I really like that kind of plot. It is enough to say that the young woman finds herself in too deep when the cops come calling and her life begins to unravel. Well written and well plotted with a lot of surprises along the way.
