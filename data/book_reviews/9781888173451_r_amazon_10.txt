I love Alice Thomas Ellis,and this is my favourite of her books. Wonderful mix of otherworldly strangeness and matter of fact, accurate observation of human interaction.  The only element that strikes the odd jarring note - until you get used to it - is the unequivocal judgement she passes on her characters from time to time; you have to remember that she was a committed Catholic, very opposed to the liberal wing of the church.  She is an odd mixture of liberal and reactionary, which partly makes for the interesting tension in her books.
