This is an interesting book that revolves around one of the oldest ideas in Eastern and some schools of Western philosophy: the "Law of Attraction," that proposes a correspondence between our attitudes and beliefs and the circumstances of our life. The book is full of interesting experiences drawn from her own and other people's lives.Lynn Grabhorn clearly believes that the key to change lies in our feelings, rather than our thoughts. She gives some techniques for helping overcome negative expectations that can prevent change. I'm sure that she is right that emotions and our reactions to them are important, but I'd hate to throw out the baby with the bathwater: thoughts, desires, beliefs and attitudes all come into play when we are working on ourselves and trying to change our circumstances.The weak point comes when she takes on a New Age tour of "Everything in this world is made of energy," (It isn't, by the way), and then mixes up energy and vibration. Some of the writing is a little difficult to follow at times. It looks as if the book was probably written over a period of time, and different sections appear in different voices.Despite these problems, there are some useful techniques which will be of interest to the explorer. But how far can feelings alone result in manifesting new life circumstances? About that I am a little doubtful: it is more likely to need the coordination of each aspect of your being.Read moreRead less
