One of my favorite books of all times.  It is the poetic and thought-provoking story of an American engineer sent to China to look for a site for a dam which would wipe out several cities, the traditional jobs of the boat people, and completely change the culture of the area, but also bring electricity and make the river safer for river travel. The naive ideas of the young Western engineer are pitted against the traditional river culture of the people who take him up the river, the people whose lives will be changed forever if the dam is built.  Hersey raises questions about who has the right to determine the fate of a people, whether new technologies are inherently better than traditional methods, and whether it is possible to see past our prejudices.The characters are unforgettable, the descriptions of the river beautiful and frightening, and the engineer's dilemma haunting.This book is fiction, but sixty years after the book was written, a dam was built on this river submerging the villages along miles of the river including the village of Single Pebble.John Hersey is also the author of Hiroshima.Read moreRead less
