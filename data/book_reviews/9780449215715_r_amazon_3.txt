I’ve been a Mary Stewart fan for over 50 years. This is one I had missed. Though I enjoyed the book (and the narration) very much, I found the plot a bit far fetched and the main character’s actions, despite plot twists, a bit of a stretch which diminished my enjoyment somewhat.
