I enjoyed this book more the first time I read it, which was about 50 years ago.  I still recalled parts of the story line, and I’m trying to read through Heinlein’s books again, so this was on the list.  Some of the characters were downright repellent, but I’m sure that was intended, just unpleasant.  Some of the concepts in the future civilization were also unpleasant, but I won’t reveal all of them.  I’d rather spend my reading time on more pleasant material.  The parts about roughing it and making do with materials at hand after leaving modern times were most interesting.  Contract bridge and cats were pleasant parts of the story.  Racial, sexual, and class discrimination issues were more obnoxious to me as I read the book this time than before.  Not Heinlein’s best.Read moreRead less
