I first read this book in the late 90s.  Since then, it has been my favorite. I have read it at least half a dozen times and have gone through 4 copies.  I keep lending it out, and people keep running off with my copy because they don't want to give it back.  The characters are great, the story is over the top, and parts of the book are so funny, I have to stop reading to catch my breath from laughing.  Just read the chapter titles if you don't believe me.Anyone that grew up in the 90s should read this book.Read moreRead less
