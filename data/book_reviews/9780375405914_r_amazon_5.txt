Having viewed the movie several times, I became enchanted by the setting; the chateau, the vines, and the town of Provence. Watching the credits roll by several times I discovered the movie was not an original idea, but based upon the book by Peter Mayle.Off to Amazon to find the book. For a reasonable price, I downloaded for the Kindle software and began to enjoy the book as much as the movie.The book has several interesting twists not present in the movie version. However, both are excellent. I will not reveal the twists, as that would spoil the read.I suggest viewing the movie first. The characters in the movie are played by first rate actors and remembering their faces and mannerisms while reading the book make it all the more enjoyable and memorable.
