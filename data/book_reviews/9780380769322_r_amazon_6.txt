This was a very enjoyable book, from start to finish.  I adore this cozy series.  I love the friendship between Jane and Shelley.  This series is revolved around their friendship.  They both are wonderful mothers.  There is lots of food and fun in this series.  The atmosphere is fun and cozy.In this particular book, Jane and Shelley attend a night class to write an autobiography.  The class was short, and only a few students took the class.  The students were to read each other's autobiography.  Will secrets be revealed in those autobiographies?This is a murder mystery, so someone is murdered.  The clues are clever and plentiful and good.  Jane is determined to find the killer.I highly recommend this book, as well as all the others of the series that I have read so far.Read moreRead less
