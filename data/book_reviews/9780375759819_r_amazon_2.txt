I bought this book for my mother some years ago and bought it again, but for myself recently. It's well worthwhile to you tube the man the book is about "Luca Turin" and see his TED talk, the book is fascinating and also gives the backstory of Luca's head palming frustrations with the scent industry and scientific establishment. If you resent people with talent then perhaps leave this book for others because Luca is one of those rare people with startling talent in more than one field. As a sniffer extraordinare, and also as a highly competent scientist capable of making those intuitive leaps that other scientists often resent. Indeed, in the later parts of the books the author shows us Luca's encounters with the Scent industry's establishment. This book is worth reading even for the science story alone, to see industry and establishment's antagonism to innovation.
