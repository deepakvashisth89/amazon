this is the ninth book in the amazing series written by L. Frank Baum. Baum is a master at creating mystery and adventure and he has consistently done that in most of the books in this series. All of them are so interesting. Not just for kids but also for some adults who want to read different kind of fiction. Baum has been known to like this book the most as his personal favourite for the amount of time it took him to write this one and the new stages, plots and characters he introduced. Believe me guys, this one is different.The story is simple. This is about Captain Bill and Trot's journey to Oz and with the help of the Scarecrow, the former ruler of Oz, overthrow the villainous King Krewl of Jinxland. Captain Bill and Trot had previously appeared in two other novels by Baum, The Sea Fairies and Sky Island.
