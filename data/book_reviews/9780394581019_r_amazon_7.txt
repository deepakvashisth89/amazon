I've been a fan of Moore since picking up Birds of America back when it came out. While her voice doesn't speak to every reader, those it does speak to can't seem to get enough. Her play with structure and language remind me of Miles Davis's advice to young jazz artists - "learn your scales and practice the hell out of them,then forget all that s*** and have fun with it." Something like that, anyway. Like Life is a younger voice of Moore's, one that might seem a bit esoteric and strange at times, but again just reading her makes me remember what made me fall in love with the short story to begin with. If you only have enough cash for one book today or you just want to see Moore at her best, get Birds of America. Then maybe Anagrams. Then either this one or Who Will Run the Frog Hospital depending if you're more a short story or novel fan.
