This book gets five stars because of the brilliance of Lewis's scholarship. The reader must beware that this book deals with the history of courtly love in literature and interacts with several authors that most modern readers have never heard of and have very little interest in. You will probably want to pass on this book unless you are interested in the development of allegory and the theme of courtly love.
