This book was enjoyable and written with the same flair and humour from occasionally bizarre workaday characters that you should expect from Sue Townsend. However, it suffers from the same weaknesses as any book of hers where the ending is always clever but a tad unsatisfying, particularly with the kindle edition. It tells you that you still have a sizeable chunk of the book left but this turns out to be a sample of 'The Woman Who Went to Bed for a Year'.Read it for the excellent satire we've come to expect from Townsend and the insight into the human condition she so expertly lampoons.Read moreRead less
