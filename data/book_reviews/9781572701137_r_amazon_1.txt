I enjoy watching You-Tube videos about book recommendations. And this title kept coming up. One You-Tuber, for some reason, owned several versions of this book, in several languages! Why?I have aways loved books and films that are based around a beautiful home, a gothic mansion. Stories that are atmospheric. That put you in beautiful scenes. This book was that.As many reviewers have already said, the interesting thing about this story is that it focuses on a woman who isn't even alive anymore. I have to be careful not to give too much away, but what I loved about this story is that it is not what you are certainly going to think during the major first part of the book. You are going to have one idea, one sense, but you are going to be shocked, so surprised that things were not what you thought, what you imagined.It is really a Love Story. I think that I identified with it, because we all have our insecurities at certain phases in our lives. We all compare ourselves to others. We sometimes suffer from low self-esteem. We often get an impression of others that turns out to be a mistaken assumption.The story really gets good towards the latter half of the book. It is when you will not want to put it down. When the pages will turn and turn, until you get to the end.I found myself rooting for the two main protagonists and loved their relationships with close friends and relatives. I enjoyed the way things unfolded, even though there are many nail-biting moments. I find it fascinating how human personalities are so much like ocean currents that create a tempest of waves flowing in so many different and unexpected directions. Being human, yourself, you find yourself identifying with these currents and also knowing that life seems to have its own personality, you never know where things will go. That is what made this story so good.I am glad that I read this book and will remember it for years to come.Read moreRead less
