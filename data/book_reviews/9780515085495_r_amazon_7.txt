This is the first of eight or nine volumes in what is probably Griffin's best series. It is the story of young officers in the US Army, starting in 1945, and continuing through the late 1940's. They have very different backgrounds, but they receive their commissions in unusual ways and then go on to start their improbable but not implausible careers. The author is clearly familiar with the administrative minutiae of US Army, indeed I wonder if he was not a company clerk during his military service and this lends authenticity to the story. However, some of the stories are a bit over the top, especially the escape of one of the protagonists via Odessa given the distances and the positions of the front lines in early 1945. All the same, these books are enjoyable, especially for veterans, so much so that I read some of the books more than once since the 1980's.Read moreRead less
