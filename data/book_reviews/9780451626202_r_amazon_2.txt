In 1831 Charles Darwin embarked on a trip that changed forever the way we view our world and its living species.  Its importance would lead us to read Darwin's account even if it were deadly dull--which it is only occasionally, in long paragraphs about minute differences in species. Overall, the gripping story with apt descriptions of people and places is sure to draw in any reader with even a passing interest in natural history.  Darwin is astute, honest and blessed with abiding curiosity about the world and all its inhabitants.  Skip over some tedious detail (I did) but keep reading.
