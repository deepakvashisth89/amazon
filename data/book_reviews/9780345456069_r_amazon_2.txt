I gave this 5 stars because it is so much not my kind of book and I really enjoyed it. The author has a wonderful gift to immediately capture the reader and made me feel as if I knew all these characters, even though they come from a cultural background a continent away from mine. Written in such a light hearted manner and with such a delicate touch, that it wasn't until I finished the book that some of the  serious underlying themes began to surface in my mind and kept provoking me for days. Would highly recommend as a "light" read which is absolutely not just froth and left me feeling well satisfied.Read moreRead less
