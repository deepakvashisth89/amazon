I had high hopes for this book but was very disappointed. While it was in fact well written, I found the characters, especially the main character Jemima, very unlikeable. She seemed cold, entirely self absorbed, selfish...someone I don't want to follow through an entire series. The mystery became almost secondary to how she viewed religion, politics, her affair with a married MP. I found myself skipping entire passages just to find the mystery and not listen to Jemima. I definitely will not be reading any of the remaining books in this series.Read moreRead less
