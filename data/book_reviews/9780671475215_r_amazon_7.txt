I believe this is #7 in a series and found it to hold up to the standard established in the earlier books.  There is a lot of banter and musings among the characters which, at times, makes the plot almost secondary.  As an example, in the first 4 chapters there is a murder in the red light district.  That's all we learn.  The rest is who will do what in the investigation, did the pimp deserve it, who gets the coffee, does the adjutant have a nice body and so on.I will defer to an authentic resident of Amsterdam but it seems:  Dutch police get the job done despite the rules they have to live by.  They eat more on the job than USA cops.  They seem to be racially tolerant but their language is not constrained by the PC crowd.I do recommend starting with the first book, Outsider in Amsterdam, in the series as there is character development and in The Streetbird one of the cops has a life-changing event.
