I've read all of the Friendship Ring Series. And believe me, Rachel Vail is an awesome author. But the thing that is really bugging me about this series,is that every book has practically the same plot, being told by different characters. This form of writing is very interesting and  intriging at first, but after you have read about 3 to 4 books, the story  gets old. Now, book 5 was a bit of a change, and I applaud Ms. Vail for  that. Book 5 had the end of the first 4  books' plot and another story in  it. Kind of interesting. Not really. What I am mainly saying is that  basically, will every for books be like this? A repeated plot told by four  different characters? I sure hope not. Because if what I am fortelling is  true, her books will certainly not be bought by me. But who knows? She  could comepletely do a 360 and make her stories interesting. I hope so, her  writing is so good, to good, to be thrown away by repeating plots. Too  good.
