This book wasn't the most original of ideas, but it did do a good job of exploring the way that psychic powers work and can be used.  I thought, the characters were unique, enjoyable, and well rounded.  I particularly  enjoyed Macy.  She is someone that I would like to know.  The relationship  between Allie and Chuck was creative and entertaining.  There were some  areas that I thought were glossed over a little too quickly (like Allie's  mom), and the book needed an epiloque, at least, but it is definitely worth  a read.
