Pam Crooks writes with such determination, excitement, grit, humor, and romance.  The beauty of Wyoming, cattle barons claiming their land and defending it at all cost, cowboys who completely capture your heart, and a daughter who wants nothing more than to be accepted by her father, an orphan boy given a better life, the “boss” - Lance.  He is has loved Sonnie for years from a distance.  Their love story is tedious and tender.  There are so many facets to this story.  It is a great book and I highly recommend everyone to read it.
