Henry Tibbett of Scotland Yard is invited by a group of elite writers to give them a talk on police procedures. Meanwhile Tibbett begins to get parts of a crossword puzzle in the mail, a few clues at a time. Guessing that the writers are playing a joke on him, to test his detection powers, Tibbett asks a friend who’s expert at crosswords to crack the clues and fill in the puzzle, and off he goes to address the group.The puzzle suggests several possible murders in the murky past. Was one of them an actual murder? And are the accidents that happen during Tibbett’s involvement in events truly accidental?The plot is intricate and clever, in the style of the Golden Age, and with its classic themes and setting.
