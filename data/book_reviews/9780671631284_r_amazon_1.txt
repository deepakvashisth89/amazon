This guide for girls in elementary and Jr. HIgh  School is as timely  and and applicable as it was in was in 1986.Tina Yothers, who played "Jennifer" on Family Ties was only thirteen and in the sixth grade when she wrote this book.Young girls will find It full of useful tips and stories about growing up, written by a peer and popular T.V. star with a lot of responsibility and a high self esteem. Tina comes across as the 'girl next door' as she was in real life, without glamour or pretense.Tina answered thousands of letters and phone calls from other kids across the country, and addresses the most common issues troubling them with advice she followed herself. Tina lived outside of Los Angeles, in a house with her mother, father, brothers and sisters, rode her bicycle, went to sleepovers and was an everyday kid when she wasn't at her demanding job as a well known actress.An easy read, young girls can have Tina as a friend, once again, even after all these years.
