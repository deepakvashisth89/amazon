This sketchy1946 title involves a lot of running around New York City and a lot of talking. The characters  - except for the charming old lady who was murdered - had no personalities. The author never even bothered to explain (to the reader or to the other characters) who the protagonist, Gamadge, actually was, or why he was so chummy with the police. I kept plowing through the chatter hoping for a plot twist, but when it finally came I wasn't very surprised by it. The best passages in the book were the descriptions of old NYC streets and buildings; on the strength of those descriptions, I gave The Wrong Way Down two stars instead of one.
