The first in a series of 32 Inspector Alleyn Murder Mysteries was written by one of the queens of British mysteries, Ngaio Marsh who is celebrated along with Agatha Christie, Dorothy L. Sayers and others.  It is comfort food for enthusiasts of the genre.  It has the necessary elements: a fabulously wealthy politician, his sumptuous estate, an international group of invitees with tangled but obscure relationships with the host and each other, sexual tensions, political intrigue and a version of The Murder Game to be played out during the weekend in the country.
