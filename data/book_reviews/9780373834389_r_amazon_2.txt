Anything written by this author is a real treat, especially if it involves cowboys. As far as I'm concerned, she one of the top three writers of cowboy series.From the very first moment you enter Jessica and Nat's world, you'll be sucked in so deep you can't put the book down until the very last page. But even then it leaves you wanting more. This story will make you laugh, cry, and fall in love with these rough tough cowboys and the women.  This story also has a sit on the edge of your seat in suspense, but by the end of it....you'll be laughing and jumping for joy. This whole story was absolutely perfect. Every bit of the dialogue in this story felt so real, and spot on. The camaraderie between the four cowboy friends was so heart warming. And little Elizabeth was a real charmer, so many times she made me laugh. Spoiled much?
