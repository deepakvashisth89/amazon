This book is incredible.  I keep it as a go to reference staple in my library.  Like another reviewer said, way ahead of its time.  Dr. Northrup writes about many aspects of women's health from a holistic and not completely empirical (but still data based) view.  It does it's best to connect the dots between your emotional health and bodily health and actually manages to explain it without sounding like a shaman. It proves it through the lens of western medicine.  It doesn't bother repeating what other books already tell you.  Everytime I read something from it, I'm sure that info. wouldn't be found in a different book.  The most valuable thing about this book is it leaves you with more curiosity to learn, therefore I'd consider it an introduction, despite its size at over 800 pages.Read moreRead less
