Thomas H. Cook is indeed a brilliant author. By today's standards he might be considered "wordy," or too detailed. He is a literate read.Instruments of Night is dark. It's painful, but not without hope. And this hope we need badly. Especially in today's world, or the mess we've made of it.Intricate. Painful. Mysterious.  Moody. Perhaps unrelenting. At times, it perhaps drags a bit. It is well worth the ride.The edition shown above is not the edition I read through Kindle. I would caution against the Kindle edition.
