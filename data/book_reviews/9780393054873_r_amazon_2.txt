Robert Tucker's book, "Stalin as Revolutionary," is a must read if you want to answer: "How did the Communists come to power in Russia?" and "How did Staling gain control of the Bolsheviks?" This book should be required reading for students of Russian history. But you have to be motivated to plow through the Russian names as well as know foreign words (Russian, French?) that the author sprinkles throughout the text without translating. Tucker's book gets down into the weeds. But he provides an insightful of Stalin's psyche. So be prepared.
