I think I read his best works first: Last Exit and Requiem. This collection of stories doesn't hold a candle to the former. I find his writing in these dull and repetitive. There seems to be a Harry in every story (as well as the lead in Requiem being named Harry, and another character in Last Exit, too). Three of the tales are decent enough, some of them (The Sound and I'm Being Good) are absolutely awful.
