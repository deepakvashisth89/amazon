I really liked the book - about the first two, three encounters. Then Mr Ferguson, who was a beggar on this trip, let's face it, committed one cardinal sin - he mocked even those people who were not unpleasant. When somebody goes out of his or her way to make sure you reach a safe destination you do not put that person up for public ridicule. That is just nasty, Mr Ferguson! Either shame on you, or on your parent who forgot to teach you good manners.Still, I could not help but laugh about some of his stories, particularly those where he mocked himself. Or where he described his dealings with some of the aforementioned unpleasant people. His put down of the (drunk?) sailor in the café of the first port was a thing of beauty.He was also very good at describing more sentimental moments. I have no trouble with his writing, not at all. That is what got him to 3 stars. If he had been a little less judgemental about the quirks people in a completely different society show, he would have gotten 5. He is a good writer, just not a good guest. Would I invite Mr Ferguson to my place? Seriously? Of course not. Not after I read how harsh his judgements are. Sorry Mr Ferguson. Oh, and just do me a favour, do not visit Germany and try the same here ... you would not get very far, mock ceaselessly about our haRRsh,  jeRRman eggscent and complain that after only a few years you are not seen as one of us ...
