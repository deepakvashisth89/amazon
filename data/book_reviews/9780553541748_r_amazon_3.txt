To say that this is a horror novel would be inaccurate because it is in many ways a mystery novel.  More precisely put, this novel is a combination of both mystery and horror, mixed with a little mysticism, romance, and some social commentary.Nevetheless, just because the novel doesn't fit exactly into any of the established genre doesn't make it bad.  In fact, this is a an excellent story that will keep you away from doing anything else but finishing this story.  It certainly did for me.The novel does go into certain length to introduce the characters and set up the background, which tend to leave the reader hanging.
