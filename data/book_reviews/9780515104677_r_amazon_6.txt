This is an excellent follow up to the original story (Rolling Thunder) and in fact a better story than the first.  It is certainly 5 stars for the research and insight into the Viet Nam war, BUT the book is riddled with simple typos and misspelt words and for the life of me I can't understand how the author has allowed this to go out with the errors in place?Also, heaps of repitition (every time Court lights a cigarette he tells us the zippo lighter has a rubber band around it, yet never says why??)  And many others like that.  What was 'George' the animal that cursed in the briefings??  It never gets clearly explained!Very good characters in this novel and while it is certainly better to read the first novel first, the author does try and briefly explain anything that reflects back to the first book, so it is virtually stand alone.It certainly brings out the futility of this war (or conflict) and I never appreciated the way the US Forces were so hogtied by their leaders and LBJ.  This book is a 'must read' for any war action buffs.  The details are superb and the arial dogfights so brilliantly portrayed.Just please clean up the typos!Read moreRead less
