Although the book gets off to a bit of a slow start, and there is just a TAD too much description, this is a fantastic read. Once the book crosses a certain point, it becomes a page-turner. You realize that some of that earlier over-description really helps provide a striking backdrop to the later action. By the book's crescendo, Harold Bell Wright's writing is SHEER POETRY. His understanding of atmosphere and human nature, and how the two combine, is stunning. He spins plot and story beautifully, and you're sorry to see it end.If you're reading this because you've seen the movie, don't look for much similarity. The differences are so huge, you can pretty much say Hollywood borrowed the title, the setting, and a few character names (as another reviewer wrote), and told their own story. I love the movie, but the book is very different and clearly better.Read moreRead less
