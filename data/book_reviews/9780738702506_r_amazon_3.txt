I give Von Braschler two stars for the obvious earnestness and good intentions of this book - but personally I just found it insufferably boring and kind of pointless.The author, by various methods, succeeds in inducing a long series of what may be interpreted as Out-of-Body experiences or maybe lucid dreams, in which he soon encounters a variety of spirit guides who give him teachings about himself and the world. Von Brascler portrays himself as so obtuse and inept in these encounters and worlds that it must be an honest account, but it just seemed to me that little of interest or relevance (at least to other people - it seems to have been very transformative for the author) ever takes place. The book becomes essentially one long dream diary, and the dreams aren't all that interesting, to boot.The most distressing thing for me was that like many sensationalists, Von Braschler isn't content with any kind of inner wisdom or exploration or insights - the mark of any true inner 'experience' must of course be some kind of manifestation in the external world! Right? So suddenly he starts seeing impossibly large and nonexistent species of butterfly visiting his cottage in the woods (he runs to get his camera to prove it to the world, but comes back just a moment too late! - the butterfly has disappeared). He gets deeply involved in Kirlian photography and claims to have taken numerous 'real deal' pictures of his aura and the spiritual residue of the dream mentor guide that is hovering around him during waking life... and so on.Not only do I find all of this laughably implausible, it just seems to me that this kind of unsubstatiated reporting about the miraculous effects in this world of inner exploration do a great disservice to those of us who are honestly trying to find out what's going on inside the mind. It seems a sign of spiritual, if not just general, immaturity that someone seeking real inner wisdom or understanding thinks that the appearance of a butterfly is some kind of seal of spiritual understanding or progress, or that 'ghosts' hovering around you and impressing your friends means you've tapped some real source of understanding. These are exactly the kind of things real mystics warn about and caution us to ignore utterly and leave behind quickly.To sum up, dull and unbelievable, even if totally honest, reportage.
