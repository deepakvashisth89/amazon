First of all, let me preface this review by saying that I do not usually write reviews unless a book has really affected me in a positive way.  This book definitely falls into that category.My husband and I (both mildly myopic) purchased this book with the hopes of obtaining some exercises we could perform to strengthen our eyes to the point where we could ditch our glasses and contacts.  This book does contain some great exercises (my husband did indeed improve his eyes and no longer wears glasses).  The book also, however, contains so much more.I was fascinated to read Liberman's explanations of various causes for why one's eyes would deteriorate in the first place.  I definitely see how many of the causes he outlines apply to me. I notice how, for instance, that when I think hard or focus strongly upon something, I end up with headaches and my vision becomes worse.  I love that Liberman also includes techniques for alleviating these issues and thus improving how the eyes see and process information.By using the techniques in this book, I have significantly cut down on how often I need my contacts (only about once per week now).  I am still working to eliminate them for good, but I feel confident that I will be able to.This book is a very thorough, well-done treatise on how the eyes work, the intricate connection between the eyes and the mind, and how to improve one's vision naturally.  Liberman has a definite talent for presenting technical information in an engaging, easy-to-understand manner.  This book offers a great alternative for those seeking to correct their vision through natural means.Read moreRead less
