Reads very much like Agatha Christie.  This mystery is a British novel written in 1947. Consequently, for an American reader, the use of archaic language using language common to Britain, humorously, I might add, contributes, although unintentionally, I'm sure, to the mystery. And it is a good mystery in its own way.  However the way in which the mystery is resolved is completely unrealistic in this age, a deus ex machina. Even though the solution is somewhat logical, it rather spoiled the process for me. Various characters were fully explored with various motives, but for me the ending fell rather flat.
