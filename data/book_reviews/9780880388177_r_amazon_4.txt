This is a 2nd Edition Dungeons and Dragons book, but it is so helpful to the Dungeon Master that I recommend it for any role playing game you play. We learn about style of play, a realistic look at preparation and especially useful player management. Thinking about starting your own RPG group? Then this book is for you. It also includes a variety of ways to approach planning out your campaign.
