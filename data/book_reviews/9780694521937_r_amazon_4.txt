I never knew too much about the Lewinsky scandal. When it was going on I was like five and of course wasn't aware of the political things going on. It wasn't until I was 18 that I heard about it. Some guys at work were like, Bill Clinton got a BJ in the White House. I didn't believe it, I didn't know presidents did stuff like that but I looked it up and it was true. So about the book, the author does a good job at telling the story in a sympathetic and appropriate way. Sure, having sex with a married person is wrong but I didn't get that, I felt for Monica and liked her. It was terrible the way Star treated her. I love this book and let a friend borrow it.
