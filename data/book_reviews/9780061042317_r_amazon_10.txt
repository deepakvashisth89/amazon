I enjoyed this story but was a little miffed that the hero couldn’t just be a carpenter. No he had to actually be an architect who left the big city after a bitter divorce, though it seems the bitterness was more on his ex-wife’s side than his. He of course is perfect. A little too good to be realistic. I liked the story better when difficulties arose in their budding relationship. My father was a carpenter and a very intelligent hardworking man so I see no need to have the hero be perfection. Her big city friends and relatives are a bit condescending about him as a sort of earthy rather grubby son of the soil. I found that part of the story a bit hard to take.
