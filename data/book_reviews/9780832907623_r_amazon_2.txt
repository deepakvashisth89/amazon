I read this book first as a boy. I borrowed the book from the local library (Oh, those were the days!) and loved it then and still love it today. Gene Hill was a great outdoor writer and his articles in Field and Stream were the first I would read each month. Gene could put to words the deep feelings that being involved in the outdoors makes us all to feel but not know how to express. I have often said after reading one of the short stories, "amen or exactly". He seems to know my friends and even me and I can see myself in his stories. A great writer and a great book.
