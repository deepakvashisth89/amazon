This is the third book in a high fantasy series and picks up soon after the end of book two. I liked it very much, though not as much as books one and two. It feels like a middle book, where greater scenes lie before and ahead. And, indeed, the closing chapter introduces a powerful turn in the multi-book tale.I continue to love Tristen, my favorite new (to me) character in quite a while.Four out of five owl stars.About my reviews: I try to review every book I read, including those that I don't end up enjoying. The reviews are not scholarly, but just indicate my reaction as a reader, reading being my addiction.
