I love Jill Shalvis' work.  I've read a number of her books.  I recently went looking for a book, pulled up her page and sorted by publication date.  The publication date on the list is July, 2011.  While I was reading the book I kept thinking - this must be a mistake.  This isn't like any Jill Shalvis book I've ever read.  It's slow, wordy, with typos, way too many typos.  It is painful to read.  The story is dragged along with so many unnecessary thoughts and details.  And yet, I read it right through to the end thinking something would change.  She's such a good author!  Right after the last page it has the date and copy write 1998.  Ah, I get it - it's a very old work, now it makes more sense.  And there I was having pushed myself to read this book, wanting a quick, light, easy read with some steam, romance, and great Jill language...there I was so let down.  It must be a reprint of an older work.  The date is deceiving and this isn't a new piece.  I feel ripped off for my time I spent reading and the money I spent on the book.  It's a shame because I'm not sure I'll buy another book by this author, even with loving her work.  Now I can't know for sure if it's a new work or simply an old work reproduced with a new face.
