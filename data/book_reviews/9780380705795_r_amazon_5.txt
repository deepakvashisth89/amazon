I'm not usually a fan of short stories, but I had to read these because I love Ms. Allingham's Campion books so much.  These are good stories.  The only fault that I had with them is that we didn't get to see the wonderful Lugge in any of them.  But we do see Campion and his friend Superintendent Oates of Scotland Yard.  We also get some pretty good glimpses into the enigmatic Campion's life around the books.  We see a lot of robbery and confidence crimes in these little stories, but they are each great in their own right.  Ms. Allingham's skill of great characterization and plotting transfer themselves quite well to the short story venue.
