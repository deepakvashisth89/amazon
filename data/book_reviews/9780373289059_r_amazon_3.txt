I will make it brief: Diamond is 18 and she winds up with a man who is much older (never explicitly stated how much) who does not listen to her when she says 'no' and instead forces himself onto a woman in the deep throes of grief. This is played as if she actually likes it most of the time but his ignoring her 'no' extends to every aspect of her life and not the general romance trend of "I'm going to kiss you anyway because you actually do want it".It's cliche but if you like these cliches (Tough Girl who isn't tough, man who only wants whats best, some elements of forbidden romance) you'll like it! I love cliches too, but Diamond just didn't do it for me. I liked Ruby more.I do like the sisters and look forward to reading about Pearl. She was my favorite part in this book.
