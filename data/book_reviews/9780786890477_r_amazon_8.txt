Danny Bonaduce will never win any prizes for grammar, because he writes the way he talks. But he does win the prize for biggest turnaround, and his book is both entertaining and serves as a warning for those who would follow in his footsteps. He gets a little smug at times, not with his accomplishments, but with his own witticisms, but this does not detract from the value of this book - he is simply being himself.I bought the book because I listen to his radio show - (...) - and I'm pleased to say the book was well worth it.
