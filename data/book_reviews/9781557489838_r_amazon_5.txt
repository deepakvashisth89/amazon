Mysterious Monday is a clean story with light Christian references which would probably be enjoyed by tweens. The story line isn't slow but the flow is choppy, jumps around and at times is hard to follow. Unfortunately, the author consistently misuses the word 'was' in place of the word 'were'; I found the grammar mistakes to be distracting. The story would be much better if the author would edit it and re-publish. A side note is that Carolyn Keene, the author of the Nancy Drew series wasn't 'an old woman' writer. 'Carolyn Keene' was the pseudonym for multiple authors, both men and women. Over-all Mysterious Monday is a nice, clean, family-style story.Read moreRead less
