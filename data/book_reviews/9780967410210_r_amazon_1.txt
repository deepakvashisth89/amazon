I ADORE this strip. The characters are so modern-life both in positive ways and in their shortcomings.  You can't help but love 'em all :  the fiesty traveling grandma.  the bug-collecting tom-boy little sister.  Val's fractured feminist fairy tales.  I love that Wally ( the sweet, kind, bald, cookin' insurance salesman) caught the girl.  How many strips have portrayed the misguided messages women sometimes tell themselves with parenting toddlers or falling for the wrong guy? Or broach the subject of the neglected teen? (Yea Joan and Wally!) All this without resorting to the same gags, dark humor, or making the characters one-dimensional. ( Even Holly has great lines sometimes. ) I read these comics on-line, but have no qualms getting the books too.
