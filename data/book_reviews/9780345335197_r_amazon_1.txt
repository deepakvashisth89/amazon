Just finished Joe Klein's seminal biography of Woody Guthrie. It is an excellent biography that goes into every facet of Woody's life. From the young dreamer reading every book in the local library to the not-so-likable person that keeps skipping out on his family. I really appreciated learning about Guthrie's service in the merchant marine in World War II (his first ship got torpedoed!) and even getting called into the Army. His communist leaning may have had something to do with getting call up by the Army when significantly past the prime age and having already served on a few merchant marine cruises.Reading his sexually explicit letters to Marjorie was also eye opening. Great songwriter, great literary stylist. Just like the early bluegrass guys, Woody was more beat than the Beats. Excellent biography from a time (1980) when America was more cerebral, lived a life of less distractions, and was really able to dig into things. Highly recommended.Read moreRead less
