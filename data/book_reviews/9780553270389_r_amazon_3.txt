The previous reviewers have, quite rightfully, pointed out the flaws in the noted aviatrix's telling of her early years.  I agree with the comments made and while not giving here posthumous license to lie, I do see this as part of her character.  Her accomplishments as a pilot are well recorded and verified.  I read the book primarily to get an impression of her feelings about those she had to deal with in the WASP program (Arnold, Love, Hobby) and the program itself.
