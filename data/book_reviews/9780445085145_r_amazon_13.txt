Wow! What a wonderful book. Beautifully written in great detail. I only wish that the author had devoted more words to Edward IV and Richard III, as strangely, over half the book's content is taken up with Richard II. The lives of Henry IV, Henry V, Henry VI, Edward IV and my own hero Richard III are condensed into the second half, with only 70 odd pages being devoted to Richard III and his tragic life. The author is clearly a Ricardian like myself, who firmly believes in his complete innocence in the matter of the Princes in the Tower and exposes the Tudor myths for the lies they are. Other Ricardians will love this book and even those who still stubbornly cling to the Shakesperian characature, will appreciate the author's beautiful prose and story telling style in describing this fascinating dynasty. This edition is also illustrated with beautifully drawn battle maps. I am so glad I have this book in my library.
