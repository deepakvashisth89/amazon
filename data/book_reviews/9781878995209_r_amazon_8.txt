James Kavanaugh has outdone himself with the poetry he has composed in his book “Quiet Water.” The poems that fill this book are eye-opening and reminders of the important things in life. While reading Kavanaugh’s book I felt deeply tuned into the significance of not letting the small things go unnoticed. We each have challenges day to day; in life we tend to forget about what is truly important. James Kavanaugh writes in a way that reminds me of the good things that fill my life. The poems in “Quiet Water” are refreshing and easy to relate your own life to.It is nice to read something someone else has written and be able to relate so well to it. Kavanaugh definitely uses his words to benefit his readers. The topics he writes about in this book are deep and really show that we are not alone in the things we face. James Kavanaugh writes a lot about the different things that occur in life such as body image, love, and even God. His poems are spiritual and touching. Personally when I am struggling with something I tend to read or write. James Kavanaugh is definitely an author that I will be reading a lot of work by. I have come to the understanding that James Kavanaugh's poems are meant to make his readers feel less alone. Human beings are not meant to feel like a tiny speck in a huge world. His poems opened my mind and showed me that I am not alone; that others face the same struggles as I.Some readers may think that Cavanaugh sounds like a very depressed person through reading his poems. I do not think he is depressed at all. His poems are filled with spirituality and emotion. James Kavanaugh writes to tell the truth; the ugly truth about how life can be. “The Time Came” demonstrates just how deeply tied Kavanaugh is to his work. The first line of that poem reads, “The time came when all that is merely human failed.” “The Time Came” shows how disgusting the earth, life, and people can be. The poem goes on to form a connection with God. In the beginning I thought the poem was going to be about destruction and disappointment but he turned the flow of things completely around and transformed the poem into something inspiring.He also writes positively. We each have our own things that are important to us and he reminds us of those things. Kavanaugh has incorporated such vivid scenes. One of my favorite scenes is an excerpt from his poem, "The Script"; "We changed jobs, dreamed of mounting investments and every possible success, chased hobbies and rainbows. Made love and knew that it would all go on forever." In that simple stanza James Kavanaugh writes with so much hope. He lets the readers know that even if life seems bad and if a sticky situation will not end; that it eventually will move past you and you will continue your journey.The connection to the actuality of life in these poems is astounding. I have never read poetry that is this deep in a sense of relatability. James Kavanaugh’s “Quiet Water” is definitely worth the read. I think the title plays along with the topics that Kavanaugh writes about. I picture water as always flowing and moving, kind of the same way as people. People are busy and moving. Quietness is a character trait that allows you to see what is going on around you and to truly observe. We get carried away by things in our life and forget what is important.
