Not as good as some of her other books which I loved but still a great start to a series. As usual the highlight is the complex world building and strong characters typical of Kate. Lots of space travel to different planets and layers of war producing some interesting and complex plots for further books in the series.It's a bit slow going but by the end of the book I appreciated the journey as we needed to see our heroine evolve and develop some purpose in her actions in order to move the plot into further books. Keep going to the end and I am sure you will appreciate it and, like me, be wanting more of these books.Read moreRead less
