Many people might find the writing too wordy. John Fowles likes to describe using an extensive vocabulary. Keep a dictionary/reference handy. Also he philosophizes and explores tangled and complex human emotions and relationships. I didn't mind as I found enough depth in the underlying story which shifts back and forth through various stages of the life of the main character. John Fowles write in several voices: sometimes as the observer of the character, sometimes as the main character and a couple of times as the voice of one of the 2 principal female characters. To my mind, he does the female voice particularly well. This is the second time I've read it, and I did get more out of it. Older and hopefully wiser, like the author when he wrote it.
