I got this book as we are planning a trip to Finland.  It is both a commentary on customs and cuisine as well as a cook book.  As an introduction to the Finn's preferences and eating customs, I found the book very helpful.  It is translated in a simple and endearing style.  You can almost hear the accent of the writers.  Now I know what to expect, even what to bring to a hostess at dinner or what to eat in a sauna.  As for the cook book aspect, I did not find the book particularly helpful.  It is all in metric with no translation.  (I had to look up the definition of a "dl".)  Furthermore, much of the food was unappealing to my palate.
