Norman Spinrad's The Void Captain's Tale is an 80's semi-erotic sci-fi tale. Set in the distant future, an incompletely understood faster than light mechanism that relies on the creation of a 'field' that is then merged with a pilot's psyche causing an instantaneous jump through space. The pilot is female and experiences an orgasm as a result. The experience is also physically demanding and draining requiring some downtime in between. As a result, travel occurs in discreet jumps. Travel has evolved such that economy class travels in hibernation, while the upper class treat the whole trip as one continuous party akin to first class travel by steamship in the 19th century. The pilot typically is socially isolated, but in this case, the captain develops an obsession with her and eventually acquiesces to her desire to jump blind with the expectation that she will merge with whatever is delivering her orgasms, but will leave the ship stuck in the vast void of space.While the sci-fi element for space travel is never fleshed out, Spinrad spends most of the tale dealing with a ruminating captain who has clearly become disillusioned and bored with his career. The decadence of the ship's passengers is frankly carnal and boring.
