This summer I am working at the university daycare and I am in charge of working with the younger children ages three and four.  We pick names for our groups and this year we are the Fireflies.  I picked up this book  because I thought that the children would enjoy a story about fireflies.  I  never imagined it would become their favorite.  In the first four days of  school I read it four times.  They love to tell me why they think the  firefly will not twinkle and the answers are as varied as the children.
