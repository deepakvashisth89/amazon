Get this book if you are a beginner in taking a biostatistics class or self-learning. Dr. Hassard did an excellent job explaining the principle ideas of statistics, which includes probability and distribution, estimation, t-test, analysis of variance ( one-way and multi-way), chi square, linear regression (simple and multiple), analysis of covariance and experimental design, all of the contents in less than 300 pages. The writing and exposition emphasize on understanding which fits well with the title of the book. The mathematical prerequisite is high school algebra and no differential and integral calculus knowledge is needed.The book is also suitable for brushing up your statistics if you haven't dealt with it for a while and do not want to read through those modern, brick-thick textbooks.Read moreRead less
