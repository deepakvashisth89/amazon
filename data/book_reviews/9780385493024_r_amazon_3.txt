In this book Naomi Wolf gives us her perspective on birth. This was the first pregnancy book I've read where I found myself nodding along in agreement. After reading the book, I switched my care from an OB/GYN (who had answered my questions with assurances that everything was done only if it was medically necessary) to a midwife, who had numbers to back up her information. Although I plan to go into childbirth better prepared than Naomi Wolf did, I found her book to be very eye-opening and as useful as others I'm reading.Read moreRead less
