Exciting story!  The Night & Fog was a Nazi tactic used to punish people who rebelled.  They would kidnap folks and their families would NEVER hear what became of them.  It was a very good terror tactic.  Now many prominent men are missing with no apparent ties.  Their children start searching for their missing fathers.  The reader is trailing along with the searchers looking for clues as to who took these men and how are they tied to other missing men.  Several stories come together eventually.  I enjoyed the ride very much.  This was not book 1 of the series (which I didn't know) but it was fine as a stand alone.Read moreRead less
