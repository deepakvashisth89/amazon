Jan Wong writes a wonderful and amusing account of her determination to be a 'good communist' despite the gradual realisation that communism doesn't quite live up to her early expectations.  As she increasing becomes aware that there have been many hidden truths she is intrigued to uncover the reality of life in China leading up to the Maoist era and following his demise.  I recommend this to anyone.  I enjoyed it for itself as a story of a young woman in China and also for the history of the time.Read moreRead less
