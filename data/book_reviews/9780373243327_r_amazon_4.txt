This the 2nd of the Wilders of Wyatt County series. This is Rachel Wilder's story of her love for Native American Grady Lewis. Grady is back in town with the little boy, Cody, who looks so much like him. Rachel and  Grady were going to be married after veterinary school but only Rachel  became a vet after her pride wouldn't let her  listen to Grady's  explanation of Cody. Grady is a *prince of honor* because he takes Cody as  his own and waits for Rachel to know her own power to love. I enjoyed the  book and am looking forward to the others in the series.
