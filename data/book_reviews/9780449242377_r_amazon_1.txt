This is the first of 3 books where Robert Fenwick is a character. However he is not the main character. Colin Grant an art advisor is the main character. This book does introduce Renwick and the work that he does. It is full of action and plot twists. However it also is very descriptive of Vienna and all of the characters in the story. It is definitely a page turner - be sure to block off room on your calendar for a great read. You won't be able to get much else done once you start the book. I highly recommend it.Read moreRead less
