I see where a couple of reviewers wasn't too impressed with this book. I guess we all see things from different perspectives. I enjoyed this story very much. Granted a background of Military, firearms and aviation pretty well sums me up so the story was made to order; still, I would think anyone who likes a good adventure story would enjoy this one.Without reveling any of the content, I found this book never  to have a letdown in action or interest. As far as the technological aspect it was right on, at least as far a my knowledge goes and that made it good enough for me. At least there weren't any unbelievably, far-fetched scenarios that only a superman could perform written into the story. All quite believable and entertaining to read.Do yourself a favor if you like this kind of stuff and be sure to read it. I plan on reading many more of Bob Meyer's stories.(No, I don't know the author,  I don't work for him or the publisher or anyone connected to the book. I just really liked it.
