Leo is driving his friend Tim to New York, where Tim has an appointment with a publisher who is interested in Tim's drawings, when the two are seized by strange, dirty aliens who take their blood. Leo awakes with no  memory of what happened, and there is no trace of his friend Tim. His story  of what happened--"I don't remember"--sounds like a more than  ordinarly pathetic excuse, until a psychiatrist begins working on him to  recover memories. Strangely, those memories are not of what really  happened....This book lacks Sleator's usual  carefully structured plot--it  just flows along, helter-skelter. Also lacking is strong characterization  and the tensions between humans that Sleator usually works in as a  sub-plot, and that's quite a loss. But it's a good, fast-paced read, with a  eyebrow-raising twist at the end.Read moreRead less
