Before I read Margaret Dielh's "Men" I had picked up a copy of her autobiographical, "The Boy on the Green Bicycle." Memoirs usually leave me lukewarm, but Diehl's Boy...was a great prelude to her  novel "Men." The woman can write! She is not afraid to tackle  head on the usually squeamish subjects of sex and death; pleasure and pain;  growth and loss, and it's all written with a searing intelligence and  literary sensibility that reads like a page-turner. Diehl should be tied to  her keyboard and not allowed out until she completes another novel, or book  of non-fiction. A wonderful find in a world of hyped up dreck.
