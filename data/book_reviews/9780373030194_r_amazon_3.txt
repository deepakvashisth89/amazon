Bethany Campbell wrote a number of these light-hearted romances for Harlequin, many years ago. I always found them to be fun and funny, with some really hilarious dialogue - she has a great ear for dialogue that sounds like real people, not robots saying what they're programmed to say. And, as I said, often (deliberately) hilarious. The Snow Garden is a good example, and doesn't disappoint.I've kept the ones I got, and have re-read them more than once, and wish all these little gems, including this one, were available in Kindle format.Read moreRead less
