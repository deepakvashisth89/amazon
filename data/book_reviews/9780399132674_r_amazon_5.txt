A wonderfully strange and unique adventure through time and space. This book is full of Mr. Robert A Heinlein's charm, humor, and provocative social commentaries on the uniqueness of humanity and the moral paradoxes that are a part of everyday life. I really love the main character, Maureen Johnson Long who is a woman that is not afraid to express herself sexually as a woman and who adapts to changing circumstances with humor and courage. Fans of Doctor Who will love this book because it deals with time travel, immortality, and some very unique time settings. It does have some things in it that may be objectionable to some sensitive readers, but this is an adult oriented science fiction novel, and it is for mature adults, and that's why i loved this novel.
