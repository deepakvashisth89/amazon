I am not a 'chicken soup for the soul' fan.  However, this book was given to me after my mother passed and it was one of the best books to help me through her passing.  I have since bought it for a friend of mine who just lost a parent.  She also claimed it has helped her too.  The reason this book is good is because it deals with real stories about people going through the same process.  Unlike most books about grief which tell you how you'll feel or that it is okay to feel a certain way and then explain the process of grief, this book offered uplifting, and comforting stories.Read moreRead less
