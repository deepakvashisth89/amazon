I can identify with a lot in this book. After working for the church for years and being treated like trash, I finally broke ties. It was painful.This book is organized very well, and it includes many perspectives. The reasoning is clear, and not too mean spirited. Overall, it is written with compassion for the women.After reading this, I don't think many people will be able to disagree with it. My favorite quote was something like, "Men submit to the church and get promotions, cars, vacations, and power. Women submit to the church and get used." After being lied to, having my health insurance cut, and living on $20,000 per year for full-time pay, I cannot help but say that quote is right on the mark. Then you have to ask yourself: Can a church that operates this way really be the true Church? It's hard to say yes.
