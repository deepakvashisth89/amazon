As an e-book, it was easy to read, and not a lot of mistakes. Some of the words with French diacriticals came out wonky, but that wasn't a problem for me. Four stars.As a story: slow start, but I was slowly drawn into Evelina's world, and stayed up until midnight to finish the book on day 3. The book is written as a series of letters, which is a form I *really* like. Lots of really evil people in this book, but they are entertainingly evil. Very moralistic, but were the lessons learned the wrong lessons? Three stars.As a contemporary romance from the 1700s: it knocked my socks off. I couldn't believe that it was so clear. The period details were astounding, and I kept thinking that if I ever wrote a historical romance, I'd want to include this detail or that. It's not as sweet and mannered as Jane Austen, but it's not as bawdy as Tom Jones. And speaking of Jane Austen, this book was mentioned in her letters. So it was cool to think that I was reading something that Jane Austen had read.Read moreRead less
