David Gerrald crafted a classic science fiction series with this powerful and haunting story line. The events are vivid and occasionally unsettling as the situation for humanity and our protagonist grow more dire as things unfold. There are images that will stick with the reader for years after the pages close. They certainly have with me.The  concepts are rich enough that the author could go back even now and add to this saga. I'll say no more about that so as not to hint at spoilers.
