"Wolf to the Slaughter" is another in a long line of most excellent mysteries by the greatest living mystery author, Ruth Rendell.  Her Inspector Wexler mysteries are not as "dark" as some of her other works, but all of them are absolutely can't-put-down-until-finished stories!!  Her psychological twists and turns keep one glued to the story, and sorry when it ends -- as it always does -- with exactly the right/believable solution.  I can't get enough of her books and having read most of them, sincerely hope she is busily writing a new one!!!Read moreRead less
