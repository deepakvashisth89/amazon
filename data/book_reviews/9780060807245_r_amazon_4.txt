Detective Chief Superintendent Masters and his team of Chief Inspector Green and Sergeants Reed and Berger are returning from a case in Middlesbrough when their car breaks down. Whilst waiting for assistance from the local constabulary Masters and Green go for a walk round a disused church yard and find a body in a well.
