The title works out to be ironic.  The powers-that-be at that time in the 1970s had become impatient with those not going along with the official version of the JFK assassination.  The House Select Committee on Assassinations (HSCA) was supposed to be the last investigation.  The big issue addressed in this book is what happened to the HSCA.  This is essential reading for a complete understanding, not only of the assassination itself, but of the fifty-year cover-up.  There is important is information about David Atlee Phillips and David Sanchez Morales.  One must turn to other authors for information about Lyndon Johnson and J. Edgar Hoover.  See Phillip F. Nelson's book.[Postscript January 9, 2017]  I am Wynne Murphey Johnson.  This book turned out to be extremely important to me personally, because in June of 2014 a couple of paragraphs on pages 141-142 triggered my remembrance of the encounter on September 7, 1963, in the Southland Center building in Dallas of David Phillips, Lee Oswald, and Antonio Veciana.  I was there and even figured in a conversation in English which Veciana seems not to have understood.  I was only 15 years old at the time, but I suspect that a remark made by Oswald about me affected Phillips's plans about how to employ Oswald.  Although I do not seem to be believed by researchers, I am now really the primary public witness to that encounter, since my companion Vicki does not seem to remember.  I remember well now.
