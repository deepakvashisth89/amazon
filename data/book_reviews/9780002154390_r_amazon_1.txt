If you grew up in the 80's, and if you're into those horror/zombies/slasher low-badget films - then this book is a must-have. Reading it really takes you back to those nights when you waited for your parents to go to bed, so you could enjoy another cheesy and bloody slasher....We all know that the 80's is a legendary decade, overflowing with those flicks which can be summarized as a concoction of much-too-red fake blood, ridiculous plots, bizarre killings and piles and piles of actors and actresses who believed that those movies will help them making their way to become superstars (before vaporizing without a trace...). All of those movies came from the same pattern, you could always tell what was gonna happen and you knew you'll never be in for any high standarts of directing or acting. still - you know you can never get enough of those flicks - because they always delivered the goods, which is nothing but pure fun. I've always ignored those self-righteous critics who claimed that those movies are boring and sick - because I knew they were meant to be entertaining, and not to set an example of how to create a work of art.Now, if you agree with me so far - all I can say is that you have to get a copy, only to find out how many flicks are out there that you never came across. We're talking about hundreds and hundreds.... all of the titles usually include term such as "massacre", "blood", "night", "terror" - so trying to distinguish one from another is useless, and again - keep in mind that we're dealing with a genre which spawns instant movies only to serve us with buckets of blood and buckets of helpless naked blondes begging from a masked psycho not to drown/electrocute/strangle/guillotine/boil/freeze them etc....The reviews are just great. Each one is funnier than the previous one, and you get the essence of the movie with all the important details. You just can't go wrong with those reviews and I must say that the author did a splendid job. That book is like a thesis in slasher science, and it even treats you to estimators and enumerators which count how many dead bodies, beheadings, crotch-spikings and nudity galore you can find in every film. Read the reviews for films like "Don't open 'till christmas", "Nail-gun massacre" or "He knows you're alone" - and you'll laugh out loud from the stingy criticisms ("Tedious even at fast-foreward", "no skin, no blood, no point, no plot").I hope my review will convince you 80's kids to get a copy. It really sparks some nostalgic moments from that wonderful decade. I'll add and say that my favorite one is "Slaughter high" from 1986 (I wrote a review on that film as well), so I'd highly recommend to see that one, in case you haven't seen it already. That film knitted together every essential 80's-slasher element possible, so that's why I think this one is the ultimate.Read moreRead less
