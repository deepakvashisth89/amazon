Nothing is quite as it seems in this SF adventure. A spaceship has been sent to explore a distant world. But it's not your usual spaceship.  Is the intrepid crew human? No. Well, maybe, in a sense. Don't get too metaphysical, we are warned in a book full of metaphysical scenarios And what about those aliens? Are the Spinners beneficent, or is some nefarious plot afoot?Meanwhile, the human race hasn't been doing all that well apparently, as the Earth has fallen strangely silent. Time for a quick visit if we can avoid precipitating a disaster.Echoes of Earth is a fantastic ride, full of unexpected twists and turns.
