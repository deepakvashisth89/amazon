This was a very good, readable and lively account of St Francis' life and works. It also includes a great deal of short biographical information in breif of the key players, ideas and events of the time in the margins. I took away from this a sense that St Francis was very courageous and selfless in giving away everything in favour of faith. The life and deeds of St Francis also teach us many things about the modern world and what is important in todays modern world. He teaches us that materialism and desire for money and material gain are not really necessary for happiness and that true happiness lies with a content soul and love of God. St Francis also teaches us more so than any prophet or Saint the importance of venerating and appreciating Gods creation, i.e. nature, the sun, moon, stars, birds etc. The Canticle of the Sun is very beatiful as is the soul of St Francis.Read moreRead less
