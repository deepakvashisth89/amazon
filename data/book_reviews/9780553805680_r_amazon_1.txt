This book is full of love. Love for family, love of God, and love for others. The author creates realistic characters full of emotions and feelings so evident in the world around us. Through the characters leads us to be thoughtful of our hearts and mindful of our attitudes showing how to deal with daily situations and others in our sphere. Even showing that it is okay to admit that we don't have all of the answers.The story shows the value of family and friends. Angela and Thomas are left to care for their young siblings. Dealing with busybody neighbors, a mild drama queen, and a few caring friends just adds to busy farm life. This book however deals with the state of people's hearts and caused me to examine my own.Read moreRead less
