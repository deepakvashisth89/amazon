This book was recommended by someone on a book discussion site and now I suspect it was either the author himself or close relative.  Given the number of glowing reviews written here, back before Amazon.com caught on to this ploy, they have to have been generated in an effort at self-promotion that takes one's breath away.  Read a few and notice how similar they are.  Due to this dark plot by a talentless hack, I have read an old novel about WWII that may be one of the worst books I've read in my life.  I love historical fiction, but not romance novels.  This is not even that.  In fact, it is impossible to describe how poorly written, plotted, and inaccurate this thing is.  The melodrama is way over the top, the characters one dimensional, and the flowery speech like something from, well, a romance novel.  I feel as if I've been robbed of time I could have spent reading a decent book, but I couldn't stop myself because this was too horrible to believe.  To compare this to Tolstoy, Joyce, Hemmingway?
