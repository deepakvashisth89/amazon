Having the earlier book of Presidential Anecdotes for over thirty years, I was glad to find this one; the stories of the ladies behind the poor saps that took the Oath due to election, succession, or in Mr. Ford's case, succession via appointment. It really does take a strong woman for that spot.  Makes me almost pity Mr. Pierce; he didn't have that in his wife. At least Mr. Buchanan had his niece.
