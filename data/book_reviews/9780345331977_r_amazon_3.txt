This was my first time reading Interview with the Vampire after watching the TV series, and I absolutely loved it. The characters are deep and engaging, and the storytelling is rich and immersive. It hooked me right from the beginning, and the pacing is perfect for getting lost in the world of vampires.Some sections move a little slower, but they build atmosphere beautifully. I didn’t like Louis as much as I did in the series — he comes across as a bit whiny in parts — but I wish I’d read the book first. I think I would have understood him better from the start.I was sick in bed and ended up finishing it the day after I got it. I can’t wait to start the next one, and I fully intend to read the entire Vampire Chronicles series.Highly recommended for fans of vampire stories, gothic fiction, and emotional, character-driven tales.Read moreRead less
