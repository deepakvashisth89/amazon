This is a great book!  My wife and I both read it after having lived in the Middle East for several years, and were pleased at the author's care to present Arabian life and culture in an accurate fashion. But this is not a  story about Arabia, nor is it a story about war; it is an engrossing tale  of espionage, love, betrayal, and three people trying to save a precarious  government from collapse.  I would like to give the book five stars,  but the edition I read contained several spelling errors near the end; it  looked as if the proof-reader had been in a hurry.
