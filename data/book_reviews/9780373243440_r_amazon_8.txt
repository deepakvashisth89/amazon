The title Lost and Found Groom led me to believe that this book would be something different to what was written.  Nevertheless it was a most enjoyable story with the overhanging mysterious curse, leading one to understand that Kendra and Daniel's love was fated.  I found the events during the hurricane deeply touching and I felt myself being pulled into the setting. The connection between Kendra and Daniel was of a nature that it was inevitable that they would meet again.Daniel's reappearance in Kendra's life was sad compared to their deep connection in Santa Estella, as the feisty woman on the island had become scared and unsure, despite the deep longings of her heart.  I felt Daniel's rejection and wanted to shake Kendra out of her uncertainties.
