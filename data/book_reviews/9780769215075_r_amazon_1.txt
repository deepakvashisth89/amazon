For years I just had one score from this book (the theme song) and enjoyed it. I was always frustrated that it didn't include the Title, which is more beautiful than the theme (Lara's theme) in my opinion. I saw the Title was available in this book but was leery of the price. Anyway, I'm glad I bought it and am really happy with some of the other songs from the movie I'd forgotten about, particularly the Marches. These are really fun to play and get everyone's attention.The only issue I have is there's no fingering for many of the scores. The title involves 10-12 span reaches in some cases for both hands so it was definitely scored for an expert pianist (or Jarre just didn't want to dilute his work on lesser mortals).
