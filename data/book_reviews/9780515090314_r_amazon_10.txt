I've seen the film, hasn't everyone? but did we know it is based on true events, well I certainly did not. The film doesn't come even close to capturing what it was really like for the poor children of the post war east end of London in the 1950's.The book, however, is a true masterpiece as relevant today as back then. Braithwaite gets the children to debate and argue their points of view while respecting those with opposing views. He teaches them manners and self respect, respect for others, how to think for themselves. I read the book very quickly, I could not put it down.
