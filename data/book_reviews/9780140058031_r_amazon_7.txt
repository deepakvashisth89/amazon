I love the characters in Lovesey's books!  Thackery is a gem.  You can just see him walking along in his bowler hat and black suit.  This book goes into the life in the music halls that were popular in the Victorian era.  Lovesey has a great sense of period, and that's what makes his books so entertaining.  I am really enjoying this series.  The mystery in this particular books isn't quite as good as the previous two in the series, but there is certainly enough to maintain your interest if you're a Victorian Mystery buff like me.Read moreRead less
