I got this book, along with 3 other Sloane & MacHale lateral thinking puzzle books for Christmas one year.  At first it seemed like a sorry excuse for a gift, but as I read this one first, I found that it was a challenging endeavor of the mind.  Lateral thinking, it seems, is taking a situation and finding a solution that is not the most obvious, but usually the most logical.  That said, this is challenging to say the least.
