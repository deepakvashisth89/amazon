This is a review of the audio book.Set in the 1960s, the book tells the story of Mitsu, an English professor in Tokyo, and his brother Takashi. Following the suicide of one of his friends, Mitsu, along with his wife, returns to hometown on Shikoku where they meet Takashi who has also just returned home.Though the trip home may be seen as an attempt at a refresh of life, the brothers become embroiled in family secrets. An ancestor who once led a rebellion one century prior, becomes Takashi’s inspiration against a former Korean slave from WWII who has now developed some economic might and has become a dominant businessman in the area.The story does not resemble my usual read, but I wanted to try it based on the author’s reputation. There are a number of graphic sexual references in the book. I am not downgrading my rating for these references. I’m just mentioning it here for anyone reading this review. I give it four stars as it is deeply moving and will leave you changed. At the same time, I sped up the narration as it was too slow for me at times.Read moreRead less
