Mr. Gardner has a fine talent for creating narrators that feel real.  You get involved with their problems and sympathize with their angsts - as well as cheer their successes.  It's a most engrossing style.  All of this in his quirky sci fi world where moral dilemmas are always just a little bit trickier than you expect.In this novel, Faye Smallwood is a middle-aged woman who inhabits a world shared by humans and Divians, a fun alien species.  She's done her best to rebuild herself after a plague decimated most of the Divians - as well as her teenaged innocence.  However, she slowly learns that, as traumatizing and terrible as the plague itself was, there may have been more to it than the obvious.  Help comes from a savvy off-worlder, as well, and fans of this series will certainly recognize her.Great book, with more than one, "Woah!" moment to knock you off your feet (or just make your coworkers think you're crazy for exclaiming at a book).Read moreRead less
