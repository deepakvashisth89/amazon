We completed that circle on a cold February morning, under dark skies, in the rain, with Ayanna being Nicole’s lungs and me being Nicole’s heart, then me being Nicole’s lungs, and Ayanna being Nicole’s heart.”This angel and his beautiful writing have yet to disappoint me. This was so beautifully written and captivating from the very first page. 🕊️This was so much more than just a love triangle. Like his previous books I’ve read, it was deeply layered and touched on so many important topics in society—religion, sexuality, sexism, and racism, just to name a few.The love between the three characters was all-consuming, unconditional, soul-crushing love that makes it hard to let go. There were so many moments throughout the book where I wanted the main character to just walk away, because it was clear Nicole had moved on—even though she still loved him.His love for her made it damn near impossible to walk away, and it was so heartbreaking to read because this man was truly willing to endure so much just to be with her. It reminded me of the song John Redcorn by SiR—that song fits the vibe of their dynamic perfectly.I didn’t really like Nicole all that much. I felt like she was manipulative. Even though I do believe she loved them both deeply, I also feel like she took advantage of their love for her. And toward the end, when we find out she was having an affair with Ayanna, I was just done with her—I was angry and fed up.That said, I did give her some grace, considering her upbringing and the situation with her very religious mother. I feel like the way she was raised had a strong impact on her confusion and her inability to fully accept her bisexuality. In turn, that led her to hurt the man she loved. And while it was wrong, it also felt very real—because situations like that happen all the time in real life.By the end of the book, I was so emotional—and that’s what I love about his books. They always bring so much emotion out of me. I was so happy that he finally found the strength to walk away. I know how hard it is to let go of someone you love that deeply, but it had to be done—for both of them to be free.Surprisingly, I was also really happy with the way Ayanna and the MMC bonded. It was so beautiful, and I love how the author crafted that connection. I also really loved the symbolism of the bracelets and what they represented for the MMC at the end. Genius!This book was beautiful. It was written so well. It’s definitely going to be living in my head rent-free, and I highly recommend it!Read moreRead less
