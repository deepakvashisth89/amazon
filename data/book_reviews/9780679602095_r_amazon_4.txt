This is a well written translation, although I'm not a scholar on translations from the Italian of Dante.  It's easy to read and poetic.  Extensive annotations explain the characters and the process of translation, especially where there is ambiguity or nuances of the the Italian that can't be translated.  Surprisingly enjoyable read.As for the work itself, it is important for its place in history and the effect it has had over the centuries.  Much of it sounds like Dante's revenge against his political enemies (I wonder where his political opponents in Florence would put him it hell).  The descriptions are vivid, horrid and fascinating.
