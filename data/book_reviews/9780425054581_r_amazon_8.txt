As I said in my review of Triplanetary, I first read these books many years ago, and they were considered classics then. Nothing has changed in the 35-plus years since then.The Space Opera continues... The humans get the word to go to Arisia... The first Lenses come out... The ultimate Police Force comes into being. This book has everything that makes escapist reading fun!!! Romance, intrigue, epic battles, murder-most-foul, tech way beyond Star Trek, and a bit of comedy to lighten things up. Anything more that I could say would require Spoiler Alerts, and I want you to find all this out for yourself.
