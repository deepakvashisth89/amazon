This is a wonderfully written story which I enjoyed very much, and would recommend to anyone who likes classic fictional literature.It was smooth and easy to read, despite it's age, I personally very much like this writing style.The only gripe I have is the writer's dated outlook on the world which comes up from time to time e.g. a divide between the colonial whites and natives, and animals are there to be killed. If you can see past this, you will enjoy the story (it helps that these divisions are not written maliciously; simply a sign of those times).The first Henry Rider Haggard book I have read, and it won't be the last.Read moreRead less
