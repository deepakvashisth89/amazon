I read a lot - my work is serious and so I read lot of novels for light relief.  I bought this novel because it was a freebie and almost didn't read past the first couple of pages.  I love flash fiction and short stories, so to start a novel where the author spent days on one piece of scenery, or several paragraphs on a character's attitude, was almost tedious.  But the story intrigued me and I kept on to where, I was reading it as much as I could.  The archaic form of writing didn't draw me in, but the simplicity of the story did.  And in contrast to all the novels I use for light relief - this one gave me something back.  I loved it and was hugely satisfied by the unexpected ending.  So much that I have bought more of Arnold Bennett's writing and if his other work is half as good as The Old Wive's Tale, then I am a happy woman.Read moreRead less
