I have a two year old and it was remarkable how accurate this book is!  It isn't a 'how to' parenting guide at all, but rather a descriptive book providing very detailed information about the workings of the two year old.  Yes, the book is dated, but I thought that made it fun.  The book was published in 1976, so it was a new book when I myself was a two year old.  It was fun to imagine my mother reading this kind of book when I was little.  The question and answer section at the very end is nothing short of hilarious and I laughed out loud many times while reading it.  I recommend this book to anyone raising a two year old!Read moreRead less
