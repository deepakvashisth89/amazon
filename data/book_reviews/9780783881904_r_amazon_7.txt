Arthur C. Clarke was a Grand Master of Science Fiction for a reason and this story demonstrates that mastery as well as any classic science fiction could. Clarke built stories that put you in the action and included just enough science fact (and supposition) to make the story realistic and believable. 3001 is an excellent end to a wonderful story of the future of mankind.Clarke's stories stand the test of time, and  although there are moments that date his writing, the reader can still see his influences on the future (our present) for both real science and for science fiction.Read Arthur C.
