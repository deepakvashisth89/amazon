Our public library has a monthly poetry group in which we read and discuss poems with a retired English professor and poet.  The group thoroughly enjoyed reading and analyzing Ms. Adair's poems. There is a mix of mood in these poems--some are light and humorous, and some take a more somber view of life.  As Ms. Adair's life had much tragedy in it with the suicide of her husband, she was well aware of the gloom in the shadows of life. It shows in her poetry.Her poetry is brilliant and unaffected.  You like the person behind the voice.  Some of the poems have great depth and give up insight and wisdom if you take the time to reflect upon them.
