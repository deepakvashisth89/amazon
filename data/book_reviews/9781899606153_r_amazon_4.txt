"Meals in Moments" is the book of the 10-minute daytime Channel 5 television show in which celebrities cook with chef Simon Rimmer.  I caught two episodes of the show quite by accident.  I haven't got any time for celebrity worship but I was surprised at how good the show was over those two episodes, in which the recipes were Simple Beef Wellington and Aubergine Cutlets with Pesto, both of which looked excellent.  So I ordered the book.There are over 60 recipes in the book, all accompanied by a full page photograph. 15 of them were made by celebrities and the rest by Simon Rimmer.  Each is accompanied by a line or two of explanation or description, a serving guide, preparation time, cooking time, Kcals per serving and an indication of whether it is low in salt or sugar.  A list of ingredients is followed by bullet-pointed instructions.  In many cases there is a wine recommendation too.The Contents only lists the 15 recipes by the celebrities - you have to go to the index to see the full listing of recipes in the book.  The book is organized under the headings Soups and Starters, Brunch and Salads, Pasta and Rice, Main Courses, Puddings.  The mix of recipes is a great mix of styles and nationalities.Starters include Tom Yum Soup with Chicken and Prawns, Mozzarella and Parma Parcels and Chicken Liver Pate.  Brunch and Salads include Parsi Scrambled Eggs, Seared Beef wtih Vegetable and Potato Salad and Eggs Benedict Rustic Italian Salad.  Pasta and Rice includes Indonesian Style Fried Rice, Seared Scallops with Ginger and Spring Onion Vermicelli Noodles and Risotto Bianco.  Main Courses include Lamb and Mint Koftas, Cajun Pork with Pineapple Salsa, Creamy Squash Curry, Mediterranean Chicken Stew, Fruity Turkey Burger, Seafood Paella and Pancetta-topped Crushed New Potatoes. Puddings include Greek Honey Balls, Truffles and Sparkling Blueberry Jellies.Most of the recipes take 10 minutes to prepare, although most take half an hour or more to complete.  This is ideal for me because although I enjoy more elaborate cooking on occasion, I need practical and rapid solutions for daily use. The instructions are easy to follow and produce great results.There are a lot of photographs of celebrities (many of whom I've never heard of) and a page devoted to each of them - which is okay if you're interested in celebrities but not of any value if you're not.
