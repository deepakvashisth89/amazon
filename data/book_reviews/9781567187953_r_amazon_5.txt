Even though I have rejected religion and all other forms of mysticism I still find it interesting that so many people are believers in spite of a lack of compelling scientific evidence in these esoteric subjects.  I had never read a book that focused on angles and spirit guides so when I saw this soft cover book (Spirit Guides and Angel Guardians by Richard Webster 342 pages) on Amazon for a bargain price I purchased it.I did find this book to be an interesting read.  The author clearly ignores those who might question the existence of these invisible helpers and writes as if angels and spirit guides are not figments of our imagination but real beings sent to help us.  The volume is organized into two parts.  The first part covers angels, your guardian angel, creating your guardian angel, working with the Archangels, ministries of healing, Angel, creativity and the arts, your angel diary, and other information about angels. The second part explains all about your spirit guides and covers ways to contact your spirit guides, spirit guides and emotional health, your limitless guides, psychic circle and other topics.If you are a true believer in religious dogma, mysticism and other esoteric subjects you will find this book very interesting and even helpful.  I admit it is very appealing to think we all have personal angels and guardians who will help us with all our problems; however, as a scientific minded person I remain with my feet firmly planted in reality.Rating: 4 stars.
