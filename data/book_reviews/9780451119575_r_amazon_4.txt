Harriet is the ringleader.  Her friend follows the lead no matter where Harrietgoes.  One summer the friend is attracted to a much older, much unhappilymarried man.  Being only 13-years-old and Harriet a year older, she's notcertain what she should do about her feelings.The girls ... Harriet, actually .... come up with a plan to entice, thenembarrass, and humiliate the man they call The Tsar.
