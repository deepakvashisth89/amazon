I purchased this book, "The Adventurers" by Harold Robbins, as a Mass Market Paperback and used, for $0.01 plus shipping, to read again to determine if my memory serves me well, and determine if I still consider this to be one of the best books I have read for entertainment.  As I recall this book is a life story, fiction of course, based on the life of a Diplomat's son who is introduced into a world of travel and intrigue, where he is orphaned and left to be cared for by Fat Cat, an overseer who devotes his life to look after being a servant ward of the father.  Politics and Revenge for the murders of his Mother and Father move the story of Dax and Fat Cat as they travel and live.
