I really enjoyed the movie "Blackhawk Down" and remember speaking to an ex Air Guard pilot tell me a little bit about his memories of being active Air Guard at the time it happened.  The author has obviously put tons of man hours into research and recounts many first hand accounts of the Rangers and Delta soldiers involved.  You'd think that it was being written by a soldier that was there first hand.
