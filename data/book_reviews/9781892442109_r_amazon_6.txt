My husband Anthony and I had the privilege of meeting you at Costco in Ogden yesterday. Thank you for taking the time to be there and introduce us to your books. Your personality is what sold us and so we took home two of your works.  I just completed the first one, "I Hated Heaven" a couple hours ago. It captivated me from beginning to end and I never expected the final pages to end as they did. It was AWESOME and so breathtaking to read of such true and committed love. Not only from a husband to his wife, but from God to us.Truthfully, I was often disturbed while reading it. I was really frustrated at the rigidity of heavens' structure, or I guess I should say that of  Paradise, not Heaven. I kept thinking to myself that I would not fit in there either, nor was I sure I wanted to. It really bothered me... And I really liked Stan's free thinking style which was also a bit troubling, due to his nature. I think the reader will be amazed at what a character he turns out to be. You cannot imagine how surprised, and thrilled I was at all the twists and turns that you had weaved into the books final pages. I LOVED everything about this book.  I am not sure how I have missed it all these years, but am so grateful to have run into you yesterday and found my new "favorite" book. You may well become my new favorite author, too, as I am about to start reading Lightland.
