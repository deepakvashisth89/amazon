This book asks the question, "What would happen if a prostitute became a member of the Justice League?" This would have been great if it were a Vertigo book, but because it's published by Image, they changed the names of everyone on the Justice League, and turned it into a satire. Here's the lineup:*Superman is The Saint in this book*Batman is The Knight*Robin is The Squire*Wonder Woman is The Lady*Green Lantern is The Lime*The Flash is Speedo*The Justice League is The League of HonorIt's a really funny book, and The Pro is a really great character. My big gripe with this book is that Ennis came up with this awesomely cool character, and then put her in the middle of mediocre derivative satire. As an example, it answers the question first posed in 1995 inMallrats, about whether or not a mortal woman could have Superman's baby. Ennis obviously feels that Brodie was correct in that discussion. In this book, The Saint (Superman) says, "Oh no, move your head. In God's name, move your head."Most people probably won't be as bothered as I was by this book only having one good character, with the rest being really flat characters, for gags and laughs.This book is also very mature, violent with plenty of nudity and crudity.Pros:+The Pro is a great, unconventional superhero+The Pro uses her super powers to make more money in her business+Cool artwork+Very funny+Wonder Woman's costume in this book is awesome!+Green Lantern diesCons:-This is a pretty short read-Justice League satire seemed a waste of obviously great writing talentRead moreRead less
