I have no idea what everyone is reading. This book is a mess. I mean who is the focus of this novel? It seems every 4 or 5 paragraphs we have a narrative shift to some character I don't care about or even know who the character is. You could have several narrative shifts in a single chapter as if the the author just got bored and started writing another random scene no one cares about.There is no structure, no plot, no semblance of sanity. Normally, I like action in a novel. But the action is so random, so over the top, you would think that these characters (the dozens that appear) just wake up every day casting spells, fighting, and then just find themselves magically in another place doing the same thing over and over with no reason for it. Honestly, I cannot even tell you a single location in the novel as they all seem to blend into one. (Ok, I remember 2 - there were some trees and possibly a throne room/castle somewhere? Can't really be sure as my mind was pretty numb with the content I was reading).Where are the supposedly main characters (the band of 4)...They rarely appear and when they do..They fight, cast spells, fight, say random stuff to each other, and then disappear for 5 chapters.This is the third book in the series and they seem to get worse and worse. Have no idea why I have stuck with the series other than I spent the money on the books and feel I need to get some worth out of them...But I think I would have been better off giving someone the money, have them kick me in the nuts, and then just move on with life.
