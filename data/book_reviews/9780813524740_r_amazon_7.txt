I learned so much about Gertrude Stein in this biography written by Linda Wagner-Martin. She packs so much information about Gertrude's life, which includes information about her family, her life in Paris with Picasso and Matisse, her experience during two World Wars, her private life with Alice and her persistence in getting her work published.
