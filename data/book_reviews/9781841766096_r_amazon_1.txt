Starting with the LaGG-3 early in the war, pilots hated the fighter, calling it 'The flying coffin'. Then with the upgraded models, (called the La series) the Russian pilots steadily gained the upper hand over the German counterparts.This book is truly wounderful!! The best book I have purchased yet! There are 3 chapters in this book; LaGG-3-The Original, La-5-The Breed Improves and La-7-Best of the Breed. I spent 2 or 3 days reading this book and absolutely loved every minute of it!!I have read quite a few of Ospreys books and have had 3 books that are wonderful, beautiful and fantastic; 'Finnish aces', 'Bf-109 aces of the Russian Front' and 'LaGG and Lavochkin aces'.George Mellinger does a fantastic job of this book! He gives you information in the beginning of the chapter about the plane, then lists aces of the plane. Absolutely a great book!!This book has aces such as Ivan Kozhedub, Dmitrii Titarenko Aleksei Alelyukhin and more. Even Finnish LaGG-3 ace Eino Koskinen!The book boasts 85 pages of wonderfully drawn color plates, information on aces and pictures.If you are looking for information on Russian aces, then I very highly recommend this book!!
