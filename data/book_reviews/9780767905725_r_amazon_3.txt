If you play golf on weekends with your buddies, you will enjoy this book.  Every group can identify with a couple of the players in the foursome.  We all play for the friendly competition with a couple of bucks on the line to make it interesting, but we fantasize about the dream trip with some higher stakes.  The mysterious venue pulls you into the plot and the competition features the hustler we all know that can talk his way into a match without giving strokes reflective of his superior ability.  As the betting escalates it gets really interesting.  In most good books about golf, because it is in the nature of the game and in sports in general, you learn something about yourself during and after the competition.  This novel does not disappoint in this category.  This is the book you will want to buy your Sunday golf buddies for Christmas.....especially if you want to increse the stakes in your weekend game.
