This book, a sort of prequel to Phillip Pan's "Out of Mao's Shadow", consists of three extended essays on Chinese dissidents in the post-Mao era. The first is about one lawyer's battle against the legal system; the second on an young architect's struggle to preserve historic Beijing sites; and the third and longest is on the Falun Gong controversy. It was this last chapter that I found most illuminating and informative, since I had never fully grasped the nature of the exercise cum meditation movement and why it drove the Commissars into such a frenzy of persecution. Johnson crisply explains it all and introduces us to some of the persecuted.
