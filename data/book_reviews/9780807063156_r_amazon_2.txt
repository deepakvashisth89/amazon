Corregidor is a book examining the origin of family scars (physically and psychologically), bigotry, domestic abuse, and rape. The central protagonist, Ursula's account, reveals to the world how history often repeats itself and how difficult it is for one to shake their past when it's deeply rooted within them.Jones's telling of the narrative is still relevant now, and although her choice of words may not be for all, it is a remarkable story that should be studied by many.Read moreRead less
