This was an absolutely stupendous first novel.  I had read Social Crimes recently, and enjoyed it quite a bit, so I set out to explore Hitchcock's other novels, beginning with her first.  I was NOT disappointed.  The premise is incredibly interesting and the plot is an absolute page-turner.  This work is also psychologically disturbing without relying on cheap effects.But what elevates this author's work in my opinion is her ability to enrich the basic plot with an intelligent narrator who tosses out one fascinating insight after another.  Hitchcock's sensibility is that of an aesthete, and both her subject matter and her writing style reflect that.  In Trick of the Eye, she tends to each sentence with great care, a rarity these days.By the author of It All Started with a BicycleRead moreRead less
