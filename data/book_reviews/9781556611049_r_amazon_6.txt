Although I liked the story line and found many points of interest, there didn't seem to be a lot of depth in the character portraits.  Who was the guy with the scar and why didn't he take more drastic steps when he had the opportunity.  Why did Ma have a bundle of money, why did Pa take such good care of his brother-in-law and totally ignore his own family obligations for so long.  The book does offer many paths for future sequels - the Indian boy who seems to like Corrie, the very friendly and helpful lady friend and the city slicker pastor who is out to save the whole town of sinners.  Maybe I will like the next book better.  It was an easy read and thought provoking in some spots, but not one I would take to the book club for review.Read moreRead less
