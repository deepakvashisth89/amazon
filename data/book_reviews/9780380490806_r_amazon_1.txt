An absolutely first rate mystery with an plot that becomes increasingly dark as it unfolds. Also featuring an intelligent and resourceful heroine. The worse her situation the more she rises to the occasion.My first encounter with the author was with one of her rather amusing mysteries set in agricultural college. This one is both less comedic, and more compelling. Both mysteries play fairly with the reader so that it maybe possible to pinpoint the guilty but the plot of this one keeps revealing its complexity in stages throughout. I found this a very satisfying read and intend to read the next. Please may I have some more?Read moreRead less
