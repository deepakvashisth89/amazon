I found this book fascinating, and fast reading.  So well written; you felt like you were right there!  The ups and downs of her childhood, marrying Albert, her 9 children and many grandkids and gr-grandkids!  Lots of information about her personal life because she journaled daily.  Details about so much, including her death.  Lots of details about hers  and Albert's relationship, his work, his health, his worries and his death, and her mourning of him.  Lots to tell about her Highland Servant John Brown.  Good information about her 1st son, the future King Edward VII, who gave his parents so much anguish.  I think the movies, "Mrs. Brown" and "Victoria & Albert" took a lot of information from here.  This book seems well researched.  An enjoyable and interesting read.  I am reading for pleasure and information, not as a scholar, so it's fine that this book covered a minimum of the events taking place around the world and England.  I was interested in her and her private life.
