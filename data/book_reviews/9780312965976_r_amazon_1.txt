The heroine in this story, Maddie, finds herself in a predicament familiar to many women, picking up the pieces of her life after divorce. Maddie, is longing to connect with an aloof and resentful teen daughter, while trying to maintain a semblance of a normal relationship with her ex-husband. As the story unfolds, Maddie finds herself battling not to vie for their daughter's affections, as her ex-husband attempts to manipulate his daughter's love in his favor.Maddie finds her life becoming a bit of a drama, as she finds herself being the glue that must hold her extended family (mother, siblings, etc.) together after her father's mysterious murder. She is the cool head in the midst of several storms, she is the go to person ensuring that justice for her father's murder is served, and she is the supportive, and loving friend to her two best girlfriends.Her life is once again rocked when, her ex-lover from twenty years prior, Dan Hawke, pops back up in the little seaside town. He is determined to win Maddie's heart again, which he thought he lost when she sent him packing after a horrific event twenty years prior. In the face of her family's resentment and misgivings about him, Maddie is also struggling with her own morals.This romantic mystery was engaging on all levels. It kept me entertained as I rooted for their love, while I worked to solve the mystery of Maddie's father. I found some characters trifling, and others endearing. In a shocking twister at the end of the story, I find myself not having sympathy for one particular character that the author attempts to paint in a sympathetic light. I loved it!Read moreRead less
