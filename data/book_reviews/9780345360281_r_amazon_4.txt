While I enjoyed this book and felt it was well written I think I prefer the lighter tone of the previous book "Fat Tuesday".Essentially I prefer a story where the murder victim isn't someone I'd miss much... This book might be both more realistic and a better mystery... But it's kind of a downer.As slight side note: I once (1980s) wandered from the backroom of junk/antique store's basement into a Chinese bar / gambling hall not more than 4 blocks from where this story is set. While nobody was unfriendly, it was remarkably like a Western movie when the music stops when the stranger steps in the saloon. I just smiled and went back through the "secret" passage.Another small note: Even back in the eighties, many folks called this area the International District...
