Phenomenal photography of sacred sites from around the world.  Not all the sites are considered sacred from a traditional standpoint but are sacred from the standpoint of moving the observed to a sense of reverence.  From Ayer's Rock in Australia to Machu Picchu in Peru to Glastonbury Tor in England this is a collection of landscape photographs and paintings that are sure to leave a reader with a sense of awe.  With each picture there is also a poem or appropriate prose.  At another time and place they may have been inspirational words but here they only distract.  The last dozen pages of the book contain a few articles about the earth and our responsibilities for the earth and they are excellent articles.This is a book to purchase and keep out in the open where you can open it to a random page and enjoy the beauty that it portrays.
