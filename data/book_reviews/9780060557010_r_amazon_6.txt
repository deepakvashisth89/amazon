Dancing in Red Shoes Will Kill You is an important novel by first time author, Dorian Cirrone.  Ms. Cirrone tackles the politics of female body image through the story of Kayla, a ballet dancer with size double D breasts who is heckled by classmates and encouraged to pursue plastic surgery by her dance instructor if she wants to get leading roles as a ballerina.  Another more subtle message in the book is females supporting other females in their success.Dancing in Red Shoes Will Kill You has a fantastic cast of characters--from Paterson, the feminist, to Joey's humor to Gray, Kayla's hot love interest--and a plot that will keep the reader turning to the next page and the next and the next...This is a must buy book for mothers and fathers who want to reinforce the importance of a positive self-image for their daughters.Read moreRead less
