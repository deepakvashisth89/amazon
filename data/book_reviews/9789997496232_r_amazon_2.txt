Part of the "Harry Palmer" series -- though the character is only "Harry Palmer" in the films -- by Len Deighton. Of them, this was the least convincing to me as a story. Deighton tries to bring the real world into the fictional one by having "Harry" -- or our MOOC(P) Op -- take on a fanatical right-wing militia funded by a billionaire whose goal is to force the USA and USSR into a conflict he expects the USA to win.
