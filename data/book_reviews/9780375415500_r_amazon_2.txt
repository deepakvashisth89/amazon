Just like the title of my review says, this book was incredible - it’s very well written and takes you on one hell of an emotional roller coaster ride. Just be aware that while some of those highs are really high and funny and heartwarming, some of those lows are really, really low. And dark. And brutal. And bleak.Another reviewer praised the book but felt like they’d been through a war after reading it…and I couldn’t agree more. This is like one of those award-winning movies (say, Million Dollar Baby?) where I can say without a doubt that it was an incredible experience, but one I’ll never put myself through again.
