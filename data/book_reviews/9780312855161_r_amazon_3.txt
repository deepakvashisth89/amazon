Greg Bear creates a very fascinating world, but he doesn't go anywhere with it. The main character's very vague mission is to study the planet and the society that develops there. All this takes place with the background plot of a weak love story and a civil war. I found the descriptions of the 'ecoi' very interesting and enjoy that kind of writing. It's too bad he couldn't think of a plot, other than the main character wandering around describing things, to go with it. A book I would read once and will never pick it up again.Read moreRead less
