While this story has a lot to like about it, I couldn’t give it four stars because there were too many moments where I felt jarred by the characters’ actions and choices.  To name a few: the female protagonists use profanity that seems too modern; the male protagonist’s English deteriorates over the course of the book instead of improves; the characters’ financial problems seem to be taken too cavalierly; the ending lacks sufficient foreshadowing to be satisfying (the reader needs to know more backstory about the deceased father); and the secondary romance seems corny.  The misadventures of the protagonist are entertaining in a tv western melodrama sort of way, so the book might be appealing to younger female readers (college-aged) who are looking for a brain break.
