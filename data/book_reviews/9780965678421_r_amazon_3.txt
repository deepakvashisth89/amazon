I was so impressed with Chris Bird's 5th edition that I didn't hesitate to give the 6th edition as a gift to my brother before he got his Concealed Handgun License (CHL). He told me he read it completely before taking the CHL class and could not put it down for its wealth of knowledge. It prepared him enough pass the CHL test (yes we have one in Texas) and gave him numerous insights into the real world aspects of being given a CHL. He does not keep many books but told me that this is a book he can go back to over and over to keep his firearms skills and knowledge sharp and up to date.Anyone thinking of carrying or even just having a firearm around the house my advice is to consider this book.Chapter Eleven alone is worth the price of the book. It describes Deadly Force: When to use it and What Happens When You Do. Most people think that the law will protect you in a justifiable shooting. Mr Bird gives examples and plenty reflections on what to expect and it's not what you expect. Firearms are good to have but without the knowledge of how to use them properly and lawfully you will have more trouble than you expect.Save yourself the grief and unpleasant surprises, check this book out.Read moreRead less
