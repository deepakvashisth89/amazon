Having never heard of Billy Haines before this book, I started it out of boredom. Within the first few pages I was hooked. The greatest writer alive couldn't imagine a story like this. Haines shines through the pages. William J Mann balances Haines career and personal live perfectly. Every important detail is there and not once did I find myself getting bored. Haines love affair with Jimmie Sheilds is described beautifully, and Mann doesn't intrude on their private lives. I can't say enough about this book. You don't have to be a fan of William Haines to be interested in this book. It's what every biography should be. I finished this book being sad it was over. I plan to read more of William J Mann's books. Amazing writer.Read moreRead less
