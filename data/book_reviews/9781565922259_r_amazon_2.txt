I recently ran into a problem where I had to process some *massive* text files (many 10+ GB text files with medical data)  and this book helped save me.  In my case, I had datasets where the structure was changing over the years so I needed to preprocess them before I could load it into either SAS or R.  I found partial solutions on the web but this book quickly got me up to speed on these ancient but wildly useful tools.  If you, like me, find part of a solution that uses sed or awk on the web and want to know what the heck it is actually doing grab this book.The introduction to all things UNIX is surprisingly gentle.
