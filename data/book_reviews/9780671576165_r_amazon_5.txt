This book is one that takes a bit of concentration to read, and you have to really pay attention to all the details. I thoroughly enjoyed reading it, even though there was quite a bit of violence in it. It is a battle between good & evil, that keeps you on the edge of your seat (or it did me). I didn't want to put it down when I started, but sleep won out more than once. I could have finished it in a day, but I let it take me 2 days so I could really take it in. If you don't believe that there is a battle between good & evil, then this book may truly open your eyes. It is written as fiction, but there is a spiritual battle going on around us daily; however, in the end good will win out, because love conquers hate, light conquers darkness & good conquers evil.Read moreRead less
