I adored "Soul Catcher," by Colin Kersey:  I couldn't put it down.  I was attracted to the book due to my interest in NWC Indian shamanism, especially soul catchers (shamanic magical medical instruments: abalone-inlaid tubes of bear femur employed to catch errant souls lost during dream travels, or suck-out evil spirits).  Kersey's American Indian details were well researched & a titillating framework for the story.  The shamanic scenes were satisfyingly well crafted, and the idea of a lost Haida colony quite compelling.  This book would make a great film!  Amazing for a first novel.Read moreRead less
