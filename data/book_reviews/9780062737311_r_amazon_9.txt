I bought this volume for my 82 year old mother who has always had an interest in oriental studies. I scanned it briefly before sending it to her and was fascinated by the descriptions for my own birth year and that of other relatives. I found the comments to reflect our natures very accurately and was looking forward to her reaction. She hasn't been able to put it down and keeps  it by her side at all times, for a quick glance. My mother is almost totally blind but loves to read, even though she cannot read for long she uses her optical aids while she still can. We both think that it is the best guide on this subject we have read, fascinating, candid and a joy to dip in to.I hope it stays in print and becomes revised again in the future so that it can bring as much pleasure to others as it has to us.Read moreRead less
