Star Trek lore has moved on since this was published in 1980, but this is a very entertaining read about the future history that leads to the Star Trek TOS setting. At the time, that, the cartoon, and one movie was all we had. And this book.The Chronology presents itself as a history book for Starfleet Cadets. It opens with 1957, has a timeline for each century or era of space exploration, and gives detailed information (and gorgeous illustrations) of landmark space ships. My personal favorite part are the "news articles" that cover important moments in space history - fictionalized first hand accounts or reporting about humanity's first alien contact, transporter development, the Romulan War, and much, much more.Parts of this have since been contradicted by subsequent movies and TV shows, parts haven't. Regardless, this is a wonderfully creative look into the historical lore of Star Trek, and any fan now burdened by too much pedantic insistence about what constitutes "real history" in Star Trek should enjoy it.Read moreRead less
