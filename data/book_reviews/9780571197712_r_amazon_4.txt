Wow. This is a disturbing book. It’s written and illustrated rather unevenly, with the occasional clever bit, although it’s very British, so if you’re very British yourself you might appreciate more clever bits than I did. I’m giving it three stars for being a book experience quite unlike any other I’ve read in as long as I can remember. It’s basically a dark, practically black, comedy that just sort of ends at one point. No payoff, no satisfaction, no moral, no reward for reading to the gory conclusion. It just is.Read moreRead less
