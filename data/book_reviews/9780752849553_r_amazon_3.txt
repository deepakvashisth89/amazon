This book about one woman and her family was engaging and entertaining on every page.  It is not a quick read.  Although it takes place in the nineteen fifties it refers back to both world wars of the twentieth century.  There are many characters and identifying and keeping up with them is the most difficult task in reading the book.  Nevertheless, I enjoyed it immensely and found it very hard to do my daily tasks rather than read more pages.  Without a doubt I will be looking for more books by this author.Read moreRead less
