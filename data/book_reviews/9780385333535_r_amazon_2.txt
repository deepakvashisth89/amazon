Lescroart is an excellent writer and knows well the court system of San Francisco. His main character is an defense atty named Dismas Hardy, who Lescroart decribes as an ordinary guy with high standards for the law. His adversary is the DA and her/his ass'ts who bend the law, even at time to the point of breaking it in their misguided attempts to convict the wrong person. But Hardy's best friend is a homicide detective, Abe Glitsky, half Jewish, who also happens to be the head of the homicide detail. At times, despite being on opposite sides, they cooperate to find the right perpetrator, while at others they are at odds.
