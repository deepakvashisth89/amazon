I first read "Jane" in the late '70s, and it did as much as Jane Austen's heroines to shape the path I took in life. I loved Dee Wells' wry humor, her distinctive characterizations, and the colorful, noisy, cluttered, and hopefully joyous life Jane lived. I always wondered why Dee Wells never wrote another novel, as she was so clearly suited to the art form and had such an original voice. But oh well, I guess it's true what they say: we all have at least ONE book in us. I laughed, I cried, I followed Jane's lead into creating for myself a life filled with dramatic twists, turns, ups and downs.
