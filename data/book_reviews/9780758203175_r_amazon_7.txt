I found this book to be totally rivetting.  Although it got a bit wordy at times, I followed the storyline throughout the novel.  I could not put the book down and read the whole thing within 24 hours.  This was 4 years ago.I just reread the book this past week and found it even more enjoyable.  Details,thoughts and some fellings escaped me the first time around.  I REALLY couldn't put it down!  This time, I actually visualized every character and situation.  A screenplay of this would probably ruin it for me.  Everything has become so vivid.  I only wish that I could picture "Winston" in his later years.I compare andy Schell's writing talent to that of another favorite writer - David Sadaris.  They both have a wit and a way with words that enwrap you in the moment or situation, without you realizing that they are doing it.My hat's off to Andy Schell for taking this brave move and look forward to MANY more books!Read moreRead less
