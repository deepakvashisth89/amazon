I wasnt quite sure what to expect when I read the blurb on the back of this book, but that was a GOOD thing. This book is catagorized as "Time Travel", but it mixes elements of a "Ghost" story and more. Its hard to explain without giving the story away. And THAT is what makes it so interesting! I could hardly put it down because I wanted to figure of what would happen next. So if you like a dash of mystery, a little lovin', hints of a ghost, and just a little time travel thrown in to bring it all together, you'll like this book. Because it gives you  enough of all those things to make things unpredictable. And that's just what I like in a book : )P.S.- If I could I would've given this book 4 1/2 stars.Read moreRead less
