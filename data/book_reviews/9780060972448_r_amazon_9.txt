This excellent collection of short stories (no novels) was put together in 1967 by old "P G " himself.It includes all 34 of the Jeeves stories, most of which were featured in the remarkable TV series JEEVES AND WOOSTER. Contrary to what it says in this Website, there are no novels in it! Only short stories... But what short stories!The TV series makes you smile, not laugh out loud, but when you remember some of the scenes, you crack up... The stories in this book are funnier and chock full of verbal fireworks, the likes of which, according to Stephen Fry who played Jeeves on TV, make them very popular where they are needed the most -- in hospitals and prisons!An ideal place to start if you are new to Wodehouse, and certainly a keeper!
