Jane Urquhart published, “The Underpainter”, in 1997, and I am just now getting to it via an enthusiastic recommendation from a reading/reviewing friend. I’m so glad I did, it’s an arresting and heart-wrenching story.First-person narrator painter Austin Fraser, now 83 years old, has been pursued by memories of his past lately, and these he shares with the reader.In examining his memories of the past, Austin comes to realize that he has always been a bit of a jerk: “A man in love with the stifling order he had imposed upon his own life.” The story deals with several of the people who were important in his life, and who he never really knew, because Austin was a “bad friend” and he is now realizing this. I was compelled to keep reading to find out what his final conclusions would be.This is the second Jane Urquhart novel I’ve read recently, the other being “The Stone Carvers”, which I loved. She has a true talent for writing about artists and their experience, and for spinning a fascinating plot filled with memorable characters.Read moreRead less
