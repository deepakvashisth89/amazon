This is the first book that I've read from Marianne Curley and I cannot say that it is a spectacular book but it's good enough that I find myself searching for more of her works. It's a story of a witch who grew up with magic, met a guy who she is certain has powers too but being clumsy and with no much of a self confidence he doesnt believe her. But soon they discovered that a curse is laid on his family and he must learn to accept his power or the curse may destroy him and all that surrounds him.Read moreRead less
