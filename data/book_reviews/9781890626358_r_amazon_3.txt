This may be of interest to those more interested in cultural analysis than philosophy. I purchased this book to use in a class on contemporary pop cultural manifestations of nihilism I am teaching. Unfortunately, I don't think I'll be using much of the book. The introductory historical work is broad and helpful for those without a philosophy background, if a bit too quick and superficial to do full justice to the complicated roots of nihilism. The chapters and analysis, however, are sketchy and ungrounded. Theorists are introduced in a page or two, and then "applied" to the examples in ways that seem to pick and choose what the author deems relevant aspects of the work he draws from arbitrarily. This work also seems to attempt to lay out the author's own theory of a typology and/or genealogy of nihilism, which is interesting, but isn't explicated in enough detail to be supported by the examples and analysis he provides.Read moreRead less
