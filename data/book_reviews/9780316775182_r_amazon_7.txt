The story of Purim is a Jewish holiday and celebration based on Queen Esther who saved her people in the Bible.From Wikipedia: Purim (Hebrew: About this sound פּוּרִים (help·info) Pûrîm "lots", from the word pur, related to Akkadian pūru).Purim is celebrated annually according to the Hebrew calendar on the 14th day of the Hebrew month of Adar (Adar II in leap years), the day following the victory of the Jews over their enemies.
