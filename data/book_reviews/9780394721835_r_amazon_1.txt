Many profound insights into the impact of wounding fathers of females. Wonderful job, really! Nice section explaining how our society has become more Amazon-like in some respects as a result of so much wounding for so many, but also how that development has had many constructive aspects and benefits for society. Author does excellent job of pointing out the dilemmas various types of wounded daughters often face in their lives and where their personal development/fulfillment/happiness can be stalled or stuck or move forward depending upon what is done by them in a situation. Author does seem to feel that marrying and having children brings the ultimate fulfillment for women--a stance some in our society feel constitutes a bias or personal prejudice on the part of the author.That is to say that there seems to be a larger perhaps stereotypical idea of womanhood alluded to in various areas of her book. Regardless of whatever one's own vision of mature womanhood looks like, you might find this book to be yet another powerful reminder of just how much all of us are shaped by our parents or even by a lack of one or both of them.Read moreRead less
