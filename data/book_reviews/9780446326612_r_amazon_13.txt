It's hard to come up with an original style in crime fiction, that's for sure.  Lochte seems to have done it---by combining TWO styles (from two narrators, of course).  And they're complimentary and engaging.  I really enjoyed the interesting experience of having the same (or closely related) events, incidences, or plot turns described by two separate participants, one a prematurely mature (precocious, if she were younger) young teen and one a hard-boiled, mildly world-weary shamus.Yes, I really enjoyed it and immediately bought another in the series.Read moreRead less
