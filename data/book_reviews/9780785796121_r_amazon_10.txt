Amazon threw this in front of me to review having just purchased the Kindle copy to go with my multiple paper versions. How do I possibly review a book like the Halloween Tree? It's a bit like asking a mere mortal to review Dickens or Joyce - it's writing at that sort of level. It would be presumptuous to even attempt it. So I'll go with the purely personal and just say that this book has become as much a part of Halloween as pumpkins or trick-or-treat. I re-read it every October alongside Zelazny's Night in the Lonesome October. Neither ever get old; every year I find something new, either in the story on in my reading of it. I know some find the prose flowery or even difficult, but to me, it's magical - perfectly suited to the content, necessary even. October wouldn't be the same without it.Read moreRead less
