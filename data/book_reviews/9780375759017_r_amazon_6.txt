An excellent book.  George Eliot writes beautifully.  Her characters are so well formed and individual.  The story flows well and I was always drawn to read "just one more chapter".  I finished it off over a long breakfast.  Eliot drops little hints for attentive readers so the plot turns are not surprising but they develop in a way that makes you feel as if you are in the community and know the poeple so well that you have a feeling for how things are likely to turn out.  I shall be seeking out more of her novels in the future.Read moreRead less
