This finishes up S.D. Perry's mini-series focusing on the Siskos. It wraps up loose ends, but it's a bit awkward, a bit shoe-horned together. Honestly, I think there is a little too much going on, which is making the plot of the relaunch too slow. I like it, but they need to wrap up loose ends.
