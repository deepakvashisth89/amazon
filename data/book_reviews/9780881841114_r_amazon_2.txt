I enjoyed the book. The only thing new to add to the other reviews is what I think is a flaw in the reconstruction of the crime. It mentions a couple of times the finding of the piece of "green chiffon fabric" in Madame Dauvray's car. The first is when they locate the missing car. But in Marthe Gobin's recounting, she observes Celia exiting a "carriage" when her dress is caught in the door. I didn't make the connection until the recounting of the crime when it states that somewhere on the way to Geneva the car slowed and Celia and Adele transferred from the car to a horse drawn landau. So the car never actually took Celia to the house in Geneva. So if Mathe Godin saw her catch the train of her dress exiting at the house in Geneva, how did the piece of green chiffon end up in the car for Hanaud to discover when they found the car?
