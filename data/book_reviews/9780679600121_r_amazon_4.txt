What an amazing, incredible book!  So many movie versions...the play...the movie of the play...you think you know the story and what Victor Hugo was telling us about our humanity, but nothing compares to the book itself.Every single page hold truths that are so beautifully written that reading this book is like mining for diamonds.The scope of this one man's understanding of life, love, religion, politics and everything else in between, is so wide and deep, he makes understanding the homeless little boy living by his wits in the streets of Paris as relevant as understanding the French Revolution; and makes understanding the causes of  all revolutions not nearly as important as understanding the joys and pain of a first love.  The details of the story he tells become the story, so that the main characters become only vessels carrying those details for our benefit...certainly not theirs!  They suffer so we might learn.All else that you know of this story is merely a synopsis of the characters.  This book is limitless in scope and timeless.Read moreRead less
