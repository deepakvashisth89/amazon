I first read this book about 5 years ago.  I recommended it to all my friends.  Just lately I came across it again and decided to read it again.  It will continue to be one of my favorites.  I try to imagine what my life would have been like if I had lived in Cuba and lived the life Orestes Lorenzo lived.  I can't imagine being so terribly misled by my government and taught to believe all the bad things about the USA.  But once he comes here - I find his task of bringing his family here just too unbelievable. It sounds like fiction, but I have read reviews by newspapers and know it must be true!I only hope I would be as brave to save my family from a government like that.Read moreRead less
