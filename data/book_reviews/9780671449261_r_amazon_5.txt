This is one of the best Dick Francis novels I have read.  The story takes the reader through many emotions: anger, sympathy, frustration, hope that doesn't turn out, hope that does turn out, thrill, vindication, satisfaction, and with a nice romance over the course of the book. Kelly Hughes, the jockey, has been accused of throwing a race and comes into the Stewart's enquiry without worry. A series of falsehood testimonies leaves him and his trainer, banned indefinitely. The book is about he goes about determing who the real villain is. As usual with Dick Francis heroes, he gets beat up and otherwise injured several times before he brings the villain to Justice. Everything about this book is engaging. I rate this book 9.5 of 10.Read moreRead less
