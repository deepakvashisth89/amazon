Hazzard, a contemporary novelist and friend of Greene's, describes one of his vacation spots where he spent much time writing and socializing with friends. Hazzard describes the island, its history going back to Roman times, and, more recently, the European and for a large part German artists' colony in Capri since the 19th century. Her description of Greene is more anecdotal than about his life and work as a writer. Many of the habitues of the island and its cafes are well known, some of them movie stars, and the villas the rich and famous have built for themselves are architectural wonders.
