Mr. White is a very moving writer who draws you in immediately. But I must say I have NEVER been so irritated with a main character---Stuart---as I was while reading this! His relationship with his wife was so patronizing and allowing her to smoke around the baby was unconscionable!! And Bobbie!! Their relationship was unbelievable to me...where was the love and passion? I was really amazed that they got married eventually. He was such a milquetoast in many ways...always apologizing! I was screaming at the pages and nearly put it down. I didn't get the blood work stuff either. But...aside from all of that, it was a good read and had a satisfying ending.Read moreRead less
