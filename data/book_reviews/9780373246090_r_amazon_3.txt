This is book #3 in Sherryl Woods Perfect Destinies series.  Here we learn more about Ben who has pretty much made himself a recluse. To keep himself busy he is an artist.  His aunt, Destiny, considers his art so good she would like him to have a show.Destiny manages to get Kathleen, a gallery owner, together with Ben.  She tries to convince Ben to let her show his paintings.  He is adamant that he will never show his art work.  He paints for pleasure and gets much enjoyment out of it.  He feels if he were to have a show a critic might not like his work and have neagtive things to say about it.  That would ruin it for him and he will not let that happen.Kathleen is determined and keeps showing up to Ben's studio.  Destiny did tell Kathleen that Ben has a sweet tooth. Because of that every time she visits she makes sure to have some sort of goodie.  Soon they both start having feelings.  Ben had a very bad exerience with a past love and he is wracked with guilt over it.  He is determined to never open his heart again.  Kathleen had a bad marriage that left her with very low self esteem.Now you have to read the book to see where this goes.Read moreRead less
