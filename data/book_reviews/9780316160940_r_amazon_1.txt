I found "By Silence Betrayed" to be an outstanding work on the problem of child abuse. Crewdson states in the introduction that he is stumped as to what the solutions might be but believes the problem should be outlined for the general public none the less. I think the solution is awareness and Crewdson does make a compelling case for child abuse being a secret problem of massive proportions. In several chapters he details several of the daycare abuse cases of the 80s and covers all angles of the cases and the pitfalls encountered by the prosecution. Crewdson takes a very measured and balanced approach in explaining the daycare cases. His treatment of the daycare angle alone is woth the price of the book. Child abuse is a unique and, by its nature, secret problem that most of us would prefer to ignore.
