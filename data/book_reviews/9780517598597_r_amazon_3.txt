I read the book and really put a lot of effort into it. Being a highly nearsighted person, -11.5 LE and -10 RE, i spent 3 months strugling without the use of any glasses neither contact lenses and i was able to reduce my condition on -2.5 on each eye, so now i use glasses with a reduced prescription.During the process i was able to get my eyesight not blurry for closer and still blurry for farther. The truth is that for a nearsighted person as i am, it is very hard to function without any prescription glasses or contact lenses. It was a daily strugle that i decided to end due to not much improvement.The reason that i am writing this review and why i am giving 4 stars on this book  is  to help young children or people with lesser prescription than me to try this method, because i believe that it is possible to lose your glasses for good or perhaps never use a pair of glasses! THE MOST IMPORTANT PART OF ALL IS THAT YOU WILL HAVE TO STOP USING YOUR GLASSES IN ORDER TO SEE ANY BENEFITS.I also had impressive moments of great view when my eyes were overly relaxed, but those moments didn't last for long. During those moments i could understand that our brain sees it not our eyes, the perfect "eyesight" is in there at all moments! Another important thing to consider is that after seeing this improvements i try to take my glasses off as often as i can in order to help on my eyesight. During my experiement i also noticed that if you put your glasses on even for a short period of time, it is enough to get your vision blurry in no time, so stop using glasses or contacts if you want to see any improvements.
