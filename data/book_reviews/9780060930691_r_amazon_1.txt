Bly may be better known among many as founder and leader of the so-called men's movement, but he is first and foremost a great poet.  This is an excellent retrospective of his work over the years - work that remains quite consistent in style and quality across the decades.  I was especially moved by the poems that dealt with the death of his father, and the long poem about Vietnam.  The latter was written at the height of that war, and really brought back the sense of intense anger and emotion that gripped the country in those days.
