This was a great sourcebook for storytellers back in the day. This book expanded on the history of the Sabbat, introducing a couple new bloodlines in the process. The Ahrimanes are an all-female offshoot of the Gangrel that practice an animistic Discipline that lets them deal with nature spirits. The other bloodline introduced is the Kiasyd, a twisted limb of the Lasombra family tree that is infused with fairie blood.The Path of Evil Revelations gets an in-depth treatment, and Dark Thaumaturgy is released on the world. I particularly liked the "witch's marks" you could get that gave your villain nifty little perks, but they were dropped in revised rules.A lot of the mechanical stuff in this book was made obsolete by the revised rules, but, it's still a great read, and it's always fun to watch the evolution of a game setting.Read moreRead less
