Magdalen Nabb wrote detective stories that are of their place - Florence. Her hero, Marshall Guarnaccia of the carabiniere, doesn't think much of himself, he thinks he just bumbles around, and occasionally gets lucky. His boss Captain Maestrangelo thinks otherwise but the Marshall would never believe it if he said so, so he keeps his counsel.This is the first of the series which introduces the Marshall. Not much happens I guess, but it's atmospheric, almost filmic; beautifully written, with Florence as a main character. The writing just wraps itself around you like a warm blanket.I first read it maybe 20 years ago; have just re-read it and it's as wonderful now as I rememberRead moreRead less
