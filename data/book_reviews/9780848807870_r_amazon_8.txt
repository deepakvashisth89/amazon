I enjoy reading old children's books. I had thought the Wizard of Oz books would be too cloying, but they are funny, fun, inventive and sweetly old fashioned. Entertaining, which was L. Frank Baum's intent.The 14 books in the Oz series by Baum are certainly not meant to be enjoyed by adults, but if you are in the mood, they are just fine. For your kids, I doubt they would think they were cool, but they are a lovely alternative to modern children's books that parents may find too mean, serious, violent or petty.One interesting thing, even though these books are written at the turn of the century, their heroes are often girls. They are not the rock-em, sock-em girl heroes we have in modern books, but they are respected by the other characters, who are of course as silly as they are. Some will think the author is condescending to girls. I was waiting for that issue to come up and found the author surprisingly fair.I have read 5 of the books in sequence and look forward to reading the rest.
