This book was a page turner. I read it almost nonstop. It was heartwarming and sad. Alex and Mary Rose had quite the war romance adventure during the 1715 Jacobite rising in Scotland. Alex a multi-generational Duke and Laird of the clan. Mary  a British aristocrat blending two different cultures together with a ready made family during a war which the upheaval of present German-born  king and exiled Scottish king presented that was a double edged sword indeed. She had grit and determination. He had honor and was a born Laird of a warrior clan. Both were fierce in their own ways.
