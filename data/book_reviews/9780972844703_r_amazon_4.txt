For those new to the Rosary this book is a wonderful collection of stories related to the power of the recitation of the Rosary. Although the Rosary is primarily a Catholic tradition, this book covers Christians from other denominations who have had miracles and prayers answered by this devotion. Easy to read, short stories, and each one has its own powerful message. It's the kind of book you can open to any page and find a short testimonial to the prayers.Read moreRead less
