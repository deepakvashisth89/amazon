Okay, this book started out fine.  The development of the relationship between Elizabeth and Ramiel was intriguing and bristling with sexual tension.  Then the story takes an rather odd turn involving the husband, the mother and the children.  With a smattering of mystery on the part of one of Ramiel's staff.  I am still scratching my head over that one.  The ending was lovely, though.  Glad I read it but this will not be a re-read.Read moreRead less
