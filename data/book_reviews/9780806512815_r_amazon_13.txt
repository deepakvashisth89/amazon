Johnny had a gun and went to war with it at the warmongers’ sayso, he and other young men like him. Now he is forever stuck in a No Man’s Land between life and death, neither one nor the other, in painful mental isolation. And they need to keep him there, else young men would refuse to fight. Exquisitely written, this book exposes the deception, futility and personal agony of war as no other book I’ve read has done. No wonder it is a classic. A must-read.Read moreRead less
