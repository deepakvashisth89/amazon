In The Visitors, Simak shows us, step by step, what could happen when an alien race comes to earth (the United States, specifically) and doesn’t try to kill or enslave everyone, but instead simply settles in and does its business. It’s a very benign invasion, but with great consequences, and we see it through the eyes of a few reporters, a few government officials, and a scientist or two. We never actually hear, directly at least, from the aliens themselves. It’s not an action-packed thriller, but a rather talky and thoughtful examination of the real world reaction to a bunch of aliens suddenly coming to our world and just kind of being here. I found it fascinating and very realistic (keeping in mind it’s set in 1980 or so, when it was published), but if you’re looking for an “Independence Day”-type man vs. alien novel, this is not it.
