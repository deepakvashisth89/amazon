A caper full of laugh aloud moments and sharp comical observations on bureaucracy. There are touches of Joseph Heller and Douglas Adams in this novel, and it is simply a delight to read. So very different from the other Perry novels I've read, it's a glorious romp and a major takedown with a twist of espionage all wrapped in a caper that is almost accidental.The writing is stunningly deft. Not once did I get irritated by the plot or characters. Though, I do think Perry has used his turkey hunting metaphor more than once, but I'm not sure. Maybe everyone knows it but me! In any event it's lovely to read real craftsmanship in a "thriller." Even better when it makes me laugh.Read moreRead less
