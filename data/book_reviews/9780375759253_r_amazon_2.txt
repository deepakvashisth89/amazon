This book, which takes place in the 1920s, could just as easily been set in the 2020s. It follows an American businessman as he slowly diverges from the life of a stiff-collared socially constrained white male. Leaving his family behind, he begins  hanging with a crowd of ne’er do wells, sleeping with an emotionally needy woman and drinking himself into a stupor every night.
