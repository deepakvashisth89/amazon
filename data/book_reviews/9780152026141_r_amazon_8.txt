This book did a wonderful job of showing how the animals of the rainforest depend on each other and the resources of the rainforest to live. I love how the book left you thinking about the man in the story and his decision not to cut down the tree. I used this in my classroom as a resource to show how/why the rainforest is endangered. The pictures in this book are great and really helped my students visualize what the rainforest looks like.
