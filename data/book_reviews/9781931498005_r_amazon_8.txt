In Julia Alvrezes "A CAFECITO STORY"  the main character  was a man by the name of Joe. Joe was a farmers son who grew up in Nebraska for all of his childhood, his dream was to follow in his fathers footsteps and become a farmer.  As Joe grew older his father had to sell the farm and his land to pay bills so Joe had to find another job.  He became a school teacher married a city girl and moved to Omaha.  Unfortunately his marriage didn't last long and he began to feel lost.  One Christmas Joe decided that he was going to go on a vacation to get away from his everyday life and hopefully find himself, so he took off  to the Dominican Republic.  While there he was having a cup of coffee one morning and while there  a women approached him and asked if he would like the stains on his coffe cup read to predict his future,  they said that he would start a new life in the mountains.  Joe decides to go to the mountains for the weekend and meets a family who owns a coffee farm.  While there Joe stays with the family and learns about the struggles that they go through everyday and how they are eventually going to have to give up there life as they know it which is growing organic coffee and sell the farm to an industry that will make large amounts of coffee in a smaller time which will make more money and the only way that they can prevent this from happening is if someone buys land and helps the family out.  Joe decides that this is a great idea.  He soon buys land in the mountains and him and the family grow coffee the way that family has been for years and avoid being taken over by commercial business.  Joe finally finds his passion.In my opinion this is a good book.  I think that it shows the true meaning of happiness.  I also think that it gives the message that you have to follow your dreams and not be afraid of change.  Joe left his home, job, and friends to go somewhere totally new and start up a new life.
