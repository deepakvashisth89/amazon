I have multiple copies of Paradise Lost but during a recent move, they got packed away in one of what seems like a million boxes. Since I read Milton every day, I cannot do without a copy. This one is lovely: bound beautifully, sturdy with normal (not “onion skin”) paper. And I believe that everyone can learn to love Milton. He had me from the start, when I read a quote years ago that says the mind is its own place—it can make a hell of heaven and a heaven of hell. Reading a good Milton biography is also a great assistance for appreciating it. But he should be read the way one reads the Bible—a little each day followed by some contemplative thought.
