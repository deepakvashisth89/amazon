The best anthology of modern French poets. These are post-Rimbaud poets like the surrealist poets Andre Breton, Paul Eluard, and Robert Desnos, among many other directly or peripherally related to surrealism. Wallace Fowlie is THE Master translator of these French poets a peer only to Mary Ann Caws.
