Curiously enough, Patricia Highsmith considered this to be one of her least successful novels but I think that it one of her more accessible works for the reader who is approaching her for the first time.The story centers around three, on the surface, of the most prosaic characters you could meet: Robert, a lonely man going through a divorce, Jenny a nondescript working girl and Greg her betrothed or so he thinks. How these three manage to evolve into a mystery concerning attempted murder, lust, obsession, voyeurism, is a testament to Highsmith's craft.This book like all of her work must be read with particular attention (miss a key sentence and the paragraph loses its depth).
