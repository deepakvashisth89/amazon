This book was advertised as an action/adventure/thriller located in the wildest parts of the US near Yellowstone Park in areas that are seldom accessed by the public.  The book was also about a second string cop, a subway cop because he had a handicap that limited his access to the top of his profession. It was also advertised as a novel about politics, a New York governor who had Presidential ambitions and hinted that white extremists may become the bad guys were behind the diasters that followed. The governors anti-gun political positions where an explanation, at least until the last trite ending.  The book seemed right up my alley. In a short time however, I felt I was reading the script from one of those half hour serials. Each week, the hero, sometime with his heroine is faced with the most incredible life threatning situations that nobody could excape.  But next week, miraculously, with the help of some added footage which explains just how the hero manages to excape for the next 30 minutes just in time to face another life threatning problem which you had to wait a week.  At least this book doesn't require waiting.  The hero survives for another few pages before being faced with another  disaster.I could point out several pages which went beyond belief, thereby creating a book that some may find entertaining like reading about a serial killer. No plot, just blood.  This book is not that bad, but as someone who has spent time in this neck of the woods at the request of the USAF's survival school about the same time of year this adventure takes place.  All the characters are unclimatized to the altitudes and all are city folk, and most of the characters are not young. Without sleep, food or mountain experience, no human could survive even a few minutes in a mountain stream with rapids and large enough to float a raft. That amounts to being swallowed by an alligator and cutting a hole in its belly with your pocket knife to exit from that disaster.And to add the part about making love in a tent, in a snow storm seemed physically impossible if not foolish. Love making in such a situation as romantic as its sounds, is waste of calories necessary to live. And what kind of people are the  hero and heroine to psychologically make love while their comrades are falling right and left. In my personal experience the sudden death of one person, even a stranger is enough to make the majority of normal people puke, not make love. Not my idea of people I would want to kike in the high country.If you like action, blood and gore and do not need a lot of realism or plot; this book does provide that. The author provides just enough descriptions that I found realistic when describing the mountains and setting. And I heard an interview of the authur who stated that if he had any intention of sending a message, it was that nature in those settings that still remain, like the Rockies, and the Everglades where he was living when he wrote the book are beautiful but dangerous.
