I really enjoyed this book and couldn't wait to get back to it when I was forced to set it aside. The book starts with Robin returning to the home of his brother, The Marquess of Wolverton, and he's carrying the weight of the world on his shoulders. His spying career is at an end, the woman he thought he loved married another, he had unresolved issues from his childhood and is burdened by guilt over many of his actions as a spy. He's an angelically beautiful mess! When Maxima, a half-Mohawk pocket Venus, stumbles upon Robin in the woods while running away to London, Robin's in desperate need of exactly the diversion she's offering.This is an adventure/travel story, which is one of my favorite story-lines. Robin is super playful, loves to tease and is a total chameleon due to his spying career. Maxima is beautiful, barely five-feet tall, competent, pragmatic and determined to get to London to discover what happened to her father. Robin honorably tags along with her out of a sense of duty to provide an escort, but mostly he's intrigued by her. Their developing relationship during their journey is so well plotted and described that it was like traveling along with them.Maxima's Aunt Desdemona sets off in search of her run-away niece and ends up confronting Wolverton about Robin, accusing him of nefarious intentions. Wolverton also sets off in pursuit of the run-aways to ensure his brother isn't falsely accused of wrong doing by Desdemona. Since they are both on the same trail, Wolverton and Desdemona keep crossing paths and we end up with a bonus, second romance which is very sweet! Highly recommend!Read moreRead less
