The teacher in my continuing ed class gave us handouts of vocabulary aids: pages with postage stamp-sized drawings of things to learn. It's much easier to remember the word for "automobile" or "book" when you can visualize the pictures. We didn't use a textbook, so I had to look for the source from the heading on the picture pages. This book is quite different from others in that it provides detailed discussion of pronunciation and shows exactly how to write the Cyrillic letters in cursive. It also uses cartoon-style illustrations for building vocabulary and conjugation skills. There are conversations to translate and connect with real-life situations. The author is very creative in his teaching style.
