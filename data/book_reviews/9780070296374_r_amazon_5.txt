I acquired a Fourth Edition at a used book sale at a public library. In my opinion, the book was very interesting and a quick way to get a survey history of Medieval Europe, e.g. the essential themes of Reason and Revelation and the temporal power struggle between the Papacy and Monarchy. I will consider looking at the updated edition now.Invaluable for the interested reader are the “Suggested Readings” most with his brief evaluation of the listed work at the end of each of the major three parts Early, High, and Late Middle Ages; the author suggests General Works, specific topics, and primary sources.One is amazed at the high price of college texts in current editions. It appears that colleges and professors are held hostage to someone at those rates when students could obtain editions of primary sources for less, yet regrettably must “use” a current edition.
