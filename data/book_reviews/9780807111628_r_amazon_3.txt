For students of the Civil War, the conflict in Missouri has always been a bit confusing. The state itself was a patchwork of loyalties that literally split families, towns, counties and regions. Dr Brownlee is somehow able to sort through the complex political fabric of a state at once garrisoned but also protected by the Federal troops. At the same time while hosting the horrors of the war as prosecuted in the border zone between Kansas and Missouri the nature of warfare changed and evolved in ways still recognizable in today's conflicts around the world.
