I stumbled on this author by chance, but it was such luck.  These books are well written and edited.  Traditional Recency romances, quick reads that will leave you with a smile on your face.  I've read two so far, the heroine in both is a plain but unusual woman, the hero a Lord who needs to learn to unbend.  The path to romance is resolved to a happily ever after that is wrapped up tickly and competently.  Well worth reading.Read moreRead less
