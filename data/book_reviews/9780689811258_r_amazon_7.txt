The author did an excellent job of getting into the psychology and methodology of cults and cult recruitment. Dorry was the perfect mark for a group like Fishers and by preying on her loneliness and insecurity, they were able to swallow her whole. What the author didn't do, was get into what it takes to deprogram someone from a cult. It's not always as easy as saying "I quit" and walking away.This book is a fast read and it introduces YA readers to a segment of society that's rarely in the mainstream or discussed in popular culture. The writing is decent. It still just felt like the reader is still getting into the groove of Dorry's story when it comes to a swift end, hence the three stars.Read moreRead less
