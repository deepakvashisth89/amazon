I don't consider myself a "gifted" reader, but I got through this book in about 7 minutes! I'm not kidding, it was that easy! I don't really understand what was happening with the plot, but it all seemed believeable. Except the part about flying. I don't think that is true. Also, I think that telling kids that "It's the same big world for all of us" is kind of cruel. Obviously some folks get a better chance than others at succeeding, but I'm not bitter.
