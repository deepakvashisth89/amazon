This book is great for introducing the concept of subtracting money! Throughout the book Alexander spends his money on gum, renting a friends' snake for an hour, and placing bets against his brothers. Each event costs Alexander some money and by the end of the book he is left with bus tokens. This book is a double-threat because it uses a non-conventional approach to introduce money and it will get students hooked on reading! It's a great buy!Read moreRead less
