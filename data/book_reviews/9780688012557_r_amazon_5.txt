This is a beautiful story of a family growing in spite of themselves.  The loss of wife and mother, Jane throws them into a frozen state. Why do they have to go on?  How can they go on?  The thaw comes slowly and with the help of neighbors and work but mostly due to the love of an ad executive who had given up on marriage and children.  Ruth takes Jane's family into  her heart and soul and the story of healing begins.  RKSmith does a masterful job relaying the emotions of the children, Hailey and Bobby and the deep feelings of Paul and Ruth. Was Jane the perfect partner, neighbor, mother everyone remembered?  Where was their value now that she was gone?  How would they fit into the world without her guidance and direction?  Could anyone fill her shoes?  The humor is lifesaving as they struggle to unite as a new family but the issues of each one's happiness being "allowed" stops them from moving forward. This is a roller coaster ride for everyone.  One minute they are safely grounded in their memories and traditions and the next they are lifted into an unknown quaking space without a clue as to when the ride will end.
