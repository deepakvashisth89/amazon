Schnitzler's "Dream Story" is a wonderful little novella, whose surreal exploration of deceit, paranoia, and libidinal suppression will intrigue many.  By the end of the story, readers must confront themselves with the question, how real are the very people around us?Fans of the movie "Eyes Wide Shut" will notice a notable correspondence between the film's script and this translation of the novella, not only scene-by-scene but often word-for-word, as well."Dream Story" is thoroughly engaging and easy to finish in a single or a few sittings.  I gave it 4/5 stars because this translation has a few noticeable typos, including blatant misspellings and even extra em dashes or other characters--something which, in as short a story as this, I find quite unacceptable.
