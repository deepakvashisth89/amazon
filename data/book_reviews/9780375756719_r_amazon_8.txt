I bought this book because I had already read Pointed Firs and wanted the rest of Jewett's stories. This is a great Penguin classic and is just the right size to tuck in my purse. The stories are beautifully-written vignettes of Maine life as experienced by Jewett, told through the eyes of wonderful characters that will stay with you forever.
