Brad Meltzer's writing always manages to make you feel like you're right at the scene experiencing the drama first hand. Part of it is due to the first person dialogue but more is the subtle location description with  clear detailed motion . This book is about  a White House legal counsel who becomes involved with the President's daughter and all the drama of complicated scheming by a "trusted" family friend who was given a White House appointment.
