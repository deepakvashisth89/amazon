Winesburg Ohio doesn’t really have a plot. It’s just a description of characters from the mythical Ohio town (even though there is an actual Winesburg Ohio).  At first it was hard to get into the book but the further I went the more engrossed I became. There is no conclusion to the book. It just stops. That was a bit jarring. Kind of felt like being in a car and the driver hit the brakes for no good reason. I don’t think I’ll ever read it again but I’m glad I read it.Read moreRead less
