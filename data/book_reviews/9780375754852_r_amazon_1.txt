A fun to read and very lengthy collection of stories that attach one part of history to the next. This big novel takes a thorough look at flight and space flight from the past century, the story about how things came to be and the big events between that time. The amount of detail put into this novel makes it a serious read as well. There are some pages dedicated to photographs. I thought it was written more like a narrative novel than a history book which I found to be an interesting approach.The paper is typical of novels, with exception of the two photograph sections which used a glossy white paper. The cover is paper with a toothy texture. The ink is black as it should while the font is small to fit so much into this book. The margins are narrow but all good. The spine appears well glued and the pages are folded into small segments to ensure the book will have a long life.
