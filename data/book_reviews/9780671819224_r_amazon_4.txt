The Commissaris and de Grier go to Japan, under cover, at the request of the Dutch Ambassador to Janan — to help investigate a Yakusa crime operation. It’s an unlikely thing for them to be doing, but it makes possible an exotic setting for this book.The story starts out in Amsterdam with a missing person case. A young Japanese man has disappeared, and his fiancée fears he’s been murdered. He worked for the Yakusa and tried to quit — a dangerous move.In Japan the Commissaris falls in love with the Japanese bath and becomes best buddies with a Yakusa boss for a few memorable hours. De Grier proves himself to be a most efficient Dutch samurai — and also finds himself freed of all earthly attachments. The adventure as a whole is replete with philosophical musings.Meanwhile Grijpstra stays in Amsterdam to solve the murder. While de Grier is enjoying the company of a shapely Yakusa hostess — Grijpstra suffers from the company of his ferocious flabby wife.I enjoyed this book though it seemed a bit far-fetched compared to most other books in the series. The action scenes are very good however. And there’s lots of Japanese ambiance.Read moreRead less
