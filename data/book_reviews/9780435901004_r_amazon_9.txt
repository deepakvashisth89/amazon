These stories by that superb master of the novel, Chinua Achebe, look at the lives of men, children, and women who rarely appear in his novels--young women exploited during the Biafra war, but immensely courageous and putting the army officers to shame; a child who takes revenge on her employers by feeding their baby red ink after they break their promise to send her to school; a man who has lost everything during the Civil War, but can still retain optimism. Every storu is compelling, and together they provide a more rounded sense of Nigerian society.Read moreRead less
