Love Margaret Moore books. I had only read four books in the "Warrior" series before and this one didn't disappoint at all.It was nice to see the very sexy Etienne DeGuerre make a brief appearance, prior to his marriage, as well as have other characters mentioned such as Fritha Kendrick and her husband Urien Fitzroy.In "The Norman's Heart" we meet Mina Chalcott, part Saxon, part Norman, who seems to have had very little love in her life since her mother died. Her brother Reginald seems to have been the only family member to have cared anything about her in recent years. But he is both vain and a bit of a poppinjay who seems to be concerned mostly with his looks and the latest fashions.Mina wants to take her rightful place in society by being both loved and respected, not just by the people around her in her new home, but by her husband also.Mina has her pride, and is headstrong, with all the fire and passion one would expect of a flame haired beauty, but she is also afraid of falling in love, (if such a thing even exists).She has moments of jealousy when she realises that the only woman her husband doesn't seem to be attentive to, is herself and she spends many hours alone when he is visiting his other estate.Sir Roger de Montmorency has all the stubborn, iron will one would expect of a Norman overlord. He demands loyalty and obedience from all those around him, including his new wife.Having been trained by Urien Fitzroy in his youth and having sworn fealty to the imposing Baron DeGuerre, Roger is used to both taking and giving orders as a good soldier/landowner should.He isn't always one to compromise, and he will not tolerate what he believes to be his wife's capricious behaviour with other men. He doesn't believe in love, as it something that only exists in songs and poetry.However he enters a battle of the sexes with his new wife Mina and learns that the pain of loving someone can be just as painful as a wound in battle.Read moreRead less
