‘The Crimson Pagoda’ is well-written and well-researched piece of historical fiction. Constance is just a teenager when she sets sail to China in 1890, joining her older missionary husband in a loveless marriage. Seeking distraction and passion, the young American woman finds herself embroiled in turbulent times and subject to the whims of a ruthless empress. Set in the middle of history-making events, this is a recommended read for anyone interested in a gripping and highly descriptive story.Read moreRead less
