I am Mexican, but I bought this as a present for a friend interested in Mexican cuisine as I thought it would use ingredients easy to find in UK. However, when I was writing a dedication for my friend I read some of the recipes and got really disappointed. I don't know where the author learned to cook, or if these recipes are good or not, but I can tell you that 80% of these recipes just barely resemble Mexican food. Maybe the "taste of..." in the title implies that the author is just giving her version of Mexican meals. BUT, if this is the case, then the back cover ("these 130 recipes remain true to the tasty culinary traditions of Mexico") is highly misleading: adding chilli, tabasco sauce or corn oil to a recipe does not make it "Mexican." Even very simple (and delicious) recipes like quesadillas, frijoles or refried beans are totally wrong. More than half of these recipes were totally unknown to me... They may be delicious, but I would not call them "Mexican" at all! I started writing corrections and annotations for my friend, but I'm on page 56 and I'm frankly tired.
