From the back cover:Take one very special baby cradle, carved long ago with love and care...and you get three romantic stories celebrating the joys of motherhood in one treasured collection.Jill Marie Landis, bestselling, award-winning author, tells the heartwarming tale of a mother-to-be who's lost her memory in CRADLE SONG.Debbie Macomber, bestselling auhtor, has written a touching story of a jilted bride longing for marriage and motherhood in ROCK-A-BYE BABY.Gina Ferris Wilkins, bestselling author, brings the three stories full circle as the heirloom cradle works its unexpected baby magic on a completely unsuspecting woman in BEGINNINGS.And my review:One thing that was good about this anthology was that there were only three stories in it, rather than the usual four or five, giving the authors more room to work. And two of the stories turned out quite well.The first story, CRADLE SONG, is a historical novella which starts off the theme of a special cradle that brings couples together. In it, the hero rescues a white captive from a Nez Perce camp. It's clear to him that the mistreated woman has lost her mind, or is on the verge of losing it. He's moved enough to trade just about everything of value that he has for her in order to free her.It was a promising premise, but this story fell flat in the execution. It was just starting to unfold nicely when the author had the couple rush into bed. Considering that the heroine was still acting insane (she hadn't even been able to tell the hero her name or even bathe herself), it really felt like the hero was taking advantage of an extremely vulnerable woman. Not very heroic. I lost interest in the story after that, as I felt that this "love" scene was only a small step removed from rape. One star.The second story, ROCK-A-BYE BABY, is a contemporary novella in the "cute" Debbie Macomber tradition. Her stories are always full of bright, off-beat characters and matchmaking parents and / or children. This story was no exception. I really like watching the free spirit heroine loosen up the stuffed shirt hero. I also liked how each found healing for disasterous past relationships by finding love with each other. The cradle also plays a central role in this story. Four stars.BEGINNINGS was also an enjoyable contemporary novella. It was a refreshing change for a romance novel to have the hero and heroine actually dating in a normal relationship, dealing with real-life relationship issues. Often, it seems as if romance authors will have their character interact in every which way except for dating! So reading about the ups and downs of a true-to-life relationship was enjoyable. The story of the cradle comes full-circle in this story (but I won't give it away here). Four stars.With most romance anthologies, you're lucky if you can find even one decent story. So finding two in this book is pretty good. While I wouldn't recommend shelling out a lot of money for this book, it's a pretty decent find if you come across it at a used-book store or a garage sale. Three stars overall.Read moreRead less
