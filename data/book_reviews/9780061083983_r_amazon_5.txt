First Dodd book I've read and definitely for a more sophisticated palate.  I loved it. It has been a VERY long time since I have read such an emotionally satisfying historical romance that wasn't filled with anachronisms and insta-sex/insta-love.  Dodd's writing is nuanced and pays attention to character development and historical accuracy seldom seen in historical romance authors. It is always hard to watch the heroine or hero make such epic mistakes and be forced to own up to them before any personal growth can happen. But that is what gives a book gravitas. Dodd manages to do this with realism and honesty. Identifying with character flaws is often what draws me to a book; however, I don't always have to like a hero or heroine to understand their journey. I find it marvelous that an author can be so versatile and capture so many different personalities, even the ones I love to hate.Read moreRead less
