They call Zlata the Anne Frank of Sarajevo for good reason. This is the story of the Siege of Sarajevo told through a child's eyes. The Siege of Sarajevo was the longest siege on a capital city in modern history and during the telling of this story Zlata goes from a child talking about shopping trips and birthday presents to a child worried about her and her family having the basic needs for survival met.I ready Zlata's Diary in preparation for a trip in which I visited Bosnia shortly after the war and not only is it a book that I will never forget, I think it is a book that gives us a unique insight into a horrible period of time in the history of the world.By the way I found out "the rest of the story" about this book from the Associated Press while I was in Sarajevo. During a lull in fighting while school was actually in session, the AP was asked to write a story about the war. Since nothing was going on that day the AP reported on a Diary contest going on at a local school. Zlata had the winning entry and the publicity created from that one news article prompted the book "Zylta's diary"Read moreRead less
