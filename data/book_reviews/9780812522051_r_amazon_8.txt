I loved the first half of this story, and it was well written throughout. I found the end to be predictable, but this book has been out since the 1970s and the story has undoubtedly influenced others written after it. It's a classic, obviously. I did find it scary, especially with the author's forward about experiencing misfortunes while writing thebook. That could just be goodmarketing, but the idea gave me the creeps as I read this book.
