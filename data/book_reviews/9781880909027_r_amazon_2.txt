This is a decent book, why has no one else bothered to write a review?  This story will make you miss California even if you've never lived there.  A likeable, independent woman, Anne,  finds her way through relationships  and career.  She manages to stay afloat despite the forces wanting to pull  her under.  The book is rather relationship-heavy, however.  I wanted to  enter more into the marine biology aspect of the novel.  I nodded knowingly  at Elliot's fence-sitting because I know a non-committal modern man just  like him.  So exasperating but so real.  Try this one.
