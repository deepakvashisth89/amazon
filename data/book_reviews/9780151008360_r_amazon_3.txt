Based on the customer reviews, especially the reference to Gatsby and The Big Chill, I purchased this book and read it. I was not as impressed as the previous readers. It was an easy, light summertime read....but I found most of the characters to be self-absorbed, immature and completely neurotic. I could see possibly hanging out with Susie, Kay, Dodge and maybe Billy....but Elise and Ron were irritating and mean spirited and I can't imagine most people could put up with them for one weekend, let alone a whole summer. That said, the writing was very evocative of the Hudson Valley and the easy feeling of warm summer weekends and Ms. Clements character development (regardless of whether you liked the characters or not) was excellent.Read moreRead less
