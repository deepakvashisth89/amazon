I hope this book picks up in popularity again now that Cher is making another comeback. I am a long devoted Cher fan. She isn't always my first choice for music or movies but I love who she is and what she stands for. That said, I knew this book would make Cher look bad but I thought I wouldn't believe it. However, after finishing it, I cannot believe I'm team Sonny when it comes to who was the bad guy in the relationship. The break up of this world famous couple is old news now and Cher seems to only speak fondly of Sonny these days, regardless, after reading this I feel like its clear that Cher couldn't understand her feelings or feel safe in expressing them due to a traumatic childhood -- not because of how Sonny treated her. She acts powerless and avoidant yet unwilling to advocate for herself and therefore expects Sonny to solely forge a career for her as a solo act and them as a duo to Rolling Stones levels of stardom. Sonny seems to have recognized that Cher was projecting her pain from her father's abandonment onto him. But since Cher couldn't see that in herself they could never properly communicate when things got rough between them. Sad to see. I still don't think Cher understands this. She announced today she's writing another autobiography, perhaps she will have a new understanding of her life at this point.
