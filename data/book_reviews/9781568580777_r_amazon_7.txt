Mr. Aczel is to be commended for a nice quick tour through mathematics and Fermat's Last Theorem; for a math book it is quite a page turner. Aczel's insight on the inner-world of world-class mathematicians is informative and entertaining---once again, for a math book a surprise. The publisher included diagrams and photos, and while a trade paperback, the quality was good enough to convey the story. If you're looking for a quick primer of math history, this book may be for you---as according to Aczel the roots of Fermat's Theorem has roots back to Pythagoras.
