A book set (unofficially) in the universe of Issac Asimov's original Foundation trilogy, this story explores the social and ethical repercussions of the "ideal" empire that would result from the ascension of the Second Foundation of mathematical psychohistorians to galactic power.  Following a young mathematician who was (to some extent) lobotomized, this book explores various topics familiar in science fiction, such as transhumanism, mental enhancement, personality, artificial intelligence, and more!  This is an excellent read for any fan of Asimov's universe, and, in my opinion, forms a more fitting conclusion to Seldon's plan than the official cannon.
