With her great knowledge of the peoples and history of China and love for them Pearl S. Buck tells the story of the woman who became the last empress of China.  She tells the story of the rise of a young woman called Orchid at home among her Manchu family who finally became Old Buddha to her people, because her prayers caused the rain to fall.The book makes clear distinction between Manchus and Chinese.  The Manchus had conquered the Chinese roughly two hundred years earlier and still ruled China during the time in which this book is set.  The emperor and the advisers were mostly Manchu although a few were Chinese.This is a fascinating look at a formidable woman and a pivotal time in Chinese history told from a Chinese perspective!
