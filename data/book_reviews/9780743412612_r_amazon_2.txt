I felt like I knew Mary Higgins Clark after reading her memoir, "Kitchen Privileges." Written with great warmth and humor, Mary shares her story, very much an American success story. While I expected to marvel at her phenomenal professional success as a writer, I was equally impressed by her incredible spirit and work ethic. A young widow, Mary returned to work full-time writing radio shows and wrote her fiction for two hours each morning before she woke her five children for school. Inspired by an acrtress friend, Mary even finds the time and energy to attend Fordham University to obtain a degree!
