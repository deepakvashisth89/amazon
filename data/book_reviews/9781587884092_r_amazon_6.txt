Luxury cars, gorgeous women, a power trip between men, and a sweltering hot southern October gives Iles' readers yet another intense page turner full of twists and turns.  He makes other thriller writers seem like amateurs by the way he is able to create such solid plots and deep characters, while creating a universe that you feel like you are a part of. Somehow Iles can create a story that feels like truth by weaving historical facts with action packed adventure.It is in this story that Penn and Caitlin meet for the first time (I had read The Devil's Punch Bowl and Turning Angel prior to this) and we are able to see Penn as a recent widower.  When he brings his daughter back to Natchez to regain some peace in their lives, he is instantly swept up in hot blooded warfare and bringing back a civil rights cold case from the 60s. As always, Iles goes a bit over the top (bullets are always flying, homes burning, criminals lurking at every corner), but face it -- this is what we love.  It is simply amazing that so much can happy in the little city of Natchez, Mississippi.As a female reader, I wanted to reach into the book and hit Penn over the head when it comes to his decisions regarding women in this particular novel, but you can't help but be on his side even when he is breaking the law by burying evidence or making back alley deals with murderers.  Why?  Because he is ultimately one of the "good guys" and everything he is fighting for revolves around justice, family, and community.Another job well done by this incredible writer.Read moreRead less
