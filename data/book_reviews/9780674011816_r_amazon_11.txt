This book is a wonderful narrative about the discovery of the complexities of bug life. Besides the high quality photos and illustrations, what makes this book a true marvel is the down-to-earth narrative of how discoveries were made, how ideas were born and facts established. If you are intrigued by these little creatures that surround us, you can't miss this one. If you are not, you risk becoming an adept.
