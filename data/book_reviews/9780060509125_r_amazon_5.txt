I enjoyed this book very much. It was interesting to look at the child's perspective of being raised in a Group Home. As I'm not a teenager, and this was most likely aimed at a teenage audience, I found it a little too light reading for me. Not much of a challenge, but well written, and interesting enough to finish it in just over a night. The ending may have been a little too 'sweet' for me too. Well worth it, if you're looking for a nice, light afternoon read, or for teenagers.Read moreRead less
