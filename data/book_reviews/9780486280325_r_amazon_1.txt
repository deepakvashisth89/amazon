I have purchased several of these Dover Children’s Thrift Classics books for my grandkids. They are a great value! These aren’t heirloom hardcover books so I don’t need to worry about them accidentally ripping a page or dog-earing the corners or spilling fruit punch on the cover. They’re just budget friendly, kid friendly books with simple illustrations that keep kids engaged in the stories.
