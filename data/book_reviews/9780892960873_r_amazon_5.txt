This is, by far, my favorite book from Eldredge yet. If you like his style, imagination and many anecdotal examples you won't be disappointed. I found this book to be a very good read. I am drawn back to several places in to relive the fresh encounters with Jesus. I read several chapters with my sons (ages 9-14) at bedtime and they were asking to go back and read parts over again because they were so powerful. I don't want to spoil any of those moments for you so I won't expand on them. I'll just say that there are some visual images painted by the author that will push you around emotionally.There is surprisingly little content about the cross in this book, which may seem strange in a book about Jesus. However John wants this book to be less about the works of Jesus and more about his personality. John has plenty of other books and audio on Jesus' works.Much of the later content of the book focuses on what John calls the "Religious Spirit".  I love it that he shines light on this and shows the damage that it causes.  It's so prevalent in our walk that we tend to just ignore it and even accept it as part of the deal.  John holds no punches and shows how Jesus feels and reacts to this particular enemy.  If you are a Christian then you will be challenged on this!After reading Beautiful Outlaw you feel like you know Jesus from a perspective you've never seen before. Before the book you could probably answer the question "what would Jesus do?" but after this book you feel like you know him well enough to go a couple of steps further and even start answering the deeper question "what would Jesus say?"I was always moved when I heard the late, great, Rich Mullins say "I want to know the Jesus with dirty fingernails". This book is a walk with that Jesus!Read moreRead less
