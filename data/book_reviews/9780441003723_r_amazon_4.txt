An entertaining read. Lots of clever ideas. Unfortunately, science has rendered so many obsolete (impossible), that no realistic rewrite could fix it.The general story has interesting, if a bit one-dimensional characters. Better than Star Trek -- this story is NOT a Star Trek universe story, but shares a number of features of that style, including no real concept that space flight, even with gravity control and "thrusters" has to at least pay some homage to Newtonian physics.This is a golly-gee-whiz yarn, woven of really wild physics ideas and space travel that would fit time-wise in 40 minute segments.I agree that Sawyer is one of the top writers is SF, but this is not one of his better efforts. I can see why when published, it drew a lot of plaudits, but it tried too hard, and the ideas are now as dated as "atomic energy" in early A E vanVogt stories.
