This book is a masterpiece of American story telling. Fleming draws on his experience as a teacher, a frontiersman, and an adventurer to tell the story of two young friends on a summer of discovery. Be careful to catch  all of the elements of classical mythology in this wonderful novel. It is  one of my very favorites! If you can get your hands on this book, by all  means, do it! I take pride in the fact that I am one of the lucky readers  of this book; but I am even more proud of the fact that I attended Mr.  Fleming's Senior High School Mythology class in Seguin, TX.
