About a year ago I was given a website to go to.  There I found stories that touched my heart and  my life.  After reading what others shared, I decided to share a story or two of my own.  I never dreamed it would lead  to my being published and having 4 stories in Heartwarmers.  I think what  makes Heartwarmers so special is that the stories are true and written with  such genuine emotion that the reader becomes involved. They  shed a tear,  or a giggle, they may break out in a fit of laughter, but the reader  "feels" something because of what they have read.  How do I know?  Because they write and tell us so.  They let us know they had a similar  experience, or a similar problem.  They reach out to us and thank us for  the courage, inspiration, encouragement, or humor we added to their day.
