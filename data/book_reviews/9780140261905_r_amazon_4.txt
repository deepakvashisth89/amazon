I loved everything about this book.  I'm first generation American, from a Latvian family that lived in German DP camps for years. My family came to America as displaced people with no home to go back to after the war. While reading this book I could feel my grandmother's presence beside me. I felt so connected to what Agate's family went through. It elicited so much emotion for me. After reading this book I called my aunt immediately and asked her to confirm many aspects of Agates story to see how they compared to our history. It started a long dialog that caused me to document all that she told me so I could pass our family's story to my kids. The book is well written, full of emotion, heartbreak, and as my parents never spoke too much of what they went through, it gave me insight into why they were the kind of parents they turned out to be.
