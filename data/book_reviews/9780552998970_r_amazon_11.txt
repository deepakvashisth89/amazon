This is the fourth set of twins I've met thanks to Joanna Trollope. My favourites by far are Tassy and Flora in The Soldier's Wife but back to more Spanish matters. The last thing I felt like reading about was Christmas 🙄 and the book begins with a very accurate description of the pressures faced by the woman of the family at Christmas. It must have been well written because I started to feel stressed. Lol. Anyway we later see Lizzie and Frances' parents and begin to understand the similarities between Barbara the mother and Frances, one running away to Marakesh, the other to Spain. Of all the JT books I've read, I found I liked very much at least one character but I couldn't find a character I really liked in this book. The women are selfish and insensitive and there isn't a rock character who holds the family together.I wasn't keen on the ending, too melancholy for me but over all, it's well written and worth a readRead moreRead less
