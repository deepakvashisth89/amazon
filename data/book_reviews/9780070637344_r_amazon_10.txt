While this book is not as comprehensive as Jack Schwager's Complete Guide to Futures it is also half the length.I took a lot away from this book and still refer back to it when examining a market I am unfamiliar with. It clearly explains all aspects of Futures trading from Fundamental and Technical Analysis to hedging and explanations on factors affecting all major commodities.
