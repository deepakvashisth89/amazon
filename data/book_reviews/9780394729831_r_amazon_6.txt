I’ve got to say it: This UK book is written by a British Veterinary surgeon, David Taylor, B.V.M.S.  F.R.C.V.S., who also wrote the ‘Zoovet’ series of books. This book is similar to the US book “The Complete Dog Owner’s Manual” by Ann Marder, VMD, which is a 2-in-1 book: a care manual and a breeds book.“You and Your Dog”, however, only covers only 69 dog breeds, which I had to count myself. On a positive note, the author compared breed standards between the UK and US when there were differences. However, the book didn’t give the number of breeds like my other dog books do, to help buyers know more about this book before buying it. The editor should have made sure that this information was included either on the back cover or in the Introduction.Also, unlike other dog books that I have, this book doesn’t say how many color photographs and color graphics, tables, etc. that this book has. It seems that there are at least 1 color photo or illustration, black and white photo, graph or table per page. There are too many to count, which is good! This is useful information for buyers to know before they buy a book. Again, the editor should have made sure that this information was included either on the back cover or in the Introduction. This is important marketing information for buyers.Unlike other excellent dog books that I have, the dog breeds section is lacking specific characteristics to help the reader learn more about a particular dog. These characteristics deal with coats, how trainable they are, if they are particularly people-oriented, etc.This undersized 1986 soft-cover book with 288 semi-glossy pages contains multiple color photos, graphics, tables, charts.The book is published by Dorling Kindersley, London.The ‘Contents’ list 13 main Sections.
