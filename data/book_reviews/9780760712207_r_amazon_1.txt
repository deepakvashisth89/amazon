I am in awe of the extensive quotations in Writers on Writing: 1,200 Memorable Quotations by Thomas Brennan. There is 153 pages of quotations. Each quote is followed by the writer's name, source, and year. "Memorable" is in the mind of the reader. Some of the quotes selected by Mr. Brennan might not be of interest to the reader. Yet, there are enough quotations in this book to be of encouragement to a beginning writer like myself.
