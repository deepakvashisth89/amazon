Harold Bell Wright's classic story about the lives and loves and losses of characters based on real people in the Ozark region around Branson, Missouri was published in 1907, immortalized in two movies, and adapted for a stage play that has run in continuous production since 1960 at the outdoor theatre near Silver Dollar City in Branson, Missouri.  Based on these facts alone, you have to admit - there must be something to this book.  Wright was an unlikely, yet ultimately prolific and successful writer, the first American author to sell over a million copies of a book.  Although some of his other titles might come off as preachy, this book will stand the test of time because of its beautiful prose; understated yet clear themes of truth, honor, and love; and, its endearing characters who were portrayed by Wright as being possessed of the full range of expressions of humanity in all its shame and all its glory -- not as illiterate, foolish country hicks.
