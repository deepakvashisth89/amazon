This is the 4th book in Ron Handberg's series. All these crime novels take place in a top newsroom in the Twin Cities. Although all 4 were excellent, I thought this one was the best. The plot is based on something that really happened in Minneapolis in 1951. Handberg changes the timeframe and the characters, but the background of the story is the same. This book has it all...dirty politics, missing children, day-to-day working of a newsroom, great characters and superb writing. I highly recommend this book.Read moreRead less
