Being familiar with the film, I was unaware of this book so I was very intrigued to read it. As a book it has the luxury of being able to go into greater detail of characters and events.It is well written, painting vivid pictures of both sides in this conflict. Without losing sight of the inherent injustice of the British invasion, you gain some sympathy for the individual characters. The heroes, the less brave, the wise and the foolish.If I had to criticise, I would say, it ends a bit too soon.As a postscript, I can also recommend, for a little further reading.
