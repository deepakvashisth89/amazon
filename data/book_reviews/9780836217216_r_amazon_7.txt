Adam Newman is a stay-at-home dad, putting up with his three kids, through enormous amounts of coffee and sheer inevitability. Ingenuity kicks in more than a few times (to avoid actual work), while his wife surely has the grandest old time working at the office.The backcover text talks about the gender reversal in this cartoon: true, but it misses the vast fun in here. Adam is a man, and so he'll put a tv on his lawnmower (can't miss a second, can you? the game's on!), he'll be delighted by his son's Father's Day card (with missiles & lasers shooting Godzilla), and maybe one day he'll write his book about a stay-at-home dad. Maybe...Basset has been creating the cartoon since 1984 - what a pity there are so few collections to be had!
