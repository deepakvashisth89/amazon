I am finding this little book so helpful.  I read it every night before  going to bed and it puts the whole day in a more peaceful perspective. It helps me realize that I'm not the only one who  struggles with sadness or worry, and that I don't have to take it so  seriously. By following the exercises in the book, I feel like I'm actually  making progress. I seldom read a book more than once, but I'm starting this  one for the second time.
