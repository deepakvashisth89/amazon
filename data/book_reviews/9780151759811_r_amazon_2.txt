Elizabethan England was a violent police state and a dangerous place to be if you were a Catholic. If people desired to remain Catholic it was at their own very considerable risk.In 1593, Christopher Marlowe, who was quite possibly a spy for the Crown, and who was most assuredly a superstar playwright, was murdered in a tavern by two men he had had lunch with. Marlowe, no saint, had himself been involved in a murder of another man.This book delves into that murder, and those times, to try to come up with why Marlowe was murdered and by whom. The book is a fascinating description of those dangerous times and of the life and death of Christopher Marlowe.Read moreRead less
