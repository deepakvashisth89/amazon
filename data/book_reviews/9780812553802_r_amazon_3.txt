Scott Russell Sanders has created a dystopian classic that fans of Orwell can admire.  His setting is the near future in which the Earth has turned hostile, and man has enclosed himself in gargantuan cities, a human terrarium closed from any chaos.When one thinks of a terrarium, one might think of a beautiful display of life enclosed in a glass bubble.  In the human Enclosures, however, things are not beautiful.  People are living a fantasy, out of touch with nature--and often reality.Sanders has a good story telling style, and the book is an easy read.  Everyone should read this book; tech junkies, to politicians, to parents.  It helps us to see the world around us in a different light.
