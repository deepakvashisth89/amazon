This book was recommended to me by my financial advisor.  Once I got into it I found it difficult to put down.  It really does outline principles on how to negotiate any number of things in life, and not to be overbearing about it.  It is very real, as Herb points out he has been on the receiving end as well as the 'giving' end of negotiations.  It is not technical, any person from someone who has not finished high school to a university grad (me) would find this an easy, practical, engaging read.
