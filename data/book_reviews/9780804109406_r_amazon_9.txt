Certain circumstances over the past few months have forced me to tell my family that I was abused when I was 9 years old. It was something I never thought I would have to tell anyone about. It was my secret and had been locked away in the depths of my mind for over 50 years. The effect on my life now has been devastating and I never dreamed that being forced to re-live that particular chapter of my life could be so painful after all this time. I chose this book after a lot of research. I chose this book mainly because it has been written, not by someone who has done a course at college and read a lot of books on the subject, but by a 'victim'. You need to have suffered the abuse and its aftermath to fully understand the pain it causes. I have now read this book twice. It wasn't easy at first but it does get easier. I now realise the impact of what happened to me has had on the rest of my life and this book has helped me through my journey.
