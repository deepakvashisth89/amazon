It was recommended to me as an "academic" novel, i.e. one set on a college or university campus.  It is not an academic novel in the usual sense.  It's more of a "chick" novel, if there is such a genre.  But that is not meant to be a criticism.  It deals intelligently with some major social issues, but I don't wish here to be a spoiler, and is nicely spiced with enough well turned phrases and plot surprises to keep one's interest, at least mine.Read moreRead less
