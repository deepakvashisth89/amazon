I saw the movie based on the true account of this story which this book is also based on, so it made for an interesting read. I say this because certain aspects of the movie kind of twine in with the story told here. I was able to give faces to the main characters which again were based on the movie. This telling differs though from the actual crime. I like where Bainbridge took her characters and how she gave them some of the same characteristics of the actual perpetrators, but still had a story that went in a different direction.
