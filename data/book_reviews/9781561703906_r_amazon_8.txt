An excellent book for anyone who may be interested in spiritual healing, in whatever form. It expains how to know if something is an ego based message, or if it is coming from a place of love. I liked the explaination of the various types of clairvoyance that one may pocess. I believe, as does the author, that we all have this capacity within us, and it can take time to develop - also, we may have blocks preventing that development from reaching its full potential - which is usually fear based. I liked the author's story of her struggles and triumphys, to reach where she is today. Her self-honesty in this book helps, in my opinion, to give hope, that there is always potential. There is also a section on forgiveness in the book, which can be useful I believe, explaining the difference between forgiving the person, rather than condoning their actions.
