Run don't walk to buy anything that Peter Dickinson has written. This is one of his earlier books but is still brilliant. The protagonist is busy trying to teach a chimpanzee to communicate at the behest of an old college friend who happens to be the sheikh of a small but oil rich kingdom. Dickinson is one of the brightest but also most elegant of writers. With a delicate sense of irony and a smidgen of dry humour. One of the great things about the Kindle is how it has made it possible to read books that were published many years ago and which have sunk from sight. Dickinson was never terribly popular over here but is well worth reading.
