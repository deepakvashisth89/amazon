I was really enjoying Gibbon's book [decline and fall] but after 150 pages or so I got bogged down in his dense treacle prose on the early christian church. This, on the other other hand, is easy to read and to grasp the features of the medieval world with confidence. I hated latin and greek classes at school; I couldn't see why such boring, obscure and useless languages were being taught at all. At last, the mystery is revealed in these pages.You do not have to understand Ancient Greek or Latin to appreciate the significance of the Greco-Roman tradition on the medieval world and on our world today.
