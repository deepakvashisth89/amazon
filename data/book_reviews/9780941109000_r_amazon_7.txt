I found this book to be on target for the predominant message that it portrays.  The data offered is similar to that offered by several other channeled entities whose messages were published in one form or another.  What is unique to this book is how the material is presented.  It reminds me of a college text book on the steps to enlightenment.  It is written with a rather dry syntax that leaves no room for any character development or emotional undertone of either the channeled entity (Michael) or the authors.  I think of this book as a primer for those of us newly introduced to the concept of ascension and the help and guidance of other-than-human sources.
