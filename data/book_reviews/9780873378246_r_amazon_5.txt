No one likes to plan for the end of his life, and a surprising number of people refuse to plan for all contigencies--most notably, what will happen if you have to be put in a long-term care facility. Every year, these policies get more expensive and restrictive, because the cost of the facilities go up, and it's not uncommon for people to simply ignore the realities, and necessity, of making those decisions ... until it's too late.In a recent instance I'm aware of, a friend of mine saw her grandfather put into a facility. He was suffering from dementia and unable to live at his with his wife, who like him was well advanced in years (both are 90).They did not have a long term care plan, and like many people, probably felt their safety net was Medicaid. Unfortunately, in order for the U.S. government to cover the cost, they require you to liquidate your assets to cover expenses after an initial stay, which means that you'll have no estate--they had hoped to leave their estate to their children and grandchildren. Now, they cannot.The time to do this kind of planning is WELL before you need a policy, if you want to avoid the financial and emotional churn that will result when you're faced with putting a parent into a facility--and they have not made any plans whatsoever.This book is a clear, concise, and authoritative roadmap on what you need to know about long-term care. If you're a baby boomer and have parents, or in your fifties/early sixties and want to plan for the future, the small price you pay for this book will pay dividends. Knowing what to do and knowing what your options are will make a tremendous difference down the road, when at the end of your parents' lives, or your own, hard decisions have to be made.YOU should make the decisions--not the circumstances--and Nolo Press's books are a great place to start.
