The after story of a nun in denial about the suspicious death of a newborn. I read this for a book club and generally enjoyed it. The writing is good. Stage directions are given at the start of the play- you will not see much throughout. I liked this choice, because it allows for a casual reading. You do not feel like you are reading a play, even, so long as you can activate your imagination. It's a quick read, but encourages its audience to think.Read moreRead less
