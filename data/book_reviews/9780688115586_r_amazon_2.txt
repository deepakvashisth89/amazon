In my opinion, this is the best of all the Oz books. It contains two parallel stories. In one, Dorothy and friends go on a marvelous tour of the land of Oz. In the other the evil Nome King plus a series of new and terrifying villains plan to conquer Oz. Fortunately, the Scarecrow and Ozma save the day. Greatly imaginative, filled with puns, whimsy, and containing Baum's wonderful set of imaginative characters, this is an Oz book that simply must be added to your bookshelf.
