Rating: 1.0 stars. _Implied sexual content, none graphic. _Note: Stand alone read.SKIP IT!  Good supporting cast and a spectacular setting.  HOWEVER, the complex storyline is poorly explored, the writing repetitive, the heroine's “can’t-help-myself” emotional swings from anger to lust are annoying, and the romance lacks believabiltiy.THE STORY introduces hospital administrator, Carolyne McKensie, her mentally fragile mother, Isobel McKensie Thornton, and architect, Vaughan Slater.  Decades earlier, her mother had an affair and was abandoned when she became pregnant with Carolyne.  Fourteen years later, Vaughan, an architectural student, rents a room from Isobel.  Isobel fancied herself in love with Vaughan and Carolnye catches them in a compromising conversation.  After he leaves, Isobel has a complete breakdown, and Carolyne is forced to become the caregiver, but she never forgets, nor forgives, Vaughan.Fast forward 10-years, Isobel marries Julian Thornton, and her new husband asks Carolyne to work with Vaughan to complete the new house he is building for Isobel.  Needless to say, things do not go smoothly.OVERALL, the writing fails the complex storyline, lots of lust but little romance, and the “feminine” emotional swings are insulting. Don’t waste your reading time.You may also enjoy the complex characters and storytelling of N. Bruhns, Anne Stuart, and E. Palfrey. For funny, heart touching romances with great dialogue and descriptive imagery, try Susan Fox, Cara Colter, and Nikki Logan.Read moreRead less
