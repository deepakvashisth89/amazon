A tale of one family. The doctor is Frank, a self-serving tyrant of a man who philanders his wife to drink, and berates his children into their own little world. Andrew and Nina grow up clinging to each other as life support in a situation that cries out for drastic change. The alcoholic mother explains that she'd leave her husband, if only she didn't have children.The story is told from three viewpoints, first, Nina. Then the mother. Then Andrew.Grown up Andrew is sort of a drifter. Once-divorced, searching for the woman of his dreams. He shares snippets of his relationships with his younger sister.Amazing what gets revealed to us about the people we know and love, through others. What they won't or can't reveal or tell us, others spit out through spite, or hurt, or even apathy.Grown up Nina is a traumatized widow, hiding from the pain others may bring to her.An interesting study of these people. Beattie knows how to reveal just the right parts of their personality to help enrich our understanding of their motives, their shortcomings. Fantastic book.Read moreRead less
