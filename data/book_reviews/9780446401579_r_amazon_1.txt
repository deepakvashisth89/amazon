I was introduced to Peter Abrahams in a series written under his other persona, Spencer Quinn. That series is about a detective agency, narrated by a dog. This is the first I've read as a Peter Abrahams novel. It's a very well written, down and dirty, pull no punches story of a convict, recently released after serving a lengthy term on a questionable conviction. His journey to unravel the events that put him in prison is credible and fascinating. The ending is powerful and unexpected.Read moreRead less
