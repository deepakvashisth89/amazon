I attended a three day seminar based on this book on how to make meetings constructive and effective. This book distills all the advice of that seminar. It describes different types of meetings, reasons to hold meetings, why many meetings are unnecessary, etc. I was able to put into practice the concepts when I was a manager, as a volunteer on many committees and boards, and as a department head at a school.
