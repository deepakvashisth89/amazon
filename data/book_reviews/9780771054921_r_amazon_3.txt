I read this book in a few nights, so it held my interest long enough to finish the book. I found the book didn't really have much insight into the Hell's Angels M.C..If your looking for that info find another book.This guy Quesnel was just a psycho punk who tried to prove himself in the crime world.  Looks like both sides used him. He was a money grubber all the way to the end. Seems Serge would sell him self to anyone with the highest offer. At the end he talks alot about redemption. My guess is he will never lead a normal life and will screw up once he is on the outside again. Then the Police will have to answer for that mistake.The most amazing thing about this story is what the Canadian police force will do to put a patch holder behind bars.  They will pay a murdering rat fink almost anything he wants just to testify against someone they want to get!  Is that how justice works?
