Those familiar with Thursh Green will find this book taking you there for a two week vacation to catch up on all the news.  Miss Read takes you into the life of the small village and makes each person important to the character of Thrush Green. The crochety, the loving, the eccentric are all described in compassionate and non-judgemental vignettes that make each one a facet of the jewel that is Thrush Green. I have been reading the Miss Read book for many years and I am grateful that Amazon continues to carry these series.
