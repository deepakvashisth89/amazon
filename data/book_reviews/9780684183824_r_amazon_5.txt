First, I love her style of writing and have since ordered her complete writing collection.  Unfortunately, the stories were of little substance. It seems as if she never intended to publish these. I see her writing them late at night and deciding to throw them to the side.... Writing exercises or an attempt to ready her mind for bed... Too tired to crumple them up and take them to the trash can. This comes from someone who loves an old story without gore and overdone suspense. I love the eerie subtlety that usually accompanies an old ghost story. I had an open mind when I started every story, but every single ending left me frustrated.
