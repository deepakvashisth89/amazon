I am ordering several of these as gifts! What a great all-occasion "stocking stuffer"!  When I moved to my present location, I passed the library - where they were giving away books to make more  room(?)! I came across the original edition of this book and it started me  to thinking of how I could actually put His teachings to work in my life on  a daily basis, and become a better example of what Christian-  "ity" is really supposed to be: Christian,literally translated,  meaning - "Christ-like" - and all actions coming from a center of  love. Or: WWJD? I hadn't been aware of WWJD?, but I soon came across the  same book, in paperback, on a revolving rack at a drugstore - then the  jewelry started popping up. Christmas, I found WWJD? music on CD! This is  what the author was intending to happen when he wrote the book back then!  This is an extremely readable book, for all ages - young and old - with a  wonderful storyline that represents an entire town that has met the  pastor's challenge of trying for a full year to do as Jesus would do.  They  thought, as Christians, it would be a simple thing - but as you will see in  this book, sometimes the simplest things can be the hardest to do! After I  read the book, I wanted to be a part of something like was illustrated  here. I'm working on it! It would be especially fun, even, for small towns  to try it and see if they could set a prototype of this sort - or even  challenge the church-goers where you live to try it.
