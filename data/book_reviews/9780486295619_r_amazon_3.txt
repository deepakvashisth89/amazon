Long ago, in college I took German and we did some translation which I enjoyed, but didn't know it could have been a profession; and so the years passed in other pursuits. Now I have rediscovered the joy of translation combining memory, a good dictionary, and a "dual language" book with German, French or whatever on one page, and English on the opposite page.  You can accept the translator's version (Stanley Appelbaum in this book) or root around and translate the sentence your own way!  This book has five stories including the famous "The Metamorphosis" about Gregor Samsa who, you'll recall, wakes up transformed into an insect of some kind; and the fabulously surreal "A Country Doctor."
