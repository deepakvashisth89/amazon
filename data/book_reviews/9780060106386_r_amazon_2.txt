This is a good basic primer for simple gunsmithing tasks. Some of the chapters are pretty project specific and it might have been a bit more useful if it included a few other general/fundamental repair/refurbishment items like plugging drilled holes and attaching fore-end tips, etc., but it does give you easy-to-follow instructions on a number of woodworking and metal-working jobs and several step-by-step projects.
