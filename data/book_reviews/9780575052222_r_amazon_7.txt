Another great Discworld novel.  Small Gods tends towards the more thoughtful end of the spectrum, and felt a little deeper (though a little less consistently funny) than, say, the Watch novels.  Pratchett walks the line of satire perfectly, and there's no shortage of parallels to be drawn between real world traditions, though it rarely feels too on-the-nose.While Pratchett is a humanist and the tone is definitely flavored with realistic skepticism throughout, I wouldn't call it anti-religion at all - just anti-authoritarian-organized-religion.
