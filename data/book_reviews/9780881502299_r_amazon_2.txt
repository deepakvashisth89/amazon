I love Phoebe Atwood Taylor's writing and would give this book 5 stars out of 5 except for one thing: the transcription to e-book is decidedly shoddy. It isn't a matter of typos alone but of outright changing words, so that what in the original novel would be 'yarn' is in this e-book written as 'yam', 'distract' is in this e-book written as 'distinct', and many other similar errors.I would strongly urge the publishers to redo this to actually be legible; I'm able to somewhat skim past errors because I've read the paperback form of this novel before, but I would suspect that readers who are new to this author may be put off by the confusion factor.On to the novel itself. Taylor's Asey Mayo series, and this novel in particular, does a great job of romping through the rural New England scenery while mingling humor with very real and human pathos. While the overall tone is lighthearted (and fits well within the notion of a 'cozy' mystery), the depictions of human interactions and the display of personalities is sweeping and highly enjoyable. I would recommend her novels to anyone who likes Agatha Christie or Dorothy Sayers; it's a more American take on it, but deeply pleasurable.Read moreRead less
