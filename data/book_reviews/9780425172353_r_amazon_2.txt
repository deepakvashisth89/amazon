This was the first book I read of Ms. Hamilton's. I have since read this one and her others many times. The premise of her book THE CELTIC RIDDLE is interesting even though it is far fetched. Lara, an antiques store owner accompanies her employee and neighbor Alex to Ireland. Alex has inheritance from an obscure figure from his past. Accompanying Lara and Alex is Lara's possible mate Rob, (the relationship is confusing to her and us!) and his teenage daughter, Jennifer. Alex's inheritance come by way of a unique method whereby clues derived from an old Irish poem lead to more clues that lead to an eventual treasure. The relationships between the characters and the adventure lead to murder, mayhem, and mystery, components found in all of Lyn Hamilton's novels.As with all of Ms. Hamilton's books, there is a story from the past that interlocks and connects with the present. While people have commented that her books are not plausible or believable, keep in mind many pieces of fiction are just that, FICTION. Lara, the main character continues to discover murder, mayhem, and mystery where ever she goes. However inaccurate the characters might be, you do find yourself involved with the relationships between the characters.
