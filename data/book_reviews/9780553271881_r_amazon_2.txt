I loved this book, it was one of the most influential that I have ever come across.  The way Branden describes our ties to reality and the clarity in which we must feel and act is something I never had considered before.  Having been a fan of Ayn Rand before reading this I can say there is a large Randian influence that I felt in many passages (as would be expected).  He develops a powerful argument for deep individualism and integrity, as well as a strong drive for self-knowledge and awareness.
