I have loved reading and writing all kinds of poetry most of my life. I am always seeking interesting poetry books and I picked up this one (Passionate Hearts: The poetry of sexual love compiled and edited by Wendy Maltz) at a bargain price on Amazon. This wonderful collection has many contributions from poets I was not familiar with and one of the many things I loved about this poetry collection is that all the poems were shorter rather than too long. I have always loved the shorter poem styles rather than the long drawn out boring poems which seem to be popular with some academic pseudointellectuals.This wonderful collection is organized into five chapters. Chapter one explores the poetry of tender awakenings. There are 12 poems in this section. The second chapter has poems about passionate pleasures. There are 31 poems in this section. Chapter three deals with varied dances and has 30 poems in this section. Chapter four has 23 poems about deeper intimacies. The final chapter called “Graceful transformations” has 20 poems.  There is also a list of poets at the end of this volume.If you are a poetry lover this is a book you should check out.  I found the poems so compelling I could not put it down until I had read all the material.  I only have one complaint.  This is a collection of poems about sexual love but it is the most sterile poems about this erotic activity I have ever read.  I guess using too earthy and specific sexual terms was not welcome in this collection.  A little too much political correctness possibly?Rating: 5 Stars.  Joseph J.
