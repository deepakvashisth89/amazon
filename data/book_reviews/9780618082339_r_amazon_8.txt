David Huddle's poems and short stories have been cherished reading for me for the past ten years. His novel lives up to my expectations. Huddle has a  remarkable ability to create and capture his characters and bring them to  life. His characters, male or female, are made real and believable. He  creates believable lives and situations that allow me to look at life from  a different perspective,  perspectives that never fail to give me insight  into my own life. I hope you will read this book; it is both a collection  of short stories and an epic tale of a group of people you will grow to  care about.
