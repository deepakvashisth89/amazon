Actually there are three murders in this book. All the victims are women — and all have been decapitated.  The police gave up on the first murder, but Inspector Cockrill quickly determines that the second murder could only have been done by one of the respectable people in a lovely country home.  They all have appealing personalities.  We don’t want any of them to be guilty.
