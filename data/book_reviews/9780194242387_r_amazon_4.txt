This stands alongide the same author's "The Death of Grass" among the finest examples of post-apocalyptic fiction. For anyone familiar with the John Wyndham's "cosy catastrophes" this dark depiction of Britain consumed by glacial ice will some as some surprise.Perhaps not as bleak as "The Death of Grass", it still manages to unsettle the reader, with every human frailty exposed. Unlike the Wyndham school, there is no comfortable solution, no return to normality.
