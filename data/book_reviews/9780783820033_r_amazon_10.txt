I enjoyed Jodi Picoult's novel "Mercy".  It is excellent, very well-written.  What a contrast between Jamie and Cam's love for their wives!  Allie was quite naive to bring home Mia, a stranger, and have her overnight. Cam didn't waste any time kissing her. Jamie killing his wife was a mercy killing. Jodi Picoult does not shy away from controversial topics. In Canada, we finally have M.A.I.D., medically assistance in dying.  It was a long time in coming. I will insist on M.A.I.D. if I get dementia.  My mum got dementias and didn't know me for 10 years.  It was dreadful to visit her. Both Allie and Cam changed and developed throughout the novel and I feel their relationship was better for it. I also like the passages about the wars in Scotland against the English. It was very interesting and I would recommend this book.  It's not a Harlequin romance.  It's not for the faint-hearted.Read moreRead less
