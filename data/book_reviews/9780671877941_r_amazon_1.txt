I have recently read a lot of David Drake's stuff and have really enjoyed most of it. This book includes 4 different stories the first is the best of them with lots of action and great character development. The 2nd and 4th ones are also very good. The 3rd one I didn't like as much mainly because  it was missing a lot of the great action scenes the other three had.David Drakes writing is good as always. I enjoy how he doesn't spent to much time overly describing each characters appearance just giving enough detail to let me build the look of the person in my own mind's eye. His descriptions of the environment  and equipment used in his story is also near perfect to help draw you into the world.I would recommend this book for anyone who enjoys military science fiction. Its also a great book for those trying out the genre for the first time.Read moreRead less
