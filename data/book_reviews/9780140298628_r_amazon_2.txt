This MD has written about the medical system v medical care. As a nurse I found it informative and inspiring. As a patient, I was frustrated with what happens in the name of patient care and "standards" of care. This is well worth the read. These stories provide some warning and some strength of purpose when facing a provider who doesn't get it.
