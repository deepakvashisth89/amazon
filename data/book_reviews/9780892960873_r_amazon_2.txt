I gave this book a five star rating because it addresses the most serious problem of the Church today: in the words of Jesus, "the love of many will wax cold", and also, "I have this against you, you have left your first love!"  Of course this love, is all about loving Jesus, and I have become greatly concerned with my observation of this missing love for Jesus, and a missing love for others in the many churches I have visited in the past 20 years. The Church of today seems to be a blend of a business, a social club with varying rules, organizations for social justice, and doctrinal dissemination and protection of Church doctrine. Very few churches truly are functioning on the two Commandments of the New Covenant that Jesus gave of loving God with our entire being, and loving others like we would want to be loved ourselves! Jesus rocked my world when He began a conversation with me when I started genuinely seeking Him nearly twenty years ago, and my heart has been his ever since. This book stoked the fires of our relationship at just the right time, a time when I was feeling discouraged due to suffering and isolation. This book turned my heart back in the direction of Jesus once agsin, and I found Him running to me with arms wide open and his heart overflowing with amazing love that sent my spirit soaring! The Church desperately needs to grab hold of the teaching of this book, so that we can be a fully functional and dynamic Body of Christ that will transform the world through the love and power of Jesus Christ!
