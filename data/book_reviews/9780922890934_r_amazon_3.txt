Ed McBain. The 87th Precinct. What more can I say? This is one of the most enjoyable police procedural series ever created. Indeed, it created the genre. This is one of the earlier books, so I enjoyed seeing the world of the late 50s through McBain's eyes, and seeing how his characters were at the beginning of their literary lives.
