I really enjoyed this novel...If you are familiar with TOPPER in any of it's forms, movie, radio or TV, this novel is a bit more edgy then all of them. It is a really rather hedonistic story and that reflected the majority of author Throne Smith's unfortunately short career with TOPPER and the sequel novel TOPPER TAKES A TRIP the apex. Hopefully, if you purchase this, it will bring you to the rest of Smith's work all of which were a joyful celebration of life, love and good times.
