Although it is more than 30 years since Dr. Wilder Smith wrote this book, I feel indebted to him for his clear and logical treatment of this vexed subject.The book inevitably uses some scientific language (useful glossary included) but is nevertheless accessible and enjoyable for the thoughtful reader.  Science books have a tendency to become out-of-date with the rapid growth of knowledge, but this volume is still worthy of its place in the library of the common man who seeks to explore the question of origins.Dr. Wilder Smith deals politely but thoroughly with the `clumsy' Darwinian argument that postulates evolution of life without intelligent design.  For me, he soundly demolishes the last traces of that `scientific' mythology, leaving a real sense of wonder at the immensity of the wisdom and intelligence of the Creator.Wilder Smith is not a polemical writer but he carefully and explicitly exposes the `nonsense' being taught in some modern universities where reason has been sidelined in favour of dangerous and illogical ideas which inevitably foul-up the scholar's thought processes.Read moreRead less
