JUMP and the Net Will Appear is entertaining but also thought-provoking. It's hard not to compare your life and all it's challenges to Crow's real life stories. Although Crow is a musician by trade, he bridges the gap between the music world and everyday people's world. The humorous stories reveal strong messages we all need to take to heart. I wish I read this book 10 years ago. Crow has a keen ability to use life's experiences into lessons top push himself further instead of just taking situations as face value. That should be a lesson for all of us.My recommendation is to grab the book, read it cover to cover and start a new day with a whole new prespective!Read moreRead less
