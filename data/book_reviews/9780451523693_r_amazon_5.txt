I had heard about this book many times, but when I finally read it (listened, actually) I was blown away.  It's a memoir, so you must understand that from the beginning. But it reads like a novel. The detail is beautiful and engrossing. There is a lot of sail jargon, so take it slow and look words up and it will help you get a fuller picture. When you are done, you will feel the salt spray on your face and thank God you survived all those storms.  Whew, what a life to be a sailor back then, when ships were wood and men were iron.  There is no war, but there is battle between man and the elements, man and man, and man and himself.  Read this book, and when you wake up in your nice warm bed in the morning, it will seem just a little sweeter for knowing what those guys went through.
