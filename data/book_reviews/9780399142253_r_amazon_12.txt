This is the second book in the Bridge trilogy and by far (so far) the most puzzling. Unlike other Gibson novels this coalesces very gradually but the concept and the world in which it all happens are at times quite breathtaking. There is no denying that this story shows a genius at work just consider the opening of chapter 21!!! Just crazy.
