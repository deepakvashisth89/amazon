I first got the name of this book from Douglas Adams. It was one of the books he spoke of in several of his articles (a collection of which can be found in hid posthumous book "Salmon Of A Doubt") when he was still alive. As a big fan of Douglas's work I decided to get a copy and see what all the fuss was about. I'm only about 50 paces in and I can say that although it's not exactly what I expected I will recommend this book to others and pass this copy on to friends when I am done with it.
