I teach reading, and I like Rylant's work.  For me, BIBBA was personal.  Rylant grew up near where my father's family lived for generations.  My Dad left to escape the poverty-- he didn't want to be a miner like his Dad.  As I grew up in Utah, I knew very little about my Dad's early life.BIBBA was a wonderful read, especially since Rylant is only a few years older than me.  I vividly remember Bobby Kennedy's charisma, and the shock of his death.  There are many places in BIBBA to cry-- when Rylant's father dies just before she is to see him for the first time in many years, for example.  There is also the simple joy of that first kiss, and all those little moments of growing up.  Read this book!Read moreRead less
