I love the Lonely Planet travel guides but this one tried to cover too much ground.  So many disparate states that really only share an accident of geography... the coverage was spotty, very hit or miss, which is understandable since so much sparsely populated real estate is covered.  I bought the book for a few places I was visiting in Vermont, not all of which were in the book and those that were covered were not given a great deal of coverage.  For what it tries to do, the book succeeds I suppose.  I downloaded the Kindle version and found it a bit clunky to navigate, especially getting around the maps, zooming them in and out, etc.  It would be great if Amazon could figure out a way (or simply license) a Google Maps type of interface so that a map at the beginning of the book could be swept up, down, left, and right, zoomed in and out, and clicked on to take you to the relevant section of the book (without an internet connection needed).Read moreRead less
