I rarely fail to complete a regency romance. I've been reading them every night before bed for years as they relax me and help me sleep. This is one of the rare exceptions. I worked as a counselor for over 25 years and found it most difficult to work with people who are very much like the mother figure in this book. So well depicted is she that I didn't want her in my life even for the day or two it would take me to complete this little book. Kudos so Hazard and her writing skills but no thanks to the experience of reading this book. I made it to Chapter 3 - 40 pages before deciding to give this one a pass.Read moreRead less
