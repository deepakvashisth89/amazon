This is a beautifully written, beautifully illustrated book, a sad story with a happy ending.  Unfortunately, my experience with it was quite negative, and I'm sharing my story to hopefully prevent a similar situation from happening.The reflection part of the story introduces us to a young, happily married couple with a baby.  Fearing for the safety of her husband who is out at sea fishing, the young woman takes her baby out in a boat to search for him.  They become lost in the storm, but do find land.  The woman and her infant son settle into a new home, and she raises him alone, never to see her husband again.  I promise you, the story does have a happy ending, but this part of the story may be too sensitive for some audiences.My 10 month old son was given this book by his grandmother, my mother-in-law.  My husband and I had only been married for 3 years, and I was pregnant with our second child.  My husband works swing shifts, and was working the night I sat down with my son in a rocking chair to read this book to him.  When I got through this part of the story, I was sobbing and had to stop reading. I did go back and finish reading the book alone, but I was still very upset by it, and was convinced it was a message from my mother-in-law. Our relationship, at times, was strained.  My husband was upset too when he read it the next day, and we had to have an awkward discussion with her about it.  She said she had not read the story, and had only purchased it because of the beautiful cover.In our situation, this book was not an appropriate gift. I do suggest that you read this book before giving it as a gift.  I feel it would be appropriate for an older child, ecspecially one who has, or is going through a loss situation.Read moreRead less
