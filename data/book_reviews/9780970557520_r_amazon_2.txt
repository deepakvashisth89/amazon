"Plowin' Newground" by Jerry W. Brown, ISBN 0-9705575-2-3ReviewAt the suggestion of my wife, I have just finished reading  "Plowin' Newground", Jerry Brown's new 400 page fictional novel.  I found it to be very entertaining and fast moving.  I recommend it to persons who are still "plowing new grounds", i.e., coping with new circumstances as we all must.The descriptions of the flora and fauna and sights and sounds of the north Louisiana swamp and hill country, as written by Brown are most realistic.  One can almost experience the calls and habits of the whippoorwills and the giant "good-God!" woodpeckers.  His word pictures give one the same feelings of being there as do those of the frontier west as penned by the late Louis L'Amour.Brown's characters seem real and are so typical of those of the times and places described.  They give one an appreciation of the strengths, failures and achievements of many of us.If five stars are awarded to the best of books, then I think this book merits more than four stars.James T.
