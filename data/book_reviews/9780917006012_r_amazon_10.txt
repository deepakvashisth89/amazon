I would highly recommend this concordance. This is the single most useful bible study tools I have ever come across. It is great for a person who can't remember a verse they are thinking of or someone who is looking at any theme as a whole throughout the bible.The concordance clearly lists all words found more than once in the King James Version of the bible (with the exception of "and", "the", etc.). But what is great about this edition is it also links you to the Greek and Hebrew words as well. For example, when you look up "Oracles" there are 4 places to find it in the KJV (Acts 7:38, Romans 3:2, Hebrews 5:12, 1 Peter 4:11) and you are given the code "G3051" - which if you look it up you will find "λόγιον logion neuter of G3052; an utterance (of God): - oracle." This is very useful in ensuring that you understand the full meanings of individual words and the texts as a whole - as the writers intended them to be understood.There are newer versions of this concordance, however, I find this one to be the most useful and appropriate for all levels of readers.
