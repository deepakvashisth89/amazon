For the first couple of stories I found the punctuation distracting but then I stopped noticing. The depth of Selby’s characters is wonderful, you feel you’re right behind their eyes, on the inside looking out. Some of his characters are the sort of people who don’t usually get a voice. Selby covers depression, homelessness, frustrated fathers and many more characters in the fifteen short stories in this book. Sometimes you’re surprised at the places people find joy. It reminded me a little of A Streetcar Named Desire, Selby’s men leap from the page full of energy (which is sometimes destructive energy) like Stanley Kowalski does.
