I love this series, it's kind of a closet/secret pleasure of mine. I picked up the first book not knowing what I was getting into (a romance novel series) and found all of the other elements, those that were unrelated to the romance sub-theme, to be quite fascinating. At this point I've read the series probably at least a full 3 times, would love to see movie/tv adaptations of this series, and have been thinking about writing some fan fiction for it.It has so many great elements to it, a unique fantasy setting, magic, intrigue, and lore. The only thing I don't really like about it is the fact that it is a romance novel and there is a bit of a reoccurring cliche/theme that involves misunderstandings that nobody bothers to settle or clarify until much later than they really should.I did enjoy the original five books more than three from the Enthroned set, but I still love all eight.
