If you are a fan of Lawrence Sanders or his detective, Edward X Delaney, don’t read this book. You will be badly disappointed.In his other books also, Sanders delves upon the philosophy of life, the ways a criminal mind works, a cop’s responsibilities in the crime-filled society, and the internal politics in Police Departments. But in this book, these musings overshadow the story, slow it down and add to the volume of the book. Irrelevant characters and unnecessary events could also have been avoided. The climax is boring and needlessly protracted.Read moreRead less
