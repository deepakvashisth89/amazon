I was unacquainted with the author before I bought "The Last Tsar."  Apparently Edvard Radzinsky has a sterling reputation for his work and if you read this book you'll discover why.  The subject matter is fascinating: the life and demise of the last Tsar of Russia.  If you are unfamiliar with the circumstances, it doesn't matter.  Even though you might know what happens, the circumstances, in the hands of Mr. Radzinsky, offer a thrill comparable to the best mystery.  And of course the story is true.  Mr. Radzinsky's scholarship is impeccable: it is amazing the research and background he has made available.  This could be tedious organized by someone else, but  Radzinsky is a master of detail and building suspense.  Still there is more, a sensitivity that perhaps reflects the depth of soul found in other Russian artists.  I am going to buy more of his books.
