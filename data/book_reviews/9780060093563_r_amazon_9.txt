The first book by Martha Stewart, considered a classic of English soft mystery, was first published in 1955. The young widow Charity Selborne spends a holiday with her friend Louise in the picturesque South of France when she is drawn into a whirlwind of strange and, as it soon turns out, very criminal events. For the first third, you wait for the story to get going, the second third starts to build, and for the last third, you wait for the case to be resolved. Of course, emotions are also mixed into the story. Well, yes. Instead of picturesque France, I read it on the no less picturesque main island of the Azores, Sao Miguel.
