My uncle often referred to "tup'ny bloods" for those books that were okay, but hardly the best of writing.Not to be uncharitable but this definitely falls on the side of better than what I could do but still pretty rough. The Posleen sections are difficult to read but passable and the human combat is largely enjoyable yet it doesn't feel too simplistic for someone who enjoys a battlefield memoir.I do have to point out that if the AIDs are so clever and able to perform actions autonomously why do the Earth militaries not design mobile slabs of armor and guns that are slaved to an AID which can provide the fire support without putting a person in immediate danger? In fact why not deploy semi autonomous gun droids, simpler than the ACS suit but they still pack a punch and you can deploy them by the thousands. We can churn out armoured vehicles by the thousands and I have a problem believing that an AID would be incapable of controlling a multitude of them at once especially given the lack of tactics employed by the Posleen.All in all, I enjoyed it despite the inconsistencies and occasional typos which seem to be common in these free or very low price books.Read moreRead less
