If you've got the original Conversion Book, you'll want to stick with it and not bother with getting Dark Conversions unless you want the information regarding Nightbane in the Rifts Setting. The original book included everything else for Monsters, Supernatural Creatures and Demons.The Newer Revised Conversion book is still a good catch-all for Monsters and being Rifts' Defacto Monster Manual. The beginning part is a new write up of rules clarifications and clearer discussions on how to integrate the modern lines of Heros Unlimited, Mutants, Palladium Fantasy, Ninjas & Super Spies with discussion of culture shock into the world of Rifts of 20th & 21st Century characters. There are new creatures listed here not in the older version of the Conversion Book and plenty of new art work. Gone now in the Revised version are the Werebeasts and certainly all of the Demons and Devils. Those now get found in the Dark Conversion book with other information.Yes you can get monsters unique for Rifts from nearly any World Book, the Conversion Book will allow for far greater variety and inclusion of critters that have a more familiar feel and it's the book to help ease the cross-over of several of Palladium's other game lines so that they can be treated as Dimension Books.Read moreRead less
