The Spinning Man was a pretty interesting novel except for the discussions on Philosophy which completely lost me. The author was writing way over my head. That aside, the plot was easy to follow with a good pace. The characters seemed real enough but there were none I really cared about or cheered for. In my opinion, Evan grew progressively less likable as the chapters went by. Ellen, who I didn't like at all, became at least tolerable as the novel unfolded. I liked the detective until the end when he seemed to muddle the whole investigation. The twins were...there..not much more. And then there was the end of the book which has been bugging me since I finished the story and following multiple rereading of the last two chapters. It still bugs me because I can't decide what happened. Did he do it or not? I have to give it to author Harrar. If his intention was to leave an impact, he nailed the ending.
