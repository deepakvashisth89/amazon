Little Drummer Girl is what I consider to be the first glimpse of Le Carre's modern post cold war style(even though he wrote it a third of a century ago and the cold war had a bit left to run).  Le Carre's earlier Smiley works appeal to me not at all.  In attempted rereads I could not force myself to finish them,where from Drummer Girl on the magic of his writing glows and lends a pleasure as I reread his later works for the third time.  Le Carre's old work was well respected but his newer stuff is among the finest writing of my times.  England was dank,dark and depressing back in The Spy Who Came In from the Cold days.  Modern,multicultural,multicolored England is a bustling,thriving,vibrant country.  Le Carre reflects this profound change in British society effortlessly and almost by the way in his writing.  Old England was boring-modern England is a very different place.
