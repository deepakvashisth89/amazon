I was surprised at well this book pulled me in. Card is very gifted in his storytelling and word usage and imagery and...I could go on and on. I adore fantasy but I know that many of them can rely too much on cliche and sometime feel to much like a rip off of Tolkien. This one stood out, no elves, no dwarves, but is does have magic and cruelty and revenge. It's a book I constantly go back and reread. Any lover of fantasy should read this.Read moreRead less
