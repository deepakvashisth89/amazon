This guide is filled with facts about the literature of the 20th century up through 1996. Each year gets 1-3 pages which list the year's bestselling books and prize winners, as well as births and deaths of authors. I appreciated the many photographs of authors and the neat organization. After this chronology, there are a few informative entries: an explanation of the literary prizes, a list of America's poets laureate, a list of 99 books recommended by Anthony Burgess, a very abbreviated list of banned books, a discussion of authors' astrological signs (!), an interesting list of many authors' other occupations, pseudonyms, and romances between authors.This almanac is not encyclopedic, but it is easy to use and contains interesting information There is no index.Read moreRead less
