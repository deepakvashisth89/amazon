i read less than zero and american psycho prior to reading the rules of attraction. And ive really enjoyed them all. I really liked how this book is told from the warped minds of about 10 different people. And how both Clay from less than zero and Patrick from american psycho have a chapter of their owo. Although patricks character does not seem to be fully developed yet. The lives of these young rich brats is like a book version of a reality show, theres sex violence drama drugs sex partying lieing back stabbing ignoring real life and creating your own all new world where you believe what you want to believe and cant seem to understand others. There are so many mixed signals going on between the characters that its comical. None of them truly know each other, and each one is searching for a connection with another. A love hexagon i suppose. And all the men seem to be bisexual? A lingering theme from less than zero that im guessing Ellis can relate to in real life? And as always, the story ends and nothing has changed for anyone. You are left knowing that they continue being just as messed up as they were at the beginning, if not more messed up.
