Ann Cowart Lutzky uses her intellectual powers and sensitivities, to fuel the extensive research required to transport us into the daily life of Ancient Sumeria.Enki, Gilgamesh, and others come to life, as though we traveled to those times and places through a StarGate, transcending the limits of time and space.Then, we are transported back to the 20th century to view the life of their descendants, now living in the San Francisco Bay Area.Ms. Lutzky generates a swirl of magic through her use of time shifts, character insights, and intimate relationships – a powerful alchemical mixture, resulting in a collapse of the past and present as distant reflections of each other.Read moreRead less
