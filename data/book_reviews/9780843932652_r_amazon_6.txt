This was my first Cassie Edwards book...and my last.  The book starts off great.  Good story, strong characters, lots of heartache...but then it just fizzles.  Where does it go wrong?  When Shane first meets Melanie and they immediatly get it on.  I'm sorry, but there is no way you can just start going at it in the woods with some wild man and be instantly in love.  I guess if you want to buy this book, pick it up in the used department.  Hopefully you can find it for a penny...cause that's as much as I would pay for it.Read moreRead less
