_The Thrill of Fear_ is the rarest of books -- a scholarly study that can be read and enjoyed by the layman. Covering 250 years  of literature and art is an awesome task, but Kendrick attacks it with aplomb and pulls it off just as neatly as you please.  This most entertaining book is highly reccommended to anyone with an interest in the horrid and the macabre -- from Gothic novels to EC comics to the gore-filled mayhem that passes for horror movies these days; it's all here!Read moreRead less
