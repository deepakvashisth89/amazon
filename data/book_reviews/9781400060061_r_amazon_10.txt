Delightful!  After reading Adriana Trigiani's The Shoemaker's Wife, I was looking forward to reading The Queen of the Big Time and I wasn't disappointed.  The characters are well developed and the writing flows beautifully.  At times hilarious, at times very sad, but always true to life.  Adriana Trigiani has a gift for showing us the extraordinary in ordinary lives.  This book is not on the ''epic grand scale'' but it is very enjoyable.  I have already purchased this author's other books and I can't wait to read them.  I highly recommend The Queen of the Big Time.Read moreRead less
