Oaken Rings is scintillating.  It marks the return of the classic hero epic coupled with the depth and accuracy of a well-researched and highly accurate historical reference guide.  While most medieval period pieces stress outlandish wizardry and farcical innacuracies, Oaken Rings stands as an entertaining and factual account of life, love and the enduring human spirit set against the harsh and colorful backdrop of the middle ages.  Beginning with a basis in modern reference, the reader is gradually drawn back to days of yore, where the sights, smells and sensations of Europe's age of chivalry come alive and are delivered with all the impact of a champion jouster's lance  aimed directly at one's emotional and intellectual core.  It is a MUST READ for anyone concerned with experiencing the true essence of serious modern literature.
