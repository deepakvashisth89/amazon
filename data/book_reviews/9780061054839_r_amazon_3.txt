Vampire books are generally not scary. They've become such a part of American pop culture that they're overly familiar to us and have lost the horror of the original legends from Eastern Europe and the Middle East. Not so with this book. Aycliffe does a great job of melding the familiar vampire tropes with their ancient roots.You eventually get to an old castle in Eastern Europe, but, fortunately, nothing sparkles in this dark journey. Give this book a chance. I've read it twice, and it was just as good the second time if not better.Aycliffe is one of the few "horror' writers that have spooked me while reading his material. He can be a slow burn, but it works. His books aren't overlong, and he paces them well. They require a little patience that's well rewarded. If you're looking for a nonstop thriller, look elsewhere. This author is all about the classic English ghost story, and he does it better than anyone writing today. I highly recommend his other books as well.Read moreRead less
