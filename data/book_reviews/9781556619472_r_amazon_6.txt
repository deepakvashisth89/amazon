After Julia's husband dies, she is shocked to discover that the bank is forcing disclosure on her home. All that she and her children have left to their names is an old coaching inn in the country. Will Julia manage to survive without the luxuries that she has lived with since childhood? Will her friends manage to overcome their difficult pasts and find hope for the future?This book is a tale full of romance, although it does not focus on one couple but several. The book is clean, and the couples who are pursuing relationships and working through struggles do so in a godly manner.The book does contain a ghost story, although it is not the main emphasis of the tale. The ghost seemed out of place in a romance book at first, and seemed more like something that would be a part of a Mandie or Nancy Drew book. The ghost has a place later on in the book, however.The book can stand on its own without the sequel. Don't let the fact that it is book #1 stop you from reading it. If you were to only read the first book you would not feel like you were missing out. The conclusion is a good one.The book is interesting and the characters are well developed. I only gave it four stars because it is somewhat similar to other romantic tales that I have read, and not especially imaginative or original. It is still a good read, and I do recommend it.Read moreRead less
