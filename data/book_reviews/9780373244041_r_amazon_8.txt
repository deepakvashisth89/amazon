I love cream puffs, and I enjoy reading a cream puff story every once in awhile. Almost a Bride is light and airy with some dark chocolate laced on top. Just like the characters. They are likable, lovable, and of course maddening to each other. I enjoy the Wyoming scenes, the explanations of ranch management within the romance story line. The book is predictable - but it's supposed to be. The characters run the story, just as they should. This is a perfect book for a fun, fast, romantic night's read.Read moreRead less
