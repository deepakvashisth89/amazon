This three-volume series is a bit different to other books by Dolores Cannon. Communication is established with Nostradamus, in various stages in his life, using a number of different vehicles (past-life regression subjects). Newcomers to Dolores Cannon's work might be better reading "Between Death and Life", first, in order to get the most out of this series of books. Nostradamus gives his own explanations of the various "quatrains" (in many cases significantly different to other more "traditional" translations). The books give an insight into the Life & Times of Nostradamus, and the conditions under which he had to work (keeping well clear of the Inquisition). Remember that the various prophecies (originally written in Archaic French & Latin) should be considered as things that COULD happen, rather than things that WILL happen... although some things have already "come to pass" since the books were originally published.
