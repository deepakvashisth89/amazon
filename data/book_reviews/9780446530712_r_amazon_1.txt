This book is an easy read, with tasteful, simple drawings, addressing a major part of love making that has historically been avoided. I have given this book as a gift on 3 occasions to female friends who had expressed indirectly their disappointment in their partners' ability to satisfy them sexually. All 3 of these women loved the book and thanked me for giving them a "tool" that opened the door for them to have long overdue discussions with their partners. 2 said that it brought their love making to a pinnacle they had never achieved before. The 3rd person thanked me for removing the guilt that she always felt that somehow she was a lesser woman because her partner was achieving orgasm each time they made love, but she wasn't....ever, until she read the book. Sadly, her partner proved his lack of commitment to their relationship  by not being willing to do his part  (even after a quick read of the book) to understand his partner's needs. But, with her confidence emboldened, and her guilt erased, she broke off the relationship and is now in a caring, loving relationship with a man who, after reading the book himself, is giving her the time and attention that she needs, and who loves the "benefits" that naturally come when his partner is sexually satisfied. My understanding is that the book is out of print, so one's best bet is to find a used one, but I strongly urge anyone, female or male who is in a relationship in which the woman rarely achieves orgasm.
