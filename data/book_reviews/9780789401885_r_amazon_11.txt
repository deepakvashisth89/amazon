The Eyewitness books are by far my favourite travel guides because of their great illustrations and photos and the one on Ireland is no exeption.I had never been to Ireland before and the guide gave some good advice on interesting places to visit. The illustrations and photos gave mostly a realistic impression of the scenery and landscape.But we visited a country house (Bantry House) which had a double-sided feature in the travel guide, I know now why the illustrations were sketches and not photographs: the building was in need of renovation and pretty run down. But after looking into the guide I had expected a grand house. Some realistic warning what to expect would have been more honest.Also, there is no realistic advice on travel times in mountainous, rural areas. The roads were much more narrow and scary in places (Caha Mountains) and there was no mention of this at all in the guide.Overall, a beautiful, informative travel guide but it would benefit from some realistic updating.
