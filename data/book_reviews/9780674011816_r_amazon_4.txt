For a Great Scientist (of which Eisner is one) to share their love for their subjects and science is a rare event, and Eisner is a master communicator.  He describes how simple observations about everyday insects color patterns, foul and pleasant scent, reactions to shadows or being touched lead to insights into insect behavior that are remarkable, wonderful, and that have similarities in so-called higher life forms.
