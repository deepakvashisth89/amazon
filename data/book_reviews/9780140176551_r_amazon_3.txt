This is the story of the development of a multimedia children’s encyclopedia at Microsoft. It’s interesting in part because multimedia as a software category seemed so important for a few years, but went away so utterly by the turn of the millennium. Profiles of technology development can be fascinating windows into how problems get solved and how people of differing skills can collaborate, or be managed into cooperating towards a common goal, and how the future gets shaped. But this book? Fred Moody has obvious contempt for all the Gen-X Microsoft employees he writes about. He mocks their lack of organization, their neuroses, their office habits. He clearly thinks they would do better with somebody a little bit older in charge, somebody more like Fred Moody himself.For most people, unless they have a tie to Microsoft or the individuals discussed in this book, stick with Tracy Kidder’s “The Soul of a New Machine”.Read moreRead less
