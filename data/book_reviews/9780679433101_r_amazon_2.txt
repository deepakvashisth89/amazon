Julian May has written a magnificent series spanning 6 million years.  The series is so enjoyable, so well-crafted, and engaging.  Not for the meek, this series of books is what I like to call PSY-fi, or psychological fiction.  she writes in depth of the human psyche, and isn't afraid of using big words.  I admit I struggled the first time I read these books, but I found I did understand what I was reading, and loving it!This is the 3rd set of these books I have acquired, as the first 2 series have been stored in attics, and eves, and are not easily accessible.  I have found it easier to spend the money to order the series again, whenever I have wanted to revisit her world for another read.  Enjoy!Read moreRead less
