This was one of the best books I have ever read.  I had read many books about gnosticism and much about spirituality in general and this book really helped me to organize the things I have learned over the years. Many of the gnostic scriptures and stories I had read were meaningful, yet I couldn't say why or how they moved me exactly. This book put into words the things I couldn't and gave new meaning to what I have learned thus far. It left me feeling hopeful that maybe the SEARCH was over and now it is time to simply absorb and experience all the spiritual food I have been digesting all these years. The style of writing was excellent. I really apreciated how the book was written by more than one person and that gave it a unique voice which was sometimes funny and sometimes quite moving.
