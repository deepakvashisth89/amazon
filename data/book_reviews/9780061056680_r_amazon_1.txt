I LOVE WoD, especially the Vampire: The Masquerade series, and their pre-V:TM novels.  Urban grittiness, dark humor, ecstatic despair, wit, well crafted characters and strong foundation of a world with its own rules, and history, for many authors to build upon.  There were several of their earlier books that had a similar cover: A large 'The World of Darkness' logo with a flat color and posterized image, and underneath the logo it said Vampire, Werewolf, Wraith or Mage,  A couple of those were huge favorites of mine, it was back when World of Darkness weren't focused on a continuing storyline and the novels were more of a slice of life from a random vampire or werewolf, wraith or mage.  There weren't recurrent important that eventually would have to be mentioned in every WoD book.  This is one of those early books, pre-Vampire The Masquerade, and it is a collection of short stories.  Some are better than others, but I wasn't turned off or particularly bored by any of them.  The only problem is, these are NOT NEW SHORT STORIES.  They are collected from other short story collections by World of Darkness, such as The Beast Within (collection of vampire short stories), and Death and Damnation (collection of wraith short stories), and I recognized couple from the mage anthology, I can't at the moment recall the name...  Anyhow, WoD's wraiths, werewolves, mages and especially vampires are the best for my buck, and there is no better universe in any other series of novels. It's a great collection, but if you already have the other collections, don't buy it unless it's a from a collectible standpoint, I like the design of the early covers. They seemed to have printed this to make a short story anthology that contained all of their staple 'creatures'.  Another cool aspect is that every story takes place in San Francisco, and these authors know their neighborhoods and streets.
