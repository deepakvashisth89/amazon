Fast light read. This is a re-read and I found that I had changed. Originally I remembered an action packed adventure in sureal Ecological nightmare crescendo of a terraformed Venus. That was Good, but this time I found numerous things I had NOT noticed before. The lines about women NOT being allowed to live on the surface, nor have military marriages, nor serve in the Mercenary Forces. That the forces are mercenary is itself weird, but you have to give it to the author for the story. That the true issue is the demise of the human race from nuclear weapons is again a given. The weird thing is that the real plot centers on the imprinting of the young hero by means of video enactment virtual reality into a hair triggered killer.
