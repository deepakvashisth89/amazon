Now a millionaire, he's back and the new owner of the house that had been in Esme's family for centuries....Living in close proximity to the man Esme had worshiped as a teenager will be difficult enough. But she's worried that Jack will find out a secret she has kept for 10 years....Mistress to a MillionaireMY REVIEW:  Ten years ago one night, she believes he wanted her sister and in fact had been having her sister before that night.  Jack recalls their one night too.  She was 16 on that one night and whisky was involved.  He believed she had been 17.  He does remember that night.  He had always felt guilty about the way he had used her.  Yet Jack left the next day and Esme felt that he had just used her as a fill-in for her much prettier sister, Arabella.  She never contacted Jack again, but he did send her a note telling her to contact him if there were any problems.  She didn't receive the note and never contacted him.  They meet up again when he comes to look at buying her family home.  Esme didn't think that she would ever see Jack again and she has no intentions of revealing her secret to him.  I thought overall Esme was a strong character, but she seemed a bit weak when faced with her nasty mother and sister.  When it was discovered that Esma had become pregnant her mother arranged for the baby to be placed for adoption.  Esme decided to keep the child and went out on her own.  She came back home after a few years and her mother let her stay on the estate rent free in the cottage.  Later her mother sells the cottage out from under her and of course the buyer is Jack Doyle.There was chemistry between Esme and Jack and I liked the way he helped with Harry and the bullies without ever really knowing the truth.  You just know that one of the nasty people from Esme's life will show up to throw a wrench into her life - Arabella is the one.  She believes that Jack couldn't possibly be interested in Esme especially when she, Arabella, is around.  Overall it was an okay story. I, however, was a bit bothered that Jack didn't throw more of a fit when he finds out about Harry, but then that was towards the end of the story.
