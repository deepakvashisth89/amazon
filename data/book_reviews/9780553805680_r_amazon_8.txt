A very good story where two teenagers are left in charge of their siblings after the deaths of the parents.  Reading about their hardships, such as fear they'd be split up, would they lose the farm and were they handling the raising of their siblings well or failing?  All of this takes place back in the days of scrubbing tubs, boards and clothes lines.  I cannot imagine the lifestyle, but I could relate to the variety of personalities involved, the anxiety and better things in life ... things that matter and last.  Janette Oke tells a good story.
