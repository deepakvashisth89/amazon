I found this book to be highly inventive as well as exhibiting authenticity about slavery as manifested in the United States. The author, an escaped slave, does an excellent job of creating realistic scenes even if many of his characters are stock figures (the evil minister, for example). Nonetheless, despite the shortcomings with respect to characterization of some of the characters, the exposure of our most unfortunate institution is compelling. If you first can't get into the story, try again. That's what I did.
