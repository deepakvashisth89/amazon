This book includes short daily reflections, taken from a selection of Emmet Fox's writings. Each day of the year includes a short Bible reference and the writer's insights flowing from that biblical quote. Often these insights shed entirely new light on familiar bible verses. Many of the daily entries are truly inspirational and encourage the reader to reflect on aspects of life in a whole new way. The book is very accessible and easy to understand. It is designed to be read over the course of a year, one page each day. However, it would also work if a reader wanted to open the book at a random page and reflect on that passage. I wouldn't recommend trying to read several days' worth of entries in a single sitting though... give yourself time to reflect on each daily quote to get the most out of this true gem of a book.
