I’m not normally a mystery reader, but I love historical fiction. The characters in this novel are very compelling and complex, not cookie-cutter embodiments of mystery tropes, and the writing is very good. One big hurdle I had to get over early: the connection with Thomas Cromwell made me constantly aware that this book is not Hilary Mantel, not by a long sight. What can ever compete with that? But it’s not this author’s fault he covers some of the same ground. Eventually I relaxed and was able to enjoy this book, realizing how unfair a comparison with Mantel must be. In spite of being set in the same times with some of the same characters, Sansom’s is a very different type of book than Mantel’s great trilogy.
