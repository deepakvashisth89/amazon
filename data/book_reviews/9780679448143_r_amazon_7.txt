I can't believe Random House actually published this book.  As another reviewer mentioned, you have to wade through these two guys, Marty and Tom, going on and on about their relationship, joshing and attempting to be humorous to ferret out the gardening advice which turns out to be pretty lame and often inaccurate.  Strawberries, for example, are listed as an easy to grow ground cover.  Not recommended as a gardening book nor as humor.
