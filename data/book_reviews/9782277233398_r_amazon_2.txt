This is a story of a young man and his family. His family becomes disintegrated and has to do several jobs and at some point he becomes an aspiring poet. He is naive and not ready to face alone the challenges of life. This is the story of how he learns from his mistakes. Very recommended for teenagers and it reminded me a lot of a book by Balzac called Lost Illusions. The book in general is a typical example of French literary realism.
