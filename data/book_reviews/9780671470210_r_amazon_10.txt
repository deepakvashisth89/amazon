Even 50 years after the early books, Dick Frances continues to produce exciting stories with intriguing plots that take the reader into a world apart from our own. The wonderful characters with definite class levels and mixed personalities that you love or loath and amusing ‘dated’ references to the technology of yesteryear. But in some books, like this one, he will take you to other countries, America and the Everglades, South Africa and a gold mine, Canada on a train, and Norway in the snow. As always it has been a joy to experience and with only 13 more ‘Frances’ books left to read I hope that Frances Junior can continue to fill the literary void left by his wonderful father.
