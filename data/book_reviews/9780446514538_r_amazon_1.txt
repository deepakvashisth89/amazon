I was in high school when Kennedy was assassinated, so I was intrigued by the premise of the president surviving the attempt on his life. Granted there are some fairly dry political discussions in the book but the character development and storyline are excellent enough to overcome this.(SPOILER ALERT!)- I was so shocked and outraged when Agent Sullivan was killed that I had to put the book aside for 2 days before I continued reading. It is that intense!I am an avid reader, devouring several books per month, and Promises to Keep is my all time favorite...I've read it 3 times and it is on my bookshelf waiting for the next time.I would recommend this to every reader in America.This would make an excellent movie.Read moreRead less
