I liked Joan Bauer's other books (Squashed and Best Foot Forward), but Thwonk was no where near as funny or organized as her other novels. The main character, "AJ McCreary" was incredibly useless and annoying. All she does throughout the book is complain about how horrible her life is, and she always ignores other people's advice when they try to help her. At the end of the book, AJ doesn't change. The more I read the more annoyed I became. Overall, Thwonk is not worth reading.Read moreRead less
