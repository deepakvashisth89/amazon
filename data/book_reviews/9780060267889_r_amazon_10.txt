What a bargain! Such good value, this treasury includes three full frog and toad books in a handy A5 size. I was worried it would be too big and bulky as a treasury and considered getting the box set instead but I needn't have worried its nice and small. The stories are timeless, some very funny (had me laughing out loud so my daughter couldn't understand me), some charming and some just plain lovely and leave you with a nice warm feeling (like one where frog and toad secretly go to each others gardens to rake up the leaves as a nice surprise, except the wind blows the leaves all over the place again before they get back to their gardens so they never know). I think I like the stories better than my daughter! Shame. This is a book i'd happily read over and over.Bit of an update since I wrote the paragraph above. I bought this for my 5 year old in the hopes that it would get her interested in reading but it's actually my 2.5 year old who likes this best. We've read it every night for over a week and I have to read at least 5 stories (the downside of a treasury I guess). She laughs along every time. But, my originally disinterested 5 year old listens along too and tonight started reading it herself. I think she was quite pleased that she COULD read it. So many stories are just a bit tricky for her. this is just right.
