I just finished the entire Jewels of Texas series. I am 14 books away from reading everything she has ever written and I will honestly say this series comes in the top 3 for me. I don't want to give away too much but i found it very funny and different after Onyx Jewels funeral the illegitimate kids starting coming out of the woodworks. And what marvelously interesting creatures each daughter (and son) turned out to be. Diamond is the first book in the series, She is a pants wearing cowgirl that gets right in there with the ranchers working, and is tough as nails, yet doesn't know the first thing about men or how to be a lady. She thought she was her father's only child before his murder. I love the feeling I got from this series. After i read each book I thought ok this has to be my favorite it was so good, then i felt that way after each book in this series. If you enjoy historical western romances this is the perfect series. In fact i have even tried to convince my hubby to read them.Read moreRead less
