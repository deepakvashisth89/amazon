Bought as part of a promotion, I was attracted to the cover at first. The story, told by Lewis (Louis), reads like something from an old movie from the fifties, but obviously set in our modern world, where computers exist but still use floppy disks.  No spoilers here, but as I got engrossed in the story, I could almost imagine myself being there with him. Some bits actually made me feel a bit prudish,but as Lewis is 13 and discovering things about himself, I should have expected it. A great read, kept me on the edge of my seat, in parts.
