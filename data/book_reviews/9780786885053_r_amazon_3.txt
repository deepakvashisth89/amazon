In his book Pure Drivel, Steve Martin has some very humorous moments.  While some parts of the book fall a little flat because they lack a little direction, I enjoyed most of the book.Some of the chapters which are about writing in itself are humorous to anybody who can see how badly some people write.  These chapters include "Writing is Easy!", "Times Roman Announces Shortage of Periods", and a favorite of mine " A Word from the Words."  "Side Effects" and "The Hundred Greatest Books I've Read" are also among my favorites.  While the stories are short in length, most of them have some sense of closure.  Other stories, such as "The Paparazzi of Plato" and "Mars Probe Finds Kittens" show Martin trying too hard to demonstrate intelligent humor.  As a result, the joke is more cute than laugh out loud funny.As a whole, I did enjoy this book.  Amazon.com lists Jon Stewart's Naked Pictures ... as a book also enjoyed by readers of this book.
