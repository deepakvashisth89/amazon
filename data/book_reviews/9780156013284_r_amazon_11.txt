I got this book for my Kindle as a holiday read while in Sri Lanka and I was glad that I did. Those areas of this gorgeous island that I had also visited I recognised totally as being well observed, and I'm sure the others places referred to were also. However this book is much more than a book set in Sri Lanka. The characters are diverse and complex, and well described such that you could imagine them and know them easily. The interwoven plot lines were quite clever - without being overly complex - and, on the whole, well resolved. Or at least, as satisfactory a resolution as those characters could have expected themselves, given the situations they'd put themselves in, or been put in.On the whole I would certainly recommend Cinnamon Gardens, whether you've visited Sri Lanka or not - and it's a perfect holiday read for that destination.Read moreRead less
