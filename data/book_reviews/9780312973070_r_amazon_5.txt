Allie has been trapped since her early childhood, cowed by her cold (deceased) father, monopolized by her sister and haunted by unfounded guilt about her mother's suicide. She dutifully fulfils her role and accepts that life has nothing warm and wonderful waiting for her.Jeff is a broken man, riddled with such guilt and remorse about shooting a young man in self-defence that he turns his back on life and retreats to the bottom of a bottle.The sheriff brings them together when Allie desperately needs help for basic maintenance on her property. Both are reluctant but see not alternative.The story is a wonderfully tender romance as Allie and Jeff complement each other step by step. Allie finds the warmth and love that she never expected and she in turn brings out the best in him, allowing him to regain his sense of self-worth and purpose. Their relationship has a constancy and honesty that sees them though the malicious actions of others.The story is beautifully written.
