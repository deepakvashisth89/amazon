This monumental work was intended for brighter intellects than mine but it is nevertheless interesting. Gertrude Stein obviously was immensely charismatic but boy, was she pleased with herself. Such writing that is quoted in this book to me, at least, is unmitigated gobbledegook. However, despite polarising people from beginning to end, she undoubtedly charmed and influenced a great many . Her poor wife, Alice Toklas seems to have had a pretty tough time of it and to have been ill served by Stein at the end of her life.
