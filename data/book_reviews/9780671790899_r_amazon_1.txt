This book is no longer considered canon but I have to tell you: when I was a kid in 1980, this book came out right when the movie came out. It was absolutely fascinating to me. It was probably the coolest Star Trek book I've ever owned and I'm glad I was able to track down a nice copy of it again. The art is fantastic.
