This is a very funny fantasy instead of a cozy murder mystery.  Professor Peter Shandy and two of his friends, along with spouses.  are at an academic conference in England to finds ways to fight the giant hogweed that is terrorizing the countryside .  They manage to fall (maybe through a convenient hole in the space-time continuum)  into a fantasy realm where they must solve the resident's problems. Then they are able to return to reality and conveniently get rid of the hogweed.  I enjoyed the story.  It helps to have read the previous books to understand the characters.
