Amazing novel! Nero Wolfe and Archie arrive fully themselves - in thinking, personality, values, quirks, everything!  I’ve never read a first novel  with such formed characters. Rex Stout must have put a great deal of thinking into Nero Wolfe and Archie before starting the series - I’m VERY impressed!I’ve read most of the novels, and this is one of the very best! And I’ve given 5 stars to every single one! So this one deserves way more than 5 stars!This novel is especially interesting because the plot is intricate, twists and turns, changes dramatically several times, involves unusual characters, and hangs with suspense until the very end.This is the BEST NERO WOLFE BOOK I’ve read!
