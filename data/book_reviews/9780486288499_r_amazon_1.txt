My grandmother read these stories to all of her grandchildren, of which I was one. I read them to my children and grandchildren and my granddaughter is now reading them to her little ones.  Each story uses animal characters to teach children good morals to live by in a fun and interesting way.  These books were written when my grandmother was a child and there's a reason why they have been reprinted over all these years.  I recommend them highly.
