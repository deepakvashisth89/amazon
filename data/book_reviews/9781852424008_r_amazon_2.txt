I finished this book and immediately started reading it all over again from the beginning. Which says it all, right? It reminded me of a more contemporary version of Jane Bowles‘ Two Serious Ladies, or perhaps a more plot-heavy Speedboat (Renata Adler) - though still fairly skimpy on plot. If you like cerebral funny books about slightly meandering lost women, this is for you!
