There are many stories of people affected by a major hurricane in Florida. There are so many characters that it was difficult to remember who was who and what part that he or she played in the plot. There are financial manipulations, love stories, and a collection of varied life styles, of the characters ultimately being affected in some dramatic and life changing way by the hurricane. The storm itself is described in very vivid photographic terms. I enjoyed reading the book, although I admit I was confused at times by the use of so many different characters.Read moreRead less
