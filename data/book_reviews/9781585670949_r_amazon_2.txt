I bought this book because it was coupled with a book by Cara Black. Talk about mislabeling! The dialog is a pitiful attempt to replicate the delightful repartee of contemporaneous period pieces. The gratuitous racism might have been real (but I doubt it). The story builds slowly and I never developed the least interest in any of the characters.
