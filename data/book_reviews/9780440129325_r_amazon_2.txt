This is one of the more original Scotland Yard detective books I've read, though, sadly, it falls a little short.The book begins with the death of Miss Holland, the young, attractive, cheerfully competent headmistress of Bramthorpe College for Girls. The local DCI believes the death to be either accident or suicide. The Chief Superintendent finds it hard to believe that the cheerful Miss Holland would commit suicide, and one of the town's local gentry (who, it turns out, was going to marry Miss Holland), find it hard to believe that her poisoning was accidental. So, they call in Scotland Yard.What makes this different from most Scotland Yard detective series is that a team of four detectives arrive (most of the novels of this type offer only a detective pair, each one a foil to the other). The four detectives who arrive all seem like cardboard cutouts. They're given little depth, and only the briefest mentions of life outside of work. The focus in this book is the secondary characters -- the local residents, especially those at the school where Miss Holland taught. Every secondary character is well-drawn, and are much more "alive" than the detectives.As for the plot -- it is interesting enough. It's typical British Cosy stuff. The momentum of the investigation moves the story along, though it does, at points, seem to drag across the same territory over and over. And, it doesn't follow the typical "formula" of this genre of book -- there are few red herrings, no real "dark secrets"; in fact, the suspect pool is rather small, so small that you sort of feel pushed along into believing the ending (which, while not awful, was no great surprise either).Would I read another book by this author? Perhaps, though he's out-of-print. If I found a used copy around, I'd probably pick it up. But, I wouldn't rush to read another either.Overall, in spite of the flaws, there's something enjoyable about the tale. There's just enough dry humor, and just enough questions to keep you reading right through to the end. I'd give it a 3.5 star rating if I could.
