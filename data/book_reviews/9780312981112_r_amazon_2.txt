A good book that takes a look at serious female offenders residing at three locations, Bedford Hills, Dwight and Chowchilla, which are all maximum security womens' prisons. This book looks at approximately a dozen different female criminals and has a chapter telling their stories. It's well written and informative and a very good book for anyone interested in true crime, murders or female criminals. I've been reading true crime books for over 20 years and have read thousands of them. On the scale of 1 to 10, 1 being the worst book ever and 10 being the best book ever written I would give this book a 6, and I like all of the other books that I've read by author Wensley ClarksonRead moreRead less
