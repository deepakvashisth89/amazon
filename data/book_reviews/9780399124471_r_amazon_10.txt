Being a big Marilyn Monroe fan, I read Susan's other book 'Marilyn & me' many years ago, but never knew she had also written an autobiography until I found it on here a few weeks ago.Well written, funny & sad, Susan comes across as a very likable, interesting person. There are many things that I did not know, for example I was shocked to read about her volatile/often violent marriage to the actor Chistopher Jones. It's also nice to read about her legendary parents, Lee & Paula, from a daughters perspective (having only read mostly negative things about Paula), she tells us about their good & bad sides & what it was like growing up in such a theatrical environment, not to mention all the big stars that passed through their home at one time or another.All in all, definitely worth reading- esp. if you're a Strasberg fan!Read moreRead less
