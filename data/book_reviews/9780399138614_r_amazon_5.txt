I started reading horror in my late teens, Shirley Jackson, Stephen King, a couple by Straub. I was mesmerized. So I went for a deep dive through the genre. I was sadly disappointed. Most of them were just hyped up trash reads. Phil Rickman’s Curfew is in the top ten of horror stories I ever read. He brought back the deep connection between the people, place and the skewed reality of excellent storytelling. I am pleased that he might do more.Read moreRead less
