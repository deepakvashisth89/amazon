The Mammoth Books do a fine job in their respective categories, however, this collection surpasses them all.  It is perfectly conceived with representative stories from the masters of the noir/hardboiled style as well as underappreciated authors who created a few gems.  Black Pudding by David Goodis is remarkable and probably my favorite in the collection, but it really is hard to pick a favorite since there are so many stories.  The best part is that each story stands on its own and they don't start running into each other.  Long after you put the book down, you will remember each story.Read moreRead less
