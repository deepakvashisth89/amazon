As with every book I've read by Dekker, this one is another great story. From being the boy who healed thousands with an envious love, passion, commitment, and connection to christ that even the most devout followers can scarcely imagine, to being completely lost in his faith, Caleb's story of new beginnings, surrender, and healing mesmerize from the first page. This series was another one in which i could not put down. I would highly recommend this series to any fans of Dekker, and to anyone who struggles with life and faith.Read moreRead less
