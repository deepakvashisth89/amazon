Overall, this "Modern" translation is not bad. Her overall goal of making the high intellect and deep concepts more easily accessible works, however, for me at least, it all falls down. She appears to not be a strong woman of Christian faith and believes that all faith leads to Heaven. She also does not believe in Satan or Hell. As a result, she removes all references to Hell and Satan, the great evil that manipulates you. Instead of Satan coming against you, it is merely the fact your soul is not unified with the great divine God. She refers to it often as the broken self, or the unfulfilled self. If you were going to translate a Christian text, you do not have the authority to decide that the Christian themes, are not truly what the author meant.
