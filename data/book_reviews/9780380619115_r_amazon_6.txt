I love the Peter Shandy series.  There's something very charming and whimsical about these novels.  The eccentric characters and their strangely wonderful names are addictive.  There may be murder and certainly mayhem but the villains are mostly motivated by greed not psychopathy (in the main).  So restful not to be inundated with forensics or psychology, or tortured detectives seeking some sort of salvation.This isn't to say the author doesn't provide a good mystery, she does but in a way that doesn't shock or disturb a reader.  Her villains are dastardly and her heroes are  charming eccentrics.Certainly, these are not intricate crime novels laden with intricate forensic details or twisted plots.
