The book is thoroughly enjoyable; I could not put it down. Since at one time, I lived in the area in which the book is set I am well aware of the area and the cultures.  My only negative comment is Jickie's sudden attack upon the doctors.  The reader is not aware of the discontent so rampant in Jickie and this comes as a surprise.
