Another great book and a wonderful ending for this series, Mind Kill takes newly promoted Tanaka and retired Fogarty on their greatest case, which is really the closure of an unresolved case from the beginning of Fogarty's career. Now Tanaka stands alone, having lost both his mentors. Is this truly the end of the journey?
