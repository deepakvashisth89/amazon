Most of the chapters are a distillation of a given scholarly paper on the subject. As the author admits many of these studies were on captive and laboratory deer and probably not useful for understanding wild deer behavior. So more than half the book is an overview of material that neither the author nor the reader will find useful.The meat of the book is the premise that esterus is timed around the first full moon after the equinox. It gives a good overview of deer behavior throughout the rut. And the best hunting strategies for any given period in the breeding cycle. The chart outlining these dates for a given year is now expired, but just match the current year's full moon date to a year on the chart with the same date and it will all work out. There is also a lot of good advice on effective hunting techniques in general, but nothing a savvy hunter doesn't already know.I had expected more discussion of how the moon effects deer's feeding and bedding cycles during the non-breeding season.
