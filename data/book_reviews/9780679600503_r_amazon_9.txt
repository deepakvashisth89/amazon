Though the story is of Christian Catholic priests, it is written so beautifully creating an image after image of what the priests had to go through in New Mexico just after the Americans bought it. It was this very point that pushed me to select this book in the current world of America under Mr Trump wanting to acquire more land by force if necessary.The book doesn’t deal with such politics overtly, but it records injustices that were handed to the native people of the land.Another interesting point to note - in addition to the wisdom it contains- are the usage of old English words which would have been the norm of the time when the book was written. Today those words are a pleasure to learn.I would recommend this book to anyone who wants to escape into a bygone time. Really enjoyed reading and losing myself in this bookRead moreRead less
