Holmes does a marvelous job of sharing a fictional/kinda-could-be-true story of the couple who were chosen to bring the Saviour of the world into their world.  Tender, enlightening, sweet, funny and amazing are some of the adjectives to describe this read.  It makes a great gift for people who enjoy novels. This is not an exact account, it's just Marjorie Holmes idea of what may have been.  She makes no claims to an exact true story and her rendition is charming and heartfelt.  This is 1/3 books in this series.  Worth a read, worth gifting and is surprisingly enlightening in its special way!  ENJOY!!Read moreRead less
