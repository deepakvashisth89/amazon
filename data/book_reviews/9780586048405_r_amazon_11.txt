Ever wanted to know about the history of the Mongol conquest of central Asia? This novel is basically Piers Anthony's attempt to tell the saga in a format where it isn't obvious that he's shoving a history lesson down our throats. And he does it very well.The central character gets snatched out of the depths of the past via a time scoop, and flung into the far future, where the major entertainment is a vast, galaxy-spanning role-playing game. (This was written 10 years before the internet even impinged on the world, and twenty years before MMORPGs became a staple of modern amusement). The players fly spacecraft and duel with high-tech weapons, but they are still playing out the history of the Mongols. The hero, snatched from the realities of the past, uses his knowledge of what is going on to become a powerful warlord (no prizes for working out who he will eventually be).The book is entertaining, but by no means the best novel Anthony has written. I'm not entirely surprised it was never successful enough to warrant a sequel, but if you want something different and unusual, away from the typical science fantasy novel, this isn't a bad choice.Read moreRead less
