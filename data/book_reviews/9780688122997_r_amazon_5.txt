Faludi provides an eloquent analysis of where we are (circa the turn to the 21st century) in terms of gender issues, with a particular focus on masculinity and its challenges.  She combines ethnography, interviews, and popular culture references to create a snapshot of these issues at that time.  More importantly, she uses historical data and references to show how we got there, and thoughtful analysis to suggest effective ways forward.
