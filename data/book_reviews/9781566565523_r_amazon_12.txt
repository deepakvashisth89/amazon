Author David Ray Griffin has done some extensive research on the events of 9/11 and raises a number of vital questions.  Why didn't NORAD send up interceptor planes immediately when four planes were hijacked?  On whose authority would these planes have been sent to investigate?  If it was a commercial jet which struck the Pentagon, why weren't the engines or any other recognizable part of the aircraft ever recovered?  How did the pilot of this plane who barely made it through flight school perform such perfect aerial acrobatics to bring this plane down to ground level and slam into the Pentagon?  Why was the steel from the wreckage of the Twin Towers taken away, cut up and sold to China before it could be analyzed to determine why those buildings collapsed in 10 seconds?
