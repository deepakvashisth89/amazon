The One Legged Cricket is a genuine, deeply personal story of a woman's journey back from a devastating loss.  It is an engaging testament to the versatility of the human spirit and its desire to connect  with others.Julie's life is turned upside down, the pain is too much for her and she retreats from life unable to bear her loss. Then something wonderful happens, she meets Ulysses, 'the one legged cricket'.  Their relationship, real or imagined, is her first step in embracing life again.  It is a must read.  A truly touching and inspiring story that everyone can relate to. There are times in life when one cannot seem to find the light in all the darkness that pain can sometimes bring.  This little is book is overflowing with inspiration. I couldn't put it down. Although it starts out with a tragady it leads you where we all must go if we want to triumph over the pain of a great loss. C.J. Macgenn touched my heart with this enchanting story.
