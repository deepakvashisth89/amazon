A new door for fans of intrigue has been deftly and intelligently opened.  Game theory (as exemplified by the well-known Prisoners Dilemma) as a backdrop for a novel is unique and refreshing.  And MacDonald is a great writer.  His choice of writing the book in first person, rather unusual for a thriller, was a good one because readers can really get into the main character's head as the mind games unfold and explode.  The settings are fun - delightfully sunny African beaches and dusty stoic Oxford brick.  Fast-paced and memorable.Read moreRead less
