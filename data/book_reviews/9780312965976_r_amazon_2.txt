Enjoyed this book and the story of Maddie and Dan. There were plenty of twists to keep you on your toes, but I did get bogged down in some of the details about Michael. I found his side of the story a little odd. But my heart was in it and I had to know if Maddie would get her love story. There was also a great cast of characters although I found myself annoyed with many of them for not supporting Maddie. I was pleased with the ending and also just really happy to read a stand alone book that wasn't a part of a series with a cliffhanger. A great leisurely story with a nice mix of romance, suspense, and steam.Read moreRead less
