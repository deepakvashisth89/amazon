This is by far one of the best books ever written about Kenny Everett that I have read. It is not a biography as such, but more the story of how Cleo and Kenny meet and their life experiences together. The highs and lows, the laughter and the sadness of two really close friends. It is one of my favourite books of all time and I have been hoping for years that this would eventually be released on the Kindle(and I'm over the moon to see it's finally here)! Cleo Rocos has written a moving account of her time spent with Kenny. It is clearly not one of those books written just to make a bit of money but more a true and honest account of a great friendship and it comes across that way. It is written from the heart. It wouldn't really matter if you had never even heard of Kenny and Cleo - it's a story about love and friendship which will stay with you. I only wish that Cleo Rocos would write more books because she is clearly a talented writer. What more can I say? Five Stars!Read moreRead less
