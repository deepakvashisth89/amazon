This is the second book I've read by Jeffrey Carver. This world of Star Riggers is a breath taking ride. The book takes space travel in a totally different direction than I ever imagined. The "riggers" must pilot with their minds. They must have a certain personality and imagination to succeed. The premise of space flight using pilots imagination is exciting with new challenges.Read Jeffrey's book Panglor as a prequel, or jump right in with this book. Both ways you win.
