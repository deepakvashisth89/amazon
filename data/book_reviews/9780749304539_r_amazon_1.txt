The Willow King mysteries of Natasha Cooper are of an old style that had been modernized.  There is little explicit violence; all sex is tasteful,  but the plots and characterization is modern.  I was reminded somewhat of  P.D. James.Willow King leads a double life -- bureaucrat in a fictional  British government bureau -- Department of Old Age Pensions and as a writer  of romance novels. This twist of personalities helps to make the story  interesting.The characterizations and the evoking of a keen sense of  place were highlights for me.  The actual plot, though interesting, is not  particularly unusual, although there was a very interesting twist in the  end that I should have seen coming, but didn't.Read moreRead less
