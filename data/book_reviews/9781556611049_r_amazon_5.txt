This historical is set in 1852 in California and has a 15-year-old heroine, so I think it's a young adult story. Corrie's the eldest of 5 children. Her father and Uncle Nick ran off to California when the kids were young, leaving their mother to care for them. When they got word of where Uncle Nick was, the mother took them on a wagon train to go find him, but she died along the way. Now they're in California, but how are they to find their Uncle? Will he want them? How will they get along if he takes them in? And why did their father leave? All of these questions are answered as kind people help the kids find their relatives and adventures happen.The main characters reacted realistically to events and were likable. Some parts pushed believability, but most YA adventures do. A woman who helped them answered some of Corrie's questions about God (and she did a good job of it). There was no sex or bad language.
