Another very very well written story by an author I look forward to each new book going back to The Note.  This books takes one of the strangest times in the history of the Roman Catholic Church when it had 3 Popes.  Add to that the historical figure of John Hus and bringing the living during that time to us through the life of a talented and passionate young girl, a local Lord and Hus in the country of Bohemia as victims during the struggle of a church trying to survive its internal strife and you have the making of an unpredictable unfolding of events all leading to a perfect ending.
