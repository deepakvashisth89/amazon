This is a silly book--reads like a stand-up comic's routine about married life. There's nothing helpful or thought-provoking here and a lot of it is somewhat stereotypical. It's very lightweight. If you read at even a modest rate, you can finish it in one sitting. I was expecting some wisdom mixed in with the humor but it's really just an account of a woman who is enjoying her life and wants to talk about it... to an audience willing to pay $16 each to listen.I wasn't really expecting a marriage counseling session, but I did think that if it got published, there would be a little meat on the bones of the text.
