An old favorite that I recently re-read, having just been at the vendor table sitting next to the marvelous David Gerrold this past March. I read this when it first came out in the early 70's, and it struck me at the time as being a very important story. Upon re-reading, my opinion hasn't changed much. The concept of 'being human' and what that means is fraught with all manner of perils, and Gerrold does an admirable job of navigating it.
