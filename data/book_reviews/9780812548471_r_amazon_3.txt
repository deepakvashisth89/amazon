I loved The Firekeeper!  Not only is this a blockbuster of a story, it is filled with fascinating, memorable characters that Robert Moss created out of impeccable research and his own rich imagination.  All the elements one could wish for in a good read are here:  a stunning evocation of time and place, the time of the French and Indian wars; a plot filled with history, mystery, and drama; characters that come alive and that I wanted to follow to the end, whether I liked them or not; and love and lust in good measure.  Woven and flowing throughout the novel is the importance of dreaming to the lives of the characters, particularly but not exclusively the Mohawk.  The power of dreams to foretell the future, and the shamanic way of healing, are essential underpinnings of the novel and presage Robert Moss's profound work and many nonfiction books on Dreaming and Dreamwork  I highly recommend this novel!Read moreRead less
