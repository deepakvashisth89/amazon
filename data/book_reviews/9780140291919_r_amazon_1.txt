This book is a jewel...but not for everyone.  The book is written about a period in history...1st quarter of the 19th century...which I have always enjoyed.  What absolutely fascinated me was the level of detail contained in the book.  Tyng writes about his life at sea, visiting different ports, overcoming dangerous storms and putting down mutinous crews.  One wonders how he was able to accomplish what he did, although reading his story one is able to see what developed his character.  Some may be turned off by the detail and discount it as minutia, but I found it riveting.  You cannot know what it must have been like to sail through a gale or hurricane and to survive, but this will bring you as close as you can imagine to the real experience.Read moreRead less
