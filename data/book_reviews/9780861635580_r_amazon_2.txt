'Five Get Into a Fix' was written in 1958.  It is the seventeenth book in Enid Blyton's popular  'Famous Five Series'.The setting for 'Five Get Into a Fix' is set in a different location  - The Famous Five are staying in a chalet in the Welch mountains and having lots of fun in the snow.  There's some strange business going on at night in the mysterious house near the chalet.  But according to the caretaker nobody has lived there for years.  t's up to the Five to investigate!  Another mystery to solve as 'The Five get into a Fix'.'Although, these stories are around 50 to 70 years old, they remain fresh and exciting today.What is Blyton's evergreen book formula?It's simple! Enid Blyton created 'believable' characters complete with faults. Young readers identified with these kids back then, and still do so today!5 STARS for 'Five get into a Fix' as well as the ENTIRE 'Famous Five' series!I read this book as a child, and now I'm sharing the excitement with the little ones some 60 years later!Hope my review was helpful ...
