This is a great slow cooker cookbook! All the recipes I have tried so far are a big hit.  The recipes are not too complicated and don't involve a lot of pre-preparation.  When a recipe calls for chopped veges (onion, celery, carrot, etc) I chop the evening before and store in a sealed container in the refrigerator.  This way, in the morning I just have to toss this into the slow cooker with the other ingredients and can be done in a minute.
