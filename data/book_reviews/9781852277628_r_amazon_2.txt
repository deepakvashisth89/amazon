I stumbled across this book while browsing idly, and decided to read it as I had recently finished reading Mercury and Me. As Kenny Everett and Freddie Mercury knew each other, I thought it would make an interesting comparison.I have to say it is the better book by far. Even if you had never heard of Kenny Everett, Cleo Rocos tells a wonderful story of an unusual friendship that lasted many years until Everett's death. It's captivating, hilarious, touching and sad.
