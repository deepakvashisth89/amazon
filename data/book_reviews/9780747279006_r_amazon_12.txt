I have read around two thirds of all Laymon's books and enjoyed pretty much all of them. Of course you have to be in a Laymon mood and ready for a cheap thrill ride - but what an excellent ride Laymon can take you on. I wish I had discovered Laymon when I was 13 as he would be the perfect guilty secret writer for any teenage boy. That said, 35 years older and he can still hold my attention. Now having said all that, Alarums struggled to hold its own. The plot is less well conceived than in his other writing and the thrills, perhaps, felt a little too cheap. That said, you still want to know what's going to happen and there is a nice trick in the tail. If you are looking to read your first Laymon, don't go for Alarums, there are better Laymon choices out there.
