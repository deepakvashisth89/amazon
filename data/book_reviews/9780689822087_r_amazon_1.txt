Great book, character development, light hearted, easy to read, perfect for my daughter . . . and the third in the series (Leading Ladies) is even better!  Marlee's writing only gets more compelling and the book gets harder and harder to put down.  I love how engaging Megan is and with such an indomitable spirit, moreso than her "signing disabled" classmates (no offense intended).  I've started my hearing daughter down the path of learning sign language and deaf culture and given her age and reading ability, this light-hearted book series fits a niche I was looking for.  Some other books I'm having her read as she gets older:  "A Loss for Words," "Finding Zoe," "Inside Deaf Culture" (very rich in content, although a much heavier academic read).
