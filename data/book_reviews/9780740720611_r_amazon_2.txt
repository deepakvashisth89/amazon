Never before had I read the trials and tribulations of love in the way Cathy Guisewite described it in the introduction of this little book.  Because of Dad's love for his daughter and the later reinforcement of the fairy tales we read as children, we as women are programed to find love.  Social changes and circumstances, however, have disappointed us.  It's disconcerting to find out that there are so many losers out there and you have to search far and wide amongst the frogs for that elusive prince on a white horse.
