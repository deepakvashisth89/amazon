Reagan was a fascinating man. We hear about the Alzheimer and how it impacted his later years but the man that ran things was a thoughtful intelligent guy. This book is great to let you see the core that powered him for so long. I got this for my then in college son who was born the year the Berlin Wall came down and was reading every presidential and powerful leader's biography that he could find as he was pulling together what he might want to do for his life choices. If you are reading biographies, this is a do-not-miss bio. You can read history books all day long but nothing beats reading it in his own words.
