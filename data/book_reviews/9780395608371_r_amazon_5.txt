Although a little heavy going at times, I really enjoyed this book and the alternative perspective it provides to the much debated issue around forestry.  The parallels to the Australian situation are so similar, it is like reading about the Australian forestry debate just with different names.If you think you have all the facts in the debate on environmental politics with respect to endangered species and forestry you would be best to think again and spend a few nights around the fire reading this book.A must read.Read moreRead less
