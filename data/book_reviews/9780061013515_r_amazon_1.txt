With a wealth of information about New England fishing, especially fishing for swordfish, Junger takes us onto the longliner Andrea Gail, leaving from Gloucester in October, 1991, with a crew of six.  They were overtaken by a confluence of three storms that merged and created unheard-of waves, winds, and rain.  Ultimately the boat sank, and all on board were lost.  We meet their families, wives, and girlfriends, and we feel as though we’re on the boat with them as the storm intensifies.  We learn in excruciating detail what it feels like to drown.
