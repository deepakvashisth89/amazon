A fascinating, haunting story that pulled me in on the first page and didn't let me go until the last line.  On one level it is a love story spanning three decades.  On another level it is a study of the nature of  love  - of passionate love that brings dissimilar people together, of a  father's tough love that banishes his son's lover for his own good,  creating misunderstanding and misery, of compassionate love and of a  ruinous father/daughter relationship.  Bogner writes in an engaging,  optimistic style that makes the book hard to put down.-- Gisela H.Read moreRead less
