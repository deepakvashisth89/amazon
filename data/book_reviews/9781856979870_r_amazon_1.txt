Absolutely wonderful collection of poetry. Exactly what I was looking for. My son just turned 3 and has really been enjoying having me read him poetry before bed. We have some old books the best being an old Scribner's poetry anthology from the 50s. This collection contains classic poetry from all eras. I did not want some silly Hallmark style verses or nonsense kids poetry. This book includes fun poetry such as The Owl and the Pussycat as well as more serious poems like The Charge of the Light Brigade. There are some beautiful Native American and African verses as well as more obscure post-colonial poetry. Kids are never too young to start on good literature.Read moreRead less
