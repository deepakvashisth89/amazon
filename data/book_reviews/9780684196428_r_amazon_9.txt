If you enjoy the writings of M.F.K. Fisher, odds are you will enjoy this book. Since I am one of the few people who finds her writing style cumbersome, clunky and annoying to read, I did not enjoy this one, which is full of similar devices. I enjoy books that flow, with simple sentences and fluid prose, and this one does not really deliver on that front. It's not the authors' fault, it's his writing style, and I happen not to enjoy it. The recipes appear excellent though, and I may actually cook from it, which may elevate my rating.
