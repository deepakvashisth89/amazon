An entertaining read.  It would appeal more to the military action enthuiasts than to "hard" sci-fi readers since the basic structure of the book deals with men at arms in harm's way rather than mind bending or fantastic ideas and alien environments.  Sure the action takes place on distant worlds and under a future political structure, but the stories are humanist; some of the stories even take place on what might be called a "backward" world or someplace more fitting in human history rather than the future.Read moreRead less
