I read this book when I was 14; big mistake because I was way too young to appreciate it. However, I never quite forgot it because it was one of the few romance novels with older protagonists (they're both in their forties) who actually had a whole life before getting together. Rereading it 17 years later, I enjoyed the emotional intricacies Reavis has weaved, bringing our focus to the trials of romance, culture and family.
