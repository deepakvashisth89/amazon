I was quite happy to come across a book that was devoted specifically to Pagan Gods, on both the front of a book taking a Pagan angle, and one focusing on Gods instead of Goddesses (a book subject in somewhat short supply).That said, the book is exactly what it claims to be: a begginners guide.  THere is good information in here about Thoth, the Oak and Holly Kings, the Green Man, the Horned God, and so forth, but given the books size, it was all very quickly imparted, and as such left me with the feeling that I'd delved only a little, not reaching any real depth.Still, that said, there is a great beginning in this book.  Were the Mooreys to put out another, in depth title with a similar topic, I would not hesitate to go buy it.  If you are just beginning in Paganism or Wicca, and want to get yourself a firmer grasp on the basics of the Gods, grab this book and settle down for a great introductory read.Read moreRead less
