Not the best of Dorothy Sayers, but an interesting collection of 17 short stories, only two of which include Lord Peter. The most interesting aspect of the collection is seeing Sayers weave captivating stories in which even the brief space of a short story she can teach something significant about a subject I would never have thought about.
