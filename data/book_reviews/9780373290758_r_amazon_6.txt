I thoroughly enjoyed this book. The love scenes were poetically written and, although I didn't quite get into the Swedish male MC at first, I was loving him by the end. His comment about how married couples should treat their time in the bedroom was touching, and it was good advice as well. The only thing I didn't like was that the plot was predictable in places and seemed to falter and lose steam near the end [I found the back and forth to town over her legal issue to be confusing rather than intriguing - esp. when she seemed to forget all about it after returning home.], but it did pick back up. All in all, a good book.Read moreRead less
