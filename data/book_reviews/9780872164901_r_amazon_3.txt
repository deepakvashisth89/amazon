I started with Gellis' murder mysteries, and they were well written, so I figured I'd go to the bed-rock historical romance books.  But what I found is that the plots are basically the same -- except for the odd murder which is neither here nor there.  Basically the plots revolve around when the protagonist is going to get laid -- in spite of many obstructions, some self-imposed.  Let me not be too sarcastic.  These books are will written, the characters are believable and the action is gripping.  And there is a tremendous amount of historical, political intricacy that is woven into the plots. From an era of twisted, complex, political and personal drama. These are really pretty good stories, in a kind of medieval way -- which is consistent with the settings.  And let me repeat, Gellis' stories are well written.  I'm not sure I'm up for more of these romance dramas, but we shall see after I finish this one -- which I repeat is very, very well written.Read moreRead less
