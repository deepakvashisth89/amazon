This Author attempts to read Speer's mind on a number of very important details and, using this mythical mindreading technique, assigns guilt to Speer for many things. Clearly, he has only his own interpretation of many events and discards much of what Speer himself wrote. Tellingly, the internet now contains numerous videos of actual one-on-one interviews with Speer. The reader of this book can contrast what is found here with Speer's authenticity and interpretation of events in another way, one I found much more satisfying.
