This book is really two volumes combined; they do work well together, but the mass isn't for the faint of heart of the short of attention.  Those who persevere will find a wealth of insights into the full history of states, which Bobbitt uses to look through the fin de sicle "end of history " noise that was all the rage when the book was written. A reader from 2015 will find some of Bobbitt's predictions strange, even laughable, but others are eerily prescient.
