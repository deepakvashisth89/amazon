The Werewolf Chronicles is the first werewolf novel I ever read, and I've been hooked on werewolves since.It is so hard to find a decent werewolf novel anymore. Most are cheesy romance novels or badly written "Horrors". Please. None have come close to The Werewolf Chronicles. I thoroughly enjoyed the book and if you can get ahold of a copy, I highly recommend it.It's the story of an L.A. dancer who goes to her uncles Wisconsin ranch for some badly needed time off. But one night she's attacked by a large creature, but survives at the cost of her beloved Uncles life.Guilt stricken, she returns to L.A. and quickly rehabs, determined to get her life back on track. But, the werewolfs curse sends her life spiraling out of control.This has become one of my all time favorite novels. Check it out.Read moreRead less
