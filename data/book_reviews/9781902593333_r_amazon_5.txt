On buying this book I was concerned that it would be too serious and depressing though it really was a pleasure to read. Many of the peoms where about certain animals in a very humorous way, along with some more meaningfull but still enjoyable peoms. He manages to discuss issues important to vegans without it becoming depressing or making us think of all the things we disagree with in this world. Though many of the peoms do discuss the issues associated with milk and eggs so it may not be as good for vegetarians, though I have a vegetarian friend who enjoyed it grealy. Some of the issues even drift into other forms of animal rights that aren't as commonly discussed, for example "gender pretender" which discusses dogs are sometimes treated like toys. Yet it still somehow manages to make you laugh at the end.A brilliant and fabulous book, my respect for benjamin Zephaniah is now much higher.
