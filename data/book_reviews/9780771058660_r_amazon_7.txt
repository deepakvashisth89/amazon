This book is like a dessert after reading “Alaska” by the same author. It is much shorter, it spans just few years of time and it has only few main protagonists. But, boy oh boy, what a protagonists they are! British Lord, Evelyn Luton, hears about gold in Klondike and takes a fancy to go on expedition to Klondike (North Canada) taking the route that would not cross US territory. As his company he takes three others high born (but lower than him in a social status) friends and one Irish servant. Thus the big adventure begins. Once I started reading I could not stop.It is a wonderful adventure book but there is also a socio-psychological factor in it. Although probably not intended as such by author, the book reads almost like a satire about British aristocracy. The main protagonists do not lack knowledge, honor or stamina. But following the rigid set of social rules leads to a tragedy that could be easily avoided.I highly recommend this book to everybody who is interested in Klondike gold rush or just seeking a good adventure book.
