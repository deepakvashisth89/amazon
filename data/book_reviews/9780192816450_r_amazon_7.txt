William Beckford's 18th century Vathek is the tale of a self-indulgent middle eastern caliph who is basically a spoiled brat that everyone must kowtow to in order to stay alive. After multiple displays of his extravagance, along with his aggressive mother, he goes on a journey that has promised untold wealth. He does meet the girl of his dreams, but in the end he is hoisted by his own petard.Beckford doesn't hold back in terms of the excesses, while adding quite a bit of humor to the overall story.
