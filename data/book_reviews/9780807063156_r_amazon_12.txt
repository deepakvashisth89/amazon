I had heard of this book from the fabulous podcast Backlisted, but i came away from the book utterly underwhelmed, and failed to finish. On paper, the story should have been riveting, but the characters were unsympathetic, the dialogue between Ursa and Mutt was banal and boring, and many aspects of the story perpetuated a stereotype of underage sex, abusive marriage etc. In other words, there was very little to learn here.Read moreRead less
