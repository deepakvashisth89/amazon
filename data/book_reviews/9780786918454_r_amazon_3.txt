This paperback extra is packed with great ideas!  The only thing is that it was written for 3.0, before 3.5 came out, However, the spells are still very useful, and can easily be used in 3.5 or Pathfinder.  Three spells I regularly use myself: familiar pocket, augment familiar, and fortify familiar.Also included is a nice little piece on a mage's private home, including stables, a well, and private room for the apprentice.All in all, a very useful addition to your gaming books, if you like playing wizards or sorcerers.  (And it's currently cheap enough that it won't break you!)Some of these spells have crept into the 3.5 Spell Compendium, so if you want to just purchase some of them on the fly, this book is a good way to do it!Read moreRead less
