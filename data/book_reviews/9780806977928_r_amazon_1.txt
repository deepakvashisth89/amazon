This author gives cogent explanations of the ramifications of a blood sugar problem on the body. As a naturopath, he does not blindly cling to accepted science. He presents findings from a variety of case studies and group studies, and makes common-sense conclusions, showing just why and how this can actually affect your health. Also, he gives his own research of diets that allow your body to heal itself (while reminding readers that the diet cure only addresses one type of hypoglycemia--he does not overreach his topic or pretend to know more than he does, which I appreciate).
