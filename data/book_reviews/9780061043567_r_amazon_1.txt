While I'd read the Lord Peter stories before- I had not read the Monty Egg ones, nor the ones that did not fit into a series-  and they were WONDERFUL.Monty Egg is an engaging fellow- he sells fancy spirits- and his curiosity and intelligence means he solves various mysteries without exactly intending to, going into it.But the stand-alone stories were my favorites. Whether they were more cynical, or humorous, or both- they kept me on the edge of my seat.
