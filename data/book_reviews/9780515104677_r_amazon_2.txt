Jet jocks will get off on the detailed descriptions of aerial dogfighting in this tale. For me it got a little wonky and i found myself clicking past them.All in all this is a solid Viet Nam war-era tale, told by a guy who lived it. Solid characters fit into a well developed story line that has as its back drop the historical context of the war. Berent also elequently captures the feel of the times, and this is one of the things i enjoy most about his books.The story continues that saga of two characeters introducued in  the first book, Court Bannister, and Toby Parker as they continue training to return to the war. Bannister in F-4's and Parker in spotter planes.If you remember, or are interested in getting a feel for the sense of purpose that faded into a national malaise as a result of the news coverage and the body bags filling up, then this book, and the entire series will serve as a solid primer.An excellent read, my first comment not withstanding.Read moreRead less
