A strange mask from an indigenous tribe in the Amazon turns you into a fly the night after you put it on.  There are multiple ways to become human again, mostly centered around a special mold (some of the ways don't exactly leave you a homo sapiens sapiens, as you might have six arms or have you arms and legs be covered in thousands of black hairs), but also plenty of perils of dying as fly, or in one really fun and upbeat ending, losing all trace of your humanity and completely becoming a fly.
