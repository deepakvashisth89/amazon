Someone recently posted to a forum that I belong to, they wanted to know how many ships the Royal and Commonwealth navies had in total during WWII because they were looking to document it all.This book did exactly just that 60 odd years ago, every type/class of ship is listed along with all the names of the ships in each. Build dates, where they ended up, armament, propulsion etc it's all here in this very handy, compact and detailed book.Spotters like me will be happy to read it all but really, this is just a great reference book for anyone interested and wanting to know just how big a navy we once had. It is no wonder the US navy wanted us cut down to size and post war, we could just not afford to run it any more.People wonder why we couldn't save some of the recent ships to have been decommissioned, I am amazed that we preserved the Belfast but let some truly beautiful looking ships be scrapped and there are too many to mention, imagine Nelson, Renown, KG5, Queen Elizabeth or any of the other mighty ships we had parked up and still open to visitors instead of being scrapped as soon as the war ended. But as a nation, we were skint and could simply not afford it.
