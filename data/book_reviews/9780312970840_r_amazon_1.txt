I enjoyed the imaginative format of this mystery. Chief Inspector Alleyn uses a case to instruct new recruits. It culminated on a river cruise his wife Troy took on impulse. Unbeknownst to Troy, a master criminal was on board. Alleyn’s class alternates with the gripping story of Troy’s cruise.The plot encompasses multiple murders, theft, drug dealing, and art forgery. The passengers on the ship are widely varied, including among others an Australian priest, two American antique hunters, a charming butterfly enthusiast, a flibbertigibbet spinster, and a black doctor (who infuriates the few racist passengers). Troy interacts with all of them with increasing feelings of unease.The action heats up when the annoying spinster leaves the boat mysteriously in the dead of night.The Constables in the title are not cops, but paintings by Constable. The cruise ship is floating dreamily down the river in Constable Country. Troy sees it all with an artist’s eye. Ngaio Marsh studied painting before becoming first an actress then a mystery writer, and it’s always a pleasure to read her observations on art and artists.Clutch of Constables is a very fun read.
