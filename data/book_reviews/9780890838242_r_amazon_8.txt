I have to agree with the other readers in that this is one damn unusual romance novel, but I was actually getting tired of the girl falling head-over-heels in love with the Native American handsome buck.  This is a more realistic and untraditional love affair.  The warrior, Gray Eagle, had endured the ultimate tests to become what he is - a warrior and son of a great chief -- fearless, ruthless, uncaring for the feelings of others.  He is what we know from history as the King of Great Britain in those days.  Having an all-powerful, egotistical self-esteem.  His word was the ultimate.  Why give it up for the one girl who is White.  I mean, come on people let's give him credit.  Who the heck believes, now a days, that life was a bed of roses.  Nope, not I.
