There is no other edition out there like this one, and it would be hard to beat, but I call onto any competition to try. Though the references and mindset of the annotations can be limited, and at certain times a little superfluously comical, they are nevertheless interesting, and I am pleased that they are included. Much of the annotations come from a handful of secondary sources, but that is the fault of the scholarly field that has been taken upon The Wonderful Wizard of Oz, and not the compiler of this edition. Thus, the book itself as a meta-compalition of Oz information is at the top of the field and I highly recommend it to those wanting to begin to engage in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz in a more scholarly fashion.
