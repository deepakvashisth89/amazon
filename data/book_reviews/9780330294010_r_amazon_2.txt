A compelling first-hand description of the daily life of someone who suffered from schizophrenia for many years, starting around age 12. The author suffered from schizophrenia and spent many years in a mental hospital. She had an exceptional psychiatrist who helped her defeat her mental demons. At the end of the novel, she is able to leave the hospital, live on her own, and start a new life as an adult.This novel was made into a very popular movie.
