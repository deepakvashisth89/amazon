I read this book and its companion book "A Company Commander's Journal" by Mike Lanning years ago.  They are the two best books about life as we knew it in Vietnam.  I kept my original books, but after years of moving around, had misplaced them.  I was delighted to see that, even though out of print, I could get copies on Amazon.  I wrote the following about the companion book, but the same words apply here.  After receiving the newly ordered books, I did find my original ones.  Wouldn't you know?!If you want to know what day to day Vietnam was really like read this book and its companion "The Only War We Had" by Mike Lanning. These are two book grounded in reality - not someones sometimes faulty remembrances. Mike also has no axe to grind. he just tells it like it was. These two books are based upon the actual journal entries that he made as they happened. Very good - and he writes well.These two books set the record straight about how it was. There is no grandstanding, no nonsense to "prove" some esoteric point of view, no "ain't it awful," made up war stories. Just his story without embellishment. There is no "Rambo" crap, no Hollywood glorification of the anti-war movement, no false heroics.I was in Vietnam at exactly the same time Mike Lanning was there, and my stories closely parallel his. Later, I served in the same staff section of a higher HQ with Lanning. His daily journals - which few others kept, including me - well document the actual stuff that constituted the daily way of life in the war zone for most of us - cutting through the glamour and false crap others have written about the war. Sure, there are others that had it tougher, and others that had it easier, but for a picture of how the average, "normal" Grunts who actually did the fighting, these two books are a must read. In fact, they are really the only two books I would recommend for someone to read if they wanted a REAL picture of day to day life and activities in Vietnam. Yes, read historical books about specific battles written by HISTORIANS also, but avoid by all means the axe-grinding, wierdo, crap by others, some of whom weren't even there. These two books by Lanning instead. They are real.Finally, Lanning has written many other books. These two are his best. The others are based upon his research, and he is a good researcher, but these two are based upon his actual, documented as-it-happened life experiences. They are excellent.Read moreRead less
