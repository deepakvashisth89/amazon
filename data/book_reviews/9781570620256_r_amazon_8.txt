I've read lots of books by Western Buddhist teachers.  This may well be the one I have learned the most from.  Written in very short rich segments, it lends itself outstandingly to group discussion and to a busy person's schedule where brevity and simplicity are invaluable.  I appreciate Goldstein's focus on every day application, and his almost entirely steering clear of doctrines like reincarnation. He does take on the most difficult Buddhist concept of "no self" and devote a number of segments to try to make it understandable.  I found this section very helpful, though nothing can make this concept unchallenging.
