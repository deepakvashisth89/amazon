I have often wondered what it was like for the first settlers in wagon trains following where others had gone. However, this story follows right after Lewis & Clark and the author creates a story around a woman who traveled with her children and the men who journeyed to Astoria and the Pacific Ocean from St Louis. She was faced with challenging choices and great hardships. Jane Kirkpatrick did a masterful job of creating a vivid picture of what that treacherous journey must have been like. I felt her cold, I sorrowed with her when her children were starving, I understood how she both loved and sometimes feared her husband. The characters in this story were so real and their difficulties so serious that I felt like I was living what the journey must have been.
