If I have ever read a book about Jesus that made me shout out loud with glee, this is it.It is a simple book to read, and a pleasure. Jesus is finally 'one of us', a man with grand ideals, but just as many weaknesses as the rest of us.This is not a book about "loving Jesus", nor one about "the Jesus of scripture" - this Jesus forces you to open your mind...open your eyes and ears.Tyson's interpretation of the Fourth Gospel is a breath of fresh air - a part-time biblical 'scholar' myself, I am almost envious that she has been able to capture such a consistent and powerful perspective.I found a typo...that is why I didn't give it the full 5 star rating, but that is all I can think of to make this more of a 'balanced' review.Anyone who is fed up with the run-of-the-mill Jesus material, which Tyson herself addresses in terms of scholars running around in circles after one another (!), will find things in this book to make them angry, happy, confused, and relieved...but at the very least, it will get them THINKING!It should be made into a documentary!
