This book continues the series that started with Triplanetary. The ancient race of Arisians have successfully, so far, stymied the machinations of the Eddorians, a dictatorial, power-hungry race that came to our universe to exert dominatIon over all life forms.As part of their plan to defeat the Eddorians and develop peaceful civilizations, the Arisians create a secret breeding program to steer the evolution of several planets including Earth - this book details how the Arislans create the Lens and gift them to specially chosen individuals. The Lens enables the wearer the ability to mentally communicate over vast distances, read thoughts, and other powers unique to each Lensmen.The Lensmen use their new devices to thrawt the evil plans of the Eddorians and their pawns on Earth.First Lensman, concerns the early formation of the Galactic Patrol and the first Lens, given to First Lensman Virgil Sammy. Samms is a natural leader, intelligent, forceful, and capable. The Arisians tell Samms, the head of the Triplanetary Service, that if he comes to their home planet, he will be given the tool he needs to build the Galactic Patrol. That tool is the Lens.This book was written in the 1950s and chronicles deep space travel, battles between gigantic space fleets, and the advancement of humanity throughout the universe. The series is imaginative, ambitious, and reflects the pinnacle of scientific thought of the time period. These stories were so forward thinking, depicting space battles, advanced equipment, and strategic thought that it actually influenced US Navy fleet design and military strategy. This may have even influenced the creation of the Green Lantern Corps of DC Comics fame.I had put off reading these books for 35 years and I regret it now.
