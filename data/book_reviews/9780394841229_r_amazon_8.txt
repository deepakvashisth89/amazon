What is evil really? Who are the good guys and who are the bad? That's what this book makes you thinks. Letting you get to know Miro,Ben,Raymond,and Kate also shows how unfair life is and sometimes the bad guys do win.Also the part with Ben and his dad,and what he made him do,though a bit confusing,was a great counter plot.
