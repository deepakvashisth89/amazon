One of my favorite books of all-time, it saddens me to see someone disregard it simply because it doesn't have some false, contrived plot.I can't believe this idiot gave it one star because it didn't have an  "ending." Too used to reading Stephen King and other such pop  garbage, I guess.Anyway, I first read this book in an avant-garde  fiction class (don't let that throw you off--it's easy reading!), and have  since used it as a gift for several friends. Among other things, one said  it was an all too realistic potrayal of growing up female in  America.Basically, the book is divided into three sections, each giving  a snapshot of a girl's life in the 70's.
