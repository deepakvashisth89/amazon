This book is pretty darn good.  It surprised me because I thought I’d like it, since Emshwiller is a smart and witty guy, but I ended up liking it a lot.  It’s a page turner and suspenseful.  Yes, it has sex and violence, but it also makes you think about social issues, and not in a boring way.  Emshwiller builds his characters so that you believe in them.  And he is creative in his development of the future world, infusing the story with interesting cultural twists and new words.  I’d say that if you like science fiction, give this book a try.Read moreRead less
