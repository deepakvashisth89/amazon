It is regrettable that this talented writer seems very little known.  He has not been prolific in hard copy, and I sense he wrote mostly for his newspaper and travel magazines.  As noted by others, Swick and his Polish wife lived in Poland 1980-82, Swick returned for visits twice in the later 80s and once in 1990, finding the Poles bitterly dissatisfied with their society's course after shaking off Soviet hegemony.  It is not hard to understand why Poland is now sliding back into authoritarian rule.  This is certainly far deeper than most travel literature.  It is first-rate investigative journalism.  Swick is very erudite and quotes little-known authors like Hilaire Belloc.
