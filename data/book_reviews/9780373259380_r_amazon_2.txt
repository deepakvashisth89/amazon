Nancy Warren creates a hero-to-die for in Flashback.  Jack Thomas is every woman's dream man, sexy, sensative and endearing. The heroine, Laura Kincaide, tries her best to resist the man who dumped her for a cheerleader in high school. But all Jack has to do is level one of his suggestive blue-eyed stares at her and she's a gonner. Flashback is a fast-paced, fun read.
