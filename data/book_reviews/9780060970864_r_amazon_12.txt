This is one of the loveliest "yearbooks" - journals - I have read.  Her writing style flows along and before you know it another month has been described.  Anyone interested in beekeeping should have this in the library.  She weaves the beekeeping into the narrative without being ponderous about it and describes her friends and neighbors to the point where you want to sit around and talk with them too.  The depth of the 'questions" is as deep as you want to make it.  A lovely, gently read.  I am now going to order everything she has ever written!Read moreRead less
