In this volume there are two long stories. The first one is with Ryo, Dee, Bikky and Carol. Ryo and Dee try to catch a serial killer, who first kidnaps and then murders young teenage girls. Meanwhile Carol thinks that she is followed by a stalker. Then there is a photo of Carol found near the last victim of the serial killer and it becomes obvious, that the stalker and the serial killer are one and the same person. But by then it's nearly to late...In the second story Dee visits the grave of the person, who was like a father to him, and he tells Ryo in a cutback scene about this person, who was also a policemen, but not entirely on the right side of the law.This series has been absolutely great thus far and after lots of sweet kissing and snuggling I'm now in for a treat: Dee and Ryo making love in the last volume of the series. ;)Read moreRead less
