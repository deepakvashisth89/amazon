This one -- number six in the series -- is the one where my very high opinion began to slip a bit. It's still a wonderful recreation of the Roman milieu (this time involving an extended excursion to what is now Syria), and the characters are still engaging and interesting. But the story is perhaps a tad less compelling, and does drag a bit.  Or, perhaps,  the problem is that Falco is settling down to domesticity, no matter how exotic.
