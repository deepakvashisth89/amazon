This is a short and pithy, but reasonably full, outline of the major points in the authorship case from the Earl of Oxford camp and sets the reader up nicely to explore the detailed books of which there are many worthy examples. De Vere is the favourite "alternative Shakespeare" and this book is rather less partisan than Ogburn's, if less meaty or entertaining. I'd thoroughly recommend Mark Anderson's book on De Vere and Diana Price's Unorthodox Biog of the Stratford Man.
