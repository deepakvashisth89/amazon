This review is for Sacrifice (Burke Series) MP3 CD – Audiobook, MP3 Audio, Unabridged.The book itself is superb. As good as anything in Vachss' Burke series. So many have done a great job describing the book I'll focus instead on what prompted my own review. I'll just say, if you like the Burke series then chances are you LOVE the Burke series. Sacrifice is a wonderful part of that story. Phil Gigante does a spectacular job performing the novel, as usual.Unfortunately, when it comes to the MP3 CD Audiobook version of Sacrifice, *the chapters are literally out of order*. It took a full listen before I figured out what was going on. I've read Sacrifice a couple of times but it's been years. So I didn't realize at first anything was amiss. It was only when Burke (in the book) remembered something the child Luke said that it hit me, wait a second - he hasn't even met the kid yet. What's going on?I listened all the way to the end the CD, the credits played, and then the book STARTED at chapter one!Quality control really dropped the ball on this one. All I can do is hopefully steer my fellow Andrew Vachss fans away from this particular MP3 CD Audiobook. If you already bought the CD, not all is lost: you can fast forward the CD to track 30 or so to "start" the book, then let it play and "repeat" to keep the story going to the end.I have other MP3 CD Audiobooks and this problem is unique to Sacrifice, as far as I know. Again it's a fine book but this is not the version of the book you are going to want.Read moreRead less
