This book was written by explorers over 200 years ago as they crossed from the known world into unknown territory. The reader is taken along for the ride, and I am finding it an EPIC read. It is taking me very long compared to every other book I can remember except for the Power Broker (by Robert Caro, about Robert Moses; https://www.amazon.com/Power-Broker-Robert-Moses-Fall/dp/0394720245) which I spent a long time reading because I tried hard to imagine a man pulling so many strings for so many years.Why is this book taking me so long to read? Because I am ALSO reading what I consider the companion book - the unedited Journals - on my Kindle, which is the same story but more volume of words.Between these two sources, I feel as though I am Right There with these people as they work their way from the central US all the way to Oregon - and then all they way back again.This book will bring you all they way back to those days in 1803, 1804, 1805, 1806 and in that space, you will be transformed to the NOW of then.Read moreRead less
