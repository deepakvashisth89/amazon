This is a book that deals with the problem of war and its aftermaths honestly and with candor.  Manchester held nothing back which made one identify and almost feel the fear and the horror of this theater of war, which was, as he said, more than ignored since all the US eyes were on Europe, and area with which they were much more familiar.  One knows where Germany is, but had never heard of and would never remember the many small islands that were fought over because of their importance in the naval and the air battles.  In addiitionk, he is a master of writing.  My reading rate goes way down when I read his books since I don't want to take the chance of missing something important and since he weaves his words so well.The only thing I didn't like about it was the repetition, one battle sounding a lot like any other in his experience.
