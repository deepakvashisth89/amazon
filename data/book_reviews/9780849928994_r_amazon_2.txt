I read this book for the first time in the 90s on the recommendation of a friend and I continue to come back to it:1) When my life feels cluttered and out of order2) When things seem to get too complicated3) When relationships are not properly placed in my life4) When I feel a need for a catalyst and groundingThis book is obviously based on biblical christian virtues, but even for those that aren't christian or interested in biblical religion in any way it's written in such a delicate form that it's not off-putting in the least. The principles Anne Ortlund describes are really universal principles no matter your culture or religious preference.It is hard to get your life in order in this way, but it feels SO GOOD.
