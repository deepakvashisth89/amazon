I had forgotten that Barbara Freight books can run the emotional gamut. This one hit a home run. Daniel is a twelve year old who wants to meet his father. The man who gave his mother money for an abortion. When he finds out that his father is back in town he goes to confront him. But due to circumstances beyond his control he ends up in the hospital hanging on for dear life. But is all lost? This story was full of incredible inspiration for all. I enjoyed the story.
