I remember reading Don’t Sweat the Small Stuff when it first came out. I was in college, overwhelmed by the pressures of school, work, and the loss of my father, not to mention the usual challenges of life. Everything, even the small things, seemed to hit me hard. Richard Carlson’s words helped me gain perspective, and what once felt impossible to manage became easier to navigate.I was thrilled to get my hands on this daily book by him and his wife Kristine, as soon as it was released. Each day’s entry offers a gentle and uplifting reminder of love and gratitude, making it the perfect way to start or end your day.
