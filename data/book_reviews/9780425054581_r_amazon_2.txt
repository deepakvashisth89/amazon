The story is very good and kept my attention. This critique is mostly concerning the Kindle edition. The transcription from print was very poorly done and not worth $7.99 and the same applies to the Kindle edition of "Galactic Patrol" which is next in the "Lensman" series. I will probably just buy used for the rest of the books in the series. There were too many mistakes and errors to count in both of the Kindle books that I read. There were many misspelled words, many of which actually spelled other words that would not be caught by a spell checker and didn't belong in the story line. There were enough flat-out misspelled words that I thought a spell checker wasn't even used! There were lots of special characters imbedded in the text usually at the end of a word but occasionally in the middle. Many times there would be jumps of several lines usually caused by combinations of characters that didn't belong in the sentence in the first place. There are instances of the characters "Page nn", where 'nn' is a number, imbedded in the text at regular intervals instead of at the top or bottom where they would belong (in a book but not in the Kindle edition). The interesting part of this whole affair is that the first book in the series, "Triplanetary", contains very few errors and it was FREE!!!I am very disappointed in Amazon for selling a product like this.
