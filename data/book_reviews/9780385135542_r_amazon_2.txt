I first read "Hanta Yo" more than 20 years ago and recently re-bought the book so I could read it again.  This time I was fortunate to find an unmarked hardcover version.  I recall being so intrigued that I could hardly put it down despite the book being so long. "Hanta Yo" is so filled with respect and empathy for The People; it is hard not to feel grief that their rich, and yes, sometimes violent culture, has passed away.  Sadly, it appears no culture is free from violence. But gladly, every culture contributes its own richness to our lives despite the negatives.Since it has been so may years since my first reading of the book, I cannot comment on specifics but I can assure everyone who reads this wonderful saga of the Lakota Souix, will be glad they did.Read moreRead less
