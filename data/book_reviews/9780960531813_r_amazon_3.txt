As a newcomer to home brewing, I looked at some of the books offered by my local brew store.  This is a short volume chock full of useful information on types of beers, brewing equipment, beer ingredients, the brewing process, basic recipes, problems, and an appendix of conversion tables.  While this book may not contain enough information for all readers to get brewing, most will find enough here to gain a confident knowledge in future shopping and/or conversations.  I would recommend picking up this one alongside a good book of recipes.Read moreRead less
