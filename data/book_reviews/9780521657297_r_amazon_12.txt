The book, in terms of physicality, has not much to offer, but it is good in its form. It has a nice, sleek covering, and is a good height. It's pretty heavy but that's because of how long it is. The pages are great quality, a nice white, and the font is a good size and easy to read. The writing itself is a little hard to understand for me at times, so beware if you're a beginner to philosophy, or to Kant in general.
