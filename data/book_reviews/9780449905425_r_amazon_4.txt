This is quintessential Buffett. He’s a writer at heart and this book interweaves the songs and what he’s got rolling around in his head. The reason we are at this number of buys is just boat life (to a great deal thanks to him). We do keep “loaning” them out to fellow boaters. Sometimes they come back but due to boat life, they get wet or otherwise die (don’t think Jimmy would be mad about that) otherwise, they just keep making the rounds but don’t get back to us because they are circling the Caribbean and spreading the joy. In any case, it’s worth rebuying every time to reread or just share. All those songs about losing the shaker of salt, weird people and islands in general? I can confirm, he lived here and they are true. So, y’all, stop stealing the salt shakers and just buy this book instead. Your Keys servers and bartenders will thank you.Read moreRead less
