In this story, obsessive love is entwined with the kind of suffering that won't ease its grip until one's last breath.The writing is so moving at times, with the whole spectrum from artistic to dramatic, and from dramatic - to utterly gruesome, to the point where you feel sick yet you can't stop reading.The author's approach with perspective wasn't my favorite part, though: most of the story was told through the recollections of a secondary character, which, in my eyes, was a limiting factor distancing the reader from the main characters' subjective experience. I wouldn't have minded a deeper dive into their heads.After the more intense first half of the book, the second half feels slower paced, relying more on melancholy rather than drama.Every character felt like a real person: some of them in a haunting way, some - charmingly, and yet some others - revoltingly.The setting with its beautiful nature and remoteness from the outside world complements the premises perfectly.I enjoyed this book, and I would definitely recommend it to other readers.Read moreRead less
