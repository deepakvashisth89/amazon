This book is a vast improvement over Skom's first novel, THE MARK TWAIN MURDERS, which was a predictable and formulated mystery.  THE GEORGE ELLIOT MURDERS is not only better-written, but the characters, although "stock" in more than some cases, are better portrayed.  However,  the biggest improvement is the relationship between the heroine and her  love interest - it's more believable and interesting.I have to admit  that I am not connoisseur of mystery novels, by any means, but I read this  book at the urging of my mother (she's one of those people who have the  mystery sections of every bookstore within a one-hundred mile radius  memorized) and loved it.  This is a good read that can stand on its own  outside the genre, and I would definitely recommend this book.Read moreRead less
