"Begin to loveand that which is untrue in youwill dissolve and fall away"In my humble opinion some books are way too long and take too many hours to complete. They can feel like a hike up a huge mountain. This book is different in that it only takes half and hour to finish and the trip is quite memorable. Laurie Beth Jones' observations are filled with wisdom and her words reflect a subtle beauty. She has filled her book with reflections, poems, stories and memorable quotes. Her writing is definitely inspired and she has some quite beautiful thoughts. This would be a perfect gift for a good friend or a busy relative who doesn't have time to read a lot but still wants a inspiring experience. For me, this book was a refreshing break from the stresses of everyday life. Highly Recommended!~The Rebecca ReviewRead moreRead less
