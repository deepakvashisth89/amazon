This book starts out well enough, with good atmosphere.  Once the tone is well-established, the author shows us the Great Detective discovering the means of murder in a way much reminiscent of the Adventure of the Speckled Band.  Unfortunately, from here we start to lose the "feel" of Holmes, and by the end of the book all pretense of the iron logic and scientific approach to crimes and mysteries is abandoned, replaced by one more ye-anciente-mystic-powers story (I'm not morally opposed to them, but if I want one, I'll go looking for one).  Mildly interesting twists near the end with Holmes and his foe, but I figured out the trick to the secret passage before Holmes did, which doesn't speak well for him: Sherlock Holmes, I'm not.Overall, a disappointment, I'm afraid.  One star for atmosphere, one star for a good beginning, and one star for a couple of clever items (like the aforementioned secret passage entrance).  I'm tempted to take a star off for a somewhat-implausible deus ex machina, but will forbear.Read moreRead less
