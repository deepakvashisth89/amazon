I'm a fan of the Presents series so know what I'm getting into with one of their novels. I don't expect realism or docile heros, but I expect ones that I can find somewhat likeable. However, this one wasn't worth the time.Lucy has been Nick's secretary for almost a year on the day of his wife's funeral and ends up sleeping with him to comfort him.  Yet despite "adoring" him and having a close working relationship she has absolutely no inkling that he's been caughter in an unhappy marriage.  He's less than happy after the fact and then fast forward 8 months and Lucy is dating someone else and Nick has engaged in a series of short affairs.  Nick decides to be nosy and interfere in Lucy's relationship and basically tricks her back into bed with him.  When she winds up pregnant, he kicks her out, then has her followed.  Only then does the situation get "resolved."  There was no trust between these two and no real relationship development.
