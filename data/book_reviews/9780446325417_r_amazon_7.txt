I just love Loretta Lynn. Honestly, I never listened to much of her music. Heard it on the radio from time to time, but in my youth, rock 'n roll was what I listened to. I loved the movie Coal Miner's Daughter. I saw her on a show with the medium Kim Russo a couple years ago. She was checking out her haunted house and I just fell in love with Loretta Lynn. There's something about her that is indescribably appealing.This book is just wonderful. It's written in the colloquial voice and it works. It's like she's sitting across from you telling you her life story, and what a story it is.
