Handberg gives another excellent novel from his "Newsroom" series, and this book was intelligent, fast-paced and exciting. He develops his characters well, but I would like to have seen more of Collier in his follow-up books. But then again, he does this in Dead Silence, so maybe this is a mute point? Having only recently discovered Handberg by chance, its such a shame that he has not written more. He is a star in his genre.Read moreRead less
