Quite an unusual book for me to review. Not really hard boiled crime ,but definitely not soft boiled either. A brilliant first chapter sets up a most intriguing idea. I don't think it always works, maybe because the author ( ess) likes to play the quick one liner and comic possibilities.There are some good glimpses of 1945 Chicago ,although no sense of a World War . The ending is well done and has quite a good sting in the tail. At this price a good value read, but I see that most of her other books are priced uncompetitively ..to be polite.Read moreRead less
