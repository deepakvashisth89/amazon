'Bud, Not Buddy' is a precious book, bursting with the kind of innocence that makes my heart ache, much like 'The Mighty Miss Malone', which I read and loved several years ago.Armed with a set of rules to have a funner life and be a better liar, Bud sets out to look for his father. All he has to help him is a suitcase full of his treasures. Some of his treasures are rocks with places and dates on them. Others are flyers advertising Herman E. Calloway and his famous band. Why would his mother have kept these flyers if Herman E. Calloway wasn’t his father?Conviction, optimism, street smartness, and a little bit of luck come together as Bud travels from Flint where he lived in a Home, to the Great Rapids, where he is sure he will find his father. With Bud, we experience fear and anticipation, love and heartbreak, laughter and tears. Humour drives the story just as much as emotion does, making 'Bud, Not Buddy' a heartwarming read that we tear through and finish with a smile. I’m not surprised that this charming story has sold over four million copies and won the Newbery!Read moreRead less
