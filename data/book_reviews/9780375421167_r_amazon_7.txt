I selected this book because I love this era. While this book does not fall into the category of classic literature, it does succeed in its intended purpose: to create a fun story, replete with interesting characters (caracatures though they are) and an interesting plot. It reads like an old black and white film, where actors/actresses drop great one-liners. The author's tongue-and-cheek efforts do not go to waste. This book is a great one to discuss with friends.
