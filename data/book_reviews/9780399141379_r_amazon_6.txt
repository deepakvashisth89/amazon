As the third in the Hugo Fitzduane saga, this is O'Reilly's best book yet. He writes so vividly that I feel like I know the characters personally especially Hugo and his best friend Bear who is a huge man, tough as nails and extremely funny.  I don't understand why Hollywood movie producers haven't taken notice of these marvelous books - I can just see Antonio Banderas, Adrian Paul or Brad Pitt as Hugo Fitzduane.
