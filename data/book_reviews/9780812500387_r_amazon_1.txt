I read it first in 1981 as an Army Lieutenant in the FRG during the Cold War.  Now as an old retired LTC, I found it is a bit dated but holds up surprisingly well. It fits in to the current day world enough to enjoy this rip-roaring adventure book with some sci-fi suspended disbelief. I am so happy it came out as Kindle book.
