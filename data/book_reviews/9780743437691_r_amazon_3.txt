This is a book I've wanted to read for a while.  I remember seeing the mass market paperback on my high school library's shelf, but never got around to reading it before I graduated.I thoroughly enjoyed this book the same way I enjoyed reading books by Vivian Vance Velde. It has a lot of the same feel. The romance between the main characters is a slow burn and the focus is on their relationship with each other building as they discover more about their powers and each other.The separation of the story in parts was interesting. I did feel like the last part could have been built up further, but it was what ultimately gave it the same feel as most VVV books, and it worked well for this story.Read moreRead less
