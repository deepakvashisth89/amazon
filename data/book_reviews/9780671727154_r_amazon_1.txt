I read this book twenty years ago, as a teen, and I have remembered it all these years.  It took me quite a bit of researching to find it (I knew it was called "The Planets" or "Planets" but didn't know the author's name and there are literally tens of thousands of books on Amazon with "Planets" in it, and this being an out of print and somewhat unpopular book, it was NOT in the first twenty or so pages of results).  I finally typed the general description of it being humorous short stories into Google and actually found the author's name.  Just today, I got "The Constellations", too, which is - I understand - a continuation of some of these stories.  I cannot wait to read this book again and actually bought two copies, one paperback and one hardcover - one to read and one to keep.What I remember of these stories was the human quality in them, the honesty of character, and laughing out loud at the incongruous but believable happenings.  I cannot wait to read this again, and am eagerly looking forward to getting the books in the mail.  I would recommend these short stories to anyone who likes humorous fiction with deeply "human" characters - all with flaws and idosyncracies that make them highly believable.  Enjoy!Read moreRead less
