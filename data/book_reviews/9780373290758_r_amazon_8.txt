I read this story last night and have been thinking about it today.  I really liked the premise and the set-up.  The early chapters were good.  I liked Leah in the beginning but after they get married the longer the book went the more I started not to care for Leah.  She was very mouthy and seemed very disrespectful of Gar. The part that bothered me the most was that she enjoyed the attention of the other men AFTER she was married. That was very disrespectful and not good even in these times. A wife should never enjoy the attentions of other men. Then there is Eric Magnor. The revelation about him was an eyeroll and did nothing for the story except to destroy Leah's character even moreso. The fact she confided in HIM and that he had to give her permission to speak to her own husband was frustrating. The fact that he saved her in the end and not Gar was the final straw. So much so that I wonder what Leah got out of this marriage.  She didn't seem to need Gar. Ever!  She didn't turn to him when she needed someone the most. She seemed too far independent so much so that Gar wasn't necessary in her life.  A story that started off with so much promise turned into a book that left a bad taste in my mouth.  Gar loved her but I had a hard time believing she loved him.  She didn't even seem to want to ever be his wife until the last chapter. That was a problem for me.
