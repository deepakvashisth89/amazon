This is a fantastic collection of short works by one of the finest writers in history.  While my favorites were "Notes from the Underground" and "The Dream of a Ridiculous Man," each of these stories contains vintage Dostoevsky--memorable characters, readability, a touch of humor and, most importantly, profound insights into human nature.  While not as powerful as his longer works, these stories are sure to please any fan or even those who have a casual interest in Dostoevsky.  Highly recommended.Read moreRead less
