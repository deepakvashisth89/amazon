I like this book. I've actually owned it for over a year and haven't gotten around to reading it until just the past week. (I have the copy with the cover shown here vs. an updated 2006 version also for sale on Amazon).Its an easy read book, fairly in depth, and keeps you interested. The author is very thorough in explaining the unique nature of intuition and how it differs from say just an active imagination - and how to avoid confusing the two; and he is also good at going into detail about how intuition is actually used routinely in science though many scientists on the surface ridicule it...The one slight detraction to it that I might ad is that the exercises to develop your intuition near the end of the book, seem to me to be a bit basic and limited in scope for developing a deep sense of intuitive knowing. Since this was written in the 1980s before all the mind-expanding consciousness books came out, that's understandable.Read moreRead less
