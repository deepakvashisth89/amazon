I went to your site looking for African American Classic and found this book.  First, I had never heard of the book or the author.  I found the chapters and the book too long.  The story idea is good and still relevant to this day of age.  As an African American woman, most of us can relate to Lutie.  She was hard-working who worked hard for everything that she earned but somehow life was always there to throw a curve ball and she decides to deal with it.  Too much of the book is a tad philosophical for me.  I was tired of reading what these characters were thinking.  Darn, I just wanted her to tell the story and be done.
