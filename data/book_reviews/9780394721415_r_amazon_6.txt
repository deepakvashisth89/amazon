An American classic, but man is it dated. The men were all hard drinking wise guys and the dames were either bubble headed floosies or could chew iron and spit tacks. The best character in the book is, without question, the elevator operator. He’s the key in more ways than one.  Not a lot of cerebral challenges in this book. It’s all about the characters and the plot is secondary and wraps up rather tidily in the last twenty pages or so. There are better reads out there, but if you are a fan of the era and mysteries that are t really mysteries then you’ll enjoy this book.Read moreRead less
