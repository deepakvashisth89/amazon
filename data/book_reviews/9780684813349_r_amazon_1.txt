The central character is the minister's wife, Rosemary Sheffield, an attractive, level-headed woman. Rosemary starts a storm of gossip by suddenly losing her faith and being very open about it. Soon all sorts of rumors are circulating about Rosemary, who is a straightforward person, entirely blameless.Almost half the story is concerned with parish gossip and its consequences. I found this quite fascinating. And then there's a murder. The victim is a businessman very active in the church. Is it a crime of passion or connected with the man's mysterious business dealings?Detective Constable Charlie Peace is the very likable investigator. He's friendly, low key and intelligent. He's also black, but this is only mentioned in passing. No fuss about it. Charlie's focus is on people -- "faces, attitudes, gestures, signs of hidden woe and hidden passion."I loved this mystery.
