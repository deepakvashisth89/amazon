Very good book! It's organized first alphabetically by country and then again alphabetically within each country by manufacturer. Each aircraft is described by a dimensions and performance tabulation. There is also a short paragraph on development history. The text is accompanied by black and white photos and sometimes a color rendering and a cut-away drawing.By and large, the book describes the more well-known aircraft. The are a few lesser known types such as the British Vickers - Armstrong "Warwick" bomber / patrol plane and the German Focke - Wulf Fw 44 primary trainer. There are only a few cargo planes such as the renowned American C-46, C-47, and the C- 54 and the German Ju 52.Read moreRead less
