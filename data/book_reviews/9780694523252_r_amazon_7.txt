I think this is 3rd or 4th in the Prey Series by John Sandford - I don't remember because I've read it several times (and have reread the entire series once or twice)... I'm living back in Minnesota (after 13 years in Houston, TX) and in a rather remote northern location... Standing outside in the evening, the stars above and the crystalline quiet of a winter night made me long to read this one again. This book is one of the hallmarks for the series since it introduces us to Weather (who plays a big part in the rest of the series)... It also captures the feel of small town Minnesota, particularly in the winter.  Just like all of the other novels I've read by Sandford, he is a master of storytelling without having to use so many gosh darn words!  His characterizations are full yet subtle and the humor completely believable and enjoyable.  This economy of writing allows us to care for the characters and still have plenty of excellent actions and dialogue.  I highly recommend this book - heck, I recommend the whole series.  In fact, I'm one of those people who has to start a series from the beginning so my recommendation is to to out and get "Rules of Prey" and the other 2 or 3 between and THEN get this one.  You'll appreciate it much more, I swear.Read moreRead less
