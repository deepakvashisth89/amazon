The Last Full Measure, by Jeff Shaara, contains the events of the Civil War that occurred after the Battle of Gettysburg, from Lee's hurried retreat from the battle to his eventual surrender at Appomatox. It expounds on the battles of the Wilderness, Spotsylvania, Cold Harbor, The Siege of Petersburg, and so on - all in great detail so that the reader might gain and understanding of the events that led to the destruction of Lee's army.Shaara tells the story from the points of view of the commanders who led both armies in the war. He focuses on one character in each chapter and cycles through the various characters many times. Most of the chapters are from the viewpoints of Commanding General Grant, Commanding General Lee, or Brigadier General Joshua Chamberlain, who accepted the Confederate Surrender at Appomattox. Shaara also expounds on what happened to the commanders who survived the war, even telling how Robert Lee became a college president, Lincoln was shot, and Grant became a president and a friend of Samuel Clemens.This book is a wonderful historical resource as well as a fun narrative to read. It is a good book for any reader, no matter what their interests might be. It might also be a good idea to read Gods and Generals and The Killer Angels before reading this book as they are prequels to it.
