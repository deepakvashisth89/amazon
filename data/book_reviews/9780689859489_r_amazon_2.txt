For more than 7 decades I have been a voracious reader and my interests have ranged from the traditional scientific to the strange esoteric to martial arts and fitness genres. This is why while browsing on Amazon when I saw this 218 page soft cover book (Samurai Girl: The book of the sword by Carrie Asai) I decided to purchase it.This unique story begins at a Japanese wedding, where a young woman named “Heaven” is supposed to be getting married to a man she hates, but before the ceremony is done a murdering Ninja turns up.  This is the first book in this continuing series, and I found it to be an interesting and entertaining read.Rating: 4 Stars. Joseph J.
