The balanced reporting offered a sadly still relevant critique 30 years later of the power of the church hierarchy over the laity, and the turmoil this causes. The author provides an insightful look at the evolution of the American Catholic Church post Vatican II through the lens of one Jesuit parish in Washington, DC. This takes place before there was extensive reporting on the clerical sexual abuse and coverup by the hierarchy, so in a way the moral authority of the bishops of this era is even more questionable in hindsight today. The anchoring issue is women’s ordination, and there are some excellent theological explorations of it in these pages. The book offers perspective, thoughtful and thorough research, and a kindness towards its subjects that made it, somewhat surprisingly, a real page turner!Read moreRead less
