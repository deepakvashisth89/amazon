I'd actually bought this book in the UK but the Amazon order was for a friend in the US who knows the area.  He said that he enjoyed it but it was sad as well.  I'll give my perspective.I found it detailed, written in journalistic style, with frequent useful reminders of the players' identities in a complex story of conflicts between and within agencies and of the turmoil at street level.As they say "you couldn't make it up", I was fascinated by it.  To a UK reader the account of the operations of, and relations between, the numerous Law Enforcement Agencies are an eye-opener.  The criminal justice system in the US, its very Constitution and the fragmented enforcement provision in a large country seem to afford much opportunity to evade prosecution.  The account of the rapid and enduring shift in the balance of power between FBI handlers and their informants is compelling reading.  It's worth saying that the authors give a full and detailed account of their sources.Can't wait for the movie.Read moreRead less
