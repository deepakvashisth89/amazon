Christopher Rowley's early works (The Fenrille series and this Vang series) are highly under appreciated, where to begin?  I first read these books in the early 1990s, and after a recent re-read, they have all held up extremely well.  Tight pacing, believable characters, crime, drugs, sex, Extended Life Programs, and all of it rendered pointless in the face of unexpected alien invasion on a remote colony planet. What more could you want?Highly recommended! Why haven't some of these books been turned into films?  Their stories are far superior to most of the sci-fi trash being churned out these days.  The War for Eternity puts Avatar to shame, just as the Vang leaves other alien invasion stories in its wake!Read moreRead less
