The Dwarf is second only to Machiavelli in terms of sinister fascination and shocking commentary. Not perhaps the most politically correct in its title, it examines a truly loathsome character and still manages to make him human. Lagerkvist's brilliance comes in drawing connections between things, and it's the ability of Piccoline to become entangled in a billion different things and still manage to come out of them unscathed that adds an air of fascination to the piece. Indeed, while The Dwarf is perhaps as much about the tendency of people to only see their faults in others, it also raises interesting questions about how we can tolerate and ignore some of our own faults in others, often to an extreme, based on assumptions that we make.Read moreRead less
