I love Janette Oke's books - this is quite a different story to Book 2, so it isn't a follow-on story like some of her other works.  Janette has a gret writing style and incorporate's her Christian message into her writing without it being preachy or pushy which I think is a great talent.  This book is very interesting, not too long and contains a life lesson, as do most of her works which I have read.  I'm not American but I do love reading about the Pioneers who founded America and the sort of real lives they lived, not the rubbish we often see on TV nowadays.
