I knew nothing about Edith and the family she presided over.  Unfortunately history offers very little information about the First Family, and politics and media provide questionable insight into the president.  This book gives so much of Edith and Theodore as children that we can appreciate the powerful and loving relationship they shared.  They were effective parents, and had lasting effects on each of their children.  A family deserving lasting respect.
