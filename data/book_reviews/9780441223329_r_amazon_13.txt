As a huge Terry Pratchett fan I was looking for a new author to follow. A forum suggested Tom Holt and I thought I would start with his early work. Delightfully written fantasy reminds me very much of Neil Gaiman i.e vaguely familiar mythical gods in real live scenarios. If, like me, you are not familiar with Wagner’s The Ring, Valhalla and Rhine maidens you may learn some stuff and have a pleasurable read at the same time.
