This has been one of the best Ransom family stories Ms. Thomas has written.  I have been waiting for Elle's story for a while.The story has the most twisted plot, starting with Brennan's insecurities with love through the very end, which is startling (to say the least).  It is a story about how one woman's love opened her man's heart.  There is so much that I would love to say, but it would give the ending away.For anyone who has been following the Ransom tales, you will love the fact that the whole family makes an appearance.  Even Mama Ransom gets down and dirty with Brennan's mother.  If you haven't been introduced to the whole Ransom family, you must start with HIDDEN BLESSINGS.
