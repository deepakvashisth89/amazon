After reading this series 4+ times, I finally decided to buy it, but my only choice was to buy it from the Amazon marketplace. I chose to only buy "used-like new" books and I was very satisfied with my purchase. FYI, when buying books from the marketplace, there is $3.99 shipping added onto the price of each item. I bought the majority of my books from one seller hoping to get a shipping discount, but that was not the case. I spent $3.99 shipping on every item in addition to the selling price. All together, I spent less than if I would have bought each book new for $8.99.
