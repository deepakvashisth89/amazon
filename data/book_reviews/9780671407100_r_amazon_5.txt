I've read other speculative accounts of the life of Christ, but The Last Temptation of Christ was spectacular (for those fearing that this is an anti-Christ revisionist work, the first line on the copyright page should help: "This is a work of fiction."). Kazantzakis's epic, speculative work imagines a very human Jesus wrestling with his own identity and, ultimately, his destiny. The author takes some literary license with the gospel accounts, but that is part of job when writing such a piece that explores the lives of ancient people to whom we have limited access (think of the freedom of interpretation in shows like "The Chosen").P.A.
