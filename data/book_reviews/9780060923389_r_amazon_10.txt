It was yet another gem of a read from Patricia Wentworth featuring Miss Silver. Althea Graham spends her life taking  care of her self centred mother Mrs. Graham who makes sure that Althea has no time for herself or the man she loves. A tragedy strikes bringing Miss  Silver to Grove Hill to resolve the mystery.I liked the narration which was simple, straightforward and engaging. The plot was interesting. What I didn't like was that there were too many characters to wade through while reading.This is a mystery I would recommend for fans of Agatha Christie and Margery Allingham.Read moreRead less
