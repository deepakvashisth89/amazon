A fascinating account of a journey through Russian occupied Central Asia in 1876. Great accounts of the journey, scenery and customs of the peopleHowever only 3 stars because ther is far too much tedious detail of every encounter, every battle without an over arching description of the real fascinating politics at the time.Also no maps included when every tiny place en route is mentioned. The first part is very difficult to get involved in. It improves once the horse and camel ride starts  across the Steppes and fiana.ly to Khiva.Not enough explanation about the author himself.
