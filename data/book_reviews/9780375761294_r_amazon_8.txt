I recently reread Sister Carrie. McTeague was one of the books Amazon recommended to also read. I had heard of Frank Norris because of his association with Theodore Dreiser, but I had never read any of his work. I found a public domain copy on Amazon and immediately downloaded it to my Kindle.By modern standards the story is slow to develop, the characters do not have much depth and the language is stilted. Nevertheless, if you can stick with it the story is a snapshot of middle-class life in San Francisco at the end of the nineteenth century. It is a morality tale about the evils of greed, jealousy and vengeance. There is nothing new here, but it is interesting to see how these characters play out their roles in the low tech world of the 1890's.When this was written it would have been a story of contemporary life rather than a quaint costume drama. I felt like it provided awindow into the world as it existed when my grandparents were teenagers. I am glad I read it.Read moreRead less
