This is the fourth book out of the five book series.  Alex Marchetti of the famous Marchetti restraunt family is in no way looking for a woman.  Alex has suffered through the loss of one woman and does not want to go through it again.  Unfortunately he can not help but feel attracted to Fran Carlino.  Fran is helping Alex with a new line of frozen foods.  Once Fran and Alex mix business with pleasure the sparks start to fly.  Very interesting how their story plays out.
