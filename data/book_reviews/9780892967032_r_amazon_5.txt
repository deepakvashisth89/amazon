Joe R Lansdale has done it again with Freezer Burn.  This is actually my 3rd copy of this book.  I gave away 1 copy, and had a "pool incident" that resulted in the loss of the 2nd copy.  This is one book that I go back to read at least once a year.  It is always a "laugh out loud so hard tears come to my eyes; so kind of sad tears come to my eyes" book, no matter how many times I read it.  It is wickedly hilarious and full of pathetic, real, and somehow touching characters that involve you to their bitter ends.  Very typical Joe Lansdale, combining humor and pathos so adroitly.  Along with The King is Dead (featuring Bubba Ho-Tep), and Cold in July, Freezer Burn is a must-read for any Joe R Lansdale fan, and will make a new fan of a first time reader.Read moreRead less
