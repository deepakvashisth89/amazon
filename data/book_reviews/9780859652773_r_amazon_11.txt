I am not a fan of Marilyn Manson, nor do I subscribe to his preferred lifestyle and beliefs. However, I came across this book on recommendation by a trusted friend. The cover alone almost scared me away. Regardless, I pushed on and only a few pages in, I couldn't put the book down. It was not only one of the BEST rock star biographies that I have ever read but one of the BEST books I've read PERIOD (and I read a lot of books).
