This book offers enlightenment that I really do control my own vision.  I had been wearing glasses since a young teenager when I could not see the blackboard from the rear of the classroom.  I continued to need glasses before and during a rocky marriage and finally divorce.  At age 50 I came across this book and read it.  I did the exercises and focused on a distant leaf on a tree until I willed it into focus.  The next visit to my eye doctor I suggested I needed a weaker prescription.  He ignored this suggestion even as he was writing a weaker prescription.  Months later, he again had to write a weaker prescription.  I have not needed glasses since then.Read moreRead less
