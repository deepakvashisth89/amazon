There are very few fantasy novels about the American Indians, and I found this one to be fresh and fun to read.The plot is that a meteor is headed for earth, with all that that entails.  But it is no ordinary meteor; it's being guided by the Maya Indian lords of death.  The hero must travel to the underworld and defeat the lords of death, somehow, before the meteor slams into the earth and does extreme damage or completely wipes out humanity.This book was published in 1988 and is no longer in print, so if you want to read it you have to buy a used copy.  That means brittle and yellowed pages which smell great as you read the story.  I really recommend this as a good read.Read moreRead less
