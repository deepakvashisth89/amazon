I enjoyed the book for many reasons:- the I-perspective of a 9 year old boy telling his story- how he kept truthful to his heart and conscience until the very end despite despite his fear- the dense atmosphere of this village during a very hot summer- the way the author merely describes the adult's cruelty without apparent judgement (because Michele does not judge, he just questions)- the unexpected and kind of open end.A very good read and some food for thought.Read moreRead less
