Imagine a scavenger hunt where everyone is looking for just one item, a special item, possibly a sentient item that wants to be found.  I don't think that teaser will spoil the story for anyone, but it is the best, shortest sum-up of the story's concept as I can do.Lots of aliens are looking for the interstellar pig (which could have been an interstellar rock, or coin, or something else and it wouldn't matter), because that's how to "win the game".  A youth discovers the interstellar pig, which had been out of circulation for quite some time.  Aliens start to show up, the game is revealed, and interesting events start to unfold.Yet again, William Sleator writes an expertly crafted story targeting youth and young-adults, but is so well written that adult readers will not be able to help but to enjoy it too.
