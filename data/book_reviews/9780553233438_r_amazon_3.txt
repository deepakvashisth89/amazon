An excellent book for both teens and adults.I was struck by how all three family members were isolated by the roles the parents believed in.  They didn't seem to do much of anything together.  While the father watched TV in the evenings, his wife sewed or did other things.  Though they were in the same room, they weren't having the same experiences.By the way, the story is set near the Pacific, in either Oregon or Washington.
