MIND KILL By  Richard La PlanteThis is an amazing and wonderful book.  It introduces the idea of psychic warriors with two fascinating characters, pitted in a battle for not only life and death, but their eternal souls as well. LaPlante has moved from the world of martial arts which he depicted  so excitingly in Mantis and Leopard,  to the realms of higher consciousness, mystic teachings and the frontiers of human knowledge...bringing them into our normal lives in a chill-thrilling action suspense novel that delivers what the X-Files only hints at. His characters are real, his writing is compelling and carries you non-stop, and his ideas will open your mind and both terrify and uplift you. Don't miss it!Read moreRead less
