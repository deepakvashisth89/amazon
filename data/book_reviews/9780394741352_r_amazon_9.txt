JF Powers, Morte d'Urban________________________________________He's not exactly a popular novelist, but Morte d'Urban is an excellent read. It's a clerical novel, by one of the clergy themselves, but full of fun and good writing. It tells the story of Father Urban, a Catholic priest in Minnesota and Illinois, who struggles to rescue a down-at-heel Order, the Clementines.It follows his struggles to make religion work in a materialistic society and among back-biting and incompetent colleagues. Urban is a sympathetic character, witty, resourceful and alive to human weaknesses, including his own. He is something of a worldly priest, who likes a drink and driving fast cars, and who establishes, after much in-fighting and scheming, a golf course at a retreat in a formerly run-down parish sited on what had been a mental home (symbolic point taken). When the Bishop (a monkey at golf, unlike Urban) seeks to close the golf course, probably, Urban thinks, because he's no good at the game, Rome is threatened to be called as arbiter.
