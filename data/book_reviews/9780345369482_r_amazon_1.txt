When a banker from Amarillo ends up dead during a pheasant hunt, Sheriff Charles Matthews must find the murderer in the rural Texas Panhandle. The banker was a truly horrible man and it seems like everyone in the county had a motive to kill him, forcing Sheriff Matthews to sift through dark small-town secrets to find the killer.This is an excellent mystery. The setting comes alive and the characters are realistic with strengths and weaknesses and secrets. I highly recommend this book and the series it belongs to.Read moreRead less
