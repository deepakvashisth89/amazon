I have to say I enjoy the John Gardner 007 novels. Fast paced, action, great characters, nice thriller twists and centered in the 1980's (when most were written) before their was the technology boom (cell phones,GPS, etc...)  which to me keeps it old school Bond the way I like it. Most of the Gardner novels are page turners and this one falls into that category. This one gets a 4.5 star rating (close to 5). Bond is not saving the world in this one, but is on 'solo' mission from M and has to use his deduction skills right till the end.Read moreRead less
