Azimov is best know as a science fiction writer. He wrote over 300 books in his lifetime, but I find his non fictional works to be the most fascinating and attention holding. I first read "The Human Brain" when I was in high school. I'm 68 now and was most anxious to re read this book in combination with "The human body."
