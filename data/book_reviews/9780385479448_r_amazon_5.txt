I’ve always enjoyed this authors ability to make the impossible plausible and the unbelievable plainly believable. This book was written 4 years before Covid. I would love to see a sequel, knowing now what we know and have learned since 2020. And just like in 2020, most of what they believed was proven wrong. An excellent read I highly recommend.
