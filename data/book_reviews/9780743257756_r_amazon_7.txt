I don't disagree with any of the other reviewers as I can see both points of view as to whether or not this is a good novel about women. My problem is that the book seemed to be a series of short stories, and the author says in the introduction that parts of the book have appeared in previous publications. My only interest was in the main character Rachel and her mother, and there just wasn't enough of that plot for me. Even though Glatt used different ways to tie in the stories of the other women, I did not think of the book as a novel.
