This book is just fantastic! I grew up a Fred Gwynne fan. From the Munsters to Pet Sematary to My Cousin Vinny. I checked this book out often from the library years ago to read to my daughter & older kids I had in my home daycare. They always loved it, laughed at the pictures and overall enjoyed it. At the time I knew some of the things we read went right over their heads. Fast forward 12 years later, my daughter is almost 17 and somehow this book came up.So I ordered it from Amazon. I wasn’t expecting much of a reaction when I showed her. I was wrong!! She freaked out!!! 😂 First it was so nostalgic and brought that younger time in her life right back. Her memories were brimming and it was amazing hearing her read it now. “OMG!!! I never knew what that really meant! Wow...that’s good!”This is now a part of our current library. She also has a fondness for Mr Gwynne, as she is a budding horror fan. I have a strong feeling this will be a family book for generations to come.Read moreRead less
