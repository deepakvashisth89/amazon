A young writer goes to Italy to find out about his family roots.  He was told a story about his great grandfather who was referred to as "The Monk".  He finds out that his great grandfather had been murdered.  He is on the trail to find out why and who may have done it.  The story tells us a great deal about the history of Sicily.
