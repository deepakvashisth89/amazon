Oh, P.G. Wodehouse, how great thou art! I feel sorry for anyone who hasn't discovered the very particular pleasure of reading Wodehouse and his hilarious tales of an upper crust British world that never existed. He's as much a fantasy writer as George R.R. Martin, but so much more fun! Here's a sample: "As I sat in the bathtub, soaping a meditative foot and singing, if I remember correctly, 'Pale Hands I Loved Beside the Shalimar', it would be deceiving my public to say that I was feeling boomps-a-daisy." His greatest creation, Bertie Wooster, is forever smoking "a rather pleased cigarette" or "pronging a moody forkful" of eggs. Ahhhh. What a master! People say Wodehouse is trivial and silly. Yes.
