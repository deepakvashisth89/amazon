The author had a worthy idea; make it simple to compare players from different eras. But his simple-minded approach created bizarre projections and a failed book.The absurd home run projections for Orator Jim O'Rourke show the danger of making projections from small numbers.From 1876-1882 O'Rourke's real home run totals were 2, 0,1,1,6,0,2.  From those small numbers the author projected these home run figures: 41, 0, 38, 29, 57, 0, 27. Amazing.  The author projected 38 home runs from an actual total of 1, and projected 57 home runs from a real-life total of 6.This book looks great. Wonderful cover, nice layout. The player stats are easy to read. It's a shame that the stats are meaningless.  Like Ruth's projected 1,157 career homer runs or Aaron's 947.
