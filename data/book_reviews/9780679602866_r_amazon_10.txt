Amazon Australia has mistakenly listed this book as translated by Edith Grossman, but this is wrong! This translation is by John Ormsby (1885).I have read both translations completely and in depth and I prefer the Ormsby (this translation). Grossman's translation is written in modern English but I found it rather dry. Ormsby's translation is written in an older, but not archaic, form of English. For me, Ormsby's translation is more suited to the period of the book; it is richer, more colourful and flows beautifully. It is easier and more comfortable to read than the Grossman version.
