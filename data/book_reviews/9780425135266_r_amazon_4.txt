This was a great book and a great read!  Being a veteran it was great to read about Norman's past, experiences, and how he wanted to make changes to the US Army and DID! Yes, the Gulf War was a technological success, but as Norman kept saying in the book--it takes the soldier on the ground to win the wars!  I have told many of my veteran friends to read this book to get a better perspective on inner workings of planning such an incredible campaign!
