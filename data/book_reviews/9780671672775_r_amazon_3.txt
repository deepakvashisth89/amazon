The first novels I read by Doug were 'You come when I call you' and 'Naomi'. I was instantly hooked. Of course I just read everything I could get from him and this one doesn't dissapoint. I am very partial to his earlier writings. This novel is very good. i thought it started slow and wondered where it would go and then BAM... right in the face. You can not be dissapointed with anything he writes. very good character development and the pacing of the stories, while slow at times, always catches up but not in a hurried manner.
