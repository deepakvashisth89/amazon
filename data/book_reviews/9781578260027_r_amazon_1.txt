I just can't resist checking out fresh young voices whose books deal with New York City sleaze and whose main characters are named Nick.Nick is a young bartender in a working class bar in the west 40's.  He is a boxer who is training for his big fight.  Naturally, the local mob figures want him  to throw the fight.  His best friend, Jimmy, is a loser who patronizes  massage and porn parlors, drinks too much, and always is trying schemes  that don't work.  The author uses simple words but yet gets into the heart  and soul of the characters.  I felt their reality as they moved about in  their world, breathed the dust on the West Side Highway, smelled the beer  in the bar, felt the mugginess of the summer heat wave.  More  characterization than story, but I still couldn't stop reading.  A good  first novel and an author to watch. Recommended.Read moreRead less
