Based on a true skirmish at Glorieta Pass, the historical data is factual, but Nagle has created characters on both sides of the conflict that are colorful, believable an intriguing. Men and women, Union and Rebel, the characters come off the page and capture the imagination of this reader. Not as much a history buff as some of the readers in this genre, I am still fascinated by authors who can write such believable fiction using factual accounts and sprinkling the story with some flexibility and strong characters.
