Heinlein was one of Sci-Fi's greatest. His talent for blending the known, the theoretical, and his own imagination were pure genius -  but his stories share a simple, matter-of-fact quality which makes them accessible to almost any reader. Space Cadet, like many of his works, uses this style to tell a simple story about a young man pursuing a career as a spaceman in a future world based from 1950's culture. It rolls along at pleasant speed and doesn't trouble the reader with overly technical details. We learn of this future through the man's eyes and thrill at his steady progress overcoming small obstacles along the way. Fun.Heinlein's great talent was the social commentary and philosophical analysis which run under the surface, allowing the reader to digest deep concepts slowly without being beaten over the head with them. The concepts of ignorance, prejudice, and morality addressed leave you feeling uplifted and hopeful.
