Is there no end to Peter Lovesey's talents as a crime writer in particular and a literary stylist in general. As crime aficionados will know, this book received widespread praise from critics and readers alike when it was first published in 1982 and is considered by some to be his finest hour. This is even more remarkable, given that this was his first new venture since departing from the brilliant Sergeant Cribb novels which defined his output in the 70s. Lots has been said about Lovesey's erudite historical research and his ability to utilise this to create amazing historical crime fiction, but this is only one aspect of his talent. As he has proved with his Peter Diamond books, he can do contemporary characterisation. He is brilliant at plotting (masterful) and above all his literary writing style, and ear for dialogue are pitch perfect. Like John Le Carre, this writer has long been considered high end popular fiction writing in one genre, but he is a true literary artist and needs to be given the credit for creating deeply satisfying works of art. I long to hear that a TV company have decided to film the Diamond books. I've just read the Reaper his standalone from 2000, his short story collection 'Reader, I buried them and Showstopper from last year and again they are absolute gems. It is a privilege to read his novels and there isn't one I haven't enjoyed. He needs a lifetime achievement award for his contribution to literature.
