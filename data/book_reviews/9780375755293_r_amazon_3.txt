Agee was an early critic worth reading and he opened the way for the superior work of Andrew Sarris-Pauline Kael, who best him. Though a lovely writer, he seldom mentions the names of directors, he forgets the names of actors (and refuses to look em up) and often states he hasnt seen x or y, like the stage musical "Oklahoma!," which he then trashes.  Not good for any critic. In my edition he refers to "Double Indemnity" as another Brackett-Wilder film, when the writer  with Wilder was Raymond Chandler. He salutes many films, like "Since You Went Away," which are forgotten today and misses  Film Noir completely, now an important historical genre.  In sum, he's vastly overrated.Read moreRead less
