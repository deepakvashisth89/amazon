"The Possibilities Of Prayer" by EM Bounds focuses on guidelines for establishing and maintaining a vital prayer life.  Indeed, Bounds does a masterful job and some of his points are as follows:1.  We have not because we ask not.  God's promises are dependent and conditioned upon prayer.2.  God's promises are specific and personal and directed towards individuals.  The answers to His promises are the result of earnest prayer.3.  Nothing is too hard for God!  God can withhold nothing from faith and prayer.4.  Undue worry and anxiety about earthly concerns can be eliminated by prayer.  God is concerned about the smallest things in our lives.5.  Great faith enables Christ to do great things.6.  Ours is an anxious world. Prayer can quiet every distraction, hush every anxiety, and lift every care from care-enslaved lives.7.  The Lord's presence and power will be most felt in the prayer closet.8.  Answers to our prayers result from our abiding in Christ.9.  Christ demands specific praying and responds to specific prayers with specific answers.10. Prayer is simply asking God to do for us what He has promised us He will do if we ask Him.A lot of power is packed into this approximately 120 page.
