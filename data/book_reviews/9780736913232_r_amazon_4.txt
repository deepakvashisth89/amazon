I must admit that I did NOT like Tanner through the first half of the book.  I thought he was quite bossy, but in his defense Stacy allowed him to treat her that way...I didn't think he was abusive, exactly, but I DO believe he was treating her more like a child than a wife...Lori Wick has done it again!  This story was so heart-wrenching at times, and so romantic at others.  Tanner was a self-centered man who seemed to have only one though on his mind:  bedding Stacy.  Even after she gave birth to their second baby and he watched her breast feeding their daughter, I felt he just wanted her for physical reasons, and he was jealous he wouldn't have that...But after the accident, I believe he realized there was so much more to love than sex.  He realized that just holding her, doing things for her, and cherishing her were also important aspects.  He discovered that he had loved her this whole time, and he'd do anything to hold onto her.They found true love!  And Stacy finally found her backbone, and boy was Tanner shocked!!!Read moreRead less
