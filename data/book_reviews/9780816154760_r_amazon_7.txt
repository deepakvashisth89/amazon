Needful Things was my second King novel, first was Misery, and I really enjoyed this one. Classic good vs evil tale that seems so relatable to today’s climate. At 800 pages, I was a little intimidated at first but it is a very fast read. My only problem is there are so many characters to keep straight. I recommend maybe taking some short notes each time a new person is introduced. Looking forward to more king books.Read moreRead less
