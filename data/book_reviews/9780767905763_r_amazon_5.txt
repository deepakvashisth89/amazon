This is a book about the life of Robert Young Pelton. He is alive and kicking and is extremely candid about himself in this book. You can see the back story about his life and really see how he rose up from nothing in very difficult circumstances to become a multi millionaire and then to ultimately become one of the greatest adventurers of the last 100 years. The book is about adventure but it is way more than that too. The author is pretty brutally honest about things and pulls no punches in this. There is zero attempt to seek to hide the warts of life. If you have ever thought about someone (or are someone) who was or is in a broken situation, abused, and in a bad spot, but you have a good head on your shoulders... this is just one example of one person and how they capitalized on what they had to make something good of it all.Read moreRead less
