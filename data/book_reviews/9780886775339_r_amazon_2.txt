The book contains eighteen stories of various lengths.There were lots of 'Meh' types. Several authors clearly established that scifi is not their forte.Many authors sneaked in various exotic concepts simply because some of the characters are alien— with results that were occasionally humorous and more often eye-rolling.Then there were some stories that actually dealt with human drama at its core, presenting a dark or otherwise mirror before us. I liked them most. These are~1. Laura Resnick's "Heaven's Only Daughter";2. Virginia Booth's "Heaven Scent";3. Barbara Delaplace's "Lost Lamb";4. Jack Nimersheim's "Cain's Curse";5. Esther M Friesener and Walter J Stutzman's "Dead Ringer".They have prompted me to search for individual collections penned by these authors, but I am not having much luck.Overall, a readable collection with some stand-outs.Read moreRead less
