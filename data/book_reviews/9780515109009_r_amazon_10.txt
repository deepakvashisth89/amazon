I was engrossed in this medieval mystery from the first page. The historical facts and language used make for excellent reading and the pace at which the story moves leaves no room for boredom. The only factor which left it wanting was a much weaker ending compared to the rest of the story but given the time setting I may be judging it too harshly. Reading this on my kindle, made accessing the definitions for old English words instantaneous which helped keep the story flowing although there were one or two for which the dictionary found no meaning.
