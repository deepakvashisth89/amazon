Deus Irae is a surrealist and spiritual journey that takes place after World War III and follows Tibor McMasters, the armless and legless 'incomplete'. In this dystopian future, Earth is a barren landscape where creatures and humans of all sorts are found. Tibor McMasters an artist is on a quest to find 'GOD' and take an appropriate picture of him/her/zer to paint. Deus Irae presents a medieval & dystopian atmosphere paired with some of Dick's best characters along the lines of Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep? Though, like the Three Stigmata of Palmer Eldritch, Deus Irae's main theme is a discussion of god, or a higher power and what that can mean to each individual in the never ending quest for reason. Deus Irae ties together some of PKD's best themes and most in depth characters. Along with Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep?
