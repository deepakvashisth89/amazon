First off, a note on the Arcturus classics line: The most appropriate description I can give of the line would be simple yet elegant. The covers tend to be very nicely done in a spare but attractive way and look nice on your bookshelf or coffee table. They hold up well to multiple readings as the bindings are good and the text is clear. My only complaint would be the lack of supplemental material. Don't expect lengthy introductions, glossaries or explanatory notes. Overall these editions are well done but without notes or glossaries some of the antiquated language can defy understanding. However, if you just want to jump right into the story without being distracted by anything else, these are more than adequate.This particular edition of Far From the Madding Crowd (1874) contains a single page introduction summarizing Hardy's literary career, a map of his fictional town of Wessex where several of his novels were set, and his preface to the 1895 edition (2 pages).The story has been reviewed sufficiently elsewhere so I'll simply say that anyone who enjoys a good love story where the heroes are made to suffer - sometimes by their own poor choices and sometimes by the whims of fate - before finding eventual satisfaction will revel in this tale. Hardy's talent for describing heartbreak renders his scenes of happiness tame by comparison, and somehow makes the story all the more sweet.Fine reading!Read moreRead less
