Originally published in 2001 (with strips from 2000), this follows Book 3 directly as Jeremy Duncan continues to explore life with his friends and parents. There’s a great deal to enjoy in this (so many panels made me laugh out loud) and although a few of strips again take a surreal turn (which work, every single time), so much of this rings bells with me as the father of a teenager that it was just wonderful. There are a lot of highlights - the very question of “are we an us?”, sitting on the roof with Hector talking, big brother Chad, adventures in babysitting, Dad jokes, Mum’s stern word and gaze and life at school. Superb - both in terms of writing and artwork - this is really funny, a comic masterpiece that I loved and would highly recommend.Read moreRead less
