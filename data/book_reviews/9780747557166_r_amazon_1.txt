I've had this book for maybe five or six years, and every recipe I've tried has worked out. It has everything from vegetarian classics like daal to more unusual combinations. (Tofu and mushroom stroganoff gets my vote). Because it's organised in chapters according to ingredients, you can look up recipes for whatever you've got in your fridge, or whatever looks good at the markets.
