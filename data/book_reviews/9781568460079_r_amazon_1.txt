This is one of the many Greek classic tales that Nathaniel Hawthorne, the great novelist of mid-nineteenth century New England retold to be enjoyed by children. It is the classic “The Garden of Hesperides,” one of the many tales about the Greek hero Hercules, although Hawthorne adds suspense to the tale by not revealing that the hero is Hercules until part of the story has been told. It is a tale that children will enjoy.Many people looked for the famed garden, but none found it; in fact some who went searching never returned. In this tale Hercules went searching because his cruel cousin who was the king made him do it. The difficulty was that the garden was guarded by a dragon with 100 heads and Hercules, like the others, did not know where the garden was located..Hercules protected himself by carrying a massive club. He wore a shaggy lion’s skin from a lion he had killed.Hercules goes through many adventures in this drama and meets many strange but interesting people, including a group of damsels with whom Hercules stays for a while and tells them some of his many adventures. He also meets “The Old Man of the Sea” who can change his shape at will but who doesn’t want to tell Hercules where the garden is. He also meets with others such as the giant who holds up the sky.Children will find the tale fascinating. It is a great way to introduce them to classical literature.Read moreRead less
