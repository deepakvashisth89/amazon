This 1833 novel is a picture of provencial France and the values of the country folk.  The father is miserly to the extent that his wife and daughter are kept in virtual destitution while he accumulates gold.  He shows his wife mercy only on her deathbed, and even then the motive is gold. Unfortunately, his daughter Eugenie, after being romantically deserted by her Parisian cousin, lives to become almost as miserly as her father.
