I was unprepared for the back story on that person. And it was a really good one. For that reason I had to give this story a full five stars. Also I love the murder mysteries set in a small English backwater village. This one had a particularly good twist in that although it has the obligatory landed gentry going to seed, the main characters are a particularly vulgar group of TV serial actors and their accompanying retinue. Then you have all of the oddball people that one finds in small in-bred communities all over the world. Overall this was a straight-forward English countryside murder mystery and it is well written and very consuming. A wonderful read.Read moreRead less
