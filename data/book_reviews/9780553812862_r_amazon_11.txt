Four very different women whose lives are entwined in some way. At the beginning, they are adults embarking on a foreign holiday together and then the book goes back to when they were born, the lives they led and how their paths crossed. The characters are well drawn and believable as all have their flaws and redeeming qualities. This is a long but enjoyable read as it covers the lives of each character from childhood to adulthood with the book reaching its climax when they all holiday together. In spite of the inevitable problems and clashes of personality with 4 women thrown together on a Greek island, the book has a good ending with ghosts from the past being buried and the air cleared so you are left with a good feeling that each woman will have a positive future and the ties that bind them will grow ever stronger.Read moreRead less
