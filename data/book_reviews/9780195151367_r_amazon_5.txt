Like "Casting The Runes" in this same series of Oxford World's Classics, this edition is liberally sprinkled with asterisks to indicate explanatory footnotes -- but the footnotes aren't included.I imagine this is because Lewis's text is copyright-free, but not the editorial work to which those asterisks once alluded. To excise helpful editorial material is disappointing enough, but to leave those asterisks -- in this age of global search-and-replace -- is astonishing sloppiness from the esteemed OUP.Do please note that I refer to the out-of-print 2002 hardcover edition.
