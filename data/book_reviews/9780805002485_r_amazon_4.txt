I appreciate Sue Grafton's writing style, which is as bright as it is competent.  She writes a suspenseful story.  However, after reading this fourth book of her alphabet mystery series, I have concluded that I don't much like the main character, Kinsey Millhone.  In this book, we find out more about Millhone's early life, which no doubt shapes her exaggerated independence, spirtless lifestyle, and underdeveloped morality.  She does have a diligent sense of duty and compassion for the downtrodden, which are both admirable qualities and essential for managing her cases.  Nevertheless, despite my respect for the skilled writing and one-of-a-kind plotlines, I will likely not continue reading this series.  (Kindle Unlimited)Read moreRead less
