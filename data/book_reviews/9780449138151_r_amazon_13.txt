The Travis McGee novels are an odd mix of violence, sexual conquests and state-of-the-nation introspection.  There are moments of real drama, but overall the books seem dated, sexist and very long reads for quite short novels. In A Deadly Shade of Gold, the 5th book in the series, McGee gets drawn into a complex web of greed and murder involving gold statues that have cost the life of an old buddy.  Part of the book is set in Mexico, and it is here that the storytelling is at its most vibrant and engaging.As with other titles in the series though, the story meanders on way too long and follows a familiar pattern.  There is plenty of action and violence, followed by McGee recuperating and ending an intense and unsuccessful relationship with one of the strong female leads that get chucked into these yarns.  By the end, you don't really care too much about what has happened.  Mediocre stuff at best.Read moreRead less
