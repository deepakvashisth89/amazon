Schnitzel Von Krumm is excited by the carryings on of his family.  They are moving all sorts of things around and putting them in a car and trailer.  But so busy are they that they have no time to play.  As Schnitzel tries to see what's what he is told he is in the way.  Even bed is not a safe place as he gets tossed out and told to behave.  So he watches the action as things are piled and packed and finally they drive away.Could they have forgotten Schnitzel?  It seems that way.  But Schnitzel's cries and howls are heard by the neighbor Miss Plum.  But as Miss Plum comes to the rescue the family realizes that there is something not right.  They have forgotten Schnitzel Von Krumm!  Everything works out in the end and everyone is happy.Schnitzel Von Krumm can be seen in BASKETWORK, DOGS NEVER CLIMB TREES, and also makes appearances in a number of Hairy Maclary books.  This delightful dachshund with the very low tum is a wonderful character.  Lynley Dodd tells of his adventures in a wonderful rhyming lyrical style that uses grown-up language.
