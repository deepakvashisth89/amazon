Forget the game-theory purists; this books distills out the practicalities and provides an insight to everyday living and decision making such that I believe it should be a standard read for all curricula - college, and maybe even at the high school level. This gets to the core of providing a tool for navigating through life's decisions, and does it in a manner that does not demand the prior knowledge of specialized mathematics or other esoterica of game theory. To the critics that say it does not go far enough into the theory of game theory, I say, no; it gives us, the Everyman, an understanding of how to make better life decisions.
