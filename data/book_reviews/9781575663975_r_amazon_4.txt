This was an entertaining, easy, light read, but the coincidences built into the story line are a bit much to swallow. It's not enough that one character meets Solzhenitsyn in the Gulag,  another happens to meet JFK in  a bar, a third fights at Iwo Jima and helps raise the flag, and a fourth  character happens to be at Churchill's famous "Iron Curtain"  speech.
