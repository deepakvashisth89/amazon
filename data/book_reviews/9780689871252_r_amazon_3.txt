This is an amazing story that incorporates adventure, mystery, and sci-fi fantasy all in one.  I love the author, Robert O'Brien's work, and wanted to read his other books after reading the wonderful Mrs. Frisby and the  Rats of NIMH.  The Silver Crown is a completely different kind of tale, but  the story telling is just as memorable.  The characters are complete, and  you feel as if you know them personally, just as another fan has mentioned  below.  With 47 respondents at the time I write this looking for their own  copy, it is clearly evident that the publishers need to reprint this book.  I found it in our public library system, and just finished reading it.  I  would love to buy a copy for myself, and to read to my fifth grade class.  Come on publishers!
