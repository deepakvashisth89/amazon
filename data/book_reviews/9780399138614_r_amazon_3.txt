I picked a great time to read Curfew, or Crybbe as it's known in the UK. Like Crybbe, we've had power issues the past couple of weeks, and while the reasons for our outages was a big storm that polarized our community plus a tree branch that knocked out our power line, it does make one wonder. Phil Rickman is a great one to make a person think. In Crybbe, we ponder about past psychic energy and the affects it might have on our own mental health. In the book Curfew, Phil Rickman tells the tale of a singular, centuries old community and how old energy and energy sources can be the explanation of the ley lines through the community. A new ager wants to renew the energy by placing the rocks back on an old ley line that was pilferred by farmers and the rocks misplaced, perhaps for good reason, over the years. When the ley line is almost complete real trouble starts to brew.This book was an entertaining read from start to finish. Phil Rickman is a wonderful story teller that brings to life the border towns on the English/Wales border. He has wonderful histories built into his tales and legends that are whispered from old villagers. For a cozy winter's read I would recommend Phil Rickman and his tales any day!Read moreRead less
