This one is tied for my favorite Gardner book. It takes a little perseverance to get through the first three chapters, where the central character lives through a cataclysmic disaster that shapes the kind of person she becomes later. Without giving the plot away, I can say that this is a story where you go through hell and come out okay on the other side, but a little bit quirky. No fairy-tale ending, but no wretched dystopia either. It's one of my all-time favorites, and I can't recommend it too highly.Read moreRead less
