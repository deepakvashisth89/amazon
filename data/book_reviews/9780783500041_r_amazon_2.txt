This well written and properly researched Time-Life book consists mainly of the stories of six mass murderers, including a brief biography of each, their motivations for murder, and graphic photos of their crime scenes.  In addition there is a section titled DAYS OF RAGE which gives a brief synopsis of 14 additional murderers who killed a total of 123 people.Rather than name the big six for you, perhaps you will remember them by their crimes: the nurse killer, the Texas tower shooter,  New Orleans rage shooter. murder at McDonald's, the Canadian woman hater, and rampage in an English village.  I had previously read books about two of these killers but the other four were new to me.  In any event, each section gives you enough information to determine if you want to learn more about any of these episodic murderers.There is no poetic license in this book, only stark language and crisp descriptions about factual situations. The accompanying photos and illustrations add emphasis and clarity to each story.  Not for the faint of heart, but a good start for those who are interested in psychopathic personalities and what their personal demons (or delusions) make them do.Read moreRead less
