I usually enjoy the Texas Cattleman's Club series. I usually enjoy Sara Orwig's books.  Neither of those worked for me with this book.He's a government diplomat, she's a small town school teacher. They meet at a party, hook up, she gets pregnant.  He's in love with her, practically at first sight.  She spends 95% of the book telling him why he can't love her. It's so annoying.There's not a woman alive who has feelings for a man, is going to have his baby,  and wants nothing to do with him because he professes his love for her.  The entire plot makes no sense.Her big fear is that the town will think she's a prostitute or woman of loose morals like her mother because they had 1 night stand  and he has money & she doesn't.  News flash - they won't think that if you marry the man who is chasing you around,  in love with you, & also is the father of your baby.  That's called a future, doesn't make you a prostitute.This 172 page book could have been 25 pages if she would have stopped telling him that he couldn't love her, and just accepted that she was lovable.The secondary plot to her constant denial & whining is the TCC is trying to solve the case of the missing diamond after a plane crash.This is a stand alone, HEA book, that just happens to be a part of the TCC series.Read moreRead less
