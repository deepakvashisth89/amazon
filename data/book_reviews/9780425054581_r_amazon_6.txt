Over the years have read this series many times. It is a classic science fiction series that was written by a Master of the genre. While much of it is outdated by todays standards it is still worth reading. Keep in mind that there were authorized and unauthorized versions released by various publishers. Consequently some of the issues contain many editing errors.  Regardless of which version you may get it remains a well written story that is both entertaining and attention riveting. E.E.
