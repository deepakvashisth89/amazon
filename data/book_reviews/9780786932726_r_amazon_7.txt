This book is a great supplement to the world of Dungeons and Dragons.  It brings forth the kind of divine power that would be appropriate and allows for that power to come about over the course of the game.  The Complete Divine offers a wide variety of character options for all classes that choose to worship a deity and the effect on character development is terrific.
