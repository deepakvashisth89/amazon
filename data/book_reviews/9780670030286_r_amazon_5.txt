Despite the fact the book was written several years ago it's still the best book out there for a comprehensive, objective overview of neuroscience and behavior/personality in existence today (without author subjective interpretations of research which is more often what you'll find in similar books).Some parts of the book are extremely detailed and are good if you need to know neural-synaptic activity inside-out (there is new research that should be added). Other parts are vital for the basic understanding on how the brain works, especially when it comes to the physical representations of behaviors/thoughts/emotions.Highly recommended if you are looking for specific well defined answers to specific questions, not as a general entertaining read.
