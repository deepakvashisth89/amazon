Very interesting book. This book provides a very clear pronouncement that you don't have to settle for aging - that the primary age markers can be improved with a plan and action, both of which are explained in this book. It goes deeply into the science and explains itself completely. My only reservations about the information presented is that 1) the book is over 10 years old - I wish there was an updated edition and 2) giving checklists and worksheets on the actions required would be more helpful and 3) this book spends very little time on the nutritional aspects of improving biomarkers. But for the information it presents, it is a winner.Read moreRead less
