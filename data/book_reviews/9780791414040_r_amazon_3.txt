This is one of the few books that criticizes Buddhism, while believing it is still a worthy tradition. It does come from a scholarly perspective, which is difficult if you're not used to that kind of thing. But the one key point is that the Buddha changed his mind. Siddhartha was not a god, he was a human being that had realized a certain potential. Yet in forming his order, he made up rules as he went along, and eventually added women to them. There is debate as to whether he was misogynist, or a lot of that was added later. But regardless, the tradition can be changed, and is being changed.Read moreRead less
