I got this book based on its excellent reviews in preparation for a semester in Italy.  Usually I choose Lonely Planet or the Rough Guides for travelling, and now I wish I had stuck to those.  The book is difficult to read and its attitude makes me angry every time I open it.  I feel like it doesn't have very much history or background, and putting prices for every attraction in parenthesis in the text is distracting.
