The premise of this novel is interesting - a dead man resurrected through an act of faith by a lapsed believer. Or was he really dead in the first place? This is a Catholic novel but you don't have to be a believer to find it engaging. But the narrative tension does tend to go a bit slack as the story progresses and the prose isn't perhaps so intense as it should be for a religious novel. I enjoyed it but I can see why it might not be for everyone.Read moreRead less
