I first bought this book/CD set over 12 years ago, but as time passed I had misplaced it.  As a harmonica enthusiast, I wanted to learn some good ol' American songs and patriotic tunes on the harmonica before the Fourth of July, and I thought of this book.  So I ordered it, and I will never misplace it again.Jon Gindick does a wonderful job of making it easy to learn all of the book's songs on a C major diatonic harmonica.  No prior musical training or ability to read music necessary.  You can't help but have fun learning!  As for me, I've already got "America the Beautiful" down pretty good; now I'm working on "The Star Spangled Banner," followed by "Battle Hymn of the Republic."A harmonica-playing resource.
