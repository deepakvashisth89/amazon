"Star Trek: Log One" is basically just three episodes from the animated series put down on paper, with around 60 pages devoted to each (give or take).  Though if you're like me and never watched the cartoon, then it's like a whole new set of adventures for the Enterprise and her crew (and you can imagine it with the real actors in your mind).  The writing is pretty good, all things considered (aside from a number of typographical errors that should have been caught prior to publishing).  The problem is that the quality of the episodes themselves is nothing to write home about.  I can see them working in a cartoon but they're hardly worth the time and effort to sit and read through.
