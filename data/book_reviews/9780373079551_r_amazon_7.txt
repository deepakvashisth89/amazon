Rated:  3.5 starsNote:  Stands alone read.  Part of Conrad County seriesGOOD READ.  Well written.  Unusual storyline features two strong, engaging characters who learn that life changing events can lead to new possibilities.  Good dialogue, genuine character interactions, and a great supporting case enhance the story.  HOWEVER, the heroine's focus on her illness and her self-imposed isolation, are a little wearing at times.THE STORY revolves around longtime diabetic, Angela Jaynes, and D.E.A agent Rafe Ortiz, who meet while staying with mutual friends.  Angela is taking a break because she is unable to control her diabetes due to job stress.  Rafe is taking a break from his job, after the shooting death of an old flame leaves him with an infant son.  Turning to Angela for help, their budding friendship turns into a touching romance.OVERALL, the unique characters and storyline make for an engrossing read.You may also enjoy the complex characters and storytelling of N. Bruhns, A. Stuart, R. C. Ryan, E. Palfrey, or L. Wilde. For sweet, funny, heart touching romances with great descriptive imagery try Susan Fox or Cara Colter.Read moreRead less
