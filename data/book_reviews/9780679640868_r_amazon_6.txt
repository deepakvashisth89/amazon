I found it kind of difficult to rate this book. I enjoyed reading it very much, but it was clear from the start that it is lacking a major component. With a book discussing such a dramatically visual period of human history, the written description of the unique paintings, sculptures and architecture that punctuated that era are never enough. In this specific context, a picture certainly says more than a thousand words, and this book had none.Lucky for me, I started early on to go online to see what the sculptures, buildings, and paintings that I'm reading about look like. It helped a great deal to be reading a Kindle version of the book on my I-pad. It made Google Search and Wikipedia just clicks away. Doing so transformed the experience of reading this book. It allowed me to soar in the skies of Florence, Venice and Rome to enjoy the beauty of their historical buildings, and visit the modern museums of France, England and Italy to adore the great paintings and sculptures that are beyond description by words. The combination of online search for images with reading the book would certainly put my rating of its content at 5. I'm not sure how my rating would be if I only read the book. I would expect it to be a 3 or a 4, but I decided to chose 4.I highly recommend that any newer editions of this book have more images of the objects described in it.
