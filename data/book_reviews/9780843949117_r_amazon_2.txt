Lady Gypsy is an outstanding book written by author Pam Crooks.  I have read several of her books, but I must say, this book captured the attention of my heart.  The tension between Liza and Reece’s respective worlds, that of a railroad man and the gypsy heritage, is thoroughly developed so the reader understands what it is like to live in both worlds.  In life, it is true we make assumptions of people that are not correct.  So, in this book.  Danger, grit, unusual rituals, and most of all LOVE is weaved into every page.  I could not put the book down!  Awesome book!  A nail biter to the end!Read moreRead less
