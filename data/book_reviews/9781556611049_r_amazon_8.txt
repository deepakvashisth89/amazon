The children in this book make us root for them. They have suffered the loss of their mother in their way to be with their Uncle Nick in California. They have no idea what to expect when they get there. However, their mother had instilled in then the importance of family. So, this family bonds together through one adventure after another until they really come face to face with the true family they have.Though this is a light read, it was enjoyable.
