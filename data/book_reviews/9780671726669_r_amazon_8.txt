Remember that just because a product is "the best of its kind" that does not mean it is good.  There is a reason this book is out of print.  It just frankly is not that good.  And since this book is claimed to be the "best" then there must not exist an unbiased version of the story.  Just as the TV Movie is biased toward Dan's way of seeing things, this book is just as heavily biased toward Betty.  Even though the author pretends to show an unbiased side, the majority source for her over-explained detail is Betty and her diary.Are we really to believe that Betty's screaming, yelling, fit-throwing, and childish behavior started the minute Dan had an affair?  That is no more likely than Dan turning suddenly cold and withholding his affection to manipulate at the same time.  Many times the author takes Betty's side even when it does not make sense.  Let's see, the contempt charges are all Dan's fault and he is just trying to beat Betty down.  Hello!  How about ceasing to violate court orders?  How about talking like a decent human on the telephone machine?  Those fall in Betty's lap as things she could have easily controlled but chose poorly.I would really like to see a more factual book but am afraid such an item does not exist.
