Beware! "Celtic Mysticism: Walking with the Gods" is a misleading title for what is essentially a piece of Christian propaganda. The title would lead one to believe that the book is Pagan in focus; the word "Gods" is, after all, plural. Pagans are, however, denigrated throughout the book, and described with words like "dark" and "primitive". In describing the conversion of Celtic myths into a "Christian layman's spirituality", he says: "Nothing of value is lost." He says that "pagan concepts of deity were not so much denied as corrected" as if our concepts need correcting. He further states: "Much of pagan prayer was too often an expression of fearful relationships and was propitiatory is character." Talk about the pot calling the kettle black! Christians are told outright to fear their god, and it is only the more liberal forms of Christianity that talk of a God of love. He never stops to consider that the Pagans of old may have loved their Gods as well as he loves his. He totally ignores the fact that most of Europe was converted to Christianity at the point of a sword, and that thousands of Pagans were tortured and murdered for refusing to accept Baptism. They loved their faith and they loved their Gods, as we today who call ourselves Pagan love ours. This book is nothing but a "bait and switch" Christian scam. It seeks to draw in Pagans with the popularity of Celtic material and a deliberatly misleading title, then to convert them to Christianity.
