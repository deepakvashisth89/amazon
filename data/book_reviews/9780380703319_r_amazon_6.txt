As a fan of author MacLeod’s Shandy and Kelling-Bittersohn mysteries, I gave the first book in this series a whirl.  Typical of this author’s work, the book featured quirky characters, comical situations, and witty descriptions and dialog.  As a mystery, though, it was a disappointment.  Loads of information pertinent to the solution to the crime was withheld from the reader until the very end after the villain’s identity had already been revealed, making an unsatisfactory resolution for readers who like to play the whodunit game.Read moreRead less
