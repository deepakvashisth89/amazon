I think the original title of this book -- The Magic Flute: Masonic Opera --- fits it better, but one recognizes that "unveiling" things has long been a way to steer attention one's own way. Nonetheless, this is a marvelous book. It is great more for the realistic sense that Chailley actually had about Freemasonry as a phenomenon, that is, if compared with other Non-Mason interpreters. The overall argument of the book is less persuasive to me than the myriad discrete analyses he provides. Some of them are almost off -the- charts for perceptive grasp. I have tried  to amplify some of Chailley's insights, and also provide new ones, in the context to creating a new theory about Mozart and the Craft,.
