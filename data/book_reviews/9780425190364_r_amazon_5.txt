I liked it.  There are so many things in this novel that sound familiar.  It takes place in Chicago and all the landmarks of the near northside are there.  The Irish weddings, the Irish wakes, the cadence of the speech... this author has a deft hand with putting those into a novel.  I laughed at the chicken on the bus incident (stranger things have happened on the CTA!), and sympathized with Danny in 2nd grade.  He used to faint when the church was too hot or the sermon too long;  I used to throw up.  His aunt knew his teacher from being in the convent with her;  my aunt knew my teacher from the convent as well and spoke up for me.  Of course I enjoyed reading this book - there is a lot of truth in it and I believe a lot of personal experiences of the author.  The one reservation I have is the ending.  Without spoiling anything, I do wish the ending had been more thought out.  Despite an unsatisfying conclusion (for me) I really enjoyed this book.Read moreRead less
