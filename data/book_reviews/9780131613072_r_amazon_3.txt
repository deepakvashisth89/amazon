Alderson does a good job of putting together an overview of the singing voice and an excellent variety of vocalises that can be used in training and developing singers. This book falls short in that it is a tiny bit dated in the verbiage, and that it focuses exclusively on the classical idea of good singing, with little acceptance of other styles. But it's well organized and easy to follow. The chapters are sometimes wordy, but it's not a difficult read. Definitely a worthy addition to my shelf of pedagogical resources.Read moreRead less
