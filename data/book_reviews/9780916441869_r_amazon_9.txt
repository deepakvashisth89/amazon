5 stars & 5/10 hearts. Wow. This story contains a powerful message. What would Jesus do? The people in this story pledge themselves for a year to do nothing without first asking themselves that question. It was revolutionary. There are amazing thoughts and quotes in this book. I don’t quite agree with all the ideas, and wasn’t quite sure about the “feeling the Spirit” aspect, or the vision at the end. But this story was powerful and I highly recommend it.A Favourite Quote: “‘I want volunteers from the First Church who will pledge themselves, earnestly and honestly for an entire year, not to do anything without first asking the question, 'What would Jesus do?' And after asking that question, each one will follow Jesus as exactly as he knows how, no matter what the result may be.... Our aim will be to act just as He would if He was in our places, regardless of immediate results. In other words, we propose to follow Jesus' steps as closely and as literally as we believe He taught His disciples to do. And those who volunteer to do this will pledge themselves for an entire year, beginning with today, so to act.’”A Favourite Beautiful Quote: “It was Sunday morning and the clear sweet spring air [was] just beginning to breathe over the city the perfume of early blossoms in the woods and fields[.]”A Favourite Humorous Quote: “She was still a little agitated by the fact of receiving a proposal of marriage on the avenue.”Read moreRead less
