Am very pleased with Dr. Bergin's philosophy and approach. A very humane way to understand and work with and form an excellent bond with your dog.  Emphasizes not who is in control, but how to match personalities (or behavior styles) of owner and dog before we even start working with our dog, or understanding our own and our dogs personalities so we can match our approach to our dog's needs or style.  I've never encountered this concept and it makes so much sense.  We want our dog personality to match ours as much as possible (our mates are usually opposites, but not our pets) and Bonnie helps the reader understand this very well.  This and the sections on emotional balance and how to achieve it are the heart of this book, and something every dog owner should read and understand.  She also includes two chapters on training, teaching 43 basic or core commands and how to do it easily.  This is a real confidence building book:  laying down principles for building the bond we all want with our dog, and concrete approaches to teaching our dog specific commands that we can expand on ourselves.
