I never really leave reviews on books, but this one was so bad that I felt I should warn prospective buyers. The "hero" was so unlikeable, arrogant and completely unreasonable that I failed to find ANY redeeming qualities about him. Mina was a strong character and a lot more likeable. I would stop short of calling this a romance because the characters so rarely shared civil words with each other that I found it hard to believe they were suddenly in love in the last few pages of the book. I would not recommend reading this book even if it were free. I was VERY disappointed because I loved Margaret Moore's "The Overlord's Bride" and was looking forward to reading another of her books.Read moreRead less
