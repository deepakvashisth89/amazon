Mr. Bonaduce is an amazing person.  This guy went from obscurity to stardom, then to skid row and back.  He tells all in this book.  From the Partridge Family days of his youth, to the drugs and sleeping in his car of adolescence, up to his success with reality TV and radio.  If you like Intervention, or enjoy rooting for the underdog, you'll love this book.  I personally have read it three times, and occasionally looked back at favorite passages while dealing with my own personal drug addiction.  Several years after I first read this book, I happened to take a trip to Hollywood with my wife.  We actually ran into Mr. Bonaduce on Hollywood Blvd. in the middle of the day!  He must've been feeling nostalgic, and decided to walk thru his old stomping grounds.  (We met him across from Mann's Chinese Theater-this is where he'd park his car at night to sleep.)  I was shocked, I told my wife, "That was Danny Bonaduce!"  She's been looking the other way when he'd passed, and once I pointed him out, she ran to catch up with him.  He was very nice, and posed for pictures with us both.  In my starstruck state, I snapped the picture with the sun to his back.  He was nice enough to mention that we'd probably make a better picture if I had the sun to my back!  What a great guy!  I managed to tell him that I'd read his book several times, to which he replied, "Oh, good!  Thank you!"
