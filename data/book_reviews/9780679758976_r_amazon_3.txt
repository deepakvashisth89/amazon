Straight out of the horror stories published about cloning Dolly, the sheep, this mystery goes exponential. However, it challenges the nature vs nurture debate and tries to make a case for nurture. It’s still a good mystery and portrays a lot of the problems with academic research and norms.  And a little romance thrown in along with sex to keep the reader wondering what will come next.
