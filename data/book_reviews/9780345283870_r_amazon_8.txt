As I read the book "The Visitors" it seemed like a story of a nice visit from peaceful extraterrestrials. The few encounters humans had with the Visitors gave a hopeful impression of them. The author is very good at showing how difficult it may be for two such different beings and cultures to really understand each other. Being a suspicious human, the last paragraphs made me questioned if the Visitors were as benevolent as they seemed. I started suspecting  that they were using our weaknesses to take over without a fight.
