This was a recent selection of our library literature group.  I tried to read it while I had company visiting, and I couldn't get through it.  Best read when you can devote thought and attention to it.  I'm a pretty athletic reader and can make it through most books.  Love Austen, Elizabeth Gaskell, the book Middlemarch which isn't an easy read.  I asked our librarian and discussion leader how the group fared with the book and she indicated many found it a challenge and a tough slog.  I like to read books written in the past by authors about their time period because you get so much social history.  You feel like you are in a time machine and can discern what is important to the culture at that time period.  So, may give this book another go when it's quiet around the house.
