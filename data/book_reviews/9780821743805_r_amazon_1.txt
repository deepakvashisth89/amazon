Rosemary Trevelyan, the oldest daughter of the fifth Duke of Stedham, had always been a responsible child. Since her mother's death ten years earlier she has slowly taken over the role of running her father's estate. Her three younger sisters along with a passel of relatives depend upon her.A gambler, the Duke wasted away his fortunes until little was left. But he had a plan. He was going to pretend to die and have his heir marry one of his daughters. Then he would miraculously appear after his heir's monies had saved his possessions. Rosemary became privy to this information but she did not like the idea. Unfortunately she didn't have any alternative ideas before her father left.Aubrey, the wealthy young suitor, visits and falls in love with Rosemary and proposes marriage. Steadfast and dependable, Rosemary realizes she has to accept his offer to save everyone else. The young man is smitten and means well when he tries to put everything in order. Always in charge before, Rosemary has a difficult time letting things go.His mother, the controlling sort, sends his uncle to find out what is going on and to put an end to the relationship before he marries. Robert Avery, Earl of Barrisford, is a gentleman first. Believing that Lady Rosemary is a fortune hunter he intends on exposing the family for the criminals they must be. But first he must visit their home and nothing he finds there is as simple as he thought.A CHRISTMAS BETROTHAL is a light romance with some silly moments. Robert and Rosemary clash with the Earl trying to bait Rosemary into forsaking his nephew. Little does he realize that Rosemary would love to save her family if she could find any other way.
