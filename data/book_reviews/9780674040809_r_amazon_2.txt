Observing today's lock-step march toward the cliff (Midnight, December 31, 1999), I decided to seek illumination before I too succumbed to the madness of the crowds.  On searching Amazon.com (keyword: Apocalypses) I was  rewarded with an author, who in 1986, gave me the "Fins de  Siècle".  Eugen Weber's "Apocalypses" was originally  delivered as a lecture early in 1999 at the University of Toronto.  This  book comes over as if the reader were actually attending the lecture,  enjoying Weber's sense of humor as well as his fine tuned intellect.  If I  could only remember a smidgen of the text I would be armed for my next  dinner party, where surely the subject of the Millennium (his  Billennium") will be discussed.
