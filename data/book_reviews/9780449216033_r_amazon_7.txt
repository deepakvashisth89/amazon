I read this book together with my bookclub.  We all hated it and it was the lowest rated book in the history of our bookclub which has been meeting for more than 15 years.  It is confusing and really simply boring.  The characters have no redeeming qualities and the writing style varies from non-sentences, switching from 1st person to 3rd person narrative without any indication who the character is that is narrating and jumping all over in a non-sequential manner, even in the middle of sections.  There are no chapters.. You had to guess who the narrative was about and where it was taking place.  I only finished this book so that I could thoroughly discuss it with my group.  Everyone in my bookclub had trouble reading it, including an English teacher!!!!  If you like esoteric styles, maybe you will like this book, but for most of us, it is a waste of time!!!Read moreRead less
