I am not a professional electrician but I do have some electronic and electrical background. This book will guide you through planning and implementing wiring projects that will be safe and functional. I buy a new copy of the updated code every time I plan on doing a new project. Not a lot of stuff changes at this level most times, but the price of this book is cheap insurance for the safety of your shop or home. It is very easy reading for someone who knows the basics about electricity, but for someone with no knowledge beyond the light switch, it may take a while to get the most out of it. Do-it-yourselfers that have replaced plugs, switches, outlets, and have mustered the courage to turn of the power to the house and peek inside the main electrical panel will find they have a whole new understanding of their electrical system with this book. If you are in the planning stage of a new addition or a workshop, you should get this book so you can make informed decisions about your project if for no other reasons. Well written and organized.
