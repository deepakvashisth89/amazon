Little did I know what I was in for when I picked up INVITATION as a Book Bub offer. I’d never heard of Nancy Moser nor were any of her books familiar.  The theme of “invitation” continues throughout the story of five unique, but ordinary, people who find themselves personally invited to an undefined event in an unknown town on the same midsummer day.  The delivery of these invitations was mysteriously unique to each, as were their responses.Moser did an excellent job of creating unique but believable characters in this story.  Those invited to Haven and those who were their hosts and mentors during their short stay could have been your neighbors or people from your own part of the country. But the invitees were from nearby states, all of whom had to make an affirmative decision to accept the invitation, as vague as it was, and travel to the middle of Nebraska on short notice.The story line brought new experiences to each participant as the days (and nights) progressed. Two tension-filled incidents were used to bring the participants and their hosts together to face the crises and to bring closure to the story.  The reader is left with some problems not completely solved to create interest in upcoming books in the series.I would recommend this book to anyone who enjoys good Christian fiction.
