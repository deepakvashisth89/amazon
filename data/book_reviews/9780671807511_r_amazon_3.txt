I read and reread Peter Straub's Ghost Story so many times over the years that I finally decided, in spite of my fear that nothing else would compare, that it was time to risk reading another of Straub's novels.  Unfortunately, I was correct in suspecting that Julia would pale in comparison.  I did finish the book, but I can't say I truly enjoyed it and I would not waste my time rereading it.  If you're a fan of the genre, do yourself a favor and buy Ghost Story.  The aforementioned is one of the greatest modern horror novels ever written, but I can not say the same for Julia.  On a brighter note, I can highly recommend the seller.
