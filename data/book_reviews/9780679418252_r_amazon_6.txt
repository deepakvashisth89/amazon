I just finished listening to this audiobook. I also bought the kindle version to look up passages later. Before I read this, I was convinced that the Warren Commission was a coverup and that the Mob, Cuba, the CIA and/or the FBI had killed JFK. I was wrong. Gerald Posner is honest and meticulous. He proves absolutely that Oswald acted alone, and Ruby acted alone. He exposes how the FBI lied about a number of things they thought might make them look bad, and both the CIA and FBI tried to hide information, but none of the info that they tried to hide implicated anyone but Oswald and Ruby in the 2 assassinations.
