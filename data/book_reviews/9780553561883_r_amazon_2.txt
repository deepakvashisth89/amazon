Justice vs Revenge is actually an important concept to me.  There is a lot to this story, and clearly there is a lot of room for sequels.  Rather than discussing character development (and you see a lot in many characters), or space battles (He really handles this in a much more believable way than you see in most stories) or world creation (Dupa has a number of surprises).  I will not even discuss the novel way necessary coincidences are handled.  That could be the basis of a new book.  I find the underlying concept of justice the most important part of the story.The book is well named.  Revenge is a lot easier to get, and a lot more satisfying, and in most stories that is what you get.  The bad guy gets destroyed in a satisfying face to face final confrontation.  You do not get that here.  Instead you find someone who puts justice ahead of other considerations, and insists on justice even for the bad guys.
