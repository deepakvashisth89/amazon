I enjoyed "Warrior Planet" and its sequel back in the 80's when it was first published--enough to place it on my "keeper" shelves.  So I was pleased to discover that Baen had reissued the books in Kindle format, and with the marvelous original artwork by A.C. Farley.  "Warrior Planet" is an entertaining romp that's fun to read.  It's not a vast canvas with too many characters to keep track of or a mind-bending political treatise.  It's just a delightful story with interesting characters and world-building that's a perfect indulgence for a lazy afternoon.EDITING ISSUES:  I compared the Kindle edition against the original 1987 paperback, and Baen did an excellent job of converting the manuscript to digital format.  Italicized text and scene breaks were all faithfully reproduced, and with two exceptions, so were alien words and names (the character's name "Teel" was misprinted in two instances).  There were also a handful of hyphens that should have been dashes that weren't really a problem.  Then there was one missing period, one missing word, one missing apostrophe, one missing space ("ballas" should be "ball as"), and a handful of OCR typos.  For example:  "would he" should be "would be," "chain" should be "chairs," "finest" should be "forest," "lacked" should be "locked," "same" should be "some," and "farce" should be "force."  It's clear that Baen made a real effort to actually edit and catch any scanned in OCR errors--and they did a really great job--a hundred times better than some sloppily reissued books I have seen--even from some major publishers.
