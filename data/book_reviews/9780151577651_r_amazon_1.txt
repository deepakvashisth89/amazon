I read this book as research for a novel I'm writing, with particular interest in the legend of Mary Magdalene's life in France. This book does an amazing job of bringing all the information about Mary Magdalene together paired with a sober view. The author does not push her views on anyone, nor does she draw farfetched conclusions. She just describes how Mary Magdalene has been portrayed in art, literature, and religious sects through the ages, starting from the gospels and the letters of Paul, until the 1990s, when the book was written.
