I was so blown away by Myers The God Hater, I picked this one to read next. I love his forays into science and tech, and this premise is fascinating. The whole issue of nature versus nurture when it comes to people with violent tendencies is so interesting. Science has pretty much proven there are differences in the brain when it comes to ordinary people and violent offenders. Can violent people change? Can they help themselves?
