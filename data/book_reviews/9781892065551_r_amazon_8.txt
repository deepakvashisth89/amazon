his is another dose of wonderful Myth Inc. tales from Robert Asprin.In this one the adventurous trio go in search of a golden cow, who gives golden milk, by following a treasure milk. Many adventures follow as they come up against strange locals, vampires and way too many vegetables.This book is a really short one, and is set between the first few novels, even though Asprin wrote it in 2001. In fact it followed Asprin's 8 year hiatus from writing.This is only the 2nd Myth Inc book I've read and it was great to see how the characters develop, especially given that Skeeve didn't make an appearance in the last book I read. Skeeve is a wonderful character as the lovable, clumsy apprentice.The one thing I found with this novel that I really liked, was that Asprin took to putting very short quotes at the beginning of each chapter. Such as "Never pick up a hitchhiker - D Adams". It was very cute!If anyone has ever played the PSP game Sims 2 you will see how this novel very much reminded me of the game, especially with so many cows around!!I'm still hunting down the earlier novels, but they are proving hard to find in my local area, but never fear - I will find them.Read moreRead less
