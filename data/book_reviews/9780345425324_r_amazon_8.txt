I can't remember if I first read James because it was assigned in a detective fiction class I took or if I saw a movie/tv version of one of her books on PBS. It has been a few years since I read my first of her books. In any case, her  characters--detectives, victims, suspects, killers--are so wonderfully developed and believable. She is so descriptive that I really felt like I had a movie going in my head the whole time. This is the second of her novels I've read (although number ten in her Adam Dalgliesh series).
