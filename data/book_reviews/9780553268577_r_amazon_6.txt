Young sergeant Tait is a university man intensively trained for accelerated promotion. His first posting is a rural market town, and his boss is Chief Inspector Quantrill, who left school at age fourteen, and rose slowly through the ranks in the police force.  A young woman has just died by drowning in the neighborhood.  No sign of foul play, but Tait is suspicious. The water was too shallow. He’d like it to be murder, so he can exercise his skills and intelligence.The plot offers an interesting contrast between Tait and Quantrill.  Tait is in love with forensics, and has an aggressive interrogation style.  Quantrill is gentler with suspects and gives his attention to character.The murder turns out to be somewhat philosophical, and it takes Quantrill’s maturity and humanity to figure that out.Chief Inspector Quantrill is an appealing protagonist — somewhat of a plodder but also a thinker, and essentially compassionate.Read moreRead less
