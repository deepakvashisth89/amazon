The above review is factually incorrect. Having a Jewish great-grandmother does make one Jewish, regardless of what religion the subject practices, if any. Elvis Presley's great-grandmother was, indeed, a Jewess; she married a non-Jewish man, as did their daughter. In turn, THEIR daughter, Gladys Love Smith, married Vernon Elvis Presley, a non-Jew; their sole offspring, Elvis Aaron (his middle name's original spelling) Presley, was Jewish.
