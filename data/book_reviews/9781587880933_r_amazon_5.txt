I read this book within two days.  Deangelo draws you into this story very quickly and I found myself unable to put this novel down.  The characters are believable with faults and good qualities.  While I hated the ex-wife Joan for living a lie, I did find myself wondering "how could she have done this?"  All this thought pattern created by a work of fiction?  That's when you know it's a great book.  The ironic part is I find myself still wondering this morning what will become of Sam.  All these feelings about "characters".  Deangelo knows how to make you care about the characters.  In my opinion that makes a worthwhile read.Read moreRead less
