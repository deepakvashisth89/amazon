Some spoilers, btw. Very honestly, the first bit of this book is fairly enjoyable. I wish we had heard more about Daniel and his life as a government agent, because that was probably my favorite part. Another thing that should have been expanded on was the legend about the family and it's curse, plus a bit more of an explanation about the family, who everyone is, and who died once they hit the present day. This was supposed to be pretty important but was completely overshadowed and left kind of on the way side to Kendra's dramatics.Like I mentioned, Kendra was pretty annoying throughout the entire book. She was selfish and repetitive when literally everything Daniel did was to prove to her that he was in it for the long haul. She expected him to be perfect with his toddler child, even after he TOLD her his almost zero experience with a child like Matthew, just because of the job he'd had previously, and completely failed to take into account HIS side of the story 90% of the time. All this I was willing to deal with, but when Kendra took cruel, horrible steps to make sure that Daniel didn't get a job he was not only good at but looking forward to because it would keep him close to his son and to her, I was just done with this story. She legitimately tried to make him fail and very honestly HURT him as much as she could. Not two pages later, she admitted what she'd done to Daniel and he acted like her cruel actions were just fine and he'd just push through them like he had everything else. I completely lost respect and sympathy for Kendra and lost a lot of respect for Daniel as well because he let her get away with it. I finished the book, but it wasn't completely satisfying because of what she did. I feel like Daniel deserved much better than to be treated like that and he would have been far more deserved to be pissed off at her, and let her come to him about it after letting her stew over what she did wrong. It was ok, but I'm probably not going to reread it.Read moreRead less
