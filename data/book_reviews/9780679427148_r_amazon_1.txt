This is a beautiful cookbook! I am so amazed at all the critics! One reviewer said it's too healthy, not enough butter, cream and cheese, another said too much cheese! Sheesh! Another complained no page numbers for recipes.....there are, btw! So many veggies complaining about ONE turkey recipe by her husband. To me, it's not a big deal, but I guess if you are veggie on high moral grounds, it might bother you.In any case, these are lovely recipes, some a little on the complex side,  but many simple and easy ones, too. If you're new to cooking, some of the menus may take awhile to prepare, but (obviously) you can easily mix and match dishes based on your tastes and available time.  To clarify, she has the recipes categorized under menus, one reads- "A Casserole Supper" and has: rice and corn salad, hubbard squash flan, simple chipotle sauce, tortillas and fresh fruit in season. Another section "Four Easy Soup Dinners" and one of them reads: Lentil soup, jalapeno and cheese cornbread OR pumpernickel bread and a slice of smoked cheese. In her cornbread recipe, she points out that jalapenos lose a lot of their fire when baked in a batter, which could be very helpful to someone not familiar w cooking them and afraid of their heat. Another recipe calls for haricots verts in a salad, and she explains that they are important in the salad and you shouldn't sub regular larger and tougher green beans. Another reviewer complained about her "snobbiness" with this comment, but she's simply explaining you will get sub par results w a regular green bean. As a cook, I appreciate these comments and tips. Another menu--Rustic Autumn dinner: fresh herb salad, roasted autumn veggies, brown and wild rice pilaf, plum and walnut galette.  Each menu is accompanied by more description, anecdotes or tips, and she then also has some tip or anecdote before the actual recipe. It's very personalized and an obvious labor of love.
