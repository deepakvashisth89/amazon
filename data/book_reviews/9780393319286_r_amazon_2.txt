As a person that has spent time serving as a juror, I have been privy to similar "fractured moments" described in this book and laughed with the retelling. This book is a fine collection of court reporters sharing a myriad of their everyday stories and experiences from their job. It convinces me that if animals had the privledge of viewing we humans at our comedic and most idiotic best, the courts might be the first "zoo" they should stop at for that particular entertainment!
