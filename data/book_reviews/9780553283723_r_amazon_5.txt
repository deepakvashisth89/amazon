Featuring a strong willed and physically dangerous female lead, this book pulls you in from the first page and is a fun read throughout.The leading character, faced with a future working in the family mining business (joy), or being forced to bear children for the community, runs away from home in pursuit of her mentor, who was, naturally, kidnapped by aliens.Once away from home, she befriends the Ridani (a racial sub-class among humanity), joins a rebellion, finds love and, well, grows up.  She also learns that politics among humanity is more complicated than she could ever have imagined.  Not only is she caught up in a rebellion among the stars of her people, but she is also touched by past rebellions and living empires from billions of light years away.A thoroughly enjoyable read, although you'll have to order all 3 books in the trilogy used (darn it!)For those who enjoy this author, she has written several other books under the name "Kate Elliot".  I recommend starting with "Jaran", which begins the story of the human fight against the aliens mentioned here.  It is set roughly a generation previous to the events in these books.Read moreRead less
