This is a nice small book about how to use stories in the classroom of foreign language teaching. There is a brief introduction about the how and the why of using stories in the classroom and the rest of the book presents  many stories in a concise form and suggestions on how to use them with  students. There are many different stories drawn form many different fields  and traditions: African myths, Indian legends, European stories, Jewish  stories, fairy tales, Christian stories and more. I found very useful the  introduction as well as the stories but the suggestions on how to use them  either seem very complicated to follow or very simple for a class of  teenagers of this decade.
