This is a review of a book - a story called Out of the Blackout. Many of the reviews here have nothing to do with this book because they are for some photography product called Blackout. The story is a fascinating mystery with some surprising twists and turns toward the end and more surprising twists and turns at the very end. A little boy from London boards a train with a bunch of other children headed for the country and safety during WW II. He's not on the list of children who were supposed to board that train, and no one knows who he is or who his parents are. Fortunately, he is fostered and then raised to adulthood by a loving country couple. As a grown man he goes in search of who his birth parents are, and you will have to read the story to find out what he discovers in his search. The story is well crafted and kept my interest from beginning to end.Read moreRead less
