Tessa Redfern was a doctor when it didn't seem to be fashionable to be one.  She looks younger than her age of 27 and when Patrick Harper first sees her he mistakes her for a teenager.It all Depends on Love was amusing.  Patrick let first impressions color his whole outlook on Tessa and she didn't correct his assumptions.  So this grown man is fighting his attraction to a mischievous teenager whom he has hired to help around his estate by filling in for his other employees when they can't work for some reason.  Tessa is attracted to Patrick who is in his 30's and she flirts outrageously with him.  The of course there is the jealous other woman, Patrick's assistant, who tries to throw wrenches into the whole coming together of Tessa and Patrick.  Tessa carries on her charade of being a teenager by even dressing the part and acting as though she is just going to cruise along through life with no goals and no real ambitions.  She starts feeling guilty for pulling the wool over Patrick's eyes several times, but she reasons out that he continues to make snarky comments to her and he deserves everything that he gets.  Eventually the truth is discovered when Tessa has to revert to doctoring mode and safe the life of her competition.  Rather than being extremely upset by Tessa's little joke, Patrick is a bit contrite for his judging of a book by its cover.  However she still leaves him for a time because she believes in what she saw of him with his assistant.
