I don't typically review books because I know so much of a review is based on personal taste and the mood the reader is in when reading any particular book. But here goes; short and sweet.  I have never read anything by Bellow but was intrigued by the synopsis and reviews.  The book is an easy read and the story flows well.  I enjoy the humor, characters, story, and the overall tone the narrator sets.
