I bought this book back when I thought 'creativity' was a thing and that I could learn that thing. And that's the approach this book takes, that creativity is a thing and that you can learn that thing.And here's why this is mistaken: creativity exists ONLY in the creation of something--a building, song lyrics, a novel, a sculpture. And creativity can arise ONLY after the artist has mastered her craft (or his, but I'm a woman). And it is in the practice of the craft that creativity arises.If you are searching after that magic formula for creativity, I urge you to turn to the work you want to do, learn the rules, master the conventions, study the masters, and do the work. Your creativity will arise from that endeavor, not from doing the exercises in this bookRead moreRead less
