Read this book on a plane. It was hard to keep at it, just not that fascinating. Some flashes of humour, subtle ones though. I am fascinated by newspapers but the book really does not deal with them as a subject very much. The story is a low key, sometimes confusing and ultimately not something I would read again. The book was written between eras, some of what we would take for common today and that might have been shocking earlier is just shown as a little risque.
