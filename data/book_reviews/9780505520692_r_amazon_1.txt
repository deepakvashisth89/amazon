I fell in love with South Bass Island in Lake Erie years before I wrote the book.  The history of the island & those who live there year round fascinated me.  My first book set on the island was a mystery and although an editor loved it, the publisher decided against it.  Since I knew the history of the island, my agent suggested I write an historical romance.An editor at Dorchester knew and loved the island and bought the book.My greatest joy was being invited to the island to do a booksigning at the Historical Society.  Having life-time residents, who had read my book, come to have me autograph their copy was an amazing experience.  To have them tell me I had captured the essence of island life and stayed true to its history warmed my heart.Several residents asked how I had chosen names for the characters in the book and were not surprised by the fact that I had gone to the cemetery to get names as they knew several.  On that trip I went into the church where I had set Benjamin & Jessica's wedding and could easily imagine them walking down the aisle.I would hope that those who read my book will also fall in love with the island and visit there.Read moreRead less
