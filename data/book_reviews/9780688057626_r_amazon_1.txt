This novel is—truly—one I did not want to end. The story of Homer Wells and the orphanage that infiltrated all aspects of his life, is filled with authentic-sounding dialog and wise characters who occupy the stage. And an impressive depth of knowledge crackles throughout the book—detailed descriptions of the Burmese jungle, apple farming in Maine, and the intricacies of (and morality of) the safe, humane practice of abortion.
