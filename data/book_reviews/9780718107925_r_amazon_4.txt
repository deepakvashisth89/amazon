This book was written a long time ago for corporate executives. But as a school administrator I find the principles and the overall thrust of the book to be timeless and applicable in any domain of work and life where genuine leadership is needed. Put your people first. This is the way. Townsend articulates it compellingly and with vivid, if slightly outdated examples from his own rich experience in learning how to  lead. The structure of the book as an alphabetized assortment of comments on various corporate topics was odd at first, but remarkably, it all hangs together quite well.
