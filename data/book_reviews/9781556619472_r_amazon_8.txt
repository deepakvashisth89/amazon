This is the first in a series that I will definitely be continuing. The main character is a woman who is very strong and determined, and a recent single mother. The book is sort of slow in the beginning but I had no trouble reading it as I have with some in the past, literally closing them and never thinking twice about it. It is not the romance novel that you may thing it is. It is a Christian based novel, which you do not realize until later in the book. As a Christian I have no problem with this, but some others may. Over all I enjoyed getting the meet the main characters, along with all of the new people in their lives. The author really did a fine job of describing things and helping me fall in love with those in town. Pleasantly surprised.Recommend this to a friend?
