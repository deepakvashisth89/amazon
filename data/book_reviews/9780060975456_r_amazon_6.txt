I first read this book as a college student in 1995 as a part of a recommended reading list for one of my courses.  I read it start to finish in just a few hours and couldn't believe the impact it had on me.  I, for the first time in my life, felt less like I had read a book and more like I had just sat in a room surrounded by a group of boys, listening to their story.  Jess Mowry is a phenominal writer and this book should be one that EVERYONE reads at some point in their lifetime.Read moreRead less
