This book was rather different than I had imagined.  I found that the interesting aspects were that the book was well written, with some wonderful descriptions and some interesting characters.  The damaging points were a weak plotline and rather too much useless information/dialogue which made the book laborious to read at times.  This book is like an old fashioned police procedural whodunnit and there is nothing wrong with that.  I personally love whodunnits, but you do need to sense the old Agatha Christie drama and suspense to make the read worthwhile.  Where the book fails for me, is the total absence of the factor that makes you turn the pages, it did not grip me at all.  Although it does improve after a slow start I still found myself struggling to finish it.Read moreRead less
