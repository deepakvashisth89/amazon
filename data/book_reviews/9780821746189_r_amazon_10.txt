Mary Margaret Dennehy (Maggie), is the third of 5 sisters.  Lost and propositioned, she is taken into a brothel to recover.  Unfortunately the brothel keeper does not know her left from her right, so that is how Connor Holiday finds her.  When she awakes the next morning in her own home, she has very little memory of the previous night.As always with Jo Goodman, the dialogue flows easily.
