He's done it again and I find that surprising because most writers have maybe one good book in them and the others are just lame copies of the good one, but Wiltse has written a lot of books and everyone of them is at the very least very good and some of them well beyond that. This one comes after Heartland, which was terrific and this one is even deeper, more complex, and intensely interesting. Billy Tree is a great protagonist, extremely human, no superman he, Falls City is a fascinating ambiance, and the story with its interplay of the past and the present and social justice paired with criminal justice is a mix I've never see before.Read moreRead less
