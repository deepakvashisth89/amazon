I LOVE this vegetarian cookbook.  The explanation on how to cook beans and grains is worth the purchase price.  The author also clearly explains the whys and hows of cooking grains and beans.  The recipies include the nutritional information of each dish which is helpful for diabetics and others who want such information.  This is vegetarian not a vegan cookbook, but one could easily adjust the recipies and substitute the dairy ingredients for soy products or such.  The dishes are creative and fresh tasting.
