Cole Cameron's Mother died giving birth to him, something Cole's Father never forgave him for. So while Cole's older brother Ted basked in their Father's love Cole was left out in the cold,never treated as part of the family. Cole grew up to be wild and rebellious , tall and handsome he took full advatage of his looks especially with the girls, even married women. Then a new girl started high school Faith, Cole was immediately struck by her she was quiet thoughtful and kind. But unlike other girls she resisted Cole's charms. That was until the night of the prom. Cole knew then that he loved Faith. But the next day he was accused of something he had not done he knew why he was being accused but did not want to use Faith as an alibi. So he left town without a word asking his brother Ted to look after Faith.Nine years later and Cole has received word that his brother is dead, but he is horrified to discover that Faith had married his brother and was now his widow. Returning home the gossip in the town is that Faith was a gold digger and had married his brother not long after he had left, but she didn't treat his brother well or behave as a wife should. Cole decides to have his revenge on Faith.This book is filled with passion but not the heated we are in love sort, no this is the I am really angry type of passion. I left town without a word, and have never contacted you in eight years, I have had loads of women in that time, I was even seeing someone when we were in highschool when I was with you. But all this is YOUR fault, and, I am going to spend most of our time together throwing vile insults at you. Really is this guy the biggest jerk ever to breathe. What made this worse was the author failed miserably to redeem him even at the end of the book, there are literally two small paragraphs of his apology.
