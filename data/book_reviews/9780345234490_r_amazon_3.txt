First read this millions of years ago (in the 60's) when I lived on a solid-sci-fi diet. Doesn't seem quite so other-worldly now, especially since so much of it has apparently inspired other stuff (suspect Clarke read it when piecing together 2001,possibly also Fforde, unless the gravitube is a case of convergent evolution). The machine-plan doesn't seem so fresh any more, but it's readable and fairly well-paced. Characters could have done with a bit more filling-out, but it comes from an age of short books. Certainly worth a read to see how far fantasy has evolved since then.Read moreRead less
