I've read six biographies of Harry Truman and they're all well-written, well researched books.  But this one, penned by Harry's daughter, is by far the warmest and most personal bio of the lot.  Margaret Truman is (was) a gifted writer and she makes the reader feel like she has just stepped into your living room to share a first-hand account of one of the greatest presidents in American history.Bravo!!! 5 stars damns it with faint praise...so I'll give it 10+!Read moreRead less
