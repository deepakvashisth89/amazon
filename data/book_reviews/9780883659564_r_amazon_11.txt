I first read this book almost 30 years and thoroughly enjoyed. It's very well written and uses contemporary eye witness accounts to describe JFK's trip to Dallas over 5 fateful days. If you want a literary treat about one of the most iconic events of the 60s and impacted on the American psyche - this is the book for you. It's a factual analysis with some interpretation - but doesn't go to the conspiracy theories which emerged within weeks of the shooting. This the only book I have re-read or wanted to re-read (I thought it might have been out of print but came across it while surfing one night) and would recommend it to anyone who wants a thoroughly engrossing read.
