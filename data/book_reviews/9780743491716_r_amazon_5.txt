I love the Dominion War in Deep Space Nine. It was the one Star Trek series with a Saga to tie it all together. I have watched the episodes many times over the years and still love them as much today. This book isn't bad. Some of the stories are interesting. They really don't add to the overall understanding of the war so much. The ones with the usual characters are more interesting since you can relate to them.
