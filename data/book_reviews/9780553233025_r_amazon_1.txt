This is a story at two levels, the main character from 1935 to 1945 and again in contemporary time (ca 1983). It switches back and forth between these two era to tell the story of Paul Foster (Paul de Grunwald), a secretive highly successful businessman and his origins in Hungary and his subsequent time as a concentration camp prisoner. The 1935 to 1945 story is more interesting and may have some relation to real events of that era. The contemporary era story is more purely fiction where the characters engage in high finance and manipulative behavior. They seem to exist on champaign and caviar.
