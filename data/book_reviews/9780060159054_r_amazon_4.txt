I can not give this book enough praise for how it illustrates the change in the American lifestyle between 1790 and 1840. Not only is it a good history read, it is presented in a way that makes you want to keep turning the pages to find out more. I must admit I did think it was going to be another boring history book and I was proved wrong.The things covered in this book are almost atypical, a product of the author's discipline as curator of Old Sturbridge Village, but when you look at things like family, life vs. death, medicine, housing, population, transportation, industry, as a collective whole, it paints a whole new perspective on a time few often care about. It was the most important time frame in our nation's history, and it still has impacts to this day.I certainly recommend picking this book up for some evening reading.
