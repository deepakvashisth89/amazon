I first read these stories years ago when they came out in the Science Fiction Book Club versions. I am now recollecting them in Kindle versions and have reread them for the first time in a decade or so. Since I love Arthurian stories, Port Eternity simply fit for me, with an azi twist. Expectations simply become null and void due to an unforeseen event and everything is set on its ear. Azi emotions and reactions having to be retrained by circumstance and self? Interesting premise.Voyager in Night was one I had a hard time with UNTIL I realized that the view point was constantly changing. Whose was I reading now? I felt the despair and confusion, I wondered about illusion. What was real? I still am not sure.My favorite of these three is Wave without a Shore. I liked this one so much after reading it the first time that this was the book that I brought with me to a SciFi convention and had C.J. Cherryh autograph. The mind is a funny thing, and so is society. What is real? What is right? What is wrong? And who says it is so? You? Me? This one has got me wanting to go get a book on philosophy just the expand what it is that I just read.Must-have for Cherryh fans!Read moreRead less
