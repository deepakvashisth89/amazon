The author obviously is steeped in knowledge of the Bible as it includes many biblical illusions. In addition, he has a great understanding of the history of this time period. He describes the world of Rome at a time following the death of Christ when Christianity is beginning to spread. He describes the ways in which Christians had to hide in order to worship God and how they were persecuted for their beliefs.
