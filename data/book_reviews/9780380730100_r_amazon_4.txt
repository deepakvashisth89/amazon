I first read one of Maxim's Bannerman books years ago and thoroughly enjoyed it. Now I've discovered several more written in recent years and it's like meeting an old friend.The Kindle text is full of the kind of mistakes that result from poor scanning and OCR of printed text, including odd italics, some stray red chapters, and incorrect words. maxim deserves better for his publisher. However, his work is so good that I was able to ignore a level of poor presentation that might have led me to stop reading a lesser book.The characters are improbable, the plot hinges on coincidences, and there's plenty of weirdness to go around. Maxim's gift is to make all this, not irrelevant, but all the more charming and engaging.Read moreRead less
