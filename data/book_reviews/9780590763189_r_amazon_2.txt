Excellent, gritty thriller about the Porn industry. Complex characters. Most of the things that happened seemed possible but I have trouble believing Scotland Yard has that much money to spend on flying witnesses and policemen back and forth several times and to allocate people and resources so freely. That aside, I did enjoy the book. For those who need to know, Sex is discussed but only as athletic and innovative. No erotica. There is British profanity.
