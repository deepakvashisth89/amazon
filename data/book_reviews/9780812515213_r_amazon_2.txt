Steven Brust has a talent for taking familiar stories--in this case, a gothic-style monster/vampire novel--and envisioning them in a delightfully subversive way that evokes profound notions of good and evil while at the same time reveling in a strange sort of amorality.  A stand-alone book about the life and love of an unrepentant vampire, Agyar is a great introduction to the work of the unconventional Brust.Read moreRead less
