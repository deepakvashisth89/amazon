Although unlike Hercule Poirot or Miss Marple, Mr Parker Pyne is not a household name, these stories about him are equally pleasurable to read. Agatha Christie's plots are excellent as always and another advantage of the book is that each story is self-contained so one can enjoy them one by one and doesn't have to wait to the end of the book for a resolution.
