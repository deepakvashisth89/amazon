If you're thinking of building a rustic cabin, this book more or less gives you all the working knowledge to do it. Since it was written before chainsaws and power drills, much of the work could be done quicker. If you find yourself post-apocalyptically needing to use only amish hand tools, well, you're set. This book also lacks actual blueprints so if you're just putting together a cabin and okay with eyeballing it, you're set.
