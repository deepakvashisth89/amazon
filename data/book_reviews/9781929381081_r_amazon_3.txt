It’s the second in the trilogy, but very good. There’s a decent amount of science for the hard sci fi types but not page after page. There’s not a ton of backstory, the author probably assumed you’d read the first one. Even if not, there’s not that much you need to know if you’d just grabbed this off the paperback rack back in the day.McCollum’s work is constantly excellent.
