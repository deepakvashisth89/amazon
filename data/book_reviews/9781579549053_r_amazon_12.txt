I read this book in the first three months of recovering from alcoholism. Really useful for someone like me who had a deep prejudice against the 12 steps because they ask us to believe in miracles. It helped me to see the 12 steps do not require me to have superstitions or blind faith, that it is just a matter of perservering, building connections with others, healing the relationship with myself, and living a life of service.
