Captain's Hart book fell a bit short of my expectations as I was more concerned about the political causes and consequences of the war. Its focus is the military development, and in this regard is an excelente book, in spite of the horrible maps. A work like this deserved a refreshed edition, and good maps are a must to understand the military flow. This book was published in 1930, so the consequences of the Versailles Treaty could not be fully appraised, but Captain's Hart feeling, exposed in the last chapter offer a hint of the terrible outcome. I recommend for those looking for a compact and comprehensive history of military movements and a very interesting account of the use of then new technologies like machine guns, tanks, submarines, airplanes and their impact in the outcome of the war.Read moreRead less
