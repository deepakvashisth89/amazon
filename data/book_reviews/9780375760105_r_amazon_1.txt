Excellent story, a classic in the truest sense it never gets 'Old,' for the very reason that the perambulations and connivances of the main character and others is so true to Human Form. Regardless of the time. There was even a reasonably good 1996 movie made of the story, the most recent I've seen with Morgan Freeman and Robin Wright. In any event, there is enough drama embedded in the story to fill several lives, and more twists than a pig's tail. The movie, admittedly, doesn't really do it justice, but it is fun to watch anyway and observe how far there is still left to go.
