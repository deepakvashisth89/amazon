I greatly looked forward to reading this book, the basis for the movie "Just Like Heaven".  I had read the reviews saying the book read more like an incomplete screenplay than a book.  I agree with those reviews in part.The book move along with a nice storyline for pages at a time, then a few passages of non-idiomatic English or inaccurate cultural details utterly destroy the reader's immersion.If you liked the movie, I recommend reading this book, a fuller version of the movie with some completely different plot points.  If you didn't see the movie, you can skip this book in favor of one of the author's better written works.Read moreRead less
