I give this book a lot credit in terms of world-building and narrative. The story put forth and the tension between Adam & Almah against the Kohen and Layelah is rather masterfully done.  The society in which the story takes place is beautifully described and also serves as a warning against what a society could hypothetically become.This book is fantastic aside from being just slightly over-descriptive, but given that that was the style in the mid-1800's this book is actually a rather quick read and is essentially a novella.Read moreRead less
