I Am Morgan le Fay is by far one of the best novels I have read in a long time. Of course there are no pictures, but with the way the author writes, will make your imagination run wild with Morgan through, castles, lush green forest, & finding Avalon! I absolutely loved this portrayal of the dark Goddess. I was hooked from the first page and didn't want the story to end. Fantastic read.
