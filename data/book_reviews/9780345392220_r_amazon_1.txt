Ms. Adams has spun an intricate little yarn that just might be plausible. I mean, the folks I come across in Jesus Creek seem just odd enough to be real, or maybe her storytelling weaves me in that closely. At any rate this  is a fine little whodunit with just the right air of creepiness that again  I think, Could it be? This ain't blood and guts and hatchets and  forensics... this is ole Reb Gassler, finishing his pecan pie, beginning a  cigarette and just kinda having a talk with the good Doctor. Or is he?
