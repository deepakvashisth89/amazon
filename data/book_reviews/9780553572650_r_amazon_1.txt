I've given away I don't know how many copies of this book in paperback.  It's funny, REALLY clever, and generally one of my favorite Spinrad novels, being slightly less "dark" than some of his work but still dead-on-target in combining superb storytelling with a large social/political issue of current concern.  I've "clicked" for years to vote it into Kindle-form, and hope a new generation of readers will now get to enjoy it.  Spinrad is truly a master craftsman and it's always a privilege to recommend his work to those who appreciate a well-crafted and brilliantly-executed story.
