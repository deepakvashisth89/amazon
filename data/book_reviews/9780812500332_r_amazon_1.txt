As a reader, I was on the edge of my seat! As a student of the writer’s craft, I found this to be a perfect example of a thriller. Engaging characters, perfect pacing, and excellent use of sentence structure. Perfect balance of dialogue and narrative. Perfectly placed hooks! This novel, Psycho and Ira Levin’s Rosemary’s Baby taught me a valuable lesson. The story needs to be only as long as the story needs to be! Word count is irrelevant in a well-developed and well-written story.I recently read Psycho for the first time. I admired Robert Bloch’s writing style and wanted to study it, so I ordered Psycho 2 and Psycho House. I just finished reading Psycho 2 and couldn’t wait to post my five star review.  I grew up somehow knowing the twist in Psycho, so when I was old enough to watch the movie I expected the twist. While reading Psycho 2, I was in the dark for the entire novel until the big reveal. I can’t understand why Alfred Hitchcock failed to make a sequel. It would have been just as amazing as the original.
