The astute observation and reflections on life by a Vietnamese migrant worker in pre WWII Paris evokes a sense of temps perdu, but even better his eloquence challenges preset nations of the "staff". He works for Gertrude Stein and Alice B Toklas so there is much for him to comment on and share with us about their private life while he manages to have a wild, tasty and sad one of his own. Powerfully conveys the loneliness of the overseas domestic and the tug of home and family in ways that resonate over the ages. This is an amazing book, one to savor slowly as the language is beautiful, and her landscapes of the mind and heart poignantly portrayed.Read moreRead less
