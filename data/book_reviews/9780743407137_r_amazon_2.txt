This is another installment in the always excellent Toby Peters mystery series. The story is set in Hollywood in 1942 and features Bela Lugosi and William Faulkner as major characters. The two celebrities actually only make brief appearances, but Kaminsky manages to render an interesting and accurate portrait of each one of them. Otherwise, the novel is just a fun mystery novel about the murder of a big time Hollywood agent and how the wise cracking PI Toby Peters solves the case. The book is light and humorous and presents a delightful view of Hollywood in the forties.The only negative thing I can say about the book is that the paperback edition I read wins my award for the worst book cover of all time.
