This book is absolutely charming. I've read it twice, a few years apart, and will undoubtedly read it again, for an admittedly ignominious reason: the stories are so delightful that I'd love to be able to tell them to people - especially children - off the top of my head, and not have to resort to the book.  The fact that my audience might conclude that I invented the stories myself - especially the one about the animals around the manger - only troubles my conscience a little bit.  Who wouldn't love to be able to tell stories with the wisdom and warmth of Iezzi's Nonna?This book has the advantage of being a delight to adults - especially perhaps adults of Mr Iezzi's generation, though I think not only - as well as children.  It's rare to find a book that an adult can read to a child and both adult and child enjoy the stories at the same time.  Definitely should be on the Catholic family's bookshelf.Read moreRead less
