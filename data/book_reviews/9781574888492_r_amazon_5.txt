This book is written as if it were a (the) dissertation for a (the) Ph.D.; a dissertation not held to a high enough standard by the dissertation committee. It is a stuffy read but interesting in its way.  Lots of American history thrown in to illustrate and support points. Very good about sources; very good about indexing.  These are important things! But there are some odd things in this book e.g. page 215, when the author writes "President Reagan and his CIA director, William J. Casey, peace be upon them -".  What is that about?  Is that a joke?The author tells us that Ben Laden is making 'a good point' in crashing planes full of people into our buildings given what we (The U S of A) have done in the Middle East & especially with Israel and it is easy to conclude that he thinks we ought to apologize to (?) bin Laden and stop doing these things.  But he does not go so far as to say that this would 'fix' the situation - bring peace or make bin Laden back off and leave us to go our own (stumblingly democratic)way.He contradicts himself saying bin Laden doesn't really care about what is going on outside of the Middle East but says also  that bin Laden (and basically all Muslims) are interested in world domination.  He says bin Laden offered to show Americans the correct (Islamic) way and since we turned him down by not jumping at the chance (not rushing to  Mosques to be instructed in the Islamic way, not grabbing up those burkas and Korans) we are going to be terrorized (insurgensized?) till we take down those crosses, get on those veils and get down on our prayer rugs.  We are going to have our buildings and landmarks destroyed; we are going to be infected with terrible things (anthrax?) and we are going to be irradiated and incinerated in short order (as soon as bin Laden can come up with the stuff he needs and a good workable plan). (Scary - I mean it!)But, the author does not think bin Laden is a crazy religous guy!  He's just a Muslim guy who is smart and gentle and calm but who is mad about a lot of things.  But the author thinks we have to go to war (all out) with these "insurgents" (don't call them "terrorists") and that the Muslim insurgents are basically supported by 'all Muslims everywhere'.  But, he doesn't say how that would be done exactly.  Nuclear weapons? Just level the Middle East and incinerate all those Muslims who give bin Laden a wink and a nod?  One wonders, what shall we do with all those wink and nod Muslims settled here in this ('our') country.News break: There are Muslims (peace be upon them) who do get it - who can live with and appreciate the importance, hey, the vital necessity, for a secular state. (Reading Lolita in Tehran...)I believe however the author makes a cogent and very important argument for The U S of A to stop trying to export democracy. (Maybe we could export steel again instead). His argument on page 206 regarding the tradition and evolution of democracy is a good one.
