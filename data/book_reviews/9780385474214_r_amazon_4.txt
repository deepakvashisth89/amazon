This is the kind of book that, once read, will never be forgotten; and I never have.  I read it for the first time almost thirty years ago and, over the years, have often thought about reading it again.  Unfortunately, I ‘loaned’ my copy to some forgotten friend and it vanished forever.  (Never lend books to friends.  You feel so guilty when you try to get them back.)  Fortunately, I recently found a copy in our local library’s book store, and bought it.  So I finally got to enjoy it a second time.As before, I found it to be one of the most beautifully written, thoughtful, compelling, and thought-provoking books I’ve ever read.  And, as I did before, I concluded that it could only have been written by someone with an intimate knowledge of Jesus Christ and his teachings, and an in depth knowledge of the Catholic Church, the Jewish faith, and Protestant Churches of all denominations.  Who else could have written it but a retired Catholic priest with the insight, the will, and the courage to do so?  He must have been a wonderful minister.Bottom line: If you’ve ever wondered what might happen if Jesus of Nazareth returned, unannounced, and began living in your, or some other community, and simply went about ‘his’ business in ‘his’ own way; then this is a book you’ll surely want to read.  If you do, I can assure you: You’ll have much to think about, and you’ll never forget it.Read moreRead less
