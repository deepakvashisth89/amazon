Fans of this author will enjoy reading this heart-warming and unique story about lives, loves and experiences of an Italian family over three generations beginning in Italy and ending up in America.  The author’s Italian background makes the characters come alive with compassion and humor.Like the author I was raised in an Italian background and can relate too many of the traditions and customs and of course the food mentioned in her novels.  However, anyone with relatives from other countries and migrated to the United States can also relate to similar experiences.If you enjoyed the author’s other novels you will like the Queen of the big time.Rating: 5 Stars.
