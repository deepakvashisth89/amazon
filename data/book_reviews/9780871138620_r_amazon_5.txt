This book was recommended to me by a hunter friend when I expressed an interest in learning to hunt deer.  The book is about a group of men who get together every year to hunt rabbits.  You feel like you're right there with them.  Some of the anecdotes are laugh out loud funny.  Also, the author is white, the hunters who welcomed him into their circle of friends are all black:  The author's father in law and his friends.  Such a good book on so many levels.Read moreRead less
