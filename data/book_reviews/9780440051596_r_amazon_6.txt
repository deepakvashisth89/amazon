Corky is the 1970s answer to Norman Bates, only with a ventriloquist's dummy instead of a mother.  Author William Goldman, one of the great contemporary popular writers of the last two quarters of the 20th century, takes us inside of Corky's head as he valiantly tries to keep his sanity from whithering away completely.  He escapes to his past, returning to his childhood home and searching out his teenage crush Peggy Ann Snow, now a grown woman in a troubled and rapidly dissolving marriage.  In an isolated cabin rental, Corky makes his tragic last stand.  A wonderful novel that manages to both be a melancholic love story as well as a harrowing psychological thriller.
