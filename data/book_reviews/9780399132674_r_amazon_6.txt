I have loved this Heinlein novel ever since first reading it - it was one of his last novels (maybe his actual last novel) and completes the "Future History" timeline. IMHO, if Lazarus Long is Heinlein's "Marty Stu" then Grampaw Ira is his depiction of himself as he truly was . . . and, frankly, the old coot is deliberately made into the most lovable male character in the entire cast of characters that RAH created.
