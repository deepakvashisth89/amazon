This was a great read. I bought it on kindle but liked it so much I will buy it in paper. I had only read her Magdelene murder mystery series before. I like her writing style. The story is believable. The characters are likeable and well developed. I especially liked that the two main characters are average to ugly in looks unstead of the romance cover drop dead gorgeous couples so often described in other books. Her interests of weaving and hawking are unusual and yet believable. He is a warrior with little else except his job of fighting. I like the way she weaves the religious side of their lives throughout as well. The church was a huge part of everyones daily life during those times and I feel that it improves the story to keep bringing up how it has and is affecting their lives as strongly as the war that rages around them.Read moreRead less
