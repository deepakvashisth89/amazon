Love the pictures and quotes from the show; great for Friends lovers...I've had it for a few weeks now and have had time to read through it and try out a bunch of the recipes included. I'm extremely pleased with them. They are easy, no crazy strange ingredients that you can't find anywhere, and they're delicious! For Easter Sunday, I made the country biscuits (I used both chives AND dill instead of one or the other - I recommend doing this) and the ham in the holiday section of the book. AMAZINGGG. I've also made the banana bran muffins, which taste awesome, are moist, and a very easy grab-on-the-go breakfast or snack. The tuna steak recipe is superb! I highly recommend that to anyone who is a fan of seared tuna steaks. The brownies in this book were also a huge hit. I'm honestly very impressed with these recipes. I can definitely see many of these becoming staples that I'll rotate into my menu planning for years to come.
