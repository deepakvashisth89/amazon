The prose is unique, crafted in a way I can only think to call, "deliberately rough," but even though it takes some getting used to, once you do, you learn to appreciate just how much that style draws you into the story and world.The pacing is great, and the plot is very engaging and realistic. It did a great job of keeping me wanting to read to see the final resolution. The characters are so well written that I actually felt an emotional connection to them, a rare thing for me.The ending is satisfying and leads nicely into the next book.This is the first C.J Cherryh book I've read. Perhaps this isn't the best book to start with, but it impressed me enough to want to read every other book in this series and more.Read moreRead less
