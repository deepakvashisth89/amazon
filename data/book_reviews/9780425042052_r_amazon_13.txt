I'd read this book years ago at school and forgotten all about it until something on TV reminded me of it. Took ages to find as I couldn't remember the title, but I ordered it, and what a treat it is! It's very old fashioned and un-pc in places but the story-telling is sublime; the heroine is so feisty and likeable and the setting of the huge, decrepit stately home with the little island in the middle of the lake is delicious. Sheer, nostalgic pleasure. Can't wait to read it to younger members of the family although will requires some editing.Read moreRead less
