I read this book in graduate school (2006-ish), in my mid-twenties, and it absolutely CHANGED my life.  This book is a well-written collection of interviews from women in various age groups with varying experiences.  It was so refreshing to read stories that made you say "Hey, I'm not the only one that feels that way!"  Flux does an excellent job of putting into words what being a woman is REALLY like in the half-changed world we live in.  I have purchased this book and recommended it to many friends.
