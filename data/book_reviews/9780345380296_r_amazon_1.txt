I've liked Patricia Hampl's books such as BLUE ARABESQUE, where she discusses responding to works of art and literature as a kind of meditation or extended I-Thou encounter. In fact, one of Hampl's main contributions for me has been her demonstrating how the I-Thou encounter can have duration without becoming an intellectual manipulation of the object, the "other." I'd hoped this anthology would have a good selection of spiritual poems by modern poets such as W.H. Auden or Anne Sexton. I found it instead to be a somewhat run-of-the-mill collection of poets such as Rumi or Khalil Gibran, who usually appear in collections of mystical poetry.
