If you are looking for a light-hearted book, or a quick read, this is not the book to buy.  It is a very challenging read, which will take you to the heart of what the 'noble life path' is all about.  To bear witness to all of life (which is what Bernie Glassman is encouraging us to do), takes real courage.  It draws on our deepest reserves, especially when we are trying to bear witness to that part of life which is beyond our capacity to make sense of, or to bear, and our natural response might be to protect our emotional heart and turn away instead.  But in this book, Bernie is not only encouraging us to bear witness, he is also teaching us, how to grow the most powerful compassion, love and healing for all of life and also for ourselves.  It is a very powerful book.  Nothing that I could write in a review could sum it up.  If you read it, I think that you will come away from it changed in some way.Read moreRead less
