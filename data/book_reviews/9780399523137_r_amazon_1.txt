Its not hard to pinpoint the source which started my commitment to science and engineering; it was this book. It covers a good portion of enticing category in science that interweave science and human relation. It was more than just a book full of science facts, but a story like a short novel. Perhaps this was an accumulation of short stories base on scientific discoveries, but it work to intrigue me to look further into the so called "hard science". After reading this book early in high school, I went from barely passing biology to being recommend to the honors teacher in chemistry in person, and in advance physics. Its wasn't just because I had learned more in dept about the science from this book than was thought in the class, but because my teachers recognize I had an internal desire to read more about science which I still believe confound them for reason why in such a degree of interest.
