The book was interesting and factually accurate as to what was happening in the community as the murders developed. Having grown up in San Francisco when this happened, I remember people being very scared; many started carrying pistols when they went out at night. There was a string of creepy mass murders during that time period (Zodiac, Trailside Killer, Ed Kemper, Kenneth Bianchi in LA, Manson etc.) and the community fear was palpable.  However, my problem with the book was the leaps the author makes when he presents individual motivations and conversations.
