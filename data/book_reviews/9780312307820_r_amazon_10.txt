Mavis Cheek has excelled herself. Always a wonderful read, her latest novel 'The Sex Life of My Aunt' is even funnier, and more cutting in its observations of the behaviour of the female of the species in her middle years, than ever. If you've ever indulged in that most wonderful, painful, uplifting and insane of experiences - an extra marital love affair - be prepared to wince with recognition at every page, just before erupting into gaffaws of delight.
