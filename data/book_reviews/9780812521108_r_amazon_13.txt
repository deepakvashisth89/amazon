I have been a long time reader of Laymons work but Flesh for me was a mixed experience. The story started slowly and the characters not as memorable or well defined as the authors greatest novels, happily the gear shifted as I was loosing the plot. A good story but it suffers from a few problems. Why is there only one cop in it and why does it feel at times like a comedy when it is not the intention? Why is every other word a sexual inuendo and why did the writer not simply call this Breast because this word is well over used. Laymon is good. Many others writers who could have penned Flesh would have probably penned their best but judging this novel with the Laymon yardstick leaves the whole affair a little short.
