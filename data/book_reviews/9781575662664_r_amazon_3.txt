Mira, a psychic, saw a murder in a vision and called to tell the police, but of course they, of course, didn't believe her. Detective Wayne Sheppard, meanwhile, got the call for a dead criminalist and some evidence led him to meet Mira. Sheppard got Mira to read the dead man's house and found that she was a real psychic. FBI agent Fletcher and Steele, the dead guy, had used three criminals with psychic powers and found a way to make them stronger. But when the FBI got in trouble, they dropped the program and killed all the criminal, but three got away. Then, the three came after Steele and Fletcher.
