That's the last of the books written by Ann Victoria Roberts which trace the lives of Louisa Elliott and her family. I have perhaps read them in the wrong order, but nevertheless the books are perfectly tied together to tell a fantastic story.Ann has an incredible way of linking the books together as you can see the stories coming together as a whole. Her personal knowledge of York, combined with her research of how it must have been in times long gone, are a tribute to the time she must have spent in leaving no stones unturned in producing such a great set of books.
