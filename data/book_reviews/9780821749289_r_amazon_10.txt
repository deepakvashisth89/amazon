THESE COMMENTS ARE IN RELATION TO THE KINDLE EDITION.Like others on these pages, nothing can quite compare to 'THE WOLF AND THE DOVE' but I, like many people, give other writers a try after reading Reviews on Amazon; this is one such book.You have the other reviews for the storyline and I agree that it is an O.K. read, although the female drove me mad at times!I chose the Kindle version because of the price, but have been continually frustrated whilst I was reading it and sorely disappointed!  Who on earth put this book on Kindle?  There are errors galore! All the way through, words are connected together, misspelled and early on the Hero, Niall, turned into Nigel!  So, imagine being a human spell-checker while trying to enjoy a book; Chapters, which usually start on a new page, carry on on the same page.  It is apparent to me that this has not been done in a professional manner and wonder who actually did it(?)All in all, a very unsatisfactory read due to it being on Kindle.Saying all that, I have just bought some back numbers by Katherine Deauville, Trilogies in fact, and am thoroughlyenjoying each book in turn.Thanks again, Amazon Reviewers.Read moreRead less
