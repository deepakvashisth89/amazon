How often do you read a story where the protagonist isn't human?  Or even VAGUELY humanoid?"Nor Crystal Tears" is a gripping read about an inquisitive young alien insectoid of the Thranx species who finds himself facing a universe much bigger and more varied than he could have imagined, and his species' first contact with a terrifying race of repulsive alien giants with immense strength and durability, known as "Humans."  A highly enjoyable book about cultural differences, working together, and finding new and better ways of doing things in a changing world (or universe).It's also a great place to start reading Foster's "Commonwealth" series, which expands on the star-spanning stories of the Thranx and Humans and other species.Love this book.  Looking forward to getting the rest of the series, if I can.  Highly recommended.Read moreRead less
