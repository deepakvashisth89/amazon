I was looking for another rabbit book on how to train rabbits and looked at the 'Search Inside This Book'. Having read the first few pages of the book I was so disgusted I decided to write my first ever review. Don't go anywhere near this book - any book which says that rabbits can be left for a long weekend without anyone caring for them is a disgrace. Rabbits need fresh food and water twice a day and entirely healthy rabbits can die within 24 hours if disaster strikes. This is especially the case during warm weather when flystrike is a danger.
