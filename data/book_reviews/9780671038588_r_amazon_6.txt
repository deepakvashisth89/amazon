I did like this book, in that it was well written. However, I gave it 3 starts for one simple fact: the main character. The character of "Piper", is to me, even less likable in this novel than in the previous novel, Dreadnaught. She is WAY to obsessed with Kirk and his opinions/ideas. Piper, as a young Lieutenant Commander, has no real ideas of her own (and when she does get them, its a big mental struggle to formulate one.) She has to pass all of her mental theories with her personal image of "What Would Kirk Do in this situation?" and it gets old. In all honesty, she doesn't really know Kirk at all. In the timeline of these two novels she has only known Kirk personally for a few weeks. This is not the kind of officer I'd like to serve under, if I were in Star Fleet.
