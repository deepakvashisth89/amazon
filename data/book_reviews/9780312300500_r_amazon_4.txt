To me this was more than real life and it may be because I am a somewhat new resident here in Dallas,Tx I can visualize the whole book. I have read it more than three times,it is not slow about coming to the point of what is happening in the lives of Wes and Madison,and even as the events seem like this is it you keep reading and something else pops up and it is another WOW in her life. I enjoy this writer's clear descriptions of her characters and their emotions. The baby pulls on your heartstrings and you fall in love with Zachary...for real.
