I was skipping this review but then Amazon asked what I thought.  So, here goes.  The book starts off around the Civil War time.  I felt from the dialogue that the characters were black (neither here nor there, but it does tend to set the stage for the lives, etc.)  But then they had slaves, so that threw me.  I know that some blacks did have slaves but mostly they were their own relatives and they were listed such so they could stay with family. (got this from Ancestor's)  Anyway, then there was a setting where there seemed to be time travel.  I was a big confused, not at all excited about any of it and decided I really didn't want to time travel with these folks.  So, I quit reading.  One reviewer said the ending was a bit off for her.  I am a big fan of HEA so I figured I did the best thing for me by not continuing.  This is one that I think a reader should try if these kinds of events appeal to you.
