It is an interesting selection of Kafka's work. I'm not sure what qualifies these stories as "best," but they are good, of course, and the translations are wonderful. I was actually looking for the original German text for a class that I'm in, and also to keep my German at a high level. This book, with the translation right next to the text, sometimes tempts me to cheat and glance over, but is really well done. I have several books like this, with the original language and translation, and I find it sometimes distracting, but also very helpful. Especially with Kafka, Goethe, and others, I come across words I don't know. Instead of bringing up a website to search the word (which invariably leads to me being distracted for 20 minutes or more), the translation is right by the text.
