As a mother, I really appreciated this book.  As a mother, I'm very surprised by how well Sophie Powell was able to articulate the lives of other mothers (two in fact, sisters), seeing her picture and realizing that she is quite young, and presumably too  young to not be a mother yet.Regardless, the mothers in her story--and all their worries about their children, their misgivings towards husbands who cheat on them--were undeniably real and heartfelt.Her sense of words becomes especially sharp when the children take over the scenes and you see the world through their eyes.  You want to believe in all that they've made up.  Eventually, you do.I think this is a good book for anyone, but for anyone who is a mother out there and worries about her children (little daughters, especially), this book can really evoke something.I really wanted to give five stars to this, but it's a rare rating I give (only to two other novels).  If Amazon allowed it, I would give it 4 stars and a half.  However, I am eagerly waiting for what Powell will produce next, hopefully something even more powerful so I can give my rare rating of a full five stars.Read moreRead less
