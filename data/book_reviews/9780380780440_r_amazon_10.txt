This book started off a little slow for me, the Prologue I am still undecided that it was relevant to the story.  In that I am referring specifically to the scene with the bar maid.  I kept waiting for that to somehow tie into the story somehow.  BUT, in the end it didn't matter!  Once I finished this book I was sad that it had come to an end!  The relationship between Cole and Devon is wonderful and action packed.  This book has a little bit of everything in it!  I thought the part was so well written when she arrived at the camp covered in muck and the reaction of the men.  Devon's Uncle Monty is a great character and brought an additional dimension to the book.  I though the ending was great and I loved the twist!  Will keep an eye out for more from this author!Read moreRead less
