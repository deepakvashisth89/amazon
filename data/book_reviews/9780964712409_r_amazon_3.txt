The Korenblits hide from the Nazis in a haystack, along with Manya and her brother, Chaim. Manya and Chaim leave their family's hideout to stay with Meyer Korenblit, Manya's boyfriend and his family.The Nazis find them in their haystack. some of Meyer's sisters are sent to the same contration camp as Manya, and all of them help and encourage each other. They are sent to numerous camps together. Many of them survive, but some of the family get shot by Nazis. Manya and Meyer are married and have children. soon they reunite with Chaim and his family.It is hard for Manya, Meyer, and Chaim to realize that their families are not alive antmore. Manya and chaim do not find each other until the war is long over.This is a great book you need to read! This true story of two families and their struggles in the holocaust should really be a Newberry Honor Book! This is definitely a story of hope.When Manya hides with her brother and her boyfriend's family, the frightening Nazis find their secret hideout(that isn't so secret anymore). What will happen to Manya's family? Are they still alive?It is also a story of true loyalty and love. They never give up hope, but instead try to help their loved ones.
