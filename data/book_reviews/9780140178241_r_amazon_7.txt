Great read with an incredible amount of information, but I still have mixed feelings about the book.  In addition to all of the terrific history of the American West and the development of California are too many extraneous details about long forgotten bureaucrats and intra-agency battles.  This would have been a much better read if it had been properly edited and limited to 300 pages or so.  Amidst all of the details, there were several references to Mormons knowing how to irrigate, but what, when, and how they irrigated was never addressed.Bonus points for some occasionally witty lines.
