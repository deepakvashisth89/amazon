As an experienced Solaris and NT administrator, I have been waiting a long time for a book which covered all of the core Solaris material which I learned through experience. This book covers all of the material which  makes Solaris distinct from other flavors of UNIX (like HP/UX and Linux). I  like the way that the author has given examples of how to get things right  (like a whole chapter each on SAMBA and NFS), and how to fix things that go  wrong (like filesystem corruption - there are some great examples for using  fsck).
