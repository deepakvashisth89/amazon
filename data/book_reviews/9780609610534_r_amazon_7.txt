This is a great book for not only the University of Texas / Texas A & M fan, but also the college football fan. The book goes along with the 2001 football season.  It covers the entire season leading up to the big Thanksgiving football game between the two schools.  The book also dives into the storied past of both school and also touches on the sad and dark events that both schools had to face over time.  The book is a smooth read and its hard to put it down at times.  This is a real winner!!!Read moreRead less
