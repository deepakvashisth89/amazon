Allende is definitely my favorite author. I actually purchased "City of Beasts" by accident. While browsing through the Amazon Kindle book store on my android I saw "City of Beasts" and accidently hit the "buy" tab. I figured that if I accidently purchased an Allende book, it couldn't be a bad thing. I decided to begin reading the book and found that I could not put it down. This is usually the case with the Allende books I read. Her story and descriptions of the Amazon jungle and the people on the journey to this jungle were captivating.
