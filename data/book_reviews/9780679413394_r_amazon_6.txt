Newsflash! Marriage is an imperfect institution and requires huge commitment and compromise from both partners, often over a long period of time. Both partners will feel unappreciated, unheard, unfulfilled, at least some of the time. Both will feel that some (or many) of these needs are being unmet. Both are liable to experience a sense of sexual boredom and blandness, after the breathless excitement and variety they experienced when single.To suggest that this feeling of loss only applies to the wife is short-sighted in the extreme.It is unrealistic and unlikely that one person can fulfil all of our needs, so is an affair to only (or best) way to rediscover the joie de vivre that's missing in our life? Possibly this is why couples are now exploring the concept of open marriage as a way of getting the sexual variety, and emotional fulfillment that is lacking in the marriage. Maybe this is why so many women are embarking on affairs with other women as a way of meeting their need for communication and emotional fulfillment.Marriage still manages to provide positive benefits, but both partners need to be mature and unselfish,and communicate open and honestly about what their needs are, and they need to explore ways, maybe unconventional ways, to allow their partners to continue to grow and enrich their lives.Hopefully, the marriage can survive this.Read moreRead less
