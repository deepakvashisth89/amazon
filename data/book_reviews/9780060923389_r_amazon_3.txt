Written towards the end of the series the action is set in the fifties in an English village now on the outskirts of London. It is slower in pace than most but this allows for great character development which I enjoy. A lot of humour and the requisite body found most inconveniantly for the young lovers and their trip to the altar.All comes right as Miss Silver sorts it all out deftly dealing with all the miscreants making it all come right in the end.This series is a great read if your mind isn't up to anything complicated. It is a vacation in book form. Great reading on the beach or a cruise or if the January blahs have got you in their grip.I don't know what is going on with the kindle print editions.
