I had forgotten if I read this book or not, having probably been 20 years since I read as many PKD paperbacks I could get my hands on. The description didn’t ring a bell but amazingly after just a few pages, all these memories of the setting and the characters came back. It makes sense. It’s a very visual novel full of mutants and robits. I couldn’t remember any of the plot, but, consistent with this latest read, I’m not sure I know what I just read now either.I did like the discussion on religion and how post apocalyptic people would view a Christian god after He let the world be destroyed. Very interesting that the person that pushed the button would be hailed as a new god. The two sides of things are great, very much motivating the interesting and vivid characters.But I know I definitely didn’t understand certain key plot points. I even went back and reread them, which I almost never do. And got not much more than a slightly better understanding.  I’m not sure why. Maybe it’s the result of a mixed pacing of two authors?Overall tho, this was a good read. Would probably make a better graphic novel. And it certainly was the right length, for both the good and bad.
