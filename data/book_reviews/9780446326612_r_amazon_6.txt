I read a lot of fiction, much of which is detective stuff. Often, the stories are clever and entertaining, but the best of them create characters I'd like to know as real people.  Leo and Serendipity join that small group.I've tried to remember how many books I've read with dual or multiple narrators in the first person, and can only think of one other, a Ken Kesey masterpiece.  Sleeping Dog doesn't reach that level of literary brilliance in general, but the two narrators are seamlessly joined.  And, it makes for great story telling. Highly recommended!
