I really loved this Christian novel. It’s very well written, exciting, cliffhanger-hanging and with a wonderfully satisfying plot. Peretti keeps you guessing, which makes it so enjoyable. He cleverly weaves scripture into prose, which is helpful and inspiring. There are links to his first novel, This Present Darkness, so it’s probably best to read that first before embarking on Piercing The Darkness.
