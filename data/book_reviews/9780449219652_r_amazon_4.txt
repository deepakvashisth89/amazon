Just because they are in their 70's doesn't mean that Angela Benbow and Caledonia Wingate don't enjoy working to solve a murder mystery.  They, like most residents of their California seaside retirement community, take an instant dislike to Amy Kinseth, a new resident who proves to be rude and unfriendly.  When Amy is murdered there seem to be no lack of suspects.  Even though the police are working to solve the case, Angela and Cal feel that they possess an intuition and knowledge of the retirement home that will be helpful in finding the murderer.  When a second murder occurs the women know that their help is needed more than ever.  This delightful book is the first one of the series that I've read, but it will not be the last.Read moreRead less
