Over 30 years ago. I was in the middle of an unexpected divorce that shook me to the core of my soul. I lost my security and self esteem. While searching for help and guidance, and reading book after book hoping to find answers, I came across this book. I loved it! There was someone out there who understood what I was experiencing! I like that you can identify what you are feeling and read a section addressing the issue. I still scan through this book when I am feeling overwhelmed or sad, or whatever.
