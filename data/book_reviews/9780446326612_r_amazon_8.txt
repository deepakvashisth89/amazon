This writer ( Dick Lochte) is actually good. He does sustain interest with his quirky, well drawn characters. I find them interesting because I would never meet people like this in my real life world ! However, the graphic details about the poor dog victims of dog fighting is too much for me. The descriptions of the sordid "humans" involved in the world of dog fighting I found sickening and appalling. I am an animal advocate and am having a hard time finishing this book. The 14 year old precocious girl (Serendipity ) is refreshing and brave.  I will finish this book, but only because I want to see the outcome. However, I will not be reading any more Dick Lochte books with "Dog" in the title for fear that there will be more sad victimization of dogs in them.Read moreRead less
