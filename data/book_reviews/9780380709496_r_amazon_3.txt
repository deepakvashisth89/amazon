Solomita draws us into the real world of big city crime solving. An "old timer" cop, Stanley Moodrow shows a rookie that many of the rules for law enforcement are established by people who have never walked the streets of the inner city as a cop.The story starts with the reader knowing "who done it" so the intrigue is how will he be caught.
