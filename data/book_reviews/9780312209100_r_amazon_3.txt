This is my favorite of the Gail McCarthy series. Set in the Sierras, the descriptions of the mountains make you feel like you are really there. The mystery is completely believable and the conclusion quite climactic. The introduction of Blue Winter tells us that the possibility for a new romance is likely, and he is a very interesting character. We get little glimpses into who he is and it's very intriguing. Like all her reduced priced books a definite bargain. Well worth it.Read moreRead less
