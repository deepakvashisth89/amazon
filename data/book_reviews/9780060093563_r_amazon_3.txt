I've recently discovered that many of Mary Stewart's audiobooks are on spotify and can be listened to for free if you have a subscription.This was her first book, written in 1955. I will say, she was already a great writer right out of the gate.Charity is vacationing in France. She meets a family while there, which consists of a boy named David, his dog Rommel and his stepmother. They are on the run from David's father. She ends up right in the middle.The way Stewart writes reminds me of Hitchcock. A lot of danger lurking behind the scenes. Charity ends up being in a cross-country car chase. I have never been so riveted by a car chase. Will she get around the mule cart? The train? Now she's got a puncture??Emilia Fox was an excellent narrator and had a wide range of voices.
