Yes, the first thing out of my CHL instructor's mouth (about this book) was "it's out of date".  I still consider this book  was very helpful.  Most of the information is perfectly correct, and all the important information is and will most likely remain correct, plus it has some very well written ways of telling you in plain English, the things you need to consider before using deadly force. The book uses excellent humor to illustrate what it's trying to get across. I consider this book indispensable.  I was the only person in our class to ace the CHL written test. I recommend this book for anyone in Texas that has a gun, and plans on using it for protection. This book contains information that no one is born knowing. Owning a gun in Texas is NOT all common sense. Of course, you must stay current on all gun laws in Texas, since they're subject to change every 2 years- and they're easily obtainable online, However, I enjoyed this book and very glad I purchased it.Read moreRead less
