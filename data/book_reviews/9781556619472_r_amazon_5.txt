This was the first book I had read by Lawana Blackwell.  She did a great job with her writing.  Julia had a bit of heartbreak in the beginning.  Sounds like her husband was very distant and didn't like to share things with his wife.  The age difference was quite a big gap as well.  He left her with a big mess when he died.  It was good she and the children had a place to go.  She also had caring friends in Fiona and her former butler Jensen.  She met some interesting people when she came to Gresham.  Her kids didn't feel at home at first because their world had been turned upside down.  But they grew to love just as much as Julia did.  They had a lot to do in making The Larkspur a lodging house.  But the townspeople helped in their own way.  The people that took up residence in the Larkspur were an interesting bunch of people.  I like how God was portrayed in the book as well.  Fiona had a hard start in life it became better when she started working for Julia and also when she came alongside Julia in running the Larkspur.  She had a great admirer in Ambrose Clay the actor.  But it took almost the whole before he could declare his love for Fiona and marry her.  Love came again for Julia as well in the form of the vicar who was without a wife as well because of her passing away.  He showed more caring for her then her first husband did.  Phillip Julia son had a rivalry going with the vicar daughter Laurel but I don't the vicar or Julia knew about it.  But that all changed when Phillip offered to bring Laurel her homework after she hurt herself.  Andrew Phelps the vicar fell for Julia the first time he met her it took Julia a little longer for the feelings to come but they did.  The kids Phillip Adele and Grace needed a father who would do things with them and love and not push the off on someone else to raise.  Just like their father had done with them Julia even had a hard time being a parent in the beginning before they came to THE Larkspur because of all the servants that she had at her disposal they wouldn't really let her parent her kids.  Her move to the Larkspur in Gresham gave her independence which she so needed.  I think it did the same thing somewhat for Andrew as well.  Elizabeth the vicar oldest daughter also found a friend in Julia as well and the mother figure she so needed.  Elizabeth found her calling as well in Gresham by taking care of kids who needed her love and compassion.  The older people who stayed in the Larkspur had very interesting lives. Two of them were able to find love as well after being without their mates for awhile. I have a feeling that Mrs  Kingsbury will find love again to.  I thought she was very snooty at first but she kind of grew on you.  Looking forward to the next ones in the series.  I hope Elizabeth the vicar daughter doesn't get her heart broke again.
