This book is a jargon free look at the psychology of both men and women, written from a psychoanalytic perspective. It looks at our interaction between two members of a couple (it is not just about hetrosexual couples, lesbian relationships are looked at too, as well has how the dynamic changes between two women who are a couple), and with wider society. Some concepts I find difficult to agree with.
