I like a good alien planet story. Hostile ecosystems with seen and unseen dangers, are good fodder for the interplanetary explorers of the future.  Bios gets off to a good start and I imagine most readers will cruise through 75% of this book in little time.  It has a good blend of tech and political hierarchies conflicting with each other throughout. Towards the end RCW seems to run out of steam,there are some good SF themes running as the main characters juggle the off world crisis with the planet bases but these fizzle out and the ending is sort of sown up but is disappointing.
