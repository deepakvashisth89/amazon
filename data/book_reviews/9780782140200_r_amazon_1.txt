Having thoroughly enjoyed the original "Web Pages That Suck", I was quick to buy this sequel.  If you haven't read either book yet, just buy the "Son of" sequel, because it is mostly a needed update of the original.  It covers the same concepts in the same hilarious style, but is more current with regard to the latest bad design techniques and the current browsers, plug-ins and so forth that are now in use.
