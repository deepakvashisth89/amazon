This is a fine novel in the military/sci-fi genre, set in a vicious colonial war on a jungle planet.Paradoxically, it is also a very thought-provoking work in the realm of philosophy and metaphysics. The moral and ethical questions it poses are as timely and valid upon our 21st century Earth as upon the fictional Orlantha, Iryala, and Tyss; 20 millennia from now.Well worth a read.Read moreRead less
