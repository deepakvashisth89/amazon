The excellent synopsis almost ,but not quite tells too much; it gives the right flavour to the book.! Barnard was such a skilful writer; extremely readable with subtle pacing . This is so much more than just a crime novel set in the late 30's. The overtones of the Spanish Civil War and the undertones of the impending WW2 are conveyed through a variety of characters.Above all ,it was the realisation that the so wonderful Hallams were in many ways lacking in too many areas. In addition the author cleverly drops in some relevant information of 40 years later ; always a dangerous manoeuvre ,but easy to a writer as skilled as this . A slight regret that the book wasn't a little longer and that a few details weren't expanded on . A very good book indeed and well worth a read .Read moreRead less
