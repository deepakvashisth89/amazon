I've always preferred Gary Snyder's prose over his poetry. That is why this book is so good -- filled with essays and interviews, it manages to be simultaneously insightful and revolutionary.This work would be a great  introduction to the work and politics of Gary Snyder. Even if you dislike,  or are unsure of his poetry, I would encourage you to at least check out  this book; a knowledge of his poetry is not a prerequisite for enjoying and  learning from it.Only one work from this volume, "The East West  Interview" was excerpted in the Gary Snyder Reader that was recently  published.
