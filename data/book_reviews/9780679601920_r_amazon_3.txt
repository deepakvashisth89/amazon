I last read Northanger Abbey in 1995, in print. My notes say only "Excellent." This time, I listened to the audiobook as narrated by Juliet Stevenson. It, too, proved excellent.What to add? Jane Austen is still marvelous two centuries later. Highlights included Austen poking fun at the conventions of novels; a brief but staunch defence of novel-reading; and passages of stark horror. (There is a despicable young man who deceives and misrepresents the heroine in scenes of social atrocity worse than a monster's carnage.)And I liked the hero and heroine, of course, and their romance, of course.Five out of five sprigged-muslin stars.About my reviews: I try to review every book I read, including those that I don't end up enjoying. The reviews are not scholarly, but just indicate my reaction as a reader, reading being my addiction. I am miserly with 5-star reviews; 4 stars means I liked a book very much; 3 stars means I liked it; 2 stars means I didn't like it (though often the 2-star books are very popular with other readers and/or are by authors whose other work I've loved).Read moreRead less
