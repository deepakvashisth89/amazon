This book is a welcome change of pace for me... a military mystery with people I can relate to. This isn't a high action thriller. It's focus is on the history and viewpoint of the various types of people who served during World War II. The characters and scenery are exquisitely drawn. The plot slows down in the second half of the book, and that is why I give it 4 stars. Even so, the people who have called it a sleeper are a little harsh, in my view. The ending isn't a neat little bundle because life isn't a neat bundle you can tie up with a bow. I would read other books by these authors and would recommend this one to any history buff.Read moreRead less
