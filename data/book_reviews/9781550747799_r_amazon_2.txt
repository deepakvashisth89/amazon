Wonderful book to help explain who lives in our global village and how our resources are distributed. Putting this information in terms of a village of 100 people makes the BIG numbers understandable. Good pictures and good verbal text with each category. I used the information for a project with teens. Since hundreds of millions of people are added to our global population each year, be careful of using 2002 statistics in 2010...in other words, be mindful of the publication date.  Please note also, the author has a website that may provide updated figures.  Buy the most recent version of the book...or buy an older version like I did and update the figures.  If you don't do math or ratio's, just buy the newest version.  If you like math, it's easy to update the percentages, especially with a spreadsheet program.
