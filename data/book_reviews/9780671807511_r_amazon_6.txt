Many authors write their best novels early.  My favorite novel of Peter Straub's has always been "Ghost Story".  Until I read this book, that is. This is one of Straub's very best books and that it is not easily available for download is a real misfire, especially since utter drek can be had at a moment's notice.  It's not as if Straub is unknown.  He has been a bestselling author for decades, has done a few novels with Stephen King, and has received multiple Stoker awards (the Horror genre's Oscar). This novel was even made into a popular movie, ''The Haunting of Julia" (1977) with Mia Farrow playing Julia ("Full Circle" was the original title.)It is a ghost story through and through just like Straub's "Ghost Story" was purely a ghost story.  Straub does his best work when he sticks with ghosts.  When he wanders off into other supernatural categories, he is not nearly as good. Julia is a very wealthy American married to an older London barrister. She has just been released from a mental hospital following the death of her daughter. She has bought a house she was immediately taken with upon first sight and immediately moves into it. She can't bear to go back to her husband because she blames him for their daughter's death. He desperately wants her back. And his brother would like to take her from him and his brother is younger and gorgeous. To kick matters off, we are treated to a seance in Julia's new home!I read this book until 3am after starting it the afternoon before.
