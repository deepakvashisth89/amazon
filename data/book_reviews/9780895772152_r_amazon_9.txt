''Tess of the D'Urbervilles'' is a tragic love story about a young married couple who separate from each other when just after their marriage the girl reveals to her husband her secret that she is not a ''maiden'' anymore. I enjoyed it although I skipped several pages due to their abundance of unnecessary detailed descriptions. It was not an exciting read but very satisfying. If you are familiar with Anthony Trollope, you'll see that ''Tess of the D'Urbervilles'' is like a  concise version of his novels, which are usally very voluminous, but much, much better.Read moreRead less
