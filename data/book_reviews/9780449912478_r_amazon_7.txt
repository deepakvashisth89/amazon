Flotsam is the first of Remarque's refugee works (followed by Arch of Triumph and Shadows in Paradise).  The themes he explored in The Three Comrades are here--the ill heroine, the WWI war veteran, etc.  However, here the emphasis is on the absurdities that the nationless must face to survive.  The work is interesting from a literary standpoint in that Remarque's writing is maturing, though he does seem to return to the same characters over and over--here, the Steiner figure is similar to Ravic in the Arch of Triumph, who, is similar to the main characters of Heaven Has No Favorites, Night in Lisbon, Shadows In Paradise and The Black Obelisk.
