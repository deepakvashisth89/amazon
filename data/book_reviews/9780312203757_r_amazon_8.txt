This is a readable novel by P.T. Deutermann concerning an FBI and police manhunt of a terrorist who is blowing up bridges along the Mississippi River.  Some FBI politics here, a touch of romance, and so forth.  Deutermann once again uses the familiar "single guy teamed up with beautiful single woman" theme in this one.  The novel moves along and is a quick read, but Deutermann's best novels are those that deal with the Navy, something he knows well.  This is not one of those.Now the nits.  Firstly, Deutermann seems to have an uncontrollable urge to give his characters silly nicknames that we are supposed to believe that real people would use as their professional "names."  Here, the protagonist's real name is Bill, but he goes by "Hush" as he explains to everyone he meets.  Yeah right.  I've never seen anyone do that in real life except for Buzz Aldrin.  I suppose if you are the second man to walk on the moon you can get away with it, but that's about it. Someone told me it is an Annapolis thing (Deutermann is a graduate of the Naval Academy.)Another nit is that the villain in this story is just too resourceful and relentless to be true.  We are supposed to believe that one older guy can slip by all of the nation's mobilized police forces together with the Army and FBI and just keep coming and keep coming.  Not credible at all.  People get tired and make mistakes.  Just not credible.Last nit.  This book has what has become Deutermann's signature non-ending.  The story just ends.  No loose ends wrapped up, nothing.  Deutermann has done decent endings in a few of his novels, but not this one.A quick read and not bad.  RJB.Read moreRead less
