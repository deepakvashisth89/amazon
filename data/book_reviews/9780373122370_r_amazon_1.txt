I really enjoyed this story because there really weren't any of the Harlequin pitfalls that normally exist.  There is no confusion as to how the characters feel for each other; the characters have honest dialogue that actually deals with the issues in their relationship.  I especially liked that the h learns to stand up for herself and that the H realizes that his actions caused some of the issues in their marriage and he does everything in his power to fix his mistake.  There is an awesome part of the story where the woman that had been chasing the H gets put in her place by the H.
