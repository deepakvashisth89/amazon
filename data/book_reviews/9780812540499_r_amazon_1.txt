A bit of the Civil War that was probably little known in the East. Many brave men and women on both sides of the conflict as well as those who did terrible things. The story flows well although sometimes it was hard to remember which side a character was on as the narrative jumped from one to another. Some characters were more memorable than others, e.g., Lieutenant Franklin. Learn a bit of history and enjoy a good read. The best way to spend time with a book.Read moreRead less
