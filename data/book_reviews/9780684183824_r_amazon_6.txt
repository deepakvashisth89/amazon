These are wonderful stories that truly live up to the title, "Ghost Stories".  They are very easy reading for the most part.  They are very atmospheric and don't depend on gore to shock the reader.  Most of them take place before the invention of electricity  and the reader can imagine the darkly lit, gloomy environs.  This book would make a great gift for ghost story lovers to add to their collection although some may find them too subtle.  This collection of Wharton's is a classic.Read moreRead less
