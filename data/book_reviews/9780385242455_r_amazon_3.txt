I read this book when it first came out and have re-read it for reference a number of times since.  My husband's family hails from Tyrrell County, NC as did Dorothy Spruill Redford's ancestors.  My interest in the history of Tyrrell County was unsatisified before I found Ms. Redford's well-researched and completely interesting account of the early inhabitants of the county (both black and white.)Mrs. Spruill's interest and compulsion to discover her roots is a story that every genealogist will recognize and appreciate.This book should be required additional reading for serious students of North Carolina history.Read moreRead less
