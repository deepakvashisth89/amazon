I wanted to love this compilation of Western Buddhism with Feminism. I found about half of the contributors inspiring and definitely worth reading.I was especially disappointed in Pema Chodron, who I normally love. Her aim in No Right No Wrong seemed to be as an apologist for the inappropriate sexual behavior of Trungpa Rinpoche, which was definitely wrong. Pema has since apologized for telling a victim of sexual assault by another Buddhist teacher that she did not believe her, so I can hope she has thought better of her defense of TR as well.This anthology was compiled in 1996, so I'm tempted to just chalk up its mediocrity up to that.
