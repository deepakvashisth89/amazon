I'll be honest -- this book is pretty deep. And it recalls historic events and figures so casually that it made me, a college-educated man, feel like I slept through history and philosophy class. I didn't get through hardly any of it -- but I wish I had the patience. The premise is exceptional, but so is the text. If you've got plenty of time, this book explores some pretty important ideas.
