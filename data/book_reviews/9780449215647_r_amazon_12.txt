I read this book in the early 70's, it was published in 1964. I couldn't  remember  much of the plot except  that it had a connection  with the Spanish Riding School. I was pleased to come across it again  in Kindle. I would describe  it as a good old fashioned adventure story. The characters are likeable although possibly a bit unrealistic. The writing is beautifully  descriptive, the plot is faced paced and the tension and excitement is maintained  until the very end. It is pure escapism and as an added bonus it has horses in it with some interesting details about the Spanish Riding School.  That was a big plus for me. Highly recommended for readers who like horses and adventure stories.Read moreRead less
