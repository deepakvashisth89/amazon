Perhaps it is because her storyline Is so good and you want to see what happens, but this author always draws things out so.  This is the third book in the series.  It is very important to read them in order.  If you read the other two, you must read this one.  It is good, but it will drive you crazy how she takes so long to tell the story.  I would love to read another book of hers that just told the story and not drawn out so.  I will never forget about us all lying in the gutter.... That's the thing.
