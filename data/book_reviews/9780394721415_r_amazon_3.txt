The High Window, Chandler's first original novel (the first two were reworked from short stories), is a fast moving, beautifully written piece of hard boiled fiction.Like all Chandler literature, there are stylistic flourishes here, such as the first couple pages of Chapter 8, which I find somehow reminiscent of beat poetry - "Bunker Hill is old town, lost town, shabby town, crook town..."The plot concerns a lost Brasher Doubloon, and the relationship  of a timid secretary and her domineering harridan boss.  The part detailing the coin's background is intriguing and the criminal shenanigans surrounding the coin are excellent.Regardless, the human story of the secretary is more important to Chandler, and its resolution is what I remembered most when I read this book again after 30 years.I can read Chandler just for the beauty of his language, and his observations.
