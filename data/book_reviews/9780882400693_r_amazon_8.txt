Even though this book is out of print, I would like to extol its merits.  I picked it up when I visited Alaska several years ago.  It is a grand story of an Alaska pioneer woman and her amazing life.  With good humor and strength, she survived incredible experiences.  When I returned from my trip, I added the book to my classroom assortment of books for students to borrow when they needed a book during our silent reading time.  I had intended it to be a good book for my girls, but as it turned out, the boys seemed to love it even more.  My edition is a paperback, and over the years, it became tattered and torn, but always much loved.  I have just come across it in a major garage-cleaning endeavor after a move.
