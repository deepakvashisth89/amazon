This was the first book I read by Norman Kelley - I thought it started off a bit slow for me, but then BAAAAAAANG!!  It took off and began to run!!  I couldn't put the book down.  I couldn't wait to see what would be uncovered next.  Nina Halligan is a sho' nuff, kick-ya-butt, intellectual-educated sista that won't punk out under any circumstances.  She's "street smart" and formally educated - she's a true double threat.  I would like to see Nina and Zee hook up again - I think they would make a damn good team.  Zee provided balance to Nina's infrequently episodes of instability...Anna was funny too!!  The manner in which Nina unearthed the truth behind the entire New Nation and De Lawd, and her "resources" kept my interest.  I'd like to see Nina and Zee take on Nate Ford - Nina's demon from her past.  I thought the book was GREAT!!!Read moreRead less
