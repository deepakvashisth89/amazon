The Wooden Bowl is the quintessential book about meditation.  It inspired me to re-examine my views about meditation, and de-stressed and de-mystified the practice enough that I have become confident enough to use it nearly every day.  The book is filled with simple and expressively eloquent aphorisms that cause me to exclaim, "Of course!" even on subsequent readings.
