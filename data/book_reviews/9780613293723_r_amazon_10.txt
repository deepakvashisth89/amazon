Sometimes I agonize over whether to award a novel a four or five star rating (or for that matter lower star ratings). This time I had no such difficulties with that. Although the book was published in 1988 and the politics from 2018's perspective is different, the book is still a great adventure read from an accomplished writer, one who knows his craft., one I've had the pleasure of reading in hard and soft cover before. (This is my first electronic version.)Alright there may be derring-does and superhuman feats but it is an adventure book. Dirk Pitt's enemies throw bullets, grenades, waves of combatants at him; women their bodies; friends their loyalty. A highly recommended, entertaining read.Read moreRead less
