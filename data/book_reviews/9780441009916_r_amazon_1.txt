This is the most enjoyable, original, intelligent book, of any genre, that I have read in years.  There is no invulnerable, huge, buff hero, no gorgeous heroine with heaving bosom, no sparkly vampires, or over-sexed werewolves, no endless, pornagraphic sexual extravaganzas, no sissy elves, wizards or magicians.BUT, there is mystery, emotional and sexual attraction, bravery, friendship, honor, humor, lots of action, hairsbreadth escapes and a flawed hero and heroine.  The quality of the writing is exceptional, the characters very appealing and the editing is impeccable.  There were no distracting typos, poor grammar, wonky type fonts or other publishing faux pas.The hero, Val Con, is a former scout, re-trained as an assassin. The heroine, Miri, is a former mercenary and unemployed bodyguard, running from the interplanetary mafia. How they meet and end up helping each other, rather than killing each other is touching and entertaining. They become allies, then friends, then lovers.  All while fighting for their lives, integrity and (in Val Con's case) sanity.Usually, I am irate if a book is not "stand alone" and even more so if it ends in a cliffhanger.
