I've owned this book since it came out in hardcover when I was ten years old. It was given to me by a very dear friend - he died of AIDS in the eighties. I still have the tattered remains. I read that book every day of my very lonely tenth summer in 1982. The stories are haunting and beautiful, and written by really amazing people  - some of whom you wouldn't imagine writing stories for children. I guess that was the point. Even though I received this book as a precocious, lonely child, it was written for adults - 'for the child in us all'.  This anthology is close to my heart, and always will be..Read moreRead less
