Though this short piece of fiction by J.R.R. Tolkien may seem overly simple on the surface compared to his epic work centered on Middle-Earth, this wonderful little story touches upon all the major themes addressed in his lecture "On Fairy-Stories" in an engaging and creative way. The pen & ink illustrations by Pauline Baynes provide an innocently inviting way into a wild, and sometimes dangerous, world view that both author and artist leave to your own imagination to complete and become real.Read moreRead less
