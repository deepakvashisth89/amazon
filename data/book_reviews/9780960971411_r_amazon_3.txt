I bought all three Ripped books. A little repetitive, but the information is valuable. As another reviewer said, you may or may not agree with low fat near vegetarian diet that Clarence Bass is following, but overall the principles laid out in the book are solid. The trick to fat loss is eating fewer calories. The trick to that is eating foods low in energy density, so that you don't feel hungry most of the time.The most important point of the books is that Clarence Bass is the practitioner of bodybuilding, not "consultant" with degrees in nutrition and sports science and large gut.
