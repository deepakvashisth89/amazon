I consider Synaptic Self the best book I have read about the Mind - Brain problem. Besides I consider John LeDoux one of the best writers in the neuroscience field. He writes to the point, never exceeds the intellectual limits of his readers and is able to make a difficult issue understandable for the general reader. I think Le Doux definitely meets his aims that are expressed in the title: How Our Brains Become Who We Are. After this book it is much easier for me to understand how the brain works and especially how important the synapses are for us. Generally let me say that this is a book that I can recommend for anyone who is interested in neuroscience, who wants an easy read and still wants to be educated by reading this book and last but not least understand how our brains work. After having read this book I understand that there is no mind - brain problem. It is much easier to understand ourselves than what we think as long as we consider and understand the work of our synapses.Thank you very much John LeDoux for this enlightening book.Read moreRead less
