A strong addition to the Burke series by Andrew Vachss. All the familiar element are here - hardboiled prose, fast-paced dialogue, violence, all interspersed with some disturbingly realistic portrayals of child abuse. Perhaps not all of the plot hung together but this is so entertaining, readable and it seems for Mr Vachss, effortlessly good, that I doubt fans of the Burke series and crime fiction in general will find anything to complain about.Read moreRead less
