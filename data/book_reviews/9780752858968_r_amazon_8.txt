This was my first Inspector Rebus novel and I suppose I should have started at the beginning of the series.  The plot was quite interesting given that you knew from the beginning who committed the cime, you just didn't know why.  But I had a difficult time getting into the flow of the story.  I was easily distracted and found myself re-reading pages.  That said, I did stick with it and enjoyed the second half of the book.  Even though Rebus appears to want to be unlikeable, I did like his character.  He was certainly not afraid of tackling things his own way!There were several sub-plots in this story that kept me hopping to keep up.  The author had several good twists in the main plot and I was quite surprised with the ending!  All in all, a good read.Read moreRead less
