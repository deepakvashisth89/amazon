Compelling was my first thought as I read through the pages of this book. Intense, hard to put down followed. The prose was rich with insight and description. Why did I keep reading about a self-centred protagonist who didn't care about anyone, who selfishly used others for his own gain and who was indifferent to others needs and lacked self-awareness?  Because he is a reflection of many in society. He is almost soulless. This was a book one could pick up, read for a minute or two and put down.  It is abundantly rich in words and characters, eloquent and breathtaking in phrases and descriptions. For example - sun candles. If you have been to Lake Superior, you would understand. This is a book one could reread and learn from the history of world wars and rapid change. The author talks about art with insight and I learned from it.  The characters who faced war, hardship, isolation are our great grandparents, grandparents and parents. It is a book that one may remember for life, a rare treasure for those who have a small list of unforgettable reads. It made my top three.Read moreRead less
