If you like laughter with your romance, a snarky female lead with an equally amusing hero, a little suspense, and a host of supporting characters, this is the book for you. Of course, there is so much more to this novel; however, unlike the author, words are not my forte.It doesn't matter how many times I've read this book over the years or how many other new books have made my must-read list, this book is always near the top. I was so thrilled to find it available on Kindle Unlimited, because I can no longer afford to buy the electronic version no matter how much I love it.I thought getting an electronic book reader meant that the publishers would pass on the savings now that they didn't have to pay print costs. Boy, was I naive! This book would cost me more now than it did back in 2011! I wish I could afford to purchase the rest of this author's works or more were offered on Unlimited. It was lovely having a reason to laugh this hard & long as well as to feel this excited about what's to come in the pages to follow.
