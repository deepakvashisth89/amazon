This book was remarkable.  It was so well written.  I am a Psychiatric Nurse and I was impressed with how well the author portrayed Corin/Leah's character.  Initially, I did not realize that they were one and the same person.  This book highlights how years of physical and mental abuse can result in a child becoming mentally disturbed.  It was so sad.  What started out as a girls night joke "The Perfect Man" list resulted in both local and national controversy.  I think to many folks focused on the sexual requirements on the list and not the importance of being faithful, support, etc.The author did a great job of introducing each character to the reader.  I became invested in these individuals.  I wanted Marci to find a loving, mature and employed man.  Luna also deserved  to have a man committed to her not running around with fan girls.  Even though T.J. and her husband were high school sweetheart they had drifted apart, their needs had changed.  Jaine had been engaged three times and she was still trying to figure out why these relationships did not go the distance.  Basically, these ladies were longing for Mr. Perfect who doesn't exist.Jaine and T.J. after their experience would find that their guys may not be perfect, but can and would work to be better.  Sam and Jaine were an exciting and fiery couple, perfect for each other.  They initially responded to each other like oil and vinegar, which separately is ok, but is better together.  There was never a dull moment with these too.  I love Jaine, she was opinionated had a temper, and was loyal.  She doesn't take stuff from anyone including her siblings.
