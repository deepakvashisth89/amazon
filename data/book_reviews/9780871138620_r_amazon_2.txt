The Everlasting Stream is one of the best books I’ve ever read! To be fair…I might be partial. I read about Walt’s book in “Becoming a King” by Morgan Synder. He wrote that this book should be in every man’s library. He wrote of a journalist who would travel every Thanksgiving to a rural Kentucky town to rabbit hunt. I’m from rural Kentucky so I was interested. When I looked up the book, the rural Kentucky was my hometown. I can’t believe I’ve never heard of this book!There’s so much truth and wisdom in this book. He doesn’t hit you over the head with it. It isn’t 10 steps to a happier life. It isn’t self-help. It’s more like a father or friend talking with you. The book is about hunting, but it isn’t the focus. The focus is about the things that matter in life.Read it. Enjoy it.
