Well, part of it anyway.  The book is from the view point of two tourists, who each keep a diary, of their journey.  The book is full of images, meant to be other places, landscapes of alien planets, diagrams of spaceships, images of spaceports, aliens, and equipment.  There is also a small subplot which I will not give away as I do not wish to spoil it for you.  Kind of reminds me ofGalactic Tours: Thomas Cook Out of This World Vacationsbut unlike that book which was PURELY a tour book, with prices, lists of stopovers, and even a booking form, this is more of a story with a LOT of illustrations from different artists.  I did find reading it kind of rough as their seemed to be some words that were not...correct.  Many a time I found a word such as 'use', for example, in a sentence when it would have made more sense to have the word 'new' and so forth.  No spelling or grammar mistakes as much as the wrong word in the wrong place.  In fact one paragraph had so many mistakes along those lines that the whole page didn't make much sense by the time I had finished reading it.  Also, while the book seems thick so many of the pages are taken up by images that the story itself turns out to be very short.  You could easily read it within half a day if you wanted to.
