Few people are aware that this is the book on which the "Parent Trap" movies are based! It was written by German author Erich Kastner, and it is absolutely charming! Lisa and Lotti meet at summer camp in the mountains. Lisa lives in Vienna with her father, who is a famous composer and conductor, and Lotti lives in Munich with her mother, who works for a ladies' magazine publisher.The story will make you laugh and cry and cheer for the girls who want nothing more than to be a family again.
