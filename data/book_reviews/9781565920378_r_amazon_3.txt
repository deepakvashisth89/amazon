I would recommend this book for ANY user of SCO Unix, from the novice to the sys admin.  It nearly every command and option from SCO System V Release 3.2, Version 4.0.  In addition, this book also covers several important Unix utilities such as the vi editor, sed, awk, etc.I would give this book 5 stars except SCO OpenServer has advanced well beyond this level (this edition is from 1993).  Most of the information remains accurate, but there are some issues that exist.  Keep in mind that there are some additional commands and additional options to existing commands that are not listed.  Also, but more rare, there are commands or options that have been removed or changed in the OS.
