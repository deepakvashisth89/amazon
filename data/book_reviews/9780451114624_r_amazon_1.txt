This is a story about a mother who makes a suicide attempt during a severe bout of depression.  She recovers with a lot of prayer and there is a great deal of focus on her making amends.I can't help but think the heavy emphasis on her "sin" and Christianity will turn off a lot of people.  The thinking about depression today is considerably different than when this book was written.  Perhaps this book best serves the purpose of illuminating just one woman's path back from the brink.  If you've had family members who have struggled with depression in the past, Marilee's story might give you some insight on what it was like to suffer from a debilitating mental illness in the 1970's.Read moreRead less
