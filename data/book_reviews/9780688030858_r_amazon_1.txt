I read this book because much of my life I have heard about it. My great uncle (Father Riker*) is in the book and so I sort of HAD to read about my crazy relative. But I found the book to be very entertaining and Belli is one of those characters that could only San Francisco could produce. He would not have flourished in the politically correctness of today. He was a brilliant lawyer, a womanizer and a champion for societies underdogs.* Riker founded a cult in San Carlos, CA called "Holy City".  In the 70's a nightclub owner bought a sign from there that said "Holy City Zoo".
