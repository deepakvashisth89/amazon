Karl Popper's advocacy of critical rationalism and falsifiability is the greatest voice keeping scientists honest to this day. He says, “We are workers who are adding to the growth of objective knowledge as masons work on a cathedral.” This is a difficult but rewarding read, just take it slowly then sit back and think. My favourite is his view, “Progress is born from questioning, experimenting, and even failing. Science thrives on boldness, not the illusion of flawless certainty and consensus. True breakthroughs come when we embrace mistakes as stepping stones to discovery, not as blemishes to hide. Imperfection fuels innovation.Every solution of a problem raises new unsolved problems, the more so the deeper the original problem and the bolder its solution. The more we learn about the world, and the deeper our learning, the more conscious, specific, and articulate will be our knowledge of what we do not know. For this, indeed, is the main source of our ignorance--the fact that our knowledge can be only finite, while our ignorance must necessarily be infinite."Read moreRead less
