Poor Butterfly was a good read, as are all of Stuart Kaminsky’s books, but it seemed a bit flat for a Toby Peters novel.  The book takes place in San Francisco rather than the usual L.A. locale but all that meant was no Hollywood celebrity cameos and no run-ins with familiar cops. The supporting cast of Shelly, Gunther and Jeremy get little to do, Keopold Stokowski was a blah featured personage and the plot seemed unfocused.Not a bad book, just not meeting the high standards of previous entries in the series.Recommended.Read moreRead less
