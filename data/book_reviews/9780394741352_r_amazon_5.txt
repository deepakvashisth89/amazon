There can be no doubt that this is a well written, humorous book.This was written towards the end of an era of Catholic literature.  Increasingly, it was held that a book that directly discussed Catholicism was problematic--it was to draw into a "ghetto", even though there were many conversions using the old approach--unless it was critical of the faith.  Writers were not to "violate the freedom of the reader" by taking a position about what is true...and certainly any miracles were off limits!This book directly discussed Catholicism, but it seemed to keep the subject at arms-length...as though it were afraid to celebrate it, but also didn't quite want to directly, unequivocally condemn it.  The story is primarily about a Catholic religious order, and one priest in particular, but it did not emotionally engage the priest nor spirituality.
