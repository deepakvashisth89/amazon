The first Psycho novel was excellent. This sequel is not up to par with the original. The book started with much promise -  the first few chapters were quite good. After that it fell apart. There were scenes in the books that the author must have added for the shock value: the young actress making a porno film, the actor in a nude review, the brutal gang rape of a woman during World War II, the reference to the snuff film. These did not anything to the story - in fact they distracted from it. Aside from this often times the novel got mired in the side stories.
