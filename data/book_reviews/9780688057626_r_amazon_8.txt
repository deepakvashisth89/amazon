First of all, what I loved about this book were the trail of characters and how they intertwined and worked their way through life. And they all came back around to intersect again and again. The subject matter caught me totally off-guard. I wasn't prepared for the heavy focus on abortion. What I did like was the dedication and care presented to women in many types of situations, and the care taken in their choices without judging. Ironically, all the characters seemed to feel 'judged' in one way or another in their own lives. Very good character study. It took me a very long time to read it. I'm not sure why. I read it in between other books. If you are ready for a good long read. And you like multiple characters who are threaded through a story in surprising ways, you'll probably like this.
