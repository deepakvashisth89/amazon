Ruth Plumly Thompson was L. Frank Baum’s chosen successor in the creation of Oz stories.  The Wishing Horse of Oz, first published in 1935, is her fifteenth Oz book.  It has numerous delights.  Most notably, it is an adventure featuring Miss Dorothy Gale of Kansas, the first such tale by Thompson that I have read.  In this story, that happens thirty-five years after Dorothy first came to Oz via tornado, Dorothy is still a young girl.  People don’t age in Oz.  Dorothy’s faithful companion in this story is not a scarecrow, a tin man, or a lion.  He is a winged pig named Pigasus.  Dorothy and Pigasus find themselves in an Oz ruled by a usurper, where they are the only two who remember Princess Ozma, the rightful sovereign of the land of Oz.  Their adventures through Oz and in neighboring lands, as they work to restore Ozma to her throne, make a wonderful read.
