Trust me when I tell you that there is more than just one "hot-blooded kiss" in this novel. If you are the type that is easily offended by very steamy sex scenes, this might not be the book for you. I really never would have guessed from the cover. The wildflowers look so tame!I didn't really love this book, but I am still glad I read it. Everyone has different tastes, and the things I didn't love about this book someone else might. What I did like, was that the characters were a lot of fun even if at times they felt kind of shallow. I thought the plot was interesting and some of the dialogue was just downright funny to read. The editing was good and the ending was not completely expected to happen the way it did.What made me not love this book so much, was that the characters just seemed to be bumbling along through much of the book. There are plenty of times when I wanted to yell "get to the point!" The constant indecision of the main character got a bit tiresome after a while, in my opinion. Often I found myself shaking my head and wishing she would just make up her mind. This made me want to get to the end of the book in a hurry, but not because I was loving it.Overall, this was a light read that I would recommend to those who like their romances simple and very hot!Read moreRead less
