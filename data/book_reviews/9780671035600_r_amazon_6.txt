The thirteen current reviews are split between those who loved or hated the book.  My opinion is decidedly more ambivalent.  I basically found the book to be a grind to read with just enough redeeming features to keep me from giving up.  Which was good because the pages actually turned somewhat easily towards the end.For those who know Boulder, the book is worth the time for no other reason that to read about the Boulder "Trifecta" - a bumper sticker contest in the law office.  It really captures the essence of Boulder.For the rest of you, I'm hard pressed to encourage this book unless you're interested in either the militia movement or repressed memory issues.  Interest in (or at least tolerance for) a healthy does of lesbian politics wouldn't hurt either.  I don't know if it was the issues or the writing but this book was incredibly easy to put down.  Things warmed up in the final hundred pages but even then Wesson wimped out on the ending.  I'd much prefer a Stephen Walsh White mystery set in Boulder.Read moreRead less
