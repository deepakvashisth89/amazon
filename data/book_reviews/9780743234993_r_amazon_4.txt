Great book.  It is not anti-south but will probably rankle apologists for the South and its slave-based economy.  It sets out the planter class perspective and pro-slavery sentiments that pushed the South to secede, but also the subsequent in-fighting within the Confederacy.  I was surprised to learn about how many Southerners (especially in the west - e.g. western Tenn.) were not very supportive of the Confederacy, and how so many Southerners tried to avoid fighting for the Confederate army.
