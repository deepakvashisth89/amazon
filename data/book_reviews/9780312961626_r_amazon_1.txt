I have to say that I was pleasantly surprised by how good this book was. Originally I picked it up for a quick read and was not expecting much more than just that. This book however is well written and the characters are so well defined and developed that I found myself thinking how neat it would be to know any of them in real life. The story is well balanced between flyfishing, nature and mystery. I will say overall a very good read. I will be reading the other books soon.Read moreRead less
