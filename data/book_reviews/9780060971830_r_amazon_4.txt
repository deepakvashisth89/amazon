I first read this years ago during related advanced graduate work. The book's quality is appalling, especially in terms of the naïve conclusions drawn out of wishful thinking, and presented as if they were credible reasoning. This book may seem better than it is to a general reader because it does elaborate geography and legend which are interesting in and of themselves. But reporting on local color and reading fictional texts as history in the modern sense is not scholarship; at best it is something akin to journalism. In fact the way the author passes off legend and folk belief as fact is embarrassing.
