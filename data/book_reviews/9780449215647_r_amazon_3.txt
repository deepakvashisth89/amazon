I must've been about 10 years old the first time I read Airs Above the Ground in a Reader's Digest Condensed book. I went to Vienna recently &  in planning for my trip I was reminded of the first time I had ever heard of the Lipizzaner stallions. So I bought Mary Stewart's book for my kindle. It is a good old fashioned mystery with a G rating. The protagonist is a young, married woman whose husband plays a part in solving the mystery. Any reference to their romantic reunion at the end of the novel is very proper. The novel was engaging & entertaining. It had well rounded characters, there was indeed a mystery & some excitement. No heavy analysis required, just a fun read. I was less enthralled with the horses in person at age 62 than I was as a 10 year old child reading about them. So good job, Mary Stewart.Read moreRead less
