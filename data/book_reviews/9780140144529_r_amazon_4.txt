This is definitely one of my top five favorite Kerouac novels.  I read it about twenty years ago and found it to be good but a bit painful to read.  I recently read it again and found it to be some of Kerouac's most poignant and beautiful writing.  If you read On the Road or Dharma Bums and want something similar, this is not it.
