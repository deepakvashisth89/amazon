The story of Ani Pachen resonates with the kind of courage and spiritual certainty that perhaps very few of us our capable, but all of us admire. In contrast to her indomitable strength, the Chinese occupation of Tibet takes  on outrageous dimensions -- an ugliness and brutality hard to bear. For one  nine-month period during her twenty-one years of imprisonment, Ani Pachen  endured a dark, earthen cell slightly larger than her body where she spent  her time praying, accomplishing one hundred thousand prostrations devoted  to the well-being of all. When released by the Chinese, she took up the  cause of a free Tibet in Lhasa, demonstrating against the torture and  murder of her people and country, putting herself in the greatest possible  peril.
