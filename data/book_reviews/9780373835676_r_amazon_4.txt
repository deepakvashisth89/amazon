Both of these stories have similar plots with different supporting details. Down-on-their-luck women who endear themselves to children with neglectful mothers and handsome/succussful doting uncles. A Christmas Wish had a Sound of Music sort of feel to it. Betty Neals is a new author for me. I needed a few chapters to acclimate myself to her very charming British style of writing.
