This book was insanely hot!  Thea Devine has a different way of writing romance novels compared to other historical romance authors...and I read many different people, such as Amanda Quick, Beatrice Small, Teresa Medeiros, etc, and honestly I read Thea Devine, not for romance or love, but for the sex scenes.  I won't lie, she does an excellent job portraying them.  What she tends to lack in her novels (and I expect it) is a progression of love and romance.The biggest reason that people have given this book a negative review is because they felt that is what this story lacked, though if you have read any of her earlier work she did not have a great deal of romance and love in them - read Tempted by Fire, love is never mentioned in that book at all!  I was a bit surprised that love and romance happened at the end.  This is just not her style of writing, she does not write her stories in the manner of most romance novels where romance is flourishing, this book is exactly as the title states, By Desire Bound, lust is what binds these two together, not love (though there is implication of love on the heroine's part).The only reason I give the book 4 stars is because I have to agree with other reviewers, the story line itself is lacking...and the plot bounced around quite a bit that I had to re-read certain sections to fully understand what was going on.
