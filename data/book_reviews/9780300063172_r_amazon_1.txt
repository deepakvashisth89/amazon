This book answers the myriad of questions surrounding adoption and dealing with this delicate issue with adopted children. Our daughter is only 3 at this time and adoption is not something she has started asking questions about just yet. But it's only a matter of time. The author clearly understands the child's psyche and what type of questions he/she may ask. That is what makes this book great in my opinion. It is a parent's guide to discussing adoption from the child's perspective. I wouldn't call this so much a guide, that would probably be mislabeling the book because the author does not intend to make it seem that way. It's a candid discussion about what questions to expect and how one can think about dealing with them.
