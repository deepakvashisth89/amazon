Casella and Berger have written an excellent book on mathematical statistics, perfect for the first year graduate student.  This book is different from other books (i.e. Lehmann) in that it has a thorough  introduction to basic probability theory, for those who might need the  review.  The theorems in this book are more thorough and complete than in  some other books (i.e. Bickel and Doksum).  Unfortunately, this book is  priced rather highly for those with a casual interest in statistics.  However, if price is not an issue, I would strongly recommend this book.
