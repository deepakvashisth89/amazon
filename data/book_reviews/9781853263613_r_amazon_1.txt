This is an excellent guide for anybody reading a picaresque novel or any kind of history such that of Henry Mayhew.  It's also an interesting study of language, particularly in terms of the etymology of many of the words and phrases.  Some of the words were used by some of the older men I worked with when I started work in 1950: for example, many used the Indian term "parnie" for rain.
