The Pearl, more than century after its appearance in Victorian London's uppercrust, is much more than raw porn - which it is in spades - it's also one of the best sources for those who want to know what life was really like and what anonymous authors wanted it to be really like that's still in print. Writers of more tame bodice-rippers have gone to The Pearl's deep well of language, calling-card customs, dress, food names, and class relations with great frequency and found surprising insights into the sexy side of human nature.
