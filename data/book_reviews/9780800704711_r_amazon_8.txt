I absolutely love this book!  I have read it at Christmas time almost every year for the last 40 years.  It is such a good reminder of what Christmas is truly about.  It may be a novel, but it is based on cultural research and it makes Mary and Joseph come to life.  The shame her pregnancy caused, because who would really understand and believe the true story.  It also brings to mind that Mary knew, right from the very beginning, how it would end.  The same Scriptures that spoke of His birth, also spoke of His death on the cross.  As she looked in that tiny baby's face, she knew.  And she and Joseph still raised him and loved him and taught him the things he needed to learn growing up.
