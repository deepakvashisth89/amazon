I'm a huge Janis Reams Hudson fan, but only her historicals.  I got through three chapters of this story, Price of Honor and then skimmed it from there.  It was not believable to me that the heroine, Rachel would have refused to listen to the hero's explanation, and all these years later there's been no correspondence nor has the real story come out.  Even when Grady is back in town and sees her, she keeps refusing to listen to the real story, and that just is not like a woman to do that.  It was forced conflict.
