Good writing.  Well developed main characters, though some with lesser roles actually have the more nuanced & complex characters.  Not quite literature, but edging close, and makes the history & politics of China of the period come alive (with what degree of accuracy, I can't say - seems researched, but surely considerable artistic license is taken).  Lots of plot twists, action, drama, romantic interest(s); formidable heroine, multiple "male leads."  Some windows onto the Boxer Rebellion, the Ching dynasty, the Chinese as differentiated from the Manchu, the Christian Missionary incursion into China - and all this without any tedious bogging-down of plot or story [quite a feat!]. A few bits of plot-driving action - like two young Caucasian women "storming" into The Forbidden City & coming out better than alright - require some suspension of disbelief, BUT are so well carried-out by the author, that they become credible in context.  Altogether a riveting and exciting yarn, presented by an author who can deliver the whole with smooth flow & excellent craftsmanship.Read moreRead less
