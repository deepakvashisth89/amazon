Very engrossing and so well written, this novel was so much more than just another Downton Abbey.  At first I didn't like the fact that the characters were so slimly sketched, but as the pages turned I found that the action more than adequately described their beings.  The author was a master at showing, rather than telling, especially in the psychic impact of events on the characters.  The heartbreak of WWI was not skimmed over, as many such novels have done, but was handled in a delicately realistic fashion.
