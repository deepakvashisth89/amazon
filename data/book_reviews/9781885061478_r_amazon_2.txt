This is a great book!  When you're birdwatching, sometimes you have only a very brief time to view the bird & identify it (before it flies away)- so you need a book that you can use quickly.  This book is organized by the bird's color - just flip to the section marked in that color, and then narrow down the search by the bird's size.  Each section starts with the smallest bird of that color and ends with the largest.  Birds with large color differences between male/females are listed separately in the appropriate color section.  If there are several birds that are often mistaken for one another, the book notes that as well - and tells you what features to look for to distinguish them.  It only lists birds found in Illinois, so there's no extraneous information to sort through.  I've been able to identify every bird at my feeder using this guide so far!
