"Hard-Boiled" is a really good collection of about three dozen stories of American Noir composed between the 1920s and 1990s.  I completely enjoyed it.  There are some stories I am familiar with and have read.  There are some stories that I have never heard of that I really liked.  As an example, the very first story is "The Scorched Face" by Dashiell Hammett from the 1920s.  I really liked reading that story.  I have read Dashiell Hammett but never even heard of that story.Similarly, the second story is "Round Trip" by W. R. Burnett.  It is from the 1920s and is set in Chicago and Toledo.  It is short and enjoyable.  I don't think I have read anything by Mister Burnett.Although I have been to college and graduate school, I never really studied literature beyond the required intro level courses.  I don't even know if one can study American Noir formally.  But hypothetically this is a book that one could construct a literary course around.  I try to read all sorts of literature.  But I am drawn to American Noir like a moth to a flame.  I need to budget my intake, almost like a alcohol or some bad habit.I completely enjoyed this book.  If one enjoys American Noir, This book is worth a try.  I study each author and look for other works.  A book like this really provides a fun experience for a student of this genre.
