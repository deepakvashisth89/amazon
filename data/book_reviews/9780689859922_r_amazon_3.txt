Having loved the first four Mates, Dates, and... books, I had to get this one when I saw it at the store.Lucy, Izzie, Nesta, and TJ have all told their stories, and it's back to Lucy's turn. Having recently broken up with Tony, her first boyfriend, she's feeling pretty down. She doesn't think she'll ever meet another guy like him as long as she lives. But that all changes when she goes to this new-agey clinic with her dad and Izzie. She meets Daniel, and he's sweet, cute, and totally funny - they really hit it off, and soon start going out. Izzie and Nesta both dislike Daniel, but they don't want to hurt Lucy's feelings, but soon Daniel starts being a real jerk - not letting her hang out with her friends much, etc.This is a good book, a bit better than Lucy's first story (MAtes, Dates, and Inflatible Bras).
