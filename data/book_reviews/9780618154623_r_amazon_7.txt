I really enjoyed this yarn about a wagon train heading west in the late 1840's.  Mountain Man Dick Summers who was a major character in "The Big Sky" was the pilot to take this particular wagon train of families and other folks to head west to Oregon to start a new life.  The train started in Independence Missouri to go to the west coast of Oregon.  This book is about how this group of people's thinking was and all the problems that came on the trail.  Problems with weather, Indians, the hardship of the journey and also with the people amongst themselves.  Good yarn and now going to read the 3rd book of this series "Fair Land, Fair Land."
