Entertaining and interesting characters and setting. I wanted more archaeology, but the tease was fun. Characters were stereotypes and spoke so similarly , it was hard to know who was speaking. However, events kept me turning the pages. Smart young woman uses too many cliches of women’s liberation but we had to be like that back in the sixties and seventies. Too much romance. I’d rather see her end with continuing her education and embark on a career in archaeology. We hear nothing about that when she ends with the conventional love match with Mr Handsome.
