The first 10 pages in this book are going to be something I reread when I need a giggle. Funny funny heroine! She's aypical in the extreme, even for this writer's heroines. She eats...loves to eat, and there is very little said about her figure. She's outspoken, even when she shouldn't be. The plot moved briskly. The villain was unlikely, and I was only lukewarm to the hero. Other little subplots sneaked in, heart-warming and not-so. I was bombarded with names and theories and odd bits of scholarly bits of information, all in ways that amused me, not put me to sleep.I've loved the books by Barbara Michaels, and was only vaguely aware of the huge number of books written by this author as Elizabeth Peters.
