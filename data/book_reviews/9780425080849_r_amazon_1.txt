John Trotti's "Phantom Over Vietnam" is a remarkable gem of narrative, detail and insight. This is actually two books melded seamlessly into one. Firstly, Trotti's detailed and highly readable account of U.S. Marine F-4 strike missions is both masterfully written and absolutely chilling to read. It does truly bring you into the mission with detail, narrative pacing and description that far exceed even Tom Clancy's best visualizations. Secondly, Trotti delves into a personal insight of the Vietnam conflict, reflecting on his role in the war and its effect on him. John Trotti writes with remarkable honesty and candor on a deeply personal level, and he reveals the conscience of a nation through his experiences. Honestly, someone in screenwriting needed to pick up this book. Tom Hanks would make a great John Trotti in a film adaptation of this book. Don't miss this seldom mentioned gem of Vietnam War and aviation history.
