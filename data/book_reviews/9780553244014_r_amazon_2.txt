This book does a wonderful job not only of telling the story of the Gilbreth family, but also of capturing the spirit of pre-WW1 America. It radiates optimism that progress is inevitable and that the world is a perfectible place. It may seem helplessly naive to us today, there is a certain wisdom to their unbounded optimism that carried them over huge obstacles and created many of the good things we enjoy today. I read this book aloud to my children, and they enjoyed it as much as I do.
