This wondrous story is a must read for all!  First, as with any good fairy tale, there is magic and delightfully magical characters that live in a parallel universe to our modern one.  The seven sister's are accidentally involved in an active vendetta between two waring fairy groups: the "sang" and the "bee".  Six of the seven sisters are taken captive by these groups into the mythic universe.  With the help of a wise neighbor, a tree man, and a magical cat, the seventh sister is charged with the burden of saving the day.  I was most impressed by the lessons of this tale plus the wonderful art by Charles Vess.
