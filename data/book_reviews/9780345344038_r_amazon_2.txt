This is a depressing book although well-written and well-translated that can be difficult to read at times because of the cruelty and inhumanity it describes suffered by mostly prisoners of conscience, often for merely expressing a political opinion and sometimes for no real reason at all.  The Cuban regime was and is heartless and unrepentant in its pursuit of its "socialist paradise" which is literally a Hell on Earth for far too many who are trapped there.Cuba should at all its ports of entry have that slogan emblazoned on the doorway arches, "Abandon All Hope Ye Who Enter Here" - although foreigners may be exempt the poor Cuban people are not, and yet they still have hope.  For many of them that hope was in vain.  Although I knew most of the truth about Cuba before reading this book, this opened my eyes further and I'm not sure I want my eyes opened so painfully, it's so much more pleasant to think of how wonderful Cuba is as they and their apologists always tell us - but the truth is necessary even when it hurts, for only the truth can triumph against the evil that the Cuban government has done.  ¡Viva Cuba libre!Read moreRead less
