The title says it all pretty much. Two stars for providing a nice (even if not in depth that I would have liked) review of how an average two year old develops psychologically and where the troubles come from.What I find rather bad is that (it seems to me that) the first advice for pretty much any problem they describe is to give your child away into a preschool. That is actually one of the worst things a parent can do in this situation. A child of any age, but a child in a crisis all the more, needs firstmost a guidance of a trusted adult. Depositing him or her somewhere else is only going to worsen the relationship and, therefore, the problems.The other thing that ticks me off, is that the "techniques" offered consist mostly of tricks and manipulations. But that is to whom how I guess.There was also one of the "real stories" about a little boy who keeps getting out of his bed in the evening and coming back to his parents, so the parents ask: what should we do? A reasonable person would think about the situation: is he not sleepy yet? does he maybe not get enough interaction with the parents during the day? And what does the book suggests? Tie the doorknob to something, so that he can't get out of his room. Unbelievable.Yet another, maybe a minor, reason to complain is all this incessant talking about "Mother" who has to take care not only of a child, but also to make sure that the "Father" is not troubled too much.
