Back in the day, which shall remain unspecified as it is impossible for it to have been as long ago as reality suggests, my rural community was served twice monthly by the County Bookmobile. Among the treasured friends I became acquainted with during the summer of my eighth year was Robert Heinlein. I have no idea why I picked Rocketship Galileo off the rack in the back of the steamy hot step-truck which served as the locomotion for the Bookmobile; I'd never read science fiction before and maybe didn't even know what it was. What it turned out to be was a gateway to a different world populated by writers as diverse as Heinlein and Asimov, Robert Lewis Stevenson, Jules Verne, Gene Roddenberry, Jerry Pournelle, Ray Bradbury, Larry Nevin, John Ringo, Christopher Nuttall, and David Weber amid a constellation of other writers. Some were great, some were good, some not quite so good; regardless they had one thing in common; a view of the human condition through a prism of different circumstances.I bought the book for nostalgia.
