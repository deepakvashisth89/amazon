This is the third book I've read by Linda Greenlaw, and her material is way out of my normal range of non-fiction!  But her writing is so skillful that she grabbed my attention immediately and I was hooked (no pun intended!).I happened into "The Lobster Chronicles" while I was in Maine, and before I even finished it, I knew I wanted to read "The Angry Ocean".  From then on I searched Amazon for anything she's written.  Linda truly is just a good, old-fashioned storyteller who paints the picture, introduces you to the people so they become family or neighbors and then settles you in for a story you can't put down.  Even non-reading members of my family are reading her books which doesn't happen often.I am anxious to begin her fiction endeavors.  I already have both of them on my nightstand.
