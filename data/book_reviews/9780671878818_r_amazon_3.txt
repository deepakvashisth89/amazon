Drake is a master chef of story telling, and this is the raw origin of a great series.  The beginning drags a bit as Drake takes the time to introduce the reader to his rich world of people, politics and chivalry.In this world, we find the far future mirrors the far past of England or other countries during the great sea wars between England and France.  Leary, could just as easily be in Master and Commander as in this space setting. Only the technology differs.There are advanced weapons, but weapons are just as inaccurate as in any era. Only Leary can be said to have a purity of heart if you don't mind his lusty ways, but his smile and luck help him not as a deus ex machina but as a tuxedo helps James Bond.I think people will become as endeared by the characters with their flaws and peculiarities as much as how well they work well together.After reading this, I strongly suggest reading the next book in the aerie to see how well the characters work together to create the legend.I know I will reread them.Read moreRead less
