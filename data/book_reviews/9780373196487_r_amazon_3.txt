"Her Secret Children" is the first book that I read by Judith McWillians and the first Silhouette Romance that I have read in a very long while. In this book American Vicky Sutton learns that her frozen eggs were given to an English couple, the result is twins. Wanting to see her children, Vicky goes to England were she meets James Thayer, thier father.While I liked the plot I have admitt I wished that the story was given more time to develop.
