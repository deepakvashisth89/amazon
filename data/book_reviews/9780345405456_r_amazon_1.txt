Do you want a root and affix dictionary for quick reference? How about a clear pronunciation and spelling guide? It's all here in Random House Webster's Power Vocabulary Builder.This vocabulary builder is for the student at an intermediate to upper level (from high school to graduate school). It highlights interesting words and rarely reveals professional argot. This editorial choice has made it a very readable vocabulary builder. Its quality sets it above, say the straight forward builder from Word Smart, which is really nothing more than a series of word lists. In this way, Random House Webster's is self-contained, although having a dictionary with etymological reference (e.g.Webster's New World College Dictionary, Fourth Edition (Book with CD-ROM)) next to you is always recommended. Another aspect I particularly find useful and what makes this book more desirable than the other builders are the three separate dictionaries for prefix, suffix, and root.The detailed lesson on increasing your spelling and pronunciation power was encouraging and, as a list is provided, great for cross-referencing and general referencing.In fact, with word histories, exercises, and other interesting tidbits, I'd say this vocabulary builder which doubles as a mini-reference book is well worth the price.Read moreRead less
