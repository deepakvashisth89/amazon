A reasonably well-written, gentle romance which includes some police procedural issues and mystery elements.  The characters are well drawn and likeable.  The romance proceeds in a somewhat reasonable fashion, though it is eminently predictable in every way.  The so-called humor is supplied by a precocious child and is also very gently done.  All in all, a typical book about how a young mother finds a new life and love.  This isn't a real "page turner" and you definitely will be able to put it down to go to bed, but it is worth reading.Read moreRead less
