I absolutely love this book.  As a health counselor, I own and cook from many books on whole grains, but it is THIS one that I give to all of my clients as part of their health counseling program.Ms. Wood's writing is rich and nostalgic, stirring up a deeper love of food and the earth in everyone who turns the pages.  I often describe this book to my clients as perhaps the turning point to understanding and appreciating what they observe others  who lead a healthy & rich life talking about when we describe our love of food.The recipes are delicious, creative, and culturally diverse.
