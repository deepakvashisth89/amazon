After watching Capote vs The Swans, I read Answered Prayers to understand why the women were so upset. His writing is mild compared to the reaction it generated. I understand Babe Paley's feelings of humiliation and betrayal, but the description of her husband cavorting with Happy Rockefeller isn't shocking anymore.
