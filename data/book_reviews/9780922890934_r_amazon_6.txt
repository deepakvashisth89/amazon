One of Ed McBain's early 87th precinct stories. Amazon offered the entire series on a deal around December 2013 and I passed on all the early ones and only got a couple of the new ones. When I read them I remembered how well these stories were written and how timeless they really are. Thankfully last November they offered many of them again and I bought what I could at the time.
