Few people excel at character sketches the was Rosamunde Pilcher did. First, her characters are so purely British to the core. And then there is Cornwall, usually a secondary star of the story. I can't say Emma was my favorite Pilcher character. Because of her life-long unconventional relationship with her renowned artist father, she comes across as flighty and needy. Ben, her father, seems not only needy himself in a different sense, but he is above all self-centered and a vagabond, flitting about the world without a care for his daughter.
