Many moons ago, I saw the film ‘Stand by Me’—based on Stephen King’s novella, “The Body.” This is my first time reading it. The novella is well-written and King kept me engaged and emotionally invested.‘The Revenge of Lard Ass Hogan’ reminded me of a party that took place in my neighborhood when I was growing up. All the teenagers were at a house party. And, naturally, several of the kids were making out. As a couple were kissing, the girl got sick and puked into the boy’s mouth.
