I purchased the Acupuncture Without Needles book and use it about once per week for reference.  The diagrams are very easy to follow to find the acupressure points.  The procedure graphs are simple to understand.  The techniques described in the book are typical of most accupressure books but the language used is geared to non professionals.  The material is organized according to body part which makes finding a procedure very simple.
