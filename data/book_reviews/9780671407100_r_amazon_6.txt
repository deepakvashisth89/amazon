The book is a magical realist retelling of the Gospel of Christ. The book begins with Jesus, a cross-maker despised by his fellow Jews for making the execution instrument, haunted by horrific visions and headaches and with his father (Joseph) suffering from a disabling illness. Years earlier, Jesus had been set to propose to his cousin, Mary Magdalene, but when he suffered an epileptic-style fit the marriage was called off and she became a prostitute. Eventually, Jesus decides to pursue the path of a holy man, and he gradually has it revealed to himself that he is the messiah. When he is crucified, he  has his last temptation, whereby he becomes married to Mary and Martha (not Mary Magdalene, who is stoned to death) and lives a happy life filled with children. However, he eventually realizes this temptation came from the devil, and he returns to the cross to die for humanity.The story is somewhat interesting and does a good job touching on the complexities of faith.
