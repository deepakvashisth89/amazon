When I first read this I didn’t know much about novels, and sure didn’t know that John Irving wrote some amazing stories. I’ve always read biographies, non-fiction, history. The book kept my interest, the characters were quirky. Then I came to the whole reason the story was leading up to and IT BLEW MY MIND. Maybe some of you will see it coming a mile away. Some of you are more sophisticated readers than I am. I never studied American Literature. But wow, I really respected John Irving. Then I read a bunch of his other stuff. Then found a list of best American novels and read a bunch of those. It’s a whole world of good stuff out there that’s all made up. I still really like history and biographies but man, this is great. I bought this as a gift for a friend. He loved it and said there was a movie with a similar premise, and there is.
