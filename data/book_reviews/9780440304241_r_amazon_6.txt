William Manchester is an excellent writer & this biography is no exception.  Most recent biographies of MacArthur have been extremely critical - an unfair degree.  Thus, even if Manchester's biography is a little too favorable to MacArthur at times, it helps balance out all of the overly negative ones.  As Manchester's book shows, MacArthur was a very strong, intelligent, egotistical man with both great virtues & great flaws - hence the title "American Caesar."  Even though it was written decades ago, I still believe this should be the "go-to" biography to read of MacArthur, or, at least, the first one before reading one of the more recent negative ones.Read moreRead less
