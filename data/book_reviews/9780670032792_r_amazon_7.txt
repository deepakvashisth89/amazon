I read this book on the recommedation of a friend, and it didn't leave my hands from the moment I picked it up until I finished it.  As an aficionado of both the 1920s, the era in which the novel is set, and mystery novels/spooky settings, I was in heaven. Everything in the book is historically accurate, down to the most minute details.  As an F. Scott Fitzgerald scholar, I also enjoyed Mr. Gangemi's occasional oblique references to Fitzgerald's work. Inamorata is a real page-turner, and all-in-all an excellent novel.  I'm looking forward to reading more from the talented Mr. Gangemi.Read moreRead less
