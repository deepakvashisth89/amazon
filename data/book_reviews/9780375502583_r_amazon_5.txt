This collection may be the best I've read about the American West. Not the romantic and sappy stuff that might turn up in a Nashville song:  it's the down-and-out, quirky, off-beat hard-luck folks that Billman captures.  And the book is really funny, Billman is as witty as they come. He's playful with language, a hoot. I loved his "Cold Vanish" nonfiction, but this makes me wonder: Where is Billman's "Great American Novel"?  Maybe the same wacky set-up as the stories invest in?
