I enjoy novels by Susan Elizabeth Phillips and found Honey Moon lived up to my expectations.  The author has a knack for creating characters with interesting quirks and passions,  In this story, some of the characters had much rougher edges than we are accustomed to in Susan Elizabeth Phillips' lighter-hearted stories.  I felt she did a good job of creating an ambiance where characters could play more nuanced roles.The ending of the novel may have been a little predictable; however, the story overall kept me engaged. :-)Read moreRead less
