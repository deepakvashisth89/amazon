Family, friends, love, loyalty, passion and forgiveness. All essential ingredients for a satisfying romance, and all of these can be found in Pat Cody's delightful new book, "His Wicked Will." Set in Regency England, Cody takes her readers on an enchanting trip back in time with a tale that will touch your heart and tickle your fancy.Elizabeth Merriman works hard to live a quiet life, support her beloved Aunt Deliah, and contribute to society by training poor young women to become competent maids. But her serene life is turned upside down by a scandal from her past. Fearful of her secret's exposure to polite society, Elizabeth's stress is enhanced by the involvement of the wickedly handsome Lord Chandler.Chan finds himself pursuing the illusive Miss Merriman initially as a favor to his childhood friend. But he soon finds himself fascinated by the spirited beauty and seeks a way to help her and her family as well as his chum. As Chan and Elizabeth's friendship advances, their attraction to each other is undeniable. Will they be able to overcome the seemingly insurmountable odds to find the love of their lives?Pat Cody's delightful sense of humor and deep regard for romance can be found sprinkled generously throughout this charming book. Her love scenes are sensual, but sweet; her hero kind and gentle; and her heroine brave but stubborn. "His Wicked Will" is sure to have fans of Regency historical romance begging for more of Cody's wonderful romances.Sharon Galligar Chance, Wichita Falls Times Record NewsRead moreRead less
