In less than a hundred pages, readable in a day, the author of the important historical work 'Ashes in the Wind', a revealing account of the Nazi persecution of Jews in the Netherlands, uses historical fiction to picture the corrupting effect of the persecution on the victims. The device of fiction is used to enable this to be done concisely and in an important aspect more faithfully than can be done with raw historical facts because it is otherwise so difficult to give an impression of the emotional atmosphere which was very much a part of the reality. This is an English translation from Dutch and judging it in its own right it seems to me that the translator has done a superb job. This novelette has also been translated into other languages and seems to have a good reputation among academics in this area.
