My wife grew up in communistic Poland and though she's often shared her stories about the queues and shortages and general absurdities of it all, this book really helped me understand it on a different level. Swick's perspective as an American there during the '80s connected with me and added a lot of depth to events and experiences my wife has often spoken about.An added bonus was the author's insight on Polish language, customs and traditions. There are many things that I knew of or even experienced first-hand but had often taken for granted - his detailed description of the pilgrimage to Chestohowa is a perfect example. I knew about it, and even saw folks doing it, but Swick puts you in the middle of it and really brings it to life.Having read this, I now possess an ever greater understanding of my wife's youth and the things that shaped her as a person.
