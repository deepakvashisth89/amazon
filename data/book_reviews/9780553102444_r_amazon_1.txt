Thanks once again to AMAZON recommendations for pushing me to KEEPER.  It is in the mystery/detective/thriller genre.  Greg Rucka does a very nice job setting up this new character Atticus Kodiak, now there is a name to remember, and another series of books to read.  KEEPER is book one of several Kodiak stories.  Kodiak is a body guard, not a detective, but think of the book in that detective vain.  The story is exciting and moves quickly and the characters are crisp and keep you interest.  Bridgett Logan, a detective love interest, Natalie Trent, another bodyguard, Scott Fowler, a Federal agent not in a book to be hard to deal with, refreshing.  Looking forward to FINDER, next in the series.  HIGHLY RECOMMENDED, in the genre.Read moreRead less
