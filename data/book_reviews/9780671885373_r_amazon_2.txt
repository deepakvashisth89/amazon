I believe I was born 100 years too late. I believe in a past life that I lived in the Western United States. I have been to Arizona several times in my life and every time I have been to Arizona I get this feeling of Deja Vu in that I had been there before. In February of 2013 I made my most recent trip to Arizona and this time I traveled to Tombstone. It was an amazing experience is all I can say. To walk the same areas that Wyatt Earp, his brothers and Doc Holiday walked was amazing. I can't wait to go back.This book by Stuart Lake was an awesome eye opening account into the life of Wyatt in Wichita, Dodge City and Tombstone. I never knew that throughout his whole life as an lawman did Wyatt never suffer any kind of wound from all his shootouts with cattlemen cowboys or outright outlaws. I truly believe that the hand of providence was on his side at all times.Being that Stuart Lake was the only person to be given the first hand account of his life by Wyatt Earp in the late 1920's makes this book a true account of what actually happened during Wyatt's life as a lawman.
