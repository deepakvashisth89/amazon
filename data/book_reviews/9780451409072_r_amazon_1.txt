Sante Kimes broke new ground as a murderous grifter in the 1990s. Jailed for enslaving maids, suspected of at least five murders, chased for insurance frauds and credit card scams...was there nothing this woman wouldn't do in pursuit of financial gain? And all with the help of her son  Kenny. This book is one of four or five about Mrs Kimes and her life of crime. Like all the others, it unearths previously unknown detail. Together with Kent Walker's excellent Son of a Grifter and John Marquis's Evil and Son, it makes for great reading about one of the most compelling murder cases of modern times.Read moreRead less
