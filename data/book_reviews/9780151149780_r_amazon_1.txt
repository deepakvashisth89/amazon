Outstanding! This is an alternative history novel, and it's a brilliant one! In 1945, the atomic bomb is damaged, forcing President Truman to order the enormous and bloody, terrible, invasion of the Japanese home islands in the plan known as OPERATION OLYMPIC CORONET. Historians estimate that this invasion might have cost a million US military casualties, and twenty times, or more than that, in Japanese lives.Author Coppel, a former Air Force officer and pilot, did his research into the war plans of both sides, although he does not overdo the book with too many tactical descriptions of battle. Instead, Coppel concentrates on characterizing and describing the backgrounds, experiences, and attitudes of those fighting. He does that beautifully, not covering the usual difficult decisions of generals, but concentrating on the perceptions of individual officers and enlisted men. He describes the feelings of infantrymen, kamikaze pilots, naval officers and ratings, Army Air Force fliers, lower level commanders, and a few civilians. His characterizations are truly excellent.
