It's just an inexpensive reference and it is loaded to the brim with content suitable for all but those who would be reading a lot of historical books and documents. Should anyone require more there are always public domain PDF downloads of older editions. For me, this is actually a perfect old-school summary of "everything under the sun" so to speak. For the price I paid it was a stupendously great value and it's not tiny or short on information at all.
