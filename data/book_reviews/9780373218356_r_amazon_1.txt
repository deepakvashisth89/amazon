I've read many of Merline Lovelace's books, but "In Love and War: A Military Affair" is absolutely "the" most moving one she's written.  In her preamble she describes how her (father, two sisters, brother and husband all served in uniform, as she did for twenty-three years.The most  poignant portion of this book relates to the profound respect given to a fallen pilot who was shot down many decades ago.  In the midst of a fire barrage as she retrieved the pilot's remains, even though it was only bone fragment, she stood and rendered a crisp salute that brought tears to my eyes.  Here she was in the middle of a battle scene stopping and taking time to afford the utmost respect for a fallen comrade.  As the remains are placed in a coffin and transported in a C-130 for his final resting place, the respect described in Merline's book - I can't describe the very great pride I felt that I'm an American and living in a civilized country as ours.The heroin in this book, Tess, while an outstanding military copy is a real trip and her romance with the photojournalist Petey Quinn is amazing.This is a very  moving read, I would bet good money, and I'm not a betting person, that when you read this story, you will be moved, you will feel proud to be an American but most of all you will have a renewed respect for all those brave men and women who lost their lives to secure our freedom - who never came home - were never afforded the privilege of being buried in the country for which they gave their all.
