I bought this book years ago, when I first got into Buddhism and I didn't get around to reading it until recently. I don't normally like to give up on books but I am with this one. I feel like it goes into the history of Buddhism too deeply and it is not nearly enough about how to put it into practice in the modern word. Also I have to agree with another comment I read which said their wasn't any "zen-ful tips" as the cover claims. If you only want to know about the history of Buddhism than this book is perfect, if you want a book to help with modern day Buddhism, it is not the right book for you. Also as I said before, I have an older copy so this may not be true of the newer editions but it would have been 100 times more helpful if the book had a break down of how to pronounce some of the more difficult Buddhist words in it.
