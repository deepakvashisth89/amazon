This might actually be my favorite Ngaio Marsh book, as much as I have nostalgia for earlier books of the series like "A Man Lay Dead", "Artists in Crime," and "Death in a White Tie" Beautiful storytelling, some of the best scene-setting I've ever read, and a thrilling/complex murder mystery that involved a lot of people without being too out-of-the-blue as to who the murderer was. Also I found the characters to be the most likable/least annoying of the Marsh books before this one. I wish there were more books like this out there.Read moreRead less
