Mr. Stowers is a wonderful storyteller and researcher. I am an avid true crime reader.  He has written one of the best true crime books I have read in a very long time. I will buy more of his true crime books.One little note about the persons who murdered the three young people featured in this book:  there seems to be some controversy over the guilt of these three men.  You will find when you read this book that there is no doubt in your mind that they committed the crime and that they were duly punished.  However, there is a forth person who was equally guilty.. he is the one person who "hired" the killer to do the crime.  He was tried and found guilty, but then was tried a second time and was exonerated.  That's ridiculous but that's what happened.
