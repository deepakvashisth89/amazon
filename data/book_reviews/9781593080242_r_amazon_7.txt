I certainly enjoyed the book. I suppose the book continually left me wondering why was Antonia so important and why was it so urgent to write down what was known about her. I expected something different at the end, something more concrete because of this idea I had.Although, in saying that at the conclusion there was a sense of mystery that allowed the reader to make their own decisions. I would have like to have know more, but obviously that was never intended. Antonia was very well drawn and yes you got a wonderful feeling and image of her and her life.I suppose i am still trying to figure out why. I will revisit this review another time and hopefully with more clarity.The book for its small size did take me longer to get through than I would have imagined.
