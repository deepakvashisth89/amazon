Travels with a donkey is a well written beautiful story of an actual journey taken by RLS in France to recover from a broken romance.  Mosestine, the donkey, started off slowly and with poor equipment which was improved by and there was some doubt that they were ever to reach their destination.  Also included is the kindness of strangers, met along the way.  I enjoyed reading this little book.  As it was the Kindle Edition I felt that it may not be the whole story but even so, enough to give light reading.
