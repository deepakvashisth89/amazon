You Might Avoid Some Unnecessary Disappointment.A Tale of Two Cities Was Written By English Author Charles Dickens (1812-1870.  Dickens Received Most of the Background on the French Revolution From his Friend Thomas Carlyle.  This is a Long Book, But Because it is So Gripping, it Does Hold Your Attention For Long Periods of Time.  You'll Find Yourself Not Wanting To Put It Down.  The Characters and Lives are All Intertwined and it Adds a Depth and Richness to the Book.  This is a Historical Fiction that Spans nearly 20 Years From the Time Preceding and During the French Revolution.
