A good account of the House of Windsor from Queen Victoria to the present.  At least until 1995 that is.  A lot has happened since the book was published. However, I found the way the author wrote about things I already knew from my reading about the British Royal Family  refreshing.  I particularly enjoyed the chapter on Edward VIII and his abdication.  The author doesn't seem to be very optimist about the future of the monarchy.
