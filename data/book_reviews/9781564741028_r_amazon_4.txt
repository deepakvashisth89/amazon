Charles Kelly's book The Great Limbaugh Con should be required reading in political classes and for anybody who wants to understand the background noise machine that influences minds and politics in America in the 20th and 21st centuries. Other than Fox News, there is no other institution in America higher than Limbaugh for reinforcing thought control and indoctrinating the masses. Many working class people worship this Republican icon. Limbaugh, rich and hypocritical, is the prime voice of the Republican Party and a reminder to all that a world like 1984 is just around the corner. Limbaugh doesn't represent the interests of the working class. He is a shill and mouthpiece for the one percent - the ultra-rich who live lives insulated from the worries of the middle class and through the voice of Limbaugh, persuade them to toe the plutocratic line. This book, although it could have been stronger and a much more penetrating expose, is a wake-up call that plutocracy and oligarchy are not good for ordinary Americans.
