A lovely story of coming to know yourself and the power of faith. As Virginia struggles to be accepted by her peers I was encouraged with her honest feelings of jealousy, resentment, anger, etc. She displayed the real emotions that even the best of humanity struggle with regardless of age. Manipulated by her friend, Jenny, she makes some regretful choices, but eventually comes to realize Jenny's drive for attention and control and its harmful effects on her own sense of self, as well as the damage it reeks in the lives of those needy and immature enough to follow her lead.
