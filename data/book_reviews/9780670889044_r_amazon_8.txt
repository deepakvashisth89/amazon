This might be a decent read for a middle school aged child. It's brief (125 pages), and it is completely lacking of any historical detail. This book serves as a simple introduction of Wilson as an American President. How one can write a legitimate biography of President Wilson and not include one mention of the Espionage Acts (commonly referred to as Alien and Sedition Acts)or the German-American Internment is beyond me. There was a brief mention of the Federal Reserve Act and no mention of the 4 Amendments that were passed during his presidency, namely the 16th Amendment which allowed the Progressive Income Tax. I learned more about Henry Cabot Lodge, which I knew little about; and about "Colonel" Edward House, which I already know far too much.
