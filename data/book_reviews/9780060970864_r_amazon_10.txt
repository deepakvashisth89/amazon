Highly enjoyable read. Here is a calm yet disciplined author who is managing every aspect of her Bee keeping business and her country home, including felling a tree, if necessary. She is not just a keen observer of nature, she delves deeper to know and understand more about the behaviour of animals and insects in her premise. Her description of nature is such that I would love to join her for a stay at her home and help her in her daily activities just to get a sense of the joy she experiences daily [avoiding the bee stings of-course].  I have not finished the book yet - but, I plan to complete it when I go on a short vacation just to get into that happy mood, again !Read moreRead less
