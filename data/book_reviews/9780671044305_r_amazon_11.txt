The action takes place on the east coast of Yorkshire. As usual Jury and Wiggins are sent from London a couple of days after the murder has taken place, whilst Plant has invited himself as house guest of a family friend to be on hand as Jury's assistant. This book is a pleasure to read as the frienship between Jury and Plant develops and the crime element is satisfactorially handled with plenty of suspects and red herrings.
