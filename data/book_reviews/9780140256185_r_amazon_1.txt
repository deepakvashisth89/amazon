I really enjoyed this book, its strong characters and Eidson's wonderful descriptions.  His story development is never simple, never as expected, and has turns the ordinary person would likely not imagine.  Unlike another reviewer, I place this book up there with St. Agnes' Stand, which was once being considered for movie development.  I actually think All God's Children would be a great movie, in the right hands.(SOULS OF ANGELS was probably my least favorite, and still, I did enjoy it some for those unique Eidson touches.)  All God's Children brought to light many of the lies and hatred fomented by some, against those of a different race, color, or creed--things that have always existed across the globe.  I just marvelthat I could see no way out for the main characters at the end--yet Eidson managed to create one.
