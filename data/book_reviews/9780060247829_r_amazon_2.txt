My four year old granddaughter really likes this story and often asks for it.  So, for me, it passes the "kid" test.  I like the book for a number of reasons.  Without being "preachy" or didactic, it tells a story where characters are compassionate, understanding, cooperative, flexible and "real."  I also like the use of Spanish words in the text.  My granddaughter could readily grasp that people from different cultures speak a language that is not English and could incorporate into her vocabulary a few non-English words.  The story is compelling and yet simple.  and, I like the illustrations... sweet.
