Somehow, as a high school student I missed having to read Pearl S. Buck's book THE GOOD EARTH.  Possibly because we moved in the middle of the year.  Therefore, I never read one of her novels until  I read PAVILION OF WOMAN, a Previous deal of the day.  I loved it!  When IMPERIAL WOMAN became a deal of the day I was quick to purchase it.  This is an amazing book as well. It was so interesting to follow the life of a young woman who became probably the greatest Empresses China ever had.  Buck uses words to to paint images of the extravagance of the imperial court, and the beauty of the countryside. In my mind I could feel the elegance of the silken robes or the smoothness of the gems that adorned them. I could cringe with those who displeased her.  I truly felt transported to that place and time and place. I look forward to reading more books by Buck in the future.Read moreRead less
