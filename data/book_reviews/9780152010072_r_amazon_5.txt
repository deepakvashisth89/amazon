I read this book to my children when they were young! It's beautifully written (of course!) by the amazing Jane Yolen, and Kathryn Brown's illustrations are soft and gentle and draw you into the underground home of the mole sisters. This book will teach children not to believe the naysayers of the world and will show them that walking their own path is worth it every time. There are so many layers to this book! It's not just a sweet, lyrical romp underground and up above. It's a way to show children that there are beautiful things in the world that others will discourage them from pursuing and to encourage them to find their own truth! This is such an important lesson to teach children. They need to know that distracted grownups are not always right or tuned in. Sometimes we are the only ones who feel the breeze on our faces and see the light up above. Ack! SOOO many layers in this book that will help our children understand the world and their place in it. I am, of course, enthralled. And my grandchildren are now falling in love with Eeny, just as their mama did when she was a little girl. I am so excited to see this book back in print, and I hear there may soon be a sequel! I cannot wait to read that one, too!! If I could, I would give this book way more than five stars.
