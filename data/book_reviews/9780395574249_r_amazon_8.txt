This was actually quite a fun read for someone with some knowledge of economics, and even more fun if that someone is familiar with the politics of higher education, especially at the top schools and especially among the faculty.  Some of it is a stretch, mainly the suggestion that analysis by the "shorts" is new, but it is, after all, a work of fiction and that is still fun.  (It was especially funny to see the Harvard elite hope they didn't have to deal with Donald Trump in a section dealing with South Africa investments.  Remember, this book was first published in 1990.  So it shows liberal distaste for Trump is not only deep, but longstanding.)
