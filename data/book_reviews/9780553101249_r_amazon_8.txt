I havent finished it yet, about halfway through but i can still write a great review on it! Dinosaurs almost seem made up sometimes as we often don't know how they acted/behaved, so its like an imaginary animal to us! However Robert Bakker has really helped me imagine what a Utahraptor and other dinos could have been like! They are just like our modern animals, feelings and all. While this may seem obvious, its still very cool to read from the perspective of a Raptor who shows her feelings and why she acts certain ways! Its such an interesting read, i recommend it for middle/high schoolers and adults!Read moreRead less
