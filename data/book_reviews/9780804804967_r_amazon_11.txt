Kern was a Dutch scholar of the 19th century. Through his translation we have a translation from a Sanskrit text of the Lotus Sutra. My feeling is that only after having read the translations from the Chinese text would anyone gain from this translation. Watson and Reeves's translations done in the last twenty years are as clear as they could be. The earliest Sanskrit texts of the first centuries of the C.E. are lost; the Chinese text was probably based on an earlier text than Kern translated.
