The mystery is typical Dick Francis with a nice bit of racing in it, but the rest was lacking. The lengthy periods of imprisonment and abuse were excessive. We got the idea without hammering us over the head with it at length. Putting some periods of investigation instead of his figuring it out while sitting in the dark would have improved the novel. And the love interest was simply lame. Francis kept telling the reader that she was bright, but every word out of her mouth showed otherwise.
