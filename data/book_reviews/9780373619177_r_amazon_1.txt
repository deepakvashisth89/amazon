A real page-turner as Stony Man deploys its teams who are after a madman who is making and selling world-wide a cheap series of firearms. Able Team takes on wild bikers, the KKK and various killers as Bolan plows through bad guys in Japan and Phoenix Force mixes it up with hired killers and terrorists. Decent plot and fast action as it expected from the publisher. Lots of good one liners and Carl Lyons goes nuts and stars in this Stony Man thriller that piles up the baddies in a relentless search to stop the mad former military guy behind the fiendish plot to arm the world's thugs with cheap but deadly firearms.
