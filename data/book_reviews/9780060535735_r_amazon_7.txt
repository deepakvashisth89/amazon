I'm starting to read Lisa Scottoline novels like they are candy, one right after the other. I was really excited about this book for the plot it promised and especially because of that classic first line. However I found this as one of the books that was harder to get through. Despite the many, many twists Scottoline came up with this book was slow and even bordered on far fetched after a time. Everyone was just tossing theories around on a hunch and then the actual truth of whodunit was random for me. Yes the story is a page turner if you suspend your belief for a time but as far as great stories go, this isn't one of her best.Read moreRead less
