I really would like a good Hopi or Navajo mystery, but since Hillerman, such books are very rare. Cole has a good eye for some local color and knowledge of the Hopi area, but I find his characters really rather unbelieveable--and much, much too violent. Those who like violence as a major factor in a story might like him. But for me, I won't read another one of his books.
