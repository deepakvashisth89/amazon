Yes, I am a native of Idaho, of southern Idaho that is, and so this vision of northern Idaho was a true treat for me.  I loved every minute of reading this book.  It is a compilation of essays about the author's years getting to know and understand people and lifestyles.  That, in itself is admirable.  However, he was earning his doctorate in English while he was living there, so his writing style is outstanding!  He brings in thoughts from eminent authors that add to the depth of his stories, and he writes writes the English language like I could only dream of doing.  His description of thoughts and scenery are spectacular; they took my breath away.  From "All Sides", this book is a true treasure and will remain on my list of "favorites" forever.Read moreRead less
