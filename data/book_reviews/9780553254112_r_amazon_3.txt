I like the 4 books by Margery Allingham that I have read.  One thing I particularly like, she doesn't bore you with long descriptions, just enough for your imagination to see the person or scene, not pages.  I love her characters, often a bit different but likeable, the mysteries keep me guessing and are clever.  There is some violence in that at least one person dies, but not graphic violence for it's own sake.  I'm looking forward to whichever I read next, I know it will be good.  I recommend this and all her books.Read moreRead less
