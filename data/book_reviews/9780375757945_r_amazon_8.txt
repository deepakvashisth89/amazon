I’m a huge fan of classic literature.  So I’m always a little surprised when I pick up a book that turns out to be bland.  I remember reading the Classics Illustrated comic book of Two Years Before the Mast when I was growing up.  I have since discovered that I prefer the comic book since it highlighted the adventurous moments in the book and left out the gazillions of sailing details.  I can see why the book was a huge success in its time (1830’s) since it gave a vivid picture of life at sea to those who could never experience it.
