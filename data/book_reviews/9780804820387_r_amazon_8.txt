Remembering kanji, or Chinese characters in the Japanese writing system, is not easy.  They are very intricate and trying to remember the complex lines and strokes seems astronomical. This book has characters from first to sixth grades, as well as those for general use.  It gives the character, the Japanese definition, and examples in words and phrases.  Each character has a mnemonic.  This book is like a dictionary because it has the meaning and the history of the characters.
