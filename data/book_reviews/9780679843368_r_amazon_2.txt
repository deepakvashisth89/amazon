I first read this book while in the 5th grade and it has stuck with me all these years. I am now 33 years old and still remember the stories and still remember the author. There are not many books I can say that about from more than 20 years ago. I felt as though this book related to me and the stories were ones that touched me. I constantly told my son this was a book he should read and have no clue why I am just purchasing it now. This is one of those books everyone should have in their collection. While I have gone digital with all my other books this was one I wanted to sit on my bookshelf for my kids to read and future generations of my family to read.
