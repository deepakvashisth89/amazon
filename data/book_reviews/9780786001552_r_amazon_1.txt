A woman who can handle fire and turn any energy into flames  and her mentor, a huge furry human, are one of the only survivors of their home world. On a mission to find others of their kind, they end up as prisoners on a dangerous planet and must do whatever it takes, even kill to survive.Ms. Maxwell has written only three of this series, which could go on and on.
