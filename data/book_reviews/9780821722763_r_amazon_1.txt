Rick Hautala doesn't write, he conjures. His mastery of fiction helps him create characters and worlds so real, you don't read a Hautala novel, you live it.Little Brothers is no different. Kip Howard lost his mother a few years ago. He's still struggling with the grief but little does he know that whatever took her life is coming back.
