I first bought this book 8 years ago when I invited my new vegan friends over for dinner. I am not vegan or even vegetarian - I just like to make my friends feel at home and respect their cuisines.  It was a nice way to introduce my kitchen to recipes that didn't involve dairy or meat.Since then I've gone through about 6 copies of this book, usually giving them away as gifts and then buying another copy for myself (like right now). I absolutely love these recipes and always consult this book first for something interesting to bring to a potluck. My favorites include the Rosemary & Red Onion Foccacia Bread and the Acorn Squash with Roma Tomatoes and Chard dish. I don't think the recipes are too complicated, but it helps to read all the way through once or twice as most of the entries can have multiple things going on or that could be prepared - but for me that's half the fun. If you like to cook this is a great book to have in your collection!Read moreRead less
