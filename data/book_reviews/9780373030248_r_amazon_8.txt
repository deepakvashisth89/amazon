Just so you know, I am a huge Betty Neels fan, so I am hardly objective. However,what I lose in objectivity I gain from having many of Ms. Neels wonderful romances.One of the things I love about her books is the lack of explicit sexuality. All of her stories are sweet, old fashion romances; and while they may sound rather trite, at the end of a hard day in a cold, cruel world, it is a great comfort to wrap myself in the cozy, warm world Neels romances engender in my imagination.This book is one of my favorites and I have read it countless times. It is the usual formula: homely English nurse meets, rich handsome Dutch doctor who sweeps her away to Holland. Her characters are so real one feels they know each and every one personally, and she makes Holland sound like another word for heaven.I love the older used copies because in spite of being somewhat battered and tattered, the art work is delightful and somewhat nostalgic.I highly recommend this book..It will lift your spirits, again and again.Read moreRead less
