Hot Toasty RagReview of "Class Reunion"Author Rona Jaffe strikes gold again with her soap opera drama Class Reunion. It starts at the college reunion and goes back in time to the tumultuous youthful years, but it didn’t really need the teaser in the beginning. It’s juicy enough to just tell the college story and end at the reunion; so if you want to, just skip the prologue so you don’t have a heads-up on how everyone ended up.Four girls are set up in the same dormitory their freshman year at Radcliffe in the 1950s. Emily was accepted because of the school’s Jewish quota, and she feels she has to be perfect in order to fit in. Annabel is gorgeous and sensual, described by the author as looking like Suzy Parker (who starred in the film adaptation of Rona Jaffe’s The Best of Everything). She enjoys men and she’s not ashamed of it! Daphne is the golden girl, naturally perfect and the envy of everyone; only she’s hiding a personal secret. And Chris is the resident brain who thinks studying is the most important thing, until she completely loses her heart for the wrong boy.As dramatic, salacious, melodramatic, soapy, and feminine as you’d expect a Rona Jaffe novel to be, Class Reunion doesn’t disappoint for a minute. Yes, the ending left me feeling sad for the girls that some of them stayed stuck in bad situations. But there’s a sequel I’m dying to sink my teeth into, and maybe it’ll explore some options post-reunion.Read moreRead less
