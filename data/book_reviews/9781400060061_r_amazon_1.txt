Until recently, little has been said, outside family discussions, about the early immigrants and their lives. First of all, this compelling book is an easy to read. The story of Nella Castelucca is the story of so many... a smart, energetic young woman, from a farming family in Pennsylvania, who is singled out as one of the most intelligent students. The novel starts with the "Americana" teacher visiting her parents, convincing them to allow Nella to expand her education. They agree to let her go into the city and study. Like so many during that time, her father not only farmed but worked at the quarry. After her father is seriously injured in a quarry accident, Nella's plans changes, and she goes to work in a garment factory, where she excels as a worker and becomes a floor lady while still in her mid teens. The novel includes a first love, Renato, who vanishes from her life, and the man who she married, Franco, a co-worker who fell in love with Nell at first glance. Together, they started their own successful factory.  Through hard work and sacrifice, Franco and Nella built, a beautiful life. They became pillars of the community, sharing so much of their of their good fortune with the community. So many plans for retirement dissolved with Franco's early & sudden death.There are other characters in the novel, such as her sisters and her close friend. Trigiani brings to life the world of the early immigrants, and their struggles, tragedies and joys. This book has romance, humor and mystery. And yes, Renato returns in the most surprising way. This was the first of Adriana's book I read, and I was hooked. As for the title of the book, it is based on one of the most loved festivals that brought together the Italian community, Our Lady of Mt Carmel. Trigiani's characters are real, they exist in so many of our grandparents and great-grandparents. This novel is based on a part of American history not in our history books.
