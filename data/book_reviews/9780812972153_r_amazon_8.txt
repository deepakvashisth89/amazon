Read this book if you are interested in the history of computer games. Read this book if you are a novice computer programmer and you want to read about brilliant programmers solving problems laterally. Read this book if you really want to feel what the computer game industry was like in the 80s and 90s (and probably since).I cannot express too highly how much I enjoyed this book. The author makes the reader feel like they are there. The whole concept of Romero's and Carmack's different visions/philosophies and how they melded for a while to change the gaming landscape... spectacular! And then there was the artist, Adrian Carmack, who made the games so visually memorable.As I am old enough to have played with programming on a Commodore 64, worked with computers for three decades, and been blown away by Doom when it hit the shareware selves... well, this book brought back great memories. And when Deathmatch arrived, it was fast and furious and fun - thanks guys. So read this well written book about people who changed their part of the world, if for no other reason than for inspiration. Be assured, the pace of the story is good, the personalities are described vividly and the events seem schoolboy Machiavellian at times. Excellent.Read moreRead less
