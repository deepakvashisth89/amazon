Perhaps the adolescent angst of the story parallels the uncertainty of our times, with social and environmental unrest.  It begins on an increasingly hostile and decaying Earth and moves to a hoped-for new beginning on another planet, a cast-off of a spacefaring civilization no longer present.  It's like what we might expect to find on Mars - a place that is not suited for Earth-life, and not really up to our expectations as a new paradise when we use up this one.  All the characters are skillfully imagined and fully individual, including the goofy aliens left behind in the ruins of their formerly great empire.  I would like to know more about the mysterious "household deer", which are both concrete and ghostly - or maybe it's ok just to go with my own interior movie about what they are and how they interact with the Earth-folk.  Ultimately the story is about how Pella bears up in her transition from childhood to adolescence, beginning to discover her inner power, while at the same time dealing with the decay of her family and Earth society.
