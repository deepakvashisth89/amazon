An easy but fascinating read. Growing up I would occasionally hear my grandmother refer to some group or another, in a not so flattering tone of voice, as, "Oh, they're just a bunch of holy rollers!"  Until reading this book I never thought of this as anything more than some old, general term for an off-beat religious group. But now I realize that my grandmother, as a girl of 7-8 years old growing up in Kings Valley, OR at the time (she was a King family descendant), would have most certainly heard talk and gossip about the Creffield church - the Holy Rollers - in nearby Corvallis.  So her use of this term in later years undoubtedly stemmed from things she heard as a young, impressionable girl.  While the book is a work of fiction, I like how the author weaves the known facts of the people, time, and places into an entirely plausible and historically accurate story that is a glimpse into what life was, and could have been, like at the time - for me, just another small insight into the life and times of my grandmother's childhood in a place that was "home" to her.Read moreRead less
