This is an oddity.Never before have I seen a book that chronicles broad failure as its topic.A celebration of manic genius gone wrong, a catalogue of madness  and a nice start on detailing the idiocy of government mismanagement, this book is often hilarious as it teaches the lesson of how good ideas can go wrong and how bad ideas can, um, go wrong spectacularly.If you like Canadian history, love to laugh at government stupidity or want to feel a little smug about your own failures, this gentle tome is for you.Because as it lists failures, you also finish each epistle with a feeling of admiration for those who attempted such things.Read moreRead less
