This is an excellent book of short stories, but I agree with other reviewers, it does require a good knowledge of German. I have recently started a U3A Course, having done German O-Level at school many years ago and have found this book very interesting. The simultaneous translation into English on the opposite page is invaluable and helps one not only to understand the meaning, but also to be able to compare German sentence structure. I would definitely recommend it for those with a more than basic level of German.Read moreRead less
