I had a copy of the LL Bean cookbook for years until it mistakenly got sent to the charity shop.  So I had to replace this bible of traditional New England recipes and was relieved to find it on Amazon. It’s a treasure trove of techniques (how to do a clam bake) and recipes you won’t find in any English cookbook, and is my indispensable go-to for squash recipes and delicious old-fashioned desserts. My “new” used version is fresh & clean  (the only sign of its age being a slightly faded dust-jacket) and arrived promptly from the seller.
