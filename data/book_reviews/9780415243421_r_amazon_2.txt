Altough i am not a big SETI book reader, i am a big SETI advocate. I've done my share of research and Science Channel watching, but until i read David Lamb's book, i never really understood what was going on. The author not only tells you what sort of intelligences could be out there (machine, laser beams, etc) but also explains how to communicate to such.
