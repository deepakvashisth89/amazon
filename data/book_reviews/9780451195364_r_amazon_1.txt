What a refreshing change to find a story where the hero falls in love at first sight and sets about convincing the heroine that they are "two halves of a whole" that were meant to be together! A hero without the  usual lordly arrogance, the large succession of mistresses, the prejudice  against marriage and commitment. . . .  I hadn't quite realized until I  read this how disgusted I had become with the traditional regency hero.  Now that I have, I find myself hungering for more of the same.  Regency  authors, are you listening?Readers, you can't go wrong with this  delightful tale of a pair willed to marry each other who nevertheless  become a true love match.Read moreRead less
