I cannot overstate the perception-transforming effect of this book. It makes all the pieces of the spiritual puzzle fall into place and has confirmed what myself and many a wise person already knows intuitively: that much of what we are taught is WRONG. It resonates both on an intellectual and intuitive level. You will learn many things such as the true reason for the religious imagery of Christianity which always seemed random and vague to me; and other such facts as the true origin of the throne being the lap of the mother, hence the reason why ancient Egyptian imagery always depicts kings sitting in the lap of Isis; the association of the left side of the body (the right brain) with the divine feminine (location of the heart) and hence the deliberate patriarchal stigma of "left-handedness" e.g ancient word for left derived from the word "sinister" whilst the word for "right" always has positive connotations e.g "dexterous" (deriving from destra). The author really breaks it down to the bare essentials, even going as far as explaining the origins of words you never thought had any connection to each other such as the words menses, month, and mathematics which all were derivatives of the word "moon" and "mother" under the ancient system of goddess worship, the moon and nature and many other things being a key focal point of goddess religions because of the connection to women's cycles. There is much information packed in here (the book is quite thick being about 430 pages long). You can take much away from it factually and theoretically. The only thing I would say is that at times it felt a bit repetitive but that was due mainly to its comprehensive nature. A word of caution- this will appeal more to spiritual seekers who are quite advanced in their journey and knowledge of self. Otherwise much of the references, symbolism and commentary will go over your head. It's not solely an "intellectual" read.
