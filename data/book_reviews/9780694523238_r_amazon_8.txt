After watching a documentary about Maupin I ordered the first book in the Tales of the City series. Since I was living in California at the time Maupin was writing his newspaper series I thought it would be fun to read his books and relive the past.The book is fantastic, the characters run the gamut from poor to rich, gay to straight, and all searching for meaning in their lives.
