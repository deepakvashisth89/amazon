Being a fan of audio books, and of the Buffy TV series, I thought this audio tape, as read by Charisma Carpenter (yes, that's Cordelia Chase), would be a bit of light hearted listening.Sadly, this was expecting too much.The quality of the literatue is laborious at best, and turgid at worst and frankly, I don't expect much from a TV spin-off novel. However, these problems pale in comparison to Carpenter's slaughter of an already tiresome story. I get the impression the words were printed on some kind of semi-visible auto-cue and she had no idea of context when she was "reading" the narrative. She stutters, she fluffs, and worst of all, her usual bubbly delivery has been reduced to that of a school girl reading in class. Yes, it really is that bad.
