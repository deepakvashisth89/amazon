If you want to ignite your brain and enjoy some delicious fun, read this book. It is a a great addition to anyone's library. You can skip through and return again for smiles, giggles and pure nonsense on every page with its clever twists in meanings. DEVIL'S DICTIONARY is comparable to another old favorite of mine which is written in a similar style, VERSE AND WORSE.
