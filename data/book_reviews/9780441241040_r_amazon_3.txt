As an avid Sci-Fi reader for well over 50 years, I have found few few authors who have attained the level of proficiency and gritty realism that Drake brings to the military Sci-Fi genre. I also served with the 11th armored cav in Vietnam, although I was there before Drake. So I have an idea where he is coming from.The "Fleet"series which was originally published in 1988 and released as e-books in 2012 , certainly holds to his and Bill Fawcett's high standards. Drakes short story "When the Devil Drives"is a great example of his honest intensity and great character development. He just never lets us down.The book also features such writers as Piers Anthony and  Jody Lynn Nye. It just seams that Drake makes everyone read better.As a postscript, I have to confess that I have switched to reading most of my books on a Kindle. Never thought I'd do that.
