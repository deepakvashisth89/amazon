I found this Outer Limits book very boring.  I just could not get into it, which surprised me, since most all of the books in this series grab me by the first page, and if not, than by the end of the first chapter.  This was not the case with this one.  Even the "climax" was uneventful.  The first time I started to read it, I put it down after chapter one, and decided not to read it.  I only read the whole thing because it was the only thing I could find.  If you want to read this book, check it out of a library.  It's a total waste of money.Read moreRead less
