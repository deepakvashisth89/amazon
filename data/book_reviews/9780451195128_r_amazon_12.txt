I very much liked the cover of this book, and when I saw it was a free offer I downloaded it!  Who wouldn't?  It was an interesting and original tale which kept me reading over the course of a weekend.  It involves identical twin sisters separated at birth, and the machinations of their father to locate the other twin and get her to rescue his plan which was threatening to unravel.  He needed to have her masquerade as her sister, who had run away rather than marry the man her father had chosen.  Maeve agreed to do this, as her mother had died and she was struggling with the debts she had left.  The agreement was that these debts were to be paid off, and Maeve would only have to carry out the masquerade for a fortnight - enough time to bring her sister, Meg, back from Scotland!  Of course, the terrible Earl (Meg's viewpoint) became the desperately attractive Earl (Maeve's veiwpoint)!The writing was reasonble, and the plot unravelled well.  So why only three stars?  I did like the novel - but I felt the characters and situation to be a little unbelievable, and when I compared it with many recent books I had given four stars to I just felt it could not quite reach the mark.Do not be put off, though!  I would still recommend this as a good read (and Theo very much grows on you!).  I will certainly be happy to read other books by Nadine Miller in the future.Read moreRead less
