Minor spoilers ahead!This is a mildly enjoyable novel about an obnoxious bird and an even more obnoxious owner.  The story, which is nicely interwoven with colorful descriptions of the picturesque Welsh countryside, is marred by intermittent references to bodily functions and fluids that I found outright repugnant.  Granted, the author's purpose was to evoke certain feelings in the reader, but I still found these details unnecessary.
