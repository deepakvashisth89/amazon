Read this years ago and fancied reading it again, esp after hearing a near neighbour's vitriolic rant about the boat people, which I found disgusting and ignorant!  This book should be part of the English Lit syllabus in schools. it might give  some students a different perspective on  the immigration problem.  It is essentially a love story of two stateless young people  and the terrible way they and their compatriots were treated in the  late 1930's  by so many governments and how they survived being pushed from country to country.
