Richard North Patterson can spin one heck of a yarn. While the book was published in 98, I’m just getting around to reading it now, and it is easily the best book I’ve read in 2026. Patterson takes his time to tell the story, but it never gets boring. Each character is thoroughly examined, and the mystery is nursed along until near the very end. It’s a satisfying read, and even though there is a lot of heartbreaking events throughout the lives of the characters, the book never feels like a downer.Read moreRead less
