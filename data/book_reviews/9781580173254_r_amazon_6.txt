I like how this book gives you excellent pertinent information and then overwhelms you with it.  There is so much here and the auther keeps in excellently categorized.  It discusses the three main uses of chickens: meat, eggs, and shows, and then goes about telling you all sorts of useful information on them.  I raised poultry as a child and am getting back into it and this book brought back all that information I learned in 4-H.  But the breeding category really amazed me.  I never spent so much time and attention to the breeding of my birds.  It was a complex confusing affair but this author gives you some excellent simple techniques to keep track of your chicken bloodlines.  Wow chicken bloodlines!
