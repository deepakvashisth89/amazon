Pros1. Parallel texts on pages facing each other makes it easy and fast practice reading.2. The stories are literary and of interest to students of German literature. (They may be boring for some people, but I am a literature student.) As such, you will run across colloquial and dated phrases and grammar variations which will definitely help you get the sense of written German.3. The book is small, light, and a reasonable length. I stick it in my backpack to carry to school.Cons:1. The translations are not *exactly* as literal as the book claims to be, but they are nonetheless extremely faithful and will help you figure out unfamiliar words and phrases without needing to go to a dictionary for one or two words.2. The German and English pages don't *exactly* match up (sometimes the German for the bottom of an English translation is found on the top of the next page instead). It's usually only a sentence or two.Other comments:1. Good for an advanced intermediate (or just advanced) reader. One thing I tried early on was to read an entire page (then an entire story) in English, then go back and read the German again--that helps if you are just at the intermediate level and can't handle only German yet.Read moreRead less
