I am halfway through this book and it's making me sick.  Other reviewers were gushing over the male protagonist's involvement with his newborn son and that's fine to a point.  There were so many chapters devoted exclusively to this father's avid interest in the day-to-day care of his son that it got to be excessive.  But what started to make me sick was this guy's unseemly interest in watching a complete stranger (heroine) breastfeed his son.  There should have been some propriety at play here because this scenario would never have occurred in reality.  Then, this father started to become sexually obsessed with his wet nurse and the inner thoughts about "having her now" were repeated over and over again but he was thwarted often.  Spoiler alert:  the heroine had been raped and the father became aware of this eventually but, still, he was intending to act on his sexual impulses without regard to the heroine's feelings or wishes.  The whole sexual obsession really made this guy disgusting to me.  He wasn't an alpha male type either.  The guy seems to have a problem where he attaches himself to a woman in an unhealthy way.  I look to escape when I read romance novels but this book is actually making me stressed out.  The sex scenes and this guy's sexual thoughts were uncomfortable to read and not romantic in any way.  It's going to take a lot to finish this very long book, if I do.  This is my first and last book by this author that I intend to read.Read moreRead less
