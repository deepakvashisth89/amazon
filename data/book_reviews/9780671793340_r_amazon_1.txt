Not a surf novel, per se, although even a passing acquaintance with surfing and surf culture will materially enhance your reading experience.  Could be classified as a sense of place novel, the place being the northern coast of California and, of course the sea.  Not only in that respect, but also in the strength and outlandishness of its characters, Dogs reminds me of Keysey's Sometimes a Great Notion.  Fletcher, the surf dropout anti-hero, foils with another character, ex surf hero Drew Harmon, along with the mysterious and forceful Kendra, who is married to Harmon and lives with him on the remote coast.  Together, these three, and assorted Indians, witches, and tribal officials enact with considerable suspense.  Mystical landscapes and malevolent seas are of equal impact with action and story, no small accomplishment, since the plot is twisty and somewhat complicated.
