An anthology is probably not the best way to read short stories.  In any event, the stories became monotonous after a while (perhaps Margaret Atwood only likes one particular type of short story).  A variety of people (usually women) who are very sad, and often otherwise psychologically disfunctional, numbly lament their sadness, dysfunctionality, family, mistakes, etc.  Maybe if there had been a few stories that contained something other than elegant writing about depression, then I would have enjoyed the depression stories more.
