I haven't completed the German version yet but I read the English version at the library and then decided to purchase the German version which is a translation of the English.  As far as I can tell, this is the first book in the series of Dirk Pitt novels and originally came out around 1976.  It still reads well even with the changes that have taken place in technology and lifestyles since that time.  I guess those of us who have been around since before that time may be able to relate to it better, however.  I have now read two of his books and plan to read most, if not all of them, in the coming months.  I like to read them in English and then in German.  It helps me to maintain my competence in the German language.
