I have to admit that the first two chapters of this book disturbed me!! I checked to see when it was written because the characteristics of the main male character were similar to a sexual predator and wondered if his behaviour was acceptable 24 years ago  when it was written. The dialogue says "he put his hand to the nape of her neck and pulled her to him and kissed her hard.She struggled and pushed against him begging him to stop. He saw that she was afraid and softened his kiss"! but he didn't stop!!!Then on the next page the dialogue continues "She realized she had provoked him in to that violent confrontation. Had she wanted him to touch her"? This smacks of sexual harassment and the victim being made to feel guilty. I almost stopped reading feeling this is not a romantic novel. However Diana Palmer is one of my favourite writers and I put my trust in her and continued to read. It did get better and I did enjoy the rest of the story.Read moreRead less
