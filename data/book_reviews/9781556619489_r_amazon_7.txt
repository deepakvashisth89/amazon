This book continues the storyline from the end of The Widow of Larkspur Inn.  Julia and Andrew are planning their wedding. Julia's son Philip is preparing to go to secondary school as is Andrew's daughter, Laurel.  Andrew's older daughter, Elizabeth, has been seeing the curate from a neighboring town.  She realizes he plans to ask her to marry him and believes she will accept as she understands the role of the wife of a clergyman.  However, she continues to have feelings for Jonathan, her old beau who proved himself untrustworthy.  As time goes on, she becomes uncomfortable with his expectations regarding her behavior and comes to question whether to continue their relationship.  Meanwhile, Jonathan has come to a belief in Christ and comes to their town and ends up as the new schoolmaster.  Andrew is infuriated by this and becomes very protective of Elizabeth.A parallel storyline follows Seth Langford who is serving twenty years in London's Newgate Prison.  Ten years into his sentence, Seth is summoned to the warden to discover the charges against him have been proven false and he is being released.  The person who framed him was the child of his employer and the boy, now a young man, had finally confessed.  Seth was being offered five hundred pounds as compensation and in return he has to agree to not charge the young man with false accusation resulting in imprisonment. Seth accepts the money and tells the young man he forgives him.  After release, Seth looks for his former fiancee and discovers she had married and had a child but she and her husband had been killed in a fire and the boy was in an orphanage. Seth ends up adopting Thomas and they relocate to the village of Gresham where Seth purchases a cottage and starts a horse farm.Meanwhile, one of the lodgers at the inn, Mrs. Kingston, is being pursued by Squire Bartley.  She is elusive and doesn't encourage him as she believes that will actually encourage his pursuit of her.  This courtship brings much entertainment into the book.  Mrs. Kingston also encourages Mercy Sanders as she slaves away in cooking, cleaning, gardening and canning for her overbearing father and six brothers.  Mercy's mother has died and any suitor who shows up is scared away by her father and brothers.  Her only friend, an elderly woman who led her to Christ and taught her to read, has also died.  Mercy is very alone and has no way out of her predicament. Mrs. Kingston has a way of dealing with Mercy's father and assists in the plan to achieve a marriage between Mercy and Seth.All the storylines are enjoyable and have satisfying endings, but I loved the story of Seth and Mercy.
