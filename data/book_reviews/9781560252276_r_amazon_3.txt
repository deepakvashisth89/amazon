It's huge. It slumbers on my night table. I bought it used and it opened to James Dean's "Ode to a Tijuana Toilet". On the facing page was John Gilmore's painting of Dean, and beyond that, a photo of Dean's original typed, annotated copy of the poem, provided by Gilmore. Readers will find famous names from the worlds of music, acting, literature, and even, yes, poetry. I open it at random and find uncommon good stuff and passages I will abandon immediately.
