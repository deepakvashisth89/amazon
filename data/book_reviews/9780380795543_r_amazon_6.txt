First of all, I found it very cool that this is a contemporary romance that references 30s Hollywood and actors of the era.  I happen to adore Tyrone Power and Cary Grant, so  immediately I was drawn to the premise of this book.  I also like the added aspect of the mystery of Trevor's disappearance 60 years before and the major fantasy aspect of him suddenly reappearing in the present.
