Kaminsky's Toby Peters is the perfect "gumshoe" and gets hit on the head at least once every book.  Despite the obvious plot line, the stories are interesting and filled with bits of "factoids" from the 40's.  The events of WW 2 are sketched as the backdrop as is life on the west coast and the internment of all Americans of Japanese descent. Howard Hughes was an interesting and contradictory person and we see glimmers of his later descent.  Kaminsky is always a quick fun read.Read moreRead less
