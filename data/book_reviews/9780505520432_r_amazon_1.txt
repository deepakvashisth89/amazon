Alena Yor and Garner Rie are both trapped by the circumstances of their birth.  As a member of the highest caste, Alena is forbidden to associate with those considered beneath her, but when she meets Garner, the token low caste officer on the Moon Courier, she risks everything to be with him.I've had this book for more than ten years, but I re-read it maybe once a year.  I love it!  The richly drawn world and excellent characterization pulls the reader in.  A classic tale of two people who defy the laws of their society to be together!Read moreRead less
