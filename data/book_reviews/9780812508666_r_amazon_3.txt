Ernest Hogan has a very special way of interweaving Aztec lore with popular culture, extrapolation of current trends and barrio language and idiosyncrasies. High Aztech allows the classic science fiction reader, who might not be not very knowledgeable of real Earth cultures, even though he/she can understand the politics and customs of Coruscant of Arrakis, an opportunity to get immersed in a very interesting and original milieu and even better story.
