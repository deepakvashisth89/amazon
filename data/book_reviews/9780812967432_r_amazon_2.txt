Everyone has heard of Oliver Twist, Tale of Two Cities and Great Expectations.  But Dombey and Son?  The thread running through this classic I would best describe as a meditation on women's rights and the value placed on women in Dickensian England.  Yes, the title is "son" but is really about the women in Mr. Dombey's life.  If you are looking to expand into other Dicken's novels, this is a really good one to read next.  And, a good starter for the more complex plots of Our Mutual Friend, Little Dorrit, and Bleak House.One thing I really like about the Penguin Classic version is the annotations.  I have two bookmarks, one where I last stopped the read, and one for the annotations, making it easier to flip back and forth.
