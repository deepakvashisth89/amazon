A mixed bag edited by Mike Baker of work by writers under 30, such as 24-year-old Edgar Alan Poe's MS Found in a Bottle.  Some other work by classic writers like King, Bloch, and Ramsey Campbell, then a lot of stuff by other writers, including a bunch of first sales.  Of the latter group I found a couple real good, some interesting, and several with endings I found, at best, confusing.  Among these was Clark Perry's Little Black Bags which by itself I found almost worth reading the whole book.  Poppy Z. Brite and Christa Faust's book-ending story was disturbing, in a good way.
