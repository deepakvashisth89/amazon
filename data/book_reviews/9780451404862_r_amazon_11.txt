It’s a fascinating world that Putney has created. Descriptions of place and scene invokes ones imagination and interest. The personal development and thought journeys of the lead characters results in a deeply felt love story that is rich and fascinating and believable. The characters are both physically skilled so that their playacting, combat scenes and not so truthful storytelling to each other comes as natural as breathing. Amidst searching for spies and information to save another, the woven love story of these soulmates as they find each other does not come without  cost and sacrifice. Of course the result is being known in love and knowing someone else more intuitively. A couple of times I had to put this book down to get on with the ordinaries of life, but returning to it was like easily slipping  into engaging world Putney created.
