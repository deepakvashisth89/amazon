After reading more than half this book, I came to a scene that encapsulates how oblivious the author is. After failing to plan appropriately for his journey, he finds he must seek help from the poor inhabitants. Having lied and misrepresented throughout the book to get things he wants, he finally sent me over the edge with this scene. He meets a man who initially refuses to sell him food. Later, he meets the same man again, except this time the man is intoxicated. The man agrees to sell the author food for exorbitant prices. When the author goes to the man's hut, he finds it filthy and a shambles. A sick woman lies in the corner of the hut. A crying child begs the author not to take the food (clearly the only food they have). The drunken man beats the child and takes the money from the author (obviously to purchase more alcohol).
