Don't be put off by the fact that this a story about a middle-aged second-hand book-seller. His life and experiences are uniquely beautiful even when they appear to be repellent. We think we know what we like in books but this one expands the boundaries of our appreciation and we end up looking at our reading experience in a new way. Go on, read it and see for youwself!
