Mostly terribly written. Just immature writing skills with the maximum amount of expositional dialogue you will ever suffer through. However, I was more interested in the subject matter than the writing craft so I made it through. It follows a group of (poorly written) people who “stumble” upon an entity named Michael. He has all the answers to all of the universal questions we seek answers for. The book is painfully divided into upper and lower case text for the seance group and all caps for Michael. A tough read but some of the thoughts were intriguing (though heavily biased by the author) and overall, what it will do, is make you seek out better books on the same subject.
