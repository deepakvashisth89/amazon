I wanted to give Ruth Rendell 5 stars because I really like her writing.  Her books about obsessive people are really good character studies.  But this book?  I gave it two stars because I do not like the main character.  He's a rapist who is prone to violence against women and paralyzes a man.  He's done despicable things to other people and blames them for their misery.I got 33% of the book read and didn't want to spend any more of my time reading about this guy.
