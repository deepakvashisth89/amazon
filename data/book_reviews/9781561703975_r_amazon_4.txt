Since my reading interests are wide and varied I have begun to explore topics that are unique and unusual in nature.  I just finished reading this interesting 222 page soft cover book (Angel Therapy: Healing messages for every area of your life by Doreen Virtue), which I bought on Amazon for a bargain price.This book explores the subject of angels, claiming we all have guardian angels that guide us and keep us safe.  It further claims that because we have free will, our guardian angels will not interfere in our lives until we personally ask for their help.  I have to admit that I liked this book. Whether you believe in angels or not, this book gives a positive and hopeful approach that is appealing.The author relates that whether we realize it or not, we all have guardian angles who want to help and guide us through life and death.  This book also claims our pets also have their guardian angels.  Indeed, would it not be wonderful if it is true?  In this respect, this book provides hope and inspiration to those seeking assistance in their lives.This informative and esoteric book is organized into two parts. Part one covers the healing messages for a wide range of issues.  Some of them include: addiction, anger, anxiety, betrayal, blame, burdens, child abuse, children, crime, depression, death, fear, grief, guilt, happiness, love, and numerous other topics. The second part explores communicating, healing and living with angels.  This section gives an overview of how to contact your angles and provides information on the archangels.If you are interested in learning about angles and specifically your own guardian angel(s) this is a book you may want to check out.  I found this book so interesting I could not put the book down until I finished it.Rating: 4 Stars.  Joseph J.
