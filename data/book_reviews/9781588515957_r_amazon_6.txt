Nancy Mehl is an artist of the highest quality.  Her story is extremely well crafted, her character development superb and her plot so full of mazes, you'll at time feel like a pretzel.A beautiful young wife is murdered right before the eyes of her young daughter but the killer doesn't know she sits in the shadows watching him.  Led by a series of nightmares and with the help of a sensitive and caring psychiatrist  seven year old Cally is able to draw the face of her mother's killer.  This enables the justice system to prosecute and convict him.Fast forward and Cally is now grown up and a young college student, who works part time for the police department as a sketch artist putting faces to descriptions given by crime victims.  Cally meets and falls in love with a young defense attorney who likes to study transcripts of old trials.  After giving him the transcripts of her mother's trial the clever young man uncovers enough inconsistencies to cast doubt on the original conviction.From there Cally is faced with a series of what ifs.  What if the man convicted of her mother's murder and who later dies in prison, wasn't the man who really killed her mother?  As Cally begins to share her doubts,  someone becomes concerned enough to want her dead.The nightmares return.  Two young men are in love with her, but what if one of the young men wants her dead?  Her father has withdrawn himself from her, a bitter, hateful old man but what if the face in the nightmare she can't make out is that of her father.  What if it was her father who killed her mother?Their old pastor, a kindly man and a man who had been counseling her mother just before her murder, suddenly expresses an interest in Cally's reoccurring nightmares.  What if the good pastor is actually a murderer?No one is above suspicion.  Do for yourself a fun thing and buy this book.  You'll feel like you've run a marathon and if you're successful at figuring out this who done it before the climax, you can give yourself a star because you'll have proven beyond any doubts you're a first class sleuth.
