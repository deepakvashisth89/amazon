Jock Carroll manages to lampoon just about everything held sacred in the United States of the late 1950s and early 60s.  "The Shy Photographer" follows the adventures of a Swampy Cree named Arthur as  he climbs the ladder of success with "Light" magazine.  Nothing  is what it seems, but very little ever is.  Carroll puts Arthur and his  partner Joe in to one unlikely situation after another, each one raising  Arthur's fame and prestige.Beisides that, it is just plain funny.Read moreRead less
