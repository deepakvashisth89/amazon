The story is immersive because so many aspects of the characters’ lives are described that the reader can truly feel a part the action.  However, the pacing is very slow.While Martinez and Sula are fascinating characters, they aren’t ones to inspire the reader to zealously root for them because they have serious moral flaws.  I’m glad they can fight in space battles, but they aren’t folks I’d take home with me.One aspect of the story that really irritated me is the glorified honoring of Martinez that was somehow glossed over.  The idiots in government are too stupid to lead, but they can present a seriously impressive award, yet the author just blips over this moment.  Everything else is described in painful detail, but when our hero should have his moment, it’s stunted.The writing is of high caliber in terms of vocabulary, syntax, grammar, and mechanics, but it’s a slog through lots of information to get to the good parts.
