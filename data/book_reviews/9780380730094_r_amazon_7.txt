John  Maxim writes a hell of a good novel, and the last 70% of this one is up to his usual standards; however the first 30% or so is essentially a rehash of the first in the series. I understand the need for backstory in a novel, but most of that first part is essentially a cut & paste of the end of Bannermann Solution and, it seemed, unnecessary.
