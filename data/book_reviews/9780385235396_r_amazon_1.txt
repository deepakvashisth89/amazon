Eugene Kennedy has been one of my favorite authors since my high school years. He is really in tune with the human nature and how human beings are related to their creator (even though he does not dwell much on this). I have read and made every effort to have his books by me. He is practical, down to earth, and realistic. I will recommend his books as companions to the Scriptures (i.e. depending on one's orientation)and to everyone who wants to face life as it is. There is no escape from real life. I am always fascinated by Eugene Kennedy's writings. He is a gem. He has made me come to love psychology.
