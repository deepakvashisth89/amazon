Low profile, ex-cop, private detective Matthew Scudder is the kind of guy people turn to when they need a street-smart investigator, who will leave no stone unturned until the truth is revealed. In this case, Scudder is being asked to prove the innocence of a disturbed Vietnam vet, who apparently gunned down a young lawyer at a phone booth on the streets of NYC. But then, strangely enough, Scudder is asked by the widow to investigate her husband’s death.Scudder starts digging. He has a huge network of cops, informants, street people, etc that he can probe, but most importantly, it seems that the victim has a shady past. It could be that he had some serious enemies.Scudder pursues all of this while navigating his own uncertain life. A recovering alcoholic, he still frequents all of his local hangouts. He maintains his relationship with Elaine, but his widow client is proving to be a distraction. It’s easy to identify with Scudder; he has to realistically deal with everyday life. He has no super human powers; all he has are smarts and grit. One of the better books in the series.Read moreRead less
