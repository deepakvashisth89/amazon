The most amazing thing about this book is, in my opinion, the fact that although this is not fantasy work, it reads like it is.  Maybe it's the surrealism that pervades the whole story.  Rosa Montero does have a knack  depicting decaying urban landscapes.  The somewhat disturbing array of  characters is worthy of an Almodovar's movie.I thought the ending  would satisfy me more (divine justice), but felt sad for the protagonist.
