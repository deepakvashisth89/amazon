Lerner tackles conservatives who think religion should defend the status quo and liberals who think secular society has all we need. His basic argument is that 'spirit matters' -- people's need for meaning, connection,  love, awe, mystery, rest and joy are essential to our happiness; and, when  absent, are a source of profound personal and collective malaise.  If you  don't share Lerner's spiritual optismism, as I don't, you nevertheless will  have to confront his insistent questioning about the sources of your  pessimism, fear and despair. Not content to rest with generalities about  spirit in society, Lerner applies his ideas to medicine, law, education and  the environment. Get the book. Read it. Argue about it.
