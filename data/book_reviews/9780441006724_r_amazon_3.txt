The authors have created a grand and epic universe with 40,000 years of history, galactic empires, and various types of humans from "high" to "pristine". Yet the majority of this story is a narrow and near-sighted tale taking place on a prison planet. In many ways it reminds me of the Aliens 3 movie, which I consider the worst of the franchise.The story starts off great. And it ends great. But the stuff in-between (the bulk of the novel) is slow paced and boring.This literary universe deserves more, and is capable of a far better story than the novel provides.Not recommended, unless the series picks up in the next two novels.
