Another great story by Bernd Heinrich. I don't know what was left out in the abridged version but I'm happy I got the original one with supposedly more content in it. Even so, towards the end I felt I wanted more about Bubo but it seems that his breakup from Margaret disturbed him such that in the 3rd summer he did not keep a journal as in the first two of which the book is comprised.
