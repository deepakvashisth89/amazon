I've always loved these types of stories, since I was a kid. These types of books that are told in a mature and realistic fashion, through an animal's perspective of their life. Nothing watered down or simplified for the sake of reaching a wider audience by trying to be "family friendly". Nope. I dislike that type of silly narrative. I like to see the these types of stories that treat nature's dark side head on, as a fact of life. This is that type of story...or as far as the science back in the 90's at the time that the book was written was at when it came to what was known about dinosaurs then.I would love to find and read more books like this one. It reminds me somewhat of another animal perspective book I love, WaterShip Down. The perspective of rabbits...and a story with a lot of darkness, but I loved in nonetheless as a kid. I still love it today as an adult and keep it on my bookshelf. So, take this with a grain of salt, since this could be my nostelgia for these books coloring my bias.
