This is one of the lesser known Philip Marlowe tales, but it is very good.  All of the Marlowe stories are slightly different in that they tend to emphasize different aspects of mystery writing.  For example, The Long Goodbye really gets into Marlowe's psychological insights and emotions.  The High Window is much more plot-driven, and the plot is very complicated.  In fact it is slightly overcomplicated, which is why I have given it four stars rather than five.  But for all that, it is still an excellent read and a full member of the Chandler genre.
