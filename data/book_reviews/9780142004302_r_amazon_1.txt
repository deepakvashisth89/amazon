The Protestant Reformation was a complex transition in the U.K.  This book shines a light on all the major societal changes that it involved.  It makes it much easier to understand, and it clarifies how different people felt about it.  The author is highly knowledgeable about the reformation.  And, importantly, he is an outstanding writer.  It’s a gripping story that is hard to put down.  It is highly entertaining and enjoyable, a real page turner.
