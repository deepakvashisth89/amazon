Like most non-fiction works there are parts where the underlying excitement of the narrative are overshadowed by quotations or references or other devices intended to inform. But these are personal preferences and it would be unfair to comment on them negatively as another reader might find them insufficient rather than a touch too much as I did. Basically the book is a brilliant read and tells so much about Britain's expansion and global empire in the 18th century, together with the thinking processes of it's participants. And this work offers so much more of the contextual jigsaw to fit around Darwin's life and work, and of discovery/sailing at that time in general. If you are interested in sailing or Darwin or history, you will not be disappointed. This is factual historical adventure. 9.5/10Read moreRead less
