Catherine Gaskin's books are a pleasure to read and Family Afairs is no exception. The book is well written and just flows. The Characters also are interesting and you feel you get to know them as the story weaves its magic and hooks you in from the beginning. Some twists and turns to keep you guessing and hooked until the end. So disappointed to reach the end, because I enjoyed the story so much.  For me, Catherine Gaskin's books are not being transferred to Kindle quickly enough, with the last one being available summer 2018! I'm hoping The Ambassador 's Women will be next! If you've never read Catherine Gaskin, give her a try and I'm sure you won't be disappointed! I previously read some of her novels several years ago and I am enjoying rereading some and discovering new books which I have not read.Read moreRead less
