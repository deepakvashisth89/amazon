In the "Hidden Jesus" Donald Spoto has written another excellent work that deserves a place with many great pieces of literature on the subject of Jesus of Nazareth.  The critics that believe it in not a well written biography have failed to see the expressed intent and the approach of the author.  He wrote: "I believe in other words, not in some more or less reconstructed historical figure of Jesus, nor do I give myself to what he once said and did.  It is only to the living and forever risen Lord that I direct my gaze; to him who is hidden but no less present for all that. `I was dead, and look - I am alive forever and ever,' says the glorified risen Jesus in Revelation." (page 251) His chapters on the resurrected Christ are priceless.  Those ancient writers upon whom he depends (Mathew, Mark, Luke and John) did not really write real biographies either.  They wrote their witness to the meaning of the life of Jesus.  This Spoto has also accomplished.Much of this book is so well written it is poetry to the reader.  I read every word and found it hard to put down. The skill of composition is great.  Few books do I read with such pleasure.  This may be Donald Spoto's finest creation.Read moreRead less
