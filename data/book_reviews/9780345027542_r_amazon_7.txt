In general I disliked most of Arthur C. Clarke's novels on the basis that they are boring and self conscious.But this collection of short stories is delightful.The stories concern a group of writers and hangers-on who habitually gather at a London pub to exchange tall tales. The topics generally revolve around dubious science and history. They are full of good humor. I recommend this to all who enjoy hilarious science fiction.Perhaps Clarke should have stuck to the art of the short story.Read moreRead less
