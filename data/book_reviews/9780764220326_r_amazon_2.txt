This 'Rocky Mountain " series was nothing short of wonderful.So sorry to have them end. I kept reading and could not put them down,I miss the characters even now and would like the series to continue.The story is set in the developing west and tells of self respect, trust in God and the strength of the human spirit.It also shows the difference between country raised and city groomed personalities.It is set around a young a vibrant woman who does some things a little "over the top'" but aftergetting to know her, you can expect it!
