Laing was not only a follower of the ancient approach to mental suffering which prescribes philosophy (empathy and discussion) as a treatment for mental suffering, he was also a forerunner to the latest psychotherapeutic treatment methods in the 20th century. Talk therapy, which is what Laing' approach amounts to, has been empirically proven to be the most effective long-term treatment for so-called mental illnesses. Unfortunately, psycho-pharmacology came in and bulldozed biological psychiatry into existence in the 1960's. Since then inter-personal approaches, like those of Laing, have not been popular. The pervasiveness of the "chemical imbalance" theory has made people into "cases" with brain problems. This book is well worth reading as a counter-argument to the bio-medical and pharmaceutical approach to mental illness.
