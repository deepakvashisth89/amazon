A perfect example of old style Sci fi!I heartily recommend the Vang titles to any fan of science fiction. The writing is minimalist, yet conveys an abject horror that is missing from so many books today. For new readers, seek here for an example of how a writer can generate emotion without simply telling you.The Vang are no one's friends, as an enemy species they are akin to the Aliens of classic movies. I would dearly love to see this series in movie format, only if helmed by a master like Carpenter.After a long time in hiding, a Vang battle master is freed and wreaks havoc, it's abilities straining an entire world to contain it.The Vang, as a side note, were the original inspiration for the Flood, but the Vang bring a fear with them that is far greater than Halo ever provided. Truly they are an enemy to frighten explorers both young and old.Read this series and remember, always look up!Read moreRead less
