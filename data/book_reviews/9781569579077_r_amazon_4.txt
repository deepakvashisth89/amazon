Contrary to the publisher's description, this is not a "children's book" but a sensitive and intelligent (albeit highly condensed) retelling of Attar's Sufi classic, "The Conference of the Birds," an allegorical tale of the soul's journey to the divine.  A Sufi teacher once said that "children's books" are so appropriate for spiritual remembrance, because they speak to an earlier and more trusting layer of our psyche, bypassing the head and going straight to the heart.  Here we have a great tale of courage in the face of despair, the overcoming of obstacles, the loss of loved ones, and the heart's final illumination with love.
