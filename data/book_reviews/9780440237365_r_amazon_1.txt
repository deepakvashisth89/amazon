This is just about the most incredible book I have ever read.  I would never be so pretentious as to refer to a book as a masterpiece, but I have to in this instance.  J.M. Morris has written a spellbinding, chilling and completely original novel that immediately calls to mind the brilliant psychological suspense novels of Patrick McGrath and Barbara Vine.
