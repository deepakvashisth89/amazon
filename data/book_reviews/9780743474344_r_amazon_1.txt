Whether they like his Acting, Music, or him for the role he played in life.It includes:Numerous pictures (I haven't seen a good bit of them until now)Lyrics/PoemsScripts (for music videos and other cool stuff)Documents (such as Birth Certificate, Passport, Legal documents...)Letters[I thought this was pretty cool, there is a page where he wrote the song order for the CD "Me Against the World" it might have more albums outlined but I'm not 100% positive]And other cool things...If you like to read full length biographies or autobiographies then I wouldn't recommend it for that. It's more of a story book with pictures and lots of, if not only, words from him.
