This is a good, short, diversionary mystery set in the Berkshires. While I don't care for James Joyce, his letters serve as the pretext for bringing Professor Kate Fansler, her troubled 10-year old nephew, and two English grad students to a deceased publisher's summer house. Kate's lover, Reed Amhearst, is an assistant D.A. who visits Kate and proposes marriage.Lots of literary references sprinkle the mystery. Even the policeman quotes some lines by Alexander Pope! In the end, I felt the mystery wasn't so interesting as the characters involved. I enjoyed the wit and conversation between Kate and Reed especially.
