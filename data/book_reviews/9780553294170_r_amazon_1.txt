A book touching several themes including the clashing of, and the upholding of classes, sex and power, the theme of feminism and general philosophy of life and death. Moreover, a book more than just a crime book. Personal criticism would be the female characters being of such higher morale standard than the males despite their likeness of riches, power and some of their slight arrogance towards the lower classes fighting against tyranny, one male almost becoming divine once turning from male to a female. Some detailed descriptions typical of crime-books may very well have been skipped altogether. The description of how the bullets from a gun is flying and missing one of the lead characters may be rather just annoying reading.
