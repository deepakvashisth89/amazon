Jack Chalker is an amazing writer who can pull you into his books on page one. His ideas and imagination are so far beyond other writers that few can challenge his expanse of mind.Yet his writing is so well done that you will feel like it is so much pulp fiction. You will read it and think you are just putting frosting on the cake, but in fact it is an ambrosial delicacy that will make you crave more.Thankfully he was prolific before his passing.But read this book, and when you are done ask yourself if this isn’t the model George Lucas used in explaining his “midiclorians” and how he began to add various force powers later in the Star Wars series.Read moreRead less
