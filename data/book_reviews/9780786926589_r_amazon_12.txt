It's a very good book redefining many rules for the epic roleplaying in D&D 3.5. Players will find a big list of equipment, from the Souldrinker (a very powerful sword) up to the ring of universal elemental immunity; also they'll find new epic prestige classes, rules for epic casts and many more. Gamemasters will also have their fun with the new epic monsters, starting at some "weak" monsters around lvl 22, coming to very cool monsters like the demilich up to extremely strong monsters with e.g. 100 attacks per round (lvl 57) or superepic dragons (up to over lvl 70). So if you want to play you charakters even after level 20, this book is a MUST HAVE.Read moreRead less
