An enjoyable read, that I finished in just two days.  I really liked Natasha Cooper's writing style: scenes were very vivid, and the characters are all 3-dimensional.  Trish Maguire is refreshingly different, likeable and cliché-free as a sleuth.  The plot twist at the end was good - the clues are there, but the reader (this reader anyway) doesn't spot them.  The first book I've read by this author, and I'll definitely be reading more.Read moreRead less
