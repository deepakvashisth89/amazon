This is well written with no major typos or grammatical errors and the plot and characters are very believable. The problem that I had with the story was the heroine, I just could not identify with her. She seemed too uptight, too caught in family rules, too worried about societal norms, too much of it all for me. I'm not a complete free spirit but until the storm I did not really see any gumption in this character. Other characters called me more than the heroine and that is the reason for 3.5 stars. Others will probably really like this story and plot as it does have some good twists and a family murder mystery to solve. All in all a good summer read.Read moreRead less
