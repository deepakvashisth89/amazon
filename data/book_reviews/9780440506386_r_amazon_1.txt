I learned about Ilge and Ames while majoring in education in the 70's. I have over 40 years of experience working with children as a counselor, Montessori teacher and a public school teacher. These books helped me maintain my own sanity when our daughter was a rambunctious 3 yr. old. The Gesell Institute books explain stages of child development from birth to teens and they are incredibly accurate and parent-friendly. That being said, there are a few things to overlook because they were written in an earlier time: The books use the term "Mother" for the primary care giver, which is outdated and there are some differences between gender roles as interpreted then as apposed to now. Ignore the outdated things and focus on the actual child development phases and you will be so relieved at the incredible insight they offer.
