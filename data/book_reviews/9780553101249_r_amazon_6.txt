This is my second time reading this book. Years ago, my company was considering buying an SCS-40 mini-supercomputer whose main virtue was that it emulated a Cray instruction set and able to execute software written for Cray computers, the premier scientific computer of the time.Dr. Bakker was kind enough to let my colleague and I visit his SCS-40 installation and pick his brain about it. When Raptor Red was published, I bought a copy and read it, I enjoyed it then.Upon my second reading, after a lifetime of absorbing whatever science I could, this book impressed me more than my first reading because I was more able to appreciate the depth of Dr. Bakker’s thinking.
