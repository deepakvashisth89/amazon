Commenting not just on the meaning of the text of John's Gospel, but also its credibility, Blomberg has added something truly helpful to the library of Bible students.  No more can we simply teach the text.  We have to be ready to answer the question of others and those we have ourselves as to whether and why a given passage is authentic and/or believable as written. As it happens, the deeper we look into John, the more interesting it gets.
