By this time I have read all the  J A Jance books, some of them more than once.  She is an outstanding author. I started with the J P Bearmont books, went on to the Jonna Brady books, then to the Ali Reynolds books and finally the Walker Family books.  I even really enjoyed the J A Jance Casebook which is a collection of her short stories.  I definitely recommend all of Jance's books, and I recommend they be read in the order written.
