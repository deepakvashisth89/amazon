I bought a copy of "Name Your Baby" when I was a teenager. My family and friends had a ball looking up their names and the names of their family members and friends. The author gives you the classic name, its original spelling, the language from which it was derived, its meaning, the various forms of the name in other languages, and a few famous people who had the name.  I'm so sorry it has gone out of print!I bought this used copy for a woman I met, in her forties, who was having her first baby.  She really appreciated it for giving her all of the information that I've listed above.
