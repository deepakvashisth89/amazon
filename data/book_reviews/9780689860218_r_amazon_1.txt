This is a very good book, the story was well-conceived and well-executed by the author. Apparently this book is intended for teenage readers, but I am 68 and I thoroughly enjoyed it.I’ve read many times that near the end of the war, as Germany struggled to find soldiers, very young German boys, who’d been raised in the Hitler Youth and who were thoroughly indoctrinated in National Socialism and the “master race” propaganda, were being used as replacements.  Veteran soldiers wanted nothing to do with these young true believers who were convinced that Germany would somehow win the war. The boys would shoot veteran soldiers who suggested surrender.  Hughes excellently captures this situation with his characterization of Dieter Hedrick.Hughes also does a wonderful job capturing the really foolish outlook toward the war held by many young American boys.  It seems that teenage boys always hunger to get into the “action” and become war “heroes.” Hughes provides a vivid picture of young recruits suffering through basic training.I would also recommend to any reader the novel The Bridge by Gregor Dorfmeister which is based in part upon the author’s own war experience and which depicts a squad of young teenage German soldiers trying to hold a bridge against advancing Americans.
