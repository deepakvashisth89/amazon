This is an amazing biography from Sharon Gmelch.  The writing style was difficult to understand at times, but that was only because Nan herself didn't speak proper English.  I really felt for this woman, saw how hard her life was and how honorable she was for staying with her husband Mick.  When I finished the last sentence of the book, I cried but it was not a sad, pity cry.  I was crying for the woman that may have had a difficult life with raising so many children, but she was always trying to do what was best for her children.
