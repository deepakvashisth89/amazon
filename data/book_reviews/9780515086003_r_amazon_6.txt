A Tapestry of Dreams by Roberta GellisHistorical Romance - May 1st, 20114 starsLady Audris did not want a husband. Having a husband would mean handing over her home, lands, and wealth to a man and leaving her uncle, who took care of her estate, without a place to stay. Marrying would also deprive her of her freedom to do whatever she wishes. But when she meets Hugh Licorne, she never expects to fall in love and contemplates giving everything up to follow him. Unfortunately, Hugh is a common squire who is not even a knight! He owns no land and had no illustrious lineage. How could such a lowly warrior ever hoped to obtain the king's blessing and her uncle's permission to marry the Lady Audris?Audris was also blessed with the gift of foresight. When Hugh arrived at Audris's castle, Iron Fist, she foresaw that their love would cause the destruction of her home and the people that depended on her. Could she really doom her people for love?This was a highly detailed medieval romance, so much so that sometimes it felt more like a historical book than a romance novel. Still, A Tapestry of Dreams was a delight to read. I felt like I learned more about the day to day life in the medieval times than I did reading an encyclopedia or watching a documentary! Gellis is such a skilled writer that this book doesn't feel boring or slowly paced, even with all the details. In fact, I really liked Audris and Hugh. I enjoyed that even though Audris was in love, she still thought of her uncle and aunt. She's courageous and resourceful. Hugh was also a memorable character. He was honest and deeply honorable. His desire to do right by Audriswas touching. Together they make a wonderful pair that you can't help but cheer them on.If you love a rich medieval romance that does not skimp on the history and details, A Tapestry of Dreams is just the book for you. An enjoyable and deep read, this is a standout among romance novels.Reviewed by Pauline from the Bookaholics Romance ClubRead moreRead less
