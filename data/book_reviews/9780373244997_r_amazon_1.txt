Shannon Blanchard is a contestant on a game show whose prize is a two week stay at a castle in Bonaventure (near France and Italy.) When the only other woman in the competition cannot name the wives of Henry VIII in order Shannon wins.Shannon and her cousin Marcie are flown out a couple weeks later to spend the two weeks at the castle with Princes Devin and Micheal. Michael and Shannon are drawn to each other almost immediately, but they think that there are too many obstacles to their relationship.After spending a day at a secluded house of Michael's they give in to their passion. Several days later Shannon is back in California, missing Michael.Several months go by and Shannon finds out that she is pregnant.
