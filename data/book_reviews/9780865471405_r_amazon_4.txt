I ripped through this book, it's not for everyone but I enjoyed the raw, honest feelings expressed by the young man. Children are much more honest than adults and this speaks out in the exact tone of the anxiety, sexual and non sexual feelings associated with growing up.  I don't read a lot of fiction, in fact, I read maybe one fiction book a year.
