A thoughtful, well researched and engaging history of The Fiend.Peter Stanford explores the nature and possibility of Evil through the many and varied representations of Satan. Is the power of the Devil declining and the seductive nature of temptation losing its charm in an increasingly affluent and amoral age? What responsibility does God have in casting Lucifer into the world to plague us? Is the Devil another face of God?
