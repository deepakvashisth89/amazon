If you like exciting action, that thirst will not be quenched. This is an interesting but somewhat painfully slow read at times. The characters are reasonably well formed, but it’s like there are too many of them. I questioned what purpose some of them really played in enhancing the story line. Taking in account the timeframe this work was published helps, as I the reader, harkened back to what I was reading in the media in the late 1980’’s, early 1990’s.
