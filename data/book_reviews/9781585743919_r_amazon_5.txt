Having not too long ago lost my father, and by my love of the outdoors, I was drawn to this book. What kept me immersed in it was the author's ability to draw insight from the every day, ordinary experiences and, from some not so ordinary ones. He presents insight with sensitivity and accessibility that most anyone can relate to.His description of nature and the outdoors of upstate NY, in which he was so vitally connected by way of his father, is set in beautiful prose. (Several times I re-read sentences simply to enjoy them more than once). He makes his self vulnerable, submits his self as a real human being amid the travails of growing up during the Vietnam war era. His balanced, realistic and sensitive outlook has helped me to have greater empathy and appreciation for the US white working class folks of our declining manufacturing and industrial life ways.Read moreRead less
