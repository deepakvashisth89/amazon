This is hands down my favorite scary story collection. The stories are simple and to the point, which make them that much more frightening. I read this as a child and fell in love. I read part one as well, which is also very good, but this book is the better of the two. If you have a child reluctant to read, who also happens to like a good scare, get them this book.
