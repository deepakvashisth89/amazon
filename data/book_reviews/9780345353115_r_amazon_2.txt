Heinlein's love of his time at the Naval Academy shows in this story of a young man who becomes a cadet at the Space Academy.  Many of the lessons Heinlein learned about adulthood and leadership come through these pages.  He is not preachy, but still conveys strong moral lessons and insight into what Duty and Honor actually mean.  It has a strong science fiction story line about missions unique to space travel, but there is a common theme of taking risks and doing the right thing, even if it is not the easy thing.
