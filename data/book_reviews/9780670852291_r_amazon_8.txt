The book is classic Iris Murdoch - cleverly written, funny, pointed and sad at the same time. We find the characters in the beginning of the book to be "asleep" in the fairy tale sense of the word. They are locked in a kind of childhood trance from which they must emerge in order to grow. Enter the Green Knight. Havoc ensues and lives are knocked around until they are able to break out of their fairy tale slumber. All of the character (except one, perhaps) are immensely likable. Yes, they are very introspective, which may put some people off. But those who stay with it, (savoring the lovely prose along the way) are rewarded in the end with a mysterious, complicated story that will have them thinking for some time - and just might spur a bit of their own growth in the process.Read moreRead less
