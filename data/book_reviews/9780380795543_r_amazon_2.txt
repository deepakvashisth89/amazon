There I are so many things about this novel that I found difficult reading.  The characters seemed to have little realism or depth to them.  The time travel back and forth I found distressing and the scenes of movie life were to skimpy for me to enjoy them even though they may have been realistic.  After saying all that there were things I found moving and that stirred my imagination.  The portrayal of a child accepting the attitudes of her father because she wanted to be loved, the boy who couldn't bring himself to follow his parent's wishes, the attempts to "be normal" all stirred my emotions.  Dreams may be unrealistic but I found myself wishing that they could become real and could change who they were instead of who they thought they should be.I think this book is worth reading and worth exploring the feelings and lack of feeling the characters experience.Read moreRead less
