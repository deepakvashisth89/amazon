Parts of this novel were well written and I enjoyed them,but the author seemed to miss the mark. I was hoping though that the main character would go back with Jesus and his faith would be restored as he watched the biblical story unfold, since that seemed to be what the author had in mind at first.  The writer seemed to get lost somewhere along the way though, wondering away from the story of Jesus. He pretty much abandoned the fact that the character was searching for the truth, the fact that this young man was to grow into the Messiah of the bible.  The writer really lost any hope of anything higher than 2 stars though when it was revealed that he had been telling his story to John Denver.  John Denver? Really?  What was the point?
