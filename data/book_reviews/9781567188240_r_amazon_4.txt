The subtitle is "Recipes for Cooking up a Delicious Life," and I couldn't agree more. This book is filled with comfort foods and little inspirational tips to help you make the most of your life. I've already tried the Sangria of the Gods and the Upside Down Apple Gingerbread Cake -- both were amazing.
