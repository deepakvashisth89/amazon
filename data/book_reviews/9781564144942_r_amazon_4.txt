The book is a good reference work, with plenty of background and overview.  I agree with the other reviewers that the book doesn't provide enough on the visualization or energy concentration aspects of the spell casting or development.  Having said that, the book is a great read for anyone interested in the Craft, has a great set of references and other information to refer to, and is a must before deciding to walk down the path as a practitioner.
