A pretty good Trek read involving the Romulans and an annoying alien who has a propensity for theatrics, right down to the "behind the curtain" motif that's associated with a Stage Play.  This book, like Gerrorld's "The Galactic Whirlpool" sticks with me for some reason.It's actually well written, pretty irreverent at times, and has a good deal of energy from the original characters injected into it.  Perhaps that's why I have such fond memories of it, but perhaps also why I can't remember too much of it other than it was a fairly good read.If you're an original series Star Trek fan, then this should prove entertaining.
