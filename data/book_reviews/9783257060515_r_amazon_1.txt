In this, the second of Donna Leon's novels about Guido Brunetti set in Venice, Italy, a young man's body is found floating in one of the canals, apparently the victim of a robbery gone wrong. As police Commissioner Brunetti delves further into the case, however, the cause of the death of the young American soldier - as it turns out - becomes ever murkier, and the involvement of the American military, wealthy businessmen, high-level politicians, the Mafia, and, in the end, his own father-in-law creates a poisonous brew that makes a satisfactory conclusion impossible for Brunetti. As for many in real life, he is left with the best he can do.This is a deeply moving story of an honest man who works, with some others of character, in a world, not only tainted by dishonesty, but built upon it.
