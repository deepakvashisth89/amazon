Schickler's latest novel, Sweet and Vicious, is a fast-paced, delightful read.  While I thought the characters were a bit implausible, the plot worked for me.  This book reads like a softer, gentler episode of "The Sopranos".  I found myself caught up in the overall motion of the story and I enjoyed reading about Henry and Grace's cross-country adventure.  The secondary characters were entertaining, as well.I'm surprised to see so many negative reviews of this book.  True, if you're looking for highbrow fiction, then this isn't the novel for you.  However, if you're looking for a unique story that's not too terribly taxing on the brain cells, then Sweet and Vicious will deliver.Read moreRead less
