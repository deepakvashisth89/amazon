Amazing book. Got me hooked right away. The voiciness of the narrator reminds me a lot of The Catcher in the Rye. He has such a strong character. This one will appeal to older teens who have gone through hell and back and want to read real books about real issues that they can relate to. It will also teach teens lucky enough to not have experienced those things that they happen, and perhaps increase their empathy for those that do have to endure them. Full of heart, suspense, and a feel good ending that will stay with you long after reading the last page. This story is a true champion of the underdogs.Read moreRead less
