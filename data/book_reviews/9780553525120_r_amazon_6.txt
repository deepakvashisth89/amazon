If you are looking for a light, undemanding read, look elsewhere. However, Neville, who may be on the inner Magic Circle herself, does write an intriguing book.The characters and action are not what kept me interested. It was the history and some surprising points of view that fascinated this reader. It is helpful to jot down a family tree as you wade through this novel. Another way to read it is to read the chapters that take place 30 years after the time of Christ first. Then the chapters that are set in the 1970's.I have read The Eight and The Fire. The Eight is a better novel. The Magic Circle may hold some secrets that are more valuable than a plot. What Neville is trying to do in this novel, I am not sure.
