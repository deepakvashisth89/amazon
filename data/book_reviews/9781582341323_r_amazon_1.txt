I highly recommend this book to fans of Holmes and students or admirers of Tibet.The book describes what Holmes was up to during the period he was presumed dead. The tale brilliantly folds into the Holmes canon and gives satisfying explanations for certain of Holmes's behaviors. And it's a ripping yarn, full of the mysteries of the (then) exotic East!It's also an amazing piece of work by a writer whose native language is Tibetan. To my critical ear, Norbu flawlessly recreates 19th-century British English, as well as English as spoken by an Indian civil servant and Holmes's particular voice.One cool thing I discovered is that if you Google the place names in the book, you can locate old photos of the towns Holmes and his party pass through on the way through India to Tibet. It's quite mind-bending!Note that this is the novel also titled "The Mandela of Sherlock Holmes." There are more reviews there; also spoilers, so beware.Read moreRead less
