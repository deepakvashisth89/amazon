I have mixed emotions about this book.It a book that drags the reader kicking and screaming through the lives of several characters.  It's good because you really end up liking and caring about the characters.  It's bad because you end up caring about the characters and all their flaws.Some of the characters, at least for sections of their life, come directly out of 'characters' in my life.
