This little book contains page after page of razor-sharp insights into why it is so easy to fall for the 'wrong' person. I was especially taken with the examples from movies and literature. The charm of the work is the focus on the well-springs of the underlying human needs that account for the mostly misguided sexual attractions that we wishfully imagine are love.
