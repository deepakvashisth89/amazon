Max, the kind hearted goof had given up on the idea of working a full time legitimate job so he supports himself, his long time wife, and sundry relatives and hangers on in the Ravenswood (a Chicago neighborhood) house in which he grew up with penny ante schemes and scams.  He finds a north shore lady's misplaced designer purse full of cash and credit cards and gets it into his head that he must do the right thing and find her and return it.  Kind of a love note about Chicago and its suburbs giving snippets of history on how certain streets and landmarks came to be named and other interesting if obscure facts and legends.
