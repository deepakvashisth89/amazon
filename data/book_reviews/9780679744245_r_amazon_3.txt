The whole thing reeks of self-pity: the lonely girl who doesn't have a college degree selling tickets in a porn cinema (in the final decade before the internet brought hard-core to everyone's fingertips); the cinema owner who dreams of art-house films; the girl's early-death sister and her vaguely-hinted unhappy family; the pimply nephew of the cinema owner who is pushed into taking the girl out ...On and on it goes, the easy-sell, grey cynicism which passes for cleverness.
