I was aware of this book for some time before purchasing it as at first I thought this was a non fiction book. It is an excellent novel based on fact and has similarities in theme to Umberto Ecos 'Name of the Rose'. I am a big fan of Philips Bernie gunter series and this is of similar high standard. The copy I bought is an American import and it is inexplicable as to why this book has not been published in the Uk as it was published in 2002.
