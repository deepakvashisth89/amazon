Martha Grimes novels have strangeness to them, she takes the cosy mystery, juvenile fiction, and traditionalmystery series and throws it all together with equal measures of humour and melancholy to produce this long runningdetective series that I find addictive and comforting.  I don't like cosy mysteries but I am a sucker for off beat stuff and Grimes writes outside the box.
