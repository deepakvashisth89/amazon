I happened to read the 2nd book in Simon Brett’s Mrs Pargeter series first, and preferred it, which is the only reason for 4 stars. However, this start to the series is delightful!  I particularly appreciate that this book, written 40 years ago, does not bend to the new ‘rules’ of inclusivity and political correctness and retains it’s very, very old-fashioned British-ness in the way of an Agatha Christie mystery. Mrs Pargeter’s presumably diseased husband, whose mysterious career has left Mrs Pargeter quite wealthy, put her in touch with colleagues who have interesting skills, such as safe-cracking, which they are only too happy to put to use out of their fondness for both Mr and Mrs Pargeter.  Thus, when the exceedingly competent Mrs Pargeter suspects murder, she has Mr Pargeter’s friends to call upon when their useful skills are needed to help her investigate.
