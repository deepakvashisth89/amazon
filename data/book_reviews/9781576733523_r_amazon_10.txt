Four people are specially invited by God to go to a town called Haven, and there given 'training'. The purpose of the exercise is for them to work out for themselves, but by the end of their visit they have to make the decision whether or not to truly follow God, even when his instructions may not seem to make sense. What will they each decide? And what of the others who are drawn into the adventure? Finally, what effect will their decision have on the life of each 'after Haven'?I have a feeling I'll be coming back to reread this one - something I don't do with many books now, because there are just  so many new ones to choose from. Though the premise is somewhat 'weird', inasmuch as it involves God acting in our lives in a way we don't see him ever doing now, it made for a very thought-provoking and inspiring book.
