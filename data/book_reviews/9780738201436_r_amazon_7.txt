I haven't finished the book.  When reading it I found that the author is not accurate in descriptions of physical ideas. The latter concept was developed much later after Newtonian time and was not known to Newton or Galileo.So the reader who is in science profession finds this disturbing and this puts under question historical propositions in the book about Newton's life where one may not have knowledge of.
