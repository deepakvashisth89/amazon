Lovely old book and fun reading. This was an early book, before his big hits. He was truly a back-packer with a classic stubborn donkey as a companion. The last third, however, reads like experimentation with descriptive writing styles. The first 2/3 have occasional over-the-top descriptive passages, but it is a travel book, after all.
