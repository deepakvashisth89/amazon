A magnificent true adventure monologue.  Exquisitely written.  A majestic lyrical compelling narrative of the Middle East during WWI, including, its diverse cultural loyalties, languages, religions, geography.  Insightful portrayal, via the lens of an Oxford educated British intelligence officer, who courageously participated in the hazardous guerrilla warfare with the Arabs against the Turks and Germans.  All for love of England.For instance, in describing military strategy:  "... [W]e went to [British Middle East Commander-In-Chief General] Allenby, who said  ... he would make a grand attack....  Our role would be as laid down ... we must make the Deraa raid on the two thousand new camels" (p. 529).Surprisingly and unexpectedly, Lawrence's verbiage expresses modern vernacular prose, despite that this extraordinary memoir was composed 100 years ago.  Touching, entertaining, introspective.  Consequently, I read, at a snail's pace, to fully savor Lawrence's captivating experiences.  Highly recommend for ALL.Moreover, an epic movie was filmed concerning this book entitled "Lawrence Of Arabia" starring Peter O'Toole, Alec Guinness, Anthony Quinn.  This stupendous revelation surpasses that majestic production.For more about Lawrence, highly recommend the outstanding authorized biography by Jeremy Wilson entitled "Lawrence Of Arabia."Read moreRead less
