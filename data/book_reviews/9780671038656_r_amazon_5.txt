If you've read your way through Carl Hiaissen, Elmore Leonard, and James  W. Hall but haven't discovered Pete Hautman,  "Mrs. Million" is a  great place to start.  Reviewers who were expecting big action or complex  plotting in this book were probably disappointed, but only because they  missed the point.  Hautman's work is very easy to escape into because it IS  odd-ball.  It doesn't have to make sense!The characters in this book,  like in those in "Short Money," are very offbeat, but immensely  likeable.  Except for the villains, of course, who are equally offbeat but  easy to despise.  But like Hiaissen's villains, they always get what's  coming to them.I've only read two of Hautman's books, but I'm using an  Amazon gift certificate to stock up (and then fortify my local library).  Keep up the good work, Mr. Hautman.Read moreRead less
