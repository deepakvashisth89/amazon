The Journal of Leroy Jeremiah Jones, a Fugitive Slave by K.J. McWilliams, is a fun, read-aloud book for children. Set in the Appalachian region of Alabama, young Jeremiah Jones, 'knowd here 'bout's 's THE ROCK,' is taught an early lesson about leadership when his pappy dies. Struggling to keep his family together while working for an oppressive master, Leroy befriends the master's young boy, who is his age. Like many youngsters would do in the same setting, they frolic and get into mischief on the rice plantation. When rumors begin to surface about changes on the plantation that threaten to split up his family forever, Leroy springs to action with a plan to escape. In the book are photos and etchings of slave life from the era, that give the reader a strong, historical context for the story.
