This book came out before the more recent show, Breaking Bonaduce, and it certainly reveals how clever, honest and funny Bonaduce can be - in print. Perhaps it caught the public's attention and saved his flagging celebrity. It was certainly an entertaining read..at the time.But that was then and it seems that Bonaduce has had to go to more extreme lengths to maintain the public's attention. That includes his most recent decision to appear on a reality show, Breaking Bonaduce.  Unfortunately, having watched nearly every episode of Breaking Bonaduce, I can't help but wonder at where his ego ends and the truth begins. It has given me a different perspective on the book, if only in hindsight.In the book (just as on the television show) he SEEMS to be honest about his drug addiction, his rehab, and his contrition. Far from being a braggart, he actually disparages himself, leading one to think that perhaps he is facing up to unpleasant realities - and making some serious changes in his life.Perhaps he truly was doing so when he wrote this book. But my opinion of him has dropped more than a few notches since the recent show appeared and I saw his willingness to expose not just himself but his wife, his children and a therapist (who seem clearly baffled and out of his element at times) to his erratic behavior - and "erratic" is putting it nicely.It is one thing to reveal himself on the written page but quite another to expose his family to public humiliation. The show has  gotten plenty of attention, but the type that usually accompanies car wrecks- you can't HELP but look... but mostly in horror.
