I fell in love with Oscar Peterson's music listening to a radio recording at age 11.  As a (struggling) student of classical piano at the time, I felt as if struck by lightening by the revelation that a piano could sound and feel the way the piano sounded on the radio that night 43 years ago.Oscar Peterson once said in an interview:  “I love what Pablo Picasso did, because he was an adventurous person. But I was really impressed by his cubist-period, because when I first saw it, my first look, I couldn’t imagine what he was trying to portray. And then as I stood there staring at the painting, a particular painting, it suddenly shifted visually for me and I saw corners and depth and distances between different parts of the person’s body that he portrayed. So I find him to be a very important innovator as for making people see things, and I wish I could make people hear things the way he makes them see things.”Thousands of listening hours later, including several concerts, my profound love for the Maestro's musical virtuosity has inspired my professional life (as a photographer) and enriched my personal life in ways I could never have foreseen back in 1975.  Thank you Mr. Peterson!
