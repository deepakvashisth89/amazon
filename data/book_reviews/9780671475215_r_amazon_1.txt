The Street Bird refers to a pet vulture that belongs to an obeah man named Uncle Wisi.  In Surinam, vultures are called street birds because they do a fine job of cleaning up the streets. Uncle Wisi’s tame vulture is, of course, the only one of its kind in Amsterdam.The crime that brings the murder squad to the red light district is the gunning down of a superpimp who was the prince of the quarter. He was bad to the bone. His two competing superpimps are the most obvious suspects but there are other possibilities too...Uncle Wisi is a person of interest, because he seems to have shared some of his power with the victim. He’s an amazing healer.  No one suspects him of shooting off the murder weapon — but could he have manipulated a killing somehow through his potent magic rituals?We get a taste of those rituals, as the Commissaris and then Adjutant Grijpstra visit the witch doctor.There are philosophical musings, as usual in this series. There are also fun action scenes including a wild car chase.  The solution of the crime is unexpected and stirs up ethical quandaries.This is a particularly exotic mystery, steeped in black magic and set in the steamy world of Amsterdam’s whores.Read moreRead less
