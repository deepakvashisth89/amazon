While reading the book, I had the persistent feeling that something in the book didn't fully connect. The book is about a pretty narcissistic middle aged man who is going through money issues, and has to confront the state of his life that requires him asking money from his aging and bitter father. The book is written in an interesting way, and yet I can't say I developed any sympathy for the main character; He is unwilling to confront both reality, and the pain he is causing both his father and his family by ignoring that reality. Even his supposed affection for his kids seems just an excuse to feel sorry for himself.There isn't much happening in the book in terms of story line or character arch - it is more of an attempt at introspection by a troubled mind, and in that it is somewhat interesting, and I found myself kind of wanting to keep on reading till the end, but not too excitedly.Read moreRead less
