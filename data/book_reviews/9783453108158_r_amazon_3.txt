As a Dean Koontz story, you really can't go wrong here with this novella. About halfway through, I thought I knew where this was going but Dean outsmarts the reader again by actually taking our theory and presenting it as a possibility in the book. Does it prove right? I'm not going to spoil it here, but suffice it to say you'll want to stick around for the ending to see.The reader here does a great job of keeping all the voices different. Between Chase and "The Judge", things could have gotten muddled, but he really puts great emotion into the story. The story was originally part of a larger collection, and I'm not sure why Brilliance Audio only released this rather than the whole thing, but I'm glad to have it.It's not Dean's best work, but I would easily say it's from one of his peak writing periods as far as quality goes.
