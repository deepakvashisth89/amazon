This little novel is like a happy soap opera.  There are numerous characters and multiple plots; all the characters are different and likable and all the plots have happy endings. The lead character owns a dance studio besides being the wife, mother, employer and sister of the other players.  It's a slice of life, in her  busy family, that doesn't let the logistics of day-to-day living impede moving the story forward.
