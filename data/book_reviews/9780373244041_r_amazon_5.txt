There were several things I loved about this story - Dave Currick being the greatest. I liked his easy-going style. The other major positive was the clever humor. That made it enjoyable to read. The way the past of the two characters, Dave and Mattie, was interwoven with the present was also well done. However, Mattie began annoying me about halfway in, and by the end, really annoyed me. Her need to do things on her own to the point of hurting herself and her relationship did not make sense to me. At some point, she really needed to let go of her stubbornness. The storyline flowed at a nice pace, despite some minor editing errors. Book did contain a lot of sexual content of a moderate nature. I was less offended though because the couple was legally married and obviously in love with each other. Overall, interesting enough of a storyline that it hooked me and kept me reading.Read moreRead less
