In book five of this series, author Janwillem van de Wetering broke up his detective trio of Grijpstra, de Gier and the commissaris and sent two of them off to Japan to help solve a mystery. He’s done the same thing here, sending de Gier and the commissaris off to Maine. They don’t go to solve a crime, but they find one when they get there and help the local sheriff investigate it. I like this book better than I did the one set in Japan, but I still prefer it when the characters stay in Amsterdam and investigate quirky Netherlanders. Here they end up meeting and investigating quirky Americans, and the results just aren’t the same. I do like the depiction of small town Maine and its police, and the mystery is a clever one, so I rate this one higher than the one set in Japan, but it’s not the best of the series, in my opinion. (One other note: There is no actual massacre, as in a bloody attack with a lot of people being killed at once, so if that sort of thing puts you off, don’t worry.
