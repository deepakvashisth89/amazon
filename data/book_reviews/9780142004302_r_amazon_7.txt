I first read the third book in this series..."Sovereign". I enjoyed it so much I've purchase several more of the books in the series including this first book in the series...Dissolution. CJ Sansom gives us an interesting story with a lot of history of the times. In this case, he details life within a particular hypothetical monastery during their Dissolution as decreed by Henry VIII and engineered by Thomas Cromwell. The writing is suspenseful without being unrealistic. You can relate to the characters while imagining the circumstances actually happening. You don't have to stretch credibility to enjoy the story. The main protagonist, Matthew Shardlake, isn't the slick, handsome rake who kicks butt and seduces women. He's actually a self-conscious hunchback, who also happens to be intelligent but with normal human flaws, like in this story, jealousy, envy and contempt. He also happens to be a good detective with exceptional reasoning skills. Throw in the fact that he struggles with his own ethical role and morality while working for the Crown and you have a complex lead character that you can relate to and sympathize with.If you enjoy a realistic storyline, with lots of great history of the period, and a suspenseful narrative that is very challenging to figure out you'll like this book. I read about 5 books a month and I've been looking for an author/series that grabs me enough to stay with the whole series. Based on the first two books I've read this series is a keeper. I highly recommend it, and will read all the Matthew Shardlake series books.Read moreRead less
