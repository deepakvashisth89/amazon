This is the first Willa Cather writing I've undertaken to read--and probably the last.  She's supposed to be such a big deal classic writer, but I must be missing something.  I had to force myself to finish it--I hate loose ends.  The saving feature of the book was the excellent descriptions of both the starting out setting in Europe and the remaining setting, pretty much the entire Southwest of North America.  You could practically visualize the beauty and vastness of the all but unsettled land, but the story itself was a total drag and went nowhere.  A priest left Europe and went to the Southwest and traveled around among the Hispanics in a bunch of random little stories and then died.  Maybe some of her other things are better, but I don't know if I want to gamble on it.Joyce CoreyRead moreRead less
