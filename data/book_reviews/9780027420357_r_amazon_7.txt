So this book is supposed to teach about recycling. It's a backwards countdown from various animals doing things that are green (ie 12 alligators carpooling).I like the concept, but there is only 1 sentence per pg with little explanation for the kids as to why it's important to recycle. The pictures are OK, but the book is so thin that it was bent after the first reading.
