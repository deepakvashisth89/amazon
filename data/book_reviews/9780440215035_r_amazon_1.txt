A mother whose children were killed in a car accident.  An old woman whose husband lies in the grip of Alzheimer's.  A man who longs for the sister he loved as a child.  A fifteen-year-old girl who aborted her baby.  A little boy whose mother abandoned him as an infant.  One by one these and others are led by the ones they grieve for to the hidden town of Revenant.  The ghosts are not merely of the dead, but of those who once were or might have been.  Revenant feeds on the grief of the living.  Some will break free of their emotional chains; others will be consumed.  In her third solo novel, Melanie Tem again displays her mastery and understanding of human nature with astounding depth.  Though her award-winning first novel, 'Prodigal', remains my favourite, this book is also remarkable in it's way.  Truly one of the finest writers of original, modern, *meaningful* horror fiction.
