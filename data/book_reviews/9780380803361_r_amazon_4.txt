On the whole, I really enjoyed this book, and I think it is a worthwhile read for someone already well read on the slave history, but it is such a narrow occurrence that it may not be useful for some just getting beginning to dive into American slavery.  It is the story of Celia, a slave who murdered her master in Missouri, but it does not give the vast historical understanding that some other texts can.
