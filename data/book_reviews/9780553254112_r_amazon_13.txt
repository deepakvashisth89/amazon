I'd never read any Margery Allingham but I kept coming across shining endorsements of her work from respected authors and critics - 'the very best of the golden age' etc. I decided to dip into her oeuvre via this duo of novellas.I liked them. Ms Allingham shows herself adept at the novella format. Characters, place, time and atmosphere are quickly developed. The clever plots then unfold to satisfying, if disturbing, conclusions.These stories may be short but they have complexity and depth.Is Ms Allingham to be placed above the sainted Agatha, Ms Sayers and Ms Marsh?
