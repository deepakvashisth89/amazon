In Beautiful Outlaw, John Eldredge does something both bold and refreshing: he invites us to meet Jesus Christ not as a distant religious symbol, but as a real man—fully alive, deeply masculine, tender, strong, playful, and utterly uncompromising in truth...This is a book about personality. Eldredge paints Jesus as captivating and fearless: a man who laughs, asks piercing questions, confronts hypocrisy head-on, protects the vulnerable, and loves with ferocity. The title says it all: Jesus is “beautiful” in His goodness and compassion, and an “outlaw” to systems of power, pride, and moral preening. Jesus was a rebel of his times, sceptical of organised religion, authentic in his outreach to ordinary people.What stands out is how affirming this portrayal is for men. Jesus is not neutered, passive, or apologetic. He is courageous, emotionally present, decisive, and self-sacrificial. For anyone tired of caricatures that frame masculinity as something to be restrained or ashamed of, Beautiful Outlaw is quietly revolutionary. It presents Christ as the ultimate model of integrated masculinity: strength under control, authority exercised in service, and confidence rooted in love."Everyone talks about his “great acts of humility, faith, and compassion.” What about his great acts of playfulness, or cunning? What about his brilliance, his wit, his irreverence, the scandalous freedom with which Jesus lives, his exasperation and impatience? Not to mention his humanity; we have nearly forgotten he was a man."For Christians, the book rekindles awe and intimacy with Christ. For sceptics, it challenges the shallow stereotypes of Jesus that dominate popular culture. And for anyone searching for meaning, purpose, and a compelling vision of the good life, it offers a striking reminder: Christianity is not about following rules—it’s about following a Person.Published in 2011, but timeless by many perspectives.
