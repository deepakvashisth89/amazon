Thomas Perry is an outstanding writer, and I’ve loved all his books that I’ve read.  This one, not quite as much.  I think this is one of his earlier works, and it shows in the completely unbelievable story and lack of character development.  In Dead Aim, a middle-aged man rescues a young woman who tried to drown herself.  When she actually does kill herself a day later, he becomes obsessed with her, feeling he could have done more, said something to change her mind, and wondering what led her to this point.  He hires a private investigator to help him, and they start searching through the woman’s past, finding a boyfriend who was murdered; and at this point the story falls off a cliff.  It turns out there is a school high up in the mountains that trains people in “self defense” for an astronomical fee, but certain special students are given the further opportunity to learn “hunting”, and are given a support team to go hunt a human they may wish to kill.  The dead girl, after learning her boyfriend was a world class jerk, went to this school and became one of the special students.  I’m sure you can guess the rest of the story, like how the middle-aged and untrained hero manages to wipe out a half dozen highly skilled, armed killers.  The story telling was slow and dry, and the characters were bland and not well developed .  The plot was ridiculous, and the book felt like a waste of time.  Do yourself a favor – skip this one and go pick up one of the “Butcher’s Boy” books, or a “Jane Whitefield” book instead.  He’s a great writer, but this is not a great book.Read moreRead less
