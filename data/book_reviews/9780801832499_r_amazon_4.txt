I understand that this book is a classic of sorts in Theodore Roosevelt studies, but it was simply too long and long-winded to keep my attention (although I did read the entire book).  He examines very carefully much of Roosevelt's foreign policy (see the review below mine if you want to read a couple pages of info), and for that I commend him.
