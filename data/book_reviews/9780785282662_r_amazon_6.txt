Barbara Scott and Carrie Younce have written an important story showing us what is at play in the supernatural when human beings dabble in the occult, make life and death decisions that are influenced by evil beings and take lightly the power and work of Satan and his minions in the world today. Indeed the book is not for the faint of heart as characters struggle with unimaginable evil and terror, bloodshed and death. You will come away from reading the book knowing that there is a very real devil and he is out to destroy people. You will also learn that God has made the provision for the protection of those who turn to him and seek him. This fast-paced read will keep you on the edge of your seat.Read moreRead less
