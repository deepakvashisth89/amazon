A good recreational read.  The only thing I can add to previous reviews is that while there are some superficial similarities to Patrick OBrian's writing, there are also some significant differences.  O'Brian's writing tends towards more descriptive detail and his plotting doesn't move nearly as fast as Drake does.  With regards to setting, the Republic of Cinnabar matches the middle Roman Republic far better than it does Georgian England and the conflict reminds me more of the Punic wars than the Napoleonic wars.That being stated, like many readers of science fiction, I have a bad habit of trying to fit settings into frameworks I am familiar with rather than taking the setting as it is.  My only excuse is that the undeniable superficial resemblance of the two main characters to O'Brian's Aubrey and Maturin force this type of comparison.The main characters are compelling and the story is a good tale;I highly recommend it.  It is, however, NOT O'Brian - which is a good thing.  I consider it to be more an homage than a copy.Read moreRead less
