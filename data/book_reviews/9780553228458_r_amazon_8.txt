How ever can I thank you for finding this book for me?  I owned a paper-back copy for many years and it has accompanied me to various parts of the world Dubai, America several times, Bangladesh, Med cruise etc etc. I must have read and re-read this book at least 15 times, putting it away for a while, alternating with Wilbur Smith's Egyptian trilogy about Taita the slave.  Zemindar was my constant companion to many beaches, unfortunately becoming battered, falling to pieces and sun burned almost to a crisp.  Perhaps you can imagine my delight when, having searched on your pages for another copy, I discovered a hard-back version.  It took but a few days for it to arrive and your service can not be beaten.  Thank you so much, I relish this thrilling tale which leads to the Indian mutiny and these colourful evocative characters all over again and will continue to do so many times.  Zemindar is a story of epic proportions that grips the reader through brilliant narrative and descriptive prowess.  If you like history, romance with a touch of intrigue, disasters that make you weep sad tears, thrill with adventure and delight in the finale, this one's for YOU!Read moreRead less
