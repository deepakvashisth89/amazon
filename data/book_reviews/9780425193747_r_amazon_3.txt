While George Harrar's "The Spinning Man" is technically well written it is neither gripping nor compelling.Harrar's tale revolves around 50 year old Pearce College philosophy professor Evan Birch.  Due to circumstantial evidence, Birch is the prime suspect in the disappearance of a 16 year old cheerleader who was last seen working an information booth in a local park.  The trouble begins when Birch's grey VW Jetta was observed at the park on the day of the disappearance.The crux of the story revolves around the changes that occur in those characters that are an integral part of Birch's life.  Being implicated of a crime puts a strain on Birch's wife Ellen and their relationship.  Birch's twin 10 year old sons Adam and Zed start manifesting behavioral changes as a result of the on going investigation conducted by a local detective.  Birch becomes a pariah among his colleagues at the university.  That unfortunately is about all there is to the story.  As I said, not particularly riveting.Read moreRead less
