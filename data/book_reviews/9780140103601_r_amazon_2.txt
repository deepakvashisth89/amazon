This novel is comprised of lovely symbolic episodes of a young Greek girl's life with her cruel, military father and her weak, submissive mother.  The story deals with her building skills to deal with the heartrending treatment disbursed to her by her father - - not letting him know how deeply she feels his callous and inhuman treatment.  She learns about life through animals, friendship, sexual experience and family.  This is a beautifully written and extraordinarily insightful book.Read moreRead less
