I see why the other reviewer complained about the shortage of alphabets, and can only surmise they were counting letters, not whole alphabets when they created the hyperbolic title. While I would have liked more charts, I imagine that cramming 500 alphabets into one book would make it at least 500 pages long--or micrographically small charts, so I'm content with the number of variations. The charts themselves are large enough to read easily, well colored, and simple to follow. Because of the specific craft I'm using them for--loom-beaded straps--I'd appreciate knowing how many rows high each alphabet runs, but it's a simple matter to count the squares.
