I read this book years ago. I got it from my local library and it reminded me a lot of the truth and dare trap. Skip is new in town, and has a sister with a mental disability. She sits on her building stoop and watches the people go by. One of the people is a girl named jean. She is beautiful and powerful. She has a group that skip wants desperately to get into, a group that she dares to do bad and often embarrassing things. Jean doesn't care that she's being mean or cruel or even who she's hurting.  Her home situation isn't great and sometimes you feel bad for her, but then she does something awful and that feeling passes.
