I loved Patti Berg's If I Can't Have You when it first came out. I recently reread the e-book version and love it even more. Trevor Montgomery comes to life in this book--I feel as if I should be able to watch his old movies, find his memorabilia, and read about his life as the dashing movie star right up there with Clark Gable, Errol Flynn, and Cary Grant. Anyone who's ever fallen in love with a movie star will enjoy this book. But aside from that, there's also the very down-to-earth story of a man who drinks too much and lives too hard...and knows how it can (and does) destroy a life. Highly recommended!Read moreRead less
