For those not "in the know", Ruth Plumly Thompson wrote more Oz books than L. Frank Baum.  She finished his last Oz book, The Royal Book of Oz, and went on to produce many more, around 20.This is one I have not seen, and was very happy to read via Kindle.  The story is full of action and not as insipid as a few of her stories can be.  The only down side is the lack of drawings; I really love O'Neill's drawings and do not know why there are few if any electronic versions of Oz tales with his drawings included.  They certainly add to the print versions of the books.Anyone new to Oz should get the Baum series first, then go on to Thompson in my opinion.
