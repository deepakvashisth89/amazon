Excellent book.It is quite difficult to write a book that is at once academically sound yet enjoyable to read.  Jamie Sayen has done precisely that.  Albert Einstein's scientific work may be difficult to understand, but his dealings with the issues that we all face - from the mundane to the philosophical - are quite understandable.  We might not sympathize with all his actions, but it is fascinating to see an extremely competent mind at work.This book chips away some of Einstein's pedestal, but the human it reveals is quite worth the effort.Read moreRead less
