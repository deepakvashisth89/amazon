It is not often I feel compelled to write a review of a book, but I have to say this is a book that hooks you from the start and you just cannot put it down !  Sam and Jaine are brilliant hero and heroine, they compliment each other so well, the humour they share with each other just hit the right note, and with the added bonus of a crazed killer after Jaine and her friends because of their "List" then you are in for a lovely read.  I was sorry when I had finished the book, I was enjoying that much.  Certainly a five star book for me!.Read moreRead less
