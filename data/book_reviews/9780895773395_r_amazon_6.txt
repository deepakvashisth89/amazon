Surprisingly this book, written many years ago, was very, very funny.  If you are sensitive to prejudice the book is not for you.  He makes fun of most people in other countries but you have to be able to look past that and simply enjoy the writing.  It is a book about a cruise that goes transatlantic and then stops at many ports in Europe.  Mark Twain was a very gift writer.  He is very descriptive and you can actually get a good visual of what he is seeing.  If you are going to travel to Europe or you have travelled to Europe you will get a real kick out of it.
