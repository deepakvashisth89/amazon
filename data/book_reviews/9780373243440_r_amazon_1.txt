LOST AND FOUND GROOM is a standalone read with a happy ending.  Good story, great hero.  He is a pilot, flies food and medical supplies into a government corrupt country. He also flies children desperately in need of medical supplies to hospitals in safe countries.  No one knows who he is.  The government would stop him if they can find out.  He is legend in the area and a hero.  If you had a loved one in need of rescuing, this is the guy you would want to go after them.  Balls of steel.  Wow. What a  guy.The story. She is a journalist after the story of who this guy is so she can put it all in print to further her career.  There is a hurricane brewing in the area and she refuses to leave, determined to find him.  He knows who she is and what she is after.  As the hurricane hits, he saves her life by pulling her to shelter, etc.  Of course, there is a wild time going on because she is sure they are going to die. She thinks this guy cannot speak English and she really has no idea who he is other than the name he gives her.She is pregnant. She tries to find the guy by the name he gave her with no luck. She has the baby and proceeds to raise him on her own in Wyoming.  He shows up on her doorstep 2 years later to claim his son and make them a family.Good so far.  Her heroine, in my opinion, was a bitch.  She is so sure she has the answer to everything.  Because her father, an air force pilot, was killed she thinks that everyone that flies a plane is going to die and leave their family behind because that is what her father did.  Since the hero is a pilot, she wants no part of him, even tho she know she really loves him.The fear just about drove me up the wall.  She does not want him to fly into areas and help people desperately in need of his skill, etc.  That seemed a bit unreal to me.  So, no policeman can go to work, or firefighter, or rescue personnel because they all risk getting killed every day.  They must sit at home under their wife and be safe.  Her thinking was just stupid to me.  She wants to protect her 2 year old son from being hurt.  Really, you can keep another human being from suffering pain?  In what existence does that happen.  Besides he is 2, what is he going to remember?  She was just unreasonable time after time after time after time. Oh she got on my last nerve.  Now this was a world trotting journalist before she got pregnant.  So let's assume she wasn't stupid, she just acted that way.  She was so good at analyzing him and telling him what all his problems were but she never looked in the mirror and said,do you realize how screwed up you are? Plus, people get killed every day just driving to work or play, you can't prevent someone dying.  It is going to happen.  People get killed riding a bike, that is not dangerous work, it is play and yet they get killed all the time, so to her, no riding bikes.  Just dumb way to live. Oh well, enough.  I really liked the story despite my problems with her.  A great hero and extra characters.  I would definitely recommend it.I was not paid for this review.  I am not a writer nor do I know one.
