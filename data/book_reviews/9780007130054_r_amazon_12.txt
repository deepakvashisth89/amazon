A very interesting book.  I am particularly interested in Marconi's Bournemouth and Poole connection.  Although I have yet to finish it.  I would like to point out some errors: Page 178 Brownsea Island is situated within Poole Harbour, not outside it. One of Charles and Florence van Raalte's daughters names was Margherita not Margarite. I appreciate these points might be considered by some to be trivial, but as an historian I feel it then throws doubt as to the accuracy of the sources used.Geof CurtisNational Trust Brownsea Island Archive TeamRead moreRead less
