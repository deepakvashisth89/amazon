Of the Plainsman books I have read so far, this ranks up at the top.  First i knew nothing of this part of the Indian wars of the 19th C. Second, the characters, both whites and Indians were much more developed than I usually find in Johnston's books.  Usually Indians get a lighter go, but not this time around.  I would say some of the most compelling characters are Indians in this book.  I recommend this one.Read moreRead less
