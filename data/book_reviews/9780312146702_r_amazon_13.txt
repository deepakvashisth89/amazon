I am enjoying this series, even though at times Holmes is frustratingly Doyle-ish....by which I mean the clinging to the "elementary" and use of interpreting minutiae.  There is a faint move to allow of his becoming open to intuition, but merely a whisper, not followed up.  I enjoy the scholarly bent, although more of a leaning towards actual historical fact and less academic (almost rabbinic) musings would be nice.  I disagree with other reviews who label this novel as a bit slow.  The solution may have been disappointingly realistic, but the nuances and emerging feminism are quite fascinating.
