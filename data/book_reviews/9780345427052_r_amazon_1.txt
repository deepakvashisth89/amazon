I could not put this book down.  Not only is Mr. Roberts a wonderful storyteller, but his examples are food for thought. The story of the horse that was claustrophobic was eye opening for me. I was raised by a pioneer family on the family’s ranch and rode from the time I was 3.  Never had I realized a horse could be afraid of tight spaces. The parts of the racing thoroughbreds was exciting and his standing in front of a stallion knowing the horse would not hurt him made me gasp.
