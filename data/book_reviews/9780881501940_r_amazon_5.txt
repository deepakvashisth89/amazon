This is my favorite of the Taylor mysteries. The setting is Cape Cod, and the detective is Asey Mayo, a mysterious handy man with extraordinary gifts, who often loses his target in a chase but gets 'em in the end with brains.  Phoebe Atwood Taylor didn't write these mysteries as serious Who-Dun-Its, so don't expect a puzzle that makes sense ahead of time or bears any resemblance to something true to life as a plot.  Read these for the delights of laughing in the face of disaster, for the descriptions of a diverse cast of characters(the best thing about Octagon House),and details of life on Cape Cod in the Great Depression.
