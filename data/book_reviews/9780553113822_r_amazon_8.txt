This is the last numbered volume in the series of novelizations of Star Trek episodes.  There is one additional unnumbered book in the series, which adapts the Harry Mudd episodes.  James Blish died before he finished this book, so his wife wrote the adaptations of two of the episodes.  She actually did a pretty good job and her efforts are about as good as her husband's.
