I loved this series. I thought they were a little over priced but I bought them all. These books do not have a lot of sex in them even though the main character is gay he does have a partner it is not pages and pages of people getting it on. I really like the main character he is someone you would want to be friends with.I like the way the story shows the changes in society as time progresses. I was  a child in the seventies so it's interesting to read this from an adult perspective. It shows a little about prejudice towards African Americans ,homosexuals , even hippies. You can kind of keep track of the years by events in the book. This author was before his time and probably didn't sell a lot of these when he was alive  but I loved them and was very sad when I closed the last book. This author is no longer living but his Brandstetter character is as excellent as they come. I would recommend this series and I will read it again .Read moreRead less
