Missing the elements of fantasy I enjoy:  magic, sword-fighting, inspirational tackling of adversity, and courageous heroism  -- none of that in the early book.  Tamison creates his own problems through fear, and spends the majority of the book growing up while railing against the unfairness of the world.  The slow paced story and hard-to-identify-with main character lost me as a reader.  When the author began adding long perspectives of other unremarkable characters and further delaying the story I began skimming...  and soon I stopped reading.Not recommended.Read moreRead less
