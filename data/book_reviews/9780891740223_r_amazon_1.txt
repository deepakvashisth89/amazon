One of  the best adventure stories I have read about Alaska, I had read The Silver Hoard written by this author but I have to say this was much better. The first book was about the salmon industry and this was about building a railroad and a bridge in seemingly impossible conditions and area, it was very tense and fast reading with some of the best characters you will read about and great dialogue that was very witty, I can't wait to read another book by this writer.Read moreRead less
