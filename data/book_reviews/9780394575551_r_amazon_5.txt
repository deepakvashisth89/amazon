I love great biographies; best way to learn history.  Ms. Morris is an amazing researcher; all the details enrich the entire timeline of Ms. Luce's extraordinary life.  Rage for Fame is a fast read -  hard to put down.  Pulls many of life's principles together - things never change, history repeats itself, human nature does not change, etc., etc.  Ms. Luce's life certainly illustrates that "women's lib" is nothing new; it's always been there for those who really wanted it.  Clare Luce just happened to live on the timeline of our US/world history (both world wars) that gave her an extraordinary backdrop for all of her ladder climbing accomplishments.  All of these accomplishments are not without a lot of personal pain and all of us know that pain is included in all of our lives; the rich and famous do not escape.  Highly recommend; enjoy.
