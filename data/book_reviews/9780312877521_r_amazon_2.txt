Adam Dawson saw more than his share of action riding with Custer's unit in the Civil War.  Now that the fighting and killing are over, he wants nothing more than to drift peacefully toward the West.  His journey through Texas lands him in the path of Chad Walker, a man who lost his ability to care about anything as a result of the same war.  Chad takes Adam under his wing, giving him a job at the ranch.  Unfortunately, trouble follows the man who still wears the red neckerchief of Custer's soldiers. There Came a Stranger is an interesting read for the history buff and the non-history buff alike.  His straightforward, unembellished writing style starkly portrays a fictional account of life in post war America.  His characters' actions are well defined even through the difficult challenges they face.  Their angst is palpable; the bleakness of the landscape and of life comes alive.
