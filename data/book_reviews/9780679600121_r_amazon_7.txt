I purchased the book both in print, and on CD (42 CD's actually). After listening to the CDs I came away with the opinion that this was a good compromise. Reading the unabridged book would have been very difficult, and taken much more time (probably years). Listening to the book, read by a professional with excellent French diction, allowed me to focus on the story and the characters. Reading the book would have required much re-reading of certain parts. Although listening to such a book has its drawbacks (multiple replays of certain tracks to understand), for me it worked well.In the end, I justify the compromise; through the CDs, I came away exposed to a work of art that I otherwise would not have been.
