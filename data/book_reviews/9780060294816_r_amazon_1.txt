Wonderful evocation of the conflicts and bullying endured by conscientious objectors. Wartime brings out the worst in us, leaving young people in particular torn, at a time when they need solidity in their lives. These characters were all completely believable, and the story valuable in the lessons it teaches. The historical framework is detailed and interesting.
