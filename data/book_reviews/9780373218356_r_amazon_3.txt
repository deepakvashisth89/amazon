As a fan of all three military authors, it was a real boon to find this anthology.In A Military Affair, Lovelace presents a fast paced, action packed story about a female MP and a male photo-journalist on assignment to uncover a rebel weapons cache on a foreign island. Teresa Ross is an MP guarding the plane that brought in a special forensic team to locate the remains of WWII pilot discovered on the island.  The two become intertwined first out of necessity, but later out of love. Generally I don't care for reporters as the main character, but Lovelace does a superb job on fleshing out his character. Pete Quinn comes across as a dynamic man with integrity when it comes to reporting a story.In Comrades in Arms, Lindsay McKenna gives us a true romance with a story of a government translator working with the armed forces in Afghanistan.  This story focuses more on the developing relationship between the two characters, and the Afghanistan background provides a perfect backdrop to progress the story.In Unconditional Surrender by Candace Irvin, we are presented with a love story with a more gritty setting. Danielle Stanton is a POW; kidnapped from her undercover assignment that Capt. Jack Gage finds while he is working trying to infiltrate a renegade faction during the Kosovo conflict.All three stories are compelling and it is a real tribute to these authors who were able to flesh out these stories with so few pages.Read moreRead less
