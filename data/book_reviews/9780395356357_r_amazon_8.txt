When you need to dive deep into Tolkien, this is one of the important milestones on that trek. The title essay is a trenchant attack on those who simply cannot open their minds and hearts to the world of fantasy and how it can reflect reality. Strongly recommended, but do know that some of the other essays are extremely high-toned and for those who have thoroughly imbibed his work, especially his translations of Orfeo, Gawain, etc.Read moreRead less
