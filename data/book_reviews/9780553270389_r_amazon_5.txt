If you want an introduction to Jackie Cochran’s remarkable life, this book is a good place to start—at least in the way Cochran wanted her story told. Written by her ghostwriter, Maryann Bucknum Brinley, it delivers the version of events Cochran approved, which isn’t necessarily the life she actually lived. It’s engaging and full of her voice, but it leaves gaps and glosses over controversies. Cochran had a tendencey to tell her life's story in multiple versions. This is one of them.
