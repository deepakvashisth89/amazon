While watching the movie "EYE FOR AN EYE" with Sally Field, I noticed in the credits that it was allegedly based on this book. Since I liked the movie enough, I got curious to see what the book was like, since books are usually better--not so in this case! As a matter of fact the book isnt even anything like the movie at all! It's just a completely different story, and a few times I had to make sure via google that I was reading the correct book. But about the only relation this book even has to the movie is that it has a main character named Karen, who overhears her daughter being raped and murdered over the phone, and a justice system that is too lenient on the criminal, leading to his release, and her need for justice. Aside from that, this book has zero, zilch, nada, no relation at all to the movie with Sally Field. I'm not sure how such a decent movie was even conceived from a mess like this!The story in this book is completely ridiculous nonsense and "Karen" already has her revenge on the criminal, whose identity is never too clear, within the first 50 pages of the book!. The rest of the book is so childish and sloppy with a very childish storyline so incoherently written, I don't even have any idea how a mess like this got published; but I do know that after reading this book, I understand why this author only wrote one other novel, which I will make a point to avoid like the plague!But anyway, I'm writing this review only because I wish someone had posted a review like this for me so I could have been warned to avoid this abomination of a story that is only related to the movie by a few thin threads.
