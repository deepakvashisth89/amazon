This is the sequel to the book Zoom. I liked Zoom better than Re-Zoom, but both are really good. The first page provides an interesting drawing. The next page is the same drawing, but the artist is pulling away, adding more details as the original drawing becomes a little smaller. You discover that what you thought was a particular scene is actually a postage stamp. As you continue, you discover the postage stamp is part of an ad on a public transit bus. Very entertaining. I think I love it as much as my grandkids do. You never get tired of  going over the drawings again and again as there is always something you miss. I highly recommend this book!Read moreRead less
