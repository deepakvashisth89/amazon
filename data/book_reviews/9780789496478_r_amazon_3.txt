This book models chaos.  The material, if you are a Tom Peters fan, is not new.  The book's design and lay-out, however, models today's business environment.  It is unpredictable and chaotic.Each page bombards the reader with visual stimuli.  Multiple type faces, italics, cap strings, colors, type sizes, sidebars, background images.  The first few chapters drove me crazy.  I may talk a great line on the subject of chaos, but this was not what a dedicated, old-fashioned book reader had grown comfortable with.That is the point and Tom Peters is to be complimented for it.  With Re-imagine! he challenges the art of book design.  It underscores his message.  I believe we are on the threshold of an economic revolution.  My only hope is that that I will have the energy to participate when it finally erupts.In the meantime, Tom Peters will remain the Tom Paine pamphleteer of it.
