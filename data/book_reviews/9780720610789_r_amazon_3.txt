Having recently visited both Chatsworth and Hardwick House, I was very interested in learning more about Bess of Hardwick. Though I struggled at times reading about the various Marys, Francis, Elizabeths, Williams, etc.and their peerage,  I found the book immensely interesting. I do wish that besides the genealogy charts that the author would have included some maps.  The times represented and the customs of marriage,  gift giving and the pounds paid for various duties and objects were a revelation.
