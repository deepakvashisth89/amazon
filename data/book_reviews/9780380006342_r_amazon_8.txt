I loved this book.  I really enjoy tagging along as Helene gets to view all the sights in London.  My only complaint is that she doesn't seem to integrate her whole life in her story.  I was disappointed to learn of the death of an important person in just a sentence in a parentheses.  All these London experiences are wonderful, but we only get to skim the story of her life.  In "Underfoot in Show Business"  we finally get to hear some of her early family life, so I would have enjoyed to have it all integrated more.  Would I recommend this book?
