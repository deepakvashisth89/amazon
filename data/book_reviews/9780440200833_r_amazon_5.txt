Tim McCarver has become an icon in the world of baseball announcing. His commentary during games is exceptional for combining the experience of a former ballplayer with a sharp wit, a fierce intelligence, and strongly held opinions. His style and personality comes through in "Oh Baby, I Love It," even though the book was written near the beginning of his announcing career. I could easily hear McCarver's voice as I read.The book loses momentum when McCarver is simply reporting events, such as the Mets' entire 1986 season.
