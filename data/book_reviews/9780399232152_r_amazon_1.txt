Spot-on story set in a 1940s central Louisiana logging community.  Bought this for my ten-year-old granddaughter, can’t wait to read it together! This is National Book Award winner Kimberly Willis Holt at her best.  She is excellent in creating settings, time periods, and characters that touch your heart, and she certainly does with this story.As a retired librarian, I’ve thought of doing a read-aloud of this book with older adults who would remember the setting (near my home) and perhaps even lived this story themselves.
