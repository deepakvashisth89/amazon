Picked up the paperback edition in an Ontario Provincial Park gift shop and read it almost nonstop. Would be nice if some more photos were included since this was such a long journey through so many regions. The writing was about what I hoped for. Occasionally the voice--it's written as though Joanie crafted it, although both are named as authors--turns a bit spacey, e.g., projecting thoughts onto the people they meet when there seems no foundation to support these notions. And I got a tiny bit tired of the idealization and romanticization of the native peoples of northern Canada--it appears a few times, not constantly. But then I look at the pictures and think, okay, they were quite young when they wrote this, cut them a little slack…Overall, a very worthwhile narration of a daunting undertaking, the sort of trip I dream about, and am glad to read about someone's experience of doing it.Read moreRead less
