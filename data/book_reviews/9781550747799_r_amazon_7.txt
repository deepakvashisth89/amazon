As an educator, I am often inundated with data of student demography that is mind boggling and hard to conceptualize.  This book helped me conceive the broad representation of our distinctiveness throughout the world and how I am part of a larger and rich community.  For the young students I have taught, it provides them opportunities to connect with particular pages as their home countries are identified and to connect with ones for which they are not familiar.
