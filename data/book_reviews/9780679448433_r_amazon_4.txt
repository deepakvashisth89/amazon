As one of the countless number of people enthralled by George Sheehan's ardor and writing, I found this final chapter of his work compelling.  Some of the most heartfelt parts of the book are the personal comments by his many children that begin each chapter. He was "seventysomething" when he wrote it; that is my age too.  He is so right when he says that "[g]rowing old is a game of verve and imagination and excitement."  One could say the same about his writing.  Although this work is not as outstanding as his masterpiece, "Running & Being," it is a moving account of his face-off with cancer and impending demise.
