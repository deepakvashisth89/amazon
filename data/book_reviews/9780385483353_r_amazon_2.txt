This is probably the definitive biography of Julia Child. I have always known about Julia Child but went for decades without paying much attention to this extraordinary woman's life. When I finally began to get serious about cooking, I connected. Not only - per my family's feedback - have my culinary skills taken a giant leap forward, but I also found in Julia an awesome role model. It's all there: the story of a great marriage, of a woman who found her calling late in life, who "did it her way" (which was a very civilized and advanced way, I must say) and succeeded in building an extraordary career and in advancing the way a whole country approached dining. Julia made it not only okay, but "the thing to do," to strive for pleasurable dining experiences - dining experiences raised to the level of art - versus simply aiming to put three squares on the table.Along with all of this, Julia models a way to live - with a great sense of humor, gusto, fearlessness and joy. What's not to like? Noel Riley Fitch does a great job of capturing Julia and of sharing this great personality with us. As Julia might say, Bien fait! (Well done!)Read moreRead less
