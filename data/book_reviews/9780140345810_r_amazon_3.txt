I read William Sleator books in middle school and as an adult I had some nostalgia for some of his books, since they were game-changers for me in terms of the story-telling.This book was pretty good! The character development outside of Tycho was pretty limited which is where I'd like to have seen a difference. I couldn't empathize well with Tycho's siblings. However, there are some scenes in the story that really resonated with me, and I enjoyed this book to lounge with for a weekend.Read moreRead less
