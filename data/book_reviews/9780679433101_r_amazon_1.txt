I'm 65 and I've been an avid reader my whole life. About a year I decided that I wanted to go back and re-read some of the really great books I read in my younger days that I still have fond memories of. Julia May's Pliocene Exile Series is wonderful, filled with imagination, very well written, filled with fun exciting characters. The whole series reads more like a believable future history. While not as serious as Frank Herbert's "Dune" series  but just as good. Now that I'm thinking of it I do see parallels To Steven R Donaldson's "Thomas Covenant The Unbeliever" series but more Sci Fi. This is a great series well worth reading over and over again.Read moreRead less
