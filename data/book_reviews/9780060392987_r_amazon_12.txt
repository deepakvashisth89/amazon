I am a huge wresting fan, so just had to read this, as I love the Rock – one of the hottest stars in showbiz. The book provides a great succinct account of his life so far, and journey into the showbiz world of WWE. The Rock uses a lot of his wrestling catchphrases in this book, which some people won’t like, but I didn’t mind and this will also make the book more appealing to kids.  Needless to say, the Rock has gone on to much bigger things in Hollywood since this book was written which therefore aren’t covered here.
