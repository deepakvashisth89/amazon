Obviously I have seen both movies - 2001 and 2010, but this is the first book in the series that I have read.  It was thoroughly entertaining but my one caveat is that I’m missing all of the visual impact of the movies.  My imagination didn’t run as far as I was hoping from the book.  I found the written story lacking a bit on the detailed descriptions I have associated with other authors.  Please read the final notes by Arthur C. Clarke after the book ending, it was enlightening to see what a curious coincidence developed in two different sci-fi franchises.
