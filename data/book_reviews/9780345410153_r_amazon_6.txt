Very well written and captivating, with the benefit of a first hand account from the survivor. It is realistic, thrilling and thought provoking and what was most expected (I was looking for the survivial-adventure part) was the human reflection: on our insignificance, connection to our surroundings, true needs, resilience and madness. The authors direct reflection on these topics elevated the book, and brings readers closer to a relatable human. There were pompous moments (overly elaborated topics, dramatic inclusions, unnecessary alliterations) and overly complex moments (overly technical descriptions of contraptions, sailor jargon for credibility). But, despite these mild annoyances, the book was very well done with great adventure, survival and human story telling.Read moreRead less
