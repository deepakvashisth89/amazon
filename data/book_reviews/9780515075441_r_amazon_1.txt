I'm retired military.  I served as enlisted, as a NCO, and finally as a Commissioned Officer.  Reading The Brotherhood of War series brought back memories of the most rewarding time of my life.  THE COLONELS is part of W.E.B. Griffin's great reflection of how a group of men and their families lived within the requirements of the military.  Mr. Griffin is a genius at combining historical facts and a novel to make an exciting and interesting book.  THE COLONELS takes it's place as a necessary book within the Brotherhood of War Series.
