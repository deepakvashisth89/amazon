Good classic read, better than some of the others.I was a little surprised at how the ending went down, although I guess it wasn't any more contrived than some others of its period or our own.  As one other reviewer says, there is a streak of casual anti-Semitism that fits with the period, but is still distasteful.  The characters are fun and quirky, and all the classic elements are represented - English country house, dislikable people getting offed, cut telephone wires, inquests and gossip - also the anti-German thing about the dachshund was entertaining.
