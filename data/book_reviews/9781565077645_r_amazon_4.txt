I DOUBT THAT I WOULD HAVE BOTHERED TO ENTER A REVIEW OF TOM KINKADE'S GLORY OF CREATION HAD ANOTHER REVIEW NOT BEEN POSTED WHICH I CONSIDER INAPPROPRIATE. LIKE MANY OTHER REVIEWERS WHO SEEM TO WANT TO ARGUE THE MERITS OF A PRODUCT WHEN THEIR OPINION CONTRASTS WITH THE OPINION OF ANOTHER, THIS AFORE SAID REVIEWER SEEMS DISTURBED THAT MR. KINKADE MAKES A VERY NICE LIVING FROM THE SALES OF MANY OF HIS WORKS IN FORMATS OTHERS THAN BOOKS. I AGREE THAT HE DOES. HOWEVER, I VIEW THE PURPOSE OF A REVIEW AS SHARING ONE'S OPINION ABOUT THE SPECIFIC WORK IN QUESTION AS OPPOSED TO PASSING JUDGMENT ON AN AUTHORS LIFESTYLE OR OTHER WORKS. THEREFORE, I WILL FOCUS MY COMMENTS SOLELY UPON THE BOOK GLORY OF CREATION PUBLISHED IN 1998.I ENJOY LIMITED SELECTIONS OF MR. KINKADES PAINTINGS AND THIS SHORT BOOK(ONLY 49 PAGES) CONTAINS SOME 17 OF HIS PAINTINGS INCLUDING MANY IF HIS BEST KNOWN WORKS. THE BOOK ALSO FEATURES QUOTES FROM FAMOUS AUTHORS WHICH ACCOMPANY EACH PAINTING. THE QUOTES FROM WM. WORDSWORTH, PERCY SHELLY, HELEN KELLER, HENRY DAVID THOREAU ET. AL. OFFER A BASIS FOR REFLECTION AND CONTEMPLATION OF THE PAINTINGS FEATURED IN THE BOOK. MY PERSONAL OPINION IS THAT THE BOOK IS VERY ENJOYABLE AND I FOUND IT TTHE TYPE OF BOOK ONE MAY OPEN TO ANY PAINTING/PASSAGE FOR BRIEF MEDITATION OR JUST A THOUGHTFUL BREAK IN ONE'S DAY.
