I worked on the Kaibab National Forest as a firefighter for a few of the seasons the author worked on the North Rim of Grand Canyon National Park.  I never worked a fire with him, but was in at least one fire training session with him.  He is familiar with a classmate of mine from the Northern Arizona University School for Forestry, Gross Tom.  He has a lot of hands on experience and has worked in a large bureaucracy, which is similar for both the U.S. Forest Service and the National Park Service. His descriptions of firefighting and his frustrations with management are right on.All of the books this author has written are excellent, this man knows his stuff and just as important he is good writer.  If you haven't ever fought a fire or don't understand fire ecology, but want to know something about them, the author does a good job of explaining both in an entertaining way.I recommend this book, you will enjoy it.Read moreRead less
