Another one of my favorite Harlequin novels from the 80s or early 90s. The heroine is Tessa a surgeon in her late 20s who collapses at work from overworking herself and is forced my her superior to go on a three month holiday. She decides to visit her godfather who is also away on holiday but leaves her in the care his housekeeper Mrs. Benson. Her uncle's dog is a lovable yet Beethoven wild animal who causes havoc and is constantly breaking through the crack in the fence to go into the mansion next door and is idolizes Patrick the hero who resembles former owner and inherits the mansion from his late uncle turning the mansion into a think tank for him and his employees. When Tessa tries to investigate the crack in the wall, Patrick assumes she is applying for the job as the new Girl Friday and believes she is only 18 since she looks so young. Out of amusement Tessa decides to apply for the job and pretends to be a directionless 18 year old drifter.Patrick flirts and teases Tessa but also encourage her to do something with her life until she marries and have children. He is on old fashioned guy who detest career women and believe women should be housewives who catered to their husbands and children. Being a career woman herself, Tessa and Patrick constantly butts heads over the topic. They are attractive to each other but Patrick constantly backs off because he believes he is robbing the cradle with a teenager and feels guilty for being attractive to someone half his age. There is also his beautiful ice queen assistant Ingrid who despises Tessa as a nuisance and a threat to her relationship with Patrick but melts when she is around Patrick who doesn't realize she's in love with him.Tessa and Patrick constantly butts head and he makes assumptions about her based on his perception of her. The ultimate question is when Patrick finds out the truth, will he expect Tessa to give up her  passion for him and what happens when a joke becomes unfunny and they start to fall for one another?
