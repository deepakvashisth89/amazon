I happened to find this book under the "Free" kindle books and boy what a bargain! They must have put the first of this 3-part series up for free to entice readers to purchase the other two, because that's exactly what it did for me. I didn't know what to expect of this book (seeing as I got it for free) but once I started reading I couldn't put it down. The beginning is a bit slow as the characters are being developed, and the curiosities of the celtic myths had me a bit confused, but once the story took hold my eyes were glued until the very end, and I was left itching to purchase the other two.If you're looking for adventure, suspense, fantasy and even a little humor, The Paradise War is a perfect match.
