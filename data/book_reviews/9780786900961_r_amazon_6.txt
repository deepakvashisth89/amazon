2e is the bolt-on version of AD&D.  This set of bolt-on additions is not really to my liking.  It does, however, feature loads of photo's of miniatures, so it is full of eye-candy.  This book really takes 2e AD&D from a free and open RPG, run mostly in the theater of the mind, to a very miniatures-based, game, relying very heavily on figures, on a board.  If you prefer that approach, it may suit you.  I would, however, recommend looking into 2e BattleSystem Skirmish, instead.  2e BS Skirmish is a mini's game, with a veneer of RPG laid on top, though it is still simpler than this game.  Where BS Skirmish will simplify combat, thereby speeding it up incredibly, this book will do just the opposite.  I recommend perusing a friend's copy before buying.  Cheers!Read moreRead less
