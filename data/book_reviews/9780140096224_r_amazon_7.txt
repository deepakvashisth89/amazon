This book has a poetic kind of flare to it, and is written in a style that only can be explained through reading it yourself.  It's about a boy whose father came from Mexico and assimilated into the United States when he was a boy. Educated in America, he becomes an American with a past of Mexico that is only in his distant memory.  This book is about California and the migrations to the area more than anything, and it covers a wide range of issues regarding Mexican history, what it means to be Mexican, what it means to be an Indian, and also what it means to be an American.Read moreRead less
