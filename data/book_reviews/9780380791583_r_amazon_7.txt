Wow! Space travel, fight-to-the-death slaves, viral servitude, high tech  in a third-world-like atmosphere, and incredibly strong characters make  this book an outstanding read. The back of the book claims the author is  like Samuel R. Delaney and Ursula Le Guin and while the notions of gender  and love are similar, Ms. Park is a much, much better writer. The book  speeds to conclusion so that I neglected reading several of my students'  theses to get to the end! I am going to read more by this wonderful author!Read moreRead less
