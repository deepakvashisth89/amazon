Reality is the background to this book filled with real scenes of life. This book is plainly real. Different situations that faced our people at home and the USA. The American Dream of "going west." This "going west" has the same meaning that our American pioneers had: to have a new and better life in new lands. They established new settlements, but we go to a world already established and try to fit into it.
