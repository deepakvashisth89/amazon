Bova has become my favorite living writer. His Grand Tour series of space exploration got me hooked from the first story I read, which was Mars. I got started a bit late, with Bova having written several books in the series prior to writing Mars, but I went back and purchased everything he's written in the series. I have also purchased the audiobook versions, so I can listen in the car while I'm driving back and forth to work.This set of short stories are assembled bits relating to characters or events from these other full-length novels. It's nice to get some additional backstory on characters that you have grown to love.His hard science fiction bent is refreshing, as much of the tech in his books is feasible, and some is even under development by space agencies now. He's predicted much of the changes that have taken effect in the realm of space exploration, including the privatization of spaceflight with companies like SpaceX, Blue Origin, etc.Read moreRead less
