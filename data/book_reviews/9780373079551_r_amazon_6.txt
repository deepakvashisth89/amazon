Let me start by saying I am new to this author and the Conard County series and have only read 3 or 4 books so far. I didn't enjoy this story as mouch as the others I have read and here's why.I got tired of all the whining that Angela did about her diabetes. Yeah I know she was depressed but I have 2 friends that have had diabetes since they were 8 or nine years old-they have good and bad days however they don't complain nearly as much as Angela did in this book. Truth be told I think she was more depressed about not having someone to love her and her inability to have children.  Some of the whining about the diabetes could have come out and not impacted the story in a negative way. Compared to what Rafe has been through she had it easy.I did like that Angela got Rafe to thinking differently about Raquel (peanut's birth mother) and her motives for not telling him about being pregnant. Angela's ability to see everything from a different perspective than Rafe is what made her a strong female lead IMO because he was forced to examine his feelings about Raquel.Having said that it is still a good story and I would recommend this book and this series to my friends but I can't give it more than 3 stars.Read moreRead less
