A tale of science gone wrong. A researcher with some good ideas, but poor understanding of possible outcomes. The guy can't relate to anyone around him. He gets caught performing experiments using company resources, he loses his job. He injects himself with his experimental cells to try and continue his experiments. He doesn't realize the impact of that action. The cells have collective intelligence  and the ability to mutate any living cell. The story calls to mind the "Thing". The race to stop the cells and the transformation of North America by intelligent cells is the meat of the story.  The story is good but trying to fully describe the enormous and significant changes caused by the cells is difficult. However, it doesn't take away from the story.
