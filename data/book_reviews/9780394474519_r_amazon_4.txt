After reading Mr. Alvarez's  "Biggest Game In Town" about the World Series of Poker, I discovered his book on suicide. As a combat veteran who has seen the number of suicides now surpass those killed in battle, I decided to read his book and look for reasons why. His study of suicides in literature combined with his own attempts to kill himself offer a deep and personal look into a situation that has no quick or easy answers, one that varies with each individual.
