This book came out in the late 1980s, so you should take that into consideration. There are some things about it that wouldn't really fly in today's world, such as a father shouting and threatening his sons with beating and whipping, or boys getting teased for being friends with girls. The whole concept of Julian basically telling tall tales (lies) can be a questionable one for some parents.However, there is a lot of beauty in the imaginative tales contained here, and a lot of what's being described is relatable to kids.
