I enjoyed the six plays in this volume so much that I bought two other collections of his plays. Moliere (1622-1673) was an actor and playwright. His 1662 play "The School for Wives, which is in this volume, was his first great success. The fool Arnolphe raises a female from childhood and gives her no education believing doing so would protect him when he marries her from having another man seduce her. He followed this success a year later with “The School for Wives Criticized,” where almost a dozen people discuss whether the previous year’s play was sensible. He played Arnolphe in the first play and took the part of a man who criticized the play in its follow-up. Tartuffe, which followed the next year, in 1664, was about a man who acted as a pious fellow. But he was a hypocrite. He did so to steal a naïve man’s money and his wife. Moliere was criticized for this play because too many people thought he was mocking religion.
