If you're a senior in good health or you're handling an elderly family member's affairs, you probably have hardly given a second look at the handbook mailed by Medicare or the Summary of Benefits pamphlets sent by your HMO. Most of the time, it is when you or the elderly loved one needs medical services and long-term care that you find yourself forced to get rapidly acquainted with what is covered, the cost involved, the options available and the myriad rules you'll need to navigate to get the care appropriate to you or your loved one.  Long-term care is a subject that many would prefer to keep out of mind and only a few actually plan for it in advance.Whether you have an immediate need for information or whether you're just educating yourself on the subject of long-term care, Joseph L. Matthews' "Long Term-Care: How to Plan & Pay for It," should be included in your must-read list.  Mr. Matthews have written a comprehensive guide that describes the different care options available such as adult day care, assisted living, hospice care, etc.; the level of care each provides, their advantages and disadvantages, their costs and whether government programs would pay for them.  The author also dedicates individual chapters to Medicare, Medicaid and long-term care insurance (LTCI) and explains how each may impact your finances. In the final two chapters of the book, he gives advice on elder fraud and on the preparation of health care directives and powers of attorney.At the time I got this book, I was looking into long-term care insurance policies and needed a resource that would provide me with an unbiased look at LTCI since most of the reading material I had came from the insurance companies themselves.  I found Mr. Matthews' chapter on LTCI very educational and helpful, especially on what to look for when shopping for policies.  Reading this book not only has increased my understanding on the different types of long-term care, but also has opened my eyes as to how Medicare, Medicaid and LTCI work.  I feel that Mr. Matthews has saved me a lot of research time by bringing all these different aspects about long-term care into one volume.
