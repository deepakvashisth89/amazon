This book captures the subject matter like no other book could.  The research that went into this story was well worth it.  The story covers a military conflict like no other that I have read.  The intensity, fear, courage and just sheer determination to survive when others are falling around you was just awesome.  This book covers the one feeling of war that no other book has dared to cover, the feeling of not leaving a comrade behind when things go so very wrong.  You will definitely not feel the same after reading this story.  You go in young, very much like the troops who were there, and you come out a person who is definitely more mature.
