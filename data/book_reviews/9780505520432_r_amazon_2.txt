This book is rather different from others mainly because it is more realistic. It's heartwrenching to know that two people in love cannot be together just because they are from different castes. I love the author's creativity about the caste bracelet, with platinum designating the elites and brass as the lowlies.  I really love the ending where Alena eventually sold her platinum bracelet, just to be with the man she loved in the Outlands, where uncasted civilians are put.Very good story-telling.
