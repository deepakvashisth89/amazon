I just re-read Trader as an ebook for the first time. I’ve been re-reading it every year or two since... 2002 when my future wife moved in with her books and said “read this”.For me it is still a wonderful story. Not only well edited (I read too many books to discount a good editor). The story is engaging, well paced and written, but more importantly for me, the primary characters make the story glow with energy and joy. They interact, grow, learn, and change. Well... Devlin doesn’t change, but that’s kinda the point of his character, eh?I do think it’s worth reading the other linked books, but Trader absolutely stands on its own as a delightful read.Read moreRead less
