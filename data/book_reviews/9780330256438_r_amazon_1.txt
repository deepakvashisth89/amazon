I bought this book soon after it was first published in 1978. I had wanted to be vegetarian for a few years but there seemed to be only one recipe book available then and it was very uninspiring, little more than variations on cauliflower cheese, so I gave up being a vegetarian until I discovered Gail Duff's book. It was just what was needed, written by someone who didn't see vegetarian food as some kind of penance, for people who actually liked cooking. Many recipes became favourites - haricot beans cooked with apples and cider, brown lentil burgers, and the really wonderful red lentil, celery and apple salad which few of the people I have prepared it for even realised was made with red lentils. Gail Duff's recipes were so unlike the boring, brown mush which most of the vegetarians I knew at the time seemed to exist on.  Times have changed and vegetarian food is much more interesting now but Gail Duff's book will always remain a great favourite.
