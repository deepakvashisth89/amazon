It took me over a year to read this beast.I can't say I like or dislike the story... it's too long for me to remember if I loathed or loved it.I put this paperback through h. e. double hockey sticks.  It sat in the car in the summer, laid with me at the beach, assisted me with prepping dinner (there are meat juice splatters on the cover and first couple pages), it's smashed spiders, acted as a drink coaster, it traveled across country and more... some of the pages have creases including the cover. but it's still in ok shape... only the plastic sheathing of the cover is peeling from one corning.As for the story... it's a classic book, it takes into detail Russian life as a city dweller and farmer... some of which gets pretty boring. IT took a very long time for me to be interested in what was happening. The near end of was unexpected to me, but made the book better, I would have for sure hated the entire thing if it had turned out otherwise.
