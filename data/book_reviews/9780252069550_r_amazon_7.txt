I start from the premise that Robert Martin Adams' translation is graceful and his introduction is very helpful.  However, the book is a monument to misogyny. I was initially shocked and then quickly became bored with the Edison and Ewald characters' repetition of their hatred for women - at least those who think independently.  Additionally, the middle of the book has Edison explaining how he constructed his android - an explanation that makes little sense.  So, why four stars?  Because the last fifty pages of the book will blow your mind. The author elevates the android, Hadaly, to become the center of the novel and posits a spirituality, power and philosophical depth to her character that renders Edison and Ewald puny in comparison. The end of the book is filled with incredible irony.  You have to work at it and do some serious re-reading of some of the chapters (I re-read the last fifty pages twice).
