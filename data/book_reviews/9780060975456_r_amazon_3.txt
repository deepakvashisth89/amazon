It is hard to say what makes a good book and it is even harder to say what makes a great book. I think that too many books today are judged "good or great" by how many copies they sell and how much money they make  for the publishers and if they get made into movies or not. I also think  that it depends more on if the author goes on the Oprah Winfrey show or  gets blown up by the news media and publicity. I think that Jess Mowry's  book, "Way Past Cool", is one of the truly great books written in  this century and I also think that it will be a classic of black literature  and will be around as long as Ralph Ellison's Invisible Man. I read a lot  of books by many different authors but I still can't say in words why I  like this one so much. In a lot of ways it is like "Romeo and  Juliet" except in this story they get to live instead of die. And the  kids in this book are our own kids that a lot of us see every day but do  not pay much attention to. It is also one of those books you will want to  read again. I have read every book by this author and I sincerely recommend  all of them to you. Jess Mowry reminds us that we are a magical people and  a story-telling people. He has made a lot of predictions in his books which  have all come true. It is a shame that so many of them are sad and that we  might be losing a whole generation of our kids to the violence caused by  wanting material things and money. These are not our own values but are  values which have been taught to our children by a greedy and materialistic  society and "Way Past Cool" shows us that. This book is a warning  and a reminder to us that if we want to change things we must do it  ourselves. "Way Past Cool" tells it but does not preach. There is  no justice, there is "just us". In many ways Jess Mowry's work is  ahead of its time by being too real in the present like so many truly great  author's works were, and I think that some day in the future we will surely  regret not having more of his books to read and pass down to our children.  If you only buy one book by Jess Mowry I think it should be this one. Then  you will probably buy all the others. I agree with that reviewer from  Seattle not to pay any attention to what Kirkus Reviews has to say about  this author and his books. It is clear that they don't know what they are  talking about. Its true that not everyone will like a book, but I did not  find that review "helpful" to me and I think that a socially  conscious book is likely to offend someone because it would not be socually  conscious if it didn't. I believe that this author's books are real black  diamonds just like our own children and we should recognize that fact and  cherish them.
