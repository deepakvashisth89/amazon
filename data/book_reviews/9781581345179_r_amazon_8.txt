I give this book an OK rating mostly because there seemed to be so much more that Grudem could have said about utilizing our businesses for God's glory rather than our own profits and comfort.  The book begins well with lots of promise, then starts to fall off toward the middle.  Chapters are usually too short to deal thoroughly with their subject matter.
