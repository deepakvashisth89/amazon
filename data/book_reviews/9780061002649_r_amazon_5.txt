This is one of my favorite Phyllis Whitney books.  Since it was written long before the unrest and distrust of the muslim world that we have today, you can see some of that world without the political and social undertones that exist today.  It's still a little weird for a Western World person to read, but interesting.  The story is fairly basic to her usual plots, though I think the villain is a lot harder to guess than usual.  I loved the cat and the double meaning to its name.  I was very glad to see this one come out on Kindle since I think it's one of her best.Read moreRead less
