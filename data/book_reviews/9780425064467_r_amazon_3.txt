Forget 1984!  Forget Brave New World!  THIS is the book that has most correctly predicted the future. Why this book isn't taught in high school literary classes is a puzzle to me.  Lawrence Sanders is at the top of his game in his novel about a society that is ruled by science.  I first read this book as a teen in 1977.
