This is my favorite book by America's best satirist, Sinclair Lewis, which I like even more than Elmer Gantry. In it he educated this white-skinned reader as to how it was not just slavery that subjugated what was then known as the American negro; nor was segregation something that was limited to the south. Even though he idealizes people of color, and some of the language is somewhat stilted, the effect of this book is akin to a kick in the gut, forcing even this progressive to reevaluate the effect that racism continues to have in our society.
