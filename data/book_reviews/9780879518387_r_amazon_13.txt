I was hesitant to order this book as I've never read a book of letters before and I thought it might be boring (plus is was £12.99 - ouch!). But, having just read Jane Eyre, I was very curious to know what sort of a person could have written it. I don't like biographies, so I thought, well, it's a book of letter, or nothing. I did get a bit bored in the beginning, but I stuck with it and about  a quarter of the way through, I got hooked. I became completely fascinated with the many facets of the Brontes' lives. The fact that the book is in effect written by the Brontes (mainly Charlotte, who is a very interesting person, not dissimilar in personality to Jane Eyre) made it seem very immediate and vivid.
