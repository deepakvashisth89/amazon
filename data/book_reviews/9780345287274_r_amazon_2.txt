Better known for his ghost stories  (e.g., Topper) Smith writes about characters who suddenly encounter surrealistic and weird events.  The lure of this new freedom works if you accept the backdrop--a staid, society where conventions make everything dull and depressing.  There are good lines in his books, and a lot of silly situations, often involving sex.  If you liked old movies such as My Man Godfrey, or the Marx Brothers' movies, you would probably enjoy this.  Not to everyone's taste, but the rebellion against stuffiness is attractive.  The technique doesn't work as well in modern society.  In part, the book is a send-up of business values of the period.
