Written 82 years ago, "It Can't Happen Here" describes the rise of Comrade Trump in a work of speculative fiction. The similarities, between fact and fiction, are eerie. The story is well told and better written. Sinclair Lewis earned his enduring admiration. His imagination is remarkable as is his writing.
