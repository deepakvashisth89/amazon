This is just a phenomenal book coupled with an epic performance, and listening to it is a true joy.  The book itself is an epic masterpiece with all that entails--heroic deeds, great characters, tremendous plot and subplots with twists, character development, and elevated language.  Tolkien wasn't just an author; he was a philologist who had mastered languages like Old English (and invented a few of his own) and wove them into his literature.  The way he wrote is a pleasure to read or listen to.  Add to that the genius of Mr. Serkis who doesn't merely read the 3 books but performs all the characters with different voices and accents with alacrity--and he does a fair bit of singing, besides--with so much energy and skill, and you have a masterpiece that will loom for centuries.
