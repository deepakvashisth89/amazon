While this book does have a lot of fluff (what self-help book doesn't?) the nuggets of useful information in here are fascinating and useful. Some of the ideas in this book made me look at my troubles in organization in a whole new light, and I feel more like I can find solutions to them now.Unlike other organizing books I've looked at, I felt like this book was talking to be. At numerous places in this book, I thought, 'Yes, that's me!"
