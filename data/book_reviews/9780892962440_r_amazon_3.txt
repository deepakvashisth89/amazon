These short stories are REALLY SHORT.  They're bizarre little art pieces unto themselves and, while engaging, are rather eccentric.  Little Tales of Misogyny is a Highsmith book to pick up after you've read some of her more notable work, for the simple reason it isn't as definitive an indication of who she is as a writer as other works.
