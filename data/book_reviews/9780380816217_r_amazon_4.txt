I purchased this book because I thought it was next in line in the Leo Waterman series....the only thing common with Leo is the setting....Seattle at its best and worst.  The character of Frank Corso, tough and cynical, reminds of who Leo Waterman might become if G.M Ford continues to write the series (please,please!)  Frank, who has demons of his own, stays aloof from relationships with others, but determines to not only care for an old girlfriend, but find who caused her injuries.  There is plenty of action, but also courtroom drama as well.  I would count myself lucky to have a friend like Corso!  I will continue to read the Corso books as well....G. M. For is an excellent writer and I truely enjoy his books.Read moreRead less
