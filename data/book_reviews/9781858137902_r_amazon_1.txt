I was very happy with this HarperCollins edition of Grimm’s Fairy Tales. The collection itself is filled with classic stories that are imaginative, dark, memorable, and surprisingly timeless, but the real highlight for me was the quality of the physical book for such an affordable price. The cover design is simple but attractive, the binding feels solid, and the pages are comfortable to read. It has a much nicer presentation than I expected from a budget friendly classics edition. I also appreciate that it looks great on a bookshelf alongside other Collins Classics releases.
