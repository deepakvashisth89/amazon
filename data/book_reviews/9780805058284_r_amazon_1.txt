An important book told from an insider’s perspective about the Catholic annulment process. I’m a Catholic convert who has recently been researching and writing about the annulment process. And everything I’ve learned makes Ms. Kennedy’s account and those she interviewed ring true. No, it’s not a Catholic “divorce,” but it might as well be given the exorbitant rate of annulments handed out in the U.S. and the number that are overturned by Rome. To be fair, there are marvelous priests and canon lawyers out there. But entrenched systems can be hard to go up against, as I’ve learned about our family court system as well. I’m in awe of Kennedy’s courage, strength, integrity and profound patience. She’s obviously a marvelous mother who cares deeply about her children. Many passages broke my heart with their sadness and sincerity. We have a serious marriage crisis in our country and has produced devastating results to communities and children. Bravo! I only found this book recently but I’m glad I did. It’s an important contribution to books about marriage and how we value (and don’t value) it in the U.S.Read moreRead less
