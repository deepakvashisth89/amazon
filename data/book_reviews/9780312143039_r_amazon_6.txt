This book is really a wealth of psychological dramas interacting and propelling one another towards a huge crash! One set of dramas involve Neville and Helena Fielding (2 people, 2 dramas) and the other set is the village of  Radnesfield and its residents (As many dramas as there are people!). Neville is slowly turning into the obnoxious soap-opera character that made him a star, which puts him on a collision course with  Radnesfield, while Helena, who has subordinated her acting talent to being Neville's wife, tries to keep the peace with every player.That she has failed is obvious on the first page of the book, where we find Helena serving the final day of a prison sentence for murder. Released, Helena keeps trying and keeps failing. After flashbacks that tell us exactly how Helena ended up where she is, there's another murder.The people and the place in this novel are all alive and all become increasingly creepy......Highly recommended!Read moreRead less
