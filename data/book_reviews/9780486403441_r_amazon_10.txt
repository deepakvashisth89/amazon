This is the eleventh book in the series and with another scenario. I guess this one has the biggest shocker where the author has made the princess Ozma go missing. That really is a great start and keeps you hooked to know more. Dorothy wakes up one day and gets to know that the the princess Ozma is missing. That creates a lot of confusion and mystery. What should she do next? That is where the book gets a bit predictable. The big guns or the leading citizens decide on a search and rescue mission with Dorothy, who else apart form her, leading the pack. They are further dismayed and confused by the fact that it is not just Ozma who is missing. This has them confused even more. Many of the artifacts, magical stuff and money is gone too.
