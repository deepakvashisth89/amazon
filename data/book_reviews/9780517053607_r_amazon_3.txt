This is a wonderful leather bound book containing all of Charles Dickens greatest hits, so to speak.  If you are a collector of fine volumes for classic literature, this edition would be excellent in that case.  I read two of these in high school and even though i was an English major in college, Charles Dickens somehow never came up as anything to read for class.  Which is unfortunate, because he was such a major player in the formation of literature as we know it today.  So many of our contemporary authors cite him as a major influence for getting into the game.
