I have been reading western novels for 45 years and have amassed a vast collection of Westerns in my library over the years, ranging from the Winnetou stories of Karl May to today’s top authors McCoy, Nesbitt, Kelton and Boggs. Sam Brown’s books are my favourites of any western writer’s. His depiction of cowboy life is so genuine and heart felt, and his stories so well-plotted that his stories are un-put-downable.
