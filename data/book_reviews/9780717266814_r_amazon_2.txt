All in all this is a pretty unique and neat book; well, actually it is two books in on.  The first part of the book deals with one of my favorite little critters, the Chipmunks and the second addresses another favorite of mine, beavers.As a kid I spent hours and hours watch, chasing, trying to photograph and simply enjoying the antics of chipmunks; both the Eastern and the Western varieties.  We did a lot of traveling when I was a kid and even though I was more familiar with the Eastern Chipmunk, I also spent much time with the Western variety too).  This work is filled with the most wonderful photographs of these interesting little creatures and the text pretty well covers their entire life cycle and the life habits.  It is absolutely brimming with information.  The young reader will most certainly learn much from this work...I know I did and I am not one that just fell off the turnip wagon when it comes to chipmunk lore and knowledge.The second part of the work of course deals with beavers.Beavers have always fascinated me.  I loved reading about the old Mountain Men and their trapping exploits when I was a kid.  At that time we had very few beaver here in the Ozarks - a testament, I suppose, to the skill of those old trappers of the past...well, that and mindless greed.  But that is not the case now.  Since the collapse of the Soviet Union beaver and other fur bearing animals have made a wonderful comeback as the price of furs is so low that any but the diehard simply do not waste their time.  We have quite a number of these strange little animals that inhabit the creek behind our home and it has given me many late evening hours of watching enjoyment.This book covers the life cycle and ways and means of beavers is some detail.  Like the chipmunk portion of the book, it is simply filled with facts and neat little stories of how these critters make their way in the world.Again, the book is filled with dozens of wonderful photographs showing various aspects of the life of a beaver.This is a very educational and enjoyable work well suited for the younger reader.Don BlankenshipThe OzarksRead moreRead less
