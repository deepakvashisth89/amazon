I am a fan of all things L.E. Modesitt Jr. I love all of his books. They always start a bit slower but that's because he writes with such description. You get a logical feel for the storyline. Plus his character development is just awesome. If you are a thoughtful person whom enjoys great stories I would advise reading his books. The series have a familiar feel when reading this author but each series has different elements so while you get the familiar feeling from the author's work you get a great and varying experience with each story.
