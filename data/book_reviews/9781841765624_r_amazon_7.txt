An overview of the struggles between the Takeda and the Uesugi clans during the 1500s. Makes great emphasis on the 4th Battle of Kawanakajima, explaining the series of events leading and following the famous battle.Amazing artwork from Wayne Renolds.Due to focusing on that specific battle, most details of the skirmished and sieges before and after it are not as detailed and give only an idea of what had happened. Though fascinating, the book is very brief on the details, giving a very light glimpse on the whole situation.Great book for people beginning to get an idea on Japanese history, but a bit too basic for people serious on Japanese history.Read moreRead less
