I found this to be a great collection of short stories, and I think those who are interested in life in the Soviet Union/Russia of the 20th Century will also find it to be a compelling read.  The author clearly knows how to  handle the short story genre (although the "novella" piece at the  end of the book was my personal favorite - great development of characters  in this particular piece!), and his experiences living in Russia have  obviously influenced the way his stories ring true.If you like short  stories, and you're interested in the sociology/psychology of life in the  Soviet Union/Russia, you'll really like this collection.
