Couldn't get enough of EastEnders back in the day during those years when I became hooked on it when a boyfriend at the time watched it faithfully each week.  It was tough, in the beginning, getting all the words from some of the characters whose accents were quite thick, and it was tough figuring out all the different characters in and out of the series over the years, and who was whom and who did what to whom, and where'd they go, oh...now they're back.  I despise soap operas, but this one hooked me and hooked me good.  I no longer have access to this television program of the BBC's so thought I'd relive the moments and get caught up by buying some books on it...I ordered 3.  I'm in the middle of reading each one now and am enjoying having a better understanding of the characters lives I missed prior to my beginning to watch it, and better understanding what their characters were really all about, and to see who's who now since I'm no longer viewing it.
