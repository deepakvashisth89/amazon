This is another one of Wells books that I had not read before. I have read some other invisible Man stories along with many of Wells works but it's always nice to get to the ones that I had missed.  If you are familiar with his stories, they all tend to the unusual. This one has prompted the production of so many movies over the years countless other tales from assorted authors. The story is so very different from many of the other adaptations I have experienced over the years but in some ways you can really feel the ideas that he was going for. It's clear from the way it reads and from some of the other past works that I have experienced that the idea of whatever he created that gave him the invisibility also added to driving him mad. I hope I have the opportunity to get through the rest of his works at some point. For any classic sci-fi fan this is a must read.Read moreRead less
