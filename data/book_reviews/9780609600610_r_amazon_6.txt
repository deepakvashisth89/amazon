Bearing Witness:  I was looking for a book that would give the ordinary person suggestions or thoughts about bearing witness in this time of vulnerability for people of color, non-Christian religions and the planet.  The author doesn't tell you what to do, but suggests you follow your heart in ways that suit your life, beginning with a meaningful chapter on a retreat at Auschwitz that stressed the oneness of people from varied backgrounds and religions.  This is a book I'll read again to remind myself to pay attention to what's around me and ask myself if there is a need to be filled.Read moreRead less
