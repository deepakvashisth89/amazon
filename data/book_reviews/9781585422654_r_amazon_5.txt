I've read this book once when I was little, as a friend had given it to me. I loved it. It remained in my mind for the past few years as I've had a craving to read it again, but couldn't since I didn't own the book.So, finally, after a lot of research, I found the book again and purchased it, and just like back then, I loved it.It's a different, fantastic version of Santa Claus that has roots within history and ties it closely with magic, and it's a very good, well thought tale that will make you fall in love with it as a whole, especially with all the Famous People joining in the Giving-presents crew.A book for children and for those adults who don't mind to return to childhood for a short while...Read moreRead less
