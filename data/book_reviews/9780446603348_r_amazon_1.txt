A good book that desperately needs to be heavily edited.  The first third of the book is barely readable and bears only a faint resemblance to the previous books in the series.  Given that if you are a fan of the series you will still want this book for your library.  Even with all it's faults it still manages to move the story forward and entertain.One thing is very noticeable though, there is such a huge gap between the last book and this one that it almost seems that an entire book went missing.  Perhaps a very good editor could bridge the gap, either with the help of the author or another author willing to step up and smooth the transition.In any case since it does advance the story I still recommend adding it to your library IF you don't mind spending what is being asked for such a horribly edited book.  I can only hope that Amazon will step in and demand that the publisher correct the many problems with this book, and the ones in the others in this series by the same publisher (note: this refers to the kindle editions of this series).  If Amazon does I certainly hope that it will enable those who have already purchased books in the series to upgrade to corrected versions for free.
