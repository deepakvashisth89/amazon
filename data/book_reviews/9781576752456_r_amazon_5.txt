This is a great book about time management.  When I received it in the mail I was optimistic about reading it to discover how much time I actually have un accounted for.  The text is in big, bold print, and the verbiage is easy to comprehend and well written.  After reading this book cover to cover I found out I can be doing various things to conserve my time, and better ways to spend the time I have recently freed up.
