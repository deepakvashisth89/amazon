--Amy can't figure out why students from her school, Parkside Middle School, are mysteriously disappearing. She notices that a girl named Melissa, who has sustained head injuries after a terrible car crash, has recently returned to school. After checking a false lead regarding a liked neighbour, Amy knows that Melissa must be at the bottom of the disappearances, and figures out that she gained strange pyschic powers from the accident she was in. --The science fiction was pretty good in this book. --MarisaRead moreRead less
