What did you expect? The first books of the Skeeve adventures were kind of superb but the quality was dropping rapidly at the end...As Robert Asprin promises in his foreword he needed this book to come back into the writing style of the Skeeve advaetures. I think he did a good job with this -- the book has the usual wiredness, funnyness and some nice surprising elements.It is not the best book in the sequel, but if you like Skeeve, Tananda and Aahz you should read it.
