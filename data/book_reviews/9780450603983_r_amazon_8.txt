A great combination of innovative speculative scenarios grounded on hard science, plausible characters and a prescient socio-economic background (written in the mid-1990s, it predicted a crisis eerily similar to what we've seen since 2008). Writing is gripping - I bought it on a nice vacation, but  could not put it down until I finished it.Still, it is not for everyone - one needs to be fluent in science and technology to appreciate it.
