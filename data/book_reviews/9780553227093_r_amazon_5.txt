This is truly a masterful work of action & suspense!  Craig Thomas puts the reader in Michael Gants shoes as he infiltrates Moscow & steals the Soviets coveted Mig 31(There is a REAL Mig 31, it's called the Foxhound.)  The movie is equally entertaining.  Craig Thomas is up there with aviation great Stephen Coonts.  I HIGHLY RECOMMEND this to any aviation fan & those who like action-adventure novels.  The rivalery between the U.S. & the Soviet Union continue to this day.  None was this more "heated" than in the Cold War.
