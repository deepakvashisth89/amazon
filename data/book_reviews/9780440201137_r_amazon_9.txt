I had things to do this Sunday morning, that didn't involve lying in bed reading my book, but Simon Brett is such a good writer that resistance was futile. This was an excellent read and the style of writing utterly beguiling- I could not put it down. I am already looking forward to reading more of Mrs Pargeter, a character in herself, and loved the subtle way details of her past emerged through the novel.
