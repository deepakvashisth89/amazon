I had mixed feelings about this book.  On the one hand, I anticipated the spider would run into problems by ignoring buddies who wanted to play with him.  By the end page, I realized the main idea was about persistence.  This would be a good topic to discuss in a reading/writing assignment.  The illustrations were wonderful as is usual for an Eric Carle book, and if you're reading this to youngsters, they learn about various animals.  If a child is a nonreader, I'd recommend from age 2 to kindergarten.  If they are independent readers, I'd recommend for 1st and 2nd graders.
