I had this book-loaned it-NEVER got it back. The presentation is balance of fact and less of "maybe" that confronts so many J.J. biographies. He has a second OR with the title, FACT AND FICTION CONCERNING THE CAREERS OF THE NOTORIOUS JAMES BROTHERS OF MISSOURI. As a professor, he includes a large bibliography as well as a great index. Since I visit the topic now and then, this is a great primer to connect some dots in the larger tomes. The first pages to 159 deal with the brothers through Jesse's death and Frank's colorful "surrender". The latter section gives some details on the "legend" and interpretations of history.
