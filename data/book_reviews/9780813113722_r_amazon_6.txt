Living myself in a section of Appalachia I thought I had read much of the best literature available that dealt with the history, natural setting, poverty, mining, folks, culture and language of the area. But I had somehow missed the one and still classic book written in the '30s by James Still. No other book will top it - yes, it is that good. No one can bring the language to life as he did (it can put off a few readers at first but stick to it and you will be mesmerized as it goes on). With Mr. Still and his characters you will feel that you are right there in the Kentucky coal patches in the '20s. And what formidable characters he creates, each and every one, from the major ones to the minor figures. The 8 year old narrator is equal to any other similar young narrator in any novel, and Uncle Jolly is unforgettable. This is a story of the cycle of lives. It can be a sad and harsh life but it goes on despite all and rebirth is possible. And if you are in love with nature as present in Appalachia this book is a nature lover's delight. Some books and storys get old - this one never will. Don't miss it.Read moreRead less
