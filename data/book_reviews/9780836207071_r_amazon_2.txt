I have always loved quote books of all kinds.  I have also had the pleasure having dogs and I have been owned by numerous cats in my life time. As anyone with cats know, we never really own a cat but they will allow us to sponsor them.  I have read numerous other cat quote books but this volume has  become my favorite.  The introduction briefly explains the mystery and attraction cats seem to hold on we cat lovers.  As most people know, cats used to be worshiped as Gods by the ancient Egyptians.  All cats seem to remember those ancient times and always seem to give the impression humans are merely their servants.This 374 page book has a quote on every page, as well as some interesting information about cats.  The quotes do not follow any specific order and come from a wide variety of sources.  Here is a small sample of great cat quotes you will discover in this volume.  "The smallest feline is a masterpiece."  Leonardo DaVinci.  "A cat is a tiger that is fed by hand." Yakaoka Genrin  "If you want to know the character of a man, find out what his cat thinks of him." Anonymous "Even the stupidest cat seems to know more than any dog."  Eleanor Clark.In conclusion, if you are a cat lover you will want to own this book.Rating:  5 Stars.  Joseph J.
