I just finished reading this book last night.  What a timeless story, perfect read for those interested in the "plight of the Black man" in America.  I love this book because it is NOT political and NOT hateful; this book just presents the facts with detailed footnotes.  One Man's Castle recounts a famous trial in Detroit in the mid-1920s and the back ground history of some of the key players.  It is difficult to read about all the racism, but we need to be reminded of the past so that we don't repeat it.  *****when you buy this book, if you are not familiar with the story or background, don't peek at the end of the book or Google any of the names.
