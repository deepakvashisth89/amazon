Each of these six novellas are quite good as one might expect since they were written by Silverberg during his peak years.My favorite was the title piece, "Sailing to Byzantium", about a 20th century man who finds himself in the fiftieth century.  In this future there are five recreated cities of the past (though they are changed every so often).  This 20th century man is confused about the point of it all.Another interesting tale, "Thomas the Proclaimer", imagines what would happen if God revealed his existence by having the Earth stop rotating.
