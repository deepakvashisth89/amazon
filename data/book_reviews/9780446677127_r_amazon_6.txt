Since I am relatively new to Scott Turow, I continue to be amazed by his writing.  The story has been well described by others.  While it is a very interesting story, what make this book (and his other books as well) exceptional is the depth of emotions and the insights of individual characters about themselves and others.  This puts Turow well above other authors on this genre.  The Burden of Proof explores the vast range of feeling connected with Sandy Stern's dealing with the suicide of his wife, his family members and friends as well as the very complex and fascinating legal situation of his brother-in-law and how the rest of his family was involved.  I found the book to hold my interest from beginning to end.  Who knew when reading "Presumed Innocent" that Sandy Stern would be such an interesting character!Read moreRead less
