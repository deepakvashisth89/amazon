I like Joe Lansdale. A lot of times,  he gets it right, that balance of humor and savagery, social critique embedded in the behavior of ugly characters. This one is a mix of The Postman Always Rings Twice and Tod Browning’s FREAKS and very crude version of Southern Gothic. (He self-describes it that way). It reads like a bunch of ideas from late at night, written in a single draft and not read by anyone who might have called him in some of his imagery - and that includes any editor he had. I get that a novel, particularly one that is ½ fantasy, doesn’t have to be “real to life,” but the main character’s development isn’t true to the human he gave us at the beginning, and some things he has the characters do remind me of adolescent males in a gross out contest. Lansdale remains a really fine writer…beautiful landscape descriptions and some of his descriptions of people are just really fine, the way the move, the way they look. But some of the over/the-top grossness not only doesn’t help the story, it’s just not what humans do in the circumstances he puts them. Even the degenerates he is describing. Although he doesn’t mention him, the book reminds me of an attempt at mimicking Harry Crews - but missing the mark that Crews nails.
