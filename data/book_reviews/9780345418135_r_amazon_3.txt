RNP has outdone himself with this exciting and dramatic legal story. I have read his first two books with these main characters and was so delighted to obtain this book and couldn't put it down for two days. In my opinion RNP is the master of courtroom scenes. How he manages to keep your interest in all of his main characters to  the point  you feel as though you know them personally, I will never  know other than he is  an incredible writer. Chris Paget  and his new lover, Terri  and their two children from other husbands and lovers keeps your interest going until the last page. This author comes up with new plots and twists that are unimaginable. If you love suspense, drama all rolled up in legal issues that are unending, then you must get this book. Not to mention how delicately he handles child abuse connected to this particular trial. I have read nearly all of RNP's books,  but must say this is one of my favorites. I want him to hurry and write another asap. I will be waiting for it as I know many others will too.Read moreRead less
