I enjoyed this book, but I found myself frustrated with Natalie and her blind descent into madness. But then, maybe that's the point -- we are often the last to realize we've gone over the edge. Even so, Natalie's  actions seemed too much over the top, even for a genre in which over the  top is the norm.
