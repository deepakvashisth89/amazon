If the American short story format experienced some sort of revival in the 70's, then let this particular edition of BASS stand for the culmination of this trend in terms of how the following decade handled it. The stories are varied and depict a number of different situations. Yes, most of these stories depict family goings-on but their essence is hardly confined to the musings of mostly one gender or age group, to address the dismissive Mr Gerke below. There's plenty of male regret here too, partner, and one need only pay sufficient attention to see that. A good chunk of these stories managed to settle deeply into my subconscious and I would gladly read them again.
