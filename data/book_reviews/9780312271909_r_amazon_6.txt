We know from the beginning Jean is the type of person who attracts danger, and when she meets Stephen Cain this becomes a fact.  I found little to like in either character, and couldn't understand why anyone such as Jean  would still want to be around Stephen after he slammed her hand in the car  door.  However, knowing her past of seeking dangerous situations, maybe it  gave her a thrill.Yes, the story was suspenseful in the beginning but  seemed to end in a very boring, expected way.
