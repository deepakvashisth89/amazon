One of the last of the Bantam novels dealing with the original Star Trek series, the Starless World deals with the Enterprise and her crew encountering a Dyson sphere.  The story isn't very memorable, and Spock unlocks the secret of the world with what I would characterize as a hokey discovery and revelation about stars themselves.  Even for Star Trek I thought it was pretty absurd, but that's the story the author intended.I can't say it was very memorable in any way.  Unlike Kathleen Sky or Eklund's son's own Star Trek novel, this one didn't have too much energy injected into either story or characters, and the whole thing about the Dyson sphere is more just for show.It nothing else it'll pass some time and tell an okay story.Read moreRead less
