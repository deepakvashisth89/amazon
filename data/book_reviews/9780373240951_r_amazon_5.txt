The Mother of His Son focuses on the relationship of Beth and Luke.  Due to a grouchy grandmother, Beth and Luke are separated for sixteen years.  After sixteen years, Beth receives a phone call about her father's deathbed  wish and rushes home with her fifteen year old son, Ryan.  After a few  weeks, Luke and Beth reaffirm their love.  The story drags in places and  never really achieves the momentum it needs to be a great story.  Most of  the plot is predictable and the characters do not have a lot of depth.
