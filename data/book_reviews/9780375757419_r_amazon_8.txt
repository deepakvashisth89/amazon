I love Thomas Hardy -- his novels have never failed to disappoint and Jude the Obscure is no exception. When I first started reading it, I was skeptical -- it seemed simple, predictable, but after the first few chapters, the story and characters unfold in all their complexity. Without giving too much away, I will just say that the main messages of this novel relate to relationships and marriage as well as religious doctrine, society, and conflicting emotions. By the end of the novel, I was not sure what Hardy's position on these issues actually was, due to his brilliant ambiguity. He is questioning conventions and rebellion against conventions from beginning to end. To sum up, I'd like to say that this is one of the best novels I've ever read.Read moreRead less
