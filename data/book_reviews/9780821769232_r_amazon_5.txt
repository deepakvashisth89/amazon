I adore Janis Reams Hudson's books set in the 1800's, but this book was not anywhere near as good. The typos alone were highly irritating. Initially, it's hinted to be 1950's but then a comment about a song from the 1990's, and later a mention of internet left me curious about just when the story takes place. Cell phones are never mentioned, and farming as if it is 1860 is constantly mentioned... I think I've read all of the authors good books!
