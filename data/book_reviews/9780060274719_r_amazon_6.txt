I am an adult literacy volunteer and this book was selected for our group to read together. It was the perfect choice with short chapters and a fantastic story line of a community coming together and connecting over a garden. Well written and created many lively discussions over the weeks that we met together. I purchased extra copies for friends because I love the story and message so much.
