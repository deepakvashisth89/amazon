Undeniable Rogue was quite interesting read. This is a time period when it was common for marriages to be arranged, and for a wife of one brother to marry another after the husband had been killed in battle. With this story Gideon was single, but had formed a pack among other warriors that any of the others families would be taken care off if something should happen that they die. Gideon had helped his friend in battle, who appeared to be dying, agreed and promised to marry his friends sister. Gideon was a strikingly handsome womanizer, and never planned to marry, as his childhood showed him nothing he wanted to have to do with marriage. Grudgingly Gideon had went back to one of his homes, where he was to marry his friends sister. Not really planning to have anything to do with her, other then house her, and see that she was fed and clothed, he when he seen her, how beautiful she was, and qiute defiant with what she though a poor soul without means, plus she was expecting.The more Gideon seen of this woman the more he was challenged to win her over, with neither trusting the other. How the 2 spared with the other, then slowly grow together was quite an entertaining read, especially when Gideon finds out, that not only will he be seeing to the needs of a coming baby, but finding out that he had twin boys that Sabrina had kept him from knowing about.I enjoyed the story and wish some would not take a fiction story, based in another time frame, as it needing to be historically correct. If that is so important, some need to stay with reading strictly history books and let others enjoy this authors talent.
