The Mondays legendary dancin sidekick proves that he was more than that,in this very streetwise and candid account of his pre mondays days as a petty criminal to joining the mondays and then suddenly shuttled to nationwide rock an roll stardom. the way bez comes across as he relays his story like a true commoner will really charm you,like the story was beind told to you by one of youre best mates......cool.bez leaves no stone unturned as he take you on a tour of his life of crime from  his adolescence,always into scams an gettin grief of the local filth(police),to his days in a young offenders institute,to chillin out in morroco tokein knock out double zero(opiated cannabis).The book is also very very funny in places take for incidence the time bez and his mates where raiding a warehouse for designer gear an sports equipment, suddenly the alarm is triggered an bez an his ilk are fleeing like rabbits and in there haste knock over a basket full of hundreds of golf balls and cant run off due to tripping up on them...only to be nicked by the old bill....Classic. Most of the book focuses on bez's relationship with the band an shaun ryder in particular and there drug taking of course, funnily enough bez was never invited to join the band he just got up onstage one time an started dancin like a gibbon and the others really took a shine to him.
