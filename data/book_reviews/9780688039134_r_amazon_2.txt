This book succinctly describes the Morita method,Japanese psychotherapy based on the precepts of Zen Buddhism, but also highly applicable to everyone regardless of spiritual background. The method is focused on fully accepting all emotions and then moving on to positive life habits rather than recycling old feelings and wallowing in negativity.He uses lots of case examples and stories to illustrate the application of the concepts.  This book helped me finally deal with old emotional detritus and move forward and act to achieve my life's purpose.
