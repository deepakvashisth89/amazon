When I first read this, it was like "WHOA! Can you DO that in a comic book?".  Well, I guess you can.  It's definitely a satire, and not everybody will find it to their liking.  After all, she is a prostitute- and a cynical, nasty, profanity spouting one at that- who gets super powers. Amanda Conner gets the joke being pulled by Ennis, and her artwork is great at showing expressions that help provide the punchline. Others here have done a good job in summarizing the plot, so I won't belabor the point. If you're into off the wall comics that really push the boundaries, this one is for you.
