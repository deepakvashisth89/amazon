This is not your usual Medieval romance book, this is a sequel to No Dark Place, where we were introduced to Hugh, in this book Hugh is now the rightful heir to the Earldom of Wiltshire and he has also been betrothed to the daughter of the Earl of Lincoln who he has no intention of marrying, he is still in love with Christen and trying to find a way to marry her, suddenly the Earl of Lincoln is murdered and Hugh's friend is accused of the crime and now it is up to Hugh to find the evidence to clear his name. Although this is a romance book the romance is secondary to the murder mystery, it is very well researched and you can almost imagine yourself in Lincoln all those hundreds of years ago. Hugh is the medieval Hercule Poirot, not only has he to find the real murderer he has to foil his Uncle's plot to marry him off to someone he dislikes he also has to try to find a way to marry the woman he does love. This book has everything, mystery, romance and a murder. A very enjoyable book but read No Dark Place first.Read moreRead less
