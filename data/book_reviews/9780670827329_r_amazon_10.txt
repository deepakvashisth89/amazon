Some families have more exciting stories to tell than others and this book just goes to prove it. Johanna Angermeyer grew up in suburban California in the 50s and 60s. Her grandparents were German speakers who had escaped the Russian Empire to avoid the First World War. Her mother's story is no less exciting as she was married first to a daring aristocratic Ecuadorean pilot and, following his death, to a German who had left his country for the Galapagos islands before the Second World War both to avoid the Nazis and to go back to nature. This autobiography looks at Johanna's journey from being a young girl in America longing to learn about her late father, to moving to Ecuador and to the Galapagos islands, tracing her history and learning to live a Robinson Crusoe like lifestyle before mass tourism reached the island.
