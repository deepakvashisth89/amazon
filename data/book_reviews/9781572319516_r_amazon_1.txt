This book lays out in short precise steps how to insert, create, manipulate or use most every  feature within FP2000.  That is great for a user who would like to simply  apply something to there page to see how it works.  Unfortunately, there is  only cursory information here: barebones really.  For somethings like  formatting text, that is fine, but for advanced level users, we need more  in depth explanation.
