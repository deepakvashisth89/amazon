If you are familiar with Jack Vance , you know how this  book from the trilogy (read it with the Green Pearl and Madouc) will read. To get the benefit of the story, read the other two books in the series first, otherwise you will be lost and will not understand the personalities and drivers of his major characters.Vance is an expert at setting the pace and detailed in drawing his characters. He can make the most mundane observation entertaining, and tie it all together at another point in the story. While there is magic in this book, it is not that of a raging sorcerer that can kill anything it wants, or an all powerful warrior. This is a story that is driven by the characters, and like real life they have their own insecurities and problems. While there are powerful sorcerers in this book, they are all held, uneasily, in check by none other than Merlin himself, who keeps a tight rein on them all to prevent their talents from impacting the world in to great a fashion. This is the story of how the grandson of a powerful king was taken by fairiers, how he was first concieved, and great background on the child's parents., .  If you have never read Jack Vance, it will take a little bit of time to get used to the writing style, which is unique among fantasy authors.
