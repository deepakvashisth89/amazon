To put it my way, the thesis that World War-I & II-continue is not fundamentally novel-if one holds to the idea that any "world" war warrants being regarded as "the war to end all wars." The complex set of conditions that make such wars likely has not gone away. Bobbitt presents an interesting thesis re: how so called "world wars" morph and, in effect, restates Hegel's view regarding the connection between conflict and History. I read this book as an interpretation of events as if they were a kind of script still to be fully understood.
