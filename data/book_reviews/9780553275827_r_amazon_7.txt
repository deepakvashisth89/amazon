I had read this book few years back before Kindle and a few years after I returned from Vietnam where I too had been a platoon leader but up in I Corp. At that time the book had not made a very good impression because Vietnam was still too fresh in my mind. But when I ordered it and re-read Platoon Leader I realized how very accurate and explicit the author portraid the responsibilities and dangers that a Platoon leader could expect to experience during his tour of combat duty. That is not to say any other platoon leader experienced the same situation or conditions. This is absolutely his story and the experiences are unique to Mcdonough.But because of the honest and humble style the author uses to bring to life his story it leaves no doubt he has "been there and done that."Read moreRead less
