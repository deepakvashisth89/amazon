This was well researched and well written. The author not only told the story of a ship, but also told of the crew in such a way as to make the reader feel like he knew each of them. This was interesting and thought provoking. What actually happened to the ship will never be known. What I found so despicable was the small amount of money the company that owned the ship offered to the families of those who were lost.
