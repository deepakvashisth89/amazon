Firstly, I am a huge fan of Louis L'Amour and have all of his books in print. I have started to add some of the best ones to my Kindle so I can take them anywhere. I have probably read this book 50 times over the last thirty years and highly recommend it to anyone who likes a straight forward western. No gushy romances, just great characters and a great plot.
