The book is very well written, the story quite complicated with the war going on and the heroine's and hero's emotional turmoil.The explicit parts could easily have been left out or mentioned rather than detailed. They can also easily be skipped when reading.I found my self not really liking Kate, Colin or Ramon. None of them were really very good people.  They all were pretty heartless at times. Kate a flirt without a lot of morals after her marriage, and leading men on often hiding her heart. Colin no more expressive than Kate was, yet also harsh though he claimed love. Ramon didn't really have any respect or true love for Kate. She was just an object to him and she knew it.Then when she realized Colin was still alive and wanted her, she was and not able to make up her mind.  Not to be clear with Colin of her situation.It was kind of a mess, but of course war is. In the end Kate and Colin were good to each other, but it was almost like they were different personalities. I had to keep reminding myself Kate really was very young throughout the story.Ultimately it was a well developed story even though the characters were not my favorites. There did seem to be more details of the war than needed for the plot.Elisabeth Kidd is a great author though. This is the first story of hers I've read with explicit scenes.
