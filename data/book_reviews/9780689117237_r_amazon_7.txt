If you enjoy Gary Jennings writing - Aztec, Journeyer etc you will enjoy this book. It is more than just about the circus; it is about the human condition as Jennings sees it with the circus as a platform. It is also about the circus as it was just after the American Civil War.I see no need to reiterate the plots and summarize the book. Others have done that and it would be good to read them if you have an interest in reading it.I have put this book in the list all time favorites I've read - in decades of reading I've read hundreds of almost every genre there is to read. In fact I majored in literature in college. Most books, modern light reading are read and forgotten. Like Les Les Miserables or War and Peace, you won't forget this one.Considering the age of this book be sure to get a good copy.
