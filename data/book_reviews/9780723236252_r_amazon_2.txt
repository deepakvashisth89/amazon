As a member of the Beatrix Society since the 1990s, I find the Journal to be revealing of her life as a bright young Victorian girl/lady. The Journal was written in code, Beatrix's invented code, as a record of her young life --  her activities and critique of these experiences.  The code served to keep it personal, not for prying eyes particularly those  her Mother with whom she had a dutiful yet seemingly antagonistic relationship.Young Beatrix possessed a brilliant curiosity about nature and the arts, particularly painting.Read moreRead less
