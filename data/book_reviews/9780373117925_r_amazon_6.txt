Her crime was to have loved him...... "You go to pieces when I touch you..." However hard she tried, Georgie couldn't deny how physically attractive she still found Raphael Bernanza. But four years ago he had devastated her emotions and her pride, and she had vowed never to let him get that close again. So it was with a sinking heart that Georgie realized she was stranded in Bolivia, a country where only Raphael could help her. But at what price?Yes, it does have the usual macho nonsense you might expect from the back cover, but it is still a good read. It has good chemistry and good dialogue overall and I recommend it.
