"Cuba Strait" is hands down one of the best novels I have ever read. Along with Mr. Stroud's other novels, "Black Water Transit" and "Cobraville," "Cuba Strait" is one of the books on my short list of “go to" books for rereading several times every year. The characters are nuanced and believable, the dialog crackles off the page like 4th of July fireworks, and the story is compelling and grabs the reader from the first paragraph and doesn't let go until the end.I cannot recommend "Cuba Strait" more highly.Read moreRead less
