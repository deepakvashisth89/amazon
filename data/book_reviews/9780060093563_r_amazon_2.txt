A long time has past since I first read this book, so I was a little afraid it might be dated or that it would no longer hold the charm it did for me then. I’m happy to say, it was still a lovely read. What a great trip back to kinder, gentler times.  There’s a nicely plotted mystery, some great suspense and action, and a soupçon of romance thrown in. Mary Stewart was an excellent writer so it’s not surprising that these books have been re-released in audiobook form. Luckily, they found a marvelous reader who handles the French names for the settings and brief speech of some of the characters, as well as the humorous mispronunciations of the non-French speakers, to perfection.
