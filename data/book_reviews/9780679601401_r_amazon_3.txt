A friend of mine who is a book nut has an extensive library in his house and told me about Chandler.  I told him I wanted something to read on a plane for a long trip that would be interesting.  These stories were so good, I actually felt a bit sad when I had to stop to get off the plane, yet I kept the consolation that I could pick the book back up again.  I had a great trip yet at the end of the days of having fun, I went back to the book.  These stories were a fantastic read.  Well thought out, interesting and the plots were plausible.  Wish I had discovered Raymond Chandler earlier.  However, I will be looking into his other works.  Fans of well told mysteries stories will not be disappointed buying this book.Read moreRead less
