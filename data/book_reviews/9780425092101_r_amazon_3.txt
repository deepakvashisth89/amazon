This is a series of essays that are reflections of the author's experience as editor of a leading periodical in his time. He had the chance to meet and interview many dignitaries during his life. I find his observations on our need to act as a Family of Humankind just as pertinent today as during the Cold War Mutual Assured Destruction age during which they were written. I have recently re-read this and recommend it highly.
