Read this book many years ago when I lived in Nashville near Vanderbilt University.  Jesse Stuart was an illustrious Vandy alum, and had some affiliation with the famous group of writers there known as the Fugitives.  The Thread That Runs So True is extremely well-written and many people will probably--like me--read it cover to cover at one sitting.  I have used this book as a gift to many of my teacher friends through the years.  It is both thought provoking and inspiring.Read moreRead less
