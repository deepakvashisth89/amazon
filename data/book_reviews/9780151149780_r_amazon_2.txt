One of the first novels dealing with the invasion of Japan.  In this case, a thunderstorm destroys the first atomic bomb test, setting the Manhattan project back several months, so the President - Harry Truman - orders the execution of Operation Downfall, the invasion of Japan.  This novel is well-researched and, although there is an underlying story dealing with an officer who lived in and among the Japanese he later fights, it is factual and very authentic.  It actually deals with the second phase of Downfall, Operation Coronet, the invasion of Honshu with the goal of the capture of Tokyo.
