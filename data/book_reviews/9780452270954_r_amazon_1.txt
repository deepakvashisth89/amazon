HaH!!  these books are great!  I adore bizarre trivia/ weird headlines/ odd story books and this one is chock full of all of them!  If you just want to relax and enjoy a chuckle or even a guffaw or two, this is a book for you!  I have chronic pain and consequently spend a lot of time in bed with pillows, pain pills and Ben Gay (and 2 snuggly doggies)  so I find this type of book perfect for when I can't concentrate on a novel or a more complicated book.  Plus I get the added benefit of humor!  Laughter is important for everyone, but it is essential for those of us in  chronic pain or anyone who is immobile much of the time.
