An older book of Phillips. Very good. It has romance, it's tender & emotionally strong. The best parts are how a fairly unloved feisty 16 year old pushes to help her family keep their amusement park going. Along the way she becomes a renown wealthy TV star. So it's a rags to riches thing as well. Worst parts for me were an abuse like rear end slapping by who she thought her father figure was & who she ends up falling madly in love with, altho he's far older. Also how she's shunned by the sexy co star she has a young crush on.  He has his own tortured past. But they meet up again years later when both are down. It's an emotionally moving book I recommend. Honey Moon stays with you. It's a great journey of her many changes, maturity, passions & growth. Read it if you can!Read moreRead less
