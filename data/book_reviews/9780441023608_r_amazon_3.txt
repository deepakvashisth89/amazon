The Myth Series will always be favorites of mine. I read them as a kid and have randomly picked them up through the years and read one when I wanted something light and easy to read. They make me laugh while still making me care about the characters and I highly recommend them to someone who is not sure about the fantasy genre. Reading Another Fine Myth recently definitely reminded me that it is a first book of a series.
