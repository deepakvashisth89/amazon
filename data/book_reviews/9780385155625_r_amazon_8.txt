I am a fan of English murder mysteries written by Agatha Christie, Dorothy L Sayer, etc. Because of this I have been disappointed in recent mysteries till now. I was surprised to find Rest You Merry, the first book in the series in my Kindle because I had not remembered buying it. Once I started reading it I was happy I did.  The Luck Runs Out was just as enjoyable. I enjoin the characters that are reoccurring in the book plus the addition of new ones.
