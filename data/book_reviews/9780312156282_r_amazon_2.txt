I love a good spoof on academia, so I picked this up and starting reading the first story (Queen of the Jungle) late one night and stayed up all night finishing it! It was genuinely suspenseful and intriguing. I must add that I found the final scenes disturbing as they depict animal cruelty. I started the next story and had trouble finishing it- I didn't really care what happened. The final story had completely lost my interest. The problem with unlikeable characters is that after a while you need a reason to care.
