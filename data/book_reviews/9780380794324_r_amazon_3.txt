Thomas Lipinski has done for Pittsburgh what Les Roberts' Milan Jacovich series did for Cleveland: namely, put PI procedurals squarely in the middle  of real, rust belt locales. "Death in the Steel City" adds a shot  of Janwillem van de Wetering to the mix, as PI Dorsey ruminates on the ways  of life and death, fathers and sons, and the polarities of race, creed,  gender, and generations. Underneath it all is story good enough to keep  fans of the genre turning pages and detail rich enough to keep expatriate  Pittsburghers (like this reviewer) looking homeward.
