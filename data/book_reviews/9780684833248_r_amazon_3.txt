Removed a star because of the many typos likely due to a poor scanning job of the original text and no editing to insure readability. Watkins’s memoir is otherwise fascinating, giving the reader an insider’s view of life as a Confederate private during the Civil War. Watkins writes very well, conveying the multiple emotions evoked by his combat experiences and also explaining his motivation for putting his life on the line for one of the worst causes ever. The book kept my attention throughout. Highly recommended.Read moreRead less
