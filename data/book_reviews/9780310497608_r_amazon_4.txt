I picked this book up about 5 years ago and didn't read it right away because I though it might just be about his cancer battle.  I have always liked Paul and did want to know more.  What a nice surprise. The cancer is a thread that is woven throughout the book, but the book tells about his parents and family, his golf days in high school and college, how he met his wife, getting on tour, winning, winning the PGA, his faith renewed in God and how he finally beat cancer.  It is a very easy read and a page turner and an insight on why people like Paul Azinger. He is a genuine guy and a genuine Christian.Read moreRead less
