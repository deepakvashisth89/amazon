This woman knew everyone as well as everyone's secrets. An intimate of Hitler. He first husband was the heir to Guinness whom she left to become (along with her sister) the mistress of Oswald Mosley. She later married Mosley in Magda Goebbels living with Hitler as witness. Strong willed and unrepentant she spent more than three years in prison during the WWII. De Courcy's writing is graceful, intelligent, appropriately gossipy as she recounts the time and the politics of this close witness to histories greatest and most evil times. Sometimes all the nicknames of the Mitford sisters became confusing. And the history of the British fascist movement although carefully documented could have been broadened of more analysis of its roots.
