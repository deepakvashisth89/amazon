Laurence Gough’s The Goldfish Bowl has one of the most hilarious chase scenes I’ve ever read in a crime novel. What’s more surprising is that it occurs in a thriller studded with chilling murders committed by a creepy serial sniper. No wonder this first book in a series featuring Willows and Parker—phlegmatic Jack and sensuous Claire—was so well reviewed in North America and Britain and won the Crime Writers of Canada award for best first novel.
