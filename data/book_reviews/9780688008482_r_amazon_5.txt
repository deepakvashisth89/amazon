I've loved this book since I was a tiny toddler and, as an adult, I introduce every child I can to Alfie and Annie Rose and Shirley Hughes.  I've never come across a child who did not adore her books!  The simply story lines are so relatable to children and the beautiful illustrations just draw them into the story.  The books are well-written and not at all tiring to read for an adult, as so many children's books are.Read moreRead less
