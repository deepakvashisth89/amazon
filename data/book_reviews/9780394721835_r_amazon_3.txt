A very significant book for women and men. The book is a valuable tool for women who want to understand their relationship wounds, whether the wound began with the father or the other men in their lives. It delineates the multiple feminine aspects that become broken and distorted and shows how to heal them; such as, women who have become too passive (the doormat), or too strong, self-protective, and assertive (the warrior), or women who remain the perpetual girl-child, etc. The book discusses how to redeem the father, process the rage and the tears, and heal the feminine spirit so that women can speak with an integrated, and true, feminine voice.The book is also invaluable to fathers of daughters who want to send their daughters into the world emotionally and mentally healthy. It sheds light on the masculine and feminine within us all, and by laying out what is broken, indicates a way to heal the dysfunction of modern feminine and masculine relationships of all kinds.Read moreRead less
