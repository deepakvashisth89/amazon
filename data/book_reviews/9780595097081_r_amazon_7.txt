This novel, although not graphic, portrays the burgeoning love of two young men from different backgrounds. They come together in their teens, one a farm boy with loving parents, the other, growing up in the city, whose father is a preacher's son.  When their situation starts to unravel as a result of a drunken kiss at a rural dance, they come  together, although there is much torment and pain on behalf of the latter boy. There are many twists and turns, but hope survives in spite of the challenges they face in the small rural town, where gossip runs rampant.I certainly look forward to reading the continuation of their story....Read moreRead less
