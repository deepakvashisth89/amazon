Tall, Dark & Texan by Annette BroadrickSilhouette Desire # 1261 - January 2000Man of the Month / Man Talk seriesDan just wants to be left alone. He's been betrayed and disappointed for the last time. His condo on the south Texas coast is the perfect retreat. Shannon's had a crush on Dan since he played football with her older brother in high school. Now between jobs, she's looking to fill a vacancy in Dan's company and to maybe get Dan to finally notice her. So she follows him to south Texas and barges in on his solitude. Then the craziness happens ... and after a conk on the head, a fist to the jaw, and a threat of a shotgun, Dan realizes this woman is the kind of trouble he has needed in his life.I don't recall ever reading a Silhouette Desire before this one. What a wonderful read. It was witty and fun, and the love story of Dan and Shannon was really special. I'll be looking for Ms. Broadrick's precursor to this, Lean, Mean and Lonesome; that tells the tale of Mandy and Rafe who are mentioned in this book.Read moreRead less
