I am a big fan of J.R.R. Tolkien's work, especially of how he creates various fantastical worlds and realities and fills them with characters, places, events, and histories and of how creative, interesting, and unusual his stories of these things are. This book is an example of this sort of handiwork. Roverandom, along with Letters from Father Christmas , are stories Tolkien wrote for his children, which I think is an awesome thing to do. But Roverandom goes beyond being simple and shallow and has a lot of content that stretches the minds and abilities of children and provides entertainment for adults at the same time. Although I did not catch all of the jokes and references, what I did catch and what I read about in the notes I found to be very amusing and quite clever.Okay, now it is confession time. My first time reading this book was by myself when I was in high school. I loved it.This last time reading it, I read it to the whole family I now have, and we all loved it. My very young children, my wife, and myself all enjoyed reading about the strange adventures of this puppy called Rover/Roverandom. Actually, my children seemed much more interested in it than they have in many other stories recently.So basically, this story comes highly recommended by us.Read moreRead less
