I first read this book as a young girl - 11, actually - and I LOVED it. I think I fell in love with Ben Travis then and there :). It is a classic knight-in-shining-armor/damsel-in-distress story, very well done and fun. The bad guy is very bad, and the conflict between him and Ben Travis is well done and at times harrowing. Sabrina herself is a pretty kick-butt woman. It's been a while since I read it, but I will be ordering it again now that I found it! I would definitely recommend it.Read moreRead less
