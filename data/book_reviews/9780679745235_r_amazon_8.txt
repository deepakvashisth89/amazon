This stunning and at times shocking book is beautiful and amazing. The sadness is that Spalding Gray has now passed on and will never write another. The story is manic and intense as it covers Brewster North's attempts at 'living in the moment' through various journeys and experiences. With incredible detail, Spalding Gray manages to convey a story that is both electric and inspired, that covers a massive amount in quite an impressively slim paperback.
