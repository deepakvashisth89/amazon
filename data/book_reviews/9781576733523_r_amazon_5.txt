Undeniably the  best book I have ever read except the bible and I have read literally thousands of books in my lifetime!!  I love this book from start to finish. Its similar to Scrooge and the christmases as well as the movie "Purgatory"!  Its easy to believe this could actually happen and personally I'm not so sure it  hasn't at some time and place. The reactions of the characters are very interesting and contain some strong life lessons. This whole concept is very thought provoking and makes one do some personal soul-searching.
