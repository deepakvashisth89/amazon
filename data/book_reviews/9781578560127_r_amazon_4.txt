The first in a series by Angela Elwell Hunt. I would say a classic. The Silver Sword is a great adventure book with a christian story. The author does extended research to put together a book that shares the truth using storybook characters and historical figures in world history in a story of adventure and love that is captivating. After reading The Silver Sword, I went on to look up information about some of the real characters mentioned in the story and expand my horizon with this knowledge. This book was not just a good read but a great way to share the gospel of Christ.Read moreRead less
