My 2-year-old daughter loves this book!  We sit and read through it and she is able to pick out all the rabbits it asks for. This is a great teaching tool to get your kids to learn the names of other objects.  It is always a great surprise to ask your child if they can find "x" and they point it out quickly when you weren't even sure they knew what that object might be. I am happy for the board book option so that I know it will hold up to rough use and future children.  This was a great gift for Easter and I would buy again or even as a gift.
