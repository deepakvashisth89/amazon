After reading the mixed reviews I decided to go ahead and download this book. After all, it was Kindle Unlimited, so if I wanted to delete it I wouldn't be out anything but a little time. The setting intrigued me and I was pleasantly surprised by the story and characters. The novelty of the foot race and the interaction of the contestants pulled me in and kept me there. I found the police officers human, funny and painstaking. I liked their personalities and felt that, for their time, they were portrayed realistically. Not supermen or idiots, just cops trying to do their job. Of course the humor helped to make the read worth while. There were a few pages I skipped, but I often do that even in five star books. If you would enjoy something a little different go ahead and try this story. I will more than likely read more of the Sergeant Cribb books to see what they are like.Read moreRead less
