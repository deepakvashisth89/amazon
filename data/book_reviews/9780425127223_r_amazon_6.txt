Probably the best Lawrence Sanders book I have ever  read and I have read at least 10 of his novels.  Where Some of his books are just marginal, this one is about as good a book as I have ever read and no question a page turner.From the start to finish you will find it hard to put down and if you think you know where the book is going, Forget it, as it takes a turn mid-way through the book that is fantastic.READ IT.Read moreRead less
