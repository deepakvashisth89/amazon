Readers familiar with Phil Rickman will love this one! As usual, the story begins slowly. Each turn of the page sets the stage. The atmosphere is creepy and threatening. The characters are eccentric. Slowly the story builds, becoming creepier and scarier. When the final scenes unfold, the reader is completely engulfed in the scenes. I chose this book because it was almost Halloween and I was in the mood for a creepy scare. I was not disappointed. This is a long book, so, if you want a quick read, this is not for you. But  if you want a nice long dose of Phil Rickman, this one is vintage! I got the audio as well which was just okay. Audio is convenient if you need to get something done and can't bear to stop the story. I heartily recommend this creepy tome!Read moreRead less
