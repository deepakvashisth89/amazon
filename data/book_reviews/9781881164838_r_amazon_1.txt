A wonderful discovery and a perfect mystery novel, really; it is fully realized from a credible female perspective.  Plotted just right and written superbly well.  I had been into the DeKok series by Baantjer also from Intercontinental Publishing, when I saw an ad in the back of one of the DeKok books for Joyce Spizer's The Cop Was White As Snow.  It is a West Coast book that maximizes the California scenery.  It's also an honest and funny read.  All the characters ring true and the placement of guilt is held with impressive tension until the last pages.
