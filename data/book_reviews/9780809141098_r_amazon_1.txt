I would have to disagree with the previous reviewer who gave this book one star. I think maybe he was looking for something different. I am a professional historian/editor and I understand that authors need to make choices when they write books; not every work can be historically exhaustive, especially when you are talking about landmarks and history that extends several millennia!As someone who has traveled to Rome several times and with a degree in Church History, I was very delighted by this book. I cannot argue that the Korn's glasses aren't rose-colored, but honestly, who has traveled to Rome, dreamed of returning, and didn't want to be reminded of all the beauty and fabulous feelings one experiences only in Rome? His last two sections on "The Roman's Rome" and "Nearby Rome" are utterly romantic! I loved being transported back to sitting under the umbrella trees of Rome, almost seeing the golden light characteristic of Rome, and hearing the distinct sound of Roman police cars in the distance.****If you want to reminisce about your trip to Rome and dream of returning, this is the book!****Read moreRead less
