A personal history of the Rupert and Dorset area of Southern Vermont. I have found the author's style of writing to be very interesting. She gives good daily life stories that make the reader feel as though you lived the experiences with her. Good details about the area so anyone who has ever been in the area knows exactly the location she is talking about.
