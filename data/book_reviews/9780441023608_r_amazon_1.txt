Another Fine Myth (1978) is the first Fantasy novel in the Myth series.  It starts on Klah, a rustic dimension within the multiverse.In this novel, Skeeve is the apprentice of Garkin.  Skeeve has mastered levitation of feathers and keys, but is not working as hard as his master would want.  Actually, Skeeve only sees magic as a convenient tool for a thief.Garkin is a master magician living in humble circumstances on Klah.  He took Skeeve as an apprentice to save him from the winter cold.  Garkin doesn't approve of Skeeve's ambition to be an accomplished thief.Aahz is a green scaly creature with four inch pointed teeth from the dimension of Perv.  He had been a master magician, but lost his magical powers due to a practical joke by Garkin.Tananda is also green, but not scaly.  She is a Trollop from the dimension of Trollia.In this story, Garkin is showing his impatience at Skeeve's progress or lack thereof.  Skeeve has been levitating a feather for three years, but he hasn't yet mastered fire casting.  This time, he focuses on the candle wick and manages to produce an ember of flame, but he loses his focus and it goes out.  A small white plume of smoke rises from the wick.Garkin praises Skeeve for the effort and tells him that future efforts will be easier now that he knows that he can do it.  Then Garkin begins a demonstration of a more spectacular use of magic:  demon summoning.As he casts the spell, an assassin appears within the cabin.  Garkin turns to confront him and the assassin looses a crossbow bolt at the magician.  Then flame surrounds the assassin and leaves only a charred body.Garkin lies on the floor with the bolt protruding from his chest.  Then Skeeve notices that one candle in the pentagram has gone out.  And something with gold-flecked yellow eyes is standing within the star.Thus Skeeve meets Aahz and learns that demons are dimension travelers.  Although Aahz has lost his own powers, he still knows much more about magic than Skeeve.  So Aahz starts instructing Skeeve in magic while trying to return to his own dimension.This tale takes Skeeve into more difficult situations with wouldbe masters of the multiverse, assassins, dragons and many other strange creatures.  He also meets Tananda, a friend of Aahz.  And he learns a little about the multiverse and more about magic.If anyone notices the similarities between this hero andHarry Potter, keep in mind the dates of publication.  This author seems to have started the craze.  And this series is funnier and maybe more fun.This novel is only the first in a long and wild series.  The next installment isMyth Conceptions.  Read and enjoy!Highly recommended for Asprin fans and for anyone else who enjoys tales of magical powers, various mythical dimensions, and a young apprentice magician.-Arthur W.
