Having a keen interest in medieval Welsh history I found Griffri an excellent read. The book is set in 12th century Gwynllwg and tells of the struggles of native Welsh rulers trying desperately to hold off the advances of greedy Norman lords. The book covers key historical events like the Abergavenny massacre and the murder of Morgan ap Owain and also fills in the many gaps to give the reader an insight into life in 12th Century Wales.
