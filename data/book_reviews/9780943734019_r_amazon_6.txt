A beautiful account of a woman who was called by a higher power.So many of us spend our lifetime trying to figure out our purpose ... the very thing that sits in our soul that is a full, satisfying and complete expression of our essence ... who we are. Peace found it early in her life and then lived it in complete fullness.I loved, loved this story in the early part of the book. I loved reading about Peace's travels, her encounters and her experiences. She touched a part within me that resonated deeply. The part of me that truly embraces just how intimate God and his provisions are and how rich this life truly is.If you are wrapped up in a life of fear about the economy, how much money you make, whether or not your needs can be met from day to day ... this book will touch that part of you ... that inner knowing that everything is going to be okay. If you are challenged by people, their personalities or the way they rub you, this book will invite you to see the good in all humanity. You can receive for yourself peace from reading Peace's accounts.Chapter 6 is where the book takes a turn and the author takes the reader through all of Peace's teachings. This is where I lost interest in the book. While some call Peace a "Prophet" and perhaps she was, reading her interpretations of what's wrong in the world and her solution for fixing it, was a huge disinterest to me.Perhaps she's correct, perhaps her solutions were valid but, I didn't pick up this book to read it to have someone tell me how to fix the problems of life ... according to them. I've never done well with someone "Telling" me much of anything. This part of the book didn't sit well with me.Then in chapter 8, the book picks up pace again where Peace begins sharing more about herself, her experiences and her passion of peace. She will also share what she's learned and how she processed a lot of the happenings in her life.To me, it's in these pages that I learned the most because I could take meaning for myself of Peace's life lived and her experiences.As a reader, you may begin to evaluate your life, all of your possessions and the stuff you've accumulated over the years and even perhaps your purpose and what you are truly living for. There's a reason you'll pick up this book and read it because a book never comes to the reader unless there's something in it for them.For me, I closed the past page of this book with a great sense of peace and satisfaction knowing and sensing that if God can care for a soul who walks the earth and has every one of her needs met when all she has in her possession is a comb, a toothbrush and a pen along with the clothes on her back, then I too am worthy of being cared for intimately with the life I've been given as well.Read moreRead less
