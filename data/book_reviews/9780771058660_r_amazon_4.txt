As someone who’s read most of Michener’s other, much longer, masterpieces, it struck me how it took nearly as long to get through the barely 200 pages of this book as it has for me to read some of his other 1,000+ page works.I’ve always loved reading about 19th century adventurers who have the mettle to brave the harshest conditions Mother Nature can muster, but somehow this story just didn’t grab me.Whether it was the monumentally unlikeable protagonist, Lord Luton, or simply the overall feel that this was a somewhat rushed and unfinished work, I was glad to get through this book but without the satisfaction of having read something truly special.If you’re new to Michener and think dabbling in this shorter work is a good place to start, DON’T!  It would be a shame to be forever turned off to one of the great storytellers of the 20th century based on what is surely one of his lesser efforts.Read moreRead less
