This is a surprisingly good book. Since I live in New Mexico I knew that there had been a Civil War Battle at Glorieta, but I did not know the details. This book is very accurate as to geography and persons. The main characters are, of course, fictional, but the historical parties are very accurate. The author does a good job of showing the diverse make up of the armies during that conflict, and the pressures exerted upon individuals to serve one side or the other.The pace is fast and the vision of the horrors of war are striking.This not a classic, but it is informative and entertaining.  I recommend this bookRead moreRead less
