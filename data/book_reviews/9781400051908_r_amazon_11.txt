Con: although set in 2002 it nevertheless has the flavour of an older writer who hasn’t quite caught up with the times.Pros:We know who the serial killer is. The question is why? This turns out to involve an early trauma that’s been buried. The memory of it is recovered via scent. I came to this book while researching perfume and quite a few people mention it. Crime readers will appreciate the lack of gruesome violence and the fact that Rendell is more interested in the psychology of her characters. They are an interesting bunch. The landlady who runs the antique store downstairs is grieving her dead actor husband. One tenant is a beautiful young man with Fragile X syndrome. He has a very low IQ, but not everyone believes that. His aunt and guardian loves him and is trapped by him. The teenagers blackmailing the killer are led by a very smart boy from a good Muslim family. The beautiful sales assistant in the shop makes a good living off wealthy men who think she’ll marry them. Little do they know she has a partner and 3 kids at home. The cops fail to impress. Freddy is weird and either charming or rather annoying depending on your point of view but well drawn.So: psychologically very interesting, very well plotted, presents London well but totally convincing 2002 vibes aren’t quite there.Read moreRead less
