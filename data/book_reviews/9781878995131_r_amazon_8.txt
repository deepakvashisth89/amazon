This is a great little book of poetry.  I bought it for my nephew's memorial service.  He passed away very young and will always be remembered for his sensitivity, the chickens in his backyard, and his music.  The poem describing "men too gentle to live among wolves" described him perfectly and I read this to our family.  I think everyone was touched by James Kavanaugh's words.
