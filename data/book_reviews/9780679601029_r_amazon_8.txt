I bought this book because it was referenced in a book and movie I love, "84 Charing Cross Road".  In the movie, Anne Bancroft's character quotes a breathtaking beautiful John Donne passage and falls in love with his writing.  She states she's, "dizzy with Donne".However, I failed to do my research.  If I had, I'd have realized that Donne was a 17th Century writer whose English was very different than my 21st Century version.  Although Donne's sermons, prose, and verse are eloquent, it takes a minimum of three readings of every line for me to understand them.I'm working my way through the material, very slowly and learning to appreciate Donne's brillianceI would recommend this book to scholars and those who have the interest and patience to "translate" archaic language and sentiments.
