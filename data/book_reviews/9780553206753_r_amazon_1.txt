One of my favorite books isThe Daughter of Timewhich this book's intro references and the book itself resembles.  The premise is simple -- well to do workaholic is forced into early retirement due to a bad heart.  He is bored but then an old skeleton is found in a priest hole in his home.  Ah-- the game is afoot. He ponders, he measures, he inquires and researches--and there is a present day murder to worry with as well. It's not deep or life altering, just a good book, with smart, witty main characters and a nice old school mystery.  Loved it.Read moreRead less
