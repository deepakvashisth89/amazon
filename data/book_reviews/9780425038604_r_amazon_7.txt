I am a fan of Ms. Marsh and found this one of her best. It is one of the few where I was able to deduce the "who-dunnit" early. Her novels are travel guides to the settings, both time and place. While I solved the case, there was enough of a twist that I was more than pleased. The first book of hers I bought, Artists in Crime, was on the $2 rack at a bookstore. I need a read for lunch and thought this cheaper than a magazine or newspaper. I finished it that Saturday evening and immediately ordered more from Amazon.
