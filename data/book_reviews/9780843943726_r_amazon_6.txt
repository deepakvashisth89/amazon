I absolutely loved this book. It definitely keeps you interested page by page. My heart just bled for Dalton when he was a ghost, even my eyes teared up and i had to come back to reality. I would have liked to have seen Lydia caught with another man by her husband and seen her horse whipped like Dalton was when she watched. The Author did an excellent job with this book. I rarely buy books that are not on Kindle but this one just sounded too interesting to pass up, so i bought the book. I am glad i did since it might not ever come to Kindle.
