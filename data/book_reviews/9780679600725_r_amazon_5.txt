Gibberish! Pure gibberish!  Unless you're an English professor or other culturally elite person, or perhaps, of course, a pretentious elitist.  For most of us - with little time, minimal exposure to high culture, and a pragmatically-oriented education at State U - this book is indecipherable.If you want to try Faulkner relatively painlessly I suggest some of his great short pieces, such as those from the superb book "Go Down Moses," and in particular "The Bear" (accessible enough for bright high school students) and "The Old People."  Additionally, try a collection of his best short stories.  Try "A Rose For Emily" for Southern Gothic at its finest.
