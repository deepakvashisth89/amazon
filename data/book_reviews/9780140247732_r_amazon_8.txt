"never judge a book by its cover"...especially this book- I purchased Death of a Salesman from Amazon and received what I presumed, after reading the cover, to be the book in a timely manner, which was important since i was going on vacation soon after the order came...however, upon opening the book on vacation, I soon realized that I was reading a somewhat trashy fiction murder mystery called Murder in the Rue Dumas by ML Longworth. It kept my attention well enough for me to finish the novel (considering that I was on vacation and had limited access to other potential selections). However, when reviewing this item, I gave it one star based on the fact that I ordered Death of Salesman, and received a different book  inside the cover of the book I ordered!!! Hence the "don't judge a book by its cover" pun...Are these misprints worth any money? Still trying to figure out how to file the type of complaint needed to get the proper book without paying anything extra.
