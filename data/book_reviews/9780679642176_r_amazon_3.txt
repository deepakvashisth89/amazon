This is an excellent TV version of Dickens' novel.  The actors portraying Gradgrind and Bounderby showed their respective fact-stressing and insensitive natures very well. Louisa was well shown as a woman whose feelings had been kept inside all of her life. There is also the gloom of Coketown, an atmosphere impossible for its citizens to escape from.  This adaptation is very close to the book and there are no great departures from the original story.  There is another version of Hard Times available (which I have not seen) that had some poor reviews, so I chose to purchase this one instead.  Surprisingly, this one was not included in any other Dickens DVD collection, but it is available separately here.  If you love Dickens, this is well worth getting since it's one of the best miniseries  created.
