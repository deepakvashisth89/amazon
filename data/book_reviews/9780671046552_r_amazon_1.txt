The cover design for "Immortal" is striking.  This is not a young adult novel.  Authors, Golden and Holder understand the "Buffy" series thoroughly enough to write dialogue that the reader would never question.  The authors do not go into great detail over how "Veronique", the villian, came into being or why she was chosen to receive immortality on top of being a vampire.  "Veronique" is not a villain one can sympathize with, but the reader may wonder why she choses to commit so many acts of cruelty and murder to bring hell on earth for beings who most likely will betray her.  Evil beings from hell, not trustworthy; especially hideous, rotting corpse eating beings.The relationships between Buffy and Willow is touching and enviable to anyone who doesn't have their own SamWise Gamgee.  In fact, all of the relationships between the characters are handled well.  The authors even explain why Willow cut her hair.  The answer is simplistic and will endear you to Willow even more.Josh Whedon must have admired Golden and Holder's writing, because the material from this novel was used in later episodes of "Buffy" and "Angel" (Joyce's death, Willow's abuse of magic, Angel's life in the 1940's).
