The collection of poems, Quiet Water, gives its audience inspiration to help know that things will get better and there is light at the end of the tunnel. Quiet Water also gives readers a sense of hope in situations they may find darkness or cloudy patches. This collection of great poems demonstrates the faith one must obtain when being faced with many trying obstacles. Quiet Water is a very good read because like everyone else, we go through hard times and need some type of uplifting or inspiration to keep pressing forward, and these poems give you just that!I asked the river is a particular poem that gives inspiration because it lets you know you can go as far as you want. The approval of others is never needed when determining your own destiny. In its first stanza, it says, “I asked the river where he was going and how he would know when he got there, he only laughed at me splashing across the rocks.” I think what James Kavanaugh was trying to get across to the readers is no one has the answers to your success. No one can tell you when to stop or how far you should go, only you will know that. That is very inspiring because many of us do feel as if we need the approval of others to see how well we are doing. But yet we have to rely on our own thoughts, and approvals to do what suits us best. Further in the poem, James continue to say, “Finally I was silent, As if there were no one else to please, And I spent my time laughing With the river, the mountains, and trees.” It shows how finally he realizes that no one has an answer, and he finds it funny because all the while he has been searching for the approval or the answer and no one had it.Somewhere Along the Way shows hope within this poem. “Somewhere along the way A persistent voice taught me I was in competition with every other man in the world, I listened carefully and I learned the lesson well.” This poems shows how the weight of the world can sit on one’s shoulder thinking that they must compete with the next man. That the things that gave you life and you found it joyful, entertaining was not enough in life, but what was enough were the things that drained life right out of you. He gives you hope knowing that is not true. Somewhere along the way you find hope in a simple laugh, in having fun, being alive for one day longer, being in competition with no one. It is a hopeful feeling knowing that you too can enjoy life for what it is.James Kavanaugh shows the faith throughout all his poems but one particular poem that snatched my attention was The Quiet Mornings. He talks about all the peaceful things that take place on a quiet morning. One line says, “and the heat of the day has not yet aroused the flies to search the seaweed for breakfast.” I think what James Kavanaugh is making reference to is the trying things people face daily. I do not think he is literally speaking about the waves or the seagulls. I think he is making references to life and the people in it. He is trying to show that although we may go through these things, we must have faith because soon enough, within all the craziness, there will be another quiet morning where all the commotion will be silence and there will be peace.James Kavanaugh is very amazing with his work. He takes peaceful settings to show a deeper meaning within it. He uses relaxing and intriguing topics to later give you that cling to hope. He lets you know through his words that nothing bad lasts forever, there is always an end to the rain. He tries to get his readers to not give up when times are tough, but to push harder because that is more rewarding in the end. I enjoyed reading these poems because they helped with many situations I have gone through and it helped me make light of those situations.I highly recommend this book to anyone who is in need of a refreshing, positive outlook on life and its obstacles.
