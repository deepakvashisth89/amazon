I am a magazine feature writer and novelist; CROSSWINDS AT CAMPO CARCASSO was born of what I learned from The Chicago Manual of Style--the manual of manuals. I write for a living in the semi-darkness of my lonely office, with a gooseneck lamp, two laptops, three printers (2 always out of ink cartridges)where only the ghosts of my stories linger, waiting to be put down on paper (or on WORD), anticipating to come alive and stun the world with my literary genius and use of wonderful words--then wham, bam, I hit a wall. Where do the elipses go? Can I use three dots or is it four? The em dash, semi colon or the colon...yes, no, maybe?  It is then that I reach for my big tome titled the Chicago Manual of Style 15, that will tell me all I need to know about journalistic quandries. I must admit it was my best Amazon.com buy and it has a place of honour among my reference books that stack on my desk waiting for a turn to tell me what I want to know.  If you buy but one single book, let it be the manual.  Anita F. VeneziaRead moreRead less
