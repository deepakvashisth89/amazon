This book changed my life when I first read it 30 years or so ago and I made the decision to stop eating meet. Now I have been a happy and healthy vegetarian for more than half of my life on this fragile planet I have turned vegan and enjoyed reading the book again in its updated version. The environmental, moral and health arguments for not eating meat are stronger than ever and this book really is food for thought. Read it and then ask yourself whether you can really justify other living creatures having to suffer and die just so you can eat stuff that is essentially not good for you anyway and the production of which is contributing to the destruction of the world in which you live and the only habitable world that is available to you.Read moreRead less
