From the very beginning, Julie and Tony felt completely real to me—less like characters on a page and more like dear friends or close relatives. As a story centering on an artist and a writer, it completely captured my heart.C.J. writes about the creative world with exquisite detail. The descriptions of Julie’s pottery and her studio are beautifully realized, and the insights into the creative process—especially the line, "You don't push it and you don't pull it. You direct it, you just give it direction and guidance"—stuck with me long after I finished reading.At its core, The One-Legged Cricket is a profoundly moving exploration of grief and loss. It explores the extraordinary pain of heartbreak, but it also serves as a powerful wake-up call to live in the present moment. It leaves you with an inspiring reminder: "Do the things you always wanted to do. Don't let anything hold you back ever again."Read moreRead less
