This is a truly excellent collection of some 13 mostly science fiction short stories exploring the concept of the "far frontier".Mind you the definition is a bit loose.  The first of a planned series of such collections, this one lands with a *bang*.  There are several excellent short stories in here and only one that I found myself skipping--above the normal for a collection of this nature.  The stories themselves range from an eccentric billionaire on one last dying quest to find a mysterious alien, to Dyson sphere inside which live people who no longer BELIEVE in other worlds, to a man intent on keeping his frontier world lifestyle who finds it threatened by progress moving in.I really quite enjoyed this collection and definitely recommend it for nearly anybody who are fans of such collections...you won't go wrong.Read moreRead less
