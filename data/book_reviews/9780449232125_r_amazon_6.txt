Started well but I'm weary of it after getting halfway through when I started skipping pages and pages of descriptive and repetitive  filler. Caldwell is a very good writer but she's much too in love with wordiness for wordiness' sake and it seemed like she was at times flogging the dictionary.  A condensed version of this book world be really great (Reader's Digest anyone?). As it is I'm tired of the story and characters and don't plan on finishing it. One event in particular was where I laid the book aside as it outaged me and was so unworthy of Taylor Calwell's normally very astute mind. Also, I find the cynicism to be over the top. This book can't be considered as reading for pleasure, perhaps new readers will find some wisdom here though I would like them to find it if they can in a more hopeful venue.  TC does have some interesting and very truthful things to say about the direction of our nation and the world.Read moreRead less
