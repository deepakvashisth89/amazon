I read some classic Ken Follett many many years ago, and liked his style. Recently bought “Nevr” and though a little disappointed with the ending only, the book was great, and rich, smart, interesting, wonderful storytelling.The third twin is not. I just looked up the date of publication, and was very surprised that it came out in 2010. From the cliche characters, and even description of technology, etc. I would have guessed mid 90’s.Some of the problem is the reveals. Even the title tells us too much. Worse than that the reader is ahead of the characters in knowledge, and can also easily guess the situation, even to the point of thinking “why isn’t this smart person in the story adding it up? It’s obvious!”. It gets worse, because even when that smart character finally discovers a part of the puzzle, they can’t make the very small next step to solving it.All of the main characters have very, very bad impulse control. Maybe that was the authors idea so make a point but it just didn’t seem realistic, nor did many if their decisions and actions.I’m near the end of the story now, but wanting it to be over. It’s just not a where near his good books, like Eye of the Needle, and Never, which are really, really way better than this.Read moreRead less
