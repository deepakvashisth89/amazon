I enjoyed this book from the beginning. I got caught up in the hardships, hard decisions, Jewish culture.  I had no idea it would be such a long story, perhaps a series would have suited it better.  About 2/3 into the book I thought, are all those Jewish stereotypes true?  Sara's behavior became so irritating in that way.  Toward the end I felt some of it forced and unrealistic but I had to follow it through.  I'd become too attached.  It's worth the read, but be prepared for the long of it.Read moreRead less
