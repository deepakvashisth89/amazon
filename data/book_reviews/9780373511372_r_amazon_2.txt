I love a poor relation story.  Give me a marriage of convenience and an H who doesn't want to love his wife, and you usually have a winner in my book.  This book had a lot going for it, combining the two, and with an h who had some backbone.The main problem I had with this book that, although it started out rather dark, in the middle part it descended into farce combined with the author's idea of an 19th century travelogue of Europe.  How accurate that is I can't say, but I found it boring.  There were plot points introduced here that come into play at the end, so you can't really skip it.  I found myself getting interested again in the last quarter of the book.This author writes well, with good dialogue, which is important to me.  The H and the h were annoying at times.  I appreciated the fact that the h did not collapse into a heap when the H was unkind.  He was the usual mess of a guy who "can't love."  All in all, this book started out with a five "star" rating from me, plummetted in the middle, and then came back up to a four at the end.  I doubt I would re-read.Read moreRead less
