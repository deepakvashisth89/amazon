A friend of mine recommended this book to me twenty years ago and it remains one of my favorite sources of inspiration.The mind mapping exercises are incredibly helpful. As I navigated my career path, they offered me tools to articulate interests and strengths and draw connections, deep below the surface. The book’s exercises focusing on growth mindset, diverse skill development and unlocking potential are reflective, specific, and engaging.The global pandemic drove me back to this book and da Vinci’s principles, particularly sfumato and dimostrazione.SFUMATO is defined as a willingness to embrace ambiguity, paradox and uncertainty. The lessons of sfumato have served me well and helped me hang on to hope during this disorienting time.DIMONSTRAZIONE is defined as a commitment to test knowledge through experience, persistence and a willingness to learn from mistakes.
