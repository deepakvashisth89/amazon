Over the years I have read numerous books on energy healing methods and the principles behind the various healing techniques such as hand and foot reflexology, Reiki, and other esoteric systems.  I recently purchased this interesting and informative 238 page soft cover book (Reflexology for beginners through foot massage pressure points by David f. Vennells) on Amazon for a bargain price.This book is filled with clear illustrations showing the numerous areas of the foot and how each of these relates to certain areas of the body.  The material covered in this text includes the following:  What is reflexology? Locating the reflexes, basic techniques, beginning a treatment, the main treatment, practical advice, case studies, disease and the mind, simple meditations, and the future of reflexology.  The appendix sections are also excellent covering the history of reflexology, meditation groups, books on Buddhism and hypothyroidism.The author combines reflexology along with other healing arts such as meditation, Zen and Buddhism. If you are seeking a simple and easy to learn book on foot reflexology you may want to check out this volume.Rating:  4 Stars.  Joseph J.
