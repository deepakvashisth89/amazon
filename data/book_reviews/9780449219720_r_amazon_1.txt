Lustbader's Nicholas Linnear series is as satisfying today as it was when I first read it thirty some years ago.  Sure, nobody uses phone booths anymore and the description of the computer technology gives away how long ago this was written.  Nevertheless, the plot is well thought out, the characters developed through a series of flashbacks and the suspense is maintained throughout the book.Years ago i read this first trilogy - The Ninja, The Miko and White Ninja and thereafter didn't have tome to go back to Linnear's adventures.  now, though, I'm anxious to pick up his subsequent adventures and get lost in these books for a few hours at a time.Read moreRead less
