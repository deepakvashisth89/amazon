It is unfortunate that Grace Paley is rather not well known as she should be. Yes, her writing is very New York City Jewish, but she is very funny, engaging and engrossing. I am getting a Master's in Literature and she isn't even mentioned in the curriculum of any class. I love 20th century writers and have just discovered her. I am not Jewish or live in a big city, but she touches women's hearts and minds especially appealing to a women who are mature.
