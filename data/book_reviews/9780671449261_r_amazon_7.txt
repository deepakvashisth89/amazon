Enquiry was written in 1969.  It was Dick Francis’ 7th novel; he was hitting his stride.  This book has an audio file and is whisper synced.  The hero, Kelly Hughes, is unjustly convicted of not trying to win a race.  It is important to understand when it was written: no cellphones, no PCs; no internet.  Dick Francis creates intriguing characters and deftly explores controversial topics.  He is well-known for his clincher beginnings.  Enquiry is no exception. The opening paragraph is, “Yesterday I lost my license.”He also writes books that “wear well”.  By that I mean that I read this book about once a year and always find it interesting and the characters intriguing.  One of Francis hallmarks is his endings.
