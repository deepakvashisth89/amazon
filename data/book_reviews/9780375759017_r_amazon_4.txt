This is a typical 19th Century novel written by one of the greatest writers of the time.  The descriptions are magnificent and sometimes require a dictionary. (I would recommend the Oxford Third Edition.)  At times the dialogue which trys to impart the local brogue is a bit difficult to follow.  The character development, however, is superb.  Eliot is not satisfied with the usual caricatures.  Each character has subtleties that truly make them unique personalaties. Today she would have been a psychologist. The ending is a bit prosaic and truncated, but overall it is a great read.Read moreRead less
