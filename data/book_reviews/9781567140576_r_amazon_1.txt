I read all four of the books in this series as a Child. I have always been an avid reader ever since I was able to read. I got the series as a gift when they came out on the market, and for the few years that I had the books I must have read each of them at least 50 times or more. I have read the Goosebumps series and many other teen horror series, but I have to say even though this series was only 4 books, it had me totally enthralled from the moment I picked the first book up. I just recently ordered the whole series from Amazon again. I highly suggest that anyone looking for a good read should turn to this series if they can get their hands on some copies. In my humble opinion, I doubt that if your a fan of the genre that anyone would dislike this series.
