Thomas Hardy was a favourite author of mine in my younger days and re-reading The Return of the Native reminded me why. The descriptions of the Dorset countryside are wonderful and bring the heath to life. Each character is described at some stage of the story and each was an individual. The main characters, Eustacia Vye, Clym Yoebright, his cousin Thomasin and his mother, Diggory Venn and Wildeve are all believeable people and even the smaller characters such as Grandfather Cantle and Charlie hold the attention. i shall read this book again sometime taking it slowly to absorb it fully.Read moreRead less
