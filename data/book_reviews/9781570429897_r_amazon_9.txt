I had just finished reading the Tenth Justice and decided to move onto this next. At first I wasn't feeling the heart racing adrenline of Tenth Justice but then First Counsel came into its own.I can easily say that this book was one of the better thrillers I've read. The plot was simple and easy to follow but there was an added dimension to this book that put it in a league of its own. Mr Meltzer has an uncanny knack of keeping you completely enthralled when a lesser author may have lost your attention.Everything about this book screams buy me and its one I would recommend to anyone looking for a heart pumping thrill ride.Completely under rated in this genre Meltzer is a force to be reckoned with and this book is one I'll read again and again.Read moreRead less
