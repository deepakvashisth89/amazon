Many people both here in the United States and all over the world have family and loved ones in the military and armed services. They do not, however, know necessarily what it is like to serve in a fight where death is a common occurrence and tomorrow is definitely not a guarantee. This book, written by Lt. Carey H. Cash does a very good job of portraying what the life of a soldier is like. Compared to the media and news stories we hear so often, this book tells quite a different story. The happenings that occur in this book are not only an example of miracles happening in a foreign country during wartime, but also spiritual journey that shows the power, grace, and mercy of God. A Table in the Presence is an eye-opeing experience that is inspiring to anyone, regardless of their faith.
