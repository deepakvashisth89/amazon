My third Leonard novel I did not like this one as much as Rum Punch  or La Brava. The idea here is great and the novel is sustained by the rich situational comedy of mobsters and Hollywood types collided together. As you would expect the writing is fast-paced snazzy and clever and the comedy high. However I did not like the characters as much as in the previous books (a character called Ronnie appears and disappears like a puff of smoke, a character called Bear seems invented out of desperation, an interesting and essential character called Leo is consigned to oblivion at the drop of a hat), and the early novel seems slow-moving and light on action.
