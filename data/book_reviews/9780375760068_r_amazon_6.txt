In this marvelous work by Thomas Hardy the reader is taken into the quaint town of casterbridge England and into the lives, minds, hearts and in some cases even the very souls of a few of it's inhabitants. Mainly of the town's prominent corn merchant Michael Henchard. Without going into meticulous detail and spoiling the novel for any future potential reader Thomas Hardy takes us on a uniquely human journey which delves into our nature and emotional frailties and insecurities through the lens of the various characters in the novel. Although a bit breathy and winding at times this work by Mr. Hardy delivers in the end through the many various plot twist and turns to leave the reader in the end feeling that the journey was well worth it.
