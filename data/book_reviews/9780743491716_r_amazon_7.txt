Well done anthology, laid out chronologically with a great mix of characters, styles, and authors. The Peter David story struck me for its strong anti-war message, something I wouldn't have expected from his other works but plays out so well with his use of his New Frontier characters. Some of the stories are better than others, much like episodes of the shows, but that's part of the value of such an anthology in looking at smaller stories without the oftentimes cumbersome storytelling of a too-long novel.Read moreRead less
