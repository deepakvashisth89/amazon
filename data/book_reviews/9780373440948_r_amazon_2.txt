ANDREW IN EXCESS is one of the funniest books I've read in a long time. The heroine, Kat, is a delight from page one, with her quirks and her little dog Toto. And Andrew is fabulous! Watching Kat de-starch the stuffy  Andrew had me laughing out loud. Every character, from Kat and Andrew, to  the secondary characters are well-drawn and multi-dimensional.
