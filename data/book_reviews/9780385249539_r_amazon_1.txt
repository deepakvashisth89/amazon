There are countless 4-star books. This book, however, is a towering 5-stars, as is the movie of the same name, available to stream and starring Tilda Swinton. It's about a highly dysfunctional family (sexual abuse), and the acting is sublime. The book, of course, lets the reader inside the head of the protagonist, a teenaged boy who knows what's happening to his sister (not a spoiler; it's the theme of the whole book) and the Mom, so besotted with her newborn she sees nothing outside of that pink, prolactin haze. Factor in economic hardship and an attempt to make a new start without social support in an isolated area on a stretch of UK coast, and you have real volatility. "The War Room" refers to the situation in the home and to the old WWII bunker left on the coastal cliff near the family's rented home.Not for the faint of heart, but the rewards are great.Read moreRead less
