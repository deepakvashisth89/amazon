A English murder mystery from the WWII era.  A "fair" mystery in the sense that the reader is given all of the information that the detective has, as opposed to some of the Perry Mason stories where Paul Drake would whisper something in Mason's ear which proved to be the pivotal clue, and the  reader isn't given that piece of information until after the case is solved.  Basically, two persons are murdered and beheaded on an English country estate, with no apparent motive, and a short list of possible suspects, most of whom alibi each other.  Almost a reversal of the classic locked room mystery in that while the victims are killed outdoors---in both cases an article is left with the body which was taken from the inside of the locked main house.  This is the first Brand novel I have read, and while it is decently plotted---with surprises---the writing style took me a while to get used to.
