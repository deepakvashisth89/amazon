Reading this book, while having studied Manichaeism as part of my postgrad studies, brings Mani's life alive. The author has written in such a way as to bring the characters alive with their individual quirks, while - in a manner - resurrecting the roots of Manichaeism showing how that belief system commenced and was propagated all the way from Byzantine Empire in the West to the Üyğhur Kağhanate ın the East. I'm going to buy a couple of more copies as presents for family and friends. My vote overall is 10 stars,  but Amazon only gives 5 . Get it.
