I sought out this book after reading Finnegan's 'Barbarian Days'. I'd read BD three times, so wanted to find more by the same author. Finnegan discusses his journalism in BD, and this is a collection of long essays about growing up in the US in the 1990s. It is fascinating, well-written and very prescient. He antipates future issues with the labour market and housing market that Millenial youth would face in the following decades. It also provides great insight into youth subcultures and the lives of young people (and their families) on the margins.Read moreRead less
