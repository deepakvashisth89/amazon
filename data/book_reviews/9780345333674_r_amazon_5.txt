I am a huge Oz fan and have fallen in love with every Oz story L. Frank Baum wrote. As much as I love all the stories in the series the Lost Princess of Oz is my absolute favorite. The Lost Princess is much like all the other stories written by Baum, rich characterization can be found, fantastic adventures across the land of Oz is included and a feel good sensation is created but what makes it stand out is its intrigue. The Lost Princess of Oz is like a Who Dunnit mystery story that has you wondering till the end as to who could have stolen Ozma of Oz.
