This book describes two famous battles at Landing Zone X-Ray and Landing Zone Albany in the Ia Drang Valley during the Vietnam War. The contrast between the two battles is stark and a demonstration of the critical nature of battlefield leadership and unit cohesion. This is a well-researched example of very approachable military history. It is also a rare example of the author consulting with both sides of the two battles described in this account to get the complete picture of what was known at the time.Read moreRead less
