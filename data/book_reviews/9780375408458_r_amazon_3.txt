Not everyone will like every work that is written by a writer, but I have to say, Patricia Cornwell is one of the best writers of our time. She takes you to the locations of the crimes, shares all the little details whether they be gruesome or not, and leaves you with the can't put the book down feeling.....that is what a great writer does, makes you become a part of what is being written about and never disappoints. Those who cry and whine that they didn't like a particular book, we don't want to hear you babbling and crying if and when Ms. Cornwell decides not to write anymore.
