A slight little collection of stories with an emphasis on misunderstandings and humor. Clean and chaste, a buttermint of a book.My favorite of the stories was the third one, in which there are two parallel romances, and the younger brother falls in love with the hoydenish companion because he sees her do a cartwheel.
