This book will be an enjoyable read for anyone really interested in Old English culture. As many have mentioned before me, it is a scholarly work. Most of the material in it was derived from Tolkien's lectures on the subject at Oxford. You will need to know a fair bit of Old English, as well some Old Norse and Latin, to get anything out of this book. Much of it is devoted to lengthy discussions on names, places, and early English history. If you don't have a love for the languages and the time period, you will find this book deathly boring. If, however, you do love the languages and the history, you will find a dear friend in this book and a kindred spirit in Professor Tolkien.Read moreRead less
