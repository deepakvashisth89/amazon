Hard to find this book with an intact dust jacket. This one had one and the entire book condition was virtually damage-free. No markings and other artifacts you found in vintage books.The only thing that was slightly disappointing is the book is entirely B&W, which is fine for the photographer's portraits. But, the other two sections could have benefited from a little color - either 4C for the photographs or 2C for design elements. The choice of paper is clearly archival, so it is a bit rough especially for a book of this size.
