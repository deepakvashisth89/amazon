Everything by Watts is brilliant. This booklet is a case both for and against the practice of meditation, written is Watt's characteristic self-contradictory way. He writes of the benefits of stilling the mind, and yet goes on to state that the only reason for such practice is that one happens to enjoy doing it, just like one may happen to enjoy listening to music. He says one eventually reaches the realization that spiritual or religious practices of any kind are unnecessary, because there is no way for you physical organism to get away from what it truly is, and the ego is merely a delusion that has no power whatsoever to affect any change in anything.
