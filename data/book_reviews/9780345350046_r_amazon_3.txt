Quite some time ago I read most of the Conan Flagg books and enjoyed them, but this one made me angry.  I am a native Oregonian and have lived about 2/3 of my life here.  I have lived in many parts of the state, and have never heard natives talk like the characters in this book.  This book was written in 1976.  I was in an Oregon college in 1966 and had a friend in my living group from Burns (the main town in this book.)  Neither she nor any of her friends I met talked in this hillybilly slop.  I don't think I have ever heard the word "resevoir" pronounced "rezzavoy" at least not by an Oregonian.One of the characters was the owner and editor of the local newspaper.  She was supposedly from Pendleton and I lived there for nearly 15 years.  Ms. Wren makes people seem like uneducated, ignorant idiots.The story was good, but this sloppy, inaccurate language made me so angry it was difficult to enjoy the book.Ms. Wren was from TX and maybe ranchers down there are ignorant,  lazy and sloppy in their speech, but we aren't.  She had been in living in OR for about 25 years when she wrote this and should have known better.Read moreRead less
