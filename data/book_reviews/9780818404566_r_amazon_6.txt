As a retired social worker, whose clients were often dealing with emotional distress, I wish I had been aware of this book to recommend it to them. Albert Ellis has a very conversational style of writing that makes the information he shares very accessible and enjoyable to read. The charts in Chapter 12, "REBT Insight No. 7: Solving Practical Problems as Well as Emotional Problems", were especially helpful.
