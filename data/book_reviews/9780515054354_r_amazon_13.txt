If you've never read Ngaio Marsh, do so immediately. I read all her books in paperback many years ago, and still have a lot of them on my shelves, but wanted to re-read so put on my kindle.  Shame so many of them are still very expensive to buy, but all her stories are tremendous and she has an exceptionally good writing style.  Most were written in the first half of the twentieth century, so make allowances for some old fashioned references and attitudes (though not racism, thank goodness).Read moreRead less
