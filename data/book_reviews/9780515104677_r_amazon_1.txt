I lived through this period in Viet Nam and it is a pleasure to read an account (though fictionalized) that tells the story from our point of view, and without the ever present political spin that we have been fed. The events reflect the actual war, the characters are very close to those I served with and the politics are accurate. My congratulations to Mr. Berent on another fine book.
