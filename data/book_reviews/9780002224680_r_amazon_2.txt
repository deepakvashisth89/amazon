Treachery in the high stakes world of oil.  A thrilling engaging entertaining novel of a billionaire oil cabal and their actions to render a state-of-the-art offshore oil drilling platform inoperable.  To that extent, a myriad of unexpected events result from a Scottish Lord, his kidnapped daughters, two ex-police officers, and, a cast of sane and deranged characters.
