Overall a good story about a ship in a storm. The beginning of the book was very good because it had a nice story line and a historical look at the Windjammer cruise line and the Phantome. But then it got sooooo technical. I learned more about hurricanes in this book than I ever care to know...lat/long, mb, wind speed, blah blah blah. I wanted more of a story line and not technical data about the storm.Therefore, I skipped a few chapters as it go really drawn out.
