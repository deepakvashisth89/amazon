"The man who was not Terrence O'Grady had come quietly." Hands down my favorite opening line to a book ever!  And it just gets better from there.  All of the books in the series do a fantastic job of balancing great characters with depth and various motivations of their own with exciting action too.  And the cultures of the Liaden universe this book established are fascinating.  I am a busy person and can't waste time on shallow writing. I want worlds in which I can immerse myself and Lee and Miller supply that in abundance but in prose that is amazingly effortless to read.  I really couldn't say enough good things about this book. I came across it years and years ago in its first paperback edition and bought it again after that first paperback literally fell apart from use. Now I'm happy to have it on Kindle so I can pull it out any time I want to revisit a great world with characters who make me laugh and sometimes bring tears to my eyes.
