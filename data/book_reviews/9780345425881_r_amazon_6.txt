This was my first Sally Wright novel, and I enjoyed it thoroughly, both in terms of the suspense and the development of the setting and characters.  Wright writes excellent dialogue that provides not only enjoyable reading, but insight into who these people are.  Her characters are developed thoughtfully, and they do come alive on the page--always a plus, in a mystery or any other kind of fiction. As a former inhabitant of the academic cauldron in which this story takes place, I was especially interested in--and often amused by--the often arcane battles among the well-defined denizens of various departments and disciplines. The ending had me on the edge of my seat (figuratively speaking--I was reading in bed), and the result was clever and satisfying.
