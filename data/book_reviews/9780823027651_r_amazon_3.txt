There are plenty of things about this book that are helpful and insightful. The Kindle edition, unfortunately, was clearly rushed to market and is full of careless oversights. The index, for example, has no links. This wouldn’t be so bad if it had contained page numbers — which it doesn’t. Therefore it serves just list the things that are dealt with somewhere in the book. Godspeed finding them.Within the text there are plenty of active links.
