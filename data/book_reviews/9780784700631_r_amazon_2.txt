This Christian children's book goes from Aa to Zz. Aa is for angel, Bb is for baby, and so on. Each letter of the alphabet has a Scripture verse relating to the word it represents. Example, for Aa: God sent the angel Gabriel to a town in Galilee. Luke 1:26. Children will learn their abc's while they hear the Christmas story via short Scripture verses that they can grasp.
