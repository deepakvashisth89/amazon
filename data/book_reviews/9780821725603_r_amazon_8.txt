I just recently discovered the Mary Roberts Rinehart mystery novels.  After reading several of them, The Circular Staircase, The Man in Lower Ten, etc., I think this one, The Wall, so far is my favorite.  I've read that she is considered the "Agatha Christie of America" and honestly, I like the Mary Roberts Rinehart mysteries better than Agatha Christie.  If you like mysteries, this is a must read.
