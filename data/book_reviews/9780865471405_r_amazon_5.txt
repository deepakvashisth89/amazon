As a member of a gay bookclub for over 16 years I have read, and also refused to read, scores of gay oriented fiction.  I thought our club had pretty much exhausted the best work, and certainly the historically significant writing at this point in our curriculum, and when this book was suggested it seems that none of us had ever heard of it.
