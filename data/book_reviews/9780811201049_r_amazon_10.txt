This is the first book by Merton I've read and was my first venture into this subject matter (along with a couple of other books at the same time) and I learned a LOT with just this one book. He covers the affinities and contrasts between eastern and western mysticism with the deep thoroughness of one who not only understands both from experience, but expresses subtle, difficult ideas with plainness (and as much clarity as possible, given the subject) and a great talent with the written word.
