Given the many reviews on this work, I can only add that this is a FABULOUS book for the uninitiated on the assassination. As you go through the materials, the information Fonzi presents seems incredible to fantastical. Check the background. Fonzi's reputation. His "friendship" with Specter. His unbiased investigative work. His accepting the price he would have to pay professionally and personally. The most riveting part of this book is his relationship with the "I'd really like to talk to that man" Cuban refugee and the Phillips/Bishop dichotomy. Probably the most valuable part of this book is that you can put yourself in Fonzi's shoes. He's not outraged. He's not vengeful. He doesn't have an agenda save trying to find out the truth.And that's the thing. No matter what you think this first assassination was all about you'll be changed by this book. It's hard to describe, but Fonzi made it real for me. And once you start to question, you start to see that the truth was right in front of us all this time. "In the councils of government, we must guard against the acquisition of unwarranted influence, whether sought or unsought, by the militaryindustrial complex. The potential for the disastrous rise of misplaced power exists and will persist." Before you judge my tin foil hat, do a little research about how Mr. Powers should not have survived the shoot down the way he did.If you can grapple with the possibilities, you'll be overwhelmed by the sadness of what our nation and world could have been. Just remember that there have been 5 decades of obfuscation by the establishment government, media and industry so don't expect it to be easy.
