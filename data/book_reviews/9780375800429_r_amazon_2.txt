This book is loaded with fun pictures that the authors and illustrators creatively designed to include as many words that start with the letter of the page, without being disjointed.  The "S" page, for example, features a park scene where various characters are going down the slide, playing on the swings, running through the sprinklers, and even eating a sandwich by a sandbox!  Some letter pages have more words than others, but really, how creative can you get with letters like X?!  The number section is darling too, and even introduces counting by 10's.
