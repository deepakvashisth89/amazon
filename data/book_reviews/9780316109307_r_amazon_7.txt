Bronowski's was a keen intellect with the rare ability to to convey his complex, often subtle subject matter in a way that makes it accessible to us of mere mortal IQ. If you didn't get to experience the original presentation on PBS this book is a good substitute, and allows one the opportunity to read, reread, and digest the material before moving on. For a presentation that is now 40 years old it is still immensely relevant to this day.
