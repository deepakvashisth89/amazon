Having lived and worked as an ex-pat in the same region of Africa as the book is set in, I found Boyd's storytelling hilarious, and I confidently put it up there with Catch-22 as a continual laugh out loud book that runs too closely to the truth!  I loved living and working in West Africa, the people were wonderful, the cultures welcoming, and life was colorful and constantly surprising.  Boyd's colonial cast of characters is sadly accurate in many cases that I am familiar with over that decade, although that generation is disappearing.  Highly recommended!Read moreRead less
