Since the book is a classic, the rating is a given.  I wanted to read it before considering seeing the movie. It was an engrossing story that was not as difficult to follow as I had expected.  One does need to get a good handle on the Russian naming conventions (patronymics, nicknames) to help not getting lost.  There were some political type discussions that would be better appreciated a second time around, perhaps.  This version is helpful in terms of the introduction and footnotes.
