I've got a ton of Falcon guides for activities all over the country and find them an outstanding resource -- but Tennessee... Oh man, what a poor ass (i.e. poverty stricken) excuse for a state--I can't believe it is in the first world, let alone the United States.  If you're planning a trip to Tennessee, this guide will only confirm that there is nothing outdoorsy to do in the Volunteer State but hunt ducks on private land and piddle around on lakes several-hours-drive away.  It's different down South is a true statement.  Sigh...  But, having considered Tennesseans deep seeded hatred for literacy, I gave this book three stars for fighting the good fight!
