I've read many OBE books over the years and must rank this one right up there with my favorites. It isn't that the book is rife with techniques like William Buhlman's or Robert Bruce's books, as you'll be disappointed if that's what you're expecting. On the other hand if, like me, you enjoy reading of someone's personal encounters, then you'll enjoy this book, which is filled with Braschler's experiences, both in AND out of the body. His meetings with guides who teach him the inner laws of nature and other higher truths while on the astral plane is fascinating.Another thing that makes this book so interesting is that the author's inner experiences began having an influence on his normal waking state so that he began having remarkable encounters while fully awake. This gives the book an even more mystical, if not chilling, tone.Overall an excellent book for someone looking for lots of stories with mystical content and perhaps a smattering of techniques thrown in.Read moreRead less
