A very clever portrayal of a vain, selfish manipulator.The romance element is somewhat lacking as the hero and heroine start the book already in love and engaged - we don't see their courtship.The hero's mother carries the book - every other character pales beside her performance.  Hazard does the mother's portrayal all through dialogue with no authorial intrusion.  It's brilliant, really.Thank heavens the heroine is not a stupid girl and she finally twigs to what's going on and leaves the hero, hoping he'll wise up and man up.  He does and they live happily ever after.  This is one for the keeper shelf just because it's so different and so very, very well written.Read moreRead less
