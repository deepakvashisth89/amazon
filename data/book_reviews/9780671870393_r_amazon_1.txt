This book has everything... the young adult teen girls headed to a foregn land (namely Mykonos, Greece), the hunky guys they meet when they get there, the drama the mystery. But what it also has is Pike's crazy way of teaching you things. Things about Greece things about mythology... He is like the young adult Michael Chrichton. And in between those lines are a dark story that turns this little tale into a modern-day Greek tragedy and an adventure I have not forgotten since I read it 16 years or so ago.
