Before reading this, I assumed it to be about the frontier.  It is ... but yet, so much more.  A magnificent work written nearly 200 years ago.  The prose remains fresh.  No old-fashioned language.The protagonist, young Deerslayer, sets off to rendezvous with his friend, Chingachgook, a Mohican Chief.  Though uneducated and unable to read or write, Deerslayer demonstrates in actions throughout, that Wisdom is not Knowledge.  His unique character is an inspiration for living simply with integrity.During Deerslayer's journey to meet Chingachgook, he unexpectedly meets Henry (aka "Hurry Harry") March and the Hutter family.  Thereafter, nonstop action ensues.  Suspense, adventure, romance.  A captivating entertaining exciting experience.
