Within the bounds of marriage God gave us a gift - sex. It's that simple. When Adam and Eve were standing naked before God and before there was shame, God said "be fruitful and multiply". Essentially God said, "Adam, here is your wife and Eve here is your husband, you are to enjoy each other." When God created, He created it all "good". About man, He said "very good". God intended sex to be good, too, within marriage between a man and a woman - for both man and woman.This book treats the subject matter with utmost respect and dignity, but the lesson is not lost.
