Walter Benjamin put everything he knew into everything he wrote. It all resonates. This makes for challenging reading - at times, it seems like what he is saying is simply too much at a tilt with everything one thinks  one knows to seem comprehensible. Then, suddenly, one tilts, and the  extraordinary reach, eloquence and power of this man's reading hits home.
