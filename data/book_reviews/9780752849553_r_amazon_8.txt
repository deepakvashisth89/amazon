The third book in the Spoils of Time series takes place after WWII. Barty is New York with her daughter and is now in charge of the New York office of Lytton's.Circumstances lead her into a marriage that turns out to be a poor decision. With a husband and stepdaughter, both of whom are more interested in prestige and money, Barty struggles to keep her daughter Jenna well balanced. In England, Lytton's is struggling, and everyone is shocked both by Celia's remarriage and her intention to step down from the firm. A shocking tragedy rocks the family and puts the future of Lytton's at risk. The third and likely final chapter of the series takes us to the third generation and we see how they handle a changing world. Over the course of the series I became invested in the characters, some in particular, that made this volume sometimes hard to read because of some of the events.
