I have no idea where this old tattered book came from, but I read it as a teenager out of boredom one day and out of curiosity about the rather intimate picture on top. It's a fairly innocent book in terms of language. I don't know what kind of romances were published in those days...but I really admired the strength in both characters, they really forge out a successful career and personality for themselves. They were both strong-willed and amazing. It reminds me a lot of Paradise, by Judith Mcnaught. I think this is one is less funny, it's quite somber, as I remember. But well written, with well developed characters, and interesting plot and wonderful relationship. Give it a chance, it's a great read!ParadiseRead moreRead less
