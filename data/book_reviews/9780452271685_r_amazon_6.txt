Ms. Hayslip's autobiography makes for fascinating reading, at least for this reader whose exposure to Vietnam is limited to 2 months working in Danang in 2008.  It adds a valuable historical and personal perspective on the "American War" (as the Vietnamese refer to our Vietnam War)This is not a happy tale -- she explains unimaginable suffering, loss, sorrow, torture, cruelty, failures, and hardships amidst the struggle to survive in a time of civil war and major upheaval.  Few escaped the direct or indirect effects of abuse, imprisonment or persecution by both sides.  Hayslip offers a unique perspective having been a young conscript with the communist Viet Cong.Reading her story left me with with a broader perspective of Vietnamese culture and attitudes before, during, and after the war.  It's a very useful read for anyone desiring a deeper understand of Vietnam, its people and recent history.Read moreRead less
