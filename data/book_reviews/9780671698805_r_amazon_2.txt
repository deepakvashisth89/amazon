I had waited for quite a few years to read this book. I'd heard years earlier that there was a sequel to "The Regiment", but I'd never succeeded in finding a copy. I was determined to enjoy it, once I had it, and John Dalmas made that easy on me.The plot of the book struck a particular chord with me because I did my own military service very late in the Vietnam era. The last of the draftees were getting their early-outs and a lot of the professional NCOs and officers were going with them. Morale was shot to hell, discipline was practically non-existent, drug- and alcohol-abuse was endemic. A disheartening number of the new recruits were all-but-illiterate; uneducated, unmotivated, uninterested in anything better. Real Dead-End Kids, too many of them.I wonder what would have happened if a big T'swi  sergeant had met a bus-load of recruits reporting to Boot Camp and told them, "Welcome!
