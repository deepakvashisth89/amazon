This is the first book of Sparkle Hayter I've read. It's quite interesting to read with a lot of New York flair.But part of it is illogical and / or seems constructed.Most of all: Robin Hudson's motives for her actions are not clear.She tries to solve a murder and to rescue a young couple of eloped Europeans, neither of which she needs to do.Her only "excuse" for doing so is that she accidentally meets the victim of the murder and the young couple.Out of the gathered information she draws conclusions that are not convincing to me.Maybe the motives for Robin's sleuthing can be found in the earlier novels of Hayter.On the plus side there are a lot of more or less eccentric and / or neurotic characters which lead to a lot of equally eccentric events. (An old lady that tries to teach nuns in a convent how to be really pious; a bodybuilder who always trains his muscels in his open door but doesn't speak to the world because he practices Zen; underground art-groups who put fake dog-poo on the New York streets etc.)Still, the book is noch really funny, just amusing.Read moreRead less
