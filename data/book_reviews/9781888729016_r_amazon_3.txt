This book is geared for younger, open minded travelers on shoe string budgets.  I think the author has many great tips and words of advice for global "stompers" while offering such advice as budgeting, how to cut travel costs, where to be cautious, how to avoid getting scammed, tricks to protect your belongings while traveling, where the party scenes are around the globe, how to find legal marijuana and where it's ok (and not OK) to smoke it, where work is an option and how to go about finding cheap travel deals, etc.  This book is for the kind of traveler that fits into the authors stomper sort of lifestyle but could be of value to any traveler.  For some, this book will be a bit too liberal as you can see from the negative reviews.  But overall, I'd say this is a very good book for a young person considering going out into the world and doing a little or a whole lot of traveling.Read moreRead less
