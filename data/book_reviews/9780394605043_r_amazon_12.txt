Verdict: a really good book, just not my cup of tea.Bowen could write and I understood what she was aiming at: depicting emptiness and people who simply can't communicate because they're too wrapped up in themselves. If you want depth coated in excellent prose, this is the book for you. If, however, you want the plot to go somewhere, and maybe also a bunch of characters you can at least understand, better keep away.All in all, I don't mind having read it.
