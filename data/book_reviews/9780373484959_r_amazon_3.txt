I stumbled upon this book when I was 13 (young I know). I had misread the description, but I am very glad I did. The Calhoun sisters quickly became my favorite series. Nora Roberts does such a wonderful job portraying these characters. They feel so real and you almost feel as if you are the one experiencing every bit of anger, sadness, and passion. I am still re-reading these books eight years later and each time it feels as if I am reading it for the first time. I definitely recommend the Calhoun series, you will have a hard time putting it down.Read moreRead less
