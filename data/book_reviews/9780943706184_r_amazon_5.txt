This book is by far the best I have seen to help children understand the holocaust. It is not a book for a child to read alone, but together with the parents (or any other grownup for that matter). The book describes the holocaust from the view of a small child that, of course, can not understand what happened. The author is providing a great tool for everyone with children to explain to them what happened in Germany with the Jews and what we have to do to prevent this from ever happing again.Read moreRead less
