The Narnia series is like six decent childish books for kids… and then the seventh is suddenly a brutal, terrifying, horrific and stunningly beautiful adult book that is a journey into the true dark vs light nature of the world, and the true power of truth, honor, and faith in the face to so many liars, in the face of a hopeless, dark, defeated world with few heroes, that we actually live in. One of the greatest books ever written, this sole Narnia book is better than Tolkien and Asimov combined while the first six are just fun kids books that you only need to read to get the full context of this one. I don’t read much fiction, I prefer non fiction, programming, chemistry, thermodynamics, etc. but this one is one of the few fiction books that possibly saved my life by matching the hopelessness of the world I was and remain in, of so few allies if any of truth, honor, and doing the right thing regardless of the consequences.
