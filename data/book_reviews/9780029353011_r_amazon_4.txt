One of the earlier works on Unit 731.  Written by investigative journalists after they worked on a television series for BBC.The first part of the book covers the beginning of Japans BW efforts by focusing on LTG Shiro Ishii.  It describes the man, his achievements, and how he progressed into managing a BW empire.Following this third of the book is how the US investigated Japan's BW efforts, and then followed by the data swap for immunity from war crimes.The story is fast past, interesting, and well researched.  It is not a true historical study, but a well written investigative report.
