Somehow I had never read Faulkner (surely I was exposed to "The Sound and Fury" in school but I don't recall it).  But Amazon's Kindle policy that makes the classics very cheap or even free has lured me into many of the masters and I decided to start with "As I Lay Dying" as an intro to Faulkner.  I wanted one where I had no idea of the plot line from old movies!  I didn't know what to expect and was in for quite a surprise.  The narrative is all first person but from a variety of voices and all in Mississippi rural dialect.  Since I grew up in the South (New Orleans with many childhood visits to the Mississippi Gulf Coast) the dialogue was quite understandable.  And powerful. It was hard to put the book down.  The plot line begins at the deathbed of matriarch of the family.  The family members hover around and then go on a long perilous journey to bury her in her family's town.  Through it all we get insights into each family member, their struggles and secrets.  Quite profound.  I now plan to read more of Faulkner and highly recommend this book.Read moreRead less
