A slight, almost theatrical novel by America’s great novelist. Carpenter’s Gothic is a wry satire that takes aim at the curious hybrid blend of Christian zealotry and materialist vanity that has gripped America. Depicted entirely in a Carpenter’s Gothic style house (which Gaddis lived in), we are thrown into this characteristic, dialogue-driven situation of an empire in flames. What is lost here amidst the mad brilliance, is the pathos, the development, and the depth of feeling.Read moreRead less
