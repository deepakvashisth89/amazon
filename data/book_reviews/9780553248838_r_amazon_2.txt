In this book set in 1940s New York City, antiquarian book investigator Henry Gamadge finds himself in the middle of a mystery involving people who were American ex-pats in 1920s Paris, a years-ago murder, a disappearing collection of literary letters, and a supposed Medieval manuscript.  As is typical of a novel in this series, the characters are interesting and well-drawn, and the mystery plot unfolds cleverly with an unexpected conclusion.
