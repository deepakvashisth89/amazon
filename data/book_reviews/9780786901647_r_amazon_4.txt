RA Salvatore is a genius and Drizzt is the ultimate hero. Through all the books we see Drizzt and his heartbreaks , and his stuggles, and his happiness. We watch him and his friends grow . The transformations of these characters are so dramatic and you find yourself pulled into their lives and it's such an amazing journey. And the battle scenes , Salvatore describes the fighting in a way that puts you right there , truly my favorite fantasy writer ever.
