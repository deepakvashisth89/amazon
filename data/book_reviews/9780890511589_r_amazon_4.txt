Ken Ham, director of AIG, used to be a science teacher.  His training in the general sciences show through in this book about the intersection of science and religion.  The lie correctly identifies evolution as a faith based system by exposing the non-science which is needed to believe the lie.  This book is at a easy reading level and should be used as an introduction to thinking for junior high and older children.
