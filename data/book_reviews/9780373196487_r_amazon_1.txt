Vicky Sutton thinks that she will never have children. That is why she and her former husband Zane decided to have her eggs fertilized. It is a shock then after the death of Zane in a car accident that she has become a mother to twins.The lab that kept her eggs and the eggs of other woman was selling them to infertile couples trying to have a baby. An Englishman James Thayer and his wife used Vicky's eggs to have little Edmund and Mary Rose, but then Mrs. Thayer left her husband and children to go live with another man in France.Once Vicky hears about this, she gets a lawyer and demands custody of her son and daughter.
