This is a very interesting mystery book about a young teenager named Cassie B. Jones. She always wanted to become a detective. One day she gets a letter from her cousin Alexandra. It has a ticket to England. When she arrives in London airport she finds out that Alexandra is missing. This was  her chance to become a detective.  She tried to help inspector Crandall. It  seems to Cassie that Aunt Beatrice and Alexandra's cousin Gwen are part of  the mystery. Find out how Cassie solved the mystery.Read moreRead less
