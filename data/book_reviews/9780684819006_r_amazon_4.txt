Beth Fowler is the author of this short, but cute question book.  Throughout the book is questions that ask things such as, "Could you not mind if I unintentionally shut you in the closet?", and, "Could you help with the sewing by pulling off loose buttons?", and, "Could you keep me on my toes by weaving in and out between my feet?"  Only a true cat lover could appreciate the 'love' in this book!This is a quick read and I would recommend it for the true cat lover's book shelf.
