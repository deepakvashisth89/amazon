I've just finished reading Ms. Sutcliffe's Notorious.  It's been a long time since I was so completely entranced by a story.  Jason and Destiny's love is a tangible thing, I found myself laughing, crying, gasping at the surprising plot twists.  The incredibly descriptive scenery is the perfect background for the extraordinary love and passion these two share.  England, South Africa, India.  Ms. Sutcliffe's incredible talent transports you to these places in spell binding detail.  Her use of secondary characters enlivens the story.
