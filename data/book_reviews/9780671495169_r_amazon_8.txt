Some great short stories here.  His writing is unique...powerful.  A couple of my favorites from this selection are _The Death of Doctor Island_ (a psychological thriller in SF setting)  and _Alien Stones_.  If some filmmaker was serious about a SF movie they would make _Alien Stones_.  Wolfe is not everyone's cup of tea as you must think and reread.  Meaning is teased and worked out of his texts.  It is not generally handed to you on a platter. Since Wolfe's longer works can be problematic, the short stories are a good place to start.  I do not recommend newbies diving into _The New Sun_ series until wetting one's feet first.  He is not your father's SF/fantasy writer.  Also, it might help to know that Wolfe is a paranoid/schizophrenic.  I read that on his own blog. For my money he is the finest writer of our time and belongs on the self next to "The Greats."
