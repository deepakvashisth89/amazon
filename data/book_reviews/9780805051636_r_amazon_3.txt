At first I wasn't sure I was going to like it.  It's a little different than most literature I have recently read, makes the reader peer into the darker corners of ourselves when it comes to domestic violence, hidden prejudices, and family relations.  The main reason I didn't just love it was the ending seemed too harsh, and while all the character development of Native Americans is not negative a lot was.  My husband is Native American, spent most of his life as a police officer, or in the Service of our country.  He has spent much of his time working to change prejudices on both sides.  Having said that Ms Greenberg is a talented writer, wrote a story that we should not ignore, I loved the fact that though Grandma was on the road, she was still there in spirit and support for everyone in the family, showing a rare unconditional love that we all say we should strive for.Read moreRead less
