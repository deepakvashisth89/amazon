A book on Fauvism wherein more than 3/4 of the illustrations are black & white (as is this book) is an absurdity. Most of them are rather too small, as well. If not for the interesting text, it would be a waste of paper. I paid just $7 for a copy in a used book store, so don't feel cheated.
