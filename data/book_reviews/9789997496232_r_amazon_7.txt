Billion Dollar Brain (1966) is the most readable of Len Deighton’s first four WOOC(P) secret agent books, and also the lightest in tone (relatively…we’re not talking Austin Powers here…). It concerns the activities of Deighton’s anonymous agent infiltrating a private army being put together by an American billionaire who sounds vaguely like Ross Perot a few decades before his heyday (the brain of the title is a giant computer, adding to the Perot identification). The strongly pro-American/anti-Communist organization annoys both Eastern and Western governments, including the Brits, hence the WOOC(P) involvement.The plot still has plenty of convolutions, twists and hidden agendas but I found it much easier to follow than IPCRESS File, Horse Under Water or even Funeral in Berlin, and anyone interested in Deighton might want to start here.Read moreRead less
