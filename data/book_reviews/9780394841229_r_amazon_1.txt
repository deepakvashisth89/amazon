Written in the 70s, but completely relevant… Even moreso… Today.What is it that brought about the spate of young adult literature back in the 70s, filled with death and horror. Coimer’s I am the Cheese and the Chocolate War were also examinations of our character as a society.That being said, it was along with another titled the Girl in the Box, which stole the great deal of innocence from me, in that it dealt with a world I was a part of, but not part of the evil in it. These books exposed the evil and the horror that can befall people as bland and and uninteresting as myself, thus opening my eyes to a world that needed to be seen as I became a young teenager. While I regret reading some of these books, I can’t regret their stories. These books opened my eyes to the reality of shadowy government entities, filthy terrorists, and the innocence they use to attain the desired ends. I recommend this book and every Robert Cormier book to every child from age 10 to 12. Perhaps the snowflakes need to open their eyes to the real world, much as we needed it 45 years ago.
