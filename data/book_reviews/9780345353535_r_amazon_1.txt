Anne McCaffrey described the authors as 'scary good'...and they are!This is the first book in the Liaden series that I ever read.  I'm not sure that it should have been technically...but it suited me well and while the story fits into a larger universe, it also works vert well as a stand alone.  It introduces both the Master Trader, Shan yos Galen, a starship captain and a Healer (or Empath) and the character of Pricilla Mendoza. Though he is half human he is a prominent member of a Liaden Clan known as Korval,  She has an interesting background as a 'Witch of Scintia' a magic adept exiled from her home world.  At the beginning of the book she is marooned on a planet functionally the equivalent of a 'one horse town'  by another starship captain. She is penniless and friendless.  Fortunately she is able to apply for a post with the Korval flagship captained by Shan yos Galen and does so just to get off-world.  The story follows her as she seeks and finds a place in Shan yos Galen's ship and the Liaden culture. A thoroughly enjoyable read which (I find) gets even better when re read and more details and nuances emerge.!I should say that I love all the Liaden Universe books written by Sharon Lee and her husband Steve Miller and haven't read one I didn't enjoy.  (In fact I read and re read them)  So, if you haven't experienced the authors' world building skills this is a good place to start...and if you have, you MUST read Conflict of Honors as Shan and Pricilla are important characters in the Korval Saga.Read moreRead less
