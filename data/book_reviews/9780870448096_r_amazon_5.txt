I got this from "emerald-coast-books1" and it was categorized as "very good" which I think should mean nobody would mistake it for new, yet it has no serious flaws. But there were two popups I had to repair so they'd work, and one seems to be missing a part. I would have called it "adequate" myself.As for the book in general, it is very pretty! The animals listed are very obscure. I am a biology fan and I hadn't even heard of most of the animals. But that might be useful in some way like for teaching a child that the world is big and faraway places can be very different from home. It's certainly more fun than the usual "z is for zebra" stuff in books for little kids, where the same handful of animals are discussed over and over as if there were only twenty species on Earth. Though the last page does feature koalas, yawn. And they just sit there, so from a popup-fan perspective, it's not a very interesting page. I wish these books ended on their best page, a grand finale. Kinda a downer that the last page features a boring animal and a boring display of it. Some of the earlier pages are neat though, a good variety of types of popup, like foldouts, wheels, things that slide around behind a slit, and always a big 3-D feature in the middle. Lots of fun.I would give the book itself 4 stars, but the torn up copy I got 2 stars, so I'll average that to 3.Read moreRead less
