I was very excited to read this book after checking all the 4 & 5 start reviews, not to mention the fact that I'm a huge fan of unrequited love stories...but was sorely, sorely disappointed.Robert is betrothed to Eleanor, his childhood sweetheart, and it's thoughts of her that help him through his imprisonment in a French prison when he is captured during the war. Upon his escape and return, he discovers that Eleanor had continued on without him: she is a new mother and married to another man.Katryn, Eleanor's younger sister, has always been in love with Robert and seizes the opportunity to marry Robert when he mentions that he is in need of a wife -  and quickly. She's eager to earn his love, and is willing to be second best in his heart if he could just come to love her even a little, at some point.When Robert is accused of treason, the married couple must find a way to clear Robert's name.Sounds intriguing, does it not? Except...The beginning was so forced (I think Robert found out about Eleanor and proposed to Katryn and Katryn proposed--er, I mean, accepted--within the first 5 pages) and the "thrill" of Robert's crime came across as shallow and unreal. The ending was worse: you don't understand how Katryn had ever loved a weak a hero as Robert, who spent 90% of the story panting after her sister. And you don't understand how Robert suddenly realized he is in love with Katryn because he spent 90% of the book salivating over his wife's sister.I thought Katryn was supposed to be the heroine, but the pages talked mostly about how much Robert loved Eleanor. I also thought she was supposed to have "spirit" but it mostly came across as stupidity. I mean, hello? Your husband has fears and nightmares about his imprisonment and instead of trying your best to help him, you just keep silently keep hoping he'll get better on his own and ignore all his traumas.And Robert, a hero? I scoff at that. I pitied him for marrying Katryn who "loved" him so much but did a minimal amount to go about making him love her or help him over his hardships of the war. And what he did near the end! I wanted so much to punch him!This book had so much potential. I'm so sad I'll never get the 6 hours of my life I spent reading this book back.Read moreRead less
