This is my favourite book of all time and I read it once a year as it always reassures me about the wonder of life and all that really matters.  Only a complete cynic or hard hearted person could fail to fall in love with everything about this book - the characters, the scenery, the atmosphere, the adventure, the emotional situations, the struggle between right and wrong - and all the honour and mystery of what makes a good life for each of us.I find the writing absolutely charming - personal, direct, clear, emotionally evolved.  It is as your best friend is telling you about and adventure that they experienced.  I can not for the life of me understand why this has not been made into a sweeping epic film  IT HAS IT ALL!! The beauty of Scotland, the romance of kilts and ceilidhs, love, adventure, betrayal, sex and a bit of the IRA and arms running.  You can picture ever detail vividly in your mind as the plot takes you from one place to another........................Nothing left to say except that I LOVE every word of it.  And the characters are very vivid to me.  I know exactly who I would cast in each role if I were a film director.
