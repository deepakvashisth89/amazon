This was a great book. I enjoyed reading it, but it wasn’t until the second half that I really got pulled into it. It is very heavy with military terminology and lifestyle. Anyone that has been in the military will resonate with it. If you haven’t spent time in the military, it might lose you a little bit, but is worth the read. One other issue is there were lots of characters and it was a little hard for me to keep everyone straight. In the end, you wind up following just a couple of the main characters, though, so it’s not bad.Overall a good book. Certainly just an introduction to a much longer story, it seems.
