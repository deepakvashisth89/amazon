Living in Hampton, VA, and a fan of Colonial Williamsburg, nearby - I really was intrigued when I came across this volume.I read it in a day!  I just could not stop reading.Ms. Hoffman does a wonderful job of fleshing out the characters, and makes the reader feel as if they are there.  I could almost taste the saltwater spray!  I felt sympathy for a man with a terrible past trying to live a respectable life once he had met the love of his life.  Only to be pulled back into piracy by a greedy man full of delusions.I'll not say more, as I don't want to give anything away.  You won't be sorry you picked up this book!Read moreRead less
