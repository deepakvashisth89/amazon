I found myself struggling to understand the flow of the author but I couldn't stop reading. Determined to understand why something was pulling me to remember the story. Then as the story closed and the pages fell into place in my mind, my heart lifted and I understood why this book is so special. Even as I write this I know I have seen this in my dreams and for the1st time since I kisses my son a final goodbye do I feel like I am exactly where I am supposed to be in time n the loss of my son is not a burden I cannot carry, and my son is where he was destined to be.
