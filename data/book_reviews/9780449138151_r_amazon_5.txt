I have been reading Travis McGee novel in chronological order. In this novel there is a big shift from the earlier ones, towards more violence and more casual sex.  McGee is less introspective and is harder and more aggressive.McGee actually carries a gun and is prepared to use it. There are deaths by knives, firearms, explosions, blunt objects and unarmed combat. In previous novels McGee withholds himself for his current infatuation but here he is quite promiscuous.  On the other hand the plot is more complex and elaborate as the setting moves from Florida to Mexico to California. We also find out a little more about McGee's character and his background.The book is still a compulsive read but is not as satisfying as the earlier books and the reader has less empathy with the main character.Read moreRead less
