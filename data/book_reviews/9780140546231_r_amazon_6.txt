I read this book when it was first published over 20 years ago. To be precise, I started reading it in a bookstore and made an absolute idiot of myself because I was laughing so hard. This book is a flashback of a school trip to a farm where one of the children brings along his pet boa constrictor. The child's language is straightforward, as though everything being said is perfectly normal, but the wonderful illustrations tell a different story: my particular favorite is when the children visit the hen house, and the boa is looking rapturously at the chickens who are fluttering away at flank speed. I actually came across a newspaper column some years later in which adults mentioned their favorite books, and one adult also chose "The Day Jimmy's Boa Ate the Wash"!I am getting this for my nephew for his 4th birthday, and I fully expect he will be just as enthralled as I was!
