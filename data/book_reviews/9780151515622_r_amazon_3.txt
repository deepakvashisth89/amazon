This classic, underread novel, has a little of something for everyone. Faith healing, movie-making, secret homosexual affairs, secret heterosexual affairs, farming, courtroom dramas, and shoe salesmen.  All while the narrator, a young woman named Orpha Chase, comes into her own as a wife, mother, lover, sister, and author. It's filled with wonderful details about life in the country, as well as early Los Angeles.Here's a sample of a the brilliant and humorous writing:But Lon was not only entertainment; he mixed entertainment with information like a modern novelist who not only gives you a lot of love-making, but also leaves you, when the lovers part, with a lot of information about running a hotel, avoiding sharks, or living in a kibbutz.And it deals with faith in a way that is respectful and not hokey.
