A very cool glimpse into life in England in the first half of the 1600s. So much information. All of it fascinating. As I read, I kept thinking: Oh, boy, I wish I could go back to that era for a short vacation (nothing longer than a couple weeks...I like modern conveniences too much). But the southern counties of England in the 17th century, as described by Underdown, were so socially complex and sophisticated, that I was drawn to them. He made them real for me.Don't get me wrong. This is not a light read. I think a lot of readers would find the info dry and maybe even boring. You must really LOVE history and especially minute social history to get the wow factor from this book. Underdown is a fine writer, but he's also a careful historian, so the reporting is very even-handed. But its a real pleasure to hear a thoughtful scholar describe a distant and extinct society and attempt to explain what it means.Anyone who is serious abour the English Civil War (ECW) should study this book. It sets the stage for what takes place in England in the 1640s and beyond.The time was anything but simplistic. The people were anything but one-dimensional.Read moreRead less
