I can not take my 2000 page dictionary to Spanish class everyday, so I had to find a good replacement.  I believe that the Harpercollins dictionary is just that.  The font size is just right for me along with the easy to follow definitions.  The only possible drawback is that sometimes definitions are only applicable to certain Spanish speaking countries.  This however is something that will probably never be able to be solved, especially when dealing with a pocket sized dictionary.
