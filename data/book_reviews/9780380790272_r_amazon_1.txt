This is a highly contrived story, scarcely believable in any of its major plot elements, which play out slowly and predictably right down to the bomb timer mere seconds away from zero.Fiction usually requires some suspension of disbelief, but to buy this story you'd have to accept that the 8 most powerful world leaders would agree to spend a week together holding hands and farting daises for world peace. Next, they decide to convene this summit on an ocean liner, clearly vulnerable to a wide variety of attacks and mishaps.Add to that: the US president is a kindly and brave leader but his peers from other countries are cowards. The security teams make stupid, obvious blunders so it is almost an anticlimax when the bad guys finally show up. Then there's the guy who has to get past his ancient fears to save the world, and the computer nerd forced to write software of a type he knows nothing about, in just a few minutes while held at gunpoint.
