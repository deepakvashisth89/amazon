This is an excellent writing of Ramayan and the period of 90s. I was a school going kid in 80-90s and vividly remember most of the events Jonah covered in this book. At a time it felt like the author was taking me back to my childhood. Some of the interpretation of the events I don’t agree with him but I can confidently say this was very honest effort to know/explain India and Ramayan from grassroots.
