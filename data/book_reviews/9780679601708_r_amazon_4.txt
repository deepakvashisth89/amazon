This book is an excellent read for the serious student of the civil war and a major contribution to history in my opinion. Mr. Foote pulls no punches for either side but gives an historical accounting of all the bloody events leading up to the siege and the siege itself. As he details in the book, the victory at Vicksburg was a major turning point in the war as the Mississippi River was opened up to war supplies from the north and commercial enterprise all the way to New Orleans. The reader comes away with a better understanding of the terrible cost in lives and injury on both sides in this campaign.Read moreRead less
