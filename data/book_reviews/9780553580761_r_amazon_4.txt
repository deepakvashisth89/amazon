I read one of John Straley's books back in the 90s and was impressed by his writing.  So I decided to read Cold Water Burning this year.  Straley has an interesting voice - here's a short quote from a scene with the main character's girlfriend in chapter 7:  "stroked each other, saying each other's names in the dark, filling each other up as if we could pour ourselves back and forth, spilling and refilling our bodies over and over again."The mystery wasn't predictable, but I found it hard to sustain my interest.  There was a little too much local color (blues bands and minor characters) and scenic descriptions (lots of talk about birds) - I felt it got in the way of the central story.I was particularly interested in the roommate character Todd, whom the author hints is autistic. I thought Straley did a good job describing the character's intense focus on interests and his need for routines and "protocols."
