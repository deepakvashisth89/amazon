Well, I love reading books about the war of the roses so I thought, why not? What I wasn’t prepared for was the old or Middle English it was written in.  I have  read several books of that time period written  somewhat in Middle English, so I was able to follow it pretty well. At times Richard’s bad luck got on my last nerve, hence the 4 stars.
