Having never read the Comanche series by Catherine Anderson, I was surprised at how I fell in love with Indigo in this wonderful story. I have always had a soft spot for any Indian lore, as I know I was from some tribe in an earlier life. More than likely Havasupai.  The way in which the author has the main character walk between two worlds, her Comanche and the Western one, adds great depth to this book. By far one of my favorites.Read moreRead less
