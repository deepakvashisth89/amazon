I don't usually read too many books that are historical but this one was recommended to me by a coach so I decided to give it a chance, and I'm glad I did. Noble gives the reader permission to trust their intuition and inner knowing of truth. I didn't give this book 5 stars for the reason that I would have liked to go deeper, and while this is a great read, Noble reviews many topics with medium depth, from art to astrology and more.
