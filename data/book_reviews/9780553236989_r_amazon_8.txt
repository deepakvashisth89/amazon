Note: This is my review after having read slightly more than half the book:After reading the first several pages I had high hopes for this book... then it just started to spiral into complete absurdity.1 - The author felt the need to include people where they were not and to make certain individuals famous before their time.  Yes, it is a novel, but since it covers real events, the author should have stuck closer to the facts.  And name-dropping can only get you so far.2 - The author describes various Sioux rituals that, so far as I know, are completely fabricated.  Maybe this was cool 50-60 years ago, but now it reads like pure nonsense.3 - Much of the dialogue, especially by the Sioux, is so laughable it is pathetic.I would like to finish this book only because I am interested how the author handles the battles of the Rosebud and Little Big Horn (compared to other novels).  Anyhow, my advice, you can find better reading elsewhere... such as on your standard tube of toothpaste.April 11, 2012 -- I plowed through this book as best I could, wanting nothing more than to finish.  Coming into this book I had high hopes and really expected to enjoy it.  The plot is so improbable and the writing foolish.  Sorry to say it, but the book just kept jumping the shark all the way through.Read moreRead less
