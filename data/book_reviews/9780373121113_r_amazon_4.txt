Compromising photographs, partly enhanced, are sent to Rachel Blair and Matthew Riordan for reasons unknown.  Rachel has also been the focus of annoying incidents, but can't imagine who is behind them.  Matt decides to  defuse the scandal potential by announcing his engagement to Rachel.Not  up to Napier's usual high standards.  The premise has potential, but the  application was weak.  Usually I am engrossed from the first paragraph, but  this time I found my attention wandering.  If this is the first time you  have read a Susan Napier, don't pick this book.Read moreRead less
