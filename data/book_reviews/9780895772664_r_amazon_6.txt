After a trip to Asheville, NC where O Henry is buried and a trip to the cemetery, we decided to re-read (after all these years) some O Henry.  The Gift of the Magi is a beautiful short story, very vintage in language and style.  We read it this year after Christmas Eve dinner.  I gave it to some friends.
