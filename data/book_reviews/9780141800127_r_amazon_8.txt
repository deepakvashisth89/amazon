I had seen the movie multiple times before finally deciding to read the book it was based on. Truthfully, some (many) scenes are word-for-word. They didn’t alter much aside from the ending and just a few other small portions of the book. I felt like I was rewatching the movie as I was reading it tbh. It’s almost like King wrote it for the screen. I liked it okay. I’d probably give it 3.5/5 if I could. I appreciate the novella length.
