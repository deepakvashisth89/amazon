For those of us who don't live in dream houses with unlimited budgets,  most of the do-it-yourself books are just "wish books." This one,  though, is as direct and basic as Hazelton's TV show. Maybe now we can start a  project without stopping and running to the home supply store every 20  minutes (and without re-mortgaging the house to pay a contractor to do the  work).
