Not many people under the age of 50 can remember Life Magazine much anymore and that is indeed a shame.Life, was always known for the fantastic photographs that were featured on it's pages and the nature end of that deal is on display right here in this wonderful book: "Our National Parks."This is not meant as a guide to the entire National Park service that spreads over the planet between Hawaii and Maine but it is an amazing collection of photographic images of the splendor you will find in select spots throughout the United States. The Blue Ridge Mountains to the Grand Canyon. The Channel Islands to The Arches. Mount Rainier to the Everglades. The photos are the stars of this volume and from desert to mountains from plains to seascapes it all displays the beauty that is found in this country that was wisely preserved for future generations to see in it's natural state of being.This is a bargain for what you will discover under the covers of this book.Four & 1/2 Stars!Read moreRead less
