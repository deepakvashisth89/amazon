This is an incredible biography in poetry.  Virtually each page teaches us to connect our today with our yesterdays in beautiful, powerful verse.  I received it as a present, and have found the beauty and power of his writing to be one of a kind among our young American poets.  He speaks to the horrors of his past (the shooting of his father, for example) in echoes that are reflected off the many walls of day to day life.  Buy it. Keep it. Read it once.
