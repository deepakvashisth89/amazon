Technically the second of the Chapel Hollow stories, The Silent Strength of Stones, actually takes place before The Thread that Binds the Bones. The beauty of this series is that the stories needn't be read in a particular order. Each can stand alone, and each enriches the reader's understanding and appreciation of the other.This coming-of-age story follows Nick as he discovers the magic within himself and confronts those who would use magic for darker, more selfish goals. Nick's journey takes him from an isolated boy who spends much of his time watching others from afar, to a member of a community, an adopted brother of a shapeshifter and the boyfriend of a witch.Grounded in vivid descriptions, The Silent Strength of Stones is both a wistful glimpse into a magical world just beneath the skin of our normal lives and a taste of the lush Pacific Northwest.Read moreRead less
