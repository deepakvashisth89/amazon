Another good entry in the Nero Wolfe series. This is really three novellas, not one story, which is cool, too. I love that these are not so far separated from the real lives of people that they feel dated today. The setup for the first story is just fun...a woman waiting in the office while Archie talks to Wolfe in the orchid rooms is murdered...with Wolfe's discarded necktie. Oops! In the second book, a woman comes and asks Wolfe to tell people she did not shoot her husband with a gun she had purchased. While she's there, he's murdered, of course. The third story deals with a hidden bundle of counterfeit bills found in the parlor of a woman who runs a rooming house for actors. All of these are good and Stout has some fun with them.
