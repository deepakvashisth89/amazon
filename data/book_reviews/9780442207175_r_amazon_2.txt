The advantage of this anatomy book is that it shows how the muscles attach and work with the bones. This makes how the body moves alot more clear, which in turn makes for more dynamic drawing. In my experience this distinguishes it from other artist's anatomy books. I only wish that those depictions of muscle with bone were larger and there were many more of them.
