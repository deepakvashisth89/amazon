The book concentrates too much on the names of all persons involved, even minor people and bounces back and forth in time which can be confusing at times; where you have to stop and say "wait a minute".  It tells the story, but the info from the diaries is blase; doesn't really add much at all, so don't think you'll be reading a bunch of "personal" stuff from the family.  What blew my mind, is that I have read in several sources that the tsarista COULDN'T STAND the tsar; she felt he was weak, spineless and held no attraction for her.  She felt she was the smart one, but she's the one that basically brought down the family.  In this book, everything makes it look like she's stupendously in LOVE WITH HIM.  Interesting.
