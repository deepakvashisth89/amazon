I read Levi’s more famous book about his horror at auschwitz and was profoundly moved. However, nothing he described about the Lager was surprising. This book is about the period after his liberation thanks to the Red Army and his struggle to return to Italy. Most of the book takes place in Russia and I’ve never read anything like it, what it was like immediately after a country has come back from absolute devastation to triumphant over the most despicable enemy imaginable. Although this book was banned in the Soviet Union because of its negative portrayal of Russia and in particular its army, the author clearly was fascinated by the country and admired its people so unlike his own. It made want to go to Russia, which I’ve only visited once, a wonderful week in St. Petersbur.
