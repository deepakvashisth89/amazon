I'm another one who lost or (permanently) loaned out too many copies of this treasure, so I was delighted to find it available again.  And equally delighted to find that it is just as funny (and poignant, and true) in 2000  as it was in 1975.  If you are a southerner, if you've lived among them  (like me) or if you've known southern expatriates, you will recognize the  folks in this book.  If you haven't read this 1993 edition, you will also  love the afterword, covering such burning questions as "where did all  these damnyuppies come from?", "do we really need a sequel to  Gone With The Wind?", and "is Bill Clinton a Good Ole Boy?"
