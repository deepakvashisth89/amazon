Recently, in reading Tracy Daugherty's excellent biography of Larry McMurtry, I came across a reference to Mark Jude Poirier, a writer that McMurtry had mentored at one point in time.  Out of curiosity more than anything, I bought Poirier's novel, Goats, and set it aside to read after I finished McMurtry's biography.Wow, am I glad I did!  As a reader, it's a beautiful thing when you stumble across a novel that moves you and resonates with you, one that will stay with you long after you finish it.  That's what happed to me with Goats (2001) and I can guarantee it's what will happen to you.The characters are so well written, teenage Eills and his neurotic mother, Wendy.  And, of course, Javier (or Goat Man).  Poirier's description of the desert in Tuscon is beautiful, too.  This is, in many ways, a coming of age tale for modern times, even though the novel is 20 + years old now.  It's still timely.I loved this novel.  You will, too.Read moreRead less
