I read this book when I was too young to fully understand the complexity of the main character's decision about his future.  When I started to go through the same sorts of choices, I found myself haunted by the book, even though I had finished it years ago.  The most powerful scene for me is the mother's farewell.  Even at the time it made me cry; now it is one of those scenes I use as an example to myself of what good writing really is.  Thank you John M. Ford for a very moving experience!Read moreRead less
