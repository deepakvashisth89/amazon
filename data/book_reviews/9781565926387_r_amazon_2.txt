This book is really hard core.  Very in-depth coverage.  The author apparently has had a lot of experience in his design of AD for a UK University.  A lot of coverage of design strategies, but as with all  O'Reilly books, it seems to step from one level to another very quickly and  is therefore hard to follow at times.
