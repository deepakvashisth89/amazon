I do like this novel. I have read it before, a long time ago and thought I would re read it and write a little review. It's a strange tale of reincarnation and an enduring love between two souls who live and die only to come together in several different lifetimes, not always meeting at appropriate times when their current ages differ. There is a lot of historical content which I found interesting and the characters are well described, living their "present" life in a beautiful setting in Somerset. I thought maybe it could have been condensed into a shorter length and this 500 page novel might put some readers off but for me it held my interest, both times without any page skimming and wondering if the sequel, The Lives She Left Behind, might be worth a look later.
