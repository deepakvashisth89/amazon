Bobbitt asks the question, "When and why is the use of force justified?" He's one of the few commentators I've come across who digs deeper, who goes beyond the normal polemical positions and cynical posturing and really explores why this question has become so difficult of late. Although published in 2002, the core themes seem at least as relevant today. The public demands more frequent intervention, often on humanitarian grounds, but is less constant in its support once an intervention is underway. Obama is criticized not having a strategy for Iraq and Syria. But I haven't noticed much in the way of coherent strategic advice from anyone else.
