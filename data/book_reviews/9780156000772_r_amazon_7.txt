I really enjoyed Ugly Ways in a perverse sort of way.  I had read it previously about a decade ago and wanted to read it again.  But I had forgotten the name of the book and the name of the author.  I found it again the way I found it the first time. Tina McElroy Ansa was one of the authors featured in an anthology called I Know What the Red Clay Looks Like edited by Rebecca Carroll.  I would like more of Ansa's work to be available on kindle.I live in Florida and I loved the idea of gardening at night.  The complete selfishness of the Mother is described in detail and the effects on her 3 daughters and her husband are obvious.  We have all known people almost that self-centered.  They compound their unkindness by skewering their victims if the dare mention their pain. This mother (who was apparently seldom stood up to in her life) is listening to what her family says about her now she is dead.
