I remember watching Pol Martin on his morning cooking show from Montreal in the 70's so decided to see if he had any available cookbooks.  After reading positive review after positive review I thought that it would be worth taking the plunge particularly since I couldn't go wrong with the secondary market prices.  To begin, the illustrations are incredible and everywhere.  No guesswork here about what a finished dish might look like.  And then I started making a couple of the recipes and was simply blown away at how simple and yet how terrific the food was.  My wife and I are very much into cooking and this book has very quickly found it's way to the "use it regularly" section of our extensive cookbook library.  I can't recommend it highly enough.
