It is easy for modern researchers to miss the point and the value of traditional, analog research. Mr Mann writes from the point of view of decades of experience at the Library of Congress and explains is readable prose how traditional tools assist the researcher unlock the treasures of a library. He also reveals how other methods actually miss the mark. If you are interested in libraries, or do research, this book is for you.PS. Ignore the "About the Author" link in Amazon's listing here.
