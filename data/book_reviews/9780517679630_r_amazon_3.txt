Okay,I had a copy of this book in 1979, and wanted a newer copy of that very same data. This book certainly provides that.For me and my needs, it more than serves the job. As a kid that spent his youth in the 70's, these Jane reprints are marvelous and nostalgic. I understand why anyone from today's generations would find these lacking in flash and flourish.What many are failing to truly understand is that these are actually combined reprints of 1940's Jane's guides. Based on those parameters, these volumes are wonderful glimpses into our past. I will admit I would have liked the text and pictures to be scaled up and recontrast for clarity, but these are reproduced as cheaply as possible while maintaining some semblance of usefulness.Each nation and ship class gets a B&W photo, pertinent data and simple overhead and side view in line drawings.Conway guides are generally published with more flourishes and histories, BUT remember that you will PAY for that depth and degree of thoroughness. Jane and Conway are the apex of Naval guide books, and I have many versions of their various volumes. I don't regret investing in any of them. My only advice is that you research what you're buying, because I am utterly satisfied with my Jane's guides. Yet, I was aware of what I was buying beforehand.Take care, RobertRead moreRead less
