Whatever you have heard about the recent Anita Blake novels and whether you think you would like it or not this is where it starts. This is a collection of the first 3 Anita books and they are basically harmless. While they do have murders and zombies etc (that is Anita's job) there isn't as much controversial stuff in these first ones. (Although I love all of the Anita books, new and old!)Hamilton does a wonderful job of creating an entirely new world where the undead are accepted and the unusual is normal. You owe it to yourself to visit Anita's world and I would definitely advise buying this 3 in 1 set because once you start you are not going to want to stop!!Read moreRead less
