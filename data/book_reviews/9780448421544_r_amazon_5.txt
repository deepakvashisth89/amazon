Can't believe it has been 20 years since I actually purchased this book for my then toddler daughters as part of an Easter basket!  I always thinks it is nice to have both candy/chocolate and non-edible gifts like Easter themed books, puzzles, etc in an Easter basket so the kids have something to do after the excitement wears down.  This is a lovely story about Corduroy and his friends getting ready for the arrival of the Easter bunny.  The large colorful pictures and short paragraphs of text on each page makes it ideal for reading as a bedtime story.
