We saw patch Adams speak about 2 months ago at a Marian University Medical School function.He was dressed in his full clown outfit (minus his red nose) and spoke for about 80 minutes - pacing back and forth the whole time. He never sat down once which is fairly impressive as he was nearly 71 at the time.His message is a good one if not entirely a reasonable one. His book is the same. Human nature being what it is prevents most people who go to medical school (or pharmacy, law, dental, etc) from doing what he does. To succeed in these kind of environments you must be VERY driven and competitive - otherwise you won't make it through - probably wouldn't even get in in the first place.  Speaking as a pharmacist of over 30 years I can tell you honestly that I would have never worked as hard as I did for as long as I did to do what Patch is doing. Even though I agree with the majority of what he says about our current medical system it's just not going to change as he would like - at least it won't anytime soon. When you graduate with around ***$200,000*** in debt from most professional programs you are kind of forced, at least for a long time, to earn as much money as reasonably possible just to pay that debt. My daughter just finished her 2nd year in med school and is already $75,000 in debt.But she is one of the lucky ones as many of her classmates were $45 to 50,000 in debt BEFORE STARTING MEDICAL SCHOOL and are now owe north of $150,000 - they still have 2 more years of med school plus residency. They will probably owe $250,000 to 300,000 before they even start their practice.Just to put this into the proper prospective a $250,000 loan at 4% for ***30 YEARS*** would be $1193 a month. In other words a decently nice house payment until you are about 58 or 59. This would be close to 14k a YEAR right off the top. As the VA the starting pay for newly minted MD's is around $110,000 you can see this is a huge hurdle if you want to practice as Patch wants you to.Unless this problem is solved there is quite simply no hope of changing the system much.Much of Patches views and opinions are rooted in 1960's ideology.  In simple terms they are fairly close to what most of us would call communism.I believe what makes America different than most countries is our system where anyone can make it IF THEY TRY VERY HARD AND WORK FOR MANY YEARS. I feel I'm proof of that (and my wife even more than I).  The first thing that kick started my life was the realization that life is harsh and tough - and often not entirely fair.For example, I have worked 28 Thanksgivings since 1981. How many of my readers have worked anything like this UNLESS you are a RN, MD, etc.HOWEVER IT IS ENTIRELY POSSIBLE TO MAKE IF YOU DON'T GIVE UP.I enjoyed this book but it is, IMHO, outside of what most people want for their lives - Interesting read but not very practicable.I would rate this a 7.50 to 8.0 out of 10 - only recommended if you get it at your local library.Read moreRead less
