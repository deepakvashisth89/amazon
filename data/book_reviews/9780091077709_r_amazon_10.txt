Wheatleys book recycles a series of newspaper articles on the occult, which he first published in the 1940's, recycled in 'Guns, Ghosts and Gallants', and which he recycles again in this book, together with Archive material from dubious sources. This book contains a Long list of historical mistakes, dubious theories, with painfully long articles on Magic since Adam & Eve. Wheatley clerarly lacked the ability to cross-check his sources. Does anybody actually believe in the theory of 'The Small People' any longer ? If that tribe had ever existed, they would have left behind artefacts, and archeology would have explained them. Sheer nonsense. The order of the garter is defintely not a Symbol of wizardry, and their members do not belong to a satanic coven, either. Really strange stuff. However, he met some interesting people during his research: Ian Fleming, Churchill, Aleister Crowley, Montague Summers, Henry Dewhirst etc.This book is defintely not scientific.  According to Wheatley himself, he had seemingly occult experiences in World War I. Sounds a like a minor case of war neurosis to me.
