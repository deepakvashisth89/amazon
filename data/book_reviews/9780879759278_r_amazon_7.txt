I LOVE this book.  I laughed! I cried! Then I read it again!  I grew up steeped in the Baltimore Catechism, with scratches on my scalp from bobby pins angrily shoved into my hair to mount the Kleenex seized from the nun's pocket.  I left that swamp of cruelty and pettiness in 1963, and still this book published decades later moved me, released me, assured me.The author's voice is calm and thoughtful, which provides a perfect background for the voices of the women telling their stories, quote by quote.  The stories reveal the uber message of Catholicism over the centuries: Shut Up and Obey!  Offer the teeth your husband knocked out for the glory of Jesus! If you skip the last day of school, you and your family will go to hell forever.  Suffering is good.  Obey your priest, father, husband, male boss. Male! Male! Your Holy Grail.It takes many of us *decades* to realize that the church we experienced is an extremely efficient Bully Machine, and the stories in Meehl's book provide epiphany after epiphany.  This book would make a great gift for people who have been run through the Catholic gauntlet.Read moreRead less
