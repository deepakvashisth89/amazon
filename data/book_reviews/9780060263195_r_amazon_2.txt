It took Ivan Chermayeff's passing this week to bring this book to my attention. It is, quite simply, a gem. Vonnegut takes the time to ponder what our understanding of the Trinity is: one Being in three distinct aspects. For Creator to take on human form is a process of decision (didn't need to), and discovery. The result is imagined experience that does not, fittingly enough, ever take a "God's-Eye" view of the action. There is just enough allusion to the action outside the newborn's sphere to give us context to the "sun," "moon" and "star" perceived by those young eyes. Readers who are disturbed by Vonnegut's use of the pronoun "It" when referring to the baby should take a breath and remember that he is not talking about Jesus, the human, but the Creator's experience of the world through Jesus' eyes.
