An excellent read, like eating candy.  Reminded me of Alfred Harris' The Joseph File.  I wish the title of the book had been simplier, so more people would read it.  Perhaps it could have been a little less slick in spots, but overall the novel is one of the most exciting on-the-run-being-pursued-by-a-murderous-organization book I've ever read.  I love pursuit books especially ones that take place during extreme winter weather.  Talk about exciting:  that escape over ice on a stolen kid's sled is one of a kind!  I didn't particularly like the ambiguous, open-ended ending, but I'm sure architect-turned-truck-driver David Townsend will made the right decision (about Euromedic).
