This book explodes in your face from the first few pages. I remember I read it in two sittings. I just could not put it down! It's kind of like a horrific rock video, but not in a bad way.I felt like I was in this story. The darkness and horror will chill your blood. I've seen and read many vampire movies and books. Every story slightly redefines WHAT the "vampire" is.In this book, the male vampires are 100% evil. They have absolutley no redeeming qualities and are simply monsters in every way. That said, they are frightfully intelligent and ruthless.It's the female vampires, the half-lings, that still have some tiny shred of humanity left. And this is what makes the book interesting.I don't think I fully understood "blood lust" before reading this book. This story will show you a vampire in a way you've never seen them.After reading this I felt emotionally drained. I felt like my world had been darkness for the two days I read. This book will thrill you, it will exhaust you, and it will *scare* you.Read moreRead less
