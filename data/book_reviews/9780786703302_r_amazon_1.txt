I very much like this author, Madsen Pirie, a Hillsdale guy who has written a number of books on logic and argumentation, among other topics. (My favorite of his is 101 Great Philosophers.) This book unites readers of Sherlock Holmes with people who enjoy things like Sudoku. I am a middling fan of both, so this book sits on my night table, for the most part unused, but I have the feeling that some people who used their college education more productively than I might love it.Read moreRead less
