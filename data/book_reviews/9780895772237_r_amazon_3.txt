A great saga with roaring characters lost in a blaze and a maze. Teaming with beauty and honor; terrible and terrifying;  Dickens honors us with his moments of greatness, yet leaves us dead on the doorstep from his unparalleled long windedness. I honestly don’t think I could have gotten through it without the accompanying audio book.  This book is an ultra marathon on par with War and Peace.If you think about it, how long it takes us to read it; how long must have taken Dickens to write it? Then one considers that it was published serially in the media by chapter, and one realizes that it was the only way it could be accomplishedAs far as Dickens goes this is not at the top of the heap, as a Tale of Two Cities is in a league of its own.
