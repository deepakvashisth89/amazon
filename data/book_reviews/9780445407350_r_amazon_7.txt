Hansen wrote many mystery novels featuring the Dave Brandstetter character, a whiskey-drinking insurance investigator who is also gay. Inevitably, Brandstetter ends up solving crimes that the police cannot. "Early Graves" is one of the better books in the series. A few screwed-up characters, some baffling murders, along with Brandstetter's turbulent relationship with another man combine to make for a very engrossing novel. Hansen has a deft touch as a writer, weaving a good story and creating interesting characters.Read moreRead less
