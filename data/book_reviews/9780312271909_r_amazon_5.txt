I loved this book. I couldn't put it down.This is a book about obsession. The two main characters are obsessive personalities and have found an outlet for their obsessive nature by collecting things. Jean collects pens. Stephen collects binoculars. When they meet, their obsessive natures find them drawn to one another.You sense impending disaster from the start and it keeps you enthralled until the end. I simply could not put the book down.Griner's writing is sparse yet rich... a brilliant combination. I can't get enough of this writer's work.If you want to read more of his work, you can read an excerpt from his latest novel in Southeast Review.
