Sam, a crooked news paper reporter, living on the fringes of the criminal underbelly of his town, often overhears the bad things going on, but reports none of it. One day he hears a rich man’s daughter is targeted for kidnapped and most likely killed, so he goes to the family to warn them, but don’t believe him.  So, at great risk to himself, he decides to take the girl, himself, in order to save her life. This is very well written story that feels so vivid. And, I really like the characters.Read moreRead less
