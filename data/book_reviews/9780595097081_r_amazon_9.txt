I opened this book and could not put it down.  The world of Joel and Tom engaged me right from the beginning ... their struggles, their bonding, their triumphs, their supporters and also their detractors.  Donaghe writes with an honesty and an outlook on the human condition that is accessible and encouraging.  It is especially good to read about a parent in a positive light ... struggling to understand yet loving nevertheless.  Donaghe does not shy away from the sadness, the hate, and the violence of life, but he does manage to bring a warm glow into our world.Read moreRead less
