Why this book isn't on Kindle, I'll never know.  It's an intriguing account of the openly gay lifestyle that existed in Hollywood during the 1920s, before the stock market crash and subsequent depression changed American morals for another seven decades.  William Haines was a brash, good looking youngster from Virginia, who became the Number 1 Box-office Star in America, only to see his career evaporate due to changing moral attitudes and the undying enmity of Louis B. Mayer.  When Mayer told him to choose between his career and his partner, Jimmie Shields, Billy Haines walked out of MGM with both his dignity and relationship with Shields intact.  This remarkable biography is the first of Haines that I know of, and will set a standard that is difficult to top--just like one of Billy's wisecracks.Read moreRead less
