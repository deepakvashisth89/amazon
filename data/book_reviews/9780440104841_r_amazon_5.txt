Here's a book that defies classification -- one of the most singular, entertaining novels you'll come across, concerning Mongo the dwarf detective and his hulking brother Garth.  It begins a sequence of four  novels that stands pretty much alone in mystery or SF, incredibly smart,  stylish, weird, with characters you care deeply about undergoing the  ultimate transformation, from man to animal to messiah.
