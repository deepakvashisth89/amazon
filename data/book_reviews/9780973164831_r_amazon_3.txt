Janet Tyson's book is an interpretation of the Fourth Gospel, which she argues is the first, or earliest, of the New Testament writings.Her style is casual, but reveals a high level of understanding and research - she makes connections that seem so 'obvious', but have not been suggested before.  I found the section on Jesus' crucifixion and his escape through the tunnel most intriguing.One of the most controversial elements, at least as far as I am concerned, is the notion that Jesus was married to Martha, but had a child with Mary, to whom Lazarus was betrothed!  Tyson argues that this group formed the original "Holy Family," and that the cult of Mary, and the legend of Joseph taking his family away to safety,etc., are both based on the real-life events surrounding these three (Lazarus, Mary Magdalen, and Jesus' child).This is but one of the many new ideas in this book.It is bound to offend many, I'm certain, but for those of us with a keenness to learn and explore, it offers a new trail, a new adventure.I thoroughly recommend it.Read moreRead less
