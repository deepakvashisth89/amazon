Two decades ago, Granger was hailed by no less than Ed McBain as "America's best spy novelist." A pity he is not better known nowadays. Perhaps such "spy" tales now seem dated, but this and other books in the Granger's "November Man" series are all very entertaining reads. There Are No Spies is also part of "November Man" series, and it's another cracker. With its many double-crossing characters and nefarious espionage activity, it reminds me a bit of those great Ross Thomas novels. Really, I think Granger is that good a writer.Read moreRead less
