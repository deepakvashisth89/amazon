This book meets all my basic requirements for a good book:  well-written, very few glaring proofreading mistakes, characters that are likable and believable, I finished the whole thing, etc. It doesn't however meet any of my requirements for a GREAT book: that I stayed up late reading it, or that I find myself thinking about the story or the characters long after having finished it.  Thus, the 4 stars.One thing about the plot also threatened to derail my enjoyment of the book, and that is that there are a couple of widely divergent plot elements.  On the one hand, we've got a love story unfolding on a sleepy little Cape Cod beach; on the other hand we've got an ex-husband who's being tested by the government for his abilities as a psychic investigator. The author brings it all nicely together in the end; but when the psychic-government-testing angle was first introduced, I was like, "Whhhhaaaaatttt?  How does this relate to the love story I've been reading?"  The two plot lines could use better integration earlier on.Read moreRead less
