I didn't get the outbursts of laughter of some of the other reviewers but I did thoroughly enjoy Michael Frayn's lively writing style and found the book an entertaining mix of satirizing the vanities and follies of human behaviour but done in a kindly way that made you sympathize with the characters.  I can imagine that if you are a journalist there would be things about the descriptions of working for a newspaper that would give extra enjoyment.I think that this book would probably appeal even more to men than women rather like PG Wodehouse.Read moreRead less
