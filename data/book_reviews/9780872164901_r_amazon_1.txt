The story itself, was delightful and enjoyable. However, the copying of physical book to e-book was terrible.There were multiple episodes of words run together, of unfinished sentences, of double words (ie nono), paragraphs unfinished. These mistakes were not in the original book as I had read it. They made it hard to really follow the story line and made the reading minor chore.
