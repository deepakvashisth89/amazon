Revered with Francis Crick for his work guessing the structure of DNA , which yielded a Nobel Prize (THE Nobel Prize as he not so modestly puts it) Watson has dominated a large  section of American science politics and grant funding for half a Century. He bitterly opposed the most outstanding single development which nevertheless lead to a rapid and economic completion of the sequencing of the human (and many other) genomes. Another Watson Autobiography is entitled 'Avoid boring people'. It is not clear a whether or not it is people who are boring or if the title is an admonition not to bore people. Watson has always been fascinated by his life and takes many opportunities to talk about it. I was present at one of these in Paris at the Institut Pasteur where after half an hour most of the audience had left the lecture hall. His belligerent attitude and irascible self confidence put fear in the hearts of many who had to deal with him. His 'racial intelligence correlations' lead to an ending of his directorship at the Cold Spring Harbor Research Center that he had lead for several decades.
