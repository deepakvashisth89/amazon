Well written, subtle, builds to the climax without hitting the reader over the head with obviousness. Some parts are disturbing but they belong in the story.  Mel is a strong female character who doesn’t have the typical young-adult-novel life. Her voice is clear and consistent. You won't get a lot of depth and emotion from her narration, it's mostly surface description, so when an insight breaks across her consciousness it's devastating.  The tone is serious but there is humor in a lot of the wry observations Mel makes.  You wouldn't want to have her life but you do root for her.Read moreRead less
