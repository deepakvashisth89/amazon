I live in Albuquerque New Mexico, have been to Glorieta many times, and am something of a history buff. So I really enjoyed the descriptions of the people and locations which were pretty accurate. Even learned what "glorieta" means. The story is based on real battles and events concerning the ill-fated Confederate effort to control the Rio Grande valley, reach the Colorado gold fields, and perhaps reach as far west as San Francisco during the War Between the States, of which many people are unaware. While some of the characters in the story are real, such as the Generals, most are fictional or based on composites of people who were there.
