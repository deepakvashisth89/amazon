This is a wonderful prayerbook.  As a therapist, I keep it by my chair.  I often review a particular prayer right before a client arrives so that I can feel centered and open to the next person who enters my room.  The  prayers are written clearly and beautifully.  My clients who are  spiritually inclined have enjoyed hearing some of the prayers that reflect  their own issues and situations, such as being angry with God, or having  been sexually molested.
