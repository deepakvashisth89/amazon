It's difficult to come by historical novels which are well-written, have a decent plot and are historically accurate as well. Hannah March's series starring Robert Fairfax as amateur detective certainly falls into this category and it's a shame there are only five books.This first case has been called slow but I disagree. It does not start with the crime right away but takes its own sweet time to introduce the main characters. I actually enjoyed getting to know the victim as well since they are all too often merely voiceless figures and very few authors bother to develop their character at all.Fairfax isn't much of a conventional hero. In fact, I did not like him very much in this first book. This did not reduce my enjoyment of the book, however.I thought the story was clever, reasonably believable and the characters' motivations and actions were fleshed out as well. The historical setting was not just a cheap trick either (as it so often is in modern historical writing), but there was a richness of detail. I was new to the Georgian era so there were quite a few things "to learn".The writing may not have been as rich in language as in those books published under the name of Jude Morgan, which I as non-native speaker usually fix on the number of times I need to refer to the dictionary. I enjoyed the various accents and distinctive ways characters communicated.All in all I strongly recommend this series.Read moreRead less
