This is a beautiful poem tale of a fisherman who lives in a lighthouse and loves the sea. He marries a beautiful girl.  It's the story about their faith and love for each other and how they are spiritually connected.  The whole story is written in verse, and the entire pages (top to bottom and side-to-side) are filled with the paintings of the adventure of the sea around the rocky landscape.  It's the story of faith and undying love and courage.  The story is beautiful, but what really makes this book so special is the illustrations--it's really an art book to sit on your coffee table.The only thing I didn't think was quite right with the story is that the mother who is worried about her husband decides to get in a row boat to go find him when there is a bad storm and not only that, but she takes her little baby with her in the boat!!  Later, the story says that the wife realized that she had made bad judgment, but I can't imagine any mother ever doing such a thing.  Even so, the illustrations are what really make this book so beautiful, and it's still a good story about true love and courage and faith.Read moreRead less
