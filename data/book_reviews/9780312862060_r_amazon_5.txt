I found the sexual/ social issues in this book very interesting.  Nowadays there is very little SF I can handle- it all seems to be cookie-cutter adventure series.  Shadow Man sets itself apart by looking at something I haven't seen before, namely the social issues facing humans who have been split into five sexes rather than two.  My interest was also held by the society described in the book, which is an unusual mix of technology and a more primitive lifestyle.I admit that the plot wasn't the most exciting- it was basically a vehicle for the book's social issues.  However, I found the issues discussed in the book more than enough to keep me reading to the end.I have since read two more by the same author, Trouble And Her Friends and Night Sky Mine, which are more traditional cyberpunk adventures.  While they're OK (and are unusual in that their heroines and heroes are mostly gay), they don't center around the same kind of ideas that made me think while reading Shadow Man.  It's definitely the most interesting of the three.Read moreRead less
