Walker's book is no easy read. While he does a great job at navigating the reader through alle the facets and aspects of the involved sciences and philosophies, you will have from time to time to re-read and reconsider paragraphs and pages to understand, what Walker is telling you.However, it is well worth your time. What Walker achieves is a model of the mind and the universe that is 100% based in modern hard core quantum physics. He need not involve mystical entities, does not invent new factors and does not pull any new particle or force out of his hat. He just takes what is already in modern physics, takes a very close look at it and draws the inevitable logical conclusions.Many physicists - especially the string theory addicts - could learn a great deal from the way Walker thinks and analyzes. Alas - string theoreticians could find themselves soon out of job.This book is not for esoteric readers. If your world needs some supernatural forces and some hidden deities, you will be disappointet. Walker needs no fairies to explain the world.This book is not for the religiously dogmatic reader either. There is no need for the personal God of Abraham, Mohammed and all the like. You will find, that the God of Walker is quite different, and you might get shaken in your oh so firm beliefs of your classic religious community.For you and me non-physicists, non-esoterics, and non-fundamentalists, i.e. for you and me open minded people trying to understand our world and our role in it, the book is just simply enlightening.My recommendation.
