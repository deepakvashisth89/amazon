This is a well written account that may appeal most to folks with an interest in astronomy and/or map making, but perhaps not to general readers like the "one star" reviewer.Most folks are probably unaware that the latitude [distance from the pole] of any specific location on the surface of the earth is not fixed, but varies over time by a matter of a few feet.  Another way of saying this: the physical location of the earth's axis of rotation shifts through time (in a limited range), for various arcane reasons.  When this was first discovered, the phenomena was just barely at the limits of detection with the best instruments that were available.I found this a fascinating account of how science is actually done, involving various personalities, the correspondence between theory and observation, the limits of instrumentation, not to mention the history of government funding of basic science...Read moreRead less
