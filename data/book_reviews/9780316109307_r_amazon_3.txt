Essentially a transcript of what Dr. Jacob Bronowski says to the audience through the lens of the camera (and screen of the television), this book is an essential companion to his extraordinary and landmark 13-part series of the same name (available in a stunning DVD transfer).From Man's origins (as then known) through nomadic migrations, from the ruins of ancient civilizations to the late twentieth century, from the very beginnings of science and technology through the Industrial Revolution and the splitting - then fusing - of the atom, "The Ascent of Man" takes one to the highest peaks humanity have reached, as well as plumbing the lowest depths - all presented with great drama, wit, flair, dignity and humanity, the portrayal of which will be remembered the rest of one's life.Now nearly forty years old (and even though some material may be dated), this book - and the series which produced it - are an achievement of the highest order, which have yet to be equaled, let alone surpassed.Highest recommendation.Read moreRead less
