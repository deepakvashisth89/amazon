I haven't read this book in almost forty years and yet I remember almost every detail.  I searched for it on a whim and was so tickled to see all of the reviews by people who were as touched by this book as I was.  As someone else said, I'm almost afraid to go back and read it again for fear of finding that it's full of stereotypes.
