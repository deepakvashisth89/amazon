This marks my first criticism of an Alistair MacLean work. The action is set in California, but Sgt. Ryder comes off sounding more like a Scotland Yard detective, than a hardboiled American cop. He refers to the Battles of Trafalgar and Jutland and the retreat from Mons. Really?!?! Also , there are enough sloppy mistakes to make a Californian (or American, for that matter) cringe; i.e. Western standard time, instead of Pacific standard time, and Ma Bell operated the phones back then, not the post office. MacLean also spends too much time lecturing on the history of earthquakes.
