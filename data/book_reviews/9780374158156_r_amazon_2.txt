If you enjoy family sagas, you will love this one by Oscar Hijuelos. How I've managed to miss him up to now I don't know; what I do know is that I will be back to him for more. This saga is enchanting from start to finish. Oscar Hijuelos has written a can't-put-it-down tale that kept me turning the pages until I had finished it, which is remarkable in that the book contains over 500 pages. If you love a really well-told tale, grab  a copy of this book and hold onto your hat. If you're squeamish about explicit descriptions of sex, then this may not be for you.
