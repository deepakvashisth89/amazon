This is the second in the Harry Houdini series.  This time Dash and Harry are once again out of work and find opportunities with a travelling magic show that features an act of levitation involving a woman who ultimately falls to her death.  But was the cause of death multiple trauma?  In fact the coroner finds that death was due to drowning.  How is that possible?  Before long Dash and Harry are out to prove that the man who last saw this lady alive did not do the crime.  It involves a lot of delving into the history of their employer, Mr. Kellar, the "Dean of American Magicians."  Also who was this woman who died and what was her connection to other performers in this company?  There are a lot of people that could be suspect, including another magician in town whose claim to fame is doing the exact disappearing woman act.  Could he be involved?  After all he is a rival, and this tragedy could help to attract an audience to his performance.
