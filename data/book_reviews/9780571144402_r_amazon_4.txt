'Living in Truth' provides an excellent introduction into Havel's writings. If your interest finds you wanting to get a deeper understanding of both Havel as an individual and as a thinker, I can highly recommend John Keane's excellent biography, 'Vaclav Havel, A Political Tragedy in Six Acts' and the writings of fellow Czech dissident Jan Patocka to whom Havel dedicated his essay 'Power of the Powerless'. Patocka's works 'Heretical Essays in the Philosophy of History' and 'Body, Community, Language, World' are particularly good for gaining an understanding of the philosophy that Havel drew upon so extensively.Read moreRead less
