I am vegetarian, my husband isn't. My twins are therefore still sometimes exposed to daddy wanting them to taste meat. Nothing wrong with that, honestly, but I want them to understand why people are vegetarian and what that implies. Herb is a wonderful example of peace generated by food choices. Instead of exhalting vegetarianism in itself, it promotes tolerance and life with people who are different. the book ends with Herb and Meatloaf finding a way to live together keeping their eating habits (well, ok dragons give up people eating...).
