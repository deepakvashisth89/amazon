This is book two of a five-book series by Mark Berent on the U. S. air war in Viet Nam.  I have finished books 1, 2 & 3 and am reading 4 now.  So far, I have found them to be very well-written, interesting and often exciting.  As a fighter pilot himself, Berent vividly describes aspects of aerial combat that are exciting and suspenseful.
