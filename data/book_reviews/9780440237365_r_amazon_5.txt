This book gripped me from the very first page and as I read on, it actually began to scare me, which is pretty hard to do these days.  I almost dreaded turning each page, in fear of what might happen next.  But as the pages waned and I saw that I was close to the end I started to become perplexed.  I prepared myself for a slam-bang ending, only to be severely disappointed by the climax and ending.  What a letdown!  I couldn't believe the author had copped out this way.
