This is one of the books written in the style of Victorian/Edwardian erotica whose provenance could be much more recent.  However, it is above average in this genre.  Its subject matter concerns a group of young people, mostly female, some sisters, and it describes their sexual awakening, which is both by self-discovery and with the help of related adults.
