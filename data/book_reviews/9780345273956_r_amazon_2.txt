This book manages to blend the opulent life of the Kennedy family with the grim realities of war.  Joseph Jr lived a charmed life, until he was chosen for a secret mission that caused him to lose his life.  My father flew recognizance missions out of England, but his life, in the then Army Air Corp, was quite different than the privileged life of Mr Kennedy, but my Dad came home and tragically Mr Kennedy did not.  In retrospect, its hard to believe that the Navy and the AAC could possibly have thought that the idea of planes, overloaded with explosives, serving as quasi drones could have ever succeeded with the technology then available.
