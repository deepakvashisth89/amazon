Mad Hatters Holiday is the fourth investigation for Sergeant Cribb and his trusty sidekick Constable Thackery.This story was first published in 1973 but this edition is from 2009 and is issued by Soho Press Inc.The story is set in the summer of  1882 in Brighton Sussex.We are introduced to Albert Moscrop who spends his holiday peering at beachgoers through a telescope. He spies the Prothero family and becomes infatuated with Mrs Prothero and begins to insert himself into their world.Sergeant Cribb and Constable Thackery do not appear until page 103 when we find them travelling by second -class carriage of the Brighton Express to investigate. Cribb tells Thackery " We ain't going to Brighton for a paddle you know".At the aquarium wedged down between the rock and the glass inside the crocodile tank was a human hand severed above the wrist and resting on its stump, palm facing outwards.As Cribb and Thackery persue the killer in Victorian Brighton madness is in the air.Read moreRead less
