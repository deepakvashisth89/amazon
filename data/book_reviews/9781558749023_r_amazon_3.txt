I bought this book for my best friend's (since we were 5 years old, we are 30 now)little brother who is 15 years old.In June 2008 we had a horrific thing happen to our family, our beautiful sister, daughter, cousin, freind, and aunt was hit and killed by a drunk driver on a motorcycle who was 3 times over the legal limit and racing another motorcyle (who was not injured in the accident),Our Sweet ANGEL was only 17 when she was taken from us.There is a specific story in the book that talks about "water bugs" and "dragonflies" that we can all read and instantly think of our beautiful angel, who had been taken from us in such a tragic way.I recommend this book to anyone who has suffered the pain of a loved one or freind, even if the loss was not tragic, the loss itself is the tragady and this book will help somewhat how to cope with that loss.There is nothing we wouldn't give, do, sell, buy, whatever to have her back. The reality sadley is that she is not coming back, she is gone....I know that when you think of a loved one your eyes fill with tears, but I also know that when you think about the one you lost, it is ok to smile and laugh when remembering times with them, if you don't it makes it seem as if they caused sadness, or sorrow but it is the complete opposite in fact, all they brought was unconditional love, kindness, warmth, joy, happiness, and that is just a few of many things.Please don't get me wrong it is good to cry when remembering your loved one but you should also smile every now and then when you think of them and the happiness they had brought to your life as well as each persons life they touched while they were still here.
