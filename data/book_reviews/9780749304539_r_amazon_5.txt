How lovely to see these novels available again. I read them all when they were first published back in the 90s and they have lost none of their old charm on re-reading. Willow King leads a lifestyle which many of us wish we could emulate - she can escape from real life whenever she wants, into a luxurious and expensive lifestyle where her every whim is catered for and she can transform herself into her glamorous alter ego. But she is no two dimensional heroine - she has moments of self-doubt, makes mistakes and changes her mind. The mysteries are a gentle, easy read, without protracted descriptions of police procedural or gory forensic examination. Willow King's adventures are certainly escapist; but they are a pleasurable escape to make again and again.Read moreRead less
