The Acorn People is a wonderful book written with love and insight. Although I have never worked at a summer camp for disabled children, I can identify with the children portrayed and their experiences because I have worked with disabled adults for thirty years and sometimes they told me their stories when they returned from camp. The book is a surprise and a treat. Thank you, Ron Jones, for sharing it and for being the caring person you are and a great storyteller to boot.Read moreRead less
