I LOVED this book.  First, it's gorgeously written.  Secondly, he manages to capture something that has rarely been captured in main stream fiction.  Mann has conjured a palpable sensation of what it's like to be trapped in a developmental transition which is disorienting and is resisted chaotically before something consolidates and one finds a new way to respond to ones experiences.  I was so impressed with his capacity to create a first person protagonist voice that is both un-sympathetic as well as completely relate-able.  He hasn't shied away from complexity as well as over-simplified stereotypes.  It's iconic of a trapped generation confused about its freedoms.Read moreRead less
