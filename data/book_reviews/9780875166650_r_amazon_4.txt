If you are interested in metaphysics or alternative versions of the Christ story, this is a very interesting book.  It traces the life of a young Jesus as he goes through training in the mystery schools of the Essenes, the Druids, the Hindus, the Persians, and finally the Egyptians. He then enters his public life, where he is soon crucified and passes his final test.  It does bog down a little bit as we read about one training after another. But there is a lot of great information contained in here (which I believe comes from the Rosicrucian tradition).One of my favorite tidbits is that the Jews did not invent monotheism.
