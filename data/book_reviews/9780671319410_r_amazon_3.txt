I fell in love with this servies years ago. I would love it to be a movie series....note, series. The three books,taken together smashed into one movie, would dilute what is so good about this series.  When I first discovered the books it was my first foray into military styled science fiction and I loved the combination of humor, action, detailed information and characters.  Just recently I puchased the revised edition on my Kindle as, sadly, my paperback book literally had fallen apart from too many reads. Some of John Ringo's books read like he knocked them out over a weekend, but this series has so many back stories and plots going on, divided nicely so as not to confuse, it speaks to careful planning and if not that...a lot of thought.  In my mind, the trilogy Legacy of the Aldenata, of which this book is the first,  is his best.  Don't get me wrong, I love everything he writes due to the fast pacing and his basic assumption that his main characters are all smarter than the powers that be, but this is the series I always come back to every year or so.  I cannot comment on the accuracy of the military tactics or other military stuff he includes having never been anywhere near the military, but if you want a fast paced, gripping read...this series is for you.
