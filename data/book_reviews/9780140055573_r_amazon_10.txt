I am a fervent admirer of Mr Lovesey's 'Peter Diamond' books but was disappointed with 'Wobble to Death'.  The supposed main character Sgt Cribb was not defined as well as Diamond is and came across as rather 2-dimensional, in fact his assistant PC Thackeray was better.
