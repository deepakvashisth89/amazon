Eileen Connolly is traditional yet uniquely trailblazing. Yes, she reinforces the basic concepts/principles then goes beyond. She was one of the first authors to tune me in to the next level of reading. At first read of her interpretations you may assume it is simplistic but her choice of words(and rhymes) are a tapestry of complex possibilities in the minor arcana formatting of Key, Memory, and the Guidelines. She continues with the meditations in the major arcana. There are times for any reader to refresh their images and Connolly is a great well of cool esoteric water.Read moreRead less
