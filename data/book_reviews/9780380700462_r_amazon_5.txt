This is a surprisingly complex read, that starts off slowly and builds into two distinct but humorous tales. The protagonist details his romance with his Aunt Julia, along with the workings of the radio station where he works. Interspersed with his romance are the tales of the radio serials that were popular in the 1950's, with comical effect.
