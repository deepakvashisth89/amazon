Crenshaw, one of the great musicians of the last 30 years, is a man of many talents.  Songwriter, guitarist, singer-- and author.  Clearly a lover of rock'n'roll movies, both the good ones and the bad ones, he's assembled a group of equally dedicated friends and put together this fairly comprehensive paean to the form.I've had it for 15 years or so and I end up checking it all the time-- even if you haven't seen the films, it makes for amusing and informative perusing.
