I had never heard of this case before, and found it most interesting. One immediately feels a sense of sorrow and outrage for the wasted life of Jerry Lee Harris, and immense sympathy for his widow. This was a murder that never should have happened, due solely to the jealousy of an incompetent man for the success of one who had shown him nothing but kindness for many years. It often happens in life that trying to help someone can backfire and lead to resentment rather than gratitude, but in this case, the resentment grew to hatred and then to murder. Ann Rule does a masterful job of tracing the many business transactions and complicated financial dealings of Harris that led up to the murder. And her empathy for Susan Harris is palpable.
