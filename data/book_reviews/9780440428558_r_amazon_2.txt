I read this book in 4th grade (a long time ago) and adored it! (I read Fourth Grade Celebrity before it, and was happy to find that this was a series!) Recently I dug this book up and reread it and still feel the same!  Tracy dreads a boring summer in her little one-horse town. Lonely  and insecure, she tries to win friends by acting like a pro at  everything... pretty soon she is in a big jam...  A great story, and a  must read. Way to go, Patricia!
