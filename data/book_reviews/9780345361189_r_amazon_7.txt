If you enjoy his writing, adventure, and a tale of improbable--but enjoyable--intrigue and fortune, then you will probably enjoy this book. However, the Kindle version--and perhaps every digital version--is horribly corrupt. Most of the typos are easy to decode, but sometimes you will really need to reach for your ENIGMA device. The defects are truly abysmal, and on virtually every page (please note that I didn't say "literally"). I never slam a book, or the author, because of technical glitches. A bad review tends to prejudice a potential consumer, and if the negative rating is because of technical issues which may not be a problem for other readers, then the review becomes a disservice to everyone.
