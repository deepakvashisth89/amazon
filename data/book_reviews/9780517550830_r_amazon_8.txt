It's hard to believe that I hadn't already reviewed this wonderful book...I read it to my own children over 30 years ago. Now I'm reading it to my grandchildren - and they love it just as much!I got a copy of the board book because I know my new granddaughter will enjoy turning (and perhaps gumming) the pages. What is it about this story that makes it relevant over three decades after it was written? The character, sweet goal-oriented Angelina...and the setting...a happy family with loving supportive parents...and the theme...how will Angelina find a way to dance her way to happiness. The author succeeds in touching our hearts because she writes from her own.As a kindergarten teacher and mom, I appreciate the thoughtful text, beautiful illustrations and timely timeless message.
