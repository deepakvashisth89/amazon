More of Pargeter's WWII series, not as well known as her work as Ellis Peters, but beautifully, painfully written. I especially liked "She Goes To War" as it chronicles personal experience as a woman enrolled in the UK army's special support services. A view rarely recorded, and one with explanations and explorations of how non-combat military operatives lived during the war. The English "well, it's here, we'll live through it, and let's get it over with" outlook is more deeply discussed with a real life story here.Read moreRead less
