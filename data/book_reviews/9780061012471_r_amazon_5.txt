Three brothers -  Jed, 8, Matt, 6 and Andrew, 4 - disappeared. Police investigation report considered they drowned. Fifteen years later, Alex Collier, a TV anchorman, starts his own investigation. The author knows how  to immerse us deep in Collier's investigation. And very slowly, we go  through every small step he takes. Each page you turn could lead you to a  surprise.
