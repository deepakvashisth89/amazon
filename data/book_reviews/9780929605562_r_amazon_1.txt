This is the second to the last of the Oz books written by Ruth Plumly Thompson and it is the one most in keeping with the tenor of those written by Oz's creator, L. Frank Baum. It is a nice little adventure story.As with most of Thompson's better works, it deals primarily with characters who are her own creation. The stars of this one are Kabumpo the elephant, and Randy, the King of Regalia, who have teamed up before along with the Red Jinn of Ev. A new character is Planetty, Princess of Anuther Planet.Randy is now king of his little domain but he finds life frightfully dull until his old friend Kabumpo comes by for a visit. They decide to go visit the Red Jinn and have some adventures along the way. While doing so, the encounter the standard assortment of odd little kingdoms and beings and happen upon a beautiful princess made of metal. She has made her way accidentally to the fairy realm and is having a grand old time. She is quite beautiful and Randy is smitten with her. As she joins the group, they come to Ev and learn that the Jinn has been deposed. The friends must set him upon his throne again and find a way for him to cure the princess's metallic health problems.It's a nice little fairy tale that will read best for those who have already gone through Thompson's earlier works.Read moreRead less
