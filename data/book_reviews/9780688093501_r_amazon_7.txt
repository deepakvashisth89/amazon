A good book about being afraid at night, something that most kids can relate to. As always, Grandpa has a story for Mary Ann and Louie. He tells how he was once afraid of all the frightening things he heard at night, but  the kids identify the sounds as ordinary (not scary) things. This was the  first book I read from the series of books about Mary Ann and Louie and  Grandpa. It is also my favorite, and a favorite of my 4 year old son.Read moreRead less
