There is at least one glaring error here. The introduction to my Pocket Books edition (page xxxii) makes the following statement.  "George's [IV] only child died at birth, so the crown passed to his brother who reigned as William IV from 1830 to 1837."Actually, the only child of of George IV and his estranged wife Caroline of Brunswick, Princess Charlotte of Wales, died at the age of 21. Born January 7, 1796, she married Leopold of Saxe-Coburg-Saalfield who later reigned as Leopold I of Belgium. She died November 6, 1817 hours after giving birth to a stillborn son. That is how the crown eventually passed to her uncle, William IV. Her death, after child birth rather than at birth, prompted the younger sons of George III to marry and secure the succession.
