Excellent, enjoyable noir mystery. Screwball comedy at its finest. Totally fun. Craig Rice's colorful shyster Malone is the central character here but nightclub owner and detective Jake and his Lucille Ball- like wife Helene provide much of the fun here in a story about a woman who gets a last-minute reprieve from the electric chair and pretends to be her own ghost to scare confessions out of those who framed her.Read moreRead less
