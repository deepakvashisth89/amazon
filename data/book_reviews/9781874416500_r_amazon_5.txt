I ordered this book, despite the word "INSTANT" in its title  (how "Instant" can SQL get?) only because it was Celko's latest  book. This book is  below Celko's usual caliber (I consider Joe Celko as  one of the leading authorities on SQL and especially writing about SQL).  It is intended for the begining/not-so-begining SQL programmer. The  intermediate programmer would be happier and more satisfied  with Celko's:  SQL for Smarties. It is here where you can find the Joe Celko we used to  know and admire who wrote for DBMS magazine!
