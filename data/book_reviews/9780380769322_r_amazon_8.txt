I love the Jane Jeffry books so much that I decided to reread them since there will be no more written. The author is a master at dialogue and humour. With each book you see Jane grow as a person. I love the speech she gives to Mel about how basically housewives do a lot and can be intelligent people too. As a stay at home mom I get talked down to a lot by people, mostly other women, so I understand how she feels.I enjoyed being introduced to her wonderful mother and witnessing her growing relationship with Mel. I'm not a big fan of the Priscilla storyline but that's because I typically don't like books about people writing books. I think the author does a better job than most on giving clever mystery clues. I still enjoyed the story, it was a delightful quick read.Read moreRead less
