I’ve enjoyed this book and found it extraordinarily useful for more than a decade. It’s always had a place on my shelf and this copy has given it new life. This version does contain the updated forward by Jean Houston, Ph.D., and meditations in the back for further study, but for those who are willing to absorb its wisdom without “training wheels”, feel absolutely free to enjoy Ernest Holmes’ wisdom straight from the hose, so to speak, as it contains all the original text. The fact that this book is nobody’s required reading goes a long way toward understanding why so few succeed in their own right. Granted, there are more utilitarian copies of this book, and I recommend buying one so you can really break the binding and read it comfortably, but both as a source of wisdom, and a keepsake, this copy of The Science of Mind is golden — literally and figuratively.Read moreRead less
