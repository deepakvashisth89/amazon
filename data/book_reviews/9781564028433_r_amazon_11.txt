This story concerns the continuing misadventures of the rather unlucky Captain Abdul the Skinhead, and his band of pirates. Like the previous tale 'Jolly Roger'Jolly Roger and the Pirates of Captain Abdul, he has got himself tangled up with another fiesty child - this time a girl who goes by the name Maisy Pickles. Will he neven learn?The story centres around Maisy's experiences attending Abdul's School for Pirates, which she finds out is nothing more than a ruse to kidnap the pupils and extort ransom money from the parents; cheeky pirates!This tale doesn't work as well as 'Jolly Roger' - although it's still hugely enjoyable - partly because the story is told in diary form, which detracts from the immediacy of the story slightly, and partly because it's a lot shorter; this tale can be rattled through in 5-10 minutes (convenient for some, but it means that the children will get bored of it quicker, although it is still fun to read aloud). My six-year old enjoyed it.Like most of Colin McNaughton's books, this has a happy ending, with Maisy becoming captain of the Golden Behind and only robbing from other pirates. A nice story for both boys and girls - recommended.Read moreRead less
