This has all the elements of good Science Fiction - a good story, likeable and well-drawn characters, and something to make you reflect. The story is narrated from the different perspectives of the characters.  Most intriguing are the ones from Mary, an android, who has gone 'rogue' because someone was trying to kill her.  The 'action' centres around who is trying to kill her and why, as well as the revolt by a number of androids.  The parts I enjoyed most were the scenes showing Mary's relationship with Zach who teaches her slang, sex and love. This may sound cheesy but I think it's done very well. An enjoyable read.
