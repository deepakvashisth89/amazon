The third book in the series definitely raises the scare factor. The stories are darker and a bit more intense than the first two, which makes it better suited for slightly older kids or anyone who wants more of a chill.The illustrations are as haunting as ever, adding to the atmosphere and making some of the tales stick with you long after reading. While it’s a little scarier than the earlier books, it still carries the same charm and campfire-story feel that makes the trilogy such a classic.A fitting conclusion to the series, and a must if you already have the first two.Read moreRead less
