I like science, SyFy, ETs, This book was captivating as it provides in my opinion, a fresh perspective on the different levels of existence, levels that are either observable or not observable by our ordinary human senses. There is more to space exploration than launching probes and landing on other planets via conventional propellants. And there are the "others", and their ships apparently can change -in flight- physical phase, flying through water and solid rock. Who wishes to play scientific catch-up? And where? What are the social implication? Did Mars' atmosphere oscillate into evaporation?
