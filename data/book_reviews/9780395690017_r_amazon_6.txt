I originally purchased this book after reading Richard Wrangham's "Catching Fire: How Cooking Made Us Human" and was so impressed with the overall experience that i had to explore "Demonic Males." As a person interested in human evolution and how things could be possibly led to where we are now, this book immediately sparked my interest in two ways, our human evolution and its possible explanation that makes us so prone to violence. I enjoyed the authors stories of bringing in the different kinds of great apes and also other species of animals and how their culture and lifestyles effect them. How violence is expressed and tolerated amongst these different great apes. At first, this book may seem to be all doom and gloom but the book has a buildup of a possible future where we can break through the chains of violence and live peacefully amongst ourselves and others like different species of animals do. Overall, this was a fantastic read and i would encourage forward thinkers to absorb some of this material.
