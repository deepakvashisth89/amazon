I have an original Spotmatic, which I had overhauled a couple of years ago, an essential step if you are buying an old camera and don't know its servicing history. This book not only goes into far more detail than the official instruction booklet does, but provides a lot of information that would be particularly useful to those who are new to shooting with film. The only outdated advice is to avoid 400 ASA film (or, as we would now say, ISO 400) for general use.
