I've read completely through this book almost two times now, and each time I go back to a section its significance is easier to grasp. Through my whole life I had always been existential but had been alienated from religion and spirituality due to highly negative experiences with Christianity early in my childhood. I have integrated the Eightfold Path into my everyday cognition and can truly attest to its effortless dissolution of my own suffering.  I behold the world in a completely different lens and for the first time in my life I finally have purpose.
