This book was formative in my understanding of the vast differences in native American cultures. Not all Indians wore feather bonnets or carried tomahawks! The art is beautiful, and the stories of the three children are just simple day-in-the-life tales that illustrate some universal qualities of childhood as well as pointing out the different ways of living in different climates. This book lead directly to my wanting to find out more about native people in my area, both historically and currently, and to a general interest in American history.
