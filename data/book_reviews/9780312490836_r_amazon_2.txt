My 18 month old loved this book the first time he saw it.  We are pleased at the number and types of animals inside!  There are farm animals, zoo animals, pets, etc. - many of which he recognizes already, but there are plenty for him to learn as he gets older, too.  The pictures are beautiful and clear and give us a lot to look at and talk about.
