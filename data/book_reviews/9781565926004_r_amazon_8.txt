David Pogue has long been a favorite source of mine for Mac books, and when I was planning to buy a PDA, his was the first and the best of the PalmPilot books I consulted.  Not only is Pogue a strong writer, but also O'Reilly is a respected computer books publisher.About the only downside to Pogue's book is its age -- it is definitely due for an overhaul, as it barely touches on newer Palm, Inc. handhelds and Palm OS clones.  As a Handspring owner, I was a little disappointed to not have much information pertaining directly to my handheld.Nevertheless, that's a minor qualm, as I saved myself days of quibbling with my new device, and got right to working thanks to Pogue's book.  Not only that, but the CD that comes with the book made it absolutely worth the cost.  I definitely suggest this book.Read moreRead less
