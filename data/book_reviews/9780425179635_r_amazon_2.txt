Also assigned is FBI psychic Kim Desinor.Jessica and Kim visit local shops where poetry readings are read from poems either etched or penned on the backs of the young participants.  From there they are led to teachers and writers of poetry, meeting many sketchy characters in their search to uncover the killer.
