Oh the power of the Internet! I was researching a new piece of software and was reading about the company's "flagship product" and an advertisement on the side showed cruises for "Tall Ships". I started to think about my time as a bartender on the Amazing Grace - Windjammers supply ship for their fleet of tall ships. Next thing I know I am feverishly searching Google to see if someone had maybe posted a picture of me with them at the time of their vacation. But then I came across a blog post that read "Guyan March, R.I.P." - and my heart sank. I then found myself searching for anything I could find about Capt. March.  Wikipedia directed me to this book that was written about what happened to the S/V Fantome and their captain and crew.It was 1991-1992 and I was the bartender on the Amazing Grace where Guyan was my Captain. A nicer man you will not find. I was 21 and I believe he was about 24 or so. I remember he had to leave for a month or two because his dad was ill. He let me borrow his SCUBA diving equipment so that I could have my first ever SCUBA dive experience. I went with the chief engineer of the ship - Richard Little from Texas. It was a year I will never forget and Guyan is a man that I will never forget.When I found this book about him, I bought it immediately.
