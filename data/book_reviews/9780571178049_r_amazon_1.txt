I've owned this book for over 20 years and it's my favourite out of my many vegan cookery books. It's packed with recipes which include how to make your own vegan milk, cream and cheese etc. These guys are very creative and knowledgeable. Another reviewer was unfair to criticise the use of brown rice and flour in the recipes, you don't have to strictly adhere to the ingredients in any book and these recipes work equally well with white flour etc. When the book was first published, vegan food had the emphasis on the most healthy ingredients but you can obviously adapt the recipes to your own taste.
