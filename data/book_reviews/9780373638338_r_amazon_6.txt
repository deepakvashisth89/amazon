Balam takes Kane on a dream trip throughout his previous lives, from Egyptian slave, to King Arthur's right-hand-man.  This book's nemesis is an ex-magistrate archivist (god, this sounds hokey) who discovers lost oil reserves, brings a mat-trans unit online, starts a little empire down Mexico-way , and needs the Holy Grail as the third and final component to finish the three-part "mana-machine."  As plots go, this was pabulum.  Kane must stop him, as he has throughout all of time.If we ignore the Holy Grail aspect of the book, the rest was a fun read.
