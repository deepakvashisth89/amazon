I purchased the book to read during vacation, thinking it was a so-so novel; I had never read anything of S. Barry before; and, it was at a very affordable offer from Amazon.  After a slow start - since I was busy getting used to my new vacation environment - I could not put my Kindle down, and read almost for a whole night!  S. Barry has such a nice way describing life in that era in Ireland and also about the way Anne and her cousin grow older.  I am older than the women in the book, but when growing old I never thought about the way it comes.  We all deal with it, but Anne, being a person "with a hump" and never married, always taking care of others, has worries about not having a roof over her head.  All simple facts and hardships of life are described thoughtfully and tenderly by a masterful author.Read moreRead less
