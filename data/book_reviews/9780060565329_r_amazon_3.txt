Dianne was just one of the extraordinary Mitford sisters. A gifted,intelligent  and very beautiful woman her life was one of terrible decisions. She left her first marriage to a Guiness heir for Sir Oswald Mosley, the leader of the British Fascist party. This marriage and her friendship with Hitler led to 3 years in jail during WW II. She remained loyal to Mosley and defended Hitler even after the war which led her to semi-exile in Paris. The book is well written and well researched. A compelling read.Read moreRead less
