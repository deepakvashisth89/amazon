The late Desmond Seward was an absolute master in what he did: short, engaging overview narratives/biographies, introducing the casual reader to a subject.The first history book I ever read was Seward's The Hundred Years War and since then, Henry V, Caravaggio, The Wars of the Roses and Eleanor of Aquitaine - all have been excellent, short books.This is another great read in that vein.I suppose it's basically several pamphlets put together in one book, each chapter deals with a different crusading front: Outremer, Iberia, Baltic, Mediterranean - and the different orders, such as the Templars, Knights of St John, Teutonic Order and some of the lesser known, less successful ones too.The book is pretty much chronological, starting with the formation of the Templars and ending with how the Order or St John is still going in several ceremonial forms.For anyone with an interest in the crusading orders, I would absolutely recommend reading this book first and foremost as an introduction. And I'd say the same for any of Seward's books with regards to other topics.Read moreRead less
