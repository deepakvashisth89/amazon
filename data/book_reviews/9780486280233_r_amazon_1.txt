I love these animal stories by Thornton Burgess!  They are a perfect first read aloud to small children, and as a well-read parent, I enjoy them myself.I read them to my kids ages 6 yrs, 3 yrs, and 22 months each night as they lay in bed.  Even the youngest asks for "Jimmy Skunk" or "Johnny Chuck" or whoever the character is that we're reading about most recently.Each of Burgess' animal stories books is about 30 chapters long, and each chapter takes anywhere from 2 to 5 mins to read.  This is only one of 20 or more books about animals in the Green Forest and the Green Meadow, and the more books you read, the more you get to know the various critters as they interact.I like that the stories address basic moral lessons (kindness, honestly, sharing, humility, bravery) while acknowledging the different characteristics of each animal. For example, Reddy Fox is a bit vain because of his beautiful coat, and he is sly, but he's also loveable in his own way.I also love that these stories are timeless.  They're about animals in the meadow and forest, so there are no iPhones, Fitbits, or flat screens.If you love the Hundred Acre Wood, you should give Burgess' Green Meadows a try.
