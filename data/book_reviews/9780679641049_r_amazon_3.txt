Blood Meridien is one of the most violent, and brutal novels I have yet read.  McCarthy's prose is impressive, but after a while it loses its majesty and became, for me, somewhat turgid and overwrought.  It is a novel with grand philosophical ambitions, and to its credit, it comes close to achieving its lofty goals.  However, without giving away any spoilers, the ending was heavy handed and overdone, and while the architecture of the novel definitely lends itself to interesting discussions, as a work of fiction, it is not a great novel.Read moreRead less
