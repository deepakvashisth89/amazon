I enjoyed this novel, but sometimes became annoyed by the writing style. I've read many books where the narrative switches from character to character, however this one didn't have any real dividing lines. I quickly learned that was part of the style, but it was still a little annoying at times.  The characters were well developed, but I was unclear as to the purpose of including  Ann and the mental ward patient who was coming to find her. I think the story line could have been effective even without that part.  While reading it, I thought it was just okay, but once I finished, I realized I missed it and just how good it really was.
