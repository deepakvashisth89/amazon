This combined version of two classic Levine collections should be understood as a crescendo. It begins with rather underwhelming examples of the poet's work, pitching occasionally to the caliber that later made his works worthy of a Pulitzer.  The latter half of the collection reaches the caliber for which he's more known for and whisks the reader into a more frenzied ready that relinquishes only once the reader has reached the final page.Read moreRead less
