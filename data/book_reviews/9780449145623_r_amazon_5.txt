This series (and this is the first book) has a whole lot going for it; A plausible beginning to the war (even in 2021 although the writing dates back to the 1990s), plausible characters for the most part and a broad sweep of history and possible outcomes. Perhaps the sweep is too broad. It's a bit incomprehensible to me that 4 siblings would all be fighting in the war, although their father is a retired admiral. Some of the Russian plotlines are a bit implausible as well. They do create the novelistic tension that's needed though, so I can't really fault any of this. It's written well enough that the pages do keep turning.The only reason I give this 4 stars instead of 5 stars are out of the writers control...or perhaps not. It's so poorly proofread that it's incomprehensible at times. I'm now reading the 3rd book in the series and may not go to the 4th if the proofing doesn't get better. It's like the publisher hired a 12 year old to proofread and the proofreader didn't know a lot of language or was just plain lazy.If you're prepared to gloss over all the typos, you'll be in for a good read.Read moreRead less
