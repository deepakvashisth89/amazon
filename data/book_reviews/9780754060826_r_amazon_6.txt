I chose the five-star rating because this book is great and has quite a few funny parts in which I enjoyed reading. The part where Joanna throws water at Mischief made me laugh a lot because Mischief was right in front of Uncle Quentin's study and Uncle Quentin himself came out just then so the water sloshed all over him instead of Mischief!!! The children end up getting locked in a lighthouse and they can only get out by climbing down into the foundations of the lighthouse. This way makes them end up finding lost treasure from Wreckers long ago!!! I think that this is suitable for anyone who has read the rest of the Famous Five series.
