My favorite of all the Darkover books. The political intrigue and the psi powers are fascinating, but it's the characterizations that keep me re-reading this particular book. Lew Alton, Regis Hastur, and Danillo Syrtis are wonderfully flawed, human characters caught up in a dangerous game of survival that pits a monolithic empire against a feudal society that barely survived its violent past. It's both a coming of age story and a story of crushed ideals. "Heritage of Hastur" featured the first hints of a gay romantic relationship with Regis and Dani--a beautiful love story that lives between the lines but that inspired my own writing. The book isn't a romance, but it is a romantic, sweeping novel that has and will always linger in my imagination and my heart.Read moreRead less
