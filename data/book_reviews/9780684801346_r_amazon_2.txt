This book played a huge role in how I raised my two girls. Now 22 and 20 years old.  I now have two wonderful children that I love spending time with. They are both out of the house and on their own and both doing great - considering their ages. I never dealt with terrible two’s, I never dealt with moody teenagers and my daughters trust my husband and I and our advice more than they trust anyone else in the world. This isn’t the only book I read that helped, but is was an amazing foundation and set the stage for everything else.  This is the book that I gift new parents with a healthy recommendation to actually read it.  I believe it was the keystone to everything else I did as a parent.  I had no idea how to raise a child, I didn’t have a good example. Now I’m glad for that because it led me to read a lot of child rearing books. This one was the most valuable I think.
