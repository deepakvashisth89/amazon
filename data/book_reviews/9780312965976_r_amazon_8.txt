This book is primarily romance, chick-lit, relationship fiction. But the story shows all the skill and enjoyment of an author still interested in playing with the genre. It contains a mystery with enough action to keep things moving, and some vivid descriptions of Cape Cod life-- not your ordinary middle-class life, to be sure. I found the characters human, if a bit conventional, the sex not too violent, and the dilemmas reassuringly resolved. The product placements were a bit intrusive, especially at first. If you want a book to provide security that all will be well eventually, this does the job. For it's type, a lot of fun.Read moreRead less
