Bill is a loser, a self made loser, one that has gone through life making the wrong decisions time and time again.Unwilling to risk forging his dead mother's cheques, he decides instead on a different get rich quick scheme, one involving a firework stall and the words "This is a stick~up"! But as is usual for Bill, things don't exactly go to plan, leaving him with a disfiguring 'mosquitover', and the only place seemingly fit for his hideout: a travelling freak~show.Taken in and accepted by the freaks, who at first think Bill is one of their own, he soon begins to turn his bad luck around, but when a scheming young woman rears her not so ugly head, Bill's unlucky habits prove to be coming back in spades...Heaven only knows why I hadn't read this one sooner than I did. I've been aware of it for years and only just got round to reading it. Such a good book, it had me chuckling all the way through, with Lansdale's usual brand of vivid, unusual characters and their vulgar exploits, unleashed into another modern, knockabout, Texan crime classic.One could argue that the 20page payoff by the end doesn't really justify a 225 page build up, but if you're anything like me, you'll still have had a lot of fun getting there.Personally, although lacking in a little direction at times and maybe not as action packed as some of his offerings (which is about the only critical thing I can say!), I still think that this often amoral noir thriller is almost right up there with his best stuff..Highly recommended.4.75/5Read moreRead less
