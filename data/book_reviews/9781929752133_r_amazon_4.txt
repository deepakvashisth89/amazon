I often buy overstock novels at the Dollar Tree that have amazing reviews on the cover, but turn out to suck. I learned that just because a book has good reviews doesn't mean it's good.Acts of the Apostles, on the other hand, is every bit as good as its most enthusiastic reviews. Moreso, in fact. Only an author as good as John ? Sundman themselves could possibly write a review that conveys how awesome this novel and the companion books that entangle it are. The prose is gripping, insightful, and unexpectedly funny. The plot is unpredictable and satisfying. The science fiction is well-researched. The villain is my favorite of all time. And do you like meta? I like meta. On its own, John F.X. Sundman's novel isn't very meta, but once you add in the works published by John Compton Sundman and John Damien Sundman, you will wake up drunk in a ditch clutching a stained paper bag filled with the crazed notes you have taken trying to piece everything together, and when you stagger to the convenience store, reeking simultaneously of Jack Beam's and Red Bull, you will discover ten years of your life have passed. You remember nothing of those years, but the research in that stained paper bag document every twisted path your brain turned down in its search to decipher the truth.Read moreRead less
