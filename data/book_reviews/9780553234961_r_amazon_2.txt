When amateur sleuth Henry Gamadge pays a courtesy call on elderly Miss Julia Paxton, who is living temporarily in a New York City townhouse while she closes it up as a favor to relatives who live in California, he learns that there appear to have been some shenanigans involving a valuable antique print.  A murder soon follows.  With her usual ingenious plotting, author Daly sends the reader’s suspicions in all the wrong directions.  Perceptive and perspicacious as ever, Gamadge sorts it all out and exposes a most unexpected villain.  This novel will please fans of classic whodunits.Read moreRead less
