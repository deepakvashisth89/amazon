Ms Phillips story telling talent is front and center on every page of this story. She left me reeling between compassion and dislike for each of her carefully layered characters and yet left me loving every one of them, bar one (who will be obvious to anyone reading this book.) Following Honey Moon's journey from ragtag amusement park worker to child tv star to woman and back to amusement park owner was one heck of a roller coaster ride.I think we all need a Black Thunder in our lives.Read moreRead less
