As a high school student, I have seen through Martin Gilbert's book, the astonishing and unique life of Mr. Winston Churchill. Although the book was rather long, I enjoyed that the author used precise details and added quotations from letters to, about, and from Mr. Churchill. Many have said that such a character as Mr. Churchill would surely be a tedious subject to study because of his political life, however, through Martin Gilbert’s research on Mr. Churchill and how he made the reader feel as though they were apart of Mr. Churchill’s life. I did find myself rather weary of the length of the book; however, when reading the book, I was captivated by the stories and events that made up Mr. Churchill’s days. My favorite parts of the book included his early life as a troublesome child, and the life that he lived devoted to his wife Clementine. As the book was clearly written in chronological order, it made looking up information in the book easy.
