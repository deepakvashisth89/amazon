This is a must read if you grew up in alcoholic family or married to an alcoholic or addict, regardless how old you.  I started my journey in recovery 25 years ago by going to a therapist and no one has yet to bring up this topic. I have been in AA, NA, and Al-Anon and no one wants to talk about ACoA issues.Although I found “Recovery: A Guide for Adult Children of Alcoholics” by Herbert L. Gravitz helpful and gave me awareness, this book is great because it is well organized and offers me structure to my recovery. I am 61 and still seeking answers regarding my unhappiness. I am tired of taking the blame and having no idea what trips me up. I find the 12 step programs helpful, but all of them want me to take the blame for all my problems. I will still attend meetings for the fellowship, but I won’t expect them to cover topics in this book.I am glad this book is in print and am saddened that therapists have not written more books like this. I guess it may be hitting too close to home. One thing that books tell me is not to expect other family members to join me.
