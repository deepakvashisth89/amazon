Ann Marie Sabath has a way of getting directly to the point, making this book an excellent reference for anyone wondering about the rules of  etiquette in the workplace.  Her book deals with the issues that most  executives need to know. At a time when informality and familiarity seem to  rule, Sabath's book clarifies what is appropriate for the reader's success  both personally and professionally.
