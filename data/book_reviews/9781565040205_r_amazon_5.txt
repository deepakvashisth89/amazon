Although in the product information provided by Amazon states that this book is from 1995, the first edition was published in 1992. It was one of the first Vampire the Masquerade products. In it, we already find some information about several trademark signature organizations like the Society of Leopold, the Arcanum or the Special Affair Division of FBI. As most know, WW published supplements detailing all those organizations; so the material is naturally outdated...but in 1992 it was an essential book used in many campaigns, so it is an interesting collector's item.We must also remember that many gamers don't use or buy all supplements. For those that don't want to invest a lot of time and money can use this book to run a Hunters chronicle or to flesh out interesting enemies.In this book you will also find description of the bloodline Children of Osiris (and their discipline Bardo), Numina powers (including the powerful Clairvoyance and faith), spells (specific for the hunter), motives and vampire hunting techniques.Read moreRead less
