I'm an avid student of Russian history so I was excited to pick up this book since it covered an often overlooked period of Russian history.I have to second a previous reviewer, wondering where the editor was. Not only were there various syntax errors, which I can get past, but there were several instances of outright mistakes. In describing family relations, the author gets some things wrong. Describing characters for example, as siblings on one page, but cousins just a few pages later. Or referring to someone as an aunt or uncle, when just pages earlier they were described as cousins. I read and re-read the pages over and over to make sure I wasn't imagining it, and I wasn't. Maybe this was a translation issue?The book was well-written and entertaining otherwise,it really filled in some of the lesser known gaps between Peter the Great and Catherine the Great. So I hate to give it a bad review. It is just that these simple factual errors really turned me off, and they were just some of things that I picked up on as a reader.
