The Zen Teaching of Bodhidharma is a profound yet compact book that dives into the essence of Zen. It contains the teachings of Bodhidharma, the legendary monk who brought Zen to China. The book is minimalistic—no fluff, just direct, thought-provoking wisdom. It challenges the reader to look beyond words and seek understanding through experience.The translation is clear, but the text itself can be dense, requiring deep reflection. If you’re looking for practical Zen advice, this may not be a step-by-step guide. However, if you appreciate raw, unfiltered spiritual insight, this book delivers.
