First, I love Bernard Cornwell novels, and I love seafaring adventure. I'm a modern day Pirate at heart! This is the fourth book in a seafaring series, and it strays from Cornwell's usual historical based novels to provide simple reading pleasure!This one concerns ex-Marine Nick Breakspear, now a captain of a charter yacht named Wavebreaker in the Bahamas. One day he was coming back to port when he spied a wreck. It was named the Hirondelle, and it was riddled with bullet holes and ax chops. It should have sunk, but was of the "unsinkable" type boat that is sandwiched with flotation foam.Some research on the boat showed Hirondelle had sailed not two days before it washed up a wreck from the Ill named "Murder Cay".  Enter into this soup a U.S. Senator named Crowninshield and his two twenty something children. Nick had hoped to repair his own boat, the Masquerade, and head south before hurricane season. But the Senator made him a deal he couldn't refuse, taking his kids on what amounted to a "re-hab" cruise to get them away from the drugs they were addicted to. After pleading by his crew for the money involved, and pleading by the Senator in a genuine attempt to save his children, he relented. They all set out on Wavebreaker.Things started badly, due to the kids attitude, and eventually Nick must fend off an act of Piracy at sea. He loses a crewman, his boat is sunk beneath him, and he barely escapes death.  After surviving the attack, he must find a way to get back to Murder Cay and the island to find his wife Ellen, and to get a little payback.I won't give away the ending...but it is a nice finish to a great read!Read moreRead less
