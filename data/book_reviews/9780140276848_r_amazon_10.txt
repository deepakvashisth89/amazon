Jacki Lyden rose above her childhood in a dysfunctional family with a bipolar mother and an abusive stepfather to become a noted journalist and expert on the Middle East. Her mother’s illness was catastrophic yet held an element of enchantment - as she spun elaborate fantasies and created spectacular costumes to complement her hallucinations.
