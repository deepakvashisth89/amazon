I decided to better myself and give poetry another try. I tried the old classics and found some poems to be moving, but not enough to take time away from a hectic schedule to read an entire book. Then my best friend (my wife) suggested I try Housman. I'm delighted I did. These poems are wonderfully written, rich in ideas and emotion.For context, I love reading and at this time I mainly focus on non-fiction: US history, biographies, military matters, and for work--various engineering related books. Not much in the way of art. In addition, in what seems a former life, I've spent several years in uniform. Years of endless training, patrols, running around, hunting baddies, and other such activities. I've pondered the value and cost of 'the mission comes first'. I gained and earned bothers that were closer to me than my own blood brother. I lost a part of myself when one of them died. I spent time around camp fires, joking around and singing with my mates between life shaping events, building deep friendships that only current or former soldiers can fully understand.And I was surprised to see that Housman "gets it". Love, friendship, fear, absolute focus, dark humor, dark thoughts, happy thoughts, sunny spots of joy, and duty are all represented in his poems.His life experience, sense of life, and wonderful rhymes come across in a descriptive and beautiful manner. His metaphors and rich language tell a tale to your mind and you heart. It's really great!I highly recommend Housman's work to anyone who's looking for good poetry to read.
