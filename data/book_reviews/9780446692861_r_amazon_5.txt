I was looking for some light and entertaining, perhaps a chick lit, book. And I found it. I was pleasantly happy to find out that what I thought would happen, didn't. I didn't roll my eyes and say "oh course that happened". I don't understand the constant attraction to books like "confessions of a shopaholic" which i tried so painfully to muddle through, but gave up when the same three descriptive adjectives were recyled to tears. Megan Crane actually has an entire vocabulary to pull from! How refreshing!I did think the constant trolling of pubs and chainspoking of cigarettes droned on a bit, but i could handle it. I was just glad that the main character didn't end up "snogging" the hot professor. Maybe some readers were disappointed that the story line was too close to a realistic plot. Oh, and if you are looking for any over indulgant sex scenes because I mentioned "chic lit", move on.This was an enjoyable light read.Read moreRead less
