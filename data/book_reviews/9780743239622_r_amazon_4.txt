I am not sure if the author of this delightful book ever got sued for his creation,but this comes as close as I have seen to slander. The book is drawn completely in vivid coloured wax crayon,as if by someone that is a little stunted in his development. Complete with the spelling mistakes one would expect from a child,this book tells the story of the (very close) friendship between George and Tony. The artist manages brilliantly to fit much of his personal opinion into the artwork. There were parts where I was laughing out loud. This light-hearted book will tell any reader of the future as much about the Bush era as any serious opinion piece,about the man and his conservative approach to life.
