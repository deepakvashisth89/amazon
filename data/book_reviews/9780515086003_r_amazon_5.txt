I have always been a fan of Roberta Gellis' work, from the day I first encountered "Bond Of Blood" in a used bookstore. Her research is meticulous, her characters as true to life as to the period they inhabit, and, while this must all be accomplished by hard work behind the scenes, the reader will never notice. She makes it all look easy.
