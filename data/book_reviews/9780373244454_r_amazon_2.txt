I will add my enthusiastic well done to the other reviews. This is a sweet love story between a wounded paratrooper and a nurse. Of course she tries to make him see all the reasons why there can be no "you and me" . Cal Doyle has learned the hard way that you can't make someone love you; his parents and sister weren't there for him. Thankfully Pop Doyle gave him guidance and a good foundation to live by. Kate Meehan had a first marriage that ended badly and a current boyfriend on the way out. She has three sisters who are concerned when this next door good looking neighbor starts hanging around. The dialogue is funny and realistic between Cal & Meehan; Mrs Bee and her church ladies also have opinions. If you want just a sweet love story with a truly good ending you'll love this.
