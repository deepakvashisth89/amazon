Emma Goldman was a preeminent anarchist thinker of the early 20th century, and this work that she has done is a great indicator of that. Though I would not personally consider myself to be an anarchist, some of the ideas and philosophies that were laid out in the essays (the issues of poverty, the legislation of morality, and the collusion of big business and the state) definitely struck a chord to me. I would definitely recommend this book to those who would like an introduction to the general philosophies of the anarchists of the 20th century, and anarchist thought in general.Read moreRead less
