This is an excellent book for those interested in getting an overview of IR.  The book summarizes all the important milestones of IR up to 1999 (There are 852 references in the bibliography!).  The writing is concise yet eloquent. The authors try to cover as much ground as possible, providing a gold-mine of information comparing the pros and cons of the various types of implementation. However, I believe that due to the breadth of the techniques covered, some of the explanations for the algorithms were rather brief and not very illuminating.
