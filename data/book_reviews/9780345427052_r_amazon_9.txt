I am not a horseman in any shape or form although my father rode horses in the Great War. However if you want to read a fabulously true horse story this is the book for you. The book is written in straightforward and simple form by a man who actually knows precisely what he is talking about - no fuss no bull, this guy knows his animals, (and his acquaintances), which bodes well for a really good human and animal tale. You actually feel you would like to meet and talk to this man, and the fact that he has met film stars and kings and queens would make no difference to his attitude or interest in what you have to say or do - he would treat you with courtesy and charm. A really great book. Buy it for you family and enjoy it for yourself.Read moreRead less
