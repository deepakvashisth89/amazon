I read another of Bob Mayers books, Atlantis, and I found the writing good, but parts of the story didn't appeal.Nevertheless, I gave another one a go, and I'm glad I did. The opening section of the book takes a while to get going (well after the opening action sequence), but once it does, then it's a great story all the way.In essence, the story is of America sending special forces down to colombia to take on the drug cartels. Of course we have a number of twists and turns, but it really is a solid read, full of action, good characters, and some nasty villans.Really worth giving a go.Read moreRead less
