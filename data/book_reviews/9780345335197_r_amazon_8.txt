There is a statement at the end of the book by one of Woody's children comparing Woody to Lincoln.  The comparison was how certain individuals become mythological cultural icons, yet were actually mere human beings.This book was an easy read and very entertaining.  Oh, perhaps I no longer worship Woody as much as I once did, but knowing about his many faults only makes for a more interesting story.  How can a person fight so much for all of humanity, yet be such a poor husband or father?  Thinking about this I recognize that this has been the case with many great people.
