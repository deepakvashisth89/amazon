I 100% agree with the 2008 review of this book titled 'convoluted'. This author put me off right from the beginning, using ten-dollar words that not only are completely unnecessary, but actually obscure the meaning she is trying to convey. ("Upstaging the content" is a great way to put it.) It feels very much like the author is writing to "impress" the reader with her knowledge - but trying to stuffily impress a reader usually gets in the way of helping that reader actually understand what you supposedly want them to know. There are much simpler, clearer, and more direct ways of saying what she's trying to say - but she goes for the flowery version every time. And it's not that I don't understand the words she's using - I'm an English major, and I've been working in the field of information organization for decades. It's that I understand them, but they do NOT flow. They do NOT assist understanding of the concepts as a whole. As the other reviewer said, this text requires a LOT of re-reading and decoding that in my opinion is wholly unnecessary. By trying to "impress" me, this author has left me thoroughly UNimpressed.
