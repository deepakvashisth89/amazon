This is not a quick read, and as another reviewer pointed out, it's not because it's 700 pages. (I breezed through War and Peace, which is double its length). It's because the author is painstaking with his set up and his character psychology. The novel is rich in detail and the analysis of the complexities of its characters. The author's understanding of human nature is almost forensic. Basically, you have a modern understanding of human psychology set in 19th century Spain.
