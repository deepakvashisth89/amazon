As you probably know, “The Astounding-Analog Reader” was first published as one volume, but the Sphere paperback edition split it  into two books, of which this is Book Two.This is the 1973 Sphere paperback.  It contains eight stories, selected by the editors  as those stories that most impressed them as “callow adolescents” and yet “still retain their strength upon rereading after two decades or more”.  There is also a ten page introduction by the editors.  Of these eight stories,  only two, “Clash by Night” and  "First Contact",  appeared in Campbell’s classic “Astounding Science Fiction Anthology”.Personally, I think this is an excellent selection, containing several classics, and without a weak story.  All of these are very readable.  I much prefer this selection to that of Book One, but then the stories are arranged chronologically and I would expect the later ones in this particular science fiction window to be better.The above is only my opinion.  I do not know enough about science fiction to give a detailed review. I’m also concerned that in the detail I might give away too much of the storylines.
