The 1976 edition of Mathematical Elements for Computer Graphics by David F. Rogers and J. Alan Adams is a highly valued book on my bookshelf. Its mathematical approach would delight any physicist or mathematician. I've not seen a better introduction and application of matrix algebra. The introduction to homogeneous coordinates was enlightening and gave me the momentum to learn more about matrix mechanics and tensors. Its coverage of Bezier curves and surfaces was reviewed by Professor Bezier. The forward by S. H. Chasen states, "It exemplifies the rare type of work that most practitioners should wish to place in a prominent location within their library since it should prove to be an invaluable ready reference for most disciplines.". That high praise is well earned. Even the typeface was incredibly friendly. Any polymath, autodidact, polygot (computer languages) will remember this rare book over many years.Read moreRead less
