This is not an easy read, and it's probably too long by half, but it's far from Moorcock's worst.  (See "The Sailor on the Seas of Fate" for a good example of Moorcock at his hastiest.)  The pacing here is languid  but the hallmarks of Moorcock's best work are here, albeit fitfully:  his  unique feel for the period and thorough knowledge of history and culture,  and his ability to blend the historical elements with the fantastic.
