This isn’t really a novel, though a few characters do appear throughout. It’s actually a collection of short stories, linked by what Pohl calls interstitial pieces, that focus on various people in an expedition to Mars and back on Earth whose lives are affected (both good and bad) by the discovery of Martians. It’s more a sociological study than science fiction, as Pohl examines how the existence of Martians might impact a screenwriter, a fake guru, a scientist turned businessman, a cult member and others. At times it’s amusing and at other times insightful, but the overall tone is one of cynical benevolence toward the foibles of humankind when faced with a new and different reality. As for the Martians themselves, we don’t learn a lot about them, except that there’s not a lot to learn. I enjoyed it, but if you’re looking for a hard science fiction about first contact with aliens, this isn’t the book.
