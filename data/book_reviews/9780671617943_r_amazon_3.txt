This book contains 38 interviews in 278 pages with 8 pages of black and white photos.  All the photos are of the regular cast.Gene Roddenberry is interviewed along with the regular cast including Grace Lee Whitney but not Majel Roddenberry,(Nurse Chapel).Directors include Robert Butler(The Cage), Ralph Senensky(6 eps.), Marc Daniels(15), and Joseph Pevney(14). A good mix of technical info. and personal observations/anecdotes in these director interviews.---------Other Interviews Included--------Writers Norman Spinrad and Jerome Bixby.Producers Robert Justman and Fred Freiberger.10 technical crew covering design, sound, composing, photography, casting, and editing.Guest stars: 2From the movies: James Horner and Harve Bennett.The interviewer, Allan Asherman, keeps the interviews interesting by asking good questions and has a good rapport with his subjects, keeping things positive.I would recommend this book to anyone who likes reading about behind the scenes TV production and the careers of creative people.Read moreRead less
