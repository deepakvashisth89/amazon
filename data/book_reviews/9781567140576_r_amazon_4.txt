This is a very entertaining, albeit at times strange book. The writing is smooth and there is plenty of excitement to be had in these pages. The characters are well developed and have unique personalities which is unusual for a book that is intended for this age group. I found the plot and pacing to work well together and thought the pace was pretty even from beginning to end.
