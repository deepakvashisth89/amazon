I miss Ann Rule.  She was and always will be one of my all time favorite authors.  She had a unique way of telling other people's true crime stories.  When you finish reading each person's story, you almost feel as though you knew the person personally and that is due to Ann's writing.  The victims she wrote about in each and every book meant something to her.  She didn't write these books to make a ton of money, she wrote her books to get their stories out there and give them a voice and let them be heard.You cannot go wrong with an Ann Rule book.  You just cannot. I re-read books of hers that I initially read decades ago. THAT is how good her books are. I even find myself Googling some of the names of people she wrote about and looking for updates on them.You don't need a special reason to read Ann's books. They're perfect , always.
