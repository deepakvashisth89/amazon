I read this book while in High School and it was life changing!! An interdimensional mind-blower for me at that age in the late 80's. So I bought it and now am reading it to my young teenagers. I'm giving it 4 stars because I didn't realize back then how juvenile much of the inner and conversational dialouge is - between supposedly sophomore/junior age high schoolers. My current 15 and 17 year old teens get quite a smirk out of the immature tone of things. But it's still a good read. Also, today's kids are much more savvy about things like quantum physics, black holes, time/space kind of stuff, so it's not as earth shattering for them as it was for me. I give it four HAPPY STARS! The words "ana" and "kata" have been with me since I read this book. I'm still trying to figure out how to turn in those directions!!!!! Hope you enjoy the book.Read moreRead less
