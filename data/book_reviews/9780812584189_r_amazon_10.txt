ngelmass is a brilliant, intellectually stimulating piece of sci-fi that hits that perfect balance between fast-paced adventure and deep, ethical questioning. The core concept—a black hole emitting particles called "angels" that force people to be calm and incapable of lying—is fascinating and creates so much natural tension.The conflict between the Empyrean colonies, who embrace the power of the angels, and the Earth-based Pax empire, who fear it as a tool for subversion, feels incredibly grounded and high-stakes. Following Jereko Kosta as he gets pulled into the middle of this escalating political and military crisis makes for a gripping journey. It isn't just an action-packed space thriller; it makes you really think about the nature of morality and what it means to choose "good" behavior when you don't actually have a choice.
