This is not an important book, but it is very enjoyable.  It introduces us to the amazing (and, for me, hitherto unexpected) diversity among different types of chickens.  It is a wonderful tribute to the variety and beauty that can be developed by either natural selection or selective breeding.  Or, if you are creationist in your outlook, it is a wonderful example of the Creator's whimsy.
