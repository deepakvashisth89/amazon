I first read this book in the library many years ago. I re-read it again & deceided to buy the book, but it saddens me to say this book is now out of print. From start to finish, its a non-stop interesting story. It  about boy from a tribe following their common ritual quest to manhood.  There are unexpected comrades & enemies that help come to his aid &  jepordizes his quest. Finally, in the end he is unexpectedly greatly  rewarded. Back then, I remembered that I couldn't wait for the next  installment! I swear I thought this book was book one to a trilogy. I think  Greg Costikyan is a great writer but he has very little works of art  published. Those of you who wants to read a good old fashion fantasy, I  think this will be a great book for you.Read moreRead less
