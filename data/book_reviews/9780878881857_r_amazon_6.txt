I rated this book a 5/5 stars because it is not only a good read, but it captivates your attention and you want to keep reading. I would recommend this book to anyone who likes stories about growing after a big event in life. It was inspirational. I do wish, however, that we could learn of what happens with Davey and Wolf. Does he come and visit her? Does he find the letters in the caves when he returns to Los Alamos? Lots of unanswered questions there but I guess we just leave it to our imaginations. Overall, this was a very well written story and kept your attention. I loved it.Read moreRead less
