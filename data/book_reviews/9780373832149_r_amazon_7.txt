Second book in the Cord & Stacey series. The story revolves around Colter Langston, a friend of Cord & Stacey, and Natalie Crane. They meet at the Fiesta San Antonio when Natalie's nephew and Colter's daughter Stephanie grab for the same piece of candy tossed from a float. The boy left where his Aunt Natalie left him to fetch a drink. Colter helps his aunt find him,and the two keep running into each other during the Fiesta. Natalie is at the end of her rope on how she & her nephew can survive financially & s working jobs at the Fiesta.Colter's daughter takes a liking to Natalie's nephew & the four are constantly thrown together. Colter needs a wife as a mother for his daughter & none of the women he knows fit the bill.So he proposes a marriage where Natalie has a home for her nephew & Colter has a mother for his daughter. This is a very good story. Cord & Stacey make a brief appearance when a tragedy happens.This is the debut of Travis McCrea who will appear in For bitter O worse & A Land Called Deseret!
