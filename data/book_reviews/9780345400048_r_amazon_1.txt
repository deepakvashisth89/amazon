What marvelous cultural differences are found in this world. The magic is well done putting both the caster and the environment at risk when used. Some of the most unlikely characters are likely to become your favorites. The book has several plots twists, including the ending, but all very satisfying. Many places in the book have you sitting on the edge of your seat, other times the pressure is there but less imminent. This book reminds me of the Wheel of Time series except condensed.
