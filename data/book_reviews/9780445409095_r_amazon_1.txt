Excellent mystery novel concerning Toby Peters, a private eye set in Hollywood of the 1940's.  In this novel he wakes up a sees John Wayne with a gun pointed at him.  It gets stranger from there.  Working for John Wayne, Peters comes across a number of well known people of that era, including Charlie Chaplin and Harold Lloyd as well as the Duke's buddies Ward Bond and Paul Fix.  The plot is very twisted and the other characters quite interesting. At the end all makes sense.  The book itself was in good condition.Read moreRead less
