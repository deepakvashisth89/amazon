I was immediately drawn in by the premise of this book. Unfortunately, the story didn't bring out its full potential. The main characters had personalities but still seemed distant. It was like getting to know 'about' someone but not knowing them personally. I think that's why I couldn't get any sense of chemistry between Susannah and Jedidiah. They just spent a lot of time getting frustrated with each other.I would not describe the plot as uneventful. However, I will say it was not exciting. Susannah and Jedidiah were in a serious situation, but there was only a small sense of danger, hence little tension. The events that occurred during the journey were underwhelming.
