Rosemary Wells' "Max" books are wonderful and we especially love them for our first grandson, born February 16, 2000 because his name is MAX!  Our Max's mom has four of the Max series and we have just ordered  four more.  The board books are easy for a young baby to see and for the  mother to handle while also handling the baby!
