I have at least all of the Foxtrot books, and I can read them any time, over and over, and they still make me laugh, even though I know "what's coming" -- they kept me laughing -- through several serious illnesses, (and I mean "serious illnesses") -- they kept my spirits up -- I can just think of Jason and his "antics", and it brightens my spirits and makes me laugh -- we really need more "Jasons" in this old world --  I have been around this old world a long, long time, and I can't remember any other "comic strip" having that effect on me, so I thank "The Good Lord" for Bill Amend, and his "Foxtrot family" -- I was somewhat saddened when he went to Sundays-only strips, but, heck, I can't blame him, I just really hope that he keeps on going and doesn't quit altogether -- that would be a terrible loss --- that might sound like an odd statement, but, gee, just read the daily news, and you can appreciate that we really, really need "Foxtrot".  So, Bill, if you're out there reading this, please don't retire completely for a long time --- thank you for all you've done, I don't think you realize just how much your strip helps so many of us out here.
