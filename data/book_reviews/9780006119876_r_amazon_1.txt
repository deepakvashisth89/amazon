I do not read romances - any other than the ones by Victoria Holt; I've been reading her books since I was in my teens! She is a fabulous writer, creates strong characters - her female protagonists are in 1st person, smart women whose roles one can easily and joyfully metamorphose into! Ms Holt uses all the elements of a great story: suspense, mystery, interesting realistic characters, and accurate historical scenes - it's just a joy to forget oneself in her books.Only three of her books are available on Kindle; I don't understand why they aren't all made available. Holt was one of the most popular authors in the world; when she died in 1993 she had sold more than 100 million books. She wrote under various pen names, Jean Plaidy, Philippa Carr; those were historical romance type books, but not as interesting (to me at least) as her Holt series. However, she wrote murder mysteries under the pen names Eleanor Burford, Elbur Ford, Kathleen Kellow, Anne Percival, and Ellalice Tate.
