Reads like a bad Agatha Christie fan fiction. I love the idea but hate the execution. The "story" relies on pages-long monologues of dialogue. There is far too little exposition and the exposition that is included is littered with odd phrases. "so the saying goes" was injected in the book at least twice. It is clear that the authors know the accounting and economics principles quite well but don't know how to write murder novels. Perhaps they should hire a real writer to rework their book so that it is at least marginally enjoyable. I can't say I derived any utils at all after reading this sorry excuse for a book. Attempts to include the economics principles were very heavy-handed. There was no real character development; just caricatures (writing a German adult film star with dialogue such as "Zee"). There were at least three references to a Blackberry, making this come across as dated.
