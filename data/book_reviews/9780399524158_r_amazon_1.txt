This is a real simple book to review.  If you want to run a four hour marathon and spend four months training for it, then buy this book.  It is slim, it is focused, easy to follow, enjoyable to read.  It gives you what the title says it gives you.If you want to gain a deeper understanding of marathoning, or want to run one in three hours, etc.  DO NOT BUY THIS BOOK.  It is not written with that puropose.I wanted to run my first marathon in four hours.  I bought this book.  I am as happy with it as anyone could possibly be.  I cannot imagine any complaints.(In a different review a reader talks about the quality of the advice (shoes selection, clothing, etc.)  Hogwash.
