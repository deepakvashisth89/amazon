Secret, Silent Screams is a great book for teenagers. It has to deal alot with life. Helps teens see that life is not always fun and perfect. The problem is that an investigation takes alot of time and most people believe it is pointless because he committed suicide. There isnt much time though, if Barry was killed his murderer is still out there he could finding his next victim. In this book Marti is trying to convince people to help her and to believe her when she says it wasn't suicide.
