After a short (10-pages) preface, she jumps right into the Songs and divides them into 31poems (versus 8 chapters in the King James version).  I particularly like how she distinguishes the male, female and group voices (something KJV does not do). Her poetic voice has a nice rhythm to it.  There are 6 black and white, wood print images by Barry Moser interspersed.
