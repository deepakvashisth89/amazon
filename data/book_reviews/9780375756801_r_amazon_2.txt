THE VOYAGE OF THE BEAGLE by Charles Darwin that I ordered was set in such tiny type that it was impossible to read 221 pages.  I would have liked to know before purchase  that this particular edition was not standard.  As is, the purchase was  total waste of money.  I ultimately got a copy in standard print  (509 pages)  from the library, and enjoyed the book very much.  Darwin was an exceptional observer with a vast knowledge of the natural world.
