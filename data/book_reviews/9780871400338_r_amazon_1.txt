This is a difficult review to write, because this review is more about the publishing of the version of this book than the book itself.I ordered the hardcover of this book, but it did not look like the picture.  The cover looked like the paperback version, but had a hard cover.If I was the publisher of this version of the book I would be embarrassed.  It looks like a very bad third or fourth generation photocopy that someone made in college from a book in the library.  That means that it's a photocopy of a photocopy of a photocopy, etc.  The quality of the copy is so bad that it is hard to read at times.  It is clearly a photocopy, because you can see where someone underlined sentences.  The photos of paintings in the book are so degraded by multiple copying that you can hardly see them.  They are basically black and white blobs on a page.There are several pages that are actually missing!  The following pages are completely missing from the book:  18, 21, 38, 74 and 83.  These pages were printed twice: 34, 39 and 75.  There is also a blank white page between pages 29 and 30.The information on Amazon says that this is published by Moran Press.  It should be Moron Press.  What idiot publishes a book with missing pages?  Didn't anyone review this before it went out?  Within the book itself, there is nothing about "Moron" Press.  I guess they were too embarrassed to put their contact information in the book.Save your money.  DO NOT BUY FROM THIS PUBLISHER!  Find a used copy of the original book.
