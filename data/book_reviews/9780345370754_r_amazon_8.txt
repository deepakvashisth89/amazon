After being pretty impressed with recent Agatha first book nominees, I've been venturing back into some of the older nominees.  This book, a nominee in 1991, wasn't very impressive.  The story is set in a B&B in a small town in Tennessee.  Far too much of the book is about the B&B and small town life.  Such mystery as there is only gets rolling in the second half of the book.  It seemed to me that the author's real agenda was to write about life in her town and the mystery was an afterthought.Bottom-line: Not sure what this book did to earn a nomination and not surprised the series hasn't gone on to further critical acclaim.
