Fun and colorful book that describes the basics of Feng Shui.Feng Shui principles encompass decor, layout, direction and location that help bring peace and harmony to your life and work through the flow of "chi" energy.I received a copy for Christmas and it makes a great gift.My favorite section is finding your element--your personality. By noting the last digit of your year of birth, you will find your element in the chart. For example if you were born in 1960, METAL is your element and your personality is yang (extrovert). The chacteristics of METAL are: controlled, organized, idealistic and authoritative.
