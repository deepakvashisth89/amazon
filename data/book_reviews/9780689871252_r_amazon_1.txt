This book was first published in 1968. I read it in the summer between 5th & 6th grade, a few weeks before my 11th birthday. Normally I would not have selected the book, but I was a twice a week library patron and the librarian had just finished covering the brand new book. She recommended it to me, and I was forever grateful to her. This book touched me in a way no other book had. I became a lifelong fan of sci-fi & fantasy fiction after reading this book.At 64 yrs old, I reordered this book to bring me back to that hot New England summer when I spent a few days exploring another realm with a peer named Ellen. Recommend for ages 12+ unless the younger child is mature and able to separate fictional scenarios, such as parents’ deaths, from reality.Read moreRead less
