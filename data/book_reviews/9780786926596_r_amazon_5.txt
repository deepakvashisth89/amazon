I think D20 Modern used a lot of stock elements, such as setting it in New York, like they usually do. But it kept me really busy during boring down time at school, and there were some cool ideas in this supplement, so it wasn't a bad idea, all things told. I liked the horror theme that it entailed in bringing D&D to the modern world, but mostly, I just liked the idea of "what would it be like to put D&D in the modern world".
