Even though this is not your typical romance, most readers will really enjoy it.  It is the story of Honey Moon, a young girl from a rough background who rather accidentally finds herself the star of a TV show.  The romance in the book is generally pretty good, but the more interesting part of the book is Honey's growth from a tough, unloved girl, to an emotionally dependent wife, to an independent and fully mature woman.  As usual, Susan Elizabeth Phillips is adept at adding touches of humor and creating interesting, multi-dimensional characters.  It's a good read.
