If you want the facts of the Broderick murders, this book is the best.  It is much more comprehensive than the others on this subject and I felt as if the author was unbiased.  I bought it due to a recent TV airing where a reporter said that Betty Broderick wasn't portrayed accurately in any of the movies about the case.  Unfortunately, I disagree with the reporter.  After reading this book, I feel that she was definitely portrayed correctly in the movie, A Woman Scorned.  She is one of the most self centered, narcissistic mothers that has ever existed.  I think she should have received the death penalty because I sincerely believe that the murders of her ex-husband and his wife were premeditated. I do have to say that the book did give me a better insight into Linda Kolkena. I didn't realize until after reading this book how much her actions may have played a part into Betty's anger.
