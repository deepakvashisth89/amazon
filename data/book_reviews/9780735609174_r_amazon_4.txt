It teaches chess tactics and combinations so simply. It provides a "stepping-stone" style of building up what and how you calculate positions on the chess board.The start of a chapter will sometimes provide a position that's very puzzling. It then teaches a concept and provides exercises. By the end of the chapter, it returns to that same board position and asks you to look at it again. Even when I couldn't solve it all the way through, I was able to correctly head down the right line of reasoning and had a much better idea of the winning combination/tactic that I was looking for.There's a massive quiz/test section at the very end.
