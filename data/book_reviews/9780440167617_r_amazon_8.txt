It appears that many of the reviews have nothing to do with "Out of the Blackout" by Robert Barnard, but rather with some blackout curtains. I cannot speak to the curtains. I can speak of the book. It is excellent. It is what I perceive as an onion-skin novel. As the onion is peeled, the characters and the circumstances are revealed anew -- revealed anew in an unexpected and yet in retrospect revealed as just right. As is common for Barnard's work, this is as much a novel as it is a mystery.Read moreRead less
