It is an interesting way to pursue learning Japanese.  The book contains both Japanese to English puzzles (clues in Japanese and answers in English ) as well as English to Japanese (clues in English and the answers in either Hiragana or Katakana).  You get the chance to practice both vocabulary and simple grammar--They are easy crosswords after all.  For someone  new to Kana and Japanese who likes crossword puzzles they would be fun for a while.The one less than stellar thing about he book is the printing of the crossword grids.  The boxes that you don't use are only lightly shaded making them less than distinct.  It is a small thing, but it would have been easy to correct before the book was printed.The book contains 47 puzzles. 24 Japanese to English and 23 English to Japanese covering food, colours, and many everyday items.
