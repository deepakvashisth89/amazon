I enjoyed Book 2 just as much as Book 1 and hastily moved on to number 3.  This is a chronicle of a large, close family in turbulent times.  It has been compared to "Downton Abbey," but I think the comparison fits better with the Angela Thirkel's Barsetshire Chronicles that I feel are classics.  The Cazelet family is just one step below the gentry of Barsetshire and just as interesting and fun to read about.  Howard seems to be equally at home in describing the thoughts of both the adults and children of the family.  Sometimes they are very amusing, sometimes I get a little angry with some of them, but they are always very human.
