Odd book. The writer is obviously witty, thoughtful, and well-informed about many things. I enjoyed the writings on wild as well as companion felines (I hesitate to use the word domesticated when referring to the two creatures with whom my wife and I share our house...). The stories were great.  But at the same time, it was a book of stories and philosophical musings with a heavy dose of anthropomorphism. Virtually all evidence presented regarding feline behavior was anecdotal.If you'd like a light read getting inside the head of a cat lover who has insight into behavioral science, this book is for you. If you'd like a more serious, evidence based treatise on cat behavior, you may end up like me, a bit frustrated and disappointed.Read moreRead less
