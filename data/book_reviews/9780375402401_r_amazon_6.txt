Despite having heard her praises sung for years by various other authors, this is the first book by Ms. Winterson I've read, and while it was not what I was expecting, it did not disappoint.Each of the entries in the book is less plot or story than exquisitely rendered sensations of time, place, and emotion.  They often read as unmetered poetry more than prose, with several turns of phrase repeated (yet slightly different) several times throughout the collection. Many (but not all) have a dream-like quality, like gauze over a crystal chandelier.  The effect is not for every reader, but those in love with words and the power of raw prose will find much to enjoy.Read moreRead less
