Original Publication: 1990As usual Ms. Garlock has written another wonderful and heartfelt love story. This one takes place in an old abandoned mining town. Katy and her sister Mary, along with Mary's daughter Teresa are the only people left and are trying to survive on their own. After two months alone Garrick Rowe rides into the Montana ghost town and ends up saving Katy's life. Rowe believes they are soul mates and have met in other lives. Katy however is a skeptic but cannot deny a connection between them. Rowe intends to make Katy his and to bring the dead town back to life once again. Meanwhile his half-brother has plans of his own and will stop at nothing to destroy Rowe and everything he holds dear.Let me just say that there's a whole lot going on in this book. There are a ton of people to get to know and once again you'll get to greet many of the characters from MIDNIGHT BLUE (Book 1) although you don't have to read that story first in order to follow along and understand what's happening in this one. And if it weren't for Katy's pure childish behavior the first two-thirds of the way through, I honestly would have loved it and probably given it five stars. As it stands though her attitude became a bit too overbearing at times. On the other hand once she finally stops fighting her feelings for Rowe and fully accepts him, the love between these two was absolutely beautiful to behold!In the end it was just a really good book and like all of Garlock's work the romance was stunning. She somehow has a way of always making me feel as if her couples were made only for each other. I especially loved Rowe however who was smart, strong, tender, patient and handsome. I simply adore the way DG writes and she has never once failed to pull me in and make me feel a part of the people and places I'm reading about. She was a truly talented author who will be greatly missed by long-time fans like myself. Overall I'd recommend this story to all lovers of historical romance but especially those of the American West.
