Preface: Tolstoy's a risky guy to read. Specifically regarding Christ, his thoughts should all be disregarded.I enjoyed this work. He provides some good arguments against modern-day philosophy of art. A focus of his was criticizing the idea that art is an analog to written philosophy. He sees art as more of a "database of feelings". I like this critique and how poignant it is to today's shenanigans (think: banana on the wall).
