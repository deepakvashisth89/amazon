Beware! I should have been warned when I saw the copyright date from the 80’s. This is a very early work with all the clumsy mistakes of an inexperienced writer and with the domineering men of that era.The H is a loud-mouthed bully who treats his employees with abuse and contempt. He lies to the h to get her into bed. She is a schemer who tries to control him. Ugh. You don’t want to know these people.The writing lacks the skill, grace, sophistication and humor of Krentz’ later work.  I could not have imagined her early work was sooo bad.
