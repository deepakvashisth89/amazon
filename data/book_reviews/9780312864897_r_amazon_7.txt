I'm clearly in the minority here, and this was a tough one to rate simply because through most of the book it was looking like a 4 or maybe 5 star book.  It was interesting, there were different story lines going on, and it was like a puzzle trying to figure out how they all linked together.  I was really enjoying it.  But the last 10-15% was just action-packed good guys vs. bad guys violence, beyond being even slightly believable.  Suddenly it felt like a 10-year old boy who had seen too many Die Hard movies was writing a script, regardless of how outrageous his ideas were. The stories got lost, and in the end I think there were still a lot of loose ends.  I don't mean loose ends in the sense that they have to tie everything up - I understand this is a series - but in terms of not explaining certain things in THIS story that were important.  This was my first Blaine McCracken novel, and I don't think it hurt to pick up in the middle.  The author did a good job of filling in the back story.  I also think I would try another, in case it didn't fall apart like this one did.  But if it ends with me picturing Jack Bauer meeting Sly Stallone and Bruce Willis (yes, I get that I mixed characters with actors, but you get the picture....) then the next will be my last.
