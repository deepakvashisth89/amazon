Passion to Die For was a good book, good for someone who wants a quick book to read without to much backstory and overdetailed storylines, it wasan excellant mystery though. The culprit was totally out of left field, and not even mentioned as a suspect, and the climax of the situation was very quick and abrupt. The author packed a lot in this novella and delivered a great story with character development. This was my first book by this author and I would definitely read another. i was intrigued trying to figure out the killer.I was hooked from the very start of the book. I wanted to know ... what was Ellie's past. And when I learned - it truly touched my heart. I think anyone reading this book will enjoy it.Read moreRead less
