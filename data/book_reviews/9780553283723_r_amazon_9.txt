I have liked Kate Elliott in the past, but the problem with this book is that she has created quite a complex "universe" and I really didn't understand how it worked most of the time.  I also couldn't grasp the "geography" of the universe, or even their means of travel.  I know it is great to leave stuff out, and to show not tell, but I really could have done with more tell.Its an okay story, I read to the end.
