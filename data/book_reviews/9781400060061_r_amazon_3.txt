I was first introduced to this wonderful author when I read one of her newest..."The Shoemaker's Wife".  I have continued to read almost all of her novels (going backwards...) and find her to be an exemplary storyteller, with a marvelous ability to create characters that you get to know and feel deeply about.  Her stories can be "laugh out loud" delightful and incredibly sad all at the same time.  You grow to love these families that she draws you into.
