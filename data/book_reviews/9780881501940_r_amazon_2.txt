The setting is a tiny town in Maine, population 800. The focus is on a new post office with an elaborate mural that caricatures the townsfolk — who have no sense of humor and are furious.The characters we like most are a poor but genteel older gentleman and his daughter, who live in an octagonal house with an octagonal barn. The two people we dislike most are murdered.  That engages the interest of an amateur sleuth called Asey Mayo.There is also the matter of a big valuable lump of ambergris found on the beach that stirs up a lot of murderous greed. I had to look up ambergris, but maybe most readers will know what it is — a solid waxy substance from the intestines of a sperm whale used in the making of perfume among other things.This sounds like the makings of a very good mystery, and it is, if you enjoy a romp with a manic plot. This is a bit silly a book for my taste, but it has some appealing characters and an amateur sleuth with a sort of quirky charm.Read moreRead less
