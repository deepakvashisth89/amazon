This book is a MUST for ANY D&D campaign including Dragons. WOTC could not have done a better job to template the dragons and religions listed in this book. it gives GREAT descriptions of what a "blah" Dragon should be like, personality, nature based off age, and natural abilities. This book has great descriptions of each age category and size indications according. Also, if your using Pathfinder, it is not difficult at all to draw up a Dragon based off the template in the book. the CR rating is an easy adjustment due to the extensive detail about each age/class. however, the book i ordered was "used" but the description was Very Good "with no bent corners or visable use" and the book i recieved was very worn on the corners and had visible spine damage. be wary of the description. i would buy new if i had the chance. overall, 8/10.Read moreRead less
