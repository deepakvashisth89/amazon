Trying new (to me) authors, I picked up 2 of Mr. Simak’s books. This is the 2nd that I’ve read. The prose in both books is superb. I think I like the style on this one a bit better, but it’s still a bit formal and stilted. The story, up until it stops (it does not even attempt to end) is actually fun. Nothing particularly deep, and a lot of interactions. It is an older style of writing, and a bit stiff. Personally, I will not be seeking out others by Mr. Simak.Read moreRead less
