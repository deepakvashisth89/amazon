I admit, I haven't read the whole book. Focusing on the chapter on friendship in particular, C.S. Lewis had some wonderful things to say about true Friendships - between men, for men.  He said (in effect), "I'm not a woman, so I have no idea what their friendships are like,but I think they just blabber about useless things."  I therefore found this book somewhat useless, as I was hoping to learn about friendship love and romantic love on a deeper level but all of his writing is geared toward a male perspective.There might be some nuggets of wisdom to glean in other parts of the book, but I'm not very motivated to sift through everything else to find them.I love all of C.S. Lewis' fiction, and I'm sure this book has value for alot of people.  But, I haven't found it very enlightening.Read moreRead less
