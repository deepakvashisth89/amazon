Though many might consider this one of his minor works, the fact is it is a forward looking book examining the intricacies of racism, it’s vapidity and its unmoored from reality nature. The characters might be a caricature in many senses, but Lewis allows them to develop according to their nature.Neil Kingsblood is a hero . He comes to terms with what he has learned of his ancestry not in an epiphany but in a slow struggle within himself that is human, rational and relatable. The same is true for Vestal.At the end, I was waiting for the next chapter. I didn’t want it to end , the story was that compelling.Read moreRead less
