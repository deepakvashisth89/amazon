I bought this book after watching the movie 84 Charing Cross Road. The plot was very touching, simple daily work activities became something more meaningful through the narration of the movie and that’s what prompted me to purchase this book. Again I was not disappointed. I like the way the writer narrates her experiences while living in Britain, she brings out the hospitality of the British people and introduces readers to the various parts in London and Oxford etc, if they have never been there, through her recollection.
