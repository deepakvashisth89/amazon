This really is a great read: a series of short stories about different members of a Halifax squadron, many with unexpected endings, and each dealing with a different aspect of aircrew and wartime life. The technical detail is amazing, as are the stories of the heroes who flew night after night, knowing that their chances of survival were extremely low, at least partly because their equipment wasn't up to the job.
