This is a wonderful book detailing the experiences of Brenda Peterson with the natural world and trying to come to terms with her religious and cultural biases from her upbringing.  Her experiences are just amazing and constantly led me to question: did this really happen? There is a whole other world out there that we are not privy to, mostly because we are so self-absorbed and the loss is ours.  What we have to learn about the world of other creatures and about ourselves is immense.  We just need to be more open to those experiences.  I've bought several copies and sent them to friends and family.  I found this book at our local Friends of the Library used bookstore.  Or rather, I think it found me.
