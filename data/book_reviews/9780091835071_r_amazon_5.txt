Kal is one of those wide-ranging historical fictions charting the interactions of various families in the wild west of nineteenth century Australia. Unfortunately, Nunn is not Ken Follett and it doesn't quite work. The characters are somewhat flat, descriptions of scenery lack flair and the story rather plods along.I appreciate the work that has gone into the book - Nunn has obviously spent a lot of time on research - but it didn't quite do it for me.Read moreRead less
