I just finished The Outside World by Tova Mirvis and already miss the friends I made in the story. I am not Jewish but have always had such a great respect for the traditions and beliefs of the Jewish religion. To be able to share in the traditions and lives of the characters in this story was an honor. Ms. Mirvis has an incredible gift for building the characters in her story so that you feel like you know them personally. I loved her book The Ladies Auxiliary and was thrilled when I discovered The Outside World. Without straying too much from the story Ms. Mirvis is able to explain the history and reasons behind the traditions in the story. This was a wonderful learning experience as well as a delightful read. I would highly recommend this book for anyone that is interested in the Jewish religion but not familiar with the traditions.
