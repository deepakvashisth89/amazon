On average, this novel probably deserves more than 3 stars.  But I'm writing the review immediately after reading the ending, and the ending is SO BAD, I can't bring myself to go higher (no matter how much I enjoyed most of the book).This novel is an absolute heartbreaker.  4/5th's of it are wonderful.  Absolutely a joy to read.  And then Blair absolutely destroys all the excellent work she did in the final 1/5th.  For most of the book, the characters are lovely to spend time with.  They are not perfectly written - the heroine is rather more saintlike than I find optimal.  But the story of the hero and heroine falling in love, and more importantly, the story of the hero and the heroine's children falling in love, is a deep pleasure to be immersed in.  It is a story driven by characters and relationships, rather than plot, and is well enough written that I wasn't bored at all.  There are cliches to be found, but with enough individual variation that they're not irritating.But when the wheels fall off, they fall off with a vengeance.  Villain who is more villainous than any human being has ever been?  Check!  Terrible problems that could be cleared up with a bare minimum of communication?  Check!  Heroine acting like an absolute fool due to desire to protect hero from nonexistent threat?  Check!  Hero acting like complete moron out of pride that makes no sense at all?  Check!  Oh, Ms. Blair - I loved the first part of the novel so much, I'm willing to purchase the next novel in the series, but if you betray your characters and your readers in a similar fashion in the next book, those will be the last pennies of mine you see.Read moreRead less
