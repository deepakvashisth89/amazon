Over forty years of reading fantasy novels, I find they are either: brilliant (very rare), good but flawed (lots of those), and drek. This new Greenwood book is good but flawed. It doesn't try to be anything more than  popcorn-and-beer reading, and gives the reader a whumping good ride. Lots  of cinematic scenes and blasting action but little touches of  characterization here and there that tell us these  people are real and we  should care about them. I really liked it, and will buy any sequels that  appear. Expect the Lord of the Rings, and you'll be disappointed. Expect a  really fun read, and this is it.Read moreRead less
