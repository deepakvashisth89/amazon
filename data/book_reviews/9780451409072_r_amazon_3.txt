I found the story of these grifters/cons/monsters interesting, and at  times I was outraged at the extreme audacity of the Kimes'.  However I was  not impressed by the way the book was written.  I thought the editing was  sloppy, as we are left hanging concerning some of the individuals, without  explanation.  Seems to me that when the book was proofed, someone should  have said "Whatever happened to so-and-so", but those things are  not explained, which leaves too many loose ends, unexplained relationships  and an overall disjointed read.  For pure entertainment alone though, it is  incredibly outrageous that some people will go to such lengths because they  covet material things.  After watching Sante Kimes on Larry King, you  almost believed her, but after reading the book, you see through the  smokescreen those two put up, time after time after time after time.
