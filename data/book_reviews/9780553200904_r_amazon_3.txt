I love this series! It is the true story of an amazing little boy who supports his family from age 11. It's warmth is much like the Little House on the Prairie series, but much meatier. It's filled with examples of ingenuity and follows this boy into manhood. He's smart, likeable, has great character and tells a great story. I wish I'd been aware of it when my children were young! We read the Little House series, and loved its lessons on character building.  Ralph's books not only have lessons you can teach children with but also have lessons on problem solving, getting along with difficult people, and how kindness and a good heart can help you get along well in life.
