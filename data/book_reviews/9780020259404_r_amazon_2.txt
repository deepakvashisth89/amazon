Arthur Upfield was a great writer who brought to life the many landscapes of Australia in his mystery novels featuring Detective Inspector Napoleon Bonaparte. "No Footprints in the Bush"--first published in 1940 as "The Bushranger of the Skies" was an outstanding effort even for him. As usual, he populates his landscape with vivid, memorable characters who draw us emotionally into their lives. Five stars and three cheers!Read moreRead less
