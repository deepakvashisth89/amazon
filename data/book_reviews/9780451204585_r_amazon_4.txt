Ripper's Revenge is an above average, interesting, tight and colorful, little mystery that occurs thirty years after the original Ripper killings.McCarver's pseudo-detective Prof. John Darnell, an expert in the supernatural is called to assist the investigation.  His wife Penny lends a hand.  The wily prostitutes of Whitechapel begin to see a 'ghostly' gentleman dressed in clothes of the previous generation...then...he simply vanishes into thin air.  (This is part of what Darnell is called into investigate.)  The first victim of this fiend naturally enough is horribly mutilated but here McCarver uses some imagination when more victims are discovered (these aren't spoilers!) a wonderful little plot tango to dance with his readers, (it's marvolous!)  I enjoyed the animated and varied characters and the author's writing skill for its intelligence and clarity.Quibble?  Perhaps not the most 'plausible' conclusion, but...original, a genuine twist in time and fate which kept me guessing and involved with the plot!It's a good one!Read moreRead less
