This book was very well written.  It had excellent details but not so many one gets lost or bored in reading them, the reader can honestly feel the emotional upheaval this woman went thru having her children taken from her.  The turmoil of collecting money to finance the 'kidnapping' the two children in a foreign country to bring them back to the United States where they belong.  The conclusion was very well expressed, the strain, the fear of being caught, the fear of having to return the children, the courage of the men who helped in the planning and carrying out of the rescue, and we should give medals to the two children for being so cooperative during the long,,dusty, dirty, scary trip to get back to safety of their mother's arms.
