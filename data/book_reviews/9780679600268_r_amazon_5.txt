The physical version of "The Complete Works of Jane Austen" that I have is the Deluxe Edition published in paperback by Penguin Classics.  It is a thick book with a very nice soft paperback cover which lays very softly in your hand or on your lap - a comfortable book to read even though it is thick.  The one book contains all of her novels and, printed in Times New Roman, it is easy to read.  I can't answer answer about any of the myriad other versions of Jane Austen's works on Amazon.
