I am enjoying the Inspector Rutledge books immensely , and this one was no exception.The characters and clever plotting are exceptional, with the glimpses into WWI times in the UK an added treat. What I found a bit off-putting about this  book was:1. Rutledge was on the go at a frenetic pace the whole book. He went to so many towns to see so many people and sites that it was difficult to keep up with him and who was where.2. Too many coincidences and all seemed to have a close personal connection to Rutledge.3. He identified the culprit much earlier than is typical in a whodunit and then spent the remainder of the book getting evidence to be able to make an arrest. I am not sure this is a negative, but did take me aback. I kept thinking I was being set up for some kind of double or triple twist at the very end.Still, this was a worthy read and look forward to the next book in the series.Read moreRead less
