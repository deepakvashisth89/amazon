This novel could be considered challenging for those unfamiliar with earlier 'Buffy' novels such as "Ghost Roads"; and most 'Buffy' novel readers will be unfamiliar with the flow (the random jumps from centuries to present times).  This non-young adult novel is rich with intricate character details and history.  The novel doesn't pander to casual readers and it doesn't forget its core fan base.  Highlights: Faith's well-rounded characterization (Why didn't Buffy offer Faith her house to stay in?), Willow's and Buffy's friendship, a non-annoying Cordelia, Saint Xander and that spunky warrior Buffy.
