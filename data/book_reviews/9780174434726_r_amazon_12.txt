Shakespeare has written such amazing plays that over time you come to expect more and more out of his works. After reading some of his plays, I could feel that this play was a bit over-stretched. Written in the late sixteenth century, King Richard III is a continuation play by William Shakespeare that talks about Richard's re-rise to power and throne. He has described him as a vicious person and shown the dark side of the person both as a ruler and a human being. Lady Anne's role is interesting and adds the spice to the play. It was really heart wrenching to see Lady Anne finally agreeing to marry Richard despite the proclamations by the king on the lady as how he would not commit to her for the long term and rather throw her away once she gets into the bond. This is where Shakespeare's mastery comes into picture and the play takes some amazing twists and turns. It's a funky ride after that which gets intense in the end. A decent play by Shakespeare.
