As a biography, it's less than historically accurate, but that's not the point.  Jackie Cochran was known for exaggerating and had a reputation for having her own P.R. spin and that's evident here, but it's also a wonderful view of who she was and who she thought she should be.  There are conflicts between this book and other sources, especially during her time in the ATA and the politics of the WASP program. She was clearly preoccupied with name dropping and sounding impressive.  For example, the fried chicken for generals story sounds glamorous, but she reportedly also made fried chicken in England with much less fanfare for some of the ATA pilots.If you're looking for information on the WASP program specifically, this probably isn't the place to look, but there is still value in this older book.Read moreRead less
