I finished Joe College last night. I enjoyed this funny book and could relate to Danny quite a bit - although I didn't go to Yale I went to college at about the same time as the people in the book so there was a narcissistic apsect to my enjoyment of this book.I see two themes in this book. One is that even though in Untied States we don't think of ourselves as stratified into classes we are, of course, stratified into classes. Danny is moving up. it seems. His dad is a lunch truck operator in New Jersey and he is going to Yale. He has two girlfriends (well three) who are all on the move as well. Cindy, I think, sees Danny as a way up and out, and ultimately chooses a different route. His other girlfriend, Polly, is also on the move so to speak.Ironically a couple of Danny's close friends at Yale come from much higher social class but strive to appear from a lower class, or to be heading toward a lower class; but we assume they will outgrow that concept!The second theme of the book, it appears, is that Danny is pretty lucky. He seems to get himself into and out of a few tight situations without much consequence!Read moreRead less
