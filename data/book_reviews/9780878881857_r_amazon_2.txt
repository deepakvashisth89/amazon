Tiger Eyes is one of those books that stays with you long after you finish it. Judy Blume captures the raw emotions of grief, identity, and growing up with such honesty and care. Davey’s journey through loss and healing felt incredibly real, and I appreciated how the story didn’t rush her process. The setting in Los Alamos adds a unique backdrop, and the characters—especially Wolf—bring warmth and depth. It’s a quiet, powerful read that speaks to anyone who’s ever felt lost and needed to find their own strength. Highly recommend for teens and adults alike.Read moreRead less
