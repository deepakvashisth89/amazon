Feng Shui is new to me. I have studied most everything spiritual, but never thought about Feng Shui. We've been 3 years in our home now and 4 years in this neighborhood and I've never been so unhappy in my life. I decided to research "vibes" and "energy" and a number of FS books started to come up. I bought two others, but Feng Shui, Tips for a Better Life resonated with me. The language is plain and very easy to understand. The book speaks to the lay person. I really like the book and how it is organized by the type of need I may have (for example, business and career, love and romance, etc).I really do believe that energy (negative or positive) exists in our lives and can have a positive or negative effect on our lives. I enjoyed reading about Chi (or energy) and how it flows through our body and our home and basically our entire lives. It is all around us. I have learned that my neighbor is the one giving off the negative energy that has caused a lot of people on this block problems. Feng Shui teaches what we can do to ward off this type of negative energy.What I really, really love though is that the book has given me a number of ideas about what to look for in our next home from having a red front door to what color to paint our rooms to affect the energy and/or the mood.We will be buying a new home next year and I will be sure to read this book again before we start looking.Read moreRead less
