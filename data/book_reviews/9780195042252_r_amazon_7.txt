Very interesting play about Benedetta Carlini and life in the Medieval age at that time. The women portrayed in the play had the misfortune of living when corruption in the Catholic Church and the mysoginistic culture of the time resulted in their own ill-treatment and lack of options in life. The relationship between Benedetta and Bartolomea is intriguing and quite alluring! I recommend reading the play, Vile Affections, if this book of interest!Read moreRead less
