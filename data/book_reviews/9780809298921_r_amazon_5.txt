This is a brilliant account of the history of attempts on Everest and the mystery of Mallory both as a mountaineer and as a man.  Firstbrook writes in a clear no nonsense style that had me gripped from beginning to end.  This is the only account where I felt that I was able to gain some  understanding of the psychological motivation behind this great British  mountaineer.
