It took me a while to get into reading this book, but once I did, I found it to be just as great as my friends said it was. Carol really caught the essence of the part of Cape Cod she was writing about. I have lived here since 1992 and many of the things she wrote about made me laugh and sometimes sigh, as I saw the dual nature of our area. We have people coming just for the summer, but those of us that live here year-round know it can be a struggle if you're between jobs or you husband is unemployed.
