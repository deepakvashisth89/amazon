Trixie and her friends are visiting her cousins in Idaho, enjoying camping in some of the wilderness the state has.  Their first night, however, there is an awful smell and sound, and Trixie’s cousins Knut and Cap are exchanging looks.  Early the next morning, Trixie wakes to find a Sasquatch outside their camp.  Is it really there?  And what about the rest of the strange things happening around camp?Since this is one of only two Trixie books set in states I had been to when I first read them as a kid, I wanted to like this book.  Really, I did.  However, the story just doesn’t quite hold together.  It starts out pretty slowly, and then when things do get more suspenseful, it feels almost out of control.  The resolution is quite good, however, with plenty of great clues to help Trixie along the way.  Meanwhile, the characters are mostly good although Di gets short shafted there, coming across as far more whiny and fearful than she usually is.All in all, this is not a bad book, but it’s not the best the series has to offer either.
