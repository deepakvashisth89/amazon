This another great Roger Angell book. In the last year I have gone back and re-read his four books that cover the seasons from 1962 to 1987. I read them many years ago and enjoyed them then. They have aged well as I am sure I enjoyed them even more now. Angell IMO is easily the best baseball writer ever to pen a book. His prose is sensational. Anyone who enjoys baseball, especially if they were a fan in those years I mentioned, will love this book. This book covers the 1977 to 1981 seasons. All of the stories are told eloquently. You just feel his love of the game and his growing disillusion with the high salaries and the general money take over of the sport which resulted in a mid season strike in 1981. But he off sets that with his Web of the Game chapter that is just brilliant!This book for some reason is not offered for Kindle. You can only get it in hard or paperback. The other three books in this series are offered through Kindle. So I am not sure why this isn't?
