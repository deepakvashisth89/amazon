Great second part to this trilogy.  It's more of a character study than a great adventure story. Especially Scotty or shall I say Peter Raeder is a dynamic personality that deals with the inequalities and incompetence of the military star fleet.  Far from the Star Trek mold there are good officers and bad.  It's far more realistic and true to the way human beings actually interact to stress in combat situations. The space pirates in this trilogy are broadly drawn and not too in depth. However it doesn't take away from the sheer fun of this novel.  I've always wanted to see the Scotty character in an environment other than our favorite Treks, and this book delivers .  It's surprising to me that this series didn't get more notoriety.  The action moves at  a fast pace and you don't have to  endure any gratuitous scenes of inappropriate violence.  It's the kind of book that you can loan to a fan of Scotty and it takes you to an alternate universe where he functions independently, out from under the shadow of Captain Kirk.Read moreRead less
