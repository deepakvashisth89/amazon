this is a very good fictional writing of the modoc war.  its is historical and very accurate of the events that took place...the main characters in the book are fictional but the real people are intertwined with the fictional people. I am of modoc decent and many stories have been passed down to the younger generation. so mr Johnston did a excellent writing job in this book. One thing that wasn't totally accurate , was the portrayal of the soldiers in a bad light. They were facing one of fierces clan of native American of the era..it was said in a news paper  of that time ,only modocs kill like that..  I highly recommend this bookRead moreRead less
