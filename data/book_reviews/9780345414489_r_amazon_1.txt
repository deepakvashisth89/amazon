When his former girlfriend, Dr. Marsha Helms, calls Nashville PI Harry Denton and asks him to see her as she’s about to enter her ninth month of pregnancy, he drives to Reno. Harry wants to be there for his baby. On the desert drive, he is accosted by two lowlifes and nearly killed. Nevada law enforcement official Dell Kanon comes to Harry’s rescue.In Reno, a lawyer talks Harry into taking an undercover role as an employee at the notorious Mustang Ranch cathouse. The lawyer thinks the ranch plays a significant role in money laundering. When one of the girls is murdered, Harry is framed, and his only way out is to work for the Feds, who he doesn’t trust.Steve Womack’s prose is unique, and he knows how to tell a great story.I highly recommend Dirty Money.Read moreRead less
