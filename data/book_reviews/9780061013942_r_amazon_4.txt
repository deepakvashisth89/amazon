Laura possesses real skill with computers, and the descriptions of her hacking are fascinating.  Her complex past, along with her search for her husband and daughter, help the reader understand her more bizarre behaviors.  I admired the way she was able to rein in her dependence on Ritalin--it was difficult to read about her continual pill-popping and the effects that the meds had on her.
