If you are struggling to learn Latin (one of the world's great languages), this could be a way to get in some pleasure reading while learning at the same time. Magus Mirabilis is the Wizard of Oz translated into Latin by W. W. Denslow. Who hasn't seen the movie? The very familiarity of the material makes this an easy read, as compared to Virgil for example.
