Batavia's Graveyard – Nightmare IslandThe scope of this book is breathtaking. The facts, the accounts, the research behind it make this one of the  most intense reads I have ever had to put myself through: I say this with the greatest respect for the writing. It is quite possible one of the most horrific episodes in human sea-history. The fates of those this book recounts are beyond imagination, and the conclusions are frightening.In all, I have not said much about the content of the book, simply because the horrific nature of the journey and the death of so many at the hands of a madman are just too much for a modest reader like myself to put into words. Mr Dash has done it for the reader, and if I felt my stomach churn reading this account of the shipwreck, it was instructive in many ways. I had to put it down for a while, before resuming the narrative on two occasions.Mr Dash's writing reads easily, clearly, intelligently, and gives some very rich contextual indications about the Dutch East Indies, Dutch Society in the 17th century, about the way people could hope to live and die in those days. In this sense, it is more than a sea-story. Much more.Mr Dash has totally impressed me.Read moreRead less
