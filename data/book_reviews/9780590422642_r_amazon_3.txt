This book was one of the first  I picked up in grade school. I read from cover to cover so many times I lost count and was one of the books responsible for a life long love of reading. It is a fun adventure story about a young man stranded on  Iceberg whose only companion is an orphaned Polar Bear cub. The story is told in a very strait forward manner as you would expect for a book written for young readers. I would describe it as a Young Adult Life of Pi, which it pre-dated by 40 something years.
