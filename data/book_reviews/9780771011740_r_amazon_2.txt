I was living in Iran at the time Dr. Beny's book was published.  I had the distinct privilege of meeting Dr. Beny. He presented a copy of this book, signed, to my husband and myself.This book really brings to you the beauty that is Iran and its people.We had, in our Iran office, about 15 copies of Dr. Beny's book to take to our Los Angeles office.  However, when the revolution began, and the Shah fled the country, the Revolutionary Guard went into our office and confiscated all of these books and burned them!
