The Invitation is a thought-provoking Christian novel. A few individuals receive unusual invitations to a town called Haven. Some of the individuals meet before arriving in Haven. The varied group includes a mother with her 2 young children in tow, a television newsman, a former politician, a student, and a homeless man. As they experience what Haven and its residents have in store, these individuals  (and the reader) learn not to judge by outward appearance or current lot in life. Unusual alliances are formed and soon lives are transformed. Pull up a chair and watch as eyes and hearts are opened, minds are changed, lives are lost and lives are saved.Read moreRead less
