The book is considered, by quite a few people, to be a corner stone of steam punk literature. K.W. Jeter coined the term, so this in itself makes it an almost "must read" for the genre.  I beg to differ among it being a must read.  A book that is not enjoyable, dragging and extremely boring at times will overcome all the other things it has contributed to a genre.  I was not a fan, perhaps if you are a steam punk aficionado you will beg to differ or be interested in it.
