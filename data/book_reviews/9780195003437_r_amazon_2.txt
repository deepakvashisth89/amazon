A hard read unless you are well versed in that specific poetic literature--which I am not. But I bought it because of the psychological, sociological, historical and relational import that it contains. If there is any one book I've ever read, seen, or heard of that can put our modern day take on romantic relationships in perspective, this is the book. That isn't Lewis' primary goal, but it sure is an available takeaway for anyone that wants to meditate and examine it. After reading the book and highlighting the heck out of it, it became quite clear how insidiously our views of romance have been socially and psychologically "engineered" over 100's of years via the influence of the poetic and other arts. But, I never hear or have heard of a historic analytic or a councilor articulating the serious importance of realizing what kind of "product" we are because of it; how we might therefore judiciously re-access what we want to do about it personally and inter-personally.Read moreRead less
