Purim Play is a book about kids who put on a play to celebrate the life and important works of Queen Esther. It is a book geared a little more toward those who know about or have actually taken part in this type of celebration, although its story and lessons could apply to anyone.If you do not know much about Purim, you are not alone. Purim is a Jewish celebration that honors Queen Esther and it is well- known in certain religious circles. The characters in this story are putting on a play and some of the cast members are sick. The kids are placed in a difficult situation, but they make the best of it and complete the play, so everyone is happy in the end.One thing that sets this book apart is the illustrations. The colorful, artistic images are a little more complex than what you find in most children's books and for this reason, adults may find themselves gazing at the pages even more frequently than the children for whom the book is targeted. The images, and the story itself to an extent, are a little advanced for a kids book and this is why the book is really best for children who are a little older, say seven and up.A book like this will make the most sense to those familiar with this type of event, but there are still many valuable lessons to learn from Purim Play. It teaches some good lessons and best of all, it exposes kids to traditions and cultures for which they likely know little about, but hopefully want to learn more about after reading. This, and the interesting illustrations, is enough to earn this book an easy recommendation.Read moreRead less
