As Choose-Your-Own-Adventure books go, this one is great. The idea of "hyperspace" gives the author an excuse to write things that couldn't happen in any other CYOA book. You meet characters who know they're imaginary, you get told you're only a dream in someone's head, you meet an alternate universe version of yourself and you find yourself reading a Choose-Your-Own-Adventure book...WITHIN the Choose-Your-Own-Adventure book. The writing is not too hard and always fun to read. If you know a kid who likes CYOA books, and you think they can handle their mind being stretched a bit, this is a great choice.Read moreRead less
