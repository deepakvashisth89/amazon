The story covers many years beginning in the Summer of 1872 with Lance Harmon and what shaped him as a child, then as a teenager, and into adulthood. From the slums of New York City, later on an orphan train, to being the ranch manager at The Rocking M in Wyoming.Sonnie Mancuso is the sixth daughter and last child of Vince and Barbara Mancuso. While she is more tomboy than her sisters, her father does the unthinkable, he sends her away to Boston to live with her Aunt Josephine. She will receive schooling, live amongst society, and travel to Europe. While her father thinks he has molded her into a fine lady, he is unaware she has been taking courses in horticulture and veterinary classes and learned about animal husbandry.The story is mystery, suspense, life and death danger, and surprises. While in Europe, a telegram will reach Aunt Josephine and Sonnie that Vince has taken ill and she needs to return to Wyoming. She intends to make it permanent, but that isn’t what Vince has in mind—but time will tell. Issues are happening on Mancuso land with squatters, cattle theft, and even the poisoning of animals. The larger cattle ranches are in a range war with those who steal—and it seems the courts aren’t any help. If anything, Sonnie coming home now is dangerous to her—and that is seen firsthand. Bringing in a hired gun, Tom Horn will take care of issues the courts won’t.I liked the characters and their interactions. The chemistry between Lance and Sonnie is definitely there, but both try and avoid it. The trouble between a squatter and his cohort places the Rocking M on alert, especially when it seems they are not only targeting the land and cattle, but they have their sites on Sonnie.The story has the good, the bad, and the ugly of people. Secrets will be revealed and lead to hurt. Life and death moments will have people willing to risk their own life to save another.
