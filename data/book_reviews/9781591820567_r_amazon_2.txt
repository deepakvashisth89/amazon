I came across this series by accident.  I was bored and was going through an on-line site were you can read mangas for free.  However, I couldn't stand the fact that some of the words were obscured since each pages were scanned.  So I decided to go through Amazon.com and buy each copy.  I first checked the internet to ensure that all of the volumes were out in print since this manga was from Tokyopop.  For those who don't know, be careful when you buy anything from Tokyopop, a lot of their mangas were not completed since they closed their English translation side of their publication. Which frustrated me to no end when I started to buy a series published by them, only to find out that the rest of the series were never published. Some of the unfinished work include but are not limited to these to example: Shinobi Life and Maid-Sama. Thankfully, Tokyopop was able to complete MARS prior to closing.What I love about this story is the simplicity of the art work.  I feel that the author did this so that the focus is in the story.  And believe me, the story is excellent.  Each volumes will draw you in the story and make you fall in love with Rei.  With Kira, I had a love hate relationship with her character but as you get further into the series you will be able to understand her much better and you'll realize that she's one though cookie. What I also like is the fact that the mangaka focuses on Rei's trauma first.  Then it takes us through Kira's trauma and then she blends the two together to show how TRUE LOVE can conquer all.The one thing that I noticed when I picked up volume 5, is the necklace that Rei is wearing on the cover.  I was shocked to see that the necklace that he was wearing was exactly the same one that I had bought for $1 at a flea market years ago prior to me ever knowing about mangas.
