I first picked this book up off of a donation shelf while deployed to Central America on a humanitarian mission. At first I was just bored and just by fate it was this beauty! I am actually looking for it for Kindle, but it hasn't made it yet. The idea of this Red Headed demon is fascinating alone, but the story will bring you the rest of the way and you won't want to put it down. There's only about 250 pages, so it's an easy read if your not an avid reader (like me). Pick this one up and you won't be disappointed!Read moreRead less
