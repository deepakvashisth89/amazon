This is the second book in Mark Berent's excellent series on Vietnam. For those who were there in the mid 1960's it brings back a lot of memoiies and is right on the money describing the conditions in Nam at the time as well as the frustration of the pilots who placed their lives on the line everyday under the absolutely disgraceful rules of engagement of LBJ and McNamara. I am re-reading this book as I previously read it when it was first published. This is a must read for anyone who was in Nam and should be an eye-opener for those who are to young to know anything about it. Be sure and read the whole series.Read moreRead less
