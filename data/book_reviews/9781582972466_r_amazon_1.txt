This is one of those books that tells you what you already know. So why buy it? Because, if you are like me, when you are deep into a story, you get so engrossed in the process of writing that you forget all the myriad details you intended to say about someone. Edelstein does a good job of providing prompts of the traits that make up just about everybody. Need a psychopath for your thriller? She has that covered. Need a nervous wallflower on her first date? Edelstein can help you flesh out all of her traits.I also use her descriptions to make sure my characters are consistent. If my nervous wallflower suddenly becomes the belle of the ball, I want to know why. Edelstein's work serves as a checklist of what to expect, and what can change.Read moreRead less
