Lisa, Carole, and Stevie have to go to England to compete in a horse show. The competetion is taking place at an OLD CASTLE. There is a legand that says the man that used to live in and own the castle had a horse that was wild. One day the barn burned and the horse perished in it. The man buried his treasure where the horse stall used to be. About a year later he was found dead holding one fire opal. It is said that the rider with fire in his heart will get his treasure.  I loved this book because it has adventure, danger and it is a mystery. I also love horses.
