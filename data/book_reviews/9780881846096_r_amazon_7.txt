My memory of this book, read around 1980, was that it was very good, enthralling even, like a true crime story. Unique. And, for the times, it was (notice the killer was described as a "mass killer" rather than serial killer).  But on my second read (2022), I was thinking that all the side stories of the other major characters were going to tie in and go somewhere because so much time was spent on them. (I'm not including reporter Kenton in this observation.) Which was especially frustrating considering what was revealed in the very ending. So, although I did finish it I was pretty disappointed that it didn't live up to my memory (of a much younger reader).Read moreRead less
