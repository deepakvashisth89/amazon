Charlotte Beauchamp is not an animal person. So when her beloved Nana dies, and Charlotte inherits Nana's potbellied pig Toto, Charlotte quickly becomes desperate enough to ask for help from animal behaviorist Tex Brubaker, despite the fact that there is no love lost between the two at their first meeting.But can Tex really fall for a woman who can resist the antics of the adorable Toto? And will Charlotte survive all her ill-fated attempts to become gainfully employed, after having spent ten years caring for her Nana?Actually, it's the pig who turns out to be the real hero here.
