Riveting powerful, frustrating, heartbreaking all rolled up into one true nightmare story. I went to school with the babysitter, remember that scary summer day, terrified until the killer was arrested (even though I lived in the next town over), and reading the news accounts in next few years that followed. I also knew a few of the legal folks from the case. The author is true to form with the personalities of the characters, things that happened, description of THAT day. Oh I remember the weather that day very well 30+ years later. Mr. Bosworth's writing nails the details very well.
