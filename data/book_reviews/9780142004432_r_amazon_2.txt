Lately I am reading books about women, who sink into such depths it takes longer and longer for them to rise out of them.Lucinda is smart, funny and possesses distant knowledge of what's important, but she seems to lack backbone.My husband hates slapstick, because unlike the majority of us who are forgiving to human foibles, he is not. He does not think being clumsy or messing up is funny.The book flows. It is intelligent, and though all ends well, like my husband, I find that the good ending is too short for the long, frustrating journey of Lucinda.I have been very judgmental of her inability to stand up to anyone or anything.
