This is the third novel of a trilogy. You need to read the other two first or this one won't make sense.As in the second (The Reprieve), you are in the minds of the characters. It is the summer of 1940. Germany has just quickly and decisively defeated France. French soldiers (mostly draftees) don't know if or when they will be sent home. Is the war over? Is this peace?
