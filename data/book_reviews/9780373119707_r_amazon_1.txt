BACK COVER:  The price of deceit...It has been twelve years since they met--Felicia had been only a child--no wonder Joshua Tagget doesn't recognize her. But Felicia remembers him and how he betrayed her stepsister. And now, by making his attraction to Felicia more than clear, Joshua has unwittingly given her the means to pay him back in kind for his deceit....But Felicia isn't immune to Joshua's charms, and she could find it's herself she's deceiving if she things she can walk away from him with her heart intact....MY REVIEW:Felicia is in China getting ready to go on a tour when she meets ups w/ Joshua Tagget.  She knew him from 12 years ago and he was seeing her married sister on the side.  Joshua doesn't know Felicia is "Lissa" the little 13 year old he knew years ago, but he feels that they have met before.  Felicia takes advantage of the situation to seek revenge for what she believed that Joshua drove her stepsister to do.Different spin on the revenge plot because usually it is the H seeking to punish the h for something she did or her father, her brother, her sister, perhaps even her best friend's brother's wife's third cousin twice removed!I have to say I also fluctuated in my like/dislike for Felicia in this story as well.  She got to know Joshua and he didn't seem like the cold, ruthless guy she though he was.  Still Felicia sought to embarrass him as part of her punishment for what she believed he did to her "angel" of a stepsister.  Joshua was a good guy and Felicia didn't have all the facts at the age of 13, but she soon learns some things and she gives in to her feelings - of course you knew she would.  I didn't like the way she treated him at times, but I just slotted her role into the one that is usually occupied by vindictive Hs and it worked for me.  She does come around in the end and she gets explanations and opens up to seeing things the way they truly were.  Because apparently there were a lot of things that were hidden away from Felicia in order to set stepsister up to being so great!  Although even though Felicia's stepsister explained things about her marriage and why she went outside of it, one wonders how Felicia could still think her sister doing that was okay.  Let alone the fact that dear "angel" used a 13 year old to facilitate that affair.  Overall, it was a good book.Read moreRead less
