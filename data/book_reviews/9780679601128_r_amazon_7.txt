Shelby Foote is one of the great writers about the Civil War.  He was featured in Ken Burns' movie The Civil War.  This situation made his books instant best-sellers.This book is a segment of his trilogy-it is specifically about the Battle of Gettysburg.  It is well written.  Shelby Foot discusses in detail J.E.B. Stuart's Great Cavalry Dash-the controversial move of the Confederate Cavalry where they capture a large wagon load of Union Supplies only to leave Robert E.
