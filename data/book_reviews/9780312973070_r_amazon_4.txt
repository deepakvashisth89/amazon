I found this novel to be pleasant to read - a good way to spend a chilly January Sunday.  But first, the author could use a proof reader - there are several missing words.  Second, she needs to bone up a bit more on historical settings - for example, how do two sisters, with no livestock of their own, a weekly grocery order with no refridgeration, and limited funds manage to have fresh meat to eat every day?
