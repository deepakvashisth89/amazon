Written as if Brandstetter were aging and near retirement, this story continues to use characters from past Hansen novels. In Early Graves, Hansen focuses squarely on gay men confronting AIDS, societal responses and his protagonist’s personal connection to it all. The book gives more attention to Brandstetter’s personal history and highlights his connections with characters who were introduced previously.
