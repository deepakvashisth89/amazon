With a successful business and a kind boyfriend, Janna has almost everything she wants, but she still feels that something's missing. She becomes friends with Alex and Philip, a young gay couple who soon end up living in her huge house. When she stumbles upon them having sex, she begins an awakening which changes the course of everyone's lives. Insightful and sensuous, "Trio Sonata" is a beautifully written novel that unfortunately has a glaring flaw. Most of the novel is told in the third person point of view, except in the several flashbacks into early experiences by Janna, Philip, and Alex, which are all told in the first person point of view. This creates confusion in the reader and almost derails a wonderful story.
