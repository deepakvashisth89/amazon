This 4th book in the delightful and funny Miss Seeton (”MissEss") series reads like the old "What's up, Doc?" movie that was directed by Blake Edwards and starred Barbra Streisand and Ryan Reynolds. Or, if you prefer, it reads like a "Pink Panther" movie script.It's quirky and fun, filledwith some new characters as Miss Seeton makes her first trip abroad for Scotland Yard, nabbing criminals in Italy, Switzerland and France, while hob nobbing with the financial elite of the Continent.A word of caution: while "light" like a usual cozy mystery, there is a page of darkness near the end that I had to skip, but the cozy fare quickly returned for the finale.Read moreRead less
