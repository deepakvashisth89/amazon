I bought this book years and years ago and it changed my life.  Since then I keep buying again because I lend out or gift a copy to someone who wants to read it also.  One of the five covered "baggage" characteristics is "you cherish your rage."  That simple statement opens up a personal examination to how we carry lifelong anger over some perceived injustice long past its relevance or reality.  Another well made point is that every good characteristic we merit has a flip side, a down side when pushed to the extreme.
