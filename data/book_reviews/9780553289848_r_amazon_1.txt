I read Short Blade in my early teens when I entered puberty at 11  when I found it in the children’s fiction department at my public library.Ironically I was not allowed to borrow a book on Men’s Sexual Health..Your work was an eye opening experience & addressed so many political issues & discussed sexuality in an inclusive troupe, discussing gender differences & attraction. It also discussed the fluidity of sexuality, body dysmorphia & transition.The only other series I found that did this was Dune.In the 1980’s your work provided a sense of freedom & exploration to a small town boy who could not access appropriate literature for his growing mind.I also realised reading Short Blade that I was a Trans individual, I felt validated knowing someone out there has similar notions or at the least possessed an academic mind that was curious, that was willing to discuss & share such notions.To quote you “The body is not me, I am a moveable thing.”Thank you for this & so much more.Picking up that paperback with Sentiva Alvedine on the cover in a yellow jump suit changed my life, for this small thing thank you Peter.On a side note I was unable to ever gain a copy of Level’s until now my 43rd year 22 years later.Read moreRead less
