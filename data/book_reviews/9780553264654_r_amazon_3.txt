Harlie is a computer who has become self-aware.  This is a story of development, learning, and becoming human.  It really gets you to identify with Harlie.  It looks at humans and computers from a different perspective.I loved this story.  While the story is complete in itself, I was very happy to see Harlie show up again in later stories, such as the Dingilian series where he plays a major role.  Read Jumping Off the Planet, Bouncing Off the Moon, Leaping to the Stars, and Hella.  There is another book 8n the series coming out.
