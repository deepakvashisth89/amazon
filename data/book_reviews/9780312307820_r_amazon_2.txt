This is a story about a woman following her heart ... or not. It is about the limits set upon us by our upbringing, or by ourselves, and the dilemma of whether to `blow it all sky high' for love .. or not.Well-written, witty, well-paced, but I thought the was ending a bit abrupt and weak compared to the rest of the book.Though maybe not all that likeable in her own right, I found myself feeling very sympathetic towards the heroine, Dilys, and I feel that every reader will find there's a part of Dilys they'll be able to relate to.Looking forward to reading more from Mavis Cheek.Read moreRead less
