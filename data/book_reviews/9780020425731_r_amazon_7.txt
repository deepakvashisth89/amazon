This book is not one of my favorites. The writing was stilted and although the characters were believable, they were almost transparent. You could almost foresee what the characters were going to do, next. Yes, the first book is usually a world builder and slower than the rest, but this one just took forever. Perhaps it is not the writer at all and it's me just being sick of dystopian stories. Take all of this review with a grain of salt.Read moreRead less
