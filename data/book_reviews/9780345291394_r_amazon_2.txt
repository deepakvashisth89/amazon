I bought this book when I was a kid because I liked the cover. I never finished it. Decades later, I understand why.Unlike a lot of other science fiction writers, Simak isn't especially concerned with plot or even character--at least not here. Yes, there are both in Project Pope, but the book is more about religion, spirituality, and our need to believe in something bigger than ourselves.So, while there's some action here, there are also plenty of big, abstract ideas to take in. Admittedly, it's not a perfect novel, and frankly, it drags at times, but if you're looking for a break from the tried-and-true space opera, it's well worth a read.Read moreRead less
