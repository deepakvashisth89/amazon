Do any of you remember a time in grade school when you felt like you didn't quite fit in, and the cool group seemed inaccessible?  Times haven't changed in that respect and this book takes Alex through some real escapades as she learns how to cope with "cool kids" being rude to her.  Kids will relate and learn valuable life lessons at the same time.  My daughter had tears streaming down her face in parts because she was laughing so hard.
