Michael Newton‘s first book—Journey of Souls—charted the progress of a soul dying and being reborn. In this book, more information from his case studies is taken more deeply to present a more purposeful definition of the human universe by focusing on certain essentials. The prose is very nice. This book is also challenging because Michael Newton describes reincarnation better than almost anybody. And the universe is inscrutable. (It is very big.) When Michael Newton started talking about changing guides every 500 years, I felt like a speck of dust.
