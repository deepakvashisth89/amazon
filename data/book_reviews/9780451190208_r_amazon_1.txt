I missed buying this book when it first came out. I heard it discussed and wanted to get it but somehow didn't. This story is hero-centered, and the hero involved is a man who is "different", not what you'd expect of a Regency. aristocratic hero. But he's a hero in more ways than one, and deep to his core, a man of honor. A cavalier at heart, in a century when there are no longer cavaliers.
