The sublime artwork of this book is what first attracted me, but the wonderful story is what I will hold in my heart. In verse, this amazing writer introduces us to Caleb, an an elderly lighthouse keeper, and then he begins to reflect upon his past. It is a story that warms the heart, and I wish that it would have been a novel!
