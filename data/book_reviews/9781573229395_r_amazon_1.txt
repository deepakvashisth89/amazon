A very good post-holocaust story.  While I have the utmost respect for holocaust survivors and all of the compassion I know how to have for the victims and their families, I feel like I've heard that story.  I will never forget it; it's imperative that we as a civilization do not, but now I mostly want to hear a story I haven't heard before.  War Story is just such a story.
