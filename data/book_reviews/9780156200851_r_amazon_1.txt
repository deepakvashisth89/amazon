Yes, like all other readers, I turned immediately to “The Man in the Brooks Brothers Shirt” to see if its comic underpinnings held up over the years. It proved even better than my memory. Or, perhaps, it had stayed the same while I had changed into a better reader. Masterful in every way but especially, for me, is Mary McCarthy’s command of plot with all its revelatory details and the wonderful hinge of that plot’s  famous quote from Chaucer: “I am myn owne woman wel at ese.” To which The Man in the Brooks Brothers Shirt replies, “Golly, you are at that!” Whether the other five episodes hold up as well time, as always, will tell.Read moreRead less
