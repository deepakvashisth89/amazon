Written by the late, but definitely great, Paul Zindel, Loch is a prime example of teen fiction at its very best! Engaging and engrossing from its very first moments, you're pulled in and kept in place 'till it ends. The characters are extremely likeable, so don't expect any of that wretched emo moping that's saturated most modern fiction. Rather will you, or your child, be treated to a thoroughly enjoyable science fiction/horror experience. I don't think I need to delve into the story in too much detail as I wouldn't really want to spoil it for anyone, but the basic plot involves our teenaged protagonist Loch and his sister accompanying their father on an expedition to Lake Alban where a team intends to hunt down a Nessie-like cryptid. The expedition meets some bumps early on when a hotshot cameraman meets with an "unfortunate accident". If you're wondering; then, yes, there is a little gore dotted here and there through the story, but it doesn't go into TOO MUCH detail; kinda like Jurassic Park. Talking about the audiobook in particular now; the narrator does a damned fine job of it. He doesn't come off as forced, dull, whiny or even over the top. Mr. Guidall's voice is the perfect choice, fitting neatly into every single sound. Whether he's putting on a voice or just simply narrating, I consider him perfect for the role.All in all, I'd say it's definitely worth it!
