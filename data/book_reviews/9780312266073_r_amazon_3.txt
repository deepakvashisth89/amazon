Beautifully written. The author focuses more on Jackie's mother, Janet, who lived a life more removed from the limelight. What I really enjoyed about the book is the author's ability to illustrate the humanity of Jackie's mother. Her love of family, her desire for security, her love of nature and animals, the importance of art, and even her imperfections, weave a very human story that many wives, mothers and grandmothers will wholely understand.Read moreRead less
