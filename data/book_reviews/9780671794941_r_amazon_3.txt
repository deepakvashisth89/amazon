I recently acquired this book that was published in 1993,30 years after the Assassination of JFK.The author is Bob Callahan,a former speechwriter for Senator Robert F. Kennedy.One of the most impressive things about this book is the striking illustration on the cover.The book has many illustrations by Mark Zingarelli,one of America's great masters of"noir" graphics.The graphics in the book really set this book aside from the plethora of other books on the JFK Assassination.Also,the size of this book ,at only 160 pages,is one of the smaller JFK books; but,don't let to a conclusion about the many conspiracies that have been offered over the 30 years.It doesn't do that;but instead is a summary of the most well known theories,assumptions,official explanations,or whatever have been offered .Overall,each of the major conspiracy theories as well as landmark books and investigations are covered briefly.There is also a very detailed Index ,particularly of the names of the numerous people whose names have appeared or involved.The book is presented in 38 chapters and under SOURCES,there is very good bibliographical information about the source of the information in each chapter.This is a very different type of JFK book,and would be very helpful for any person who has read few or many books about who killed JFK and would be a valuable reference..I only have one criticism about the book,and that is;It is now 23 years since it was published,and many more books and theories have been brought forward since then,therefore an updating and expansion is really needed.Nonetheless,this is a very valuable resource and belongs on the shelf of  anyone who wants to know who may have been involved with the JFK Assassination.For instance,I am just starting to read "Reasonable Doubt" by Henry Hurt,publihed in 1985.It appears to be a good book,but not merntioned .As it stands today,36 years after JFK was shot,,a conspiracy seems more likely as time goes on.Read moreRead less
