Having read several of Margaret Frazer's later Sister Frevisse mysteries It was  interesting to go back to the beginning.  As usual the plot was well crafted and well written.  The early characters were quite different to what they have become in subsequent novels.  The story line does keep you engaged from the beginning and it is certainly worth reading for that as well as giving a basis for the development of major characters and introduction of new favourites as the series progresses.Read moreRead less
