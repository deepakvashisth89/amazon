I read and loved The Big Sky. I was very excited to read this and see what had happened with those characters. Overall, I liked this book. However, I don't feel it is as strong as the first. Which is fine, its a hard one to follow. Some of the characters feel a little underdeveloped. The danger just doesn't feel as on edge here. As a born and raised Oregonian I love the imagery, and descriptions of the trail. I think I had my expectations in the wrong area. I was expecting a adrenaline packed sequel similar to the first, and this just isn't that. It's more of an introspective book. I will still read the third book, and do recommend this, it's just not what I had expected. As the days go on, I still wonder about certain characters and the choices they made.
