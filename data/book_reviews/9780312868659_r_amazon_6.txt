As someone with career long interest in both human consciousness and AI consciousness I have been going through every single one of Robert Sawyer's novels. I have greatly enjoyed every single one and recommend them all. I have several more to go and am reading more slowly because I don't want to finish. Golden Fleece was his first novel and even it is very impressive. Sawyer's knowledge is vast and his writing skill keeps you glued to the pages, unless your brain gets a little tired thinking.
