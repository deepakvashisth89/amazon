Spandau's dairy by Albert Speer is a soul searching, tormenting battle within for survival.The diary explores Speer's days as Hitler's friend, his days as Minister of Ammunition and Armement, but, especially, his day to day life inside Spandau prison.His relations with guards, fellow inmates, his fears and sense of guilt, the experiments he pursued inside Spandau in the hope of passing time and retain his satiny are to say the least astonishing.I was deeply mooved by Speer's story, and I strongly recommend anyone interested in the 3rd Reich, WW2 and human interactions to read this wondeful book.
