I plowed through Bone Hunter in just a few days--uncommon for me.  I made time to get to the end because I was so entirely fascinated by the story line, the characters, and the tidbits I was picking up about geology and  paleontology. Andrews just seems to get better and better and her Em Hansen  more and more interesting as a character. The supporting cast is excellent  in this book and they are people you like and want to know about. I highly  recommend it for an interesting read that assumes you know a little about a  lot of things. Andrews never talks down to her readers but imparts lots of  information through her narrative.
