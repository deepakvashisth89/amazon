I love short story collections and I'm a huge fan of Mike Resnick, so when I saw that he edited this collection is was pretty much an "instabuy" for me.  I was not disappointed.This is (by today's standards) a fairly large collection of 18 stories that explores the solving and detection of "future crimes".  Each story is set up with a premise provided by Resnick to the author who had to then write a story along those lines.  Sometimes there's  MacGuffin in the form of a Nifty New Technology but usually the stories focus on detectives that solve things by intuition, hunches, and lots of walking around.  The quality ranges a bit but all of the stories show Resnick's touch as an editor; I was particularly taken with "Murder On-Line" and "An Incident at the Circus".  All of the stories are clever and well thought through, however, and I enjoyed the entire collection.Highly recommended for anybody who appreciates good science fiction, clever detective stories, and/or who just plains likes stuff with the Mike Resnick touch.Read moreRead less
