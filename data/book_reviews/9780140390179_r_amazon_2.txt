Overall, this book presents itself forcefully on to the reader. Descriptive heavy in some parts but that goes back to one of the themes in the book: human nature. The plot leads us into many different directions and at different speeds. The reading level/vocabulary is not to difficult so this book can be read from early teens above. Some obvious themes are greed (very prevalent) and nature vs. nuture (men as animals). An exaggeration (one may wish) on some parts of race, class, and gender by the author, which leaves an odd taste after reading it. Mixed emotions with the two leads (Trina and McT).
