This isn't merely another trip on well-worn rails for long-time Chandler fans. For one thing, it offers in its titulary essay on the art of the detective story one of the best and most influential pieces in modern literary criticism. And the essay has all the virtues of the best of Chandler's private eye stories: it's sparse, witty and seldom pretentious. The stories themselves are all worth the read; two of them good enough to be called minor classics. Highly recommended.Read moreRead less
