What a brilliant novel from this titan of American literature. The writing is extraordinary: sometimes you have to re-read a page to take in the precision and poetry of the writing. It is set in the 50s and 60s and is about a privileged woman who just needs more than a society marriage. In other words, it's a thoughtful pointer to the need for women to take their place in society. This this novel is about the waste of intelligence and potential down the generations, the advent of feminism (don't panic: he doesn't use the term) and about class divides. I've now read his last novel, Crossing to Safety, and his Big Rock Candy Mountain. All masterpieces and I can't wait to read more.Read moreRead less
