Julian Stockwin offers us a rival to Horatio Hornblower!  I enjoy his naval series, especially the later novels when our hero, Kydd, rises to his true level within the Royal Navy.  It is hard NOT to compare these books  to the Hornblower series ... especially since they operate within the same era.  Stockwin definitely uses historical events handily to outline his narratives, and has the gift of making historical episodes interesting!  I also appreciate the relationship between Kydd and his author-friend.  Through them, Stockwin does manage to impart unfamiliar (or long forgotten) historical events through his stories.  A bonus is his use of language, with words and phrases that are unfamiliar, causing one to consult google.  This offers the added benefit of challenging my weekly coffee-gang with unfamiliar (at least to me!) words and phrases!
