Shakedown by Gerald Petievich is a gritty, hardboiled crime story which unfolds partially in southern California but mainly in Las Vegas.  John Novak is an FBI agent assigned to the Las Vegas office of the Organized Crime Task Force, a unit created to fight mob related crime using the resources and personnel of a number of federal agencies (US Customs, Treasury, FBI, etc.).  Sounds like a pretty elite organization, right?  Wrong.At least in this novel, the Task Force is a dumping ground for malcontents, malingerers and burnout cases.  But Novak is a fairly conscientious lawman whose mission to put a murderous crime boss behind bars brings him into contact with an amoral shakedown artist named Eddie Sands.Eddie Sands is a former Las Vegas police detective who has gone over to the other side of the law. To make things even more interesting, his girlfriend Monica is an accomplished con artist in her own right.This is a fast moving, economically written tale of cops and robbers which pulls no punches.
