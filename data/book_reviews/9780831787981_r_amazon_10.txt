Like the TTA books, this work was available in the strangely sci-fi centric library of my childhood in post industrial central Scotland. It's a deeply 70s work and very much of it's time but it's relentlessly upbeat and semi utopian outlook on the future combined with the artwork centric stories caught my imagination and paved the way for a life long love of science fiction. The upbeat tone is very much at odds with today's fashion for dystopian science fiction and it's all the better for it. The future can be clean, competent and fun not stupid, scary and frankly grubby. More of this sort of stuff please. RecommendedRead moreRead less
