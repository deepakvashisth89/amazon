I loved this book as a teenager and just bought the kindle edition.  I think of this book as part of a trilogy with Don't Care High, Son of Interflux, where Gordon started to focus on high school characters, luckily for me while I was in high school.  In a side note Don't Care High inspired me to make one of my schools more memorable campaigns for class president.Anyway, this book is the best of that inspired lot.  It's funny and the characters are absolutely unique.  There has never been a Jardine anywhere else, and he would probably say that's good for them.  I have quoted these poems to hundreds of people at coffee shops and will sometimes refer to myself as Jardine in stressful moments.The only negative is I hate the new ebook cover, it totally misses the point of the Garbage bag concept.
