After a Chicago adventure in You Bet Your Life, Book Four finds Toby Peters back in Los Angeles solving a case that involves Howard Hughes and some industrial espionage.  Kaminsky has his cast of regular characters in place by this time:  Toby's cop brother Phil, landlord Jeremy Butler, landlady Mrs. Plaut, shoddy dentist Shelly Minck and little person neighbor Gunther Wertham.The series is nearly at its comic hard-boiled peak and Kaminsky provides one of his best celebrity guest stars in the person of Basil Rathbone, who is both a winning personality and integral assistant to Toby in solving the case.So happy to be rereading this series in order.Highly recommended.Read moreRead less
