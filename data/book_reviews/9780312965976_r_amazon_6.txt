Your Customer Rating:After reading A Charmed Place, I felt as though I had just returned from a summer vacation by the sea. Stockenberg's vivid descriptions transported me to the coastal town of Sandy Point. I enjoyed strolling through Rosedale Cottage's garden (I could almost catch the scent of the old world roses). I felt the sea breeze on the beach down by the lighthouse.The characters came to life with their realistic dialogue, thoughts and emotions--Maddie's lively exchanges with her good friends, Norah and Joan; her feelings of worry, frustration and joy with family; and her roller coaster ride of emotions toward Dan Hawke (college love from years ago who has just moved into the nearby lighthouse when the story opens).If romance, humor and drama weren't enough, there was the suspense of a murder mystery added to the mix. All were woven together beautifully in this book.I think I'm ready for another seaside vacation, and am about to download my second Kindle Book, this author's "Beloved," which apparently will take me to a ghost-inhabited cottage on Nantucket!I'll tell you about the trip when I get back.Read moreRead less
