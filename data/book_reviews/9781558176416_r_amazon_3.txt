"This time he couldn't find one before he had swelled to bursting with his need for a death."How's that for an opening line! This is the second book I've read by this author and for sure i'm going to read more.Manhattan police are stumped by a serial killer whose victims seem to have nothing in common. The women are different races, different different ages, different physical looks, different jobs. What they do have in common is a great mystery which when we find out what it is shows how ingenious this writer is.Suspense is my favorie genre and it doesn't get any better that this!
