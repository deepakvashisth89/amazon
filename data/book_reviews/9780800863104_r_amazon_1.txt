Review of Pioneers of the Unseen by Dr Paul Tabori.CITATION:  Tabori, P. (1972). Pioneers of the unseen. New York: Taplinger Publishing Co. Inc.Reviewer: Dr W. P PalmerThis book was obtained as it contains the only available biography of Hereward Carrington, whose life I was researching. This hard-backed book contains the brief biographies of six researchers into psychic phenomena and each of these men receives a chapter briefly reviewing their lives and the impact that their research has made on the study of psychic phenomena. After an introduction the following researchers are considered:Hereward CarringtonSir Oliver LodgeCharles RichetBaron von Schrenk-NotzingCesare LombrosoRaphael Schermann.The introduction is interesting as Tabori explains that the only person in this group that he met in person, was Hereward Carrington by whose company he greatly enjoyed. More generally, the introduction surveys the field of psychic research and briefly introduces each of the researchers. Hereward Carrington was a psychical researcher, though his main source of income was as a writer and editor. Sir Oliver Lodge was a physicist and Charles Richet was a physiologist. Baron von Schrenk-Notzing was a neurologist, Cesare Lombroso was a physician and criminologist and Raphael Schermann had a complex career, but is referred to as a `psychographologist'.Generally, Dr Paul Tabori writes well and is accurate, but I found out in my research on Hereward Carrington that there are a number of biographical errors in his descriptions of Carrington's personal life which would have been difficult for Tabori to discover back in 1972 without the aid of the `internet'. Carrington was not always consistent in portraying his own life accurately, which may explain a number of the errors in this book.Overall this is a very useful book on a controversial area and provides information about a number of men whose lives are little known.BILL PALMERRead moreRead less
