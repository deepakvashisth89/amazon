I could not bear the writing in this book. The author tries to load every sentence with as many words as possible. It is so badly done that I can not imagine why it was published at all or who would enjoy this book. Even Mother Theresa, were she alive, would find it impossible to care about the dimensionless characters buried beneath the amateurish writing.
