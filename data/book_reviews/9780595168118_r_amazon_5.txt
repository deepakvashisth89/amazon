Several years ago, a friend lost the love of her life, her husband, in a tragic accident. I spent a whole evening searching for just the right gift to give her to help her through this most difficult of times. I didn't use lists of best books on grieving or any other cognitive tools. I relied solely on my intuition and chose this book out of the hundreds available about death, loss, grieving, and recovery.She received many such gifts from friends and relatives, including dozens of books on this subject. She told me that this book was the only one she could bring herself to read out of all of those books. In fact, it was so helpful she kept it on the nightstand next to her bed for a long time, to read from in times of neediness.I don't think this book is particularly religious.
