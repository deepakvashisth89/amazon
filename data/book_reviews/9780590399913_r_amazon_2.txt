Yes, I am in love with Goosebumps Body Squeezers Part 1 because it talks about all kinds of gross things.  This book is about a boy named Henry.  He thinks his neighbor is a big, green alien.  So he camps out  in his backyard to watch him.  Then he hears something so he chases it all the way to the ball park.  It was a body squeezer squeezing the blood out of something.  Last, he tries to tell his parents but they do not believe him.  I recommend this book for grades 3-7.Reviewed by Jamin, age 9Read moreRead less
