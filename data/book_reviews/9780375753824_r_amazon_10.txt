I have just finished this incredible book, which I couldn't put down. Far from the bright lights of New York's Fifth Avenue lies another America, full of irredeemable poverty. William Finnegan explores the druggy underbelly of black New Haven, deepest, dirt poor rural East Texas, the Mexican immigrant population of Washington State's wine-growing Yakima Valley and neo-Nazi (and anti Nazi) skinhead gangs of LA's Antelope Valley, Finnegan reports from an America we seldom see.
