I picked up this book when I was about 10 years old. I never was a huge reader, but the cover interested me, and once I started reading, I couldn't put it down.I then read it three more times over the course of the next few years.Now, over 20 years later, I buy it again, this time to share with my family or five....a good read-aloud. I am enjoying it now even more than the first time, as I am now able to really comprehend the full meaning of the story. But my kids, ages 4, 8, and 12, all enjoy it as well. It appeals to all ages.
