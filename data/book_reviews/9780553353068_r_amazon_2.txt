REading this book for the first time in 2013 as part of a next-stage exploration in my personal and religious life, I found Maria Harris' framework on women's spirituality to be timely and appropriate.  Her steps are perennial as the grass, creating a spiral dance that need never conclude, always suggesting next steps.I selected exercises to do from some steps, noticed other activities I have already done, considered how to introduce yet other activities into group process in the future.This book delivers a wealth of insight with elegant simplicity alongside effective prompts for reflection and discovery.Since finishing a first read-through, which I did quickly over a few days, I have already picked it up several times to look up excerpts.
