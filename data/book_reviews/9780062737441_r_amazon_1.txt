This is one of a series of books about bird feeding I bought here, and on Ebay.  This one does an excellent job of offering good solid information in an interesting and enjoyable way, without getting too technical.  And it also does not get bogged down and waste a lot of your money by taking up a great deal of book space describing different bird species. (No matter where you live, only a small portion of the hundreds of birds described will be indigenous in your area). I recommend this book. Especially for the novice feeder.  But like all the other bird books I have reviewed here, I suggest you only order it with free shipping, or locate it used.
