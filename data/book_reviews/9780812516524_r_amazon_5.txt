Though it lacks the career-defining arc of ALONE WITH HORRORS, I really feel like this is the strongest collection of Campbell's stories, and thereby one of the strongest horror collections period.  This can sit on the shelf beside GHOST STORIES OF AN ANTIQUARY, easy.When I reach the conclusion of any given Campbell story, I find myself re-reading the tale immediately, to catch all the little hints and bits of foreshadowing that he'd been dangling in my face all along.  These tales are especially strong in that regard.  Better every time you read them--there's always something more waiting for you.Read moreRead less
