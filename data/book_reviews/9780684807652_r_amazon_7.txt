This is a quick, light overview of a complex character, and yet it is ultimately very readable.  Not a book for the serious historian, HLM is still I nice summer read, or a good book for a young adult who just needs a little encouragement in studying history.  I do, however, agree with the reader who recommended VICTORIA VICTORIOUS in addition to this one.  They are similar in style, and both good.  I am a longtime fan of Erickson and while this is by no means her best, much of the criticism leveled at it is unfair.
