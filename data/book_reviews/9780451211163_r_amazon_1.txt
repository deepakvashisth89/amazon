Sometimes a book comes along that makes you laugh, cry, worry, and feel vindicated right along with the characters. This is such a book. I was barely in the first chapter on my kindle when I had to stop, back out, and order two paperback copies. One for my mother, and one for that delightful discovery by a guest perusing reading possibilities when staying at my house. It's quick, witty, captivating and wise. Characters evolve in believable conflicts and resolutions. Good values and principles are satisfyingly triumphant. And life events blow in like a blizzard, each one layering on more conflict, each one snowballing in complexity, just like what happens in real life. And your heros, whom you come to love, come out dancing after the storms. Perfect read.Read moreRead less
