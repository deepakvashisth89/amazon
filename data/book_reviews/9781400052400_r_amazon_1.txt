I was hit by a truck three and a half years ago and lost my left leg below the knee. This last summer I rode the Seattle to Portland ride, 206 miles in two days and now regularly ride 30 or 40 miles a day on the weekends. That I do this is in no small part thanks to Mike Magnuson. I got a copy of "Heft on Wheels" two years ago and last year started riding again. Mike is a regular guy (well regular in a very witty, well-read and erudite college professor kind of way) who one day wakes up and realizes that he needs to turn his life around, and so he does. The way that Mike went from being an out of shape lump to a lean mean racing machine is probably not something you want to emulate, he himself admits that it was kind of insane, but reading about what he did is inspirational even if you decide to take a longer path to redemption through cycling. Others have criticized Mike for his digressions in the book on literature and other subjects, personally I really liked them, when Mike writes about something he loves he does so with great gusto, whether it is cycling, Proust or his family, and it's a true pleasure to read.If you are thinking of taking up cycling as a way of getting in shape buy this book. You don't have to emulate what Mike did to get in shape, in fact you probably shouldn't, this book is not a training manual, for those check out the works of Chris Carmichael, but as a source of inspiration, as the kind of thing that will motivate you to get out and ride when you're not feeling your best and the weather is awful, the kind of thing that will show you that it is possible to get into shape and turn your life around this book is one of the best.Read moreRead less
