The title is not really what people will think you are reading.  The players are people in show biz and their love interests/marriages.  Jenna, Bettina and Frankie are university pals who end up sharing Frankie's home.  Frankie and Jenna are long-term roommates.  Frankie and Bettina come from old family money.  You will go on adventures with all three girls in their careers and love lives.  Twists and turns, heartbreak, abusive relationships, misplaced trust, difficult family issues, dangerous people that want to take advantage of naivety.  Lucinda Riley wrote a few novels under Lucinda Edmonds and she will not dissapping in her early writing.
