A great introduction to a baby sitting charge with a disability- as a person with disabilities myself I was wondering when that would happen in the series.  A good change as there aren't many books written by /for people with disabilities. I liked the story and characters, but the language wasn't really a 'secret' one, as thiusands if not millions of people communicate by signing, I guess the 'secret' element was to make the title compelling for the agegroup  the BSC is aimed at.
