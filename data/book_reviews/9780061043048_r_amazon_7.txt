I love this book by Dr. Ben Carson.  It is very easy to read and I would even recommend it to children beginning around age 8 or 9.  It is very inspirational and sometimes humorous.  It teaches about hard work, how learning and reading can open your world to so many more dreams and helping dreams come true.  I also teaches about values in terms of not becoming arrogant, humility, schoolmate relationships, and family relationships.  It is written in a VERY easy to read style so that even children would not find it cumbersome.  It holds your interest and you just want to keep reading to find out more about Mr. Carson's life and also how he thought about things in certain situations... some situations got him into "trouble" or helped him understand and grow from a mistake.  That is what is so beautiful about this book, is that he takes his mistakes and learns from them and makes himself a better and stronger and wiser person.
