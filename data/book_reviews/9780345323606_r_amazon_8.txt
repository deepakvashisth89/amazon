I read this book as a child and about a year ago suddenly remembered it. I'm 50 now. It's a great story with lovely illustrations and written in an easy manner with just enough attention to detail to keep a child interested without boring them. I didn't realise there were so many books in the Oz series. Now that I've read this again, I shall give it to my 10 month old nephew and start collecting them for him for when he's older.
