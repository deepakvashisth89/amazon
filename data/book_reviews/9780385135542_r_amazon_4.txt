Love it or hate it- you can't deny this books influence on American Indian Studies and with the pubic in general in igniting debate even though it's a work of fiction.  It's worth another look after all this time if no other reason than it got a lot of people thinking about things from a different point of view- as did the movie Dances With Wolves- another piece of fiction that in the process of telling its story raised real issues of thought.  As does this book. If you read it years ago you might want to pick it up again with most of the controversy on the liberties taken in the book have passed.
