I have been reading Tony Hillerman for several decades now.  I grew up on the Navajo Reservation, in the Four Corners area, just south of Shiprock.  It really warms my heart, in my old age, to re-read about the people and landscapes of this area, which was and is very dear to me.  Although the majoprity of this story takes place in  Washington D.C., it still speaks to me of the areas I am so familiar with.  I have a LOT of respect for the Navajo Tribal Police, having known a few of them.  I've always wondered if Tony Hilleman had "inside" information about some of their cases, LOL.
