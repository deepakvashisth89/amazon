Stockwin does an amazing job, and his Kydd series is a master class in the genre. Thomas Kydd has become as faithful a friend as C.S Forester's Hornblower, or O'Brian's Jack Aubrey. Well done, Mr. Stockwin. The tales are woven with great care and technical details are phenomenal, but the character development is where he truly stands apart. Thomas Kydd comes to life in these books, they are worthy of reading and rereading. I have the entire (so far) collection on kindle, and I adore them so much, I am working on purchasing each paperback.
