This book is from the series of Oz books by L. Frank Baum, the first of which is The Wizard of Oz.  All of the Oz books are full of wonder with eccentric, quirky, and imaginative characters.  When one finishes one of the books one wishes that he could go right back to Oz on another adventure.  One reason I appreciate all the Oz books (except frankly the first one) is because the gifted artist John R. Neil (a different artist from the one who illustrated the first book) drew such marvelous drawings and paintings!!!!!  In The Emerald City of Oz the original books had 17 watercolor pictures, 16 of which were embellished with sparkly metallic green paint on the edges of a lot of the pictures, but that practice was discontinued.  THIS book is a reissue of the 1910 edition with the green sparkly paint restored.  It is worth every penny!!!!!Read moreRead less
