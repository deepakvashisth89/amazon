The story in this book drew me in with lively, well-defined characters and beautiful descriptions. The Gears don't shy away from, or candy-coat, the rituals and beliefs of the Native Americans, eerily akin to what we are seeing environmentally today.  The reformatting to e-publish took it's toll, however, on the nuts and bolts of the book, i.e. typos and generally having it make sense.
