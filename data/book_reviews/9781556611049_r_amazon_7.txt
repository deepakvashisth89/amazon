I' ve always been drawn to the writings of men & women who have put their thoughts down in a journal. To consistently write of their lives, surroundings, & thoughts, is downright amazing. The storyline revolves around a young girl, her family, & those good & sometimes not so good characters entertwining in their lives as she lives it & records it in her journal."My Father's World" is interesting, with the right amount of history/mystery to make for enjoyable reading.
