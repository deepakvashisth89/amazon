This is a good deck for beginners, but be aware that many of the pose names differ from what’s commonly used in most yoga studios, books, or online classes. If you’ve been practicing yoga for a while, you might find the terminology confusing or inconsistent.I’ve been doing yoga for over 15 years, and several of the pose names in this deck were completely unfamiliar to me. It would be very helpful if each card included an “Also Known As” section to reference the more standard or widely recognized names.For example:“Cat and Dog” is more commonly known as Cat-Cow“Proud Warrior” corresponds to Warrior II“Through-the-Hole Stretch” is better known as Reclining PigeonI also wish the deck included a few blank cards so users could add common poses that are missing.Some poses I regularly practice that are not included: Rabbit (Hare), Warrior I, II, and III, Reverse Warrior, Lizard, Boat (the one included isn’t actually Boat Pose), Wheel, and Headstand, among others.I shared a picture of my deck, the yellow sheets are missing poses I have had to add.Read moreRead less
