This book is different than any other workout book in so many ways. For example, it has workouts for the gym AND the home. It has pictures for each excercise. But the singularly most effective aspect of this book is that it explains that (just using this as an example) the triceps, deltoids and chest each has 3 "heads"-you can't just do 3 sets of one triceps exercise and expect that to impact your entire tricep. So, Brad Schoenfeld has detailed 3 different exercises (3 sets of 15 reps for each muscle targeted) just for the triceps alone! Same goes for other body parts. In the beginning, the book is geared toward people just starting to workout, to get the basics. I've had this book for years (paid $20. back then) I have shown this to many friends (happy about the Amazon price!) who have, in turn, ordered this book. Highly, highly recommend.
