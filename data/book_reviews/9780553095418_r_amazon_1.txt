This is an underrated gem. It takes place right after Return of the Jedi and shows the fallout of the battle of Endor. One of the best characterizations of Luke, Leia and Han I have read. It also shows the after effects of the Emperor's attack on Luke. This shows the real Luke Skywalker, brave, compassionate and dedicated. The aliens are fearsome as well, and the Empire is the Empire. I really enjoyed this book and it is one of my top 10.Read moreRead less
