I loved this book! Last year my son moved to Brooklyn and I must admit that I have become fascinated by the the Hasidic residents. To satisfy my curiosity I have read several books recently to find out more about these unusually dressed people. This book really stood out from the dark stories of oppressed women yearning to breathe free. It was so universal! It could have been about orthdox Christians, Muslims, Hindus, etc. and their struggles with young adult children and change. I loved the characters, I loved the ending.
