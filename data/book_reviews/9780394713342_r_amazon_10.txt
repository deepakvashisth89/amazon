On the plus side, this book has several hundred pages of ideas/quotes from 'experts' that were shown to be inaccurate or totally false. There is a large index and bibliography to make finding information easy and quick. I like the premise of this book because it shows us that if we place our faith in a person who is supposed to be an 'expert', no matter how many letters behind their name, we give away our personal power and deny our own logic/intuition. When leading doctors and authorities say that cigarettes are healthy, or that radiation is good for us, or that we can't overcome a medical diagnosis, it is our faith in their 'ultimate knowledge' that can cause us grave harm. By being aware of the fallibility of so called 'experts' we empower ourselves by acknowledging that their words are not the last word.On the not-so-positive side, just like the 'experts' that are lacking true wisdom, the fact that there are numerous people citing inaccuracies and misquoted sources in 'The Experts Speak' doesn't do much to add to my confidence that the information being presented is accurate. Certainly, just like we shouldn't take the 'expert's' words as the sole truth, we should not take quotes in a book as sole truth either; however, while I believe that most of the quotes in "The Experts Speak" are authentic, I don't want to spend time and research validating every one of them.So, I give this book 3 stars because I like the premise, the organization of the information and the content itself and, even though I would never take one source the final authority, the idea that some of the quotes are inaccurate (or possibly false) casts a shadow of doubt and knocks the rating down.Read moreRead less
