I'm a big fan of Canterbury Classics and own several of their leatherbound hardcovers. This is the first of their flexibound books that I've bought, and I wasn't disappointed. The cover feels lovely, like leather, with really cool embossed quotes. The pages are thick ivory paper, and the printing is good quality. As for the contents, so far I've only read The Legend of Sleepy Hollow and thought it was a spooky masterpiece. Irving's descriptions, especially of Ichabod Crane, are a hoot!
