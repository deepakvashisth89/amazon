Emily Arundell is a wealthy spinster with some family members who would like a share of her money. A dangerous nighttime fall down the staircase during a holiday visit of her nephew and nieces raises her suspicions and, while recuperating, she writes to Poirot. Because of a delay in posting the letter, Miss Arundell has died of an illness by the time the letter reaches him. Poirot suspects foul play.Though I've long been a fan of Christie's Miss Marple, Hercule Poirot has never been a favorite. I read a couple of Poirot stories many years ago and thought him an insufferable character -- boorish and arrogant. Having now run out of Miss Marple stories to read, however, I decided to try Poirot again. I'm glad I did. He comes across as much less over-bearing here than I recalled from other books and the story is first-rate.POIROT LOSES A CLIENT is a typically well-plotted and entertaining Christie mystery.
