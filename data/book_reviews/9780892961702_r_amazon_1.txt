Robert Campbell doesn't seem to be widely known to readers of crime fiction nowadays, but he should be; this guy was a terrific writer and his "La-La Land" series of novels with the P.I. known as Whistler are very engaging reads. Tough and gritty, funny and furious.I've read reviews that call Campbell's writing style "Chandler-esque" and compare to writers such as James Ellroy and Elmore Leonard. All of those comparisons are valid. This guy was that good. Like Elmore Leonard, he had a real flair for writing snappy, believable dialogue. Throw in some very warped, bordering-on-bizarre bad guys, and comparisons to Carl Hiaasen are also appropriate. Yes, plenty of weirdness going on.I read these "La-La Land" books about 20 years ago and enjoyed them very much. They all must be out of print nowadays because I don't see them in bookshops any longer. But I found a secondhand copy of this novel recently and decided to re-read it and see if it was as good as I remembered it. It was indeed. Quite violent and shocking in parts, but also compassionate and moving in others, and downright funny in other places. In other words, a very entertaining page-turner of a novel.If you can find anything by this author, grab it and read it. Robert Campbell died over a decade ago, but he still deserves to be a better known author. He really was a brilliant, first-class writer.Read moreRead less
