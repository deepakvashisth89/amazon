This is one of my two favorites so far of the amusing series of short novels featuring private eye Toby Peters. I’m having a ball listening to these books.I enjoy the way Stuart Kaminsky humanizes the 1940s movie stars and studio moguls (the scandals; I looked them up! #ErrolFlynn) along with other famous people like Hemingway, Faulkner and Dashiell Hammet. Howard Hughes (so interesting). Joe Louis. (I now know how to breathe to recoup my energy. No yoga breaths.)He talks about the patriotic spirit during the War, and rationing of sugar, gas, oil, ect., even the Internment Camps.You could get a complete dinner for about 75c. A tank of gas for about 25c. A studio apartment for $25 per month. I recognized the streets, a few of the restaurants, and landmarks like the ‘Hollywoodland’ Sign.The audiobooks are outstanding. This one was narrated with charm, irony and humor by Stephen Bowlby.His sidekicks need their own separate series. But unfortunately Mr. Kaminsky passed on in October, 2009. There’s his best friend and neighbor, a very formal, well-mannered Little Person originally from Switzerland. Gunther is a translator by trade. Another favorite character is quiet, stoic, well-read Jeremy, poet, mystic, former wrestler. You can picture the events in your mind as a moving picture.In this one Toby is trapped inside a mental hospital. You can feel his frustration and desperation. No he’s not crazy! Mae West may die if he doesn’t break out of there!He basically has the one suit, eats Wheaties or ‘shreddies’ for dinner and is always destitute. Yet, haha, he repeatedly turns down the thousands freely offered to him by the rich and famous clients. “No it’s free. It’s on me”. “$30 a day is what I charge.” Maybe it’s the innumerable injuries and concussions he suffers.If producers toned these two last items down to something plausible, I think it would make a fantastic retro series for a Mickey Rourke prototype.Read moreRead less
