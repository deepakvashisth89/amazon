This book shows what it's like to be a star rigger, one who navigates the hyperspace streams in our galaxy.  Carlyle is a star rigger who luckily escapes a hyperspace accident that killed his shipmates.  He finds and rescues an alien catlike creature and together they try to navigate their way to their intended destination.The book starts slow but then gathers steam as we learn what it's like to be a star rigger.  Carlyle is a sensitive shy soul and the alien is very standoffish.  How they get along makes for an interesting and touching story.  It has a very satisfying ending.Read moreRead less
