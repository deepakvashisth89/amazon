I checked out 'Marvin Wanted More' from our community library because of the appealing illustrations.  But this is far more than just a pretty looking children's book.  This is a book about a sheep who compares himself to others, and is filled with envy and greed.  In an attempt to be as 'good' as the others, he eats everything he can find.  More, and more, and more, Marvin wanted more! Until finally he eats the whole world.  But don't worry, Marvin learns his lesson. And so now I am purchasing this book because it is an important lesson for sheep, and children (and this mommy) to rememeber.  Trust me, you want this in your collection :)Read moreRead less
