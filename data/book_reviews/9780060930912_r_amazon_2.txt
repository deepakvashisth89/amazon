The beginning of this book focuses on one character, to the point of boredom, in my opinion. I kept waiting for something to happen. Later on, though, the focus shifts to the mother of the character and the book gets a lot better.I think the author should have focused more on the mother in this story, as her story was the most interesting and kept the other part of the plot as a background or side story.Not a very original story but I still really enjoyed reading it and I recommend it.Read moreRead less
