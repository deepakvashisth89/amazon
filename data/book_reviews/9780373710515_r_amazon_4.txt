As always Bobby Hutchinson writes a story filled with love and feelings true to our hearts. The Family Doctor brings together all sorts of family issues, in a setting most of us would find surreal. Tony's demeanor is beyond trying throughout, but it is so fun to watch him slowly melt under Katie's 'attentions'. Tony's family is interesting to say the least, and Katie has her hands full too! The Family Doctor is another example of Bobby's excellent abilities to craft a story filled with love, struggles, and as alway - happy endings, even in the most trying of circumstances!
