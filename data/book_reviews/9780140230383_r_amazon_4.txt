This is an amazing little book, about a fathers love, and a boy's achievements in chess at a level will beyond his years. I will never play that well, but I can appreciate someone else's skills. Fred Waitzkin tells the good, the bad, and the ugly of the chess world, the minimal support in the US, the old Soviet system, and the shenanigans of chess parents.
