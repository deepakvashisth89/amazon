This is a unique anthology that allows hundreds of canonized Catholic saints, from all historical periods including the present day, to speak directly in their own words on a variety of topics.  The quotations are  brief, and are arranged thematically. while the topics range from the very  profound and serious ("Time and Eternity", "Suffering",  "Mystic Union") to the very human and everyday ("Food",  "Travels").  Each of the 65 thematic sections is introduced by an  authoritative and often witty commentary that makes one wish that the  author's voice could be heard even more often.  The overall effect is  one of great abundance and variety.  Saints, the reader soon discovers, are  found  in every personality type, every social class, intelligence level  and temperament.  They are a lively and diverse bunch.  You will meet old  favorites like Francis of Assisi and Thomas Moore, and encounter completely  unfamiliar heroes, like Charles Lwanga of Uganda.  There is something here  to fit every mood, and to inspire much further reading.  THE WISDOM  OF THE SAINTS is ideal for inspirational reading, for hunting quotations to  surprise, edify and dazzle audiences, or as a reference resource. A  biographical appendix tells the brief life story of each of the 200 saints  who have shared their written or spoken thoughts with the reader.
