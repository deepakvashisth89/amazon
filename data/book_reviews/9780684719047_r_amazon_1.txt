Jesse Stuart's memoir of becoming an educator, beginning in the 1920s in rural Kentucky, is filled with thrilling incidents and passionate insights. The writing is fresh and inventive, the stories he tells are surprising  and sometimes violent. Stuart's start in life is a classic story of a determined person in the somewhat uncivilized, seat-of-the-pants rural and small town America of the period. It is hard to read this book without alternately stiffening with nervous tension and smiling. The book made me grateful all over again for the best teachers I had growing up.Read moreRead less
