Margery Allingham is one of the best of the "grande dames" of mystery authors, along with Agatha Christie and Ngaio Marsh.  Her books featuring Albert Campion are among her most charming.  These two little novellas do not feature him, but they're still reasonably entertaining.  Not quite up to her usual standard, in my opinion, and rather on the predictable side, but definitely worth a read, and if you're interested in Allingham but would rather not invest the time required to read a full-length novel, these will definitely do the trick.Read moreRead less
