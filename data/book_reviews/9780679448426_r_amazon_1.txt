I am sucker for technical thrillers,  especially ones with a biological theme. There, I said it. I think Carriers is one if the better bio-thrillers out there.  I lost my paperback,  and since it seems to be out of print I obtained a very nice used hardcover to re-read. It's a real page-turner,  but it is dense with the appropriate background science, which adds to, rather than takes away from,  the plot and characters. This is a very chilling, well thought-out and well-paced novel regarding a rather terrifying viral outbreak. The story unfolds like a mystery- told from different points of view. Summary: a woman sends her twin children to visit with her ex-husband, a botanist working in Africa. When radio contact with the research camp is lost, she goes to find out what happened.
