The title of this review says it all. Every book I have read by Graham Masterton seems better than the last. I loved the Indian heritage and stories that I hope are factual. I have lived in and visited homes and areas that are beyond dark and dreary. Places where you wonder if anyone could ever truly be happy there. I recommend this book to anyone who loves horror, as well as those who like puzzle books, as this story was revealed in snippets and pieces of the puzzle. Great ending as I never saw the last few twists Graham had in store for readers. Fantastic book as always. Thank you, Graham, for another great read.Read moreRead less
