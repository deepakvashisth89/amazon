A 'greyt' book about the amazing history of the greyhound. The book has a substantial amount of historical detail and many fascinating photos of artwork involving the greyhound breed over the centuries. I also very much enjoy author Cynthia Branigan's writing, her passion for this beautiful dog really shows. Her other book "Adopting the Racing Greyhound" is a must have reference for someone adopting one these dogs such as I have.Read moreRead less
