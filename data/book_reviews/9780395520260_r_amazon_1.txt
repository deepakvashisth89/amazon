This book delves deeper into the Enneagram typology than the more quickly-read Riso/Hudson book, "Discovering Your Personality Type," (2003).  It discusses the "Passion, Virtue, Fixation, and Holy Idea" of each type, so that the book is more psychological in orientation.  It contains a section on 'misidentifications' of types -- how certain types are similar to each other and how to discern the difference between them; and it has a section on "Advanced Topics," which, among other things, discusses each type's "missing piece."  I found the section on "Recommendations" for each type to be very helpful, as it highlights where each type is 'going wrong'; what to guard against; how to avoid pitfalls.  This is a good in-depth book, for people who want to examine the healthy, average, and unhealthy levels of each type, and go deeper into psychological aspects than mere personality-typing.Read moreRead less
