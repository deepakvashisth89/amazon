David Farland is one of the fine fantasy writers of today. Wizardborn is the third of his Runelords series, and he just keeps ratcheting up the danger. This is a new and different world from any you have previously known. It contains creatures and dangers you have only dreamed about. Good has a most difficult time besting evil, and it seems the end of the book is only a breathing space, one to enjoy while reaching for the next book in this dark and dangerous series.
