I was hooked by the jacket copy that said "if you ever wanted to go to Tibet, now's your chance" but was sorely disappointed. It is a thin book in every way, thin on substance, interest, detail, ideas. It's alternately pompous then aimless...the point of view is constantly ambivalent. Perhaps that is true to his experience, but it doesn't make for a good read. I'll keep searching for a real meaty travelogue of Tibet.Read moreRead less
