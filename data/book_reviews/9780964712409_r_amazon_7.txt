A good friend and co-worker loaned me this book and it captivated me from the very beginning.  It makes you feel the fear and danger of hiding from the Nazis... capture, and the horrors of the work camps.  It is beyond my comprehension how they could endure and survive like they did and unite again after it was all over.  So many people were involved with helping the Jews; hiding them, feeding them and assisting in their escapes at great risk to themselves and their families.  I recommend this book to everyone.  You also realize how a nation can be swept up in a movement and end up doing unspeakable acts under the guise of "following orders."
