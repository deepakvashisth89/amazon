This book reminds me of undergrad. The atmosphere and town are really well described, and that supports the B-movie premise of a mind control parasite really well. He’s a little gratuitous with descriptions of female characters, but that’s part and parcel to reading a Laymon novel. The main characters are likeable and relatable, though there is only a little character growth throughout the book. I finished it in two sittings - the pace is brisk.Read moreRead less
