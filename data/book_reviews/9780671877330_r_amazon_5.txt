Long time ago, while stationed at MCAS Iwakuni, Japan I picked this book up from the tiny book store on Mainside of the base.  In under 2 days I had read the entire book.It was an amazing book then as it is today when I read it once more.Redliners deals a lot with the effects of PTSD and the stigma and effects of it.  It also shows both viewpoints of it, Civilian and Military.  Thoughts and actions from both as well as the preconceived ideas on what each is to the other.When the Military think of a Civilian, I admit that it generally does not have a very nice perception of them.  It was probably the same way for some of the Civilians when they thought of the Military as well.For any Veteran, I would recommend this book highly.  For any Civilian I would recommend it as well.As more is learned about PTSD the more options become available for every that is effected by it, be they the family, the general public, or the individual suffering from it.  A lot of the cases in the book dealt with some of the extreme cases and the triggers, as well as how some people will react to themSimply an amazing book by one of my favorite authors.Read moreRead less
