I'll admit that the 'scifi cyberpunk' (or is it 'metafiction' or 'weird fiction'?) sub-genre is not my thing and I didn't relate to 'LoV' nor - to be blunt - really enjoy it, despite the rating here. It was in too many ways quite depressing and dark and even bordered on horror for me. And really in terms of characterizations, everyone just struck me as either being an utter bastard or just pathetic in more ways than I care to describe. The book is not poorly written, please do not misunderstand, even if ultimately the construction didn't pull me in. I do wish the author had spent more time looking at the (side-)consequences of what develops up to nearly the end- including what seems wound up being the harvesting of LoVs from living (?) children - and not just hinting at same in passing. This was why in large part I found the ending particularly unsatisfying as we're left with no real outcome for any of the players, human or otherwise. Quite clearly, what is happening would only result in massive global upheaval and even war, not only at an economic level but most likely in very literal terms. But that just gets pushed away as we're then left with our 'cameras' focused on other scenes.
