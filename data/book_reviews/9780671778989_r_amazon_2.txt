This is the second copy I've had of this study...I loaned the first one to a friend and never got it back.This book is a study done on "the aging process of humans" by Tufts University Medical School. In it, the research shows the machinations of "aging" and actually how some processes can be stopped and even reversed through exercise. The study needs to be done again to update research in this area. It follows a group of sedentary seniors over an extended period of time and shows how changing their lifestyle actually reverses and slows down the aging process.I highly recommend this book to everyone and have altered my lifestyle to avoid the pitfalls that many of us fall into as we get into those "golden years".
