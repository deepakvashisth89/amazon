Great book, although its probably quite a bit dated by now; it was published in 1992 and again in 2000. The reason I gave the book 4 Stars instead of 5 Stars is because of this. The first nine chapters discuss different types of ships such as bulk carriers, tramp ships, cargo liners, passenger ships, coastal shipping, fishing vessels, and so forth.Chapter 11 discusses modern merchant ship propulsion with an especially detailed discussion on marine Diesel engine development. Chapter 12 discusses the world-wide ship building industry, at least as it existed in the late 1990s / early 2000s.Chapter 1 describes the world shipping in terms of deadweight tonnage by type of carrier and control of shipping by nations. Unfortunately, the data only extends to 1991 or so.A consequence of the book being dated is that it totally ignores the current extent of the involvement of China in world trade and ship building.Read moreRead less
