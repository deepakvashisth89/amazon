The Starfleet Cultural Survey Bureau has developed new technology which will allow Federation members to explore newly discovered planets without violating the prime directive. A small device, called a telescan cephalic implant, is placed behind the ear and is linked into the mind of an indigenous member of the planet. Being linked to the mind of a citizen allows instant knowledge of language and cultural differences on the planet. In an effort to get Spock to find her attractive a young ensign causes an incorrectly loaded implant to be placed in Spock.Spock's implant holds the engram of a megalomaniac that is set on taking over his home world of Kyros. By linking with Spock it has caused Spock to develop a Messiah complex. In order to convince the population of his power he has stolen the modulator crystals that help stabilize the matter/anti-matter mix that allows the dilithium crystals to power the warp drive. To make matters worse an unexplained radiation cloud is heading directly towards the Enterprise. Kirk and his group must find Messiah Spock and neutralize the implant in eight days, otherwise the Enterprise will have to be abandoned so that no deaths will occur due to the oncoming radiation. If they can't find Spock the Enterprise crew will be stranded on Kyros as the radiation has also disrupted contact with Federation Headquarters to inform them of where the Enterprise is located.This is an OK read. It is one of the early Bantam novels, published in 1976. The story got a little tedious and the over sexualization of Ensign George doesn't seem to fit in with what the TV series sets as the Star Trek standard.
