Without revealing too much of the plot, I just want to say one thing.A friend of mine handed this book to a White American, telling him, "This will explain the Indian mindset to you."I can't think of another book that would do a better job.  No one can summarize a billion people in a single book, but at least as far as middle-class Indian men go, Nataraj is as close to the quintessential Indian as I have ever read.
