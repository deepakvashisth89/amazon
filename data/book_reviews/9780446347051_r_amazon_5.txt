THERE ARE NO SPIES by Bill Granger is my first by this author and I must go back and acquire the others in the November series. Fast paced with just enough foul-ups to be believable as bureaucrats on both sides fight for power and survival.Devereaux (November), Hanley (Spy-master), Alexa (amoral killer for KGB) and Denisov (retired KGB) join forces for a price to stop the Nutcracker. Betrayals of country and honor makes this a terrific read in the spy/espionage/thriller category. If you haven't met Bill Granger's characters they are well worth the read.Nash Black, author of WRITING AS A SMALL BUSINESS and SINS OF THE FATHERS.Read moreRead less
