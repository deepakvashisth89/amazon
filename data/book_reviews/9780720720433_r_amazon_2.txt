A basic first aid book for your pet. I assembled a first aid kit for my pets and this book had pretty good reviews so I bought it. It’s basic first aid. However I was hoping it would include first day forecast as well and it did not. Review the book before an emergency so you are well at hand and know what to do. It gives great suggestions on keeping emergency vet information handy to refer to should there be a need to contact your vet immediately.
