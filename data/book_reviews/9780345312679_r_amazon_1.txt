While not much of a mystery, Nana is one of the better written Delacorta  novels. It seems to be the story of Gorodish, mostly. A sub-plot of the  book tells the story of how Gorodish and Alba meet, and is worth reading  just for that. Delacorta goes into a bit more detail and description in  this novel than he does in most of his others. And despite the lack of  proper plot, I couldnt put the book down. I highly recommend this one,  especially if youre new to the series.Read moreRead less
