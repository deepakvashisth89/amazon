Don't buy this book as a soothing bedtime story- one of my favorites is about a crocodile who likes to eat boys with mustard and girls with caramel.  It ends with the parent tucking the kid in bed listening to the crocodile coming up the stairs.  A year ago my kids would have flipped out.  But, at 6 and 8, they love this stuff now- as long as it isn't as bedtime.
