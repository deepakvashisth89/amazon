Michael Glennon is trying to save his prized possession. His famous magazine, "Manhattan" from a hostile buyout. You find out who your trusted employees, family and friends are when you need them the most. It's the story of very powerful and famous people living in the 70's where Studio 54, Elaine's and Costello's were the place to be to rub elbows. It takes you inside Manhattan and the plastic world of the West Coast. The story is filled with mystery, suspense, sex and the criminal world and backstabbing of the rich of one of the worlds most famous people of "Manhattan".
