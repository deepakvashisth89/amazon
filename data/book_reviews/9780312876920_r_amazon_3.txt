A Neanderthal quantum physicist, Ponter, falls into a parallel world on Earth, in which neanderthals are extinct (unlike his world, in which the opposite is true). There are many things about his world that are superior, including the lack of crime and pollution, and the existence of extinct mammoths and other animals, and more!This book has everything: it's thought provoking, suspenseful, and has some romance. The characters are vividly portrayed—both Homo sapiens and Neanderthals.I liked the courtroom drama (in which Ponter's friend, Adikor, is accused of murdering him) and the sarcastic passages about the press in our world, some of which made me laugh out loud.I have started the second book in the series and will also read the third.Highly recommended!Read moreRead less
