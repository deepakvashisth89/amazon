First you have to remember that the Mrs. Pargeter novels were written about 20 years ago, so technological difference have to be accepted.  Mrs. Pargeter is a great character as the wife of a man who had great connections from his "not-exactly-legal" business ventures.  His former connections are also interesting characters who, because of the late Mr. Pargeter's help in their own "not quite on the up and up" business ethics, are more than willing to do anything they can to help the Mrs. solve murders.
