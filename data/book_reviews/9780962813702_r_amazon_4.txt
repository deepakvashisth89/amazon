As someone who has been seriously involved in numerous Japanese Martial arts (Judo, Jujitsu, Karate-Do, Kobudo, and Kenjutsu) and cultural activities (Haiku, Senryu and Tanka poetry forms and Taiko drumming) for more than 55 years I also have an interest in Japanese Kanji writing.  While checking out some books on Amazon I saw this 216 page soft cover book (Kanji Pict o Graphix by Michael Rowley) for a bargain price and decided to purchase it.This is an excellent book for anyone interested in understanding Japanese Kanji and Kana Mnemonics.  In fact this book shows and explains over 1,000 of these characters along with illustrations showing the meanings.  The eleven chapters cover a wide variety of topics showing Kanji about the world, food, animals, people, the body, spirit, power, learning, tools, places and journey.If you are interested in an easy to understand explanation of Japanese Kanji and Kana Mnemonics you may want to check out this very informative volume.Rating:  5 Stars.
