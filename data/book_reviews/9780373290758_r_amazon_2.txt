By and large an enjoyable read. I like this author's writing style. Gar's personality was a little weird to me. He's a stiff Swede and he goes from stern and abrupt to kind, then loving and an abrupt way. Leah is likeable and her interactions with the children is really sweet. So, is Gar's for that matter. Although, at times Leah's emotions don't fit her personality.The story is a little predictable and everyone's troubles are taken care of with a nice little bow tied around them. I have a few pet peeves and one of them is to have the characters constantly saying the other's names. Seemed like they were unable to say anything to the other without first identifying (for the reader perhaps) who was speaking to whom. This is not a heavy read with lots of sweet moments.Read moreRead less
