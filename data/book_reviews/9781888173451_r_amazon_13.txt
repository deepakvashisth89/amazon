Didn't really enjoy this book as much as I expected, although that is not to say that it was poorly written. I wasn't sure whether it was all supernatural, part or none at all.A times I was engrossed and scared, and others, complete at a loss to the plot and where the book was heading. I had little empathy with any of the characters and only really liked poor Harry and Eric.I'm found the setting so bleak and desolate it was somewhat depressing.
