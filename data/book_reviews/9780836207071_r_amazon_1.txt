If you are a cat lover then this is a book to own.  Each quote was written by those who understand these amazing animals.  I found more quotes and phrases that describe the two cats I own purr-fectly.  There are quotes  from Einstein, Jim Davis, Abe Lincoln, T. S. Elliot, Erma Bombeck, and Mark  Twain just to name a few.  Only a cat lover could understand exactly what  the author meant by his or her quote.  There are also little tid-bits of  cat lore, information about the animal, nursery rhymes, and superstitions  mixed among the quotes themselves.  I found this book a "Must  Have" for any one who owns a cat or has owned a cat and feels that he  or she truly understands this amazing creature. I laughed and smiled as I  read each page.
