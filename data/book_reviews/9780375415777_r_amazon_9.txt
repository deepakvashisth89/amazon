This series of books gets better and better as you move forward. This one, based largely in the Med and Adriatic sees Jack and Stephen again in developing crises. Stephen's ongoing failure to learn much from his many years at sea is explored with great humour throughout the book. The geopolitical and religious complexity of the eastern Mediterranean in this era is also dealt with extremely well. A book that both entertains and informs, well worth the read.Read moreRead less
