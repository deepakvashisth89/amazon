L. Frank Baum's original 14 Oz books are incredible stories. It's not all yellow brick roads and silver slippers! Far from it! Baum's writing is at times extremely dark and always witty. I do not recommend Rinkitink in Oz as it is not canon and a departure from the overall storyline.
