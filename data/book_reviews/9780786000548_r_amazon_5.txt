The daughter of a vampire and a human, Nicole Radioux has always known  that she is special. She can communicate with animals, run faster than most  people, and can manipulate objects, and sometimes people. She discovers her  father's 'secret' one night while following him and discovers him having a  midnight snack. Stunned and confused beyond reason, she flees her home with  the help of an artist friend and travels to Paris. Her first night destroys  her illusion of beautiful Paris, and she finds herself left without money,  clothing and shelter.It is around this time that she makes the acquaintance  of mysterious Marchand LaValois. While falling in love with Marchand and  trying to deal with her own problems, she becomes drawn into Marchand's  incessant problem with a shady political group. Unfortunately, in seeking  guidance, she meets old time 'friends' of her father who are not quite who  they seem to be. Her problems escalate as she tries to solve all her  problems revolving around her father,his so-called friens,and  Marchand's  faction. This is a story of a girl who has the image of her father  shattered and seeks solitude in an attempt to self-discovery. She falls  desperately in love and when everything is said and done, we meet a young  woman in charge of her destiny and a daughter any parent would be proud of.
