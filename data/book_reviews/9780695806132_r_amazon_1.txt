Medium level book. It's divided into six chapters: battleships, aircraft carriers, cruisers, destroyers, submarines, escorts. Each chapter describes 2 to 10 representative ships of the type. The longest and most extensive chapter is on battleships: 10 ships and around 46 pages. Each chapter also contains a one-page tabulation of displacement, main armament, secondary armament, speed, and applicable maximum armor thickness.This is decent book for potential readers who are just getting interested in warships of WW II. If you are already knowledgeable of the subject, then I suspect that you already have several books on warships of WW II and won't find anything new in this one.Read moreRead less
