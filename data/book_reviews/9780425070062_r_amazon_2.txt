I REALLY don't know what books the negative reviewers here were reading.  I DO know that this is a really good light read.  It is a good story that really seems as if it were a movie script turned into a book.  I have even written to Mr. Foster about the possibility of making a movie out of this and another called "Into The Out Of".  BOTH of these good Foster books are worth it for me to re-read.  Jake Pickett is a good character and his "abilities" might not be deeply defined, but basically the unknown stays fairly unknown.  A scientist does figure out what he can do, but the greedy company man just doesn't care.  They see only an old man.  They are typical A- to B+ movie characters that are fun to read about.  Forster writes in a way that makes it easy to SEE the story unfold like a fun action movie.  I mention "Movie" a lot here as Foster often writes books from popular movie scripts.  He is very good at it and SLIPT is written much like those novelizations of movies.  I will ALWAYS recommend this book, though it is apparently out of print.  If you can buy a copy, I think you will not be disappointed.  I was fortunate to find a First Edition / First Printing hardback copy here recently!  My paperback copy is nearly worn out from all the times I lent the book out to friends and family.  NONE of them had a bad thing to say about it.
