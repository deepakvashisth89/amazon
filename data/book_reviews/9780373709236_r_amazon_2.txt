This is a sugar sweet story, one of broken promises, promises made and every attempt to fulfill those promises. Whitney has promised her sister that she'll find her missing daughter and take care of her. The sister, Morgan, has led a less than exemplary life style, and she dies. So Whitney goes in search of this missing 3 year old child. Whitney thinks she's found the missing father, concocts a bizarre story that Rhys believes, for whatever reason, and sets out to get custody of the baby. Lots of twists, turns, and just a couple surprises, but eventually we get to the point that Whitney and Rhys come to a meeting of the, well not necessarily minds, but bodies. Sex is present but its as G rated as it can get. I enjoyed reading this since its about keeping promises, has the element of a bad guy who is not really bad at all, and lots of angst. It has a wonderful ending I wasn't really expecting and if you just want a nice sweet read, this is your book.Read moreRead less
