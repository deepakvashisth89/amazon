I had read this book many years ago.  It left an impression.  I had never read a book before that gave so much insight into insanity. I don't know the history of the author, but if the author hadn't experienced mental illness, a great deal of investigation must have taken place to get such a grasp.  I am not a mental health professional, but I find this story very believable. The "light at the end of the tunnel" for the mentally ill must get a little dim sometimes and I think this book handles it very well.  A fascinating read.Read moreRead less
