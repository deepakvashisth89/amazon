9/8/22  I thoroughly enjoyed reading this book.  The selected writings of Frank Kingdon-Ward portray vivid images of the landscape and the people. His descriptions paint word pictures. Frank Kingdon-Ward's time among the Tibetans, and others, puts the reader front and center.  There is suspense, adventure and humor in these excerpts. It is not necessary to be interested in horticulture to appreciate this book. I was sorry to come to the end of the book.  Frank Kingdon-Ward's closing comments were particularly moving.  He stated that while many in London and environs spent their life working in offices and going to tea, he was grateful for the life he chose - plant collector and explorer.
