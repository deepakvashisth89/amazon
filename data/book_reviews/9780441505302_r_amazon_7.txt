Suldrun's Garden is a great story, well written and very entertaining.  In the first dozen or so "episodes" it takes a bit of patience to allow all of the characters to settle,and their roles to become clear.  Once that is done, the story becomes fascinating and many of the  "mini-tales" are actually good enough to stand as short stories on their own.I am not generally a fan of a barrage of characters and endlessly entwined plots, but Vance does a great job with the approach. The exhaustive array of just names of things, places, and people is a bit distracting, but I got used to it.A vintage tale with vintage quality to go with it.Read moreRead less
