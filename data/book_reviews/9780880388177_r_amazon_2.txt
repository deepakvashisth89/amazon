This is a remarkable sourcebook for Advanced Dungeons and Dragons 2nd Edition and a wealth of knowledge for both fledgeling DungeonMasters and the experienced alike. It offers invaluable information on how to run an engaging and interesting campaign, underground, as the name implies, or otherwise. Even DungeonMasters long-in-the-tooth can find gems of wisdom within these pages and it still makes for an interesting read, even if most of the advice herein has already been learned through trial and error, overseeing for years, hapless adventurers exploring the depths of the eponymous 'dungeon', one 10 foot by 10 foot stone corridor section at a time.Read moreRead less
