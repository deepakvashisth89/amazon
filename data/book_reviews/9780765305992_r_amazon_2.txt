Interesting premise certainly and action as always  - this action is Land's forte .While the action moved always toward the planned conclusion,  it occasionally seemed artificial. Contrived and frenetic even. I enjoyed the book and will certainly read others by Land,  but somehow this book left me feeling unsatisfied. A few points of actual annoyance popped up as a I turned page after page. One (silly perhaps) example is his use of the spelling "Koran." It's not wrong,  but it somehow seems slightly out of touch. His spelling of the word isn't the one used most often currently at the time this book was published. It made me feel that the author wasn't quite in synch with the setting of his written work.
