First let me say that this book is beautiful, compelling in its narrative and engaging in its prose. It is not about how to make icons, nor is it particularly about the experience of making icons, although the author does make one in the course of the story. Instead it is a description of the intensely observational thought process that some writers experience, and how those observations inform not only the writing but the very existence of the writer/observer. Another reviewer mentions that this book is not about meditation, and I somewhat agree, however for a writer existence itself is a meditation, a constant inward reflection of the stage show all around us, that occasionally is captured in a way that exposes not only the story, but the author as well, in a light that is exquisitely penetrating and final. To capture something like this takes skill and courage, and Ms. Neville displays an abundance of both in this wonderful book.Read moreRead less
