I bought this edition from amazon almost two years ago to get a reliable text of the novel and the extra material that makes Norton Critical Editions worth the price.  The Penguin edition has more complete notes, but theirs are in the back and Norton's are at the bottom of the page.  I am adding this to the other reviews because I have just discovered a serious misprint in my book.  I had already read the modern articles on the book as well as the novel, but I just started reading the selections from Charlotte's other writings (journals, letters, etc.) and discovered that four pages are printed out of order.  All is well through page 412, then comes page 415, followed by a section in which eight pages (four sheets) are interspersed out of order.  The last number out of order is 438, followed by the rest of the pages in order (yes I checked) starting at 441.
