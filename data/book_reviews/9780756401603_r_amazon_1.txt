I read this back in the 1970s as a member of the Science Fiction Book Club, and recently tracked it down in a Kindle version. Better than I remembered it! CJ Cherryh creates a unique mixture of alien races interacting within their own culture as well as with other species (including humans) in a fascinating plot that ends with multiple twists and tweaks. My only complaint is that she creates a unique vocabulary for each race and sprinkles seemingly each sentence with so many alien words that it becomes a distraction...at least for me.  (There is a glossary in the back of the book.) Nevertheless, it's a great read, plus there are more tales of the main Iduve race available. Enjoy!Read moreRead less
