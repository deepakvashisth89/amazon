This is the twelfth book in the series and I had read this long back. Felt so good to read the story again and was quite refreshing. this is about Tin Woodman and Scarecrow and their journey in the magical Land of Oz. Baum has this special narrative skill with which he keeps the reader engaged and he has done exactly that in this book as well. The Tin Woodman sits on the glittering tin throne of his splendid tin castle, ruling the Winkle Country of the Land of Oz with the help of his best friend, the Scarecrow. All is peaceful and well, but when a young wanderer named Woot asks the Tin Woodman how he came to be made of tin, the emperor recalls his days as a flesh-and-blood woodchopper and his love for Nimmie Amee, a Munchkin girl so fair that the sunsets blushed when they fell upon her.Good part of the series and very interesting.Read moreRead less
