This is the second time I'm reading this (not exactly linked) series (Sweet Starfire, Crystal Flame, Shield's Lady) and I'm enjoying it just as much as when I first read them!  I love her alien/futuristic books because the stories always feel believable and work very well, and without going into long and boring descriptions, she still seems to give you enough detail to have a clear picture of what the alien animals/vegetation/buildings etc look like.
