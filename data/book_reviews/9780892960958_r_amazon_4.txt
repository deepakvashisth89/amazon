Return to Golden Age Hollywood with Stuart M Kaminsky: one of my time's greatest Detective Fiction writers.  Mae is 'in a heap of trouble' and she needs former Warner Brothers security guy, Toby Peters, to get her beyond it.  Kaminsky shows Mae to be 'sassy' in real life as well as on the screen.
