Reading this book takes you to the heart of old China. Pearl S. Buck grew up in China, as her parents were missionaries. She really understands the ways and traditions of the old Imperial system.I love the detail and understanding of human nature that Pearl brings to this story.I have read many of Pearl's books and find them fascinating.
