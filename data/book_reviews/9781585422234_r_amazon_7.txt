This book explained the intricacies of women's behavior and indirect politics that occur in the workplace. It also focuses on responding to bullying coworkers in a positive manner with the end goal to cultivate relationships and not burn bridges. The writing is very easy to understand, it feels more like a dialogue than a painful academic case study. I would highly recommend to any woman who feels the sting of office politics.
