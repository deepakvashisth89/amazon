What happens when a smart professional career woman moves to the Heartland to pursue the American Dream?  She goes through disillusionment, trial and travail, snowbound winter, and the fear that she has lost her identity. But she also finds joy and draws  unexpected strength from the prairie and the people.  Her journey is laugh out loud funny at times and poignant at others.
