I purchased this book to use for a college history class paper. This is a far superior and engaging way to understand the life and experiences of these woman than dense academic volumes that read as sterile and unemotional. Some stories are presented in the woman's exact words, others have been compiled and grammer corrected, which takes away from the story, in my opinion. This isn't the fault of Gates or Andrews. They are simply publishing the collected works. This book would be appropriate reading for junior high age and beyond. If this is a topic of interest to you or part of your curriculum, please read this book. Also, the smaller size of the book makes it easy to carry or store.Read moreRead less
