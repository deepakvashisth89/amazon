I have read this book many times and I will continue to do so. It was first published in 1985 and it is as relevant today as it was then. There is an outstanding chapter on The Rich-Gumpert Evaluation System that rates the startup's odds for success based on management and marketing stage of the business. I continuously use and recommend this system to entrepreneurs wether they need to raise money or not.  While the tools used today for sales and marketing have changed, the principals are the same for a sound marketing plan.
