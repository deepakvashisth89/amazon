The second-last book in the Asey Mayo series.  It's 1946 and everyone is adjusting to peacetime with its strikes, shortages and general annoyances.  Doc Cummings demands that Asey drive him to the home of the Douglass family and introduce him to their houseguest, Carolyn Barton Boone, a senator's wife and president of a small liberal arts college.  They arrive at the Douglass home, where Cummings wants to rush to meet Mrs. Boone, but Asey becomes fascinated with a narrow gauge railway on the property, complete with an engine and an antique Pullman car.  Exploring inside the Pullman, they discover the body of Mrs. Boone.  This sets off a chain of events often found in a Phoebe Atwood Taylor book, with a disappearing corpse, cars being stolen and later turning up somewhere else, chases through the woods and confusing lanes around the neighborhood, and various people doing inexplicable things for their own reasons and being unable to account for their whereabouts at any given time.Not quite as good as Spring Harrowing, my all-time favourite Asey Mayo mystery, but I still enjoyed this one.Read moreRead less
