Riders of the Purple Sage by Zane Grey is a wonderful cowboy novel that takes place in Utah. The book starts out with the Mormon elders coming angrily to the house of a Mormon woman called Jane Withersteen with a complaint about the wrongdoing of one of her cattle ranch workers. It's at that part that Lassiter, who is a stranger, makes his appearance by coming to the aid of Jane.I've been to Utah several times so it was interesting reading a western novel about Mormons that takes place there. Jane Withersteen is a perfect example of a tough female character. In the book she has a tough job of handling a ranch but she handles it just fine. The stranger Lassiter is a man who always knows what to do to in any situation. He can stop the bad guys and he can organize and drive a herd of cattle back to the ranch.The novel has romance, action, adventure, and great descriptions of Utah scenery.
