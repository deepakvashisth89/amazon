In the seventh of Rice’s John J. Malone mysteries, the author takes her trio of amateur sleuths - lawyer Malone and his friends Jake and Helene Justus - out of Chicago to New York. There they become embroiled in a murder in their hotel involving a decapitated head on the wrong body. As a rule, the three work together to solve crimes, but in this tale Rice has them each going off on their own, for various reasons, to pursue separate lines of inquiry. It’s an interesting choice and gives the reader something to look forward to, knowing as we do that each character has a piece of the puzzle and when they finally come together to share their info, the picture will become clear.
