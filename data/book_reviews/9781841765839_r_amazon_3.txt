This book is probably the best study out there on the life and experiences of the common Mongol warriors, and makes an extremely fascinating read.  Wayne Reynolds provides eight excellent color plates for this title that really flesh out the text, as do some photographs of modern Mongol families and their ponies, weapons, and yurts.  Some of the information is slightly repetitive from the previous men-at-arms title on the Mongols, but there is enough distinct information in this book to make it worth your money.Read moreRead less
