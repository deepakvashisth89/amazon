I truly love the writings of William Tapply. He is my favorite author. The reason I gave this book a three star is because the ending was once again a surprise. I was not able to piece it all together which kept my interest, But and for me it's a big but, I think Mr Tapply drifted too much on the fishing angle in this book. I felt like I was reading more of a fishing expedition than a mystery. Sorry My Tapply, I do love your books, this was not one of them.
