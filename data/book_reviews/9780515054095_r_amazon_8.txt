Another English manor house, this time cut off by the snowstorms.  Multiple guests invited to a weekend.  Problem is they all have major issues with at least  one person on the guest list.  The manor owner is playing a "people game".  Characters are full bodied and interesting.  Alleyn doesn't appear until two-thirds of the story has transpired.  I really enjoyed it and looked forward to my reading time.  And Ngaio Marsh seldom disappoints. Good choice for a good read.Read moreRead less
