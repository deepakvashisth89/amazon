As far as 'series' books go, the Alex Collier books are some of the best of the genre.  Mr. Handberg makes Alex a real person and his knowledge of the Twin Cities certainly adds authenticity.  The premise of Dead Silence  is based on a real event that I remember vividly.  Just like in the book,  every once in awhile some journalist will attempt to do an update of this  event.  Maybe this book will inspire someone else to dig up some new facts  of the story.At any rate, the book is a wonerful, 'hard-to-put-down'  read.  When you start it, curl up for the long run.
