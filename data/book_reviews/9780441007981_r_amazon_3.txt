This book was rife with tension and danced on the edge of giving you something wild and extraordinary. At every cliff we swerved hard back on to the path of banality.Russo sets up a lot of really good plot points that never seem to resolve in a satisfying way.*spoilers*When the crew finds the alien ship adrift, they explore it with very little event.Save for a few injuries and a death. The finding of the lone survivor in the strange ship felt exciting but lead to what felt like a very quick resolution of them dumping her into space. I feel there could have been more time spent on this character to understand what was actually happening on the alien ship.Even the solution to leave the ship felt very long and boring. What little excitement there was to be had was nothing short of bland. This had the potential to be something akin to Event Horizon meets Nightflyers but falls short.Read moreRead less
