Another story about Dorothy and all the old and some of the new characters bound together.Dorothy was travelling with her uncle to Australia when there was a storm, and Dorothy and a yellow hen were left alone in a chicken coop in the middle of the sea. They managed to get ashore. The yellow hen's name is Bill, and she can talk. They're not exactly in Oz but in the land of Ev. Wheelers frighten them, but the mechanical man comes to their rescue. The land of Ev is without a royal family because the king Nome took them and turned them into ornaments. Ozma from Oz and her companions are coming to save them.Read moreRead less
