This was a gift from my husband because he knows I love “reading through” cookbooks. I also love romantic story telling and this book is both. Nevertheless I’ve tried reading through this book twice and don’t seem to be able to finish it. Capon is laborious with details. I really appreciate his spiritual insights . I’m going to pick it up and try again. I don’t think I’ll use very many of his recipes but I am intrigued by the idea of the leg of lamb being used for several meals. It actually reminds me of how I learned to cook post college using another cookbook, More with Less, written by a Mennonite.Ruth JackinsRead moreRead less
