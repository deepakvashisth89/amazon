I read several comments in an online book club this book was an underrated sci-fi gem. I think they are right.On the 4th of July an alienship crash lands. The army swoop in and then lock down the townspeople. Imposing martial law over several weeks they attempt to excavate the crashed ship in secret. After the soldiers confiscate the townspeoples weapons tensions reach a boiling point.I usually prefer more action but found this book engrossing. The townspeoples hatred of the soldiers, creeping paranoia and the escalating interactions between the army colonel and town sheriff are all believable.  I'll definitely be reading more for James A Moore in the future.Read moreRead less
