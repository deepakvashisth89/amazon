Rating: 2.5/5 stars.I was really excited when I found out I would be receiving this book. I feel that it's never too early to teach our young ones about the importance of being environmentally responsible/conscious individuals.From the book description, "carpooling crocodiles, litter-collecting condors, newspaper-bundling bears", I assumed this book might be in song form... which would have been really cool! (Like the 12-days of Christmas song). I also thought it would at least have been written the same way as it is described in the product info! Instead, the book is not written that way. I was also confused by the title, "3 Pandas Planting"... the author almost made it seem that it would be written that way but in the book it is "3 Pandas. We're planting" and "12 Crocodiles. We're carpooling". I was confused by these discrepancies.Each 2-page picture contains a few short phrases and the picture of how the animals are helping the planet. Each set of pages are counting from 12 to 1 with different animals (bears, pandas, tigers, condors). No detailed descriptions, no explanations, just a title of what the animals are doing, Ex: "11 tigers. We turn off our faucets". This book needs an adult to help explain what the animals are doing and how/why their actions would help the environment.For each number, there are that many of a certain type of animal on the page. The one fun thing was counting the number of animals on each page and describing what silly things some of them were doing (crocodiles packed in to a small car and driving it), but again, some of these concepts might be too difficult for a younger child. On the other hand, the book seemed too simple for a 6+ year old. I found that the pictures were old and outdated looking. The children did not seem too engaged in the book.Overall this book is just 'okay'. It is not something I would recommend that a parent -must- get for their child. I think there could have been more done with this book. Instead, it's just an interesting concept that falls short.Read moreRead less
