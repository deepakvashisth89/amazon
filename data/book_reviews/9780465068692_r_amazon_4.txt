Reflections Of An Affirmative Action BabyI bought this book years ago when it first appeared on the bookstore shelves.  I've read hundreds of books, 99% of them non-fiction.  Until I read this one, I didn't know what a thoroughly enthralling book was.  Of course, I agreed with it wholeheartedly, but that's beside the point.  Carter's writing style, namely logical, is what get's his points across so well.  Even those who might disagree with him would be hard pressed to formulate a valid argument against him, because they are so intelligently crafted.I've since bought a couple of his subsequent books with almost equal admiration.
