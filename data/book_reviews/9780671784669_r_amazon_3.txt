I had difficulty getting through this famous title by Graham Greene. Greene wrote many timeless classics, some favorites of mine like The Quiet American (a better spy novel) and The End of the Affair. Our Man in Havana, meant as an absurdist sendup of the intelligence world, shows its age. It would have amused in its time in the late 50s but it has lost its edge. Yet its by the great Graham Greene therefore I made my way through it, and the last section of the book was more satisfying. The setting was great, and Greene puts us into the heart of a sultry, licentious 50s Havana that’s ripe for revolution. The “hero” Wormold is a vacuum cleaner salesman anti-Bond.
