My pet peeve among children's books is when they are too darn LONG.  It takes forever to read this one aloud as it is written, and my 4-year-old gets pretty bored.  (Of course, your four-year-old's mileage may vary.)  I usually make up a short, one or two sentence synopsis for each page, and "read" that instead of the long paragraph.Also the illustrations are just, well, funky-looking.  They've got a strange, hard-edge, shadowy look that's nothing like the movie.
