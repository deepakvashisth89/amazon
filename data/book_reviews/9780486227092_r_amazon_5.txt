This is an amazing guide for reference material and for helping figure out those issues you might have with hands.  I will say that it's not for a beginner, but if you really want to understand more about the hands in-depth it's a wonderful study guide.There are several pages where you see sketches that show partial hands that are really beautiful, and many parts where it goes in-depth with the hands, actually pointing out which muscles and joints are what, and how they fit into each other and how they move.  It starts out with the back view, palm view, thumb side, little finger side, then goes into the fingers and knuckles, ect.
