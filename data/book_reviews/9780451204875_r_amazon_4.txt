Stella in Heaven is a very fast read.  The idea that a dead woman can communicate with her husband is witty and thought provoking.  I thoroughly enjoyed the way novel is presented -- Roger's Story and Stella's story as each side gives his/her accountability of the issue being discussed.  I thought the character "Mimi" who is Roger's mother is outstanding.  If you are looking for something different, with a twist of articulate humor -- then I recommend the book.  If you've been married for a number of years (like I have) and suddenly your mate dies (which I fortunately haven't) you would like to think you could somehow, someway stay in touch -- atleast until the living can move on.
