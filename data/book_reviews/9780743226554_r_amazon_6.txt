This book has aged very well. It is probably of more value now than when it was first published. In an age of reality-show politics and Twitter rants - it is important to step back and remember there was a gentler wiser time. A time when great men wrote out their thoughts in longhand and revised them before mouthing off. Regardless of your political slant - this book takes you back to a different America.
