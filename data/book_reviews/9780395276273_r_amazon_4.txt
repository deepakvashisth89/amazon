This is a generalized review for all of the Thrush Green series of books.  Each is charming and can be read either as the series progresses or each book can stand alone.  I love the characters and the lovely English countryside and villages that are so wonderfully described by the marvelous author.  She was a former teacher who penned this series and also the Fairacres series, and I am indebted to her for enabling me to have many hours of reading pleasure.  You won't be disappointed with any of these books, and I think it likely that you, too, will wish you lived in Thrush Green.Read moreRead less
