This novel is the first in the Chief Inspector Quantrill series, featuring Detective Sergeant Tait. Quantrill is an old hand, having come up through the ranks and Tait is a young man, a college grad and graduate of the police academy.  I found the repore and conflict between the two to be interesting. The plot involves the death of a young woman, Mary Gedge, who is found lying in a shallow river. In time it becomes evident that she was murdered and Quantrill and Tait set about investigating several suspects. The novel comes to a dramatic and surprising ending.
