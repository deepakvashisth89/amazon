It was immediately apparent when I opened the package that this book was printed in some sort on non-standard way, but it wasn't until I literally reached the last page that I understood why.  It was printed the day after I ordered it.  It was a print-on-demand product!  Nowhere in the product listing was this mentioned.  I thought that I was ordering a brand new book from a reputable publisher (Farrar, Straus and Giroux), not a company that apparently goes about essentially photocopying books (presumably legally).The poor print and image reproduction quality was a real detriment to enjoying the text.
