I really love the Peter Shandy mysteries and have not read this one before.  Great to follow the further relationship of Peter, Helen and the rest of the Balaclavians. As always, Charlotte MacLeod has produced a carefully crafted mystery with multiple characters (with fabulous names!) and despite all the twists in the plot, it all resolves to the reader's satisfaction.
