Reviewed by ShannonDo you like books about working together? Then Pulling Together by Dawn L. Watkins is a terrific book for you.One reason is this book teaches a lesson. A good example is when the horses Ben and Dolly had to work together to pull the logs. Read the book to find out if they pulled them or not. Working together is better than working alone.The next reason is the characters seem like real people. The characters Papa and Matthew were talking about the wagon. Their names seem real like John and Brigges.If you like horses, this book is especially for you. I recommend this book for everybody.Read moreRead less
