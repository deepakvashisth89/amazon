For me this is the best of Robin Schone's novels.  She writes erotic scenes very graphically but in this novel they work better because there is more emphasis on the unfolding of the characters' stories than on the erotica.  Elizabeth, married and pregnant before she was eighteen, is a loving mother to two dear boys and a superlative helpmate for her politician husband but an unhappy, neglected and frustrated wife; she has reached desperation point.  She believes her husband has a mistress and does not understand why but wants to be proactive in winning back his affections so she coerces Ramiel, a well-known rake of scandalous parentage, to teach her the finer points of seduction.  Of course, the relationship that develops is between Elizabeth and Ramiel.
