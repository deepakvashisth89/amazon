One of the things I really like about this book: DuBois shows his appreciation for John Brown's commitment to destroying slavery and working for liberation. But he is firm about discussing how Brown has extra freedom to be this extreme because he is white, and that regardless of his success or failure, racism will ensure that black and brown folks pay a much higher price.
