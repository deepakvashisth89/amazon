This is the third Elvis Cole book and while I think it is the weakest of the first three written it is still a good and solid offering from Robert Crais.Elvis is hired by a top Hollywood director to find the wife and son that he left years earlier. This sounds a piece of cake for Cole but it turns out that the former wife has reasons to be lying low and others are involved who will make Elvis and his partner Joe Pike regret getting involved.I found the novel to be a good read and way better than a lot of writers can produce but just that certain something seemed to be missing here.
