I discovered Cathy when spending time in Mc Lean,Va years ago and became addicted to the sense of humour portrayed in the strips. My daughter and I built up a complete collection of the books as they appeared but unfortunately, when I was checking our book lists after moving house, I couldn't find 'Abs of Steel, Buns of Cinnamon', one of our especial favourites. People will 'borrow' paperbacks....... The problem then was to find another copy. Luckily, Amazon directed me to a secondhand reseller and there we are, complete again.
