J.M. Dillard's novelization of the movie Star Trek VI The Undiscovered Country does a masterful job of filling in the gaps of what was left out of the movie. The addition of reference to events in previous films adds greatly to the enjoyment of this novelization.  I know that due to time constraints the producers of the movie could only put so much on the screen but if they could have expanded it to include all of the scenes and dialogue in this novelization I would gladly have sat through a much longer movie.
