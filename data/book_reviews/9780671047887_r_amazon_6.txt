My first Mary Higgins Clark and I am surprised to say I liked it. My mother-in-law enjoys her books and has recommended them to me. I am not sure why I never took a chance on her. Only read this one because it was given to me by my librarian because of a book suggestion thing that I signed up for.There are many different people involved in this one with the point person being Nell an attractive, well off, practically perfect woman with trama. So basically every female character in a good suspense or romance story. I am impressed with how the plot pointed in so many directions but rarely gave away too much. There are a few eye roll moments and I am not comfortable with psychics so hence the lower rating. Probably would have given an extra star if not for the psychics. I think I will give some of her other stories a try.Read moreRead less
