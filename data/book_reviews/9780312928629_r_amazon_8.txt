I had already read and reviewed Mayer's book "Section Eight" on June 22nd and was favorably impressed (be sure to purchase that one as well as it was still free as of 7/13/2014 last I checked). If it's anything like "Section Eight" expect likeable characters, a great storyline and plenty of action after the initial set-up which generally last no longer than the first third of the book. I'll leave you with an excerpt of my remark I made at the close of my review of "Section Eight" - 'No doubt I'd read more of Bob Mayer in the future' and I am definitely going to honor that commitment by grabbing this book and highly recommend others do the same.
