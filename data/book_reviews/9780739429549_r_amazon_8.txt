I have been a fan of Jane Kirkpatrick for many years.  This is the first time I have ever been disappointed.  I felt the book was disjointed.  I found it very difficult to get into the characters.  Usually I can find the thread that Jane Kirkpatrick uses to sew her stories together.  This time I felt the thread was more of a "tailor tack," just inserted randomly.
