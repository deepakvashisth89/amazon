I'm not sure why everyone gave all the bad reviews.  They must have been looking for something else.  I went in knowing this was a end of the world, reconstruction type thing.  I didn't care about characters or plotting or whatever.  I wanted an instruction manual on rebuilding civilization.  And that is exactly what this is.  Prior to today's e-book quick buck astonishingly foul post-apocalypse writing, I had read nearly every paper book in that genre, and I ranked this book as one of the Top 20 ( granted, near the bottom-but so what ).  This book is big picture stuff.  I've read it twice and will once again over the coming years, no doubt.
