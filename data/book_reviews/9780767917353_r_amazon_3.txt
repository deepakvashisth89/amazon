I recently completed Clubland and deeply thank Mr.Frank Owen for such an enlightening read. I remember the Club Culture Era, but never looked beyond the surface. There is always something that fascinates me about this type of movement. Although those involved teach man a great deal about unity and love, something dark always seems to be present. What Frank Owen says regarding utopian movements, "Criminals have a long history from- the French Revolution to the hippies- of exploiting the liberties opened up by utopian movements," was such a simple statement, yet illuminating for myself. It is the exploiting criminals that taint these movements, not the ideas of such movements. So Author Frank Owen has helped me to officialize many thoughts I have had on the matter. I truly had a revelation. I was deeply moved and thank Mr. Owen so much for his dedication. I so admire reporters like Mr. Owen that seek to inform the public about the underbelly of society. We need more investigative reporters with Frank's passion, for his kind is a dying breed and it is heartbreaking to see the news become a profit seeking buisness. Frank Owen has many articles and books that I plan on reading in the near future. Being a truth seeker, Clubland was an inspiration.
