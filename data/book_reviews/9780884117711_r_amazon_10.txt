This book, by Baum, is an interesting read, written before The Wizard of Oz, and re-released after Oz, and re-titled.It is quite moral and well-meaning, but oddly violent for these straight laced times. Heads and limbs are regularly hacked off and put back together, and 3 wise men are ground up to mincemeat and re- constituted into one, I was puzzled by this, but remembered that traditional children's reading did not protect children, but exposed them through myth and fable to reality, e.g., Hans Christian Anderson, and did not always spare the emotions, e.g., Eugene  Field.So this book is a worthwhile read, though I'm not sure if modern parents would read it out loud to their children, and it's well written, nicely illustrated and excellent value for money, from Eltanin Publishing.Maybe, stick to The Wizard of Oz, and the related books, but, only the L Frank Baum books as the later writers' books descend into chaos and rely on Knockabout, while Baum's are wonderful, warm and inventive (The Road to Oz is my favourite).Read moreRead less
