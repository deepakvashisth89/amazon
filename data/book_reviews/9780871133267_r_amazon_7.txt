I'm one of Patricia Highsmith biggest fan and I have read almost all of her work. Found in the Street is one of her last novels, and maybe its her weakest one. This tale of a married couple who fall for the same woman,  can't compete with the sexual tension of her earlier novel: The Talented  Mr. Ripley. Patricia Highsmith is a writer who must be read, but to fully  appreciated her you must read the Ripley's books; A dog's Ramsom; People  who knock on the door; or strangers on a train.
