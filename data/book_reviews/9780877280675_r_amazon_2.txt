This is NOT a modern, simplified interpretation of a few Taoist cultivation techniques. This is a REAL thing, a translation of thousands of years old, complete Alchemy Manual, that fully synchronizes with direct LINEAGE teachings. Enough said for a serous Tao practitioner.Do not be confused by a title "Taost Yoga", this book is not an interpretation of Taoist practice from Yoga or Buddhist perspective. This is a faithful translation of an authentic Taoist Nei Dan (Internal Elixir) scripture. A translator, Charles Luk was a Chan Buddhism (not Tao practice) initiate, and so he did sometimes selected words close to his tradition. However, in my opinion, he did this translation from the heart, based on the own experiences, with best and sincere aspirations and so did a wonderful job.The book is not a step-by-step practical guide for the beginner practitioners though, but everybody with open mind would benefit from it, at least as from a beautiful mystical tale of the Immortal Practice. The author has omitted some verbal transmission secrets or instructions that would depend on the individual's character and achievement level. However, the listed steps, inner scenery and topic elaborations are totally accurate and what they should be, the immensely helpful pointers from a direct experience of an enlightened master to a sincere and dedicated practitioner."Taoist Yoga" would powerfully augment daily practice of any Nei Dan student, enhancing the understanding and steadying the heart.
