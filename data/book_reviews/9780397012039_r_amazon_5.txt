Wonderful stand-alone novel by this author who "nailed' Florida continually during his writing career. This is fascinating from a number of perspectives:  This is written in the "grand hotel" tradition that follows the people, their interrelationships and interactions. Character development is exquisite. This is a trenchant commentary on the nasty exploitation of low-life developers, political corruption, and the like, which could have been written yesterday rather than during the 1980s. There is satisfying punishment of these people by the forces of nature, and the undeserved punishment of the innocent, and the undeserved suffering of the people who are exploited, even if they do not end up suffering capital punishment by hurricane. There is even a love story.  There are heros, one in particular, as well as villains.  Take your medicine: some of the commentary and implied commentary in the plot may feel like it is not good for you, but it is. And enjoy.Read moreRead less
