I preface this review by making it clear that my interest in heaven-related media is independent from the fact that I’m blessed with great physical health and I intend to live until at least the year 2065 in my current lifetime or longer (but I understand that death is one of the few areas that a person may not have a say in when they die). Anyhow, I also consulted with my amazing husband before getting this book and he had a feeling that this book would interest me. Additionally, as an aspiring writer, this book helps give me added insight into how to write afterlife related stories as an author. This book Stella In Heaven by Art Buchwald is about a woman named Stella Folger who sets out to connect her husband to a new wife  after she dies before him. Stella Folger does this from her new home in heaven and she also uses one of her wishes to contact him. The good; the author tells the truth about how the dynamic of Stella Folgers dying before her husband affected them both. Additionally, details are drawn between the backgrounds that Stella Folgers and her husband Roger Folgers came from. The caveats; I may be slightly biased because I’m blessed to have a mother-in-law who treats me as family and accepts me as a daughter in law but the writing leaves scant details on what maybe would help Stella Folgers and her mother in law Mimi get along better/at least be a little more civil to each other. Stella Folgers, although a self-confident and good wife when she was alive, is even a little guilty of acting standoffish and snobbish in how she treats her mother-in-law even in death (I want to explain more in detail about that yet I understand that others looking at this review may want to buy the book and still purchase the story). The other caveat; Some people may like the ending but I only wish that there could have been a follow-up book written to share how Stella Folgers goes on in heaven after the first book and I do believe that even if a person dies way before their spouse that there are spiritually enriching activities andor happy possibilities in their afterlife that can exist.
