Quite good. Good facts, sustainable interpretations, good characterizations--probably as accurate as 150 years distance can give us. Lee comes across perhaps as TOO pious. Might have emphasized other elements of his personality, as well. Chamberlain very admirable. I'd have to read more on Grant to assess that treatment. Useful maps, well-chosen actions. Glad to see a reasonably full treatment of the trap the Union forces walked into and out of at the North Anna River. Could have done more with the foul up at Saylor's Creek. A bit more Longstreet-worship than I'd like, but makes the trilogy consistent. Very readable, although seemed to pad for length at times. Good job.
