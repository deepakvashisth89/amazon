Selby is my favourite author of all time. Bar none. If you don't own every one of his books you are missing out. Drawing inspiration from the people and situations he sees around him his storytelling ability is compulsive. Well known for his reinvention of grammer his unique writing style takes you on a journey in a car with no brakes. If his ability to grasp your attention is not enough the depth of his character definition is at once deafening and hyper real.. Never an easy read Selby picks subject matter that will leave you uncomfortably shifting in your seat. Highly recommended.Read moreRead less
