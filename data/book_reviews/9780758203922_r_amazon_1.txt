This is another scintilatin and titivating book by Robin Schone and I loved it.  In fact I didn't read this book I devoured it would be a more accurate descreiption. The other two books by this author 'The Lover' & 'Gabriel's Woman' had a similar tenor to them whereas this book is very differentThe Characters have been beautifully crafted and blend with the narrative perfectly.  Elizabeth Petre is high born (during the Victorian era) and has suffered thru 16yrs of a loveless marraige, arranged by her father. Elizabeth has provided the heir and the spare and is very aware of what she has missed out on during that time. She seeks helpin the only place she knows - (Ramiel the Bastard Sheikh) when she approaches he realizes "she asked him what no woman had ever asked him to teach her the sexual techneques that Arab woman use to please a man" Elizabeth wants this in order to tantalise her husband; whom she still loves.Robin Schone has done another wonderful job, more twists and turns that the race way.
