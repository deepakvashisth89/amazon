I lived in the Bay Area and worked in San Francisco for 5 years in the mid 60's.  I don't know how I missed this book when it was first written.  Too busy having a family I guess.  I finally read it recently and the next two books in the series.  I just ordered books 4 and 5, so am really into this series now.  I loved this book and could not put it down.  The characters are vibrant and described in such great detail that I feel like I know them!  Which is why I keep ordering the next book.
