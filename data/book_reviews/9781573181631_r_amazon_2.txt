Though I thought Vol. XIII was tepid, Vol. XV is anything but!  Being a jaded scifi/horror/mystery reader, if an anthology doesn't have kick-ass  short stories as openers, middlers, and closers as well, then I generally  rate it below a two.Fortunately, this volume is one of the rare  exceptions.  Boy does it have terrific stories!I too am also a  contestant trying to get into this superb anthology.  I've read and entered  since the beginning, though with inconsistent output.  Let's hope I and the  others who haven't gotten a chance yet to be recognized for their  writing/yarning talent will be in next year's anthology.There's only one  niggling afterthought that I have to express here.  Is it me, or have the  L. Ron Hubbard  "How to write" articles within the newer volumes  become increasingly obscure and irrelevant?
