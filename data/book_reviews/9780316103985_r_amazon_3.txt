Every American in love with this country has to read this book.  What a treasure!  Just amazing to read about the intricacies and challenges our founding fathers met, and overcame, in the process of helping to found our truly special country.  The book includes a separate piece that includes extensive details on each of the men that signed the Constitution.  I also liked that each chapter begins with a quote about the feelings and sentiments of the people at the Convention - that added depth really helped almost transport me to the convention itself.
