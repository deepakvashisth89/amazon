This book, the 11th of Rice’s John J. Malone mysteries, begins with a grim discovery that’s very untypical of the series. As it turns out (minor spoiler here), it isn’t as grim as we think, and ultimately the book is as fun and amusing as the other Malone books. Malone and his friends Jake and Helene Justus are their usual reliable selves in every book, so it’s often the ancillary characters that make or break the story, and in this case they’re fun and interesting. Most are associated with a cosmetics company, and others are associated with a funeral home (hence the hearse of the title).
