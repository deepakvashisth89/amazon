Compared to some of the author's other books in this series, this one is not as well-done as the others.  Although there is some great character development, the plot gets a little muddled after an initial interesting and smooth flowing set-up. Still, if you are a real Maggody fan, I think you'll like the book; it is an easy read. However, if you are a real Martian fan, this book will not deliver the type of Sci-Fi world that most Sci-Fi readers typically enjoy. Having said that, I believe those who can laugh at themselves and their own interest in Science Fiction, should give it a try.
