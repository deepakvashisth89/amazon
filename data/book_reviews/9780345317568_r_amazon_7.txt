This is book one of a series of nine.  I read these in the 1980's - 90's and decided to re-read them recently.  I cannot put them down.  I am already on book seven.  Len Deighton was a fantastic writer of cold war espionage.  The characters that he creates will stay with you for your lifetime.  The office politics will have you laughing out loud.  The meals and beverages that his characters enjoy will have you pouring up a gin martini or a tawny port.  Very highly recommended.Read moreRead less
