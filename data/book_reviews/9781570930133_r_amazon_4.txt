The journey through the pages of Flower Child was like a journey home for me. As a child born in 1960, I am old enough to remember the flower power period and the summer of love while I was not yet old enough to participate. Flower Child brought me back to those days, to the emotional feelings of love, possibility and uncertainty that permeated the world of my childhood. The author's journey as portrayed in the pages of Flower Child reflects the search for meaning and connection that I suspect most that were touched by the love generation have experienced. Flower Child has, to a large degree, helped me to refine a lifelong and core belief that humans have the potential to live in Love.
