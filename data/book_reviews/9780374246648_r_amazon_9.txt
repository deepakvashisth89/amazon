Quite literally the best book I have ever read. The author Weidensaul does an amazing job at leading the reader along a journey or grand history of planet Earth's extinct, soon to be extinct and possibly resurrected species and how their destinies have been so irreversibly entwined with our own. The book is beautifully written and makes you wonder if at points he is describing the fictional world of Pandora or our own; so vivid and beautifully worded are his descriptions.The thing that really made me respect the book however was Scott's ability to critically engage with every opinion he came across on his journeys. It would seem that he never let his own pre-judgement affect each story, and has obviously spent countless hours researching each individual story/species extensively and provides the reader with a choice to make up their own mind on the matter.Overall I highly recommend this to anyone interested in zoology, a history of extinct species and the natural world.
