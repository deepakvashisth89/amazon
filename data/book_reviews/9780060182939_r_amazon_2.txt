I had to choose a book off of a list for my graduate cross and chose this one.  Best pick ever.  The author draws you into her story about her immigration into America from Hong Kong and I was able to relate to her experiences in so many ways, despite being born an American.  I love her writing and details and how she incorporates her first language and culture seamlessly with her story, without making it a history or course lesson.  I laughed and cried and wished I could speak with her today, that's how amazing I found the book.  I love to read, and have to read often for school and because I'm a teacher.  I recommend this book to everyone because of how powerful the story is, no matter what your background or reason for reading.Read moreRead less
