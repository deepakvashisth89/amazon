Black No More is a tale from the 1930s that has continued to be relevant through the ages, to some degree at least. Max, a young black dude, spies the girl of his dreams at a New Year party. The only problem is that she is white and informs him less than politely that she "doesn't dance with blacks". Max's disappointment turns to excitement some time later when he reads that an old acquaintance has perfected a method of turning black people Caucasian.Before long, almost every dark-skinned individual is queuing up to become white, and business is booming. Of course, this doesn't sit well with everyone, and the rest of the story is a contrast between the success of the "Black-No-More" company and those who oppose it. The novel is satirical and often light-hearted whilst at the same time painting a fascinating and disturbing picture of race relations.
