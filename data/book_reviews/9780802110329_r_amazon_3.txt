I recently taught a class on Mukherjee, and this novel was a huge hit!  I love the way Mukherjee uses the idea of incarnations as a springboard for the narrator's transformation.  I also love the way she ties in the story of Kali (goddess of death) into her tale.  If you are rusty on your knowledge of hindu gods, you may want to look a few references up.Mukherjee also does an excellent job of portraying the modern immigrant experience -- through a compelling tale.Read moreRead less
