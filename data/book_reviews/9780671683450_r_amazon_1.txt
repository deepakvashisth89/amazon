If you are interested in the mob - and you like tell-all biographies - you will probably love this book! With that being said, you should know that this book is written from a very different take or perspective on the mob life. It's a very candid and explicit story of a girl who was sucked into this life at a very young age as a "moll" or mobster's lady friend. She was obsessed with being with mobsters even if it meant degrading herself in order to do so. I was surprised at how much detail the book actually goes into. She led a very rough and risky life hanging around with very dangerous men and getting herself into very dangerous situations at times. The book tells all about her childhood, background, family and how she was raised. In the end, she turns informant for various reasons that are all her own.
