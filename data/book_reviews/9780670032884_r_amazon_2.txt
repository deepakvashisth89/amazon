I didn't realize that this is the 2nd book I've read by MMM.  My book-searching-bot presented the title and, intrigued by the description and reviews, I decided to take the plunge.In some ways this is a 'good for you' type of book. Since we live in a world where more and more people are enjoying their echo chambers (myself included), it is truly helpful to have a book full of characters I would tend to avoid or judge in real life:  Someone who gets let out early after a murder conviction, a wayward 'tween' whose associates are drug users and dealers and whose single mom is too high to engage, a do-gooder who talks too much and whose main identity is in rescuing others.  The author really was able to write all of these characters (and more) in a really sympathetic, human way.While the ending was abrupt, amidst my vague hope that in this fictional universe everything would tie up with a big happy ending where nice things happen to nice people, it was a interesting interrupted narrative which suggests that life just goes on like this: People are disappointed or hurt or inept. People disappoint and hurt others sometimes intentionally, sometimes not. People sometimes make and effort in their relationships with others and sometimes they phone it in or cocoon to avoid responsibility.It felt REAL, but not fun, pleasant, or amusing.Read moreRead less
