Like all Dennis Wheatley's books this is one I couldn't put down. It was one of the few I hadn't read before so I didn't really know what to too expect. I suppose in 1950's speak it was a ripping good yarn and  refreshingly politically incorrect particularly with the characters he has created. I know that Dennis Wheatley's understanding of the "Prince of Darkness" and his friends was quite extensive I only hope it turns out to be untrue.Whether you believe in the subject or not just let your imagination go and enjoy the story, and be glad your safe at home!
