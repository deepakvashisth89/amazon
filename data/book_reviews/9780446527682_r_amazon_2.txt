Roger Angell was one of the most prominent writers of baseball essays of the 20th and early 21st century. I have read nearly all of his books and loved them all. This one didn't disappoint. Although I thought it was a small step down from some of his other writings. I think my reasoning for this is that his focus is mainly on one player, David Cone, and not a particular MLB season like many of his other books. But still this is a very worthwhile read for any baseball fan. He started this book as look into what made a pitcher. The mechanics of it all and toil it takes on a person. But it quickly turned into how a pitcher tries to survive as he gets older when David Cone, the subject of this book, struggles through the 2000 season with constant injuries and mishaps. This is also a good biography of David Cone as he was one of the better pitchers in the 1990's. If you are a fan of Roger Angell's writing, like me, you should not pass this one up.Read moreRead less
