This was a great little book of stories.  I wanted something I could read that was short and entertaining.  The only story I did not "get" was the "Stone Woman."  I think because it was totally fantasy and I am more a mystery, thriller, horror type of reader.  It was interesting though and I kept coming back for more and trying to figure it out.  On the flip side, my favorite story was "Raw Material."  We are all left with questions on that one, but we share those questions and don't feel we are the only ones left out of the loop.I recommend this book as I enjoyed it very much.KateRead moreRead less
