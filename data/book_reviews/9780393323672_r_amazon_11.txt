WOW...I am exhausted after reading this novel. It is an excellent thriller with a constant thread of obsessiveness that echoes through the story like a heartbeat. The protagonist is not swayed or distracted by anything. All he wants is his goal. His way of achieving that goal escalates as he is rejected. Everyone around him seems to be scheming and gossiping but it never shifts his focus. On one level he seems quite normal, on another he seems disturbed. It is difficult to tell if he is talking to himself or there is another person with him at times. Reality and fantasy mingle here very comfortably.Read moreRead less
