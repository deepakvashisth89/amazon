When I was in college I had to take a non-American, non-European history class.  I took a class on Nigeria's history.  This book was required reading for the class.  I lost the book.  I had been trying to find a copy for many years now.  I wanted to reread it.  As all history is alive, so is this book, as it is historical.  God's Bits of Wood is an excellent fictional telling of a truly historic real life event.
