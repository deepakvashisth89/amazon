Having finished "Youth in Revolt" over a year ago and feeling slightly dead inside after having put such colorful characters to rest, I was more than relieved to find there was a second book of insight into the mind of the incorrigible Nick Twisp. Funny, sarcastic, and gripping, "Revolting Youth" is a great escape from reality and a good chance to delve into the conscience of daring Nick and his one and only Sheeni.
