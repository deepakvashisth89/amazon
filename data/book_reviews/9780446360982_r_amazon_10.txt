This was one of the strangest books I have ever read.  It was like three books in one.  The first part was really boring and I was about to give up.  Then Helena started work as a nurse and the book picked up and I really enjoyed that part and liked Helena as a character and admired her strength to work in such conditions especially coming from her back ground.  Then she came home and turned into a weak nyphomaniac who spent all the time having sex with her husband and the book became really Mils and Boon.  It was really excellent in places and not so good in others but worth a read.Read moreRead less
