This is a short, stunning Japanese novel told in a simplistic style underlain by layers of meaning to be mined by the reader long after he/she has finished the book.  It's a fast, engaging read that doesn't go at all where I expected it to go.  The characters are deep and complex - they have deep and complex relationships, conflicts, guilts, longings, failures, sensualities, connivances, etc.  The symbolism of the book is grounded largely in the tea ceremony and objects used therein.
