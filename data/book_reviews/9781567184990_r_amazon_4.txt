I still remember my high school biology classes where we spent hours memorizing how scientists classify every living thing known.  Sometimes it seemed the only distinction from one type of critter and another was perhaps a spot in the retina.  Well, maybe not that bad, but it felt that way much of the time.  So it is with Destiny of Souls.  Dr. Michael Newton is a scientist and he has to understand every little distinction and has to give it a name and a place in some category or sub-category.  By the time you finish the book, you feel the end is long overdue and anti-climactic.However, don't let this detract from the importance of this book.  I think someday, it will go down as one of the classics.  The understanding one gets about the life in the spirit give you a better understanding of your spiritual life as a human being.  It gives you a place in time and space; it gives you purpose;  it gives you direction.Listen, you can read all of Gregg Braden and Fred Alan Wolf you want but, you won't walk away with the personal understanding of your life like you do when you finish reading Dr. Michael Newton's Journey of Souls and Destiny of Souls.I recommend this to all.Read moreRead less
