I've never been to Tower Grove Park in St. Louis, though after reading this book I feel I know it well.  Robert Knittel reminds his readers of the joys and rewards of walking for pleasure, stopping to observe and breathe in details, taking detours recommended by a curious canine guide (the  author's airdale Herb believes in taking the trails less traveled).
