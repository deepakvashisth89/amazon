Read one John Grisham (not from here) and was hooked. Just read this and an excellent read. Have thought a bit about why these books are so good and have decided that it is because they examine in detail how people behave and what makes them tick. A sort of people watching in print. The John Grisham books I have read are not all out action books but rather considered and well written tales which consider emotion and human interaction mixed up with a good dose of legal detail. The plot lines are solid but not over complicated but the real skill lies in the creation of the characters and how they develop as the story unfolds.
