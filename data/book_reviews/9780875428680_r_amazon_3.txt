If you are interested in learning about some of the worlds magical mystical traditions it is a good start, or if you are looking for a reference manual. There are extensive lists and charts. I like the illustrated sections like the magical alphabets section. Do you need to understand all of these complex systems to find meaning and peace in this existence? I don't think so. But this is a catalog of categories to enrich your symbolic experience of the world and to make sense of things for a curious mind reaching out for understanding or order. Do you need a symbolic system to understand the world around you? Here is a formula for you. Here are many formulas for you. I love the charts. I love the language and the pictures. I like to learn about the planets and stars and colors and elements and symbols and days of the week. The opening chapter on Axioms is the best. The book is summed up in the opening statement: "Beliefs are the tools of the magician...choose the beliefs that are both true and appropriate to your will and intention"  knowing you are choosing your beliefs on some level is a powerful realization.
