I'm about 3/4 through this book. If you're thinking of reading it, you should- IF you are not sensitive to race issues/mild fetishization of middle eastern culture. I didn't see this in any of the other reviews that I skimmed. The main character and love interest bond over a middle eastern sex book similar to the karma sutra. She calls him the "Bastard Sheikh" in her mind throughout the book (I think she literally just started thinking of his as his actual name and we're starting to get to the final act). It's dehumanizing, and pulled me out of the romance. I know it's historical and all, but this lady can only mull about the exotic token brown guy so much before I'm like.. ok.. enough.*Mild Spoilers*On our hero's part, I had a hard time figuring out why he wanted her so badly (as anything other than a fun conquest). They have nothing in common, and the love blooms in a few 45 minute meetings at 4am over the course of one week. Their first meeting, she can smell the perfume of the lady he just slept with... idk. The book made a lot of her extreme inexperience vs. his wealth of experience, and the emotional attachment on his part just rang a bit false. BUT, the book is still very well written and I will be checking out more of this author.Read moreRead less
