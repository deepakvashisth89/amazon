This is my all-time favorite Jayne Ann Krentz book because rereading it is like settling into a comfortable armchair in front of a fire on a rainy night.....wait!  That's the great scene at the beginning where the hero (sopping wet from a rainy Northwest night) meets the heroine (in her bathrobe at home) and realizes he should have met her, not her sister, months ago.  I won't repeat the plot since the other reviewers have done so but I will say that there is great pleasure in reading about romance between two mature people with conservative values and real working lives.
