The Kabbalah is a Jewish mystical system (based on The Zohar), yet, according to the Kabbalah Center (of Yehuda, Michael, and Karen Berg), it has universal appeal.  The goal of the Kabbalah is to open ancient, hidden secrets to enhance life and well-being.  The approach of the Kabbalah (or the Berg's version of it) is similar to New Thought-New Age philosophy because the focus is on positive thinking--or the practice of ancient principles--and "making things happen."  It is perhaps reminiscent of an earlier movement called Jewish Science, which was a Jewish version of Christian Science.  I like this book because it is a practical, not just theoretical, approach to spiritual truth.  Truth is not just a set of ideas, but something to be acted upon, which truly makes a difference.
