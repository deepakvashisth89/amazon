This is one of my favorite Star Trek novelizations, with a very interesting take on the Romulans. It was originally published in 1987, and this is a reprint. I read the story when it first came out, and amid a number of novelizations that blur together, this one stands out.It is part of the Rihannsu series. Most of the action takes place on the Romulan planets, and is seen from the perspective of a deep cover operative, so if you are looking for lots of action on the Enterprise, this is not the book for you. If, however you are looking for a really good story, good characterizations, and good writing, I recommend this book.Read moreRead less
