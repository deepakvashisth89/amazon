"The Discovery of Heaven" is by far the most profound and dense novel I have ever read. And I'm an English major. The novel is a bit testosterone-driven, as should be expected of a novel written by a male. The main themes in this novel are the genuis-psyche, human meaning in life, and friendship. Yes--there is love, religion, travel (among so many other important things), but this novel is hugely grounded in philoshopy.When I read this novel, I discovered so much more about beauty than before. Max and Onno's friendship is just unbreakable, although confusing at times. Ada's character will disturb some feminists, but like I said, the novel is "written by a male." Don't let that keep you from reading it though -- I remember reading Angela Carter (an awesome lyrical stylist by the way) and saying to myself "yeah, their are no positive males in any of these stories -- so what?" Keep that in mind when Onno goes on his occassional ego-trips...This novel may be long, but because there are only two main characters, it is impeccably easy to follow, even when Onno OR Max drop off into one of their hyper-intellectual diatribes.
