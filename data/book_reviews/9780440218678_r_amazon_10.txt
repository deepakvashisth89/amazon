Love this author. I've only recently discovered him and rarely disappoints. This is another mesmerising read. He is as good if not better than Stephen King so not sure why he is not as well known. Characters well drawn and believable. His writing is captivating and quirky at times which fits so well with the strange and often dreamlike atmosphere he creates. The evil he portrays somehow manages to be obscenely physical and spiritual at the same time. He creates an immersive world where you feel constantly on a knife edge of fear, where what seems a normal world with very ordinary people can shift at any time into a nightmare. And yet you also feel the rollercoaster of emotions, love, loss and quandaries along with the characters. A brilliant writer and a must read for horror fans.Read moreRead less
