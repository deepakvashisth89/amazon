David Quammen is an unparalleled writer of natural history.  This collection of essays is both informative and engaging and one savors each with the intent not to squander the pleasure of accompanying him on his explorations.  His respect and wonder for the natural world is always founded on solid science; nature herself is up to the task of being awesome, and Quammen never stoops to cheap wows or sentiments.
