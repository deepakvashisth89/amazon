I made a mistake by thinking that this book would be a relatively interesting read and an insight into Ralph Nader and his analysis of capitalism and American Society. I know that this book has ben very well reviewd but I just couldn't get through it as it is dense and perhaps not for those who are general readers like myself, especially British and other non-Americal reraders. I am aware that Ralph Nader is a very important social and political commentator but this "Reader" did nothing to keep me gripped.
