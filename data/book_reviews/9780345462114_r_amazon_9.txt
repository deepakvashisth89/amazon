I am quite a fan of Carol Goodman - she writes intelligent mysteries with more than an air of literature in the subject matter, which always makes her books interesting for those also obsessed by literature. The whole world of stained glass conservation is researched well for this novel and I really enjoyed dipping into an 'arty-farty' world I know nothing about.However, the plot is a little weak and I kind of guessed the ending which really disappointed me - but it won't put me off buying her next book!Read moreRead less
