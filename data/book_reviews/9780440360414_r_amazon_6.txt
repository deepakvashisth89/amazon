I read this many years ago, and several years later I had been assigned Henry James' The Aspern Pages in a American literature course.  I kept thinking that I had read this somewhere before.  By the time I finished the James novel, I realized Plunket had gently taken the plot of The Aspern Papers and moved it to late 20th century L.A.  This dramatically increased my appreciation for Henry James -- I really had not thought of him as a comic writer.  The only problem was my classmates, and even my professor, couldn't see why I had found The Aspern Papers so funny.I do wish Amazon was able to fix the problems with the Kindle edition.
