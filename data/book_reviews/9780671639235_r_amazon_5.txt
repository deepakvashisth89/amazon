For anyone who is interested in the formation of the 1st ever Special Forces Group, 10th SFG, this an excellent point of view from the man who would become its commander. While Col Bank is a little cocky and self-centered in his view of himself, he has earned every right to be for the extraordinary achievements he accomplished for his country and the accomplishments he did for the SOF community.
