Excellent, one of my all time favorite books. Absolutely non-mainstream pre-history concepts and written from a strong womanist perspective. If you're familiar with Merlin Stone, Marija Gimbutas, and/or if you're interested in the Divine feminine and women's history then this is a great book for you.
