I think this book is probably a classic. It is the story of one man's spiritual quest/journey through the advanced spiritual communities and "Initiations" that existed around the time of Christ. It is not presented as the story of "Jesus Christ" of the Christian religion. Rather, it is a portrayal of a life that would historically have been similar to his. It describes the teachings that would have been part of his spiritual growth and development and a context for the miracles now attributed to him.
