This is an interesting collection of songs, and a lot of music for the money.  I was pleasantly surprised to stumble across obscure songs I had nearly forgotten and enjoyed singing again.  The songs are grouped by topic or theme, which makes for some strange musical bedfellows; there is no way to predict what song will come next!  One drawback is that there are only words and chords, so unless you recognize the song, you will not be able to figure out the melody.  One other word of caution--make sure when you order it that you know if you are getting the smaller size or the larger Leader book--I thought I was getting the smaller size which is more portable and easier to handle while singing (to me), but ended up getting the larger one, which feels a little unwieldy.
