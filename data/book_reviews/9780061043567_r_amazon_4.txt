I have to say that Sayers and Christie are two of the best English mystery writers I've read.  No dripping gore, no sex (in the modern sense of the word), just good plots and excellent writing.  I can recommend both of these authors to any age without worrying that some mother is going to come back and smack me upside of the head.  I found them both when I was in the 8th grade and have reread them ever since, I'm 67 now.
