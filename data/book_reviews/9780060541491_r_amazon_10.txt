At first I was a little drawn back from the title "Sexy" but I ended up buying anyway because it was a Joyce Carol Oates book and I have never been disappointed before and I szrely wasn't this time. She just knows how to portray a character, how to get you to really feel his confusion, his pains and to really understand what's going on inside. I often find myself thinking I want to know what's going to happen afterwards because she makes her characters turn out very special when at a first glance they might have just seemed average.
