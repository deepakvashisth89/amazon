Unfortunately for Rustad the bar has been raised ever so high for historical works of fiction.  No, Rustad isn't Michael Shaara or Herman Wouck, but his book is entertaining and does serve to present many with  some insight into WW II.  Yes, there are some inaccuracies, but it is a  work of fiction, a point many critical reviewers seem to overlook.  I say  bravo to Rustad!  Those looking for something on a par with The Killer  Angels, will be disappointed, but if you enjoy history and are looking for  a novel that can bring you a few hours of enjoyment, this book will be  appreciated.
