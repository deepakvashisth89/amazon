Ms. Joan Smith is one of my favorite authors, I just love her easy-going writing style, smart characters, and witty dialogues.  I usually read her work in Regency Romance, with couple of Gothics once in a while.  This story was taken place in modern time when our heroine went to stay with her famous violinist uncle in Toronto, there were kidnapping, burglaries, love, laughs, and the usual wits.  And her research on antique violin and art collections was very interesting.  My only dissatisfaction was that I wish Cassie could contribute more to capture the culprit, especially towards the end, to give her a stronger characterization and intelligence.
