Winterson is my favorite author so to give it three stars is not easy. The book is a collection of short stories, rich with symbolism and metaphor; however, the writing seems disjointed at times and difficult to follow. There are sections where I found myself lost in the verbiage and imagery. There are some good lines (e.g., "The line between life and death is a couple of inches at most. The width of a door that connects two rooms." p. 120), but sometimes the stories were opaque.Read moreRead less
