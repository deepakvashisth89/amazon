This book is crammed with affirmations related to the fears and horrors  every artist ends up going through at some point. Whereas I am not too keen  in affirmations, I found Mr Maisel's words extremely conforting and  intelligent. Although the affirmations are inspiring, this is a book with  practical advice as well, unlike so many others that adress similar  questions. It also contains a lot of quotations from famous artists who  apparently went through similar ordeals. "Affirmations for  artists" adresses many different kinds of disciplines and is  definitely a great gift for an artistically inclined person.
