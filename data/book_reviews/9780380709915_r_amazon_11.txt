I read this book having read Liam’s Story first.Wow what a book. Firstly although classed as a classic love story l would say it’s anything but. This is a family saga.The trial and tribulations that Louisa, Robert and Edward and their families go through make for a fantastic read with a lot of history thrown in.Great characters that you come to care for. Great story and plot that keeps you turning the page and won’t want to put down.Overall a fantastic book. Highly recommendedRead moreRead less
