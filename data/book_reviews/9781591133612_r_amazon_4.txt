House Thran was banished from Adylonis after Pentar Thran allied himself with the Kel and Telmet against the other Houses.  Thran Keep was held by the Tialsar until the return of the Thran Jarl.  House Thran has been working toward their return to Adylonis for generations now and Andalarn is reaping the benefits.  Andalarn returns to Adylonis and is given the keep by Adyl, one of the Tialsar priests.  He leaves Loncaer in charge of the keep and returns for his family, his wife and five sons.  During their trip back to Adylonis, their ship ends up in trouble and they end up on a beach.  Andalar and Lady Kalene lose two of their sons.  The eldest, Daepar, ends up wounded.  There just happens to be a Kel Healer in the nearest town.Baliak was with Pentar Thran during the battle that forced the Telmet into the mountains.  The Kel and Telmet are very long lived.  Baliak goes to the aid of Andalarn's son.  Baliak forms a relationship with the family.  House Suum is once again creating trouble on Adylonis.  This time, House Suum is after the Kel.  Once again, House Thran comes to the aid of the Kel and Telmet.Who will win this war?  Will the Kel and Telmet be annihilated this time?  Even with Thran's power, will it be enough to go against the combined forces of House Suum and the Tialsar Priests?  What about Daepar, how will he manage House Thran in his father's absence?  And what about the Prophecy?  Will Andalarn signal the end, or the beginning?H. David Blalock has done a wonderful job with THRAN REBORN.  He does a fantastic job of laying the groundwork for a series of books.  The characters are all well-rounded and believable.  I especially enjoyed Andalarn's turmoil as he came to grips with his power and had to learn to control it.  Loncaer takes on a mentoring role with Daepar, as does Baliak with Andalarn.  Having read THRAN REBORN, I can't wait for the rest of the series to come out!  I highly recommend this first book in THE THRAN CHRONICLES to all lovers of fantasy!Read moreRead less
