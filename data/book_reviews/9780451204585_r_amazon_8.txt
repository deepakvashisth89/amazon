I am constantly astonished at the many layers of detective John Darnell.  In each of McCarver's books his star character displays nuances of knowledge, expression, language and style.Unlike so many serial mysteries, this detective is never the cliche of himself so many others seem to become.  I appreciate McCarver's innovation and imagination as his characters and his stories continue to break new ground.Interesting twists, and, as always, a surprising and thought provoking ending.
