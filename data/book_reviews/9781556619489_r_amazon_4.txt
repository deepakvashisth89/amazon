I had never read any Lawana Blackwell books before Amazon offered (The Gresham Chronicles Book #1) for free. I loved it! I'm not a romance novel reader nor am I a religious reader - usually - but this series has me hooked. After reading book 1 I had to buy book 2 just to see what happens next....as I will be buying each book until I complete this series. These books take you into their world (and isn't that why most of us read - to travel away from where we are?). I'm not going to go into lengthy detail about the story (you need to read it) I will just let you know - If you like sweet period novels, characters that have big personalities that you will cheer for, dislike and cringe at their behavior, laugh with, and feel the heartache right along with them - then you will like this series. I was a bit worried when I started the 1st book that since it has religious tones to it it may get preachy...I was very pleased.... it doesn't. So, if your looking for a feel good period book to read - grab a cup of tea - sit back and enjoy your travels.Read moreRead less
