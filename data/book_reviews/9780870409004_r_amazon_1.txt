I foolishly lent this book years ago and never saw it again.  Happily, this copy in great condition is back in my library.  It is a guide map to the points on the body used in some forms of Asian healing, like accupuncture, accupressure and other therapies grouped in general by ailment.  The maps are exceptional and there are instructions on how to measure an individual to find the points if the ouch doesn't find it for you.  It is a great introductory learning system to another method of learning what the body is saying.
