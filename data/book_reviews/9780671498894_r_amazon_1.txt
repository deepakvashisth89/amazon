Like a previous reviewer, I live on a reading diet of Ellroy, Chandler, and MacDonald.  Nearly all other noirs disappoint me, but not this.  Both the narrative and the dialogue are written with a sure, steady hand that never falters.  The pace is deliberate, with atmosphere and character taking precedence over action, but it’s a great read with a well crafted plot and a devastatingly effective ending that clearly demonstrates that this is noir, not some cheap John Grisham airport novel.
