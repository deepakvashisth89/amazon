I was never a big fan of vampire stories, but this is more than a vampire  story. This was one of two books (the other being Daughter of Darkness)  that actually made me feel, it made me actually feel what the characters  are going through. A beautiful story that portrays the relationship between  Father and Son, Father and Daughter, and the love between two people from  different worlds and their loyalty toward one another.
