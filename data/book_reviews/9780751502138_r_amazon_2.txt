Delightful series.  The mysteries are fun - perhaps not the greatest but good enough - but the settings and the characterization are terrific.  Like Patrick O'Brian's Aubrey and Materin series (started with Master and Commander), Peters takes you into another time and place, the world when movement was at the speed of the winds (if asea) or a horse (on land) and things just didn't happen nearly as quickly as they do today.  Watching Brother Cadfael balance his inner life with his life in the world about him, seeking the answers to riddles of the world of men while pursuing inner peace and perfection through a monastic life, provides the true delights of this series.Peters may not be Louise Penny, but he's awfully good and great fun to readRead moreRead less
