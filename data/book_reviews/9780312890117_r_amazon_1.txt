Having just finished reading this book, I really don’t know what to think of it.On the one hand, it’s slow and boring, and seems like it was devised by someone high on drugs. Also, dysfunction abounds to the point of absurdity.On the other hand, it’s compelling and different and interesting. The plot is bizarre enough to be intriguing in the same way that, say, China Miéville’s writing is.I’m not sure if the good stuff was worth slogging through the bad stuff. I think it’s a solid 3½ stars but am rounding up because of its compelling uniqueness. I give it 4/5 stars.Read moreRead less
