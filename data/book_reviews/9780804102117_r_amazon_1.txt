I love historical novels and books about places where I've been.  William Martin's books are both good reads and history lessons.  This book once again inspired me to look for the Boston locales on my old maps as well as to learn more about the Irish Rebellion.  More than that, his writing brings the era/locales to life and one can hear and taste the surroundings described.  One really becomes involved with the story line and characters as well.
