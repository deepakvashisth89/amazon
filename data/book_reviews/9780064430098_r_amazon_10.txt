A delightful concept beautifully written by Gene Zion, enhanced with classic 1950's painted illustrations by Margaret Bloy Graham. Each page has a plain white background. The bold, black text is simple and well spaced out. The illustrations are detailed yet not cluttered, lending themselves beautifully to observational discussions. Superb for an insight in to the past and comparison with the present. For example there is a lovely picture of a steam train complete with sooty smoke cloud and a lorry delivering coal in the high street.Harry is a little white dog with black spots. He lives with a family and runs away from home in order to avoid the inevitable 'bath'. While away he has a dogtastic time but has now changed into a black dog with white spots. Upon his return, no one recognises him. He takes matters into his own paws and resorts to an unthinkable drastic measure!This board book edition is super sturdy. The cover has a lovely gloss finish and the internal pages a lovely silky finish.
