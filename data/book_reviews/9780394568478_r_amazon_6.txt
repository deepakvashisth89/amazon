KM is not someone I had read nor knew anything about other than her name.  Having read it I know a great deal more about her however I am not so convinced as to how much of her life and indeed work was of any real interest.  CT did not touch on her writings in any depth and often stated her early writings especially (albeit not exclusively) were of no, or even low, quality yet as a person she was obviously immensely attractive to people and on occasion garnered a very high degree of loyalty and respect, in spite of her often very difficult behaviour.  She certainly knew people of note and interest and lived through changing times and was to an extent responsible for some of those changes of attitude, lifestyle etc.  As a social history it was illuminating but I am not that bothered about reading any of KM’s work.  CT is a splendid writer of biographies.  This was one of her first and it shows as CT appears to be finding her style for this type of writing, going off in one direction and then reverting to another. She spends a lot of time providing immense detail of KM’s life and of her contemporaries too but I felt there was a fair amount of padding as, in essence, how much detail you need on KM is, in my view, debatable.
