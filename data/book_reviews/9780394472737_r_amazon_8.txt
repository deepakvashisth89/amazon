Rabbit Angstrom has floated through life for 35 years and never truly fought for anything. Over the course of 400 or so pages he will slide and regress to an infantile shell of a man because of his inability to empathize with other people and build truly lasting bonds. By the end, he is gift wrapped an opportunity to redeem himself and makes a pitiable minimal effort to have a type of happy ending. Updike takes us readers on a journey of discovery, mashing the upper class liberal the right wing twit and the oppressed minority into a story of self discovery, loss, and madness.Read moreRead less
