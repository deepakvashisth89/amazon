This is a GREAT book! It's my personal favorite!! The main girl is Luna, and she lives on a beach in California with her parents, who own a surf shop. Luna's mom is a three-time surf champion of the world, and Luna feels pressured to get 1st place in a surf contest. Her family is also running a surf camp, in which Luna and her friends help with. Luna meets a guy, David, that she starts to really like... only he hates surfing! Luna tries to teach him how to surf, but throuhout the book has some major conflicts...
