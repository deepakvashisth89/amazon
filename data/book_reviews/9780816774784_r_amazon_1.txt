A great, almost forgotten American classic, Herman Melville's great masterpiece tells a tale of human struggle against the Universe.This is not a modern novel, but rather an exploration of the place of mere humans in the Infinite. Be warned, this is not a plot-driven book. Rather it explores all of the human condition and the struggle of humanity against the huge forces of fate. It encompasses sociology, psychology, religion, and even modern biology, an incredible accomplishment for a man writing nine years before publication of "On the Origin of Species". Melville was a genius of his time who put all his knowledge and experience in this autobiographical work.This is a challenging, huge book, encompassing much of life. Conquering it is itself a voyage with the whale. But it is well worth the effort and provides the reader with many rewards before the mast of the Pequad, flying it's red flag and with the brave native harpooner pounding the last nail, sinks below the surface of the wide Pacific.
