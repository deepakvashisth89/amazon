Before James Doohan played Chief Engineer Montgomery Scott on Star Trek, he fought in WWII as a pilot / artillery spotter, and was wounded in the D-Day Invasion.  This book and the other 2 in the series are a familiar like a Star Trek novel but with a fresh perspective on interstellar war and a more realistic look at the ships and their crews.
