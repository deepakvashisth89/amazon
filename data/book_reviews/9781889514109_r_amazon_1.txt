My daughter has started really getting into reading chapter books and these are some of her favorite books to read- she'll read them in particular before she goes to sleep for the night, and lately she's been reading this series every night. They are hard to find since I believe they are out of print, but I like the strong character qualities that many of these book's characters have ( she also likes the allyssa princess of arcadia series put out by the same publisher). the laurel and alyssa characters are thoughtful, responsible and independent, qualities that are great for young girls like my own to read about and try to emulate in real life.
