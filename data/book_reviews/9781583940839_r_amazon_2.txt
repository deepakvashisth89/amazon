It took me a long time to purchase and read this book because frankly, I couldn't care less about a book trying to prove a someone was the reincarnated Edgar Cayce.  It wasn't until I learned that this book was much more than just a lot of putting clues together to prove that David Wilcox had once been Edgar Cayce.In fact, the first part of book wasn't even that interesting compared to the Ra material that Wilcox channeled.  That made the book worth buying, and reading.  What's fascinating about the Ra material, and Wilcox's need to scientifically prove the theories he was channeling, is how true they feel.  This isn't just more 'airy-fairy' channeling saying that love will find the way if you just believe in your benevolent space brothers who are coming to rescue your sorry-self if you only believe.This book offers scientific proofs that would satisfy an MIT graduate, or at least it would to my mind.  And it fleshes out some of Cayce's readings which are difficult to understand, to say the least.Here's a sample from the book.
