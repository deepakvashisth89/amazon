Sober, realistic account of what it is to lead men in time of war. His voice throughout the book remains calm, while describing scenes of extreme realism, a very competent account without the fanfare. Author James R. McDonough truly engages the reader, as the months the platoon leader, a good and caring leader until the end, serving in Vietnam becomes the story line. A combat unit that sees their fair share of combat, decisions made in the heat of the battle, or on the overall duty of a capable leader as he tried to make the right call for his men, on and off the field.Down to earth, no sensationalism in this well paced title, Platoon Leader: A Memoir of Command in Combat. One wishes it would go on a little longer, but enough is said in a brilliant way in the time it takes to read the book. Definitely one of the better books about Vietnam, certainly worth the read, and a grand chance to meet and understand the intricate, and seriously dangerous job of a Platoon Leader, and the men he leads. 4.5 Stars.Read moreRead less
