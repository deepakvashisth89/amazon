The War Between the States is over and done with, but the turmoil sure aint--not by a long shot--for the filthy-rich Brennans and their namesake town.  Shannon  Plantation has managed to keep a fair passel of its ex-slaves on as wage workers, but the boy who got away four years hence is back--and the whole set-up is tailor-made for trouble.  Not only is erstwhile house slave Titus all growed up, good-lookin', 'n' glib-talkin', but the occupyin' Yankees've gone 'n' sent the Union soldier to be the new town constable!  Old man Brennan's minx of a daughter once tempted Titus just a little too far, and now the sparks are flyin' agin.  There's trouble-makin' renegades campin' down by the crik, masked 'n' ruthless night riders terrorizin' decent black folk, 'n' as much internecine fightin' 'mongst the Brennans as there was 'tween North and South.
