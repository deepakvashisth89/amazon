This book of classic, traditional baby names includes a fair number of unusual ones, as well as ones I'd never heard of. First published in 1963, it's been revised and reprinted several times, the last revised edition dating from 1986. The only recent "trendy" names it includes are ones that were popular in the 1980s. The book hails from a time when baby naming culture was a little different than it is today. The book would be good for anyone looking for forgotten, classic baby names that are ready to be new again.
