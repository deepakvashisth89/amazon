I started reading Heinlein as a school kid. I was just at the age that he was aiming for when he wrote these books. At the time, back in the 1950's there was a big market for teen fiction just as there is today. The protagonist is a young teen, probably about 16 maybe,, born and raised on Mars.It is another of RAH's coming of age stories set against a backdrop of sci fi, like Between Planets.The only disconcerting thing to some might be his description of Venus. As in Between Planets it is Venus that does not exist. At the time, we did not know about what was under all the clouds. The hope was it was hot and swampy but now we know it is inhabitable.
