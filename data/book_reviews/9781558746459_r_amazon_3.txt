I think sometimes in a relationship you can start to take the other person for granted. You move past that "In Love" stage and move into that "In Routine" stage That's where a book like this can be a great help. The stories in this book are engaging and it is easy for the average person to relate to the characters in the book. In fact if you are like me, you will see a little bit of yourself in each one of the characters. My suggestion is to read it at night right before going to bed with your significant other right next to you.
