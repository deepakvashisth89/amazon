This is a unique method and extremely helpful and inspiring, I believe due to the fact that the author is an artist first, who, in her first book responded to a friend's request to create a program to help children learn to draw. It's uniqueness stems from the fact that it has clarity and detail in explanations in the "how" of drawing and explains away misconceptions about lessons for drawing.  One remark explains it.  Would you tell a child who wished to play the piano to "naturally" sit down and express him or herself at the keyboard without lessons?  Yet this erroneous belief has prevented children from receiving lessons in drawing. Moreso, the instructions are so structured and clear that as an dult or teenager, one can be their own teacher.  I had never seen a drawing book of my collection, anyway, that showed me where and how to start specifically, or perspective based on the inside of a room with detail in addition to start with, rather than outside, landscape wise, starting with the horizon line.  There were many particulars too numerous to note.  I love this book.  It, like her first brought to me the joy of discovery, without the fear of failure to be "perfectly" realistic that I wish I had known at 8 years of age when I first attempted to draw.  The icing on the cake, I believe a talented friend will discover her own drawing ability I see in her natural creativity when she purchases the first book by the author, Teaching Children to Draw.
