(Minor spoilers!)This was my favorite of the main (first?) four books. Olivia is the most normal, I guess, but mostly she is the best bc she goes out and does things to get where she wants to be. she doesn't mope around worrying about what other people thing about her. She's much more down-to-earth than the other 3 main characters. Her personality complements well with Morgan's, so she makes a great friend for her, in my opinion.I think these books do tell how teenagers feel and think well. Although in my experience this is a little unrealistic, that might just be me.BUT! My BIGGEST PROBLEM by far with these books is what the covers say. "No matter what, they know they'll always have each other" NO, THEY DON'T!! They don't trust and hardly like each other at all! And they don't even all have rings until the end of this one! I realize that it's advertising, but I think it's stupid and a downright lie.Read moreRead less
