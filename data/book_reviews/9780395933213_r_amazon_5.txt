This book was the textbook for a Calc 2 class that I took.  It had been a long time since I took calc 1, and had forgotten most of the information.  This book did a nice job of providing clear examples, and thoughtful exercises.  It is one of the most readable math books that I have read (and there are a lot of bad ones out there).  I would strongly recommend getting the answer guide also, since the answers in the book do not show how the problem was solved.
