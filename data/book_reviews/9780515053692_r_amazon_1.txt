This is an intricately plotted, well written mystery with three dimensional characters. It is not the fault of the entertaining author, that the publisher, years after her death is adding a commercial in the middle of the book after charging a significant fee for the book. I know we have to endure commercials on tv, and unfortunately parts of digital media, but inserted into a book?Now about the plot. A wealthy woman, who's friends and family think her to be a hypochondriac goes to a hotel for a rest with medical supervision. Here she dies under unclear circumstances. There a number of people who will benefit, some of them shady. Superintendent Alleyn and the steadfast Fox investigate.This novel has a great diversity of characters, and a long missing stamp worth a fortune. This is a prime example of Marsh, towards the end of her writing career. I must say, appreciate her writing even more after reading so many inferior mysteries written after the era of Christie, Sayers, and Marsh.
