After reading Moe's biography I'm intrigued. The man doing the writing is  introspective and revealing. I've found a person that I think I would have liked to have met and hung out with. Not the nit-wit you see in The Three Stooges shorts, but a real caring human being who looked out for his siblings and cared about his friends, and managed to survive in the hard times of the Depression Era and raise a family. Very interesting reading. I found it hard to put it down in order to go to sleep at night. The book has some very good pictures of a long and varied career of the Three Stooges.Read moreRead less
