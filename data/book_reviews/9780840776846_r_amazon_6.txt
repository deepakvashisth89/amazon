My husband was in hospice for 2 months prior to his passing. I read "Afterloss" during this time. An excellent, helpful easy read book..not too difficult to comprehend...compassionate and tender, uplifting...the individual stories wriiten by widows and others with a loss was hugely insightful..I identified with some of the stories and often re-read them to fully comprehend the author's complicated grief-journey ..and ultimately their personal reconciliation. This book was a must-have for me ...it provides me with the comfort I need.Read moreRead less
