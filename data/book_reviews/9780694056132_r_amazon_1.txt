I read this book as a consideration for an American History class at a homeschool co-op. While it had a romantic theme intertwined into the book making it a bit too mature for 5-6th graders, it is still an excellent book that gives an accurate and historically based portrayal of the civil war. You quickly grow to love Jeff, Noah, and Heifer while hating Clardy. The author did an amazing job of telling a story that keeps the pages turning.Read moreRead less
