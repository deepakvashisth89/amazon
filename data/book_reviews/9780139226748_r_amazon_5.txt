The origins of this book evolved from a pamphlet that Claude M. Bristol created and left with individuals.  From this earlier pamphlet, he wrote the Magic of Believing.  This second book published in the 1950's is an expanded version of his first writing.In 20 chapters, the authors explain what TNT really is and how to truly leverage this awesome power within you as the individual in your quest for self-improvement.  They use personal stories and concrete examples of how TNT can dramatically change people's lives.One of the most critical pieces of information within this dynamic book is repetition or what the authors wrote as "Tap, tap, tap." By visualizing a desired goal, repeating it, taking forward action to move closer to it, you can achieve it.  This is the power of TNT.At the end of the last chapter, the authors summed up TNT - "Every thought, kept ever constant, leads to action, and results follow."  If you want to achieve goals, realize your dreams and bebe The Red Jacket in a sea of gray suits, then invest some time to read this self-improvement book.
