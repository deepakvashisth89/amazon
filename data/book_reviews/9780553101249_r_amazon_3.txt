I adore this book. It may not be completely up to date, it may not be accurate, and it may not make sense if you take it too close to science, but it is a very good book. A good BOOK, with a story and characters, not a documentary on raptors. Though this book is based off of Utah Raptor, it is not meant to depict the actual creature in terms of what we know scientifically about it. It is a story, and just a story.It is a very good book. I enjoyed it -- I have been obsessed with dinosaurs since I was little and I just had to have this book. It is well written and I do like how Bakker portrayed the raptors and how they thought, mated, moved, and more. I like his take on it, even though it may not be up to scientific standards.I loved this book and I recommend it to anyone who loves dinosaurs and who isn't afraid to stray a little from fact to fiction.Read moreRead less
