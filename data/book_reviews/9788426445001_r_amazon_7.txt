I bought a number of Mafalda books in order to help me to learn/improve my understanding of Spanish. I had ploughed my way through a Teach Yourself Spanish course, messed about with Harrap's Drive In Spanish, and suffered endless Julio Iglesias songs in order to improve my learning, and I have to say that these cartoons are addictive and refreshingly 'real' Spanish.It helps if you know someone who is a native Spanish speaker. My former girlfriend, now my wife, is Colombian and she has been able to explain some of the ideological content of the cartoon strips. Occasionally Quino uses terms that are specifically Argentinan, and she has been able to identify these for me. But generally the cartoon strips are a joy to read and they frequently have me laughing out loud. And because I really want to know what the cartoons are about I am happy to spend time with a dictionary and grammar book to get to the punch-line..This review applies to all of the Mafalda books in Spanish.Read moreRead less
