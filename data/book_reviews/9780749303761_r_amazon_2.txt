I had a hard time rating this one.  I kept going back and forth.  I am still a little shaking on a "rating" per say.  If I had to, I would give it 3.75 stars.  It's definitely not a 3-star, but I am leery of a 4-star.This book is good.  It really is.  However, there is tooo much description in places there didn't need to be.  This made the book harder to read.  I couldn't stay focused once the author started describing the characters.  Sometimes there is a such thing as too much detail.  This right here is what makes it hard for me to give it a solid 4-stars.Overall, I did enjoy this book.  I wasn't a fan of the characters.  Which, I think that was the point?  Maybe.  Maybe not.
