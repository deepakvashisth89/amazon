Lem is one of the most original, creative, wickedly funny, irresistibly charming storytellers ever to grace science fiction. The Star Diaries was rightly considered to be an instant classic and it has stood the test of time. I have read a lot of sci-fi in my life, and I consider this to be one of my all-time favorites. Nobody does it like Lem does it, and nobody who purchases this book will be disappointed when they read it.
