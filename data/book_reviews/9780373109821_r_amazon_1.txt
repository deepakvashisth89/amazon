My husband was away this week, so I needed a bit of romance.A friend gave me the book and I fell in love with Adam Cooper even more than heroine Kate Flannigan.  They travel the world to exotic locales  most of the time separately but meeting up briefly just isn't enough to satisfy their fiery passion for one another.  Kate is commitment-phobic , Adam is a workaholic who finally realises there are more important things in life.  Kate takes a little longer to get to that conclusion - but of course it all works out in the end - I could do without the melodrama of trying to start  a life together with 2 adopted kids(Yuk)- but all in all nicely written!!Read moreRead less
