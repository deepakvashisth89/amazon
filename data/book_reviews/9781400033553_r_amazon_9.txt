I’m a big fan of Hampton Sides and have read all his books. Americana is different from his longer narrative histories, but equally good in its own way. This collection brings together articles and essays written for magazines over the last 3–4 decades, covering a wide range of topics and characters from across America.The variety is part of its charm – Sides has a gift for finding the extraordinary in unexpected places and making each story compelling. It’s the perfect book for holiday reading: you can dip in and out, savoring one piece at a time, or read it straight through and enjoy the sweep of voices and places.Read moreRead less
