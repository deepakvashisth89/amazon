Robert Picardo is a wonderful actor, and I actually bought this CD set thinking it was the musical Jekyll and Hyde (my mistake!) But I was surprised and pleased to hear more vocal range of characters from him as an audiobook, with a surprise song and poem track at the end! I actually forgot who was reading it for a good chunk of the book (a testament to the quality of vocal/acting range). It was a wonderful way to experience this book for the first time (I somehow was never required to read it in high school). 10/10 would recommend anything Picardo does, but even if I wasn't already a fan I would have enjoyed his portrayal in this one.Read moreRead less
