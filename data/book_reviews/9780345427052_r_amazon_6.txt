This was a very good book. The only reason I gave it 4 instead of 5 stars is it wasn't what I expected or hoped it would be. It was a story of Monty's life and all of his amazing adventures with very little said of his training methods and experiences. I purchased the book thinking it would be chock full of training tips so I was a little disappointed to find that it wasn't. However I would still recommend the book.
