The disappearances of Leslie Mahaffy and Kristen French and their subsequent murders as well as the truth about the death of Tammy Lyn Homolka at the hands Karla Homolka and Paul Bernardo is story that hits hard for Canadians. Stephen Williams wrote this sequel to Invisible Darkness and somehow managed to be thorough without being tedious and informative without salacious.
