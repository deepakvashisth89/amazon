An incredible piece of bittersweet art by one of the most prominent living novelists. Having said that, this particular copy on amazon is not a five star product. I had read the novel a few years back and bought this to keep in my collection. And the book certainly does not feel like a collectible item. As seen in the picture, the page lengths are uneven which is irritating when you try to sift through the book to go to a particular page. Also, because of the uneven sized pages, they get easily wrinkled. This was in fact the case when I opened the package and became quite annoyed.I know that some publishers do this type of printing with uneven pages, and I have no idea why.
