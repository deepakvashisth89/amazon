Michael Gear is famous for being able to sustain his writing energy through book after book (his SPIDER TRILOGY remains a landmark of sci-fi writing). With Gear and Gears' ANASAZI MYSTERY SERIES the writing has, over all, gotten better. This is the first volume in a series that already has 5 books, and the last one left enough loose ends to craft a 6th one. Throughout the first 3 books the authors bounce between contemporary archaeology and Anasazi societies 1000 years ago. Gear/Gear handle the frequent time-frame transitions smoothly.
