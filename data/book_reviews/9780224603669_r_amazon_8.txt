I read this book as an 8-year-old in 1968 and spent the rest of my years searching for a lost twin (and found many good friends as a result). My parents often fought bitterly, although they never divorced, and this book helped me navigate those turbulent years. I am a huge fan of Kastner's Emil books as well.The movies are very different than the book. I thought it was a bit of a shame to eliminate the whole class dynamic (Lottie and her mother are poor in the book).
