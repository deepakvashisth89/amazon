I am an avid student of World War II. Specifically, I am an avid student of America's and England's involvement in the Conflict. If one studies England's involvement in the War, one has to be a student of Britain's wartime Prime Minister: Sir Winston Churchill. Therefore, I am, literally, enthralled with my reading of John Colville's book "The Fringes of Power 10 Downing Street Diaries 1939 - 1955". Through his diary Colville brings to life his involvement with Churchill both in his daily activities and during the major events of the War such as the Retreat from Dunkirk and D Day.
