When I was very young, someone gave me two small books that were part of a twenty book collection. The little books were entitled "A Picture History of The Motor Car". In the books were small but Beautiful Illustrations by a very talented Artist by the name of Piet Olyslager. I have cherished these books, and I would often look through the pages over the years. Just recently I became aware of this book, "Illustrated Motor Cars of The World", which features over 900 Illustrations by the same Artist, I quickly purchased the book and I absolutely love it. As an Adult now, I am an Automotive Artist, and it is these Illustrations and other artwork by other Artists, that has helped shape my life.
