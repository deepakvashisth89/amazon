This book is a very good guide for herbs. It has a lot of both historical and current use information, along with detailed information enough for the less-than-averagely knowledgeable gardener to be able to grow the herb. It also has the important information as to what herbs included are toxic and are no longer recommended to be used. It also has information about using herbs to dye cloth. The pictures are very nice and in color. Has tons of fascinating minutia that would take an hour for me to write about.
