This book was suggested by my primary care physician. I am struggling to loose weight and I have tried different variations to make changes: intermittent fasting, keto, no fat, etc. diets. They did not work. Yes, you are shedding pounds initially but it is mostly a water weight. Then you feel miserable, and everyday is a fight with your demons in shape of yummy foods you can no longer allow yourself as you will break the diet. I read the book, it is pretty straight forward, calculated how much protein, carbs and fat I need with my active life style and created a food plan. So far the results are: I feel great, no hunger, and energy level increased, I eat more fruits and veggies and good fat with protein.I did not yet step on a scale as I want to wait a bit.
