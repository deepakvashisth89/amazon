This is an older story that was dusted up for ebook release. The hero of the story is the strong, autocratic male who knows what he wants, but in a fun twist, Dodd has her heroine, a woman with the job of a butler, against the idea of love and marriage.Margaret's already had her heart broken from her husband getting cancer and dyindg - leaving her with a pile of bills and a daughter to raise.Reid is a rich man who was raised by grandfather, who is Margaret's current employer. He's never met a woman like Margaret. She fights like a man, loves his curmudgeonly grandfather, she's conversant in topics that interest him, and she's got a body that attracts him like nothing else. Sparks fly from the instant they meet.The best part of the story for me was the birthday party. That really struck an emotional chord with me.
