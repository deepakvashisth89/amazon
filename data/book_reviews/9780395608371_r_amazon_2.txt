This book should be read by every American who is concerned about the natural world. While dealing with the old growth forest of the north west; it also covers the history of conservation going back to the first pilgrims. The ideas of mans place in nature are many and varied; and this is the book to explore  those ideas.I found both sides of the issue explained and both the good and bad points of each argument without bias.
