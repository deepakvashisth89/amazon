If you are a scientific minded lover of physics and its relationship to esoteric subjects like consciousness and soul this 368 page soft cover book (The Physics of Consciousness by Evan Harris Walker) is for you.  This book is written for, not the average person, but rather for those who understand the details of physics and Einstein’s theory of relativity.  The author gives a history of the essential role physics played in our society and how the various early scientists contributed to our understanding of the universe.The author shows how Quantum mechanics and the Heisenberg uncertainty principle has changed from the materialistic scientific approach most scientists accept to a wider view of bringing the esoteric world into the realm of the possible. Indeed, it seems the more we know the more unanswered questions seem to pop up.  It makes one wonder if there is limit to what we can understand when it comes to reality and the universe.The author explores the following topics in this academic text: Where have all the Gods gone? It’s a material world, into eternity, the light fantastic, jitterbug reality, hunt for the tin man’s heart, many worlds. Many mansions, the sound of the temple bell, golden brocade, Satori physics, looking for the Emerald City, the red shoes, to sleep, perchance to dream, a matter of will, quantum miracles, from Epicycles to Loops, the causal mind and a God for tomorrow.If you are seeking a complex but highly informative book on the relationship between physics and what is reality when it comes to the cosmos and when did it all begin, this book is one you will want to read.Rating: 5 Stars.
