It’s best to read this series in order so you get to know Chief Inspector John McLeish and his girlfriend Francesca Wilson.He is a big handsome policeman dedicated to. and talented at, detective work. Francesca comes from a remarkably musical family and has four grown brothers she looks after like a mother. She’s also a very competent executive in the  civil service.In this book McLeish is investigating the death of a beautiful young woman who was a partner in a firm of lobbyists. McLeish is stressed out by his difficult case. Francesca is stressed out by family troubles. Perhaps as a result, both fall prey to the attractions of other people. The plot is rife with infidelity and jealousy. This is as absorbing as the murder case.These books are always peppered with business meetings and discussions about corporate finance. There are also usually scenes of musical performances by Francesca’s bothers or proteges.  This odd mixture gives the books a unique realistic flavor.
