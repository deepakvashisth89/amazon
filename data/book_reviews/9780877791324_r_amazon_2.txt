A very well sourced and apparently reliable guide to English Usage. It pretty much demolished 90+% of the English Usage advice my Jr. and Sr. High School tried to teach me. And  when a  friend admonished his daughter that the fruits that she had described as a "healthy dessert alternative" were no such thing - that ,instead they were "healthful", I was able to predict that this volume would totally undermine his pedantry and win a $10 bet which he willingly paid up upon seeing the crystal clear entry in this fine tome . I bought it on the strong advice by William Safire in one of his NY Times op-ed columns . And it was and remains about the only thing I can remember agreeing with Mr. Safire on.
