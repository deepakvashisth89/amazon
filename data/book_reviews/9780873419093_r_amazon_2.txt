I have owned a copy of Cartridges of the World for decades.  I buy a new edition every 3 or 4 years since new cartridges are coming out very frequently.  I have found this handbood to be an extremely comprehensive source for all things "ammo".  This book is for shooters, hunters, collectors, law enforcement and anyone having an interest.  It fun just browsing through the book.  New, current, obsolete, foreign, military, proprietary, wildcat, rifle, handgun, centerfire, rimfire;  it's all there.  For those contemplating acquiring a rifle or handgun, it helps to know what rifle or handgun would be satisfactory for the intended purpose.
