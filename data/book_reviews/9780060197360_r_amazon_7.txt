I was thrilled with the price for an "almost" new hardcover book. This was one of my monthly book club's selections. I enjoyed the book - light fare. Though I think the author, being young and single and on a roadtrip looking for pie, could have brought even more humor into her writing. It was a bit dry and long winded at times.
