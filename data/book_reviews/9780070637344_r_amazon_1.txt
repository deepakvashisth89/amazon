This is the definitive book on learning about the futures markets.  One guy said that the futures market is the fastest game in town. And indeed it is a fast paced game. The authors go into the BASICS OF THE GAME, PLAYING THE GAME AND TRADING, LOSERS AND WINNERS, BROKERS and CHOOSING THE GAME AND IT'S MARKETS. The authors go quite in-depth in regards to explaining all of the different markets and the things which effect them.
