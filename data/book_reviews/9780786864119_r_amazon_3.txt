Simple book follows its own advice by knowing how to explain and provide just the right suggestions without being too verbose.  You don't have to subscribe to every idea to get something from this book.  For example, it may not be realistic (or you may not believe it to be desirable) to down size your house...  But if you keep a big house you need the other organizational suggestions even more!I borrowed this book years ago from my mom...  Ended up buying it for myself...  And recently bought a copy for my boss and the other directors that I work with.  We had just returned from a business seminar on how to simplify business for focus on what's important and this book came too mind (even though its not about business).  Perfect accompaniment to what we'd just learned.This book should be required college reading (perhaps a whole course), followed by students making hypotheses and then doing life experiments to prove their hypotheses about what is important.  There may be no right or wrong, but they would finish either with a new perspective OR more firmly entrenched in their previous biases...  Either way, that's a worthwhile outcome.Awesome!Read moreRead less
