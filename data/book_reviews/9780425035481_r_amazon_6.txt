I loved "By Any Other Name", the Hugo winner that birthed this one. The struggles of a post- apocalyptic New World against an alien race is not new in Sci-Fi, but Spider turns it (and us) upside down as only he can. The battles are epic. The warriors are lethal. Betrayal is everywhere.  And peace is a surprise. It's so much harder to do. Therein lies the beauty of it. "We have met the enemy and he is us" is still true, but so is "All you need is love". Have fun figuring out who's doing which. The ending twist weakened it a bit in my opinion, but I'm also going to read it again. It's worth it.Read moreRead less
