If you've ever visited Cape Cod or even the New England coastal towns, you'll appreciate the characters in Taylor's books. Asey and his extended family on the Cape solve the mysteries and keep you interested throughout the series. I hope someone has the good sense to put this series out on Kindle. I've had quite a time finding the books. I even ordered one from the UK.
