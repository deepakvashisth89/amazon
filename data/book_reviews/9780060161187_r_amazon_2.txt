Jim Chee is still stewing about his last girlfriend when a promising new lady friend asks him to help her out. She is worried/scared on both a personal and a professional level.  She happens to live in the District of Columbia so we get a chance to see our likable co-lead adjusting to an off putting urban environment.Meanwhile Joe Leaphorn finally seems to be adjusting to his wife's death after going thru the Blessing Way, a traditional Navajo ceremony conducted by Chee that seemed to remove the burden life had put on him. Leaphorn decided to continue as a cop after having submitted his resignation in the previous book, A Thief of Time, and now has a more relaxed attitude, only getting obsessive when a particularly interesting or unusual case shows up.  Both criterion are met when a strange corpse dressed in expensive clothes with handmade shoes, but no teeth or any ID is found alongside the Amtrak line. Pursuing it for no other reason than it gives life some interest the trail leads him to Washington, DC and wouldn't you know it he happens to bump into Jim Chee.  Coincidences continue but smooth writing paves the way to a delightful read with some of fiction's more enjoyable characters.
