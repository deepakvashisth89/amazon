I told him about "Superduperman," "Batboy and Rubin," "Shermlock Sholmes and the Hound of the Basketballs," and "Mickey Rodent" ("Hey, Darnold - duck!!!")  He sounded interested, so I ordered this collection, which arrived in two days, and he found it as fascinating as I'd hoped he would.
