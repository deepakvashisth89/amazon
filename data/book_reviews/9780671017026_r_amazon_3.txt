Although I haven't been an avid short story reader, I am an avid Udall fan and so his short stories seemed a good launching point for my journey.  This was a selection for my book club and it was a great choice.  This author has that wonderful ability to let you hear how his characters sound when they speak, their accents, their inflections and their emotions are all made clear.  I love that, personally.  Funny as heck and tragic beyond imagination all wrapped up in a series of beautiful stories.  I wished that I could continue into the lives and stories of all the characters in this collection.
