I am having a wonderful time rereading Stuart M. Kaminsky’s Toby Peters series decades after I read them as they were first published.Kaminsky always nails the movie stars and historical personages featured in each book and he especially uses Peter Lorre to his finest potential.The secondary cast of characters in the book are delightful as always and we finally meet Mildred, Sheldon Minck’s horror of a wife.An entertaining romp.
