This dictionary serves well as a standard reference work. The grammar at the beginning is surprisingly useful. However, one major problem is that it often fails to explain the differences between Esperanto words which  translate different senses of English words. This makes life difficult for  a learner. It is also a bit dated. Also, advanced translators may wish to  avail themselves of the more unabridged Comprehensive English-Esperanto  Dictionary, by Benson.Chi tiu vortaro servas bone kiel baza  kontrolverko. La gramatiko en la komenco de la libro estas surprize utila.
