I found this novel at the thrift store (sorry Amazon) and was completely enthralled!  Since I lived through the sixties (on the fringe of course) I could relate to many of the descriptions and circumstances.  The story completely engrossed me; I couldn't put it down.  There were some of the most evil characters I have encountered in fiction.  And the circumstances that separated the two lovers were truly heinous.  It is a study on how one has to make terrible choices to honor a love.  Amazing writer!  Why hadn't I heard of him?  Why isn't he better known?  I highly recommend for an engrossing read.
