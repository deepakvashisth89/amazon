Truthfully, this book is funny in that it's horrifyingly sound in its thought.I was increasingly mesmerised, horrified, captivated, thankful, and intrigued in how the letters from Screwtape to his nephew Wormwood applied to so much of real life. But from a different perspective.It really made me get up and make some changes and for that, I thank God and his servant C.S. Lewis.The way an entire world which I could easily envision was woven from letters from a character that wasn't even in the world himself is devastatingly genius and proof of excellent penmanship and creativity.The characters were rich and detailed even though we only ever got Screwtape's perspective. Wow. Just wow.Everyone should read this. Christian or not.It is certainly now a waste of time. It's pure timeless wisdom. And a good laugh as well. The gut wrenching "oh no, I've been a fool haven't I?" Kind of contemplative chuckle.Except instead of fear, dispair, or shame. It empowers change.
