First of all, I LOVED this book!Secondly, if you are not into Dominant Alpha males, then this book just isn't for you, period.I could just so completely relate to the character of Bryn, and her husband, Khalil ... well, let's just say he's the stuff that dreams are made of!Other reviewers have commented that Khalil was a little to harsh in the beginning, and while I do think the author, Jane Porter, did push the envelope a little, she doesn't go so far that you're just not finding the alpha male lead attactive anymore.  As things turn out, although he was hard on Bryn, by the end of the novel, you're loving him as much as Bryn does.Kudos to Miss Porter for creating the character of Bryn, where it was just so easy to project myself into her shoes.  This is a real page turner, and I read most of it in one sitting.  Best of all, this book does not rely on a lot of gratuitous sex to pull together weak characters and a boring, rehashed plot.  What is best about Bryn is that she's willing to do whatever it takes to make her troubled marriage work.  She's willing to say and do things that I might hesitate to say and do, but deep down inside, might really want to.
