I loved the book and felt sorry for poor Cheryl Crane, Lana was in my opinion a very selfish and self centered woman. She didn't know how to love or care for her own daughter and I really wish her father Steven had gotten custody of her. Nobody should have to go through the Hell Cheryl did. I still cannot believe that her step father  Lex Barker,denied he molested and raped her. I would have loved to have found out what happened to her little adopted brother though.And the fact that Cheryl is gay doesn't bother me in the least. I wonder if she's still with Josh?
