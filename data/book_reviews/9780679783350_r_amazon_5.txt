This is a superb edition of Dickinson's poems.  It is "reader friendly" with updated punctuation (which purists may not like) with an excellent selection.  The Billy Collins introduction is outstanding, being highly informative and entertaining without any pretensions whatsoever.  He adds great insight into Dickinson's use of common meter, language, metaphor, and other techniques.
