"Rewind" was a terrific story but even for a fiction book it is still suposed to be realistic aside from the fact that he is getting a second chance to live. One flaw was how fast the media would back off of everybody. The second that Kurt told the media he was just covering for a friend they backed off. So did the crazies and as desperate as they wouild be I dont think that they would do that. I also thought that some characters were just a little to gullible- that might not be the best word to describe it. Either way their are some things that left me skeptical but it is a fiction and a good one at that.
