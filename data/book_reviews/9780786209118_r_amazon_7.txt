When I picked up this book I wasn’t really expecting an adventure in a different solar system.  I just wanted to read another Krentz novel.  But I am a fan of this author’s books about New Harmony and dust bunnies written under a different pen name.  I loved how this was similar yet also different from the New Harmony books.
