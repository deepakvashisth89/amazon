My perspective is a bit colored by my own desire to take my dog on a cross country adventure.  Charley was appreciated, loved , and cared for by the author, that was very clear.  I liked his casual style, his conversational text...but... I expected far more.  There are only three or four tales in the book of encounters with "the locals"- which were the best writings in the book.  Specific personalities, reasons to relate to their characters, & ways to see their uniqueness.  They were back- dropped by their own home state/location.  I kept wanting more of that, much more.  I also would have liked some hints at the places he most wanted to visit, and why ( or why not,)  New Orleans was described as a favorite, but the music & the backdrop I had hoped to read about were non-existant due to the desegregation story-  which, while very important to the history of the nation, swallows up all the pageantry & entertainment of a major cultural center, and musical hub of the whole USA.
