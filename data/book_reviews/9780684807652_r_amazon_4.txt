I checked this book out from my local library before watch the movie, "The Young Victoria". I wanted to know more about such an amazing woman that most history books only give a paragraph or so to. I found this novel to be as my title for this review states: Historically moving. Erickson's walk through Victoria's life was like stepping into the queen's shoes and living every moment with her. I enjoyed the book so much that I did not want to give it back. The reader is taken through Victoria's young days at Kensington Palace, surviving the mechanisations of her controling mother and Sir John Conroy; her rise as a young queen and romance with Albert, her prince consort who would do anything for her love and happiness. Plus her isloating loneliness after Albert's death, her sort-of-kind-of relationship with John Brown, her Irish servent, and the intervening years that include war, India, and her countless grandchildren.
