Where did this guy come from?Recently I have been reading all of John Steinbeck's works and I finally got a copy of this, his first novel. It's already excellent and the fictional technique is mature. I don't understand where learned to be such a great writer. When you read his biography "The True Adventures of John Steinbeck, Writer",  the casual guy living his marginal life around Monterey gives no glimpse of the effective, mature novelist you find here.
