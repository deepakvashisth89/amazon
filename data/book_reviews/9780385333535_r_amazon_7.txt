I am currently reading my sixth book in the Dismas Hardy series by John Lescroart.. When I started reading this author's contribution to the world of intrigue in the mechanisms of law and politics regarding the procedures of lawyers, judges and juries I didn't know I had found a gold mine.  After the first book I was hooked.  If you really want to get the most from this author read this series in order of creation.  Not totally necessary but it will make you feel that you know these people personally.  I am losing my eye sight slowly and enjoy reading so much that I am an avid reader.  Having a Kindle has provided me the ability to change the font of the books .  I can not at this point choose a favorite of these books because each one brings a different challenge for the characters.  Truly eye opening situations happen.  I am so thankful that I have many more to read.  Thank you Mr. Lescroart!Read moreRead less
