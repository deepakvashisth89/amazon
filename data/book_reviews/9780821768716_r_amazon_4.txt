I was intrigued in the book at the start. As it went on I became a little concerned that it was going to fall into one of those formula ruts so many authors tend to us. You know the ones where you can guess the next move of the characters and be right. I am happy to report that this is not one of those books. Lilly is a wonderfully complex  character. She spent her life caring for, raising, loving, and protecting her special Mr brother. James had gone to America in search of his brothers killer whom he belief to have been Lilly though she had no knowledge of what her actions in helping her older brother and the cause. James kidnaps her and her little brother and takes her to England. From there I foolishly thought I knew the direction the story was going to take but continued to read anyway. I was so glad I did. The story had so many twist and turns I had no idea how it would come to the final conclusion. I highly suggest read this book you will be pleasantly surprised.Read moreRead less
