It's not often I get to read a book with all my favorite monsters: Vampires, werewolves, ghouls, zombies, Immortals, and an obnoxious cat, to name a few. It's even less often that I catch myself laughing through that same book. There is a wonderful mixture or horror and humor here that appeals to me and keeps me turning the pages. There is also a romance that is just heating up and I hope continues to in the next book. I don't want to rush the authors or put any pressure on them but when is the next book coming out!!!!
