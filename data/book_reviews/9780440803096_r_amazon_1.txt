Ms. Reynolds has a remarkable ability to create true to life characters with whom her readers can readily empathize while at the same time she is able to tell compelling stories that often bring me to tears of joy and/or sorrow. I am delighted to find that my 8 and 12 year old grand-daughters have enjoyed reading the younger, grade-school Alice books and I am excited about the prospect of introducing them to the pre-teen Alice books, including the delightful Reluctantly Alice (and,in time, all of the other books in this series). What pleases me most about the author's writing is her ability to create very believable, captivating and true-to-life stories that include realistic characters our kids can identify with and appreciate for all their various human qualities - including their strengths and their weaknesses.
