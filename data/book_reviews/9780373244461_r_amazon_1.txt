This amazing story had me hooked from the opening scene.  Drew is a man looking for a second chance, while Olivia is a woman seeking a new beginning.  That is their true gifts for one another.  The author writes with a blend of action and internalization that brings the characters to life for the reader.  Lisette Belisle's expressive prose is a sublime blend of sweetness, charm, humor and drama.  This novel has it all.  I loved how the characters grew and changed for each other without sacrificing the personalities that made them so interesting from the start.  It really is a unique love story.  Just wait until you get to the scene with the Christmas trees!  It brought a smile and a mistiness to my eyes.
