This is one of my favorite Ngaio Marsh novels. It is the first one set in her clearly beloved homeland of New Zealand. A wonderful, thrilling murder mystery with enough twists and turns to be highly suspenseful, while also keeping the whodunit realistic and solvable. Thoughtfully addresses issues relating to cultural differences: British versus New Zealander, and colonizer versus Maori, in a way is surprisingly modern for her treatment of the Maori culture. Of great importance, it is also one of her famous murders involving the theater, which Ngaio Marsh is the expert at writing. It's wonderful the way she works the staged aspect of a murder plot in with the inherently disguising nature of a performance piece.
