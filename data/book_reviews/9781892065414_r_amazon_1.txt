I found one book by Lee Killough in a bookstore during the 80's, the first Garreth Mikaelian one, and never came across another. Since I very much enjoyed that one it's great to be finally able to read her other books.And I liked this second Mikaelian as much as I liked the first one (in spite of having a few moments when I hoped he'd had gotten to the morgue soon enough to snap that teenaged vampire's neck before she had time to revive :)). Most vampires you encounter nowadays may be more the heroic than the monster types, but Killough's hero was among the first ones of these I found and I still think she did her vampires very well, even compared to all these later competitors.Read moreRead less
