Fantastic follow up on Flatland by Abbott. Math is basic and there is not much of it though there is an exposition of the metric tensor from relativity in linear algebra form.This book is great for scientists and curious non-scientists. If you are curious about "the fourth dimension" or all the fuss about Einstein and Relativity this is an excellent book for you. Just ignore the math, Dr. Bucker explains everything in plain English.If you know the names Kaluza and Klein what they were up to, this is a great and quick read - always look for new perspectives. This book is not technical in the strict sense but it is informative to an astute reader.Read moreRead less
