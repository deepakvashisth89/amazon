Be prepared to laugh and ride the humorous wave with Sterling Johnson.In the 1970's, George Carlin cited seven words that would never appear on TV, but time and theatrical productions have changed their acceptability.  Unfortunately, I cannot list them in this review because they would not be suitable, but they are listed on page 8 of this book.Cable TV followed the lead when they began showing these R+ rated movies.
