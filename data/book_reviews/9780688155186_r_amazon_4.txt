I hated to see the Robin Hudson series end, although I understand that Hayter is a free spirit and needed to move on to other challenges.  The setting of the Chelsea Hotel makes this book, with the characters a close second.  Plot is always way down the list for a Hayter mystery, but they are still great fun to read and make you laugh out loud.
