This is a dreadful book and I begrudged the time that I spent reading it.  The author has adopted a precious prose style.  He has also attempted to duplicate casual speech in the most annoying way.  For instance, "Le mgo" for "Let me go."  The more conventional approach would be "Lemme go."  And consider this: the male lead referring to his being Jewish pronounces it "Joosh."
