I really enjoy the Sgt Cribb books by Lovesey, and this one did not disappoint.  The treatment of class distinctions (and credulity) receives a lighthearted (yet pointed) treatment here as another reviewer noted.  The seance and fake psychic scene in the London of the era is the setting in a book that is a very quick, enjoyable read.  Due, in part, to the shortness of this book, development is rather lacking in places, but that is the nature of novellas. It was very enjoyable, and I would certainly recommend it.Read moreRead less
