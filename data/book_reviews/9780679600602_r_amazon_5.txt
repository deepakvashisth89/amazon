I found this book, the Lin Yutang translation, in 1969 in one of the many bookstores near Columbia when I was a grad student. Now, nearing old age, I am surrounded by hundreds of books in my library, but this book alone would be the one that most influenced the course my life has taken.  Alan Watts once said that his many books of wisdom hadn't made him wiser. Perhaps it's personal temperament that the poetry of this book matched my already soft outlook on life, that drew me in. Of the many translations, even the new discoveries, this remains my favorite, the most moving, the deepest.May you happen upon it.Read moreRead less
