I was pleasantly surprised by Mr. Burns' book.  This is not another book in which the author randomly muses about his favorite tomes, but rather, Mr. Burns takes a close look at the history of books, why they are written, and what they mean to him.  His description of his son's first encounter with "Peter Pan" is priceless.  I cherish "books about books," and this is simply one of the very best.Read moreRead less
