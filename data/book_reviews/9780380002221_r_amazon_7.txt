Jane is a single American movie critic living in London in the early 70's.  She is juggling three - count 'em - three lovers - a  handsome black American professor, a titled Englishman and a burglar.  From her loft apartment she deals with the difficulties of managing to keep the three of them apart while going to films and writing reviews.  She tends to prefer to review the arty films rather than the more popular ones which her newspaper would prefer her critique.  She also has a problem with the family of the titled English gentleman.  At a dinner at the family seat she wonders if anyone has ever bothered to ask the fox what it thinks of the hunt.  Her sense of humor is sharp and she has a keen eye for hypocrites.  "Jane" is a terrific story of a woman on her own trying to live an independent life considered more suitable for a man.  The writing is good, the characters entertaining and all too human and as the "plot thickens" and I won't divulge how, the reader is rooting for Jane to land on her feet.
