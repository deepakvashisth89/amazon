This is a fascinating story about how the CIA works and the courage and dedication and tremendous resourcefulness and intelligence it takes to be a successful agent told by one of the best, Antonio Mendez.  One of my favorite genres is the spy thriller and I have read well over a hundred of the novels written since World War II.  I have so much respect and admiration for Agent Mendez who spent tens of thousands of hours learning the tradecraft of the CIA,  adding to it his own specialty of costume disguise and the use of makeup learned from Hollywood.  This extremely effective new tradecraft saved many lives whether agents or kidnapped citizens, such as the 6 American diplomats who were Iranian hostages.  These 6 diplomats were given completely new identities and fortunately left Iran safely. Tony Mendez was one of the very best who created an important tradecraft and used it to rescue many of our own agents as well as facilitating defections of foreign agents who were trying to defect to the US.  The extreme concentration and dedication and intelligence needed to learn many forms of tradecraft is just stunning.
