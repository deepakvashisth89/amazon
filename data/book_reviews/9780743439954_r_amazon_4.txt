Started reading Louise Luna's books with "Two Girls Down." This was a good book with a great plot and lots of action. This one-- not so much. Confusing jumps back and forth in time left me often wondering what the character were doing and when they were doing it. Never truly told a story, but just hopped around in time in the main character's troubled life with no plot sequence. Mainly they just drank a lot, took lots of drugs and otherwise did nothing worth reading about. Don't waste hard earned dollars on this do nothing, plotless book.Read moreRead less
