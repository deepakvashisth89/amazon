I really enjoyed this book. The author has a lovely voice and ability to capture the essence of the period and the people in it. The listless man frustrated with the confines of a very strict traditional Japanese society, his wife who has come to embody the very social constructs that he struggles against, and then of course, the wild young voice of the Fox Woman. I loved the writing, the very real characters with their flaws and very human problems, as well as the fairly well moving plot.Read moreRead less
