Up front I must tell you I'm a sucker for anything from Stuart Kaminsky. So there, the bias is out. The world of a Moscow police squad in the Rostnikov books and the Lieberman and Fonesca series are just so fun to read; but Toby Peters has a special spot. !940's and WWII is going on and Toby is a private dick in LA! What a recipe! But, all his clients are the greats of Hollywood! I just love the descriptions of old LA and the characters. Kaminsky could do characters better than any of the modern authors. The tongue in cheek parts about the famous actors and actresses had just the right touch of seriousness and the plot will do justice for the characters he created.
