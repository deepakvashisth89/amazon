Mysterious strangers, a hanging in the woods found by the birthday boy Nick (Jane's son) at his own party.  Affairs, underground railroad hiding places, incest, murder(s), mental illness and that not all.  Jane seems to get involved even if she doesn't realize it at the time.  When Jane gets an odd client named Goddess (think a really outrageous Madonna) things seem to be going her way.  Then more drama happens.Daniel (Jane's assistant) gets married.  His finance Laura is pregnant.  Daniels estranged father comes to the wedding but doesn't make it to through the reception.  During all this, Winky (the cat) keeps going bananas.  Jane is dating police detective Stanley Greenberg and they keep finding dead bodies.  What does one murder have to do with the other?  Clues come to Jane when she racks her brain.  Little things at the time, but add them up and you have the identity of the killer  Is anybody really who they say they are?  So much packed into one paperback book.  This mystery has it all and then some.  Question is, will Jane live to tell about it?Read moreRead less
