You know, it's not many books I read a second time; this is one of those, however.  How come? Because though I read many hundreds of books over a year, this one has almost haunted me.  So, when I saw it was available on Kindle, I definitely got it.  Enjoyed it?  Oh, yes and twice as much this time as the first time.  This guy is a great writer.  He switches scenes smoothly and you know they will all come together, but your brain is racing to figure out just how.  Definitely recommended to all mystery readers that like descriptions as well as realistic conversational speech of the characters.  By the way, did I mention he has great characters?  Not only great, but believable.  Find out for yourself. I put this novel right up with 'To Kill a Mockinbird,' and hell, yes, I may just read it a third time.Read moreRead less
