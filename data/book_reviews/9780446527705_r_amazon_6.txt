If you look at the glorification of Henry Hill in the movie "Goodfellas" you would think he was not really a bad guy, just a guy doing bad things.  This book paints Henry Hill in a completely different light, from the viewpoint of his 2 oldest Children.  Greg Hill pulls in punches in describing what a degenerate Henry Hill was, and the amount of danger he put his children in.  There are very few books I read twice, this one was so riveting I read it twice in a week just so I didn't miss anything. An eye opening read.Read moreRead less
