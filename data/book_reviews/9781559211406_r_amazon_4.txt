This is a small collection of letters between Helene Hanff and the Charing Cross Book Booksellers in London with whom she dealt with over two decades.The book is so small in fact, one could easily complete it over one's lunch break.Nonetheless, it is well worth the read for it's charm and an insight into the lives of those living in England and the US following the Second World War.I love Helene Hanff's humour; especially the delight she takes in attempting to break through the reserve of the Britishers with whom she corresponds, in particular Frank Doel who remained a friend despite the fact they never met.The common bond was their appreciation and love of good literature and this will suit all of you who fall into that category.Read moreRead less
