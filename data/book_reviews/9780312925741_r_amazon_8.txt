I found this book in Johnston's Plainsmen series not quite as good as the previous four books.  This may be due to the fact there isn't as much source material for the Modoc War as there is for many of the other tribes and battles.  I still found this book worth reading as it tells the story of a people fighting for their way of life that is not well known.
