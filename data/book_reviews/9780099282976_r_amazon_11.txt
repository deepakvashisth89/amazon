Wonderful writing. Savage and beautiful; steeped in the small-minded cruelty of common, ordinary people. Casual police, casual vindictiveness. People easily persuaded by rumour and pre-judgement. The "hero" a sort of neutral, or negative  figure, cancelling out good through no real heartfelt interaction: a remote man, yet hyper aware of the effect of other people. Innocent victims and guilty victims. A tale of woe and disaster initiated by a repeated act of compulsion, in itself harmless, and one the protagonist wants to cease doing. His wife, or ex-wife is one of the nastiest, most spiteful characters ever created. The only good person is the doctor. Top drawer writing.Read moreRead less
