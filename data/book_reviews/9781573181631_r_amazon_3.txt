I've now read all the books through Volume 15 of this series and am very impressed with the continued high quality of both the fiction and the articles. In Volume 15, the article by L. Ron Hubbard, "Arts vs Eats," was, I thought, an insightful look at the how and why of writing that seems as applicable today as when he wrote it. And, while it wasn't writing advice, per se, the Tim Powers article provided some light fun and a glimpse of what it must be like to be a winner who gets published in these books.
