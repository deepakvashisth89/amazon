(I am actually Daniel J. Anderson's father.)I have been a Christian preacher for over 40 years. I have read many commentaries on the Book of Jonah and yet I believe that this book for children is better than any of them. It is merely the NIV text of Jonah with Mitchell's wonderful pictures of a little mouse (The Jewish Jonah) going to preach to Nineveh to preach to the Ninevites (large cats) that he hates. The pictures are gorgeous, humorous and touching AND they speak to the issue of ethnic mistrust and hatred, Jonah's anger at God for forgiving the terrible cats and God's kindness to the little imperfect & whining servant. As a lectionary preacher the Jonah text comes up only once every three years. Every third year I would bring this book to the worship service. It is a gift of grace. I am amazed that no one has reprinted it. I would also highly recommend this book to rabbi's and to Jewish parents. It is a precious gift to all of us. It is one of the best books on ethnic issues I have every read. (Search the web merely to see the cover of the book to get a grasp at one of the pictures.)Rev. Dr. F. Christopher AndersonPS.
