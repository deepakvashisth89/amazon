Grab an affordable, best-condition-you-can-get copy from here or Abe because this is THE name book, the original non-duplicable irreplaceable definitive book of names. I've been into them (and astrology) since early childhood thanks to Ms. Rule, and hers is the only book of its kind I've ever seen that's worth having on your shelf. Well, perhaps Rita Blockton's "Don't Call Me Rover," but that's for pets, and I tend to give animals "people" names anyway. (Ever met another catfish called Renshaw or Mercedes before? Didn't think so.) Within its compact pages are the thousands of options, arranged of course alphabetically (first half female and second male), plus their roots/origins/meanings/usages, nicknames, variations, and notable bearers. Alternate forms of each are bolded so as to be highly visible and unnecessary to list twice. I can think of just one name that's not included, though it is a rather unusual one and would be inserted as a variant of another. Even for one such as I who doesn't have or want kids, the book is an excellent reference (well, let's say I'm trying to figure out how one would go about having kids with absolutely zero effort and participating only in the fun parts, such as naming!) Over the years I've delighted in forming favorites and naming toys, characters, pets, and such with great skill due to having this tome at hand. The brief introduction to astrology in the beginning is a nice, detailed, interesting bonus that got me buying up every other book on the subject. There's also a little intro with naming tips. This book should be readily available. I had to replace our old copy in 2008 as it had fallen victim to my thankfully long-gone paper-tearing habit; most are still totally readable despite being coverless or cover-damaged, but this one had just fallen apart in that state.
