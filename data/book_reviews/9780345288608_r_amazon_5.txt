The stories in this book may come as a great surprise. Don't expect more first person adventures of Chandler's detective hero Philip Marlow, aka John Dalmas (if there were, I'd have made no complaint) in the murky demimonde of Hollywood. Instead these stories offer a much wider of range of characters and situations. My surprise and enjoyment of these new aspects of Chandler's talent was only spoiled by this being about the worst scanned and proofed Kindle books I have ever purchased. I've purchased many Kindle books and eBooks for $2, $1, and even less, some of which lacked a linked table of contents, or contained a few typographical errors. This book costs almost as much a new paperback, has no TOC, and is filled with hundreds of terrible typos. I had been planning to buy Kindle editions of Chandler's other novels and stories, so that I could have them all with me when on the road, but not if they're all like this.If you've enjoyed any of Chandler's other books or films, you will love this book, which contains some of his best writing, and some very different stories, but don't waste your money buying the Kindle book.
