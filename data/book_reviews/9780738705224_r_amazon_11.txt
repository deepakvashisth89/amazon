Do not buy this book if you have lost a loved one by suicide.  Within the opening chapters this author compares people who have died by suicide to people who have murdered people/abused people.  After having lost 2 loved ones in one year (1 to suicide, 1 to illness), I have read just about every book going on death/the otherside/survivors guilt etc to try to help me move on.  This is the first book which has set me back along way in this process.
