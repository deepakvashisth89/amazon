This book describes the very brutal conditions endured by POWs who were held captive in S.V.N, Cambodia or Laos.It also does a good job of describing the widely diverse backgrounds of the POWs themselves and how that either assisted or inhibited them from bonding together for their mutual well being.I served in the province in SVN, Quang Ngai, where most of the camp locations were. The camp was moved about six or seven times between 1965 and March 71, initially in Quang Tin (now Quang Nam) then later south into Quang Ngai Province.
