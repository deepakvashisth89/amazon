I’m not rating Nathaniel Hawthorne (above), to me the greatest 19th century American writer, a ten-star guy if there ever was one, but rather this edition, published by... wait for it... I’m checking the title page, nope, back side of that page, nix... ah, at the very end of the book, in tiny print, “published by Amazon”.What they did was computer-scan an older edition by some technology that doesn’t notice paragraph indentations, and, about every fifth page, copies a line with wide gaps between each word... that, plus the 9 by 11 format and crowded single-spacing, makes a heavy, floppy book that’s a bit awkward to read. But okay, ALL of Hawthorne’s novels for a low, low price... but wait:Though both the front and back covers claim to hold the complete (5) Hawthorne novels, and proudly list the titles, in fact his last novel, “The Marble Faun”, isn’t included, though a selection of his short stories is.Over at Amazon Publishing, someone’s asleep at the switch.
