Do you like stories with lots of adventure and "wow" moments? If so, then this book is for you!This story, "Calamity Jane At Fort Sanders" is about a woman named Jane living in the 1900s. Jane was a carriage driver but wore a hat and bandana to conceal her face so people thought she was a man. She usually stops at a town every now and then but the mayor`s wife kicks her out for making to much noise and being a woman but acting like a man. Jane left the town and decided to head west with her horse Jim. On her journey in the west she finds an american army base known as, "Fort Sanders." Jane decided she was going to keep pretending to be a man and call her self "Missouri Jim."Jane or Missouri Jim decided that she was looking for food, water and adventure so she joined the fort as a scout. It all worked out until one day a terrified farmer ran to the fort saying that indians had attacked his farm. They decided to check it out. When they got to the farm it looked fine and they started to doubt the farmer. They saw a small path and decided to follow it and see if they could find the indians, although they got sidetracked and saw a small pond and decided to swim.Everyone jumped in besides Jane as it would reveal her identity, but she got pushed in anyway.... Her hat and bandana fell off which revealed her face and long red hair. Before they could react they heard indians watching over them. A clumsy troop named Tuttle ran and grabbed his gun and shot at the once peaceful indians. This started a battle so the clumsy coward Tuttle ran away! During the battle the indians wounded and captured the captain of the fort and left. All the troops were thinking of a plan to rescue the captain but Jane just ran in the indian camp like a madwoman! The rest is for you when you read the book.The main character of this book is Jane. She is a female adult with long red hair and an adventurous personality! Jane is very risky and brave because she takes the risk of pretending to be a man, she is very brave because she runs into an indian camp without fear of being killed. The captain is very brave too because he leads all of the troops and scouts into indian territory. Another character in the story is Tuttle. He is very clumsy and a coward. He`s clumsy because he decides to shoot peaceful indians without thinking which leads to the captain being captured and a battle. He is a coward because when the battle starts he run away! I think it makes the story more interesting though!Now lets talk about the theme of this story. I personally think the theme is that if something does not work out or your`e not "good enough", don`t give up. I think this because lots of people said Jane`s not good enough to do what a man can do but she kept on going and does not give up. I think this is a really good theme that fits the story very well.I think this story is great for any young readers. Anyone looking for a book with adventure should really read this!
