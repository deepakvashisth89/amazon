This is a very fine effort by Richard Setlowe.  Harry Styles who is dying from lung cancer undergoes an expermental operation which he is expected to die from, but miraciously survives and thrives to become Glaucus, the gill-man.  Reader beware: this novel although excellently researched and written, is emotionally "draining".  What makes the book very different from a pulp novel is that the characters' emotions are discussed at great length.  I liked the fact that Harry Styles took a last chance in life to do something that might benefit mankind.  But how does Harry fit in now that he is no longer human?  And just as importantly, how does Ruth Styles, Harry's wife, and her (and Harry's) three kids cope with what has happened to their family/lives?
