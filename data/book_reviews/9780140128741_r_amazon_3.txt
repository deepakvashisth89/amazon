Another engrossing and delightfully-told murder mystery by Ms Cauldwell, the third of hers I have read.  The plot is a bit tricky, depending as it does on niceties of Greek classical allusions and British law of inheritance, but few could resist a tale so charmingly told.  The usual legal characters are back, including Cantrb, Ragwort, Tancred, Selena, and of course Julia.  But it is the narrator Law Prof. Hilary Tamar who carries the story, with her shrewd insights into companions and clients alike.
