Despite the blah reviews, I really enjoyed how this book harkened back to his earlier work. Examples: 1) the complex and unhappy father-son relationship (The Man Within); 2) the stark existence of the child whose worth is determined by someone else's need (The Man Within and various stories from Twenty One Stories); 3) the shady operative who turns out to be more truthful than he is initially given credit for (The Quiet American and The Comedians); 4) international intrigue that was relevant at the time of publication (also The Quiet American and The Comedians); and 5) the ruminations on love and how it sometimes leads to self-doubt (The End of the Affair).
