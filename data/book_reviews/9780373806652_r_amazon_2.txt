Kalinda Brady is seeking revenge for a lost love.  Rand Alister is that lost love.  What she does not realize is that he has never stopped loving her.  This is, in comparison to many of Krentz's work, rather a fluffy piece of romance.This book was written in the 1980's an reflects romance as seen in the 80's.
