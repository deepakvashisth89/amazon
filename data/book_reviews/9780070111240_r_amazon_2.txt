It reinforces a brand's differential status from competitors and reinforces many past and present satisfied users...a company must make continuing efforts to maintain an established product's position in the marketplace--if the product's still there, it must have 'made it,' and it needs ad support to stay there...We are not discounting  the importance of new products. But in most product categories, more that 90 percent of the sales and profits come from existing brands. Simply thinking that the company must introduce on new product after another where there aren't clear consumer needs, where there isn't a clear positioning, where there isn't unambiguous product differentiation, doesn't make sense.
