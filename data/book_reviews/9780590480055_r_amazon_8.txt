I wholeheartedly give this book 5 stars!  It is a book which should easily keep young readers entertained (older readers too! ).  It is a story of a young girl who has been taken care of so lovingly that she really doesn’t know much about herself until she has to go to live with her relatives in another state and through this new adventure discovers wonderful things about herself and others.  In a way it is sad (to me) that we have lost a lot of the simple and innocent times in which this story is set, but I am glad that it is still available for new generations of young people to read and hopefully learn from.  The story is written in an easy to read narrative and keeps you wanting to know more and not put it down.Read moreRead less
