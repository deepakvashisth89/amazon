A gripping in depth psychological study of two psychopaths who enjoy egging each other on to play mind games with others and sexually exploit them. The letter format of a book of that period can be tedious but it is well worthwhile .You constantly find yourself thinking, how can they behave like that?  At the same time there are some real moments of bedroom farce that lighten it all, as when Valmont's lover locks herself out of her bedroom and they have to invent a way to regain access without alerting suspicion as her husband is in the next room. For a modern reader,the way the book ends, seems a bit 'convenient' as the bad characters get their just desserts and the book ends like a morality play. For all that, the exploration of the minds of Valmont and Merteuil make this a deeply satisfying book - it's not just about the cheap thrills for the reader as has been claimed in the past.Read moreRead less
