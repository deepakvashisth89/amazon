*Spoilers* The darkest of the 4 Myrer novels I have read. The 3 protagonists were all unsuited for each other. Jo from the start preferred Chapin to Tip. One has to wonder if a young, deeply in love Tip would have continued to pursue a young Jo if she had told him she would have eagerly, joyously slept with Chapin if he hadn't rejected her. Her passion was clearly Chapin. At best she was fondly affectionate of Tip. Poor Tip, he paid dearly for ignoring the clear warning signs that Jo did not want to marry him. And Jo. She paid dearly for being unable to see that Chapin was a sociopath, blinded by her decades-long lust for Chapin. Her finally satisfying that lust and Chapin's revealing of their afternoon of sex on a boat to a humiliated Tip would finally destroy whatever fragile bond that still connected Tip to Jo. Eventually, 2 unfortunate lives destroyed, victims of their passion and failure to understand the closest persons in their lives. A darkly tragic ending.Read moreRead less
