Ruth Bebee Hill has given us a social, anthropological insight into a magnificent culture. Written in 1979 as a fictional account, it is loaded with historical fact and moving insight into a culture now, long lost. Hanta Yo is an enduring work that hopefully, will remind us that there is still much to learn, reconnecting to the sky above and the earth below to mention the least that Ruth Beebe Hill has imparted to us.
