I read the Bible through every 2 or 3 years; typically reading 3 chapters in 3 different books every day.  I find that this keeps me from ONLY reading "begat" or "thou shalt not" passages.  I typically start with Gen 1, Psalms 1, and Matthew 1 and use three bookmarks to keep my places.This year, I decided to read the New Testament in the generally accepted order in which the books are written.  That was helpful and different.  Then after completing the New Testament, I re-read the Gospels  as they are ordered in the Bible.  But once finished... I was looking for more information on The Gospels.I have read this book ("The Complete Gospels") previously, but I decided that the footnotes and commentaries (as part of my meditation) would give me more insights.
