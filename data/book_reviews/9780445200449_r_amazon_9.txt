I really enjoyed this book written back in 1982 by Aaron Elkins and introducing his character, Gideon Oliver, so there are many more of this series for me to read.  Gideon's wife died a couple of years before and now he is a visiting fellow from the USA, teaching anthropology at the University in Heidelberg.  He becomes embroiled in something he never could have expected and suddenly it seems we are in a crime/spy novel that never lets up and takes us on a wild ride around Europe.  I thoroughly enjoyed it.  I intend to read the next in the series shortly, and hope that it entertains me as much as this one did.Read moreRead less
