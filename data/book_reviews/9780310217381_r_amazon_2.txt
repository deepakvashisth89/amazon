Fire of Heaven is the first of a trilogy and while I have not finished reading all three books, I am greatly looking forward to #2 and 3.  I've read other books by Bil Myers and it was because of his writing style I began to buy his other works.  I find his theology sound and he creates a very believable scenario in every book.
