Unfortunately, her concentration is shot-to-hell ever since she ran into riverboat gambler Brent Connors in her last few 'assignments.'Brent Connors has seen Jewel with 3 different 'fathers,' in 3 different disguises, in as many months.  Even though he thinks she's nothing but a con artist, he finds himself falling for her hard & fast.
