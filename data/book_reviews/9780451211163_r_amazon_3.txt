This is another enjoyable book by the author who wrote Julie and Romeo - and I love THIS book, too.  Somehow, she's able to convey the beautiful chaos and harmony that's possible in a family dynamic with multiple people, and somehow she's also able to bring together a lovely story with a general sense of all-is-well or an it-all-works-out-in-the-long-run.  I love her writing, and when I got lost in this book, it was an absolute pleasure. This is a great story, well-told.Read moreRead less
