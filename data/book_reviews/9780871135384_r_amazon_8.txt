Cynthia Heimel has written another great "just say YES" book.  Yes to sex, yes to drugs, and yes to rock&roll.  Heimel writes what most of us have been thinking all along...that we're not all sweet, shy, moral creatures with perfect blow-dry  bobs.  Some of us are wild haired, trashy-mouthed girls that just wanna have fun.  Heimel tells us how to do it.  Be good to your dog, like your kids but give 'em space, treat everyone well but take no crap from anybody...and if that doesn't work, drink and play pool in a smoky gin-joint until you can pick up one of the band members.  Heimel's not unsentimental.  She writes poignantly about women struggling to raise kids on their own and women that are beginning to hit the end of their babe years.  She is giggle-to-yourself funny when writing about (and against) answering machines, fashion, and rich people.I've read all Heimel's books by now and am willing to bribe her to write another.  Fix yourself a drink, get under the covers, and enjoy this trashy, funny book.Read moreRead less
