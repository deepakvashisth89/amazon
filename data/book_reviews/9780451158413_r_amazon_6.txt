This is a very classic, old-school style of writing and time period for this material. You have to appreciate the fact that the writer also wrote stories for one of the best detectives who ever lived on television: Columbo! In all fairness, you have to at least read one of these classics to appreciate the history of Mr. McBain. He set the stage for many a detective story, and was writing way before my time! The material is obviously from the 1950's, therefore will appeal to a select audience or those who appreciate older literature. I still found it worth reading, quick, and easy to work through.
