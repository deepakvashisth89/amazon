Slade’s first book, HEADHUNTER is hands down the best horror novel I’ve ever read. The “author” is actually the pen name two criminal attorneys and a forensic psychiatrist. Their second effort, GHOUL was somewhat less spectacular, but in many ways only because the first novel was so incredibly tight. BED OF NAILS is, I believe, Slade’s 8th novel and though I read numbers 3 and 4, the downward path was clear and with BED OF NAILS they’ve hit rock bottom.Not only have they resurrected the antagonist – an astral plane time-travelling Jack the Ripper clone (currently housed in an asylum) from a previous and far better novel than this, but the plot here is so random and so full of holes that the author(s) is forced to consume 200 pages with travelogues, history lessons only marginally relevant to the plot and a lot of cheesy S&M/torture/cannibalism schtick that any fan of LAW & ORDER will be familiar with.A little research on the “author” revealed that for an unspecified reason, one of the two attorneys left the trio some 3 novels ago. No one seems to know WHY s/he left, or whether or not s/he is currently writing under another name, but two things ARE clear: (1) the departing attorney was THE writing talent in the group and (2) s/he would have seen that THIS "Slade Novel" is just a piece of scat floating to the top of the cesspool.Read moreRead less
