This is a really great book. Especially if you are currently or have experienced conflicts with your fellow women friends or other relationships like family. It provides a lot of insight and has many great quotes to take with you when you are done. It is a basic start to feminist literature and reading, but that is because one, it was written and based off of white women in the 80s, and second, because it mainly focuses on friendship. It references patriarchy quite a few times but does not delve deep enough for me. Patriarchy and how our social system is set up affects women's friendships intensely. To set 2025 standards to this book is doing it a disservice. It's a great book for the average woman to understand a bit more about her modern friendships, that is all. Some parts did blow my mind because it gave me more perspective and reasoning than I had previously. It is repetitive, but if you can get past that, it is a great start and beginner book to reading books on women and feminism.Read moreRead less
