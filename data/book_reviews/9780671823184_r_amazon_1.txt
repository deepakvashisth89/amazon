A businesswoman falls down the stairs and breaks her neck. Was she pushed?The suspects are an odd bunch. The dead woman’s ex-employee and ex-lover looks like a baboon and can be violent. The dead woman’s daughter sleeps with every man in sight, including the baboon, and she witnessed the “accident.” There are a couple of business associates who are very nervous. And there’s the nasty neighbor who poisoned the daughter’s dog...The investigators — Grijpstra, de Grier, and the Commissaris — risk their lives solving this messy case. The alcohol flows, and so does the whimsical conversation.Everything works well in this crime novel — plot, subplots, characterization.
