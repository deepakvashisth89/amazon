I loved this novel, a kind of African Thornbirds story.  A man and his young wife go to Kenya at the turn of the 19th century.  He builds, takes care of his family and kills 2 man eating lions while he’s at it, while his wife tries to survive raising children in a wild, unforgiving backdrop.
