My 9 year-old son found this at the library and loved it.  We ordered it before we had to take back the library's copy.  It seems to show every car ever made from every country.  There is a brief collection of stats at the bottom of each picture like highest speed, number of passengers; etc.  It feels like a book of baseball cards for cars.  He pours over it every night and enjoys the color pictures.  I only wish there was a more updated version.  This one was published in the 1970's but then, the older cars are more fun for him anyway.
