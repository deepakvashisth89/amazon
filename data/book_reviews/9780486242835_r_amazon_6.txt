Less known than the Brady team photographs, Russell, in his capacity as a photographer for the Railroad Contruction Corps, shows us another side of the Civil War. We see the City Point docks & railroad yards, barracks  & arsenals, Lincoln's private railroad car, bridges, fortifications,  ships, huge stables, derricks & the ruins of Richmond. This book is a  military engineer's view of the war. I find these photographs blunt,  realistic, artless (in the sense of time they were made) & utterly  fascinating.
