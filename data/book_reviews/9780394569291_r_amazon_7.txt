I read 'Through a Glass Darkly' years ago and was delighted to find its sequel.  Koen paints an historically accurate and vivid picture of time and place and creates dynamic characters who have interior lives that drive their actions.  In some ways, 'Face to Face' resembles Downton Abbey in that it jumps back and forth between 2 sets of lives and concerns occurring at the same time, but this novel moves between the England of George I and its Virginia colony.  The reader is dying to know how things will turn out in the end, yet the read is so absorbing, one hates for the novel to end.Read moreRead less
