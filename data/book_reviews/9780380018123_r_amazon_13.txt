This funny, whimsical, and utterly irreverent collection contains the descriptions of twelve 'voyages' undertaken by ace star-pilot Ijon Tichy. As usual, reading them clearly establishes that while the stories are ostensibly about far-flung planets and gigantic issues, the author was talking about petty-minded people and organisations whom we come across everyday.I liked the tales. But, despite sounding blasphemous I confess that while going for scifi satire, I would heceforth stick to Sheckley, Russell, and Tenn.Your call.Read moreRead less
