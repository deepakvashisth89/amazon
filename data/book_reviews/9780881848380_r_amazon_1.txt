This is a 1975 Pronzini stand-alone, every bit as good as his other non-series books. The streets of Singapore and the jungle of the Malay peninsula play an integral part in this story about an ex-smuggler pilot who is trying to go straight, but he gets framed by some unsavory types on a steamer ship.  The characters are well-drawn and intriguing. Pronzini keeps you captivated throughout, and he never ceases to pay off in the end. The man is amazing.Read moreRead less
