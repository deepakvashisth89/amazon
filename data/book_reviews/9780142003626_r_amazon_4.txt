T.R. Pearson has captured the essence of small town life in this wonderful book, "A Short History of a Small Place." This book is not your traditional story with a plot that makes twists and turns but moves along as the pages continue. This is a portrait of a small town. The stories center around a prominent woman who is a bit of a recluse, and all of the characters that we meet have their stories somehow connect to this woman. The book rambles through the people of this small North Carolina town's life, and weaves a wonderful and always entertaining peek at life in a small town. The narrator of this book is a teen-aged boy, and the author makes good use of the device "momma says" or "daddy says" to explain concepts which may not be familiar to the reader.
