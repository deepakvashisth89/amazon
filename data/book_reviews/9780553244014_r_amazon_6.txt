I first read this when I was a young teenager. I'm now in my 50's,and it is just as good now  It is humorous, well written and appropriate for all ages. The life of the Gilbreths is fascinating and its amazing the way this family worked together, kept the household orderly, and had fantastic adventures. The work of the father, way before "ergonomics" was entered into our vernacular, is terrific. It is a pleasant, easy and entertaining book which I'm sure all will enjoy.
