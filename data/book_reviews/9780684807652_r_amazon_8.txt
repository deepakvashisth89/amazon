Carolly Erickson's "Her Little Majesty" is an insightful and enjoyable read. The book spans the entire life of Queen Victoria, from her youth straight through her death. I was pleased by Erickson's break from her usual 'historical novel.' Supported by well-cited facts, it details her personality, her private struggles, and her relationships with friends, family, attendants, and the public. I highly recommend this for any person interested in British royalty.Read moreRead less
