It is important that you know two things before you buy/read "Where are the Children?"1) The book needs a trigger warning. There are references to pedophilia, child molestation, and child rape. They are not explicit but there were a few moments in the book that made me cringe.2) There are different types of mysteries. It is important to know that in this book, the reader isn't trying to guess who committed the crime. You know who the kidnapper is on page 1, a good portion of the book is from his POV. The reasons you read the book are basically two-fold, to find out why he kidnapped them, and to find out if they will be saved before the crimes mentioned in #1 occur.With that information down we can talk about the novel as a whole. The book is gripping and suspenseful and you just HAVE TO turn the next page to find out what happens to the children. That doesn't mean the book is perfect. I couldn't help feeling at times that the plot and all the happenstance meetings felt contrived. I think some of this is due to how the book is laid out with the popping back and forth between POV's during suspenseful moments. The book also never really gave a reason why the 'bad-guy' was actually at that location. Like why did he want to torment her?
