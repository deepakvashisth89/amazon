This book is so different, but I loved it. It was emotional to read, but felt like it was the right time for me to read it.  Just like in movies, with books you have to have an imagination. It was sad that Jenny and Luke broke up before Luke went off to college. It was even sadder that Luke gave money to Jenny to  get an abortion. I am so glad that Jenny didn’t do what he said. Jenny’s laughter and light seemed to get darker after Luke left. I was glad she had Danny as they were close and what kept her going. Her family was messed up, but tried to help her in their own ways. Danny was 12 and was desperate to meet his father, Luke. It was sad that Luke’s parents had mapped out his whole life and he had let them. He was sad even though he was rich and a well respected doctor. Unfortunately he married a woman that was a lot like his mother—haughty, uppity, money was god, and thought mostly of herself.After Danny gets hit by a car that didn’t stop and is in critical condition,Luke meets his son and sees Jenny again after 13 years. It was so interesting how Ms. Freethy handled Danny being between like and death. There were so many lessons to be learned not only by Danny, but many of his relatives. There were times that broke my heart.  The book made me think more about my own life. I strongly encourage you to read the book.Read moreRead less
