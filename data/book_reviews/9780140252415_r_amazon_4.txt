I'm a huge Wallace Stegner fan, so I had high hopes when I started reading this book.  Unfortunately, the book was a really big disappointment for me.  Maybe I shouldn't have been surprised.  "A Shooting Star" is hardly a well known book, and it's received few accolades.To reinforce what others have said, the plot is turgid, the characters are neither credible nor likeable, and it seems like Stegner couldn't get out of his own way in telling the story.  Granted, expectations have a lot to do with a reader's enjoyment of a book.  If you've recently discovered this title and you think it might be a hidden gem, then let me save you some disappointment.
