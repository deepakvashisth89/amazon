For a work of fiction, it's okay.  The chief "bad guy" has an unrealistic name (unbelievable to me that intelligence wouldn't have the name of the leading Colombian cartel leader).  Characters in the book are a bit superficial and not well crafted.  Some plot developments seem wildly unrealistic given how the Pentagon operates.  The plot (spoiler alert) seems cribbed from Clancy's "Clear and Present Danger".  But the trade craft and details for a US Special Forces ODA back in the 90's are pretty spot-on and accurate.
