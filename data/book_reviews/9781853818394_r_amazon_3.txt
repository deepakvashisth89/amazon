This is the second part of Angela Carter's Bristol Trilogy, set in the late 1960s. Although she never mentions the name of the city (the Mendips are given a brief mention), any Bristolian will recognise her description of a run-down, seedy bedsit-land of Clifton. The big Georgian terraces divided up into small flats for students and dropouts, such as the main characters in the story; Joseph, Anne, Kay and Sunny, although Viv and his mother seem to live down on the Cumberland Road, by the river. I love Viv's Bristolianisms, "My star, my lover!" As for the main character, Joseph - mad as a badger. It's not so much a storyline, more an evocation of a place and time, in this instance Clifton in Bristol in the late 1960s.
