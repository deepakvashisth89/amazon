This book appeals to me as an aging gay man living in the rural south, Holleran's elegant prose allows me to check my attitudes against his, coming from a large city and an active former life. Most of my friends and relatives are now gone and I too must make my peace with loneliness and invisibility. Of all the gay writers, I most identify with Andrew Holleran and his work and appreciate the comfort they provide at this end of life. We had much in common growing up. This book is well worth the struggle I had to find it, it seems to be "sold out" everywhere, but I found a splendid "like new" hardbound copy here on Amazon.
