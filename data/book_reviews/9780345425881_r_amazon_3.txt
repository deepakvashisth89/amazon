This is my first experience reading Sally Wright and I have enjoyed it immensely. After completing this first book in the Ben Reese series I went on to read 3 more and enjoyed them too.The series is set in the 60's between an Academic Institute in the USA and Great Britain. The main character Ben Reese is an Archivist at the Institute and also a WWII veteran with very distinguished career as a intelligence gatherer. Unfortunately the first fatality is his very close friend and he is unsatisfied with the coroner's reason for death. The author does a great job in giving the reader a sense of place in each book and I thought the writing style was good too. She draws out the suspense until the conclusion of each book.  Overall I think they were value for money and recommend them to all who enjoy a well told intrigue.  A bit Holmes(ism)  Give them a try. . .Read moreRead less
