I love this book so much I've purchased it three times.  I keep giving it away to friends.  It is accessible for even those who are not ardent fans of poetry generally.  I think it is a friendly collection with some expected standards and some works you probably haven't seen much.  I'm a fan of Keillor anyway...no lazy Sunday is the same without his show playing on the radio in the background while I read the paper.  If you are looking for a good, solid collection this will do.  I like the way the book is divided into sections for easy reference depending on your mood.
