I bought this book for my then-husband in 1989. He loves history and especially WWII, and he loved the book. He read it until it literally fell apart. Over the years I had always tried to find it for him again, but could only remember what the cover looked like, but not the author or title of the book. Just before Christmas we were chatting and he mentioned a story he remembered from the book. I googled it, and found the book! I ordered it for him immediately and he has already read it twice and is working on his third time.
