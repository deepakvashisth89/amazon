This book has a lot of detail, however, assembled into a confusing order. It seems the author is a master of cut and paste, assembling other authors as shown with a lengthy bibliography and quotations. The writing style became tedious and confusing by the overuse of em dashes and convoluting sentences by trying to incorporate too much information. A better use of commas might have streamlined the writing. Historical data would have been more understandable if presented in a better chronological  order. There is too much minutiae drawn out too extensively to the point being just clutter. Sentence structure is extremely poor and I found myself rereading a sentence and rewriting it to understand it better. It was a tedious read and I felt the author might have been wise to rely on a more expert editor.
