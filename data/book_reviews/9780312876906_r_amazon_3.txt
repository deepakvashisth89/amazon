Overall, this book covers a lot of great ideas. I love the central premise of the entire series. With that said, the writing and character development is pretty weak. The abundant embedded advertising is distracting. And the author uses the storyline to insert a variety of political themes centered around gender, equity, inclusivity, religion and race... but none of the subjects get fleshed out well which leaves much of the storyline feeling half baked.Overall, a pleasant enough quick read. The prose itself isn't super intellectually challenging but the creativity centered around the main Neanderthal premise is thought provoking and entertaining.Read moreRead less
