I bought this book because it was cheap; and I thought it might have some useful recipes (which it does).  However, it is much more than a recipe book.  The basis of the book is the author deciding to drive across America in search of one of the country's iconic symbols,pie.  Along the way, it is the story of the people she meets and is travelling with, and the places she visits. It gives a picture of the small towns she visits and the people  in them.  It is also the author's own story.  The book is well written, informative, humourous and, above all, makes you want to go and bake a pie.Read moreRead less
