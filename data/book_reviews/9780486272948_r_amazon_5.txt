Usually Dover has their act together.  I'm really surprised by this edition.  It says "Unabridged" on the cover.  When you write that on a children's classic that means it's the original text.  Not so here.  This is a rather spare adaptation, and in some cases the narrative is not only simplified but altered and added to.  Apparently they were trying to catch mermaids on Marooner's Rock, and the Never Bird was demoted to just some sea bird whose nest fell into the water and happened to float by.
