As one who served as an infantry platoon leader in Viet Nam, this book is the best I have read from the point of view of a junior officer in a combat leadership position.  I didn't read it until the late 80's and was amazed at how well Lee described the country, the frustrations, the stresses, and the leadership challenges of commanding young men only a few years younger than yourself and how similar my experiences were.  The journal that Lee kept while in Viet Nam was a tremendous idea, that allowed him to recall events with great clarity.  This book should be read by any young person thinking of going into the combat arms of our Armed Services to help them prepare for the challenges of leadership.
