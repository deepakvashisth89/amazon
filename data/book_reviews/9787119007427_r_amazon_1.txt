This anthology of 1980s stories and novellas from mainland China includes one selection from eleven different representative PRC novelists of that decade. Many of these novelists are missing from the more mainstream anthologies of modern and contemporary Chinese literature, such as the one from Columbia University Press. Since that decade was one of the best so far in terms of literary quality and its impact, this book is well worth reading. Zhang Kangkang's "Bitter Dreams," Wang Anyi's "Lao Kang is Back," and Zhang Xianliang's "Bitter Springs" are particularly well written and well translated.
