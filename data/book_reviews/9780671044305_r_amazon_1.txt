Martha Grimes is always dependable for a good mystery, full of twists and turns and colorful characters, any one of whom could be the murderer.  Richard Jury is again the intelligent police detective who figures out the killer, aided once more by his friend Melrose Plant, an earl who gave up his title to crack both witticisms and crimes.In TheOld Fox Deceived, the reader finds both a fox hunt and deception with characters both dressed in mask and costume and also masquerading under assumed names.
