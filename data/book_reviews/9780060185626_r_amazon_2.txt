Really like most of the books in this series. The mysteries are intriguing, and not usually too far fetched. However, some of the things Jance write for Beau are just not in line with a male thought pattern. Men and women think about things differently (as in Men are from Mars, Women are from Venus). In each book, there are a few instances of this. It's not enough to put me off of reading the next book, but it is sometimes annoying. Also, looking at the time span over all of these books, there are some lore long term issues that should have evolved more than they have.
