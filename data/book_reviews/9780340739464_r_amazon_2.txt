Kay Farrow is a photographer who becomes devasted when her mentor and best friend Maddy Yamada is murdered. She investigates and finds that there is a mysterious Gun club called the Godess Gun Club which is somehow involved.David Hunt had great success with Kay Farrow in 'The Magician's Tale' and he does so again here, albeit this tale is less illusion more straightforward mob crime and big money.
