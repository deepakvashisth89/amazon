I have read this book at least 7 or 8 times and have given it as a gift to a number of folks. The author takes each line of the prayer and shares a story that he has heard or experienced in his own life and a story from St. Francis' life, and then uses the stories to reflect on each line. The stories are extremely powerful - a cab driver driving an old lady (who is on her finally journey as she is heading to Hospice) around the city after midnight to pass by places from her youth and refusing to collect payment; a WWII US naval officer who shows compassion and understanding;  I could go on and on.
