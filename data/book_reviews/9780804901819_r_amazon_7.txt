The Marvelous Land of Oz was my favorite Oz book growing up. I loved the story of how Jack Pumpkinhead and the Saw Horse came to life. I enjoyed reading their adventures with Tip. Discovering that Tip was really Ozma was a happy moment. I remember how much the illustrations added to my enjoyment of the book. I'm so glad that this book has been released in an illustrated kindle version. I was concerned at first that the pictures might not show up well on the kindle. The illustrations are so beautiful, though. The publisher has truly done an outstanding job of optimizing their appearance for kindle.
