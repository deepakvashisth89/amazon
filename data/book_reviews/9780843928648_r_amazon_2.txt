I have read quite a few of CONNIE MASON's books at this point, and I guess this is the first one the proofreader messed up on. There was not a lot of misspelled words, but there was enough to throw you off at times! For example, how in the world could a person possibly get "dunk" in place of think?! Or, "the" for die? None the less, if a person can over look those things, the story was very good. The h/h were interesting and so were the characters.I especially liked the loyalty of "Sam's" brother Will.And contrary to one review, I don't think Colt used the word darlin excessively. In fact, I liked it and in some spots I thought it was endearing. I recommend reading this story folks. You won't be disappointed.Read moreRead less
