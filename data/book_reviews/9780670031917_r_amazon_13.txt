I first read the first three books of Father Anselm way back in 2002.  The first book provides a historic web of intrigue spreading from Paris in 1942 and the horrendous ‘round-up’ of Jews before their train journey to Auschwitz-Birkenau (although Birkenau is somehow misplaced in the novel) and their subsequent murders, and modern day families in Britain.  No spoilers here.  It is a good read although I question the need for an epilogue in which the whole story is re-told so as to be understood?  Maybe there was too many wood for the trees?Nevertheless, it is an honourable handling of a truly horrific piece of history.  Intrigue across generations, criminals, murderers, survivors and Father Anselm in the middle.  Joyous really.  Enjoy!  Now for book two ......Read moreRead less
