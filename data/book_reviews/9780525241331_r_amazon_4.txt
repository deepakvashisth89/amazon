Why do I enjoy reading the poetry of James Kavanaugh? Many of the characters in his poems live lives quite different from my own, and the worldview is often different, and even contrary, to mine.But I find his work incredibly readable. I don't have to look up words or allusions; I don't have to do research to find out what he's talking about. And Kavanaugh's "voice" comes across quite clearly.Most of these poems are short, less than a page long. They all have a beginning, middle and an end, and the conclusion is often a biting commentary on the situation in the poem. Irony is often the point.Perhaps that's what I like best: the irony. Life is filled with ironies, and Kavanaugh has a way of highlighting some in ways that cause one to ponder the ironies in one's own life.I bought my copy of "Maybe If I Loved You More" at a used book sale on Long Island while I was going through a period of grief. Many of the earlier poems brought uncontrollable tears to my eyes. A year later, I finally finished this short volume, and many of the poems brought a smile to my face. So, these poems can serve as a mirror, reflecting what's in the soul, and helping to put words and thoughts to the emotions that are well beneath the surface.I enjoyed this book and will undoubtedly pick it up again from time to time.Read moreRead less
