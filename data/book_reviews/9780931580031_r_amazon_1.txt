My brother gave me this book, in hardback, 19 years ago. I still have it.This book can be read from cover to cover, or each poem individually. It covers the joy of meeting a new love to the loss of the love. When you read the poems, you will feel the pain/joy of the author and some poems will bring back the memory of your love or loss. There will definitely be one poem which you will absolutely identify with. The poetry contains humor, sadness, sarcasm and joy.I've recently purchased this book for someone I love very much for a Valentine's Day gift and inscribed it with a message.
