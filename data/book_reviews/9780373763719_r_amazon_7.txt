This is the first book in a new series where we are introduced to another branch of the Fortune's of Texas family tree.This series is titled, The Fortune's of Texas;The Lost Heirs.In this first book, we meet Kane Fortune who is the son of Miranda. Who is Miranda?? Read the book. In one night of passion, Kane feels he has met the "one" and proposes marriage.
