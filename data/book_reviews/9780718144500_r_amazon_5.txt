Most murder mysteries focus on the murderer and the cops pursuing him or her. The victims often get short-shrift.Here, Nicci French (husband and wife team Nicci Gerrard and Sean French) tell the stories from the perspective of the victims . . . up to the very end. It’s an interesting approach involving three women who live in North London but differ widely in age and economic circumstances. All received threatening notes that make it clear they are being watched.Because these women have so little in common it is a particularly challenging series of crimes to solve. The same set of detectives and police officers are involved in all the cases and do a bumbling job of it. (For followers of French’s earlier books, psychiatrist Grace Schilling is brought in to do some profiling.)The victims are likeable or inspire compassion. French has cleverly planted cues right under the reader’s nose, but as Dr. Schilling could tell us, we see what we look for.An enjoyable read. A different approach to a mystery.
