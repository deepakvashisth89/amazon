There's a lot going on in this novel, as we follow a 747 flying from place to place that may be full of passengers infected with a deadly virus, a paid assassin hired to shoot them down, and the CIA folks in DC trying to keep that from happening. The book jumps from story to story and keeps all the different plots moving along at a breakneck pace. The author really seems to know his stuff, especially in the area of planes and flying, and he creates credible (if somewhat stock) characters to populate the novel. It's perfect for the beach or (ironically) a long airline flight.Read moreRead less
