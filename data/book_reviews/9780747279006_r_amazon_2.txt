I've been a huge Laymon fan for years now, so I when saw that another of his books was released I had to read it right away. This definitely wasn't one of his best works in terms of horror though. I kept waiting for something to happen but there was never any real action until nearing the end. I gave it 4 stars though because I could not put it down! This was a very well told story with well developed, believable characters. Despite this not being your typical hard-core Laymon book, it's still a very good & enjoyable book!
