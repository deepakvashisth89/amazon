Such a wonderful portrayal of the British colonists in West Africa. I think this book should be listed as a classic and be on the recommended lists for schools. This is the second book I have read by Boyd and I have to say I think he is quite brilliant. His characters are so vivid they bounce off the pages.The mark of a great writer ,in my humble opinion, is to be able to tell a story of relatively ordinary day to day happenings and turn it into a "can't wait to get back to it" event.This book will not appeal to those who like paced action as indicated by some of the reviews, but if you enjoy (and understand) subtle British humour, wonderful characters and top class writing I urge you to buy this delightful book.It is very funny in parts and I DID laugh out loud many times. Goodness.....poor old Morgan Leafy; he can't take a trick 'as the saying goes.'A very amusing book that I WILL recommend to anyone who enjoys fine literature.Read moreRead less
