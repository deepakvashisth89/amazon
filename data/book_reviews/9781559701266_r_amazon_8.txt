Baigent and Leigh create an interesting work here. Little did I know the involvement of Jacque de Molay, or the Knights Templar (historically) on modern day Freemasonry. This book is unlike the others that attempt to trace an absolute origin of Freemasonry; Baigent and Leigh simply offer ample suggestions with strong evidences behind its possible origin, and even throw in, although probably unkowingly, some political psychology in their thesis.
