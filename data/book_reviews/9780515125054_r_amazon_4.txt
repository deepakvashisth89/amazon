In my quest to find and read as many good books as I possibly can, I discovered this well written little gem. Not realizing that this is book two of "The Magical Love" series of books all about the same story-line, I read this one first. This is a stand alone story, but it would be much better if read in order with the other two books in this series. It took time and effort to round them up, but I found the other two books and now plan to read them in order.Flowers by Moonlightis book one of "The Magical Love" series."Kissed By Starlight" is book two andMagic by Daylightis book three.To understand "Kissed By Starlight" better, you should read "Flowers By Moonlight" first.All the characters are there and it explains the entire history of everything that happens to Blaic before he's turned to stone and released.Once upon a time...on a cold snowy March day our heroine, young and beautiful Human Felicia Starret has just left her beloved father's funeral. She finds herself bereft and crying in the family garden over a stone statue of a cloaked man. Her tears magically release our hero, green-eyed and handsome Blaic, an elven Prince, from a curse laid on him by his high king Boadach the Eternal, King of the Living Lands and of all the Realm Beyond the World that Dies, for disobedience to an order. Blaic is now bound to Felicia, a mortal creature owing her a debt of gratitude for releasing him from his curse. This rankled him. He did not want to be beholden to a human. But beholden to her he is. So Blaic begins to find a way to repay Felicia for her gift. At the same time he also has an ulterior motive in store for her. His curse has one more part to break that must be finished before he is fully free. Felicia's tears did released Blaic from the stone, but they didn't fully break the curse. To do that...he must betray her. When this is accomplished Blaic can then return to his Realm a free Prince. At first Blaic is not attracted to Felicia, although he is drawn to her. He tells her right from the beginning not to touch him. His People have a Law that if a Human touches one of them they must serve that mortal. This part of the story is great fun. She listens to him but as time passes in each others company their feelings for one another changes and grows. Soon they want to touch. Soon Blaic is demanding "command me." Felicia falls in love with Blaic. Slowly but surely Blaic seems to be falling in love with Felicia. Or is he?This lovely romance starts off slowly, then moves right along.Once it heats up, it never cools down.I very much enjoyed this romantic, sexy, fantasy faerie tale and recommended it.It's a keeper! Enjoy!Grade: B+P.S. Another good story about a human woman releasing a Prince magically turned to stone is calledThe Stone Prince~ 5 Stars.
