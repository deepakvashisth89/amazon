Love it!  This is a wonderful book which follows the lovable Katie as she investigates the National Gallery.  It is filled with Katie getting into mischief and introduces children to so many beautiful paintings which they can then see for themselves if they visit the museum.  James Mayhew’s pictures are stunning, as always, and children quickly become absorbed in them.  Not only is this a great book to read at home, it is also an excellent book to use in the classroom as there is so much scope for English work.  This is a wonderful book.Read moreRead less
