I grew up in Westchester which is only a few miles from Hollywood so we were always going there for movie premiere's and to cruise Sunset Blvd. and the mansion's all around that area.  I remember how Thomas Noguchi was tormented and called names by the media and I have wanted to read his books for years.  Now, thanks to my Kindle, I am able to find out his side of the story.  When Natalie Wood was killed I was working on a stage at 20th Century Fox that was next to the stage where they were shooting Hart to Hart.  I remember going in to work after she died and seeing LAPD all around the Hart to Hart stage because Robert Wagner had gotten so many death threats that he needed constant protection.  Everyone assumed that he had killed Natalie in a rage over her attachment to Christopher Walken whom she'd just made a movie with and who was staying with them on the night she drowned.  At last, Dr. Noguchi has cleared up the mystery of what really happened to her and it is nothing like what I read and believed for years.  He is a genius studying medicine and law at the same time and I have no doubt that the facts that he has released in this book and his other books are the truth.
