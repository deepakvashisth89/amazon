If you haven't read a Nathan Heller book then you are in for a treat.  The best way to describe this series is as a historical fiction detective story.  The author places his character Nathan Heller in unsolved crimes and mysteries from the past, interacting with famous and not so famous people of that time period who were involved in the case.This book finds Heller back in Hollywood and while riding with a reporter they both discover the body of the Black Dahlia.  Hired by a newspaper to help investigate and provide background for their articles covering the hideous crime.  Heller has another reason to investigate as he knew the Black Dahlia in Chicago and she called him just before her death at his hotel in Los Angeles.  While wanting to avenge her death Heller also wants to find the killer before he himself becomes a suspect.A great story that I highly recommend.Read moreRead less
