I had the honor of meeting Jack personally when I was in high school and hearing almost all of these stories first-hand. At that time, he was just publishing this book and I could not find it anywhere. Recently, I thought back on it and was happy to find it on Kindle. Of all of my high school years, Jack made the biggest impact on me. Re-reading these stories as an adult and remembering Jack was even more meaningful. And in today’s society I think it is even more important that we remember what can happen when we allow tyranny under the guise of protection.
