Actually Donna Williams felt different and distant from 'the world', and became withdrawn into 'her world', where she played 3 characters - Carol, Willie, and Donna herself. I suspect like most autistic/Asperger people she saw everything differently from other people, who often called her eccentric.After all she was trying to get into 'the world' as she dealt with various kinds of people including autistic ones.
