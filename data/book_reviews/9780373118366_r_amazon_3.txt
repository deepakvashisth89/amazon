18 year old Hope married much older rock star Jack Delacroix in haste & repented at leisure. After their child is stillborn, Hope is devastated & has a brief affair with her brother in law Guy. When she reconciles  briefly with Jack, she discovers she's pregnant again. She & Jack split for good & 14 years pass. Hope gets the news Jack has died in a car crash with his latest lover. She & Guy find themselves joint owners of the family estate & it's just a matter of time until Hope's best kept secret gets out. Enjoyable for an afternoon read.Read moreRead less
