I’ve read a lot of self help books over the years, and this one stands out big time. Learning to love yourself is a tremendous breakthrough, a missing piece of the puzzle in so many self improvement movements. This is a total game changer. Once we love ourselves, all the other modalities of self help fall into place. Before we learn to love ourselves, all the other modalities of self help feel empty, or that something is missing, and only work partially, never fully getting to the root. Self love is that profound inner shift that allows everything else to work. The book is easy to read, you learn to love love yourself by reading how the author did it for himself that we can model as an example. You’ll have to practice self love, and create a new momentum for it in your life, but when/if you do, the changes will flow, it will affect everything else in your life.I’ve seen some reviews on here that either blast the book for being “anti Christian” and still other reviews that blast it for being “too Christian.” I know, right? It is neither anti Christian nor pro Christian. It is about love and the message is compatible with the core of any religion, including Christ’s teachings. Some mention is given to some aspects of religion that fell short of love, and so, looking at history, nothing too surprising there. The point is to come to the realization of self love and to let that flow. If we had been living in self love all along in our lives, and as humans throughout history, we would not need this book.
