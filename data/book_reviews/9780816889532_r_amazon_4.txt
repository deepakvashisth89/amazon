Bob Stevens was a WW2 fighter pilot, a retired USAF Colonel and a talented cartoonist.  He created this book (a compilation of two earlier works) as a memorial to his friends and their experiences in the Air Force from WW2 through Viet Nam.  I bought my first copies of the original books for my Dad who was an Army Air Corps veteran, and most recently this one for another WW2 vet as a 'get well' gift.
