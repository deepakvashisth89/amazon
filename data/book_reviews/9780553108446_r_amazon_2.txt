Ferney by James Long is an exciting story about love, history, and destiny. It follows Mike and Gally, a couple who move to an old English village. There, Gally feels a strange connection to a man named Ferney, who seems to know everything about her past—even things she doesn’t remember. As secrets are revealed, Gally discovers she might have lived many lives before, and her connection to Ferney is deeper than she could have imagined.This book is full of mystery, twists, and emotional moments.
