An excellent book for true crime fanatics, others interested in notable criminal cases, or people who enjoy reading statistics and compounding encyclopedic knowledge about morbid things. "The Man Who Shorted out the Electric Chair" contains information about famous murders (some sections are classified by types of murders), short but informative blurbs about famous serial killers (with their respective body counts), and even a section for Pop-Crime (Dirty Harry Quotes).This book makes an interesting read for those interested (albeit superficially) in the topic of crime.Read moreRead less
