More Sten. Yay!Kilgore is still with him. Hooray!!And we get to finally hear the story of the "spotted snakes"!!!!This book is written from several viewpoints. Sten and Kilgore take up around 1/3 of it, but we get to see everything else that's going on around them, from space battles to evil plots in dark corners. Lots of fun and a problem putting it down when it's time to go to bed.Unlike previous Sten books, this one has a cliff-hanger at the end. One I never expected.
