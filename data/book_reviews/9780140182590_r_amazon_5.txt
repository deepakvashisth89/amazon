Why do I return to 'Riceyman Steps' every six years or so?  Who can resist it?  Arnold Bennett has created a fascinating world, one that we as readers are privileged to enter.  The basic story and characters grab you immediately; you NEED to know what will happen, you want desperately for it  all to turn out well.  Bennett understands how fragile we all are, and yet  how our passions can drive us.  His understanding of the human heart is as  perfect as 'Riceyman Steps.  Don't miss this one!Read moreRead less
