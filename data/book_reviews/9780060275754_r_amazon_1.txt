To call these re-tellings of some of the most beloved and best-known Western folktales "feminist" is to both belabor and miss the point.  The stories are retold from the perspective of each one's heroine, with surprising twists and breath-taking endings.  Each character rings true in her emotions and reactions, much more so than in traditional tales, and each grows and transforms as a result of the events she recounts.  One tale leads to another in this chain of transformations.  The prose is stark and luminous.  This is a jewel of a book.Read moreRead less
