This book is gut wrenching and thought provoking. It also contains more than a few historical inaccuricies  - poor fact checking or poor memory by the author,I do not know. Dr. Nyiszli's describes the conditions and atmosphere of the hell he lived. Every holocaust survivor is/was not a hero, Dr. Nyiszli was certainly not a hero, just one of the very few to survive one of the worlds most horrific crimes. There are times, I sense, when he wants to remove himself from the scene and present his facts as an observer, kind of like a fly on the wall, but he fails at this, as most humans would. Possibly survivors guilt is at play here. How does one justify to themself surviving a place that was created to kill millions of human beings and turn them into smoke and ashes? A place where everything that ever proved your existence is vaporized. Not only your physical being is murdered, but also the memory of you, papers, photographs, anything that could prove there once was a you... also, burned...A literal hell on earthThis book is an important contribution to the first hand, eye witness testimonies by Holocaust  survivors. For all its flaws, it offers a rare and unique perspective into one rarely seen area of the death machine factory. It should be required reading in all Holocaust study programs around the world, but especially in Germany and Central and Eastern Europe where these atrocities were planned and executed, to the disgrace of all humanity...it is important to remember though, that it is just one story out of many millions told from his sole account, memories, and experiences. Each survivor, perpetrator, victim, and bystander can only tell of their personal experience  told from their own perspective, however flawed. Let this book be a warning, to bystanders, potential victims, and perpetrators. Unfortunately, the world has not had enough of mass killings... Will we humans ever learn? Sadly, tragically, I doubt it... History does in fact, repeat itself, despite knowledge of the past. History proves time and time again, that the vast majority of perpetrators of crimes against humanity get away with it, this has always been and as far as I can see, always will be the way of the world...P.S. I wish the forward to this book would have been written by someone other than Bruno Bettelheim, someone with historical credibility.
