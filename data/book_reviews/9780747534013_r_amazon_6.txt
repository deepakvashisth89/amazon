The Underpainter is a beautifully written book; almost poetic. The story, while easy to read, is not easy to follow; it comprises the memories of an aging artist as he reflects on his life, his relationships, and what might have been. The reader travels back and forth in time and location and is asked to assemble personalities from vignettes. The painter's works and techniques mirror his emotions; it takes some time to figure it all out. Reading this book is like peeling away layers to find a core; the layers don't always make sense until you see the whole, but the process is seductive.
