I talked my book club into reading and discussing this book, and surprise, it was not popular.  This is not a typical feel-good fantasy.  As others have mentioned it is quite dark. There are strong elements of tragedy, and it asks several characters to examine what kind of person he or she is.  None the less, it is a powerful if uncomfortable narrative. I believe that once read, it is never forgotten.Three of OSC's books stand out head and shoulders above the others for me -- Ender's Game, Songmaster, and this one, Hart's Hope.
