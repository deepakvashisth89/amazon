After reading Mrs. Bush's first memoir, her husband's second book, and finally this book, I did have a profound respect for her dignity, love for her family, and stamina.  Reading between the lines, I did see an elitisism about which she is oblivious.  It could be argued it was well earned elitisism.  Also, although I respect her refusal to say anything hurtful or scandalous, some of the stories she sugar coated made me think she was either too involved to be objective or just plain trying to manipulate public opinion.  The "perfect family" picture is warming to read, but if true, something few can relate to I think.  Overall, I enjoyed her books (much more so the first one) but her effort to change opinions on gray issues did not change my mind one bit.
