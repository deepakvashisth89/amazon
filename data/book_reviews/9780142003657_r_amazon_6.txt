Mrs Delahunty is one of the main character in this novel, although it is probably not her real name. One day when she takes her weekly train in order to shop in one of the main cities near her house in Umbria, there is a terrorist attack at the train, with terrible results.She survives and is hospitalized, together with a little girl, an old ex-army General, and a young German.Her faithful manservant Quinty, is coping with all these patients in her large shabby and charming house; alongside two other servants.The tale unfolds as Aimées' uncle, Mr. Riversmith arrives, and things are as things unfolds, getting both funny and a bit sad. Mrs. Delahunty who is a writer of romantic fiction, misunderstands and totally confuses his attention towards her, which is strictly dry and to the point. Indeed he is a University Professor specializing in ants, and have no eye for romance at all. Although she is a warmhearted person, he makes her out to be some vulgar being persecuting him. The contrast between lively warmheartedness and cool intellectual attitudes, constitute much of the theme of this novel. Alongside this conflicts, there are lovely sceneries from Siena and Umbria.The novel is worth reading; it is something special and original, but I kept feeling something was missing in order to make it perfect.It is a book which I would recommend on a day at the beach, or at the end of the day when one needs to relax.
