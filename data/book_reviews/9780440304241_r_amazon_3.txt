Been reading a lot of biographies, and I knew little of MacArthur.  Now I know a lot more. The positives of this are the book itself is not overly long. It covers his family and himself, giving a great picture. It gives details of his "battlefield" activities, a bit briefly. It delves deep into his character.  Most notably, it gives his interactions with the Presidents up to Johnson, especially Truman and Roosevelt. Most interestingly for me is his discussions in his later years with JFK.  The negatives was that it repeated detailed information on his character flaws over and over again, with a lot of repeating. The book is great in the respect of being fair, balanced, and showing the reader the overall character of the man however.  This book leaves me to look for other volumes on his life that are a bit more detailed. If you are reading biographies that mean something to our history, this is the one!
