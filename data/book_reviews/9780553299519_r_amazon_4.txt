What can I say another terrific book from Robert Crais.  I have the advantage in that I just started reading his books & I don't have to wait for the next one to be released. Each book you learn a little bit more about Elvis & Joe & their unique friendship. There bond runs deeper than blood. It is a bond of brotherhood & complete trust in each other.  Each book has a unique plot so you don't get the same story just told a different way. If you haven't read any of Robert Crais' books start at the beginning. With each book his story telling gets better & better. Each book is riveting and worth reading at least once. Thank you Amazon for listing it as a book I might like, I did & I do.
