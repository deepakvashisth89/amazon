As someone well into their senior years and who has been a voracious reader for more than 7 decades I am always seeking books on my favorite topics that I have not read before.  This is why when I saw this absolutely fantastic 337 page hardcover book while browsing on Amazon (Never fight fair: Navy SEAL’s stories of combat and adventure by Orr Kelly) I decided to purchase it.Over the years I have read just about every book I could find about the U.S. NAVY SEALs, from their history, training, fitness requirements, assignments and anything else. I found this book to be one of the best books I have ever read on the UDT/SEALs history and development through the years.If you are seeking an important and well-researched book on the history of the UDT/SEALs and their numerous dangerous assignments this is a must book to read.
