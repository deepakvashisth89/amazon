Back in 1996 I happened to live a block away from my local public library & with a voracious appetite for literature, I was busily reading all of the novels they had by J.G. Ballard & John Barth. Alas one day as I ventured to that section of the library there were no more but as I looked to the shelf on my right in the same row - there was James Boylan's Planets. For some reason it jumped out at me and I took in home and read it. At the time I also read a lot of Vonnegut, Portis, Heller, Pynchon & Tom Robbins - Boylans novel grabbed me as much as any of these others had. It even contains what I consider one of the funniest scenes in a novel I have ever read involving a dead cat. I live in a new area now and went to the library the other day to see if Ballard had anything I had not read on the shelf - nope & then at a whim I thought I might reread Planets - nope. Then I thought maybe I should buy it - nope (since it is out of print Amazon is currently listing prices over $100).
