I'm not sure why this is a "Medical Thriller". The protagonist has a PhD in Egyptology and a job with a museum. Her boyfriend is a medical doctor, but he only appears briefly. Mostly to fail utterly to take her seriously.This is, however, a very nice Robin Cook thriller: young woman, finally visiting Egypt, witnesses a murder and gets drawn deeper and deeper into the illegal antiquities trade. It has a large number of bad guys, a well-told story, adventure, mystery, and discoveries.Read moreRead less
