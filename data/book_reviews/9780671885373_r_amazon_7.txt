I had a good time reading this novel. This was the first "Old West" novel I have read and I was very pleased. I have to admit, before reading this book I was fascinated with Wyatt Earp and Doc Holiday. I also plan on reading more about them. This book does the history justice by confirming some myths and debunking others and throughout is very fair and objective.
