This is the story of the 1st. Battalion, 5th Marines as they cross the border into Iraq in 2003.  These are battle hardened (or soon to be), rough, tough men.  It is also the story of their Chaplain, fellow warrior and friend, Lt. Carey Cash.  It is a story of war with all the terrible consequences that go with it.  Above all, it is the story of deliverance from the hands of the enemy by a loving, faithful and true God.  It is a story of miracles during mayhem, love defeating hate as well as God pursuing man and man's response.Just as in Biblical times, God is with the faithful on the battlefield as well as in His temples.If you have ever asked, "Where is God during tough times?", this is the book for you.  If you are dealing with doubts and fears, this is the book for you.  If you think God isn't listening, Yep this is the book for you.Your faith will be renewed as you read of a revival in the war torn deserts of Iraq.  Joshua, David and other 'Warriors' for the faith experienced the power of God many, many years ago.  That same God still works today.  He still performs battlefield miracles. He still calls men unto Himself.This important work by Chaplain Lt. Carey Cash was written from his heart; from his experiences in the line of fire.  Here is a warrior, a Christian and a friend like few ever know.  As he shepherds his men he also leads them to the 'shepherd' of all men.This book is a tremendous testimony of 'God in the trenches.'
