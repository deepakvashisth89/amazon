Whether you are a meditator or interested in Buddhism or an seasoned practitioner, this book gives a fresh and relevant view on how to apply certain Buddhist tenants (mainly that of following the path of a Bodhisattva) to the average persons life.  This is NOT a self help book. It is a book that will turn your views,of yourself and the world around you, on its head and teach you to at least find the humor in it all.  I have read books by other learned teachers in different Buddhist "sects" and none have made the Buddha's words so clear and applicable as Trungpa Rinpoche's writings.  If you are interested in learning about Shambhala from the source, read this book.  It is a good place to start (and return to over and over again.) Happy Reading!Read moreRead less
