Matthew Scudder comes to the rescue of a widow and finds more than he bargained for. Lisa and Greg Holtzmann seem to have it all, but when Glenn is gunned down in the streets not far from Matt's hotel, dark secrets come to light. All my favorite characters are in attendance. and Matt still isn't drinking, though he still has flaws. For me this series is one of the most enduring in crime fiction in large part because of the growth of the character.
