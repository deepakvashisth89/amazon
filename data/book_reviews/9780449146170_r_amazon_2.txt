I'll be honest, that Dolly keeps me in stitches!  Thought there might be a bit more about Grandma in this one, a little backstory.  We know she's a widow, her name is Florence, she lives at The Garden Apartments, but what keeps her going when she's not fawning over her adorable grandchildren?  Bingo?  Bridge parties?  Would a little attention from an attractive old widower be out of line?  These are the issues I was hoping this book would address but I am left with more questions than answers.
