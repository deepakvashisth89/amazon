For approximately two-thirds of the world's population... the existence of a human soul which survives physical death is as natural as babies being born. The devil is in the details of why and how a majority of people in the world understand this basic mystery of life. This book represents one of the best attempts I have ever read to explore this matter. It is done by a highly intellectual and open-minded author in a way which is credible, fascinating and extremely reasonable.
