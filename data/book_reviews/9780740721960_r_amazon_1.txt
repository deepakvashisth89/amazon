Tonra has pared down the comic strip to its barest essence.  Fans of Cathy, Marmaduke, Garfield or Dilbert won't be pinning James to their cube walls - not much topical humor here.  But if you've willingly read Scott McCloud, if you've paused to admire the nuances in Michelangelo's sketches, if you're open to the Muses at all, you're likely to get blindsided by the emotions found here.  The experiences that may come closest are 1) absorbing one of Bill Watterson's wordless Calvin & Hobbes strips on a snowy afternoon, or 2) hearing an old friend who's stopped surprising you say something uncharacteristically profound.Don't worry - you'll laugh.
