I say "Story Telling" because that is how this book seemed to read at times.  I mean, I enjoyed it thoroughly and even nearly shed some tears upon reading of Tsarina Elizabeth's end.  I give it 4 stars because there was some errors in the book of an editorial nature and dates and family lines seemed to be confused or incorrect but not so bad as to take away from the educational value of the book.  Also, the author tends to "story tell" as if he was personally there or had the ability to read the tsarinas minds and does so without much evidence or footnotes.  There was a certain flair to the narrative that bordered exaggeration and flirted with opinion rather than fact.  However, the book does a good job shedding some light on a peculiar era of Russian history in which her rulers were all colorful woman characters who all came to power through bloody coups.
