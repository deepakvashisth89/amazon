The basic theme of this book is the beginning of the direct involvement of humans in a series of space battles against a crowd of evil folks. The writing is stilted, the action is continuous, the space battles are incredible, and the aliens are truly "out of this world"! It's one of the great (and classic) space operas of the golden age of SF.---That's the good part. The bad part is that publisher of this eBook should be strung up & shot for criminal non-proofreading! There are an average of 1+ mis-spellings, weird punctuations, & etc/page.
