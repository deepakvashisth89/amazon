I can’t believe how long it’s been, how much older I am now than when I first read this book, and its predecessor.  Both of them still resonate.  In fact, both are still RELEVANT. Perhaps even more than when Frank wrote them.An incredibly worthy sequel, the call-backs and return characters don’t feel like they’re shoehorned into the story at all.  It’s definitely packed full of arcs, but never feels busy.  Pacing and structure are, like the first book, cinematic.  If only we could actually get movies of these two masterpieces.  The definitive series on spiritual warfare, I cannot recommend these enough.  Be open, be ready, and be moved.  Reading these WILL change your perspective on things.
