This is a perfect beach book.The story isn't terribly complex, so you don't have to pay too much attention to the details, but it moves right along, enough so that you won't be bored. The characters start off pretty bland, and there isn't a whole lot of development, but the events that take place don't require a huge insight into their motivations and thus it's not a problem.The writing is quite good, but fairly simple. In fact, my major complaint with the story is that it seems written for a young adult audience, say, high-school. That made it just a little too simplistic for me to give it four stars.So while it won't keep you on the edge of your seat, it will keep you amused enough to lounge in the sand.Read moreRead less
