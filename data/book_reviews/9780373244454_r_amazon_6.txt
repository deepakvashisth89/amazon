Well so far, 3 books in and each one is just better than the last.  I LOVE Ms. Reavis' heroes.  OMG what guys they are.  This one was so different.  The hero is a young paratrooper, injured when his helicopter crashes.  he and the pilot are the only survivors and both are badly injured.  The heroine is the nurse on his ward.  She has the tough job of helping these guys heal.  Tender loving care but rough as well.  She helps him get an apartment off base where he can heal on his own.  She just happens to live next door.She is older than he is so she keeps it strictly business.  She helps him without taking over or keeping him from doing for himself. He is slowly falling for her but she has nothing but friendship on her mind and helping him in his recovery.  His landlady plays a big part in this story and we have all known someone just like her.  A loving soul that wants only the best for everyone.  She is a very wise woman.  Her church friends add to the mix.  All a lot of fun.  There is even a cat that adds a lot to the story.I loved this hero.  He is a young enlisted man, so he doesn't have a lot of money or really anything else.  she is a nurse, making her own way in the world.  She owns the house next door and works at the military hospital.  This story is all about the two of them with everyone else just playing minor roles.  No bad guy, etc. just their love story.  It was great.  I loved it and highly recommend it.  He is a very different hero and one you will definitely fall for.  Great read.  I loved it.Read moreRead less
