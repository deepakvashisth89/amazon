The clues Pike plants early on in this YA thriller point to the resolution, but the backstory is so odd that it's hard to guess its details beforehand. In other words - perfectly constructed.SPELLBOUND takes its time, with twists and a couple of red herrings. While some readers seem to find the shamanism plot too far-fetched, there's something creepily plausible about the way Pike describes it. If such things were real, they might unfold in a scenario like this one.Though they might not be Pike's most compelling, the characters do become more likeable as the story goes on. (WHISPER OF DEATH, THE MIDNIGHT CLUB, REMEMBER ME, THE LAST VAMPIRE and MASTER OF MURDER are probably his best character studies.
