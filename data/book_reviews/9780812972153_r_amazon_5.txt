The book is a compelling documentary of creators who rejected convention and chose to carve their own path. They refused to follow orthodox methods, instead inventing their own way of working. Along the journey, they attracted supporters who believed in their vision and helped bring their projects to life. Success followed, and with that success came momentum—drawing in more people and amplifying their influence.However, as they continued to grow, they began to crumble under the weight of their own success. The very gravity that once pulled others toward them started to work against them. They attempted to return to their roots, but by then it was too late; the toll of their success had already been taken. In creating something entirely new, they themselves were changed—they were no longer the same people.
