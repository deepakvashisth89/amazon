I actually enjoyed the story very much as the main characters were well drawn out and likeable, especially Audrey. The author gives wonderful and accurate insight into the diamond trade and diamonds themselves. The story gets a little boring with too much detail at times and I skimmed over several parts. A bit of editing would go a long way. I will read another book by this author as I am particularly interested in gems and he really knows his stuff. Well researched medically and a bit long winded there as well. Not a 5 star and not a 3. Enjoy!Read moreRead less
