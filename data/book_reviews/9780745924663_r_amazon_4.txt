This is the first book in the "Song of Albion" trilogy--and it does not disappoint. While some authors use reams of narrative to set up a story, such is not true with Lawhead. You are plunged almost immediately in a parallel Celtic world that is undergoing a VAST upheaval which has ramifications concerning our own time period. The tale is wondrous in it's FEEL, both physically and spiritually. The elegant writing style transcends even Tolkien & Lewis, and is truly effortless to enjoy!Read moreRead less
