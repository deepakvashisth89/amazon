The Dark Destiny series of short story collections that are usually connected to White Wolf's (now Obsidian Path's) Classic World of Darkness. Though Whereas Dark Destiny: Children of Dracula focused on vampires, this one is more balanced with a number of stories focusing on the other denizens of the World of Darkness; Wraiths, Mages, and Werewolves.A few of the stories pay only lip-service to the conventions of the World of Darkness games and genre, others are spot on without devolving into game-speak (which I know is a difficult balance to maintain in any story centering around a role playing game!). The overall theme is a view of history through the lens of the supernatural beings of the world, my personal favorite was the final story, Punchline...which I won't give much away about since the title of the story does revolve around a twist.Read moreRead less
