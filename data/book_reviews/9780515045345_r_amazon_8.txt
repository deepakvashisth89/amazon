Roderick visits Ngaio’s homeland. So well written, as are all Marsh’s books, and beautifully evocative of the magical beauty of New Zealand’s South Island. Marsh’s evident love of the theater is here, as are her complex, yet accessible characters. The cultural milieu is real and at times unsavory or even nasty.
