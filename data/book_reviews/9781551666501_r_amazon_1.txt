As a actress Ms Leigh was very good - as an author, not so much.  Sacharrine story of a young girl who dreams of becoming an actress, and writes an sappy diary of her hopes and dreams.  She becomes successful and her diary full of secrets, but when she's ill many fear that they'll be revealed if she dies.I don't know how it ends, as I couldn't get past page 70 - which was a real struggle just to read that much.Possibly a semi- autobiographical story, so if you are a Huge fan it might hold some interest for you.
