My brother Vinny is a pig lover...Totally obsessed with the creatures. I have purchased several books from Amazon on the subject. This title is the least desirable of the bunch. The pictures are nice but many of the captions fail to identify the breed ofpig. The wild boar section Is the best part of this book. Wild boar pictures and info regarding them is pretty rare in most pig books so I add a star for that section.Overall , its a good book to add to your pig book collection.
