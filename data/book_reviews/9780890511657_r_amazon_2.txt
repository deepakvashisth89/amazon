This book covers Dinosaurs from a literal biblical view point. Some sections in this book include How fossils are formed, digging them up and restoration. It gives enough information to explain the process without going into too much detail. We are half way through the book and my children are fascinated by it. We use it as a small part of our Dino curriculum. The author speaks of what he believes is two specific passages of dinosaurs in the bible. Different groups are covered in the book, such as light weights, horned, plated, armored, duck billed, & boneheaded (lol), carnivores and herbivores. That flying and marine reptiles are not dinosaurs is explained. The author refutes macro evolution, pointing out the ridiculous leaps ones has to make in order to believe such a view (which explains many of the bad reviews) and points out that no 'transitional' dinos have been found. He does not believe they are the ancestors of birds, which is a popular present day secular belief. He speaks of the effects of sin and the great flood, and how, because of this, the even climate changed, which he believes lead to their eventual demise. If you believe in the literal seven day creation and the world wide flood, you will probably like this book. If you believe in macro evolution, you will not like this book.
