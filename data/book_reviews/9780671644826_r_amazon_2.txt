"Deviant" is probably the best book we're likely to get on Ed Gein, the infamous murderer, grave-robber, and necrophiliac of rural Wisconsin.  Harold Schechter provides a smooth, chronological telling, beginning with Gein's paternal grandparents and continuing down through his death from natural causes in 1987.  The problem is that, as horrific as Gein's crimes evidently were, there is still relatively little known about what he actually did.  Schechter does the best he can with the available information -- including a plausible day-in-the-life recreation of Gein's bizarre existence between his mother's death in 1945 and his arrest in 1957.  But ultimately numerous questions are left unanswered.  (I also wish Schechter had been more specific about his sources; all we get is a page or two of acknowledgments.)The Kindle edition is basically OK.  The photos from the print edition are included at the end.
