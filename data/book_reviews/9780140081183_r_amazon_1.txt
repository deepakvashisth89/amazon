This lost gem of a novel was brought to my attention through an interview with John Le Carre who was mentored by John Bingham in Le Carre's secret world and also in his publishing endeavors. If you want a very poor comparison with this  book and one of Le Carre's works then it would be with "A Murder of Quality." An imperfect one because Mr. Bingham's book is about a murder investigation, false accusations, and police and civilian psychologies on one hand and on the other hand a study of childhood events in the English world of private education, and its traumatic effects on the adults' life. It really is very British and for those who are fans of 20th Century English literature, this is a must that will never disappoint.This is suspense and thrills written with the velocity of a speeding train (I can almost hear the screams of a locomotive as it rounds a curve at too great a speed) and written with great craftsmanship.
