Sendmail has it's reputation already. One may also consider qmail and postfix. This book presents nearly every detail of sendmail on over 1000 pages. One of the authors is even the creator of sendmail. The book helped  me to understand the sendmail configuration file, especially the  complicated regular expression substitution patterns, which are very arcane  but powerful. For UNIX admins it's a must have, because there is no other  way to get the infos to handle sendmail mail servers. But it takes quite  some time to get aquainted with sendmail and this book does not make it any  faster. It's a complex topic and not really fun, but effective.Read moreRead less
