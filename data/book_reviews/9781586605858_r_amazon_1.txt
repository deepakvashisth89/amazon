Laurie Smelling is one of my favorite authors! And this was such a sweet, uplifting story. Sometimes you need to be lifted up enough to see how much love truly did come down. The faith, lifestyle and character portrayed by our main character Mac was awesome and made you wish you lived in his town. Add in a sad little boy and a mischievous and gentle horse and you have the beginning of a great story. Don't let the lack of a photo jacket on the cover dissuade you; this book is well worth reading.Read moreRead less
