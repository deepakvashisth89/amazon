As a PhD student in Classical Studies that bought this book without thinking too much (I just wanted the Latin text), I must say that this English translation is awful. The English is hard to understand and it often introduces a lot more of what you can read in the text (which limits the reader's "space" to interpret the text). Bad bad bad.Example 1: in page 68-69 he actually introduces the name of Cleopatra in the poem, even though Horace never does (he speaks about a queen that died due to a serpent, but he is not as vulgar as the translator, and never displays the name of the Egyptian queen). You can't just add whatever you like to a translation just because you have a degree from Oxford!Example 2: in page 22-23 he translates carpe diem as "pluck this".
