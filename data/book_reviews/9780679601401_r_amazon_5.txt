These were the first two novels I have read from Raymond Chandler; I got something very different than I expected, but I still could not put the books down. The plots are very complicated and sometimes a bit strained, but it still doesn't matter -- what matters is the dark, gloomy atmosphere, the complex characters and the fluid narration. It is very depressing somehow yet still impressing how little the hero can do and how hard he has to fight for it. Philip Marlowe, the private detective is someone who tries to make a very hard living; it is always an open question whether he wins or loses in the end.Read moreRead less
