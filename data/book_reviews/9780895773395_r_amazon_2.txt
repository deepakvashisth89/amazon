I went with Twain to places my feet have landed and a few they still seek to walk. His account of those places I have been gave me a wildly different perspective of place through his eyes and previous times. Some were unrecognizable to me while the verisimilitude of others transported me back to my own vivid experiences. Is it a page turner? No! but the telling and wit of Twain does elevate the work as a book that educates, elucidates, and enmeshes one into a small slice of time and our world. Who will forget his ramblings of Lake Tahoe and the Sea of Galilee? Or his musings about the Sphinx? It took me nearly three years to come to its end which is a testament to the book itself. Some days I would read ravenously, then set it down at the completion of an excursion.
