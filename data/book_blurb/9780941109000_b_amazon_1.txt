The Michael Teaching is a spiritual philosophy that describes the nature of reality, the purpose of life and the evolution of consciousness. It originated from a spiritual entity called Michael, who says they are an advanced group of souls that have completed their incarnational cycle on earth and now work as guides for those still on their journey. The Michael Teaching describes a wide range of concepts and tools for personal growth and spiritual development, including the seven soul ages, the seven roles, and the personality overleaves.
