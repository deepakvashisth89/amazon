Horses had always been a part of Natalie's life, but her father, successful trainer Grantham Slater, was strangely reluctant to let her get involved although, during his illness, she had proved she could run his stables single-handed. So it was hardly surprising that Natalie loathed the new partner at Wintersgarth before she even set eyes on him. Assured, confident and successful, Eliot Lang didn't need to win her to his side.
