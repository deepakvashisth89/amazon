This first mystery from one of America's best-loved writers introduces one of the greatest fictional detectives of all time -- Nero Wolfe, an orchid-growing, gourmandizing eccentric genius, and his irrepressible assistant, Archie Goodwin. When Wolfe receives a fer-de-lance (a deadly snake), Archie knows he's getting very close to solving two murders. The symbiotic relationship of Wolfe and Goodwin is an entertaining facet of this mystery. Complete and unabridged. 6 cassettes.Read more
