Find your faith, my son. Find your faith.The Savior is a journey through time and soul. A pathway to the other side of pain and death. A story of the cyclical nature of existence, the karmic bonds between us. Far from preachy, it's a story of a man who's been on a first name basis with death and loss; and lived to tell the tale.Toby Riordan is a slacker. He joins the military because he thinks it's an easy way to discover the world. And finds out how wrong he is when he's sent to fight in the first Iraqi Conflict. Life in Iraq, with bombs going off around him, wearing dog flea collars on his wrists and ankles to fend off the sand fleas, and eating MREs that taste more like dog poop flavored sawdust than real food is not his idea of"see the world."The death of his best friend changes that.A cave in caused by an air to ground missile buries him in, killing his friend, Bion. What appears to be a hallucination leads him to an oasis, filled with spiritual beings and mysticism. One of them finds Toby intriguing and gives him a gift that changes his life. Now imbued with immortality, the ability to travel through time, and a depression that send him through the floor, Toby lives to go home. And decides to use those gifts.The last thing his mother tells him before the cancer claims her life is "Find your faith, my son." The decision is made. Welcome to Israel in the First Century, CE, Tobias Riordan.
