Sylvia West is doing her best to juggle the demands of career, marriage and kids. But the delicate balance is thrown out of whack when her husband John vanishes without a trace.When the police prove to be of no help, Sylvia takes matters into her own hands. She puts her law practice aside and launches an investigation that takes her from high-level think tanks and posh Long Island mansions to the dingiest Times Square street corner. She eventually discovers that John has a hidden past, and an association with The Foundation, a shadowy group of powerful and ruthless people who are determined to put their man in the White House.Armed with nothing more than her wits and her courage, Sylvia puts her life on the line to stop them.Read more
