In the Miami of the 1980s, the ultimate symbol of power and wealth was the muscle speed boat known as The Cigarette, and the king of the Cigarette Boat was Don Aronow, the multi-millionaire builder and designer of powerboats. Aronow was a man with an insatiable appetite for speed, women, and money. He had it all, until an unknown assailant gunned Aronow down near his boatyard.
