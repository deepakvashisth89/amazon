Nathaniel Hawthorne’s universal classic, forged from America’s Puritan heritage: a masterful exploration of humanity’s unending struggle with sin, guilt, and pride“[Nathaniel Hawthorne] recaptured, for his New England, the essence of Greek tragedy.”—Malcolm CowleyHailed by Henry James as “the finest piece of imaginative writing yet put forth in the country,” Nathaniel Hawthorne’sThe Scarlet Letterreaches to our nation’s historical and moral roots for the material of great tragedy.
