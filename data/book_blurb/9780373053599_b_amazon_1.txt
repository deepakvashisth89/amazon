FARMER'S DAUGHTER Something wasn't right on the Courteau Pig Farm. The FBI had evidence that pointed to Patricia Courteau being a Mafia hit woman--but one glimpse of Tricia had a lump bigger than all Iowa in G-man Paul Lansing's throat. Either this angel-faced woman was a darn good actress or she was as innocent as a newborn piglet. Tricia knew something was amiss with her temporary hired hand from the moment they met. Paul Lansing had city slicker written all over him, from his "come hither" smile to his fur-lined boots. Despite her reservations, Paul's roguish good looks had Tricia's heart thundering.
