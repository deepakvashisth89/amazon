Jana Raplansky’s late-blooming love is set against the demands of her demanding career as a painter and gallery curator and her recovery from childhood sexual abuse. Obsessed with her work in New York’s SoHo art scene, Jana meets Ed, a soft-hearted arts funder, and is ready to embark on a relationship after 34 years of celibacy.
