In this powerful cultural critique, Ariel Dorfman explores the political and social implications of the smiling faces that inhabit familiar books, comics, and magazines. He reveals the ideological messages conveyed in works of popular culture such as the Donald Duck comics, the Babar children’s books, andReader’s Digestmagazine.The Empire’s Old Clotheswas widely praised when it was first published in 1983.
