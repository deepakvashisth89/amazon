Born in the heart of Islam, Samuel studied the Koran and vowed to become a political leader who would change the world. But as he experienced increasing misery and emptiness, he needed answers to lingering questions. A Korean girl shared with Samuel her faith in Jesus, and at first he was intrigued. But he would not yield his life to Christ. He soon hardened his heart so much that he opposed Christianity openly.Then one night, he heard a voice call his name, and he had a dramatic encounter with Jesus Christ. Since that moment, he has been totally transformed-from persecutor of the church to soldier of the cross!If you want your faith to grow--if you have loved ones you want to see come to Christ--you will want to read Dr. Lee's remarkable story! About the author: Dr. Samuel Lee has a master's degree in third world studies and has completed a doctoral research study in church and biblical leadership from the University of Leiden.
