Yesterday he was a Nazi spy. concealed in the U.S. Coast Guard, he fed America's secrets to the enemy. After the war he destroyed all traces of his past, watching, waiting for the moment to strike. Today he is a Washington politico. Charming, urbane, he moves in the highest circles of wealth and power. Now he is ready for his final move - the move into the oval office. Tomorrow he could be President. Soon he will control the greatest nation in the world. Soon he will build a Fourth Reich and launch a reign of terror.
