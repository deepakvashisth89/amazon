An encounter between trophy wife Carolyn Blake and her new neighbor - tall athletic artist Val Hunter - leads to a close friendship and deepening emotions that young Carolyn has never known.Suddenly, as Carolyn questions what she has always accepted, her carefully manicured domestic life begins to unravel.
