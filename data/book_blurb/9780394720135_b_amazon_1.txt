Before Sam Spade, before Philip Marlowe, before Mike Hammer, before Lew Archer, and before nearly every hard-boiled detective in mystery fiction, there was the Continental Op. Who was he? No one knows, since Dashiell Hammett never gives his name. Through these 30 tales, we see the Op solve crimes, usually murder, while being shot at, assaulted, and dishing out punishment better than he receives it.
