"A warm, moving story of the power of love." NYT Bestselling Author Debbie MacomberIn her impetuous summer of love, Jenny St. Claire found Luke Sheridan—her dashing knight in intern's scrubs. But a fast-track future and rigid, ambitious parents drove passion out of Luke's life. And the summer ended, leaving Jenny with a broken heart...and a beautiful son, Danny, to help mend it.Years later it is tragedy that reunites them—the father whom a son longs to know, and the mother who has tried desperately to forget.
