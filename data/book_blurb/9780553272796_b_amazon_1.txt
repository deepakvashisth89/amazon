A complete self-help program for adult children of alcoholicsMore than 28 million Americans grew up in alcoholic families.  They bear a painful legacy of confusion, fear, anger and hurt—and they are at shockingly high risk of marrying an alcoholic or becoming alcoholics themselves.  In this authoritative book, Wayne Kritsberg shows how to recognize—and remedy—the long-term effects of the dysfunctional, alcoholic family.
