“The Boy Who Kicked Pigs” is the work of Tom Baker, who is best known for playing the fourth Doctor Who on BBC Television and for the voice of the popular comedy show, “Little Britain”. The book was first published in 1999 and has been translated into several different languages. Tom Baker is well known for his originality and ability to make people laugh and this book, which has been called a “grotesque masterpiece”, has achieved cult status.The anti-hero of Baker’s short novel is 13 year old Robert Caligari who has no heart. He causes havoc in his home and neighbourhood by his hatred of humanity and compulsion to kick pigs. Havoc becomes disaster when his pig-kicking causes a dramatic chain of events culminating in an accident of apocalyptic proportions. A gruesome story, but compelling and irresistibly comic in the pitch and speed of Baker’s telling. Hard to put down, and possible to read in one hugely entertaining sitting.The print book was superbly illustrated by the children’s book illustrator, David Roberts, who captures the spirit of Baker’s characters brilliantly. This Kindle edition contains many of those original illustrations.“The Boy Who Kicked Pigs” has been adapted for stage by Kill The Beast Theatre Company, which staged it at The Edinburgh Festival fringe in 2013.
