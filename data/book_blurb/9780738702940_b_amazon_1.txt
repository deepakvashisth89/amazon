Llewellyn's 2003 Wicca AlmanacA Hip New Guide for Pagan LivingUrban, edgy, internet-savvy and irreverent, this is definitely not your mother's Witchcraft almanac. Earth-centered meets underground in this zine-style guide to Pagan/Wiccan/Witchy living.This all-new almanac from Llewellyn is loaded with information that you need and want―from hot fashions to cool websites, film and book recommendations, Witchy news and profiles, the inside track on festivals and travel, and a Wiccan consumer guide.The calendar section gives you the information you need to fuel your magical work―the moon's phase and sign, plus planetary, color and incense correspondences for each day.
