The House of the Seven Gablesby Nathaniel Hawthorne is a haunting tale of mystery, revenge, and redemption. Set in a decaying mansion with a dark history, the story follows the Pyncheon family, cursed by a centuries-old injustice. As the weight of their past sins looms over them, the arrival of a new generation sparks a battle between fate and free will. Rich in Gothic atmosphere, vivid characters, and symbolic depth, Hawthorne weaves a narrative of guilt, secrets, and the possibility of salvation.
