Flying With Broken Wings: A Poetic Journey is an exhilarating book about growing up, healing the pains of the past, accepting the present and embracing the future. The poems take the reader on a dazzling roller coaster ride through universal experiences and emotions that shape all of our lives. Written with honesty and sass, this book will draw readers into the enticing life of a young girl who learns about herself through her mistakes, takes the misfortune surrounding her and turns it into determination to succeed and fly above all that is 'false'.Read more
