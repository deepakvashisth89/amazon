Stewardship: A Parish Handbookis the most comprehensive single guide ever published for parishes, diocesan leaders, and lay ministers involved in developing and implementing local-level stewardship programs. In these pages, leading stewardship specialist C. Justin Clements provides an "in-the-trenches" guide to the stewardship conversion process, based on more than 30 years of hands-on experience.With a broad focus on time, talent, and treasure, Clements covers every important stewardship topic, from the "The Stewardship Committee" and "Lay Witnesses" to "Money Myths and Misunderstandings" and "Dealing With Donors."Stewardship: A Parish Handbookalso includes an extensive list of stewardship references and resources, and an appendix of sample materials for parish use.
