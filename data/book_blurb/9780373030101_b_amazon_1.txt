Kaye Reardon and Graham Forest make a perfect couple – all they need is the perfect house so they can marry and live happily ever after. But there are no houses which suit both Kaye’s taste and Graham’s list of requirements.Real estate agent Brendan McKenna is no help at all – in fact, instead of finding Kaye the perfect house, he seems to think she should be looking for a different man.
