One of the gripping and fantastic reads of all times Frankenstein by Mary Shelly deserves a place in the bookshelf of every book reader. Despite being a work of fiction that the literal level, it digs deeper into the human consciousness and how the feeling of guilt takes one through a series of complexities in life. The story of a man called Victor who, just to try out what he has learnt, creates a monster, that changes his life and those of the people around him forever.
