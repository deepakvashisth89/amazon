Detailing the travels of L. Frank Baum, author of the Wizard of Oz, and his wife Maud's travels around the Mediterranean during the first part of 1906, and first published in 1909, this travelogue has long since been out of print and a long sought-after piece of Baumiana. This new edition features brand-new cover art, updated text, a new layout, and a bonus map with major landmarks and an itinerary. This is a fascinating read, and gives the readers a new slant on the private lives of the loving couple that brought us America's most famous and favorite fairy tale.Read more
