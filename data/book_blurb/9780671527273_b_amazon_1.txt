Jessie Braddock was 'Hell on Horseback' as a child, riding free and wild across the Triple X ranch, her home. Then her parents sent her, kicking and screaming, to Charleston to learn to be a lady. Four years later she has learned to be a lady but her love of her home in Texas has never died, something she realizes too late when she discovers her parents have sold the Triple X! ...and to their ranch foreman, James Bonner.She remembers 'Jamie', she met him when, at twelve years of age, she ran away to join her older sister's cattle drive. He was cute then...but she was just a child. She's no longer a child and she's learned a lot in Charleston. Perhaps she can convince him to sell her back the Triple X. James Bonner has worked hard and poured everything he has into the Triple X. When he's warned by Jessie's parents that she's headed his way to get back the Triple X, he's annoyed to have to deal with the inconvenience of telling her to get back on the train and go home. He's not selling! But he's also not prepared for the startlingly beautiful woman Jessie has grown into.
