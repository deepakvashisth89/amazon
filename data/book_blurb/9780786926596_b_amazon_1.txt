TheUrban Arcana Campaign Settingis the first setting to be released for players of the d20 Modern Roleplaying Game. It greatly expands on material available to d20 Modern players and includes new races, advanced classes, occupations, feats, creatures, and rules for organizations and secret societies. It contains both short adventures and adventure hooks and presents a fully detailed city for adventuring, including maps.
