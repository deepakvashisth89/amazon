The Stray Lamb is a poignant coming-of-age novel that follows the journey of Peter, a young boy navigating the complexities of family, friendship, and self-discovery in a rural English village. Set against the backdrop of post-World War II England, Peter grapples with the challenges of adolescence as he strives to find his place in the world. When a stray lamb wanders into his life, Peter forms an unexpected bond with the gentle creature, finding solace and companionship amidst the turmoil of his surroundings.
