Gifts of the Nile recounts the spectacular journey of a young woman in search of true love. Rosa is a successful career woman but resultant material trappings are no substitute for a meaningful relationship.A chance meeting with a spiritual counselor transforms Rosas outlook on life and takes her on an unforgettable journey of spiritual and emotional discovery. Her quest for inner truth and love takes her to exotic places in Egypt where she meets Sean.Through love and friendship, she gains insights and wisdom into the true meaning of life and together, they come to a startling discovery.
