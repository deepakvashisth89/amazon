More Americans visit Britain than any other place in Europe. Yet often they are overwhelmed by the encounter. There is so much history in Britain, there are so many layers, that one gets confused. Here at last is a guide to England and Wales that sorts it all out. Use this guide to understand the place where you happen to find yourself. Or use it to plan an extraordinarily interesting kind of trip - tour Britain according to a historical theme. Sites are located with mileages, road numbers, and map coordinates. And there is a Best of Britain listing of the choices places - castles, abbeys, cathedrals, special museums, gardens, and much more. This unique, authoritative guide was ten years in the making.
