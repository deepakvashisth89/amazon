Regular expressions are a powerful tool for manipulating text and data. This book, with its unprecedented detail and breadth of coverage, will help you discover a whole new world of mastery over your computer and its data. Regular expressions are not a tool in and of themselves, but are included as part of a larger utility. The classic example isgrep. Regular expressions can be found everywhere, such as in scripting languages (including Perl, Tcl,awk, and Python); editors (including Emacs,vi, and Nisus Writer); programming environments (including Delphi and Visual C++); and specialized tools (includinglex, Expect, andsed). In this eye-opening book, author Jeffrey Friedl leads you through the steps of crafting a regular expression that gets the job done.
