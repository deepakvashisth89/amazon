The Night Life of the Gods by Thorne Smith is a whimsical and humorous fantasy novel that takes readers on a delightful romp through the world of Greek mythology, infused with a generous dose of 20th-century wit and charm. The story follows the misadventures of Hunter Hawk, a modern-day inventor with a penchant for tinkering and a thirst for adventure.
