Prophesied to be the catalyst in a modern-day Armageddon, Kelsey Waite flees a life in Utah, freeing herself from a polygamous lifestyle and abusive father. Ten years later, her new life in California leaves her with an illusion of safety, one that rapidly unravels when she returns home from work to find her neighbor murdered and her daughter missing.Realizing the two are linked, she is forced to return to Utah and face her demons--and the charismatic cult leader who believes she is destined to be his second wife.Will her sheer strength of will and determination be enough to save both herself, and her daughter, from the cult?Read more
