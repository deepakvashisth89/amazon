"Ozoplaning with the Wizard of Oz" by Ruth Plumly Thompson is a novel published in 1939. When the Wizard's experimental aircraft accidentally launch, carrying his dinner guests to unexplored sky-countries, adventure erupts. The Tin Woodman's diplomatic blunder sparks an invasion plot against Oz, while the Wizard and Dorothy must navigate treacherous aerial realms, confronting bizarre creatures and villainous rulers.
