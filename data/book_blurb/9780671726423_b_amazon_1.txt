Devastated when her mother abandoned her for a rich wealthy man, Ricky Blair vows revenge on the rich. Now, beautiful, smart and oh-so determined ,Ricky Blair is a top executive at a major insurance company by day. By night Ricky is the brains behind a ring of jewel thieves. It's a sweet gig for Ricky and two larcenous sisters —Baby and Gwen— until a handsome irreverent investigator appears on the scene. Cool and calculating, Granger Russell draws Ricky into a delicious game of cat and mouse that makes her lust after more than money. Through a labyrinth of desire and danger the three women find themselves within reach of life's greatest treasure of all — love. The only question is, who will live to claim it for their own.Read more
