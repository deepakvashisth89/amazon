A lonely presidential candidate falls into an affair with the wife of the stupidest man in the Senate. In order to continue the affair, he chooses the senator as his running mate. Hilarity ensues. “At its best, the cautionary tale is so blindingly funny that it's impossible to laugh and still keep one's eyes on the page; at its worst, it's rompingly absurd. Malanowski wickedly reduces statecraft to constituent elements of pomposity, puerility and payback, while he keeps the scenes in the politicians' inner sanctums always vaguely familiar, equal parts National Public Radio and Saturday Night Live . This gifted satirist's delightful election-year broadside is as accurate as it is preposterous.” -- Publishers WeeklyRead more
