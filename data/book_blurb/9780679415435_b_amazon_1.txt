He was the handsome golden boy out of USC, the star running back for the New York Giants, and—for twenty-six years—the stalwart voice of ABC’sMonday Night Football. But for Frank Gifford, the road to success did not come easy, on or off the field. In a classic memoir of a true American gridiron hero, Gifford tells his inspirational story, providing an insider’s look at the NFL replete with shocking and humorous anecdotes.Read more
