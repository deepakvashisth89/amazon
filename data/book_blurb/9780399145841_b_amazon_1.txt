WhenPresidential Dealhit the bestseller lists, fans of Les Standiford’s other books, includingDone DealandRaw Deal, rejoiced. They already knew what new listeners would find: taut suspense, tough characters, and a superb sense of mood. Now inBlack Mountain, Standiford combines high-country adventure with a tale of ruthless revenge.Black Mountain looms on the horizon east of Yellowstone. When New York’s governor goes there on vacation, policeman Richard Corrigan is an unofficial part of the security team. The first “accident” occurs when the seaplane explodes after dropping them at the campsite. The second is clearly murder. One by one, members of the camping trip are targeted by unseen, silent killers.As Corrigan desperately tries to anticipate the next attack, the danger and tension mount with each passing hour.
