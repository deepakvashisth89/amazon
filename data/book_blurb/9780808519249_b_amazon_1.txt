Bram Stoker’s Dracula is the legendary gothic masterpiece that gave the world its most enduring image of the vampire—a tale of terror, desire, and the struggle between good and evil.Told through letters, diary entries, and newspaper clippings, the novel follows young solicitor Jonathan Harker as he travels to Transylvania to assist the mysterious Count Dracula with a property purchase. What begins as a business trip soon descends into nightmare, as Harker discovers the Count’s dark secret and a group of determined allies must confront the ancient evil that threatens to engulf them all.Rich in atmosphere and psychological depth, Dracula is far more than a horror story—it is a haunting exploration of fear, temptation, and the boundaries of human courage.
