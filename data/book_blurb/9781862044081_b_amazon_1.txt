Ten Thousand Dreams Interpreted is a comprehensive guide to understanding the meanings behind the symbols and events that appear in our dreams. Written by Gustavus Hindman Miller, this book explores the depths of the human subconscious and offers insight into the hidden messages that our dreams can reveal. With over 10,000 entries, Miller provides interpretations for a vast array of dream scenarios, from common themes like falling, flying, and being chased, to more obscure symbols like animals, colors, and numbers. Each entry is accompanied by a detailed explanation of the symbolism involved, as well as practical advice for how to apply the knowledge gained from the dream.
