Our top-secret weapon is missing. And America's top test pilot is missing too. Full of a fierce, obsessive will to live, Tom Darby is about to test-fire the USA's ultimate weapon—when something goes tragically wrong. His F-15 fighter explodes in a ball of fire. He ejects into the killing heat of Death Valley. Crazed by injuries, he becomes a wild beast running for his life from unseen enemies—and from his rescuers. Even from his beautiful wife.
