New York Times–Bestselling Author—A woman in a frontier lumber town finds herself recruited to be a mill owner's bride in this historical romance."For those who like their romance emotionally complex and full of grit, Garlock holds the reins masterfully." —Publishers WeeklyIn the 1880s, Jane Love comes to Timbertown, Wyoming to escape her heritage and begin a new job and an independent new life. But upon her arrival, she discovers that she and nineteen other women were recruited, not to work, but to become wives of lumberjacks.
