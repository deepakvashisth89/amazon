Natural Gardening A-Z is an interactive information source intended to provide gardeners of all skill levels with an indispensable resource for identifying and treating a number of garden pests and diseases. Natural Gardening A-Z will also provide you with valuable information on seasonal treatment of your garden plants and trees. This book will provide you with the knowledge necessary to tend to your garden using natural plantfoods, soil conditioners,and pest and disease controls without the need toresort to the use of harmful and often polluting chemicals. Natural Gardening A-Z is intended as an alternative resource to theenvironmental hazards of gardening with conventional chemical products. When you use the advice given in this book on your own garden spaces, you will gain satisfaction in growing healthier plants, flowers and foodswithout chemical residues.
