In 2020, a swept-wing, nuclear-powered aircraft takes off on a runway at Cape Canaveral, Florida. It's three-person crew, commanded by a beautiful, young pilot, heads for Mars on a routine flight, but several days into the flight, they pick up signals from a mysterious source in space. As they near Mars, they are stunned to idendify the radio signal as coming from the lost Lunar Observer, a space ship that disappeared two years ago. NASA orders the commander not to attempt a rescue because it would risk more lives, but after they hear the desperate voices, their conscience tears at them, and they decide to take a vote, knowing that if they vote to go after the downed crew, they could be tried for mutiny, that is, if they ever get back.Read more
