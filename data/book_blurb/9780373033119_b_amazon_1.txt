"Give that man an inch and he thinks he's a ruler!"When the host of the Kansas City Morning TV show has a heart attack, the station manager brings in traveling feature reporter Quinn Randolph to "help out" the co-host, Emily Lambert. But soon Quinn isn't helping... he's taking over. And Quinn's idea of fun is making Emily's life a disaster zone.
