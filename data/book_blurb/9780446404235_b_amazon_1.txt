Hired as a bodyguard for diva Bette Davis, this wisecracking Hollywood PI better fasten his seat belt, it's going to be a bumpy night.Bette Davis has three words to describe her hotel hideaway:"What a dump!"After two days locked in a rented room with the acid-tongued actress, private eye Toby Peters is starting to feel likehe'sher husband—instead of Arthur Farnsworth—and he wants a divorce. The diva's real hubby—an aeronautics engineer with a head full of government secrets and a gang of blackmailers on his trail—has hired Peters to keep Bette safe from kidnappers. But when thugs burst through the hotel room door, it's almost a relief.Almost . . .Because Peters still has a job to do.
