Daddy's Girl is a true story--a story which required incredible courage to live, and an even greater courage to write. This book stands as a landmark in publishing, a work which explores with devastating honesty and impressive literary skill the awesome dimensions and long-term effects of the abuse which one father visited upon his child. More important, it is an inspiring story of triumph, of a singular woman's successful fight to replace shame and terror with a wholly earned self-respect.
