Jarrell, Director, National Hurricane CenterIn October 1998, the majestic schoonerFantomecame face-to-face with one of the most savage storms in Atlantic history. The last days of theFantomeare reconstructed in vivid and heartbreaking detail through Jim Carrier's extensive research and hundreds of personal interviews.
