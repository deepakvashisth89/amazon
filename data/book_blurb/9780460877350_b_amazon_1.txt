"The Time Machine: The Original 1895 Unabridged and Complete Edition" by H.G. Wells is a science fiction masterpiece that introduced the concept of time travel. The novel follows a scientist, known only as "The Time Traveller," who invents a machine that allows him to journey into the distant future. There, he discovers a world divided between the peaceful Eloi and the subterranean Morlocks.
