A dangerous deception.A marriage of convenience.A love that defies the past.When spinster Norah Linton answers a widower’s tender letters, she dares to dream of love at last. But upon arriving in Ireland, she discovers the truth—the heartfelt words that captured her were written not by Sir Aidan Kane, but by his mischievous daughter. Worse still, Aidan is no grieving gentleman, but a notorious rake rumored to have murdered his first wife.Aidan Kane has no intention of surrendering his bachelor ways. Yet when he learns Norah is the granddaughter of a revered general, he sees a chance to protect his beloved daughter from society’s scorn. A devil’s bargain is struck: a marriage for duty, not love.But as whispers of murder rise again and enemies close in, Norah and Aidan find themselves bound by more than convenience. To survive, they must uncover the truth of the past—while risking their hearts to a love neither expected.Originally published as Stealing HeavenRead more
