Combining sensationalism with acute psychological insight, this masterpiece of Gothic fiction is a powerful exploration of how violent and sexual impulses can break through the barriers of social and moral restraint.Savaged by critics for its supposed profanity and obscenity, and bought in large numbers by readers eager to see whether it lived up to its lurid reputation,The Monkbecame asuccès de scandalewhen it was published in the 18th century—not least because its author was a member of parliament and only twenty years old. It recounts the diabolical decline of Ambrosio, a Capuchin superior, who succumbs first to temptations offered by a young girl who has entered his monastery disguised as a boy, and continues his descent with increasingly depraved acts of sorcery, murder, incest and torture.This edition is based on the first edition of 1796, which appeared before Lewis’s revisions to avoid charges of blasphemy.
