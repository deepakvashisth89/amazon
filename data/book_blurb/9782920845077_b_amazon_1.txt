One of the greatest joys in life is good food.' Spoken like a true Frenchman, which Chef Pol Martin most definitely is. ... Despite his European influence, Chef Pol believes that simple techniques best capture the natural flavors and goodness of the ingredients used. He insists that exquisite cooking is perfectly possible without devoting endless time to preparation, and certainly, does not require the mastery of professional skills.
