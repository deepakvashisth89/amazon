Sixteen years ago, Luke Ryan, the love her her life, betrayed Bethany Martin and broke her heart. She's never gone home to Rangely since, and never will. Until she gets a call that her father is dying and is asking for her. She has to go, if for no other reason than to ask why he sent her and her mother away all those years ago, why he, too, had stopped loving her. She is not at all prepared for what she finds.Luke Ryan is stunned that Beth actually came home to see her father. And he's stunned to realize that for the past sixteen years, he's been lying to himself. In truth, he never stopped loving her. Then came the biggest shock, in the form of the fifteen-year-old young man at her side. The young man who could be none other than Luke's own son. And she'd never told him? The Beth he remembered would never keep father and son apart. After all these years, can he finally learn the truth? Can he fight his way back into her arms?
