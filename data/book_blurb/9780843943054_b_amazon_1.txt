Jarrett is a warrior. Brave and bold, he belongs to the battlefield, but Fate has other plans. Captured by his enemies, tortured for their amusement, he longs for the death that is denied him by the Maje who heals his broken body night after night. Leyla’s gentle touch restores his hope and her tenderness mends his damaged heart.
