The fourth novel by internationally-bestselling author, Hilary Norman.Set in New York City, Zurich and Paris, FASCINATION is the story of Maddy Gabriel, an exquisite jeweled sculpture and the havoc it wreaks on three generations of her family. Eternité was created by Maddy’s grandfather as an expression of love, but to some it has become a treasure priceless enough to kill for. To Maddy and those she cherishes most, it comes to represent anguish and terror.‘An elegantly written tale.’ Daily Telegraph‘An engrossing novel.’ Glasgow Evening Times‘Hilary Norman has developed quite a following, and Fascination is sure to please her fans.’ Chicago TribuneRead more
