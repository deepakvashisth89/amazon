Capturing all the love and pride that a parent feels for a child, this book is an ideal for children who are grown, leaving home for the first time, or otherwise separated from their parents. It is a loving reminder of the special place they will always hold in their parent's memories and in their hearts. Written by a collection of parents, the poems are heartwarming, refreshing, and filled with tender loving care. From giggles to allowances to leaving the house, every parent watches their child learn to understand the world in a different way. This poetry collection is a wonderful gift for graduation or any special occasion where you as the parent may feel you need to share with your child just how much you love them.
