The “brilliant story” (Chicago Daily News) of Joe Kennedy Jr, JFK’s older brother and the one who was meant to be President, revealed in an eye-opening book which “can take its place with the best” (Washington Post).The story of the Kennedy name is one of wealth, power, success and honor. But behind this veneer of integrity and glamor lie dark truths and tragedies.Among the earliest disasters to strike the family—and one which had profound, world-changing consequences—was the death of Joe Kennedy Jr, JFK’s elder brother.As the firstborn son, Young Joe was his father’s first choice for the White House and was groomed for the role from boyhood. By 1944 he was a decorated war hero and, with a growing reputation at home and abroad, a stellar political career was all but guaranteed once WWII was over. But in the final stages of the conflict, Joe Jr was killed in action when his plane exploded in the skies over Europe.Now, inThe Lost Prince, bestselling novelist and screenwriter Hank Searls reveals the true story of the life of Joe Jr. Drawn from dozens of interviews and firsthand research,The Lost Princecompletes the picture of the Kennedy history and adds another layer of valuable context to one of the great dynastic stories of the 20th century.‘Someday I’ll be President’ Joseph P.
