"An avalanche of revelations about birds and mammals long believed to be noble paragons of monogamy . . . A lively look at what the latest research has revealed."―Los Angeles TimesApplying new research to sex in the animal world, esteemed scientists David P. Barash and Judith Eve Lipton dispel the notion that monogamy comes naturally. In fact, asThe Myth of Monogamyreveals, biologists have discovered that for nearly every species, cheating is the rule―for both sexes.Reviewing findings from the same DNA fingerprinting science employed in the courtroom, Barash and Lipton take readers from chickadee nests to chimpanzee packs to explain why animals cheat. (Some prostitute themselves for food or protection, while others strive to couple with genetically superior or multiple mates.)The Myth of Monogamythen illuminates the implications of these dramatic new findings for humans, in our relationships, as parents, and more.The Myth of Monogamyat last brings scientific insight into this emotionally charged aspect of the ultimate dating and marriage quandary.Read more
