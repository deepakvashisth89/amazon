A major reissue in one volume of two early books by one of our finest living poets. In an essay on his career, Edward Hirsch describesThey Feed They Lionas his "most eloquent book of industrial Detroit . . . The magisterial title poem--with its fierce diction and driving rhythms--is Levine's hymn to communal rage, to acting in unison."
