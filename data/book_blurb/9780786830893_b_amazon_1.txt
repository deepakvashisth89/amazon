Disney's 34th animated feature is one of literature's most touching and heroic stories. With its rich architectural beauty and lavish landscaping, The Hunchback of Notre Dame contains some of the most beautiful scenery in Disney history. Set in the picturesque Parisian streets of the 15th century, this romantic adventure tells the story of Quasimodo, a gentle and lonely hunchback, and his friendship with the caring gypsy woman, Esmeralda.
