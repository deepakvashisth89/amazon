"Exuberant, witty...the best Treasure for some time" THE TIMES Litchester Cathedral, usually an oasis of cloistered calm, suddenly finds itself at the centre of an acrimonious dispute over the proposed sale of its 1225 copy of the Magna Carta. Merchant banker Mark Treasure is invited down to sort out the squabbling cathedral chapter. Even as he journeys to Litchester a fire destroys the ecclesiastical library - Magna Carta and all - and a body is discovered in the debris. It wasn't fire that killed the Dean's verger. Treasure's murder investigation unravels evidence of some most unholy mayhem and dark skulduggery. Litchester is a place where sins and secrets abound...Read more
