The Oracle of Clarion is a mesmerizing, magical and powerful spiritual adventure novel that is hard to put down. Written is style that elicits rich visual imagery, the reader is presented with the intrigue of a good mystery and profound insights hidden within potent allegory that abounds throughout each chapter. Weaving a tapistry from the world's most taboo and controversial topics into a spell binding plot, The Oracle of Clarion quickly draws the reader into a journey that is fast paced, deeply thought provoking and even startling. The fastest growing segment of the New Age consciousness movement is Goddess Spirituality. The Oracle of Clarion unveils the truth of the Goddess and the transformative power inherent within the return of the Divine Feminine.
