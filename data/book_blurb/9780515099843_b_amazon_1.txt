Cassie Newton is spending the summer with her famous violinist uncle, Victor Mazzini. When he disappears before his concert, she is helped in her search by an American hardware store owner—or is he what he claims to be? A missing Stradivarius violin, an empty violin case, a heist from an Italian villa—all fit into this delightful puzzle.
