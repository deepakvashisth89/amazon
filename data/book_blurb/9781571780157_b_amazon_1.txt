America's popular "bread lady" opens her personal recipe box and shares a lifetime of favorite dishes. Many, like Fourteen Boy Curry, are souvenirs of world travel that became celebration food or family staples in the Gubser household. Some were handed down through generations of traditional farm cooking. And others - Baked Eggs with Herbed Cheese Sauce, for example - were perfected under special circumstances, such as galley cooking aboard the Gubser family houseboat.
