An adrenaline-pumping, pulse-pounding legal thriller. . .Chris Lang isn't just a gifted trial lawyer—he's a courtroom grand master, poised to undertake the most challenging case of his career.Russell Lindstrom, the target of the lawsuit, is a billionaire entrepreneur who has just declared his bid to run for President. But beneath the glitz of high-stakes politics lies a web of deceit, international intrigue, and corporate treachery, including the prospect of murder, shrouded in shadows waiting to be unveiled.This courtroom thriller is a relentless rollercoaster that catapults Chris from his plush LA law firm into the breathtaking landscapes of the California’s Central Coast, the enigmatic Gulf Coast of Texas, to the rugged cliffs of western Ireland.The grand finale unfolds before the eyes of a breathless nation, a tumultuous jury trial broadcast live around the world, coinciding with Lindstrom's presidential nomination. While Lindstrom vehemently denies any involvement in the design defects of the flawed aircraft at the heart of the case, Chris Lang is convinced of his malevolent orchestration and the audacious cover-up concealing it.What unfolds is not just a trial—it's a winner-take-all, internationally televised showdown that grips the world throughout a scorching summer.
