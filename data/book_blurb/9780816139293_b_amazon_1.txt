The Secret of Father Brown is the Fourth book in the series of cases involving the eccentric yet competent detective. There are Ten Father Brown mysteries in this collection, “The Secret of Father Brown", “The Mirror of the Magistrate", "The Man with Two Beards", "The Song of the Flying Fish", "The Actor and the Alibi", "The Vanishing of Vaudrey", "The Worst Crime in the World", "The Red Moon of Meru", "The Chief Mourner of Marne" and "The Secret of Flambeau".Read more
