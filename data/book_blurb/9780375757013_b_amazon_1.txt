Great Expectations by Charles Dickens is a classic coming-of-age novel that explores themes of ambition, social class, and personal growth. The story follows the life of Pip, an orphan who is unexpectedly provided with wealth and an opportunity to rise in society, only to discover that true happiness and fulfillment lie not in material wealth but in personal integrity and human connection. With rich, memorable characters such as the mysterious Miss Havisham and the convict Magwitch, Dickens paints a vivid picture of Victorian England.
