Meet America's newest little hero, Migraine Boy--Charlie Brown's bi-polar second cousin, an existentially aware Richie Rich. Spawned in the pages of alternative 'zines and giveaway newspapers, Migraine Boy got his big break when R.E.M. chose cartoonist Fiering to do the art for the cover of Monster.
