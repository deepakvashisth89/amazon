In her debut, Caroline Heens, with Scout's help, brings readers a hilarious, interactive step-by-step guide to dogs.Scout may look like an average dog, but what other dog do you know that could teach readers all about himself and all our other canine friends? From the tip of his nose to the tip of his tail, Scout is full of information. But like most dogs, he's also full of fleas!Heens's kooky art portrays Scout in all his different moods, doing all sorts of dog things. Readers will love the funny lift-the-flaps, and the scary centerfold pop-up of Scout's worst enemy--feline, of course. Woven in are lots of dog facts, like how often they need to be fed, bathed, and taken to the vet.Read more
