Set in New York City, A Hazard of New Fortunes follows the lives of several characters as they navigate the challenges and temptations of the modern, capitalist world. The novel revolves around Basil March, an intellectual and idealistic writer, and his wife, Charlotte, who move to New York in search of new opportunities. Basil is hired by a newly founded magazine, The Modern World, which aims to tackle serious social issues through journalism, but he soon finds himself caught in the complex and often morally ambiguous world of publishing, politics, and business.The novel provides a broad portrait of New York's social landscape, bringing together a diverse cast of characters—from wealthy industrialists and journalists to working-class immigrants and artists. As Basil interacts with various figures in this complex web, he grapples with questions of personal integrity, social responsibility, and the pursuit of success. The magazine becomes a microcosm of society at large, where the clash between idealism and commercial interests, personal ambition and collective good, is played out on a daily basis.In addition to Basil’s personal journey, the book explores the lives of Fulkerson, the ambitious and somewhat unscrupulous editor of The Modern World, and Marcia Gaylord, a wealthy woman with her own set of ideals and social concerns.
