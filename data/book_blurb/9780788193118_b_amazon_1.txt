Get swept up in a passion for the world's finest coffees as you learn the brewing secrets of Starbucks, North America's leading roaster & retailer of specialty coffee beans. Olsen takes you on a journey of discovery highlighting coffee's fascinating history & lore, agriculture & commerce. Expert advice guides you in making your own selections from the world's best coffees. And simple, step-by-step instructions teach you how to brew the perfect cup of coffee every time.
