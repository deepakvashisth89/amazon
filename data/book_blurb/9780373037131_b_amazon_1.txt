She'll fake it until she makes it. Nothing gets in the way of a woman determined to find love in this fun, flirty, and feel-good romance.Freya is determined to sort out her life before she hits thirty—if only to prove to her best friend, Max Thornton, that she's perfectly capable of meeting a suitable man! Freya has the ideal candidate in mind: a gorgeous hotshot reporter . . .Max isn't impressed—not least because Freya is staying in his penthouse. Freya has always been impulsive, but even Max is surprised when Freya decides to fake her own wedding to claim a honeymoon prize!
