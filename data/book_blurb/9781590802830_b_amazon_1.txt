What waits beyond the meadow?A strange nightmare connects a man, a woman, and a fog-shrouded city. A body lies buried beneath a bathtub. A little girl sees her dead cat. An old blind woman knows more than she should. And a beautiful young woman, dead too soon, may not be as gone as she seems.InCrossing the Meadow, the dead move quietly among the living, brushing against their lives, nudging their fates, and searching for the thing that will let them move on.Eerie, mysterious, and quietly unsettling, this award-winning ghost story leads readers into a twilight place where unfinished lives linger—and where crossing over is never as simple as it seems.Voted “Best Horror Novel” in the 2003 P&E Reader’s Poll.Read more
