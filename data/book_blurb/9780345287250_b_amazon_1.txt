Turnabout is a gripping psychological thriller by Margaret Peterson Haddix that explores the complex themes of identity, memory, and morality. The story follows the lives of Melly and Anny Beth, two teenagers who wake up in each other's bodies after a bizarre accident. As they struggle to navigate their new lives and adjust to their swapped identities, they uncover shocking secrets about each other and the people around them. With their own memories fading and conflicting emotions surfacing, Melly and Anny Beth must unravel the mystery behind their transformation before it's too late.
