This is the first volume of a series where readers help develop the story. Using the instant interaction of social media and the quick turn around time from self publishing, you, the reader will be able to interact with the story in a way that could never be done before.Readers will be able to post feedback on what has been released and suggest what they would like to see in future volumes. Everything from character and location names, new characters and traits, even vote on an up coming story line. Using the latest technology will allow an interactive story involving not only the author, but the reader as well. Each volume takes under an hour to read. Vote on the story and read the results in a matter of weeks.The raunchy comedy drama series features David Allen, a twenty four year old who has done very well for himself since graduating high school. With a lot of luck and help from his friends, David managed to fumble his way into a highly successful business that he started near the end of his junior year.Now, six years later, David has a problem. A recent law passed by the state of Colorado now threatens his livelihood. The voters passed legalizing marijuana and his clients will no longer need his services in a few short months. David’s business is, ironically, still on the wrong side of the law.Together with his girlfriend Lori and his best friend Chet, David tries to figure out how to become a legal pot dealer in the new world of Government bureaucracy and oversight. He knows how to maneuver around gangs and rival drug dealers.
