Princess Teresa of Calferney decided to hit the road, but she never meant do so this literally! Taking a rare trip, she finds herself stranded on the side of the road, and when a driver swerves to miss her, she hits the dirt・・・and is knocked unconscious. Thankfully, local doctor Dylan Parker takes care of her, but her newly lost memories might prove problematic. Or they might if she weren't faking the memory loss. Delighted by the small town she has found herself in (and the attentions of Doctor Parker), Teresa tries to enjoy the quiet life while her car is being repaired. Problem is, paparazzi seem to follow her to the ends of the earth. Can she find a quiet and happy life with Dylan, or will she be drawn back to the kingdom she's fled?Read more
