High school student Juli Scott meets tragedy head-on when her father goes missing one cold, winter night. Her life is immediately turned upside down as she must also move to a new school in the aftermath of tragedy. She feels watched and followed constantly, and begins seeing a mysterious figure in the shadows that somehow no one else can see.
