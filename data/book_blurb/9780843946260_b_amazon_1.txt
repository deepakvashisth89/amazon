A chilling parable about three troubled cousins who confront a terrible conspiracy in their hometown.Welcome Back to the Night, cuts straight into the darkest heart of humanity. Unflinchingly compassionate and eerily relevant to these tumultuous times."A family reunion should be a joyous occasion.Three cousins reunite at the reunion, each with different goals for attending. Bernie Lynch, new a member of a spiritual commune, wants the family to know her as she is now, not as she was. Lindsay Hollin, mother of two young children, feels obligated to let her girls meet the multitude of relatives. Norris Lynch, a struggling, troubled history teacher, hopes he can squeeze some desperately needed money from his wealthy grandparents.But the reunion sets the stage for a terrifying connection between the cousins and a deranged woman who, for a brief time, had been part of the family. Bernie, Lindsay, and Norris’ minds and souls become fused with the woman’s, revealing a dark, chilling vision of a tortured past, a tormented present, and a deadly future not just for them, but for their entire hometown.
