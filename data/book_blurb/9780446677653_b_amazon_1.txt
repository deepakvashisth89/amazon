Your cell phone dies. Your package never arrives. Your signed contract turns into a disaster. You fight with friends and lovers and have the feeling you're just not getting through. But it's not happening to you alone. Phone companies and computer help desks across the country receive more repair calls during Mercury retrograde than at any other time during the year. Approximately four times a year, Mercury, the planet of communication, goes retrograde-appears to move backward-and chaos ensues. Until now.
