If you like humor, stories about people, or you're an aviation enthusiast, you'll enjoy reading Pie In The Sky. If you ever took flying lessons, you may be in this book. Laugh with Wallace as the big-city flying instructor learns that the riches to be found running a small town flying school are in the characters and the memories.
