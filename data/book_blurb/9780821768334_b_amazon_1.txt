A trio of romantic tales follow three reluctant brides--Anne Moore, who finds herself attracted to her childhood crush Jud Cammeron, even though she is engaged to someone else; Tina Merritt, who tries to deny her feelings for Dirk Tanger; and Tara Schmitt, who has vowed to dedicate her life to her career and ignore the advances of sexy Alek Rykovsky--as they confront many challenges and temptations before they succumb to wedded bliss.
