Leicestershire, 1814. When Rachel, maid to Joseph Pedley and his family, witnesses the burning of her employer's corn rick, she realises her own life could be in danger. Starvation and revolution are in the air. She comes face to face with the young fire setter, Jed Fairfax, but along with her growing attraction for him is the fear he might be one of General Ludd's men and part of a gang of machine breakers.
