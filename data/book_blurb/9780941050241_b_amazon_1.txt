The author exhibits a thorough insight into every aspect of the role of the cantor in liturgy as it is exercises today in its most developed state. He treats understanding the role, the responsibilities, the qualifications & the skills needed by the cantor. He analyzes each form of repertoire encountered, & details the best approach. This book is a must read for every parish cantor, & every trainer & coordinator of parish cantors. It offers direction to the beginner & a refresher for those with experience.
