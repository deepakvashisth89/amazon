"Grain" by Robert Stead delves into the heart of southern Manitoba, where young Gander Stake finds solace in the prairie's abundant wildlife and the promise of modern machines transforming his world. Amidst the backdrop of World War I, Stake navigates the complexities of family and young love, pondering the significance of the grain fields in shaping a nation's identity, questioning if the battle for prosperity at home is as vital as the conflicts raging in Europe.
