W.E.B. Du Bois's classic collection of essays about the experiences of African-Americans. The book is a foundational work in the history of sociology and a seminal tome in the development of African-American Studies. The essays include: The Forethought, Of Our Spiritual Strivings, Of the Dawn of Freedom, Of Mr. Booker T. Washington and Others, Of the Meaning of Progress, Of the Wings of Atalanta, Of the Training of Black Men, Of the Black Belt, Of the Quest of the Golden Fleece, Of the Sons of Master and Man, Of the Faith of the Fathers, Of the Passing of the First-Born, Of Alexander Crummell, Of the Coming of John, Of the Sorrow Songs, and The Afterthought.Read more
