Time Passages is a series of commemorative yearbooks, available for each year from 1940 through 2000. This collectible gift of memories offers a day-by-day, month-by-month snapshot of popular culture events including sports, world events, politics, television, movies and fashion. Full color, easily recognizable photography enhances the nostalgic experience.
