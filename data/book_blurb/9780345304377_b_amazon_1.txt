Jeffrey Konvitz'sNew York Times–bestselling horror novel about a young woman descending into demonic madness who discovers it's not simply in her mindAspiring model Allison Parker finally moves into her dream apartment: a brownstone on Manhattan's Upper West Side. But her perfect home quickly turns hellish.The building is filled with a cast of sinister tenants, including a reclusive blind priest, who seems to watch her day and night through an upstairs window. Eventually, Allison starts hearing strange noises from the empty apartment above hers.
