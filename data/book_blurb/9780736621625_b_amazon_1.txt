In Moscow, at a small British trade fair, documents change hands: military secrets that can alter the course of events in the age of glasnost and perestroika. It can destroy the people it touches -- particularly Barley Blair.A drinker, a saxophonist, a derelict publisher, Blair is perplexed to find the papers addressed to him and reluctant to front for British Intelligence. Their invitation to heroism lacks appeal. What does appeal is Katya Orlova, the lovely intermediary who offers Blair the "gift of trust.""THE RUSSIA HOUSE is faster and leaner than anything le Carre has done in years...a taut spy story embracing a fine romance."
