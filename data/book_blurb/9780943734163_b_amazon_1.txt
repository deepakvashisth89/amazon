Mahatma Gandhi's remarkably simple principles apply to today's worldwide problems of exploitation, injustice and poverty, just as they did in India's successful struggle for independence. Here for the first time the Gandhian steps are spelled our simply and precisely. Included in this information-packed guidebook are examples of problems and their solutions, recommendations for practical action, highlights of Gandhi's life and the Nonviolent Movement, and specific goals for the future.Read more
