JESSICA JENKINS FEELS STRANGELY TROUBLED.Memories flit through her head like moths, but which of them are real? Sick with glandular fever, she remembers moving house, the arguments between Mum and Dad, the woods where she found refuge and the girl from the past who called her a 'spirit'. But did the girl summon Jess or did Jess summon her?Sliding between the present day and Victorian times, Jess finds a new friend but also makes some shocking discoveries in this intriguing time-slip story.Read more
