This text provides a guide to creating the perfect outdoor space which invigorates all five senses. The fragrance of different herbs can change the way we feel and stimulate or relax. In this book, the author shows that herbs can fulfil their potential and work in harmony with other plants and garden features to offer an atmospheric setting for personal enjoyment. It offers a complete guide to growing and cultivating herbs indoors and out and features eight fully illustrated designs for gardens to enhance moods, including an open-air breakfast area and a romantic garden. All well-known herbs are included, as are many unexpected plants now classified as herbs, such as peony, lily and moss.Read more
