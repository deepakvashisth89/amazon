When newspaper reporter Johnny Rose covers the discovery of a body in the middle of the Mojave Desert - the boy of a POW still listed as MIA in Vietnam, he enters a world of deceit, unparalleled ambition, and power. Set in Los Angeles in the late 1990s, Blue Rain centers on a key question: If someone is really brining POWs home from Vietnam, why is it a secret? But if its all a mistake, why are the people who claim to have seen them being murdered?Blue Rain is a fast-paced thriller with twists and turns that will keep the reader turning the pages but at its heart, it's a book about honor and family.Read more
