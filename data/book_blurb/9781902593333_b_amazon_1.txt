Benjamin Zephaniah dedicated this collection of 22 new poems to "the caring, dedicated young vegans of the world...who will not stand for any exploitation whatever the species." Filled with the unique "radical rapper" poems that Zephaniah is famous for, this book also includes an extensive contact list of international vegan and animal rights organizations.Benjamin Zephaniah, born in Birmingham, England, in 1958, is a remarkably popular performer, poet, and political protestor in the UK as well as a very vocal advocate for veganism.
