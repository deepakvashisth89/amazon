Throughout the vastness of space, heroic beings engage in a desperate struggle against the overwhelming might of the Empire. They are ill-equipped and pitifully few, but their courage and determination have fostered new hope for freedom in the galaxy. These are the men, women, and aliens of the Rebel Alliance. Includes game statistics for the characters, droids, weapons and equipment of the Rebel Alliance.This 144-page book features: In-depth examination of the birth and early growth of the Rebellion. Illustrations, photographs, maps, and diagrams. Detailed report of the Alliance military and political hierarchy Data on Alliance Intelligence.
