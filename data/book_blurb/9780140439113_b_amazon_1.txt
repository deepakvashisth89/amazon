The Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde: And Other Tales of Terror (Clothbound Classics) is a chilling collection of gothic fiction by Robert Louis Stevenson. The centerpiece is the famous novella Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde, a psychological thriller that explores the duality of human nature, as the respectable Dr. Henry Jekyll transforms into the monstrous Edward Hyde. The book also includes additional tales of horror and suspense, showcasing Stevenson’s mastery of creating eerie atmospheres and complex characters.
