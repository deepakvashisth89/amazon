The Angels gave Quesnel a sense of belonging he had been searching for-and contracts to eliminate several of their rivals. In all he murdered 5 men. When Quesnel was betrayed to the police by an old friend, he had no choice but to sell out his "brothers" in a lucrative exchange for a new identity, and reduced prison sentence.
