To Laney, With Love,I know you have questions but I can explain. Meet me at The Rendevous, Valentine's Day. Make this our little secret.—R.The note stunned Laney Dobson. Before she could move on, Laney had to know if her husband was still alive. So she turned to her son's coach, Ben Forbes, and together they searched for answers—and found unexpected desire.But Laney also found her husband—newly murdered! She sought refuge in Ben's arms, his lips…his body, and despite her fear, dared to dream of love again.But Ben feared Laney and the boy he thought of as his own were still at risk.
