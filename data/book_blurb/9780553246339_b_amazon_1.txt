Our mission: seek out intelligent life there and, if it exists, offer full Federation protection.To help us complete the necessary surveys, Dr.  Katalya Tremain was assigned to the U.S.S. EnterpriseTM.  She is the Federation's foremost expert on the exobiology of this region--and, as I have just discovered, has a fanatical hatred of any and all things Vulcan...  including my first officer.I have logged an official protest with Starfleet Command.
