WWJD. You've seen it everywhere - on bracelets, dog tags, key chains, T-shirts, hats, even backpacks! But what does that mean? WWJD is an acronym for "What Would Jesus Do?" Every day we have a choice. To do things God's way or the world's way. Jesus is our example to follow, and He only did those things. He saw God the Father doing. (See John 5:19) answers to: What Would Jesus Do? provides simple reminders of how Jesus lived His life on earth. In this journal version, you are given the space to write down your thoughts, experiences and insights in a format that enables you to reflect on these memories for years to come. You can also write in Scriptures that confirms the attitude Jesus desires to be formed in us so that we can experience success in everything we do. 1 Peter 3:15 Paul exhorted Peter with these words: "Through thick and thin, keep your hearts at attention, in adoration before Christ, your Master. Be ready to speak up and tell anyone who asks why you're living the way you are." (The Message) Be challenged with the same words today. Think about how Jesus wants you to live in every situation - before it happened. Take time to look through these pages and find a new area in which to challenge yourself to be more like Jesus.
