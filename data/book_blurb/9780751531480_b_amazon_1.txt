Emily Boyce, daughter of a pretentious Cornish parson, incurs her father's wrath by falling in love with young Sam Hooper, a copper miner on Bodmin Moor. So when the moorland mines fail, her father ensures that Sam goes to seek a new life in South Australia. Emily is not to be thwarted, however, and when her father dies suddenly, she finds herself free to go to Australia and hunt down Sam. Her search takes her first to the Copper Triangle, then to the magnificent Flinders Ranges and, finally, to the beautiful Adelaide Hills.
