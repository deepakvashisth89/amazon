The world of the Louisiana werewolves...Sensual and seductive, it holds secrets that only the initiated may share. Daughter of a cursed father who has hidden his secret from her, Sylvie Marley is drawn to the moonlit bayous, to her lover Lucien, and to a choice between the debutante's life she knows and the werewolf's life she craves. But for Sylvie there is a hidden danger: a madman must have her power -- the power she is unaware she possesses -- to satisfy his desire to control the New Orleans Voodoos through profane black magic. All he needs is Sylvie's total surrender. And one act of ritual murder.Join the werewolves of the Voodoo Moon Trilogy in a fast-paced, decadent saga that sweeps from ancient Rome to 18th century France to the corrupt popes of the Italian Renaissance, and to the hypnotic and haunted New Orleans of yesterday and today."...a dark and powerful tale of death, curses, and Louisiana werewolves...fascinating!" -- Romantic Times"Draws the reader seductively into its embrace." -- Ray Garton, author of LIVE GIRLS and SEX AND VIOLENCE IN HOLLYWOODTHE VOODOO MOON TRILOGY is Winner of the Romantic Times Career Achievement Award for Contemporary Fantasy.Read more
