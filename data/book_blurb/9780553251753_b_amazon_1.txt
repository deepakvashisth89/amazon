Sophomores Harry and Hortense are convinced they're the only enlightened people in their messed-up, miserable high school, which Harry's nicknamed Hormone High. When Harry and Hortense meet Jason Rohr, he confides in them and asks them to help him achieve his secret mission. Then Harry and Hortense discover some horrifying information about Jason's past.
