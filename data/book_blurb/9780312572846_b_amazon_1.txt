This moral tale tells the story of King Midas, who is granted a wish by the god Dionysus. Midas, driven by greed, wishes that everything he touches turns to gold. At first, this new power seems like a blessing, as he turns objects, food, and even his own wealth into gold. However, the golden touch soon becomes a curse when Midas accidentally turns his daughter into gold with a single touch. Devastated, he realizes the folly of his wish and pleads for the ability to reverse it. In the end, he learns that love and happiness cannot be bought with gold, and he must relinquish his powers to regain his family and peace of mind.
