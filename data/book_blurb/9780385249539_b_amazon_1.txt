Compared by The Los Angeles Times to A Clockwork Orange "several steps further into the nightmare," Alexander Stuart’s The War Zone follows its teenage narrator, Tom, whose world is torn apart as he stumbles upon a complex and intensely abusive relationship between his older sister, Jessie, and their father.The novel was turned into an award-winning and highly emotional film by Oscar-nominated actor/director, Tim Roth.
