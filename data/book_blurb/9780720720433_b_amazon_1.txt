Cats are living longer than ever before due to routine inoculations which have brought infectious diseases under control, improved nutrition and a better understanding by cat owners of the risks of the "urban jungle". Whilst most cat owners don't question whether good veterinary attention is nearby, there are occasions when emergencies might occur which need immediate attention - from choking and drowning, through premature kittening, to poisoning.
