With the publication ofThe Origins of the Kabbalahin 1950, one of the most important scholars of our century brought the obscure world of Jewish mysticism to a wider audience for the first time. A crucial work in the oeuvre of Gershom Scholem, this book details the beginnings of the Kabbalah in twelfth- and thirteenth-century southern France and Spain, showing its rich tradition of repeated attempts to achieve and portray direct experiences of God.The Origins of the Kabbalahis a contribution not only to the history of Jewish medieval mysticism, but also to the study of medieval mysticism in general.
