In post-revolutionary France, one forbidden love will defy an empire.Lyons, 1804.The silk capital of the world is flourishing once more — and so is the ambition of Gabrielle Roche, daughter of the powerful Maison Roche, one of Lyons' oldest silk houses. Gifted and determined, she dreams of designing the silks that will grace Napoleon’s palaces. But her father sees no place for a woman in business and forces her into a marriage with Émile Valmont, a man of quiet honour and little passion.On the morning of her wedding, Gabrielle’s carriage collides with a funeral procession — and with Nicolas Devaux, heir to her father’s oldest rival. Their eyes meet only once, yet the spark is undeniable. He is everything she should despise, yet cannot forget.As the silk houses of Roche and Devaux compete for imperial favour, Gabrielle and Nicolas are drawn together in secret, bound by a love as dangerous as it is impossible.War and jealousy conspire to keep them apart. But even as Napoleon’s cannons thunder across Europe, nothing can silence the passion that burns between them.From the looms of Lyons to the grandeur of the Emperor’s court,Tree of Goldis a sweeping tale of passion, ambition and resilience.
