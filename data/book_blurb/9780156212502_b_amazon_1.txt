Readers of Virginia Woolf will enjoy this compilation of short stories, ordered chronologically to highlight Woolf’s different creative periods.Woolf continually used stories and sketches to experiment with narrative models and themes for her novels. This collection of nearly fifty pieces brings together the contents of two published volumes,A Haunted HouseandMrs. Dalloway’s Party; a number of uncollected stories; and several previously unpublished pieces.
