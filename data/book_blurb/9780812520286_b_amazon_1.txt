Completely revised for ebook release, in this last volume the desperate fight to recover the Panarchy of the Thousand Suns is about to commence. Brandon hai-Arkad's throne remains in the hands of his enemy, Jerrod Eusabian of Dol’jhar. The fleet has been gathered, bolstered by the once outlawed Rifters; it's a race against time, before the Suneater, the mysterious station created by a long-gone race, powers up. Brandon must defeat Dol'jhar by destroying the Suneater, or face annihilation.Meanwhile, the woman he's come to love is on the Suneater, expected to use her psi skills to bring it to full power.
