Marcel Dupre, is condemned to the penal colony at French Guiana on the east coast of South America. While a convict, he writes an expose of life in the Foreign Legion and in the penal colony. He finally escapes by sea and reaches the U.S. where he hopes to have his book published. Agents of the shaky French government will do anything to destroy him and his expose.
