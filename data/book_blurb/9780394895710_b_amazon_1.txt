Illus. in full color. "CleverCave Boy,a.k.a. Harry, tells about his inventions--a bammer (a hammer) and a boomer (drums). To top if off, Harry is the only one who pleases Chief Grump on his birthday: Harry takes his newly invented wheel and makes a tricycle for the Chief.
