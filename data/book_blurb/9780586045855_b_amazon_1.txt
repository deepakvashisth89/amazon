The Booker Prize winner. "[One of] the top 10 books about the British in India . . . the book is a joy and makes an elegiac farewell to the Raj." —Ferdinand Mount,TheGuardianIn this sequel toThe Raj Quartet, Colonel Tusker and Lucy Smalley stay on in the hills of Pankot after Indian independence deprives them of their colonial status. Finally fed up with accommodating her husband, Lucy claims a degree of independence herself. Eloquent and hilarious, she and Tusker act out class tensions among the British of the Raj and give voice to the loneliness, rage, and stubborn affection in their marriage.Staying Onwon the Booker Prize in 1977 and was made into a motion picture starring Trevor Howard and Celia Johnson in 1979."Staying Onfar transcends the events of its central action . . .
