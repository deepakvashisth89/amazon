Originally published in 1988, St. Louis: Landmarks and Historic Districts is back in print and greatly expanded and revised. With updated information and more contemporary and historic photos of the city, this user-friendly guidebook is still the first and only of its kind. St. Louis:Landmarks and Historic Districts is the foremost resource on the city's sites, buildings and districts officially designated by the City of St. Louis or the National Register of Historic Places. Designed by Paradowski Graphic Design, this richly illustrated edition features a special open-flat binding and 12 full-color, full-page images introducing each chapter.
