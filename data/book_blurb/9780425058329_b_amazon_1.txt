Autumn, 1763. After the longest siege of the Indian Wars, Fort Detroit has struck an uneasy truce with the mighty Red Nations. In a spirit of celebration, Owen Sutherland makes beautiful Ella Bently his bride and together they forge a great frontier trading company. But their new love is shadowed by the fear that Pontiac's warriors will rise again, and that civil strife will imperil the greatest city in the young land called America.Read more
