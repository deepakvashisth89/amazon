Manhattan police are stymied by a serial killer’s eight victims who appear to have no commonality. The women’s ages range from twenty-two to sixty-four. Each had different looks, different ocupations. A different weapon was used for each murder. Even different methods. Once the killer is ritualistic, next he’s a butcher.Chief of detectives Bill Dacey cajoles his friend, former New York City top criminologist Kate Berman and husband Josh, ex medical examiner, to help.The killer, Carl Nasson, is wealty, physically powerful and brilliant. The Berman’s niece Jennifer is kidnapped by Nasson. Jennifer gets a cold which leads to her acquiring a husky voice, a trait which all Nasson’s victims had in common.
