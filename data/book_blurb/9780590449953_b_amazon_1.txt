Muffie, Elliot and Quentin Bullock are three rude, crude kids. They swear. They play horrible pranks. They burp. No one will ever baby-sit for them—not if they want to make it out alive. Then Mr. and Mrs. Bullock accidentally hire the meanest hag of a baby-sitter the kids have ever seen. She's almost as bad as they are.
