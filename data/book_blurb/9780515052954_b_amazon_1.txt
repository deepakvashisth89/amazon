Greg Lyon’s syndicated cartoons are loved by all, that is until Leo the Lion starts lampooning doctors. Told to take a hike, Greg decides he will and starts a cross-country walk from New York to California. But life isn’t fair…or is it? A bad fall near a small southern Indiana town takes him to Doctor Amy Fraser’s home/clinic. She loved the Lyon’s Pride cartoons until they turned on doctors. She thinks the artist needs to spend some time with doctors, then he’d see what a doctor’s life is really like.Greg decides to take her up on that challenge, but without letting her know he is the artist. After all, he’s sure she’s just like all the doctors who treated his hypochondriac mother.As his broken ankle heals, Greg gets to know Doc Amy, sees how she treats her patients and deals with her own physical scars and emotional wounds. The longer he’s with her, the harder it is for either of them to ignore the sparks. But the question remains: What will Amy do when she discovers who he really is?Read more
