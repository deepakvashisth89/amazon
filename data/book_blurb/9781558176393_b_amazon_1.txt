Taylor returns with her bestselling Romantic Science Fiction series which is packed with action, adventures, deceptions, perils, and passions. She takes readers worldwide from an endangered Earth to a Tri-Galaxy of multiple planets filled with fierce enemies, lies, plots and treacheries.Doctor Jana Greyson Triloni didn’t know what or who to believe. The last things she remembered were lying in the arms of her captor and prior owner Commander Varian Saar (Zar) on the starship Wanderlust after being rescued from his half-brother, archrival and fierce enemy Ryker Triloni. Had Varian confessed his love and proposed marriage before they fell asleep in each other’s arms? His sapphire gaze and words had convinced her he was finally revealing the truth. Had it all been delusions, hallucinations, side-effects of an alien disease? Drugs?After she had awakened from an alleged alien virus that had almost killed her, she was in the possession of her handsome, emerald-eyed new owner and husband, the richest, most powerful, most feared and respected scientist and chemist in the Universe. On his impregnable planetoid. Was she still a pawn in an intergalactic struggle to thwart enemies, an attempt to save Earth from a cataclysmic disaster by a rogue meteor, to give one of those alien beings an heir? Could she trust or hold on to either man when both were like blazing comets speeding across destiny’s Heaven, without being burned and scarred, or without being utterly consumed. Which brother was her Love of a Lifetime, and which was a Space Demon in disguise?
