In April 1996, a man was taken into custody by police for throwing debris into New Jersey's Passaic River. The trash bags actually contained 65 pieces of a human body. The victim was cancer researcher Dr. Yakov Gluzman--and his wife had ordered the killing. Now, Tim Hays reveals the shocking details behind this horrific crime of jealousy, greed, scorn, and revenge--and of a murderous woman who eluded prosecutors until justice finally caught up with her.
