"The Magic of Oz" by L. Frank Baum follows the adventures of Dorothy, Trot, Cap'n Bill, and their friends in the magical land of Oz. As they prepare for Ozma's birthday celebration, they encounter challenges from old foes seeking to conquer Oz using magic and cunning. With the help of the Wizard and the Cowardly Lion, they embark on a journey filled with surprises, transformations, and alliances to protect their beloved land.
