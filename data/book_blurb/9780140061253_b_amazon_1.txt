First published in 1963,A God and his Giftswas the last of Ivy Compton-Burnett's novels to be published in her lifetime and is considered by many to be one of her best. Set in the claustrophobic world of Edwardian upper-class family life, it is the story of the self-willed and arrogant Hereward Egerton. In his marriage to Ada Merton he maintains a veneer of respectability, but in other relationships he steps beyond the bounds of conventional morality with both comic and tragic results…Read more
