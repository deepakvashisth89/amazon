Flower Child is the moving memoir of a young woman's search for true love and spirituality during the sixties' in California. She will capture your heart as you share her magical, mystical adventures in the City of Angels, Haight Ashbury and a Maui Zendo commune.Through her encounters with Jim Morrison, Frank Zappa, the Iron Butterly and the Brotherhood of Eternal Love, she experiences both the light and the shadow side of Flower Power.Step through the looking glass and follow her from sacred LSD and peyote trips to the Sunset Strip fashion scene, from blissful Be-Ins and Love-Ins to deep inner awakenings. Take her hand, and let Flower Child lead you through the psychedelic powerhouse that will always be known as the Summer of Love.Read more
